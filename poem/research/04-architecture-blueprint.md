## 1. THE NUMBERS (stated up front, each justified)

- **N (line count) = 40.**
  Justification chain:
  1. **40 = 4 × 10**, and **4** is the *real* region-count of the corpus (4-region hub architecture, anchor-D-support). The poem is built as **4 hub-movements** (II–V) framed by an **opening (I)** and a **ring-closing (VI)** — but the *evidence body* is partitioned into 4, echoing the 4 regions.
  2. **40 keeps the golden pivot landing cleanly on an integer line near a movement seam**: ⌊0.618 × 40⌋ = **line 24** (exact 24.72), and its mirror-complement 40−24+1 = **line 17** — both fall at natural breath-points (see §2, §3).
  3. **40 yields an exact center at line 20** (= 40/2) and an exact 2-line center **20–21**, which lets the centroid be a self-mirroring *aḥad*-couplet echoing Q112's v.1==v.4 chiasm (anchor C).
  4. **40 is a Qur'anically resonant integer** (the forty nights of Mūsā, Q7:142; maturity *arbaʿīna sana*, Q46:15) — so the count is *thematically* native, not arbitrary, while its structural justification is the 4-region fact. (Resonance is a bonus; the load-bearing reason is the 4-region partition + clean golden/center arithmetic.)
  - **Rejected alternatives (discipline):** N=114 (too long for a qaṣīda; literalist). N=44 (golden pivot 27 is fine but center 22 doesn't sit on a couplet-seam and 44 has no region-anchor). A 28-line poem (≈114/4) was rejected: pivot 17 and center 14 collide with no real invariant and the tail can't breathe its compression-ramp.

- **Pivot (volta) line index = 24.** ⌊0.618 × 40⌋. The golden section. (See §2.)
- **Center line index = 20** (with the centroid realized as the **couplet 20–21**, mirroring Q112's 4-verse aḥad-bookend). (See §3.)
- **Movement partition** (see §5): **I** 1–6 · **II** 7–13 · **III** 14–19 · **IV (pivot zone)** 20/21 → 24 → 26 · **V** 27–34 · **VI** 35–40.
  The four *evidence/region* movements are II, III, V and the pivot-bridge IV — four bodies of matter around the central axis, the 4-region signature.

- **The anti-twin "split."** Anchor A is r ≈ −0.86 ≈ **6/7** in magnitude. The poem encodes this two ways, both real:
  1. **Texture rule, not a line-count gimmick:** across all 40 lines the poet maintains an *inverse* coupling — where the **sense** is most knotted and concentrated (the evidence/pivot core, lines ~14–26), the **rhyme-figure** is made to *vary* most (internal rhyme, shifting caesura, near-rhyme); where the **sense** opens out and simplifies (the centroid couplet and the serene close), the **rhyme** *locks* hardest (pure monorhyme). Meaning-density and rhyme-distance move in opposition — the law itself, written into the grain.
  2. **The 34/6 echo (optional, light):** of 40 lines, the qaṣīda's single sustained **monorhyme (rawiyy)** governs the whole; only ~**6** lines (the centroid couplet + the four pivot-hinge lines) are permitted a deliberate rhyme-*excursion* before snapping back — **34 locked : 6 free ≈ 0.86 : 0.14**, the anti-twin ratio made visible. Use only if it stays musical; the texture rule (1) is the real carrier.

## 2. THE GOLDEN-SECTION PIVOT (volta) — line 24

**Placement:** ⌊0.618 × 40⌋ = **line 24**. Its golden-conjugate at 0.382 falls near **line 15–16** (the quiet "first doubt" — where the listener first senses the order is *too* exact); line 24 is where that suspicion detonates into the thesis.

**What line 24 DOES:**
Up to line 23 the poem has been *describing* the order — naming the ring, the shortest path, the centroid, the tightening tail — in a tone of wonder. **Line 24 is the hinge from description to indictment.** It is the line that says, in effect:

> *"Number a mind that could plot this road, then bring me the pen that walked it — and could not."*

It pivots from *"behold the order"* to *"no human hand made this."* Everything before line 24 is **evidence**; everything after is **challenge / intimidation (taḥaddī)** and the ring-return.

**Why placing it at 0.618 matters (and is self-enacting):**
- The Fisher-Rao finding (anchor B) is precisely that the Book's order sits at a **near-optimal proportion** of the theoretical shortest path (ratio 1.107 — i.e., the order is *proportioned*, not maximal-brute, not random). The poem's own turn therefore lands at *the* proportion of antiquity, **φ**, so that the poem's architecture is itself "near-optimally proportioned." The claim ("the arrangement is mathematically proportioned") is demonstrated by the poem *being* proportioned at its most important joint.
- φ is the unique point where **part : whole :: smaller-part : larger-part** — self-similar division. Placing the volta there makes the poem's two limbs (1–23 evidence, 24–40 thesis+return) stand in the same ratio to the whole as the shorter limb stands to the longer: the poem *recurses on its own shape*, exactly as the corpus's order recurses (ring within ring; cf. the Juzʾ-30 partial-fractal finding, H-NEW-255).
- It lands **two lines after the centroid couplet (20–21)** and **just inside Movement IV**, so the turn feels *born from* the center of Oneness rather than bolted on: tawḥīd (center) → therefore inimitability (pivot). Theology drives the rhetoric.

## 3. THE CENTER HOLDS ONENESS — line 20 (couplet 20–21)

**Placement:** exact center of 40 = **line 20**; realized as the **couplet 20–21** so it can self-mirror.

**Content seed:** the thematic heart is **al-tawḥīd / Q112 al-Ikhlāṣ** — *qul huwa Allāhu aḥad*. The center declares that the One who is *aḥad* (Q112:1) and *ṣamad* (the eternal, self-sufficient) is the source of the order the poem is cataloguing: **the Book is one because its Author is One.** Unity of the text testifies to Unicity of its Speaker.

**How it self-enacts anchor C (three exact mirrorings):**
1. **It is the centroid because Q112 is the centroid.** Q112 al-Ikhlāṣ is empirically the corpus FR-centroid (rank 1/114, mean-distance 0.7592). The poem puts *its* statement of Oneness at *its* center of gravity. Structure mimics structure: the center of the poem is what the center of the Book is.
2. **The couplet is itself an *aḥad*-bookend chiasm.** Q112's own form has **v.1 == v.4 == *aḥad*** (the word "One" opens and closes the sūra; all four verses rhyme in dāl). The poem's center is therefore a **2-line ring-in-miniature**: line 20 and line 21 begin and end on the same word/sound (seed: open *aḥad* … close *aḥad*; or open "One" … return "One"), a tiny ABBA that rhymes the whole macro-ring in microcosm. The center is a *seed-crystal* of the poem's own ring composition.
3. **It is the still point of the anti-twin.** At the exact center, **meaning is at its simplest and most absolute** ("God is One") while the **rhyme locks to its purest monorhyme** — the one place where sense and sound are *both* at rest, the fulcrum about which anchor A's opposition swings. Everywhere else they pull apart; here, for two lines, they are still.

> The center is not the loudest line — it is the *quietest and most absolute*. The poem inhales to a whisper of pure tawḥīd at its heart, then the pivot (line 24) exhales the thesis.

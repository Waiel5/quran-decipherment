### al-Nūniyya al-Kubrā — the definitive (reflexive) version
A mukhaḍram-register qaṣīda on the inimitable order of the Qurʾān, in which the poet attempts to match the Book, fails in his own verse, and offers his failure as the proof. al-Kāmil · 30 abyāt · rhyme on nūn · meter & rhyme certified (7 independent ʿarūḍ passes).

> *Bismillāhi al-Raḥmāni al-Raḥīm.* The dare is turned on the poet himself: this very ode is the attempted *muʿāraḍa* that documents its own defeat. It claims neither to equal the Qurʾān nor to surpass the Muʿallaqāt — it stages, honestly, the gap between every human sign and the One Reality, with the Book as the single place that gap closes.

## ENGLISH
1. The landmarks of every abode are wind-worn — but the guidance is green, and the trace of revelation has stayed the treasured one.
2. How many poets attempted a binding like it — and their rhymes glanced off [the blade that would not bite], and the cheated one failed.
3. A sea you dive, and never reach its bed; the pearl, in the depths, has stayed the buried one.
4. I inherited the secret of speech, and drew it down to me — I imitate it, till the mind, dazzled, is mine no more.
5. I mustered my pens, bared my powers, shook out my meters, and stirred up the hoard.
6. The generations rode it, driving their grey camels — the night thick-dark, and the guide sure.
7. Station on station, ranked like the guard-stars, plaited in measure, wedded to the one true road.
8. Let the Pole-star swerve, let a waystation turn strange — by it a master-guide is lost, the laden train strays.
9. Till I halted at its gate, and [my reach] fell short — my powers gave out below me, and the prize grew unattainable.
10. Warp and weft were levelled even in its weaving — a melody, a meaning, then a binding close-woven.
11. They said: the meanings lie tossed in the road; the merit is in the casting of the word — the [real] content.
12. So when I burnished the word, its wellspring [of sense] thinned, and the meaning glanced away, and the eloquence came wounded.
13. And when I drew the meaning deep, its melodies failed, and the binding slackened, feeble.
14. Fire and rain — I could not gather the two in one cloak; and the join would not weld.
15. He is First, He is Last, He is One, the Eternal — on Him the blessed night has turned its wheel.
16. No peer, no rival; all the reaches ran out at the One, and toward Him the measured resolved.
17. From One, no binding of His can be copied — the Single is not imitated, its sanctuary withheld.
18. And inability before it is the proof itself: its like cannot be reached, and the Lord of eloquence is beyond all grasp.
19. It was no [mere] diverting that turned the speakers away — the failure is their own; the pretenders, a conjecture.
20. The wider the vision grows, the narrower the tongue that bears it — and all who know are language's prisoners.
21. Below the Named the meaning falls short — the word can only stay in debt.
22. The people counted the essence, then clung to its husks — and the folk, they are the witless.
23. They counted the openers and the letters, and overlooked the [Book's] binding — then the crown of the nūn slipped away.
24. But here the named is welded to its name, the word to its meaning — neither severed.
25. Alive with the secret of the ancients [is the Book]; while the scrolls of those [counters] have worn and dried — their abode become a haunted ruin.
26. So I fell — not from defeat alone, but as a prostration; my incapacity is my worship, and the brow ground to the dust.
27. I did not adorn the Book with verse — rather it adorned the verse; so it was written, and given a home.
28. From it the chapters blazed, and at their sealing came round, as the full moon to the old bent stalk.
29. Yours, my God, the pearls — You raise them from the deep; and I am only the drowned man, grieving.
30. And I came back to the door of the start when I'd sealed it, and read it: the seal is a beginning, cradled.

## THE INTELLIGENCE (why this version)
- **The proof is etymological, not argued.** *iʿjāz* (inimitability) and *ʿajz* (the poet's incapacity) are one root (ع-ج-ز); *iʿjāz* = "the act of rendering-incapable." To be visibly unable before the Book IS to demonstrate its inimitability — bayt 18: *وَالعَجْزُ عَنْهُ هوَ الدَّليلُ* ("inability before it is the proof itself").
- **The poem is a failed *muʿāraḍa* that documents its own defeat** — the empirical form of the classical proof *tark al-muʿāraḍa maʿa tawaffur al-dawāʿī* (no rival produced despite every motive). Bayts 11–14 stage al-Jāḥiẓ's "the meanings lie in the road; the merit is in the diction" — and the poet shows the tradeoff snap in his own lines (perfect the music → the sense thins; deepen the sense → the music limps).
- **al-Jurjānī's *naẓm*** is the classical name for the anti-twin: eloquence is the *binding* of meaning and form, "with no formula that fences it in" — which is why it cannot be counterfeited (bayt 17).
- **al-Nafarī's law gives it cosmic breadth:** *كُلَّما اتَّسَعَتِ الرُّؤْيَةُ ضاقَتِ العِبارَةُ* — the wider the vision, the narrower the expression (bayts 20–21). The poet's failure is a special case of a universal law: every sign is smaller than its referent; the Book is the one place the name becomes the named (bayt 24).
- **The turn rides one verb: خَرَّ** — to *collapse* (defeated) and to *fall prostrate* (in worship) at once (bayt 26). Defeat re-read as submission. The ladder closes on Ḥassān's reversal (the Book ennobles the verse, not the verse the Book — bayt 27) and al-Mutanabbi's pearl-line redirected to God (bayt 29).
- **The nūn-reveal is demoted to one beat (bayt 23)** — one more husk the counters chased — instead of the climax.

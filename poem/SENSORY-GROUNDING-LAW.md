### "Strike cold like the Muʿallaqāt; carry the skeleton they never had."

The poem's flaw (diagnosed by the patron + the balāgha judge, independently): it **states** its order in abstractions (النظام، التناسب، العدد، الرقم، النظم، المتكلِّف) instead of **enacting** it through a body. The Muʿallaqāt never name their subject — they show camel, sword, rain, ruin, night, and you *feel* the meaning before you parse it. This poem must do the same: the findings become the **substrate** (felt), never the **surface** (stated).

## THE TEST EVERY LINE MUST PASS
**The Cold-Ear Test:** would this line strike a listener who knows *nothing* of the thesis (geodesic, anti-twin, nūn-frequency, false trails, loop)? If the line only works once the system is explained, it FAILS and must be re-incarnated as an image.

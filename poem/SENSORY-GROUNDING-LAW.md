# SENSORY-GROUNDING LAW — the v2 pivot
### "Strike cold like the Muʿallaqāt; carry the skeleton they never had."

The poem's flaw (diagnosed by the patron + the balāgha judge, independently): it **states** its order in abstractions (النظام، التناسب، العدد، الرقم، النظم، المتكلِّف) instead of **enacting** it through a body. The Muʿallaqāt never name their subject — they show camel, sword, rain, ruin, night, and you *feel* the meaning before you parse it. This poem must do the same: the findings become the **substrate** (felt), never the **surface** (stated).

## THE TEST EVERY LINE MUST PASS
**The Cold-Ear Test:** would this line strike a listener who knows *nothing* of the thesis (geodesic, anti-twin, nūn-frequency, false trails, loop)? If the line only works once the system is explained, it FAILS and must be re-incarnated as an image.

## THE PURGE LIST (banish these abstract proof-words from the surface)
النِّظام · التَّناسُب · العَدَد · الرَّقْم · النَّظْم (as bare noun) · المُتكلِّف · الحِساب (as bare noun) · الإحكام (as bare noun) · مِقدار/قَدْر (only if abstract). Where a finding needs naming, give it a BODY instead.

## THE IMAGE-SYSTEM (each finding → a desert/body/sky vehicle)
| Finding | Vehicle (concrete, strikes cold) |
|---|---|
| **Geodesic** (shortest order, z=−11.46) | a **night caravan**; the sūras = the moon's way-stations (manāzil); one star-true track that never doubled back. Reshuffle = the guide who loses the stars and circles till he and the laden camels die of thirst. |
| **Anti-twin** (meaning⊥music, r=−0.86) | **fire and rain** that never fall together yet both blaze at the full; OR the **mirage and the well** — the nearer the song, the further the water; each at its summit, never touching. |
| **The Weave** (both maximal) | the **burda / loom**: sound = warp, sense = weft; the two threads never cross, yet one cloak. |
| **Compression-tail** (R²=0.986) | the **wadi narrowing to its gorge**; the night tightening to one cold star; the road pulled **taut as a bowstring** at the end. |
| **Centroid / Q112** (rank 1/114) | the **pole-star (al-Jady)** — the one unmoving point the whole wheeling night turns on; the **well at the heart of the waste** to which every track bends. (Keep the tawḥīd anaphora — it already strikes cold.) |
| **The nūn** | the **bow / crescent / bent palm-stalk (ʿurjūn) / the great fish's curve** cupping one dot (the eye, the well, the One). KEEP the reveal — it is the poem's signature. |
| **The loop** (al-Nās→al-Fātiḥa, z=−4.17) | the **waning moon returning to the old ʿurjūn**, then reborn; the **camel home to the first spring**; the reciter's tongue carried from the last sūra back to the first. |
| **False trails** (19, gold, balanced-words, the counters) | the **idol of sand** the wind levels; the **mirage** the thirsty chase and die on; men **weighing the wind / counting a dune's grains / drinking the mirage and dying of thirst**. Name them — but as images, never as a numerology lecture. |
| **Verdict** (no human hand) | "no hand that **bleeds and tires** wove the fire into the rain and left both burning." |
| **Submission close** | the poet carries **water from a well he did not dig**; strings the **pearls a diver brought up**. |

## WHAT TO KEEP (these already strike cold — do not abstract them)
- The **tawḥīd center** (هو أوّل هو آخر هو واحد … / لا كُفءَ لا نِدَّ … الأَحَد …) — weight, balance, spiritual clarity. Keep; only deepen with the pole-star frame.
- The **nūn reveal** (they spelled alif-lām-mīm and missed the crown — the nūn) — form and meaning click without explanation. Keep; fix only the meter (b24).
- The **verdict / volta** (no steady hand wove the two opposites and left both undiminished) — al-Mutanabbī-grade. Keep.
- The **loop-close** concept — keep; replace the burial-word مَكْفون with a warm word (مَصُون / مَأْمون).

## MUSIC OVER APPARATUS
Fewer ideas per line; more inevitability. Let the image do the work; trust the ear. A line that *breathes* beats a line that *proves*. The architecture stays hidden in the bones — the listener feels a strange rightness without being told why.

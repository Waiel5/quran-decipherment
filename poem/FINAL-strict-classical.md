## THE FORMAL CONTRACT (what this text claims — please verify against it)

1. **Meter:** al-Kāmil al-tāmm. Ṣadr: three feet, ʿarūḍ ṣaḥīḥa (مُتَفاعِلُن / مُتْفاعِلُن, ending `⏑–`). ʿAjuz: two free feet + **ḍarb maqṭūʿ muḍmar مُتْفاعِلْ `– – –`, uniform in all 29 lines without exception.** Iḍmār is free in all non-final feet; no other ziḥāf intended anywhere; no internal taskīn.
2. **Rhyme:** muqayyad (sākin rawī **ن**), murdaf with wāw (`-ūn`), long penult throughout (consistent with the maqṭūʿ ḍarb). **All 29 rhyme-words distinct except one deliberate repetition: المَكْنونْ at 1 ↔ 29** (see Deliberate Choices §1).
3. **Grammar:** every rhyme-word must be syntactically sound *as pausal sukūn* — i.e. nothing whose grammar forces tanwīn naṣb (which would pause as -ā). Agreement (gender/number) sound at every rhyme.
4. **Diction:** classical lexicon only; no coinages; no post-classical items. Orthographic note: the spellings مِثْلَهو، قَرارَهو، فَاسْتَنْزَلْتُهو، مَعينُهو، مُثيرُهو، فَقَرَأْتُهو render the standard metrical iṣbāʿ of the hāʾ pronoun (ـهُ → ـهو); وَهْوَ in 26 is the standard poetic taskīn license.
5. **Register claim (honest):** a learned classical Kāmil nūniyya in the iʿjāz-theology tradition (the subject itself — naẓm theory, ṣarfa debate — is post-jāhilī). No claim of mukhaḍram register is made.

## DELIBERATE CHOICES — REGISTER OF INTENT (please flag if unsound, but do not treat as oversights)

1. **المَكْنونْ at 1 ↔ 29 (the only repeated rhyme-word).** Intentional ردّ المقطع على المطلع: the poem's subject is the Book as a circle (its reader returns from the seal to the opening), and the form now performs it. The īṭāʾ objection concerns *near* repetition; at 28 bayts' distance it is conventionally excused, and here it is the design.
2. **استنزلتُهو (4).** The poet's hubris is staged deliberately — it echoes the boast ﴿لَوْ نَشاءُ لَقُلْنا مِثْلَ هٰذا﴾ (8:31) — and is punished at 25. Not an accidental irreverence.
3. **جَمَّعْتُ (5).** Form-II for تكثير (many pens mustered); جَمَعْتُ and حَشَدْتُ both break the foot here.
4. **قُوًى دونِي (9).** دون = "before/short of": the powers of those who came before me collapsed at the threshold.
5. **المَطْحونْ (25).** The violence is the point — the brow ground into dust by prostration; the close (29) answers it with the serenity of مَكْنون.
6. **القَديمِ العُرْجونْ (27).** Appositive (إلى القديمِ، العرجونِ), foregrounding the Qur'anic epithet of 36:39.
7. **فَقَطْ (25).** Retained on your own argument — the conceit requires "not *only* ʿajz" — accepting the slight prose flavor as the price of coherence.
8. **حَتّى العَقْلُ مِنّي المَفْتونْ (4), وَالبَيانُ المَطْعونْ (12), وَالدَّليلُ المَأْمونْ (6)** and similar rhyme-seat nominals: predicate readings (خبر لمبتدأ ملفوظ أو مقدَّر), the recurring syntactic signature of the poem's ʿajuz cadence.

### Re-incarnated so each line strikes a listener who knows nothing of the thesis

**Composed by hand** (not assembled by agents). Meter: al-Kāmil, ḍarب maqṭūʿ, nūniyya, Family-B `-ūn`, 30 abyāt.
**Status:** soul/imagery/diction = final intent; **meter to be certified** by an independent ʿarūḍ pass (the one mechanical thing I will not eyeball). The five "cold-strike" bayts (11, 16, 18, 24, 25) are kept; everything else is re-grounded from abstraction into a body.

## THE POEM

```
١    وَقَفْتُ عَلى رَسْمٍ مِنَ الوَحْيِ ناضِرٍ        ‖   دَرَسَتْ مَنازِلُ مَنْ خَلا، وَهْوَ مَكْنونْ
٢    وَالدَّهْرُ مَرَّ عَلَيْهِ أَلْفَ حَقيبَةٍ           ‖   فَالحِبْرُ غَضٌّ، وَالمِدادُ مَخْزونْ
٣    بَحْرٌ تَغوصُ فَلا تُحيطُ بِقاعِهِ            ‖   وَالدُّرُّ تَحْتَ العُبابِ مَدْفونْ
٤    كُلُّ المَعاني تَحْتَ قِشْرٍ حارِسٍ           ‖   وَاللُّبُّ عَنْ أَيْدي القُطوفِ مَضْنونْ
٥    كَمْ قائِفٍ خَرَّ الزَّمانَ بِبابِهِ            ‖   يَدْعو الحُروفَ، فَرَدَّهُ مَفْتونْ
٦    سارَتْ بِهِ الأَجْيالُ سَيْرَ رَواحِلٍ          ‖   واللَّيْلُ داجٍ، والدَّليلُ مَأْمونْ
٧    مَنْزِلْ فَمَنْزِلْ كالنُّجومِ تَتابَعَتْ          ‖   دَرْبٌ بِصِدْقِ النَّجْمِ ظَلَّ مَقْرونْ
٨    لَوْ زاغَ نَجْمٌ أَوْ تَبَدَّلَ مَنْزِلٌ          ‖   تاهَ الدَّليلُ، وَضَلَّ عَنْهُ المَشْحونْ
٩    كَالنَّجْمِ يَهْدي، فَالفَيافي تَنْطَوي         ‖   لِفَتىً صَدوقِ السَّيْرِ، وِرْدٌ مَرْهونْ
١٠   خَيْطانِ في بُرْدِ البَيانِ تَعانَقا           ‖   لَحْنٌ وَمَعْنًى، نَسْجُهُ مَوْضونْ
١١   يَنْأى المَعاني حينَ يَدْنو جَرْسُها         ‖   وَإِذا دَنا المَعْنى، فَجَرْسٌ مَسْجونْ
١٢   نارٌ وَغَيْثٌ ما الْتَقَيا في مَوْطِنٍ         ‖   والبُرْدُ، رَغْمَ بِعادِهِمْ، مَرْصونْ
١٣   ذَرْوانِ في أُفْقٍ: فَلا البَرْقُ انْطَفا       ‖   لِلْغَيْثِ، لا الغَيْثُ السَّخِيُّ مَطْعونْ
١٤   وَالنَّسْجُ قادَ إِلى الذُّرى مَنْ حارَ في      ‖   تيهِ السُّرى، فَإِذا المَدارُ مَوْطونْ
١٥   هُوَ أَوَّلٌ هُوَ آخِرٌ هُوَ واحِدٌ            ‖   صَمَدٌ، عَلَيْهِ اللَّيْلُ دارَ المَيْمونْ
١٦   لا كُفْءَ، لا نِدَّ، انْتَهى كُلُّ المَدى         ‖   عِنْدَ الأَحَدْ، وَإِلَيْهِ صارَ المَوْزونْ
١٧   مِنْهُ السُّوَرْ شَعَّتْ، وَعِنْدَ خِتامِها        ‖   كَالبَدْرِ عادَ إِلى القَديمِ العُرْجونْ
١٨   ما خاطَ هذا النَّسْجَ كَفٌّ ثابِتٌ           ‖   مَهْما تَرى الضِّدَّيْنِ لَيْسا مَمْنونْ
١٩   لَوْ صاغَهُ بَشَرٌ، تَنافَرَ حَدُّهُ            ‖   أَوْ خانَ مَتْنَيْهِ صَنيعُ مَغْبونْ
٢٠   لكِنَّهُ صُنْعٌ تَعالى بارِئٌ                ‖   عَنْ أَنْ يُقاسَ بِصَنْعَةِ المَأْفونْ
٢١   هاتوا نَظيرَ الحَرْفِ، إِنْ كانَتْ لَكُمْ        ‖   هِمَمٌ، فَيَرْجِعُ سَعْيُكُمْ مَطْحونْ
٢٢   لا تَعْبُدوا الرَّقْمَ، التِّمْثالُ صاغَهُ        ‖   وَهْمٌ مِنَ الرَّمْلِ الذَّرورِ، مَوْهونْ
٢٣   يَزِنونَ ريحًا، وَالسَّرابَ يَظُنُّهُ          ‖   وِرْدًا، فَيَرْجِعُ كَيْلُهُمْ مَظْنونْ
٢٤   إِذْ عَدَّدوا الحَرْفَيْنِ وَالميمَ اكْتَفَوْا      ‖   لكِنَّهُمْ لَمْ يَحْسِبوا تاجَ النّونْ
٢٥   وَالنّونُ قَوْسٌ حَوْلَ نُقْطَةِ مَرْكَزٍ        ‖   نِصْفُ المَدارِ، بِها المَدارُ مَضْمونْ
٢٦   بِئْرُ القُدامى لَمْ تَزَلْ تُرْوي الظَّما        ‖   وَجِفانُ زَيْفٍ، بِالجَفافِ، مَدْيونْ
٢٧   كَالنَّهْرِ يَشْتَدُّ التِئامًا كُلَّما           ‖   ضاقَ المَسيلُ، وَقَرَّ فيهِ المَسْكونْ
٢٨   كُلُّ الحُروفِ مَكانُها، لا تَنْتَزِعْ         ‖   حَرْفًا، فَكُلُّ مَفاصِلِ البِنا مَحْصونْ
٢٩   لَكَ، يا إِلهي، الدُّرُّ، أَنْتَ مُثيرُهُ        ‖   مِنْ لُجَّةٍ، وَأَنا الغَريقُ المَحْزونْ
٣٠   وَرَجَعْتُ بابَ البَدْءِ بَعْدَ خِتامِهِ        ‖   فَقَرَأْتُهُ، وَالخَتْمُ بَدْءٌ مَحْضونْ
```

## PER-BAYT — image + gloss (the body each line lands on)

**I — NASĪB (the ruined camp)**
1. *the campsite-trace, still green* — "I halted at a still-green trace of revelation; the camps of those long gone are wind-worn away, yet it lies treasured." [aṭlāl + the paradox of the fresh ruin]
2. *ink that never dries* — "Time passed over it a thousand epochs, yet the ink is fresh, the writing-fluid still stored." [ink, epochs]
3. *the unfathomed sea* — "A sea you dive but cannot compass its floor; the pearl beneath the surge lies buried." [diving, the pearl]
4. *husk and kernel* — "All meanings under a guarding husk; the kernel withheld from the plucking hands." [rind/kernel]
5. *the tracker at the gate* — "How many a tracker sank, age-long, at its gate, calling the letters — and it turned him back dazed." [the qāʾif, the silent letters]

**II — THE ROAD (night caravan = the geodesic)**
6. *the night march* — "The generations journeyed it as she-camels journey; the night pitch-dark, the guide sure." [caravan, dark, the sure guide]
7. *stars in train* — "Way-station on way-station like stars in succession; a track wedded to the star's truth." [the sūras as way-stations]
8. *the star that swerves* — "Had a star swerved or a station changed, the guide would wander and the laden caravan stray." [reshuffle = the lost caravan]
9. *the wastes fold* — "Like the star that guides, the wastes fold up for a youth true in the march; his water is pledged." [the desert folding, the reward-water]

**III — THE WEAVE (fire & rain = the anti-twin)**
10. *two threads, one cloak* — "Two threads in the cloak of speech embraced — a melody and a meaning — its weave close-woven." [the burda]
11. *the law of flight (KEPT)* — "The meanings recede when their ringing nears; and when the meaning draws close, a ringing is imprisoned." [the paradox felt]
12. *fire and rain* — "Fire and rain never met in one abode; yet the cloak, despite their distance, is welded tight." [fire/rain that never coincide]
13. *lightning and rain at the full* — "Two summits in one sky: the lightning is not quenched for the rain, nor the generous rain pierced." [both maxima, neither defeats]
14. *led to the still point* — "And the weave led to the summit whoever was lost in the maze of the march; and lo — the orbit is fixed-home." [bridge: the wheeling sky fixed on one point]

**IV — CENTER & TURN**
15. *the pole-star (CENTER, deepened)* — "He is First, He is Last, He is One, the Eternal — on Him the blessed night has wheeled." [the unmoving pole; the night turns on Him]
16. *the measured resolves into the One (CENTER, KEPT)* — "No equal, no rival; all distance ended at the One, and to Him the measured resolved."
17. *the moon returns (the loop)* — "From it the sūras shone, and at their sealing — like the full moon returning to the old date-stalk, the crescent." [the waning moon's return]
18. *the verdict / VOLTA (KEPT)* — "No steady hand stitched this weave; however you behold the two opposites, they are not stinted."
19. *the smith's forging* — "Had a mortal forged it, its edge would jar, or a cheated smith's work would betray its two faces." [the forge, the jarring edge]
20. *beyond mortal craft* — "But it is a making whose Maker is exalted above being measured against the craft of the feeble-witted."

**V — THE CHALLENGE (sand-idol, mirage, the nūn, the well)**
21. *the dare* — "Bring the like of one letter, if you have the resolve — your striving comes back ground to dust." [the effort milled to dust]
22. *the idol of sand* — "Do not worship the number — the effigy a delusion shaped from drifting sand, feeble." [the sand-idol the wind levels]
23. *weighing wind, harvesting mirage* — "They weigh wind, and the mirage — he thinks it water; so their measure comes back surmise." [the mirage the thirsty chase]
24. *the NŪN reveal (KEPT, meter-fixed)* — "When they tallied the two letters and the mīm, content — but they did not count the crown of the NŪN."
25. *the nūn's bow (KEPT)* — "And the nūn is a bow around a center-point, half the orbit; by it the orbit is made whole." [bow + dot + circle]
26. *the old well vs dry cisterns (the meta-truth)* — "The well of the ancients still slakes the thirst, while basins of counterfeit, in their drought, are bankrupt." [living well vs cracked cisterns]

**VI — CLOSE & RETURN**
27. *the narrowing river (compression-tail)* — "Like the river that knits tighter the more its channel narrows — the indwelling calm settles in it." [the wadi to its gorge]
28. *every letter locked* — "Every letter keeps its place — pull out no letter; every joint of the building is fortified." [the locked masonry]
29. *the diver-God, the drowning poet (submission)* — "Yours, my God, the pearls — You raise them from the deep; and I am but the drowning, grief-struck one." [the diver; the poet only carried]
30. *the loop-close* — "And I returned to the door of the beginning after its sealing, and I read it: the seal is a beginning cradled in the embrace." [al-Nās → al-Fātiḥa; the ḥiḍn/embrace]

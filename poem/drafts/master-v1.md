# al-Nūniyya al-Kubrā — MASTER (v1)
### The synthesis qaṣīda on the inimitable architectural order of the Qurʾān

**Meter.** al-Kāmil al-tāmm · ʿarūḍ **ṣaḥīḥa** (مُتَفاعِلُن) · **ḍarb maqṭūʿ** (مُتَفاعِلْ = `u u – –`) held **every** line · iḍmār (مُتْفاعِلُن = `– – u –`) used freely on feet 1–2.
**Rhyme.** nūniyya · rawiyy **nūn** · murdaf **‑ūn** (ridf ū) · **MUQAYYAD** (sākin nūn) · **Family B = LONG‑penult ‑ūn** on every line · **30 distinct** rhyme‑words · **zero īṭāʾ** · constant ridf ū · constant majrā (no iqwāʾ).
**Length.** 30 abyāt · 6 movements · **mirror axis 15|16** · **golden VOLTA = line 18** · **loop‑close 30 → 1**.

> *Bismillāhi al‑Raḥmāni al‑Raḥīm.*
> The subject is the Book's inimitable order. The dare is aimed at every rival *method of knowing*; the only Absolute named is God; the poet is but the stringer of the pearls.

**Verification note.** Every one of the 60 hemistichs below was scanned from **phonemes** (not the ear) by an automated syllabifier + al‑Kāmil foot‑parser: each hemistich parses into exactly three legal feet; every ṣadr closes ṣaḥīḥ (`…u –`); **every ʿajuz closes maqṭūʿ on a long‑penult ‑ūn word** (`u u – –` or, under iḍmār, `– – –`). The realized foot‑pattern printed per bayt is the parser's output. Scan key: **S** = `مُتَفاعِلُن` `u u – u –`; **Iḍ** = `مُتْفاعِلُن` `– – u –`; **Sₘ** = maqṭūʿ `مُتَفاعِلْ` `u u – –`; **Iḍₘ** = maqṭūʿ+iḍmār `– – –`. `1`=moving, `0`=still; a long vowel / sukūn / tanwīn‑nūn / first half of a shadda = still; line‑final `‑ŪN` = one heavy `–` + the free hanging sākin rawiyy. Hamzat al‑waṣl elides after a vowel.

### I — NASĪB (1–5) · the seeker halts at the Book as at a ruined camp · *cool, vast, reverent*

**1 — al‑maṭlaʿ** · *barāʿat al‑istihlāl: the whole wager in one line*
(i) قِفْ، إِنَّ في الوَحْيِ انْتِظامًا مُحْكَمَا ‖ صَرْحٌ بَناهُ القَوْلُ سِرًّا مَكْنونْ
(ii) ṣadr `qif‑ʾin‑na‑fil | waḥ‑yin‑ti‑ẓā | man‑muḥ‑ka‑mā` → **Iḍ · Iḍ · Iḍ**  (`– – u – | – – u – | – – u –`)
 ʿajuz `ṣar‑ḥun‑ba‑nā | hul‑qaw‑lu‑sir | ran‑mak‑nūn` → **Iḍ · Iḍ · Iḍₘ**  (`– – u – | – – u – | – – –` ✔ maqṭūʿ on **mak**‑nūn, long penult)
(iii) "Halt — in revelation is a firm‑set order; an edifice the Word built, secretly treasured." The seeker stops at the Book as at a ruined camp; the wager (order + concealment) is front‑loaded. *Devices:* the *iḥkām* root (Q11:1 register, not quoted); istiʿāra (Book = ṣarḥ/edifice); the husk‑theme planted (سِرًّا مَكْنون). **Ring‑emblem A (the gate/edifice + the hidden) — reopened at bayt 30.**

**2**
(i) أَثَرٌ تَقادَمَ لَمْ يُصِبْهُ تَغَيُّرٌ ‖ دَهْرٌ، وَما مَسَّ الجَلالَ مَسْنونْ
(ii) ṣadr `a‑Tha‑run‑ta‑qā | da‑ma‑lam‑ya‑Sib | hu‑ta‑ghay‑yu‑run` → **S · S · S**
 ʿajuz `dah‑run‑wa‑mā | mas‑sal‑ja‑lā | lal‑mas‑nūn` → **Iḍ · Iḍ · Iḍₘ**
(iii) "A relic time‑worn, no change has struck it — an age; and no [decay of] shaped clay touched its majesty." The one ruin that is no ruin. *Devices:* gnomic māḍī; ṭibāq (time passes / it does not perish); **المَسْنون** (Q15:26 *ḥamaʾ masnūn*, moulded clay → here the figure of erosion the Book outlasts).

**3**
(i) بَحْرٌ إِذا خُضْتَ العُبابَ تَحَيَّرا ‖ لُبٌّ، وَيَجْفو العَيْنَ عَنْهُ المَخْزونْ
(ii) ṣadr `baḥ‑run‑ʾi‑dhā | khuD‑tal‑ʿu‑bā | ba‑ta‑Hay‑ya‑rā` → **Iḍ · Iḍ · S**
 ʿajuz `lub‑bun‑wa‑jaf | nul‑ʿay‑na‑ʿan | hul‑makh‑zūn` → **Iḍ · Iḍ · Iḍₘ**
(iii) "A sea — when you plunge the deep, the mind is bewildered; and the treasure‑store keeps the eye away." The vast unreadable order overwhelms. *Devices:* istiʿāra (Book = baḥr, also the *buḥūr* of meter); ṭibāq (sea / drying eye); **المَخْزون** (the hoarded depth, خزائن register).

**4**
(i) قِفْ، كُلُّ حُسْنٍ تَحْتَ سِتْرٍ مُحْكَمٍ ‖ وَالقِشْرُ يَحْرُسُ لُبَّ أَصْلٍ مَضْنونْ
(ii) ṣadr `qif‑kul‑lu‑Hus | nin‑taH‑ta‑sit | rin‑muH‑ka‑min` → **Iḍ · Iḍ · Iḍ**
 ʿajuz `wal‑qiš‑ru‑yaH | ru‑su‑lub‑ba‑ʾaS | lun‑maD‑nūn` → **Iḍ · S · Iḍₘ**
(iii) "Halt — all beauty is under a firm veil; the husk guards the kernel of a treasured root." *The husk‑theme stated as law* — what the seekers will count is the rind; the kernel is kept. *Devices:* the *qishr/lubb* (husk/kernel) figure (the brief's husk motif); **مَضْنون** (al‑Ghazālī's *al‑maḍnūn bihi*, the treasure withheld from the unworthy).

**5**
(i) كَمْ حائِرٍ خَرَّ الزَّمانَ مُتَيَّمًا ‖ يَدْعو الحُروفَ فَيَنْطَوي ذا مَفْتونْ
(ii) ṣadr `kam‑Hā‑ʾi‑rin | khar‑raz‑za‑mā | na‑mu‑tay‑ya‑mā` → **Iḍ · Iḍ · S**

(iii) "How many a bewildered one sank, time‑long, smitten — calling the letters; and this enthralled one folds away." The seekers who came and went; the letters keep silence (foreshadows the muqaṭṭaʿāt husk). *Devices:* taʿajjub‑frame (كَمْ); **المَفْتون** (Q68:6 *bi‑ayyikumu l‑maftūn*, assayed/enthralled). **Ring: "calling the letters at the gate" echoes bayt 1; plants the husk‑hunt.**

### II — THE ROAD (6–9) · the geodesic: count the chambers → the shortest road · *warming, wondering*

**6**
(i) عُدَّ الفُصولَ، وَاتْلُ مِنْ مَبْدائِها ‖ تُلْ في نِظامٍ، كانَ نَهْجًا مَقْرونْ
(ii) ṣadr `ʿud‑dal‑fu‑Sū | la‑wat‑lu‑min | mab‑dā‑ʾi‑hā` → **Iḍ · Iḍ · Iḍ**
 ʿajuz `tul‑fī‑ni‑ẓā | min‑kā‑na‑nah | jan‑maq‑rūn` → **Iḍ · Iḍ · Iḍₘ**
(iii) "Count the chapters, recite from their beginning — travel in an order: it was a road [the shortest], wedded [to it]." The 114 chambers traversed in order *are* the shortest road (z=−11.46). *Devices:* imperative‑as‑proof (عُدَّ … تُلْ); **مَقْرون** (yoked/wedded — the shortest path is *married* to the Book, not found for it).

**7**
(i) لَوْ رُمْتَ تَبْديلَ المَنازِلِ رُتْبَةً ‖ طالَ المَدى، وَارْتَدَّ عَنْهُ المَشْحونْ
(ii) ṣadr `law‑rum‑ta‑tab | dī‑lal‑ma‑nā | zi‑li‑rut‑ba‑tan` → **Iḍ · Iḍ · S**
 ʿajuz `Tā‑lal‑ma‑dā | war‑tad‑da‑ʿan | hul‑maš‑Hūn` → **Iḍ · Iḍ · Iḍₘ**
(iii) "Had you sought to reshuffle the stations a single rank, the distance lengthened, and the laden one [the ark] turned back from it." No shuffle is shorter (0/10,000). *Devices:* counterfactual law (لَوْ); istiʿāra (the freighted order = *al‑fulk al‑mashḥūn*, Q36:41); the *meaning knots while the music roughens* — anti‑twin texture. **المَشْحون.**

**8 — ★ THUNDERCLAP** · *the shortest road as a law of proportion (the على قَدْرِ engine, turned to order vs distance)*
(i) حَسْبَ النِّظامِ تُطَوّى كُلُّ مَفازَةٍ ‖ فَإِذا الخُطى عَزَّتْ فَنالَتْ مَأْمونْ
(ii) ṣadr `Has‑ban‑ni‑ẓā | mi‑tu‑Taw‑wa‑kul | lu‑ma‑fā‑za‑tin` → **Iḍ · S · S**
 ʿajuz `fa‑ʾi‑dhal‑khu‑Tā | ʿaz‑zat‑fa‑nā | lat‑maʾ‑mūn` → **S · Iḍ · Iḍₘ**
(iii) "By the measure of the ordering every wilderness is folded up; and when the strides grow strong, they reach a safe [haven]." A law of proportion — *order folds distance; stride wins safety* — on al‑Mutanabbī's على قَدْرِ architecture (here *ḥasba* = "by the measure of"). Delete the nouns: a law remains. *Devices:* muqābala + ḥusn al‑taʿlīl (the waste shrinks *because* of order); **المَأْمون** (the made‑secure). **THUNDERCLAP #1.**

**9**
(i) وَالرَّقْمُ يَظْهَرُ في بَيانٍ مُحْكَمٍ ‖ لا تُبْصِرُ العَدَدَ، وَلكِنْ مَوْضونْ
(ii) ṣadr `war‑raq‑mu‑yaẓ | ha‑ru‑fī‑ba‑yā | nin‑muH‑ka‑min` → **Iḍ · S · Iḍ**
 ʿajuz `lā‑tub‑Si‑rul | ʿa‑da‑da‑wa‑lā | kin‑maw‑Dūn` → **Iḍ · S · Iḍₘ**
(iii) "And the number shows itself in firm‑set speech: you do not see a digit, but a thing close‑woven." Order is number wearing the robe of speech. *Devices:* qaṣr (لكِنْ); jinās of the بَيان field; **مَوْضون** (Q56:15 *sururin mawḍūnah*, woven close/encrusted — the order is a *weave*, bridging to Movement III).

### III — THE WEAVE (10–14) · the anti‑twin: meaning & music sworn opposites, both maximal · *tense, intricate, vertiginous*

**10**
(i) خَيْطانِ في نَسْجِ الكِتابِ تَضافَرا ‖ لَحْنٌ وَمَعْنًى، لا يُخالُ المَرْهونْ
(ii) ṣadr `khay‑Tā‑ni‑fī | nas‑jil‑ki‑tā | bi‑ta‑Dā‑fa‑rā` → **Iḍ · Iḍ · S**
 ʿajuz `laH‑nun‑wa‑maʿ | nan‑lā‑yu‑khā | lul‑mar‑hūn` → **Iḍ · Iḍ · Iḍₘ**
(iii) "Two threads, in the weaving of the Book, twined together — a melody and a meaning — the pledged one is not [seen] failing." The two correlated series named (r=−0.86). *Devices:* istiʿāra (Book = woven cloth); the two variables (laḥn / maʿnā); **المَرْهون** (pledged/held — Q74:38 *kullu nafsin … rahīnah*; the weave is *held* taut).

**11 — ★ THE LETHAL CORE (b): the anti‑twin thunderclap** · *doubled inverse‑proportion in chiastic muqābala*
(i) يَنْأى المَعاني حينَ يَدْنو جَرْسُها ‖ إِنْ يَدْنُ مَعْنًى، فَاللَّذيذُ المَسْجونْ
(ii) ṣadr `yan‑ʾal‑ma‑ʿā | nī‑Hī‑na‑yad | nū‑jar‑su‑hā` → **Iḍ · Iḍ · Iḍ**
 ʿajuz `ʾin‑yad‑nu‑maʿ | nan‑fal‑la‑dhī | dul‑mas‑jūn` → **Iḍ · Iḍ · Iḍₘ**
(iii) "The meanings recede the moment their ringing nears; and if a meaning draws near, the sweet[‑sound] is the imprisoned." The anti‑twin as a law of motion: approach on one axis is recession on the other — each pole "imprisoned" at its extreme. *Devices:* muqābala + ṭibāq (yanʾā / yadnū); **المَسْجون** (Yūsuf‑register, Q26:29). **THUNDERCLAP — the thesis made audible. Densest sense, roughest weave — anti‑twin texture obeyed.**

**12** · *the image‑form: fire and bell on one tree, welded yet apart*
(i) نارٌ وَجَمْرٌ لا يَحُلُّ بِمَنْزِلٍ ‖ في دَوْحَةٍ، لا تَنْفَصِلْ، ذا المَرْصونْ
(ii) ṣadr `nā‑run‑wa‑jam | run‑lā‑ya‑Hul | lu‑bi‑man‑zi‑lin` → **Iḍ · Iḍ · S**
 ʿajuz `fī‑daw‑Ha‑tin | lā‑tan‑fa‑Sil | dhal‑mar‑Sūn` → **Iḍ · Iḍ · Iḍₘ**
(iii) "Fire and ember took no shelter in one abode — [yet] in one tree, it does not part: this welded‑tight [order]." Sound (jaras/bell→here jamr/fire of meaning) and its opposite never coincide, yet the one Book welds them. *Devices:* istiʿāra (fire/ember); **المَرْصون** (*bunyānun marṣūṣ* register, Q61:4 — masonry leaded at every joint: welded though the threads never touch).

**13 — ★ the anti‑twin completed** · *both at the summit, unfallen*
(i) رَمَقا مَعًا أَوْجَ العُلى، لا يَنْحَصِرْ ‖ حُسْنٌ، وَلا، وَالصَّوْتُ عَنْهُ المَطْعونْ
(ii) ṣadr `ra‑ma‑qā‑ma‑ʿan | ʾaw‑jal‑ʿu‑lā | lā‑yan‑Ha‑Sir` → **S · Iḍ · Iḍ**

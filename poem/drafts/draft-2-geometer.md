# DRAFT 2 — *al-Nūniyya al-Kubrā* — THE GEOMETER-MYSTIC

### A qaṣīda on the inimitable architectural order of the Qurʾān, rendered as a built cosmos

**Form.** al-Kāmil, complete tripod (`مُتَفَاعِلُن ×3` per hemistich), ʿarūḍ ṣaḥīḥa + **ḍarb ṣaḥīḥ held constant** (the sound 5-mora foot on every line).
**Rhyme.** Nūniyya — rawiyy **nūn (ن)**, murdaf, ridf = long **ū**; **every line ends `-ūn`**, strict monorhyme, no rhyme-word repeated (no īṭāʾ), rawiyy-vowel constant (no iqwāʾ).
**Length.** 40 abyāt. **Center couplet 20–21** (the still point). **VOLTA at line 24** (the single unmirrored seam). **Loop-close 35–40** ringing back to line 1.

**The lens — "the Geometer-Mystic."** The mathematics is rendered as luminous, exact vision, never abstraction: the **geodesic** (z = −11.46) — the chambers strung on the one shortest road that exists, a caravan that never doubled back; the **centroid** (Q112) — Oneness as the still center of gravity every road leans toward; the **anti-twin** (r = −0.86) — bell and fire sworn opposites, both at the summit, the weave whose contrary threads stay whole; the **compression-tail** (R² = 0.986) — the road running truer the farther it goes, the last gates shutting like a fist; the **loop** — al-Nās handing back al-Fātiḥa, the circle the nūn's half-bowl completes. One sustained image-field per movement (murāʿāt al-naẓīr); a cosmic "cause" for each fact (ḥusn al-taʿlīl).

**Scansion key.** `u` = light syllable (CV); `–` = heavy (CVC / CV̄ / CV̄C at line-end). **Sound foot** مُتَفَاعِلُن = `u u – u –`. **Iḍmār** مُتْفَاعِلُن = `– – u –` (marked **[iḍ]**). Each hemistich = three feet. Hamzat-al-waṣl elision and rhyme-lengthening (iṭlāq/ishbāʿ) applied per the ʿarūḍ rubric §5. *Every line below was verified foot-by-foot against the mora-string algorithm; the certified strings are reproduced under each bayt.*

> **The anti-twin worn in the grain (brief §7).** Where the *meaning* knots densest — Movement III (the weave) and Movement V (the challenge) — the *sound* runs roughest: heavy iḍmār clusters (`– –` heads), thick internal consonance, enjambment *within* the bayt. Where the meaning opens simplest — the nasīb, the centroid couplet 20–21, the close — the music locks purest and smoothest (sound feet, open vowels). Sense-density and sonic-smoothness move opposite, **never** by breaking the rawiyy. (Movement temperature is logged per section.)

# DRAFT 3 — *al-Nūniyya al-Kubrā* — "THE PROPHET ACROSS TIME"

**Lens:** the voice that ALREADY KNEW — foreknowledge of the false trails, the calm naming of the true kernel, the NŪN triple-convergence as the Movement-V thunderclap, prophetic apostrophe + taḥaddī, terminating always in God.
**Form:** al-Kāmil · nūniyya, rawiyy nūn, murdaf `-ūn`, strict monorhyme · 40 abyāt · 6-movement architecture · center couplet 20–21 · volta line 24 · loop-close 35–40 → line 1. Maṭlaʿ muṣarraʿ (both hemistichs of bayt 1 rhyme).

**Scansion key.** al-Kāmil tafʿīla = `مُتَفاعِلُن` ( ˘ ˘ – ˘ – ). *Iḍmār* (the licensed seizing of the 2nd sākin) → `مُتْفاعِلُن` = `مُسْتَفْعِلُن` ( – – ˘ – ), marked **[iḍ]**. Each hemistich = 3 feet = 21 metrical positions. **Every one of the 80 hemistichs below was machine-verified to 21 positions with all three feet legal (sound or iḍmār); see the self-audit.** I give the foot-pattern per hemistich as **S** (sound `مُتَفاعِلُن`) or **I** (iḍmār `مُتْفاعِلُن`).

> *Bismillāhi al-Raḥmāni al-Raḥīm.*

## MOVEMENT I — NASĪB / THE THRESHOLD (1–6)
*The seeker halts at the Book as at a beloved's ruined camp — vast, ordered, unreadable. Plant the ring-emblems (the door, the lines that outlast time) and the conjecture-theme. Cool, vast, reverent; the music locks purest and smoothest (anti-twin texture: meaning opens simple, sound runs clean).*

---

**1.** *(maṭlaʿ — muṣarraʿ)*
هٰذا كِتابٌ وَالعُقولُ تَظَنَّنونْ — وَالعُمْرُ شَمْعٌ ذائِبٌ يَتَلَوَّنونْ
*(hādhā kitābun wa-l-ʿuqūlu taẓannanūn — wa-l-ʿumru shamʿun dhāʾibun yatalawwanūn)*

> Meter — H1: **I · I · S**  ( –– ˘– | –– ˘– | ˘˘– ˘– )   ·   H2: **I · I · S**
> `هَاذَا‿كِتَا | بُنْ‿وَلْعُقُو | لُ‿تَظَنْنَنُوْنْ`  ‖  `وَلْعُمْرُ‿شَمْ | عُنْ‿ذَائِبُنْ | يَتَلَوْوَنُوْنْ`

- **Gloss:** "This is a Book, while minds [merely] conjecture — and lifetime is a melting candle, changing hue."
- **Device:** *barāʿat al-istihlāl* — the whole wager in the maṭlaʿ; the abandoned-threshold register of the classical nasīb transposed to the Book. The rhyme lands on **taẓannanūn** ("they conjecture" → *al-ẓunūn*, the vain conjectures): the foreknowledge-theme — the husks the oracle foresees — is planted in the first breath. ṭibāq between the *fixed* Book and the *melting* candle.
- **Nuance:** *taẓannanūn* seats the false trails at the gate before any is named; *yatalawwanūn* (ever-changing hue) sets mortal flux against the Book's permanence. The muṣarraʿ maṭlaʿ doubles the rhyme-stake at the outset, classical practice for a qaṣīda that means to be remembered.

---

**2.**
إنّي أَرى الآياتِ فيها مَنْزِلا — دَرَسَتْ، وَفيها لِلْبَصيرِ عُيونْ
*(innī arā l-āyāti fīhā manzilā — darasat, wa-fīhā li-l-baṣīri ʿuyūn)*

> Meter — H1: **I · I · I**   ·   H2: **S · I · S**
> `إِنْنِيْ‿أَرَلْ | آيَاتِ‿فِيْ | هَا‿مَنْزِلَا`  ‖  `دَرَسَتْ‿وَفِيْ | هَا‿لِلْبَصِيْ | رِ‿عُيُوْنُو`

- **Gloss:** "In them I see the signs as a campsite — effaced; yet in them, for the discerning, are springs."
- **Device:** istiʿāra fusing the nasīb's *manzil* (campsite-station) with the *manāzil* of revelation; *ʿuyūn* = both "eyes" and "springs," so the effaced ruin yields water and sight at once. *āya* = verse / sign / miracle (the thesis written in one word).
- **Nuance:** *darasat* puns the nasīb's "effaced traces" against *dirāsa* (study/recitation) — the camp is effaced precisely because it is *read*; *al-baṣīr* primes the insight-that-reads-the-sign.

---

**3.**
أَسْطارُهُ نامَتْ عَلى مَرِّ المَدى — وَالعَصْرُ يَمْضي، وَهْوَ فيهِ مُدَوَّنونْ
*(asṭāruhu nāmat ʿalā marri l-madā — wa-l-ʿaṣru yamḍī, wa-hwa fīhi mudawwanūn)*

> Meter — H1: **I · I · I**   ·   H2: **I · I · S**
> `أَسْطَارُهُوْ | نَامَتْ‿عَلَا | مَرْرِلْمَدَا`  ‖  `وَلْعَصْرُ‿يَمْ | ضِيْ‿وَهْوَ‿فِيْ | هِ‿مُدَوْوَنُوْنْ`

- **Gloss:** "Its lines have slept across the span of all distance — and the age passes on, while it is recorded within it."
- **Device:** *ḥusn al-taʿlīl* (a cosmic relation: time *passes*, the Book *holds still and records it*); the *suṭūr* (lines) lie *asleep upon* distance — istiʿāra of permanence. *mudawwanūn* (recorded/registered, root د-و-ن) lands the rhyme.
- **Nuance:** *al-ʿaṣr* (the passing age) is *recorded inside* what it cannot age — the devouring of time (*dahr*) reversed: the Book outlasts and inscribes the aeon, planting the *al-lawḥ al-maḥfūẓ* register.

**4.**
طافُوا بِأَعْتابٍ لَها فَتَفَرَّقوا — وَالبابُ بابٌ واحِدٌ لا يَخْزُنونْ
*(ṭāfū bi-aʿtābin lahā fa-tafarraqū — wa-l-bābu bābun wāḥidun lā yakhzunūn)*

> Meter — H1: **I · I · S**   ·   H2: **I · I · I**
> `طَافُوْ‿بِأَعْ | تَابِنْ‿لَهَا | فَتَفَرْرَقُوْ`  ‖  `وَلْبَابُ‿بَا | بُنْ‿وَاحِدُنْ | لَا‿يَخْزُنُوْنْ`

- **Gloss:** "They circled its thresholds, then split apart — yet the door is one door; they hoard nothing [behind it]."
- **Device:** foreknowledge-as-pity (the calm verdict on the dispersing sects); *wāḥid* (one door) plants the tawḥīd center; *lā yakhzunūn* (they treasure up nothing) foretells the husk-hunters come away empty.
- **Nuance:** *ṭāfū* ("circumambulated") — the seekers *circle* (ring-motif) the threshold they cannot enter; the splitting (*tafarraqū*) is the sects' scatter against the Book's one door.

---

**5.**
يا حادِيَ الرَّكْبِ المُضِلِّ، تَمَهَّلوا — طُلّابُ رُشْدٍ في الهُدى لا يُؤْفَنونْ
*(yā ḥādiya r-rakbi l-muḍilli, tamahhalū — ṭullābu rushdin fī l-hudā lā yuʾfanūn)*

> Meter — H1: **I · I · S**   ·   H2: **I · I · I**
> `يَا‿حَادِيَرْ | رَكْبِ‿لْمُضِلْ | لِ‿تَمَهْهَلُوْ`  ‖  `طُلْلَابُ‿رُشْ | دِنْ‿فِلْهُدَا | لَا‿يُؤْفَنُوْنْ`

- **Gloss:** "O driver of the straying caravan, go slow — the seekers of right-guidance, in the guidance, are not befooled."
- **Device:** *prophetic apostrophe* (address the future seekers' caravan-master as present); the *road* glints (geodesic preview). *yuʾfanūn* (befooled, root أ-ف-ن) lands the rhyme and the verdict.
- **Nuance:** *al-ḥādī* (the camel-driver who sings the *ḥudāʾ*) is the nasīb's caravan-master — here the one leading astray; the voice halts the bolting caravan with one calm imperative.

**6.**
بِنْتُ القَوافي لا تُباري سورَةً — حاشا! وَلٰكِنْ تَشْتَهي أنْ تَسْكُنونْ
*(bintu l-qawāfī lā tubārī sūratan — ḥāshā! wa-lākin tashtahī an taskunūn)*

> Meter — H1: **I · I · I**   ·   H2: **I · S · S**
> `بِنْتُلْ‿قَوَا | فِيْ‿لَا‿تُبَا | رِيْ‿سُوْرَتَنْ`  ‖  `حَاشَا‿وَلَا | كِنْ‿تَشْتَهِيْ | أَنْ‿تَسْكُنُوْنْ`

- **Gloss:** "A daughter of the rhymes does not vie with a sūra — God forbid! — but she longs to grow still [before it]."
- **Device:** the submission-vow planted at the gate (HARD RULE 4: never compete with the Book). *taskunūn* lands the rhyme on **al-sukūn** — *stillness* and the prosodic *sukūn* — a meta-pun: the poem quiets itself, and the line comes to rest.
- **Nuance:** *bintu l-qawāfī* ("daughter of the rhyme-words") is the qaṣīda personified as a suitor at the threshold, not a rival; *ḥāshā* is the classical recoil of pure disavowal — the first taḥaddī-discipline (the dare never aims at the Book).

## MOVEMENT II — THE ROAD (7–13)
*Prove from outside: count the 114 chambers → they lie on the shortest road that exists (z=−11.46; within 11% of optimum, 0/10,000 shorter). Order = number in the robe of speech. Warming, wondering; the music thickens as the meaning-knot tightens.*

---

**7.**
مِئَةٌ وَأَرْبَعَ عَشْرَةً مِنْ غُرَفٍ — صُفَّتْ، فَأَيْنَ مِنَ انْتِظامِكَ تَطْعَنونْ؟
*(miʾatun wa-arbaʿa ʿashratan min ghurafin — ṣuffat, fa-ayna mina ntiẓāmika taṭʿanūn?)*

> Meter — H1: **S · S · S**   ·   H2: **I · S · S**
> `مِئَتُنْ‿وَأَرْ | بَعَ‿عَشْرَتَنْ | مِنْ‿غُرَفِنْ`  ‖  `صُفْفَتْ‿فَأَيْ | نَ‿مِنَنْتِظَا | مِكَ‿تَطْعَنُوْنْ`

- **Gloss:** "A hundred and fourteen chambers, drawn up in ranks — so from what flank of your [own] order can you strike?"
- **Device:** *istifhām inkārī* (the unanswerable mocking question); the chambers *ṣuffat* (ranked, like an army or colonnade). *al-intiẓām* (falling-into-order) names the geodesic as built design; *taṭʿanūn* (you stab/breach) lands the rhyme.
- **Nuance:** *ghuraf* (chambers/upper-rooms) renders the sūras as the walled enclosures (sūra ← *sūr*, wall) of the blueprint; the line counts the exact 114 and dares any breach.

---

**8.**
طَرَقُوا المَسالِكَ كُلَّها فَتَطاوَلَتْ — وَأَتى عَلى أَقْصى المَدى أنْ يَقْصُرونْ
*(ṭaraqū l-masālika kullahā fa-taṭāwalat — wa-atā ʿalā aqṣā l-madā an yaqṣurūn)*

> Meter — H1: **S · S · S**   ·   H2: **S · I · I**
> `طَرَقُلْ‿مَسَا | لِكَ‿كُلْلَهَا | فَتَطَاوَلَتْ`  ‖  `وَأَتَا‿عَلَا | أَقْصَلْمَدَا | أَنْ‿يَقْصُرُوْنْ`

- **Gloss:** "They beat every path, and the paths grew long — and it came, at the far end of the reach, that they fall short."
- **Device:** *jinās al-ishtiqāq* on ق-ص-ر: *aqṣā* (farthest) ↔ *yaqṣurūn* (they fall short) meet at the rhyme — the seekers reach the *farthest* only to *come up short*. The shuffles that try to better the road all lengthen it (z=−11.46).
- **Nuance:** the rawiyy lands on *qaṣr*'s "falling-short" sense; every rival ordering is *longer*, so the labor *qaṣura* exactly at the *aqṣā* (limit).

---

**9.**
زَعَمُوا سِواهُ مِنَ التَّآليفِ مَنْهَجاً — هَيْهاتَ! كُلُّ مُؤَلَّفٍ يَتَوَهَّنونْ
*(zaʿamū siwāhu mina t-taʾālīfi manhajan — hayhāta! kullu muʾallafin yatawahhanūn)*

> Meter — H1: **S · I · I**   ·   H2: **I · S · S**
> `زَعَمُوْ‿سِوَا | هُ‿مِنَتْتَآ | لِيْفِ‿مَنْهَجَنْ`  ‖  `هَيْهَاتَ‿كُلْ | لُ‿مُؤَلْلَفِنْ | يَتَوَهْهَنُوْنْ`

- **Gloss:** "They claimed some arrangement other than its own to be the method — far from it! every [such] composition goes slack."
- **Device:** *uslūb al-qaṣr* by negation + *hayhāta* (particle of impossibility); the dare that no reshuffle holds (the signature dies under reshuffling). *yatawahhanūn* (root و-ه-ن, grow weak/slack) keeps the rhyme.
- **Nuance:** the comparison is bounded to *taʾālīf / muʾallaf* — *compositions* — not "every book ever written" (guardrail); every human ordering "goes slack" (*wahn*) where the Book's order never flags.

---

**10.**
عَدُّوا الفُصولَ، فَأَحْصَتِ الأَفْلاكُ ما — عَدُّوا، وَزادَتْ: في السَّماءِ فُنونْ
*(ʿaddū l-fuṣūla, fa-aḥṣati l-aflāku mā — ʿaddū, wa-zādat: fī s-samāʾi funūn)*

> Meter — H1: **I · S · I**   ·   H2: **I · I · S**
> `عَدْدُلْ‿فُصُو | لَ‿فَأَحْصَتِلْ | أَفْلَاكُ‿مَا`  ‖  `عَدْدُوْ‿وَزَا | دَتْ‿فِسْسَمَا | ءِ‿فُنُوْنُو`

- **Gloss:** "They counted the chapters — and the orbits had numbered what they counted, and added more: in the heaven are arts [past counting]."
- **Device:** *ḥusn al-taʿlīl* + the *aḥṣā*-as-worship move: human enumeration is a faint echo of the cosmos's prior, exhaustive *iḥṣāʾ* (Q72:28's register). *funūn* (arts/kinds) lands the rhyme on multiplicity exceeding the tally.
- **Nuance:** the *aflāk* (orbits) *aḥṣat* (numbered exhaustively — the Qur'an's own word for divine reckoning) before the seeker's abacus moved; the count was *received*, not imposed; *funūn* keeps it as awe-of-order, never an abjad sum (guardrail).

**11.**
لِلْحَرْفِ مَخْزَنُهُ، وَلِلْمَعْنى مَدىً — وَالنَّظْمُ خَيْطٌ، وَالعُقودُ تَنَظَّمونْ
*(li-l-ḥarfi makhzanuhu, wa-li-l-maʿnā madan — wa-n-naẓmu khayṭun, wa-l-ʿuqūdu tanaẓẓamūn)*

> Meter — H1: **I · S · S**   ·   H2: **I · I · S**
> `لِلْحَرْفِ‿مَخْ | زَنُهُوْ‿وَلِلْ | مَعْنَا‿مَدَنْ`  ‖  `وَنْنَظْمُ‿خَيْ | طُنْ‿وَلْعُقُوْ | دُ‿تَنَظْظَمُوْنْ`

- **Gloss:** "The letter has its storehouse, and meaning a span — and the composition is a thread, and the strung knots fall into order."
- **Device:** the *naẓm*-as-strung-pearls image — *al-naẓm* = the single ordering-thread through the 114; *al-ʿuqūd tanaẓẓamūn* (the knots are strung into order, root ن-ظ-م) lands the rhyme on the very act.
- **Nuance:** anchor B at its purest — "114 pearls on one *niẓām* [thread]"; the *makhzan* (storehouse) of the letter and the *madā* (span) of meaning are kept distinct, foreshadowing the anti-twin (sound and sense at maximal separation).

**12.**
حُكْمٌ يُفَصِّلُ آيَهُ ثُمَّ يَصِلُها — فَالقَطْعُ في إحْكامِهِ لا يَفْتُرونْ
*(ḥukmun yufaṣṣilu āyahu thumma yaṣiluhā — fa-l-qaṭʿu fī iḥkāmihi lā yafturūn)*

> Meter — H1: **I · S · S**   ·   H2: **I · I · I**
> `حُكْمُنْ‿يُفَصْ | صِلُ‿آيَهُوْ | ثُمْمَ‿يَصِلُهَا`  ‖  `فَلْقَطْعُ‿فِيْ | إِحْكَامِهِ | لَا‿يَفْتُرُوْنْ`

- **Gloss:** "A wise decree that divides its verse, then joins it — so the cutting, in its firm precision, never flags."
- **Device:** *jinás/ṭibāq* on فصل↔وصل: the Book *divides* its verses (fawāṣil) yet *binds* its sense (waṣl); *iḥkām* (firm precision, Q11:1's register *uḥkimat āyātuh*) names perfect order. *yafturūn* (root ف-ت-ر, slacken — register of *lā yafturūn*).
- **Nuance:** the *qaṭʿ* (clausula/cut) that never *futūr* (slackens) — the seam between verses is governed, not arbitrary; anchor A in embryo before the full anti-twin lands in Mv III.

**13.**
عَدَدٌ تَرَدّى في رِداءٍ مُبْهَمٍ — فَالرَّقْمُ تَحْتَ اللَّفْظِ سِرٌّ يَكْمُنونْ
*(ʿadadun taraddā fī ridāʾin mubhamin — fa-r-raqmu taḥta l-lafẓi sirrun yakmunūn)*

> Meter — H1: **S · I · I**   ·   H2: **I · I · I**
> `عَدَدُنْ‿تَرَدْ | دَا‿فِيْ‿رِدَا | ءِنْ‿مُبْهَمِنْ`  ‖  `فَرْرَقْمُ‿تَحْ | تَللَفْظِ‿سِرْ | رُنْ‿يَكْمُنُوْنْ`

- **Gloss:** "A number robed in an inscrutable garment — so the count, beneath the wording, is a secret that lies hidden."
- **Device:** the thesis-line of Mv II — *order = number in the robe of speech*; *al-raqm* (the marqūm, writing-that-is-also-number) fused with the wording. *yakmunūn* (root ك-م-ن, lie concealed) keeps the rhyme.
- **Nuance:** ⚠ the *raqm* here is the *image* of measured inscription, NOT an abjad-count — "the count beneath the wording" is the geodesic/architecture the project measured, *sirr* (a hidden design), never a cipher to be summed (guardrail).

## MOVEMENT III — THE WEAVE (14–19)
*The subtler proof: bell and fire never coincide — the anti-twin as paradox (r=−0.86; vs odes −0.48). Reach the threshold of the center. Tense, intricate, vertiginous; here the MEANING knots densest, so the SOUND runs most varied (anti-twin texture).*

---

**14.**
وَالسِّرُّ أَعْجَبُ: كُلَّما اشْتَبَكَ النُّهى — فَرَّ الرَّنينُ، وَفي التَّنائي يَفْتِنونْ
*(wa-s-sirru aʿjabu: kullamā shtabaka n-nuhā — farra r-ranīnu, wa-fī t-tanāʾī yaftinūn)*

> Meter — H1: **I · S · S**   ·   H2: **I · S · I**
> `وَسْسِرْرُ‿أَعْ | جَبُ‿كُلْلَمَ | شْتَبَكَنْنُهَا`  ‖  `فَرْرَرْرَنِيْ | نُ‿وَفِتْتَنَا | ءِيْ‿يَفْتِنُوْنْ`

- **Gloss:** "And the secret is stranger still: whenever the understanding interlocks, the ringing flees — and in [their] mutual distancing, they bewitch."
- **Device:** the anti-twin stated as a *law* (conditional *kullamā*): sense-density rises, music's *ranīn* (ringing) falls — the two move opposite (r=−0.86). *yaftinūn* (they enchant) lands the rhyme on the wonder.
- **Nuance:** *al-tanāʾī* (mutual remoteness) is the exact relation the correlation measures — meaning and music kept apart, *yet both at maximum*; the line is rougher-sounding (clustered ت/ف/ر) as its meaning knots — the texture-law worn in the grain.

---

**15.**
حَيْثُ القَوافي أَوْغَلَتْ في وَحْشَةٍ — كانَ المَعاني في الذُّرى تَتَزَيَّنونْ
*(ḥaythu l-qawāfī awghalat fī waḥshatin — kāna l-maʿānī fī dh-dhurā tatazayyanūn)*

> Meter — H1: **I · I · S**   ·   H2: **I · S · S**
> `حَيْثُلْ‿قَوَا | فِيْ‿أَوْغَلَتْ | فِيْ‿وَحْشَتِنْ`  ‖  `كَانَلْ‿مَعَا | نِيْ‿فِذْذُرَا | تَتَزَيْيَنُوْنْ`

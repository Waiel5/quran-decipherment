# al-Nūniyya al-Kubrā — DRAFT 1 ("THE HEIR")

> *Bismillāhi al-Raḥmāni al-Raḥīm.*
> A qaṣīda on the inimitable order of the Book — al-Kāmil, nūniyya (-ūn), 40 abyāt.
> The dare is aimed at every rival *method of knowing*; the only Absolute named is God;
> the poet is but the stringer of the pearls.

---

## I — NASĪB (1–6) · the seeker halts at the Book as at a ruined camp · cool · vast · reverent

---

**1 — al-maṭlaʿ** *(barāʿat al-istihlāl: the whole wager in one line)*

وَقَفْتُ عَلى بابٍ مِنَ الوَحْيِ مَوْزُونْ
كَمَوْقِفِ ذي العَيْنَيْنِ عِنْدَ السُّكُونْ

`ka-maw-qi-fi | dhi-l-ʿay-nay-ni | ʿin-da-s-su-kūn`
`/ / u / | / / u / | / / /` → Iḍ · Iḍ · **Iḍₘ**

(iii) "I halted at a gate of revelation, **measured** — / as the two-eyed man halts at the stillness." The seeker stops before the Book as Imruʾ al-Qays before the ruined camp. **مَوْزُون** (measured/proportioned — the thesis-word, Q15:19 register) lands first; **السُّكُون** carries a meta-pun (prosodic *sukūn* / the still center to come). *Devices:* barāʿat al-istihlāl; the fulcrum-word *mawzūn* fronted as al-Mutanabbī fronts عَلى قَدْرِ. **Ring-emblem A planted (gate · halting · stillness) — reopened at bayt 40.**

The Book outlasts the aeons (*al-duhūr*). **مُجُون** here in its old sense "wear / dissolution," paired against indestructibility. *Devices:* gnomic māḍī (O1); ṭibāq (time passes / it does not perish); the ruined-camp topos inverted — the one ruin that is no ruin.

Mubālagha confined to the Book's *order*. *(Rhyme: ridf-ū, distinct — replaces the earlier التَّكْوين, which was a ridf-ī sinād al-ridf defect.)*

## II — THE ROAD (7–13) · prove from outside: count the chambers → the shortest road · warming · wondering

---

**7**

تَلْقَ القِفارَ تَقاصَرَتْ لِلعُيونْ

(ii) `ʿud-da-l-ma-nā | zi-la-wa-t-lu-hā | min-aw-wa-lin`
`/ / u / | uu / u / | / / u /` → Iḍ · S · Iḍ

`/ / u / | uu / u / | uu / /` → Iḍ · S · **Sₘ**

(iii) "Count the way-stations, recite them from the first — / you will find the wastes drawn short for the *eyes*." Warming.

**8 — ★ THUNDERCLAP** *(the shortest road as a law of proportion — the عَلى قَدْرِ engine)*

وَعَلى مِقْدارِ الخُطى رَحْبُ الحُزونْ

(ii) `ʿa-lā-qad-ri-n | ni-ẓā-mi-ta-qā | ṣa-ra-l-fa-yā-fī`

`/ / u / | / / u / | uu / /` → Iḍ · Iḍ · **Sₘ**
*(ṣadr fully sound — the metric calm of a law being stated, al-Mutanabbī's own weapon.)*

(iii) "By the measure of the ordering, the deserts contract; / by the size of the stride, the breadth of the rough *highlands* [shrinks]." A doubled inverse-proportion on **عَلى قَدْرِ / عَلى مِقْدارِ** — the عَلى قَدْرِ engine turned to *order vs distance*. Delete every noun: still a law. *Devices:* muqābala + ḥusn al-taʿlīl (the waste shrinks *because* of order); jinās قَدْر/مِقْدار. **THUNDERCLAP #1.**

The *meaning* knots (the proof) as the music roughens — anti-twin texture.

## III — THE WEAVE (14–19) · the subtler proof: bell and fire never coincide · tense · intricate · vertiginous

---

**14 — ★ THE LETHAL CORE (a): the anti-twin law — the central thunderclap**

(iii) "When the melodies draw near, their wellspring [of sense] flees; / and when the meanings advance, the *melodies* flee." The anti-twin (r=−0.86): sense and music sworn opposites, both held. Densest sense, roughest music — anti-twin texture obeyed. *(Rhyme root س-ج-n, distinct.)*

### Prosody commitment (resolves the central craft problem)
The poem is **al-Kāmil al-tāmm with the maqṭūʿ ḍarب** (مُتَفاعِلُن مُتَفاعِلُن مُتَفاعِلُن ‖ … ‖ **مُتَفاعِل**), held every line. This is an attested classical form (e.g. Khalīl Muṭrān) whose qāfiya **requires a ridf** — i.e. precisely a murdaf `-ūn` close. It is the correct ḍarب for a nūniyya in `-ūn`, because a pausal rhyme-word `CvCūn` (e.g. *mak-NŪN* = `/ / /`) lands as the maqṭūʿ ḍarب `mutfāʿil` (`/ / /`) under iḍmār, or `mutafāʿil` (`uu / /`) when the antepenult is short. **Why this matters:** under the *ṣaḥīḥ* ḍarب (`mutafāʿilun`, `uu / u /`) a three-long `-ūn` word would NOT scan; committing to the maqṭūʿ ḍارب makes the entire rhyme-set legal. **The ʿarūḍ** (the ṣadr's last foot) is **ṣaḥīḥa** (`mutafāʿilun`) from bayt 4 onward — visible e.g. in 4/5/8 where the ṣadr ends `…u /`. **The maṭlaʿ (bayt 1) is deliberately muṣarraʿ** (taṣrīʿ): its ṣadr also rhymes (مَوْزُون), so its ʿarūḍ takes the *ḍارب's* maqṭūʿ shape (`/ / /`) — the classical opening ornament, **not** drift. After bayt 1 the ʿarūḍ reverts to ṣaḥīḥa; every ʿajuz keeps the one maqṭūʿ ḍارب shape throughout.

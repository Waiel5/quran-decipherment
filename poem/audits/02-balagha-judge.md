# 02 — BALĀGHA JUDGE (POWER + AUTHENTICITY)
### Brutal, specific verdict on al-Nūniyya al-Kubrā (master-v1, 30 abyāt)
*Judged against al-Mutanabbī's* عَلى قَدْرِ أهْلِ العَزْمِ. *Meter-legality NOT adjudicated here (owned by the ʿarūḍ auditor); all recasts kept singable in al-Kāmil with the fixed Family-B long-penult ‑ūn rhyme.*

---

## 0. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH VERDICT (read this first)

This is a **strong, real, B+/A− qaṣīda with three or four genuinely canon-adjacent abyāt and a spine of architectural ambition that almost nothing in modern Arabic verse attempts.** It is *not yet* canon-grade, and it does not stand beside على قَدْرِ — not because the ambition is wrong but because the execution sags in a recognizable, fixable way: **the ʿajuz hemistichs are systematically weaker than the ṣadr hemistichs.** Over and over the ṣadr lands a clean image or law and then the ʿajuz arrives dragging a rhyme-word it must *justify* — a tacked-on nominal (ذا المَرْصون، ذا المَدْيون، المَوْطون، المَعْجون) glued on with a demonstrative or a limp coordinating و. al-Mutanabbī never does this; in على قَدْرِ the rhyme-word is the **logical destination** of the sentence, not its toll-booth. The poem's deepest structural flaw is that **the rhyme is driving the syntax** in roughly a third of the lines, and a trained ear hears it. The cure is not more devices — the poem is over-devised — but **plainer, more inevitable ʿajuz hemistichs** where the last word is *earned*. Where it earns the rhyme (1, 8, 11, 16, 18, 24) it genuinely detonates. Where it doesn't (12, 13, 14, 17, 26, 30) it gestures.

**Closest comparison to على قَدْرِ:** the maṭlaʿ (b.1) and the volta (b.18) are within hailing distance of the benchmark's *grammar of law*. The NŪN reveal (b.24) is the single most original stroke and has no al-Mutanabbī analogue — it is the poem's true signature. Everything in Movement III (the "anti-twin," 10–14) is the weakest stretch: clever in prose-paraphrase, muddy in the verse.

## 2. THE LETHAL CORE — DOES IT DETONATE?

Applying the toolkit's own litmus (PART 2): *delete the proper nouns / referents — is the line still true, balanced, inevitable?*

### b.1 (maṭlaʿ) — **DETONATES.** ✅
قِفْ، إِنَّ في الوَحْيِ انْتِظامًا مُحْكَمَا ‖ صَرْحٌ بَناهُ القَوْلُ سِرًّا مَكْنون
Strip "الوَحْي" and you still have *"Halt — there is a firm-set order; an edifice the Word built, secretly treasured."* True, balanced, front-loads the wager (order + concealment) exactly as briefed. The قِفْ borrowed from Imruʾ al-Qays/al-Mutanabbī's وَقَفتَ register is earned. **One demerit vs على قَدْرِ:** the benchmark opens on a *preposition of measure* (عَلى قَدْرِ) — a ratio. This opens on an imperative + existential إِنَّ — a *declaration*. Slightly less law-like, slightly more sermon-like. But it lands. The synthesizer's choice to sacrifice the muṣarraʿ maṭlaʿ (to place mawzūn at axis 16) is defensible but **costs the poem its single most al-Mutanabbī-like opportunity** — a chiastic, root-jinās, mirror-the-content first line. (See change #1 below.)

### b.11 (anti-twin thunderclap) — **DETONATES.** ✅
يَنْأى المَعاني حينَ يَدْنو جَرْسُها ‖ إِنْ يَدْنُ مَعْنًى، فَاللَّذيذُ المَسْجون
Delete nothing — there are no proper nouns; it is *pure law of motion*: "the meanings recede the moment their ringing nears; if a meaning draws near, the sweet[-sound] is imprisoned." This is the toolkit's ideal: muqābala + ṭibāq (yanʾā/yadnū), the rhyme-word (masjūn, Yūsuf-register) IS the thought's destination. **The only blemish** is the number-agreement يَنْأى المَعاني (masc. sing. verb fronting a broken plural) — classically licensed but it grates one notch; a reciter feels it. Fixable by recasting the ṣadr to a singular subject (see recast pool). Still the strongest of the five cores after 18 and 24.

### b.18 (verdict / volta) — **DETONATES — the best line in the poem.** ✅✅
ما خَطَّ هذا النَّظْمَ كَفٌّ ثابِتٌ ‖ مَهْما تَرى الضِّدَّيْنِ لَيْسا مَمْنون
This is the one bayt that is *fully* al-Mutanabbī-grade. uslūb al-qaṣr (ما + negation), gnomic, zero deixis ("this ordering" is the only pointer and it survives deletion as "such an ordering"). مَمْنون lands on the exact Quranic register the rubric wants (*ajrun ghayru mamnūn* — undiminished). Delete "النَّظْم" → *"No steady hand inscribed it; however you behold the two opposites, they are not stinted."* Still true, still a law. **This is the verdict the whole poem is built to deliver, and it delivers.** If every ʿajuz in the poem were tuned to this standard it would be canon-grade.

### b.24 (the nūn reveal) — **DETONATES — the most ORIGINAL stroke.** ✅✅
إِذْ عَدَّدوا الحَرْفَيْنِ وَالميمَ اكْتَفَوْا ‖ لكِنْ هُمُ لَمْ يَحْسِبوا تاجَ النّونْ
This has **no analogue in al-Mutanabbī** and is the poem's true claim to originality: a 30-line *nūniyya* whose verdict-clause lands on النّون — the rhyme-letter and the thesis-word become one. تاجَ النّونْ ("the crown of the nūn") is a real image, and the قصر (لكِنْ هُمُ لَمْ يَحْسِبوا) has the calm of foreknowledge (oracular O7). The ṣadr (اكْتَفَوْا) is merely competent, but the ʿajuz is a genuine thunderclap and the doubled-nūn cadence جَ‑نْـ‑نونْ is a real sonic event. **This is the line a future anthologist would quote to prove the poem exists for a reason.**

### b.30 (loop-close) — **GESTURES — concept lands, word fails.** ⚠️
وَعُدْتُ بابَ البَدْءِ حينَ خَتَمْتُهُ ‖ فَقَرَأْتُهُ، وَالخَتْمُ بَدْءٌ مَكْفون
The *concept* (the seal is a beginning; al-Nās hands back al-Fātiḥa; radd al-ʿajuz across 30 lines back to b.1) is the right way to close and the ṭibāq خَتَم/بَدْء is clean. But **مَكْفون detonates the wrong charge.** كفن in living Arabic means *to shroud a corpse* (كَفَن = burial shroud); I confirmed it has **zero occurrences in the Quran** — so it carries none of the redemptive register the other rhyme-words borrow, and its only resonance is funerary. To close a poem about an *undying* order (b.2: لَمْ يُصِبْهُ تَغَيُّر) on a shroud-word is a tonal self-wound. The first line opens on مَكْنون (treasured); the last must answer it with something *warmer and live*, not a winding-sheet. **This is the single highest-leverage fix in the poem** (see recast #8). With the right rhyme-word, b.30 jumps from 2.5 to a 4.5 loop-close.

**Core scorecard: 4 of 5 detonate (1, 11, 18, 24); 30 gestures and is the most fixable.** The lethal architecture is sound; the loop just needs its last word replaced.

## 3. "AI-ARABIC" / WEAKNESS HUNT

### Confirming/refuting the synthesizer's own flags

- **b.13 (mangled حُسْنٌ وَلا…)** — **CONFIRMED, worst in poem.** حُسْنٌ، وَلا، وَالصَّوْتُ عَنْهُ المَطْعون has *two* coordinators stacked (وَلا then وَ) around an elided verb. No reciter recovers "beauty [is not hemmed in], nor [does it fail], while the sound is un-pierced by it" from this. It is the clearest "assembled-not-composed" line. **Recast mandatory.**
- **b.17 (وَإِلَيْهِهِ doubled pronoun)** — **CONFIRMED.** إِلَيْهِ + هِ is metrically-driven padding; classical grammar tolerates the long form but the ear hears a stutter, and المَوْطون compounds it. The whole bayt is the clearest case of *rhyme steering syntax*. **Recast.**
- **b.26 (ذا مَدْيون colloquial copula)** — **CONFIRMED.** ذا as a copular bridge ("their wreckage — *this* [is] in debt") is the same demonstrative-crutch that also mars b.5 (ذا مَفْتون) and b.12 (ذا المَرْصون). مَدْيون for "bankrupt" is fine; ذا is the problem. **Recast the ʿajuz.**
- **b.29 (مَظْنون stretch)** — **CONFIRMED, and worse than flagged.** The intended sense ("the *presumed/pretended prophetic rank* al-Mutanabbī grasped, here disowned") is **not legible from مَظْنون** at all; the word means "the surmised/suspected thing." A reader hears "no suspected-thing summoned me," which is gibberish in context. The clever al-Mutanabbī in-joke dies on the page. **Recast or accept that the allusion is invisible.**
- **b.30 (مَكْفون = shroud, wrong connotation)** — **CONFIRMED, highest-leverage.** See §2. Corpus check: كفن root = 0 Quranic hits; the word is funerary. **Recast mandatory.**

### Others the synthesizer did NOT flag — FOUND:

- **b.12 — نارٌ وَجَمْرٌ is a FALSE antithesis, and the briefed image (fire vs *bell*) is LOST.** The gloss claims "sound (jaras/bell) and fire" but the Arabic says نار (fire) + جَمْر (ember) — near-synonyms, both heat. There is no antithesis and no bell; the "welded opposites" concept evaporated in composition. Plus ذا المَرْصون demonstrative-tag. **This is a concept-to-Arabic failure, not just diction.** Recast.
- **b.14 — المَعْجون is the wrong-register rhyme.** "Kneaded paste/dough" (and in modern Arabic, *toothpaste/ointment*) for the *summit* of meaning is bathos — a low, kitchen-register word crowning the most vertiginous claim. The ṣadr (القَوافي أَوْغَلَتْ في وَحْشَةٍ) is good; the rhyme undercuts it. Recast.
- **b.11 — يَنْأى المَعاني** (masc. sing. verb + broken plural فronted): licensed but audibly off; appears again at **b.27 تَلاحَمَتْ فيهِ المَعاني** (fem. sing.) — the poem is inconsistent about المَعاني's agreement, which a careful ear notices.
- **b.6 — تُلْ في نِظامٍ كانَ نَهْجًا مَقْرون is pure abstraction (ḥashw).** Three abstract nouns (نِظام، نَهْج، مَقْرون) and no image. The most important *finding* in the poem (the geodesic, z=−11.46) is rendered as "travel in an order; it was a wedded road." Flat. Recast for a concrete road/distance image.
- **b.20 — المَلْعون mis-registers.** "The accursed" is a theological condemnation (Iblīs-register, Q15:34); using it to mean merely "any mortal artisan, by contrast with God" reads as *cursing humanity*, which is not the intent and clashes with the poem's submission-key. مَغْبون (b.19) already does the "mortal short-changed maker" job better. Consider swapping.
- **b.9 — وَلكِنْ مَوْضون** hangs as a bare predicate with an elided subject ("but [it is] close-woven"); grammatical but limp — the قصر "you don't see a digit *but* a weave" wants the rhyme-word to be the punch, and مَوْضون (though a gorgeous Q56:15 hapax) arrives unsupported.

**Pattern diagnosis (the real "AI-Arabic tell"):** it is not vocabulary — the lexicon is rich and largely authentic. The tell is **the demonstrative-copula crutch** (ذا مَفْتون 5, ذا المَرْصون 12, ذا مَدْيون 26) and **the bolted-on nominal ʿajuz** (المَوْطون 17, المَعْجون 14, مَقْرون 6) where the rhyme-word is *appended* rather than *arrived at*. al-Mutanabbī's rhyme-words are always the grammatical and logical terminus of the sentence; here, too often, they are luggage.

## 4. CONCRETE RECASTS (the 8 weakest)

All in al-Kāmil, ḍarb maqṭūʿ on a long-penult ‑ūn, keeping or upgrading the Family-B rhyme. Tashkīl given. I keep the original rhyme-word where it is salvageable and swap it where it is the problem (12, 14, 17, 30). **These are singable in al-Kāmil; the ʿarūḍ auditor owns final foot-legality.**

**b.13 — RECAST (fix the stammer; keep المَطْعون).**
Original: رَمَقا مَعًا أَوْجَ العُلى، لا يَنْحَصِرْ ‖ حُسْنٌ، وَلا، وَالصَّوْتُ عَنْهُ المَطْعون
Recast:
> رَمَقا مَعًا أَوْجَ العُلى فَكِلاهُما ‖ حَيٌّ، وَلا صَوْتٌ لَدَيْهِ مَطْعون
*"Both gazed together at the zenith of the heights, and each of them / is alive — no sound beside it is pierced."*

## 5. OVERALL VERDICT

### Is this canon-grade?
**Not yet — but it is closer than any synthetic qaṣīda has a right to be, and four of its lines (1, 11, 18, 24) are within arm's reach of the canon.** Canon-grade means *every* bayt survives the delete-the-nouns test and *no* ʿajuz drags its rhyme. This poem clears that bar on maybe twelve lines and fails it on eight. The architecture — 6 movements, mirror axis 15|16, volta at 18, the rawiyy-as-thesis at 24, the 30→1 loop — is **more structurally ambitious than على قَدْرِ itself** (which is a linear panegyric, not a ring). That ambition is real and it is the poem's best claim. But al-Mutanabbī wins on a simpler thing: **line-by-line inevitability.** Every one of his 46 abyāt is at least a 3.5; this poem has a basin of 2s.

### Where it genuinely soars
- **b.18** — the qaṣr verdict. Canon-grade as-is.
- **b.24–25** — the NŪN reveal + the glyph-as-cosmos. The *original* contribution; nothing in the benchmark does this. The rhyme-letter becoming the thesis-word is a genuinely new device, well-executed.
- **b.1, b.11, b.16** — the maṭlaʿ wager, the anti-twin law of motion, the mawzūn↔Aḥad clasp. All earn their rhyme.
- The **rhyme ledger itself** — 30 distinct Family-B long-penult ‑ūn words, zero īṭāʾ, each Quranically anchored — is a feat of *taṣrīʿ* discipline al-Mutanabbī never attempted (his rhyme-field is narrower). Where the words are *earned* (مَمْنون, النّون, المَوْزون, مَسْجون, مَصُون-recast) this is the poem's secret weapon.

### Where it sags
- **Movement III (10–14), the "anti-twin," is the weakest stretch** — exactly where the *concept* is most abstract (a correlation r=−0.86 rendered as imagery). b.12's false antithesis and b.13's stammer and b.14's bathos all cluster here. The poem is trying to versify a *scatterplot* and the imagery buckles.
- **The demonstrative-copula crutch** (ذا ×3) and **bolted-on nominal ʿajuz** (×4) — the structural tell of rhyme-driven syntax.
- **Three rhyme-words mis-register**: مَكْفون (shroud, b.30), المَلْعون (curse, b.20), المَعْجون (paste, b.14) — each tonally wrong for its slot.
- **The al-Mutanabbī in-jokes are invisible**: مَظْنون (b.29) as "would-be-prophet's rank" and the laka l-ḥamdu redirect are clever in the apparatus but illegible in the verse.

### Honestly, how close to على قَدْرِ?
On a 100-scale where على قَدْرِ = 100: **this poem is ~72 as it stands, ~85 with the eight recasts.** The gap to 100 is not ambition (this *out-ambitions* the benchmark structurally) and not lexicon (the word-armory is richer) — it is **the relentless line-level inevitability** that makes every al-Mutanabbī bayt detachable and quotable. He never wrote a 2. This poem writes eight of them, and they are all in the ʿajuz, and they are all fixable.

### The 3 changes that would most raise its power
1. **Re-tune EVERY ʿajuz so the rhyme-word is the sentence's logical terminus, not its toll-booth** — kill all three ذا-copulas (5, 12, 26) and the bolted nominals (6, 14, 17). This is the highest-leverage single discipline; it is what separates the 3s from the 5s and it is purely a craft fix. Start with the eight recasts in §4.
2. **Replace مَكْفون at b.30 with a live, warm word (مَصُون / مَأْمون).** The loop-close is the second-most-important line in the poem (after the maṭlaʿ it rings back to) and it currently ends on a burial shroud. One word. Enormous tonal payoff.
3. **Rebuild Movement III's imagery (12–14) so the "two maxima / inverse-proportion" is carried by ONE clean, true antithesis** (صَوْت/صَمْت, نار/جَدْوَل — fire/brook), not the false نار/جَمْر pair and not abstract paste. Keep b.11 (it already works); make 12–14 live up to it. Optionally **reclaim the muṣarraʿ maṭlaʿ** (a chiastic, root-jinás first line in the على قَدْرِ mold) if the mawzūn-at-16 placement can be preserved another way — that would put a true thunderclap in the one position al-Mutanabbī uses for his.

**Bottom line:** A genuinely impressive poem with a canon-grade verdict-line (18), a canon-grade original device (24), and a basin of eight fixable ʿajuz-sags. It does not yet stand *beside* al-Mutanabbī — but it stands closer than the synthesizer's own modest self-audit claims, and the distance left is **craft, not vision.** Fix the eight ʿajuz hemistichs and the shroud-word, and it crosses from "remarkable synthesis" into "would survive in an anthology."

*Wa-Llāhu aʿlam. The pearls are well-strung; eight of the threads need re-knotting, and one clasp is the wrong metal.*

# 0. Method, and two facts that govern the whole audit

**Notation.** `1` = moving letter, `0` = still (sukūn / long vowel ā ī ū / tanwīn-nūn / first half of a shadda). A lone `1` = light syllable **`u`**; `1 0` = heavy **`–`**. Hamzat al-waṣl elides after a vowel; shadda = two letters; the article lām (sun or moon) closes the preceding short vowel into a heavy syllable. Every string below is re-derived from the **pronounced** Arabic, not from any earlier taqṭīʿ.

**al-Kāmil feet actually used here** (the watid `ʿi-lun` = `u –` is fixed; ziḥāf hits the sabab-thaqīl *head* `muta`):

| foot | weight | role | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| mutafāʿilun | `u u – u –` | non-terminal / ʿarūḍ | sound |
| mutfāʿilun (iḍmār) | `– – u –` | non-terminal / ʿarūḍ | head `uu`→`–`; identical to Rajaz *mustafʿilun* |
| mufāʿilun (waqṣ) | `u – u –` | non-terminal | head loses one letter; **heavy ziḥāf — flag if at ʿarūḍ/ḍarب** |
| **mutafāʿil (maqṭūʿ)** | **`u u – –`** | **ḍarب** | the prescribed close |
| **mutfāʿil (maqṭūʿ+iḍmār)** | **`– – –`** | **ḍarب** | the prescribed close, iḍmār variant |
| mufāʿil (maqṭūʿ+waqṣ) | `u – –` | ḍarب | **NOT sanctioned — this is the "waqṣ-ḍarب" flagged below** |

**FACT 1 — the poem's de-facto ḍarب is `– – –` (mutfāʿil, iḍmār-maqṭūʿ), not `u u – –`.** Every one of the 11 fully-clean ʿajuz lands `– – –`; **zero** land the "sound" `u u – –`. That is fine and self-consistent (`– – –` is a standard al-Kāmil maqṭūʿ-iḍmār ḍarب, and §1.4 of the ruling file explicitly allows iḍmār on the ḍarب head without changing ḍarب identity). The poem is therefore best certified as **al-Kāmil maqṭūʿ, ḍarب `– – –`**, with the sound `u u – –` permitted as a free variant. **The fixes below all target `– – –`.**

**FACT 2 — the hāʾ al-ḍamīr takes its ṣila and lengthens (`-hu → -hū`, `-hi → -hī`) after a moving letter, as standard in classical verse.** Six ṣadrs end on a pronoun and **only scan ṣaḥīḥ with this lengthening** (B3, B5, B19, B20, B23, B29, B30); one ʿajuz too (B30). These are **clean, not defects** — they were the suspected-weak ṣadrs you flagged, and they pass once the pronoun is read long. (Mid-word, no ṣila: e.g. *matnayhi* keeps `-hi` short because it follows a sukūn.)

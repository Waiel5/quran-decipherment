# 0. Governing facts (re-confirmed from phonemes)

- **FACT 1 — locked ḍarب is `– – –`.** Every clean ʿajuz lands `– – –` (Idm) or, twice (B19? no), the bare-sound is never used; all closes are heavy-headed `– – –`. A close that reaches only `– –` (one mora shy) = orphan/too-short = BREAK. A close `u – –` = waqṣ-maqṭūʿ = ḍarب-drift = BREAK.
- **FACT 2 — the hāʾ al-ḍamīr ṣila is LEGAL, NOT taskīn.** `-hu→-hū`, `-hi→-hī` after a moving letter at a hemistich-end is *ziyādat al-ṣila* (ishbāʿ), a sanctioned lengthening — distinct from **taskīn** (silencing a normally-voweled letter mid-hemistich, a ḍarūra qabīḥa). Eleven clean bayts use the ṣila (B2 *mithlahū*, B3 *qarārahū*, B9 *bibābihī*, B10 *nasjihī*, B12 *maʿīnuhū*, B17 *lahū*, B18 *miṯluhū*, B24 *bismihī*, B29 *muthīruhū*, B30 *khitāmihī/qaraʾtuhū*). These are clean. The audit explicitly separated the two: I tested every "clean" line for a hidden taskīn (mode 1) and found NONE.
- **FACT 3 — the taskīn defect of the PRIOR version is removed.** I verified phonemically that the *old* lines scanned **only** by an illegal internal sukūn, and that the corrections dissolve each (see §B-i). Specifically: old B16 `عِنْدَ الأَحَدْ` scans `--u-uu-u----` **only** by silencing the dāl of الأَحَدِ (genitive after عِنْدَ); voweled `al-aḥadi` it BREAKS `--uuuuu-u----`. The correction `لِلْواحِدِ الفَرْدِ، اطْمَأَنَّ المَوْزونْ` scans `--u---u----` with every letter voweled. Likewise old `السُّوَرْ`/`مَنْزِلْ`.

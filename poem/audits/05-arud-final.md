# 0. Governing facts (carried from v2, re-confirmed from phonemes)

**FACT 1 — the poem's de-facto ḍarб is `– – –` (mutfāʿil = maqṭūʿ+iḍmār), NOT the sound `u u – –`.** Every fully-clean ʿajuz lands `– – –`; zero land `u u – –`. All fixes below target `– – –`. (`u u – –` is a legal free variant but the poem never uses it; keeping the close uniform at `– – –` is the lock.)

**FACT 2 — the hāʾ al-ḍamīr takes its ṣila and lengthens (`-hu→-hū`, `-hi→-hī`) after a moving letter.** Several ṣadrs/ʿajuz scan ONLY with this lengthening (B3, B5, B20, B23, B29, B30 ṣadrs; B19 ʿajuz fix). Clean, not defects — but the reciter must hold them long. (Mid-word after a sukūn, e.g. *matnayhi*, stays short.)

**Two corrections to the v2 audit (v2 mis-scored these; re-scan from phonemes overrides):**
- **B5 ʿajuz** — v2 marked its fix-line *…faradda wahwa maftūn* CLEAN. It is **WAQṢ**: *wahwa maftūn* = `… u – –` (وَهْوَ = `– u`, so the close is `wa-maf-tūn` = `u – –`). Needs the definitize fix.

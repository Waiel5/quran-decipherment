# 0. THE LOAD-BEARING QUESTION, ANSWERED FIRST: is "– – –" a legal al-Kāmil maqṭūʿ ḍarb?

**VERDICT: YES. "– – –" is a legal, attested realization of the al-Kāmil al-tāmm maqṭūʿ ḍarb — namely the foot مُتْفاعِلْ = مُسْتَفْعِلْ (iḍmār applied to the already-cut maqṭūʿ foot).** The poem's central metrical claim survives ground-truthing. This is NOT a fabrication and NOT a defect *per se*. (The defects this audit DID find are elsewhere — see §3–§5.)

## 0.1 The two realizations of the maqṭūʿ ḍarb

The maqṭūʿ ḍarb of al-Kāmil al-tāmm (ʿilla = qaṭʿ: delete the final moving letter of the terminal watid majmūʿ and silence its predecessor) is the foot **مُتَفاعِلْ**:

```
مُتَفاعِلْ  =  mu-ta-fā-ʿil  =  1 1 1 0 1 0  =  u u – –     (= the prosodists' فِعْلاتُن / فَعِلاتُن — same 6 letters, same scansion)
```

But al-Kāmil's defining ziḥāf — **iḍmār** (silence the 2nd letter of the opening sabab thaqīl: مُتَ `11`/`uu` → مُتْ `10`/`–`) — is available on **every** foot, **including the ḍarb**, and does not change the ḍarb's identity (it touches the foot-HEAD, not the cut foot-TAIL). Apply iḍmār to the maqṭūʿ foot:

```
مُتْفاعِلْ  =  mut-fā-ʿil  =  1 0 1 0 1 0  =  – – –       (written by the handbooks as مُسْتَفْعِلْ)
```

So the maqṭūʿ ḍarb has **two legal surface forms**, selected by the rhyme-word's own shape:
- short pre-tail head → **u u – –** (e.g. khabālā, al-aʿmāl, sirran-maknūn);
- heavy pre-tail head → **– – –** (e.g. an-nūn, al-mawzūn after a heavy syllable).

Both are *the same ḍarb* (maqṭūʿ); they differ only by iḍmār on the head. **This is exactly the poem's "Sₘ = u u – –" vs "Iḍₘ = – – –" pair.** The poem's framing is prosodically correct.

## 0.2 Ground-truth citations (fetched, scanned from phonemes)

**(A) The maqṭūʿ ḍarb realized u u – – — al-Khansāʾ-type shāhid** (al-Tabrīzī / al-Kāfī tradition; baytalqaseed, almerja, mawdoo3 all concur on the taqṭīʿ):

> وَإِذا دَعَوْنَكَ عَمَّهُنَّ فَإِنَّهُ ‖ نَسَبٌ يَزيدُكَ عِنْدَهُنَّ خَبالا
> ʿajuz taqṭīʿ (sources, verbatim): نسبن يزي = مُتَفاعِلُن · دكعن دهن = مُتَفاعِلُن · **نخبالا = فعلاتن (= مُتَفاعِلْ, MAQṬŪʿ)**
> My re-scan of the ḍarb foot from phonemes: نخبالا = na-kha-bā-lā = `1 1 1 0 1 0` = **u u – –** ✓.
> Pre-tail head here is SHORT (na-kha = u u) → surface u u – –.

**(B) The maqṭūʿ ḍarb realized – – – (THE load-bearing citation) — al-Tabrīzī "Talkhīṣāt al-Shāfiya" (dars 12, abuqudamah.com), form "عروض صحيحة + ضرب مقطوع":**

> وَمَنِ ادَّعَى سَلَفِيَّةً فَلْيَلْتَزِمْ ‖ فَهْمَ الصَّحَابَةِ دُونَ تَأْويلاتِ
> ʿajuz taqṭīʿ (page, verbatim, foot-labels): فهم الصحا = مُسْتَفْعِلُن · بة دون تأ = مُسْتَفْعِلُن · **ويلاتِ = مُسْتَفْعِلْ**
> The page states explicitly: **«ونلاحظ أن الضرب هو (مُسْتَفعلْ) = (مُتْفاعِلْ) بإسكان التاء لدخول زحاف الإضمار»** — "note the ḍarb is مُسْتَفعلْ = مُتْفاعِلْ, by silencing the tāʾ through the ziḥāf of iḍmār."
> My re-scan of the ḍarb foot: مُسْتَفْعِلْ = mus-taf-ʿil = `1 0 1 0 1 0` = **– – –** ✓.
> THIS IS THE SAME SHAPE the poem tags "Iḍₘ". The maqṭūʿ+iḍmār ḍarb = – – – is a NAMED, EXAMPLED classical form.

**(C) Pattern witness, heavy pre-tail → maqṭūʿ — Bishāra al-Khūrī (al-Akhṭal al-Ṣaghīr):**
> …ذُخراً يَكونُ كَصالِحِ الأَعمالِ — ending al-aʿmāl(i), penult mā LONG, seats the maqṭūʿ close on a heavy pre-tail. (Anthologised tāmm-maqṭūʿ exemplar.)

**CONCLUSION of §0:** The ruling-file's own claim is vindicated, and more strongly than it stated: not only is maqṭūʿ a standard ḍarb, but its **– – – realization is itself attested with a worked classical example** (al-Tabrīzī's سلفيّة line). The poem closing ~28 lines on "– – –" is therefore **prosodically legal**, provided two things hold, which I now test ruthlessly: (i) every such ʿajuz actually parses into a legal al-Kāmil foot-sequence ending in a genuine maqṭūʿ foot; (ii) no ʿajuz silently drops to a NON-maqṭūʿ close, and no rhyme-penult is secretly SHORT (which would force a ṣaḥīḥ ḍarb = ḍarb-drift).

# 5. DEFECT LIST (with minimal, sense-preserving, Family-B fixes)

### ★ HARD DEFECT 1 — Bayt 9 ʿajuz (ILLEGAL meter)
- **Problem:** «لا تُبْصِرُ العَدَدَ، وَلكِنْ مَوْضونْ». `…tubṣiru l-ʿadada wa-…` = `…u – u u u u –…` — the word **العَدَدَ (ʿa-da-da = u u u)**, sitting after the light `-ru` of tubṣiru and before `wa-`, stacks **four consecutive light syllables**. No al-Kāmil foot tolerates more than two. The ʿajuz **does not scan**; the poem's "S" tag for foot 2 is impossible.
- **Minimal fix (meter-verified here):** **«لا الرَّقْمُ يُحْصى، إِنَّما هوَ المَوْضونْ»** — *lā r-raqmu yuḥṣā, innamā huwa l-mawḍūn* = `– – u – / – – u – / u u – –` = **Iḍ·Iḍ·Sₘ, LEGAL**, ends mawḍūn (penult maw LONG). Sense preserved: "the digit is not to be tallied; rather it is the close-woven." (Drafter should re-voice for register; the scan is sound.)

### ★ HARD DEFECT 2 — Bayt 24 ʿajuz (ILLEGAL meter) — *the النّون thunderclap line*
- **Problem:** «لكِنْ هُمُ لَمْ يَحْسِبوا تاجَ النّونْ». `lākin humu lam…` = `– – u u –…` — the pronoun **هُمُ (humu = u u)** makes the foot-1 opening `– – u u`, one light too many; al-Kāmil after an iḍmār head (`– –`) needs the watid `u –`, not `u u`. Foot 1 **does not scan**; the poem's "Iḍ" tag is impossible. (The rhyme 'an-nūn' is fine — §4.)
- **Minimal fix (meter-verified, trivial):** contract `lākin humu` → **«لكِنَّهُمْ لَمْ يَحْسِبوا تاجَ النّونْ»** — *lākinnahum lam yaḥsibū tāja n-nūn* = `– – u – / – – u – / – – –` = **Iḍ·Iḍ·Iḍₘ, LEGAL**, ends an-nūn (penult jan LONG). **Sense identical** ("but they did not count the crown of the nūn"); the reveal, the rawiyy-landing, and the rhyme are all preserved. **A two-syllable edit saves the marquee line.**

### ⚠ ROUGHNESS 3 — Bayt 29 ʿajuz (legal but the poem's only waqṣ + mis-scanned)
- **Problem:** «… وَما دَعانِيَ مَظْنونْ». `wa-mā daʿā…` foot 2 = `wa mā da ʿā` = `u – u –` = **mufāʿilun (waqṣ)** — al-Kāmil's heaviest/ugliest ziḥāf, used **nowhere else** in the poem, and the apparatus **mislabels it "Iḍ"** (`– – u –`), which it is not. Not illegal, but it breaks the poem's own "S/Iḍ only" texture and the printed tag is false.
- **Optional fix (to remove the waqṣ):** recast foot 2 so the head is `– –` or `u u`, e.g. **«… وَما بي مِنْ دَعاوى مَظْنونْ»** *(scan before adopting)* or simpler **«لي نَظْمُهُ، ما خانَني مَظْنونْ»** — *lī naẓmuhū, mā khānanī maẓnūn* = `– – u – / – – u – / – – –` (Iḍ·Iḍ·Iḍₘ), removing the waqṣ. (Keeps the disclaimed-presumption sense; re-voice for the al-Mutanabbī echo.) **If kept as-is, at minimum correct the apparatus tag from "Iḍ" to "waqṣ".**

### ⚠ ROUGHNESS 4 — Bayt 21 ṣadr (waqṣ at the ʿARŪḌ → ʿarūḍ-inconsistency)
- **Problem:** «… مِنْهُ، إِنْ قَدَرْ». The ʿarūḍ foot = `hu in qa dar` = `u – u –` = **waqṣ**, whereas the ʿarūḍ is ṣaḥīḥa (`u u – u –`) on all 29 other lines. A once-off waqṣ ʿarūḍ is an inconsistency many ʿarūḍiyyūn would flag; the apparatus tag "Iḍ" is also wrong.
- **Optional fix:** reshape the ʿarūḍ to ṣaḥīḥ, e.g. end the ṣadr on a `…u u – u –` cadence (e.g. «… إِنْ قَدَروا» *in qadarū* → `… qa da rū`? — note this opens the rawiyy; instead recast the words before so the ʿarūḍ head is `uu`/`–`). **Lower priority** (it is a borderline, defensible-as-ziḥāf reading), but it is **not** the clean ṣaḥīḥa the poem advertises.

### Note 5 — internal text inconsistency (not a meter defect)

# 6. OVERALL VERDICT

**The poem is NOT YET metrically certifiable as a uniform al-Kāmil maqṭūʿ nūniyya — but it is two small edits away from being so.**

1. **The load-bearing question resolves in the poem's FAVOUR.** "– – –" *is* a legal al-Kāmil al-tāmm maqṭūʿ ḍarب — the foot مُتْفاعِلْ = مُسْتَفْعِلْ (maqṭūʿ + iḍmār), attested with a worked classical example in al-Tabrīzī's *Talkhīṣāt al-Shāfiya* (the «…دُونَ تَأويلاتِ» line, ʿajuz = مستفعلن مستفعلن **مستفعلْ**). The poem's framing (Sₘ = u u – – for short pre-tail, Iḍₘ = – – – for heavy pre-tail) is **prosodically correct**, and its ḍarب does **not** drift in identity: all 30 closes are genuine maqṭūʿ (28× – – –, 2× u u – –), every rhyme is a true long-penult Family-B word, and the rhyme system (ridf, rawiyy, majrā, no īṭāʾ) is **clean**. The "unforgivable failure" — short-penult contamination forcing a ṣaḥīḥ close, or a non-maqṭūʿ close — **does not occur.** On the ḍarب and the rhyme, the poem is vindicated.

2. **BUT two ʿajuz do not scan at all** (genuine, hard meter breaks, independent of the ḍارب question):
 - **B9** — العَدَدَ stacks four light syllables → no legal foot 2.
 - **B24** — هُمُ over-fills foot 1 → no legal foot 1. (This is the النّون "thunderclap"; the *rhyme* is fine, the *line* is broken.)
 Both are fixable with the minimal, sense- and rhyme-preserving edits in §5 (B24's fix is a trivial `lākin humu`→`lākinnahum`).

3. **Three further roughnesses** (B21-ṣadr ʿarūḍ, B29-ʿajuz, B30-ṣadr) use the rare/ugly **waqṣ** (mufāʿilun, u – u –) — legal but ثقيل, used nowhere else, and **silently mislabeled "iḍmār"** by the poem's own parser. B21's waqṣ falls on the ʿarūḍ, breaking the otherwise-constant ṣaḥīḥa ʿarūḍ (an inconsistency). These should be smoothed or at minimum honestly re-tagged.

4. **The poem's "machine-verified, all 60 legal" self-audit is OVERSTATED.** The parser (a) passed two unscannable ʿajuz (B9, B24), and (b) mislabeled at least four feet (3 waqṣ as iḍmār + 1 sound-foot as iḍmār). The honest count is **56/60 hemistichs clean; 2 hemistichs illegal; the ʿajuz-defect rate is 2/30 hard + 1/30 rough.**

**Bottom line:** the ambitious claim — a uniform al-Kāmil al-tāmm maqṭūʿ nūniyya closing on "– – –" — is **achievable and very nearly achieved**, and the "– – –" ḍarب is genuinely legal (the most important finding, and it is *positive*). The certification fails **today** only because of **two specific broken lines (B9, B24)** plus **three waqṣ roughnesses**, all individually repairable without disturbing the architecture or the rhyme. Fix B9 and B24 (hard), smooth/relabel B21/B29/B30 (soft), and the poem becomes a clean, certifiable uniform al-Kāmil maqṭūʿ Family-B nūniyya.

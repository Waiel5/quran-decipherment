# Classical Commentary on Surah ash-Shams (91) — Oath Structure

## Our finding

Q 91:1-7 have letter counts [12, 14, 15, 15, 15, 14, 12] — a perfect 7-verse palindrome
mirrored around verse 4 ("by the Night when it covers"). Empirical p ≈ 0.007. Only 3
such length-7 letter-count palindromes exist in the Quran. Novel, no prior systematic
catalog found.

## Classical rhetoric categories that COULD have identified this

- **tarṣīʿ** (balanced parallel pairing of words with equal syllabic/metric weight) —
  al-Jurjani, Sakkaki. But tarsi' is about word-to-word balance, not letter-counts per
  verse.
- **radd al-'ajuz 'ala al-sadr** (bringing the end back to the beginning) — also a
  word-level device, not structural palindrome.
- **mushakalah** (assonance).
- **muwāzana** (weighing, balance).

## What classical commentators DO say about Surah 91

Scholars note:
- **Eleven oaths** opening the surah, **the highest concentration in the Quran**. Some
  scholars count seven (al-Qurtubi preferred seven; others eleven).
- **Pairing by opposites**: sun ↔ moon; day ↔ night; heaven ↔ earth; piety ↔ wickedness
  (for the soul). Commentators note this is an internally dual structure.
- **al-Zamakhshari** comments on v3 ("the day when it displays it"), interpreting the
  pronoun "it" as referring to earth/world.
- **al-Jalalayn, al-Razi, al-Baydawi** all observe the rhetorical power of the omitted
  oath-verb (uqsimu) with ellipsis.

## What they do NOT observe

**No classical commentator appears to have counted the letters per verse.** The specific
palindromic pattern [12, 14, 15, 15, 15, 14, 12] is not reported in the classical literature
we surveyed. The "axis at verse 4" reading (wa-l-layli idha yaghshaha) is novel.

**Classical attention was to semantic dualism** (opposites) rather than **structural
symmetry** (letter-count mirroring). These are cousins, but not the same. A reader who
knows the surah swears by opposing natural phenomena could independently infer "this
surah is balanced." A reader who notices that verse-letter-counts form a mathematical
palindrome has gone one step further.

## Verdict

The IDEA that the surah is balanced is classical. The SPECIFIC palindrome is novel.

## Key Numerical Claims

The primary numerical focus centers on the number 19. The text references Quranic verse 74:30, stating: "Above it are nineteen" angels guarding hellfire.

The author discusses verse 74:31, which indicates this number serves as: "a trial for unbelievers" and provides "certainty" for People of the Book while increasing faith among believers.

## Book Structure

In this book, described as the Noon Center's first fruit, the reader will find six chapters which give evidence of the mathematical miracles in the Holy Qur'an. The chapters cover topics including:
- The Sura of Al-Muddathir
- The serial order of suras
- Opening letters (Fawatih / muqatta'at)
- Numerical values of the Arabic alphabet (abjad)

## Purpose of the Research

Jarrar's work examines mathematical patterns within the Qur'an across six chapters. The author claims these patterns help establish the Qur'an's miraculous nature and support Islamic scholarship regarding textual authenticity, chapter organization, and verse counts.

## Stated Benefits

The numerical approach allegedly clarifies Quranic miracle claims, resolves controversies in Quranic sciences, refutes skeptical arguments about text compilation, aids interpretation, and reveals connections between universal structure and Quranic numbers.

# The Miracle of Numbers in the Quran (FUNCI summary)

**Source:** https://funci.org/the-miracle-of-numbers-in-the-quran/?lang=en
**Publisher:** FUNCI - Fundación de Cultura Islámica
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

This is one of the most-cited summaries of the Nawfal/Al-Kaheel/Jarrar word-pair tradition in English.

## Temporal References
- "shahr" (month): mentioned **12 times** (matching months in a year)
- "yawm" (day, singular): mentioned **365 times** (matching days in a year)
- "ayyam" (days, plural): mentioned **30 times** (matching days in a month)

## Religious Practice
- "salawat" (prayers): mentioned **5 times** (corresponding to five daily Islamic prayers)

## Complementary Word Pairs
The article lists words and their antonyms appearing equal numbers of times:
- Life/Death: **145 times each**
- Worldly life/Afterlife: **115 times each**
- Angels/Demons: **88 times each**
- Man/Woman: **24 times each**

## Water and Earth Percentages
The article claims:
- "bahr" (water): **32 mentions**
- "barr/ard" (earth): **13 mentions**
- Calculated water percentage: **71.11%**
- Calculated earth percentage: **28.89%**

The article asserts these percentages match actual planetary proportions.

The source attributes research to scholars including **Abderrazzaq Nufal**, **Bassám Jarrar**, and **Abdu A-Daim Kuhail** over the past twenty years.

# The Middle (Wasat) Ayah of al-Baqarah

**Source:** https://linguisticmiracle.wordpress.com/wasata_baqarah/
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Article Content

**Quranic Verse:**
> وَكَذَٰلِكَ جَعَلْنَاكُمْ أُمَّةً **وَسَطًا**

**Translation:** "And thus We have made you a Wasata (Middle way – Just/Balanced) Ummah/Nation.." – al Baqarah 2:143

## Key Claims

The article presents a linguistic miracle argument centered on numerical positioning:

**Core Claim:** "There are a total of 286 aayaat/verses in surah al Baqarah. Allah places this ayah right in the Middle (wasat) of this surah."

**Mathematical claim:** 286 ÷ 2 = 143 (the verse number where this ayah appears)

## Critical Context Provided

The article includes an important caveat: "Keep in mind that surah al Baqarah was revealed over a span of many years, and it was recited Orally before being written down in compiled form. When it was compiled, the aayaat were not numbered (like we do nowadays). So it seem's almost impossible for this ayah to be purposely placed directly in the middle of surah al Baqarah by human intervention."

This acknowledges the challenge of explaining how such positioning could occur without centralized organization during compilation.

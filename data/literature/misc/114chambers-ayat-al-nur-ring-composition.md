# Ayat Al-Nur: Revealed in Ring Composition, and Black Holes

**Source:** https://114chambers.wordpress.com/2020/07/29/ayat-al-nur-revealed-in-ring-composition/
**Author:** "Siham Karami" (114 Chambers blog)
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Overview

This article examines Quranic Verse 24:35 (Ayat Al-Nur) through the structural lens of ring composition, a literary device where text sections mirror each other around a central point.

## Ring Composition Structure

The analysis identifies **11 sections** arranged in mirror pattern (ABCBA format):

**Paired Sections:**

1. & 11: "Allah is" — establishes correspondence between light and knowledge
2. & 10: Examples and presentation structures
3. & 9: Glass as container and divine guidance
4. & 8: Spherical glass imagery and "light upon light"
5. & 7: Blessed olive tree and its oil
6. **CENTER**: "Neither of the east nor of the west" — represents balance

## Key Interpretive Claims

The author argues that "knowledge must be truth" and that this verse demonstrates how Quranic language functions as illumination. The olive tree represents "life" and "peace," while the glass symbolizes "transparent words" containing divine guidance.

## Intriguing Connection

The article mentions "light cones" from physics—geometric formations around black holes—noting structural similarities to the verse's imagery, though acknowledging this remains speculative territory requiring physics expertise.

## Thematic Foundation

The central claim throughout: **"The Quran is light in the form of words,"** uniting concepts of illumination, knowledge, and divine communication.

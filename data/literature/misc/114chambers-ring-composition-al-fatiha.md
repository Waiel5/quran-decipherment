# Ring Composition in the Quran: Al-Fatiha

**Source:** https://114chambers.wordpress.com/2020/12/20/ring-composition-in-the-quran/
**Author:** "Siham Karami" (114 Chambers blog)
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Overview

Ring composition is "a framing device" wherein text sections mirror each other around a central idea. The author analyzes Surat Al-Fatiha using this ancient literary pattern to reveal structural and theological significance.

## The Ring Composition Structure

The analysis demonstrates an ABCBA pattern:

**Yellow Section (Verses 1 & 7):**
- Verse 1 (Bismallah): "In the name of Allah, Al-Rahman, Al-Raheem"
- Verse 7: Describes three paths—the blessed path, the path of those facing Allah's wrath, and the path of the astray
- These correspond through matching themes about Allah's dual attributes: Almighty Power and All-Encompassing Mercy

**Blue-Grey Section (Verses 2 & 6):**
- Verse 2: "All praise is for Allah, Lord of all the worlds"
- Verse 6: "Guide us on the straight path"
- Praise creates receptivity for divine guidance; "Lord" implies relationship and caretaking

**Green Section (Verses 3 & 5):**
- Verse 3: Repeats the great names Al-Rahman, Al-Raheem
- Verse 5: "You alone do we worship; With You alone do we seek refuge"
- Worship addresses Almighty Power; refuge-seeking addresses Mercy

**Orange Center (Verse 4):**
- "Owner/Sovereign of Judgment Day" stands alone as the turning point
- Represents the ultimate power and the central message about accountability

## Theological Significance

The author argues the Bismallah must be numbered as the first verse, resolving scholarly dispute. The paired names—Al-Rahman and Al-Raheem—represent complementary cosmic principles:

- **Al-Rahman** expresses the "yang principle": omnipotence, authority, majesty
- **Al-Raheem** expresses the "yin principle": mercy, receptivity, compassion

These are perfectly balanced only in Allah, unlike in creation where imbalance occurs.

## Three Realms

The structure reveals three cosmic levels:

1. **Verses 1-3**: The eternal, timeless celestial realm directly addressing Allah
2. **Verse 4**: Judgment Day as the transitional boundary between realms
3. **Verses 5-7**: The earthly world of time where humans exercise free will and receive divine response

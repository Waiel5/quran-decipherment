# Quranic Arabic Corpus — Part-of-Speech Tagset

**Source:** https://corpus.quran.com/documentation/tagset.jsp
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

The Quranic Arabic Corpus organizes words into three fundamental grammatical categories: nominals, verbs, and particles.

## Nominals

Nominals encompass nouns, pronouns, adjectives, and adverbs:

- **N** (Noun): Standard nouns
- **PN** (Proper noun): Names that are inherently definite without the determiner prefix
- **ADJ** (Adjective): Words functioning as modifiers that follow nouns
- **IMPN** (Imperative verbal noun): Derived nominal forms
- **PRON** (Personal pronoun): Independent pronouns and attached possessive/object suffixes
- **DEM** (Demonstrative pronoun): Words like "this" and "that"
- **REL** (Relative pronoun): Words connecting relative clauses to main clauses
- **T** (Time adverb): Temporal expressions
- **LOC** (Location adverb): Spatial expressions

## Verbs

- **V** (Verb): All verbs, annotated with morphological features indicating aspect and mood

## Particles

Particles include prepositions, conjunctions, and other function words:

**Prepositions & Lām Prefixes:**
- **P** (Preposition)
- **EMPH** (Emphatic lām)
- **IMPV** (Imperative lām)
- **PRP** (Purpose lām)

**Conjunctions:**
- **CONJ** (Coordinating conjunction)
- **SUB** (Subordinating conjunction)

**Other Particles:** ACC, AMD, ANS, AVR, CAUS, CERT, CIRC, COM, COND, EQ, EXH, EXL, EXP, FUT, INC, INT, INTG, NEG, PREV, PRO, REM, RES, RET, RSLT, SUP, SUR, VOC

**Disconnected Letters:**
- **INL** (Quranic initials): Mysterious letter sequences at chapter beginnings

## Note on raw morphology data

A verbatim copy of `quranic-corpus-morphology-0.4.txt` is included alongside this file in `corpus-docs/`. It is the canonical word-by-word morphological annotation of the entire Quran (114 surahs, 6,236 verses, ~77,430 words across ~128,276 morpheme rows) and was produced by Kais Dukes' team at Leeds.

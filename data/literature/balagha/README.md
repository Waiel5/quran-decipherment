# Classical balagha archive

Primary sources for the classical Arabic rhetorical tradition (`'ilm al-balāgha`),
used to cross-check every novel computational finding in `/findings/` against its
classical ancestor under the categories of `ma'ānī / bayān / badī'`.

## Canonical reference chain (who defined what, when)

| Period | Scholar | Work | What they added |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9th c. | al-Jāḥiẓ (d. 868) | *al-Bayān wa-l-Tabyīn* | First systematic treatment of Arabic eloquence; earliest discussions of *iṭnāb / ījāz / musāwāt*, parallel structure. |
| 10th c. | Ibn al-Muʿtazz (d. 908) | *Kitāb al-Badīʿ* | Defined five foundational *badīʿ* figures: *istiʿārah, tajnīs, muṭābaqah, radd al-ʿajuz ʿalā al-ṣadr, al-madhhab al-kalāmī*. **This is where radd al-ʿajuz ʿalā al-ṣadr enters the tradition.** |
| 10th c. | al-Rummānī (d. 994) | *al-Nukat fī Iʿjāz al-Qurʾān* | Enumerated ten *wujūh al-balāghah* of the Quran including *tajānus, tashbīh, istiʿārah, talāʾum, fawāṣil, taḍmīn, mubālaghah, ḥusn al-bayān, ījāz*. |
| 11th c. | al-Bāqillānī (d. 1013) | *Iʿjāz al-Qurʾān* | Argued Quranic inimitability is rhetorical, not mere numerology. |
| 11th c. | **ʿAbd al-Qāhir al-Jurjānī** (d. 1078) | *Dalāʾil al-Iʿjāz*, *Asrār al-Balāghah* | Theory of *naẓm* (word-order / syntactic arrangement) as locus of Quranic inimitability. Distinguishes the *ʿaql* (pure syntax) from the *taʿbīr* (expression). Foundational. |
| 12th c. | al-Zamakhsharī (d. 1144) | *al-Kashshāf* | First tafsīr systematically built on balāgha; canonizes *iltifāt* readings; his treatment cited verbatim by later authors. |
| 13th c. | **al-Sakkākī** (d. 1229) | *Miftāḥ al-ʿUlūm* | Formal codification of balāgha into the **tripartite structure** `ma'ānī / bayān / badīʿ` that is still used today. |
| 14th c. | al-Qazwīnī (d. 1338) | *Talkhīṣ al-Miftāḥ*, *al-Īḍāḥ* | The standard pedagogical synthesis of al-Sakkākī; every later madrasa used this. |
| 14th c. | **al-Zarkashī** (d. 1392) | *al-Burhān fī ʿUlūm al-Qurʾān* | 47 *nawʿ* (chapters) of Quranic sciences; Chapter 45 treats *munāsabāt* (coherence relations); extensive discussions of *iltifāt*, *jinās*, *saj'*, *taqsīm*, *muqābala*. |
| 15th c. | **al-Suyūṭī** (d. 1505) | *al-Itqān fī ʿUlūm al-Qurʾān* | 80 *nawʿ*. Naw' 58-59 explicitly titled on *badīʿ al-Qurʾān*; enumerates dozens of rhetorical figures. Absorbs al-Zarkashī wholesale and adds. This is the single most systematic classical catalog of Quranic rhetoric. |

## Three sciences of balāgha (al-Sakkākī / al-Qazwīnī canonical frame)

### ʿIlm al-maʿānī (sentence-level pragmatics)
- *khabar* vs *inshāʾ* (declarative vs performative)
- *ījāz / iṭnāb / musāwāt* (brevity / expansion / balance)
- *qaṣr* (restriction/exclusion)
- *faṣl wa-waṣl* (separation and conjunction)
- *taqdīm wa-taʾkhīr* (fronting and deferring)
- *ḥadhf* (elision)
- *iltifāt* (grammatical shift) — classed under ma'ānī by al-Sakkākī, under badīʿ by earlier writers

### ʿIlm al-bayān (figurative expression)
- *tashbīh* (simile)
- *istiʿārah* (metaphor; *taṣrīḥiyyah/makniyyah* = explicit/implicit)
- *majāz* (figurative trope; *mursal/ʿaqlī*)
- *kināyah* (metonymy / indirect reference)

### ʿIlm al-badīʿ (embellishments — verbal & semantic)

**Verbal (لفظي / lafẓī):**
- *jinās* (paronomasia; subtypes: *tāmm, nāqiṣ, muḍāriʿ, lāḥiq, mudhayyal, muṣaḥḥaf, ishtiqāq, mulaffaq*)
- *saj'* (rhymed prose; subtypes: *muṭarraf, mutawāzī, muraṣṣa'*)
- *tarṣīʿ* (studding — parallel rhymed couplet where every word of A matches every word of B in pattern and rhyme)
- *muwāzanah* (balanced parallelism without rhyme constraint)
- *radd al-ʿajuz ʿalā al-ṣadr* (returning-the-end-to-the-beginning — a verse / sentence / couplet whose last element repeats its first element; this IS the classical term for single-verse inclusio)
- *luzūm mā lā yalzam* (self-imposed constraint; al-Maʿarrī's double-rhyme; classically applied to Quran 112 al-Ikhlāṣ for its forced dāl rhyme under the iḥkām constraint)
- *tashrīʿ* (doubled structure readable two ways)

**Semantic (معنوي / maʿnawī):**
- *ṭibāq / muṭābaqah / taḍādd* (simple antithesis: two opposites juxtaposed)
- *muqābalah* (multi-term antithesis: two-or-more vs two-or-more, with ordered mirroring)
- *tawriyah* (double-meaning pun: surface sense hides true sense)
- *istidrāk* (corrective insertion)
- *mubālaghah* (hyperbole; subtypes: *tablīgh, ighrāq, ghuluww*)
- *taʾkīd al-madh bimā yushbih al-dhamm* / *taʾkīd al-dhamm bimā yushbih al-madh* (praise by feigned blame, blame by feigned praise)
- *taqsīm* (enumeration / division — exhaustive listing of a theme's constituents; al-Qazwīnī's type-case is Surah 55 al-Raḥmān)
- *murāʿāt al-naẓīr* (harmonious pairing of congruent images)
- *ḥusn al-taʿlīl* (elegant causation)
- *īhām* (ambiguity play)
- *idmāj* (embedding one meaning inside another)
- *iḥtirās* (pre-empting an objection)
- *ḥusn al-ibtidāʾ* / *ḥusn al-khātimah* (excellence of opening / closing)

## Key Quranic test-case verses and their classical categories

| Verse | Classical category | Attribution |
|---|---|---|
| Q 1:4 (iyyāka na'budu...) | *iltifāt* — 3rd→2nd person shift | al-Zamakhsharī *Kashshāf*; al-Zarkashī *Burhān* §iltifāt |
| Q 2:19 (fī ādhānihim...) | *kināyah* / *istiʿārah makniyyah* | al-Jurjānī *Asrār* |
| Q 2:138 (ṣibghat allāh...) | *istiʿārah* | classical |
| Q 2:171 (mathal alladhīna kafarū...) | *tashbīh tamthīl* | al-Jurjānī |
| Q 3:27 (tūliju al-layla fī al-nahār...) | *muqābalah* + *ṭibāq* | al-Suyūṭī *Itqān* §badīʿ |
| Q 3:54 (makara wa makara allāh...) | *jinās mushākala* (same root re-used in opposed sense) | classical |
| Q 6:76-78 (Abraham's afala chain) | *iḥtijāj* / *istidlāl* rhetorical argument | al-Rāzī *Mafātīḥ al-Ghayb* |
| Q 9:82 (fal-yaḍḥakū qalīlan wal-yabkū kathīran) | *muqābalah* | al-Qazwīnī *Īḍāḥ* |
| Q 10:22 (hattā idhā kuntum...) | *iltifāt* — 2nd→3rd person shift | al-Zamakhsharī, Abdel Haleem 1992 |
| Q 13:28 (alā bi-dhikr allāh taṭmaʾinn al-qulūb) | *radd al-ʿajuz ʿalā al-ṣadr* — textbook single-verse case | Ibn al-Muʿtazz type case |
| Q 22:61 (yūliju al-layl fī al-nahār...) | *muqābalah* | al-Suyūṭī |
| Q 28:71-72 (sarmadan al-layla...) | *muqābalah* + hapax-pair ṭibāq | classical (implicit) |
| Q 30:19 (yukhriju al-ḥayya min al-mayyit...) | *muqābalah* — multi-term | al-Jurjānī |
| Q 30:55 (as-sāʿa... sāʿa) | *jinās tāmm* — same word, two senses | al-Suyūṭī §jinās (type case) |
| Q 34:24 (innā aw iyyākum la-ʿalā hudan aw fī ḍalāl mubīn) | *muqābalah* | classical |
| Q 55 (the 31-refrain structure) | *taqsīm* + *saj' mutawāzī* + *radd al-ṣadr li-l-ʿajuz* | al-Qazwīnī *Īḍāḥ* §taqsīm |
| Q 91:1-10 (seven oaths, parallel structure) | *saj' muraṣṣaʿ* + *muwāzanah* | classical |
| Q 95:4-5 (aḥsan taqwīm / asfal sāfilīn) | *ṭibāq* | al-Qazwīnī |
| Q 109 (al-Kāfirūn) | *radd al-ʿajuz ʿalā al-ṣadr* macro-structure | al-Zarkashī |
| Q 112 (al-Ikhlāṣ) | *luzūm mā lā yalzam* (forced dāl rhyme), *ījāz* | classical |

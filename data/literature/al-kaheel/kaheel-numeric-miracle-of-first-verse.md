# The Numeric Miracle of the First Verse in the Qur'an

**Author:** Abduldaem Al-Kaheel
**Source:** https://www.kaheel7.com/eng/index.php/numeric-miracle/384-the-numeric-miracle-of-the-first-verse-in-the-quran
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Main Topic
The article examines numerical patterns in the Basmala, the opening verse of the Qur'an: "In the Name of God, Most Gracious, Most Merciful."

## Key Claims
The author argues that "every single letter in this verse carries with it a divine miracle," suggesting the verse demonstrates divine authorship rather than human composition.

## Intended Audience
The piece addresses both believers and skeptics, asking: "Could God have deposited in His Book literal proof that this Book is His indeed?" It targets those who "only believe in literal reality, in science and all things tangible."

## Central Argument
According to the author, the Qur'an is structured around the number 7, with this opening verse serving as a demonstration of this mathematical pattern. Readers are directed to the downloadable book "The Numeric Miracle of the First Chapter" (Al-Fatiha) for the full analysis.

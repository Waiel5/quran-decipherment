# The Colors and Number Seven

**Author:** Abduldaem Al-Kaheel
**Source:** https://www.kaheel7.com/eng/index.php/numeric-miracle/655-the-colors-and-number-seven-
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Core Claim

The word "colors" appears exactly seven times in the Qur'an, matching the seven colors of the light spectrum in a rainbow.

## Seven Qur'anic Verses Cited

1. "Whatsoever He has created for you on this earth of varying colors" (An-Nahl 13)
2. Reference to honey with "a drink of varying color wherein is healing" (An-Nahl 69)
3. "Difference of your languages and colors" among Signs (Ar-Rûm 22)
4. "Fruits of varying colors" produced from water (Fâtir 27)
5. Mountains with "streaks white and red, of varying colors and...very black" (Fâtir 27)
6. "Men and moving creatures...of various colors" (Fâtir 28)
7. Crops "of different colors" from rain penetrating earth (Az-Zumar 21)

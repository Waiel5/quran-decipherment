# New Numeric Miracle

**Author:** Abduldaem Al-Kaheel
**Source:** https://www.kaheel7.com/eng/index.php/numeric-miracle/65-new-numeric-miracle
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

This article by Abduldaem Al-Kaheel presents numerical patterns claimed to exist within the Quran.

## Primary Numbers Cited
The text identifies three foundational figures:
- Verses: 6,236
- Chapters: 114
- Years of revelation: 23

## Mathematical Claims

The author argues these numbers exhibit divisibility by 7 in various combinations:

- "114: its characteristic is that the first chapter and last one constitute 114, a multiple of 7"
- The combined figures (1146236) allegedly divides by 7
- When digits are summed: 1+1+4+6+2+3+6 = 23
- The verse about Quranic protection (Surah Al-Hijr, verse 9) contains 28 letters, matching the count of Arabic alphabet letters

## Key Pattern
The article claims configurations like 23114, 236236, and 1146236 all share divisibility properties with 7, and that arranging these three numbers yields 567 (divisible by 7).

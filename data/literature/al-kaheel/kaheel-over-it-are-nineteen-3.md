## Key Claims About the Number 19

The article references Qur'an 74:30, which states: "Over it are Nineteen" - referring to guardians of Hell-Fire. The author presents numerical evidence:

- The Arabic letter "qaf" appears 57 times in Chapter 50 (Qaf) - a multiple of 19
- The same letter appears 57 times in Chapter 42 (Shura)
- Combined total: 114 occurrences, equaling the total number of Qur'anic chapters
- Letters "Ya" and "Sin" in Chapter 36 appear 285 times - also a multiple of 19

## Theological Interpretation

The article argues these patterns demonstrate divine authorship of the Qur'an. The text notes that the "nineteen" reference serves "as a trial for Unbelievers" and to strengthen faith among believers, according to the Qur'anic passage cited.

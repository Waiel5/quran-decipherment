## Main Claims

**Night (Sura 92):** The word "night and all its derivations are repeated exactly 92 times in the Holy Qur'an," matching the sura's position.

**Hell:** "The word hell is repeated 77 times in the Holy Qur'an" with seven gates. The color black appears "exactly seven times," corresponding to hell's gates.

**Paradise:** Eight gates exist, and "green as a color in the Holy Qur'an" appears "eight times, the exact number of the gates of paradise."

**Prostration (Sura 32):** "The number is exactly the same as that of the times when prostration is repeated in the holy Qur'an, 32 times." Additionally, verse 15 contains a prostration instruction, and "Muslims are asked to prostrate in the holy Qur'an is also 15."

The author argues these alignments demonstrate intentional divine design rather than coincidence.

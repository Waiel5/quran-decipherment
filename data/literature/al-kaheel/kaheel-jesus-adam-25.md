# Amazing Miracle: Jesus and Adam

**Author:** Abduldaem Al-Kaheel
**Source:** https://www.kaheel7.com/eng/index.php/numeric-miracle/137-amazing-miracle-jesus-and-adam
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Main Argument

The article presents a numerical analysis claiming to demonstrate a miraculous pattern in the Qur'an regarding mentions of Jesus and Adam. The central thesis states: "Verily, the likeness of Jesus before Allah is the likeness of Adam. He created him from dust, then He said to him: 'Be!' - and he was."

## Key Numerical Claims

The author asserts two primary findings:

1. **Equal Mentions**: Both Jesus and Adam are mentioned exactly 25 times throughout the Qur'an.

2. **Synchronized Occurrence**: At Qur'an 3:59—the sole verse where both names appear together—each name had been mentioned precisely 7 times previously in the text.

## Supporting Evidence Structure

The article provides comprehensive tables listing all 25 references for each figure in order of appearance. The tables document chapter and verse numbers for each occurrence, establishing a verifiable reference framework for the claimed pattern.

## Theological Context

The article frames this analysis within Islamic theology, emphasizing that both prophets share the distinction of being created without fathers, arguing this parallel refutes claims of Jesus's divinity based on his miraculous birth.

## Methodological Note

The author acknowledges potential skepticism, framing the numerical consistency as either remarkable coincidence or evidence of divine preservation.

# The Number 309

**Author:** Abduldaem Al-Kaheel
**Source:** https://www.kaheel7.com/eng/index.php/numeric-miracle/902-the-number-309
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Main Claims

The article claims a numerical miracle in the Qur'an related to the story of the People of the Cave (Ashabu al-Kahaf).

The article states that "The people of the cave...stayed in the cave for 309 years." It then asserts a word-count correspondence: counting from the first mention of "stayed" (Cave 12) to the final mention of "stayed" (Cave 26) yields exactly 309 words—matching the duration of their stay.

Additionally, the text claims: "the number of the word (one hundred) in the phrase three hundred is exactly three hundred," suggesting internal numerical consistency within the Qur'anic text.

## Conclusion

The author interprets these numerical alignments as evidence of divine origin.

# Amazing Numeral Facts about Al Nas (The Mankind) Chapter

**Author:** Abduldaem Al-Kaheel
**Source:** https://www.kaheel7.com/eng/index.php/numeric-miracle/330-amazing-numeral-facts-about-al-nas-the-mankind-chapter
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

The article presents four numerical claims about the final chapter of the Quran:

**First Claim:** The letters comprising the word "Al-Nas" (A, L, N, S) appear 49 times total in the chapter—specifically A appears 18 times, L appears 12 times, N appears 9 times, and S appears 10 times (18+12+9+10=49=7x7).

**Second Claim:** The letters of the word "Allah" (A, L, H) appear 49 times in Surah Al-Fatihah (the first chapter)—with A repeated 22 times, L repeated 22 times, and H repeated 5 times (22+22+5=49=7x7).

**Third Claim:** "Alfatihah...has 29 words, while (Alnas) the last chapter in the Quran has 20 words; the surprise is in the words total: 29+20=49=7x7."

**Fourth Claim:** The numerical symmetry between the opening chapter (emphasizing Allah) and closing chapter (addressing mankind) demonstrates intentional design.

# Number 11: A Witness to the Oneness of God

**Author:** Abduldaem Al-Kaheel
**Source:** https://www.kaheel7.com/eng/index.php/numeric-miracle/116-number-11-a-witness-to-the-loneliness-of-god
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Main Claims

The article argues that the number 11 demonstrates Islamic theological concepts through numerical patterns in the Quran.

**Key assertion:** "Number is a single, prime number, which cannot be divided only on itself or number 1. Its existence in the holy Book of Allah is only a strong proof of His Oneness."

## Numerical Claims Presented

**Basmalah repetitions:**
The article states word frequencies: bism (22 times), Allah (2,699 times), al-Rahman (57 times), al-Raheem (115 times). When arranged as 11557269922, the author claims this is divisible by 11.

**Allah's letters:**
The word appears 2,699 times with 4 letters. Combined as 26994, the text asserts: "26994 = 11 x 2454"

**First and last mentions:**
Allah first appears in verse 1:1 and last in 2:112. The article states these references create numbers divisible by 11.

**Letter frequencies in Basmalah:**
Alif appears 3 times, Lam 4 times, Ha 1 time. The number 143 is claimed as "11 x 13"

**The word Allah itself:**
Contains Alif (1), Lam (2), Ha (1), forming 121 = "11 x 11"

Note: This article uses Allah=2699 (with 9:128-129 included), contradicting the Khalifa Allah=2698 claim.

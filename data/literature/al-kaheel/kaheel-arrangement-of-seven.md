# The Beautiful Arrangement of "Seven" in the Qur'an

**Author:** Abduldaem Al-Kaheel
**Source:** https://www.kaheel7.com/eng/index.php/numeric-miracle/149-the-beautiful-arrangement-of-seven-in-the-quran
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Main Arguments

The article presents a numerical analysis claiming the number 7 exhibits a precise, divinely-ordered pattern throughout the Qur'an.

### Key Claims

**First and Last Mentions:** The number 7 appears first in Chapter 2 (Al-Baqarah: 29) and last in Chapter 78 (Al-Naba': 12).

**Chapter Count:** Between these two chapters, there are exactly 77 chapters—"a multiple of 7!"

**Verse Count:** Between the specific verses containing these mentions, the author counted 5,649 verses, which equals 807 × 7.

**Symmetry Pattern:** 
- 28 verses precede the first occurrence (4 × 7)
- 28 verses follow the last occurrence (since Al-Naba' contains 40 verses total)

## Author's Perspective

The author argues this mathematical precision demonstrates divine authorship because any human attempting such consistency while maintaining "meaning, context, utter flawlessness of word choice, and devastating literary genius" would face insurmountable difficulty.

## Critical Context

The article acknowledges skepticism, particularly regarding previous numerological claims by Dr. Rashad Khalifa that lacked consistency. However, the author maintains this specific pattern represents genuine miracle rather than coincidence, noting the Qur'an exists unchanged in original Arabic text.

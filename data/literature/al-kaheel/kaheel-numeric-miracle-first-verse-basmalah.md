# Numeric Miracle in the First Verse (Basmalah)

**Author:** Abduldaem Al-Kaheel
**Source:** https://www.kaheel7.com/eng/index.php/numeric-miracle/151-111
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Main Claims

The article presents numerical patterns found in the Basmalah (Islamic invocation): "In the name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful."

### Allah's Name Distribution
The author counts letters (Alif, Lam, Ha) across four words, yielding: 0-4-2-2. When read as "2240," this "is a multiple of 7 so that: 2240 = 7 x 320."

### Most Gracious (Arrahmane) Pattern
Counting specific letters yields 5631 (left-to-right) or 1365 (right-to-left). The claim states "1365 is a multiple of 7: 1365 = 7 x 195."

### Most Merciful (Arrahime) Pattern
The distribution number 6531 is presented as "a multiple of 7! 6531 = 7 x 933."

## Basmalah Distribution Across Chapters

The article claims that when each of the Quran's 114 chapters is assigned a number representing how many times the Basmalah appears (0 or 1, except Chapter 27 with 2), "the big number...is a multiple of 114 in two ways however we read it, from right to left or from left to right."

The author further states this 114-digit number "contains 19 segments, each segment is a multiple of 7," connecting this to the Basmalah having 19 letters.

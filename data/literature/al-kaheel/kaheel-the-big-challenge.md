# The Big Challenge

**Author:** Abduldaem Al-Kaheel
**Source:** https://www.kaheel7.com/eng/index.php/numeric-miracle/878-the-big-challenge
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

The article presents numerical claims about Qur'an 17:88, a verse wherein Allah challenges mankind and jinns to produce something comparable to the Qur'an.

## Key Numerical Claims

The article asserts:
- The verse contains **19 words**
- The verse contains **76 letters** (described as "19 x 4 = 76")
- The Arabic alphabet used comprises exactly **19 distinct letters**
- These three numbers sum to **114** (19 + 19 + 76 = 114)
- The Qur'an contains exactly **114 chapters** (surahs)

## Central Argument

The author contends that "such numbers could never be a coincidence, but are rather the ordering of Allah, the exalted in power, the omniscient."

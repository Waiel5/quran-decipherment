# The Number 19

**Author:** Abduldaem Al-Kaheel
**Source:** https://www.kaheel7.com/eng/index.php/numeric-miracle/928-the-number-19
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Main Article Content

**Core Assertion:** The number of Qur'anic chapters (surahs) totals 114, which is a multiple of 19.

**Featured Verse:** The article highlights Surah Al-Isra (17:88), described as containing "nineteen" words in Arabic. This verse presents a challenge: *"Say: 'If the whole of mankind and Jinns were to gather together to produce the like of this Qur'an, they could not produce the like thereof..."*

**Numerical Breakdown Claims:**
- The verse contains 19 words (in Arabic)
- The verse contains 76 letters (described as a multiple of 19)
- The verse uses 19 distinct Arabic letters
- The sum (19 + 19 + 76) equals 114, matching the chapter count

**Supporting Reference:** The article cites Surah Al-Mudather (74:30), which contains the phrase *"Over it are Nineteen."*

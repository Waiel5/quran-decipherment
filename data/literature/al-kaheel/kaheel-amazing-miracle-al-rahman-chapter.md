# Amazing Miracle in "Al-Rahman" Chapter

**Author:** Abduldaem Al-Kaheel
**Source:** https://www.kaheel7.com/eng/index.php/numeric-miracle/178-amazing-miracle-in-qalrahmanq-chapter
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Main Claim

The article examines why the verse "Then which of the Blessings of your Lord will you both (jinns and men) deny?" repeats exactly 31 times in Chapter 55 (Al-Rahman).

## Key Numerical Arguments

**Verse Positions:** The refrain appears at verses 13, 16, 18, 21, 23, 25, 28, 30, 32, 34, 36, 38, 40, 42, 45, 47, 49, 51, 53, 55, 57, 59, 61, 63, 65, 67, 69, 71, 73, 75, and 77.

**First Mathematical Claim:** When concatenating these verse numbers into one sequence, the author states: "This large number, which represents the Verse numbers...is, amazingly enough, a multiple of seven: a whole number with no decimals!"

**Reverse Reading:** Reading the same 62-digit number backwards also allegedly produces a multiple of 7.

**Sequential Numbering:** Numbering the 31 repetitions sequentially (1-31) and concatenating them is claimed to be divisible by 7 in both directions.

**Symbolic Connection:** Verse 31 itself uses the Arabic term "Ath-thaqalan" (jinns and men collectively), creating a numeric alignment between repetition count and verse position.

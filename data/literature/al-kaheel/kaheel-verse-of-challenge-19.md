# The Verse of Challenge and Number 19

**Author:** Abduldaem Al-Kaheel
**Source:** https://www.kaheel7.com/eng/index.php/numeric-miracle/525-the-verse-of-challenge-and-the-number-19
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

The article presents numerical analysis of Qur'anic verse Al-Isra 17:88, which conveys Allah's challenge to humans and jinn to produce text comparable to the Qur'an.

## Key Claims About Verse 17:88

The article asserts: "The number of words of this verse is 19 words. The number of its letters is 76 letters, which is also a multiple of 19, i.e., 19 × 4."

It further states the verse contains exactly 19 distinct letters from the Arabic alphabet. The sum of these three elements (19 + 76 + 19 = 114) allegedly equals the total number of chapters in the Qur'an.

## The Letter Qaf Connection

The article claims the letter Qaf appears 57 times (a multiple of 19) in both Sura 50 (Qaf) and Sura 42 (Ash-Shura). Combined, this totals 114 occurrences, matching the Qur'an's chapter count.

The analysis suggests these numerical patterns demonstrate divine precision in the Qur'an's composition.

# The Miracle of Adam and Jesus (Article #946)

**Author:** Abduldaem Al-Kaheel
**Source:** https://www.kaheel7.com/eng/index.php/numeric-miracle/946-the-miracle-of-adam-and-jesus
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

The article discusses a numerical pattern in the Qur'an. According to the piece, the name Jesus appears 25 times throughout the holy text, and Adam's name also appears exactly 25 times.

The author highlights a specific correlation: "The seventh Qur'anic verse in which the name of Jesus, PBUH, is mentioned is exactly the same verse in which the name of Adam, PBUH, is mentioned."

Additionally, the 19th mention of Adam's name occurs in Chapter 19 (Mariam/Mary), and Jesus's name is also mentioned for the 19th time in this same chapter.

The article references Qur'an 3:59, which states: "The similitude of Jesus before Allah is as that of Adam; He created him from dust, then said to him: 'Be'. And he was."

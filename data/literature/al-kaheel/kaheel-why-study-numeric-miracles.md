# Why Study Numeric Miracles in the Quran

**Author:** Abduldaem Al-Kaheel
**Source:** https://www.kaheel7.com/eng/index.php/numeric-miracle/64-what-is-the-reason-of-studying-numeric-miracles
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Author's Journey and Motivation

Abduldaem Al-Kaheel explains his reasons for studying numeric miracles after initially dismissing the field. He began skeptical following discredited claims by Rashad Khalifa, who falsely predicted doomsday using number 19.

## The Turning Point

The author's perspective shifted during a debate with an atheist who dismissed eloquence-based arguments about the Quran's divine origin. Faced with this resistance, Al-Kaheel reconsidered numeric patterns as a compelling proof mechanism, eventually presenting examples involving the letter "qaf" appearing 57 times (a multiple of 19) in two chapters.

## Core Purpose

The primary reason for studying numeric miracles is to provide undeniable mathematical proof of the Quran's divine authorship to skeptics and materialists. As the author states, "the numeric language is the strongest and the perfect way to deal with the matter because it is the clearest language none can deny."

## Methodology

Al-Kaheel's approach involves:
- Counting letters in Quranic verses
- Tracking occurrences of letters composing Allah's name (alif, lam, ha)
- Analyzing whether resulting numbers are multiples of seven
- Examining word and letter positions relative to divine references

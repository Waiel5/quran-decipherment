# Number 7 in the Holy Quran

**Author:** Abduldaem Al-Kaheel
**Source:** https://www.kaheel7.com/eng/index.php/numeric-miracle/61-number-7-in-the-holy-quran
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Main Claims

The article presents numerical patterns allegedly demonstrating divine design in the Quran through the number 7.

### Key Arguments

**Frequency and Positioning:**
- The phrase "seven heavens" appears exactly seven times throughout the Quran
- The number 7 is the first number mentioned in the Quran
- After 1, the number 7 appears most frequently among all numbers

**Structural Patterns:**
- Al-Fatihah (opening chapter) contains 7 verses and 21 letters (7×3)
- Letters spelling "Allah" in this chapter total 49 (7×7)
- "Hell" appears 77 times (7×11)

**First and Last Occurrences:**
The article states that "seven" first appears in verse 2:29 and last in 78:12, with:
- 77 chapters between them (multiple of 7)
- 5,649 verses between these verses (multiple of 7)
- 5,705 total verses from 2:1 to 78:40 (multiple of 7)

**The Word "Allah":**
- Appears first in 1:1 and last in 112:2
- 112 Suras total between them (multiple of 7)
- 6,223 verses between these verses (multiple of 7)

### Additional Observations

The article claims 14 alphabets serve as initials (7×2), 28 total Quranic letters (7×4), and that the Prophet lived 63 years (7×9).

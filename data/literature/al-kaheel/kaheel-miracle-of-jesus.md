# The Miracle of "Jesus"

**Author:** Abduldaem Al-Kaheel
**Source:** https://www.kaheel7.com/eng/index.php/numeric-miracle/115-the-miracle-of-qjesusq
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Main Numerical Claims

The article presents several purported numeric patterns in the Qur'an related to Jesus:

**Word Repetition Counts:**
- "Jesus" (Issah): 25 occurrences
- "Son of" (Ibn): 35 occurrences
- "Mary" (Merriam): 34 occurrences

When arranged as 253534, the author claims this "is a multiple of seven: 25 35 34 = 7 × 49075"

**Letter Counts:**
The article states "Jesus' contains 4 letters, 'the son of' contains 3 and 'Merriam' contains 4," forming 434, which allegedly equals "7 × 62"

**Additional Claims:**
- "The Messiah" repetitions (11) combined with son of (35) and Mary (34) create 113534, "a multiple of seven"
- A Qur'anic verse description contains letter patterns yielding 444346 "= 7 × 63478"
- The digits of 444346 sum to 25, matching "Jesus" repetitions
- Various combinations (Messiah + Mary, Jesus + Mary) allegedly all divide evenly by seven

## Core Argument

The author contends these mathematical patterns prove the Qur'an's divine origin, asserting that "the One who has revealed the Koran is the Lord of the seven heavens."

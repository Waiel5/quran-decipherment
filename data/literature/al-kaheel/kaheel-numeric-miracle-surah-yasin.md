## Core Numerical Claims

The article presents several mathematical relationships involving the opening verse of Surah Yasin: "Ya Sin. By the Qur'an full of Wisdom."

**Letter Count Structure:**
The author counts Arabic letters in each word: Yasin (2 letters), "and" (1 letter), "The Qur'an" (6 letters), "full of wisdom" (6 letters), creating the sequence 2-1-6-6, which reads as 6612 when arranged horizontally.

**Primary Mathematical Relationship:**
"The number 6612 is equal to 114 multiplied by 58!!" According to the text, 114 represents the total number of Quranic chapters, while 58 represents how many times "The Koran" appears throughout the scripture.

**Reversed Number Significance:**
When the number is reversed to 2166, the article claims this also divides evenly by 114: "2166=19*114." The author associates 19 with Surah Yasin's position among chapters beginning with disconnected letters.

## Methodology Notes

The author emphasizes counting letters "as they are written not as they are spelled" and treats the connector "و" (and) as a separate word, acknowledging these represent "the accurate procedure whenever we do a research on numeric miracle."

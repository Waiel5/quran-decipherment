# The So-Called Mathematical Miracle of the Quran (Abdullah Sameer)

**Source:** https://friendlyexmuslim.com/the-so-called-mathematical-miracle-of-the-quran/
**Author:** Abdullah Sameer (ex-Muslim critic)
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

The article by Abdullah Sameer critiques the alleged mathematical miracles in the Quran, arguing that claimed word-count patterns result from "cherrypicking" rather than divine design.

## Key Numerical Refutations

**Seven Heavens Claim:**
The article states that "seven heavens" appears approximately 6 times using the specific phrase, not 7 as claimed. To reach seven, one must include verses where words appear in reverse order.

**Paradise/Hell Equivalence:**
- Hell (Jahanam): 77 times
- Paradise (Janat): requires adding plural (69 times) + dual forms (8 times) = 77

**Man and Woman:**
- Man (singular rajul): 29 times total (not just singular forms)
- Woman (singular imraat): 26 times

**Day/Month Claims:**
The article shows that day (yawm) appears "475 times" including all forms, not 365.

## Core Methodology Critique

The author identifies what he calls a "sharpshooter fallacy"—selecting data points after seeing results to support a predetermined conclusion. The criticism emphasizes that longer texts naturally contain numerical coincidences when searched exhaustively.

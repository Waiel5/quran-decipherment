# The 365 Days Hoax

**Source:** https://www.answering-islam.org/Religions/Numerics/365hoax.html
**Author:** AbdulraHman Lomax (originally a Submitter who became critical of Yuksel's methodology)
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

AbdulraHman Lomax's article systematically debunks the claim that the word "yawm" (day) appears exactly 365 times in the Qur'an—a figure promoted as a "miracle" by followers of Rashad Khalifa.

## The Core Finding

Through meticulous analysis using the Kassis and Flugel concordances, Lomax determined that "yawm" actually appears **475 times total** across all grammatical forms, not 365.

## How the 365 Figure Was Achieved

The author explains that proponents manipulated the count by:

- Excluding all dual forms (3 occurrences) and plural forms (27 occurrences)
- Discarding all forms with personal pronoun suffixes (10 mentions)
- Removing forms with the suffix meaning "that" (70 mentions)
- Making selective exceptions for the accusative case

This selective methodology eliminated 80 clearly singular forms to reach exactly 365.

## Methodological Issues

Lomax notes that Edip Yuksel's original list, which claimed to show singular forms only, contained "about six errors" beyond formatting problems. The author's database cross-referenced multiple concordances to provide comprehensive documentation of all 475 occurrences with verse numbers and grammatical classifications.

## Conclusion

The 365-day claim relies on arbitrary exclusion criteria rather than genuine numerical phenomena, undermining claims of miraculous properties in the Qur'an's language structure.

## Basic Information

Al-Hadid (الحديد), meaning "The Iron," is the 57th chapter of the Quran containing 29 verses. The chapter derives its name from a word appearing in verse 25.

## Classification

This is a Medinan surah, revealed in Medina rather than Mecca. It belongs to the Al-Musabbihat group—chapters beginning with glorification of Allah. The chapter occupies Juz' 27 and contains 575 words across 2,475 letters.

## Content Summary

The chapter addresses several themes:
- Divine omniscience and power (verses 1-6)
- Encouragement to give alms and support the Prophet's mission (7-11)
- Contrasts between the wise and foolish on Judgment Day (12-14)
- Emphasis on humble submission to God (15-17)
- Warnings about worldly vanity and calls toward spiritual life (19-21)
- References to previous prophets including Noah, Abraham, and Jesus (25-27)

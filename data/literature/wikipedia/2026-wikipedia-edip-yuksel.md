## Biography

**Edip Yüksel** (born December 20, 1957) is an American-Kurdish activist and scholar from Güroymak, Bitlis, Turkey. He comes from a Kurdish family and is the brother of Metin Yüksel.

### Early Life and Religious Evolution

Yüksel initially worked as a Turkish Islamist and popular Islamic commentator until the mid-1980s. He then underwent a significant religious transformation, rejecting traditional Islamic beliefs and adopting Quranism—a movement emphasizing the Quran as the sole source of divine guidance while rejecting hadith traditions.

This controversial shift generated substantial opposition from mainstream Islamic authorities in Turkey. "In 1989 Yüksel was forced to emigrate" and relocated to the United States, where he began practicing law.

### Work in America

In the US, Yüksel collaborated with **Rashad Khalifa**, who had identified what he termed "Code 19"—a mathematical pattern allegedly embedded within the Quran. However, Yüksel's interpretations diverged from Khalifa's on several theological matters.

Scholar Aisha Musa notes that "Yüksel's work represent a new trend that has emerged in the modern-day Qur'anist movement in the last several years."

### Critical Positions

Yüksel is openly critical of Islamic creationists, particularly opposing figures like Harun Yahya (Adnan Oktar). He advocates for theistic evolution and maintains a reformist approach to Islamic interpretation.

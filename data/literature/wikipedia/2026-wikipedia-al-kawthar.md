## Basic Structure

Al-Kawthar is identified as "the 108th chapter (sūrah) of the Quran" and holds the distinction of being the shortest chapter. The surah contains precisely **3 verses (ayat)** and consists of **10 words**.

## Etymology and Meaning

The term "Al-Kawthar" derives from "the triliteral root ك - ث - ر (k - th - r), meaning 'to increase, to be plentiful, abundance.'" Notably, "It appears in the Qur'an solely in this sūrah," making this the exclusive occurrence of this particular word in Islamic scripture.

## Classification

The surah is classified as a **Meccan revelation**, positioned in the 30th Juz' (section). According to Islamic tradition, according to Ibn Ishaq, "it is an early Meccan surah, revealed in Mecca."

## Content and Interpretation

The three verses present a message of divine blessing followed by religious instruction and reassurance to the Prophet Muhammad regarding his adversaries.

## Hadith References

Islamic traditions record various interpretations: one source notes that al-Kawthar refers to "a river in Paradise which my Lord has given me," while another states it "means the abundant good which Allah gave to the Prophet."

# Appendix 24: Two False Verses Removed from the Quran

## Core Claim

According to this appendix, verses 9:128-129 were falsely injected into the Quran approximately 19 years after Prophet Muhammad's death during Caliph Uthman's reign. The document asserts that a mathematical code based on the number 19 proves these verses do not belong in the authentic text.

## Historical Narrative

The appendix describes how a scribal committee proposed adding verses to honor the Prophet, but Caliph Ali objected strenuously, reportedly stating: *"Something has been added to the Quran, and I have pledged never to put on my street clothes, except for the prayer, until the Quran is restored."*

The document claims this dispute sparked a 50-year war, resulting in Ali's assassination and the eventual destruction of the original Quran by Marwan Ibn Al-Hakam.

## Mathematical Evidence Framework

The appendix presents 65 separate mathematical phenomena allegedly proving the verses' inauthenticity:

**Key Arguments Include:**
- Word frequency patterns (particularly "God," "Merciful," "Messenger") violate the 19-based code when verses 9:128-129 are included
- The phrase *"There is no god except He"* occurs 29 times across 19 suras—a pattern destroyed if 9:129 is counted
- Sura 9 should contain 127 verses (digits sum to 10); adding two verses creates 129, breaking established patterns
- Digit analysis of verse numbers, sura numbers, and their combinations consistently yield multiples of 19 only excluding these verses

## Historical Context from Islamic Sources

The appendix cites classical Islamic references noting that *"every single verse in the Quran was verified by a multiplicity of witnesses 'except Verses 128 and 129 of Sura 9; they were found only with Khuzeimah Ibn Thaabet."*

Notably, traditional Quran printings label verses 9:128-129 as "Meccan," despite Sura 9 being "Medinan"—a geographic-chronological contradiction that troubled Islamic scholars historically.

## Conclusion

The appendix argues the mathematical code's rejection of only these two verses demonstrates God's promise: *"Surely, we have revealed this message, and surely, we will preserve it."* (15:9)

The author attributes the 1,400-year delay in correction to fulfillment of 47:38, suggesting Muslims forfeited authentic revelation through doctrinal corruption.

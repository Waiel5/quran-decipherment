# Seven of the Pairs (Quran 15:87)

**Source:** https://www.quran-islam.org/articles/part_8/seven_of_the_pairs_(P1620).html/
**Tradition:** Quranist / Code-19 / Khalifa-derived
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Core Claim
The article presents numerical evidence that Quranic verse 15:87 references "seven of the pairs" (14 abbreviated letters), not the seven verses of the Fatiha as traditionally interpreted.

## Key Numerical Arguments

**Gematrical Value (GV) Patterns:**
- GV of "seven" = 133 (19 × 7)
- GV of "pairs" = 601 (digits sum to 7)
- GV of "of the pairs" = 722 (19 × 19 × 2)

**The 14 Letters:**
The article identifies 14 Arabic letters used in abbreviated combinations at the start of 29 Suras. Their combined GV equals 693. When added to 29: "693 + 29 = 722"—matching the GV of "of the pairs."

**Structural Claims:**
- Sura 15 has 99 verses; 15 + 99 = 114 (total Suras in Quran)
- 86 verses separate verse 1 from verse 87; 8 + 6 = 14
- Verse 87 contains 7 words and 37 letters

**Code 19 Integration:**
Multiple calculations claim results divisible by 19, with digit sums reducing to 14 or 19, allegedly confirming the design.

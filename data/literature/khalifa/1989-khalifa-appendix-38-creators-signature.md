# Appendix 38: The Creator's Signature

**Author:** Rashad Khalifa
**Source:** https://www.masjidtucson.org/quran/appendices/appendix38.html
**From:** Quran: The Final Testament, Authorized English Version
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

**Opening Statement:**
"The scriptures are not the only mathematically composed creations of God where the number 19 is the common denominator."

**Main Content:**

The passage asserts that 19 appears as a divine signature throughout creation, citing Galileo's observation that mathematics represents God's universal language.

Four examples are provided:

1. **Celestial alignment:** The sun, moon, and earth return to the same relative positions every 19 years.

2. **Halley's comet:** This celestial visitor appears every 76 years, which equals 19×4.

3. **Human skeletal structure:** The human body contains 209 bones, calculated as 19×11.

4. **Pregnancy duration:** According to Langman's Medical Embryology textbook (5th edition, page 88), pregnancy lasts either "280 days or 40 weeks after onset of the last menstruation, or more accurately, 266 days or 38 weeks after fertilization." The passage notes both 266 and 38 are multiples of 19.

The content suggests these mathematical patterns demonstrate God's signature upon creation in a manner comparable to how artists' signatures identify their works.

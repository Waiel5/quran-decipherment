# Appendix 29: The Missing Basmalah

**Author:** Rashad Khalifa
**Source:** https://www.masjidtucson.org/quran/appendices/appendix29.html
**From:** Quran: The Final Testament, Authorized English Version
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Key Points

**The Absence and Its Significance:**
"Every sura in the Quran opens with the statement 'In the name of God, Most Gracious, Most Merciful,' known as the Basmalah, with the exception of Sura 9."

The author argues this missing opening plays two roles: it's part of the Quran's mathematical code, and it signals that Sura 9 has been corrupted and needs correction.

**Mathematical Evidence:**
The document presents 12 mathematical observations linking the missing Basmalah in Sura 9 to an extra Basmalah appearing in Verse 30 of Sura 27. These observations involve calculations based on the number 19, including:

- Total Basmalahs in the Quran equal 114 (19×6)
- Nineteen suras exist between the missing and extra Basmalah
- Various digit sums and verse counts produce multiples of 19
- Complex numerical patterns involving sura numbers and verse counts

The author contends these mathematical relationships demonstrate the Quran's miraculous design while supporting the claim that Sura 9 contains textual corruption requiring removal of two verses.

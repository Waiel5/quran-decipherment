## Core Claims

The text asserts that "every element of the Quran is mathematically composed" with 19 as the common denominator. Key examples include:

- The word "God" appears 2,698 times (19×142)
- The Quran contains 114 suras (19×6)
- Total verses number 6,346 (19×334)
- The opening phrase contains 19 letters

## Quranic Initials

The appendix details how 14 different sets of letters prefix 29 suras. Each set's letter frequency within those suras allegedly produces multiples of 19. Examples cited:

- Letter "Q" appears 57 times in each Q-initialed sura
- Letters in "A.L.M." suras total 19,874 occurrences (19×1,046)
- Seven "H.M." suras contain 2,147 combined letters (19×113)

## Extended Mathematical Patterns

The document describes increasingly complex numerical arrangements, including very long numbers formed by concatenating verse numbers that allegedly remain divisible by 19 through multiple organizational schemes.

## Historical Context

The author credits discovering this pattern in 1974, noting correlation with the number 74 appearing in Sura 74, which mentions "19."

# Is Rashad's Translation of the Quran Error-free?

**Source:** https://19.org/blog/rashad-translation/
**Author:** Edip Yüksel
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Main Argument

The article argues that Rashad Khalifa's Quran translation contains errors and is not infallible, despite claims by some followers. Author Edip Yuksel provides a list of problematic verses, noting issues with "translational problems" across numerous passages.

## Key Points

**Errors Acknowledged**: Yuksel catalogs specific verses with alleged translation issues, marked with asterisks for footnote problems and exclamation marks for "missing phrases."

**Multiple Revisions as Evidence**: The existence of three different revisions demonstrates ongoing corrections. As Yuksel states: "The three revised editions...are blatant witnesses to the fact that he was in a continual learning process."

**Khalifa's Own Humility**: Yuksel emphasizes that Khalifa himself never claimed infallibility. The author notes he "encouraged us to edit and discuss the second revision of his translation verse by verse."

**Contributor Acknowledgments**: The article reveals that Yuksel made substantial contributions to corrections, which some followers later attempted to remove from editions.

**Conclusion**: While defending the translation's overall quality for conveying the Quranic message, Yuksel rejects the notion of errorlessness, describing such claims as idol-worship comparable to past religious communities' mistakes.

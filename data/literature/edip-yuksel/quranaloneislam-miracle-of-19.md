# Miracle of 19 (quranaloneislam.org)

**Source:** https://quranaloneislam.org/miracle-of-19/
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Core Claim
The article proposes that the number 19 appears throughout the Quran in mathematically significant ways, particularly related to astronomical cycles and the solar/lunar calendar relationship.

## Key Numerical Claims

**The Metonic Cycle:**
- 19 solar years align with 235 lunar months to the nearest 2 hours
- The word for "solar year" appears 19 times (12 plural + 7 singular forms)
- The words "sun" and "moon" intersect 19 times in the Quran

**The 365 Words "Day" System:**
The article claims the singular word "day" appears exactly 365 times throughout the Quran, symbolizing Earth's solar year length.

**Critical Calculation:**
- All gematrical values of the 365 words "day" = 22,781
- 22,781 ÷ 19 = 1,199
- 1,199 equals the gematrical value of verse 74:50
- The difference between 365-day and 354-day (lunar year) values = 678, which allegedly corresponds to the gematrical value of "in a protected book"

**Additional Claims:**
- 475 total word derivatives containing the "day" root = 19 × 25
- The sun completes approximately 475 rotations every 33 years
- Verse 56:78 divides the day-system at a position mathematically related to 19

## Methodological Note
The analysis employs gematrical values (numerical assignments to Arabic letters) to establish these relationships, a traditional Semitic calculation method.

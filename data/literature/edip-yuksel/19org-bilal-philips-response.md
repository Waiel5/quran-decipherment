# 19.org: Abu Ameenah Bilal Philips, a Hoaxer or a Murderer?

**Source:** https://19.org/blog/bilal-philips/
**Author:** Edip Yüksel (attributed)
**Fetched:** 2026-04-12

## Overview

This article from 19.org, attributed to Edip Yüksel, presents a scathing critique of Islamic scholar Abu Ameenah Bilal Philips and his book refuting the mathematical code 19 in the Quran discovered by Rashad Khalifa.

## Main Arguments

**Against Philips:**
The author contends that Philips studied under Abd al-Aziz ibn Baz, a Saudi cleric who issued a fatwa declaring Khalifa an apostate—"a code word for death penalty according to Sunni jurisprudence." Yüksel suggests Philips may have informed ibn Baz about Khalifa and enabled the subsequent assassination in 1990.

**Philips' Criticisms Addressed:**
The article systematically responds to Philips' seven main objections to the code 19 theory:

1. **Letter counts in Basmalah**: Yüksel challenges Philips' claim of 22 letters, stating the correct count is 19.

2. **Inconsistent methodologies**: Responding to accusations of manipulated data, the author argues most initial letters in surahs are multiples of 19, with discrepancies requiring manuscript comparison.

3. **Numerical symbolism**: The author defends the ABJAD system as historically legitimate during the Quran's revelation.

## Characterization of Philips

The author describes Philips as someone trained in "backward religious dogmas" who promotes "pedophilia and polygamy" through defending child marriage and supporting multiple wives. He's portrayed as intellectually dishonest regarding mathematical evidence.

## Theological Framework

Yüksel interprets Quran 74:31 as describing how the number 19 functions as an intellectual test distinguishing believers from those with "sickness in their hearts"—positioning himself as defending mathematical truth against religious dogmatism.

The 2026-04-28 wave of 6 pre-registered findings ([[h-new-580-five-factor-regression|H-NEW-580]], 590, 600/610, 620, 630, 660) collectively establish the Quran's mushaf-architecture as a **4-axis multiplicative composition**:

> Cohesion(window) = Compression_Tail(s) × ContentRegister(W) × OutlierStrength(W) × MuqaṭṭaʿātSubaxis(W)

## 8. Honest limits

1. **All findings use FR-roots distance**. Char-4-gram, NCD, contextual embeddings untested at this synthesis scale.
2. **The compression-tail law's R²=0.986 is in-sample**. The single-parameter description of canonical mushaf has no out-of-sample test (the canonical mushaf is the unique observation).
3. **The 4-axis decomposition is at SURAH-level**. Verse-level, root-level, and phonological-level structure may have different axis-decompositions.
4. **Muqaṭṭaʿāt-axis orthogonality at FR-roots-content scale does not preclude meaning at letter-level or numerical-symbolic level** (ʿilm al-ḥarf tradition under separate audit).
5. **Classical scholar VINDICATIONS are within the FR-roots metric scope**. al-Bukhārī Q 1 umm al-Kitāb, for instance, is partially-empirically-confirmed at content-distinctness; the deeper theological "mother of the book" claim is theological, not empirical.

### Q 1:6 — اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ
"Guide us to the straight path." Three words, nineteen letters; the only imperative in al-Fātiḥa addressed to God, and the hinge where voice shifts from third-person praise to first-person plural petition (iltifāt). The ultra-short length and `rhyme-deviation` on م in a surah otherwise rhyming on ن mark it as phonaesthetically distinct. Al-Rāzī treats it as the pivot of the entire Qur'an: every subsequent verse is commentary on "what is the straight path?" Mashriqi abjad of الصراط المستقيم = 331+201 ≈ 532 under rules tuple (Mashriqi, no-hamza-no-shadda). The `numerical-coincidence` and `kahf-finding` tags flag a claimed middle-of-Fātiḥa echo with Q 18's center; this is load-bearing only under rigid word-counting and should be treated as suggestive rather than demonstrative. Echoes at 2:142, 6:161, 36:4.

### Q 2:17 — مَثَلُهُمْ كَمَثَلِ الَّذِي اسْتَوْقَدَ نَارًا
"Their likeness is that of one who kindled a fire." The first extended parable of the Qur'an: the hypocrite as a man who lit a flame only to have God remove its light. Medinan, Nöldeke Medinan-I. The verse earns its tag density through `parable` + `fire-light` + `paradise-hell` paired-opposite vocabulary, a hapax (ذَهَبَ اللَّهُ بِنُورِهِمْ in this precise construction), and strong jinās between نُورِهِمْ and يُبْصِرُونَ through the ظلمات cluster. Al-Zamakhsharī's *Kashshāf* calls this mathal the template for the Qur'an's light/darkness imagery. Letter count 72, rhyme ن conforming. Cross-echo: Q 24:40 (the ocean-darkness parable), Q 57:13 (the wall with a door).

### Q 2:33 — قَالَ يَا آدَمُ أَنبِئْهُم بِأَسْمَائِهِمْ
"He said, 'O Adam, inform them of their names.'" The resolution of the naming contest with the angels. Medinan-I. Chiastic structure (A: God tells angels / B: angels fail / B′: Adam informs / A′: God vindicates) earns the `chiastic` + `ring-center` tags; the rhetorical question within the speech earns `rhetorical-Q`; the vocative يَا آدَمُ is a rare direct address. Al-Ṭabarī notes Ibn ʿAbbās's view that the names were of every thing, reinforcing Adam's epistemic primacy. Mashriqi abjad of آدم = 1+4+40 = 45, of أسماءهم (dropping hamza) = 1+60+40+1+5+40 = 147. Echoes with 2:31, 7:11, 15:29.

### Q 2:54 — وَإِذْ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِقَوْمِهِ
"And when Moses said to his people..." The calf-worship episode's command to self-execute. Medinan-I. Long (121 letters, 27 words), ends on the divine pair التَّوَّابُ الرَّحِيمُ — hence `divine-name-pair-ending` and `razi-99names`. The `weapon` tag flags the imperative فَاقْتُلُوا أَنْفُسَكُمْ, which al-Qurṭubī reads as a spiritual execution of the nafs al-ammāra (following one Ṣūfī reading) alongside the literal one. Rhyme-deviation on م against surah-2's ن-norm. Cross-references 4:66, 20:87–91.

### Q 2:60 — وَإِذِ اسْتَسْقَىٰ مُوسَىٰ لِقَوْمِهِ
"And when Moses sought water for his people." The twelve springs miracle. The `famous-number` tag fires on اثْنَتَا عَشْرَةَ عَيْنًا — the tribes' count structurally mirrored in 7:160. Dual-form عَيْنَا, prohibitive وَلاَ تَعْثَوْا, imperative كُلُوا وَاشْرَبُوا. Al-Biqāʿī in *Naẓm al-Durar* reads the twelve-spring detail as a hydrological prefiguration of the twelve naqībs in 5:12 — a reading the atlas tags as `intra-xref`. Mashriqi abjad of عين = 130.

### Q 2:74 — ثُمَّ قَسَتْ قُلُوبُكُم مِّن بَعْدِ ذَٰلِكَ
"Then your hearts hardened after that." The heart-stone parable with its tri-fold elaboration (stones that gush rivers, stones that split and release water, stones that fall from fear of God). `parable`, `qalb-theology`, `emotion`, `muqattaat`-adjacent by its mid-surah position. Al-Ghazālī cites this verse in *Iḥyāʾ* III as proof that even inanimate stones have a khushūʿ that exceeds the hardened heart. Long (141 letters), rhyme ن conforming. The `scholar-commentary` tag marks dense tafsīr overlay. Echo: 39:22, 57:16.

### Q 2:75 — أَفَتَطْمَعُونَ أَن يُؤْمِنُوا لَكُمْ
"Do you then hope that they will believe with you?" Rhetorical-Q addressed to the early Muslims about Banū Isrāʾīl. `graph-theory` tag flags this verse's role in intra-Quranic citation networks (it is cited in over 30 later tafsīr cross-references). `scripture-ref` fires on يَسْمَعُونَ كَلَامَ اللَّهِ ثُمَّ يُحَرِّفُونَهُ — the classical taḥrīf locus. Al-Rāzī distinguishes taḥrīf al-maʿnā (interpretive distortion) from taḥrīf al-lafẓ (textual alteration) here.

### Q 2:76 — وَإِذَا لَقُوا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا
"And when they meet those who have believed." The hypocrite-duplicity verse. `asbab-nuzul` — al-Wāḥidī records Ibn ʿAbbās's report about Jewish converts who informed the believers privately but denied publicly. Rhetorical-Q أَفَلَا تَعْقِلُونَ closes it. `hajj` tag here is lexical, not topical (cf. ليحاجوكم, same root ḥ-j-j). Phonaesthetic cluster on ʿayn/ḥā.

### Q 2:83 — وَإِذْ أَخَذْنَا مِيثَاقَ بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ
"And when We took the covenant of the Children of Israel." The foundational covenant verse listing parents-kin-orphans-poor-speech-prayer-zakāh. Long, 150 letters. `covenant` + `salah` + `zakat` tag triad flags it as the Qur'an's most compact recapitulation of mosaic ethics. Al-Ṭabarī treats it as the "charter of Medinan civil theology." Dual-form بِالْوَالِدَيْنِ. Imperatives cascade. Echo: 4:36, 6:151 (the Meccan parallel).

### Q 2:97 — قُلْ مَن كَانَ عَدُوًّا لِّجِبْرِيلَ
"Say: whoever is an enemy to Gabriel..." The only Qur'anic verse where the angel's name occurs in this form with explicit vindication. `angel`, `body-part` (عَلَى قَلْبِكَ), qul-imperative. Al-Suyūṭī's *Itqān* cites this as the asbāb for the "which angel?" debate with certain Jewish rabbis of Medina. Mashriqi abjad of جبريل (no-hamza reading) = 3+2+200+10+30 = 245; of ميكال = 40+10+20+1+30 = 101. Echo: 2:98.

### Q 2:140 — أَمْ تَقُولُونَ إِنَّ إِبْرَاهِيمَ
"Or do you say that Ibrāhīm..." Part of the qibla-polemic cluster. `baqarah-ibrahim-qibla` tag flags its ring-structural role linking the Abraham narrative (2:124ff) to the qibla command (2:142ff). Rhetorical-Q with أَم ترنيرد denial. Al-Biqāʿī reads this as the rhetorical pivot between genealogy and direction. Long (127 letters), rhyme ن.

### Q 2:187 — أُحِلَّ لَكُمْ لَيْلَةَ الصِّيَامِ
"Permitted to you on the night of fasting..." The longest fasting-legislation verse; 286 letters, 65 words — extraordinary for rule-text. `color` (الْخَيْطُ الأَبْيَضُ مِنَ الْخَيْطِ الأَسْوَدِ), `balagha` (the thread-of-dawn simile), `hapax` (several), `info-theory` flag for its information density (the atlas measures unusually low redundancy). Al-Rāzī devotes 14 columns to the thread metaphor, calling it ashraf al-tashbīhāt fī l-ḥudūd. Echo: the fasting-light cluster in 17:78.

### Q 2:214 — أَمْ حَسِبْتُمْ أَن تَدْخُلُوا الْجَنَّةَ
"Or did you think you would enter paradise..." Rhetorical challenge, parable of earlier prophets tested. `parable`, `rhyme-break` — this verse rhymes on ب against the surrounding ن-stream, a deliberate disjunction al-Zamakhsharī reads as iconic of the disjunction between easy faith and tested faith. مَسَّتْهُمُ الْبَأْسَاءُ والضَّرَّاءُ — paired hapaxes. Mashriqi abjad of البأساء (no-hamza reading) = 1+30+2+60+1+1 = 95, of الضراء = 1+30+800+200+1 = 1032.

### Q 2:217 — يَسْأَلُونَكَ عَنِ الشَّهْرِ الْحَرَامِ
"They ask you about the sacred month." The most famous naskh-carrying verse on fighting in the sacred month. `naskh`, `sacred-geo`, `hajj`. Long, 247 letters. Qul-opener establishes the prophetic address. Al-Wāḥidī's *Asbāb* gives the ʿAbdallāh b. Jaḥsh raid as occasion. The tags' `asbab-nuzul` + `naskh` combination marks it as a textbook case where context determines ruling. Echo: 9:5, 9:36.

### Q 2:219 — يَسْأَلُونَكَ عَنِ الْخَمْرِ وَالْمَيْسِرِ
"They ask you about wine and gambling." The central naskh sequence (this verse → 4:43 → 5:90–91 in classical view). Dual-form قُلْ فِيهِمَا. `asbab-nuzul`, `naskh`, rhetorical-Q. Al-Suyūṭī treats the three-verse progression as the archetype of tadrīj al-taḥrīm. Mashriqi abjad of الخمر = 1+30+600+40+200 = 871, of الميسر = 1+30+40+10+60+200 = 341.

### Q 2:246 — أَلَمْ تَرَ إِلَى الْمَلَإِ مِن بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ
"Have you not seen the chiefs of the Children of Israel?" The Samuel-Saul-Goliath prologue. `palindrome` tag flags a letter-sequence palindrome the atlas detects internally (likely around كُتِبَ علينا / علينا كُتِبَ permutation). Long, 217 letters. Rhetorical-Q and `contains-wa-allah` (وَاللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ بِالظَّالِمِينَ clause). `weapon` tag. Ibn Kathīr links this to the prophet-after-Moses chain.

### Q 2:267 — يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا أَنفِقُوا
"O you who believe, spend..." The zakāh-purity verse with the famous prohibition against giving "the bad of it" when one would not accept it oneself. `vocative`, `prohibitive`. `water` tag fires on the purifying-rain imagery in context. Al-Qurṭubī cites this against ritualized miserliness. Rhyme-deviation on د.

### Q 2:286 — لاَ يُكَلِّفُ اللَّهُ نَفْسًا إِلَّا وُسْعَهَا
"God does not burden a soul beyond its capacity." The closing verse of al-Baqara, the longest surah-closer in the Qur'an (196 letters, 49 words). `famous:baqarah-closing`, `dua`, `nafs-theology`. Reports in al-Bukhārī: "Whoever recites the last two verses of al-Baqara at night, they suffice him." Imperatives-in-supplication form (e.g., وَاعْفُ عَنَّا). Mashriqi abjad of وسعها = 6+60+70+5+1 = 142. Echo: 6:152, 23:62, 65:7.

### Q 3:3 — نَزَّلَ عَلَيْكَ الْكِتَابَ بِالْحَقِّ
"He has sent down upon you the Book in truth." The programmatic self-description opening Āl ʿImrān's body. `scripture-ref`, `self-reference`, `maryam-finding`. Short at 53 letters; rhyme-deviation on ل (against surah-3's mixed rhyme set). Al-Ṭabarī reads the triad Torah–Injīl–Furqān in 3:3–4 as a single compositional unit. Dual-form `dual-form-topic` fires on الْتَّوْرَاةَ وَالإنجِيلَ.

### Q 3:4 — مِن قَبْلُ هُدًى لِّلنَّاسِ
"From before, as guidance for people." Continues 3:3; the atlas's `code19-audit` tag here is a failed claim: the Rashad Khalifa code-19 system claimed nineteen-structured placements around muqaṭṭaʿāt-surahs; audit of this verse's position shows no nineteen-multiplicative signature that is not also producible by chance. **Refutation flag: code19 claim does not survive audit at 3:4.** Al-Zamakhsharī notes the verse's warning-clause shift (iltifāt from rahma to ʿadhāb).

### Q 3:15 — قُلْ أَؤُنَبِّئُكُم بِخَيْرٍ مِّن ذَٰلِكُمْ
"Say: shall I inform you of something better than that?" Qul-rhetorical-Q introducing the paradise-description pericope. `contains-wa-allah`, `qul-say-opener`. Al-Rāzī reads the question as the Qur'an's archetypal better-than-bargain (tijāra) move. Medium-length; rhyme-deviation on د.

### Q 3:18 — شَهِدَ اللَّهُ أَنَّهُ لاَ إِلَٰهَ إِلاَّ هُوَ
"God bears witness that there is no god but He." The famous shahida-llāh verse. Three divine names cluster (الله, الملائكة subject to Him, الْعَزِيزُ الْحَكِيمُ closure). `divine-name-cluster`, `divine-name-pair-ending`, `famous:shahida-llah`, `muhkam-mutashabih` — the verse is canonical for tawḥīd discussion. Ibn Kathīr quotes the ḥadīth on its superiority. Mashriqi abjad of شهد الله = 300+5+4+1+30+30+5 = 375. `info-theory` fires because the verse's information density ranks in the top tier of the atlas. Echo: 3:6, 112:1–4.

### Q 3:29 — قُلْ إِن تُخْفُوا مَا فِي صُدُورِكُمْ
"Say: whether you conceal what is in your breasts..." `ilm-al-harf` tag flags the verse as a locus of ḥurūf-based exegesis by Ibn ʿArabī regarding the bāṭin/ẓāhir polarity. `qalb-theology` fires on صُدُورِكُمْ. `prime-mod` tag: the verse's total letter-count (79) is prime; the atlas flags these as structurally marked points. Al-Qurṭubī emphasizes the verse as proof of divine omniscience over intentions.

### Q 3:59 — إِنَّ مَثَلَ عِيسَىٰ عِندَ اللَّهِ كَمَثَلِ آدَمَ
"Indeed the likeness of Jesus with God is as the likeness of Adam." The Adam–Jesus analogy in the Najrān delegation polemic. `innama-topic`, `parable`, `root-cartography`, `prophet-micro-ring`. Short (47 letters, 15 words), rhyme ن conforming. Al-Rāzī devotes a major discussion: if virgin-birth entails divinity, then a-fortiori Adam's no-parent creation would entail greater divinity — reductio against Christological claim. Echo: 19:35, 21:91.

### Q 3:61 — فَمَنْ حَاجَّكَ فِيهِ مِن بَعْدِ مَا جَاءَكَ مِنَ الْعِلْمِ
"Whoever disputes with you concerning him after knowledge has come..." The Mubāhala verse. `hajj` (lexical, ḥ-j-j), `intra-xref`, `hapax` (نَبْتَهِلْ). `code19-audit` is flagged: claims that this verse's position encodes nineteen-multiples fail under rigorous audit. Ibn Kathīr, al-Ṭabarī narrate the Najrān mubāhala event. Qul-opener and quotation of the proposed oath-structure.

### Q 3:65 — يَا أَهْلَ الْكِتَابِ لِمَ تُحَاجُّونَ فِي إِبْرَاهِيمَ
"O People of the Book, why do you dispute about Abraham?" Vocative + rhetorical-Q. `jesus-engagement`, `scripture-ref`, `cross-textual`. Al-Zamakhsharī: Abraham antedates both Torah and Injīl, so claiming him for Judaism or Christianity is anachronistic — this is the structural argument the verse frames. Medium, rhyme ن.

### Q 3:81 — وَإِذْ أَخَذَ اللَّهُ مِيثَاقَ النَّبِيِّينَ
"And when God took the covenant of the prophets." The prophetic-charter verse. `covenant`, `muqattaat` (by position in mid-surah of non-muqaṭṭaʿāt surah — tag is structural). Al-Suyūṭī records Ibn ʿAbbās's reading: the covenant is that each prophet would confirm the next. Rhetorical-Q and quotation. Long (146 letters).

### Q 3:84 — قُلْ آمَنَّا بِاللَّهِ وَمَا أُنزِلَ عَلَيْنَا
"Say: we believe in God and what has been revealed to us..." Near-verbatim parallel with 2:136. `mutashabih-lafzi`, `mutashabih-topic`, `form-content-outlier` — the outlier flag fires because the verse's word-frequency profile is an outlier even within Āl ʿImrān. Qul-opener + prophetic-name enumeration. Al-Biqāʿī reads the two-verse mutashābiha pair as deliberate iteration, marking community vs prophet address. Echo: 2:136, 4:136.

### Q 3:165 — أَوَلَمَّا أَصَابَتْكُم مُّصِيبَةٌ
"Or when a calamity has struck you..." The post-Uḥud reflection. `parable`-adjacent (historical simile), rhetorical-Q, qul-opener, dual-form أَصَبْتُم مِّثْلَيْهَا (doubled calamity reference to Badr). Al-Ṭabarī: the doubling is the Muslims having killed 70 and captured 70 at Badr, while losing 70 at Uḥud — a structural symmetry the verse makes explicit.

### Q 3:167 — وَلِيَعْلَمَ الَّذِينَ نَافَقُوا
"And that He might know those who have been hypocrites." The hypocrite-exposure verse, also post-Uḥud. `palindrome` flag. `weapon` (قَاتِلُوا / اِدْفَعُوا). `body-part` (بِأَفْوَاهِهِم). Long (151 letters). Al-Qurṭubī treats it as the classical definition-locus for nifāq. Echo: 4:142, 63:1.

### Q 4:9 — وَلْيَخْشَ الَّذِينَ لَوْ تَرَكُوا مِنْ خَلْفِهِمْ ذُرِّيَّةً ضِعَافًا
"Let those fear who, if they were to leave behind them weak offspring..." The orphan-protection ethics verse. Tag count 21 — high. `kinship`, `emotion`, `form-content-outlier`, `phonaesthetic`. Ibn Kathīr reports this verse is cited against any guardian who mistreats orphans. `numerical-sequence` fires on the atlas's noting of its early position in a sequence of inheritance-ethics verses. Medium, rhyme ا. `ilm-al-harf` tag flags Ṣūfī readings of ضِعَافًا as weakness of heart metaphorically. Mashriqi abjad of ضعافا = 800+70+1+80+1 = 952.

### Q 4:12 — وَلَكُمْ نِصْفُ مَا تَرَكَ أَزْوَاجُكُمْ
"Yours is half of what your wives leave..." The longest single-verse inheritance rule in the Qur'an (299 letters, 88 words). `famous-number` (the fractional rule cluster), `form-content-outlier` (extraordinarily long for its surah-position), hapax on كَلَالَةً. Al-Ṭabarī's treatment runs to eleven pages. Mashriqi abjad of كلالة = 20+30+1+30+5 = 86. The `numerical-coincidence` tag is descriptive — fractions ½, ¼, ⅛, ⅙, ⅓ appear in a structured hierarchy; claims that these sum mystically to a target value should be treated skeptically (abjad sums are unstable under rule-changes).

### Q 4:75 — وَمَا لَكُمْ لاَ تُقَاتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ
"And what is it with you that you do not fight in the path of God?" Rhetorical-Q combined with imperative. `verse-length-outlier`, `intra-xref`, `weapon`. The verse invokes the oppressed رِجَال ونِسَاء وولِدَان formula. Al-Zamakhsharī reads the threefold classification as rhetorical intensification through descending vulnerability. Long, rhyme ا.

### Q 4:78 — أَيْنَمَا تَكُونُوا يُدْرِككُّمُ الْمَوْتُ
"Wherever you are, death will overtake you." Chiastic: A (death-inevitability) / B (good-from-God) / B′ (evil-from-you) / A′ (Messenger sent). `chiastic`, `hapax` (يُدْرِك in this form). Al-Rāzī uses the verse for the occasionalist vs acquisitive-human-agency debate. Long, rhyme ا conforming. `prophet-micro-ring` flags its position in a small ring around 4:79.

### Q 4:87 — اللَّهُ لاَ إِلَٰهَ إِلاَّ هُوَ لَيَجْمَعَنَّكُمْ
"God — there is no god but He — will surely gather you..." `ayat-al-kursi-finding` tag flags this verse's structural echo of 2:255's opening formula (لا إله إلا هو) which appears only in a limited set of surahs. `tawhid`, `chiastic` with 4:171 as ring-closure. Al-Ṭabarī: the gathering is yawm al-qiyāma. Short-medium.

### Q 4:88 — فَمَا لَكُمْ فِي الْمُنَافِقِينَ فِئَتَيْنِ
"Why are you two factions regarding the hypocrites?" Rhetorical-Q, `asbab-nuzul` relates to the post-Uḥud defection controversy at Medina. Dual-form فِئَتَيْنِ. Al-Wāḥidī records the split opinions within the Companions. `phonaesthetic` cluster on fā-nūn.

### Q 4:94 — يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِذَا ضَرَبْتُمْ فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ فَتَبَيَّنُوا
"O you who believe, when you travel in the path of God, verify." Vocative + imperative. `weapon`, `palindrome` flag. Al-Ṭabarī's asbāb: a Muslim killed a man who had declared Islam, thinking him pretending. The verse establishes the tabayyun principle — verify before acting. Long (170 letters). Ibn Kathīr cites this as foundational for Islamic evidentiary ethics.

### Q 4:97 — إِنَّ الَّذِينَ تَوَفَّاهُمُ الْمَلاَئِكَةُ ظَالِمِي أَنفُسِهِمْ
"Indeed those whom the angels take while wronging themselves..." The hijra-obligation verse. `angel`, `paired-opposite` (مُسْتَضْعَفِينَ / مُسْتَكْبِرِينَ implicit). Rhetorical-Q quoted from angels: فِيمَ كُنتُمْ. Al-Qurṭubī: this is the scriptural basis for fard-hijra when religion cannot be practiced. Rhyme ا conforming.

### Q 5:12 — وَلَقَدْ أَخَذَ اللَّهُ مِيثَاقَ بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ
"And God had taken the covenant of the Children of Israel." `famous-number` — the twelve naqībs (بَعَثْنَا مِنْهُمُ اثْنَيْ عَشَرَ نَقِيبًا) paralleling the twelve springs (2:60) and twelve tribes (7:160). `covenant`, `salah`, `zakat`. Al-Biqāʿī reads the triple-twelve pattern as typological. Long (207 letters). Rhyme-deviation on ل.

### Q 5:15 — يَا أَهْلَ الْكِتَابِ قَدْ جَاءَكُمْ رَسُولُنَا
"O People of the Book, our Messenger has come to you." Vocative opener. `paired-opposite` on كَثِيرٍ / كَثِيرٍ (what is disclosed vs what is pardoned). `cosmology-audit` — the atlas flags light-and-clear-book language; claims of "cosmological secrets" encoded are unsupported by the verse's plain sense, which is prophetic disclosure of scripture. Medium, rhyme ن.

### Q 5:18 — وَقَالَتِ الْيَهُودُ وَالنَّصَارَىٰ نَحْنُ أَبْنَاءُ اللَّهِ
"And the Jews and Christians said: we are the children of God." Qul-rebuttal. `moses-finding`, rhetorical-Q, `kinship` (أَبْنَاءُ وَأَحِبَّاءُ). Al-Zamakhsharī unpacks the question فَلِمَ يُعَذِّبُكُم بِذُنُوبِكُمْ as a reductio against inherited favor. Long (134 letters).

### Q 5:54 — يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا مَن يَرْتَدَّ مِنكُمْ عَن دِينِهِ
"O you who believe, whoever of you apostatizes from his religion..." Vocative. `cosmology-audit` is incidental. `nafs-theology`. Describes a future community God will bring: يُحِبُّهُمْ وَيُحِبُّونَهُ — Al-Rāzī cites the mutual-love formulation as a cornerstone of Sufi anthropology. `weapon` clauses (يُجَاهِدُونَ). Long, rhyme-deviation on م. Echo: 47:38.

### Q 5:60 — قُلْ هَلْ أُنَبِّئُكُم بِشَرٍّ مِّن ذَٰلِكَ
"Say: shall I inform you of worse than that?" Structural inversion of 3:15 (where it was "better"). `rhetorical-Q`, qul-opener. Al-Suyūṭī notes the pair 3:15/5:60 as a deliberate chiasm across surah boundaries. Rhyme-deviation on ل.

### Q 5:64 — وَقَالَتِ الْيَهُودُ يَدُ اللَّهِ مَغْلُولَةٌ
"And the Jews said: God's hand is fettered." `body-part` (يَد), tawḥīd-polemic. `muhkam-mutashabih` — the hand-of-God phrase is the classical mutashābih locus for theological schools (Ḥanbalī affirmation without how, Muʿtazilī metaphoric reading, Ashʿarī tafwīḍ). Al-Ṭabarī transmits the asbāb: a Jewish utterance of divine stinginess. Dual-form yadāhu mabsūṭatān is the rebuttal. Long (218 letters), rhyme ن, tag count 21.

### Q 5:68 — قُلْ يَا أَهْلَ الْكِتَابِ لَسْتُمْ عَلَىٰ شَيْءٍ
"Say: O People of the Book, you stand on nothing..." Double-vocative (qul + yā ahl). Prohibitive implicit. Al-Qurṭubī reads "on nothing" as "on nothing of religion" until they establish Torah, Injīl, and revealed scripture proper. `scripture-ref`.

### Q 5:73 — لَقَدْ كَفَرَ الَّذِينَ قَالُوا إِنَّ اللَّهَ ثَالِثُ ثَلاَثَةٍ
"Those who say 'God is the third of three' have disbelieved." `tawhid`, `famous-number` (three), `deep-hypothesis` — the atlas flags trinitarian-refutation verses as a structural-hypothesis cluster. `palindrome` internal letter sequence flagged. Al-Rāzī treats this alongside 5:116 as the Qur'an's explicit Christological polemic. Rhyme-deviation on م.

### Q 5:112 — إِذْ قَالَ الْحَوَارِيُّونَ يَا عِيسَى ابْنَ مَرْيَمَ
"When the disciples said: O Jesus son of Mary..." The māʾida (table) request pericope opening. Vocative quotation. `jesus-engagement`. Al-Biqāʿī reads the disciples' request as the structural echo of Banū Isrāʾīl's quail-and-manna demand. Medium, rhyme ن.

### Q 6:9 — وَلَوْ جَعَلْنَاهُ مَلَكًا لَّجَعَلْنَاهُ رَجُلاً
"Had We made him an angel, We would have made him a man." Chiastic miniature A: angel-hypothesis / A′: man-form necessary. `chiastic`, `ring-center`, `prophet-micro-ring`, `paired-opposite`, `scholar-commentary`. Al-Zamakhsharī's treatment is canonical: the verse resolves the pagan demand-for-angelic-messenger by showing human form is epistemically required. Short (44 letters, 9 words), `time-vocab`. `asbab-nuzul` traces to a Quraysh demand.

### Q 6:12 — قُل لِّمَن مَّا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالأَرْضِ
"Say: to whom belongs what is in the heavens and earth?" Rhetorical-Q + self-answering قُل لِّلَّهِ. `intra-xref` with 6:19, 42:53. Al-Ṭabarī treats the triple "qul li-man" structure of sūrat al-Anʿām as ring-compositional. Rhyme ن.

### Q 6:14 — قُلْ أَغَيْرَ اللَّهِ أَتَّخِذُ وَلِيًّا
"Say: shall I take as guardian other than God?" `famous-number` — the verse contains the walī concept that structures later theology. Qul-opener, rhetorical-Q. Al-Rāzī uses this verse as the taproot for walāya doctrine. Rhyme-break + rhyme-conforming (the atlas detects both: the verse conforms rhyme-class but disrupts internal rhythm).

### Q 6:40 — قُلْ أَرَأَيْتَكُم إِنْ أَتَاكُمْ عَذَابُ اللَّهِ
"Say: have you considered, if God's punishment came to you..." Qul-rhetorical-Q. `intra-xref` with 6:46, 6:47, 6:50 — a five-verse chain all beginning with the same rhetorical pattern that al-Biqāʿī calls *al-silsila al-iḥtijājiyya* (the argumentative chain). Rhyme-break + conforming.

### Q 6:47 — قُلْ أَرَأَيْتَكُم إِنْ أَتَاكُمْ عَذَابُ اللَّهِ بَغْتَةً أَوْ جَهْرَةً
"Say: have you considered, if God's punishment came to you suddenly or openly..." Member of the silsila. `paired-opposite` (bagtatan/jahratan). Al-Ṭabarī glosses the pair as "whether at night or by day." Rhyme ن.

### Q 6:50 — قُل لاَّ أَقُولُ لَكُمْ عِندِي خَزَائِنُ اللَّهِ
"Say: I do not say to you 'I have the treasuries of God.'" Triple-negation disclaimer of the Prophet's ontological status. Two divine names cluster. Al-Qurṭubī: three denials (treasuries, ghayb, angelic status) constitute the Prophet's negative theology of his own person. Rhyme ن.

### Q 6:91 — وَمَا قَدَرُوا اللَّهَ حَقَّ قَدْرِهِ
"They did not measure God with His true measure." `cosmology-audit` fires on claims that قَدَرُوا encodes a cosmic measure — al-Rāzī's balāgha reading is that the phrase means "honor" not "dimension"; numerical/physical readings should be refused. Long (171 letters). Al-Zamakhsharī links to 22:74, 39:67.

### Q 6:109 — وَأَقْسَمُوا بِاللَّهِ جَهْدَ أَيْمَانِهِمْ
"They swore by God their strongest oaths." `oath-cluster`, `innama`. Al-Wāḥidī traces the asbāb to Quraysh demanding a sign. The verse's innamā al-āyāt ʿinda llāh is the epistemological point: sign-production is divine prerogative.

### Q 6:143 — ثَمَانِيَةَ أَزْوَاجٍ مِّنَ الضَّأْنِ اثْنَيْنِ
"Eight pairs: from sheep two..." `animal`, `hapax` (الضَّأْن), dual forms, `rhetorical-Q`. Al-Ṭabarī: rebuttal of pagan livestock-taboo rulings. The enumerative structure 2+2+2+2 is the Qur'an's structural polemic against arbitrary prohibition. Long, rhyme-break on ن.

### Q 6:151 — قُلْ تَعَالَوْا أَتْلُ مَا حَرَّمَ رَبُّكُمْ
"Say: come, I will recite what your Lord has forbidden." The Meccan Decalogue opener — ten commandments distributed across 6:151–153. `balagha`, `weapon`, `prohibitive`, imperatives. Al-Suyūṭī calls this *waṣāyā ʿashr*. Long (180 letters). Echo: Exodus 20, and 17:23–39.

### Q 7:22 — فَدَلاَّهُمَا بِغُرُورٍ
"He brought them down by deception." The Eden episode's punch-line clause. `iblis`, `dual-form` (throughout the verse), `animal`/`plant` (from tree imagery), `sensory` (body parts revealed). Rhetorical-Q أَلَمْ أَنْهَكُمَا. Al-Rāzī reads dallāhumā as "lowered them from high rank"; the root d-l-w is the bucket-lowering metaphor.

### Q 7:26 — يَا بَنِي آدَمَ قَدْ أَنزَلْنَا عَلَيْكُمْ لِبَاسًا
"O children of Adam, We have sent down upon you clothing." `hadid-finding` (the atlas's Q 57 / hadīd cluster), `earth-sciences-audit`, `scientific-foreknowledge` — note: the "iron sent down" claim (from 57:25) is extended by some to this verse's "clothing sent down"; **refutation flag: the anzala verbs in 26, 57:25 etc. are used idiomatically for ordination/creation across many contexts (cf. أَنزَلَ الأَنْعَامَ, 39:6) — no specifically scientific foreknowledge is demonstrated.** Al-Ṭabarī reads anzalnā as "bestowed/ordained." Rhyme ن.

### Q 7:28 — وَإِذَا فَعَلُوا فَاحِشَةً قَالُوا وَجَدْنَا عَلَيْهَا آبَاءَنَا
"And when they commit an indecency, they say: we found our fathers upon it." Quotation + rhetorical-Q rebuttal قُلْ إِنَّ اللَّهَ لاَ يَأْمُرُ بِالْفَحْشَاء. `contains-wa-allah`. Al-Zamakhsharī: the verse establishes ancestral authority as insufficient against divine prohibition.

### Q 7:44 — وَنَادَىٰ أَصْحَابُ الْجَنَّةِ أَصْحَابَ النَّارِ
"The inhabitants of paradise call to the inhabitants of fire." `paradise-hell`, `covenant`, rhetorical-Q quoted across eschatological divide. Al-Qurṭubī treats this as the first of the aʿrāf-cluster dialogues (7:44, 7:46, 7:50). `numerical-sequence` flags the chain.

### Q 7:50 — وَنَادَىٰ أَصْحَابُ النَّارِ أَصْحَابَ الْجَنَّةِ
"The inhabitants of fire call to the inhabitants of paradise." Mirror of 7:44. `rahman-finding`, `cross-textual`, `paradise-hell`. Dual-form. Al-Rāzī: the mirrored supplications establish the fixity of judgement — request for water is refused.

### Q 7:59 — لَقَدْ أَرْسَلْنَا نُوحًا إِلَىٰ قَوْمِهِ
"We indeed sent Nūḥ to his people." Opener of the prophetic-chain in sūrat al-Aʿrāf. `prophet-micro-ring`, `mutashabih-topic`, `intra-xref` with 11:25, 23:23, 26:105. Al-Biqāʿī: each prophet is introduced with laqad arsalnā or equivalent, a structural marker of the Aʿrāf prophet-rhythm. Rhyme-deviation on م.

### Q 7:71 — قَالَ قَدْ وَقَعَ عَلَيْكُم مِّن رَّبِّكُمْ رِجْسٌ
"He said: filth from your Lord has already fallen upon you." Hūd-pericope. `cosmology-audit`, `emotion`, `form-content-outlier`. Rhetorical-Q internal. Al-Ṭabarī: the verse proves that divine wrath's ontological fall precedes temporal punishment.

### Q 7:85 — وَإِلَىٰ مَدْيَنَ أَخَاهُمْ شُعَيْبًا
"And to Madyan, their brother Shuʿayb." The Shuʿayb pericope introduction. `sacred-geo`, `mutashabih-topic`, `prohibitive`. Parallel with 11:84, 26:176, 29:36. Al-Qurṭubī reads akhāhum (kinship term) consistently: each prophet is called a brother of his people. Long (156 letters).

### Q 7:169 — فَخَلَفَ مِن بَعْدِهِمْ خَلْفٌ وَرِثُوا الْكِتَابَ
"There succeeded them successors who inherited the Book." `covenant` rupture. `paradise-hell` — the near-term vs Hereafter reward contrast. Al-Rāzī reads yaʾkhudhūna ʿaraḍa hādhā l-adnā as the worldly-trinket trope. Long (172 letters). Rhyme ن.

### Q 8:1 — يَسْأَلُونَكَ عَنِ الأَنفَالِ
"They ask you about the spoils." Sūrat al-Anfāl's opening. `ilm-al-harf`, `ring-center`, `surah-opener`. Al-Wāḥidī: the asbāb is the post-Badr dispute. Qul-opener. `rahman-finding` tag is indexical. Medium (89 letters).

### Q 8:2 — إِنَّمَا الْمُؤْمِنُونَ الَّذِينَ إِذَا ذُكِرَ اللَّهُ
"The believers are only those whose hearts tremble when God is mentioned." `innama`, `qalb-theology`, `saj-rhyme`. Canonical definition-verse for īmān. Al-Ghazālī's *Iḥyāʾ* I builds its chapter on dhikr on this verse. Medium, rhyme ن. Echo: 22:35, 39:23.

### Q 8:65 — يَا أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ حَرِّضِ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ عَلَى الْقِتَالِ
"O Prophet, urge the believers to fight." Vocative + imperative. `famous-number` — the 20:200, 100:1000 ratios (a ratio reading which al-Suyūṭī links to 8:66's abrogation-adjustment). `weapon`. Al-Ṭabarī: the numerical ratios are battle-readiness specifications.

### Q 9:5 — فَإِذَا انسَلَخَ الأَشْهُرُ الْحُرُمُ
"When the sacred months have passed..." The so-called "sword verse" (according to some Sunni jurists). `naskh` — claimed by some to abrogate ~124 earlier tolerance verses, though classical jurists dispute the extent. `salah`, `zakat`, `hajj`. `ethical-universalism` tag flags the important condition clause فَإِن تَابُوا وَأَقَامُوا الصَّلاَةَ — the verse's ruling is contingent, not blanket. Al-Qurṭubī emphasizes the release-clause. Long (133 letters). Echo: 9:6 (immediate asylum provision).

### Q 9:6 — وَإِنْ أَحَدٌ مِّنَ الْمُشْرِكِينَ اسْتَجَارَكَ فَأَجِرْهُ
"If any of the polytheists seeks your protection, grant it." The asylum verse, directly following the "sword verse." `ethical-universalism` — this is the key mitigating verse routinely dropped from polemical citation of 9:5. `earth-sciences-audit` tag is incidental. Al-Ṭabarī records the verse as establishing the minimum-protection norm.

### Q 9:7 — كَيْفَ يَكُونُ لِلْمُشْرِكِينَ عَهْدٌ عِندَ اللَّهِ
"How can the polytheists have a covenant with God?" Rhetorical-Q. `covenant`, `sacred-geo` (ʿinda l-masjidi l-ḥarām). `moses-finding` tag by structural parallel. Al-Zamakhsharī reads this verse as the theological ground for treaty-obligation.

### Q 9:19 — أَجَعَلْتُمْ سِقَايَةَ الْحَاجِّ وَعِمَارَةَ الْمَسْجِدِ الْحَرَامِ
"Have you made providing water to the pilgrim and maintenance of the sacred mosque..." Rhetorical-Q equalizing pre-Islamic Qurayshī honors with faith-and-jihād. `hajj`, `sacred-geo`, `weapon`, `yawm`. Al-Wāḥidī: asbāb involves ʿAbbās's captivity at Badr and the boast about pilgrim-service.

### Q 9:24 — قُلْ إِن كَانَ آبَاؤُكُمْ وَأَبْنَاؤُكُمْ وَإِخْوَانُكُمْ
"Say: if your fathers, sons, brothers..." The eight-kinship-loyalties verse. `emotion`, `weapon`. Al-Rāzī enumerates: fathers, sons, brothers, spouses, kindred, wealth, commerce, dwellings — eight worldly attachments ranked against love of God and His Messenger. Long (170 letters).

### Q 9:28 — يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِنَّمَا الْمُشْرِكُونَ نَجَسٌ
"O you who believe, the polytheists are only impurity." Vocative, `innama`, `hajj`, `sacred-geo`. Al-Qurṭubī treats najas here as ritual not metaphysical (per the Mālikī reading) — Shāfiʿī reads it as metaphysical. `imperative-topic`: the following فَلَا يَقْرَبُوا. Rhyme-deviation on م.

### Q 9:52 — قُلْ هَلْ تَرَبَّصُونَ بِنَا إِلاَّ إِحْدَى الْحُسْنَيَيْنِ
"Say: do you await for us anything but one of the two best outcomes?" Qul-rhetorical-Q. Dual-form الحسنيين. Al-Zamakhsharī: the two best outcomes are martyrdom or victory — the structural dichotomy that governs the verse. Rhyme ن.

### Q 9:65 — وَلَئِن سَأَلْتَهُمْ لَيَقُولُنَّ إِنَّمَا كُنَّا نَخُوضُ وَنَلْعَبُ
"And if you ask them, they will surely say: we were only conversing and playing." `innama`, `muqattaat-gradient`, rhetorical-Q. Al-Wāḥidī's asbāb: mocking exchange during the Tabūk march. The verse establishes the principle that ridicule-of-religion invalidates nominal faith. Short-medium.

### Q 9:69 — كَالَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِكُمْ كَانُوا أَشَدَّ مِنكُمْ قُوَّةً
"Like those before you, they were stronger than you..." Comparative warning. `chiastic`, `convergence`, `prophet-micro-ring`, `prophet-pericope`, `maryam-finding` (structural). Al-Biqāʿī reads 9:69–70 as a ring around the prophetic-punishment typology. `ring-center`. Long, rhyme ن.

### Q 9:129 — فَإِن تَوَلَّوْا فَقُلْ حَسْبِيَ اللَّهُ
"If they turn away, say: God suffices me." The famous ultimate-tawba closing — the last verse of sūrat al-Tawba. `famous:ultimate-tawba`, `surah-closer`, `tawhid`. Two divine names (رَبُّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيمِ divine pair). Ibn Kathīr transmits the tradition that the Prophet recited this seven times would suffice. Medium. Note: rhyme-deviation on م closes the surah — which the atlas notes is rhetorically marked.

### Q 10:3 — إِنَّ رَبَّكُمُ اللَّهُ الَّذِي خَلَقَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالأَرْضَ فِي سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٍ
"Your Lord is God who created the heavens and earth in six days." `famous-number`, `gematria` (noted by the atlas; `rahman-finding` analogue), `muhkam-mutashabih`. Claims of "six days = six cosmological epochs" are **unsupported by the verse itself** — al-Ṭabarī, Ibn Kathīr take the Arabic ayyām as days (with noted discussion about their length pre-sun). Mashriqi abjad of ستة = 60+400+5 = 465. `ayat-al-kursi-finding` tag marks structural parallel to 2:255's throne-imagery.

### Q 10:5 — هُوَ الَّذِي جَعَلَ الشَّمْسَ ضِيَاءً وَالْقَمَرَ نُورًا
"He is the one who made the sun a shining-light and the moon a light." `cosmology-audit`, `fire-light`, `paired-opposite`, `chrono-critical`, `moses-finding` (as structural introduction). Claims of scientific foreknowledge distinguishing ḍiyāʾ as "emitted" light and nūr as "reflected" light are post-hoc; classical lexicography (al-Zamakhsharī's *Asās al-Balāgha*) distinguishes intensity not physical-mechanism. **Refutation flag for iʿjāz-ʿilmī over-reading.** Al-Rāzī's treatment is purely homiletic. Rhyme ن.

### Q 10:31 — قُلْ مَن يَرْزُقُكُم مِّنَ السَّمَاءِ وَالأَرْضِ
"Say: who provides for you from the sky and earth?" Qul-rhetorical-Q cascade. `muqattaat-gradient`. Al-Biqāʿī: this verse initiates sūrat Yūnus's tawḥīd-interrogation sequence (31, 34, 35, 38). Rhyme ن.

### Q 10:34 — قُلْ هَلْ مِن شُرَكَائِكُم مَّن يَبْدَأُ الْخَلْقَ ثُمَّ يُعِيدُهُ
"Say: is there any of your partners who originates creation and then restores it?" Qul-rhetorical-Q, `tawhid`. Al-Rāzī reads yabdaʾ and yuʿīd as the paired metaphysical functions that define the One God.

### Q 10:35 — قُلْ هَلْ مِن شُرَكَائِكُم مَّن يَهْدِي إِلَى الْحَقِّ
"Say: is there any of your partners who guides to truth?" Companion to 10:34. `form-content-outlier` — the repeating-question structure is formally unusual. `shirk`.

### Q 10:38 — أَمْ يَقُولُونَ افْتَرَاهُ
"Or do they say: he has fabricated it?" The taḥaddī (challenge) verse in Yūnus — the "produce one sūra like it" passage. `parable`, `mutashabih-lafzi` (parallel with 11:13, 2:23). `self-reference`, `shirk`. Al-Suyūṭī treats the three iʿjāz-challenge verses as a gradient (ten sūras, one sūra, anything-like-it).

### Q 10:59 — قُلْ أَرَأَيْتُم مَّا أَنزَلَ اللَّهُ لَكُم مِّن رِّزْقٍ
"Say: have you considered what God has sent down for you of provision?" Qul-rhetorical-Q; `intra-xref` with 5:87 and the ḥalāl/ḥarām authority question.

### Q 10:104 — قُلْ يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ إِن كُنتُمْ فِي شَكٍّ مِّن دِينِي
"Say: O people, if you are in doubt about my religion..." Vocative qul. `ethical-universalism` — yā ayyuhā l-nās addresses humanity universally. Al-Ṭabarī: this is the Meccan Prophet's definitive theological self-position.

### Q 10:108 — قُلْ يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ قَدْ جَاءَكُمُ الْحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّكُمْ
"Say: O people, truth has come to you from your Lord." `innama`, `ethical-universalism`, qul-opener. Al-Biqāʿī reads 10:104 and 10:108 as a ring around the dīn-address. Rhyme-deviation on ل.

### Q 11:14 — فَإِلَّمْ يَسْتَجِيبُوا لَكُمْ فَاعْلَمُوا أَنَّمَا أُنزِلَ بِعِلْمِ اللَّهِ
"If they do not respond to you, know that it has been sent down with God's knowledge." The taḥaddī-response verse in Hūd. `muhkam-mutashabih`, `hapax` (انّما أنزل construction here). Al-Rāzī: this verse formally closes the ten-sūra challenge of 11:13.

### Q 11:61 — وَإِلَىٰ ثَمُودَ أَخَاهُمْ صَالِحًا
"And to Thamūd, their brother Ṣāliḥ." Ṣāliḥ pericope opener. `muqattaat-gradient`, `mutashabih-topic`. Parallel with 7:73, 26:142, 27:45. `hapax` (اسْتَعْمَرَكُمْ). Al-Qurṭubī reads this as the earth-tenure theology: humans are God's settlers on earth, not owners.

### Q 11:62 — قَالُوا يَا صَالِحُ قَدْ كُنتَ فِينَا مَرْجُوًّا قَبْلَ هَٰذَا
"They said: O Ṣāliḥ, you were a hoped-for one among us before this." Reported speech. `iltifat-topic`, rhetorical-Q within. `form-content-outlier`. Al-Ṭabarī: Thamūd's disappointment is structurally echoed in Shuʿayb's (11:87).

### Q 11:78 — وَجَاءَهُ قَوْمُهُ يُهْرَعُونَ إِلَيْهِ
"His people came rushing to him." The Lot-narrative's tension moment. Prohibitive within the quoted speech. Al-Zamakhsharī reads yuhraʿūn as "driven as if by madness." Rhyme-deviation on د.

### Q 11:84 — وَإِلَىٰ مَدْيَنَ أَخَاهُمْ شُعَيْبًا
"And to Madyan, their brother Shuʿayb." Mid-Hūd Shuʿayb-pericope opener; compare 7:85. `mutashabih-topic`, `sacred-geo`, prohibitive (لاَ تَنقُصُوا). Rhyme-deviation on ط — unusual for the surah.

### Q 11:87 — قَالُوا يَا شُعَيْبُ أَصَلاَتُكَ تَأْمُرُكَ
"They said: O Shuʿayb, does your prayer command you...?" The divine-name-pair-ending on الْحَلِيمُ الرَّشِيدُ. Rhetorical-Q within quotation. Al-Rāzī reads the Madyanī mockery of prayer as paradigm for ritual-opposition. Rhyme-deviation on د.

### Q 12:5 — قَالَ يَا بُنَيَّ لاَ تَقْصُصْ رُؤْيَاكَ
"He said: O my little son, do not relate your vision..." The Yūsuf-Yaʿqūb dream-counsel. `iblis` tag is structural (shayṭān mentioned in the verse). `numerical-sequence` flags the eleven-brothers-plus-sun-and-moon count. Al-Ṭabarī: the prohibition is protective — the dream indicates ascendancy. Rhyme ن.

### Q 12:67 — وَقَالَ يَا بَنِيَّ لاَ تَدْخُلُوا مِن بَابٍ وَاحِدٍ
"He said: O my sons, do not enter through one gate..." Yaʿqūb's instructions. `palindrome` flag on internal sequence. `prohibitive`. Al-Biqāʿī reads this as the Qur'an's acknowledgment of legitimate secondary causes (taʾkhudh bi-l-asbāb).

### Q 12:87 — يَا بَنِيَّ اذْهَبُوا فَتَحَسَّسُوا مِن يُوسُفَ وَأَخِيهِ
"O my sons, go and inquire about Yūsuf and his brother." Yaʿqūb's late-narrative instruction. `emotion`, `hapax` (tahassasū). Prohibitive on وَلاَ تَيْأَسُوا مِن رَّوْحِ اللَّهِ — the Qur'an's famous "despair not" verse. Al-Qurṭubī makes this the locus of hope-theology.

### Q 12:88 — فَلَمَّا دَخَلُوا عَلَيْهِ قَالُوا يَا أَيُّهَا الْعَزِيزُ
"When they entered upon him, they said: O ʿAzīz..." Vocative. `historical-details`. Al-Ṭabarī glosses the brothers' poverty plea. The word biḍāʿa (merchandise) as hapax in this context. Rhyme ن.

### Q 12:100 — وَرَفَعَ أَبَوَيْهِ عَلَى الْعَرْشِ وَخَرُّوا لَهُ سُجَّدًا
"He raised his parents upon the throne, and they fell before him in prostration." The climax of the Yūsuf story. Divine-name-pair-ending (الْعَلِيمُ الْحَكِيمُ). `prophet-pericope`. Al-Rāzī devotes extensive discussion to the nature of the prostration (honor, not worship). Dual-form أَبَوَيْهِ. Long (169 letters).

### Q 13:28 — الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَتَطْمَئِنُّ قُلُوبُهُم بِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ
"Those who believe, whose hearts find rest in the remembrance of God." `balagha`, `classical-xref` (most-quoted Qur'anic verse on dhikr in Ṣūfī literature), `convergence`, `deep-hypothesis`, `qalb-theology`, `nafs-theology`. Al-Ghazālī builds large portions of *Iḥyāʾ* III on this verse. `form-content-outlier` — the verse's structural-lexical profile is distinct. Short-medium (52 letters). Mashriqi abjad of ذكر الله = 700+20+200+1+30+30+5 = 986.

### Q 14:1 — الر كِتَابٌ أَنزَلْنَاهُ إِلَيْكَ
"Alif Lām Rā — a Book We have sent down to you." Muqaṭṭaʿāt-opener. `muqatta-opener`, `divine-name-cluster` (الْعَزِيزِ الْحَمِيدِ divine pair closes), `saj-rhyme`, `fire-light` (min al-ẓulumāt ilā l-nūr). Al-Rāzī: the verse establishes the function-statement of scripture in one breath. Mashriqi abjad of الر = 1+30+200 = 231. `scripture-ref`.

### Q 14:21 — وَبَرَزُوا لِلَّهِ جَمِيعًا فَقَالَ الضُّعَفَاءُ لِلَّذِينَ اسْتَكْبَرُوا
"They will appear before God all together; the weak will say to those who were arrogant..." Eschatological dialogue. `paradise-hell`, `quotation-topic`. Al-Zamakhsharī reads the structural inversion of power: those who followed now speak first, those who led now disown. Rhyme-deviation on ص. Long.

### Q 14:22 — وَقَالَ الشَّيْطَانُ لَمَّا قُضِيَ الأَمْرُ
"Satan will say when the matter is decided..." The iblīs-speech verse. `iblis`, `covenant` inverted, `nafs-theology`. Al-Rāzī's treatment is canonical: Satan disowns his followers completely, revealing that his invitation had no ontological commitment. Long (187 letters). Prohibitive implicit.

### Q 16:36 — وَلَقَدْ بَعَثْنَا فِي كُلِّ أُمَّةٍ رَّسُولاً
"We raised in every community a messenger." The universal-messenger verse. `animal` tag fires on الطَّاغُوتَ idolatry context. `sacred-geo`, imperative of imperative (اعْبُدُوا اللَّهَ وَاجْتَنِبُوا الطَّاغُوتَ) as the core prophetic message. Al-Qurṭubī: every people had a rasūl — foundational text for universal-prophetology. Long.

### Q 16:51 — وَقَالَ اللَّهُ لاَ تَتَّخِذُوا إِلَٰهَيْنِ اثْنَيْنِ
"God said: do not take two gods..." Dual-form ilāhayn. `tawhid`, `innama`. Al-Rāzī: the prohibition against duotheism rather than simple polytheism is semantically precise — the target is Zoroastrian/Manichean dualism, not mere polytheism. Short (51 letters).

### Q 16:71 — وَاللَّهُ فَضَّلَ بَعْضَكُمْ عَلَىٰ بَعْضٍ فِي الرِّزْقِ
"God has preferred some of you over others in provision." Rhetorical-Q, `dual-form`, `contains-wa-allah`, `muqattaat`. Al-Biqāʿī: the verse founds the argument that worldly inequality is itself a divine test of both haves and have-nots.

### Q 16:75 — ضَرَبَ اللَّهُ مَثَلاً عَبْدًا مَّمْلُوكًا
"God sets a parable: a slave owned..." Slave/free parable. `parable`, `paired-opposite`, `shirk`. Al-Zamakhsharī reads the parable as pedagogical (comparing dependent shirk-object to independent God).

### Q 18:32 — وَاضْرِبْ لَهُم مَّثَلاً رَّجُلَيْنِ
"Set for them a parable of two men..." The two-gardens parable in al-Kahf. Dual-form throughout. `parable`, `kahf-structural`, `plant`. Al-Qurṭubī: this is one of the four exemplary parables of al-Kahf (cave, gardens, Moses, Dhū l-Qarnayn).

### Q 18:50 — وَإِذْ قُلْنَا لِلْمَلاَئِكَةِ اسْجُدُوا لآدَمَ
"When We said to the angels: prostrate to Adam." `iblis`, `angel`, `kahf-structural`, `kahf-finding`. Rhetorical-Q أَفَتَتَّخِذُونَهُ وَذُرِّيَّتَهُ أَوْلِيَاءَ. Al-Ṭabarī: this is the fourth re-telling of the iblīs refusal, each with a distinct pedagogical angle. Rhyme ا.

### Q 18:110 — قُلْ إِنَّمَا أَنَا بَشَرٌ مِّثْلُكُمْ
"Say: I am only a human being like you." The Prophet's ontological self-description. `innama`, `tawhid`, `kahf-structural`, `surah-closer`. Al-Rāzī treats this as balance against any angelolatry of the Prophet. Ibn Kathīr transmits multiple asbāb including its recitation upon arriving at Medina.

### Q 19:1 — كهيعص
"Kāf-Hā-Yā-ʿAyn-Ṣād." The longest muqaṭṭaʿāt sequence (five letters) opening Maryam. `muqatta-opener`, `maryam-nativity`, `razi-99names`, `word-pair`. Al-Rāzī offers multiple exegeses (acronym of divine names: Kāfī, Hādī, ʿĀlim, ʿAdl, Ṣādiq). Mashriqi abjad of كهيعص = 20+5+10+70+90 = 195. **The abjad value 195 has been claimed to encode various calendar or cosmological constants; audit shows these claims fail under even small rule-perturbations (e.g., switching to Maghribī abjad or including hamza).** Ultra-short. `deep-hypothesis`, `form-content-outlier` — the verse's ultra-short form-content is genuinely anomalous and structurally marked.

### Q 19:35 — مَا كَانَ لِلَّهِ أَن يَتَّخِذَ مِن وَلَدٍ سُبْحَانَهُ
"It is not for God to take a child; glory be to Him." `innama`, `maryam-nativity`, `maryam-jesus-pericope`, `prophet-pericope`. Al-Zamakhsharī: the verse explicitly closes the virgin-birth narrative of Maryam with tawḥīd-reassertion. Rhyme-deviation on ن (against surah's rhyme-variation pattern).

### Q 20:14 — إِنَّنِي أَنَا اللَّهُ لاَ إِلَٰهَ إِلاَّ أَنَا
"I, indeed I, am God — there is no god but I." The Moses-at-the-bush revelation's core. `muhkam-mutashabih`, `intra-xref` with 2:255, 3:2, 20:8. `tawhid`. Al-Ghazālī cites this as the locus of first-person-divine-speech in the Qur'an. Short (44 letters). Mashriqi abjad of إنني أنا الله = 1+50+50+10+1+50+1+1+30+30+5 = 229 (no-hamza rules).

### Q 20:98 — إِنَّمَا إِلَٰهُكُمُ اللَّهُ الَّذِي لاَ إِلَٰهَ إِلاَّ هُوَ
"Your God is only God; there is no god but He." Moses addressing Banū Isrāʾīl on the calf. `innama`, `tawhid`, `hashr-khawatim`, `muhkam-mutashabih`. Short (39 letters, 12 words). Al-Rāzī links the threefold divine-name structure here to the tawḥīd-architecture of the Qur'an as a whole.

### Q 22:1 — يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ اتَّقُوا رَبَّكُمْ
"O people, fear your Lord." Sūrat al-Ḥajj's opener. `ethical-universalism`, `hajj`, `vocative`, `palindrome`. Short (40 letters). Al-Qurṭubī: the yā ayyuhā l-nās address frames the entire surah as universal-address. Rhyme-deviation on م.

### Q 22:5 — يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ إِن كُنتُمْ فِي رَيْبٍ مِّنَ الْبَعْثِ
"O people, if you are in doubt about the resurrection..." The long embryology verse. `embryology-audit`, `scientific-foreknowledge`. **Refutation flag for iʿjāz-ʿilmī over-reading of nuṭfa-ʿalaqa-muḍgha as 1950s-style stage-embryology: the terms are classical Arabic substance-words (drop, clot, chewed-morsel) with long pre-Islamic usage. The verse's rhetorical point is resurrection-by-analogy, not microscopic biology.** Al-Ṭabarī's exegesis is homiletic. Long (262 letters). `ethical-universalism`, vocative, rhyme-deviation on ج.

### Q 22:18 — أَلَمْ تَرَ أَنَّ اللَّهَ يَسْجُدُ لَهُ مَن فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ
"Do you not see that everything in the heavens prostrates to God?" Rhetorical-Q. `animal`, `plant`, `salah`. Al-Rāzī reads the cosmic-prostration as ontological orientation, not literal bowing. Long (144 letters). Rhyme-deviation on ء (hamza).

### Q 22:49 — قُلْ يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ إِنَّمَا أَنَا لَكُمْ نَذِيرٌ مُّبِينٌ
"Say: O people, I am only a clear warner to you." Qul-vocative. `innama`, `ethical-universalism`. Short (31 letters). Rhyme-deviation on ن. Al-Biqāʿī reads this verse as the Prophet's universal-epistemic-stance.

### Q 22:72 — وَإِذَا تُتْلَىٰ عَلَيْهِمْ آيَاتُنَا بَيِّنَاتٍ
"When Our clear signs are recited to them..." Rhetorical-Q embedded. Qul-threat closure. `hapax` on يَسْطُونَ ("they would assault"). Al-Ṭabarī: the verse exposes the disbelievers' visible hostility. Long.

### Q 22:73 — يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ ضُرِبَ مَثَلٌ فَاسْتَمِعُوا لَهُ
"O people, a parable has been set, so listen to it." The fly-parable (inna lladhīna tadʿūna min dūni llāhi lan yakhluqū dhubāban). `parable`, `animal`, `shirk`, `ethical-universalism`. Al-Rāzī treats this as the Qur'an's sharpest anti-idolatry argument: even the fly's creation is beyond false-god capacity. Long (122 letters), rhyme-deviation on ب.

### Q 22:78 — وَجَاهِدُوا فِي اللَّهِ حَقَّ جِهَادِهِ
"Strive in God as is His due striving." Al-Ḥajj's closer — the longest among early-Medinan surah-closers. `hajj`, `salah`, `zakat`, `weapon`, `fatiha-finding` (structural echo with 1:5 servanthood). The millat Ibrāhīm formula and the ḥunafāʾ designation. Al-Qurṭubī: this verse collects hajj-root vocabulary in three distinct senses (root j-h-d vs root ḥ-j-j). Long (199 letters).

### Q 23:23 — وَلَقَدْ أَرْسَلْنَا نُوحًا إِلَىٰ قَوْمِهِ
"We indeed sent Nūḥ to his people." Prophetic-chain opener in al-Muʾminūn. Parallel with 7:59, 11:25, 71:1. `mutashabih-topic`, `intra-xref`. Rhetorical-Q أَفَلاَ تَتَّقُونَ. Al-Biqāʿī marks this as the typical opener for a prophet-rhythm surah. Medium (63 letters). Rhyme ن.

### Q 24:2 — الزَّانِيَةُ وَالزَّانِي فَاجْلِدُوا كُلَّ وَاحِدٍ مِّنْهُمَا
"The adulteress and the adulterer, flog each of them..." The ḥadd verse. `muhkam-mutashabih`, `naskh` — Mālik's position on its interaction with a claimed earlier ruling. `chrono-critical`, `yawm`. Dual-form throughout. Al-Qurṭubī devotes lengthy treatment. `numerical-coincidence`: 100 (lashes). Long (122 letters). Rhyme ن.

### Q 27:59 — قُلِ الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ وَسَلاَمٌ عَلَىٰ عِبَادِهِ الَّذِينَ اصْطَفَىٰ
"Say: praise belongs to God and peace on His servants whom He has chosen." `balagha`, rhetorical-Q following. `iltifat-topic`. Al-Rāzī treats this as the hinge between prophet-stories and tawḥīd-interrogations in al-Naml. Short (49 letters).

### Q 27:64 — أَمَّن يَبْدَأُ الْخَلْقَ ثُمَّ يُعِيدُهُ
"Is He who originates creation and then restores it...?" Part of the five-fold "am-man" cascade in al-Naml (27:60–64). `tawhid`. Al-Biqāʿī reads this rhetorical sequence as the Qur'an's most architectonic cosmological-argument passage. Medium (78 letters).

### Q 28:38 — وَقَالَ فِرْعَوْنُ يَا أَيُّهَا الْمَلأُ
"Pharaoh said: O chiefs..." Pharaoh's speech claiming divinity and ordering Hāmān to build the tower. `historical-details`, `mutashabih-topic`, `intra-xref` with 40:36. Al-Ṭabarī's treatment of the Hāmān figure (whom some orientalists wrongly claim anachronistic) notes classical commentators identified Hāmān as a ministerial title — the verse is not biblically-anachronistic but independent. Long.

### Q 33:1 — يَا أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ اتَّقِ اللَّهَ
"O Prophet, fear God..." Sūrat al-Aḥzāb's opener. Vocative addressed specifically to the Prophet. `cross-textual`, `intra-xref` with 66:1. Prohibitive لاَ تُطِعِ الْكَافِرِينَ. Al-Qurṭubī: asbāb relates to the pre-Uḥud diplomatic pressures. Medium (61 letters), rhyme ا.

### Q 33:33 — وَقَرْنَ فِي بُيُوتِكُنَّ وَلاَ تَبَرَّجْنَ
"Remain in your houses, and do not display yourselves..." The ahl al-bayt verse. `hapax` on tabarrajna in this form. `innama` in innamā yurīdu llāh (the taṭhīr clause). `salah`, `zakat`. Al-Ṭabarī transmits the ḥadīth of the cloak (Kisāʾ) as the asbāb for the purification-clause. Long (122 letters). Rhyme ا.

### Q 33:59 — يَا أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ قُل لأَزْوَاجِكَ وَبَنَاتِكَ
"O Prophet, say to your wives and daughters..." The ḥijāb-extension verse. `hapax` (yudnīna). Vocative + qul. Al-Rāzī: the rationale is "so that they may be recognized and not troubled." `intra-xref` with 33:53, 24:31.

### Q 33:63 — يَسْأَلُكَ النَّاسُ عَنِ السَّاعَةِ
"People ask you about the Hour." `muhkam-mutashabih`, `palindrome`, rhetorical-Q. Al-Suyūṭī: the Qur'an's refusal to specify the Hour's time is structural throughout (7:187, 33:63, 79:42).

### Q 35:15 — يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ أَنتُمُ الْفُقَرَاءُ إِلَى اللَّهِ
"O people, you are the ones poor before God." `divine-name-cluster` (الْغَنِيُّ الْحَمِيدُ divine-name-pair closes), `divine-name-pair-ending`, `ethical-universalism`. Short (47 letters). Al-Ghazālī builds his doctrine of faqr (poverty before God) on this verse. Rhyme-deviation on د.

### Q 39:10 — قُلْ يَا عِبَادِ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا اتَّقُوا رَبَّكُمْ
"Say: O My servants who have believed, fear your Lord..." Qul-vocative. `innama`, `balagha`. The famous "wide earth" clause (إِنَّ أَرْضَ اللَّهِ وَاسِعَةٌ) — Al-Qurṭubī reads this as the hijra-justification verse. Rhyme-deviation on ب.

### Q 39:38 — وَلَئِن سَأَلْتَهُم مَّنْ خَلَقَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالأَرْضَ
"If you ask them who created the heavens and earth..." Rhetorical-Q of anticipatory reply. Qul-opener for the rejoinder. `intra-xref` with 29:61, 31:25, 43:9 — the atlas flags the quadruplet as a structurally-repeated "they-will-say-God" formula. Al-Biqāʿī: the Qur'an's empirical argument is that even polytheists admit God's creation, exposing internal contradiction. Long.

### Q 41:6 — قُلْ إِنَّمَا أَنَا بَشَرٌ مِّثْلُكُمْ
"Say: I am only a human being like you." Parallel with 18:110. `innama`, `chrono-critical`, `gematria`-adjacent, `tawhid`. Al-Zamakhsharī reads the two Meccan "I-am-only-human" statements as distinct rhetorical moments — here in Fuṣṣilat it introduces the istiqāma (uprightness) command, not the encounter command.

### Q 46:8 — أَمْ يَقُولُونَ افْتَرَاهُ قُلْ إِنِ افْتَرَيْتُهُ
"Or do they say: he has fabricated it? Say: if I fabricated it..." The iftirāʾ-rejection verse. `divine-name-cluster` (two pairs), `cross-textual`. Al-Rāzī: the Prophet's hypothetical iftirāʾ is rhetorically exposed — God would have protected no fabricator.

### Q 46:10 — قُلْ أَرَأَيْتُمْ إِن كَانَ مِنْ عِندِ اللَّهِ وَكَفَرْتُم بِهِ
"Say: have you considered, if it is from God and you disbelieved in it...?" Qul-rhetorical-Q. `parable` (shāhidun min banī isrāʾīla). Al-Ṭabarī relates the witness traditionally to ʿAbdallāh b. Salām, though al-Qurṭubī reads it generically. Rhyme ن.

### Q 61:6 — وَإِذْ قَالَ عِيسَى ابْنُ مَرْيَمَ يَا بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ

### Q 67:3 — الَّذِي خَلَقَ سَبْعَ سَمَاوَاتٍ طِبَاقًا

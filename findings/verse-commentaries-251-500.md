Continuation of the verse-signature atlas walk. Ranks 251–500 by `tags_count` descending from `verse-signature-atlas.csv`, presented here sorted by surah:verse ascending. The top-100 lives at `verse-commentaries-top100.md`; ranks 101–250 in a sibling file. ### Q 2:5
"Those — upon guidance from their Lord, and those are the successful."
Medinan, Nöldeke IV. Short rhyme-ن verse near the opening of al-Baqara, pos-early, tagged `form-content-outlier` because it closes a tiny 3-verse arc (2:3–5) with a compact abstract summary resembling Meccan saj‘. Al-Biqāʿī reads it as the hinge between believer-pericope (2:2–5) and kāfir-pericope (2:6–7).
Computation: muflihūn (م-ف-ل-ح-و-ن) = 40+80+30+8+6+50 = 214; compare fajr (ف-ج-ر) = 80+3+200 = 283. Tuple: (Mashriqi, consonantal, no shadda).
Echo: same muflihūn closure at 31:5, 59:9 — defines a three-point ring.

### Q 2:6
"Truly those who disbelieved — equal upon them whether you warned them or did not warn — they do not believe."
Medinan, Nöldeke IV. Paired-opposite to 2:5 (tagged `chiastic`, `ring-center`, `paired-opposite`), enclosing a micro-ring where 2:6 mirrors 2:5's fortunate with an unfortunate inversion. Al-Zamakhshārī notes the sawāʾ ʿalayhim balance-term is itself a chiastic signal.
Computation: sawāʾun (س-و-ا-ء) = 60+6+1+1 = 68 (rules: Mashriqi, no hamza-collapse).
Echo: sawāʾ-formula reused at 63:6, forming a hypocrite/kāfir pair.

### Q 2:9
"They seek to deceive God and those who believe; they deceive only themselves and perceive not."
Medinan, Nöldeke IV, pos-early, within the munāfiqūn block. Tagged `chrono-critical` and `divine-names-topic` — the verse introduces the strange reflexive yukhādiʿūn/yukhādiʿūn jinās central to al-Rāzī's discussion of divine "deception" language.
Computation: yashʿurūn (ي-ش-ع-ر-و-ن) = 10+300+70+200+6+50 = 636; rhyme-ن closure.
Echo: identical yashʿurūn rhyme at 2:12, 16:26 — a munāfiqūn signature cadence.

### Q 2:13
"When it is said to them 'Believe as the people have believed,' they say 'Shall we believe as the fools believed?' Indeed they are the fools but know not."
Medinan, pos-early. Rhetorical-question framing embedded in a quotation-within-quotation (tagged `cross-textual`, `form-content-outlier`). Al-Ṭabarī flags the inversion: the sufahāʾ accusation is turned back.
Computation: sufahāʾ (س-ف-ه-ا-ء) = 60+80+5+1+1 = 147.
Echo: paired with 2:142 where sufahāʾ are those who object to the qibla turn; same term, opposed speakers.

### Q 2:21
"O mankind, worship your Lord who created you and those before you, that you may become god-wary."
Medinan, pos-early. First `yā-ayyuhā al-nās` imperative of the muṣḥaf (tagged `imperative-topic`, `ethical-universalism`, `prophet-pericope`). Al-Qurṭubī notes this as the first ʿibāda-command of the text-as-read-sequence.
Computation: khalaqakum (خ-ل-ق-ك-م) = 600+30+100+20+40 = 790; tataqqūn rhyme closes with the Nöldeke IV ن cadence.
Echo: phrase recurs at 4:1 (another yā-ayyuhā al-nās opener), forming Medinan universal-address pair.

### Q 2:28
"How can you disbelieve in God while you were dead and He gave you life, then He will cause you to die, then give you life, then to Him you shall return?"
Medinan, pos-early. Rhetorical-Q interrogating the whole cosmic arc; tagged `kahf-finding`, `moses-finding`, `prophet-micro-ring` for its tight 4-verb palindromic life-death-life-death pattern. Al-Rāzī: rhetorical question here is a taqrīr (establishing proof), not genuine inquiry.
Computation: mayta → yuḥyī arc counts 4 state-verbs; rhyme-ن (turjaʿūn).
Echo: mirror at 22:66, 30:40, 40:11 — each a 4-state chain on the same template.

### Q 2:36
"Satan made them slip from it and expelled them from what they had been in."
Medinan, pos-early. First appearance of iblīs/shayṭān as grammatical subject in the muṣḥaf-order (tagged `iblis`, `numerical-coincidence`). Al-Ṭabarī reads azallahumā as double causation — the dual form preserves the Adam/Hawwāʾ pairing (tagged `dual-form`).
Computation: shayṭān (ش-ي-ط-ا-ن) = 300+10+9+1+50 = 370; noteworthy since 370 is 10×37 and 37 is the 12th prime.
Echo: the "ihbiṭū" command echoes verbatim at 2:38 and 7:24 — the fall-motif rhyme-ن chain.

### Q 2:69
"Say, 'Call on your Lord for us to make clear to us what its color is.' He said, 'Indeed He says it is a yellow cow, bright in color, pleasing the viewers.'"
Medinan, pos-early, within the Baqara pericope that names the surah. Tagged `color`, `graph-theory` for the color/shade cluster. Al-Biqāʿī notes faqiʿ lawn (hapax) intensifies ṣafrāʾ to near-saturation.
Computation: baqara (ب-ق-ر) = 2+100+200 = 302; ṣafrāʾ (ص-ف-ر-ا-ء) = 90+80+200+1+1 = 372.
Echo: cow-color dialogue is the signature inter-pericope link to Saul's/Ṭālūt's cow-narrative in 2:247ff.

### Q 2:77
"Do they not know that God knows what they hide and what they make known?"
Medinan. Very short rhetorical-Q with divine-name topical resonance; tagged `muqattaat`, `numerical-coincidence`. Al-Zamakhsharī treats yaʿlamūn/yuʿlinūn as jinās taḥrīfī (letter-flip paronomasia).
Computation: yaʿlamūn (ي-ع-ل-م-و-ن) = 10+70+30+40+6+50 = 206; yuʿlinūn (ي-ع-ل-ن-و-ن) = 10+70+30+50+6+50 = 216. Δ=10 = value of يـ, sole letter-swap. Tuple: Mashriqi, consonantal.
Echo: same pair at 2:33 and 36:76.

### Q 2:80

Medinan, pos-middle. Quotation-within-rhetorical-Q with `qul-say-opener`, challenging the Jewish limited-hellfire claim. Al-Ṭabarī identifies the "ayyām maʿdūda" as the seven days of the calf-worship.
Computation: maʿdūda (م-ع-د-و-د-ة) = 40+70+4+6+4+5 = 129 (with tāʾ marbūṭa as ه).
Echo: ayyām maʿdūda reoccurs at 2:184 (fasting) — same diction inverted from punishment to worship.

### Q 2:85
"Do you then believe in part of the Scripture and disbelieve in part?"
Medinan, pos-middle, long. Rhetorical-Q at the rhetorical heart of the Banū Isrāʾīl rebuke (tagged `covenant`, `hapax`). Al-Rāzī: tabʿīḍ al-īmān (partial belief) is the diagnostic signature of hypocrisy.

### Q 2:93
"And say, 'We hear and we disobey' — and they were made to drink the calf in their hearts for their disbelief."
Medinan, pos-middle, long. Tagged `muqattaat` and `loan-word` (rafaʿnā fawqakum aṭ-ṭūr uses the Syriac-Aramaic Ṭūr). Classical: Ibn Kathīr reads shuribū fī qulūbihim as a metaphorical "drinking" — the calf-image internalized.
Computation: ʿijl (ع-ج-ل) = 70+3+30 = 103. Tuple: Mashriqi, no fatha, consonantal.
Echo: samiʿnā wa-ʿaṣaynā paired with samiʿnā wa-aṭaʿnā at 2:285 — the full covenantal antithesis.

### Q 2:106
"Whatever verse We abrogate or cause to be forgotten, We bring a better one or its like."
Medinan, pos-middle. Locus classicus for `naskh`; tagged `parable`. Al-Suyūṭī's al-Itqān quotes this as the definitional verse of abrogation, though it can also refer to abrogation of prior scriptures, not intra-Quranic.
Computation: āya (ا-ي-ة) = 1+10+5 = 16; mithlihā (م-ث-ل-ه-ا) = 40+500+30+5+1 = 576. Ratio 576/16 = 36.
Echo: abrogation vocabulary concentrates in 2:106, 13:39, 16:101, 87:6-7 — a 4-point intra-text.

### Q 2:107
"Do you not know that to God belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth?"
Medinan, pos-middle. Rhetorical-Q on sovereignty. Tagged `mutashabih-lafzi` because the mulk al-samāwāt wa-l-arḍ phrase is a multiply-recurring lexical matrix (al-Kirmānī counted 18 near-identical iterations).
Computation: mulk (م-ل-ك) = 40+30+20 = 90.
Echo: formula repeats at 3:189, 5:17, 5:18, 5:40, 5:120, 24:42, 42:49, 45:27, 48:14, 57:2, 57:5, 85:9 — 12-point lattice.

### Q 2:125

### Q 2:127
"And when Abraham raised the foundations of the House and Ishmael: 'Our Lord, accept from us; You are the Hearing, the Knowing.'"
Medinan, pos-middle. Classic `ends-divine-pair` on samīʿ-ʿalīm. Al-Biqāʿī reads the dual subject (Ibrāhīm + Ismāʿīl) as a structural mirror of Adam/Hawwāʾ at 2:35.

### Q 2:128
"Our Lord, make us submitting to You, and of our offspring a nation submitting to You."
Medinan, pos-middle, `dual-form`, `ends-divine-pair` (tawwāb-raḥīm), `sacrifice-vow`. The duʿāʾ's "ummatan muslimatan laka" is a hapax-phrase (tagged `hapax`).

### Q 2:131
"When his Lord said to him 'Submit,' he said 'I submit to the Lord of the worlds.'"
Medinan, pos-middle. Tagged `balagha`, `prophet-micro-ring`, `convergence`. Al-Rāzī notes the aslim/aslamtu matching pair is a perfect lafẓ-maʿnā echo (form matches content of submission).
Computation: aslim (ا-س-ل-م) = 1+60+30+40 = 131 — coincides exactly with verse number 131. Tuple: Mashriqi, consonantal. One of the few verse-index/gematria alignments worth flagging.

### Q 2:136
"Say: We believe in God and what was sent down to us and what was sent down to Abraham…"
Medinan, pos-middle, long. Anchor-verse of the `baqarah-ibrahim-qibla` ring (see phase-c ring-structures). Tagged `mutashabih-lafzi`, `jesus-engagement` — the prophet-name sequence is deliberately parallel to 3:84.
Computation: prophets listed: Ibrāhīm, Ismāʿīl, Isḥāq, Yaʿqūb, al-asbāṭ, Mūsā, ʿĪsā = 7 name-slots.
Echo: near-duplicate at 3:84 with identical 7-slot roster — refuted `divine-author-signature-claim`: the repetition is rhetorical parallelism, not a numerical miracle.

### Q 2:138
"The dye of God — and who is better than God in dyeing? And we are worshippers of Him."
Medinan, short. `rhetorical-Q-topic`, `elative`. Al-Zamakhshārī: ṣibghat Allāh polemicizes baptismal dye-rituals of Christians; the metaphor inverts the rite.
Computation: ṣibghat Allāh (ص-ب-غ-ة + ا-ل-ل-ه) = 998 + 66 = 1064. ṣibgha alone = 993, very close to 1000.
Echo: ṣibgha is a hapax — single attestation in the entire muṣḥaf.

### Q 2:149
"And from wherever you go out, turn your face toward the Sacred Mosque."
Medinan, pos-middle. Qibla-injunction; `hashr-khawatim` structure because the verse closes with an awareness-formula (wa-mā Allāh bi-ghāfilin ʿammā taʿmalūn), signature of the late-Medinan legislative style. Al-Ṭabarī: this is the first of three parallel qibla-commands (149, 150 × 2).
Computation: shaṭr (ش-ط-ر) = 300+9+200 = 509.
Echo: identical closing formula at 2:140, 2:144, 3:99 — a 4-point closure ring.

### Q 2:170

### Q 2:173
"He has forbidden you only carrion, blood, swine, and what is dedicated to other than God."
Medinan, pos-middle. `embryology-audit` is a misapplied tag here — the verse concerns dietary law. The `innama` hasr restricts the prohibition list to 4 items (tagged `innama-topic`).
Computation: 4 prohibitions enumerated. mayta+dam+khinzīr+mā-uhilla = 4 categories.
Echo: same 4-item list at 5:3, 6:145, 16:115 — the canonical prohibition quadruple.

### Q 2:177
"Piety is not that you turn your faces toward East or West, but piety is who believes in God…"
Medinan, pos-middle, long. Locus classicus of `muhkam-mutashabih` debates; Ibn Kathīr and al-Rāzī both identify this as a complete theological definition in one verse. Tagged `dual-form` (mashriq/maghrib).
Computation: birr (ب-ر) = 2+200 = 202; mashriq+maghrib (م-ش-ر-ق + م-غ-ر-ب) = 640 + 1242 = 1882.
Echo: ontological definitions of birr also at 3:92 and 2:189 (mutashābih redirect).

### Q 2:185

### Q 2:189
"They ask you about the new moons. Say: they are timekeeping for mankind and the pilgrimage."
Medinan, pos-middle. `asbab-nuzul` tied to a reported question about hilāl phases; `qul-say-opener`. Al-Suyūṭī notes this among the 15 yasʾalūnaka sequences (structural 15-gate motif of al-Baqara).

### Q 2:197
"The Hajj is well-known months — whoever undertakes it, no rafath, fusūq, or jidāl in the Hajj."
Medinan, pos-middle, long. Hajj injunction. `balagha` tag points to triple negation (rafath, fusūq, jidāl) — a classical example of ṭibāq and balāgha.
Computation: rafath (ر-ف-ث) = 200+80+500 = 780; fusūq (ف-س-و-ق) = 80+60+6+100 = 246; jidāl (ج-د-ا-ل) = 3+4+1+30 = 38.
Echo: triple-prohibition clause unique to this verse; no structural rhyme.

### Q 2:200
"When you have completed your rites, remember God as you remember your forefathers, or more fervently."
Medinan, pos-middle. `iltifat-topic`, `elative`, `sacrifice-vow`. Rhyme-ق is unusual in al-Baqara. Al-Zamakhshārī: the ashadd-dhikran phrase is a gradation (tafḍīl ṣarīḥ) of memorial practice.
Computation: ashadd (ا-ش-د) = 1+300+4 = 305.
Echo: dhikr-elevation also at 62:10 and 33:41 — the "aktharū/athkurū" cadence.

### Q 2:220

Medinan, pos-late. `asbab-nuzul`: reported to ease an earlier strict separation rule. `qul-say-opener`. Ibn Kathīr: revealed to replace the austere 4:10 reading with pragmatic guardianship.

### Q 2:222

### Q 2:233
"Mothers shall suckle their children two full years, for whoever wishes to complete the nursing."
Medinan, pos-late, very long. `famous-number` (hawlayni kāmilayni) anchors a classical-era biomedical gloss. `dual-form`. Al-Rāzī and al-Qurṭubī both derive a 30-month gestation+nursing cap from this verse combined with 46:15.
Computation: hawlayn (ح-و-ل-ي-ن) = 8+6+30+10+50 = 104 (dual of 52 = hawl).
Echo: with 46:15 (30 months combined), this verse produces the 30−24 = 6-month minimum gestation rule.

### Q 2:243

### Q 2:245

### Q 2:247

Medinan, pos-late, long. `cosmology-audit` (wasaʿa ʿilman wa-jisman). Narrative of Saul's appointment. Al-Biqāʿī: the objection "naḥnu aḥaqqu bi-l-mulki" is the kernel of political-theological contention.

### Q 2:249

### Q 2:256

### Q 2:258

### Q 2:261

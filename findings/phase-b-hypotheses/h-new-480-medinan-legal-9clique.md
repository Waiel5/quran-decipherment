## 3. Classical-tradition reconciliation — *al-sabʿ al-ṭiwāl* inner-structure

**al-Zarkashī** *al-Burhān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān* ch. 2 describes the ***sabʿ al-ṭiwāl*** (seven long surahs) as Q 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, + al-Anfāl/al-Tawbah or Yūnus.

[[h-new-480-medinan-legal-9clique|H-NEW-480]]'s inner-4 {Q 2, 3, 4, 5} is precisely the first FOUR of the classical ṭiwāl-list — the UNAMBIGUOUS long-Medinan-legal subset (Q 6, 7 are Meccan-register; the 7th slot is disputed between al-Anfāl and Yūnus).

**Empirical validation of classical inner-grouping**: the unambiguous-Medinan-long-legal Q 2-3-4-5 DOES form a corpus-extreme-cohesive 4-clique. al-Zarkashī's grouping is empirically valid at whole-surah FR proximity.

The EXTENDED 9-set I proposed from H-460's top-5-nearest analysis is **overextension beyond the classical ṭiwāl inner-core**:
- Q 24, Q 33 are "ṭiwāl-adjacent" (Medinan-legal, long but not in the strict ṭiwāl-7).
- Q 48, Q 49, Q 64 are Medinan-legal-short — closer to mufaṣṣal class than ṭiwāl.

The H-460 top-5 nearest-neighbor analysis captured **the full Medinan social-legal register** (including both ṭiwāl and adjacent-shorter surahs); the strict cluster at corpus-extreme level is **ṭiwāl-specific**.

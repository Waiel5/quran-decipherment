- **Surahs with N verses where N is a common verse count**: 5 surahs have 11 verses, 5 have 30 verses. Among 114 surahs with verse counts ranging 3-286, several Ns will collide with famous numbers by pigeonhole.

- **Verses at absolute position N for famous N**: Picking verse #19 or #100 of the mushaf and reading meaning into its content is the McKay 'thousand small surahs' problem. The harness reports them but they should be treated as null.
- **Lemma counts equal to small N**: Approximately 1994 lemmas have count exactly 1 in the QAC. Any small target N will have dozens of unrelated lemmas matching it. The question is *which* lemma — and that's where forking-paths bites.
- **Famous claim: Allah lemma = 2698 (Khalifa)**: Our QAC count is 2699, which is 2698+1 in the canonical Hafs text (Khalifa rejected 9:128-129 to get the 19-multiple). This is a known artifact, well-documented in Bilal Philips 1987.
- **Famous claim: yawm = 365 (solar year)**: Our QAC count for `yawom` lemma = 405, ≠ 365. The literature claim only works under one specific surface-form filter that the claimant does not disclose.
- **Famous claim: qul == qala**: under QAC morphology, all-imperative forms = 349, all-perfect forms = 1004. The claim is also stated as 'surface form قل = surface form قال = 332'. Surface count of bare قل = 294 (or 333 if you add وقل + فقل); bare قال = 416 (or 528 with وقال + فقال). Neither pairing gives a clean equality. The claim depends on a specific surface-token filter the literature does not always disclose.
- **Famous claim: hayat == mawt = 145**: `Hayaw`p` (life) = 76, `mawot` (death) = 50. NEITHER matches 145 in the QAC lemma count. Refuted under our rules tuple. Note: the claim works under a specific surface-form aggregation by Abd al-Daim al-Kaheel; we will replicate that exact protocol in a Phase A target if it's specified.

## Methodology note

- Source: `quran-no-tashkeel.json` (the *only* intact flat text per `text-shape` audit), QAC morphology v0.4.
- Letter count uses graphemes U+0621..064A ∪ U+0671..06D3, excluding recitation marks.
- Word count uses orthographic-token whitespace tokenization, filtering recitation-mark-only tokens.
- Basmala counted only as Surah 1 verse 1 (the 113 opening basmalas of other surahs are NOT separately counted).
- Abjad: mashriqi convention from `methodology.md §6`.
- Lemmas: Buckwalter LEM tag from QAC v0.4. Note that QAC uses verb root + canonical lemma, so e.g. 'qul' (imperative) and 'qaAla' (perfect) share LEM:qaAla; we filter by morphological FEATURES.
- This dossier is **exploratory**, not pre-registered. Items here cannot be cited as findings until they pass the §3 statistical-rigor protocol with two nulls.

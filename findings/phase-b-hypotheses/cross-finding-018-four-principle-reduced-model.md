### [[h-new-150-liturgical-hub|H-NEW-150]]'s length-residualization finding

[[h-new-150-liturgical-hub|H-NEW-150]] tested: does liturgical-prominence score (locked in
pre-reg from classical ḥadīth + daily-prayer-manual sources)

- **Raw Spearman ρ = 0.312**, perm p = 0.0002 (PASS at α_bon=0.025)

- MW-5 positive control: liturgical score is 17× stronger
  correlate than chronology rank (ρ_lit = 0.312 vs ρ_chrono = 0.018)

The MECHANISM of cluster-network hub-ness is NOT intrinsic
liturgical prominence; it is LENGTH-EXTREMITY. Liturgically-
prominent surahs happen to sit at length extremes:

- Very long (Q 2 al-Baqara 286v; Q 3 Āl ʿImrān 200v; both are
  obligatory raka openings in classical prayer manuals)
- Very short (Q 112 al-Ikhlāṣ 4v; Q 113 al-Falaq 5v; Q 114
  al-Nās 6v; the classical refuge-triad recited in every prayer)

Length-extremity is captured by M1's Hamiltonian-cycle structure
(front-of-ring / back-of-ring are where length-extremes
concentrate) and by M5 (length stratification). The M4-to-M1
absorption is empirically supported: the 4-region hub geography
(front Q 2-3, mid Q 50, back-upper Q 59-62, back-terminal Q 112-

simplistic "isolated surahs are everywhere-distant" reading of

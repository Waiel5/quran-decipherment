## Headline

**Q 56 → Q 57 (al-Wāqiʿah → al-Ḥadīd) is the rank-1-of-113 root-level bridge** in the Quran, with cos-overlap 0.408 and TWO shared QAC-STEM roots: **sbH (سبح, glorify)** and **smw (سمو, heavens/elevated)**. The classical tasbīḥ-echo observed in [[h-new-142-universal-hinges-chrono-rhetorical|H-NEW-142]] is ROOT-LEVEL CONFIRMED.

BUT the other two universal hinges (Q 14→15 and Q 49→50) have ZERO shared roots (cos=0, below P50). Their [[h-new-142-universal-hinges-chrono-rhetorical|H-NEW-142]] "rhetorical bridges" are SEMANTIC/THEMATIC only, not root-level.

Primary Mann-Whitney test (top-15 union vs other 98): p = 0.44, NULL. Per-feature sub-tests (root and char-4-gram): direction correct (top-15 > other by ~2×) but UNDERPOWERED (p=0.17). No test passes Bonferroni-3.

**Net interpretation**: [[h-new-142-universal-hinges-chrono-rhetorical|H-NEW-142]]'s rhetorical-bridge claim is TRUE for Q 56→57 (root-level confirmed), SEMANTIC-ONLY for Q 14→15 and Q 49→50. The overall mushaf does NOT systematically place FR-top-15 jumps at root-bridge boundaries — only the Q 56→57 case is a root-bridge. [[h-new-142-universal-hinges-chrono-rhetorical|H-NEW-142]]'s "4th classical-scholarship validation" claim is downgraded from 3 hinges to 1 hinge at root-level.

## Interpretation

### Q 56 → Q 57 is the classical-munāsabāt exemplar — confirmed

Q 56 al-Wāqiʿah closes with فسبح باسم ربك العظيم (so GLORIFY by the name of your mighty Lord). Q 57 al-Ḥadīd opens with سبح لله ما في السماوات والأرض (GLORIFIES to Allah whatever is in the heavens and earth). The sbH root repeats; the smw (heavens) root is also shared (Q 57:1 "السماوات").

This bridge is not only thematic — it is ROOT-LEVEL with the strongest bridge-score of any 113 boundaries. Classical al-Biqāʿī munāsabāt has a rigorous empirical backing here.

### Q 14 → Q 15 and Q 49 → Q 50 have NO root-bridges

My close-reading in [[h-new-142-universal-hinges-chrono-rhetorical|H-NEW-142]] saw thematic echoes (بلاغ→آيات الكتاب; غيب→قرآن) but these are SEMANTIC bridges built from different roots. Zero QAC-STEM root is shared. These are classical al-Biqāʿī munāsabāt at the SEMANTIC/CONCEPTUAL level, not the morphological level.

### So is the finding refuted?

**The pre-registered inferential claim (primary + universal ≥2/3) is NULL.** The mushaf does not systematically place FR-top-15 jumps at root-bridges. Only 1 of 3 universal hinges is a root-bridge, and even that one is a single-data-point observation (rank-1 is just one boundary).

The per-feature directional-positive effects (p=0.17) for root and char-4-gram top-15 are CONSISTENT with a small true effect that our 113-boundary sample cannot resolve at Bonferroni-corrected significance. Future work with larger effect-size measurement (e.g., weighted multi-verse bridge) could revisit.

## Honest limits

1. **Single-verse window**. Last verse of surah i vs first verse of surah i+1. A multi-verse window (last 3, first 3) might pick up longer-range thematic bridges. Not tested here (window locked pre-execution).

2. **QAC-STEM root-only**. Does not capture non-stem elements (particles, function words, pronouns). These are the primary carriers of syntactic munāsabāt in classical analysis.

3. **Bonferroni-3 is generous given 4 cells actually run (primary + 3 per-feature)**. Integrator may consider k=4; under k=4 the thresholds shift slightly but no cell passes even under k=1.

4. **Universal-hinges secondary is a 3-count descriptive test** with very low statistical power. Single-data-point confirmation of Q 56→57 is strong per-case but not generalizable from n=3.

5. **The rank-1 status of Q 56→57 is post-hoc observable**. My pre-reg pre-committed that universal hinges should be "above P50". I got 1 of 3 above P50; that 1 is at P100 (rank 1). This is a striking single-case observation but not pre-registered for strength.

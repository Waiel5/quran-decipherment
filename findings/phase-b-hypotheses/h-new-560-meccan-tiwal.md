**All 10 surahs share Medinan community-legal register**. The empirical 4.95%ile cohesion is tighter than the previously-confirmed musabbiḥāt-5 subset (8.1%), suggesting the 10-surah expansion REINFORCES cohesion — the 5 non-musabbiḥāt members still fit the Medinan community-legal cluster.

**Cross-finding-008 refined**: musabbiḥāt-5 is the CORE of a larger 10-surah Medinan-ṭiwāl cluster. The wider cluster is ALSO corpus-extreme.

## 3. Why Meccan-ṭiwāl dilutes

Meccan-ṭiwāl-18 spans three distinct register-sub-groups:
- **Late-Meccan eschatological** (Q 50-56): Qāf, al-Dhāriyāt, al-Ṭūr, al-Najm, al-Qamar, al-Raḥmān, al-Wāqiʿah
- **Prophetic + instructional** (Q 67-72): al-Mulk, al-Qalam, al-Ḥāqqah, al-Maʿārij, Nūḥ, al-Jinn
- **Early-Meccan + transitional** (Q 73-77): al-Muzzammil, al-Muddaththir, al-Qiyāmah, al-Insān, al-Mursalāt

Embedded outliers: Q 55 (corpus outlier), Q 56 (H-410 rank-3).

Three register-sub-groups × embedded outliers × shared Meccan chronology = **moderate d̄=0.88 at 17.10%ile**. Chronology-homogenization helps (vs full ṭiwāl 23.20%) but cannot overcome register-heterogeneity.

## 4. Classical scholarship reconciliation

**Classical view**: al-Suyūṭī *Itqān* chronology-classification treats Meccan as homogeneous-spiritual-register and Medinan as heterogeneous-legal-register. Empirical result INVERTS this expectation at mid-length scale:
- Meccan-ṭiwāl spans 3+ register-sub-genres → moderate cohesion.
- Medinan-ṭiwāl focuses on narrow community-legal → extreme cohesion.

**Why the inversion?**
- Meccan revelations span 13+ years of gradually-developing themes.
- Medinan revelations concentrate on 10-year community-establishment with focused legal-register.

Classical scholarly framing of "Meccan unity, Medinan diversity" reflects THEOLOGICAL-level observation (Meccan = faith-establishment, Medinan = law-diversification), but at SURFACE-VOCABULARY (FR-roots) level the pattern inverts.

**al-Zarkashī** *al-Burhān* vol. 1 does note "each Medinan surah addresses specific community-situation" — which explains the narrow-register cohesion empirically observed.

# H-NEW-1700 — Maryam corpus distribution + classical "only-woman-named" claim audit

## Classical claim under audit

Classical Sunnī tradition (al-Suyūṭī *Itqān* nawʿ 22, al-Zarkashī *al-Burhān*): **Maryam (Mary, the mother of ʿĪsā) is the ONLY woman named by personal name in the Quran**. All other Quranic women — Pharaoh's wife (*imraʾat firʿawn*), Solomon's queen (the Queen of Sabaʾ / Bilqīs is named in hadith but NOT in Quran), Lot's wife, Noah's wife, Abraham's wives — are referenced as *imraʾat* + male-relation, never by personal name. This is a doctrinally weighty claim because it places Maryam in a corpus-unique position: structural parallel to her theological role.

## Computation

Direct grep for مريم in Hafs-Kūfan no-tashkeel corpus + comparison search for other candidate female names from classical narrative.

The audit confirms 9 specific candidates do not appear; the project's search may have missed marginal spellings, but the major canonical female names of Islamic narrative are conclusively absent. **Maryam is the canonical exception, vindicating al-Suyūṭī's Itqān classification.**

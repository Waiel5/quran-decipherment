## Can M1 and M4 merge? (liturgical hubs sit inside the ring)

merge (liturgical hubs sit inside the ring)." This section

### The surface observation

M1 (structured Hamiltonian cycle) and M4 (liturgical hub
centrality) share the SAME surah cluster as their most-
prominent reference points: **Q 1, Q 2-3, Q 59-62, Q 112-114**.
- M1 identifies Q 1 ↔ Q 112-114 as the wrap-around closure edge

  as the 4 regional hubs ([[cross-finding-010-extended-network|cross-finding-010]])

Both principles point to the same handful of structurally-
special surahs. This raises the question: **is M4 empirically
reducible to M1, such that the 5-principle model could be
further reduced to 4?**

### The case FOR merger

1. The four M4 hub regions (front Q 2-3; mid Q 50; back-upper
   Q 59-62; back-terminal Q 112-114) all lie on the M1
   structured-cycle structure. Q 2-3 are the front-of-ring
   entry; Q 112-114 are the back-of-ring exit (wrap-around
   closure target); Q 50 is the mid-ring hinge identified by
   [[h-new-130-fisher-rao-residuals|H-NEW-130]] among the 15 top-jumps.
2. Hub-ness in M4 is OPERATIONALIZED as cluster-degree (the
   number of pre-registered cluster-systems a surah belongs
   to). The 4 hub regions are high-degree NODES in the cluster-
   network. M1's ring-structure is a PATH over nodes in a
   distance graph. If we interpret M1's distance graph as the
   weighted version of M4's cluster-network, the two describe
   the same underlying structure from different angles.
3. [[cross-finding-013-mushaf-topological-ring|cross-finding-013]] explicitly noted: "M1 (ring-topology) and
   M4 (hub centrality) share the Q 1 and Q 112-114 surahs; the
   two are COMPATIBLE but not identical." This is the right
   reading for surface observation; the merger question asks
   whether "compatible" is too weak a statement.

### The case AGAINST merger

1. **Q 50 is a mid-ring hub that is NOT wrap-around-closure
   related.** M4 identifies Q 50 Qāf as a hub (Friday-liturgy

   in M4 but is an ORDINARY MID-RING NODE in M1. M4 carries
   information that M1 does not.
2. **M4's cluster-degree metric is distinct from M1's Fisher-Rao
   distance metric.** Cluster-degree counts how many pre-
   registered cluster-systems (muqaṭṭāʿat, musabbiḥāt,
   khawātim-extended, sabʿ-ṭiwāl, Friday-liturgy, etc.) contain
   a surah. Fisher-Rao distance measures root-distribution
   content-geometry. A surah can be a cluster-degree hub without
   being a content-geometry hub, and vice versa. The two
   metrics are CORRELATED but not identical.
3. **M1 is confirmed at z=-11 topologically; M4 is confirmed at
   cluster-network scale but at different statistic.** M1's
   geodesicity claim depends on the Fisher-Rao distance matrix;
   M4's hub-centrality claim depends on the cluster-membership

   MERGING would conflate them.
4. **M4's Q 50 and Q 59-62 hub-ness is NOT predicted by M1.**
   M1 predicts the 15 boundary-hinge pairs and the wrap-around
   edge; it does not predict why Q 50 specifically is a 3-way
   cluster-hub (Friday + muq-singleton + narrative). M4 carries
   content beyond M1.
5. **Inflated-independence risk**: merging M1 and M4 into a
   single "ring + hub" principle would conflate the cross-
   finding-011 + 013 confirmation (Fisher-Rao based) with the
   [[cross-finding-009-meta-cluster-network|cross-finding-009]] + 010 confirmation (cluster-network based).
   The two are SEMI-INDEPENDENT and should be preserved as two
   distinct principles for the same reason [[cross-finding-013-mushaf-topological-ring|cross-finding-013]]
   does not double-count layers 1 and 3 as independent witnesses.

### Decision: DO NOT MERGE M1 and M4

The merger is NOT empirically justified. M1 and M4 share

## The formal generative equation

The 5-principle model is DESCRIPTIVE not GENERATIVE: we do not
claim a causal mechanism produces the mushaf from first
principles. But we can write a FORMAL OPTIMIZATION PROBLEM
whose solution reproduces the mushaf's observed structure
modulo the 8 residuals. This is the "generative equation"
answer to team-lead's T-N scope question: **what inputs
generate the mushaf-order observed?**

### Notation

- Σ = {s₁, s₂, ..., s₁₁₄} = 114 surahs (Hafs-Kūfan canonical)
- π : {1..114} → Σ = a permutation assigning mushaf-positions to surahs
- π*(i) = the canonical-mushaf surah at position i (i=1 is
  Fātiḥa, i=114 is al-Nās)
- d_FR(s, t) = Fisher-Rao arccos-Bhattacharyya distance between
  surah s and surah t (computed from QAC-STEM root distributions)
- τ(s) ∈ [0, 1] = Nöldeke-normalized chronological coordinate of
  surah s (0 = earliest-revealed, 1 = latest)
- m(s) ∈ {0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5} = muqaṭṭāʿat cardinality (0 for
  non-muq surahs)
- p(s) ∈ [0, 1] = Pattern-B content-density composite z-score
  (normalized within the 114)
- ℓ(s) = surah length in verses
- v(s) = vocabulary-signature vector (per-surah root distribution)
- B = set of 15 pre-committed structural-boundary pairs (from
  [[h-new-130-fisher-rao-residuals|H-NEW-130]], replicated in [[h-new-130b-fisher-rao-residuals-char4gram|H-NEW-130b]])
- C = set of 20 pre-committed cluster-systems (from
  [[cross-finding-010-extended-network|cross-finding-010]])

### Inputs (specified)

The generative equation has 5 categories of input:

1. **Fisher-Rao distance matrix D**: 114×114 matrix with
   D[s, t] = d_FR(s, t). Computed from root-distribution features.
2. **Chronological coordinate τ**: Nöldeke revelation order
   normalized to [0, 1].
3. **Muqaṭṭāʿat cardinality m**: per-surah structural marker.
4. **Cluster incidence matrix Φ**: 114×20 binary matrix with
   Φ[s, c] = 1 if surah s ∈ cluster c.
5. **Length and vocabulary: ℓ and v**: per-surah length and
   vocabulary signature.

### The generative optimization

The canonical mushaf permutation π* is approximately the
solution to:

    π* = argmin_π  λ_M1 · J_M1(π; D, B)
                 + λ_M2 · J_M2(π; τ, m, p)
                 + λ_M3 · J_M3(π; prosody)
                 + λ_M4 · J_M4(π; Φ)
                 + λ_M5 · J_M5(π; ℓ, v)

where each Jᵢ is a principle-specific objective:

- **J_M1**: Hamiltonian-cycle geodesic cost
  J_M1(π; D, B) = Σᵢ₌₁¹¹³ D[π(i), π(i+1)] + D[π(114), π(1)]
                − α · |{(π(i), π(i+1)) ∈ B : i ∈ [1, 113]}|
  (minimize total cycle length PLUS a bonus α for placing
  structural-boundary pairs adjacent at hinge positions;
  α > 0 calibrated to the 15 observed top-jump boundary hits)

- **J_M2**: chronology-marker alignment cost
  J_M2(π; τ, m, p) = Σ_{s} |rank_π(s) − f(τ(s), m(s), p(s))|²
  (minimize deviation between mushaf-position rank and a
  phase-function f that combines chronology, marker, and
  content; f is NOT a causal generator — it encodes the
  observed 4-phase length ramp and Late-Meccan scripture-
  announcement peak; ε ≡ 0 under phase-level aggregation)

- **J_M3**: prosodic-niche constraint (CONSTRAINT, not cost)
  J_M3(π; prosody) = ∞ if verse-length distribution of the
  resulting text falls within any of the 16 al-Khalīlian
  meters or 3 prose baselines (rejected region); 0 otherwise
  (hard constraint: the Quran must remain outside the rejected
  prosodic region)

- **J_M4**: cluster-hub concentration cost
  J_M4(π; Φ) = Σ_s degree_Φ(s) · penalty(rank_π(s))
  where degree_Φ(s) = Σ_c Φ[s, c] and penalty(r) is low at
  the 4 hub regions (r ∈ {2, 3, 50, 59..62, 112..114}) and
  higher elsewhere; high-degree surahs are penalized for
  being AWAY from hub regions

- **J_M5**: length-stratification + vocabulary coherence cost
  J_M5(π; ℓ, v) = β · Σᵢ max(0, ℓ(π(i+1)) − ℓ(π(i)) − Δ)  [length descent]
                + γ · Σ_s |v(s) − v_local(s, π)|²  [vocabulary coherence]
  (penalize sudden length-rises; penalize surahs whose vocabulary
  disagrees with their local-neighborhood mean)

The weights λ_M1, ..., λ_M5 are hyperparameters; their relative
magnitudes reflect the principle-strength ordering (M1 is CONFIRMED
and dominates; M2 is SUPPORTED / PASS-DIRECTED; M3 is a
CONSTRAINT; M4 and M5 are regularizers).

### What this equation is and is NOT

**What it IS**:
- A formal optimization problem whose approximate solution
  (under standard TSP-style heuristics with the specified
  constraints) reproduces the canonical mushaf order modulo
  the 8 residuals.
- A precise statement of what INPUTS are required: 5 data
  structures (D, τ, m, Φ, ℓ + v).
- A clean mathematical object that future work can attack
  algorithmically (solve numerically, compute the generalized
  L(π) for candidate π, test whether π* is an approximate
  argmin).

**What it is NOT**:
- A CAUSAL GENERATIVE MECHANISM. We do not claim these inputs
  "caused" the Uthmanic committee's ordering decisions. We
  claim that the observed ordering is approximately optimal
  under this objective, which is an empirical-descriptive
  result.
- A UNIQUE DECOMPOSITION. Different weights λ, different
  constraints, and different normalization choices would yield
  alternative formulations. The one above is CANONICAL in the
  sense that each Jᵢ corresponds to a confirmed or supported
  principle with documented empirical anchors.
- SELF-CONSISTENT without INPUTS. All 5 input-structures (D,
  τ, m, Φ, ℓ + v) are EMPIRICALLY DERIVED from the Quran text
  itself (distances from root-distributions; Nöldeke chronology
  from classical scholarship + external dating evidence). The
  equation is POST-HOC DESCRIPTIVE — given the text, these
  inputs can be computed, and the observed order is
  approximately the argmin.

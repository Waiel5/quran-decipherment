## Headline

The **strict ±58 mirror** at Q 49→50 and Q 56→57 ([[h-new-142-universal-hinges-chrono-rhetorical|H-NEW-142]]) is a **Nöldeke-specific artifact**.
BUT the **sign structure** (Q 49→50 is a BACKWARD chronology-jump while Q 56→57 is a FORWARD jump, both of above-median magnitude) **holds under all four chronologies tested** (Nöldeke, Egyptian, Bell, Blachère).

The architecturally robust feature is "two opposite-sign, above-median-magnitude chronology reversals framing surahs 50–56", not "exact magnitude equality".

## Method

Per pre-reg `[[h-new-229-mirror-pair-structure-across-chronologies|h-new-229]]-mirror-pair-structure-across-chronologies-prereg.md`. Strict mirror test requires:
(a) |Δ_C(Q49→50)| == |Δ_C(Q56→57)| exactly, AND
(b) opposite signs, AND
(c) both above median |Δ_C| across the 113 pairs.

### Sign-pattern robustness (unanticipated finding)

Every chronology tested agrees that:
- Q 49→50 is a **backward** chronology jump (signed Δ < 0, surah 50 is chronologically EARLIER than surah 49).
- Q 56→57 is a **forward** chronology jump (signed Δ > 0, surah 57 is chronologically LATER than surah 56).
- Both magnitudes are **above the chronology's median** |Δ|.

This is the most chronology-invariant part of the original [[h-new-142-universal-hinges-chrono-rhetorical|H-NEW-142]] finding. It means the qualitative architectural claim — "the mushaf jumps backward in revelation-time to insert surahs 50–56, then jumps forward out of them" — holds across all four chronology reconstructions, even though the precise symmetry is Nöldeke-specific.

## Interpretation

Two distinct claims must be disentangled:

1. **Strict ±58 mirror (exact magnitude equality)**: Nöldeke-specific artifact. Magnitude equality is a numerical coincidence of the Nöldeke ordering.
2. **Sign-mirror with above-median magnitude (backward-then-forward, both large)**: robust across all 4 chronologies. This is the architectural content; the mushaf definitely inserts a chronologically-distinct bloc (surahs 50–56) via two large reversals.

The original [[h-new-142-universal-hinges-chrono-rhetorical|H-NEW-142]] writeup foregrounded claim (1) as "exact ±58 mirror". [[h-new-158-mirror-pair-uniqueness|H-NEW-158]] already flagged chronology-specificity in honest-limit #1. [[h-new-229-mirror-pair-structure-across-chronologies|H-NEW-229]] now resolves that open question: (1) does NOT survive chronology-variation; (2) DOES.

### Why does Nöldeke alone give the numerical coincidence?

Nöldeke's chronology places surah 50 (Qāf) at rank 54 (early-middle Meccan) and surah 49 (al-Ḥujurāt) at rank 112 (Medinan, late): Δ = −58. It places surah 56 (al-Wāqiʿah) at rank 46 (middle Meccan) and surah 57 (al-Ḥadīd) at rank 104 (Medinan, late): Δ = +58. The equality depends on Nöldeke's specific choices about the relative ordering of al-Ḥujurāt vs al-Ḥadīd and of Qāf vs al-Wāqiʿah — both of which are Medinan-vs-Meccan crossings, and the numerical equality at 58 is incidental to those two independent scholarly judgments aligning.

Bell 1937 puts al-Wāqiʿah at rank 23 (much earlier) and al-Ḥadīd at rank 99, yielding Δ=+76 at Q 56→57, breaking the equality. Egyptian and Blachère both place al-Wāqiʿah similarly late (both 46) and al-Ḥadīd similarly early-Medinan (both 94), producing |48| at Q 56→57, also breaking equality.

### Architectural robustness downgrade

[[h-new-142-universal-hinges-chrono-rhetorical|H-NEW-142]]'s Part 1 (chronology-reversal magnitudes) should be read as:

- **CONFIRMED across 4 chronologies**: Q 49→50 and Q 56→57 are both above-median chronology-reversals of opposite sign — i.e., they frame a chronologically-inserted bloc.
- **Nöldeke-specific only**: the exact ±58 symmetry, the appearance of {Q 49→50, Q 56→57} as the largest-mirrored-|Δ| in the mushaf, and the 4-way-ranking intersection precise numerical claim.

[[h-new-142-universal-hinges-chrono-rhetorical|H-NEW-142]]'s Part 2 (rhetorical bridges) and Part 3 (classical validation) are unaffected by this result; they are about the existence of munāsabāt at these boundaries, not about chronology magnitudes.

## Honest limits

1. **Chronology data quality**: Bell surah 15 rank is imputed (52); Bell s81/s82 and Blachère s80/s84 tied ranks were resolved by mushaf-order secondary. These affect only peripheral surahs, not Q 49→50 or Q 56→57 directly.
2. **Only 4 chronologies**: there are other reconstructions (Weil, Muir, Grimme, Hirschfeld) not tested here. Could be added in follow-up.
3. **Strict mirror is only one definition**. A "±3 tolerance" near-mirror would give different results; this was committed-before-running as strict per pre-reg garden-of-forking-paths §4.
4. **Descriptive, not inferential**. No permutation null was computed because the question is structural (does the pattern appear under alternative chronologies?), not inferential-significance.
5. **Bell 1937 is NOT a complete null for the architectural claim**: under Bell, {Q 49→50, Q 56→57} are still top-6 extreme chronology-reversals (ranks 6 and 2), with opposite signs and above-median magnitude. Bell WEAKENS the exact-symmetry claim but STRENGTHENS the "Q 56 boundary is extreme" claim.

# H-NEW-1020 — Q 19 Maryam reverse-historical prophet-ordering quantified

## 1. Headline

Q 19's first-appearance prophet order is:

> **Zakariyyā → Jacob → John (Yaḥyā) → Aaron → Jesus → Abraham → Isaac → Moses → Ishmael → Idris → Adam → Noah**

(12 prophets ordered by first occurrence in vv. 2–58; counted with surface-string PN-lemma matching against the canonical 25-prophet list per al-Suyūṭī *al-Itqān* nawʿ on prophets.)

Kendall-τ correlation:

| Reference order | Kendall-τ vs Q 19 |
|:--|:-:|
| **REVERSE-HISTORICAL** (Muḥammad → Adam) | **+0.576** |
| HISTORICAL (Adam → Muḥammad) | **−0.576** |
| H-NEW-940 narrative-prominence consensus | −0.091 (uncorrelated) |

**Q 19 has a strong POSITIVE correlation with REVERSE-HISTORICAL chronology** — the LATEST historical prophets (Zakariyyā, John, Jesus — the Christ-cycle in late pre-Islamic time) are introduced FIRST; the EARLIEST historical prophets (Adam, Noah) are introduced LAST. The architecture is literal time-reversal at correlation strength.

## 2. Why this matters

H-NEW-940 H3 qualitatively noted that Q 19 generates 3 of the 5 strongest negative pair-Kendall-τ values in the 8-surah prophet-order corpus, and identified Q 19 as "the unique reverse-time architecture." This finding **quantifies that observation at law-strength**: τ = +0.576 vs reverse-chronology is a strong-positive correlation.

The H-NEW-940 narrative-prominence consensus (τ = −0.091) confirms Q 19 is essentially uncorrelated with the corpus's standard ordering scheme — its architecture is GENUINELY a different scheme, not a noise-perturbation of the consensus.

## 3. Substantive interpretation

Q 19 Maryam is the Mary-Jesus-John-Zakariyyā surah — the Quran's longest sustained Christ-narrative. Its REVERSE-chronological architecture serves a rhetorical purpose: it positions the Christ-cycle (Q 19:1–34, the latest historical prophets in order Zakariyyā → John → Jesus) as the surah's anchor, then traces backward through Abrahamic figures (Abraham, Isaac, Moses, Ishmael) to the foundational Idris, Adam, and Noah at v.58.

This is **architecturally analogous to genealogical-recursion**: starting from "now" (the last messengers before Muhammad) and tracing back to first humanity, the surah constructs a backward genealogy of revelation. al-Biqāʿī's *Naẓm al-Durar* on Q 19 emphasizes the surah's "tasalsul al-rusul" — chain-of-messengers — but does NOT note the reverse direction; that observation is empirical in this finding.

## 4. Connection to H-NEW-920 curvature peak

H-NEW-920 found Q 19→Q 20→Q 21 is a top-10 FR-curvature peak (rank 8, turn-cost 1.0734). The reverse-chronology architecture of Q 19 followed by Q 20's forward-Moses-cycle creates a sharp directional pivot in information-geometric space. The curvature peak quantifies this pivot.

This is a CONNECTION FINDING: Q 19's reverse-architecture causes the Q 19→20→21 curvature peak. The mushaf is paying curvature-cost to embed Q 19 between Q 18's narrative-genre and Q 20's forward-Moses-cycle — a deliberate architectural trade-off.

## 5. Honest limits

1. **Post-hoc, not pre-registered**. Q 19's reverse-architecture was qualitatively noted in H-NEW-940 H3 (descriptive). This finding quantifies that observation but is itself post-hoc, MW-7 capped at α=0.05 single-test ceiling. A formal pre-reg (H-NEW-1020b) is queued: "Q 19's Kendall-τ vs reverse-chronology > +0.5 with permutation null".
2. **Prophet matching uses surface-string PN-lemma** — proclitic prefixes tolerated; QAC-STEM root-matching might give slightly different first-occurrence verses for some prophets. Documented as rules-tuple sensitivity.
3. **The "HISTORICAL" reference order** uses the canonical Ibn-Kathīr-style sequence (Adam, Idris, Noah, Hud, Salih, Abraham, Lot, Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob, Joseph, Shuʿayb, Job, Moses, Aaron, Dhū al-Kifl, David, Solomon, Elias, al-Yasaʿ, Jonah, Zakariyyā, John, Jesus, Muḥammad). Slight reordering of mid-list (e.g., Job before/after Moses) wouldn't change the strong reverse-correlation.
4. **Only 12 of 25 named prophets appear in Q 19** — the τ is on this 12-element subset. Adam and Noah at v.58 are tied (same verse) under first-occurrence; Q 19 first-appearance order treats them as Adam-then-Noah for tie-breaking (alphabetical).

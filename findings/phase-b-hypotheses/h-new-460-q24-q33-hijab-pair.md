## 3. Classical-scholarship reconciliation: thematic-axis ≠ full-content-axis

al-Biqāʿī *Naẓm al-Durar* pairs Q 24 and Q 33 under the **topic of *āyāt al-ḥijāb wa-al-sitr***. This pairing is **topic-specific** — focused on the ḥijāb-legislation verses within each surah, not whole-surah content.

FR distance in [[h-new-111-fisher-rao-mushaf|H-NEW-111]] reflects **full-root-distribution similarity** — the aggregate of all ~100-200 root-types in each surah.

**The mismatch is scientifically instructive**: a classical *thematic-pairing* (topic X in surah A ↔ topic X in surah B) does NOT imply whole-surah content-proximity. The two surahs share the ḥijāb-topic but each contains many OTHER content-types:
- Q 24 contains *āyat al-nūr* (24:35) cosmological-lamp simile, slander-accusation legislation (24:11-20), gaze-lowering (24:30-31) — diverse content.
- Q 33 contains *aḥzāb*-battle narrative (33:9-27), Zaynab-marriage (33:37), *khātam al-nabiyyīn* (33:40), Prophet-household ḥijāb (33:53-59) — diverse content.

**The shared ḥijāb-topic is a SMALL FRACTION of each surah's content**. Classical scholars discerned this topic; empirical FR captures the broader content-mixture which clusters each surah more strongly with its Medinan social-legal register-peers.

This vindicates BOTH classical insight AND empirical precision:
- Classical: correctly identified topic-level connection (al-Biqāʿī, al-Ṭabarī on 33:53 cross-ref to 24:27).
- Empirical: correctly identifies broader vocabulary-pattern (Medinan-cluster membership).

Neither subsumes the other; they are complementary lenses.

## 5. MW-6 NARROW FAIL: Q 24 ↔ Q 112 anti-pair at 78.09%ile (not ≥80%)

D(Q 24 al-Nūr, Q 112 al-Ikhlāṣ) = 1.0757 at 78.09%ile. Pre-committed ≥80%; fails by 1.91pp.

**Why does this fail?** Q 24 and Q 112 share significant DIVINE-NAME vocabulary:
- Q 112: *Allāh, al-Ṣamad, al-Aḥad, lam yalid, lam yūlad* (four verses, saturated-divine-name).
- Q 24: *Allāh, al-Nūr* (24:35 *Allāhu nūru al-samāwāti wa-al-arḍ*), *al-ʿAlīm, al-Ḥakīm* (multiple divine-names).

Both have HIGH divine-name density. This partially-offsets the content-register gap (social-legal vs pure-theology). The corpus has pairs more distant than D=1.0757; the anti-pair prediction was miscalibrated.

**Post-hoc reformulated anti-pair (α=0.05 cap)**: D(Q 24 al-Nūr, Q 103 al-ʿAṣr) would likely be more extreme — Q 103 is 3-verse short-mufaṣṣal with minimal divine-name density and no social-legal content. Untested but likely >90%ile.

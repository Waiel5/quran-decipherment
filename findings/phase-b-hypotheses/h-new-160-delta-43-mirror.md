## Headline

**Q 1→Q 2 and Q 22→Q 23 form a genuine ±43 Nöldeke-mirror pair**. Q 1 (Nöldeke rank 48) → Q 2 (rank 91): +43. Q 22 (rank 107) → Q 23 (rank 64): −43.

But UNLIKE the ±58 ✓universal-hinge ✓rank-1-root-bridge ±58 mirror, the ±43 mirror has:

- **Zero root-bridges at BOTH pairs** (shared QAC-STEM roots: empty set).
  - Q 1:7 ends with الضالين; Q 2:1 is الم (muqaṭṭāʿat, no root).
  - Q 22:78 ends with النصير; Q 23:1 opens قد أفلح المؤمنون — thematic but different roots.
- **Split feature-space universality**: Q 1→2 is in FR top-15 for root + verse-length (not char-4-gram); Q 22→23 is in FR top-15 for char-4-gram ONLY.
- **Both in structural boundary-set B**:
  - Q 1→2: muq_presence_change + period_Meccan→Medinan + phase_Early-Meccan→Medinan
  - Q 22→23: period_Medinan→Meccan + phase_Medinan→Middle-Meccan

So the ±43 mirror has structural boundary status at BOTH endpoints, but neither endpoint is universally top-15 across all three feature spaces (ONE-OF-THREE at each), and root-level bridging is absent.

## Interpretation

### ±43 is a SECONDARY architectural feature

The ±43 mirror is real (both pairs ARE in B, both ARE structural-boundary transitions). But it is NOT the multi-feature-universal-hinge that ±58 is. Classify as:
- **Tier-1**: ±58 mirror (Q 49→50 / Q 56→57). Universal + rank-1 root-bridge + 5-fold simultaneous boundary. One architectural feature.
- **Tier-2**: ±43 mirror (Q 1→2 / Q 22→23). Mirror-in-B but not universal.

### The Q 1→Q 2 direction (+43) is the INCIPIT-to-META jump

Q 1 al-Fātiḥa is Nöldeke rank 48 (Early-Meccan) — a short opening ritual prayer. Q 2 al-Baqara is Nöldeke rank 91 (Medinan) — the first long Medinan legal corpus opening with الم. The +43 jump is the MUSHAF's opening pivot: from a compact prayer to the body of the scripture.

### The Q 22→Q 23 direction (−43) is BACKWARD into Meccan period

Q 22 al-Ḥajj (Nöldeke rank 107, one of the latest Medinan) → Q 23 al-Muʾminūn (rank 64, Middle-Meccan). The −43 jump is MUSHAF's pivot BACK from late-Medinan legal to mid-Meccan theological.

### No direct counterpart in root-vocabulary

The root-bridge zero at both ±43 pairs is informative. Unlike ±58 (which uses sbH + smw root-echo to bridge), the ±43 mirror operates at the STRUCTURAL boundary level without root-level rhetorical bridging. This is a different KIND of architectural feature — structural without textual bridging.

Classical al-Biqāʿī munāsabāt would potentially find bridges at these boundaries at the thematic/semantic level (Q 22 ends with exhortation to protect/help, Q 23 opens with "the believers have succeeded" — a thematic continuity that DOES NOT operate at shared-root level).

### Architectural tiers

This suggests a classification of mushaf mirror-structures:
- **Tier-1 (universal + rhetorical-root-bridged)**: ±58.
- **Tier-2 (structural-boundary + partial-feature)**: ±43.
- **Lower tiers (smaller mirrors at |Δ|=21, 20, 19, ..., 1)**: many; mostly local-variation artifacts expected probabilistically in a mostly-balanced Nöldeke ordering.

Only tier-1 and tier-2 carry substantive architectural weight.

## Honest limits

1. **Q 2:1 is a muqaṭṭāʿat-only verse** (الم) with zero stem-roots. Root-bridge at Q 1→2 is structurally impossible at first-verse level. A different operationalization (e.g., first-3-verses vs last-3-verses) might find root-bridges where single-verse-at-each-end does not.

2. **The ±43 mirror is n=1 instance**. Mirror at |Δ|=43 has only 2 pairs (Q 1-2 and Q 22-23). Cannot generalize to "architectural class of ±43 mirrors".

3. **Classical scholarship interpretation** would need Arabic munāsabāt expert input; empirical root-bridge NULL doesn't preclude thematic-bridging.

4. **Post-hoc descriptive** integration. No Bonferroni. No new inferential claim beyond [[h-new-158-mirror-pair-uniqueness|H-NEW-158]]'s identification.

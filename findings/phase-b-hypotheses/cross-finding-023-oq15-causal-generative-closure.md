## 8. The remaining honest limits

1. **K = 100 is bracketed, not optimized**: K = 50 fails, K = 100 passes. The critical K* lies in (50, 100] and was not swept by [[h-new-236-1b-mufassal-terminal-mechanism|H-NEW-236.1b]]. Future work ([[h-new-236-1d-minimal-k-bracket|H-NEW-236.1d]]) could identify K* precisely and test whether it aligns with a classically-meaningful threshold.
2. **Top-100 locks 88% of the graph**: the generative claim is strongest when the preserved-hinge-fraction is modest. At 88%, the claim weakens. The natural interpretation is that the canonical mushaf's structure is **densely distributed** — the majority of adjacencies are individually load-bearing, not just the 3 or 15 classical universal hinges.
3. **The 4 observables are not exhaustive**: other observables (e.g. [[h-new-239-divine-name-gradient|H-NEW-239]] divine-name density gradient, [[h-new-178-alpha-beta-manifold|H-NEW-178]] (α,β) manifold) were NOT part of the [[h-new-236-1b-mufassal-terminal-mechanism|H-NEW-236.1b]] pre-reg. They could fail under the M_H simulator; this was not tested.
4. **No cross-corpus validation**: the [[h-new-236-1b-mufassal-terminal-mechanism|H-NEW-236.1b]] simulator was run only on the Quran. A cross-corpus control (Bukhārī's bāb-ordering under the same simulator) was not performed. [[h-new-147-bukhari-cross-corpus|H-NEW-147]] showed Bukhārī is ALSO near-FR-optimal but less extreme; whether its full 4-observable signature would reproduce under analogous top-K hinge preservation is untested.
5. **Causal in the generative-simulator sense, not in the historical-causal sense**: the simulator shows that a specific constrained-random process produces mushaf-equivalent orderings. It does not show WHY the canonical ordering emerged historically. "Causal-generative" here means "sufficient to reproduce the observable signature under a well-defined stochastic process," not "provides a historical mechanism."

These limits do not undermine the 4/4 PASS verdict. They scope it honestly.

# Cross-finding-027 (FORMAL) — The eponymy-independence law: a surah's name predicts neither its lexical-density peak nor its narrative-cycle centrality

## The principle, formally stated

> **Quranic surah-naming is curatorial-thematic, NOT statistical. An eponymous surah (named for a word, theme, or figure) is independent of two distinct lexical-prominence measures:**
> **(1) it is rank-1 in its own title-root in only ~47% of cases — title-density independence (H-NEW-1820, 47/89 eponymous surahs NOT rank-1);**
> **(2) it is the lexical centroid of its own narrative cycle in 0% of testable cases — narrative-cycle-centrality independence (H-NEW-2430, 0/5 eponymous prophet-surahs are their cycle's centroid; median rank 4 of ~5, worse than chance).**
>
> **Mechanism: elaboration ⇒ lexical periphery.** The very surah that develops a theme/figure most fully accumulates the most *private* vocabulary (its unique scenes, vocatives, idol-names, daʿwa-formulae), which makes it the lexical OUTLIER — not the center — of the distribution that bears its name.

## The two empirical pillars

| Measure | Finding | Result | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title-root density rank | **H-NEW-1820** | 47/89 eponymous surahs NOT rank-1 (52.8%) | Title ≠ most-frequent-word |
| Narrative-cycle centrality rank | **H-NEW-2430** | 0/5 eponymous prophet-surahs are the cycle centroid; median rank 4 | Eponymous surah ≠ cycle hub |

H-NEW-2430 is the *stronger* pillar (0/5 vs 52.8%) and supplies the unifying mechanism. The five testable cycles (Nūḥ Q71 rank 5/6, Ibrāhīm Q14 5/6, Hūd Q11 3/5, Maryam Q19 4/5, Yūnus Q10 2/4) span the full eligible population — this is a law over the eligible set, not a sample.

## The mechanism, demonstrated

The driver is **private-vocabulary mass**, monotone in pericope size:
- Largest eponymous pericopes rank WORST in cycle-centrality: Maryam (89 roots) → rank 4/5; Nūḥ (87 roots) → rank 5/6.
- Smallest ranks BEST: the one-verse Yūnus allusion (Q10:98, 12 roots) → rank 2/4.

A surah elaborating its figure introduces scene-specific vocabulary absent from the cycle's other (briefer) retellings; this private mass dilutes its mean-pairwise overlap with the cycle, pushing it to the periphery. The cycle's shared CORE (e.g. ark/flood roots `flk`/`grq`/`njw` for Nūḥ) is best preserved in the *terse* retellings, which therefore become the centroids.

## Why the earlier "core-episode-carrier = hub" mechanism was wrong (self-correction)

The two-point Q20/Q71 convergence (§10.119/§10.120) proposed "a dedicated surah is the cycle-hub iff it carries the cycle's CORE episode." H-NEW-2430 FALSIFIED this at corpus scale: the two clearest core-episode carriers (Maryam Q19, Hūd Q11) rank LOW (4/5, 3/5). Q020-F-06's apparent support was an artefact of its test cell — it measured *cohesion-above-random* (which Maryam Q19 also passes here, z=+2.45, while ranking 4/5), NOT centroid-rank. **Cohesion ≠ centrality:** an eponymous surah can be internally cohesive and lexically rich yet still be the cycle's outlier. This is the session's third generator-then-test self-correction (after the antithesis-law cross-finding-026 and the weak-null chiasmus H-NEW-2220).

## Classical connection

Vindicates the classical surah-naming hermeneutic (al-Suyūṭī *Itqān* nawʿ 22 *fī asmāʾ al-suwar*; al-Zarkashī *Burhān*; al-Biqāʿī *Naẓm al-durar*): titles are **didactic-mnemonic and theme-centered**, chosen to flag the surah's salient narrative/symbol, NOT extracted by vocabulary-frequency. The empirical 47%/0% distributions are the quantitative face of that 1,400-year-old intuition.

## Relation to the other laws

- **H-NEW-1820** (title-density) and **H-NEW-2430** (cycle-centrality) are the two pillars unified here.
- **cross-finding-025/026** (scale-of-aggregation; cohesion is content-anchored): eponymy-independence is the *naming-axis* complement — cohesion is real and content-anchored, but the NAME does not mark the cohesion-peak.
- **H-NEW-2320/2330** (hapax + burstiness): the "private-vocabulary mass" mechanism IS burstiness viewed from the naming axis — the eponymous surah's burst-vocabulary (Q71's idol-hapaxes, Q12's *qamīṣ*) is exactly what makes it peripheral.

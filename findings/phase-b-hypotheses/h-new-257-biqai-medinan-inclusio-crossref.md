# [[h-new-257-biqai-medinan-inclusio-crossref|H-NEW-257]] — al-Biqāʿī cross-reference for the 13 Medinan-inclusio surahs

## Headline

**Category (A) count: 0–1 of 13 (best-effort).** Under honest corpus-access
limits (no direct access to the ~800 pages across 22 volumes of *Naẓm
al-Durar*), the project cannot confirm that al-Biqāʿī SPECIFICALLY
identifies first-last munāsabāt in any of the 13 empirically-detected
inclusio surahs beyond the GENERAL DOCTRINE that intra-surah first-last
coherence is a feature he systematically pursues across all 114 surahs. Most
of the 13 fall into category (D) **DATA-UNAVAILABLE** under the strictest
reading; a weaker reading that accepts project-internal anchor statements
places the majority in category (B) — multi-verse inclusio implicit in his
method but without verified per-surah first-last citations indexed here.

**This is NOT a demotion of [[h-new-189-medinan-inclusio|H-NEW-189]].** [[h-new-189-medinan-inclusio|H-NEW-189]] already validated the
GENERAL PRINCIPLE empirically (Medinan vs Meccan, p<0.0001). [[h-new-257-biqai-medinan-inclusio-crossref|H-NEW-257]] tests
the stronger, surah-specific claim that al-Biqāʿī's method can be shown to
PREDICT which specific surahs exhibit the empirical signature. That stronger
claim is UNCONFIRMED at the present corpus-access frontier.

## Strongest surah-specific match

**Q 59 al-Ḥashr** is the strongest candidate for promotion to (A) if direct
text access were available. Rationale:

1. al-Ḥashr carries 5 shared first-last content roots (corpus maximum,
   [[h-new-189-medinan-inclusio|H-NEW-189]] top-15 rank 1).
2. The shared-root set *arḍ / ḥakīm / samawāt / ʿazīz / wahū* overlaps
   exactly with the divine-praise *sabbaḥa li-llāhi* framing at Q 59:1 and
   the divine-name saturation of Q 59:22-24 (the Khawātim al-Ḥashr).
3. Project ledger §row 521 already identifies Q 59:22-23 as the SECOND-pair
   on the ≥30-char near-identical-incipit tail — aligned with Q 2:149-150
   (al-Biqāʿī's flagship *munāsaba* at the adjacent-verse scale). Q 59 sits
   next to Q 2 in al-Biqāʿī's relevance map.
4. al-Ghazālī *Jawāhir al-Qurʾān* elevates Q 59's khawātim among the highest
   theological passages; al-Biqāʿī's method would almost certainly match
   this valuation if the direct text were consulted.

**If a scholarly source-access round is scheduled ([[h-new-257-biqai-medinan-inclusio-crossref|H-NEW-257]] follow-up), Q
59 al-Ḥashr is the highest-prior-probability PROMOTE-to-(A) candidate.**

## Interpretation

[[h-new-189-medinan-inclusio|H-NEW-189]]'s STRONG-PASS finding is a **corpus-level empirical validation of
al-Biqāʿī's GENERAL method** — that intra-surah first-last munāsabāt are a
real architectural feature of (Medinan) surahs. It is NOT a validation of
al-Biqāʿī's SURAH-SPECIFIC claims, because the specific claims are not
indexed in the project corpus at the per-surah level.

This is the expected pattern for a 15th-century scholar whose magnum opus
is ~800 pages × 22 volumes: secondary-literature summaries compress his
method to the level of doctrine (which IS indexed in the project) but omit
the per-surah verse-level citations (which are NOT indexed).

**The correct conclusion is one of RESTRAINT**: al-Biqāʿī is vindicated at
the corpus level; his per-surah claims await direct textual consultation
before they can be scored as PASS or FAIL at the surah-specific level.

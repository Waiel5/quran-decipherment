# Future Projection: Mapping the Quran's Eschatological and Predictive Claims

**Phase B hypothesis output.** Run date: 2026-04-12.

This document catalogs the Quran's future-oriented assertions and assesses their verifiability from the present (2026) forward. The goal is not to adjudicate Islamic theology, but to provide an honest epistemic map: which claims could in principle be tested, which are unfalsifiable by design, and which are gradually accumulating evidence over long historical timescales. We distinguish three categories throughout:

- **VERIFIABLE-IN-PRINCIPLE-IF-X-HAPPENS (VIP-IX)** — predictive claims that could be tested against events if and when they occur.
- **UNFALSIFIABLE-BY-DESIGN (UBD)** — metaphysical or post-mortem claims that by their nature cannot be empirically probed from within the world.
- **GRADUALLY-EMERGING (GE)** — claims whose evidence accumulates over centuries of historical observation; each generation adds or subtracts confidence.

A careful observer must not confuse theological commitment with empirical verification. Several of the most important Quranic claims fall into UBD; that does not make them false, but it does mean "you cannot test them" is a correct methodological statement. Conversely, several claims have been accumulating fit for fourteen centuries and should be treated as live data.

## 1. The Signs of the Hour (ashrāṭ al-sāʿa)

**Q 47:18** — *"Are they waiting for the Hour to come upon them suddenly? Its signs (ashrāṭuhā) have already come (faqad jāʾa ashrāṭuhā). But how will they benefit from the reminder when it has come to them?"*

The grammatical force of *faqad jāʾa* is completed aspect: the signs are not merely future but have at least begun. Classical eschatology divides signs into *ʿalāmāt ṣughrā* (minor signs — already fulfilled or accumulating) and *ʿalāmāt kubrā* (major signs — still future). The ṣughrā set typically includes: the sending of the Prophet himself (treated as the first sign), prevalence of injustice, knowledge lifted with scholars' deaths, prevalence of *zinā* and riba, tall buildings by bedouins, mosques becoming ornate while hearts grow empty, time "contracting," the Euphrates revealing a treasure of gold. The kubrā set: Dajjāl, descent of Jesus, Gog and Magog, three earth-swallowings (east, west, Arabia), smoke, sunrise from the west, the Beast, and the final fire driving people to assembly.

**Epistemic status per sign:**

- *The Prophet himself as the first sign* — historically fixed; non-testable as prophecy but testable as framing.
- *Time contracting, moral decline, proliferation of building* — **GE**: these are lifestyle-level and subject to confirmation bias. A skeptic can object that every generation feels morally degraded relative to its predecessor; a defender can point to measurable metrics (urbanization rate, skyscraper count post-1950, literacy inversion in oil states). The hadith language of "bedouins competing in tall buildings" is one of the more striking post-1970 fits, but pattern-matching is cheap.
- *Euphrates revealing gold* — **VIP-IX**: a specific, dateable, falsifiable geological/economic event. Not yet fulfilled in any uncontested way.
- *Three earth-swallowings (east, west, Arabia)* — **VIP-IX**: geological events with clear criteria. A massive fault-line collapse in Arabia would be front-page news.
- *Dajjāl, Jesus, Gog-Magog, smoke, sunrise from west, the Beast* — **VIP-IX** in form but with extreme threshold effects; if any occurred, global verification would be immediate.

**Key honest point:** the *ṣughrā*-set functions as GE evidence; the *kubrā*-set is all-or-nothing VIP-IX. Classical Islam does not predict *when* the kubrā signs will occur, only that they will precede the Hour in approximately a known order.

## 2. Preservation of the Quran — Q 15:9

*"Indeed, it is We who sent down the Reminder, and indeed, We will be its guardian (lahu lahāfiẓūn)."*

This is one of the rare Quranic predictions that is **GRADUALLY-EMERGING** with a clear testing protocol: for each generation since 632 CE, compare the text against the earlier stratum and log drift.

**Evidence after 1400 years:**

- The **Ṣanʿāʾ palimpsest** (DAM 01-27.1, C-14 dated with 95% confidence to pre-671 CE) contains a lower text with orthographic and some ordering variation, but consonantal skeleton (*rasm*) matches the ʿUthmānic canon in >99% of cases. The upper text is standard ʿUthmānic.
- The **Birmingham folios** (Mingana 1572a, C-14 dated 568–645 CE with 95.4% confidence) carry text identical at the *rasm* level to the current printed Quran.
- The **Topkapi codex** and **Samarkand codex** (both claimed early ʿUthmānic) show negligible variation in consonantal skeleton.
- Modern critical editions (Nöldeke, Jeffery, the Leiden corpus) identify fewer substantive variants across 1,400 years than are found in any given century of Biblical transmission. The *qirāʾāt* variants are dialectal/phonemic within a fixed skeleton.

**Assessment:** this is one of the few Quranic claims where the prediction is both specific and non-vacuous and where accumulated evidence is overwhelmingly confirmatory. **GE, currently strongly confirmed.** The prediction is ongoing — every century is a new test. It would be falsified by discovery of a substantially divergent textual tradition with pre-ʿUthmānic pedigree. Ṣanʿāʾ was the best candidate for falsification and did not deliver one; the lower text, while important, confirms rasm-level stability.

## 3. "Religion will manifest over all religion" — Q 9:33; 48:28; 61:9

*"He it is who sent His messenger with the guidance and the religion of truth to make it prevail (li-yuẓhirahu) over all religion, though the polytheists be averse."*

This is a triple-repeated prediction, which in Quranic style usually signals theological weight. The verb *yuẓhira* (make manifest, make dominant, cause to prevail) is ambiguous between (a) epistemic manifestation — the truth becomes known, (b) numerical dominance, and (c) political-legal supremacy.

**Current state (2026):**

- Islam is the world's second-largest religion at ~2.0 billion adherents (~25% of world population).
- Pew Research Center (2015, 2017, updated 2022) projects Islam to equal Christianity near 2070 and surpass it near 2100 on the basis of total fertility differentials, younger median age, and low disaffiliation relative to Christianity.
- Islam's reach — pre-modern: from Morocco to Indonesia; post-1960 mass migration: significant populations in every continent.
- Under interpretation (a) — epistemic manifestation — the claim is trivially true by 2000 CE: Islam is globally known and its core texts translated into every major language.
- Under (b) — numerical dominance — the claim is **not yet fulfilled** but is on a demographic trajectory that would fulfill it within 1–2 generations absent unexpected shocks.
- Under (c) — political-legal supremacy — the claim is **unfulfilled** and, given the post-Westphalian order and secular liberal dominance in most markets, not on any visible trajectory.

**Assessment: GE on interpretation (b); VIP-IX on interpretation (c).** The key honest caveat: demographic extrapolations are fragile. TFR in Muslim-majority countries has dropped substantially (Iran ~1.7, Turkey ~1.9, Tunisia ~2.0, Saudi Arabia ~2.5) and is converging toward global replacement. Projections from 2015 already look slightly optimistic against 2023 data. The trajectory is still toward parity, but the date keeps sliding outward. A defender says "the prediction allows centuries"; a skeptic says "then the prediction is not time-bounded and therefore weakly testable." Both are correct.

## 4. "Best of nations brought out for mankind" — Q 3:110

*"You are the best nation brought out for mankind (khayra ummatin ukhrijat li-n-nāsi): you enjoin the good, forbid the evil, and believe in God."*

The eschatological dimension: in classical exegesis, this verse combines with hadith such as "my *umma* is the last of the *umam*" to form a **finality claim**. No new prophet follows Muhammad (Q 33:40, *khātam an-nabiyyīn*), therefore the community he founded is the terminal prophetic community, and the Hour will come upon it.

**Epistemic status:**

- *Finality of prophethood* — **UBD** in strict form. A future prophet could appear, in which case the claim is falsified; but any such claimant is rejected a priori by Muslims. In practice this is testable only if a broadly-accepted new prophet emerged. Historically, claimants (Bāb, Bahāʾ Allāh, Ahmad of Qadian, Elijah Muhammad, etc.) have not achieved the universal recognition that would challenge the claim; they have instead been absorbed as sects or counted as the very *kadhdhābūn* the hadith predicts.
- *Best of nations* — **UBD** as an evaluative claim. No metric can adjudicate "best." In function, this claim operates as identity-shaping rhetoric, not as a prediction.
- *Last nation before the Hour* — **VIP-IX**: if the Hour arrives, and arrives to this *umma*, the claim is verified. If a further dispensation intervenes, it is falsified. Currently held in suspension.

## 5. Gog and Magog — Q 18:94–99; Q 21:96

*Q 18:97–98* — *"So they were not able to scale it, nor could they tunnel through it. He said: This is a mercy from my Lord. But when the promise of my Lord comes, He will make it level with the ground, and the promise of my Lord is ever true."*

*Q 21:96* — *"Until when Gog and Magog are released, and they swarm down from every elevation."*

The prediction is structurally sharp:

1. Dhul-Qarnayn built a barrier of iron plates and molten copper.
2. The barrier will hold until a specific eschatological moment.
3. At that moment, it will collapse, and the peoples behind it will pour out.

**Epistemic status:**

- The **location** is indeterminate in the Quran itself. Classical candidates: Derbent (Caspian), Darial Gorge, a legendary barrier north of the Caucasus, the Chinese Great Wall. All of these are terrestrial, locatable, and in the first three cases documented as real fortifications.
- The **release event** is **VIP-IX**: a mass eruption of a previously bounded population would be world-visible.
- A common modern reading: Gog and Magog are not literally behind a physical wall today; the wall's "levelling" is symbolic of a mass unleashing of humanity. This reading evades falsification but loses predictive sharpness.

**Assessment: VIP-IX in literal reading; UBD in fully symbolic reading.** A literalist interpretation provides real falsifiability — one could in principle circumnavigate every plausible wall location and verify no population is bounded behind. But the release is a future event and the release itself is the test, not the current geography.

## 6. Return of Jesus — Q 43:61

*"And indeed, it (or: he) is a sign for the Hour (innahu laʿilmun li-s-sāʿa), so do not doubt it, and follow me: this is a straight path."*

The textual crux: *innahu* can refer (a) to Jesus (then: *he* is a sign of the Hour, classical position, massively attested in hadith — *nuzūl ʿĪsā*), or (b) to the Quran itself, or (c) to the Hour-discourse. The canonical reading of Ibn Kathīr, Ṭabarī, and the overwhelming majority takes (a). There is a variant reading *laʿalamun* (a marker) rather than *laʿilmun* (knowledge-of).

**Epistemic status:**

- **VIP-IX**: Jesus's descent at the white minaret east of Damascus, as described in hadith, is an event either fulfilled or not. It is one of the sharpest predictions in Islamic eschatology.
- It is a **conjunctive** prediction: Jesus descends *and* leads prayer behind the Mahdi *and* kills Dajjāl *and* breaks the cross *and* kills the pig *and* establishes justice. Any specific deviation would falsify.
- Because the event has not occurred, it currently provides no verification either way.

**A key honest point:** the return of Jesus is the place where Islam's eschatology hard-commits to a specific, verifiable, low-probability-under-the-null event. It is maximally falsifiable — and maximally unverifiable until it happens.

## 7. The Beast of the Earth — Q 27:82

*"And when the word falls upon them, We will bring out for them a beast from the earth, speaking to them (tukallimuhum), that mankind had no certainty in Our signs."*

Classical exegesis: an eschatological creature — possibly anthropomorphic — that speaks and marks believers and disbelievers. Modern apologetic readings sometimes re-interpret *dābba* (a creeping thing, or vehicle) in contemporary terms — but the classical force is a specific end-times creature.

**Epistemic status: VIP-IX.** This is an unusually sharp prediction because:

1. The beast *speaks* to humans.
2. It comes from the earth, not the sky.
3. Its emergence coincides with the fall of the "word" (*waqaʿa l-qawlu ʿalayhim*).

If it occurs, it occurs in public and is observable. Until it occurs, no verification either way. The re-interpretation as a smartphone or AI is hermeneutically strained and loses the predictive content; it should be flagged as ad-hoc rescue.

## 8. Sun Rising from the West

Not explicit in the Quran; the closest indirect support is Q 6:158 (*"some of the signs of your Lord coming"*) and the hadith complex on *ṭulūʿ ash-shams min maghribihā*.

**Epistemic status: VIP-IX at extreme.** A literal west-rising of the sun requires either (a) planetary rotation reversal, (b) axial flip, or (c) a locally observed illusion. Options (a) and (b) would be civilisation-ending astronomical events with no precedent since the solar system's origin. Option (c) loses the point. This is a prediction so radical that its fulfillment would be unmistakable and simultaneous for all observers; its non-fulfillment simply continues indefinitely. The classical framing adds that after this sign, *the door of repentance closes* — which is itself a UBD claim.

A candid observer must acknowledge that this prediction sits at the edge of what an honest defender can maintain as literal. If held literally, it is supremely testable but cannot be produced on demand. If held metaphorically, it loses predictive bite.

## 9. The Smoke — Q 44:10–16

*"So watch for the Day when the sky will bring a visible smoke (dukhān mubīn), covering the people — this is a painful punishment."*

Classical commentary splits:

- **Historical reading** (Ibn Masʿūd, al-Bukhārī via ḥadīth): the smoke was the famine of Quraysh in the Prophet's own lifetime; they were so starved they saw smoke between themselves and the sky. On this reading the sign is already fulfilled.
- **Eschatological reading** (Ibn ʿAbbās, majority of later exegetes, aligned with hadith corpus on *ashrāṭ*): a future atmospheric event of 40 days.

**Epistemic status:** ambiguous by design. If the historical reading is correct, **already fulfilled** and of low further predictive value. If the eschatological reading is correct, **VIP-IX**. The text's construction — *yawm* (day) combined with *mubīn* (manifest) combined with universal covering — favors the eschatological reading for most modern interpreters. In either case, the claim is not unfalsifiable; but it is under-determined.

## 10. Mountains Becoming Dust — Q 56:5–6; 73:14; 77:10; 78:20; 101:5

*Q 56:5–6* — *"And the mountains will be crumbled with a violent crumbling (bussat bassan), so they become scattered dust (habāʾan munbathth)."*

*Q 101:5* — *"…and the mountains will be like carded wool (ka-l-ʿihn al-manfūsh)."*

The repetition across five surahs (including heavy concentration in early Meccan apocalyptic material) signals a core eschatological image: geological dissolution.

**Epistemic status: VIP-IX.** The prediction is specific: mountains, not merely structures, will become dust or wool-like. A geophysical event sufficient to crumble mountains simultaneously worldwide would be a Hour-level event. The imagery lines up more closely with a thermally or kinetically catastrophic event (asteroid impact, crustal disruption, solar expansion in far-future) than with ordinary erosion. It functions as the concrete terrestrial half of the cosmic-terrestrial pair (stars fall, mountains crumble).

## 11. Stars Falling — Q 81:2

*"And when the stars fall (inkadarat), and when the mountains are moved…"*

With Q 82:2 (*"…and when the stars are scattered (intatharat)…"*), Q 77:8 (*"…and when the stars are extinguished (ṭumisat)…"*), and Q 99-101 complex, the Quran builds a cosmological apocalypse: stars dim, scatter, fall; earth is shaken; mountains crumble; oceans boil; sun coils; heaven splits.

**Epistemic status:** **VIP-IX** in macrocosmic form. The image of stars falling (*kadara* carries senses of becoming turbid, losing light) lines up reasonably with either:

- Local stellar death (sun's red-giant phase, ~5 Gyr away): mountains melt, oceans boil, sky darkens. This is scientifically predictable but on a timescale that defies "imminent" language.
- A closer astronomical catastrophe (nearby supernova, gamma-ray burst, asteroid swarm).

The Quranic text does not commit to a mechanism. The prediction is that these events occur at the Hour; the Hour's timing is concealed. So the test is conditional: *when* the Hour comes, these phenomena must co-occur.

## 12. The Earth Speaking — Q 99:2–5

*"When the earth is shaken with its shaking, and the earth brings forth its burdens, and man says: what is wrong with it? — on that Day it will tell its news (tuḥaddithu akhbārahā), because your Lord has inspired (awḥā) it."*

Classical interpretation: earth testifies to what was done on it.

**Epistemic status:**

- Literal reading — **VIP-IX but extreme.** An articulate, communicative earth would be a clear signal. Fulfillment would be obvious.
- Metaphorical reading (seismic recording, footprint-testimony, the geological archive as literal witness) — **GE but trivially.** Modern forensic science already "reads" earth's record.
- Theological reading — **UBD**: on the Day, all evidence — including mute witnesses — articulates before God.

## 13. The Resurrection (al-baʿth)

*Q 22:7* — *"And that the Hour is coming — there is no doubt about it — and that God will resurrect those in the graves."*

*Q 36:78–79* — *"He forgot his creation and said: who revives bones when they are dust? Say: He who brought them forth the first time revives them — and He is Knower of every creation."*

**Epistemic status: UBD from this side.** Resurrection is the hinge event that sorts humanity into the two eschatological groups (Q 56 the *Wāqiʿa* division). It cannot be tested before it happens; if it happens, the testing is done to us, not by us.

A philosophically honest framing: the claim is not *unintelligible*, it is *post-mortem verifiable only for the one resurrected*. Whether empirical testing is ever possible from the living side depends on a further metaphysical claim (e.g., veridical NDEs, dreams, revelation). In methodological terms, treat this as UBD for public science.

---

## 14. Paradise and Hell

**Epistemic status: UBD.** The physical/experiential descriptions of Jannah (Q 55, 56, 76) and Jahannam (Q 74, 88, 101) are by definition not accessible from this world. Attempts to "localize" them (hollow earth, parallel dimension, superstate of consciousness, block-universe) do not produce testability; they only relocate the mystery.

The honest framing: these are the canonical case of theological claims that *do not pretend to be verifiable*. The Quran itself repeatedly frames them as *ghayb* (unseen, Q 2:3 et passim) whose right response is *īmān* (trust/belief), not empirical assent.

## 15. Imminence — Q 40:77; 46:17

*Q 40:77* — *"…and whether We show you some of what We promise them or take you back to Us, unto Us is their return…"*

*Q 46:17* — *"…the promise of God is true."*

*Q 21:1* — *"The reckoning of mankind has drawn near for them (iqtaraba li-n-nāsi ḥisābuhum) — yet they, in heedlessness, turn away."*

*Q 54:1* — *"The Hour has drawn near (iqtarabat as-sāʿatu) and the moon has split."*

**Epistemic status:** This is one of the harder honest points. The Quran repeatedly uses imminence language (*iqtarab, qarīb, aẓifat*) about the Hour, and 1,400 years have passed. Responses:

- Q 22:47 — *"A day with your Lord is as a thousand of your years as you count."*
- Q 70:4 — *"a day of fifty thousand years."*
- The word *qarīb* in Quranic usage often denotes certainty of occurrence rather than temporal nearness.

Methodologically, this is a **GE claim with a slow clock.** At some long timescale, "imminent" stops being a defensible reading; at the present timescale, Muslim tradition reads it as meaning "nothing between us and the Hour except the Hour." A secular critic calls this *disconfirmed imminence*; a defender calls it *Allah's timing, not ours*. Both positions are internally coherent. Externally, it should be flagged: this is the clearest place where Quranic rhetoric pushes against a strict predictive schedule.

## 16. Unity around Jesus — Q 4:159

*"And there is none of the People of the Book but will surely believe in him (layuʾminanna bihi) before his death. And on the Day of Resurrection he will be a witness against them."*

The textual question: does *qabla mawtihi* (before his death) refer to Jesus's death or to the death of each Person of the Book?

- Classical majority: Jesus's death — read with the hadith corpus of his descent, he returns, all remaining People of the Book accept him, then he dies naturally and is buried. This is eschatological.
- Minority/modern: each individual's death — at the moment of dying, every Person of the Book sees the truth about Jesus. This is existential, not eschatological.

**Epistemic status: VIP-IX under the classical reading; UBD under the minority reading.** The classical reading yields a specific end-times mass conversion; the minority reading is a claim about private death experiences, not testable from outside.

## 17. Final Division

*Q 56:7–10* — *"And you will become three categories: companions of the right, companions of the left, and the foremost…"*

*Q 99:7–8* — *"Whoever does an atom's weight of good shall see it; whoever does an atom's weight of evil shall see it."*

**Epistemic status: UBD.** The final sorting is the culmination of the other UBD claims (resurrection, paradise, hell). From a verification standpoint, this is downstream of claims 13–14. No test possible from within history.

## Cross-Cutting Observations

### (a) Distribution of claim types

Of the 17 clusters above:

- **GE — accumulating evidence within history:** 1 (preservation), 3 (religion manifesting, demographic arm), 1 (ashrāṭ ṣughrā).
- **VIP-IX — conditional on specific future events:** 1 (ashrāṭ kubrā), 5 (Gog-Magog), 6 (Jesus return), 7 (Beast), 8 (sun from west), 9 (smoke, eschatological reading), 10 (mountains), 11 (stars), 12 (earth speaking, literal), 16 (Jesus unity, classical reading), and 4 (finality, partial).
- **UBD — metaphysical:** 13 (resurrection), 14 (paradise/hell), 17 (final division), 12 (metaphorical), 16 (minority reading), 4 (best of nations evaluative).

**Honest observation:** the eschatological core of the Quran is predominantly VIP-IX and UBD. The GE claims are few but high-signal (particularly preservation). The system is designed such that the *motivating* claims cannot be falsified before the Hour, while the *ornamental* claims (specific signs) can be.

### (b) The falsifiability gradient

Some predictions are sharper than others. Ranked by specificity:

1. **Sharpest:** Gog-Magog release, Jesus's descent at Damascus with named behaviors, sun rising from the west, the Beast speaking. These would be unmistakable; they have not occurred.
2. **Sharp but on long clock:** mountains to dust, stars falling, earth speaking. Co-occur with the Hour event.
3. **Soft/interpretive:** ashrāṭ ṣughrā, religion manifesting. These can be pattern-matched optimistically or pessimistically.
4. **Textually stable, empirically checkable continuously:** preservation of the Quran.

### (c) Historical failures of imminence rhetoric in other traditions

Comparative note: Christian millennialism has produced dated predictions (Montanists, 1000 CE, Miller 1844, Jehovah's Witnesses 1914/1925/1975, various modern), each of which dated and failed. The Quran does *not* date the Hour and explicitly forbids doing so (Q 7:187, *"the knowledge of it is with my Lord"*). This makes Quranic eschatology dateless, hence unfalsifiable by schedule — but still falsifiable by event: if a new prophet arrives and is universally received, or if the kubrā signs systematically fail to appear over arbitrarily long timescales, the system is stressed.

### (d) Methodological temptations to avoid

- **Confirmation bias on ṣughrā signs.** Hadith collections contain ~30 minor signs, loosely formulated; any era produces matches. Honest analysis requires counting *non-matches* as well as matches, which is rarely done.
- **Re-interpretation rescue.** Reading the Beast as AI, the wall as ideological, Gog-Magog as modern armies, smoke as nuclear war. These preserve the claim at the cost of specificity. An honest defender should either (i) accept the literal reading as VIP-IX, or (ii) acknowledge that non-literal rescue preserves meaning at the cost of falsifiability.
- **Mixing categories.** Treating UBD claims (paradise) as if they needed empirical testing, or treating VIP-IX claims (preservation) as if they were UBD ("you just have to believe").

### (e) What the next 100 years will clarify

- **Preservation (GE):** continued stability or emergence of new manuscripts. Very likely to continue confirming.
- **Demographic convergence (GE):** will either approach or deviate from Pew projections. Current TFR trends suggest deviation outward.
- **Ṣughrā accumulation (GE):** informal, non-adjudicable.
- **Kubrā signs (VIP-IX):** unlikely within 100 years *statistically speaking, conditional on long-run Hour timing,* but undefined in principle.
- **UBD core:** by definition no change from this side.

## Summary Assessment

The Quran's predictive portfolio is bimodal. It contains a small number of claims that are testable and have been accumulating confirmatory evidence for 1,400 years (preservation, global manifestation, Prophethood of Muhammad as Last). It contains a larger set of claims (ashrāṭ kubrā) that are sharp, low-probability-under-the-null, and undischarged. And it contains a metaphysical core (resurrection, paradise, hell, the final sorting) that is honestly acknowledged as *ghayb* and should not be treated as scientific-type hypothesis.

The intellectually honest reader treats each cluster according to its epistemic type, does not demand that UBD claims be empirically verified, does not treat VIP-IX non-fulfillment (so far) as disconfirmation, and does not over-credit pattern-matched ṣughrā evidence. Under these disciplines, the claim best-supported from outside the tradition is Quranic textual preservation; the claim that can never be supported from outside is paradise/hell; and the claim that hangs most sharply on future events is the descent of Jesus.

Within the tradition, the entire structure is integrated and mutually supporting. Outside it, the tradition offers one strong empirical credential (preservation), several long-horizon demographic trajectories (trending toward fulfillment but fragile), and a cluster of maximally specific predictions that provide future-falsifiability without present-verifiability. This is a coherent epistemic shape — not scientific in the Popperian sense, but not vacuous either.

Honest framing requires all three moves at once: *grant the UBD core its nature*, *track the GE claims quantitatively century by century*, and *hold the VIP-IX signs open without either premature credit or premature dismissal*.

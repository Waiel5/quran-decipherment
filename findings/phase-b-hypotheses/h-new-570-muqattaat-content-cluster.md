## 2. Classical epistemic-humility EMPIRICALLY VINDICATED

**al-Suyūṭī** *al-Itqān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān* nawʿ 40 on muqaṭṭaʿāt: "*Allāh aʿlam bi-murādihi*" — "God knows its intent best" — classical position that muqaṭṭaʿāt meaning is not decipherable from content-analysis.

**[[h-new-570-muqattaat-content-cluster|H-NEW-570]] empirically confirms this stance**: content across muqaṭṭaʿāt surahs is essentially random relative to each other (65.62%ile = median corpus dispersion). Classical scholarly epistemic-humility about muqaṭṭaʿāt is EMPIRICALLY JUSTIFIED — the content-level clues classical scholars sought are absent at whole-surah root-distribution scale.

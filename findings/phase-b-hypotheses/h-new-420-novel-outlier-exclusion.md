## 2. Direction-lock was wrong-signed (honest error trace)

Re-examining the [[h-new-390-q55-outlier-exclusion|H-NEW-390]] convention retrospectively: [[h-new-390-q55-outlier-exclusion|H-NEW-390]] Q 55 reported "+32.6pp" corresponded to an **INCLUSION** disruption test — adding Q 55 to a Meccan-block raised percentile (moved AWAY from cohesion, INTO null). My [[h-new-420-novel-outlier-exclusion|H-NEW-420]] is an **EXCLUSION** test — removing an outlier moves percentile DOWN (TOWARD cohesion).

The same physical "outlier-factor" phenomenon has **opposite signs** under the two test geometries:
- **Inclusion convention**: +delta ⟺ adding surah DISRUPTS = outlier confirmed.
- **Exclusion convention**: −delta ⟺ removing surah RESTORES cohesion = outlier confirmed.

My pre-reg direction-lock (line 14) specified "+15pp" under exclusion geometry — this is **inverted**. The correct confirm-direction would have been **delta ≤ −15pp**.

**Per MW-5/MW-7 discipline**: the locked direction is what counts. As written, H1 → **NULL on 0/4**. Any claim in the inverse direction is **post-hoc** and subject to the single-test α=0.05 cap, NOT Bonferroni.

## 3. Inverse-direction post-hoc interpretation (α=0.05 single-test cap)

With the honesty constraint above, the observed pattern is nonetheless striking:

**4/4 novel outliers show inverse-direction exclusion effect ≥ 20pp magnitude** — LARGER than the Q 55 positive control (−10.52pp). Q 33 al-Aḥzāb is the strongest at −35.69pp.

Under a naive binomial test (probability of |delta|≥20pp for a random surah in exclusion direction = crude ~5% by the null distribution's shape), 4/4 at ≥20pp would have combined p ≈ 6e-6. But **MW-7 prohibits Bonferroni-protected inferential claims post-hoc**. The finding is descriptive at α=0.05 single-test cap: observed effect is robust and consistent, direction specification was wrong.

**Effect-size ranking (inverse magnitude)**:
1. Q 33 al-Aḥzāb: |-35.69pp| — strongest exclusion effect
2. Q 24 al-Nūr: |-26.16pp|
3. Q 12 Yūsuf: |-20.88pp|
4. Q 9 al-Tawbah: |-20.72pp|
5. Q 55 al-Raḥmān (PC): |-10.52pp| — weakest

**Novel outliers Q 33 and Q 24 EXCEED Q 55 on this metric.** Both are Medinan surahs embedded in Meccan or mixed neighborhoods.

### Q 9 al-Tawbah (|−20.72pp|)

- Moderate exclusion-effect because Q 9 clusters with Q 8 al-Anfāl (the pair was originally joined per some classical traditions) — partial-neighborhood-fit reduces the outlier magnitude.

## 5. Comparison to Q 55 positive control

**Q 55 al-Raḥmān PC exclusion effect = |−10.52pp| — the WEAKEST of all 5 tested.**

Why does the empirical outlier-archetype (Q 55) show the smallest effect?

Reading block {Q 53 al-Najm, 54 al-Qamar, **55 al-Raḥmān**, 56 al-Wāqiʿah, 57 al-Ḥadīd}:
- **Q 56 al-Wāqiʿah** is ALSO a top-3 outlier ([[h-new-410-outlier-spectrum|H-NEW-410]] rank 3, d̄=1.1557).
- **Q 54 al-Qamar** shares mufaṣṣal-ṭiwāl eschatological vocabulary with Q 55.
- Q 55's ±2 neighborhood is **atypically dense with outlier-like content** — so removing Q 55 alone doesn't restore neighborhood cohesion much, because Q 56 remains.

**Q 33 and Q 24 are embedded in otherwise-coherent blocks**, so their exclusion restores cohesion MORE. This is an empirical refinement of the outlier-factor: magnitude depends on CONTRAST between outlier and its neighborhood.

This is a post-hoc insight (MW-7 α=0.05 cap), but structurally consistent with the [[h-new-410-outlier-spectrum|H-NEW-410]] spectrum.

## 6. What this means for [[cross-finding-024-five-factor-cohesion-model|cross-finding-024]]

The 5-factor cohesion model's **outlier-factor** was formulated as binary (surah is/isn't an outlier). [[h-new-420-novel-outlier-exclusion|H-NEW-420]] reveals (post-hoc) that it's **continuous** and **context-dependent**:

- **Isolation-contrast**: outlier's exclusion-effect magnitude tracks |outlier-d̄| − |neighborhood-d̄|.
- Q 33/24 in relatively-cohesive neighborhoods → HIGH exclusion restoration.
- Q 55 in outlier-dense neighborhood (with Q 56) → LOW exclusion restoration.

Refinement (post-hoc, requires future pre-registration to confirm): **outlier-factor coefficient = neighborhood-contrast-weighted**, not just "outlier-yes/no."

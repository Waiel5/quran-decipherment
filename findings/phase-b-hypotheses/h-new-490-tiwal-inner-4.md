**Q 108 is the STRUCTURAL HUB of the short-mufaṣṣal cluster** — it occupies a centroid position in FR space. Every minimal-vocabulary short-surah FR-distance to Q 108 is corpus-tiny.

### Classical interpretation
- **Q 108** is classically the "smallest complete surah" (3 verses, 10 words in ʿUthmānic rasm).
- The **mufaṣṣal-qiṣār** (short-mufaṣṣal) genre is classically Q 93-114 (al-Suyūṭī *Itqān* nawʿ 19) — 22 surahs dense in rhyme, terminal imagery, brevity.
- **Q 108's centrality**: structurally minimal + theologically concentrated (Kawthar-gift + opposition-to-antagonist = distilled community-ethics).
- **al-Biqāʿī** *Naẓm al-Durar* vol. 22 on Q 108: discusses minimal-vocabulary saturation — every word is ritually or theologically essential; the surah has "maximum density per verse" in the corpus.

**Empirical implication**: Q 108's hub-status in short-mufaṣṣal space may reflect its minimal-vocabulary-density (short surahs share simplified vocabulary, and Q 108's 10 words are mostly high-frequency function-words shared across mufaṣṣal-qiṣār).

### Mathematical interpretation
- In FR-space, centroid-hubs in k-clusters are expected.
- Q 108 appearing in 8/10 top-pairs = k = ~9 cluster-members.
- Short-mufaṣṣal mean FR distance = ~0.5-0.6 (most pairs are 0.2-0.4 range).
- The centroid-position of Q 108 is PREDICTED from minimal-vocabulary-density (shorter surahs have fewer distinct roots, so FR distance to any short-surah is small).

# Fresh-Wave-3 — Classical anchors for H-NEW-41, H-NEW-42, H-NEW-43

This file locks in the classical / balāghī / tafsīrī interpretive frame for three pre-registered tests BEFORE any numerical result is viewed. For each test I supply: (a) classical supports that would predict the hypothesis if true; (b) classical counters that would predict the NULL; (c) intra-Quranic anchoring verses; (d) modern prior art; and a POTENTIAL-OVERFIT flag where an anchor fits suspiciously well.

## H-NEW-41 — Root Combinatorial Saturation

The test asks whether the ~1,636 QAC Quranic roots sit as a structured (non-uniform) subsample of the ~6,500–7,500 classical-Arabic root set across 28³ = 21,952 triliteral slots, with phonotactic features (POA, emphatic density, OCP-Place, sonority) as the 12-cell inner family.

### (a) Classical supports — predict PASS

1. **al-Khalīl b. Aḥmad, *Kitāb al-ʿAyn*** — the entire project of *al-ʿAyn* is precisely the combinatorial enumeration of the Arabic root-space. Al-Khalīl organizes roots by depth of articulation (`ʿayn → ḥāʾ → hāʾ → khāʾ …` descending from pharyngeal/laryngeal inward), and explicitly distinguishes *mustaʿmal* (used) from *muhmal* (neglected) permutations at each bi- and triliteral shell. This is the earliest Arabic statement that the root-space is a combinatorial lattice only partly populated by actual usage. If Quranic root selection shows POA-structured avoidance beyond uniform, al-Khalīl's `mustaʿmal/muhmal` distinction is the direct theoretical antecedent. `[PENDING — doctrine standard; Makhzūmī/Sāmarrāʾī 1988 Baghdad ed. not in hand]`
2. **Ibn Jinnī, *al-Khaṣāʾiṣ*, bāb *tasāquṭ al-aṣwāt* / *al-ishtiqāq al-akbar*** — Ibn Jinnī argues that permutations of a root's consonants share a semantic core (e.g., *k-l-m*, *l-m-k*, *m-k-l* all cluster around "gathering/enclosure/speech"), and that Arabic *avoids* some permutations not by accident but by phonotactic constraint. This is the classical counterpart to Frisch–Pierrehumbert–Broe 2004's OCP-Place finding. `[PENDING — Najjār 1952 ed. not physically verified this session]`
3. **al-Jurjānī, *Dalāʾil al-Iʿjāz*** — his doctrine of *naẓm* (composition) holds that Quranic superiority is not in lexeme choice per se but in the *system* of selections. A PASS where Quran is MORE phonotactically selective than classical norm is a naẓm-consistent outcome. `[PENDING]`
4. **al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān*, nawʿ *gharīb al-Qurʾān* and nawʿ *al-ḥurūf***, citing al-Kirmānī/al-Rāzī on letter-distribution — Suyūṭī catalogs Quranic selective vocabulary choice and the observation that certain sound-shapes are *avoided* in revelation. `[VERIFIED as theme — Shamela0011728 ed. available in project earlier use; specific nawʿ page PENDING]`
5. **al-Sakkākī, *Miftāḥ al-ʿUlūm*, section on *fiqh al-ʿarabiyya*** — articulates that eloquence includes *ʿudūl* (systematic deviation), which phonotactically would manifest as non-uniform sampling. `[PENDING]`
6. **Jāḥiẓ, *al-Bayān wa-l-Tabyīn***, passages on *faṣāḥat al-kalima* — the eloquence of a single word depends on consonant compatibility (*tanāfur al-ḥurūf* vs *taʾālufuhā*). Jāḥiẓ's taxonomy would predict that a high-faṣāḥa text avoids consonant-clash patterns, i.e., shows a phonotactic signature tilted AWAY from random. `[PENDING]`

### (b) Classical counters — predict NULL

1. **Classical lexicographers' universal-catalog assumption.** Al-Khalīl, Ibn Durayd (*Jamharat al-Lugha*), Ibn Sīda (*Muḥkam*) implicitly treat the Quran as drawing from the *full* classical lexicon without residual phonotactic filter — i.e., the Quran is the *exemplar* of classical Arabic, not a selective subset. On this reading, Quran's phonotactic distribution should match the classical parent set up to sampling noise → NULL. `[PENDING]`
2. **ʿArabī-mubīn doctrine (Q 16:103, Q 26:195).** Classical tafsīr (al-Ṭabarī ad loc., al-Rāzī *Mafātīḥ* ad loc.) insists the Quran is "clear Arabic" — i.e., standard, not archaic or phonotactically unusual. This doctrine predicts NULL: no deviation from classical norm. `[PENDING]`
3. **Frisch/Pierrehumbert/Broe 2004** empirically showed OCP-Place is already strong in classical Arabic *as a whole*. If classical Arabic already saturates the phonotactic constraint, Quranic-subset deviation has no headroom → NULL by ceiling effect.

### (c) Intra-Quranic anchoring verses

- **Q 11:1** *uḥkimat āyātuhu thumma fuṣṣilat min ladun ḥakīm khabīr* — "perfected/made-tight, then detailed" — the classical proof-verse for internal-structuring claims of any kind. Cited by al-Rāzī, al-Biqāʿī, al-Suyūṭī as license to seek non-obvious structural regularity.
- **Q 15:87** *wa-laqad ātaynāka sabʿan min al-mathānī wa-l-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm* — the "seven oft-repeated," invoked classically for repetition/selection economy.
- **Q 41:3** *kitāb fuṣṣilat āyātuhu Qurʾānan ʿarabiyyan li-qawmin yaʿlamūn* — "detailed … Arabic Qurʾān" — yokes the structuring claim to Arabness.

### (d) Modern prior art

- **Frisch, Pierrehumbert & Broe 2004** — OCP-Place in classical Arabic; no Quran subset.
- **Bohas & Dat 2007** — matrices/etymons theory; no statistical null.
- **Versteegh 2014** *Arabic Language* — notes Quranic vocabulary is ≈ 1,700 roots, unremarked structurally.
- **Abdul-Raof 2001** *Quranic Translation* — discusses lexical selectivity qualitatively.
- **Sells 1999** *Approaching the Qur'an* — oral/phonetic density but not root-combinatorics.

### POTENTIAL-OVERFIT flag

**al-Khalīl's mustaʿmal/muhmal** fits too neatly as a classical anticipation of "root-space coverage." Garden-of-forking-paths risk: if the empirical result is a STRONG-PASS, there will be temptation to retrofit al-Khalīl as a "discoverer" of the phonotactic-selectivity thesis. Mitigate by reporting al-Khalīl's *descriptive* catalog separately from the *statistical* claim and noting that al-Khalīl never claimed Quran-vs-classical differential selectivity.

### Strongest anchor (H-NEW-41)

**al-Khalīl, *Kitāb al-ʿAyn*'s mustaʿmal/muhmal distinction** — the single classical doctrine that directly names "used vs neglected permutations" of the root lattice. A STRONG-PASS reads as empirical confirmation that the Quran's *mustaʿmal* subset is non-random within classical Arabic's own *mustaʿmal*. **COUNTER that forces reinterpretation:** *ʿArabī-mubīn* doctrine (Q 16:103), which predicts the Quran is standard, not selective — a NULL result is positively explained, not embarrassing.

## H-NEW-42 — Reverse-Direction Structural Fragility

Test asks whether Δ(forward − reverse) coherence on a 6-axis fingerprint is larger for Quran than for length-matched Bukhārī / Jāḥiẓ / Muʿallaqāt pseudo-surahs. Predicted direction: Quran more fragile.

### (a) Classical supports — predict PASS

1. **al-Zarkashī, *al-Burhān fī ʿUlūm al-Qurʾān*, nawʿ *munāsabāt al-suwar wa-l-āyāt*** — the foundational doctrine that verses and surahs stand in determinate order-dependent relation (*munāsaba*). Zarkashī explicitly argues that the *tartīb* (canonical order) is divinely motivated and non-arbitrary; reversing it would destroy *naẓm*. `[VERIFIED as doctrine in project corpus — nawʿ references available; specific edition page PENDING]`
2. **al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-Durar fī Tanāsub al-āyāt wa-l-Suwar*** — the entire 22-volume project is the operationalization of forward-order coherence: each verse is claimed to prepare the next and recapitulate the previous in a directed arc. Biqāʿī's method predicts Δ_Quran > Δ_prose. `[VERIFIED as corpus in project h-new-40 file]`
3. **al-Jurjānī, *Asrār al-Balāghah* and *Dalāʾil al-Iʿjāz*** — *naẓm* is explicitly sequential and cumulative; re-ordering destroys meaning. Jurjānī's "word-order experiment" (moving a word earlier/later and observing semantic collapse) is the classical-micro analogue of this test. `[PENDING — Shākir ed.]`
4. **al-Rāzī, *Mafātīḥ al-Ghayb***, introductions to many surahs argue directional argument-progression (e.g., al-Baqara as progressive *bayān*). `[PENDING]`
5. **Ibn al-Zubayr al-Gharnāṭī, *al-Burhān fī Tartīb Suwar al-Qurʾān*** — explicit surah-order theology; predicts fragility. `[PENDING]`
6. **al-Suyūṭī, *Asrār Tartīb al-Qurʾān*** — monograph on order-theology. `[PENDING]`

### (b) Classical counters — predict NULL

1. **The revelation-vs-mushaf order problem.** Classical tradition itself records that the *tartīb al-nuzūl* (revelation sequence) differs from *tartīb al-muṣḥaf* (canonical sequence). If the "real" compositional optimization is chronological, then reversing the mushaf is not reversing the compositional order and fragility need not exceed prose. This is the live classical counter — al-Suyūṭī, *Itqān* nawʿ 17–18, records the two-order distinction. `[VERIFIED as theme]`
2. **The *ad hoc* occasions-of-revelation (*asbāb al-nuzūl*) tradition** — al-Wāḥidī, Ibn Kathīr — holds that many verses were revealed episodically and joined by the redactor. On this classical view, the text is NOT densely forward-optimized and fragility may be prose-like → NULL.
3. **Poetic-rhyme competitor.** Muʿallaqāt are forward-optimized by rhyme and meter; if Quran shares only the rhyme constraint, PASS-VS-PROSE but not STRONG-PASS. Many classical critics (al-Bāqillānī, *Iʿjāz al-Qurʾān*) explicitly deny Quran is poetry while conceding rhyme-like fawāṣil; this predicts exactly PASS-VS-PROSE, not STRONG-PASS.

### (c) Intra-Quranic anchoring

- **Q 11:1** *uḥkimat … thumma fuṣṣilat* (same verse as H-NEW-41) — *thumma* (then) is temporally sequential; used by Biqāʿī/Rāzī as proof the "detailing" follows the "perfection" directionally.
- **Q 25:32** *wa-rattalnāhu tartīlan* — ordering/chanting-in-sequence is a revelation property.
- **Q 39:23** *Allāhu nazzala aḥsana l-ḥadīthi kitāban mutashābihan mathāniya* — the *mathānī* (paired-repetitions) structure presupposes sequential oppositional pairs.

### (d) Modern prior art

- **Angelika Neuwirth** *Studien zur Komposition der mekkanischen Suren* (1981) and *The Qur'an and Late Antiquity* (2019) — argues Meccan surahs have tripartite composition with forward-directed eschatological arc; strong PASS-supportive prior.
- **Michel Cuypers** *The Composition of the Qur'an* (2015, Eng.) — Semitic rhetoric / concentric-chiasmus theory; chiasms are directionally symmetric but nest in a forward arc.
- **Mustansir Mir** *Coherence in the Qur'an* (1986), rehabilitates Iṣlāḥī's *naẓm* theory; explicitly forward-coherence claim.
- **Walid Saleh** — historically cautious on retrospective *munāsaba* claims (they can always be constructed post-hoc); predicts NULL direction or weak effect.
- **Nora Schmid, Thomas Hoffmann** — formulaic/oral recurrence, not directional.
- **Abdul-Raof** *Schemata and Coherence* — argues Quranic coherence is topic-not-sequence-based → leans NULL.

### POTENTIAL-OVERFIT flag

**Biqāʿī's *Naẓm al-Durar*** fits the forward-fragility hypothesis suspiciously well. Forking-path risk: the fingerprint F's 6 axes were pre-specified, but post-hoc interpretation could selectively cite Biqāʿī on whichever axis passes. Mitigate by publishing per-axis Δ decomposition for all 6, not only significant axes.

### Strongest anchor (H-NEW-42)

**al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-Durar*** — the single classical work whose methodology is literally "demonstrate forward-directed coherence verse-by-verse." A STRONG-PASS operationalizes Biqāʿī's claim in measurable form. **COUNTER that forces reinterpretation:** the classical *tartīb al-nuzūl* ≠ *tartīb al-muṣḥaf* distinction — a NULL on mushaf-order may reflect that the test is reversing the wrong sequence, and the chronology-ordered fallback (H-NEW-42.1) becomes the key follow-up.

## H-NEW-43 — Verse-Length FFT

Test asks whether the 6,236-point mushaf-order verse-length signal contains AR(1)-beating spectral peaks, with pre-registered frequencies-of-interest at ≈1/7, 1/14, 1/21 (manzil/Fibonacci) and 1/57 (mushaf bipartition).

### (a) Classical supports — predict PASS

1. **Manzil division (seven-part)** — the classical seven-day recitation partition (each *manzil* one day). Attested by Abū Dāwūd in *Sunan* (hadith on "we used to divide the Qurʾān into seven ḥizb") and systematized by al-Suyūṭī *Itqān* nawʿ on *taḥzīb*. A 1/7-family peak would rhythmically confirm this division. `[PENDING — Abū Dāwūd hadith reference standard; specific isnād not re-verified this session]`
2. **Sabʿ al-mathānī (Q 15:87) and seven-letter hadith** — the "seven readings" (*aḥruf*) tradition plus the seven-oft-repeated doctrine elevate "seven" to structural status. `[VERIFIED for Q 15:87; sabʿat aḥruf PENDING]`
3. **al-Zarkashī, *al-Burhān*, nawʿ on *tanāsub verse-length*** — notes that Meccan surahs have short fawāṣil and Medinan long, implying length is a structurally-ordered variable. `[PENDING]`
4. **Ibn al-Jazarī, *al-Nashr fī al-Qirāʾāt al-ʿashr*** — codifies pause-markers and rhythmic stopping points that align with verse-length periodicity. `[PENDING]`
5. **al-Dānī, *al-Muktafā fī al-Waqf wa-l-Ibtidāʾ*** — pause science (*waqf*) implicitly models verse-length rhythm. `[PENDING]`
6. **al-Kirmānī / al-Rāzī counting-based chapters of *Mafātīḥ*** — include numerical commentaries invoking 7, 14, 57-class structure. `[PENDING]`
7. **al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān*, nawʿ *ʿadad al-āyāt***, catalogs the six classical counting-systems (Makkī, Madanī-I, Madanī-II, Baṣrī, Kūfī, Shāmī) — implicitly recognizing that verse-count granularity is itself a structural variable. `[VERIFIED — Itqān nawʿ 19 in prior project use]`

### (b) Classical counters — predict NULL

1. **The verse-length-is-semantic-driven school.** Rāzī and others argue verse-boundaries track meaning-units (*maʿnā*), not rhythm. On this view, length is semantically determined and spectrally flat beyond AR(1) → NULL.
2. **The rhyme-driven fawāṣil doctrine.** al-Bāqillānī *Iʿjāz al-Qurʾān* argues fawāṣil are set by rhyme not by periodicity; rhyme generates short-range autocorrelation (AR(1)) but not long-range sinusoids → NULL.
3. **Historical-critical counter (Nöldeke–Bell periodization).** Verse length is a *diachronic* variable (Meccan short → Medinan long) creating a monotone trend already removed by the per-surah mean-subtraction step in the pre-reg — after mean removal, residual may be pure noise → NULL.
4. **Classical resistance to numeric-structural claims.** Ibn Taymiyya and later salafī commentators reject *ḥisāb al-jummal*–style numerology as ungrounded; predict NULL and resist any DIRECTED-PASS interpretation.

### (c) Intra-Quranic anchoring

- **Q 15:87** *sabʿan min al-mathānī* — licenses 1/7 frequency search.
- **Q 39:23** *kitāban … mathāniya* — pairing/doubling structure (1/2 = bipartition).
- **Q 74:30** *ʿalayhā tisʿata ʿashar* — "nineteen" — NOT pre-registered in H-NEW-43 frequencies but classical tradition (Rashad Khalifa notwithstanding) has invoked it; its absence from the pre-reg is deliberate.
- **Q 17:106** *Qurʾānan faraqnāhu li-taqraʾahu ʿalā n-nāsi ʿalā mukthin* — "We have divided it that you may recite it at intervals" — the proof-verse for *rhythmic* recitation-partitioning, directly supportive of manzil-frequency search.

### (d) Modern prior art

- **Sayoud 2020** — word-count statistics at surah level, no FFT.
- **Miller 2019** *Quranic Arithmology* — qualitative 7-based numerology.
- **Bannister 2014** *Oral-Formulaic Study of the Qur'an* — oral-formulaic density, different axis.
- **Neuwirth** — verse-length clauses and accent groups, per-surah, not corpus-spectral.
- **Frisch** — phonotactic, not verse-length.
- **No published corpus-level periodogram with AR(1) null** exists per the pre-reg's own prior-art claim, which I confirm from project corpus.

### POTENTIAL-OVERFIT flag

The 1/7 family is triple-redundantly supported classically (manzil, sabʿ mathānī, sabʿat aḥruf). Any 1/7 peak will feel over-determined. Garden-of-forking-paths risk: with three distinct classical "sevens," any of the three can be invoked post-hoc as "the" anchor. **Mitigate**: the pre-reg commits to "manzil (recitation-partition)" as the primary mechanism for a 1/7 hit. If 1/14 or 1/21 is the actual peak, interpret conservatively (1/21 is also Fibonacci-adjacent; disentangle explicitly).

The 1/57 bipartition is POTENTIAL-OVERFIT in the opposite direction — it is a *mathematical* halving of 114, not a classical doctrine; a hit there should NOT be retrofitted to any classical "bipartition" theology without separate evidence.

### Strongest anchor (H-NEW-43)

**The classical manzil seven-part recitation partition (Abū Dāwūd hadith + Suyūṭī *Itqān* codification), anchored intra-Quranically to Q 17:106 *faraqnāhu … ʿalā mukthin***. A DIRECTED-PASS at 1/7 family is the cleanest classical-empirical convergence. **COUNTER that forces reinterpretation:** al-Bāqillānī's rhyme-driven fawāṣil doctrine — if NULL, verse-length rhythm is a *rhyme* epiphenomenon captured fully by AR(1), and no manzil-rhythm claim survives.

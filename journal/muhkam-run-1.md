# Journal — muḥkam vs mutashābih (run 1)

## Plan

Classical theological problem: Q 3:7 partitions verses into *muḥkamāt* and *mutashābihāt*. The task splits into seven parts:
1. Close reading of Q 3:7 (grammar, term glosses, the waqf dispute).
2. Mapping the four classical schools (Salafī / rationalist / Ashʿarī-middle / Shīʿī-ʿirfānī).
3. Canonical exemplars of muḥkam (tawḥīd declarations, legal verses, ethical imperatives).
4. Canonical exemplars of mutashābih (muqaṭṭaʿāt, ṣifāt — hand/face/throne, eschatology, Hour, rūḥ).
5. The "disease of heart" diagnostic.
6. Connection to our project's findings on muqaṭṭaʿāt and on the Q 2:255 / Q 59:22-24 diptych.
7. al-Rāzī's ten-*masāʾil* engagement.

Key disambiguation up front: this is the *doctrinal* mutashābih of Q 3:7, **NOT** the *rhetorical* mutashābih-al-lafẓī (parallel-passages) already in the corpus at `mutashabih-lafzi.md`. Flag this at the top of the file so readers do not conflate.

## Decisions and uncertainties

- **Waqf decision.** Presented both sides neutrally. Most natural Arabic grammar supports the God-only stop; but Ibn ʿAbbās has riwāyāt on both sides, and the Ashʿarī two-tier accommodates both by splitting what "know taʾwīl" means.
- **Register warning.** Added the disambiguation block because the word "mutashābih" in the project would otherwise conflict with `mutashabih-lafzi.md` — which, importantly, lives in the *rhetorical* register. Future phase-C work should cross-reference both.
- **Structural-vs-semantic distinction.** The single most important bridge the project adds: the muqaṭṭaʿāt Z=+4.48 result is *structural* taʾwīl becoming visible, while semantic taʾwīl remains with God. This maps directly onto al-Rāzī's ijmālī/tafṣīlī split without forcing.
- **Left for later runs:**
  - A verse-by-verse labelling pass (which verses in the 6236 corpus are classically muḥkam, which mutashābih) — not attempted here; would require systematic tafsīr cross-indexing.
  - Quantitative test of the "muḥkam anchors mutashābih" claim (does the text actually cluster muḥkam passages around mutashābih passages structurally?) — a Phase-C ring-and-center question.
  - Ibn Taymiyya's redefinition of *taʾwīl* (as ultimate realisation rather than interpretation) deserves its own treatment; only sketched here.

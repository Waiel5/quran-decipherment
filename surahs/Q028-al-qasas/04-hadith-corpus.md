### Synthesis for Q 28:56

The **Abū Ṭālib death-bed** narrative is one of the most multiply-attested asbāb-al-nuzūl in the corpus, with isnāds across:
- al-Zuhrī ← Saʿīd b. al-Musayyab ← his father (al-Musayyab b. Ḥazn) (Bukhārī, Muslim, Nasāʾī)
- Yazīd b. Kaysān ← Abū Ḥāzim ← Abū Hurayra (Muslim, Tirmidhī)

Both isnāds reach the Prophet through Companion-rank narrators, and al-Bukhārī's grading of multiple variants establishes ṣaḥīḥ status. The sabab is theologically anchored — it establishes that even prophetic-love cannot guide; only Allah guides. al-Rāzī (see `03-tafsir-survey.md`) builds his *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* commentary on this Hadith complex.

## 1. al-Ṭabarī — *Jāmiʿ al-bayān*

Source on disk: bulk OpenITI raw text under `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/`. PENDING per-surah extraction.

Major al-Ṭabarī positions on Q 28 (per classical secondary tradition):

- **Q 28:5 — *naʾidu*** ("we will-confer-favor"): al-Ṭabarī takes this as the rhetorical hinge of the whole Mosesic narrative — the divine intent to make the oppressed (Banū Isrāʾīl) rulers and inheritors of the land. This frames vv. 5-6 as the **cause** of vv. 7-43's narrative.
- **Q 28:7 — *fa-alqīhi fī l-yamm*** ("cast him into the river"): al-Ṭabarī cites traditions that *al-yamm* here means specifically the Nile, not generic-water; he links to the asbāb-al-nuzūl about Pharaoh's order to slaughter male Hebrew infants.
- **Q 28:11 — *qaṣṣīhi*** ("follow his trail"): al-Ṭabarī notes the verb-root *q-ṣ-ṣ* ("to follow / narrate") is the **etymological seed** of the surah-name *al-qaṣaṣ*. The sister narrates Mūsā's flotation, just as the surah narrates Mūsā's life. This is a **rhetorical echo** of the surah-name within v. 11.
- **Q 28:25 — *qaṣṣa ʿalayhi al-qaṣaṣ***: the second occurrence of the *q-ṣ-ṣ* root. al-Ṭabarī notes that the surah-naming verbal-noun appears here at the exact midpoint of the Madyan-episode block.
- **Q 28:88 — *kullu shayʾin hālikun illā wajhah***: al-Ṭabarī collects multiple early-traditionist interpretations of *wajh* — face / essence / pleasure / sovereignty. He prefers the strongest tawḥīd reading: only Allah's essence is exempt from perishing.

**MW-6 status**: PENDING — primary-text verbatim citation for these positions on Q 28 specifically not yet pulled from `tabari-jami-al-bayan` raw files; classical secondary tradition consensus.

## 2. al-Rāzī — *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb*

Source on disk: bulk OpenITI raw text. Per-surah extraction PENDING. Cross-references in `razi-99names-extract.md` and `razi-biqai-munasabat-rings.md` (on file).

Major al-Rāzī positions on Q 28:

- **Q 28:1-2 muqaṭṭaʿāt + book-reference**: al-Rāzī treats the ṬSM opener as the **book-revelation iʿjāz signature** — the same letters as Q 26 ṬSM, marking these as a "doubled-claim" surah-pair (asseverating the Quranic miracle through letter-then-book-reference).
- **Q 28:88 *kullu shayʾin hālikun illā wajhah***: ⭐ **al-Rāzī's iʿjāz-of-meaning anchor.** He devotes extensive commentary, treating this verse as one of the supreme expressions of *iʿjāz al-maʿnā* — the meaning is so concentrated (eight Arabic words; the ontological hierarchy of all-things vs Allah's essence) that no human language could compress more theology into less syllables. al-Rāzī cross-references Q 55:26-27 (`kullu man ʿalayhā fānin wa-yabqā wajhu rabbika`) as the structural twin and considers the two verses together a *single iʿjāz-of-meaning lock* on the doctrine of divine permanence.
- **Q 28:56 *innaka lā tahdī man aḥbabta***: al-Rāzī devotes commentary to the Abū Ṭālib death-bed asbāb-al-nuzūl tradition, harmonising it with al-Bukhārī #3884 / Muslim #24 (verified — see `04-hadith-corpus.md`). al-Rāzī treats the verse as **establishing the limits of prophetic guidance** — even Muḥammad's love for his uncle could not change Abū Ṭālib's heart; only Allah guides.

**MW-6 status**: PENDING for verbatim Arabic; SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED for the iʿjāz-of-meaning treatment of v. 88 (multiple modern tafsir-summaries cite this as a Rāzī signature).

## 3. al-Qurṭubī — *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān*

Source on disk: bulk OpenITI raw. Per-surah PENDING.

Major al-Qurṭubī positions on Q 28:

- **Q 28:23 *wa-abūnā shaykhun kabīr*** ("and our father is a great old man"): al-Qurṭubī harmonises with Ibn Kathīr's tradition that the father is **Shuʿayb**, identifying the daughters' father with the Madyan prophet of Q 7 / Q 11 / Q 26. Some scholarship (including al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī's school) holds him to be a different elder (Yathrūn / Ḥubāb). al-Qurṭubī surveys both views, leaning toward Shuʿayb.
- **Q 28:27 *thamāniya ḥijaj*** ("eight ḥajj-cycles"): al-Qurṭubī derives **fiqh** about contracted-labor + delayed-marriage-payment from this verse. The *mahr* is the labor itself; the eight (or ten) years are the contractual term.
- **Q 28:77 *wa-bta-ghi fīmā ātāka llāhu al-dāra l-ākhirata wa-lā tansa naṣībaka mina d-dunyā*** ("seek with what God has given you the Hereafter, and do not forget your share of the world"): al-Qurṭubī develops the classic *zuhd*-vs-*kasb* (asceticism vs lawful earning) balance, citing this verse as the canonical mid-position.

**MW-6 status**: PENDING.

## 4. Ibn Kathīr — *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm*

Source on disk: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/ibn-kathir-ar-openiti-*` (bulk; Q 28 specifically PENDING). Q 1-Q 2 English Darussalam extracts on file.

Major Ibn Kathīr positions on Q 28 (per classical tradition):

- **Q 28:7 nursing-via-Pharaoh's-wife**: Ibn Kathīr emphasises the *isrāʾīliyyāt*-derived elaborations (the names of Mūsā's mother *Yūkhābid*, his sister *Maryam bint ʿImrān*-the-mosaic-Maryam-not-Jesus's-mother, etc.). He treats these as cautionary supplemental detail — useful for *qiṣṣa* but not doctrinally binding.
- **Q 28:23-28 Madyan-episode**: Ibn Kathīr identifies the father as **Shuʿayb** by name and provides the Madyan-watering-scene narrative in fuller form than the Quranic text — drawing on traditions from Ibn ʿAbbās. He treats the eight-or-ten years as **literal** and the `ʿuqdat al-lisān` reference (Q 28:34) as Mūsā's residual childhood speech difficulty (*lukna*), connecting to the Q 20:27 prayer.
- **Q 28:38 *ya hāmān*** Pharaoh's command to Hāmān: Ibn Kathīr addresses the Biblical-historical concern that Hāmān (in the Hebrew Bible) is a Persian official under Ahasuerus, not an Egyptian official under Pharaoh. He argues that *Hāmān* is a generic title or a different person; rejects the orientalist objection.
- **Q 28:76-82 Qārūn**: Ibn Kathīr identifies Qārūn as a **first cousin of Mūsā** (*ibn ʿamm*), drawing on Ibn ʿAbbās. He treats the swallowing-by-the-earth as literal-physical event and notes parallels in Numbers 16 (Korah).
- **Q 28:88 *kullu shayʾin hālikun***: Ibn Kathīr treats this as the closing-tawḥīd lock, stylistically equivalent to Q 55's *kullu man ʿalayhā fānin*.

**MW-6 status**: PENDING for Q 28 verbatim Arabic; SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED for the Shuʿayb / Qārūn-cousin / Hāmān-positioning narratives (these appear consistently in classical secondary summaries).

## 5. al-Suyūṭī — *al-Durr al-manthūr* + *al-Itqān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān*

Source on disk: `suyuti-al-itqan-fi-ulum-al-quran-english.pdf`, `suyuti-itqan-word-counts.md`. Per-surah Q 28 PENDING for *al-Durr al-manthūr* extracts.

Major al-Suyūṭī positions on Q 28 (per *al-Itqān* nawʿ-references and on-disk excerpts):

- **Surah-classification**: Q 28 is **mid-Meccan** (*al-Itqān* nawʿ 1-3). Some traditions place it specifically as the 49th revealed surah (Tanzil/Egyptian Standard). The TSM-cluster Q 26-27-28 is treated as a **revelation-window cluster** — three closely-revealed surahs with shared muqaṭṭaʿāt.
- **Asbāb al-nuzūl for Q 28:56**: al-Suyūṭī (in *Lubāb al-Nuqūl fī Asbāb al-Nuzūl*) connects this to Abū Ṭālib's death-bed event (Bukhārī #3884, Muslim #24). The verse is a *post-event* revelation.
- **Asbāb al-nuzūl for Q 28:85**: al-Suyūṭī cites al-Ḍaḥḥāk's tradition that this verse was revealed during the Hijra, when the Prophet was at *al-Juḥfa* — meaning Q 28 has at least one Medinan/Hijra-period inserted-verse claim. (al-Suyūṭī treats this as exceptional.)

**MW-6 status**: VERIFIED for general Meccan classification (per on-disk *al-Itqān* PDF nawʿ 1); PENDING for verbatim asbāb-al-nuzūl quotations.

## 6. al-Biqāʿī — *Naẓm al-Durar fī Tanāsub al-Āyāt wa-l-Suwar*

Source on disk: `biqai-nazm-al-durar.pdf` (129 MB, full work). Q 28-specific page-range PENDING.

Major al-Biqāʿī positions on Q 28:

- **Q 27 → Q 28 munāsabah**: al-Biqāʿī interprets the Q 27 (al-Naml — Sulaymān-glory) → Q 28 (al-Qaṣaṣ — fall-of-Pharaoh, fall-of-Qārūn) sequence as a **kingdom-and-its-fall** macro-progression. Q 27 is the **rise-of-Sulaymān-kingdom-with-tawḥīd-of-Sheba**; Q 28 is the **fall-of-Pharaoh-and-Qārūn-via-tyranny**.
- **Q 28 → Q 29 munāsabah**: al-Biqāʿī interprets the Q 28 (Qārūn fall) → Q 29 (al-ʿAnkabūt — fragility-of-tyrant-houses, "the spider's house") as the **post-fall-trial-of-believers** progression. The spider's-house metaphor in Q 29:41 is the structural equivalent of Qārūn's collapsed dwelling.
- **Q 28's internal munāsabah**: the Madyan-episode (vv. 22-28) and Qārūn-episode (vv. 76-82) are al-Biqāʿī-paired as the **two transitions of Mūsā's Mosesic-cycle**: from-impediment-to-prophecy (entry to Madyan) and from-rivalry-to-judgment (Qārūn).

**MW-6 status**: PENDING.

⚠️ **Empirical correction**: The al-Biqāʿī claim that the TSM-letter-cluster has shared content has been **falsified 4×** in prior project work (Wave-FALSIFIED §3.7). Q028-F-02 explicitly tested whether the TSM Moses-content twin-pair (Q 26 ↔ Q 28) is closer than either is to Q 20's Moses-block (Q 20 has ṬH, not ṬSM). **F-02 NULL: Q 26 ↔ Q 28 cosine 0.67 is LOWER than Q 28 ↔ Q 20 cosine 0.82.** This consolidates the al-Biqāʿī falsification: the muqaṭṭaʿāt-letter-cluster is **not** a content-cluster; al-Biqāʿī's specific pair-by-pair munāsabah claims may still hold in *narrative-sequence* terms, but they are NOT empirically coupled to the muqaṭṭaʿāt-letter-set.

However, F-05 partially rehabilitates al-Biqāʿī on a **different axis** — *narrative-density* (Moses + prophet + narrative-marker tokens). The TSM 3-surah triple does cluster on this axis (p_perm = 0.0017). al-Biqāʿī's intuition of Q 26-27-28 cohesion is correct on the narrative-density-axis but FALSE on the content-cosine-axis. This is the **rules-tuple-bidirectional rehabilitation** noted in `MEMORY.md` (Ikhwān al-Ṣafāʾ rehabilitated under maghribī, etc.).

## 7. Synthesis — points of consensus vs disagreement

**Consensus among al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, al-Qurṭubī, Ibn Kathīr, al-Suyūṭī**:
- Mid-Meccan placement.
- Madyan-watering-and-marriage episode = corpus-unique.
- Q 28:56 asbāb-al-nuzūl = Abū Ṭālib's death.
- Q 28:88 = supreme tawḥīd / iʿjāz-of-meaning verse.

**Disagreements**:
- Identity of the Madyan-elder (Shuʿayb [Ibn Kathīr] vs unnamed [some traditions]).
- Hāmān's historical position (al-Rāzī harmonises; modern orientalist objection — see Ibn Kathīr's response).
- Q 28:85 revelation-locus (Mecca vs Hijra-period).

## 2. Classical names

- **al-Qaṣaṣ** (القصص) — "The Stories" / "The Narration." Named from v. 25 where the verb-form `وقص عليه القصص` ("and he related to him the story") appears. The cognate noun *al-qaṣaṣ* is a verbal-noun referring to the Moses-Madyan story specifically.
- Also occasionally named **سورة موسى** (Sūrat Mūsā) — though this name is more commonly applied to Q 20 Ṭā-Hā or Q 17 al-Isrāʾ in some classifications.

## 3. Opening formula — muqaṭṭaʿāt + book-reference (TSM-cluster member)

Q 28 opens with **ṬSM** muqaṭṭaʿāt (ṭāʾ-sīn-mīm) followed by *tilka āyātu al-Kitābi al-Mubīn* — "These are the verses of the Clear Book." This places Q 28 in the **ṭ-s-m letter cluster** and is the **third and last member** of the consecutive triple Q 26-27-28:

- Q 26 al-Shuʿarāʾ — opens **ṬSM** (ṭāʾ-sīn-mīm).
- Q 27 al-Naml — opens **ṬS** (ṭāʾ-sīn).
- Q 28 al-Qaṣaṣ — opens **ṬSM** (ṭāʾ-sīn-mīm).

The Q 26 ↔ Q 28 muqaṭṭaʿāt are character-identical (`طسم`). This is the only consecutive triple where the muqaṭṭaʿāt-letters match across all three. Q 28's opener-formula `تلك آيات الكتاب المبين` is **identical** to the openers of Q 12 (Yūsuf), Q 26, Q 27 (substring), and Q 28 itself — a four-surah formal-isomorphism among prophet-narrative surahs.

## 5. ⭐ Unique structural features

1. **The Moses-Madyan exodus narrative (vv. 3-43)** — the corpus' only extended treatment of Mūsā's flight to Madyan, his marriage to Shuʿayb's daughter, and his return-of-staff-revelation at Wādī Ṭuwā. No other surah carries this episode at this length:
   - Birth + casting on the Nile (vv. 7-13);
   - Killing of the Egyptian + flight (vv. 14-21);
   - **Madyan-water-watering-marriage episode (vv. 22-28)** — corpus-unique;
   - Return + Mt. Sinai theophany + signs (vv. 29-35);
   - Pharaoh confrontation + drowning (vv. 36-42);
   - Authorial address + Meccan-relevance (vv. 43-50).
2. **The Qārūn (Korah) episode (vv. 76-82)** — the corpus' only extended Qārūn narrative. All other Qārūn mentions are passing references (Q 29:39, Q 40:24). Q 28 contains **2 of 4** corpus-wide `قارون` attestations = 50% of corpus share (verses 76, 79). The episode is structurally a "wealth-and-arrogance → being-swallowed-by-the-earth" parable.
3. **Q 28:34 — the impediment-reference echo** — `وأخي هارون هو أفصح مني لسانا فأرسله معي ردءا يصدقني` ("and my brother Hārūn is more eloquent than I in tongue, so send him with me as a helper to confirm me"). This recapitulates the *ʿuqdat-al-lisān* motif of Q 20:27 in narrative-prose form rather than prayer-form (the actual relief-prayer is Q 20:25-28). [Garden-of-forking-paths note: the dispatch prompt mis-located the relief-prayer at Q 28:35 — corrected here, see Q028-F-04 pre-reg.]
4. **Q 28:56 — *innaka lā tahdī man aḥbabta*** — the "Abū Ṭālib death-bed" verse, asbāb-al-nuzūl-anchored to the Prophet's uncle's refusal to embrace tawḥīd. Cross-referenced in Bukhārī #3884, #4675, #4772, Muslim #24, Tirmidhī #3188, Nasāʾī #2035 (verified IDs from `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json`). One of the highest-attested asbāb-al-nuzūl verses in the corpus.
5. **Q 28:88 — *kullu shayʾin hālikun illā wajhah*** — al-Rāzī iʿjāz-of-meaning anchor; cited by classical scholars as a thuluth-al-Qurʾān-tier theological miracle.

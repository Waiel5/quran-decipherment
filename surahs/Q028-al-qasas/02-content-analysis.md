## 2. Madyan episode (vv. 22-28) — corpus-unique

This is the most lexically-distinctive 7-verse block in the surah by hapax-payload (28 corpus-unique tokens out of 134; 20.9% hapax density). Tokens that are **corpus-wide hapax legomena** (computed in `Q028-F-01`):

`أتممت, أنكحك, أيما, ابنتي, استأجرت, استأجره, استحياء, الأجلين, الرعاء, امرأتين, تأجرني, تذودان, توجه, ثماني, حجج, خطبكما, سقيت, شيخ, فجاءته, فسقى, ليجزيك, نجوت, نسقي, هاتين, وأبونا, وقص, يدعوك, يهديني` (28 unique corpus-wide hapaxes).

Many of these are connected to:
- **Marriage / contract terms**: `أنكحك` ("I marry you to"), `استأجرت` / `استأجره` ("hire him"), `الأجلين` ("the two terms"), `تأجرني` ("you serve me as wage"), `حجج` ("eight years"), `ثماني` ("eight"), `أتممت` ("complete").
- **Watering-and-shepherding-scene**: `الرعاء` ("shepherds"), `تذودان` ("the two of them holding back [their flock]"), `نسقي` ("we don't water"), `سقيت` ("you watered"), `فسقى` ("he watered").
- **The two daughters dialogue**: `امرأتين` ("two women"), `ابنتي` ("my two daughters"), `هاتين` ("these two"), `استحياء` ("modesty").

This is a **corpus-unique semantic cluster** — the marriage-by-contract + watering-scene + bride-introduction motifs do not recur elsewhere at this density. The Madyan-episode passes as a highly distinctive verbal-event narrated for the first and only time.

## 3. Qārūn episode (vv. 76-82) — vocabulary IS shared with eschatological closing

Counter-finding: F-03 found that the Qārūn-block's distinctness rank is **80/82** — i.e., the Qārūn-block is **less** lexically distinct from the rest of Q 28 than 79 other 7-verse windows. Why?

Inspection of Q 28:76-82 vocabulary:
- `قارون` (proper noun, 2× in Q 28)
- `الكنوز` ("treasures")
- `مفاتحه` ("keys-of")
- `العصبة` ("the band")
- `زينته` ("his adornment")
- `فخسفنا` ("we caused to be swallowed")
- `بداره` ("with his dwelling")
- `الحياة الدنيا` ("the worldly life") — recurs in Q 28:60, 61, 79, 80
- `الدار الآخرة` ("the Hereafter Abode") — recurs in Q 28:77, 83
- `الكنوز / الزينة / المتاع / الحياة الدنيا / الدار الآخرة` — these terms ALSO populate Block J (vv. 60-67) and Block M (vv. 83-88).

The Qārūn episode uses Q 28's eschatological-closing vocabulary while introducing a few proper-noun-and-action hapaxes. The dunyā / ākhira contrast that frames the entire second half of the surah is *concentrated* in Qārūn — making him the narrative *exemplum* of the dunyā-orientation that Block J warns against.

This is a **non-trivial finding**: the Qārūn-block is structurally a *summative parable*, sharing vocabulary with the surah's overall ākhira-mauʿiẓa framework.

## 4. Repetition patterns

**`فلما`/`ولما`/`وإذ`/`إذ` narrative-marker frequency**: Q 28 has 30 occurrences in 1,438 tokens = **2.1%** narrative-marker density (cf. corpus median 0.51% per F-05). This is **above the 95th percentile of Meccan surahs** — Q 28 is a heavily-narrated surah.

**`موسى` (Mūsā)**: 18 attestations in Q 28, distributed:
- vv. 3, 7, 10, 15, 18, 19, 20 (Egypt + flight)
- vv. 29, 30, 31, 36, 37, 38, 43, 44 (Sinai + Pharaoh + revelation)
- vv. 48, 76 (polemic + Qārūn)

**`فرعون` (Firʿawn)**: 8 attestations (Q 28:3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 32, 38, 38) — Q 28 is the second-densest Firʿawn surah after Q 26 al-Shuʿarāʾ.

**`قارون` (Qārūn)**: 2 attestations (Q 28:76, 79) of 4 corpus-wide = 50% share.

**`مدين` (Madyan)**: 3 attestations (Q 28:22, 23, 45) of 10 corpus-wide = 30% share.

**`هامان` (Hāmān)**: 3 attestations (Q 28:6, 8, 38). Hāmān is corpus-wide attested only in Q 28 (3×), Q 29 (1×), Q 40 (3×). Q 28 holds 43% share. The triplet (Pharaoh, Hāmān, Qārūn) co-occurs in Q 29:39 and Q 40:24 — Q 28 is the first-introduction-of-Hāmān surah.

## 5. Refrains

**`إن في ذلك لآية`**: 0 occurrences in Q 28 (cf. Q 26 al-Shuʿarāʾ which has the famous 8× refrain). Q 28 is *adjacent* to Q 26 in the muṣḥaf but uses a different rhetorical structure.

**`أين شركائي الذين كنتم تزعمون`**: occurs at Q 28:62 AND Q 28:74 — the only **internal double-call** in Q 28. The two Day-of-Resurrection apostrophe-questions form a structural envelope around vv. 63-73 (the polemic on tawḥīd vs shirk).

**`له الحكم وإليه ترجعون`**: occurs at Q 28:70 AND Q 28:88 (closing) — bracketing the second half of the surah.

**`من جاء بالـ-`**: vv. 84 (×2), 85 — the al-Qaṣaṣ refrain `من جاء بالحسنة...ومن جاء بالسيئة...`.

## 7. Content register

Q 28 is **predominantly NARRATIVE** with two large parable-blocks (Mūsā vv. 3-43, Qārūn vv. 76-82) and theological-eschatological connectives (vv. 60-75, 83-88). It is the corpus' **most narratively-dense Meccan surah after Q 12** (Yūsuf, which is 100% narrative). Narrative-marker density 2.1% places Q 28 in the top 5% of Meccan surahs.

## 8. Vocabulary distinctness summary

- **164 orthographic-exact corpus-hapaxes** in Q 28 (out of 1,438 tokens) = **11.4%** corpus-hapax density.
- 28 of those concentrated in vv. 22-28 (the Madyan episode) — supports F-01 H1.
- The two largest hapax-dense blocks are the **birth-narrative** (Block B, vv. 5-11) and the **Madyan-episode** (Block D, vv. 22-28).
- This makes Q 28 a surah of **fresh vocabulary delivery** — narrative material that does not recur elsewhere.

## 9. Honest limits

- The block-boundary delimitations (B, C, D, etc.) follow the universally-recognised tafsir-tradition divisions (al-Ṭabarī, Ibn Kathīr, al-Biqāʿī all draw similar boundaries). Other delimitations are possible but the choice was locked BEFORE F-01 / F-03 ran.
- "Hapax density" uses orthographic-exact tokens (not lemma/root). Under root-collapsing, the hapax count would be lower (a derivational form `استأجرت` and `استأجره` would collapse to one root); the relative ranking would likely persist.
- The Q 28:34 "impediment-reference" labelling is mine — classical mufassirūn (al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī) note that this is Mūsā's narrative-form recapitulation of the impediment, but they do not give it a special technical label.

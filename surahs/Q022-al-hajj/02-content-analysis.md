## 2. Verse-by-verse content summary

### BLOCK I — Cosmic Eschatology (vv 1-18)

- **v 1**: Universal vocative *yā ayyuhā al-nāsu ttaqū rabbakum inna zalzalata al-sāʿati shayʾun ʿaẓīm* — "O mankind, fear your Lord; truly the earthquake of the Hour is a tremendous thing." Open with cosmic-trumpet (zalzala) theme. This verse is corpus-distinct: paired *yā-ayyuhā-al-nās* + *al-sāʿa-zalzala* opener appears nowhere else.
- **v 2**: The day-of-shaking — every nursing mother forgets her infant, every pregnant woman miscarries, people appear drunk while not drunk.
- **v 3**: First dispute-without-knowledge mention (*wa-mina al-nāsi man yujādilu fī llāhi bi-ghayri ʿilmin wa-yattabiʿu kulla shayṭānin marīd*).
- **v 4-5**: Eschatological warning + resurrection-by-stages argument (*nuṭfa-ʿalaqa-muḍgha* embryological proof).
- **v 6-7**: Allāh is the Truth, He brings the dead to life, the Hour is coming without doubt.
- **v 8-10**: Second dispute-without-knowledge passage; the disputers' fate.
- **v 11-13**: The *ḥarf*-believer (worship-on-the-edge) parable — the believer who follows God only when prosperous.
- **v 14**: Believers + righteous-works admitted to gardens-under-which-rivers-flow.
- **v 15**: God will not be defeated; let the one who thinks otherwise stretch a rope to heaven and cut himself off.
- **v 16**: The Quran's claritas as guidance for whom God wills.
- **v 17**: The 7-religions catalog (believers, Jews, Sabians, Christians, Magians, polytheists) — God judges them on the Day.
- **v 18 ⭐ SAJDA #1**: *a-lam tara anna llāha yasjudu lahu man fī al-samāwāti wa-man fī al-arḍi wa-l-shamsu wa-l-qamaru wa-l-nujūmu wa-l-jibālu wa-l-shajaru wa-l-dawābbu wa-kathīrun min al-nās* — "Do you not see that to Allāh prostrates whoever is in the heavens and earth, the sun, moon, stars, mountains, trees, animals, and many of mankind...?" The cosmic roll-call of creation prostrating; classical *balāgha* identifies this as *waṣf-ʿumūmī* (universal-descriptive sajda). Linked semantically to Q 13:15 and Q 16:49 (cf. Q022-F-01 VINDICATED — Q22:18 clusters with these two).

### BLOCK II — The Two Disputants (vv 19-24)

- **v 19**: *hādhāni khaṣmāni khtaṣamū fī rabbihim* — "These are two opponents who have disputed concerning their Lord." Classical: the asbāb tradition links this to the Badr champions (al-Wāḥidī, *Asbāb*; al-Bukhārī ḥadīth #3965 records ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib affirming the connection).
- **v 19b-22**: The fate of the disbelievers — fire, garments of fire, scalding water; iron-mauls for them.
- **v 23-24**: The fate of the believers — gardens, silk-and-gold ornaments, the guidance to the goodly word and the Praiseworthy way.

### BLOCK III — Ḥajj Legislation (vv 25-37)

- **v 25**: The disbelievers obstruct from the Sacred Mosque; al-Bayt = the assembly-place for the resident and the visitor; iḥrām-violations + punishment.
- **v 26**: *wa-idh bawwa'nā li-Ibrāhīma makāna al-bayt* — "And when We assigned to Abraham the location of the House" — the Abrahamic foundation of ḥajj.
- **v 27 ⭐**: *wa-adhdhin fī al-nāsi bi-l-ḥajji yaʾtūka rijālan wa-ʿalā kulli ḍāmirin yaʾtīna min kulli fajjin ʿamīq* — "And proclaim the pilgrimage to mankind; they will come to you on foot and on every lean camel from every distant ravine." THE founding ḥajj-proclamation imperative.
- **v 28**: *li-yashhadū manāfiʿa lahum wa-yadhkurū sma llāhi fī ayyāmin maʿlūmātin* — pilgrimage benefits + the days-of-known-rite (*ayyām maʿlūmāt* = the 10 days of Dhū l-Ḥijja per al-Suyūṭī).
- **v 29-30**: *thumma l-yaqḍū tafathahum wa-l-yūfū nudhūrahum wa-l-yaṭṭawwafū bi-l-bayti al-ʿatīq* — completion of the rites + the ṭawāf around the ancient House.
- **v 31**: Hanīf-monotheism; whoever associates anything with Allāh is as if fallen from the sky.
- **v 32**: *wa-man yuʿaẓẓim shaʿāʾira llāhi fa-innahā min taqwā l-qulūb* — "whoever magnifies the rites of Allāh, it is from God-consciousness of the hearts."
- **v 33-35**: Cattle/sacrificial-animals as benefit-until-named-term + *al-bayt al-ʿatīq*; the prostrating-and-bowing imperative for the Muḥsinīn.
- **v 36**: *wa-l-budna jaʿalnāhā lakum min shaʿāʾiri llāhi* — the *budn* (sacrificial-camels) as part of the rites; eat from them, feed the unimportuning and the importuning.
- **v 37**: The flesh and blood of sacrifices does not reach Allāh, but *taqwā* (God-consciousness) reaches Him.

### BLOCK IV — The First Permission for War (vv 38-41)

- **v 38**: Allāh defends the believers; He does not love the treacherous, the ungrateful.
- **v 39 ⭐**: *udhina lilladhīna yuqātalūna bi-annahum ẓulimū wa-inna llāha ʿalā naṣrihim laqadīr* — **"Permission has been given to those who are being fought (against) because they have been wronged — and Allāh is competent to give them victory."** Q 22:39 is the corpus's FIRST explicit permission for fighting (per al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān*, nawʿ 11, on al-nāsikh wa-l-mansūkh: *hādhihi awwalu āyati nazalat fī al-qitāl*).
- **v 40**: *alladhīna ukhrijū min diyārihim bi-ghayri ḥaqqin illā an yaqūlū rabbunā llāh* — those who were driven from their homes without right except that they say "Our Lord is Allāh." The hijra-narrative-anchor + churches-temples-mosques-synagogues-list.
- **v 41**: *alladhīna in makkannāhum fī al-arḍi aqāmū al-ṣalāta wa-ātaw al-zakāta wa-amarū bi-l-maʿrūfi wa-nahaw ʿan al-munkar* — those who, when established on earth, establish prayer, give zakat, command good, forbid wrong. THE classical proof-text for the *al-amr bi-l-maʿrūf* program.

### BLOCK V — Prophetic Cycle + Creation Arguments + Idolatry Refutation (vv 42-72)

- **v 42-44**: Prophets-rejected catalog — Noah, ʿĀd, Thamūd, Abraham, Lot, Midian, Moses; all denied; the cities-destroyed-with-walls-fallen-in.
- **v 45-46**: Travel-the-earth-and-see-what-happened-to-them; *afa-lam yasīrū fī al-arḍ*.
- **v 47-48**: The arrogance of seeking-the-day-quickly + the seeming-slowness of God's reckoning (1 day in your Lord's reckoning = 1000 years).
- **v 49-51**: Prophetic-warner role; rewards-and-punishments dichotomy.
- **v 52**: *wa-mā arsalnā min qablika min rasūlin wa-lā nabiyyin illā idhā tamannā alqā al-shayṭānu fī umniyyatihi* — the famous *tamannī* verse classically linked to the *gharānīq* narrative (cf. Q 53 specialist's audit at [[Q053-al-najm/05-classical-claims-audit|Q053-F-02]]). This verse is the SCRIPTURAL anchor of the Sīra-narrated incident; for the empirical-text + canonical-ḥadīth audit, see [`05-classical-claims-audit.md`](05-classical-claims-audit.md) §4.
- **v 53-54**: The *fitna li-lladhīna fī qulūbihim maraḍ* — the trial-of-Satan's-suggestion; believers given certainty.
- **v 55**: *wa-lā yazālu lladhīna kafarū fī miryatin minhu* — the disbelievers' persistent doubt.
- **v 56-57**: Day-of-judgment kingship belongs to God.
- **v 58-60**: Reward for the emigrants killed in God's way; the reciprocal-vengeance permission *wa-man ʿāqaba bi-mithli mā ʿūqiba bihi* (with subsequent forgiveness preferred).
- **v 61**: The night-day alternation as proof-of-God's-power.
- **v 62**: *dhālika bi-anna llāha huwa al-ḥaqqu* — God is the truth, what they invoke besides Him is the false.
- **v 63-66**: Cosmic proofs (rain-from-the-sky, ships-in-the-sea, He-who-gave-life-and-causes-death).
- **v 67**: *li-kulli ummatin jaʿalnā mansakan hum nāsikūhu* — "For every community We have appointed a rite which they perform." Classical *mansak* polysemy: rite/sacrifice/pilgrimage (al-Rāzī engages this at length, *Mafātīḥ* on Q 22:67).
- **v 68-69**: Allāh will judge between you in what you disputed.
- **v 70**: God's knowledge encompasses everything.
- **v 71**: Idolaters worship that for which He has not sent down authority.
- **v 72 ⭐**: *wa-idhā tutlā ʿalayhim āyātunā bayyinātin taʿrifu fī wujūhi lladhīna kafarū al-munkara yakādūna yasṭūna bi-lladhīna yatlūna ʿalayhim āyātinā* — the disbelievers' face-of-denial when the verses are recited; *qul a-fa-unabbiʾukum bi-sharrin min dhālikum al-nāru waʿada llāhu lladhīna kafarū wa-biʾsa al-maṣīr*.

### BLOCK VI — Cosmic Witness Bridge (vv 73-76)

- **v 73 ⭐**: *yā ayyuhā al-nāsu ḍuriba mathalun fa-stamiʿū lahu, inna lladhīna tadʿūna min dūni llāhi lan yakhluqū dhubāban wa-lawi jtamaʿū lahu* — **"O mankind, a parable is struck, so listen to it: those whom you call upon besides Allāh will never create a fly, even if they were to gather for it."** The fly-creation parable. (Note: the SECOND universal vocative re-opener; Q 22 is bracketed by *yā ayyuhā al-nāsu* at v 1 + v 73 + an implicit one at v 77's imperative believer-address.)
- **v 74**: They did not measure Allāh with His true measure.
- **v 75**: *allāhu yaṣṭafī min al-malāʾikati rusulan wa-min al-nās* — Allāh chooses messengers from angels and from people.
- **v 76**: Allāh's knowledge of front and back; all matters return to Him.

### BLOCK VII — The Imperative Closing (vv 77-78)

- **v 77 ⭐ SAJDA #2**: *yā ayyuhā lladhīna āmanū rkaʿū wa-sjudū wa-ʿbudū rabbakum wa-fʿalū al-khayra laʿallakum tufliḥūn* — "O you who believe, bow and prostrate and worship your Lord, and do good — perhaps you may succeed." The IMPERATIVE-active sajda — distinct typologically from 22:18's cosmic-passive-witness sajda.
- **v 78**: *wa-jāhidū fī llāhi ḥaqqa jihādih, huwa jtabākum, wa-mā jaʿala ʿalaykum fī al-dīni min ḥaraj, millata abīkum Ibrāhīm, huwa sammākum al-muslimīna min qablu wa-fī hādhā li-yakūna al-rasūlu shahīdan ʿalaykum wa-takūnū shuhadāʾa ʿalā al-nāsi, fa-aqīmū al-ṣalāta wa-ātū al-zakāta wa-ʿtaṣimū bi-llāh, huwa mawlākum, fa-niʿma al-mawlā wa-niʿma al-naṣīr* — the great closing exhortation. Note: Q 22:78 is the verse where the **name *al-Muslimīn*** (Muslims, the submitting ones) is given by Abraham — the closing verse explicitly names the religious community.

## 4. Content register

**MIXED**: legal (vv 25-37, 39-41), eschatological (vv 1-18), narrative-prophetic (vv 42-46), polemical (vv 19-24, 71-74), exhortative (vv 77-78), wisdom (vv 32, 37, 41). Q 22 spans all five major registers — making it an architectural-bridge surah.

The mixed-register character is consistent with Q 22's Mecca-Medinan hybrid chronology and its mid-mushaf bridging position (between long Meccan-style Q 21 al-Anbiyāʾ and short Medinan-style Q 23 al-Muʾminūn).

## 5. Vocabulary distinctness — pilgrimage-density

Q 22's vocabulary distinctness is anchored on the pilgrimage-cluster (per Q022-F-04 VINDICATED):
- **Hapax-in-Q22 ḥajj-cluster**: *al-budn* (the sacrificial camels, vv 27, 36 — only the plural attestations elsewhere are at Q 5:2 *al-hady*); *manāsik* (rites, vv 28, 30, 67 — Q 2 has 1 attestation at 2:128).
- **Q 22-cluster + Q 2/5/108**: *ḥajj* (vv 25, 27 — Q 2 has 7 attestations; Q 3:97); *naḥr* (verb at 22:36; Q 108:2 *fa-ṣalli li-rabbika wa-nḥar*).

Vocabulary-distinctness on the QAC root level: Q 22 has 328 unique roots (cf. Q 22's word count 1,354 → roots-per-100-words = 24.2; corpus average ≈ 22-26 depending on surah-length; Q 22 is mid-range).

## 7. Cross-surah content references

Q 22 cites or alludes to:
- **Q 2 al-Baqara**: Abraham-and-the-House (Q 2:127); the ḥajj-pilgrimage details. Q 22:26 *wa-idh bawwaʾnā li-Ibrāhīma* parallels Q 2:127 *wa-idh yarfaʿu Ibrāhīmu al-qawāʿida min al-bayt*. The "ancient House" cross-verse network includes Q 14:35-37 (Ibrahim's prayer).
- **Q 5 al-Māʾida**: *hady* (sacrificial animals, Q 5:2). 
- **Q 53 al-Najm**: the *tamannī* verse Q 22:52 is the legislative-companion to Q 53:19-23 (the polytheism-rebuke triad).
- **Q 13 al-Raʿd, Q 16 al-Naḥl**: the cosmic-roll-call sajda triad (Q 13:15, Q 16:49, Q 22:18 — Q022-F-01 VINDICATED).
- **Q 24 al-Nūr**: the parable-of-faith framing recurs (Q 22:31 hanif-monotheism falling-from-sky parallels Q 24:39's mirage parable).
- **Q 47 Muḥammad**: the war-permission expanded.

## 8. The double-sajda typology

Within Q 22, the two sajdas form a structural-rhetorical pair:

| | v 18 | v 77 |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Mode | Passive cosmic witness | Active imperative command |
| Subject of prostration | Creation (sun, moon, stars, mountains, trees, animals, mankind-many) | The believers (*yā ayyuhā lladhīna āmanū*) |
| Theology | *waṣf-ʿumūmī* (universal-descriptive sajda) | *amr* (command) |
| Block position | END of Block I (cosmic eschatology) | START of Block VII (imperative closing) |
| Inter-verse delta | LOW (vv 17-19 thematically continuous) | HIGH (sharp shift from monotheism block to imperative closing) |
| Classical *balāgha* type | *jamʿ-bayna-l-mukhtalifāt* (gathering disparates under one verb) | *jamʿ-bayna-l-mutashākilāt* (gathering similars under chained imperatives) |

This typology is empirically confirmed by Q022-F-08 DIRECTIONAL_SPLIT — v77 is at top-30% block-boundary, v18 is mid-block.

The double-sajda is therefore not merely a quantitative singularity (Q 22 is the only surah with 2 sajdas) but a TYPOLOGICAL singularity: Q 22 is the only surah that pairs both major sajda-types within a single surah-arc.

## 9. Honest limits

- The block-decomposition follows classical *munāsaba* (al-Biqāʿī, al-Rāzī) anchored by Q022-F-08's content-similarity-delta analysis. Alternative block boundaries (e.g., breaking vv 42-72 into 42-54 prophets + 55-72 cosmic-arguments) are defensible.
- The "first permission for war" status of Q 22:39 is classically over-determined (al-Suyūṭī, al-Bayhaqī, al-Wāḥidī all agree); empirically, the verse contains the corpus's first imperative *udhina* (was-permitted-passive) construction in war-context.
- The vocabulary-density of pilgrimage (Q022-F-04) is robust at rank 2/114 (after the 10-word Q 108 al-Kawthar singleton).
- The Q 22:52 *tamannī* verse's connection to the Q 53 *gharānīq* narrative is classical-tradition-encoded but empirically-text-NULL on canonical-ḥadīth attestation (see Q 53 specialist audit at [[Q053-al-najm/05-classical-claims-audit|Q053-F-02]]; not re-audited here).

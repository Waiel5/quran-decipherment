**The C-block (vv. 15-44) is the surah's narrative-center**, occupying 30 of 93 verses (32%). Within it, the Hudhud-Bilqīs sub-block (vv. 20-44) — 25 verses — is the most extended royal-court scene in the Quran.

## 2. Verse-by-verse summary (compressed)

### A. Opening (vv. 1-6)
- **v. 1**: ṬS — *tilka āyātu al-Qurʾāni wa-kitābin mubīn* — "ṬS. These are the verses of the Qurʾān and a Clear Book."
- **v. 2**: *hudan wa-bushrā li-l-muʾminīn* — "guidance and good tidings for the believers."
- **v. 3**: those who establish prayer (*yuqīmūna al-ṣalāt*), give zakāt, and have certainty in the hereafter.
- **vv. 4-5**: the disbelievers — their deeds beautified to them; they wander; theirs is the worst punishment.
- **v. 6**: the Qurʾān is given from One All-Wise, All-Knowing.

### B. Mūsā (vv. 7-14)
- **vv. 7-8**: Mūsā sees the fire; voice from it: *būrika man fī al-nār wa-man ḥawlahā* — "Blessed is whoever is in the fire and around it."
- **vv. 9-12**: God's self-disclosure (*innī ana llāhu al-ʿazīzu al-ḥakīm*); staff-into-serpent sign; hand-into-white sign.
- **vv. 13-14**: Pharaoh's people see and reject (*juḥūdan*); fate at end-time.

### C1. Sulaymān + the ant (vv. 15-19)
- **v. 15**: *wa-laqad ātaynā Dāwūda wa-Sulaymāna ʿilman* — "We gave David and Solomon knowledge."
- **v. 16**: Sulaymān inherits David; *ʿullimnā manṭiqa al-ṭayr* — "We have been taught the speech of the birds."
- **v. 17**: hosts gathered: jinn, men, birds — marshalled.
- **v. 18**: ⭐ "Until they came to the Valley of the Ants. A namla said: 'O ants, enter your dwellings — let not Sulaymān and his hosts crush you, while they perceive not.'"
- **v. 19**: Sulaymān smiles in laughter; prays *rabbi awziʿnī* — "My Lord, inspire me to be grateful…" and "include me, by Your mercy, among Your righteous servants."

### C2. Hudhud + Bilqīs's letter (vv. 20-31)
- **v. 20**: *mā lī lā arā al-Hudhud* — "What is wrong, that I see not the hoopoe?"
- **v. 21**: threatens severe punishment unless clear excuse.
- **v. 22**: hoopoe arrives; "I have learned what you have not."
- **vv. 23-26**: hoopoe's report on Sheba's queen and her solar idolatry; *lahā ʿarshun ʿaẓīm* — "she has a magnificent throne."
- **v. 27**: Sulaymān: "We will see whether you spoke truth."
- **v. 28**: "Take this my letter; cast it to them."
- **v. 29**: Bilqīs to her chiefs: *innī ulqiya ilayya kitābun karīm* — "An honored letter has been delivered to me."
- **v. 30**: ⭐⭐ *innahu min Sulaymāna wa-innahu bismi llāhi al-raḥmāni al-raḥīm* — "It is from Sulaymān, and it is: 'In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.'"
- **v. 31**: *allā taʿlū ʿalayya wa-ʾtūnī muslimīn* — "Do not exalt yourselves over me; come to me in submission."

### C3. Throne and conversion (vv. 32-44)
- **vv. 32-35**: Bilqīs consults; chiefs offer war or obedience; she says kings ruin towns; will send a gift to test.
- **v. 36**: Sulaymān refuses gift: *atumiddūnani bi-māl* — "Are you supplying me with wealth?"
- **v. 37**: threat to expel them shamed.
- **vv. 38-40**: Sulaymān to his chiefs: who can bring her throne before she comes? An ʿifrīt, then "one with knowledge of the Book": *ana ātīka bihi qabla an yartadda ilayka ṭarfuk* — "I will bring it to you before your glance returns to you." Throne arrives.
- **vv. 41-44**: Sulaymān has her throne disguised; she enters; the pavilion of glass mistaken for water; *kashafat ʿan sāqayhā* — she uncovers her shins. Sulaymān: it is a smooth pavilion of glass. She: *aslamtu maʿa Sulaymāna li-llāhi rabbi al-ʿālamīn* — "I submit, with Sulaymān, to God, Lord of the Worlds."

### D. Ṣāliḥ (vv. 45-53)
Ṣāliḥ sent to Thamūd; two factions; nine evildoers in the city plot against him; their plot annihilated.

### E. Lūṭ (vv. 54-58)
Lūṭ's people's licentiousness; deliverance of his family except his wife; rain of stones.

### F. Theological refrain (vv. 59-66)
The famous *amman khalaqa al-samāwāti wa-l-arḍ* — "Is He better, or what they associate?" repeated **5 times** as anaphoric refrain — an architectural rhetorical device:
- v. 59: *amman ja-ʿala al-arḍa qarāran*
- v. 60: *amman khalaqa al-samāwāti wa-l-arḍ*
- v. 61: *amman jaʿala al-arḍa qarāran*
- v. 62: *amman yujību al-muḍṭarra…*
- v. 63: *amman yahdīkum…*
- v. 64: *amman yabdaʾ al-khalqa…*
(then v. 65: *qul lā yaʿlamu man fī al-samāwāti wa-l-arḍ al-ghayba illā llāh* — only God knows the unseen.)

This **anaphoric refrain block (vv. 59-64)** is one of the most architecturally regular passages in the Quran.

### G. Resurrection-denial polemic (vv. 67-75)
The disbelievers' mockery; *aʾidhā kunnā turāban wa-ābāʾunā…* — "When we and our fathers are dust, shall we be brought forth?"

### H. Eschatological closing (vv. 76-93)
- v. 76: this Qurʾān relates to the Children of Israel most of what they differ in.
- vv. 77-78: guidance and mercy; Lord judges.
- vv. 79-81: Prophet trust in God; you cannot make the dead hear.
- **v. 82**: ⭐ *wa-idhā waqaʿa al-qawlu ʿalayhim akhrajnā lahum dābbatan mina al-arḍi tukallimuhum…* — "When the word befalls them, We bring forth from the earth a Beast that speaks to them: 'people were not certain of Our signs.'"
- vv. 83-86: gathering of nations; trumpet; mountains pass like clouds.
- vv. 87-90: those with good — better; those with evil — face cast in fire.
- vv. 91-93: Prophet's commission: *innamā umirtu an aʿbuda rabba hādhihi al-baldati alladhī ḥarramahā wa-lahu kullu shayʾ… wa-an atluwa al-Qurʾān* — "I am only commanded to worship the Lord of this town who has made it sacred… and to recite the Qurʾān." Final: *al-ḥamdu li-llāhi sa-yurīkum āyātihi fa-taʿrifūnahā wa-mā rabbuka bi-ghāfilin ʿammā taʿmalūn*.

## 3. Content register

- **Mixed**: theological frame (A) + 4 prophet-narratives (B/C/D/E) + theological refrain (F) + polemic (G) + eschatology (H).
- **Dominant block**: Sulaymān-Bilqīs (vv. 15-44, 32% of verses) — royal-court narrative.
- **Distinctive feature**: the **anaphoric *amman…* refrain** (vv. 59-64) — six-fold repetition, architecturally rhythmic.

## 4. Repetition patterns (refrains)

1. **The *amman X* refrain** (vv. 59-64) — six anaphoric clauses.
2. **The pattern *qālat / qāla / qālū…* dialogue** — vv. 18 (*namla qālat*), 22, 27, 29, 30, 32, 35, 36, 38, 39, 40, 42, 43, 44 — extended quoted dialogue.
3. **Sulaymān's name repeated 7 times** in vv. 15-44 — the highest concentration in the Quran (Q027-F-03 confirmed rank 1; 41% of corpus attestations).
4. **rhyme on -ūn / -ī(n)** — sustained nūn-rhyme (90.3% of verse-endings).

## 5. Cross-surah content references

The Sulaymān-Bilqīs material has parallels:
- **Q 21:81-82** — Sulaymān's wind dominion, jinn-divers (brief).
- **Q 34:12-14** — fountain of molten copper, jinn workers, the staff incident at Sulaymān's death.
- **Q 38:30-40** — Sulaymān's horses, his throne-test (different incident from Q 27).

The Q 27 narrative is the **only** surah giving the Bilqīs/throne-bringing/pavilion-of-glass account. Q 34 mentions only Sulaymān's death; Q 38 his horse-incident. Together Q 21, 27, 34, 38 form the **Sulaymanic narrative network**.

The Mūsā fire-encounter (vv. 7-14) parallels Q 20:9-23 and Q 28:29-30 — the latter being the immediate right-neighbor in the mushaf. **The ṬS-ṬSM cluster (Q 26-27-28) shares Mūsā material**, suggesting deliberate textual echoing.

The al-Dābba (Beast) of v. 82 has a parallel in **Q 34:14** (*dābbatu al-arḍi taʾkulu minsaʾatahu* — the "earth's creature" eating Sulaymān's staff). Notable: both al-Dābba mentions are in surahs naming Sulaymān heavily (Q 27 = 7 attestations; Q 34 = 1).

## 6. Vocabulary distinctness

- *manṭiq al-ṭayr* (speech of birds) — hapax phrase, Q 27:16.
- *al-Hudhud* (the hoopoe) — hapax token (1× corpus).
- *namla / al-naml* (ant/ants) — only attested in Q 27:18.
- *Bilqīs* — never named; she is *imraʾatun mina ṣabaʾ* / *malikatun*.
- *al-dābba* (the Beast) — Q 27:82 (and conceptual parallel Q 34:14, but with different referent).

## 7. Honest limits

- Verse-counts use the Hafs-Kufan tradition (93 verses total). Other counting traditions (Madinan-2, Baṣran) may differ; not pursued here.

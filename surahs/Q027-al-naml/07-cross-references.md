The triplet shares: muqaṭṭaʿāt opening, Mūsā material, prophet-narrative form, mid-Meccan revelation period. They are linked at the **canonical-adjacency-cost** level, but **not** at the FR-roots whole-surah cohesion level (see §6 below).

- **Sulaymān-naming cluster** (7 surahs): Q 2, 4, 6, 21, 27, 34, 38. Q 27 = rank 1 (7/17 = 41.18% of all *Sulaymān* attestations; Q027-F-03 CONFIRMED, p < 0.0001).
- **Hapax-fauna surahs**: Q 27 contains **2 hapax animal lemmas** in the corpus — *naml* (the 3 in v.18 are the entire corpus *naml*-orthographic count) and *al-hudhud* (1 attestation, corpus-wide). Other surahs with hapax fauna: Q 2 (*ʿijl*; *baqara* → most-concentrated cluster), Q 105 (al-Fīl). Q 27's ecology is distinctive even among hapax-fauna surahs because it includes **two** corpus-relative-hapaxes.
- **Surahs with second-occurrence canonical openers**: Q 27 is the **only** corpus surah where the basmala formula recurs as an interior canonical verse. (Other openers — *al-ḥamd* / *sabbaḥa* / *qul* — also do not recur as exact verse-citations within other surahs.)
- **Eschatological-Beast surah cluster**: Q 27:82 is the unique Quranic mention of *al-Dābba* in the eschatological-emergence form. (Other *dābba* attestations refer to "creatures" generically; Q 27:82 is uniquely apocalyptic.)

## 9. Pre-Islamic poetry comparators

For the prophet-narrative form, the *qaṣīda* corpus has no exact analog (pre-Islamic poetry rarely sustains a single-narrative form across 80+ verses). Closest comparators:

- **al-Aʿshā Maymūn** (sometimes anthologized): hagiographic *qaṣīda* on Hātim al-Ṭāʾī's generosity — sustained narrative, but ~30 verses.
- **Imruʾ al-Qays *Muʿallaqa*** vv. 1-30: extended autobiographical scene (the *fadda* desert-night) — comparable to Q 27's Sulaymān-court scene in narrative-density, but 1/3 the length.

The Quran's sustained-narrative form (Q 12, Q 27, Q 28) is structurally distinctive at the genre level — neither pre-Islamic poetry nor Bukhari hadith narrations sustain a single-protagonist plot of 50+ verses with embedded dialogue. (See `data/baseline-corpora/raw/`.)

## 10. Honest limits

- The neighbor analysis uses Fisher-Rao on QAC stem-roots (no-tashkeel default rules-tuple). A different distance (cosine on TF, char-NCD) might shift the top-5 ordering. Q 28's #3-rank in Q 27's FR-nearest is robust under most metrics; the precise distance values would shift.
- The Q 27 ↔ Q 55 maximal-distance (FR) is a strong empirical fact, but the Q 27 ↔ Q 1 mid-distance fact relies on a specific rule-tuple — under different metrics the ordering could shift modestly. The structural reading (basmala-link is formal, not content-cohesive) is robust.
- The 4-cell cross-finding-026 typology has Q 27 in the anti-iʿjāz-al-fawāṣil cell, but the cell's empirical strength varies — Q 33 is the foundational exemplar (rank 1 UAS), Q 27 is rank 23 — solidly within the cell but not anchoring it. The cell's structural fact is cluster-driven (n=4-6 surahs), not single-surah-driven.

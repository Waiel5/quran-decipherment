# Q 96 al-ʿAlaq — Cross-References

## 1. Cross-finding integrations

### cross-finding-008 — Muqaṭṭāʿat as book-introduction markers

**Q 96's relationship**: Q 96 is **NON-muqaṭṭāʿat-opened** (no disjoined letters at v 1) — places Q 96 OUTSIDE the 29-surah muq-set. However, Q 96:4 invokes *qalam* explicitly, joining the H-NEW-56 EXTENDED writing-cluster {*kitāb*, *qurʾān*, *qalam*, *satr*}.

**Pattern**: Of the 29 muq-opened surahs, 25 (86%) reference one of the 4 extended-writing-cluster items in vv 1-3 (H-NEW-56, p = 8.6 × 10⁻¹³). Q 96 references *qalam* in v 4 — outside the v 1-3 window but within the first-revealed-block (vv 1-5). Q 96 represents a **non-muq member** of the broader writing-thematic surah class.

The classical-academic significance: Q 96 demonstrates that the writing-introduction-marker tradition extends to NON-muqaṭṭāʿat surahs at the literacy-vocabulary level, even when the formal disjoined-letter marker is absent.

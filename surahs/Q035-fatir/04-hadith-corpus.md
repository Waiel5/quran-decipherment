## 1. The Tirmidhī §3309 ḥadīth on Q 35:32 (the all-three-saved tradition)

**Citation**: al-Tirmidhī, *Sunan al-Tirmidhī*, Kitāb tafsīr al-Qurʾān (Chapter 47), idInBook §3309.

**Source**: `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/tirmidhi.json`. Tier: **VERIFIED**.

### Arabic text (full)

> حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو مُوسَى، مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ الْمُثَنَّى وَمُحَمَّدُ بْنُ بَشَّارٍ قَالاَ حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ جَعْفَرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنِ الْوَلِيدِ بْنِ عَيْزَارٍ، أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ رَجُلاً، مِنْ ثَقِيفٍ يُحَدِّثُ عَنْ رَجُلٍ، مِنْ كِنَانَةَ عَنْ أَبِي سَعِيدٍ الْخُدْرِيِّ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَنَّهُ قَالَ فِي هَذِهِ الآيَةِ ‏:‏ ‏(‏ثمَّ أَوْرَثْنَا الْكِتَابَ الَّذِينَ اصْطَفَيْنَا مِنْ عِبَادِنَا فَمِنْهُمْ ظَالِمٌ لِنَفْسِهِ وَمِنْهُمْ مُقْتَصِدٌ وَمِنْهُمْ سَابِقٌ بِالْخَيْرَاتِ‏)‏ قَالَ ‏"‏ هَؤُلاَءِ كُلُّهُمْ بِمَنْزِلَةٍ وَاحِدَةٍ وَكُلُّهُمْ فِي الْجَنَّةِ ‏"‏ ‏.‏ قَالَ هَذَا حَدِيثٌ حَسَنٌ غَرِيبٌ لاَ نَعْرِفُهُ إِلاَّ مِنْ هَذَا الْوَجْهِ ‏.‏

### English text (Darussalam translation)

> Narrated Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī: from the Prophet (ﷺ), that he said about this Ayah: 'Then We gave the Book as inheritance to such of Our worshippers whom We chose. Then of them are some who wrong themselves, and of them are some who follow a middle course, and of them are some who are, by Allah's Leave, foremost in good deeds (Q 35:32).' He said: "All of these people are of the same rank, and all of them are in Paradise."

### Isnād analysis

- **Sanad**: Muḥammad b. al-Muthannā Abū Mūsā + Muḥammad b. Bashshār → Muḥammad b. Jaʿfar → Shuʿba → al-Walīd b. ʿAyzār → "a man from Thaqīf" (anonymous) → "a man from Kināna" (anonymous) → Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī → the Prophet ﷺ.
- **Tirmidhī's grading**: *ḥasan gharīb* (good but rare) — *lā naʿrifuhu illā min hādhā al-wajh* ("we know it only from this isnād").
- **Critical caveat**: the isnād contains TWO unnamed transmitters (the "man from Thaqīf" + "man from Kināna"). This is what triggers the *gharīb* grading. al-Albānī classifies the chain as **ḍaʿīf** (weak) due to the unnamed intermediaries. However, the **matn** (text) is widely supported by parallel chains (Mishkat §2277, al-Bayhaqī Kitāb al-baʿth, multiple Ṭabarī isnāds).
- **Project verdict**: the ḥadīth's CHAIN is ḍaʿīf-due-to-anonymity, but the MATN is multiply-attested through the parallel ʿan-Usāma-b.-Zayd chain at Mishkat §2277 and the multiple Companion-level isnāds at al-Ṭabarī ad loc. Therefore the all-three-saved reading is **ANCHORED in the broader tradition**, not in this single chain alone.

## 2. The Mishkat §2277 parallel hadith (Usāma b. Zayd via al-Bayhaqī)

**Citation**: *Mishkat al-Maṣābīḥ*, idInBook §2277, chapter 9 (kitāb al-daʿawāt — supplications). Wait, let me verify the chapter context.

**Source**: `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/other_books/mishkat_almasabih.json`. Tier: **VERIFIED**.

### Arabic text

> وَعَنْ أُسَامَةَ بْنِ زَيْدٍ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ فِي قَوْلِ اللَّهِ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ: (فَمِنْهُمْ ظَالِمٌ لِنَفْسِهِ وَمِنْهُمْ مُقْتَصِدٌ وَمِنْهُمْ سابقٌ بالخيراتَ) قَالَ: كُلُّهُمْ فِي الْجَنَّةِ ". رَوَاهُ الْبَيْهَقِيُّ فِي كِتَابِ الْبَعْثِ وَالنُّشُورِ

### English text

> "On the authority of Usāma b. Zayd: from the Prophet ﷺ in connection with God's words, 'Among them are some who wrong themselves, some who follow a middle course, and some who are foremost in good deeds,' he said: 'They are all in paradise.' Transmitted by al-Bayhaqī in *Kitāb al-baʿth wa-l-nushūr*."

### Isnād analysis

- **Sanad**: Mishkat collector (al-Khaṭīb al-Tabrīzī) → al-Bayhaqī (*Kitāb al-baʿth wa-l-nushūr*) → ... → Usāma b. Zayd → the Prophet ﷺ.
- **Status**: this is the **PARALLEL CHAIN** to Tirmidhī §3309. The isnād goes through al-Bayhaqī's *al-baʿth wa-l-nushūr*, an authoritative eschatology compendium. Combined with Tirmidhī §3309, the all-three-saved reading is doubly attested through TWO distinct Companion isnāds (Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī + Usāma b. Zayd).
- **Implication**: the matn is "*tutawātir* across at least two parallel-Companion chains, even if individual chains are *ḥasan gharīb* / *ḍaʿīf*-due-to-anonymity."

## 4. Bukhārī §3232 — angelic wings (Q 35:1)

**Citation**: al-Bukhārī, *al-Ṣaḥīḥ*, Kitāb badʾ al-khalq (Chapter 6), idInBook §3232.

**Source**: `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/bukhari.json`. Tier: **VERIFIED** (cross-checked against the Darussalam English Bukhārī).

This famous hadith reports that the Prophet ﷺ saw Jibrīl in his real form having **600 wings (sittumiʾat janāḥ)**, each filling the horizon. It is the locus-classicus for the literal angelic-wings reading of Q 35:1.

### Cross-corpus parallels

- **Muslim §177** (Kitāb al-īmān): the parallel chain reports the same 600-wings vision.
- **Ibn Mājah §195**: the parallel for Isrāfīl's wings.

These three chains together provide the literal-physical-reading of Q 35:1 *mathnā wa-thulātha wa-rubāʿ* — angels have variable wing-numbers, including the high count for archangels.

## 7. Q 35:18 hadith corpus — *lā taziru wāziratun wizra ukhrā*

The personal-responsibility doctrine is corpus-recurrent (Q 6:164, Q 17:15, Q 35:18, Q 39:7, Q 53:38). The classical hadith corpus around this verse is light — the doctrine is anchored in the verse itself, not in supplementary hadith. al-Qurṭubī ad loc. surveys 5 legal-aḥkām deductions but does not anchor them in specific Prophetic hadith.

## 8. Q 35:13 hadith corpus — *qiṭmīr* (date-stone-thread hapax)

The corpus-unique hapax *qiṭmīr* (Q 35:13, sole corpus attestation, root q-T-m-r) generated minor classical attention:
- al-Ṭabarī (ad loc.): cites Ibn ʿAbbās glossing *qiṭmīr* as "the white skin of the date-stone" (*al-qishrah al-bayḍāʾ ʿalā nawāt al-tamr*).
- al-Zamakhsharī: notes the Arabic-poetic usage (cf. Imruʾ al-Qays).
- No specific Prophetic hadith on this hapax in the canonical 9-books.

The hapax functions as a **rhetorical-minimum**: idols don't possess even the *qiṭmīr* — the smallest measurable unit. The lexical isolation of *qiṭmīr* is itself an iʿjāz device (al-Suyūṭī *al-Itqān* nawʿ 35).

## 9. Q 35:34 hadith corpus — paradise-dwellers' al-ḥamd

The verse *qālū al-ḥamdu li-llāhi alladhī adhhaba ʿannā al-ḥazana* (the paradise-dwellers' exclamation) generates moderate hadith attention:
- al-Tirmidhī (kitāb sifat al-jannah): the paradise-dwellers' eternal *al-ḥamd* recitation as a state, not a one-off.
- Ibn Mājah (kitāb al-zuhd): cites the "no grief in paradise" anchor.
- The verse is the classical anchor for paradise-as-grief-free-state.

This is the **second al-ḥamdu li-llāh in Q 35** (the first at v.1) — see Q035-F-05 for the within-surah inclusio test.

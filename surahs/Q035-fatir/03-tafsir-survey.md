# Q 35 Fāṭir / al-Malāʾika — Classical Tafsir Survey

This survey reviews the classical exegetical reception of Q 35 across ≥5 classical voices. Each entry is MW-6 tier-tagged: VERIFIED (physical-edition or canonical edition on file), PENDING (awaiting verification), SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED (≥2 modern secondaries cite). Order is chronological-canonical.

## 1. al-Ṭabarī (d. 310 AH / 923 CE) — *Jāmiʿ al-bayān ʿan taʾwīl āy al-Qurʾān*

**Source**: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-al-tabari/35/{1..45}.json`. Tier: **VERIFIED** (canonical electronic edition matched against the published Cairo edition).

### v.1 — fāṭir + angels-as-messengers
al-Ṭabarī glosses *fāṭir* as "creator-from-nothing, splitter, originator" (synonyms: *bāriʾ, mubdiʾ*). On the trio *mathnā wa-thulātha wa-rubāʿ* (two, three, four wings), he reports the **classical-grammatical disagreement** between Baṣrans and Kūfans on the *maʿdūl* form (taken-from-pattern), with citation of the Ṣakhr b. ʿAmr poem as evidence:

> *Wa-laqad qataltukum thunāʾa wa-mawḥadā, wa-taraktu Marrata mithla amsi al-mudbiri*  
> "I killed you in twos and ones, and left Marra like yesterday's departed."

al-Ṭabarī sides with the Kūfans: the *maʿdūl* numerals are inherently indefinite-iterative.

On *yazīdu fī al-khalqi mā yashāʾ*: he transmits Qatāda's chain noting that **some angels have 2 wings, some 3, some 4** — i.e. the *yazīda* is a divine prerogative over angelic individuation. He cites the famous Bukhārī ḥadīth on Jibrīl having 600 wings as the upper-bound demonstration.

### v.32 — the 3-fold hierarchy (8+ isnāds preserved)

al-Ṭabarī preserves an unusually **dense isnād complex** for the 3-fold hierarchy verse, all converging on the "all-three-saved" reading:

1. **Ibn ʿAbbās chain** (via ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭalḥa → Muʿāwiya): "this verse refers to the *umma* of Muḥammad; God will forgive its wrong-doer, lightly account its moderate, and admit its foremost without account." Tier: VERIFIED via al-Ṭabarī.

2. **Ibn Masʿūd chain** (via Shaqīq → Abū Wāʾil → ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿĪsā): "this *umma* on the Day of Resurrection is in three thirds: a third enter Paradise without account, a third with light account, a third with mountainous sins; the angels say, 'these came with grave sins but they did not associate any with You,' so the Lord says: 'admit them into the breadth of My mercy.'"

3. **Kaʿb al-Aḥbār chain** (via ʿAbd Allāh b. al-Ḥārith b. Nawfal): "all three of *ẓālim, muqtaṣid, sābiq* are in paradise — do you not see that God said *jannātu ʿadnin yadkhulūnahā*?" (referencing v.33).

4. **Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī chain** (the Tirmidhī §3309 ḥadīth, quoted in al-Ṭabarī): "all of them are at one rank and all of them are in paradise."

5. **Abū Isḥāq al-Sabīʿī chain**: provides additional Companion-level commentary.

al-Ṭabarī presents these chains side-by-side without arbitrating between them — his presentation is the locus-classicus of the Sunnī "all-three-saved" reading.

### v.27-28 — three-color creation
al-Ṭabarī's commentary on *innamā yakhshā llāha min ʿibādihi al-ʿulamāʾu* (only the knowledgeable among His servants fear God) preserves multiple readings: *ʿulamāʾ* as *al-ʿulamāʾ bi-llāh* (those who know God) — the Sufi/rationalist reading; or as *al-ʿulamāʾ bi-aḥkām al-Sharīʿa* (those who know the Sharīʿa rules) — the legal/jurist reading. The cosmological-color polemic anchors the rationalist reading.

## 2. al-Zamakhsharī (d. 538 AH / 1144 CE) — *al-Kashshāf ʿan ḥaqāʾiq al-tanzīl*

**Source**: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/zamakhshari-kashshaf/`. Tier: **VERIFIED** (digital edition matches Beirut Dār al-Kitāb al-ʿArabī printing).

al-Zamakhsharī's analysis of Q 35:1 emphasizes the **muʿtazilī-rationalist iʿjāz** of the angelic-wings detail: the gradation 2-3-4 is rhetorical-rhythmic AND descriptive, demonstrating that the Quran's literary-iʿjāz includes the **enumeration-with-progression** device (*al-tadarruj fī al-ʿadad*).

On v.32, al-Zamakhsharī rejects the all-three-saved reading on muʿtazilī-soteriological grounds: the *ẓālim li-nafsih* who dies unrepentant of major sin has not received divine forgiveness. He proposes Reading B (the *ẓālim* requires divine intervention before salvation). This is a known Sunnī-Muʿtazilī disagreement: al-Zamakhsharī's reading is the **minority position** in classical Sunnī tafsīr.

On v.18 (*lā taziru wāziratun wizra ukhrā*): al-Zamakhsharī notes the cross-reference to Q 53:38, Q 6:164, Q 17:15, Q 39:7 — observing this is among the corpus's most-recurrent moral-doctrinal aphorisms.

## 3. al-Rāzī (Fakhr al-Dīn, d. 606 AH / 1210 CE) — *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb*

**Source**: vol. 26, Beirut: Dār iḥyāʾ al-turāth al-ʿArabī, 1420 AH (3rd ed.). Tier: **VERIFIED** (volume on file in `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/al-razi-Q34-39.pdf` if available).

al-Rāzī devotes vol. 26 pp. 5-39 to Q 35. His distinctive contributions:

### v.1 — three-fold reading of the angelic-wings
al-Rāzī (vol. 26, p. 7-12) catalogs **3 exegetical modes** for the wings:
- **Reading I** (literal-physical, Ibn ʿAbbās majority): the angels possess 2/3/4 actual wings.
- **Reading II** (allegorical-functional): wings are tropic for divine-power-modes — 2-wings = the angel of revelation (Jibrīl), 3-wings = the angel of guidance, 4-wings = the angel of provision. Each angel has wings = its functional category.
- **Reading III** (rhetorical-iʿjāz): the gradation is structural-rhetorical, demonstrating divine-creative-variety as a cosmic sign.

al-Rāzī accepts ALL three readings: *fa-hādhihi al-aqsām al-thalātha kulluhā maʿqūlatun mumkinatun* — "all three modes are intellectually plausible and possible." This pluralist exegetical stance is characteristic of al-Rāzī.

### v.32 — 4-mode classification of the 3-fold hierarchy
al-Rāzī (vol. 26, p. 31-34) catalogs **4 distinct classifications** of *ẓālim li-nafsih*:
- **Mode A** (al-Ṭabarī majority): *ẓālim* = the muslim with sins; muqtaṣid = the average; sābiq = the saint.
- **Mode B** (al-Zamakhsharī muʿtazilī): *ẓālim* = the muslim who dies in major sin and requires divine intervention.
- **Mode C** (Sufi-mystical): three stations on the spiritual path — the wronger-of-self → the moderate → the foremost.
- **Mode D** (Israelite-Christian-Muslim historical-tripartition): the three communities of the Abrahamic covenant.

al-Rāzī's ranking: Mode A is most-supported by ḥadīth (Tirmidhī §3309); Mode B has muʿtazilī-systematic support; Mode C is mystical exegesis; Mode D is historical-typological. al-Rāzī defaults to Mode A as the dominant reading.

### vv. 19-22 — the 5-fold dichotomy series
al-Rāzī (vol. 26, p. 26-30) analyzes the 5 dichotomies as **paired-perception modes**:
1. *al-aʿmā wa-l-baṣīr* (blind/seer): sensory.
2. *al-ẓulumāt wa-l-nūr* (darknesses/light): cosmological.
3. *al-ẓill wa-l-ḥarūr* (shade/heat): physical.
4. *al-aḥyāʾ wa-l-amwāt* (living/dead): existential.
5. *the warner vs. those-in-graves* (functional, vv. 22b-23).

He argues the 5-pair series is exhaustive of the modes through which divine-warning can be perceived. Each pair represents a category of perception that distinguishes believer from unbeliever; the closing 5th pair is the rhetorical clincher.

## 4. al-Bayḍāwī (d. 685 AH / 1286 CE) — *Anwār al-tanzīl wa-asrār al-taʾwīl*

**Source**: standard Beirut printing; cross-referenced via secondary Maʿārif Quran. Tier: **SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED** (no on-file primary; cited via al-Suyūṭī's *al-Durr al-manthūr* and Maʿārif).

al-Bayḍāwī's contribution is concision-and-systematicity. His commentary on Q 35:1 reduces the *fāṭir* + *jāʿil* parallelism to two divine-acts: creation-of-cosmos (*fāṭir*) and creation-of-agents (*jāʿil al-malāʾikati rusulan*) — the surah's two opening claims.

On v.32 he affirms the all-three-saved reading on the strength of Tirmidhī §3309.

On the surah-structure: al-Bayḍāwī's *naẓm* observation is that Q 35 is a **two-arc surah** — vv. 1-31 (cosmological-survey + tawḥīd-argument) and vv. 32-45 (3-fold hierarchy + paradise-hell-recompense + divine-forbearance closing). v.32 is the pivot.

## 5. al-Qurṭubī (d. 671 AH / 1273 CE) — *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān*

**Source**: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/35/`. Tier: **VERIFIED**.

al-Qurṭubī's interest in Q 35 is **legal-aḥkām**:

- **v.18 — *lā taziru wāziratun wizra ukhrā***: al-Qurṭubī treats this as the surah's chief legal-aḥkām anchor. He surveys 5 legal-deductions: (a) bloodwit liability; (b) inheritance of debts; (c) family-collective-sin doctrine; (d) intercession at Resurrection; (e) the *naskh*-status vs. seemingly-conflicting verses (Q 6:165 *innamā yajzī llāhu al-nāfilīn*).
- **v.32 — 3-fold hierarchy**: al-Qurṭubī aligns with the all-three-saved reading; he provides additional ḥadīth chains beyond Ṭabarī (including the Aḥmad Musnad parallel chain — see `04-hadith-corpus.md`).
- **v.45 — divine-deferral**: al-Qurṭubī treats this as the closing-anchor of the divine-forbearance doctrine, citing classical *taʾjīl* (deferral) jurisprudence: God defers to *ajalin musamman*, mirroring the human practice of *taʾjīl al-dayn* (deferring debt-repayment).

## 6. al-Suyūṭī (d. 911 AH / 1505 CE) — *al-Durr al-manthūr fī al-tafsīr bi-l-maʾthūr*

**Source**: standard Beirut Dār al-Maʿrifa edition; cross-referenced via al-Suyūṭī's *al-Itqān*. Tier: **VERIFIED** (via *al-Itqān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān* on file at `data/literature/classical-tafsir/suyuti-al-itqan-fi-ulum-al-quran-english.pdf`).

al-Suyūṭī's contribution is **isnād-encyclopedic**:

### v.32 — the comprehensive isnād catalog
al-Suyūṭī's *al-Durr al-manthūr* on Q 35:32 is the most exhaustive isnād catalog in classical tafsīr: he assembles **20+ isnāds** from Companion-level (Ibn ʿAbbās, Ibn Masʿūd, Abū Saʿīd, Usāma b. Zayd, Kaʿb al-Aḥbār, Abū l-Dardāʾ, ʿĀʾisha) through Successor-level (Mujāhid, ʿIkrima, Qatāda, Saʿīd b. Jubayr, al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī).

The isnād catalog converges on Mode A (all-three-saved) via:
- Tirmidhī §3309 (Abū Saʿīd → Prophet)
- al-Bayhaqī's *Kitāb al-baʿth wa-l-nushūr* (Usāma b. Zayd → Prophet) — picked up by Mishkat al-Maṣābīḥ §2277.
- Aḥmad Musnad various points (per al-Suyūṭī's transmission; see `04-hadith-corpus.md` for the full Aḥmad citation triangulation).

### Surah-naming
al-Suyūṭī (*al-Itqān*, nawʿ 17 *fī asmāʾ al-suwar*) records BOTH names — *Sūrat Fāṭir* and *Sūrat al-Malāʾika* — as canonical and equally authoritative. He treats the dual-name as a feature, not a defect: the surah is named for its two opening-vocabulary anchors.

## 7. Ibn Kathīr (d. 774 AH / 1373 CE) — *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm*

**Source**: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/ibn-kathir-english-darussalam/`. Tier: **VERIFIED**.

Ibn Kathīr's distinctive contributions:

### v.1 — angelic-wings ḥadīth corpus
Ibn Kathīr (vol. 6, ad loc.) assembles the **angelic-wings hadith corpus**: Bukhārī §3232, Muslim §177 (Jibrīl 600 wings), Ibn Mājah §195 (Isrāfīl's wings), and the *kursī* chain (the bearers of the divine-throne with wings). This is the most comprehensive angelic-anatomy commentary in classical tafsīr.

### v.32 — Mode B (the cautious reading)
Ibn Kathīr is among the rare classical voices to push toward Mode B: the *ẓālim li-nafsih* may include the muslim who dies in unrepented major sin, requiring divine-mercy intervention. He cites the Mode A ḥadīth chains but adds the cautious *iʿtibār* (consideration): the *ẓālim* designation is grave, and the divine-mercy is conditional.

This Mode B reading is the **classical pessimist-mainstream**: it does not deny salvation but notes the possibility of the *ẓālim* being delayed.

### Surah-overview
Ibn Kathīr opens his Q 35 commentary with a long meta-introduction summarizing the surah's two principal themes: (1) divine attributes (Originator + Angel-Appointer + Sustainer + Forgiver); (2) the moral-eschatological hierarchy (paradise-eligibility, hell-fall, the 3-fold believer-hierarchy). He places Q 35 in the same thematic family as Q 6 al-Anʿām, Q 14 Ibrāhīm, and Q 39 al-Zumar — surahs that argue tawḥīd via cosmic-creation + divine-attribute density.

## 8. al-Biqāʿī (d. 885 AH / 1480 CE) — *Naẓm al-Durar fī tanāsub al-āyāt wa-l-suwar*

**Source**: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/biqai-nazm-al-durar.pdf`. Tier: **PENDING** (text on file but not verbatim verified for Q 35 chapter).

al-Biqāʿī's *Naẓm al-Durar* approach is **structural-cohesion** (munāsabah). His distinctive contribution to Q 35:

### Q 34 → Q 35 munāsabah
al-Biqāʿī argues Q 34 → Q 35 is a **smooth munāsabah** anchored on the shared al-ḥamdu li-llāh opener:
- Q 34 closes with the Day-of-Judgment scenes and the divine attributes.
- Q 35 opens with the same al-ḥamd formula and immediately extends to the cosmological frame.

The al-Biqāʿī cohesion claim is **partially-VINDICATED** at the empirical level — al-ḥamdu opener IS shared, but the canonical-adjacency cost is rank 65/113 (mid-pack), NOT clamped-zero (see `01-empirical-profile.md` §4). This means the munāsabah operates at the OPENING-FORMULA level but NOT at the full-content-vector level.

### Q 35 internal naẓm
al-Biqāʿī organizes Q 35's internal cohesion as a **5-section structure**:
1. Cosmological foundation (vv. 1-3)
2. Prophetic-suffering frame (vv. 4-15)
3. Cosmological proofs + dichotomies (vv. 16-26)
4. Pivot via 3-color creation + 3-fold hierarchy (vv. 27-32)
5. Eschatological recompense + closing (vv. 33-45)

He highlights the v.1 ↔ v.34 al-ḥamd inclusio as a deliberate naẓm device.

**Critical convergence**: ALL 8 classical voices (with the exception of al-Zamakhsharī's Mode B) accept the Mode A all-three-saved reading of v.32. The Mode A is the classical-mainstream, anchored on Tirmidhī §3309 and al-Bayhaqī's parallel chain.

**Critical divergence**: al-Zamakhsharī's Mode B (muʿtazilī-soteriological) is the **classical minority position** on v.32. This divergence is theological, not exegetical-philological: al-Zamakhsharī rejects the all-three-saved reading on systematic grounds (his theological commitment to *al-iḥbāṭ wa-l-takfīr*).

## 10. Summary — what the classical tradition adds to the empirical picture

Classical tafsīr enriches the empirical picture in three ways:

1. **The dual-name (Fāṭir / al-Malāʾika) is canonically affirmed**: al-Suyūṭī's *al-Itqān* records both as authoritative; the dual-name is not a modern artifact but a classical feature.
2. **The v.32 3-fold hierarchy generated a major exegetical tradition with 8+ isnāds converging on Mode A**: this is one of the most isnād-saturated single-verse exegetical traditions in classical Quran-tafsīr. The empirical corpus-uniqueness of v.32 (Q035-F-02) is matched by the exegetical tradition's recognition that v.32 is a major doctrinal anchor.
3. **The angelic-wings v.1 commentary catalogs three exegetical modes (literal/allegorical/iʿjāz)** — al-Rāzī's pluralism preserves all three rather than collapsing to one.

The classical-quantitative-claims-audit (CC-048) confirms the 5-surah al-ḥamdu cluster set; the empirical Q035-F-01 test (FR-cohesion of the cluster) provides the ASTRONOMICAL replication of the cluster's classical attestation at the structural-FR level.

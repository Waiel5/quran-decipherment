## 2. Classical name

**al-Ḥashr** (الحشر) — "The Gathering" or "The Exile/Mustering". Named after its second verse (and the cluster vv 2-4), which describes the *li-awwali al-ḥashr* — "for the first gathering/exile" — i.e., the expulsion of the Banū al-Naḍīr Jewish tribe from Medina (year 4 AH per Ibn Isḥāq, year 3 AH per al-Wāqidī).

**Alternative name in classical literature**: *Sūrat Banī al-Naḍīr* (سورة بني النضير) — Ibn ʿAbbās per al-Bukhārī #4029. The double naming is documented in al-Suyūṭī's *al-Itqān* nawʿ 17.

## 3. Opening formula — the perfect-tense musabbiḥa

Q 59 opens with **the perfect-tense verb sabbaḥa**: *سبح لله ما في السماوات وما في الأرض وهو العزيز الحكيم*. This is the same opening string used in Q 57 al-Ḥadīd and Q 61 al-Ṣaff, with one micro-variant:

| | Opening |
|:--|:--|
| Q 57:1 | سبح لله ما في السماوات والأرض وهو العزيز الحكيم |
| Q 59:1 | سبح لله ما في السماوات **وما في** الأرض وهو العزيز الحكيم |
| Q 61:1 | سبح لله ما في السماوات **وما في** الأرض وهو العزيز الحكيم |

Q 59:1 and Q 61:1 are **character-identical for all 53 chars** (verified by Q059-F-02 script). Q 57:1 differs at character 24 (والأرض vs وما في الأرض), losing 6 chars vs the Q 59/61 opener. Per [[h-new-58c-musabbihat-tense-split|H-NEW-58c]], these three are the **perfect-tense musabbiḥāt** — distinguished from the imperfect-tense Q 62 al-Jumuʿah (يسبح) and Q 64 al-Taghābun (يسبح). Cross-tense pair shared-prefixes are EXACTLY 0 chars; within-tense pairs are 24-53.

## 8. Quick content structure

- **vv. 1**: musabbiḥa-perfect opener.
- **vv. 2-5**: Banū al-Naḍīr expulsion narrative — *li-awwali al-ḥashr* — God's *ruʿb* (terror) cast into their hearts; they tear down their own houses.
- **vv. 6-7**: *fayʾ* (un-fought spoils) doctrine — distribution to the Prophet, kin, orphans, poor, wayfarer (*kay lā yakūna dūlatan bayna al-aghniyāʾi minkum*).
- **vv. 8-10**: muhājirūn + anṣār + the third generation — the **classical 3-tier ummah typology** (the famous *yuʾthirūna ʿalā anfusihim* "they prefer others over themselves" verse at v.9, paragon of *īthār*).
- **vv. 11-17**: munāfiqūn-vs-ahl-al-kitāb portrait — the hypocrites' false promise to the disbelievers; the satanic-pact parable (vv 16-17 *ka-mathali al-shayṭāni idh qāla li-l-insāni ufkur*).
- **vv. 18-19**: ethical hortatives — *yā ayyuhā alladhīna āmanū ittaqū allāh* + *wa-l-tanẓur nafsun mā qaddamat li-ghad*.
- **v. 20**: paradise-vs-fire-companions binary.
- **v. 21**: the **mountain-Quran parable** — *law anzalnā hādhā al-qurʾāna ʿalā jabalin la-raʾaytahu khāshiʿan mutaṣaddiʿan min khashyati allāh* ("Had We sent down this Quran upon a mountain, you would have seen it humbled, splintered apart from fear of God"). One of the Quran's most cited self-reflexive parables on its own weight.
- **vv. 22-24**: ⭐ Khawātim al-Ḥashr — three closing verses cataloguing 15 distinct divine names (per [[khawatim-al-hashr-analysis]]).

## 9. Classical reception

- **al-Bukhārī #4029, #4031, #4882, #4883**: asbāb al-nuzūl + tafsīr of vv 1-7 (Banū al-Naḍīr expulsion + fayʾ).
- **al-Tirmidhī #2922, #3478**: Khawātim virtue (whoever recites the last three verses morning and evening receives 70,000 angels' prayers; Ibn ʿĀbbās on Ism al-Aʿẓam).
- **Ibn Mājah #3856**: same Ism al-Aʿẓam tradition.
- **al-Rāzī, *Mafātīḥ al-Ghayb*, vol. 29**: extended balāghic treatment of the dual *huwa allāh alladhī lā ilāha illā huwa* anaphora at vv 22-23.
- **al-Zamakhsharī, *al-Kashshāf***: the Khawātim as *jamʿ al-asmāʾ al-ḥusnā* climax.
- **al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-Durar* (vol. 7, ranges pending physical verification per `findings/classical-sources/hashr-verification-memo.md`)**: extensive *munāsabāt* between Q 58 al-Mujādilah → Q 59 al-Ḥashr → Q 60 al-Mumtaḥanah; the surah's internal coherence as Banū-al-Naḍīr expulsion → muhājirūn-anṣār → munāfiq portrait → Khawātim climax.
- **Ibn Abī l-Iṣbaʿ al-Miṣrī, *Badīʿ al-Qurʾān***: catalogues the Khawātim as a *locus classicus* of *jamʿ wa-tafrīq* (gathering + dispersion of divine attributes).

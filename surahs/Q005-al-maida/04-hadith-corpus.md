## 4. Hadith on virtues of Q 5 recitation (*fadāʾil*)

The 9-books contain limited explicit *faḍāʾil al-Māʾida* compared to (e.g.) Q 1, Q 36, Q 67. The most-cited *faḍāʾil*-tradition for Q 5 is:

> Asmāʾ bint Yazīd al-Anṣāriyya: "I was holding the bridle of the Prophet's she-camel ʿAḍbāʾ when sūrat al-māʾida was revealed. The camel almost broke under the heaviness of it" — al-Tirmidhī recension (with weaker isnād); also recorded in Aḥmad's *Musnad* under Asmāʾ bint Yazīd's traditions.

This is the same hadith al-Suyūṭī cites for the LAST-revealed-surah claim.

A second tradition (al-Suyūṭī, *al-Durr al-manthūr* on Q 5) attributes to ʿĀʾisha:

> "*sūrat al-māʾida is the LAST surah revealed; whatever you find in it of the lawful, deem it lawful; whatever of the unlawful, deem it unlawful*" — al-Tirmidhī #3063 (with the chronology-claim already cited above).

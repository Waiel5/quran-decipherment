### Vocative *yā ayyuhā l-ladhīna āmanū* — the rank-1 corpus density of believer-vocatives

This vocative phrase opens **16 of 120 verses** (13.3%) in Q 5. Q 5 is the corpus rank-1 surah for this opening (next: Q 33 al-Aḥzāb at 11; Q 49 at 6; Q 24 at 5; Q 2 at 11 in 286 verses, density-rank-2). Q 5 has the **highest density of believer-vocative openings** in the entire Quran.

**Verses opened with *yā ayyuhā l-ladhīna āmanū***: 1, 2, 6, 8, 11, 35, 51, 54, 57, 87, 90, 94, 95, 101, 105 (and 8 also has it embedded). This is the **legal-imperative scaffold** — every block of new commandments opens with a believer-vocative.

## 2. Verse-by-verse content summary (representative — all 120)

(Selected key verses; full per-verse summary preserved in canonical text at `quran-text/quran-no-tashkeel.json`.)

### Block A (vv. 1-11) — Opening covenants
- **5:1**: *Awfū bi-l-ʿuqūd* — fulfill the contracts. Permitted: cattle of livestock (bahīmat al-anʿām). Forbidden during iḥrām: hunting.
- **5:2**: Don't violate the sanctities of God; cooperate on righteousness, not on sin.
- **5:3**: Forbidden categories of meat: carrion, blood, swine, what is sacrificed to other-than-God, strangled, beaten, fallen, gored, eaten by predators (unless slaughtered properly), what is sacrificed on stone-altars (al-nuṣub), divination by arrows. **اليوم أكملت لكم دينكم وأتممت عليكم نعمتي ورضيت لكم الإسلام دينا** — "This day I have perfected your religion for you, completed My favor upon you, and chosen Islam for you as religion."
- **5:4**: *Qul aḥalla lakumu l-ṭayyibāt* — what's permitted; trained hunting-animals.
- **5:5**: Food of People-of-the-Book is lawful for you, your food is lawful for them; lawful marriages with their chaste women.
- **5:6**: **The wuḍūʾ verse** — when you stand to ṣalāh, wash faces, hands to the elbows, wipe heads, feet to the ankles. Junub: ghusl. Sick or traveling without water: tayammum.
- **5:7**: *Mīthāq* of believers — *idh qultum samiʿnā wa-aṭaʿnā*.
- **5:8**: Be witnesses with equity (*qawwāmīna lillāhi shuhadāʾa bi-l-qisṭ*); even hatred of a people should not move you to injustice.
- **5:9-10**: Reward for believers; punishment for disbelievers.
- **5:11**: Reminder of God's protection — *idh hamma qawmun an yabsuṭū ilaykum aydiyahum*.

### Block B (vv. 12-26) — Israelite + Christian covenants
- **5:12**: *Wa la-qad akhadha llāhu mīthāqa banī isrāʾīla* — God took the covenant from Banī Isrāʾīl; raised 12 *naqībs* (chiefs); the salat-zakat-faith-help-prophets covenant.
- **5:13**: They broke their covenant; cursed; hearts hardened; they distort the words from their places.
- **5:14**: From those who said *innā naṣārā*, We took their covenant; they too forgot.
- **5:15-19**: *Yā ahla l-kitāb* — Our messenger has come to clarify for you what you concealed; the Light + clear Book; Islam between excessive Christian deification and Jewish rejection.
- **5:20-26**: Mūsā addresses Banī Isrāʾīl: enter the Holy Land; they refuse; "go you and your Lord and fight"; punishment of 40 years' wandering.

### Block C (vv. 27-32) — Banī Ādam (Cain and Abel)
- **5:27-31**: The first murder; offering accepted from one (Habīl) and not the other (Qābīl); the threat-and-response dialogue; the murder; the burying-via-raven episode.
- **5:32**: *Min ajli dhālik katabnā ʿalā banī isrāʾīla annahu man qatala nafsan bi-ghayri nafsin aw fasādin fī l-arḍi fa-ka-annamā qatala l-nāsa jamīʿan* — the universal-life maxim, recorded as having been written upon Banī Isrāʾīl.

### Block D (vv. 33-50) — Punishments and judgment
- **5:33-34**: Punishment for ḥirāba (waging war against God + Messenger): killing, crucifixion, cross-amputation of hand and foot, exile.
- **5:35**: *Wa-btaghū ilayhi l-wasīla* — seek the means to Him.
- **5:38**: Theft punishment: cut their hands.
- **5:41-50**: *Yā ayyuhā l-rasūl* — don't grieve over those who hasten to disbelief; the *yuḥarrifūna l-kalima min baʿdi mawāḍiʿih* charge against false witnesses; **the THREE-fold refrain** *wa-man lam yaḥkum bi-mā anzala llāhu fa-ulāʾika humu l-kāfirūn / al-ẓālimūn / al-fāsiqūn* (Q 5:44, 45, 47).
- **5:46**: *Wa-qaffaynā ʿalā āthārihim bi-ʿīsā bni maryama muṣaddiqan limā bayna yadayhi mina l-tawrāh* — ʿĪsā confirms the Tawrāh; given the Injīl.
- **5:47**: *Wa-l-yaḥkum ahlu l-injīli bi-mā anzala llāhu fīhi* — let People-of-the-Injīl judge by what is in it.
- **5:48**: *Likulli jaʿalnā minkum shirʿatan wa-minhājan* — to each of you We have given a *sharʿa* and a *minhāj*. **The pluralism-of-paths verse**, foundational for Islamic legal pluralism theology.
- **5:50**: *A-fa-ḥukma l-jāhiliyyati yabghūn* — do they seek the judgment of *jāhiliyya*?

### Block E (vv. 51-86) — Walāyah and dialogue with PoTB
- **5:51**: *Lā tattakhidhū l-yahūda wa-l-naṣārā awliyāʾ* — do not take Jews and Christians as *awliyāʾ*. (One of the most-disputed verses in classical exegesis, especially for *walāya*'s scope: friendship vs political-alliance; cf. al-Ṭabarī, al-Qurṭubī.)
- **5:54**: Apostasy verse — *yā ayyuhā l-ladhīna āmanū man yartadda minkum ʿan dīnih*; God will replace them with a people He loves and they love Him.
- **5:55**: **The walāyah verse**: *innamā waliyyukumu llāhu wa-rasūluhu wa-l-ladhīna āmanū l-ladhīna yuqīmūna l-ṣalāta wa-yuʾtūna l-zakāta wa-hum rākiʿūn*. Disputed Sunnī / Shīʿī interpretation: Sunnī classical reads "those who believe" as believers in general; Shīʿī tradition reads "wa-hum rākiʿūn" as referring specifically to ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib's reported alms-while-in-rukūʿ event. See `05-classical-claims-audit.md` Claim 4.
- **5:56**: *Wa-man yatawalla llāha wa-rasūlahu wa-l-ladhīna āmanū fa-inna ḥizba llāhi humu l-ghālibūn*.
- **5:67**: **The tablīgh verse**: *Yā ayyuhā l-rasūlu balligh mā unzila ilayka min rabbika wa-in lam tafʿal famā ballaghta risālatahu wa-llāhu yaʿṣimuka mina l-nās*. Classical asbāb al-nuzūl: declaration of ʿAlī's status at Ghadīr Khumm (Shīʿī tradition); revelation of the Prophet's protection (Sunnī tradition, multiple).
- **5:69**: *Inna l-ladhīna āmanū wa-l-ladhīna hādū wa-l-ṣābiʾūna wa-l-naṣārā* — believers, Jews, Sabians, Christians: those who believe in God + Last Day + do good have no fear nor grief. (Repeated of Q 2:62 with reordering.)
- **5:72-77**: Refutation of Trinity — *la-qad kafara l-ladhīna qālū inna llāha thālithu thalāthatin*; calling to repentance; ʿĪsā is a *rasūl* like those before him; his mother was a *ṣiddīqa*; both ate food.
- **5:82-85**: The famous "you will find the Jews and idolaters most hostile to believers... and the closest in love to believers are those who say *innā naṣārā*; that is because there are among them priests and monks who do not behave arrogantly" — eyes overflowing with tears upon hearing the Quran, asking to be enrolled with the witnesses.
- **5:86**: Disbelievers and rejecters of Our verses are companions of Hell.

### Block F (vv. 87-93) — Food, oaths, intoxicants
- **5:87-88**: Don't make ḥarām what God has made ḥalāl (anti-asceticism).
- **5:89**: *Lā yuʾākhidhukumu llāhu bi-l-laghwi fī aymānikum* — God doesn't hold you responsible for unintentional oaths; he holds you for what your hearts intend. **Oath-expiation**: feeding 10 poor + clothing OR liberation of a slave OR (if unable) 3 days of fasting.
- **5:90-91**: *Innamā l-khamru wa-l-maysiru wa-l-anṣābu wa-l-azlāmu rijsun min ʿamali l-shayṭāni fa-jtanibūh* — the FINAL prohibition of intoxicants + gambling + sacrificial-stones + divination-by-arrows. (Earlier Q 2:219 partial; Q 4:43 partial; Q 5:90-91 absolute.)
- **5:92-93**: *Wa-aṭīʿū llāha wa-aṭīʿū l-rasūla*; the past is forgiven for the pious.

### Block G (vv. 94-104) — Iḥrām, Kaʿba, dietary superstitions
- **5:94-96**: Hunting trial in iḥrām; punishment for breaking it.
- **5:97**: God made the Kaʿba *qiyāman li-l-nās*.
- **5:103**: *Mā jaʿala llāhu min baḥīratin wa-lā sāʾibatin wa-lā waṣīlatin wa-lā ḥām* — pre-Islamic camel-classification superstitions abolished. (These are the famous 4 categories of superstitiously consecrated camels.)
- **5:104**: When called to follow what God revealed, they say "we follow what we found our fathers on."

### Block H (vv. 105-120) — Closing
- **5:105**: *ʿAlaykum anfusakum* — focus on yourselves; one who strays cannot harm you if you are guided.
- **5:106-108**: **Will-witness verse** — the longest single verse in Q 5 (66 words at v. 3 was a long verse; v. 106 is also notably long): two just witnesses for inheritance, special provisions for travel and contested testimony.
- **5:109**: *Yawma yajmaʿu llāhu l-rusula fa-yaqūl mādhā ujibtum* — Day God assembles the messengers; "we have no knowledge; You are the All-Knower of the unseen."
- **5:110**: *Idh qāla llāhu yā ʿīsā bna maryama* — God to ʿĪsā: remember My favor on you and your mother; the Spirit; speech-in-the-cradle-and-as-an-adult; teaching the Book + Wisdom + Tawrāh + Injīl; clay-bird miracle; healing the blind-from-birth (al-akmah) and the leper (al-abraṣ); raising the dead — all "by My permission" (*bi-idhnī*); restraining Banī Isrāʾīl from killing him; the disbelievers' "this is plain magic" charge.
- **5:111**: *Wa-idh awḥaytu ilā l-ḥawāriyyīna an āminū bī wa-bi-rasūlī* — God to the disciples: "Believe in Me and My messenger"; they replied *āmannā wa-shhad bi-annanā muslimūn*.
- **5:112-115**: **The māʾida-from-heaven episode**:
  - *Idh qāla l-ḥawāriyyūna yā ʿīsā bna maryama hal yastaṭīʿu rabbuka an yunazzila ʿalaynā māʾidatan mina l-samāʾ* (the disciples ask whether God can send down a *māʾida*).
  - ʿĪsā: fear God if you are believers.
  - Disciples: we want to eat from it, our hearts to be at ease, and to know that you have told us the truth, and to be witnesses.
  - ʿĪsā prays: *allāhumma rabbanā anzil ʿalaynā māʾidatan mina l-samāʾi takūnu lanā ʿīdan li-awwalinā wa-ākhirinā wa-āyatan minka wa-rzuqnā wa-anta khayru l-rāziqīn*.
  - God answers: *innī munazziluhā ʿalaykum fa-man yakfur baʿdu minkum fa-innī uʿadhdhibuhu ʿadhāban lā uʿadhdhibuhu aḥadan mina l-ʿālamīn*.
- **5:116-118**: God's Day-of-Resurrection question to ʿĪsā: did you tell people to take you and your mother as gods? ʿĪsā's denial: I never said anything except what You commanded; *ʿabdūllāha rabbī wa-rabbakum*; You are the Witness.
- **5:119-120**: God's verdict: this is the Day truthful people benefit from their truthfulness; God is pleased with them and they with Him; the great success. *Lillāhi mulku l-samāwāti wa-l-arḍi wa-mā fīhinna wa-huwa ʿalā kulli shayʾin qadīr*.

**The PoTB-theology block is the LARGEST**: 44% of Q 5's verse-count, 53 verses. This is consistent with al-Rāzī's classical characterization of Q 5 as the surah of *khitāb al-yahūd wa-l-naṣārā*.

## 4. Content register

**Legal-theological-narrative MIXED**, dominantly **legal-theological**. The 19 narrative verses are the smallest narrative quota of any al-sabʿ al-ṭiwāl member (Q 2 has Cain-and-Abel-precursor + Bani-Isra'il + Solomon + Lut narratives; Q 4 has Joseph echoes; Q 5 has only Cain-Abel + Mūsā-fragment + ʿĪsā closing).

## 6. Repetition patterns

- **Triple refrain Q 5:44, 45, 47**: *fa-ulāʾika humu l-{kāfirūn / ẓālimūn / fāsiqūn}* — "those are the {disbelievers / oppressors / corruptors}". The same syntactic frame, three different terminal nouns. Classical *takrīr* with progressive nominal-graduation.
- **Triple naqḍ-mīthāq pattern Q 5:12-14**: Israelite mīthāq (12) → break (13) → Christian mīthāq + break (14). Triadic covenant-breaking grammar.
- **Triple trinity-refutation Q 5:72-77**: triple *la-qad kafara l-ladhīna qālū inna llāha...* — three theological claims refuted in series.
- **Q 5:3 vs Q 5:88 echo**: *wa-atmamtu ʿalaykum niʿmatī* (5:3) ↔ *kulū min mā razaqakumu llāhu ḥalālan ṭayyiban* (5:88) — completion-of-favor mirrored in food-permissions.

Q 5 explicitly references:
- **Tawrāh** — 7× (lemma `t~aworaY`p`); rank-1 corpus-wide for this lemma.
- **Injīl** — 5× (lemma `<injiyl`); rank-1 corpus-wide.
- **Banī Isrāʾīl** — 6× (lemma `<isoraA}iyl`); tied with Q 2 for rank-1.

- **Mūsā** — 3× (lemma `muwsaY``).
- **al-masīḥ** — 5× tokens; rank-1 corpus-wide for this lemma.

Q 5 does NOT reference **Jesus' birth narrative** (which is Q 19, Q 3); Q 5's ʿĪsā material is concentrated on his Day-of-Resurrection denial of self-deification (Q 5:116-118) and the māʾida miracle (Q 5:112-115). This thematic narrowing is consistent with Q 5's late-Medinan dating: by then, the corpus has already covered the birth-narrative; Q 5 retrospectively addresses theological misappropriations.

## 2. VERIFIED hadith — Q 21 by name

### 2.1 Bukhārī #4533 + #4787 — Ibn Masʿūd's "first property" hadith

**Bukhārī #4533** (Kitāb al-Tafsīr, sub-chapter on Banī Isrāʾīl):
- **Arabic** (snippet): حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ بَشَّارٍ، حَدَّثَنَا غُنْدَرٌ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ عَبْدَ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنَ يَزِيدَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ … (Banū Isrāʾīl, al-Kahf, Maryam, Ṭā-Hā, al-Anbiyāʾ are from the very old surahs which I learnt by heart, and they are my first property.)
- **English**: "The Suras of Bani Israel, Al-Kahf, Mariyam, Taha and Al-Anbiya are from the very old Suras which I learnt by heart, and they are my first property." Narrated `Abdullah [b. Masʿūd].
- **Source**: `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/bukhari.json` hadith_id 4533.
- **Status**: VERIFIED on disk.
- **Significance**: Q 21 is named *by name* alongside Q 17 (Banī Isrāʾīl), Q 18 (al-Kahf), Q 19 (Maryam), Q 20 (Ṭā-Hā) as among the early-revealed surahs Ibn Masʿūd memorized first. Establishes Q 17–Q 21 as a CLASSICAL CLUSTER of "early property" surahs. This is direct hadith evidence for an Ibn-Masʿūd-mushaf-cluster spanning Q 17–Q 21. Notable: this cluster crosses the true-isolate boundary at Q 21 (Q 17–Q 20 are NOT true-isolates; Q 21 IS).

**Bukhārī #4787** (Kitāb Faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān):
- **Arabic** (snippet): حَدَّثَنَا آدَمُ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ عَبْدَ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنَ يَزِيدَ، سَمِعْتُ ابْنَ مَسْعُودٍ … (parallel chain.)
- **English**: "Surat Bani-Israel, Al-Kahf (The Cave), Maryam, Taha, Al-Anbiya' (The prophets) are amongst my first earnings and my old property, and (in fact) they are my old property." Narrated `Abdullah bin Mas`ud.
- **Source**: `bukhari.json` hadith_id 4787.
- **Status**: VERIFIED on disk.
- **Significance**: Parallel to #4533 with slight variation in wording — same Ibn Masʿūd chain, different *isnād* in Bukhārī. The repetition of the same content across two ḥadīth-numbers is itself a strong canonical signal.

### 2.3 No direct Q 21:30 cosmological hadith found

The cosmological iʿjāz reading of Q 21:30 is primarily a *tafsir* and *kalām* tradition, not a hadith tradition. No hadith citing Q 21:30 directly was found in the on-disk corpus by the search. This is consistent with classical practice — cosmological iʿjāz arguments are primarily presented in *Iʿjāz al-Qurʾān* literature (al-Bāqillānī, al-Khaṭṭābī, al-Suyūṭī *al-Itqān*) rather than in the hadith corpora.

## 3. VERIFIED hadith — Q 21 prophet narratives (indirect)

The 14 prophets named in Q 21:48–91 each have extensive hadith narratives elsewhere. Within the bukhari/tirmidhi/etc. corpora, the prophets in Q 21 are cited heavily:

### 3.1 Tirmidhī #29030 — Ibrāhīm and the three "non-lies"

- **English**: "Ibrahim, peace be upon him, did not lie about anything at all - except for three: 'Verily I am sick (37:89)' while he was not sick. And his saying about Sarah: '…'."
- **Source**: tirmidhi.json hadith_id 29030.
- **Status**: VERIFIED on disk.
- **Significance**: While the verse cited is Q 37:89 (not Q 21), the "three non-lies" hadith is part of the broader Ibrāhīm narrative cycle that Q 21:51–73 instantiates. The fire-trial (Q 21:68–70: *qulnā yā nāru kūnī bardan wa-salāman*) is the sister-passage. Tirmidhī #29030 confirms Q 21's Ibrāhīm-cycle as a recurring hadith-narrative target.

### 3.2 Aḥmad and Bukhārī chains for Yūnus / Dhū-l-Nūn

The supplication *lā ilāha illā anta subḥānaka innī kuntu mina al-ẓālimīn* (Q 21:87) is reported in multiple hadith chains as a recommended supplication for distress. The verse is not directly named in the on-disk JSON results in the search performed; this is a DATA-GAP (the supplication is well-attested in Tirmidhī's du`a chapters but the search did not surface it on the specific Q 21:87 reference key).

## 5. *Fadāʾil* of Q 21

The Bukhārī #4533/#4787 hadith is the single explicit *faḍīla* citation found on disk for Q 21. Q 21 is not in the elite-*fadāʾil* category of Q 1, Q 2, Q 36, Q 67, Q 112, Q 113-114 — it does not have its own dedicated *faḍāʾil* chapter in the standard collections.

This is itself an empirical signal: Q 21 is *liturgically-prominent enough to be Ibn-Masʿūd-memorized-first*, yet *not architecturally elevated to a stand-alone faḍīla*. This places Q 21 in the **mid-density faḍāʾil** band (cf. cross-finding-026 §13.5b mild-iʿjāz-al-maʿnā divergence — though Q 21 itself is structural-anti-iʿjāz, not iʿjāz-al-maʿnā).

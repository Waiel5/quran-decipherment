## Substantive observation

Q 41:30 and Q 46:13 carry the same opening phrase but diverge in continuation:
- **Q 41:30 continues**: *tatanazzalu ʿalayhim al-malāʾikatu allā takhāfū wa-lā taḥzanū wa-abshirū bi-l-jannati allatī kuntum tūʿadūn* (angels descend with reassurance and paradise-tidings).
- **Q 46:13 continues**: *fa-lā khawfun ʿalayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn* (no fear upon them, nor will they grieve).

The *fa-lā khawfun ʿalayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn* clause is itself a corpus-recurrent reassurance-formula (multiple loci including Q 2:38, 62, 112, 262, 274, 277; Q 3:170; Q 5:69; Q 7:35; Q 10:62; Q 46:13). The HM-twin shares the *qālū rabbu-nā Allāhu thumma istaqāmū* opener and bifurcates into two different reassurance-modes (angelic-descent vs absence-of-fear).

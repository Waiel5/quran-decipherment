## 1. al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān* (d. 310/923)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/qurtubi-jami-ahkam.openiti.raw.txt` (carries al-Ṭabarī verbatim) and the consolidated al-Rāzī file (which cites al-Ṭabarī).

Key positions documented in al-Ṭabarī ad Q 46:
- **Q 46:1 (ḥā mīm)**: al-Ṭabarī classifies the muqaṭṭaʿāt as among the *mutashābih*; refuses lexical-decoding interpretations. He preserves Ibn ʿAbbās's view (ḥā = name-of-God; mīm = name-of-God) and Ibn Masʿūd's view (ḥā mīm is a name of the Qurʾān itself).
- **Q 46:10 (the witness from Banū Isrāʾīl)**: al-Ṭabarī catalogs Companion-traditions naming **ʿAbdallāh b. Salām** (most attested, via Saʿd b. Abī Waqqāṣ, ʿAwf b. Mālik, Anas, Ibn ʿAbbās chains); also catalogs the dissenting view (al-Shaʿbī, Masrūq) that the verse was Meccan and ʿAbdallāh b. Salām's conversion was Medinan, hence the *shāhid* must be a Meccan Jew or a generic abstraction.
- **Q 46:15 (30-month bearing-and-weaning)**: al-Ṭabarī establishes the linkage Q 46:15 + Q 31:14 → 6-month minimum gestation; cites the famous *qaḍāʾ* of ʿUmar / ʿAlī (the 6-month-pregnancy adjudication that classical Sunnī jurisprudence anchors on this verse). al-Ṭabarī catalogs both Companion-supported derivations.
- **Q 46:17 (the rebellious son)**: al-Ṭabarī catalogs the candidate-identifications (ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Abī Bakr per Marwān b. al-Ḥakam, **rejected by ʿĀʾisha** — al-Ṭabarī preserves ʿĀʾisha's reservation; or generic per al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī).
- **Q 46:21 (al-aḥqāf)**: al-Ṭabarī gives the lexical gloss (curving sand-dunes) and catalogs the geographic candidates (between ʿUmān and Ḥaḍramawt; al-Shiḥr; Yemen).
- **Q 46:29-32 (jinn-listening)**: al-Ṭabarī establishes the Companion-traditions (Ibn ʿAbbās, Ibn Masʿūd) on the jinn-encounter at Nakhla on the Prophet's return from Ṭāʾif; cross-references Q 72.
- **Q 46:35 (ūlū al-ʿazm)**: al-Ṭabarī surveys the Companion-traditions on the identity of the Resolute Messengers — the dominant view names five (Nūḥ, Ibrāhīm, Mūsā, ʿĪsā, Muḥammad); minority traditions name six or expand.

## 2. al-Zamakhsharī, *al-Kashshāf* (d. 538/1144)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/` (OpenITI; verify presence at zamakhshari raw consolidated file).

al-Zamakhsharī's distinctive Muʿtazilī framing on Q 46:
- **Q 46:1 muqaṭṭaʿāt**: rejects gematric/numerological interpretations; reads ḥā mīm as a *tanbīh* (drawing-attention) device, parallel to other muqaṭṭaʿāt — the rationalist *naẓm*-aware reading.
- **Q 46:10 (witness)**: al-Zamakhsharī takes the witness as a generic *jins* (kind/category) reference — any one of Banū Isrāʾīl who recognises the Qurʾān's content from prior scripture; refines (not rejects) the ʿAbdallāh b. Salām attribution.
- **Q 46:15**: al-Zamakhsharī develops the rhetorical-grammatical reading of *iḥsāna* as accusative of unrestricted intention (good treatment in any form); the *thalāthūna shahran* arithmetic is preserved.
- **Q 46:29-32 (jinn)**: al-Zamakhsharī's rationalist Muʿtazilī defence of the literal jinn-presence against any allegorical reading; he insists the *nafar* are real, observed-by-the-Prophet entities — but rejects later popular embellishments.

## 3. al-Rāzī, *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* (d. 606/1210)

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/razi-mafatih-al-ghayb.openiti.raw.txt` (consolidated raw file; surah-46 section locatable by Arabic-name marker).

al-Rāzī's distinctive features:
- **Q 46:1 muqaṭṭaʿāt**: extensive philosophical disputation — al-Rāzī's standard 19-position survey of ḥā mīm interpretations, including the cipher-of-divine-names hypothesis; his *Allāh aʿlam* conclusion preserves all positions.
- **Q 46:13 (*thumma ʾstaqāmū*)**: detailed analysis of the *istiqāma* — al-Rāzī develops a multi-tier taxonomy of *istiqāma* (steadfastness in tawḥīd; in worship; in conduct) and identifies Q 46:13 as the parallel of Q 41:30 (al-Rāzī notes the Q 41 ↔ Q 46 echo *within* HM-7).
- **Q 46:15 (gestation-fiqh)**: al-Rāzī gives the **strongest classical jurisprudential development** of the 6-month minimum gestation doctrine — full kalām-fiqh integration, citing the ʿUmar-precedent and al-Shāfiʿī's adoption.
- **Q 46:29-32 (jinn-listening)**: al-Rāzī rationalist defence of the empirical reality of the jinn-encounter; surveys the metaphysical-philosophical debates on jinn ontology.
- **Q 46:35 (ūlū al-ʿazm)**: al-Rāzī's most-developed kalām treatment of the *ʿazm* concept — *ʿazm* = firm-resolution-after-decision; the five-prophet identification.

## 4. al-Qurṭubī, *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān* (d. 671/1273)

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/qurtubi-jami-ahkam.openiti.raw.txt`.

al-Qurṭubī's fiqhī orientation surfaces strongly in Q 46:
- **Q 46:15**: foundational *aḥkām al-walad* anchor — al-Qurṭubī derives the 6-month minimum gestation from Q 46:15 + Q 31:14 + Q 2:233; integrates with the ʿAlī/ʿUmar 6-month-pregnancy adjudication; full Mālikī fiqh derivation. Q 46:15 is one of the **most-fiqh-loaded verses in al-Qurṭubī's tafsir**.
- **Q 46:17 (rebellious son)**: al-Qurṭubī follows ʿĀʾisha's rejection of the ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Abī Bakr attribution; reads the verse as generic, rejecting the Marwān b. al-Ḥakam political-instrumental reading.
- **Q 46:21 (al-aḥqāf)**: al-Qurṭubī catalogs all geographical candidates with isnāds; preferred locus is between ʿUmān and Ḥaḍramawt.
- **Q 46:29-32**: al-Qurṭubī catalogs **two distinct jinn-encounter traditions**: (i) the Nakhla return-from-Ṭāʾif occasion (Ibn ʿAbbās, dominant) and (ii) the *layla al-jinn* tradition with the Prophet at Baṭn al-Nakhl (Ibn Masʿūd's distinct narrative). He notes the apparent multiplication and preserves both.

## 5. al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-durar fī tanāsub al-āyāt wa-l-suwar* (d. 885/1480)

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/biqai-nazm-al-durar.openiti.raw.txt`.

al-Biqāʿī's *munāsabāt* (inter-passage coherence) on Q 46:
- **Q 46 ↔ Q 45 transition**: al-Biqāʿī links Q 45's closing eschatological-judgment (Q 45:34-37 *kadhālika ʾllāhu yufsiq…*) to Q 46:1-3's revelation-creation creedal opening — the closing of Q 45 sets up the universal-creator theme that Q 46:3 *mā khalaqnā al-samawāti wa-l-arḍ illā bi-l-ḥaqq* articulates.
- **Q 46 internal munāsaba**: al-Biqāʿī organizes Q 46 as a **descending universalism** — from cosmic (vv.1-3) → scriptural (vv.7-12) → familial (vv.15-18) → historical (vv.21-26) → cosmic-recipient (vv.29-32, jinn) → exhortation (v.35). The *aḥqāf* geographic anchor + the jinn cosmic anchor frame the surah's "where the Qurʾān is heard" theme.
- **Q 46 ↔ Q 47 transition**: al-Biqāʿī notes the topical jump from Q 46's universal-recipient theme to Q 47 Muḥammad's specifically-Medinan jihād / kuffār-confrontation theme. He treats this as a CLEAR break (consistent with the empirical FR-distance Q 46↔Q 47 = 0.9905, the cluster-exit).

## 6. Ibn Kathīr, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm* (d. 774/1373)

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/ibn-kathir-tafsir-quran.openiti.raw.txt` (offsets within the consolidated file; surah-46 section locatable by Arabic-name marker سورة الأحقاف).

Ibn Kathīr's salient contributions:
- **Surah-name preface**: identifies *al-Aḥqāf* as the geographical name from v.21; establishes the Meccan classification consensus.
- **Q 46:10 (witness)**: anchors the ʿAbdallāh b. Salām attribution at multiple Bukhārī and Muslim isnāds (Saʿd b. Abī Waqqāṣ chain — Bukhārī k. *al-faḍāʾil al-ṣaḥāba* / Muslim k. *al-faḍāʾil*); provides the strongest hadith-corpus anchor for the named-witness reading.
- **Q 46:15**: Ibn Kathīr cites the **Abū Bakr / Umm Kulthūm (Abū Bakr's mother) tradition** — the asbāb-al-nuzūl about the Qurʾānic vindication of Abū Bakr's filial conduct; cross-references the *qaḍāʾ* of ʿUmar.
- **Q 46:17 (rebellious son)**: Ibn Kathīr gives the **strongest defence of ʿĀʾisha's rejection** of the Marwān-era political reading — ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Abī Bakr is *not* the verse's referent (per ʿĀʾisha at al-Bukhārī, k. *al-tafsīr*, sūrat al-Aḥqāf chapter; chain to be verified against `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/`).
- **Q 46:21 (al-aḥqāf)**: Ibn Kathīr preserves the geographic candidates; preferred locus is the *aḥqāf* of Yemen.
- **Q 46:29-32**: Ibn Kathīr provides the **canonical Sunni jinn-encounter narrative** — the Prophet's return from Ṭāʾif, the Nakhla recitation, the *nafar* of jinn returning to their people. Cross-references al-Bukhārī k. *al-adhān* / Ibn Masʿūd's *layla al-jinn* tradition (Muslim, Tirmidhī, Aḥmad).
- **Q 46:35**: Ibn Kathīr's preferred *ūlū al-ʿazm* identification is the five-prophet (Nūḥ, Ibrāhīm, Mūsā, ʿĪsā, Muḥammad), with Q 33:7 + Q 42:13 + Q 46:35 forming the three-verse covenantal foundation.

## 7. al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān* (d. 911/1505)

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/suyuti-al-itqan-fi-ulum-al-quran-english.pdf`.

al-Suyūṭī's relevant entries on Q 46:
- **nawʿ 17 (*asmāʾ al-suwar*)**: catalogs *al-Aḥqāf* as the surah-name; notes the geographic-locus naming (parallel to *Maryam*, *Yūnus*, etc., in being content-anchored rather than letter-anchored).
- **nawʿ 19 (*al-makkī wa-l-madanī*)**: classifies Q 46 as Meccan; notes some traditions exempt v.10 (the witness verse) as Medinan-revealed but al-Suyūṭī sides with the Meccan-throughout consensus (Ibn ʿAbbās's chronology rank).
- **nawʿ 67 (*fī al-anbiyāʾ wa-l-rusul*)**: Q 46:35 *ūlū al-ʿazm* is the canonical anchor; al-Suyūṭī enumerates the five-prophet identification.
- **nawʿ 70 (*mubhamāt al-Qurʾān*)**: Q 46:10 (witness from Banū Isrāʾīl), Q 46:17 (rebellious son), Q 46:29 (the *nafar* of jinn — number, names) are all catalogued among the *mubhamāt*.
- **nawʿ 4 (*ʿilm al-asbāb*)**: Q 46:15 + Q 46:17 are foundation-cases for the *qaḍāʾ ʿumar* tradition on 6-month minimum gestation.

## 10. Empirical correlates of classical positions

- **Q 46:13 ↔ Q 41:30 *istiqāma* echo**: al-Rāzī's classical observation of the parallel is empirically anchored at the **Q 46 ↔ Q 41 FR-roots distance of 0.7254** (Q 46's nearest neighbor corpus-wide; see [[01-empirical-profile|empirical profile §3a]]). The classical *naẓm* link is the empirically-densest content link.
- **Q 46:21 hapax eponym**: classical lexical observation that *al-aḥqāf* is geographically specific is empirically anchored at the **corpus-hapax status of the Hqf root** (1/1 attestation; verified `data/morphology/root-index.json`).
- **Q 46:29-32 ↔ Q 72 jinn-listening pair**: classical observation of the two-passage cluster is empirically anchored at the lexical *nafar* + *samiʿa* + *jinn* + *qaraʾa* shared signature (Jaccard 0.154 between Q 46:29-32 and Q 72; see [[02-content-analysis|content analysis §4]]).
- **Q 46:35 ūlū al-ʿazm**: classical five-prophet identification is empirically anchored at the **corpus-hapax status of the phrase *ūlū al-ʿazm*** (1/1 attestation in the Qurʾān; verified `quran-text/quran-no-tashkeel.json` regex).

**Mālik *Muwaṭṭaʾ* #1625 is the canonical Q 46:15 fiqh-anchor**. Verbatim opening (verified `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/.../malik.json`):

*وَحَدَّثَنِي مَالِكٌ، أَنَّهُ بَلَغَهُ أَنَّ عُثْمَانَ بْنَ عَفَّانَ، أُتِيَ بِامْرَأَةٍ قَدْ وَلَدَتْ فِي سِتَّةِ أَشْهُرٍ فَأَمَرَ بِهَا أَنْ تُرْجَمَ فَقَالَ عَلِيُّ بْنُ أَبِي طَالِبٍ لَيْسَ ذَلِكَ عَلَيْهَا إِنَّ اللَّهَ تَبَارَكَ وَتَعَالَى يَقُولُ فِي كِتَابِهِ {وَحَمْلُهُ وَفِصَالُهُ ثَلَا[ثُونَ شَهْرًا]…}*

This is the **single most-foundational fiqh hadith for Q 46:15**, establishing the 6-month-minimum-gestation doctrine via the explicit derivation Q 46:15 (30 months bearing+weaning) − Q 31:14 (24 months weaning) = 6 months minimum gestation. ʿAlī's preservation of the woman's life via Qurʾānic-jurisprudential derivation is one of the most-cited *qaḍāʾ ʿAlī* (judgments of ʿAlī) traditions.

The "Abū Bakr's mother" thread in the user-prompt asbāb al-nuzūl reference is anchored more to **Q 46:17** (the *uff lakumā* verse) than Q 46:15. **Umm Rūmān** (ʿĀʾisha's mother, Abū Bakr's wife — the *Umm al-Muʾminīn*'s mother) appears in the Bukhari/Muslim Q 46:17 cluster (see §4 below).

## 4. Q 46:17 — *uffin lakumā* (the rebellious son) — ʿĀʾisha's rejection

The user-prompt's asbāb al-nuzūl reference points to **Bukhārī #4621**, the canonical ʿĀʾisha-rejection ḥadīth. Verified verbatim opening (`data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/bukhari.json` idInBook 4621):

*حَدَّثَنَا مُوسَى بْنُ إِسْمَاعِيلَ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو عَوَانَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي بِشْرٍ، عَنْ يُوسُفَ بْنِ مَاهَكَ، قَالَ: كَانَ مَرْوَانُ عَلَى الْحِجَازِ اسْتَعْمَلَهُ مُعَاوِيَةُ، فَخَطَبَ فَجَعَلَ يَذْكُرُ يَزِيدَ بْنَ مُعَاوِيَةَ، لِكَىْ يُبَايِعَ لَهُ بَعْدَ أَبِيهِ، فَقَالَ لَهُ عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ أَبِي بَكْرٍ شَيْئًا، فَقَالَ خُذُوهُ. فَدَخَلَ بَيْتَ عَائِشَةَ فَلَمْ يَقْدِرُوا عَلَيْهِ، فَقَالَ مَرْوَانُ: إِنَّ هَذَا الَّذِي أَنْزَلَ اللَّهُ فِيهِ {وَالَّذِي قَالَ لِوَالِدَيْهِ أُفٍّ لَكُمَا أَتَعِدَانِنِي}. فَقَالَتْ عَائِشَةُ مِنْ وَرَاءِ الْحِجَابِ: مَا أَنْزَلَ اللَّهُ فِينَا [شَيْئًا مِنَ الْقُرْآنِ إِلَّا أَنَّ اللَّهَ أَنْزَلَ عُذْرِي]…*

**Translation summary**: Marwān b. al-Ḥakam (Umayyad governor of the Ḥijāz under Muʿāwiya), preaching for Yazīd's bayʿa, was rebuked by ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Abī Bakr (ʿĀʾisha's brother). Marwān ordered his arrest; ʿAbd al-Raḥmān fled to ʿĀʾisha's house. Marwān then publicly declared, "this is the one about whom God revealed *and the one who said to his parents 'uffin lakumā…'*" (Q 46:17). ʿĀʾisha, **from behind the curtain**, rejected the attribution: "God did not reveal anything about us [from the Qurʾān] except [God's revelation of my exoneration in al-Nūr]…"

This single ḥadīth establishes:
1. The **classical political abuse** of Q 46:17 by the Umayyad establishment to attack ʿAbd al-Raḥmān.
2. **ʿĀʾisha's authoritative rejection** — the Mother of the Believers, herself the subject of Q 24's *ifk*-vindication, denying the Q 46:17 attribution.
3. The verse's actual referent remains *mubham* (per al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān*, nawʿ 70) — the classical scholarly consensus follows ʿĀʾisha against the political reading.

**Umm Rūmān cluster** (ʿĀʾisha's mother — the Q 46:17 family-context anchor):
- al-Bukhārī #3249, 3731, 3971 + 3 more (6 hits) — Umm Rūmān narrations on the *ifk* and family matters.
- Muslim #3356, al-Tirmidhī #3264, Abū Dāwūd #4935, Ibn Mājah #1610, al-Dārimī #1541 — parallel attestations.

These confirm Umm Rūmān as the maternal figure in the ʿĀʾisha-Abū Bakr family-context that the classical asbāb tradition associates with Q 46:17 (REJECTED by ʿĀʾisha herself per Bukhārī #4621).

## 6. Q 46:35 — *ūlū al-ʿazm*

No discriminating-phrase search returned dense canonical hadith citations at the exact phrase *ūlū al-ʿazm*. The doctrine is **doctrinal-exegetical** (anchored by tafsir + *ʿulūm al-qurʾān* literature) rather than canonical-hadith-anchored. al-Suyūṭī *al-Itqān* nawʿ 67 is the classical anchor, plus al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, Ibn Kathīr ad Q 46:35.

The *ūlū al-ʿazm* identification with five prophets (Nūḥ, Ibrāhīm, Mūsā, ʿĪsā, Muḥammad) is corroborated by the **covenant-list verses** Q 33:7 (*wa-idh akhadhnā mina al-nabiyyīna mīthāqahum…*) and Q 42:13 (*sharaʿa lakum mina al-dīn…*); ḥadīth-corpus citation density on this specific identification is sparse in the 9-book canonical pull.

## Claim 1: Q 43:4 *umm al-kitāb* = al-lawḥ al-maḥfūẓ (al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, al-Qurṭubī)

**Source**: al-Ṭabarī ad Q 43:4 (citing Mujāhid, Qatāda); al-Rāzī ad loc.; al-Qurṭubī ad loc.

**Claim**: *umm al-kitāb ladaynā* refers to the celestial preserved tablet (*al-lawḥ al-maḥfūẓ*).

**Empirical test**: The phrase *umm al-kitāb* occurs in 3 places in the Qurʾān: Q 3:7, Q 13:39, Q 43:4. Q 3:7 contrasts *umm al-kitāb* (the *muḥkam*) with *mutashābih*; Q 13:39 references God's act of effacement and confirmation in the *umm al-kitāb*. Q 43:4 anchors the *umm al-kitāb* to the celestial-preservation. The Q 85:22 *lawḥ maḥfūẓ* and Q 56:78 *kitāb maknūn* are the parallel attestations of the celestial-tablet doctrine.

**Verdict**: **VINDICATED** at the textual-classical level. The doctrine is structurally anchored across Q 43:4 + Q 85:22 + Q 56:78, with classical exegetical convergence.

## Claim 2: Q 43:31 named candidates = al-Walīd b. al-Mughīra and ʿUrwa b. Masʿūd al-Thaqafī (Mujāhid via al-Ṭabarī)

**Source**: al-Ṭabarī ad Q 43:31 (citing Mujāhid); Ibn Kathīr ad loc.

**Claim**: The two unnamed *qaryatān* leaders were al-Walīd b. al-Mughīra and ʿUrwa b. Masʿūd.

**Empirical test**: The verse names two cities (Mecca and al-Ṭāʾif) but not specific persons. The Mujāhid identification is one classical tradition; Ibn Kathīr preserves alternates (e.g., ʿUtba b. Rabīʿa for Mecca). The 9-book hadith pull yields 12 hits anchoring al-Walīd b. al-Mughīra in sīra contexts.

**Verdict**: **DOCUMENTED** — al-Walīd is a well-attested classical candidate; ʿUrwa is Mujāhid's identification but has alternates. Q 43:31 is *mubham* — the text does not name; classical tradition supplies plausible candidates.

## Claim 3: Q 43:61 anchors ʿĪsā's second coming (Sunni-mainstream)

**Source**: Bukhārī Hadith of Descent series; Muslim parallel chains; al-Ṭabarī ad Q 43:61; Ibn Kathīr ad loc.

**Claim**: Q 43:61 *innahu la-ʿilmun li-l-sāʿati* refers to ʿĪsā as a sign of the eschatological Hour.

**Empirical test**: The 9-book pull yields 7 verified hadith chains (Muslim ×3, Tirmidhī ×1, Abū Dāwūd ×1, Ibn Mājah ×2) on the Hadith of Descent. The classical exegetical consensus (al-Ṭabarī, al-Qurṭubī, Ibn Kathīr) reads Q 43:61 as ʿĪsā-second-coming.

**Verdict**: **VINDICATED** — strong canonical hadith anchor + classical exegetical consensus.

**Limit**: An alternative reading exists (the Qurʾān itself as the *ʿilm*-of-the-Hour), preserved by al-Rāzī as a secondary; the dominant reading is ʿĪsā-second-coming.

## Claim 4: Q 43 is one of two HM-7 surahs sharing the *wa-l-Kitābi al-mubīn* qasam opening (Q 43:1-2 = Q 44:1-2)

**Source**: al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-durar* ad Q 44:1.

**Claim**: Q 43 and Q 44 open with verbatim-identical first-two-verse formula.

**Empirical test**: Verified directly:
- Q 43:1 = Q 44:1 = حم
- Q 43:2 = Q 44:2 = والكتاب المبين

**Verdict**: **VINDICATED at exact-string level** — Q 43:1-2 = Q 44:1-2.

# Q 43 al-Zukhruf — tafsīr survey (≥6 mufassirūn)

## 1. al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān*

- **Q 43:1-3**: muqaṭṭaʿāt + qasam *wa-l-Kitābi al-mubīn*; al-Ṭabarī treats the *qasam* as a divine self-affirmation of the Qurʾān's clarity.
- **Q 43:4 (*innahu fī umm al-kitāb ladaynā*)**: al-Ṭabarī cites Mujāhid and Qatāda — *umm al-kitāb* = the *al-lawḥ al-maḥfūẓ* (preserved tablet). This is a foundational verse for the Sunni doctrine of the celestial Qurʾān.
- **Q 43:31 (rich-man objection)**: al-Ṭabarī names al-Walīd b. al-Mughīra (Mecca) and ʿUrwa b. Masʿūd al-Thaqafī (al-Ṭāʾif) via Mujāhid and Qatāda.
- **Q 43:57-65 (ʿĪsā passage)**: extensive — Q 43:61 (*innahu la-ʿilmun li-l-sāʿati*) is read as a sign of the Hour, with cross-reference to the Hadith of Descent (al-Bukhārī, Muslim).
- **Q 43:81 (*qul in kāna li-l-Raḥmāni waladun*)**: al-Ṭabarī treats *in* as conditional negative ("if [hypothetically] He had a son…"); the verse becomes a *bi-burhān al-khulf* (proof by impossibility).

## 2. al-Zamakhsharī, *al-Kashshāf*

- **Q 43:11**: rationalist treatment of provision-as-test.
- **Q 43:33-35 *zukhruf***: extensive discussion of why God restrains worldly *zukhruf* — al-Zamakhsharī gives the wisdom-of-inequality argument.
- **Q 43:81 conditional**: rationalist analysis treating *in* as a negative-affirmation device.

## 3. al-Rāzī, *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb*

- **Q 43:4 *umm al-kitāb***: al-Rāzī gives extensive treatment — the *celestial-Qurʾān* doctrine, with cross-references to Q 85:22 (*lawḥ maḥfūẓ*) and Q 56:78 (*kitāb maknūn*).
- **Q 43:31**: al-Rāzī treats the rich-man objection structurally as Quraysh's economic-political resistance to a non-elite Prophet.
- **Q 43:57**: al-Rāzī details the ʿĪsā objection by Ibn al-Zibaʿrā — the rhetorical-logical confrontation pattern.
- **Q 43:61**: al-Rāzī surveys multiple readings — (a) ʿĪsā as second-coming sign; (b) the Qurʾān as a sign of the Hour. Sides with (a) as classically dominant.

## 4. al-Qurṭubī, *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān*

- **Q 43:4 *umm al-kitāb***: extensive fiqh-theological treatment.
- **Q 43:33-35**: treats the *zukhruf* passage as a worldly-asceticism (*zuhd*) prooftext.
- **Q 43:46-56 (Mūsā/Pharaoh)**: cross-references the Pharaoh-Hāmān parallel from Q 28.
- **Q 43:61**: sides with the ʿĪsā-second-coming reading; cites the Bukhārī/Muslim Hadith of Descent.

## 5. al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-durar*

- **Q 43 ↔ Q 42 transition**: al-Biqāʿī notes the rhetorical pivot — Q 42's defensive close-block on revelation (Q 42:51-53) prepares Q 43's offensive opening on the Qurʾān as *kitābun mubīn* (Q 43:2).
- **Q 43 internal munāsaba**: the surah's name (*al-Zukhruf*, from v. 35) is taken as a structural marker — the surah's central polemic is wealth-as-test, with the *zukhruf* passage as the climax.
- **Q 43 ↔ Q 44 transition**: the Q 43 closing apologetic prepares Q 44's eschatological *dukhān* — both surahs share the *wa-l-Kitābi al-mubīn* qasam opening.

## 6. Ibn Kathīr, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm*

- **Q 43:31**: catalogs al-Walīd b. al-Mughīra and ʿUrwa b. Masʿūd as the two named candidates (via Mujāhid).
- **Q 43:57**: details the Ibn al-Zibaʿrā confrontation; the asbāb al-nuzūl is preserved.
- **Q 43:61**: sides with the ʿĪsā-second-coming reading; cites the *Hadith of Descent* (Bukhārī #3448-class, Muslim #156-class).
- **Q 43:81**: treats the conditional as theological-impossibility proof.

## 7. al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān*

- **nawʿ 17**: al-Zukhruf as primary surah-name.
- **nawʿ 70 (mubhamāt)**: Q 43:31's "two cities" identification (al-Walīd / ʿUrwa) is cataloged as classical *taʿyīn*.

# Q 11 Hūd — Hadith Corpus Citations

This file collects all Q 11 references in the canonical hadith collections
on disk (`data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/`). Each
citation includes collection + ḥadīth-number + chain-grade. The cluster
of *shayyabatnī Hūd wa-akhawātuhā* narrations is given an extended audit
because it is the locus of [[Q011-F-04-shayyabatni-hud-cohort|Q011-F-04]]'s
empirical test.

## 1. The *shayyabatnī Hūd* cluster (the surah-naming-cluster hadith)

Source files: `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/other_books/shamail_muhammadiyah.json`
(al-Tirmidhī, *al-Shamāʾil al-Muḥammadiyya*, chapter 5 *bāb mā jāʾa fī shayb
rasūli Allāh*).

### 1.1 Shamāʾil al-Tirmidhī #40 (the 5-surah list)

**Chain (Shamāʾil ed.)**: حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو كُرَيْبٍ مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ الْعَلَاءِ → حَدَّثَنَا مُعَاوِيَةُ بْنُ هِشَامٍ → عَنْ شَيْبَانَ → عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ → عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ → عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ.

**Text**: *قَالَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ‏: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، قَدْ شِبْتَ. قَالَ‏: شَيَّبَتْنِي هُودٌ، وَالْوَاقِعَةُ، وَالْمُرْسَلَاتُ، وَعَمَّ يَتَسَاءَلُونَ، وَإِذَا الشَّمْسُ كُوِّرَتْ.*

**Translation**: "Abū Bakr said: 'O Messenger of Allah, you have grayed!'
He said: 'I have been made gray-haired by **Hūd** (Q 11), **al-Wāqiʿa**
(Q 56), **al-Mursalāt** (Q 77), **ʿamma yatasāʾalūn** (Q 78 al-Nabaʾ),
and **idhā al-shamsu kuwwirat** (Q 81 al-Takwīr)'."

**Grading**: ḥasan-gharīb (al-Tirmidhī's own grade in his other works);
the chain transmits from ʿIkrima (the freed-slave of Ibn ʿAbbās, classical
disputation about his mukhtalaṭ-status — but a known Quranic exegete).
The chain-grade *ḥasan-gharīb* means: a sound-good chain, but rare in
this exact wording. Modern scholars (al-Albānī's *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Tirmidhī
al-Shamāʾil*) classify the variant as ṣaḥīḥ-li-ghayrihī (i.e., raised to
ṣaḥīḥ by the second corroborating chain).

### 1.2 Shamāʾil al-Tirmidhī #41 (the abbreviated form)

**Chain**: حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ بْنُ وَكِيعٍ → حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ بِشْرٍ → عَنْ عَلِيِّ بْنِ صَالِحٍ → عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ → عَنْ أَبِي جُحَيْفَةَ.

**Text**: *قَالُوا‏: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، نَرَاكَ قَدْ شِبْتَ. قَالَ‏: قَدْ شَيَّبَتْنِي هُودٌ وَأَخَوَاتُهَا.*

**Translation**: "[The Companions] said: 'O Messenger of Allah, we see
that you have turned gray.' He said: 'Hūd and its sisters (*akhawātuhā*)
have caused me to turn gray'."

**Grading**: ḥasan; the *akhawātuhā* designation (without enumeration)
gave classical commentators wide license to debate which surahs are the
"sisters." The Shamāʾil #40 enumeration is the single most-cited
classical resolution: {Q 11, 56, 77, 78, 81}.

### 1.3 Variant traditions (extended cluster)

Some classical commentators (Ibn Kathīr, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm* on Q 11,
per `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/ibn-kathir-tafsir-quran.openiti.raw.txt`)
include **Q 99 al-Zalzala** in the "sisters" cluster, citing al-Tabarānī's
*Muʿjam al-awsaṭ* with a different chain. Other classical sources include
Q 80 ʿAbasa, Q 79 al-Nāziʿāt. These are NOT in the Shamāʾil #40 enumeration
and are treated as **variant-cluster** identifications.

The empirical test [[Q011-F-04-shayyabatni-hud-cohort|Q011-F-04]] uses the
**Shamāʾil #40 5-surah list** as the locked cohort. A follow-up
[Q011-F-04.1] testing the {+Q 99} 6-surah list is queued for future work.

## 2. *Faḍāʾil* of Q 11 — recitation virtues

### 2.1 Nasāʾī #955 / #5448 (al-Nasāʾī, *al-Sunan al-Kubrā*, kitāb al-faḍāʾil)

**Chain**: قُتَيْبَةُ → اللَّيْثُ → عَنْ يَزِيدَ بْنِ أَبِي حَبِيبٍ → عَنْ أَبِي عِمْرَانَ → أَسْلَمَ عَنْ عُقْبَةَ بْنِ عَامِرٍ.

**Text (composite of #955 and #5448)**: *اتَّبَعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَهُوَ رَاكِبٌ، فَوَضَعْتُ يَدِي عَلَى قَدَمِهِ، فَقُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ أَقْرِئْنِي سُورَةَ هُودٍ وَسُورَةَ يُوسُفَ. قَالَ: لَنْ تَقْرَأَ شَيْئًا أَبْلَغَ عِنْدَ اللَّهِ مِنْ {قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ}.*

**Translation**: "I followed the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) when he was riding,
and I placed my hand on his foot and said: 'O Messenger of Allah, teach me
**Sūrat Hūd** and Sūrat Yūsuf.' He said: 'You will never recite anything
more eloquent before Allah than {Qul huwa Allāhu aḥad} (Q 112)'."

**Grading**: classical scholars (al-Mundhirī, al-Bayhaqī) grade this as
ḥasan; the chain is sound, with ʿUqba b. ʿĀmir → Aslam (the freedslave of
ʿUqba) → Abū ʿImrān → Yazīd b. Abī Ḥabīb → al-Layth (b. Saʿd, a
mid-2nd-century Egyptian imām). The hadith pairs Sūrat Hūd with Sūrat Yūsuf
in a single recitation-instruction request — confirming that the **Q 11 + Q 12 pair**
was prominent for early companions as a unit.

### 2.2 Dārimī #2659 / #2660 (recite Sūrat Hūd on Fridays)

**Chain (#2659)**: يَزِيدُ بْنُ هَارُونَ → هَمَّامٌ → عَنْ أَبِي عِمْرَانَ الْجَوْنِيِّ → عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ رَبَاحٍ → عَنْ كَعْبٍ.

**Text (#2660)**: *قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: اقْرَءُوا سُورَةَ هُودٍ يَوْمَ الْجُمُعَةِ.*

**Translation**: "Recite Sūrat Hūd on Fridays."

**Grading**: the chain ends at Kaʿb [b. al-Aḥbār — a Tabiʿī Jewish convert,
not a Companion]. The marfūʿ form (raised to the Prophet) is debated; Dārimī
records both the marfūʿ and mawqūf forms. al-Albānī's *Silsila al-aḥādīth
al-ḍaʿīfa* classifies the marfūʿ form as ḍaʿīf; the mawqūf-to-Kaʿb form is
sound. This is the **classical anchor for the Friday-recitation tradition**
of Q 11 — but its textual provenance is weak. We flag it as
classically-disputed and report the Dārimī attestation honestly.

## 3. *Asbāb al-nuzūl* for Q 11:114 (the *good-deeds-erase-bad-deeds* verse)

This is one of the most-narrated *asbāb al-nuzūl* in the entire 9-book
corpus. The story is: a Companion ("a man") came to the Prophet confessing
that he had committed an unlawful kiss/embrace with a woman; the verse
Q 11:114 was revealed as a generalizing answer.

### 3.1 Bukhārī

- **Bukhārī #516** (kitāb mawāqīt al-ṣalāt, bāb al-ṣalāt kaffāra): "A man kissed a woman..."
- **Bukhārī #4481** (kitāb al-tafsīr, bāb sūrat hūd): same narration, fuller asbāb chain. [Verified on disk.]
- **Bukhārī #4687** (kitāb al-tafsīr, bāb sūrat hūd): close parallel.

### 3.2 Tirmidhī

- **Tirmidhī #3196**: ʿAbdullāh b. Masʿūd narration, with full chain Qutayba → Abū al-Aḥwaṣ → Simāk b. Ḥarb → Ibrāhīm → ʿAlqama wa-l-Aswad → ʿAbdullāh.
- **Tirmidhī #3197**: Muʿādh b. Jabal narration variant.
- **Tirmidhī #3198**: same Ibn Masʿūd narration via Yaḥyā b. Saʿīd.
- **Tirmidhī #3199**: Abū al-Yasar narration variant.

### 3.3 Other 9-book attestations

- **Muslim** has parallel narrations in *kitāb al-tawba*.
- **Ibn Mājah #1132**: full asbāb narration, parallel chain.
- **Abū Dāwūd #4470**: same narration via Musaddad → Abū al-Aḥwaṣ.
- **Aḥmad b. Ḥanbal Musnad #482**: tangentially-related narration on ablution
  before sin, citing v. 114.

**Grading**: ṣaḥīḥ (Bukhārī + Muslim agreement); the asbāb tradition is
**among the most strongly attested** in the entire 9-book corpus,
with parallel chains across Ibn Masʿūd, Muʿādh b. Jabal, and Abū al-Yasar.

## 4. *Asbāb al-nuzūl* for Q 11:46 (Nūḥ's son — *innahu ʿamalun ghayru ṣāliḥ*)

### 4.1 Tirmidhī #3015 (kitāb al-tafsīr, bāb sūrat hūd)

**Chain**: يَحْيَى بْنُ مُوسَى → وَكِيعٌ وَحَبَّانُ بْنُ هِلَالٍ → هَارُونُ النَّحْوِيُّ → ثَابِتٌ الْبُنَانِيُّ → شَهْرُ بْنُ حَوْشَبٍ → عَنْ أُمِّ سَلَمَةَ.

**Text**: "The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) recited this Ayah: *Innahu ʿamalun
ghayru ṣāliḥ* (11:46)."

**Significance**: this hadith specifies the Prophet's *qirāʾa* (reading) of
Q 11:46. The variant reading turns on whether *innahu* refers to **the son
himself** (so the meaning is "he is one whose deed is unrighteous") or to
**the act of intercession** (so the meaning is "the intercession is an
unrighteous act"). The hadith narration via Umm Salama supports the
**latter** reading (the intercession is the unrighteous act, not the son
being inherently unrighteous).

**Grading**: Tirmidhī's grade is *ḥasan*; some scholars (al-Bayhaqī, al-Suyūṭī)
treat this as a variant-reading-attestation rather than asbāb al-nuzūl.

## 5. Other Q 11-related hadith

### 5.1 Hadith on Q 11:42-44 (the deluge-iʿjāz)

There are **NO 9-book ḥadīth specifically commenting on Q 11:42-44 as a
literary marvel**. The deluge-iʿjāz is **a classical-tafsir-and-balāgha
construct** (al-Bāqillānī *Iʿjāz al-Qurʾān* + al-Zamakhsharī *Kashshāf*),
not a hadith-narrated Prophetic-statement claim. Honestly published
**DATA-GAP-CLASSICAL**: classical tradition holds the verse as iʿjāz, but
no Prophetic narration corroborates the claim.

### 5.2 Hadith on the specific phrase *ʿalā al-ʿarsh* of Q 11:7

Q 11:7 contains the phrase *وكان عرشه على الماء* ("and His Throne was upon
the water"). **Bukhārī #3192, #7418** cite this verse as part of the
extended *al-ʿarsh* discussion (kitāb al-tawḥīd). The verse is cited as
proof-text for the throne-on-water doctrine.

### 5.3 Hadith on the moral lesson of Q 11

ʿĀʾisha's narration about the Prophet being so affected by the surahs that
made him gray is cross-attested at:
- **Aḥmad b. Ḥanbal Musnad** (multiple chains) — see Musnad #2095, #3469.
- **al-Bayhaqī's *Shuʿab al-īmān*** corroborates.

## 7. Honest limits

- The Shamāʾil al-Tirmidhī (the source for *shayyabatnī Hūd*) is NOT in the
  9-book canonical list — it is a separate work also by al-Tirmidhī; we use it
  because it is the corpus-source for the canonical 5-surah enumeration.
  An audit citing the *Jāmiʿ al-Tirmidhī #3297* (which the prompt mentions)
  was attempted but **the exact #3297 wording corresponding to *shayyabatnī*
  is in the Shamāʾil, not the Jāmiʿ**. We honor the data on disk: the
  primary attestation is **Shamāʾil #40-41**, not Jāmiʿ #3297. This is a
  numbering-edition convention difference; the substance of the hadith is
  identical to what the prompt indicates.

- *al-Albānī* hadith gradings (modern, 20th-century) are widely respected but
  not classical. We cite classical grades (al-Tirmidhī's own *ḥasan-gharīb*)
  alongside modern grades where relevant.

- The Dārimī Q 11 Friday-recitation hadith is classically *mawqūf-to-Kaʿb*
  rather than marfūʿ-to-the-Prophet. We flag it as such.

- No 9-book ḥadīth corroborates the deluge-iʿjāz claim of Q 11:42-44 directly;
  the classical iʿjāz-of-Q-11:42-44 is balāgha-grounded, not ḥadīth-grounded.

- Q 11 has lighter direct-attestation in the 9 books than Q 1, 2, 9, 12, 18,
  36 — but the cluster anchor (*shayyabatnī Hūd*) is corpus-singular: no other
  surah is named in a Prophetic statement as making him gray. This unique
  status is why Q 11 is given an extended 5-surah-cluster identity in the
  classical tradition.

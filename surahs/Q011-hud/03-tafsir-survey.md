## 1. The opener (Q 11:1) — *iḥkām + tafṣīl*

Q 11:1: *الر كتاب أحكمت آياته ثم فصلت من لدن حكيم خبير*

**al-Ṭabarī** (`spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-al-tabari/11/1.json`):
treats *uḥkimat* as "rendered firm and unchanging" (*muḥakkamat lā yataṭarraqu
ilayhā fasād*) and *fuṣṣilat* as "made distinct, with explanations laid out"
(*fuṣṣilat bi-bayānin wa-tafṣīlin yufaṣṣil ʿan baʿḍ*). The two together
mean that the Quran is BOTH a sealed/coherent unity AND an articulated
multiplicity. al-Ṭabarī reads *Wise All-Aware* as referring to the
divine source.

**al-Zamakhsharī** (`Kashshāf` on Q 11:1, surah-marker search): treats the
verbal pair as a *takhṣīṣ-ʿumūm* relation — *uḥkimat* is the universal
characteristic of every verse (firmness), *fuṣṣilat* is the per-verse
distinction (each verse has its own meaning + occasion). al-Zamakhsharī's
reading anticipates a meta-statement on Quranic structure: the Quran is
both ONE sealed text AND a multiplicity of distinct verses.

**al-Rāzī** (per `razi-biqai-munasabat-rings.md` and OpenITI raw): the
verbal pair is read as *iḥkām* (the divine intent at composition) followed
by *tafṣīl* (the prophetic delivery as articulated speech). al-Rāzī
treats this as the ḥikma + khabīr pair that closes v.1 — divine
craftsmanship + divine knowledge.

**Convergence**: all four (Ṭabarī, Zamakhsharī, Rāzī, Ibn Kathīr) read
*uḥkimat-fuṣṣilat* as a divine-craftsmanship statement. The convergence
is striking and the reading has empirical correlates: Q 11 IS internally
multi-block (anthology) yet ring-structured (opener-mirroring-closer
at v.123). This is the *iḥkām + tafṣīl* duality at the level of
surah-architecture.

**al-Biqāʿī** (`biqai-nazm-al-durar.openiti.raw.txt` surah-marker سورة هود):
takes this further — treats the *uḥkām + tafṣīl* opening as the surah's own
*manhaj-statement* (methodological declaration) — Q 11 will demonstrate the
Book's divine origin BY iteration of warner-narratives, each *firm* in its
moral lesson and *distinct* in its historical detail. al-Biqāʿī's reading
maps onto the empirical observation that Q 11 contains a frozen formulaic
template (the wa-ilā-akhāhum lattice, [[h-new-270-hud-template-lattice|H-NEW-270]])
that is repeated across DISTINCT prophet-blocks — the *iḥkām* (constancy of
form) + *tafṣīl* (distinction of content) operative at the structural level.

## 2. Why Q 11 is named after Hūd

**al-Biqāʿī** (per `biqai-nazm-al-durar.openiti.raw.txt`): the surah is
named after Hūd because his pericope is the **structural center** of the
123-verse surah (Hūd-block at vv. 50-60, midpoint of the surah). al-Biqāʿī
treats Hūd's confrontation with ʿĀd as the *paradigmatic* warner-confrontation
that the surrounding pericopes variantly replay. In al-Biqāʿī's
*munāsaba* analysis (the *Naẓm al-Durar* method), Q 11 is named for the
*central type-specimen* of its anthology.

**al-Rāzī** (`razi-biqai-munasabat-rings.md`): supplements al-Biqāʿī with
the observation that Hūd is the FIRST of the prophets after Nūḥ in the
post-deluge generations (per the classical chronology of Ibn Kathīr's
*al-Bidāya wa-l-nihāya*). The surah is named after Hūd because his is the
**chronologically earliest** of the post-deluge cycle — Nūḥ's narrative
in the surah (vv. 25-49) is the *transition-from-pre-deluge-history*; the
post-deluge cycle proper begins with Hūd at v. 50.

**Ibn Kathīr** (`spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-ibn-kathir/11/1.json` and Q 11:50
file): adds that Hūd's name has a special emotional weight in the corpus
because he confronted *the largest people-of-strength* (ʿĀd, given gigantic
stature in classical lore — *azzādakum fī al-khalqi basṭa*, Q 7:69) and
was destroyed by *the most violent wind* (*rīḥ ṣarṣar ʿātiya*). The
narrative carries the *pathos* that the classical "Hūd and its sisters"
hadith (al-Tirmidhī Shamāʾil #40, Shamāʾil #41) seems to invoke:
this is the surah of warners-meeting-the-largest-tyrant, and it is the
most heart-shaking of warning-sūras.

**Convergence**: Ibn Kathīr + al-Biqāʿī + al-Rāzī all converge: Q 11's
naming-after-Hūd is **structural**, not narrative-density-based. Empirically
this is corroborated: the Hūd-block is NOT the longest in Q 11 (Nūḥ is
longer) but it IS at the structural center (vv. 50-60 of 123) and it is
the *earliest* post-deluge cycle entry. The naming convention ("eponym
of the structural-central type-specimen") is the scholarly verdict.

## 3. The Hūd narrative (Q 11:50-60)

**al-Ṭabarī** (`spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-al-tabari/11/50.json` —
verbatim Arabic quoted in §0 source-file scan): reads v. 50 as
introductory-narrative — "And to ʿĀd We sent their brother Hūd, who
said: O my people, worship God; you have no [other deity] besides Him —
you are nothing but inventors [of false gods]." al-Ṭabarī notes the
parallel to Q 7:65 (which uses *afa-lā tattaqūn* instead of *in antum
illā muftarūn*); the Q 11 ending is a sharper rebuke than the Q 7
question-form.

**al-Qurṭubī** (`spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/11/50.json` per
section): treats the *akhāhum* phrasing as *brotherhood by ethnicity-of-people*,
not by genealogical descent. Hūd was OF ʿĀd (*akhū qabīlatihim*) but
selected as warner. al-Qurṭubī adds: this is the model for prophet-tribal
relationship (the prophet is one of his own people, not foreign). The
Q 11 lattice (Hūd brother of ʿĀd, Ṣāliḥ brother of Thamūd, Shuʿayb brother
of Madyan) makes this a **template** assertion: prophets are ALWAYS
internal to their peoples.

**al-Zamakhsharī** (Kashshāf on Q 11:50): reads *muftarūn* as "fabricators
of false gods" — i.e., ʿĀd is condemned not for unbelief generically but
specifically for the *fabrication of deities* (idolatry as creative invention).

**al-Rāzī** (Mafātīḥ al-ghayb, surah-marker search): the dialogue between
Hūd and his people (vv. 53-57) is read as a *paradigmatic-debate* — Hūd's
people demand a "clear sign" (*bayyina*); Hūd's response (*innī tawakkaltu
ʿalā Allāh rabbī wa-rabbikum*, v. 56) is a *tawḥīd-affirmation* that
classical scholars treat as the strongest single statement of monotheism
in the prophet-cycle pericopes. al-Rāzī notes the *inkār* + *iʿjāz* dynamic:
Hūd's people deny him (*qālū yā Hūdu mā jiʾtanā bi-bayyinatin*, v. 53);
the destruction by *rīḥ ṣarṣar* is the answer (v. 58, 60).

## 4. Q 11:42-44 — the deluge-iʿjāz pericope

Q 11:42-44 contains the canonical "deluge-iʿjāz" verses where Nūḥ's son
refuses to embark, the flood rises, and v. 44 issues the cosmic
imperative *yā arḍu blaʿī māʾaki, wa-yā samāʾu aqliʿī*.

**al-Bāqillānī** (Iʿjāz al-Qurʾān, Aḥmad Ṣaqr Cairo ed., pp. 252-258): treats
v. 44 as **the** paradigm of structural-iʿjāz at the verse level. The dual
imperative (*yā arḍu...yā samāʾu...*) addresses earth and sky in parallel
clauses with reverse-order vocabulary; *blaʿī* (swallow) addresses the
earth-water; *aqliʿī* (cease/clear) addresses the sky-rain. al-Bāqillānī's
analysis is referenced in `data/literature/classical-tafsir/classical-on-shams-palindrome.md`
and the broader `data/literature/balagha/` resources. al-Bāqillānī treats
this verse's compactness + cosmic-scope + parallel-form as a marker of
divine speech.

**al-Zamakhsharī** (Kashshāf on Q 11:44): expands al-Bāqillānī's analysis
with the observation that *blaʿī māʾaki* uses the *iḍāfa* (genitive
construction) "your water" — assigning the floodwater to the earth as
*its own water* — rather than a generic "the water". This is a stylistic
achievement: the floodwater becomes the earth's own self-reabsorbed
substance. al-Zamakhsharī's reading is canonically *bayānī*
(rhetoric-focused) and is taught in the *balāgha* curriculum to this day.

**al-Rāzī**: notes that v. 42-44 contain ELEVEN distinct narrative actions
(Nūḥ calls his son, son refuses, mountain-refuge claim, Nūḥ's response
about no-protection, son drowns, flood rises, water rises around the ark,
v.44 imperative pair, water recedes, ark-settles, *qīla buʿdan* destruction
declaration) compressed in TWO verses (after v.41 setup). The compression
is itself iʿjāz.

**al-Biqāʿī** (`biqai-nazm-al-durar.openiti.raw.txt`): adds the *naẓm*
(structural) reading: vv. 42-44 are the **mid-point** of the Nūḥ-block;
they are the moment of *narrative pivot* (from Nūḥ's effort to save his
son to the cosmic imperative that ends the flood). al-Biqāʿī's structural
analysis confirms that v. 44 is the literary climax of Q 11's longest
sub-narrative.

This claim is audited empirically in `05-classical-claims-audit.md`.

## 5. Q 11:114 — the *good-deeds-erase-bad-deeds* verse

Q 11:114: *وأقم الصلاة طرفي النهار وزلفا من الليل ۚ إن الحسنات يذهبن السيئات*
"And establish prayer at the two ends of the day and at the approach of
the night. Verily good deeds drive away bad deeds."

**al-Ṭabarī** (Q 11:114 file): reads *ṭarafayi al-nahāri* as the dawn-prayer
(*fajr*) and the sunset-prayer (*maghrib*); *zulafan min al-layl* as the
night-prayers (*ʿishāʾ* and post-ʿishāʾ). The classical 5-prayer schema
is read OUT OF this verse by Ṭabarī.

**al-Qurṭubī**: enumerates the multiple readings:
- *ṭarafayi al-nahāri* = ṣubḥ + ʿaṣr (the two extremes); *zulafan* = maghrib + ʿishāʾ.
- ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib's reported reading: ṣubḥ + maghrib + ʿishāʾ + tahajjud.
- Ibn ʿAbbās's reading: this is the timing-instruction for the 5 prayers in compressed form.

al-Qurṭubī notes the verse is the **basis of the 5-prayer schema** in the
classical jurisprudential tradition (alongside *aqim al-ṣalāta li-dulūki
al-shamsi*, Q 17:78). The verse is a hadith-cited *fadāʾil al-Qurʾān*
target — see 04-hadith-corpus.md.

**Ibn Kathīr**: identifies the *innā al-ḥasanāt yudhhibna al-sayyiʾāt* clause
as the basis of the *kaffārāt al-ṣaghāʾir* (atonement of minor sins via
prayer-frequency) doctrine. The hadith (*al-ṣalawāt al-khams kaffāra
li-mā baynahunna*) corroborates this reading.

## 6. The *fa-staqim kamā umirta* verse (Q 11:112)

Q 11:112: *فاستقم كما أمرت ومن تاب معك ولا تطغوا ۚ إنه بما تعملون بصير*
"So stand firm as you have been commanded, and those who repent with you,
and transgress not — verily He is Seeing of what you do."

**Ibn Kathīr** + **al-Ṭabarī** + **al-Qurṭubī**: all link this verse to the
classical "Hūd and its sisters" tradition (al-Tirmidhī Shamāʾil #40 +
parallel-narration #41) — the Prophet (PBUH) is said to have aged from
the burden of *Hūd and its sisters*; the verse *fa-staqim kamā umirta* is
sometimes singled out as the verse-within-Q 11 that specifically aged him
(Ibn Kathīr's tafsīr cites this from al-Suyūṭī's *al-Durr al-manthūr*).

The reading: *fa-staqim* commands ABSOLUTE rectitude, with no permitted
deviation, and the Prophet feels this as a heavy charge.

## 7. Q 11 chronology

**al-Suyūṭī** (*al-Itqān*, English translation by Mahmood Faraz Husain
in `data/literature/classical-tafsir/suyuti-al-itqan-fi-ulum-al-quran-english.pdf`,
nawʿ on chronology) places Q 11 at **revelation-order #52** (per
`data/revelation-order.csv` row 52, citing al-Suyūṭī). This is **late
Meccan** in al-Suyūṭī's chronology — after Q 10 Yūnus (rev #51) and
before Q 12 Yūsuf (rev #53).

**al-Biqāʿī** (Naẓm al-Durar) corroborates the late-Meccan placement and
draws the *munāsaba* (thematic-connection) between Q 10 → Q 11 → Q 12 as
an ALR-cluster narrative arc: Q 10 (thesis on the Book), Q 11 (warner-anthology
substantiating the thesis), Q 12 (single-prophet narrative as
counter-balance to the multi-prophet anthology).

## 9. Honest limits

- The al-Biqāʿī *Naẓm al-Durar* analysis is treated authoritatively here
  on classical-design questions, but al-Biqāʿī's overall *munāsaba* method
  was empirically falsified in muqaṭṭaʿāt content-clustering tests
  (4 NULL replications; cross-finding-008). His structural-design claims
  for individual surahs (the *naẓm* of a single surah) hold better than
  his cross-surah *munāsaba* claims.
- Q 11:42-44 deluge-iʿjāz is a *taste claim* in al-Bāqillānī. It cannot
  be empirically tested as "is this verse stylistically superior" without
  a specific operationalization. We treat it as VINDICATED-AS-CONSENSUS
  (10+ centuries of unanimous classical reading) but flag it as
  NOT-DIRECTLY-EMPIRICAL in 05-classical-claims-audit.md.
- The "Hūd and its sisters" cluster identification (al-Tirmidhī Shamāʾil)
  is investigated empirically in [[Q011-F-04-shayyabatni-hud-cohort|Q011-F-04]].
  The empirical result is NULL on the locked 4-axis cohesion test;
  this is a classical-vs-empirical divergence point. See 05-classical-claims-audit.md.

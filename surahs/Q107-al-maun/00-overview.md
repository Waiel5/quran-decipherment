## 2. Classical names

- **al-Māʿūn** (الماعون) — "Small Kindness/Charity"; from v.7.
- **al-Dīn** (الدين) — alternative classical name from v.1 *yukadhdhibu bi-l-dīn* ("denies the Judgment").
- **A-raʾayta** — sometimes referenced by opening verb (parallel to Sūrat al-Fātiḥa being identified by its opening *al-ḥamdu*).

## 3. Opening formula

Q 107 opens with **a-raʾayta lladhī** ("Have you seen the one who...") — a rhetorical-question form that recurs at Q 96:9 (*a-raʾayta lladhī yanhā*), Q 75:36 (*a-yaḥsabu l-insānu an yutraka sudā*), and as **a-raʾayta** family across Q 25, Q 96, Q 19. Q 107 is one of the very few surahs OPENING with this rhetorical-question form (only Q 107 + Q 105 + Q 109 in the immediate mufaṣṣal-tail use direct rhetorical-question openings as signature openings).

## 7. Quick content structure

- v. 1: Rhetorical-question opener — *a-raʾayta lladhī yukadhdhibu bi-l-dīn* ("Have you seen the one who denies the Judgment?")
- v. 2: First moral-symptom — *fa-dhālika lladhī yaduʿʿu l-yatīm* ("That is the one who repulses the orphan")
- v. 3: Second moral-symptom — *wa-lā yaḥuḍḍu ʿalā ṭaʿāmi l-miskīn* ("And does not urge feeding the destitute")
- v. 4: ***fa-waylun li-l-muṣallīn*** — "So woe to the worshippers" — the **rhetorical pivot** (the *waylun* is one of 14 corpus *waylun* curses)
- v. 5: Specification — *alladhīna hum ʿan ṣalātihim sāhūn* ("Who are heedless of their prayer")
- v. 6: Specification — *alladhīna hum yurāʾūn* ("Who make a show [of religion]")
- v. 7: Specification — *wa-yamnaʿūna l-māʿūn* ("And withhold *al-māʿūn* / small kindnesses").

A 3+1+3 ring: **vv.1-3 the denier-of-Judgment-and-his-symptoms** → **v.4 the *waylun* pivot** → **vv.5-7 the religious-show-and-charity-failure**.

## 8. Why this surah is structurally distinctive

Three signature facts:

1. **mEn (māʿūn) is a corpus hapax — Q 107 is named for a 1/1 root**: per `data/morphology/root-index.json`, the root **mEn** attests **only at Q 107:7** (1 surah, 1 occurrence). This makes Q 107 the third surah in the immediate quad (Q 105 al-Fīl, Q 106 Quraysh, Q 107 al-Māʿūn) to be **named for a corpus-hapax root**. Q 104 al-Humaza is the lone exception in this quad (HTm appears 6× across 5 surahs). The pattern across Q 105-107 of corpus-hapax-named surahs is empirically remarkable: Q 105 fyl (1/1), Q 106 $tw + Syf (each 1/1), Q 107 mEn (1/1). The classical exegetes (al-Ṭabarī, al-Zamakhsharī, al-Rāzī) all spend extensive lexical real estate on these hapaxes precisely because the wider Arabic context provides limited disambiguation; the surahs themselves are the **lexical anchor** for these roots in the Quranic register.

2. **3+1+3 ring with the *waylun* pivot at the syntactic-semantic exact midpoint**: 7 verses split symmetrically as vv.1-3 (3 verses describing the *yukadhdhib bi-l-dīn* portrait) → v.4 (the corpus *waylun* curse, structurally pivot) → vv.5-7 (3 verses describing the *muṣallīn* failure-modes). The pivot's *waylun* connects Q 107 to the H-NEW-1190 **wa-mā adrāka** family at the *waylun*-formula axis (cf. Q 104:1 *waylun li-kulli humaza*; Q 77's 10× *waylun yawmaʾidhin* refrain). Q 107 is not in H-NEW-1190 but uses one of the same rhetorical signatures.

3. **Classical-empirical alignment on religious-hypocrisy critique**: Q 107 and Q 104 jointly anchor the **Early Meccan moral-condemnation** sub-corpus — Q 104 condemns the slanderer/wealth-hoarder, Q 107 condemns the orphan-repulser/charity-withholder/show-worshipper. Both surahs deploy the *waylun*-curse formula. The pair forms a coherent moral-rebuke micro-cluster within H-NEW-1200's broader short-Meccan-eschatology cluster.

# Q 92 al-Layl — Tafsīr Survey

Five+ classical commentaries surveyed. Arabic passages read from
`data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/{edition}/92.json` (keyed by ayah). Each citation gives
scholar + work + ayah. Where a position has an empirical correlate in the project, it is flagged.

## 1. al-Ṭabarī — *Jāmiʿ al-bayān*

Source: `spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-al-tabari/92.json`.

- **v1–3 (the oath):** al-Ṭabarī reads *wa-l-layli idhā yaghshā* as God swearing by the night when it
  *covers the day with its darkness and removes its light* (ayah 1). The oath-triad sets up opposed pairs.
- **v4 (jawāb al-qasam):** "*inna saʿyakum la-shattā* — I say: your deeds are divergent (*mukhtalif*),
  O people, for among you is the disbeliever in his Lord … and the believer in Him." He explicitly marks
  v4 as the **answer to the oath** on Qatāda's authority: *waqaʿa al-qasam hāhunā* (ayah 4). → empirical
  correlate: v4 is the rhetorical hinge between the cosmic-pair oath (vv 1–3) and the moral antithesis
  (vv 5–10), which Q092-F-01 quantifies.
- **v5 / v8 (the antithesis):** al-Ṭabarī glosses *aʿṭā wa-ttaqā* as "gave what God commanded him to give
  of his wealth, and feared God by avoiding His prohibitions" and *bakhila wa-staghnā* as "withheld
  spending in God's path … and felt independent of his Lord" (ayahs 5, 8), citing Ibn ʿAbbās via ʿIkrima.
  He preserves the symmetric reading: the two poles are defined by the same template inverted.
- **v11 (*idhā taraddā*):** he reports the exegetical split — *taraddā* = "falls headlong into Hell"
  (Abū Ṣāliḥ) vs simply "dies" (ayah 11).
- **v17 (*al-atqā* / *al-ashqā*):** notes the grammatical point that the superlative *afʿal* stands for the
  simple adjective *faʿīl* (*al-atqā* = *al-taqī*), citing the poet Ṭarafa (ayah 17).

## 2. al-Qurṭubī — *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān*

Source: `spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/92.json`.

- **v5 (asbāb al-nuzūl — Abū Bakr):** "*fa-ammā man aʿṭā wa-ttaqā* — Ibn Masʿūd said: this means **Abū Bakr**,
  and the generality of the exegetes said so" (ayah 5). He narrates Abū Bakr manumitting elderly Muslims
  and weak women, and his father Abū Quḥāfa objecting that he should instead free strong men who could
  defend him — Abū Bakr replying "I want only what is with God." → links v5 to the surah's closing exemplar.
- **v6 (*bi-l-ḥusnā*):** glosses, on Ibn ʿAbbās, as *al-khalaf min Allāh ʿalā ʿaṭāʾihi* — affirming **God's
  recompense / replacement** for what is spent (ayah 6). He adduces the Muslim ḥadīth of the two angels who
  pray daily "O God, give the spender a replacement (*khalafan*)" (ayah 5; the ḥadīth is Muslim, in the
  Zakāt section). → ties the giving (v5) to *bi-l-ḥusnā* (trust in recompense).
- **vv 17–21 (al-atqā = Abū Bakr):** "Ibn ʿAbbās: it is **Abū Bakr**, who is removed from entering the
  Fire" (ayah 17). On v19 he narrates the manumission of **Bilāl**: when the polytheists tortured Bilāl
  (crying *aḥad aḥad*), the Prophet told Abū Bakr "Bilāl is being tortured for God"; Abū Bakr bought him
  with a *raṭl* of gold and freed him; the polytheists said "he freed him only to repay a debt owed to him"
  — and so *wa-mā li-aḥadin ʿindahu min niʿmatin tujzā* ("owing no one a favour to be repaid") was revealed
  (ayah 19, via ʿAṭāʾ and al-Ḍaḥḥāk from Ibn ʿAbbās).
- **v20 (*illā btighāʾa*):** discusses the grammar of the *istithnāʾ munqaṭiʿ* ("but"), citing the qirāʾa
  of Yaḥyā b. Waththāb (*ibtighāʾu* in the nominative) and pre-Islamic *shawāhid* (Bishr b. Abī Khāzim) — a
  clean instance of the qirāʾāt + balāgha attention typical of al-Qurṭubī (ayah 20).

## 3. Ibn Kathīr — *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm*

Source: `spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-ibn-kathir/92.json`.

- **Surah header (faḍāʾil / recitation):** Ibn Kathīr opens the surah by recalling the **Muʿādh ḥadīth** —
  the Prophet's reproach "*hallā ṣallayta bi-Sabbiḥ isma rabbika al-aʿlā, wa-l-Shams wa-ḍuḥāhā, wa-l-Layli
  idhā yaghshā*" — and the **Abū al-Dardāʾ / ʿAlqama** account of the reading of v3 (ayah 1, citing Aḥmad ←
  Shuʿba ← al-Mughīra ← Ibrāhīm ← ʿAlqama). → directly links Q 92 to its liturgical neighbours Q 87, Q 91,
  Q 93 (the Muʿādh cluster; see `01-empirical-profile.md` §1 and `04-hadith-corpus.md`).
- **v5:** "*aʿṭā wa-ttaqā* — gave what he was commanded to give and feared God in his affairs" (ayah 5).
- **v17:** "*wa-sa-yujannabuhā l-atqā* — the godfearing, the most godfearing, will be pushed far from the
  Fire" (ayah 17). Ibn Kathīr (in the fuller text) reports the Abū Bakr identification while noting the
  verse's wording is general (*al-ʿibra bi-ʿumūm al-lafẓ*).

## 4. al-Baghawī — *Maʿālim al-tanzīl*

Source: `spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-al-baghawi/92.json`.

al-Baghawī follows the mainstream: the oath-triad of vv 1–3 grounds the divergence-of-deeds thesis (v4),
and the giver/miser antithesis (vv 5–10) is the surah's moral core. He transmits the asbāb al-nuzūl linking
vv 5–7 and vv 17–21 to Abū Bakr's spending and Bilāl's manumission, consistent with al-Qurṭubī. (Read from
the per-ayah JSON; the Abū Bakr / Bilāl material is the shared classical sabab.)

## 5. al-Suyūṭī — *al-Itqān* + *al-Durr al-manthūr*

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/suyuti-al-itqan-fi-ulum-al-quran-english.pdf`.

- **Muqābala / ṭibāq (nawʿ 59):** al-Suyūṭī catalogues *al-ṭibāq* (antithesis) and *al-muqābala* (placing
  one set of items against an opposed set) as marked Quranic figures. Q 92:5–10 is a paradigm muqābala:
  three giver-items (*aʿṭā / ittaqā / ṣaddaqa*) set against three miser-items (*bakhila / istaghnā /
  kadhdhaba*), with paired outcomes (*yusrā ↔ ʿusrā*). → this is the classical figure that **H-NEW-2360**
  (§10.103) tested and **Q092-F-01** confirms is *frame-driven and content-overlapping*, not content-disjoint.
- **Surah-naming (nawʿ 17, asmāʾ al-suwar):** al-Suyūṭī catalogues naming conventions without claiming the
  title-word must be the surah's most frequent. → the empirical correlate is **H-NEW-1820**: Q 92 is named
  al-Layl yet ranks 48/49 in the *lyl* root (Q092-F-01 Arm C). The naming is rhetorical-mnemonic, exactly
  as al-Suyūṭī's framing implies.

## Disagreements / interpretive cruxes

1. **Who is al-atqā (v17)?** — General (al-ʿibra bi-ʿumūm al-lafẓ, Ibn Kathīr) vs specifically Abū Bakr
   (Ibn Masʿūd, Ibn ʿAbbās, "ʿāmmat al-mufassirīn" per al-Qurṭubī). The surah's wording is general; the
   sabab is Abū Bakr. Both are preserved; see `05-classical-claims-audit.md`.
2. **The reading of v3** — *wa-mā khalaqa al-dhakara wa-l-unthā* (Ḥafṣ/ʿuthmānī, our text) vs *wa-l-dhakara
   wa-l-unthā* (Ibn Masʿūd's reading, defended by Abū al-Dardāʾ in Bukhārī #4736–4737). Our canonical text
   is the *wa-mā khalaqa* reading (see `quran-text/quran-no-tashkeel.json` v3 = وما خلق الذكر والأنثى).
3. **What is *al-ḥusnā* (vv 6, 9)?** — God's recompense/replacement (*al-khalaf*, Ibn ʿAbbās), or the
   *kalimat al-tawḥīd* / Paradise / the good reward generally. al-Qurṭubī prefers *al-khalaf*.
4. **What is *taraddā* (v11)?** — "perishes/dies" vs "falls into Hell" (al-Ṭabarī reports both).

# Q 102 al-Takāthur — Tafsīr Survey

Six classical authorities surveyed from the on-disk corpus, source files cited per passage. Three
exegetical questions recur: (1) the *sabab/referent* of *al-takāthur* and "visiting the graves" (death vs.
literal grave-counting); (2) the balāgha rationale for the v3/v4 doubling; (3) the referent of *al-naʿīm*
(v 8). The Arabic of al-Ṭabarī, al-Qurṭubī and al-Baghawī was read directly; al-Jalālayn and Ibn Kathīr
from the on-disk English editions; the *kallā*-census from al-Suyūṭī ← al-Dānī.

## 1. al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān* (on Q 102:1, 2, 4)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-al-tabari/102/{1,2,4}.json`.

**On v 1:** *"alhākum ayyuhā al-nās al-mubāhāt bi-kathrat al-māl wa-l-ʿadad ʿan ṭāʿat rabbikum"* — the
rivalry that distracts is **boasting in abundance of wealth and numbers** away from obedience to your
Lord. He cites **Qatāda** (via Bishr ← Yazīd ← Saʿīd): the people "used to say *naḥnu akthar min Banī
Fulān*" ("we are more than the clan of so-and-so") — tribal numbers-boasting.

**On v 2:** *"ḥattā ṣirtum ilā al-maqābir fa-dufintum fīhā; wa-fī hādhā dalīl ʿalā ṣiḥḥat al-qawl
bi-ʿadhāb al-qabr"* — "visiting the graves" = being buried, and **this is a proof of the soundness of
[the doctrine of] punishment of the grave (ʿadhāb al-qabr)**. He grounds this with the **ʿAlī** narration
(isnād: Abū Kurayb ← Ibn ʿAṭiyya ← Qays ← Ḥajjāj ← al-Minhāl ← Zirr ← ʿAlī): *"kunnā nashukku fī ʿadhāb
al-qabr ḥattā nazalat hādhihi al-āya"* ("we used to doubt the punishment of the grave until this verse was
revealed"). **This is the same isnād family as Tirmidhī #3439** (al-Minhāl ← Zirr ← ʿAlī; see
`04-hadith-corpus.md`).

**On v 4:** the explicit balāgha rationale for the doubling — *"wa-karrara qawlahu (kallā sawfa taʿlamūn)
marratayn, li-anna al-ʿArab idhā arādat al-taghlīẓ fī al-takhwīf wa-l-tahdīd karrarū al-kalimata
marratayn"* ("He repeated *kallā sawfa taʿlamūn* twice, because the Arabs, when they wish to intensify a
warning and a threat, repeat the word twice"). He also reports **al-Ḍaḥḥāk's** reading that v 3's *kallā
sawfa taʿlamūn* addresses the disbelievers and v 4's the believers.

**Empirical correlate:** al-Ṭabarī's *takrīr li-l-taghlīẓ* is the stated device whose cross-corpus
correlate is the *thumma*-doubled adjacent threat-refrain family (Q102-F-01 Arm B, `06-novel-findings.md`).

## 2. al-Qurṭubī, *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān* (on Q 102:1, 8)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/102/{1,8}.json`.

**On v 1:** *"Sūrat al-Takāthur — wa-hiya makkiyya fī qawl jamīʿ al-mufassirīn, wa-rawā al-Bukhārī
annahā madaniyya, wa-hiya thamānī āyāt"* ("Meccan by the statement of all the mufassirūn; al-Bukhārī
reported it Medinan; it is eight verses"). He gives **two readings of *alhākum***: (1) *shaghalakum* —
"it preoccupied you" (the mubāhāt in wealth/numbers, away from obedience, until death and burial); and
(2) *ansākum* — "it made you forget" (Ibn ʿAbbās, al-Ḥasan). On the *takāthur* referent: wealth/children
(Ibn ʿAbbās, al-Ḥasan); tribal/clan boasting (Qatāda); preoccupation with livelihood and trade
(al-Ḍaḥḥāk). He cites the Imruʾ al-Qays *shāhid* (*fa-mithluki ḥublā qad ṭaraqtu wa-murḍiʿin / fa-alhaytuhā
ʿan dhī tamāʾim mughyili*) to fix the sense of *alhā* = "to divert/distract."

**On v 8 (*al-naʿīm*):** al-Qurṭubī leads with **Muslim's** ṣaḥīḥ narration via **Abū Hurayra**: the
Prophet, Abū Bakr and ʿUmar go out by night driven by hunger to an Anṣārī's house, eat fresh dates,
meat and drink cool water, and the Prophet says this is of the *naʿīm* about which they will be
questioned. This grounds the *naʿīm* = the favours of food, drink, shade and security (the dispute over
the exact referent is surveyed across the verse).

## 3. Ibn Kathīr, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm* (on Q 102:1)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/en-tafisr-ibn-kathir/102/1.json`
(Arabic at `.../ar-tafsir-ibn-kathir/102/`).

Section-title: **"The Result of Loving the World and Heedlessness of the Hereafter."** Ibn Kathīr glosses
*al-takāthur* as preoccupation with the world's delights and adornments that distracts from the
Hereafter "until death comes to you and you visit the graves, thus becoming its inhabitants." He then
cites, **explicitly by book**: *"In Sahih Al-Bukhari, it is recorded in the Book of Ar-Riqaq (Narrations
that soften the Heart) from Anas bin Malik, who reported that Ubayy bin Kaʿb said: 'We used to think that
this was a part of the Qurʾan until the Ayah was revealed which says: alhākum al-takāthur.'"* — i.e. the
valley-of-gold saying (*law kāna li-ibn Ādam wādiyān…*) was regarded as Qurʾān until al-Takāthur
abrogated/replaced it as the admonition on greed. (**On-disk verification:** this is **Bukhārī #6201**,
Kitāb al-Riqāq, immediately following the valley-of-gold ḥadīth **#6200** — see `04-hadith-corpus.md`.)

## 4. al-Jalālayn (Maḥallī & Suyūṭī), *Tafsīr al-Jalālayn* (on Q 102:2, 4, 7)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/en-al-jalalayn/102/{2,4,7}.json`.

**v 2:** "until you visit the graves — either in that you have died and then been buried in them, or it
means to the extent that you actually count the dead as a something to rival one another by" — the
**two-reading** crux (death-burial vs. literal grave-counting-boast), stated tersely.

**v 4:** "Again, no indeed! You will come to know — the evil consequences of your mutual vainglory **at
the moment of the extraction of the soul**, then you will come to know **in the grave**" — al-Jalālayn
distributes the doubled threat across two moments (death-rattle, then the grave).

**v 7:** "Again you will surely see it — **repeated for emphasis** — with the eye of certainty; *ʿayna*
is a verbal noun, as both *raʾā* and *ʿāyana* have the same meaning." Confirms the *ʿayn al-yaqīn* =
direct-sight grade and the repetition-for-emphasis reading of the *thumma*-led v 7.

## 5. al-Baghawī, *Maʿālim al-tanzīl* (on Q 102:2)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-al-baghawi/102/2.json`.

al-Baghawī glosses *ḥattā zurtum al-maqābir* as "until you died and were buried in the graves." He gives
**two asbāb**: (1) **Qatāda** — revealed about the **Jews** who said *naḥnu akthar min Banī Fulān…*,
preoccupied in boasting until they died astray; (2) **Muqātil & al-Kalbī** — revealed about **two Quraysh
clans, Banū ʿAbd Manāf b. Quṣayy and Banū Sahm b. ʿAmr**, who vied over which had more chiefs and nobles,
**counting even their dead** in the graveyards to win the numbers-boast. The Banū Sahm-vs-ʿAbd-Manāf
grave-counting account is the classic *sabab* for the literal "visiting the graves" reading.

## 6. al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān* (nawʿ 40 ← al-Dānī) — the *kallā*-census anchor

Source: project finding `findings/phase-b-hypotheses/h-new-2230-qac-lemma-numerical-rerun.md` (claim 7),
which records the al-Suyūṭī (← al-Dānī, *al-Muktafā*) tradition: the rebuke-particle *kallā* occurs **33
times**, concentrated in the latter (mufaṣṣal) half of the muṣḥaf. Q 102 carries **3 of the 33** (vv 3,
4, 5). This census is the classical anchor for Q102-F-01 Arm A (`05-classical-claims-audit.md`,
`06-novel-findings.md`); the QAC-lemma re-verification (POS:AVR LEM `kal~aA`, homograph-clean) confirms
the count = 33 exactly.

## 7. Points of agreement / disagreement

| Question | al-Ṭabarī | al-Qurṭubī | Ibn Kathīr | al-Jalālayn | al-Baghawī |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Chronology | Meccan | **Meccan (all); Bukhārī: Medinan** | Meccan | (Meccan) | Meccan |
| *al-takāthur* referent | wealth/numbers boast (Qatāda) | wealth/children + tribal boast | love of the world | mutual vainglory | Jews / two Quraysh clans |
| "visit graves" (v 2) | death+burial → **ʿadhāb al-qabr proof** | death+burial | death → grave-dwelling | death OR literal counting | death; literal grave-counting (Muqātil) |
| v3/v4 doubling | *takrīr li-l-taghlīẓ* (Arabs double to threaten) | — | — | death-extraction + grave (two moments) | — |
| *al-naʿīm* (v 8) | — | food/drink/shade (Muslim ← Abū Hurayra) | — | — | — |

The defining exegetical convergence is the **Meccan classification** (al-Qurṭubī: by consensus of the
mufassirūn, with a lone Bukhārī report of Medinan) and the **grave-torment** weight of v 2 (al-Ṭabarī's
ʿadhāb al-qabr proof via the ʿAlī narration). The doubling of vv 3-4 is read by al-Ṭabarī as a fixed Arab
threat-intensification device — the classical motivation for the empirical Q102-F-01 Arm B.

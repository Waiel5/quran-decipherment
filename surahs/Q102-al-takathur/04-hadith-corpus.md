# Q 102 al-Takāthur — Ḥadīth Corpus

**VERIFICATION NOTE.** Every ḥadīth number below was read from the on-disk database
`data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/{collection}.json`, field `idInBook`

Q 102's ḥadīth corpus is built around four narrations: (1) the "māl mālī" servant-ḥadīth, (2) the
valley-of-gold / Ubayy abrogation, (3) the ʿAlī grave-torment occasion, (4) the witr-recitation.

## 1. The "māl mālī" servant-ḥadīth (Muṭarrif ← his father ← the Prophet, reciting v 1)

The Prophet, reciting *alhākum al-takāthur*, comments: *"yaqūlu ibn Ādam: mālī mālī! wa-hal laka yā ibn
Ādam min mālika illā mā akalta fa-afnayta, aw labista fa-ablayta, aw taṣaddaqta fa-amḍayta"* — "The son
of Adam says 'my wealth, my wealth!' — but do you own of your wealth anything except what you ate and so
consumed, or wore and so wore out, or gave in charity and so made it endure?" Isnād: **Muṭarrif b. ʿAbd
Allāh b. al-Shikhkhīr ← his father ← the Prophet** (via Qatāda).

## 2. The valley-of-gold + the Ubayy "we used to regard it as Qurʾān" abrogation

The admonition on insatiable greed (*"law kāna li-ibn Ādam wādiyān min māl la-btaghā thālithan, wa-lā
yamlaʾu jawf ibn Ādam illā al-turāb…"*) and Ubayy b. Kaʿb's report that this saying was once held to be
Qurʾān until al-Takāthur was revealed (cited by Ibn Kathīr on v 1, `03-tafsir-survey.md`). In Bukhārī's
Kitāb al-Riqāq the valley-of-gold ḥadīth and the Ubayy/al-Takāthur report sit **adjacent**:

immediately after the valley-of-gold ḥadīth #6200.

## 3. The ʿAlī grave-torment occasion (v 2 → ʿadhāb al-qabr)

ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib: *"mā ziltā nashukku fī ʿadhāb al-qabr ḥattā nazalat {alhākum al-takāthur}"* — "we did
not cease to doubt the punishment of the grave until al-Takāthur was revealed." Isnād: **al-Minhāl b.
ʿAmr ← Zirr b. Ḥubaysh ← ʿAlī**.

## 5. Faḍāʾil note

**No dedicated *faḍāʾil* (specific virtue-of-reciting) ḥadīth for Sūrat al-Takāthur was located in the
on-disk 9-book database.** The Aḥmad #639 narration places it in the witr mufaṣṣal recitation set but is
not a virtue-claim. **Flagged as data-gap, not as an absence-of-evidence claim** — a dedicated faḍāʾil
sub-collection search (e.g., Ibn al-Ḍurays *Faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān*, al-Thaʿlabī's chains) is outside the
on-disk 9-book set. (This is consistent with the short-mufaṣṣal block generally; faḍāʾil ḥadīth are
concentrated on Q 1, 2, 18, 36, 55, 56, 67, 112, 113, 114.)

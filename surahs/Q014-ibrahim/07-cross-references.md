## 5. Verse-twin / structural pattern relationships

### Q 14:1 ↔ Q 12:2, Q 13:1, Q 15:1 (post-muqaṭṭaʿāt book-reference)

Q 14:1 *kitābun anzalnāhu ilayka* fits the muqaṭṭaʿāt → book-reference pattern (cross-finding-008-muqattaat-book-intro-markers). Parallel openers:
- Q 12:2 *innā anzalnāhu qurʾānan ʿarabiyyan*
- Q 13:1 *tilka āyātu al-kitāb*
- **Q 14:1 *kitābun anzalnāhu ilayka***
- Q 15:1 *tilka āyātu al-kitāb wa-qurʾānin mubīn*

The pattern is corpus-distinctive: the muqaṭṭaʿāt-opener surahs Q 12, 13, 14, 15 ALL have post-muqaṭṭaʿāt book-self-references. Q 14:1 belongs to this 4-surah cluster.

### Q 14:46 ↔ Q 13:31 (mountains-removed iʿjāz topos)

Q 14:46 *wa-in kāna makruhum li-tazūla minhu al-jibāl* (their plot was such that mountains might be moved by it) echoes Q 13:31 *wa-law anna qurʾānan suyyirat bihi al-jibāl* (if there were any Qurʾān by which mountains could be moved). The two verses share the *moving-mountains* topos in adjacent mushaf-position surahs, reinforcing the Q 13 ↔ Q 14 thematic twinning at the verse-level.

### Q 14:35-41 ↔ Q 2:127-129 (Abrahamic Mecca-prayer pair)

The Qurʾān contains **two Abrahamic Mecca-prayer blocks**:
- **Q 2:127-129** (canonical Medinan; Abraham's Kaʿba-foundation prayer at *idh yarfaʿu Ibrāhīmu al-qawāʿid*)
- **Q 14:35-41** (Late Meccan; Abraham's Mecca-foundation prayer at *waj-nubnī wa-banīya*)

al-Suyūṭī (`03-tafsir-survey.md` §5) treats Q 14:35-41 as the **late-Meccan revelation extension** of the Q 2:127-129 prayer-block. The verses share lexical features:
- *rabbanā ighfir lī* / *rabbanā wa-ijʿalnā muslimayni*
- *wa-min dhurriyyatī* (Q 14:40 / Q 2:128)
- *wa-arzuqhum min al-thamarāt* (Q 14:37) / *wa-arzuq ahlahu min al-thamarāt* (Q 2:126)

The Q 14 prayer is structurally the more-extensive (7 verses; 107 words) vs Q 2 (3 verses).

### Q 14:24-27 ↔ Q 36:33-44 (creation-as-sign parable cluster)

The good-word parable in Q 14:24-25 is one of the corpus's most-extended creation-parable structures. Related parables: Q 36:33-44 (signs in earth, sun & moon, ships), Q 24:35 (light-verse mishkāt parable), Q 7:175-177 (the dog-of-the-disbeliever parable). Q 14:24-25 belongs to the *parable-of-the-believing-word* sub-class.

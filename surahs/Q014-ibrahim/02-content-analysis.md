## 2. Verse-level content highlights (Arabic-anchored)

### vv. 1-3: opening
- **Q 14:1**: *الر ۚ كتاب أنزلناه إليك لتخرج الناس من الظلمات إلى النور بإذن ربهم إلى صراط العزيز الحميد* — **the unique Quranic mission-statement of bringing mankind from darkness to light**. The phrase *min al-ẓulumāti ilā al-nūr* recurs in Q 2:257, Q 5:16, Q 33:43, Q 57:9 — but Q 14:1 is the form attached most directly to the *kitāb anzalnāhu* (the book sent down) self-reference.
- **Q 14:2**: *الله الذي له ما في السماوات وما في الأرض* — divine-sovereignty-of-cosmology formula; recurrent.
- **Q 14:3**: prefer-this-world-over-the-next + obstruction-from-God's-path (*yaṣuddūna ʿan sabīli Allāh*).

### v. 4: universal-prophet-language
- **Q 14:4**: *وما أرسلنا من رسول إلا بلسان قومه ليبين لهم* — every messenger sent in the language of his people. **Foundational verse for the doctrine of revelation-in-mother-tongue** (al-Suyūṭī's *al-Itqān*, nawʿ on revelation cites this verse extensively).

### vv. 5-8: Mūsā compressed cycle
- **Q 14:5**: Mūsā sent with God's signs to bring his people from darkness to light + remembrance of *ayyām Allāh* (the Days of God).
- **Q 14:6**: Mūsā's address — remember the Pharaoh's affliction (slaughter of sons, sparing of women) — exodus-typology.
- **Q 14:7**: *لئن شكرتم لأزيدنكم* — the gratitude-receives-increase formula.
- **Q 14:8**: God's self-sufficiency from human gratitude (Mūsā's recognition).

### vv. 9-15: generic prophet-cycle confrontation
- **Q 14:9**: catalog of past peoples: *قوم نوح وعاد وثمود ۛ والذين من بعدهم ۛ لا يعلمهم إلا الله*. The disbelievers' response: *إنا كفرنا بما أرسلتم به*.
- **Q 14:10**: messengers' rebuttal — God is the *fāṭir al-samāwāti wa-l-arḍ* (Originator). The disbelievers' demand for "clear authority" — bashar mithlanā (just like us).
- **Q 14:11**: *إن نحن إلا بشر مثلكم* — affirmation of messenger-humanity; God favours whom He wills.
- **Q 14:12**: *وعلى الله فليتوكل المتوكلون* — corpus-frequent *tawakkul* formula.
- **Q 14:13-14**: disbelievers' threat to expel messengers; God's promise to destroy wrongdoers.
- **Q 14:15**: *واستفتحوا وخاب كل جبار عنيد* — the failure-of-the-tyrant theme.

### vv. 16-22: eschatology
- **Q 14:16-17**: hell as a place where one drinks *māʾ ṣadīd* (water of pus), barely swallowing it.
- **Q 14:18**: parable of disbelievers' deeds as ash in a windy day (*ka-ramādin ishtaddat bihi al-rīḥu fī yawmin ʿāṣif*) — the *ash-in-wind* parable, a Quranic balagha hallmark.
- **Q 14:19-20**: cosmological assertion — God created heavens and earth in truth, can replace humans with new creation; "this is not difficult for God".
- **Q 14:21**: Day of Judgment scene — the weak (followers) and the proud (leaders) confront each other; the proud confess they cannot help.
- **Q 14:22**: ⭐ **IBLĪS'S ESCHATOLOGICAL SPEECH** — *وقال الشيطان لما قضي الأمر* (Satan said when the matter was decreed): "*God promised you a true promise; I promised you and broke my promises to you. I had no authority over you except that I called you and you responded; do not blame me, blame yourselves.*" — **the corpus-UNIQUE Iblīs-as-Day-of-Judgment-orator** (compare Q 15:39, where Iblīs is shown in the rebellion-discourse with God; Q 14:22 has Iblīs in self-disavowal-speech to the damned).

### vv. 23-27: parable of the good word
- **Q 14:23**: believers' reward — gardens, rivers, eternity, *taḥiyyatuhum fīhā salām* (their greeting is "peace").
- **Q 14:24-25**: ⭐ **THE GOOD-WORD PARABLE** — *كلمة طيبة كشجرة طيبة أصلها ثابت وفرعها في السماء ۞ تؤتي أكلها كل حين بإذن ربها* (a good word is like a good tree, its root firm and its branch in heaven, giving its fruit every season by its Lord's permission). One of the most-cited parables in classical balagha. Hadith: Bukhārī #61 / Muslim parallel — the Prophet's identification of this tree with the date-palm; see `04-hadith-corpus.md`.
- **Q 14:26**: contrast — bad word as bad tree, uprooted from the surface of earth, no permanence.
- **Q 14:27**: God establishes believers with the firm word (*al-qawl al-thābit*) — interpretive bridge to the Bukhārī hadith on the questioning of the dead in the grave.

### vv. 28-30: parable of the disbelievers
- **Q 14:28**: *ألم تر إلى الذين بدلوا نعمت الله كفرا* — those who exchanged God's grace for disbelief and led their people to ruin.
- **Q 14:29-30**: hell — *jahannamu yaṣlawnahā wa-biʾsa al-qarār*; God's anger at idol-establishment.

### v. 31: instruction to believers
- **Q 14:31**: *قل لعبادي الذين آمنوا يقيموا الصلاة وينفقوا مما رزقناهم سرا وعلانية* — establish prayer + spend secret-and-public, before the day comes when there is no transaction or friendship.

### vv. 32-34: cosmological-grace recital
- **Q 14:32**: God who created heavens & earth, sent water, brought fruits, subjected the ship to the sea by His command, subjected the rivers.
- **Q 14:33**: subjected the sun & moon (*dāʾibayni* — continuously moving), subjected day & night.
- **Q 14:34**: *وآتاكم من كل ما سألتموه ۚ وإن تعدوا نعمت الله لا تحصوها ۗ إن الإنسان لظلوم كفار* — "He gave you of all you asked Him; if you count God's grace, you cannot enumerate it. Indeed, mankind is greatly unjust, ungrateful." — **classical anchor for "uncountable grace" theology** (Tirmidhī cites this verse in *al-Daʿawāt*).

### vv. 35-41: ⭐⭐ **THE MECCA-PRAYER OF ABRAHAM** ⭐⭐

**The corpus-MAX prayer-density 7-verse window. 16 prayer-vocative tokens / 107 words = 14.95 / 100w (corpus rank 1 of 5,569 7-verse windows).**

| Verse | Petition / declaration | Distinctive feature |
|:--:|:--|:--|
| 14:35 | *وإذ قال إبراهيم رب اجعل هذا البلد آمنا واجنبني وبني أن نعبد الأصنام* | "make this city safe; protect me and my children from worshipping idols" — **corpus-unique grandparent-child intercessory framing** |
| 14:36 | *رب إنهن أضللن كثيرا من الناس ۖ فمن تبعني فإنه مني ۖ ومن عصاني فإنك غفور رحيم* | "they (the idols) have led many astray; he who follows me is mine, but he who disobeys, You are forgiving and merciful" |
| 14:37 | *ربنا إني أسكنت من ذريتي بواد غير ذي زرع عند بيتك المحرم ربنا ليقيموا الصلاة فاجعل أفئدة من الناس تهوي إليهم وارزقهم من الثمرات* | "I have settled my offspring in a barren valley near Your Sacred House; make hearts of mankind incline toward them and give them sustenance" — **the Mecca-foundation prayer**; classical anchor for the Hagar-Ishmael Mecca-settling tradition (Bukhārī #3364) |
| 14:38 | *ربنا إنك تعلم ما نخفي وما نعلن ۗ وما يخفى على الله من شيء في الأرض ولا في السماء* | "You know what we conceal and what we reveal; nothing is hidden from God in earth or heaven" |
| 14:39 | *الحمد لله الذي وهب لي على الكبر إسماعيل وإسحاق ۚ إن ربي لسميع الدعاء* | **"praise be to God who granted me Ishmael and Isaac in old age; my Lord is hearer of prayer"** — **corpus-UNIQUE *al-ḥamd-for-prophetic-progeny* declaration**; the only verse where Abraham praises God by listing his sons by name |
| 14:40 | *رب اجعلني مقيم الصلاة ومن ذريتي ۚ ربنا وتقبل دعاء* | "make me an establisher of prayer, and from my offspring; accept my prayer" |
| 14:41 | *ربنا اغفر لي ولوالدي وللمؤمنين يوم يقوم الحساب* | "forgive me and my parents and the believers on the Day of Reckoning" — **corpus-rare three-generation (parents + self + believers) intercession** |

**Classical attention**: al-Bāqillānī, *Iʿjāz al-Qurʾān*, treats this block as a structural-iʿjāz exemplar of "compressed ethical-and-pious self-positioning". al-Rāzī (Mafātīḥ al-ghayb) devotes a long section to the prayer's **logical sequence**: starts with Mecca-safety (corporate) → idolatry-protection (personal-and-progeny) → Mecca-foundation (descendent-corporate) → cosmological-confession (theological framing) → praise for late-life sons (al-ḥamd anchor) → prayer-establishment (ritual continuity) → final intercession (eschatological closure).

The block contains **9 occurrences of *rabbi*** and **5 of *rabbanā*** (Q014-F-01 prayer-density measurement) — corpus-distinctive vocative-density.

### vv. 42-47: eschatological closure
- **Q 14:42**: *ولا تحسبن الله غافلا عما يعمل الظالمون* — God is not unaware of what the wrongdoers do.
- **Q 14:43-44**: scene of the resurrected — heads raised, eyes not blinking, hearts empty (*hawāʾ*); *yawma yaʾtīhim al-ʿadhāb* — they will plead for delay, then be reminded of their original denial.
- **Q 14:46**: their plotting — *وإن كان مكرهم لتزول منه الجبال* — "though their plot was such that mountains might be moved by it" (the *iʿjāz-singular* echo of Q 13:31, the *suyyirat bihi al-jibāl* theme).
- **Q 14:47**: God will not break His promise to His messengers.

### vv. 48-51: Day of Judgment scene
- **Q 14:48**: *يوم تبدل الأرض غير الأرض* — Day when earth is changed to other earth, and the heavens too. The corpus-rare "altered cosmos" formula.
- **Q 14:49-50**: criminals chained in shackles; their garments of pitch; faces enveloped in fire.
- **Q 14:51**: God's justice in repaying every soul; God is swift in reckoning.

### v. 52: closing
- **Q 14:52**: *هذا بلاغ للناس وليذكر أولو الألباب* — "this is a message for mankind, that they may be warned, and that those of understanding may remember." The classical *balāgh*-closure formula.

Q 14 ranks **#4 / 114 on whole-surah prayer-vocative density** (computed Q014-F-01: 1.92 prayer-tokens per 100 words; behind only Q 1 at 6.9, Q 106 at 5.9, and Q 71 Nūḥ at 2.2 — all of which are dominated by single prayer-blocks). Note: the Q 22 verse and the Q 15:32-44 Iblīs-rebellion-discourse are the corpus's two most-developed Iblīs speeches; they are TYPOLOGICALLY DISTINCT — Q 14:22 is post-Judgment, Q 15:32-44 is pre-creation. (See `07-cross-references.md` §4.)

Q 14:39 is the corpus-UNIQUE verse where Abraham names his sons by name (*Ismāʿīl wa-Isḥāq*) within a *ḥamd*-construction.

## 4. Repetition patterns / refrains

Q 14 has the **lowest refrain-frequency in the head-mushaf zone** — no Q 55-style repeating refrain. Instead, the surah's lexical cohesion is via:
- *rabbi / rabbanā* clustering in vv. 35-41 (16 occurrences in 7 verses).
- *al-ʿazīz al-ḥamīd* / *al-ʿazīz al-ḥakīm* / *al-ḥamīd* — divine-name closures of vv. 1, 4, 8 (the head-section's al-ḥamīd anchor).
- *kafara* / *kufr* derivatives — corpus-frequent in vv. 7, 8, 13, 18, 22 (the eschatology-band).
- *ẓulumāti* / *al-nūr* — Q 14:1 + 5 (light-from-darkness mission).

## 5. Cross-surah content references

- Q 14:1 *kitābun anzalnāhu* — parallel to Q 12:2, Q 13:1 (post-muqaṭṭaʿāt book-reference); fits cross-finding-008-muqattaat-book-intro-markers pattern.
- Q 14:4 *bi-lisān qawmihi* — al-Suyūṭī's anchor verse for the universal-prophet-language doctrine.
- Q 14:24-25 good-word parable — referenced in Bukhārī #61 (the date-palm hadith); see `04-hadith-corpus.md`.
- Q 14:35-41 Mecca-prayer — extends Abraham-prayer-block in Q 2:127-129 (the Kaʿba-foundation prayer at the al-Baqara stage). Q 2:128 *rabbanā wa-jʿalnā muslimayni laka* is parallel form. Q 14's prayer is **the late-Meccan extension** of the Q 2 al-Baqara version. (Note: Q 2 is canonically Medinan but contains Abraham-Mecca-prayer material; the typological connection is intertextual not chronological.)
- Q 14:46 *li-tazūla minhu al-jibāl* — echoes Q 13:31 *suyyirat bihi al-jibāl* (the iʿjāz-mountains topos), reinforcing Q 13 ↔ Q 14 thematic twinning.
- Q 14:48 *yawma tubaddalu al-arḍu ghayra al-arḍ* — corpus-rare altered-cosmos formula (related: Q 39:67-69 the rolling-up-of-heavens-as-a-scroll).

## 6. Content register classification

**Mixed**: cosmological + prophet-narrative-summary + parable + prayer-block + eschatology. The surah is a **late-Meccan thematic-mosaic** characteristic of the al-Suyūṭī-classified late-Meccan period (rev #72 Tanzil). The prayer-block (vv. 35-41) is the structural and lexical centre.

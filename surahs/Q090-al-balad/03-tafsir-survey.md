# Q 90 al-Balad — Tafsīr Survey

Five classical commentaries surveyed from the on-disk corpus, cited per passage. Two exegetical
fault-lines structure the surah: (1) the opening **lā** (negation vs. *zāʾida* emphatic), and (2)
**al-najdayn** (the two ways vs. the two breasts). The qasam→jawāb identification (v 1 → v 4) is
agreed by all.

## 1. al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān* (on Q 90:1, 4, 8, 10, 11–13, 17–20)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-al-tabari/90/{1,4,8,10,11,12,13,17,18,19,20}.json`.

- **v 1** *lā uqsimu bi-hādhā al-balad*: "أقسم يا محمد بهذا البلد الحرام، وهو مكة" — He swears by this
  sacred City, namely **Makka**; "wa-kadhālika qāla ahl al-taʾwīl." Chains: Ibn ʿAbbās (via Muḥammad b.
  Saʿd's family isnād) and **Mujāhid** (via Sufyān ← Manṣūr): "Makka / al-ḥarām."
- **v 4** *la-qad khalaqnā al-insān fī kabad*: al-Ṭabarī states outright **"وهذا هو جواب القسم"** — *this is
  the jawāb al-qasam*. Via Qatāda: "waqaʿa hā-hunā al-qasam." The disagreement on *kabad*: "fī shidda
  wa-ʿanāʾ wa-naṣab" (hardship/toil — Ibn ʿAbbās via ʿAlī, al-Ḥasan via Manṣūr b. Zādhān, Qatāda "ḥīna
  khuliqa fī mashaqqa").
- **v 8–9** *a-lam najʿal lahu ʿaynayn* …: "two eyes by which he sees God's proofs against him, a tongue
  by which he expresses what he wants, and two lips — as a favour from Us."
- **v 10** *wa-hadaynāhu al-najdayn*: "al-ṭarīqayn — the two roads; *najd* is an elevated road." The
  dominant reading: **najd al-khayr wa-najd al-sharr** (the way of good and the way of evil), cross-citing
  Q 76:3 *innā hadaynāhu al-sabīl*; via **ʿAbdallāh [b. Masʿūd] ← Zirr ← ʿĀṣim**. A minority (al-Rabīʿ b.
  Khuthaym): "they are not the two breasts (*laysā bi-l-thadyayn*)."
- **v 11–13** *fa-lā iqtaḥama al-ʿaqaba … fakk raqaba*: "he did not storm/cross the pass"; *al-ʿaqaba*
  reported as "a mountain in Hell" (al-Ḥasan, Ibn ʿUmar via ʿAṭiyya, Qatāda). On *fakk raqaba* (v 13)
  al-Ṭabarī cites **Qatāda ← Sālim b. Abī al-Jaʿd ← Maʿdān b. Abī Ṭalḥa** that the Prophet was asked
  "which slaves are greatest in reward? — *akthuruhā thamanan* (the most expensive)" — **the exact
  prophetic gloss verified in the hadith corpus** (Abū Dharr, Bukhārī #2418 / Ibn Mājah #2259, see
  `04-hadith-corpus.md`).
- **v 13–14** the **qirāʾāt**: *fakku raqaba … aw iṭʿāmun* (Madina/Kūfa/Shām, on iḍāfa + maṣdar) vs.
  *fakka raqaba … aw aṭʿama* (al-Kisāʾī and Mecca/Baṣra qurrāʾ, verb-readings, supported by *thumma kāna*
  in v 17). al-Ṭabarī: both are well-attested and either reading is correct.
- **v 18–20** *aṣḥāb al-maymana* = the people of the right (to Paradise); *aṣḥāb al-mashʾama* = the people
  of the left; *nār mūṣada* = "muṭbaqa" (vaulted/closed-over), via Ibn ʿAbbās and Qatāda ("aṭbaqahā Allāh
  ʿalayhim fa-lā ḍawʾ fīhā wa-lā faraj").

## 2. al-Zamakhsharī, *al-Kashshāf* (on Q 90:1–7, 8–16, 17–20)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/zamakhshari-kashshaf.openiti.raw.txt` lines 71847–71968
(plus the balāgha cross-note at line 69406).

- **Oath structure (vv 1–4):** "aqsama subḥānahu bi-l-balad al-ḥarām wa-mā baʿdahu **ʿalā anna al-insān
  khuliqa maghmūran fī mukābadat al-mashāqq wa-l-shadāʾid**, wa-uʿturiḍa bayna al-qasam wa-l-muqsam ʿalayhi
  bi-qawlihi *wa-anta ḥill bi-hādhā al-balad*." — i.e. the **jawāb is v 4** and **v 2 is a parenthesis**.
  (Confirmed by his explicit balāgha remark elsewhere, line 69406: "alā tarā kayfa laqqā *lā uqsimu
  bi-hādhā al-balad* bi-qawlihi *la-qad khalaqnā al-insān*.")
- **v 2** *wa-anta ḥill*: two faces — (a) Makka's people deem you *ḥill* (fair-game) despite its sanctity,
  as they hunt outside the ḥaram; consolation to the Prophet for what he endured; or (b) **future sense**:
  "you will be *ḥill* in it — free to do as you wish (killing, capturing)" — fulfilled at the **Conquest**.
  He answers the chronology objection (the surah is Meccan by agreement, the Conquest later): the
  present-tense for the future is "wider in God's speech, for future states before Him are like the
  present" — citing *innaka mayyit* (Q 39:30). He quotes the Makka-sanctity hadith ("inna Allāh ḥarrama
  Makka yawma khalaqa al-samāwāt wa-l-arḍ…", with al-ʿAbbās's *illā al-idhkhir*).
- **v 3** *wa-wālid wa-mā walad*: the Prophet and his offspring (or Ādam and his children, or every
  begetter and begotten); *tankīr* "li-l-ibhām al-mustaqill bi-l-madḥ wa-l-taʿajjub."
- **kabad** (v 4): etymology — from *kabida al-rajul* (his liver swelled/pained), extended to all *taʿab
  wa-mashaqqa*; "minhu ushtuqqat al-mukābada." (al-Zamakhsharī's morphology, not asserted as empirical.)
- **vv 5–7:** the pronoun in *a-yaḥsabu* refers to "baʿḍ ṣanādīd Quraysh" (a Quraysh chief who oppressed
  the believers); *māl lubad* = "kathrat mā anfaqahu" in the riyāʾ/boasting the Jāhiliyya called *makārim*.
- **v 10** *al-najdayn* = "ṭarīqay al-khayr wa-l-sharr; wa-qīla: al-thadyayn."
- **vv 11–16** *fa-lā iqtaḥama*: "he did not thank those favours by good deeds — freeing necks, feeding
  orphans and the poor — then by *īmān* which is the root of all obedience."
- **v 20** *mūṣada*: qirāʾa *mūṣada* (with wāw) and *muʾṣada* (with hamza), from *waṣadtu / aṣadtu al-bāb*
  (I shut the door); the famous Abū Bakr b. ʿAyyāsh anecdote on the hamza.
- **Faḍāʾil note:** al-Zamakhsharī closes with "ʿan Rasūl Allāh: man qaraʾa *lā uqsimu bi-hādhā al-balad*
  aʿṭāhu Allāh al-amān min ghaḍabihi yawm al-qiyāma." **This is the Ubayy b. Kaʿb faḍāʾil-al-suwar
  formula** that runs through the end of every surah in al-Kashshāf and is judged **fabricated/mawḍūʿ** by
  ḥadīth critics (Ibn al-Jawzī, *al-Mawḍūʿāt*); flagged here as NON-authentic, see `04-hadith-corpus.md` §4.

## 3. al-Rāzī, *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* (on Q 90:1–4, 8–10, 11)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/razi-mafatih-al-ghayb.openiti.raw.txt` lines 259170–259345.

- **v 1:** "ajmaʿa al-mufassirūn ʿalā anna dhālika al-balad hiya **Makka**." al-Rāzī then catalogues
  Makka's merits (the ḥaram-amān, the qibla, maqām Ibrāhīm, the ḥajj, the prohibition of game) to justify
  the oath.
- **v 2** *wa-anta ḥill*: **five faces** (al-wajh al-awwal … al-khāmis): (1) "you are resident/dwelling in
  it" — exalting Makka via the Prophet's presence; (2) *ḥill* = lawful: the Quraysh, despite the City's
  sanctity, deem your harm/killing lawful; (3) Qatāda: "you are not sinning — it is lawful for you to
  fight in Makka" (the Conquest, with the killing of Ibn Khaṭal and Miqyas b. Ṣubāba and the sparing of
  Abū Sufyān's house); (4) "you commit nothing forbidden in it, out of reverence"; (5) "you are one of
  the *ḥill* (people) of this honoured City — they know your lineage and lifelong purity."
- He raises the **chronology objection** (Meccan surah, but the Conquest came at the end of the Hijra
  period) and resolves it as al-Zamakhsharī does (present for future; *innaka mayyit*).
- **v 3** *wa-wālid wa-mā walad*: "maʿṭūf ʿalā *lā uqsimu bi-hādhā al-balad*, wa-qawluhu *wa-anta ḥill…*
  muʿtariḍ bayna al-maʿṭūf wa-l-maʿṭūf ʿalayhi" — confirming the **parenthesis** parse. Faces of *wālid*:
  Ādam + progeny / Ibrāhīm + Ismāʿīl + Muḥammad / every begetter.
- **v 10** *al-najdayn* (lines 259316+): "al-sabīl ism li-l-jins" — the two ways of good and evil.
- **v 11** *fa-lā iqtaḥama al-ʿaqaba*: parsed with the elided *lā* (cf. *fa-lā ṣaddaqa wa-lā ṣallā*,
  Q 75:31, which al-Rāzī cross-cites at line 250765).

## 4. al-Qurṭubī, *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān* (on Q 90:1, 10)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/90/{1,10}.json`.

- **v 1 (header):** "Sūrat al-Balad, **Meccan by agreement (مكية باتفاق)**, and it is **twenty verses
  (وهي عشرون آية)**." — the canonical type + count.
- **The *lā* crux — six positions:** (1) **al-Akhfash**: *lā* is *zāʾida* (redundant), as in *lā uqsimu
  bi-yawmi-l-qiyāma* (Q 75:1) — for He DID swear by it in *wa-hādhā al-balad al-amīn* (Q 95:3), so He would
  not negate an oath He elsewhere makes; with a poetic *shāhid* (*… wa-kāda ṣamīm al-qalb lā yataqaṭṭaʿu* =
  *yataqaṭṭaʿu*); (2) al-Akhfash also allows *lā* = *alā* (the attention-particle); (3) the Arab-idiom
  "*lā wa-llāhi lā faʿaltu*" — *lā* as a discourse-opener; (4) **a genuine negation** in context: "I do
  NOT swear by this City [now that] you are not in it, after your departure from it" (Makkī); (5)
  **Mujāhid (via Ibn Abī Najīḥ): *lā* is a *radd*** — a refutation of the deniers, then a fresh oath
  begins; this is **Ibn al-ʿArabī's choice**; (6) al-Qushayrī: *lā* refutes the self-deluded man's
  presumption (v 5). al-Qurṭubī adds al-Wāsiṭī's minority view that *al-balad* = **Madina** but concludes
  "**wa-l-awwal aṣaḥḥ li-anna al-sūra nazalat bi-Makka bi-l-ittifāq**" (Makka is correct).
- **v 10** *al-najdayn* = "al-ṭarīqayn: ṭarīq al-khayr wa-ṭarīq al-sharr… qawl Ibn ʿAbbās wa-Ibn Masʿūd";
  the **prophetic report** (via Qatāda): "yā ayyuhā al-nās, innamā humā al-najdān: najd al-khayr wa-najd
  al-sharr, fa-lima tujʿal najd al-sharr aḥabb ilayka min najd al-khayr." The minority **two-breasts**
  reading (ʿIkrima, Saʿīd b. al-Musayyab, al-Ḍaḥḥāk, from Ibn ʿAbbās/ʿAlī) — "they are like two roads to
  the child's life and sustenance." Etymology: *najd* = elevation (whence the region *Najd*); a shāhid
  from Imruʾ al-Qays.

## 5. Ibn Kathīr, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm* (on Q 90:1–2, 4, 8–10, 11–16)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/en-tafisr-ibn-kathir/90/{1,...}.json`.

- **vv 1–2** section-title: "Swearing by the Sanctity of Makkah and Other Things that Man was created in
  Hardship." "Here Allah has sworn by **Makkah, the Mother of the Towns**, addressing its resident
  (during the non-sacred months) free in this city." On *wa-anta ḥill*: Ibn ʿAbbās — "O Muḥammad, it is
  permissible for you to fight in it" (also Saʿīd b. Jubayr, ʿAṭiyya, al-Ḍaḥḥāk, Qatāda, al-Suddī, Ibn
  Zayd; al-Ḥasan: "for one hour of a day"). He grounds it in the **agreed-upon (muttafaq ʿalayh) hadith**:
  *"inna hādhā al-balad ḥarramahu Allāh yawma khalaqa al-samāwāt wa-l-arḍ, fa-huwa ḥarām bi-ḥurmat Allāh
  ilā yawm al-qiyāma, lā yuʿḍadu shajaruhu wa-lā yukhtalā khalāhu…"* — **verified: Bukhārī #1303/#1765,
  Muslim, Nasāʾī #2880, see `04-hadith-corpus.md`.**
- **v 4** *fī kabad*: "man was created in hardship" — Ibn ʿAbbās: "in toil and the hardship of creation."
- **vv 8–10**: the two eyes, tongue, two lips, and *al-najdayn* = "the two paths of good and evil" (Ibn
  Masʿūd, Ibn ʿAbbās, others).
- **vv 11–16** *al-ʿaqaba*: the deeds that "break through" the pass — freeing a slave, feeding on a day of
  hunger (the orphan-relative, the destitute) — read as the upward moral struggle (*iqtiḥām*).

## 6. Points of agreement / disagreement

| Question | al-Ṭabarī | al-Zamakhsharī | al-Rāzī | al-Qurṭubī | Ibn Kathīr |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| *al-balad* = ? | Makka | Makka | Makka (ijmāʿ) | **Makka** (vs. Wāsiṭī's Madina) | Makka (Umm al-Qurā) |
| jawāb al-qasam | **v 4 (explicit)** | v 4 | v 4 | v 4 (implicit) | v 4 |
| the *lā* | (negation context) | — | elided-*lā* parse | **six positions** | (swearing) |
| *wa-anta ḥill* | — | parenthesis; future/Conquest | **5 faces**; parenthesis | — | "free to fight" (Conquest) |
| *al-najdayn* | two ways (Ibn Masʿūd) | two ways / two breasts | two ways | **two ways (majority) / two breasts** | two ways |
| *fakk raqaba* anchor | **Abū Dharr "most-expensive slave"** | — | — | — | (manumission) |

**Synthesis.** The five agree the oath is on **Makka** and that **v 4 is the apodosis**. The two live
disagreements are the **opening *lā*** (al-Qurṭubī's six positions, with Ibn al-ʿArabī favouring *radd*)
and **al-najdayn** (two ways vs. two breasts). The empirically-relevant point is that al-Ṭabarī's *fakk
raqaba* gloss is anchored to a **verified ṣaḥīḥ hadith** (Abū Dharr, "*aghlāhā thamanan wa-anfasuhā ʿinda
ahlihā*"), and the Makka-sanctity frame to the **muttafaq-ʿalayh** Conquest hadith — both confirmed on
disk (`04-hadith-corpus.md`).

## 7. Honest limits

- al-Rāzī and al-Zamakhsharī are read from full OpenITI raw concatenations; the Q 90 blocks were located
  by `# {verse-text}` section headers (lines cited) and read in context.
- al-Suyūṭī's *al-Durr al-manthūr* and al-Ṭabarsī's *Majmaʿ al-bayān* on Q 90 are not surveyed in depth
  here; the five above already satisfy the ≥5-mufassirūn requirement with full passage citations.
- The *kabad* etymology (al-Zamakhsharī) and the *najdayn* glosses are philological/exegetical, not
  empirical; the project's hapax finding is indifferent to which gloss is adopted.

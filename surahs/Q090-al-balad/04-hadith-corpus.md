# Q 90 al-Balad — Hadith Corpus

All ḥadīth numbers below are `idInBook` values read directly from
`data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/{book}.json` and re-verified by Arabic
substring match. Q 90 has **no asbāb al-nuzūl** in the canonical sense and **no sound faḍāʾil**; its two
genuine hadith anchors are the two verses with direct prophetic glosses: v 1–2 (Makka's sanctity) and
v 13 (*fakk raqaba*).

## 1. Anchor A — Makka's sanctity (vv 1–2: *bi-hādhā al-balad* / *wa-anta ḥill*)

The mufassirūn (Ibn Kathīr, al-Zamakhsharī, al-Rāzī) all gloss vv 1–2 with the **muttafaq-ʿalayh**
Conquest-day declaration that Makka was made sacred "the day Allah created the heavens and the earth,"
and that it was made *ḥalāl* for the Prophet only for one hour on the day of the Conquest — the exegetical
key to *wa-anta ḥill bi-hādhā al-balad*. Verified attestations:

| Collection | # (idInBook) | Chapter | Narrator | Note |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī | **#1303** | Funerals / (ḥaram rulings) | Ibn ʿAbbās ← ʿIkrima ← Khālid | "ḥarrama Allāhu Makka… uḥillat lī sāʿatan min nahār, lā yukhtalā khalāhā wa-lā yuʿḍadu shajaruhā…" |
| Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī | **#1765** | Penalty of Hunting on Pilgrimage | Ibn ʿAbbās ← ʿIkrima ← Khālid | parallel wording ("inna Allāha ḥarrama Makka…") |
| Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī | **#112** | Knowledge | Abū Hurayra | the Conquest-day Khuzāʿa sermon (basis of the *uḥillat lī sāʿa* clause) |
| Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim | **#3182** | Ḥajj | Abū Shurayḥ al-ʿAdawī | "on the day following the Conquest… he praised Allah, then said: Allah made Makka sacred…" |
| Sunan al-Nasāʾī | **#2880** | Ḥajj | Ibn ʿAbbās ← Ṭāwūs ← Mujāhid ← Manṣūr | **"hādhā al-balad ḥarramahu Allāhu yawma khalaqa al-samāwāti wa-l-arḍ, fa-huwa ḥarām bi-ḥurmat Allāh ilā yawm al-qiyāma"** — the exact phrase Ibn Kathīr quotes on Q 90:1 |

**Reading.** This is the strongest Q 90 hadith correlate: the mufassirūn's reading of *wa-anta ḥill*
("you are made free/lawful-to in this City" → the one-hour licence at the Conquest) rests on this
exact ṣaḥīḥ family. The Ibn ʿAbbās ← ʿIkrima chain (Bukhārī #1303/#1765) is the same chain that glosses
*al-balad* = Makka in al-Ṭabarī.

## 2. Anchor B — *fakk raqaba* / "the most precious slave" (v 13)

al-Ṭabarī, glossing v 13 *fakk raqaba*, cites a prophetic report (via Qatāda ← Sālim b. Abī al-Jaʿd ←
Maʿdān b. Abī Ṭalḥa) that the best slave to free is *akthuruhā thamanan* (the most expensive). The
ṣaḥīḥ form of this is the **Abū Dharr** hadith, verified:

| Collection | # (idInBook) | Chapter | Narrator | Wording |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī | **#2418** | **Manumission of Slaves (Kitāb al-ʿItq)** | Abū Dharr ← Abū Murāwiḥ ← ʿUrwa | "ayyu al-riqāb afḍal? — *aghlāhā thamanan wa-anfasuhā ʿinda ahlihā*" |
| Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim | **#156** | Īmān | Abū Dharr ← Abū Murāwiḥ al-Laythī | "the slave whose emancipation is best — one valuable to his master, of high price" |
| Sunan Ibn Mājah | **#2259** | Manumission of Slaves | Abū Dharr ← Abū Murāwiḥ | "anfasuhā ʿinda ahlihā wa-aghlāhā thamanan" |

Plus the general manumission-merit hadith glossing the *fakk raqaba* reward:

| Collection | # | Chapter | Narrator | Wording |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī | **#6471** | Expiation for Unfulfilled Oaths | Abū Hurayra ← Saʿīd b. Marjāna | "man aʿtaqa raqabatan muslimatan, aʿtaqa Allāhu bi-kulli ʿuḍwin minhu ʿuḍwan minhu min al-nār" |

**Reading.** v 13 (*fakk raqaba* — "freeing a neck") is the only Quranic occurrence of the *fakk*-of-a-slave
phrasing, and al-Ṭabarī anchors it to exactly this Abū Dharr "most-precious slave" report. The chain
(Abū Murāwiḥ ← ʿUrwa) is identical across Bukhārī #2418, Muslim #156, and Ibn Mājah #2259 — a solid,
verified empirical correlate of the surah's social-ethics core.

## 3. Anchor C — *al-najdayn* "the two ways" (v 10)

al-Ṭabarī and al-Qurṭubī gloss *al-najdayn* (the two highways) as the way of good and the way of evil,
citing a prophetic report (via **Qatāda**, marfūʿ/mursal): "*yā ayyuhā al-nās, innamā humā al-najdān:
najd al-khayr wa-najd al-sharr, fa-lima tujʿalu najd al-sharr aḥabba ilayka min najd al-khayr.*"

**Verification status: NOT FOUND in the 9-book corpus on disk.** A normalized substring search for
`النجدين` / `نجد الخير` / `نجد الشر` across all nine collections (Bukhārī, Muslim, Tirmidhī, Abū Dāwūd,
Nasāʾī, Ibn Mājah, Mālik, Aḥmad, Dārimī) returns **zero** matches. This report is **tafsīr-transmitted**
(Qatāda mursal, in al-Ṭabarī/al-Qurṭubī), NOT one of the canonical-9 ḥadīth. Flagged accordingly — it is
cited here as an exegetical transmission only, not as a verified canonical ḥadīth.

## 4. The al-Balad faḍāʾil report — FABRICATED (honest flag)

al-Zamakhsharī closes his Q 90 commentary with: "*man qaraʾa lā uqsimu bi-hādhā al-balad aʿṭāhu Allāhu
al-amāna min ghaḍabihi yawma al-qiyāma*" ("whoever recites al-Balad, Allah grants him security from His
anger on the Day of Resurrection"). This is one verse of the long **Ubayy b. Kaʿb faḍāʾil al-suwar**
chain that al-Zamakhsharī (and al-Thaʿlabī, al-Wāḥidī) append surah-by-surah.

**Verification status: NOT in any of the 9 canonical books** (no faḍāʾil of al-Balad found in the corpus
on disk). The Ubayy faḍāʾil-al-suwar series is **judged fabricated (mawḍūʿ)** by ḥadīth critics —
classically by Ibn al-Jawzī (*al-Mawḍūʿāt*) and noted as such by al-Zamakhsharī's own commentators. It is
recorded here **only** to flag it as NON-authentic, per the project's anti-hallucination discipline; it
carries no evidentiary weight.

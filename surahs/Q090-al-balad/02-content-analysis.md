# Q 90 al-Balad — Content Analysis

cross-checked against Saheeh International (`data/translations/en.sahih.txt`, verse-offset 6023–6042).

## 1. Verse-by-verse (close reading)

### Block A — The oath (vv 1–4) | rhyme: -d

| v | Arabic (no-tashkeel) | gloss | w / L |
|:-:|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | لا أقسم بهذا البلد | *lā uqsimu* by this City (Makka) | 4 / 15 |
| 2 | وأنت حل بهذا البلد | and you [O Muḥammad] are *ḥill* (free / lawful-to) in this City | 4 / 15 |
| 3 | ووالد وما ولد | and [by] a begetter and what he begot | 3 / 11 |
| 4 | لقد خلقنا الإنسان في كبد | **la-qad** We created man *fī kabad* — in toil | 5 / 20 |

- **v 1** is the *qasam* (oath). The opening *lā* is the famous crux (negation vs. *zāʾida* emphatic;
  see `05-classical-claims-audit.md` Claim 1). The *muqsam bihi* (sworn object) is *hādhā al-balad* =
  Makka, by exegetical consensus (al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī "ajmaʿa al-mufassirūn").
- **v 2** *wa-anta ḥill* is grammatically a **parenthesis (*iʿtirāḍ*)** between the oath and *wa-wālid*
  (al-Zamakhsharī, al-Rāzī): it heightens Makka's honour ("even you, with your standing, are treated as
  fair-game here by its people") or foretells the Conquest ("you will be *ḥill* — free to act — in it").
- **v 3** *wa-wālid wa-mā walad* — a second sworn object (a begetter and offspring); the indefiniteness
  (*tankīr*) is for "wonder and praise" (al-Zamakhsharī). Note: `wld` recurs as both *wālid* and *walad*.
- **v 4** is the **jawāb al-qasam** (apodosis): *la-qad khalaqnā al-insān fī kabad*. al-Ṭabarī states
  explicitly "هذا هو جواب القسم"; al-Zamakhsharī, "alā tarā kayfa laqqā *lā uqsimu* bi-qawlihi *la-qad
  khalaqnā al-insān*." `kbd` (kabad) is a **corpus-hapax root** (Q090-F-01).

### Block B — The self-deluded man (vv 5–7) | rhyme: -ad / -d

| v | Arabic | gloss | w / L |
|:-:|:--|:--|:--|
| 5 | أيحسب أن لن يقدر عليه أحد | does he think none will ever have power over him? | 6 / 20 |
| 6 | يقول أهلكت مالا لبدا | he says: "I have destroyed [spent] wealth heaped-up" | 4 / 17 |
| 7 | أيحسب أن لم يره أحد | does he think no one has seen him? | 5 / 15 |

- The man (a Quraysh chief — al-Zamakhsharī names Abū al-Ashadd / al-Walīd b. al-Mughīra as candidates)
  boasts of wasteful expenditure (*māl lubad*, "heaped-up wealth") and presumes God neither has power over
  him nor witnesses him. The double rhetorical *a-yaḥsabu* frames human self-delusion against the
  faculties God will enumerate next.

### Block C — God's bestowed faculties (vv 8–10) | rhyme: dual -ayn

| v | Arabic | gloss | w / L |
|:-:|:--|:--|:--|
| 8 | ألم نجعل له عينين | did We not make for him two eyes? | 4 / 14 |
| 9 | ولسانا وشفتين | and a tongue and two lips? | 2 / 12 |
| 10 | وهديناه النجدين | and guided him to *al-najdayn* — the two highways? | 2 / 14 |

- A **dense run of three dual/anatomical nouns** in the rhyme position: *ʿaynayn, shafatayn, al-najdayn*.
  Two of the roots are **corpus-hapaxes**: `$fh` (*shafatayn*, the only attestation of "lip") and `njd`
  (*najdayn*, the only attestation of "highland/upland-path"). *al-najdayn* = "the two highways"
  (al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, al-Qurṭubī: the way of good and the way of evil — cross-referencing Q 76:3
  *innā hadaynāhu al-sabīl*) — a minority reading (ʿIkrima, al-Ḍaḥḥāk) takes it literally as "the two
  breasts." The faculties are the *evidence* of accountability the man (v 5–7) denies.

### Block D — The steep path (*al-ʿaqaba*) (vv 11–16) | rhyme: -aba / -ah

| v | Arabic | gloss | w / L |
|:-:|:--|:--|:--|
| 11 | فلا اقتحم العقبة | yet he has not assaulted *al-ʿaqaba* — the steep pass | 3 / 14 |
| 12 | وما أدراك ما العقبة | and what will make you know what *al-ʿaqaba* is? | 4 / 16 |
| 13 | فك رقبة | the freeing of a neck [a slave] | 2 / 6 |
| 14 | أو إطعام في يوم ذي مسغبة | or feeding, on a day of *masghaba* — severe hunger | 6 / 19 |
| 15 | يتيما ذا مقربة | an orphan of near kinship | 3 / 12 |
| 16 | أو مسكينا ذا متربة | or a destitute [one] in the dust [of misery] | 4 / 15 |

- **The social-ethics core.** *al-ʿaqaba* (the steep mountain pass) is the metaphor for the hard moral
  ascent. The *iqtiḥām* (storming/assaulting) of the pass is defined as concrete charity: *fakk raqaba*
  (manumission), feeding the orphan and the destitute on a *day of hunger*. `sgb` (*masghaba*) is a
  **corpus-hapax root**. The *fakk-raqaba* deed has a direct verified hadith correlate (the Abū Dharr
  "most-precious slave" report — see `04-hadith-corpus.md`).
- Note the **qirāʾāt split** at v 13–14 (al-Ṭabarī on 90:13): *fakku raqaba … aw iṭʿāmun* (Madina/Kūfa/Shām,
  noun-readings) vs. *fakka raqaba … aw aṭʿama* (Mecca/Baṣra, Kisāʾī — verb-readings). al-Ṭabarī: "both
  are well-known and either is correct."

### Block E — The two companies (vv 17–20) | rhyme: -ah

| v | Arabic | gloss | w / L |
|:-:|:--|:--|:--|
| 17 | ثم كان من الذين آمنوا وتواصوا بالصبر وتواصوا بالمرحمة | then he was of those who believed and counselled one another to patience and to mercy | 9 / 45 |
| 18 | أولئك أصحاب الميمنة | those are the companions of the right hand (*al-maymana*) | 3 / 17 |
| 19 | والذين كفروا بآياتنا هم أصحاب المشأمة | and those who disbelieved in Our signs are the companions of the left (*al-mashʾama*) | 6 / 32 |
| 20 | عليهم نار مؤصدة | over them is a Fire *mūṣada* — closed-over / vaulted | 3 / 13 |

- **v 17** (the surah's longest, 9 words) folds the social deeds back into *īmān* + the mutual counsel
  (*tawāṣī*) of patience and mercy — the same *tawāṣī* construction as Q 103 al-ʿAṣr (*tawāṣaw bi-l-ḥaqq
  wa-tawāṣaw bi-l-ṣabr*), Q 90's 2nd-nearest FR neighbor.
- **vv 18–19** the antithetical pair *aṣḥāb al-maymana* / *aṣḥāb al-mashʾama* (right/left, blessed/cursed).
- **v 20** seals with *nār mūṣada* (the closed-over Fire) — echoing Q 104:8 (*nār… mūṣada*), the only
  other corpus attestation of *mūṣada* (root `wSd`).

## 2. Major thematic blocks

| Block | Verses | Register |
|---|---|---|
| A. Oath on Makka + the begetter; jawāb: man in toil | 1–4 | oath / anthropological |
| B. The self-deluded boaster | 5–7 | polemical / eschatological |
| C. The bestowed faculties + the two ways | 8–10 | argument-from-design |
| D. The steep path of charity | 11–16 | social-ethical / legal-paraenetic |
| E. The two companies + the sealed Fire | 17–20 | eschatological-creedal |

**Content register:** mixed — an early-Meccan **oath-paraenesis** that fuses (i) accountability polemic
against the wealthy denier with (ii) a concrete **social-justice** program (manumission, feeding the
orphan/destitute), closing on the standard two-destinies eschatology.

## 3. Vocabulary distinctness

Q 90's root-distribution is distinctive in two ways:

1. **Four corpus-hapax roots** (`kbd, njd, $fh, sgb`) — concentrated in the concrete imagery (toil /
   the two highways / two lips / hunger). This drives the Q090-F-01 enrichment (4 vs null-expected 0.42).
   **But** this is a **short-Meccan-register** trait: the MW-6 control Q 91 al-Shams is equally enriched
   (4 hapaxes), so the hapax-density is a property of the early-Meccan concrete-oath block, not a Q 90
   singleton (see `06-novel-findings.md`).
2. **Content-centrality:** despite the rare roots, Q 90's *common* roots (Allāh-adjacent: *khalaqa, āmana,
   kafara, raʾā, qadara, hadā*) place it very close to the corpus centroid (FR-mean 0.837, z_content =
   −0.85; nearest neighbor Q 112). The rare-root surface sits on a creedally-central root core.

## 4. Repetition patterns (refrains, recurring phrases)

- *bi-hādhā al-balad* (vv 1, 2) — the City named twice in the oath.
- *al-ʿaqaba* (vv 11, 12) — the steep pass named, then asked-about (*wa-mā adrāka* device).
- *a-yaḥsabu an…* (vv 5, 7) — the doubled rhetorical question framing the denier.
- *wa-tawāṣaw bi-…* (v 17, twice) — the mutual-counsel doublet (patience / mercy).
- *aṣḥāb al-…* (vv 18, 19) — the antithetical company-labels (maymana / mashʾama).

## 5. Cross-surah content references

- **Q 76:3** (*innā hadaynāhu al-sabīl*) — al-Ṭabarī cross-cites for *al-najdayn* (v 10): the two ways
  of guidance.
- **Q 103 al-ʿAṣr** (Q 90's 2nd-nearest FR neighbor, 0.411) — the *tawāṣī bi-l-ṣabr* construction (v 17 ∥
  Q 103:3); both surahs pair faith with mutual counsel.
- **Q 104:8** (*ʿalayhim nār… mūṣada*) — the *mūṣada* (closed-over Fire) of v 20 occurs only here and in
  Q 104; root `wSd` is a corpus-2-attestation root (Q 90 + Q 104).
- **Q 112 al-Ikhlāṣ** (nearest FR neighbor, 0.395) — shared creedal-central root core (khalaqa/Allāh
  vocabulary), though Q 112 has no oath and no narrative.
- **Q 56 al-Wāqiʿa** — the *aṣḥāb al-maymana* / *aṣḥāb al-mashʾama* company-pair of vv 18–19 is the same
  classifier used at length in Q 56:8–9 (*aṣḥāb al-yamīn* / *aṣḥāb al-shimāl*).

## 6. Honest limits

- The "self-deluded man" identification (Abū al-Ashadd / al-Walīd b. al-Mughīra) is exegetical
  conjecture reported by al-Zamakhsharī, not an empirical claim.
- The *al-najdayn* = "two ways" vs. "two breasts" split is a genuine tafsīr disagreement; the empirical
  profile is indifferent to which gloss is correct (root `njd` is a hapax either way).
- Word/letter counts use the no-tashkeel orthographic-token convention; a lemma or surface-token count
  would differ slightly.

## 1. Why no basmala? — five classical positions

al-Qurṭubī, *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān*, opening of sūrat Barāʾa (`qurtubi-openiti-Q009.txt` lines 1-95), gives **five classical positions**:

### Position 1 (al-Qushayrī Abū Naṣr): treaty-revocation convention
Pre-Islamic Arabs convened that when a treaty was being broken, the renunciatory letter omitted the basmala. When Q 9 came as a *nabdh al-ʿahd* (treaty-renunciation) declaration to be proclaimed by ʿAlī at the Ḥajj, it was structured according to this convention.
> "كان من شأن العرب في زمانها في الجاهلية إذا كان بينهم وبين قوم عهد فإذا أرادوا نقضه كتبوا إليهم كتابا ولم يكتبوا فيه بسملة فلما نزلت سورة براءة بنقض العهد..." (al-Qurṭubī, lines 9-15)

### Position 2 (ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān, via Ibn ʿAbbās; al-Tirmidhī ḥadīth #3086)
The Companions did not know whether Q 9 was a continuation of Q 8 al-Anfāl or an independent surah; the doubt was resolved by NOT writing a basmala. al-Qurṭubī cites this via al-Nasāʾī (lines 18-32). Ibn Kathīr (lines 12-25) cites the same chain with full *isnād*: al-Tirmidhī said *ḥasan*; al-Ḥākim said *ṣaḥīḥ al-isnād*. al-Zamakhsharī, *al-Kashshāf* (lines 9-14) gives the same answer as the second of multiple positions.

### Position 3 (Imām Mālik via Ibn Wahb, Ibn al-Qāsim, Ibn ʿAbd al-Ḥakam)
The opening of Q 9 was originally as long as Q 2 al-Baqara, but parts were *unrecorded* (lost from active recitation but not from theological status); when the opening fell away, the basmala fell with it.
> "إنه لما سقط أولها سقط بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم معه" (al-Qurṭubī, line 32)
Ibn ʿAjlān's chain says the surah equalled al-Baqara in length. Saʿīd b. Jubayr makes the same claim (al-Qurṭubī line 36). **al-Rāzī rejects this position** explicitly: he says accepting it would compromise the *tawātur* (mass-transmission) of the Quranic text and is *bāṭil* (false) (`razi-openiti-Q009.txt` line 8276-8281).

### Position 4 (Khārija b. Zayd, Abū ʿIṣma)
The Companions disagreed whether Q 8 + Q 9 form one surah or two. To accommodate both views: a blank line was left between them (recognition of two surahs); but no basmala was written (recognition of unity for those who hold one-surah). al-Qurṭubī, lines 41-47.

### Position 5 (ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib via Ibn ʿAbbās; al-Mubarrad; Sufyān b. ʿUyayna)
The basmala = mercy-and-protection (*amān*); Q 9 is a sword/wrath proclamation; mercy-protection cannot precede an instrument of severance.
> "البسملة أمان وبراءة نزلت بالسيف ليس فيها أمان" (al-Qurṭubī, lines 50-52)
> al-Mubarrad: "البسملة رحمة وبراءة نزلت سخطة" (line 52)
Sufyān b. ʿUyayna: "the basmala is mercy and *amān*; this surah was revealed regarding the *munāfiqūn* and the sword, and there is no *amān* for the *munāfiqūn*" (al-Qurṭubī line 56).

al-Qurṭubī himself (line 60) sides with **Position 1** (Qushayrī): "the correct view is that the basmala was not written because Jibrīl did not bring it down within this surah." This is the *qarīna textile* (revelatory-evidence) position, distinct from the **theological** Position 5.

## 2. The barāʾa-proclamation: who proclaimed?

Ibn Kathīr (lines 29-90), al-Ṭabarī, al-Qurṭubī all converge on the foundational narrative:
- 9 AH, after Tabuk, the Prophet ﷺ sent **Abū Bakr** as *amīr al-ḥajj* (commander of the pilgrimage).
- Subsequently **ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib** was sent on the Prophet's own she-camel *al-ʿAḍbāʾ* to recite Q 9 to the people on the Day of Sacrifice (10 Dhū l-Ḥijja).
- The verbal-rubric ʿAlī proclaimed (al-Tirmidhī ḥadīth #3088, ḥasan, via Zayd b. Uthayʿ): four points — (i) no polytheist may circumambulate naked; (ii) no polytheist may perform Hajj after this year; (iii) only a believer enters Paradise; (iv) those with valid treaties have their treaties honoured to term.
- Special detail (al-Zamakhsharī lines 26-32, al-Qurṭubī line 81): Jibrīl came down to Abū Bakr en route saying "no one may convey on your behalf except a man from yourself" — hence the dispatch of ʿAlī.

This is the **barāʾa-by-ʿAlī** ḥadīth, foundational in Sunnī fadāʾil-ʿAlī literature and central in early Shīʿī polemics.

## 3. Q 9:128-129 as the last revealed verses

al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān*, nawʿ 8 ("معرفة آخر ما نزل" — knowledge of the last revealed), `suyuti-itqan-openiti-Q009.txt` from line ~1742:

The major chains:

### Ubayy b. Kaʿb tradition (al-Bukhārī)
> أحدث القرآن عهدا بالله هاتان الآيتان: {لقد جاءكم رسول من أنفسكم عزيز عليه ما عنتم} إلى آخر الآيتين
> "The most recent of the Quran with God are these two verses: 'There has come to you a Messenger from yourselves...' to the end of the two verses."

Multiply transmitted: Ubayy → Yūsuf b. Mihrān → ʿAlī b. Zayd → Shuʿba → various students. al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān* (lines 10970-11000) gives FOUR independent variant *isnāds* via Shuʿba.

### Saʿīd b. Jubayr concurs (al-Bukhārī, Ibn Kathīr line 4011)

### al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī (al-Rāzī line 8268)
> هاتان الآيتان آخر ما أنزل الله من القرآن، وما أنزل بعدهما قرآن
> "These two verses are the last God revealed of the Quran; nothing of the Quran was revealed after them."

### al-Ḥārith b. Khuzayma chain (al-Bukhārī ḥadīth #4986, recorded in Ibn Kathīr line 4027)
The last two verses of Barāʾa were found ONLY with al-Ḥārith b. Khuzayma when Zayd b. Thābit was compiling the muṣḥaf during the caliphate of Abū Bakr — confirming both their late-revelation status AND a specific manuscript-recovery story.

### The competing claims (al-Suyūṭī harmonizes, *Itqān* nawʿ 8 line 1742)

- **Q 4:176** (*kalāla* / inheritance): al-Barāʾ b. ʿĀzib via al-Bukhārī.
- **Q 2:281** (*āyat al-ribā*): Ibn ʿAbbās; ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb (Aḥmad, Ibn Mājah).
- **Q 5:3** (*al-yawm akmaltu lakum dīnakum*): minority position, ʿUmar at *Ḥajjat al-wadāʿ*.

al-Bayhaqī's reconciliation (cited by al-Suyūṭī, *Itqān* line 1800): "These [conflicting] reports — if they are sound — should be reconciled by understanding that each Companion answered with what reached him."

## 4. Hypocrite-typology and the al-Faḍiḥa naming

**Saʿīd b. Jubayr → Ibn ʿAbbās tradition** (multiply-attested, al-Bukhārī, al-Suyūṭī *Itqān* nawʿ 9 line 3392, al-Qurṭubī line 6):
> قلت لابن عباس: سورة التوبة. قال: التوبة، بل هي الفاضحة، ما زالت تنزل: «ومنهم… ومنهم…» حتى ظننا أنه لا يبقى أحد إلا ذكر فيها
> "I asked Ibn ʿAbbās: 'Sūrat al-Tawba?' He said: 'al-Tawba? Rather, it is *al-Faḍiḥa*, the Exposer; verses kept being revealed «and among them … and among them …» until we feared no one would remain unmentioned in it.'"

**ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb-paraphrase** (Abū al-Shaykh via ʿIkrima, al-Suyūṭī *Itqān* line 3393):
> ما فرغ من تنزيل براءة حتى ظننا أنه لا يبقى منا أحد إلا سينزل فيه
> "Barāʾa's revelation did not finish until we feared none of us would remain unmentioned in it."

**al-Zamakhsharī** (`zamakhshari-openiti-Q009.txt` lines 2-7) catalogues 11 names of the surah:
> براءة، التوبة، المقشقشة، المبعثرة، المشردة، المخزية، الفاضحة، المثيرة، الحافرة، المنكلة، المدمدمة، سورة العذاب
("Disavowal, Repentance, the Scrubber, the Strewer, the Disperser, the Disgracer, the Exposer, the Stirrer-up, the Excavator, the Punisher, the Smother, the Surah of Torment")

Each name is a verbal pattern from a root attested heavily within Q 9 (q-š-š scrubbing, b-ʿ-th-r strewing, sh-r-d dispersing, kh-z-y disgracing, f-ḍ-ḥ exposing, etc.).

### Empirical correlate

[[Q009-F-02-hypocrite-density]] **VINDICATES the al-Faḍiḥa naming**: Q 9 ranks **5/114** in QAC root *n-f-q* density (7.85/1k tokens vs. corpus mean 1.02). The differential nfq-rank vs. kfr-rank = −12 — Q 9 is *more distinctively* a hypocrisy-surah than a disbelief-surah. Saʿīd b. Jubayr's classical observation is now law-strength.

## 5. The Q 9-Q 8 unity question (Ibn ʿAbbās)

al-Zamakhsharī (`zamakhshari-openiti-Q009.txt` lines 22-30):
> وقيل: سورة الأنفال والتوبة سورة واحدة، كلتاهما نزلت في القتال، تعدان السابعة من الطول... فقال بعضهم: الأنفال وبراءة سورة واحدة. وقال بعضهم: هما سورتان

al-Ṭabarsī (Imāmī Shīʿī, *Majmaʿ al-bayān*, `tabarsi-openiti-Q009.txt` lines 3-12) cites the same: Anfāl + Tawba together fill out al-sabʿ al-ṭiwāl (the seven longest surahs) as a unit; alone neither is long enough.

## 6. Qurʾānic *munāsaba* (intra-text coherence)

al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-durar* (`biqai-openiti-Q009.txt` lines 1-90), characterises Q 9's connection to Q 8 al-Anfāl through a *negative-correlate* relationship:

> مقصودها معاداة من أعرض عما دعت إليه السورة الماضية من اتباع الداعي إلى الله في توحيده وأتباع ما يرضيه...

"Its [Q 9's] purpose is the *enmity-against* those who turned away from what the previous surah [Q 8] had called them to: following the caller to God in His unicity and following what pleases Him..."

al-Biqāʿī also discusses the *al-Mubarriʾa* naming: "the surah that *cures* (*tabarriʾa*, like *al-qash* curing wounds by scab-removal) hypocrisy." This is the etymological-rhetorical interpretation.

al-Rāzī (`razi-openiti-Q009.txt` line 1406) on Q 9's *munāsaba*:
> هذه السورة من آخر ما نزل من القرآن
"This surah is among the latest of the Quran" — and uses this to argue against Position 3 (lost-opening): a late-revealed surah cannot have its opening lost since the redactors heard it directly.

## 7. al-Ṭabarī on the legal-historical setting

al-Ṭabarī (*Jāmiʿ al-bayān*, lines 167490-): the surah was revealed in stages, with vv. 1-37 revealed last (just before the *ḥajj* of year 9), vv. 38-72 around the *Tabuk* preparations (Rajab year 9), vv. 73-99 during the expedition itself, and vv. 100-129 in subsequent months — culminating in vv. 128-129 as the absolute last.

al-Ṭabarī weights the *kalāla* (Q 4:176) as last-legal-verse, but the *last-sūra* identification with *Barāʾa* is consistent.

## 8. al-Rāzī's epistemic caution

al-Rāzī uniquely critiques **Ḥudhayfa b. al-Yamān**'s tradition (`razi-openiti-Q009.txt` lines 8275-8282):
> أنتم تسمون هذه السورة بالتوبة، وهي سورة العذاب ما تركتم أحدا إلا نالت منه، والله ما تقرءون ربعها
> "You call this surah *al-Tawba*; it is the *Sūrat al-ʿAdhāb* [the Surah of Punishment] — it touched everyone — by God, you do not [now] recite a quarter of it."

al-Rāzī rejects: "this report MUST be considered false, because if we permitted it, this would be evidence of the entry of *ziyāda* and *nuqṣān* (addition and subtraction) into the Quran, and that would invalidate its evidentiary status."

al-Rāzī's epistemic stance: even sound-chain reports that compromise the *tawātur* of the Quran's text must be rejected. This is a **methodological keystone** for Sunnī Quran-redaction theory.

## 9. al-Suyūṭī's *Durr al-manthūr* — hadith-tafsīr corpus

al-Suyūṭī, *al-Durr al-manthūr* (`suyuti-durr-openiti-Q009.txt`), is the *tafsīr bi-l-maʾthūr* — a compilation of all hadith and athār (Companion-reports) bearing on each verse. For Q 9:128-129 alone (lines 6137-6160), al-Suyūṭī collects:
- Ubayy b. Kaʿb chain (4× variants, via Yūsuf b. Mihrān, ʿAlī b. Zayd, Abū al-ʿĀliya).
- Ibn ʿAbbās chain (2× via Yūsuf b. Mihrān).
- Saʿīd b. Jubayr's confirmation.
- al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī's confirmation.
- The Abū Bakr-redaction story with al-Ḥārith b. Khuzayma.

This convergent attestation is the strongest single evidence for Q 9:128-129 = last-revealed.

## 10. Methodological summary

The 8 mufassirūn surveyed agree on:
- Q 9 is Medinan, late, Tabuk-era.
- ʿAlī recited it on the day of Sacrifice in year 9.
- Q 9:128-129 are among the LAST verses revealed (with rival claimants Q 4:176 and Q 2:281 each generating their own legal-verse traditions).
- Q 9 is a *hypocrite-exposure* surah; its multiple names reflect this.

They DISAGREE on:
- **Why no basmala** — five distinct classical positions (Qurṭubī catalogue).
- **Whether Q 8 + Q 9 = one surah** (Ibn ʿAbbās yes; majority no).
- **Whether the surah's opening was truncated** (Mālik/Saʿīd b. Jubayr yes; al-Rāzī no, on tawātur grounds).

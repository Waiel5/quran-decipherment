## 1. Foundational redaction ḥadīth: ʿUthmān on no-basmala

### **Jāmiʿ al-Tirmidhī, ḥadīth #3170** (al-Tirmidhī's *Tafsīr* book)

Narrated Ibn ʿAbbās: "I asked ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān: 'What was your reasoning with al-Anfāl — while it is from the *mathānī* (surah with less than 100 verses), and Barāʾa while it is from the *miʾīn* (surah with about 100 verses) — then you put them together, without writing the line *bismillāhi r-raḥmāni r-raḥīm* between them, and you placed them with the seven long (surahs) — why did you do that?' So ʿUthmān said: «*The Messenger of Allah ﷺ used to receive surahs of many verses; whenever something was revealed to him, he would call those who used to write for him and say: «Place these verses in the surah in which such-and-such is mentioned.» Now al-Anfāl was among the first revealed in Medina; and Barāʾa was among the LAST of the Quran. Their stories were similar, and I thought it was part of it. The Messenger of Allah ﷺ passed away without having clarified for us whether it was part of it. So I joined them, and I did not write the line *bismillāhi r-raḥmāni r-raḥīm* between them, and I placed them among the seven long.*»"

al-Tirmidhī's grading: **ḥasan**.
This is the classical Sunnī authoritative answer to "why no basmala in Q 9?" The Quran-text-redaction question — whether Q 9 is part of Q 8 or separate — was unresolved at the Prophet's death; ʿUthmān's editorial decision was to NOT write the basmala, accommodating both views.

Parallel chains: **Sunan Abū Dāwūd #786**, **Musnad Aḥmad** (multiple), **Sunan al-Nasāʾī #8005-8007**, **al-Ḥākim *Mustadrak*** (declared *ṣaḥīḥ al-isnād*).

### Empirical correlate

[[h-new-890-numerical-reaudit]] T1: Q 8-Q 9 FR-distance ranks 81/113 (more dissimilar than typical adjacent pairs). The redactional doubt was historically real, but the modern FR analysis decisively places Q 8 and Q 9 as a **costly canonical pairing**, not a unit-doubled. The mushaf's decision (no basmala, separated by a blank line) is itself a rules-tuple-stable architectural marker.

## 2. The *barāʾa-by-ʿAlī* proclamation

### **Sahih al-Bukhārī, ḥadīth #4655** (in Bukhārī's *Kitāb al-Tafsīr*, sūrat al-Tawba)

Narrated Abū Hurayra: "Abū Bakr sent me in the company of those announcers during *Ḥajjat al-Wadāʿ* in Minā: 'No mushrik shall perform Hajj after this year, and no naked person shall circumambulate the Kaʿba.'"

The *bāb*-name in al-Bukhārī's Tafsīr-Bara'a chapter is itself titled "*Wa-adhānun mina llāhi wa-rasūlihi…*" (Q 9:3), confirming this hadith is the canonical asbāb-al-nuzūl source.

### **Sahih al-Bukhārī, ḥadīth #4656** & parallel chains

The fuller form: Abū Bakr was *amīr al-ḥajj*; ʿAlī was sent *afterward* with the verses, riding the Prophet's camel *al-ʿAḍbāʾ*. ʿAlī recited "thirty or forty verses" (per Mujāhid via al-Tirmidhī), or "thirteen verses" (per al-Zamakhsharī's count). al-Tirmidhī ḥadīth **#3088** (Zayd b. Uthayʿ → ʿAlī, *ḥasan*) summarises ʿAlī's four-point proclamation: (i) only believers enter Paradise, (ii) no nude tawāf, (iii) no Muslim/mushrik joint-ḥajj after this year, (iv) treaties valid to term, no-treaty = 4 months grace.

### Empirical correlate

The opening of Q 9 (vv. 1-37) is the textual instantiation of this *barāʾa* proclamation. The four-point proclamation maps onto:
- (i) Q 9:113 — *no istighfār for polytheists*
- (ii) Q 9:28 — *innamā l-mushrikūn najas* — *fa-lā yaqrabū l-Masjid al-Ḥarām*
- (iii) Q 9:5 — four-month grace
- (iv) Q 9:4 — *fa-atimmū ilayhim ʿahdahum*

## 3. Tabuk-expedition narratives — Q 9's setting

### **Sahih al-Bukhārī, ḥadīth #4222** — ʿAlī as Medina-deputy
Narrated Saʿd: "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ set out for Tabuk, appointing ʿAlī as his deputy (in Medina). ʿAlī said: 'Do you want to leave me with the children and women?' The Prophet ﷺ said: '*Will you not be pleased that you will be to me like Aaron to Moses?* But there will be no prophet after me.'"

This is the famous *ḥadīth al-manzila* (rank-hadith) — a foundational Sunnī fadl-ʿAlī text and a major Imāmī Shīʿī proof-text. Both interpretive traditions originate from this Tabuk-departure scene.

### **Sahih al-Bukhārī, ḥadīth #4221** — Abū Mūsā at *Jaysh al-ʿUsra*
"My Companions sent me to Allah's Messenger ﷺ to ask him for some animals to ride on as they were accompanying him in the army of *al-ʿUsra*, and that was the Ghazwa of Tabuk..."  
*Jaysh al-ʿUsra* (the Army of Hardship) = Tabuk; mentioned in Q 9:117 as *sāʿat al-ʿusra*.

### **Sahih al-Bukhārī, ḥadīth #4223** — Yaʿlā b. Umayya
Yaʿlā: "I participated in *al-ʿUsra* (Tabuk) along with the Prophet... that Ghazwa was the best of my deeds to me."

### **Sahih Muslim, ḥadīth #45-46** (auto-index hits) — Tabuk provisioning
Multiple narratives of the food-provisioning miracle and joining of dhuhr/ʿaṣr prayers en route.

### **Muwaṭṭāʾ Mālik, kitāb #9, ḥadīth #329** — Joining prayers at Tabuk
"The Prophet joined ẓuhr and ʿaṣr on his journey to Tabuk." Foundational for the fiqh of *jamʿ taqdīm/taʾkhīr* (combining prayers in travel).

### **Sahih Muslim, ḥadīth #6843** (Kitāb al-Tawba) — Kaʿb b. Mālik's narrative

Narrated Kaʿb b. Mālik: "I never remained behind Allah's Messenger ﷺ from any expedition which he undertook except the Battle of Tabuk and that of Badr..." (long narrative) ... ending with the divine verse — Q 9:118 — *wa-ʿalā ath-thalāthati lladhīna khullifū…* The 50-day social boycott; the moment of *kāda yazīghu qulūb farīq minhum* (Q 9:117); Kaʿb's truth-telling vow.

### **Sahih al-Bukhārī, ḥadīth #4224** (in Bukhārī's *Kitāb al-Maghāzī*)

Narrated ʿAbd Allāh b. Kaʿb b. Mālik: "[from his father Kaʿb...] I did not remain behind Allah's Messenger ﷺ in any Ghazwa that he fought except the Ghazwa of Tabuk... [continues 4-page narrative] ... the Prophet ﷺ delayed our case for 50 days, and the people stopped speaking to us. Then this verse was revealed: 'And [He also turned in mercy] upon the three who stayed behind, until when the earth, with its vastness, was straitened upon them...' (Q 9:118)."

This is the foundational asbāb-al-nuzūl for Q 9:117-118. Kaʿb b. Mālik names the other two: **Hilāl b. Umayya** and **Murāra b. al-Rabīʿ** (Bukhārī #4224, line ~30 of the long narrative, and explicit in Muslim parallel #6843).

### **Sahih al-Bukhārī, ḥadīth #6446** — Kaʿb's wealth-renunciation
"In the last part of his narration about the three who remained behind (from Tabuk): 'As a proof of my true repentance (for not joining the Holy battle of Tabuk), I shall give up all my property for the sake of Allah and His Apostle (as an expiation for that sin).' The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Keep some of your wealth, for that is better for you.'"

Foundational on the fiqh-question of total vs. partial renunciation as *kaffāra* (atonement).

## 4. al-Faḍiḥa naming — al-Bukhārī foundational

### **Sahih al-Bukhārī, ḥadīth #4674** (Kitāb al-Tafsīr, sūrat al-Tawba)

Narrated Saʿīd b. Jubayr: "I asked Ibn ʿAbbās about *Sūrat al-Tawba*, and he said: '*Sūrat al-Tawba?* It is exposure (of all the evils of the infidels and the hypocrites). And it continued revealing (the oft-repeated expression): «and of them … and of them» until they started thinking that none would be left unmentioned in it.'"

## 5. The Ibn ʿUbayy funeral and Q 9:80, 84

### **Sahih al-Bukhārī, ḥadīth #1318, #4670** (Tabuk-context)

Narrated ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb: "When ʿAbd Allāh b. Ubayy died, Allah's Messenger ﷺ was called upon to offer his funeral prayer. When Allah's Messenger ﷺ stood up to offer the prayer, I got up quickly and said: 'O Allah's Messenger! Are you going to pray for Ibn Ubayy, the hypocrite who said this and that?'... " — ending with the revelation of Q 9:80 and Q 9:84 forbidding *istighfār* and funeral prayer for hypocrites.

### **Sahih al-Bukhārī, ḥadīth #4464** (parallel, ʿAbd Allāh's son requests the Prophet's shirt for shrouding)

The shirt-shroud parallel narrative; same Q 9:80, 84 occasion.

### Asbāb impact

al-Suyūṭī (*Lubāb al-nuqūl*, supplemented by *al-Durr al-manthūr*) and Ibn Kathīr both treat these as the asbāb-al-nuzūl for Q 9:80 and Q 9:84. The Q 9:80 *seventy-times* hyperbole became foundational in Sunnī-Shīʿī polemics over *istighfār* limits.

## 6. Q 9:31 — *aḥbārahum wa-ruhbānahum arbāban*

### **Jāmiʿ al-Tirmidhī, ḥadīth #3095** (al-Tirmidhī, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān*, *bāb sūrat al-Tawba*)

Narrated ʿAdī b. Ḥātim: "I came to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ with a cross of gold around my neck, and he ﷺ said: 'O ʿAdī! Throw away that idol!' Then I heard him reciting Q 9:31. I said: 'O Messenger of Allah, but they did not worship them.' He ﷺ said: 'Yes — they made for them what was lawful unlawful and what was unlawful lawful, and they (the people) followed them, and that is their worship of them.'"

al-Tirmidhī: ***ḥasan gharīb***.

This ḥadīth is the foundational *tafsīr* of Q 9:31 across Sunnī-Shīʿī traditions: "*ittakhadhū aḥbārahum wa-ruhbānahum arbāb*" is interpreted as *taqlīd-fī-taḥlīl-wa-taḥrīm* (following them in declaring lawful/unlawful), not literal worship.

## 7. The Q 9:128-129 last-revealed traditions

### **Sahih al-Bukhārī, ḥadīth #4986** (Kitāb al-Faḍāʾil, Faḍāʾil Qurʾān)

Narrated Zayd b. Thābit: "Abū Bakr sent for me when the people of Yamāma had been killed (i.e. a number of the Prophet's Companions who fought against Musaylima). I went to him and found ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb sitting with him... [continues describing the compilation of the Quran]... Until I found the last verses of *Sūrat al-Tawba* (Repentance) **only with Abū Khuzayma al-Anṣārī** (in some versions: al-Ḥārith b. Khuzayma) and not with anyone else: «*Laqad jā'akum rasūlun min anfusikum…*» till the end of *Barāʾa*."

This is the redactional-canonical proof: Q 9:128-129 was so recently revealed that only ONE Companion had memorized it when Abū Bakr's compilation began.

### **Musnad Aḥmad and al-Ṭabarī ḥadīth chains** (for Ubayy b. Kaʿb)

al-Ṭabarī's *Jāmiʿ al-bayān* (lines 10970-11000) preserves FOUR independent chains via Shuʿba → ʿAlī b. Zayd → Yūsuf b. Mihrān → Ibn ʿAbbās → Ubayy b. Kaʿb. Ubayy's testimony: "**The most recent of the Quran with God are these two verses: Q 9:128-129.**"

### Empirical correlate

[[Q009-F-04-last-revealed]]: across 10 OpenITI tafsirs, Q 9:128-129 is cited 64× in last-revealed contexts vs. Q 2:281 (61×), Q 4:176 (49×), Q 5:3 (9×). al-Bayhaqī's harmonization (al-Suyūṭī *Itqān* nawʿ 8) — that each Companion answered with what reached him — is empirically the most defensible reading: all three verses (Q 9:128-129, Q 2:281, Q 4:176) are genuinely late-revealed; the data does not establish a single winner.

## 9. Q 9:5 — the so-called *āyat al-sayf*

The classical reception of Q 9:5 is governed by hadith principles of *takhṣīṣ* (specification) and *naskh* (abrogation). Key ḥadīths:

### **Sahih al-Bukhārī, ḥadīth #25**

"I have been ordered to fight the people until they testify that *lā ilāha illā llāh* and that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah, and they establish *ṣalāt* and pay *zakāt*..."

This *ḥadīth* is consistent with Q 9:5 (combat continuing till conversion) but qualified by the asylum verse Q 9:6 (*wa-in aḥadun mina l-mushrikīna stajāraka...*).

### **Sahih al-Bukhārī, ḥadīth #1399** — Abū Bakr's caliphate-opening interpretation
Abū Bakr: "By Allah, I will fight whoever distinguishes between *ṣalāt* and *zakāt*..." — applying Q 9:5's principle to the *ridda* wars. Foundational on the question of "do *ridda*-resisters fall under Q 9:5 vs. Q 4:88?"

## 10. Q 9:108 — the *masjid al-Taqwā* (Qubāʾ)

### **Sahih Muslim, ḥadīth #1399** (a different #) — the *masjid* founded on *taqwā*

Q 9:108 — *la-masjidun ussisa ʿalā t-taqwā min awwali yawmin aḥaqqu an taqūma fīh* — "A mosque founded on righteousness from the first day is more deserving that you stand in it." 

Parallel ḥadīth in Sunan al-Nasāʾī, **Aḥmad #15700** (auto-index): the mosque referred to is identified as either **Qubāʾ** (al-Wāqidī, al-Ṭabarī) or **the Prophet's Mosque in Medina** (Ibn ʿUmar's narration). al-Bukhārī ḥadīth #483-484 establishes the Prophet's regular Saturday-foot-visits to Qubāʾ as a sunnah-act — implicitly identifying it.

## 11. Q 9:111 — the *bayʿat al-jihād* (the trade)

The transactional verse "*inna llāha shtarā mina l-mu'minīna anfusahum…*" is referenced in:
- **Sahih al-Bukhārī, ḥadīth #4079** (Kitāb al-Maghāzī) — Bayʿa al-ʿAqaba narrative.
- **Jāmiʿ al-Tirmidhī, ḥadīth #3091** — directly tafsīr Q 9:111: "The Prophet ﷺ said: 'There are gates of Paradise, none enters them except those who fight in God's path... Allah is more pleased with such a person than with all the people of the earth.'" (paraphrased)

Foundational for the entire *jihād* genre of fiqh + the *fadl al-shuhadāʾ* genre.

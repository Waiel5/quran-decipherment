## 5. Q 9's role in the dual-iʿjāz typology

Per [[cross-finding-026-iʿjāz-architecture]]:

- **Structural-iʿjāz** (al-Bāqillānī *iʿjāz al-fawāṣil*): high UAS, fāṣila-virtuoso. Q 9 SCORES HIGH on UAS (rank 4) but LOW on sig_A (rank 107). Q 9 is structurally significant by **content**, not by *fāṣila*.
- **Theological-iʿjāz** (al-Khaṭṭābī *iʿjāz al-maʿnā*): low UAS but high *thuluth al-Qurʾān* status. Q 9 SCORES HIGH on UAS — NOT a *iʿjāz al-maʿnā* surah by hadith-fadāʾil.

Q 9 occupies a **third quadrant** in the dual-iʿjāz typology: HIGH-UAS-LOW-sig_A. This is the **content-driven structural distinctness** quadrant — shared with Q 17, Q 18, Q 33, Q 48, Q 54.

This third quadrant is, classically, the **al-Suyūṭī iʿjāz al-bayān-by-tasallul** type — where the *flow-of-content* (e.g., the systematic *wa-minhum* enumeration in Q 9, the long Joseph-narrative-arc in Q 12, the historical-typology in Q 33) IS the rhetorical signature, distinct from the *fāṣila*-cadence of mufaṣṣal surahs.

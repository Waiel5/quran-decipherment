# Q 9 al-Tawba — Verse-by-Verse Content Analysis

**Source text**: `quran-text/quran-no-tashkeel.json` (Hafs-Kufan, no-tashkeel). All Arabic quotes verified against this file.

**Translation gloss**: `data/translations/en.sahih.txt` (Sahih International English) for verse paraphrase only — NEVER as primary text.

## Macro-arc

| Macro-block | Verses | Theme |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **A. Barāʾa proclamation + ḥajj reform** | 1–37 | Repudiation of treaties with mushrikūn; four-month grace; ḥajj rules |
| **B. Tabuk + hypocrite-typology** | 38–99 | Tabuk expedition narrative interleaved with extended hypocrite-exposure (al-Faḍiḥa portion) |
| **C. Repentance + closing** | 100–129 | Three-who-stayed-behind, *masjid al-ḍirār*, *bayʿat al-mujāhidīn*, Prophet's mercy |

The block boundaries are NOT sharp; vv. 38-99 is the longest and most interleaved, alternating *ʿadāwa* (hostility) and *ḥarakāt al-munāfiqīn* (movements of the hypocrites) with refrains.

## Block 1 (vv. 1-7) — The opening barāʾa proclamation

**v.1** — *Barāʾatun mina llāhi wa-rasūlihi ilā lladhīna ʿāhadtum mina l-mushrikīn* — "A disavowal from God and His Messenger to those of the polytheists with whom you had a treaty." Direct, stark; no formula.

**v.2** — *Fa-sīḥū fī l-arḍ arbaʿata ashhur* — "So travel about the land for four months." Four-month grace period.

**v.3** — *Wa-adhān mina llāhi wa-rasūlihi ilā n-nāsi yawma l-ḥajji l-akbar* — "And an announcement from God and His Messenger to the people on the day of the Greater Pilgrimage..." → the *ʿAlī*-proclamation verse (al-Bukhārī ḥadīth #4655 chain via Abū Hurayra, al-Tirmidhī #3088).

**v.4** — Exception: those who fully kept treaty terms — *fa-atimmū ilayhim ʿahdahum ilā muddatihim* — "complete their covenant with them until their term."

**v.5** — *fa-iqtulū l-mushrikīna ḥaythu wajadtumūhum* — the so-called *āyat al-sayf* ("verse of the sword"). Verse 5 has been heavily debated; classical position is that it is *muqayyada* (qualified) by v.4 (existing treaty-keepers) and v.6 (asylum for any seeking it).

**v.6** — *Wa-in aḥadun mina l-mushrikīna stajāraka fa-ajirhu* — "And if any one of the polytheists seeks your protection, grant him protection so that he may hear the word of God." The asylum verse — directly counter-balances v.5.

**v.7** — Rhetorical: *Kayfa yakūnu li-l-mushrikīna ʿahdun ʿinda llāhi…* — "How can there be a covenant with the polytheists with God…?"

**Refrain marker**: `kayfa` (how) — this is the first of three rhetorical *kayfa*-pivots in vv. 7-8 setting up the legal-covenant case.

## Block 2 (vv. 8-29) — Treaty law + holy-mosque rules + jizya verse

**vv. 8-12** — On treaty-breakers: *yarḍūnakum bi-afwāhihim wa-taʾbā qulūbuhum* (v.8 — "they please you with their mouths but their hearts refuse") — distinctive Q 9 motif of mouth-vs-heart hypocrisy.

**vv. 13-15** — Direct call to fight: *a-lā tuqātilūna qawman nakathū aymānahum* — "Will you not fight a people who broke their oaths?"

**vv. 16-18** — Test of believers; the rule that *masājid Allāh* are tended only by believers (v.18: *innamā yaʿmuru masājida llāhi man āmana bi-llāhi*) — basis of classical fiqh on whether non-Muslims may enter a mosque.

**vv. 19-22** — *aja'altum siqāyata l-ḥājji…* — the Companions' debate (al-ʿAbbās's water-sharing ministry vs. early-Islam jihād) is ranked: the *al-sābiqūna l-awwalūn* are pre-eminent. al-Suyūṭī *Durr al-manthūr* attaches asbāb-al-nuzūl from al-Nuʿmān b. Bashīr.

**vv. 23-24** — Family vs. faith: *lā tattakhidhū ābāʾakum wa-ikhwānakum awliyāʾa* — "Do not take your fathers and brothers as allies if they prefer disbelief over faith."

**v. 25** — *Yawm Ḥunayn* — the Battle of Ḥunayn (8 AH) — the only Quranic naming of a specific Companion-era battle. Recall reversal-after-flight at Ḥunayn (vv. 25-26).

**v. 28** — *innama l-mushrikūna najas* — "Indeed the polytheists are *najas* (ritually unclean)" → the rule that polytheists not approach *al-Masjid al-Ḥarām* — basis of the classical Ḥaram-prohibition.

**v. 29** — **al-Jizya verse**: *qātilū lladhīna lā yu'minūna bi-llāhi… ḥattā yuʿṭū l-jizyata ʿan yad-in wa-hum ṣāghirūn* — "Fight those who do not believe in God… until they pay the *jizya* (tribute) willingly while they are humbled."  The single most-cited Q 9 verse in classical fiqh on dhimmī-status.

## Block 3 (vv. 30-37) — Anti-trinitarian / anti-Jewish polemic + sacred months

**v. 30** — *Wa-qālati l-yahūdu ʿuzayrun bnu llāhi wa-qālati n-naṣārā l-masīḥu bnu llāhi* — explicit naming of the two People-of-the-Book groups for over-deification (Q 9 is one of only three places in the Quran to name them together; cf. Q 5).

**v. 31** — *ittakhadhū aḥbārahum wa-ruhbānahum arbāban min dūni llāh* — "they took their rabbis and monks as lords besides God." The famous ḥadīth (al-Tirmidhī #3095, ḥasan): the Prophet ﷺ explained to ʿAdī b. Ḥātim that this means following them in declaring lawful what God forbade. (See `04-hadith-corpus.md`.)

**v. 32** — *yurīdūna an yuṭfi'ū nūra llāhi bi-afwāhihim wa-yaʾba llāhu illā an yutimma nūrahu* — "they wish to extinguish the light of God with their mouths but God refuses except to perfect His light" — paired anaphora with v. 33 (*huwa lladhī arsala rasūlahu bi-l-hudā wa-dīni l-ḥaqqi li-yuẓhirahu ʿalā d-dīni kullihi*) — the *iẓhār al-dīn* (manifestation of religion over all religions) verse.

**v. 34-35** — Warning to those who hoard gold and silver — *fa-bashshirhum bi-ʿadhābin alīm yawma yuḥmā ʿalayhā fī nāri jahannam* — graphic eschatological imagery.

**v. 36** — **Sacred-months verse**: *innaʿiddata sh-shuhūri ʿinda llāhi thnā ʿashara shahran fī kitābi llāhi yawma khalaqa s-samāwāti wa-l-arḍa minhā arbaʿatun ḥurum* — "Indeed, the number of months in the sight of God is twelve, of which four are sacred." Foundational for the Islamic lunar calendar.

**v. 37** — *Innamā n-nasīʾu ziyādatun fī l-kufr* — the prohibition of *nasīʾ* (intercalation, the pre-Islamic month-shifting practice).

**Block boundary**: After v. 37, the surah pivots from doctrinal/legal-foundational to **narrative**. This is the strongest internal seam in Q 9.

## Block 4 (vv. 38-72) — Tabuk expedition narrative + extended hypocrite typology

**v. 38** — *yā ayyuhā lladhīna āmanū mā lakum idhā qīla lakumu nfirū fī sabīli llāhi thāqaltum ilā l-arḍ* — "What is wrong with you that when you are told 'go forth in the path of God,' you sink down toward the earth?" — the rebuke for sluggish mobilisation. Per al-Suyūṭī *Itqān* nawʿ 8 quoting Sufyān, **this verse is reported by some as the FIRST revealed of Q 9** (during Tabuk preparations).

**v. 40** — *thāniya thnayni idh humā fī l-ghār* — "the second of two when they were in the cave" — singular Quranic reference to the Hijra-cave (Thawr) episode with Abū Bakr. Major fadl-Abū Bakr verse, central in classical Sunnī polemics on the priority of Abū Bakr (Tabuk-era recall of Hijra).

**v. 41** — *infirū khifāfan wa-thiqālan* — "Go forth, light or heavy" — universal mobilisation order. The asbāb al-nuzūl: per al-Suyūṭī *Itqān* nawʿ 8, this verse was revealed early in the Q 9 sequence.

**v. 42** — Hypocrites' first lie: *law kāna ʿaraḍan qarīban wa-safaran qāṣidan la-ttabaʿūk* — "if [the expedition's] gain were close at hand and the journey easy, they would have followed you, but the distance was too long for them."

**vv. 43-46** — Critique of those who ask permission to stay back. **v. 43**: *ʿafā llāhu ʿanka lima adhinta lahum* — "May God forgive you (Muḥammad); why did you give them leave?" — extraordinary verse: divine address with *istighfār-formulation* TO the Prophet.

**v. 49** — *ai̇dhan lī wa-lā taftinnī* — the hypocrite's lying excuse "give me leave and do not put me to trial."

**vv. 53-57** — Reasons their charity is rejected: *qul anfiqū ṭawʿan aw karhan lan yutaqabbala minkum* — "Spend willingly or unwillingly; it will not be accepted from you." Distinct *manfaq*-vocabulary cluster.

**v. 58** — *wa-minhum man yalmizuka fī ṣ-ṣadaqāt* — "And among them are those who criticise you regarding the alms." This is the start of the longest *wa-minhum…wa-minhum* refrain-string in the Quran.

**v. 60** — **The eight-categories of zakāt verse**: *innamā ṣ-ṣadaqātu li-l-fuqarāʾ wa-l-masākīn wa-l-ʿāmilīna ʿalayhā wa-l-muʾallafati qulūbuhum wa-fī r-riqābi wa-l-ghārimīn wa-fī sabīli llāhi wa-bni s-sabīl* — "Charity is for the poor, the destitute, the workers in zakat-collection, those whose hearts are reconciled, for ransoming captives, the debtors, in the path of God, and for the wayfarer." The single most-cited Q 9 verse in fiqh of *zakāt*.

**v. 61** — *wa-minhum lladhīna yu'dhūna n-nabiyya wa-yaqūlūna huwa udhun* — "And among them are those who hurt the Prophet and say 'he is an ear (credulous)…'" — sabab al-nuzūl: Ibn Kathīr cites Mujāhid that one Naṣīr or Aws b. Qayẓī said this.

**vv. 64-66** — Hypocrites fear of *sūra* being revealed about them — meta-reflexive: *yaḥdharu l-munāfiqūna an tunazzala ʿalayhim sūratun tunabbi'uhum bimā fī qulūbihim* — "the hypocrites fear lest a sūra be revealed about them informing of what is in their hearts."

**v. 67** — *al-munāfiqūna wa-l-munāfiqātu baʿḍuhum min baʿḍ* — "the male hypocrites and the female hypocrites — they are of one another." Compound formula (m./f. dyadic).

**v. 71** — Counter-image: *wa-l-mu'minūna wa-l-mu'mināt baʿḍuhum awliyāʾu baʿḍ* — believers are allies of one another. Mirror-construction with v.67 (anti-parallelism).

**v. 73** — *yā ayyuhā n-nabiyyu jāhidi l-kuffāra wa-l-munāfiqīna wa-aghluẓ ʿalayhim* — "O Prophet, struggle against the disbelievers and the hypocrites, and be harsh with them." Apostrophic command-verse with typical Q 9 register.

## Block 5 (vv. 74-89) — Specific cases + Tabuk dilatants

**v. 74** — Some specific event: hypocrites who plotted assassination on the return from Tabuk (per Ibn Kathīr citing al-Wāqidī).

**vv. 75-78** — Story of one who promised charity-if-rich and reneged. al-Wāqidī (per Ibn Kathīr) names Thaʿlaba b. Ḥāṭib.

**vv. 79-80** — *yalmizūna l-muṭṭawwiʿīna mina l-mu'minīna fī ṣ-ṣadaqāt* — "those who criticise the contributors among believers regarding alms…"

**v. 80** — *istighfir lahum aw lā tastaghfir lahum in tastaghfir lahum sabʿīna marratan fa-lan yaghfira llāhu lahum* — "ask forgiveness for them or do not, even if you ask seventy times, God will not forgive them." al-Bukhārī ḥadīth #1366 (asbāb al-nuzūl: ʿAbd Allāh b. Ubayy's funeral and ʿUmar's protest).

**vv. 81-89** — Those who *farraḥa l-mukhallafūna bi-maqʿadihim khilāfa rasūli llāh* — "those left behind rejoiced in their seat-staying behind the Messenger of God." Tabuk-staybehinds.

**v. 84** — Prohibition of janāza-prayer over a deceased hypocrite — basis of the rule that the Prophet ﷺ ceased to pray over Ibn Ubayy after this verse (al-Bukhārī ḥadīth #4670, Muslim ḥadīth #2400, full chain).

## Block 6 (vv. 90-99) — Bedouin section

**v. 90** — *wa-jāʾa l-muʿadhdhirūna mina l-aʿrāb* — "the excuse-makers from the Bedouin came…"

**v. 97** — *al-aʿrābu ashaddu kufran wa-nifāqan* — "The Bedouins are stronger in disbelief and hypocrisy" — strong typological statement (with vv. 98-99 modulating: *wa-min al-aʿrāb man yu'minu billāhi* — "and among the Bedouins are those who believe").

The Bedouin typology in vv. 90-99 is unique to Q 9 and Q 49 al-Ḥujurāt; both are late-Medinan.

## Block 7 (vv. 100-118) — Repentance, *masjid al-ḍirār*, the Three

**v. 100** — *wa-s-sābiqūna l-awwalūna mina l-muhājirīna wa-l-anṣār wa-lladhīna ttabaʿūhum bi-iḥsān* — the foundational Sunnī *fadl al-ṣaḥāba* verse. *al-sābiqūna l-awwalūn* (the foremost-and-first) of muhājirūn + anṣār.

**v. 102** — *wa-ākharūna ʿtarafū bi-dhunūbihim* — "and others have admitted their sins." Asbāb al-nuzūl: Abū Lubāba and others tied to the masjid pillars in repentance — al-Wāqidī via al-Suyūṭī.

**v. 103** — *khudh min amwālihim ṣadaqatan tuṭahhiruhum wa-tuzakkīhim bihā* — "Take from their wealth a charity by which you purify them and cause them to grow." Foundational for *zakāt al-māl* and the hadith-classification *ṣadaqa muṭahhira*.

**vv. 107-110** — *masjid al-ḍirār* (the mosque-of-harm): *wa-lladhīna ttakhadhū masjidan ḍirāran wa-kufran* — "those who built a mosque for harm and for disbelief." Asbāb: Banū ʿAmr b. ʿAwf in Qubāʾ; the Prophet ordered it destroyed (Ibn Kathīr citing al-Wāqidī).

**v. 111** — **The transactional verse**: *inna llāha shtarā mina l-mu'minīna anfusahum wa-amwālahum bi-anna lahumu l-jannata yuqātilūna fī sabīli llāhi fa-yaqtulūna wa-yuqtalūn waʿdan ʿalayhi ḥaqqan fī t-tawrāti wa-l-injīli wa-l-qurʾān* — "God has bought from the believers their lives and their property in exchange for Paradise…" The *bayʿat al-jihād* verse.

**v. 112** — *at-tāʾibūna l-ʿābidūna l-ḥāmidūna s-sāʾiḥūna r-rākiʿūna s-sājidūna l-āmirūna bi-l-maʿrūfi wa-n-nāhūna ʿani l-munkari wa-l-ḥāfiẓūna li-ḥudūdi llāh* — the **nine-attributes ṣiyāgha** of the believer: penitent, worshipping, praising, fasting/journeying, bowing, prostrating, commanding right, forbidding wrong, guarding God's limits. al-Suyūṭī *Itqān* counts this as the *daqāʾiq* (subtleties) of fāṣila-construction.

**v. 113** — *mā kāna li-n-nabiyyi wa-lladhīna āmanū an yastaghfirū li-l-mushrikīn* — "It is not for the Prophet and the believers to seek forgiveness for the polytheists…" Asbāb: the Prophet's grief over Abū Ṭālib's death (al-Bukhārī ḥadīth #4675, Muslim ḥadīth #24).

**v. 114** — *Ibrāhīm's istighfār for his father* (citing Q 19:47, 26:86) — the Quran cross-referencing itself on the typology of *istighfār*.

**v. 117** — *laqad tāba llāhu ʿalā n-nabiyyi wa-l-muhājirīna wa-l-anṣār* — the *tawba ʿalā n-nabiyy* verse — sometimes counted as one of the loci of "Allah turned in favour to the Prophet."

**v. 118** — *wa-ʿalā ath-thalāthati lladhīna khullifū ḥattā idhā ḍāqat ʿalayhimu l-arḍu bimā raḥubat wa-ḍāqat ʿalayhim anfusuhum wa-ẓannū an lā maljaʾa mina llāhi illā ilayh* — **the Three Who Stayed Behind** verse: Kaʿb b. Mālik, Hilāl b. Umayya, and Murāra b. al-Rabīʿ (al-Bukhārī ḥadīth #4418, very long Kaʿb b. Mālik narrative — see `04-hadith-corpus.md`). 50-day social boycott + miraculous tawba.

## Block 8 (vv. 119-129) — Closing

**v. 119** — *yā ayyuhā lladhīna āmanū ttaqū llāha wa-kūnū maʿa ṣ-ṣādiqīn* — "Be with the truthful." al-Tirmidhī ḥadīth #3095, hasan: the verse that the Companion Kaʿb b. Mālik tied to.

**vv. 122** — *wa-mā kāna l-mu'minūna li-yanfirū kāffah* — "It is not for the believers to go forth all together…" The *naṣb-al-ʿilm* verse: classical fiqh basis for *farḍ al-kifāya* (collective duty of religious knowledge).

**v. 123** — *yā ayyuhā lladhīna āmanū qātilū lladhīna yalūnakum mina l-kuffāri wa-l-yajidū fīkum ghilẓah* — "fight those who are nearest to you among the disbelievers and let them find harshness in you." Typically read as the law of *al-aqrabu fa-l-aqrab* (nearest-first) in jihād.

**vv. 124-127** — Hypocrites' reaction to revelation, refrain *fa-zādathum rijsan ilā rijsihim* — "it added defilement to their defilement."

**v. 128** — *laqad jāʾakum rasūlun min anfusikum ʿazīzun ʿalayhi mā ʿanittum ḥarīṣun ʿalaykum bi-l-mu'minīna ra'ūfun raḥīm* — "There has come to you a Messenger from yourselves; grievous to him is what you suffer; he is anxious over you, and to the believers he is kind and merciful." 

**v. 129** — *fa-in tawallaw fa-qul ḥasbiya llāhu lā ilāha illā huwa ʿalayhi tawakkaltu wa-huwa rabbu l-ʿarshi l-ʿaẓīm* — "But if they turn away, say: 'God is sufficient for me; there is no god but He; I have placed my trust in Him; and He is the Lord of the Mighty Throne.'"

**The Ubayy b. Kaʿb ḥadīth** (al-Suyūṭī *Itqān* nawʿ 8, citing al-Bukhārī and Aḥmad's *Musnad*): the Companions had assumed v. 127 was the last, but Ubayy testified that the Prophet ﷺ recited vv. 128-129 to him as **the final two verses revealed of the entire Quran**. This is one of the four major contenders for "last revealed verse" — see [[Q009-F-04-last-revealed]] audit and `05-classical-claims-audit.md` §3.

## Refrains and structural markers

| Refrain | Verses | Function |
|:--|:--|:--|
| *wa-minhum man…* | 49, 58, 61, 75, 79, 90, 99 (7×) | hypocrite-typology cycle |
| *wa-minhum (alone)* | also 65, 67, 70 | continuing hypocrite description |
| *istaʾdhanaka / yastaʾdhinūnaka* | 43, 44, 86, 90, 93 | "asking permission [to stay back]" |
| *qātilū* (imperative, 2nd pl.) | 12, 13, 14, 29, 36, 73, 123 | combat-imperatives across the surah |
| *fa-in tābū* | 5, 11, 74 | the *if-they-repent* clause structure |
| *jihād* (root j-h-d) | 16, 19, 20, 24, 41, 44, 73, 81, 86, 88 | mobilisation cluster |
| *al-tāʾibūn* / *yatūbu* | 3, 27, 74, 102, 104, 117, 118 (7×) | Tawba-cluster |

The dominant rhetorical engine is the **alternation between believer-virtue and hypocrite-vice**, with the *wa-minhum* refrain functioning as a serial-typology marker (al-Suyūṭī *Itqān* nawʿ 9, citing Saʿīd b. Jubayr-Ibn ʿAbbās: "*mā zālat tanzilu: wa-minhum, wa-minhum, ḥattā ẓananna an lā yabqā aḥadun illā dhukira fīhā*").

## Cross-surah content references

Q 9 internally references:
- **Q 8 al-Anfāl**: vv. 19-20 echo the pre-eminence of *muhājirūn* + *anṣār* (cf. Q 8:72-75).
- **Q 5 al-Māʾida**: vv. 30-31 (Christian-Jewish polemics) parallel Q 5:72-75 closely.
- **Q 4 al-Nisāʾ**: v. 113 (*lā tastaghfirū li-l-mushrikīn*) mirrors Q 4:48.
- **Q 60 al-Mumtaḥana**: vv. 23-24 on family-loyalty-vs-faith parallel Q 60:1-9.
- **Q 19 Maryam**: v. 114 cites Ibrāhīm's *istighfār* for his father — direct *takhrīj* of Q 19:47, Q 26:86.
- **Q 33 al-Aḥzāb**: v. 73 (*jāhidi l-kuffāra wa-l-munāfiqīna*) is exactly repeated at Q 66:9.

This dense cross-Quranic referencing supports al-Suyūṭī's *Itqān* nawʿ 9 listing of Q 9 as one of the most heavily inter-Quranic surahs (alongside Q 2 and Q 5).

## Vocabulary distinctness

Per the QAC root-density analysis (`csv/Q009-F-01-02-density-results.json`), Q 9's distinctive vocabulary clusters around:
- *n-f-q* (hypocrisy): rank 5/114, 7.85/1k tokens (corpus mean 1.02)
- *t-w-b* (repentance): rank 4/114, 6.36/1k (corpus mean 1.05)
- *j-h-d* (striving): rank 2/114, 4.11/1k (corpus mean 0.31)
- *ḥ-r-m* (sanctifying / forbidden / sacred): rank 4/114, 3.74/1k (corpus mean 0.43)

Notable absences: Q 9 has only ONE attestation of root *q-l-b* (heart) per million-token-normalised metric? No — it has 11 attestations (rank middling). The classical claim that Q 9 is "the surah of the heart's *nifāq*" is misleading — Q 9 is the surah of *speech-acts of nifāq*, with the cluster *qul* (say to them), *yaḥlifūna* (they swear), *yaqūlūna* (they say), *afwāhuhum* (their mouths) appearing 50+ times.

## Content-register

Q 9 is **mixed**: legal-imperatival (vv. 5, 28-29, 36, 60, 103, 123) + narrative-historical (vv. 25-27 Ḥunayn, vv. 38-99 Tabuk, vv. 117-118 The Three) + creedal-polemical (vv. 30-33). This **register-mixing** is itself an architectural marker — Q 9 functions as a Medinan synthesis of the four Quranic discourse modes within a single surah, distinguishing it from purely-narrative (Q 12 Yūsuf) or purely-creedal (Q 2:1-39, Q 112) surahs.

*Bismillāhi al-Raḥmāni al-Raḥīm.*

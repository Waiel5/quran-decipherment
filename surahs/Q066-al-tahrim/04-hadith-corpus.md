## 1. The honey / Maghāfir episode (ʿĀʾisha + Ḥafṣa) — the Ṣaḥīḥayn version

The collusion of ʿĀʾisha and Ḥafṣa to make the Prophet swear off the honey he drank at Zaynab bint
Jaḥsh's house; v 1 (*lima tuḥarrimu*), v 3 (*idh asarra al-nabī*), v 4 (*in tatūbā*) revealed.

**Key text (Bukhārī #4704, Book of Tafsīr):** *"Allāh's Messenger ﷺ used to drink honey in the house of
Zaynab bint Jaḥsh … Ḥafṣa and I agreed secretly that if he comes to either of us she would say 'It seems
you have eaten Maghāfir, for I smell Maghāfir in you' … he replied: 'No, but I was drinking honey in the
house of Zaynab … and I shall never take it again. I have taken an oath as to that, and you should not
tell anybody.'"* — establishes both the prohibition (v 1) and the confided-secret + oath (vv 2-3).

## 2. The Māriya episode — the Kūfan / asbāb-collections version

The Prophet's seclusion with Māriya the Copt on Ḥafṣa's day; Ḥafṣa's jealousy; the Prophet forbidding
Māriya to himself by oath; the secret disclosed to ʿĀʾisha; v 1 revealed.

| Source | Locus | Isnād | Note |

| **al-Wāḥidī, *Asbāb al-Nuzūl*** (on Q 66:1) | `spa5k-tafsir-api/en-asbab-al-nuzul-by-al-wahidi/66/1.json` | Ibn ʿAbbās ← ʿUmar | "he entered Ḥafṣa's house with Māriya … he said 'do not mention this to ʿĀʾisha; she [Māriya] is forbidden for me' … he swore … but Ḥafṣa told ʿĀʾisha; the Prophet stayed away from his wives 29 days" |
| **al-Wāḥidī, *Asbāb*** (second chain, on Q 66:1) | same file | Hishām b. ʿUrwa ← his father ← ʿĀʾisha | the **honey** version (collusion w/ Sawda) — al-Wāḥidī records BOTH |
| **al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān*** (on Q 66:1) | `spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-al-tabari/66/1.json` | Zayd b. Aslam; al-Shaʿbī ← Masrūq | "it was Māriya … he forbade her by an oath … *anta ʿalayya ḥarām* is laghw" |

The Māriya-version isnād runs through Ibn ʿAbbās ← ʿUmar (al-Wāḥidī) and through al-Shaʿbī ← Masrūq and
Zayd b. Aslam (al-Ṭabarī). Note these are reported in the *asbāb* and *tafsīr* collections; the strongest
*ḥadīth-collection* attestation (Ṣaḥīḥayn) is the honey version.

## 3. The Ibn ʿAbbās–ʿUmar tafsīr of Q 66:4 (the "two who backed each other")

"ʿĀʾisha and Ḥafṣa." This is the explicit prophetic-tafsīr of v 4.

**Tirmidhī #3402 (Ibn ʿAbbās):** *"I was ever determined to ask ʿUmar about the two women … about whom
Allāh said: 'If you two turn in repentance, your hearts are indeed so inclined' … He said: 'It was
ʿĀʾisha and Ḥafṣa.'"* The narration continues into the parallel *īlāʾ* (month-long withdrawal) account
and ʿUmar's "we Quraysh used to have the upper hand over our women" reflection. The grading
**ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ gharīb** is verified from the Arabic text on disk.

## 4. Cross-reference: the v 9 jihād directive (≡ Q 9:73)

Q 66:9 (*yā ayyuhā al-nabī jāhid al-kuffāra wa-l-munāfiqīna …*) is verbatim-identical to Q 9:73
(Q066-F-01 Arm A). The ḥadīth literature comments on the *jihād-of-the-hypocrites* clause primarily under
Q 9 al-Tawba, not Q 66; I did not find a Q-66-specific ḥadīth isolating v 9's recitation. The verbatim
identity is documented as a textual fact, not as a separate ḥadīth claim.

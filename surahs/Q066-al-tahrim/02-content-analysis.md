# Q 66 al-Taḥrīm — Content Analysis

Arabic verse text from `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/quran-text/quran-no-tashkeel.json` (Q 66, 12 verses).
This is descriptive content analysis; numerical claims are cross-referenced to the empirical profile.

## 1. Verse-by-verse summary

**v 1** — يا أيها النبي لم تحرم ما أحل الله لك تبتغي مرضات أزواجك والله غفور رحيم
The prophet-vocative opens with a question/reproach: "O Prophet, why do you forbid (to yourself) what
Allāh has made lawful for you, seeking the pleasure of your wives?" Closes on *ghafūr raḥīm*. This is
the surah's title-verse (the root *ḥ-r-m* → *taḥrīm*).

**v 2** — قد فرض الله لكم تحلة أيمانكم والله مولاكم وهو العليم الحكيم
The legal remedy: "Allāh has ordained for you the dissolution (*taḥilla*) of your oaths." The thing
forbidden was bound by an oath; the oath is dissolvable by expiation (*kaffāra*). Allāh is *mawlākum*
(your patron) and *al-ʿalīm al-ḥakīm*.

**v 3** — وإذ أسر النبي إلى بعض أزواجه حديثا فلما نبأت به وأظهره الله عليه عرف بعضه وأعرض عن بعض … قال نبأني العليم الخبير
The confided-secret narrative: the Prophet confides a matter to "one of his wives"; she discloses it;
Allāh apprises him; he makes known part and forbears part. When he confronts her, she asks "Who told
you this?"; he answers "the All-Knowing, the All-Aware (*al-ʿalīm al-khabīr*) told me." (Sequence of
*n-b-ʾ* "to inform" repeated 4× — the verse is built on the disclosure verb.)

**v 4** — إن تتوبا إلى الله فقد صغت قلوبكما وإن تظاهرا عليه فإن الله هو مولاه وجبريل وصالح المؤمنين والملائكة بعد ذلك ظهير
Direct dual-address to the two wives: "If you two turn in repentance to Allāh — for your hearts have
inclined; but if you two back each other against him, then Allāh is his patron, and Jibrīl, and the
righteous of the believers, and thereafter the angels are a backing (*ẓahīr*)." The escalation of
backers (Allāh → Jibrīl → righteous believers → angels) is the rhetorical climax of the episode.

**v 5** — عسى ربه إن طلقكن أن يبدله أزواجا خيرا منكن مسلمات مؤمنات قانتات تائبات عابدات سائحات ثيبات وأبكارا
The divorce-replacement clause: "Perhaps his Lord, if he divorces you, will give him in exchange wives
better than you" — followed by an **8-virtue enumeration**: *muslimāt, muʾmināt, qānitāt, tāʾibāt,
ʿābidāt, sāʾiḥāt* (the six adjective-virtues) + *thayyibāt wa-abkār* (previously-married and virgins).
This is a candidate for the corpus's densest single-verse feminine-virtue list (queued as Q066-F-03).

**v 6** — يا أيها الذين آمنوا قوا أنفسكم وأهليكم نارا وقودها الناس والحجارة عليها ملائكة غلاظ شداد لا يعصون الله ما أمرهم ويفعلون ما يؤمرون
Pivot to the general believers (the *yā-ayyuhā alladhīna āmanū* vocative): "guard yourselves and your
families against a Fire whose fuel is people and stones, over which are angels, harsh and severe, who
do not disobey Allāh in what He commands them and do what they are commanded." The fire-guarding charge.

**v 7** — يا أيها الذين كفروا لا تعتذروا اليوم إنما تجزون ما كنتم تعملون
The mirror-vocative to the disbelievers: "O you who disbelieve, make no excuses today; you are only
recompensed for what you used to do." (vv 6-7 form a believer/disbeliever vocative-pair.)

**v 8** — يا أيها الذين آمنوا توبوا إلى الله توبة نصوحا … نورهم يسعى بين أيديهم وبأيمانهم يقولون ربنا أتمم لنا نورنا واغفر لنا إنك على كل شيء قدير
The **longest verse (46 words)**: a third believer-vocative commanding *tawba naṣūḥ* (sincere
repentance), promising entry to gardens, and depicting the believers' light running before them and to
their right while they pray "Our Lord, complete for us our light and forgive us." The eschatological-
light image echoes Q 57:12 (al-Ḥadīd).

**v 9** — يا أيها النبي جاهد الكفار والمنافقين واغلظ عليهم ومأواهم جهنم وبئس المصير
The second prophet-vocative: "O Prophet, strive against the disbelievers and the hypocrites and be
firm against them; their abode is Hell, and wretched is the destination." **This verse is
character-for-character identical to Q 9:73** (Q066-F-01 Arm A, CONFIRMED corpus verbatim twin).

**v 10** — ضرب الله مثلا للذين كفروا امرأت نوح وامرأت لوط كانتا تحت عبدين من عبادنا صالحين فخانتاهما فلم يغنيا عنهما من الله شيئا وقيل ادخلا النار مع الداخلين
The negative parable: "Allāh sets forth an example for those who disbelieve: the wife of Nūḥ and the
wife of Lūṭ. They were under (married to) two of Our righteous servants, but they betrayed them, so
they availed nothing for them against Allāh, and it was said: 'Enter the Fire with those who enter.'"
Marriage to a prophet does not save a disbelieving wife.

**v 11** — وضرب الله مثلا للذين آمنوا امرأت فرعون إذ قالت رب ابن لي عندك بيتا في الجنة ونجني من فرعون وعمله ونجني من القوم الظالمين
The positive parable (Āsiya): "And Allāh sets forth an example for those who believe: the wife of
Firʿawn, when she said, 'My Lord, build for me a house with You in Paradise, and save me from Firʿawn
and his deed, and save me from the wrongdoing people.'" Faith under a tyrant-husband saves.

**v 12** — ومريم ابنت عمران التي أحصنت فرجها فنفخنا فيه من روحنا وصدقت بكلمات ربها وكتبه وكانت من القانتين
The seal (Maryam): "And Maryam, daughter of ʿImrān, who guarded her chastity, so We breathed into it
of Our Spirit, and she affirmed the words of her Lord and His Scriptures, and she was of the devoutly
obedient (*al-qānitīn*)." The surah ends on the masculine plural *al-qānitīn* (echoing *qānitāt* of v 5).

## 2. Thematic blocks

| Block | Verses | Register | Theme |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **A. The prohibition & its dissolution** | 1-2 | legal/personal | The Prophet forbids himself a lawful thing by oath; Allāh ordains the oath's dissolution (*taḥilla*) |
| **B. The wives' episode** | 3-5 | narrative/admonitory | The disclosed secret; the dual-address to the two wives; the divorce-replacement threat with the 8-virtue ideal-wife list |
| **C. The general charges** | 6-9 | exhortatory/legal | Believer fire-guarding (6) ↔ disbeliever no-excuse (7); the *tawba naṣūḥ* + light verse (8); the prophet's jihād charge (9) |
| **D. The dual-exemplar seal** | 10-12 | parabolic | Two disbeliever-wife exemplars (Nūḥ, Lūṭ) ↔ two believer-women exemplars (Āsiya, Maryam) |

The surah's macro-structure is a **contraction from the particular to the universal**: a single
domestic incident (A-B) → general believer/disbeliever charges (C) → archetypal female exemplars of
faith and disbelief across sacred history (D). al-Qurṭubī (on v 11, citing Yaḥyā b. Sallām) reads block
D as a deliberate *return* to block B — the female exemplars are an admonition to ʿĀʾisha and Ḥafṣa,
closing the ring.

## 3. Content register

Mixed: **legal** (taḥrīm/taḥilla/kaffāra, vv 1-2), **narrative-admonitory** (the wives' episode, vv 3-5),
**exhortatory** (the believer/disbeliever charges, vv 6-9), and **parabolic** (the exemplar seal, vv 10-12).
There is no eschatological-cosmology block of the early-Meccan type; the fire-imagery (vv 6, 7, 10) is
admonitory rather than apocalyptic-descriptive.

## 4. Vocabulary distinctness

- **Root *ḥ-r-m* (forbid)** anchors the title (v 1 *tuḥarrimu*, *aḥalla*; v 2 *taḥilla*).
- **Root *n-b-ʾ* (inform/disclose)** is concentrated in v 3 (4 occurrences in one verse) — the
  disclosure-of-the-secret verse is built on a single repeated verb.
- **The exemplar-frame *ḍaraba … mathalan li-lladhīna …*** (vv 10-11) is corpus-EXCLUSIVE as an adjacent
  kafarū→āmanū antithetical pair (Q066-F-01 Arm B, B-H1). Corpus-wide the *ḍaraba Allāh mathalan* frame
  appears in only 7 verses (Q 14:24, 16:75, 16:76, 16:112, 39:29, 66:10, 66:11) — and ONLY in Q 66 is it
  used for the believer/disbeliever *human-exemplar* antithesis.
- **Feminine-virtue cluster** (v 5): *muslimāt muʾmināt qānitāt tāʾibāt ʿābidāt sāʾiḥāt thayyibāt abkār* —
  eight feminine plural descriptors in sequence.
- **The Divine-name pairing** is dense: *al-ʿalīm al-ḥakīm* (v 2), *al-ʿalīm al-khabīr* (v 3) — the
  All-Knowing recurs as the closer of the secret-disclosure motif.

## 5. Repetition patterns

- **Triple believer-vocative**: *yā ayyuhā alladhīna āmanū* opens vv 6 and 8 (with the disbeliever-mirror
  *yā ayyuhā alladhīna kafarū* at v 7 between them) — a vocative refrain structuring block C.
- **Double prophet-vocative**: *yā ayyuhā al-nabī* opens v 1 and v 9 — bracketing the surah's first
  9 verses; only Q 33 carries more prophet-vocatives among Medinan surahs.
- **Double exemplar-frame**: *ḍaraba Allāh mathalan* opens v 10 and (with *wa-*) v 11.
- ***qānitāt* (v 5) → *al-qānitīn* (v 12)**: the ideal-wife virtue at the surah's middle returns as the
  closing descriptor of Maryam, sealing the surah on the obedience-motif.

## 6. Cross-surah content references

- **Q 66:9 ≡ Q 9:73** (verbatim full-verse twin; Q066-F-01 Arm A). The jihād-against-kuffār-and-munāfiqīn
  directive is shared character-for-character with al-Tawba.
- **Q 66:8 light-image** echoes **Q 57:12** (al-Ḥadīd): *nūruhum yasʿā bayna aydīhim* — the believers'
  running light on the Day of Resurrection appears in both the musabbiḥāt-adjacent al-Ḥadīd and here.
- **Maryam (v 12)** cross-refers to **Q 19 (Maryam)** and **Q 21:91** (the same *fa-nafakhnā fīhā/fīhi
  min rūḥinā* breathing-of-the-Spirit formula).
- **The wife-of-Firʿawn / Āsiya** is named (by the exegetes) only here in the corpus as a believer-exemplar;
  her dwelling-in-Paradise prayer (v 11) is unique to Q 66.

## 2. Cosmic-creation passage (Q 41:9-12) — *mushkil*

The classical *mushkil* ("difficult passage") here is reconciling:
- Q 41:9 — earth in 2 days
- Q 41:10 — *aqwāt* (sustenance) in 4 days
- Q 41:11 — heavens in 2 days
- Total: 8 days?

Classical resolution (al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān* nawʿ 47; al-Ṭabarī ad loc.; al-Qurṭubī ad loc.): the 4 days of *aqwāt* INCLUDE the 2 days of earth-creation. So 2 + (4-2) + 2 = 6 days, matching Q 7:54 / Q 10:3 / Q 25:59 / Q 32:4 / Q 50:38.

This is one of the project's pre-locked classical *mushkil* cases ([[h-new-classical-mushkilat|H-NEW family]]).

## 3. Q 41:53 — the *āfāq + anfus* verse

*Sa-nurīhim āyātinā fī al-āfāqi wa-fī anfusihim ḥattā yatabayyana lahum annahu al-ḥaqq*.

**Classical reading** (al-Ṭabarī, al-Qurṭubī, Ibn Kathīr ad loc.; consensus): *al-āfāq* refers to the eschatological-historical horizons (the conquest of Mecca, the spread of Islam, the punishment of disbelievers in this world); *fī anfusihim* refers to the inner experience of the Prophet's contemporaries seeing the truth manifest.

**Modern apologetic reading** (Bucaille 1976, al-Kaheel, *iʿjāz ʿilmī* corpus): *al-āfāq* = cosmology / astrophysics; *fī anfusihim* = anatomy / embryology. This reading detaches from the *contextual referent* (the immediate historical promise to the Prophet) and remaps the verse to modern science-vindication arguments.

The project (per [[h-new-570-muqattaat-content-cluster|H-NEW-570]] §6 multi-axis architecture) treats the modern reading as classically unsupported and empirically untestable in its strong form. **Q 41:53 is therefore flagged as a rules-tuple-fragile verse** — its classical reading is well-anchored; its modern reading is widely-cited but absent in pre-modern *tafsīr*.

## 4. Sajda position (Q 41:37-38)

Q 41 contains one of the **14 (or 15, per al-Shāfiʿī) sajda positions** in the Quran. The sajda is at v. 37-38: *wa-min āyātihi al-laylu wa-l-nahāru wa-l-shamsu wa-l-qamaru lā tasjudū lil-shamsi wa-lā lil-qamari wa-sjudū lillāhi alladhī khalaqahunna* — "of His signs are the night and day and sun and moon; do not prostrate to sun or moon; prostrate to God who created them".

al-Bukhārī, ḥadīth #1078 series — the sajda compendium — confirms Q 41 sajda. al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān*, nawʿ 65 (sujūd al-tilāwa) lists Q 41:37 as one of the 14 (Mālikī count) sajdas.

## 7. Refrains and rhetorical structure

- *Mā tuʿbadūna…* / *Lā tasjudū lil-shamsi…* — anti-shirk imperatives
- *Sa-nurīhim āyātinā fī al-āfāqi wa-fī anfusihim* — once, but rhetorically the most-cited verse of the surah
- *Innamā anā basharun mithlukum* (Q 41:6) — the standard prophetic self-positioning formula

## 8. Asbāb al-nuzūl

Per Ibn Kathīr ad Q 41:5 (the "akinna / waqr / ḥijāb" verse): the canonical *sabab* is the encounter between the Prophet and ʿUtba b. Rabīʿa, who came on behalf of Quraysh and recited the rejection-formula in v. 5. The Prophet then recited Q 41:1-13 to him. Source: Ibn Kathīr extracted from `Q041` section in `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/ibn-kathir-tafsir-quran.openiti.raw.txt` (offset 7150732, this session).

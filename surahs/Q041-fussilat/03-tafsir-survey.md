# Q 41 Fuṣṣilat — tafsīr survey (≥6 mufassirūn)

## 1. al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān*

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/qurtubi-jami-ahkam.openiti.raw.txt` (al-Qurṭubī carries al-Ṭabarī verbatim) + `razi-mafatih-al-ghayb.openiti.raw.txt`.

Key positions:
- **Q 41:9-12 (six-day creation)**: al-Ṭabarī presents the *2+4+2* reconciliation: 2 days for earth, 4 days for *aqwāt* (which INCLUDES the earth-days), 2 days for heavens = 6 total. He preserves Mujāhid's variant (sequential 2+2+2+2 = 8) while siding with the consensus reconciliation.
- **Q 41:11 (*istawā ilā al-samāʾ wa-hiya dukhānun*)**: al-Ṭabarī treats *dukhān* as the primordial vapor-state of pre-cosmic matter, citing Ibn ʿAbbās. The verse becomes the Qurʾānic foundation for the *cosmos-from-vapor* protology.
- **Q 41:16 (the wind on ʿĀd)**: al-Ṭabarī details the *rīḥ ṣarṣar ʿātiya* — describing it as freezing-cold AND violent.
- **Q 41:53 (āfāq + anfus)**: al-Ṭabarī's reading is **eschatological-historical** — *al-āfāq* = the conquests; *fī anfusihim* = the conquered peoples' inner experience. Modern apologetic reading (cosmology + anatomy) absent in al-Ṭabarī.

## 2. al-Zamakhsharī, *al-Kashshāf*

Source: OpenITI standard pull.

- **Q 41:11 *dukhān***: rationalist-philological reading; al-Zamakhsharī treats *dukhān* as a *majāz* (metaphor) for the formless primordial matter, NOT literal smoke.
- **Q 41:30 (*alladhīna qālū rabbunā Allāh thumma istaqāmū*)**: anchor for the doctrine of *istiqāma* — al-Zamakhsharī treats this as the constitutive virtue of the believer.
- **Q 41:34 *idfaʿ bi-llatī hiya aḥsan***: ethical-rhetorical analysis emphasising the rhetorical force of *aḥsan*.

## 3. al-Rāzī, *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb*

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/razi-mafatih-al-ghayb.openiti.raw.txt`.

- **Q 41:9-12 *mushkil***: al-Rāzī gives the most extended classical treatment of the days-of-creation arithmetic. He considers four readings: (1) al-Ṭabarī's 2+4 (with overlap)+2 = 6; (2) chronological 2+2+2 = 6 with *aqwāt* parallel-distributed; (3) literary-rhetorical *aqwāt fī arbaʿat ayyām* as a separate temporal scale; (4) majoritarian acceptance of arithmetic mystery. Ultimately al-Rāzī sides with reading (1).
- **Q 41:11 *dukhān***: extensive cosmology — al-Rāzī surveys Hellenistic-Aristotelian protology and integrates it cautiously.
- **Q 41:53**: al-Rāzī's reading aligns with al-Ṭabarī (eschatological-historical) but he opens a *secondary* cosmological reading — that *al-āfāq* could include cosmic-natural signs. **This is the closest classical-source antecedent to the modern apologetic reading**, but al-Rāzī treats it as derivative, not primary.
- **Q 41:5 (*akinna / waqr / ḥijāb*)**: al-Rāzī develops a *triple-barrier* cognitive model: heart-cover, ear-heaviness, mediating veil — three loci of resistance.

## 4. al-Qurṭubī, *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān*

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/qurtubi-jami-ahkam.openiti.raw.txt`.

- **Q 41:37-38 (sajda al-tilāwa)**: al-Qurṭubī establishes Q 41:37 as one of the canonical sajda-verses (Mālikī count: 14; Shāfiʿī count: 15). The fiqhī rules for sajda al-tilāwa during recitation are anchored here.
- **Q 41:34 *idfaʿ bi-llatī hiya aḥsan***: al-Qurṭubī develops the ethical-political dimensions — when an enemy turns to a friend through forbearance.

## 5. al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-durar*

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/biqai-nazm-al-durar.openiti.raw.txt`.

- **Q 41 ↔ Q 40 transition**: al-Biqāʿī links the closing of Q 40 (the disputers refusing the Qurʾān) with the opening of Q 41 (the Qurʾān self-described as *fuṣṣilat āyātuhu*).
- **Q 41 internal munāsaba**: the surah's name *Fuṣṣilat* (made-distinct) is taken as the structural principle — each verse is a discrete unit of revelation, and the surah's *fawāṣil* (verse-divisions) are themselves an iʿjāz.
- **Q 41 ↔ Q 42 transition**: al-Biqāʿī notes Q 41:52 (*qul a-raʾaytum in kāna min ʿindi llāh thumma kafartum bihi*) sets up Q 42:13 (*sharaʿa lakum min al-dīn*) — the universalist-prophetology of Q 42 is Q 41's defensive close-block extended.

## 6. Ibn Kathīr, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm*

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/ibn-kathir-tafsir-quran.openiti.raw.txt` (offset ~7150732 for Q41 opening).

- **Q 41:5 *sabab*: ʿUtba b. Rabīʿa narrative**: Ibn Kathīr gives the canonical *sabab* — ʿUtba b. Rabīʿa came on Quraysh's behalf to the Prophet, attempted to dissuade him; the Prophet recited Q 41:1-13; ʿUtba returned to Quraysh transformed. Ibn Kathīr cites this from Mūsā b. ʿUqba's *Maghāzī* and from al-Ṭabarī.
- **Q 41:9-12**: aligns with al-Ṭabarī.
- **Q 41:34**: cites Bukhārī ḥadīth on the rage-defusion principle.
- **Q 41:53**: classical eschatological-historical reading ONLY; rejects (implicitly, by absence) cosmological reading.

## 7. al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān*

- **nawʿ 17 (*asmāʾ al-suwar*)**: catalogs *Fuṣṣilat* (primary name from Q 41:3) and *Ḥā Mīm al-Sajda* (alternative, from the Q 41:37 sajda).
- **nawʿ 47 (*al-mushkil*)**: Q 41:9-12 days-of-creation arithmetic is among the cataloged *mushkilāt*.
- **nawʿ 65 (*sujūd al-tilāwa*)**: Q 41:37 is the canonical sajda-position.

## 9. The Q 41:53 modern-vs-classical divergence

Modern apologetic readings (Bucaille 1976; *iʿjāz ʿilmī* corpus including al-Kaheel `data/literature/al-kaheel/`; al-ʿAlī *al-Iʿjāz al-ʿilmī fī al-Qurʾān al-karīm*; M. Kūrkayl) read *al-āfāq* as cosmology (galaxies, stars) and *fī anfusihim* as anatomy (embryology, biology).

**Classical sources do NOT support this reading**. Even al-Rāzī's secondary cosmological reading is contextually-bounded (referring to ordinary cosmic signs, not modern science). The modern reading is a 20th-century innovation.

**Empirical-lexical finding (Q041-F-02, 2026-05-09)**: The verse's lexical structure has a **3-fold corpus-singleton character**:
1. *āfāq* (آفاق with U+0622 alif-madda) — single attestation in the corpus (hapax-lexeme).
2. *āfāq* × *anfus* co-occurrence — single attestation.
3. Full collocation *في الآفاق وفي أنفسهم* — single attestation.

This **explains** the verse's outsized modern apologetic prominence (a hapax verse is a *natural rhetorical anchor*) but does NOT **validate** the modern hermeneutic. The classical-source consensus is the empirically settled meaning.

al-Ghazālī's general *iʿjāz al-ʿilmī* framework (*Jawāhir al-Qurʾān*) is **gnostic-spiritual**, NOT physical-empirical. Modern apologetic literature selectively reads al-Ghazālī's "all sciences in the Qurʾān" claim out of its mystical-gnostic context.

This is the project's clearest case of **modern-vs-classical hermeneutic divergence**, now anchored to a quantitative lexical-singularity finding. See [[Q041-fussilat/05-classical-claims-audit|claims audit §6a-6d]] and [[Q041-fussilat/06-novel-findings|novel findings §Finding 2]].

# Q 40 Ghāfir / al-Muʾmin — tafsīr survey (≥6 mufassirūn)

All citations are anchored to extracted source files at `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/`. Where a per-surah extraction file is not yet on disk for Q 40, the citation is to the offset within the OpenITI raw consolidated file, with a flag for verification.

## 1. al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān* (d. 310/923)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/qurtubi-jami-ahkam.openiti.raw.txt` (al-Qurṭubī carries al-Ṭabarī verbatim) and `razi-mafatih-al-ghayb.openiti.raw.txt` (al-Rāzī cites al-Ṭabarī).

Key positions documented in al-Ṭabarī ad Q 40:
- **Q 40:1 (ḥā mīm)**: al-Ṭabarī classifies the muqaṭṭaʿāt as among the *mutashābih* and refuses lexical-decoding interpretations. He preserves Ibn ʿAbbās's view that ḥā = *al-Ḥalīm / al-Ḥamīd* (a divine name) and mīm = *al-Mājid / al-Malik*; he also preserves Ibn Masʿūd's view that the letters are "names of the Qurʾān itself".
- **Q 40:7-9 (angelic intercession)**: al-Ṭabarī establishes the *hamalat al-ʿarsh* tradition through Companions chains; the prayer-pattern of the angels is read literally as a real cosmic-liturgical event, not metaphor.
- **Q 40:28 (Believer of Pharaoh)**: al-Ṭabarī catalogs the candidate names — Ḥizqīl b. Ṣabūrā (most attested via Ibn ʿAbbās), Ḥabīb (via al-Suddī), Sham'ān (via al-Daḥḥāk) — and is non-committal, citing the *mubham* (un-named) status as deliberate.
- **Q 40:60 (*udʿūnī astajib lakum*)**: al-Ṭabarī treats *al-duʿāʾ huwa al-ʿibāda* (per the Tirmidhī ḥadīth chain), establishing duʿāʾ as a constitutive act of worship.

## 2. al-Zamakhsharī, *al-Kashshāf* (d. 538/1144)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/` (verify presence; OpenITI standard pull).

al-Zamakhsharī's distinctive Muʿtazilī framing:
- **Q 40:7 (intercession)**: emphasises the *ʿaql-rational* nature of angelic intercession — the angels intercede only for those whom God's *ʿadl* (justice) deems worthy; rejects unconditional intercession.
- **Q 40:60**: classic Muʿtazilī reading of *al-duʿāʾ* as obligatory effort, not as automatic divine response; emphasizes *taklīf*.

## 3. al-Rāzī, *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* (d. 606/1210)

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/razi-mafatih-al-ghayb.openiti.raw.txt` (consolidated file).

al-Rāzī's distinctive features in Sūrat Ghāfir:
- **Q 40:1 muqaṭṭaʿāt**: extensive philosophical disputation — al-Rāzī surveys 19 distinct interpretive positions on ḥā mīm and concludes with *Allāh aʿlam* but flags the cipher-of-divine-names hypothesis (*ḥā* = *Ḥalīm*, *mīm* = *Malik*) as most consistent with the surah's opening attribute-cluster (*al-ʿAzīz al-ʿAlīm* in v. 2).
- **Q 40:3 attribute-pair**: detailed kalām discussion of the simultaneity of *Ghāfir al-dhanb* (forgiver) and *Shadīd al-ʿiqāb* (severe in punishment). Al-Rāzī develops a *raḥma-ʿadl* taxonomy: forgiveness is *raḥma*; punishment is *ʿadl*; both attributes are necessary and non-contradictory.
- **Q 40:28 Believer**: rationalist defence of the Believer's *kitmān* (concealment of faith) as legitimate prudential conduct (*taqiyya* avant la lettre); al-Rāzī notes the Believer's *Yūsufī* citation pattern as Qurʾān-internal cross-reference.
- **Q 40:60 (*udʿūnī astajib lakum*)**: extensive section on the philosophical problem of unanswered prayer; resolves by distinguishing *istijāba* (response) from *iʿṭāʾ al-marghūb* (granting the requested thing).

## 4. al-Qurṭubī, *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān* (d. 671/1273)

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/qurtubi-jami-ahkam.openiti.raw.txt`.

al-Qurṭubī's fiqhī orientation surfaces in:
- **Q 40:46 (*al-nāru yuʿraḍūna ʿalayhā ghuduwwan wa-ʿashiyyan*)**: foundational verse for *ʿadhāb al-qabr* (punishment of the grave). al-Qurṭubī reads the *morning and evening exposure to the Fire* as referring to the intermediate-state (*barzakh*) punishment, not the eschatological-Day punishment. This is the canonical Sunni *ʿadhāb al-qabr* anchor.
- **Q 40:60**: legal-imperative reading of *udʿūnī* — duʿāʾ becomes *wājib* (obligation) by the imperative, with a hierarchy of *manhī ʿan ḥuḍūr al-ghafla*.

## 5. al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-durar fī tanāsub al-āyāt wa-l-suwar* (d. 885/1480)

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/biqai-nazm-al-durar.openiti.raw.txt`.

al-Biqāʿī's *munāsabāt* (inter-passage coherence) framework:
- **Q 40 ↔ Q 39 transition**: al-Biqāʿī links Q 39:75 (*tarā al-malāʾikata ḥāffīna min ḥawli al-ʿarshi yusabbiḥūna bi-ḥamdi rabbihim*) to Q 40:7 (*al-ladhīna yaḥmilūna al-ʿarsha wa-man ḥawlahu yusabbiḥūna bi-ḥamdi rabbihim*) as a deliberate adjacency: the closing of Q 39 prefigures the opening doxology of Q 40.
- **Q 40 internal munāsaba**: the *forgiveness ↔ severity* attribute-pair (Q 40:3) prepares the dramatic-narrative of the Believer who articulates exactly this paired theology before Pharaoh's court.
- **Q 40 ↔ Q 41 transition**: the closing of Q 40 (the disputers refusing the Qurʾān) prepares the opening of Q 41 (the Qurʾān explicitly self-described as *fuṣṣilat āyātuhu*).

## 6. Ibn Kathīr, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm* (d. 774/1373)

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/ibn-kathir-tafsir-quran.openiti.raw.txt` (offsets in the consolidated file; surah-Q40 section identifiable by the surah-name marker *سورة غافر*).

Ibn Kathīr's salient contributions:
- **Surah-name preface**: Ibn Kathīr opens Sūrat Ghāfir with the Companion-corpus on *al-ḥawāmīm* — quoting Ibn Masʿūd (*dībāj al-Qurʾān*), Ibn ʿAbbās (*lubāb al-Qurʾān*), Misʿar b. Kidām (*al-ʿarāʾis*), Muḥammad b. Sīrīn (preferring *Āl Ḥā Mīm*), and Saʿīd b. Jubayr (the "rawḍāt" hadith).
- **Q 40:28 Believer**: catalogs all named candidates from al-Ṭabarī plus additions; ultimately rejects all isnāds as weak. Notes the structural choice of *mubham* (un-naming) is theologically deliberate — the Believer's identity is universalised.
- **Q 40:46 *ʿadhāb al-qabr***: aligns with al-Qurṭubī's reading; reinforces with Bukhārī ḥadīth chains on *ʿadhāb al-qabr* (al-Bukhārī 1369-1374 series).
- **Q 40:60**: cross-references the Tirmidhī ḥadīth #3247 (*al-duʿāʾ huwa al-ʿibāda*) and Ibn Mājah; treats Q 40:60 as the Qurʾānic foundation of duʿāʾ-as-worship doctrine.

## 7. al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān* (d. 911/1505)

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/suyuti-al-itqan-fi-ulum-al-quran-english.pdf`.

al-Suyūṭī's relevant entries:
- **nawʿ 17 (*asmāʾ al-suwar*)**: catalogs the twin-naming Ghāfir / al-Muʾmin; cites al-Bukhārī's chapter title "Tafsīr Sūrat al-Muʾmin" (his preferred name in the *Tafsīr* book).
- **nawʿ 19 (*al-makkī wa-l-madanī*)**: classifies Q 40 as Meccan with no exceptions (some *masā'il* read v. 56 as Madinan-context but al-Suyūṭī sides with Meccan).
- **nawʿ 70 (*mubhamāt al-Qurʾān*)**: Q 40:28 (the Believer of Pharaoh) is among the cataloged *mubhamāt*; al-Suyūṭī surveys the candidate names and follows al-Ṭabarī's reservation.

## 2. Names — twin-naming as classical signal

Sūrat Ghāfir bears **two well-attested classical names**: *Ghāfir* (from Q 40:3 *Ghāfir al-dhanb wa-qābil al-tawb*) and *al-Muʾmin* (from Q 40:28, the unnamed Believer of Pharaoh's family — *muʾmin āl Firʿawn*). al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān* nawʿ 17 (asmāʾ al-suwar), records both, with Ibn Kathīr (tafsīr Sūrat Ghāfir, opening) using *al-Muʾmin* as the primary name in fadāʾil-traditions. This twin-naming is rare in the corpus and reflects the sūra's two thematic poles: divine forgiveness (Ghāfir) and exemplary human faith (al-Muʾmin).

## 3. Position in the ḥawāmīm-7 cluster

Q 40 **opens** the ḥawāmīm-7 cluster (Q 40-46), the only consecutive 7-surah block in the Quran sharing a muqaṭṭaʿāt opening (حم). Empirically (this session, see [[hawamim-7-cluster-bifurcation]]) it falls into the **HM-A sub-block** (Q 40-42) characterised by HIGH rhyme entropy and structural-iʿjāz signature, in contrast to HM-B (Q 43-46) which is near-monorhyme.

Classical citations on the ḥawāmīm as a unit:
- **Ibn Masʿūd** (via al-Ḥākim, in al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān*, nawʿ 17 PageV01P200): *al-ḥawāmīm dībāj al-Qurʾān* — "the ḥawāmīm are the brocade of the Qurʾān".
- **Ibn ʿAbbās** (via Abū ʿUbayd al-Qāsim b. Sallām, *Faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān*; cited in Ibn Kathīr, opening of Sūrat Ghāfir): *li-kulli shayʾin lubābun, wa-lubābu al-Qurʾāni al-ḥawāmīm* — "everything has a kernel, and the kernel of the Qurʾān is the ḥawāmīm".
- **Misʿar b. Kidām** (Ibn Kathīr, ibid.): called them *al-ʿarāʾis* — "the brides".
- **Muḥammad b. Sīrīn** (Ibn Kathīr, ibid.): disliked the plural *al-ḥawāmīm*; preferred *Āl Ḥā Mīm* — "the family of Ḥā Mīm".
- **Ibn Masʿūd** (Ibn Kathīr, ibid.): *idhā waqaʿta fī Āl Ḥā Mīm fa-qad waqaʿta fī rawḍātin ataʾannaqu fīhinna* — "when you fall into the family of Ḥā Mīm, you have entered gardens I delight in".

## 4. Distinctive features

- **Twin-name** (Ghāfir / al-Muʾmin) — exegetes pivot freely between them; *al-Muʾmin* is the more dominant name in early *fadāʾil* traditions.
- **Believer of Pharaoh's family** (Q 40:28-45) — the longest dramatic monologue of an unnamed Egyptian believer in the Qurʾān, addressing Pharaoh's court with cosmic-history examples (Nūḥ, ʿĀd, Thamūd, Yūsuf).
- **The cosmic prayer of the angels** (Q 40:7-9) — the only verse in the corpus describing the angelic *hamalat al-ʿarsh* (bearers of the Throne) explicitly interceding for believers.
- **Eschatological intensity**: Q 40:46 (*al-nāru yuʿraḍūna ʿalayhā ghuduwwan wa-ʿashiyyan*) — "the Fire, to which they are exposed morning and evening" — a verse classical scholars (al-Qurṭubī ad loc.) cite for *ʿadhāb al-qabr* (punishment of the grave).
- **Q 40:60** (*udʿūnī astajib lakum*) — "Call upon Me; I shall answer you" — one of the most-cited duʿāʾ verses in ḥadīth (Aḥmad, Tirmidhī).

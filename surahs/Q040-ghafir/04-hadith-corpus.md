## 6. Q 40 in faḍāʾil literature

Beyond the 9-book canonical search, the *faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān* literature (Abū ʿUbayd al-Qāsim b. Sallām; al-Bukhārī's chapter on *faḍl al-Qurʾān*) contains:
- Ibn ʿAbbās's *li-kulli shayʾin lubābun, wa-lubābu al-Qurʾāni al-ḥawāmīm* (cited via Ibn Kathīr from Abū ʿUbayd, not extracted in 9-book pull).
- Saʿīd b. Jubayr's *idhā waqaʿta fī Āl Ḥā Mīm fa-qad waqaʿta fī rawḍāt* (cited via Ibn Kathīr from Abū ʿUbayd).
- Misʿar b. Kidām's *al-ʿarāʾis* designation (also via faḍāʾil literature).

These are NOT in the 9-book canonical pull (verified absent); they are *faḍāʾil*-collection traditions and require separate source-pull. **Flagged as Q40-cluster-DATA-GAP** for future expansion.

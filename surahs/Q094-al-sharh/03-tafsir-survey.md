# Q 94 al-Sharḥ — Tafsīr Survey

Six classical commentaries surveyed from the on-disk corpus, each cited scholar + work + passage. The
two exegetical pivots are (1) the *sharḥ al-ṣadr* of v 1 — read both as illumination-for-Islam and as
the Isrāʾ chest-opening miracle (*shaqq al-ṣadr*); and (2) the doubled assurance of vv 5-6 — on which
the tradition splits between a **taʾkīd** (emphatic-repetition) reading and a **definite-ʿusr /
indefinite-yusr "two-eases"** reading, with al-Jurjānī's grammatical objection preserved by al-Qurṭubī.

## 1. al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān* (on Q 94:1, 5-6)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-al-tabari/94/{1,5,6}.json`.

**On v 1** (`.../94/1.json`): al-Ṭabarī glosses *a-lam nashraḥ laka ṣadrak* as God reminding His Prophet
of His favors (*mudhakkir ālāʾahu ʿindahu*), inciting gratitude — *"a-lam nashraḥ laka — yā Muḥammad —
li-l-hudā wa-l-īmān bi-llāh wa-maʿrifat al-ḥaqq — ṣadrak — fa-nulayyin laka qalbak wa-najʿaluhu wiʿāʾ
li-l-ḥikma"* (We softened your heart and made it a vessel for wisdom). His v-1 reading is the
**spiritual/cognitive** expansion (faith, guidance, wisdom), not the physical chest-opening.

**On vv 5-6** (`.../94/5.json`, `.../94/6.json` — al-Ṭabarī treats them together): he reads the hardship
as the Prophet's *jihād* against the mushrikūn and the ease as God's promised relief/victory (*rajāʾan
wa-farajan bi-an yuẓfirakahum*). He then transmits the famous marfūʿ-attributed report **"lan yaghliba
ʿusrun yusrayn"** ("one hardship will not overcome two eases") through **al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī** by several
chains — al-Muʿtamir b. Sulaymān ← Yūnus; Ibn ʿUlayya ← Yūnus; ʿAwf al-Aʿrābī; and ʿAbd al-Aʿlā ← Ibn
Thawr ← Maʿmar (al-Ḥasan: *"kharaja al-nabī yawman masrūran fariḥan wa-huwa yaḍḥak wa-huwa yaqūl: lan
yaghliba ʿusrun yusrayn"*) — plus **Qatāda** (same wording). He adds **Ibn Masʿūd**: *"law dakhala
al-ʿusru fī juḥrin la-jāʾa al-yusru ḥattā yadkhula ʿalayhi"* ("if hardship entered a hole, ease would
come in after it"); and **Mujāhid**: *"yatbaʿu al-yusru al-ʿusra"* (ease follows hardship).

**Verification note.** al-Ṭabarī presents *lan yaghliba ʿusrun yusrayn* via al-Ḥasan **mursal** (and
Qatāda *dhukira lanā*), i.e. without a connected Companion isnād to the Prophet — confirmed against the
9-book search in `04-hadith-corpus.md`, where the marfūʿ form is **absent** and only ʿUmar's letter
(Mālik Muwaṭṭaʾ #1007) carries the phrase.

## 2. al-Zamakhsharī, *al-Kashshāf* (on Q 94:1-4, 5-6)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/zamakhshari-kashshaf.openiti.raw.txt`, surah-94 block
(locator: the section header *"ألم نشرح لك صدرك (1)"*, and *"فإن مع العسر يسرا (5) إن مع العسر يسرا (6)"*).

**On vv 1-4:** the istifhām is *inkārī* (negative-rhetorical) and therefore **affirms** the expansion
(*"fa-aʿāda ithbāt al-sharḥ … fa-kaʾannahu qīla: sharaḥnā laka ṣadrak"*); hence the following verbs are
coordinated on the *meaning* ("we expanded") not the surface negative. He reads *sharaḥnā ṣadrak* as
"We widened it to hold the universality of prophethood and the call to both thaqalān," or "to bear the
afflictions of your people," citing al-Ḥasan (*muliʾa ḥikmatan wa-ʿilman*). On *rafaʿnā … dhikrak*: the
Prophet's name is conjoined to God's in the shahāda, adhān, iqāma, tashahhud, khuṭab. He notes the
**fā-ʾida of *laka*** (the *ibhām thumma īḍāḥ* device — "did We not expand *for you*…" first hints there
is something expanded, then *ṣadrak* clarifies it).

**On vv 5-6:** the celebrated *fa-in qulta … qultu* dialectic. (a) *How does v 5 connect to what
precedes?* — the mushrikūn taunted the Prophet and believers with poverty; the verse reassures: "We
granted you what We granted; do not despair of God's grace, for **with the hardship you are in there is
ease**." (b) *What is the maʿiyya (accompaniment) of ease and hardship?* — God will bring ease soon after
the hardship; the imminent ease is rendered "as if joined" to the hardship, *for consolation and
strengthening of hearts*. (c) *What of "lan yaghliba ʿusrun yusrayn"?* — al-Zamakhsharī gives the precise
grammar: the **definite** *al-ʿusr*, repeated, is one-and-the-same (like *jāʾanī Zaydun Zaydun*; whether
the *al-* is ʿahd or jins, "huwa huwa"); the **indefinite** *yusran*, on a fresh (non-coordinated)
restart, denotes a *different* portion of the genus — hence **two eases**. (d) *What are the two eases?*
— either the conquests in the Prophet's days + the conquests in the Caliphs' days, or **dunyā-ease +
ākhira-ease** (cf. *iḥdā al-ḥusnayayn*, Q 9:52). (e) *Why the indefinite?* — **tafkhīm** (magnification:
"a *mighty* ease, and what an ease!").

## 3. al-Qurṭubī, *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān* (on Q 94:1, 5)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/94/{1,5}.json`.

**On v 1** (`.../94/1.json`): heads the surah *"Sūrat A-lam Nashraḥ, makkiyya fī qawl al-jamīʿ, wa-hiya
thamānī āyāt"* (Meccan by all accounts; eight verses). *Sharḥ al-ṣadr = fatḥuhu* (opening it for Islam);
cites Abū Ṣāliḥ ← Ibn ʿAbbās (*a-lam nulayyin laka qalbak*), al-Ḍaḥḥāk ← Ibn ʿAbbās (the "signs of an
expanded breast" report), and al-Ḥasan (*muliʾa ḥukman wa-ʿilman*). Crucially he then cites the
**Isrāʾ chest-opening (*shaqq al-ṣadr*) ḥadīth "fī al-Ṣaḥīḥ" via Anas ← Mālik b. Ṣaʿṣaʿa** (the basin of
gold / Zamzam water / heart washed and refilled with faith and wisdom) — verified on disk as Muslim #321
and al-Tirmidhī #3430 (Book of Tafsīr, *ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ*); see `04-hadith-corpus.md`. He grounds *a-lam
nashraḥ* = *qad sharaḥnā* grammatically: the coordinated *wa-waḍaʿnā* (perfect, not jussive) shows the
sense is past-affirmative; *lam* is a *jaḥd*, and a *jaḥd* inside an *istifhām* returns to **affirmation**
(cf. *a-laysa Allāhu bi-aḥkam al-ḥākimīn*).

**On v 5** (`.../94/5.json`): the fullest classical treatment in the survey. al-Qurṭubī lays out the two
readings of the repetition:
- **taʾkīd / emphatic-repetition** (al-Farrāʾ): the second *inna maʿa al-ʿusri yusrā* reinforces the
  first, as one says *irmi irmi*, *aʿjil aʿjil*; cf. *kallā sawfa taʿlamūn thumma kallā sawfa taʿlamūn*
  (Q 102:3-4).
- **definite-is-same / indefinite-is-other** (Thaʿlab): the Arabs, repeating a *maʿrifa* (definite) noun,
  mean the same referent; repeating a *nakira* (indefinite), mean a *different* one — hence two eases,
  "stronger for hope, more rousing to patience." He attaches **Ibn ʿAbbās** (*"khalaqtu ʿusran wāḥidan
  wa-khalaqtu yusrayn, wa-lan yaghliba ʿusrun yusrayn"*), the marfūʿ report *"lan yaghliba ʿusrun
  yusrayn,"* **Ibn Masʿūd** ("if the hardship were in a rock, ease would seek it out"), and **ʿUmar's
  letter to Abū ʿUbayda b. al-Jarrāḥ** containing the phrase (= Mālik Muwaṭṭaʾ #1007, verified on disk).
- **al-Jurjānī's objection** (*qawl madkhūl*): on the two-eases logic one would have to say that *inna
  maʿa al-fārisi sayfan, inna maʿa al-fārisi sayfan* yields **one rider, two swords** — a *reductio*.
  al-Qurṭubī's preferred resolution: the hardship is the mushrikūn's taunting of the Prophet's poverty;
  the **first** ease is *dunyā* relief (fulfilled — the Ḥijāz and Yemen opened to him); the **second**,
  introduced by the **fāʾ-less restart (*ibtidāʾ*)**, is *ākhira* ease, a general promise to all
  believers. **"al-dalīl ʿalā ibtidāʾihi taʿarrīhi min fāʾ aw wāw aw ghayrihā min ḥurūf al-nasaq"** —
  the syntactic proof of the fresh start is precisely v 6's *being stripped of the connective fāʾ/wāw.*

This last point is the classical correlate of the project's Q094-F-01 (the single-fāʾ delta carries the
exegetical weight): al-Qurṭubī reads the very absence of the connective in v 6 as the marker of a
**second, distinct** ease.

## 4. Ibn Kathīr, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm* (on Q 94:1-8)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/en-tafisr-ibn-kathir/94/5.json` (English) +
`ar-tafsir-ibn-kathir/94/5.json` (Arabic, for the grammar formula).

Ibn Kathīr runs the whole surah in one section: v 1 *sharḥ al-ṣadr* = illumination + the Law made vast
and easy (cross-citing Q 6:125); **v 2 (*waḍaʿnā ʿanka wizrak*) glossed by Q 48:2** (*li-yaghfira laka
Allāhu mā taqaddama min dhanbika wa-mā taʾakhkhar*) — the burden as the forgiven matter; v 3 *anqaḍa
ẓahrak* (the weight that made the back creak, *al-inqāḍ = al-ṣawt*); **v 4 (*rafaʿnā … dhikrak*)** via
Mujāhid ("I am not mentioned but you are mentioned with Me — *ashhadu an lā ilāha illā Allāh wa-anna
Muḥammadan rasūl Allāh*") and Qatāda (the name raised in this life and the next, in every khuṭba,
shahāda, and ṣalāh).

**On vv 5-6** the Arabic gives the exact grammatical formula: *"wa-maʿnā hādhā anna al-ʿusra muʿarraf fī
al-ḥālayn fa-huwa mufrad, wa-l-yusra munakkar fa-taʿaddad … fa-l-ʿusru al-awwalu ʿaynu al-thānī wa-l-yusru
taʿaddad"* — **the hardship is definite in both states (hence singular/one), the ease is indefinite (hence
multiplied); the first hardship is the very same as the second, while the ease is plural.** He cites the
al-Ḥasan/Qatāda transmissions of *lan yaghliba ʿusrun yusrayn*, then **Abū Hurayra** (al-Ḥasan b. Sufyān
… ← Abū al-Zinād ← Abū Ṣāliḥ ← Abū Hurayra: *"unzila al-maʿūnatu mina al-samāʾi ʿalā qadri al-maʾūna,
wa-nazala al-ṣabru ʿalā qadri al-muṣība"*), and **al-Shāfiʿī's** verses (*ṣabran jamīlan mā aqraba
al-faraja*). **On vv 7-8** (English): *fa-idhā faraghta fa-nṣab* = "when you have finished your worldly
affairs, rise to worship with zeal," *wa-ilā rabbika fa-rghab* = direct your hope and intention to your
Lord alone; he attaches the *muttafaq ʿalayh* prayer-etiquette ḥadīth *lā ṣalāta bi-ḥaḍrati al-ṭaʿām…*

## 5. al-Jalālayn, *Tafsīr al-Jalālayn* (on Q 94:5-6)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/en-al-jalalayn/94/{5,6}.json`.

Terse and representative of the *prophet's-hardship → assisted-victory* reading: v 5 "For truly with
hardship comes ease"; v 6 "Truly with hardship comes ease — **the Prophet ﷺ suffered much hardship at
the hands of the disbelievers but then he enjoyed ease when he was assisted to victory by God over them.**"
al-Jalālayn does not develop the two-eases grammar; it takes the maʿiyya as the historical hardship/relief
of the Meccan period — the same frame as al-Ṭabarī and al-Jalālayn's *Kashshāf* source.

## 6. Tanwīr al-Miqbās (attrib. Ibn ʿAbbās) (on Q 94:5-6)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/en-tafsir-ibn-abbas/94/{5,6}.json`.

The brief *tafsīr-by-gloss* tradition: v 5 — God consoling His Prophet "about his poverty and the
hardships he faced," *"with hardship goeth ease"*; v 6 likewise. It frames the doubled assurance as
direct **consolation for poverty/hardship** — consistent with al-Qurṭubī's preferred occasion (the
mushrikūn's poverty-taunt) and with the al-Ḍuḥā ↔ al-Sharḥ consolation-pair reading.

## 7. Points of agreement / disagreement

| Question | al-Ṭabarī | al-Zamakhsharī | al-Qurṭubī | Ibn Kathīr | al-Jalālayn | Tanwīr |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| v 1 *sharḥ al-ṣadr* | spiritual (faith/wisdom) | spiritual + capacity | spiritual **+ Isrāʾ shaqq al-ṣadr** | spiritual (Law made easy) | (n/a) | consolation |
| vv 5-6 repetition | hardship=jihād, ease=victory | **two-eases** (grammar) + tafkhīm | **taʾkīd (Farrāʾ) vs two-eases (Thaʿlab)** + al-Jurjānī objection | **two-eases** (*muʿarraf/munakkar* formula) | hardship→assisted victory | consolation for poverty |
| *lan yaghliba ʿusrun yusrayn* | al-Ḥasan **mursal** + Qatāda + Ibn Masʿūd + Mujāhid | cites it (Ibn ʿAbbās/Ibn Masʿūd) | Ibn ʿAbbās + marfūʿ + Ibn Masʿūd + **ʿUmar's letter** | al-Ḥasan/Qatāda + Abū Hurayra | — | — |
| the two eases | (relief/victory) | conquests-then-Caliphs OR dunyā+ākhira | dunyā-ease (fulfilled) + ākhira-ease (general) | dunyā/ākhira multiplicity | — | — |
| v 6's dropped fāʾ | (untreated) | non-coordinated restart → different portion | **ibtidāʾ; the *taʿarrī min fāʾ* IS the proof of a 2nd ease** | (untreated) | — | — |

The defining exegetical fault-line is the **vv 5-6 repetition**: the *taʾkīd* school (al-Farrāʾ) reads
one ease emphatically restated; the *two-eases* school (Thaʿlab, Ibn ʿAbbās, al-Zamakhsharī, Ibn Kathīr)
reads the definite-ʿusr / indefinite-yusr asymmetry as encoding one hardship and two eases; and
al-Jurjānī (preserved by al-Qurṭubī) objects on grammatical grounds. The empirically-relevant element —
that the two readings hinge on the **single-fāʾ orthographic delta** and the **definite/indefinite
asymmetry** — is exactly what Q094-F-01 Arm C verifies as an exact textual fact (see `06-novel-findings.md`).

## 8. Honest limits

- al-Zamakhsharī is read from the full OpenITI raw concatenation, not a clean per-surah extraction; the
  surah-94 block was located by the verse-text section headers and read in context.
- The *theological* "two eases" inference (which the two-eases school draws) is out of project scope
  (Protocol §10); the tafsīr survey reports the positions; only the orthographic substrate is tested.
- al-Suyūṭī's *al-Durr al-manthūr* and al-Ṭabarsī's *Majmaʿ al-bayān* are on disk
  (`raw/suyuti-durr-manthur.openiti.raw.txt`, `raw/tabarsi-majma-bayan.openiti.raw.txt`) but not surveyed
  in depth here; al-Suyūṭī's chains for *lan yaghliba ʿusrun yusrayn* are queued if the isnād map needs
  extending.

# Q 6 al-Anʿām — Tafsīr Survey

Six classical mufassirūn surveyed on Q 6's structurally-significant blocks. Citations to OpenITI raw extracts in `data/literature/classical-tafsir/`.

## 1. al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān* (d. 310 AH)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/tabari-jami-al-bayan.openiti.raw.txt` (search "الأنعام" / Q 6 chapter).

### Key positions

- **Q 6:1 *al-ḥamd lillāh***: al-Ṭabarī begins with the Meccan-revelation tradition (Ibn ʿAbbās via al-Ḥākim chain): the entire surah descended at Mecca in one instalment. Notes the dispute over vv. 91, 151-153 being Madanī.
- **Q 6:84 the prophet-list**: al-Ṭabarī works through each name — Isḥāq, Yaʿqūb, Nūḥ, Dāwūd, Sulaymān, Ayyūb, Yūsuf, Mūsā, Hārūn — explicating that "Nūḥ" is mentioned BEFORE the Davidic line because Nūḥ predates Ibrāhīm's line; *min qabl* ("from before") refers to Nūḥ's pre-Abrahamic priority.
- **Q 6:103 *lā tudrikuhu al-abṣār***: al-Ṭabarī cites the major dispute: (a) *al-abṣār* refers to physical eyes — vision cannot encompass Him in this life; (b) the verse permits eschatological vision (the Beatific Vision tradition, ʿĀʾisha's negation in [[04-hadith-corpus|Bukhārī #4855, #7380]] vs. Ibn ʿAbbās's affirmation in al-Tirmidhī #3279). al-Ṭabarī's preference is for the Sunnī-orthodox reading: vision in this world is impossible, in the next life (per *wujūh nāḍira* of Q 75:23) is possible — the verse covers this-life only.
- **Q 6:151-153 Decalogue parallel**: al-Ṭabarī catalogs the 10 commandments in the verse: (1) shirk-prohibition, (2) parents-honoring, (3) child-killing prohibition, (4) immorality-shunning, (5) life-protection, (6) orphan-property protection, (7) honest measure, (8) speech-integrity, (9) covenant-fulfillment, (10) following the straight path.

## 2. al-Rāzī, *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* (d. 606 AH)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/razi-mafatih-al-ghayb.openiti.raw.txt` (Q 6 introduction + verse-commentary).

### Key positions

- **Q 6:1 four-fold *al-ḥamd*-typology**: al-Rāzī develops the al-ḥamd-opening typology (5 surahs total — Q 1, 6, 18, 34, 35). Q 6 honors God for **horizontal creation** (heavens-earth-darkness-light); Q 18 for **vertical revelation**; Q 34 for **eschatological dominion**; Q 35 for **angelic mediation**. Q 1 is the prayer-formulation. Q 6's *al-ḥamd* is the cosmological one — mapping God's creation of physical opposites (light/darkness).
- **Q 6:103 *iʿjāz al-tawḥīd***: al-Rāzī cites this as the *iʿjāz al-tawḥīd* locus (following al-Bāqillānī) — the verse compresses the apophatic-cataphatic tawḥīd in 8 words: "vision does not grasp Him; He grasps vision; He is the Subtle, the All-Aware." al-Rāzī notes the chiasmic structure (negation-affirmation-attribute pair). [Empirically, this verse is the UNIQUE 4-cell verse in the entire Quran per [[Q006-F-05-q6v103-tawhid-ijaz-prereg|Q006-F-05]] — al-Bāqillānī's claim is quantitatively confirmed.]
- **Q 6:84-87 prophet-list**: al-Rāzī observes the order is **genealogical-thematic**, not chronological — Ibrāhīm-line first (Isḥāq, Yaʿqūb, the descendants), Nūḥ comes second as pre-genealogical-history, then minor prophets categorized by group (the four "righteous" in v. 85, the four with-special-preference in v. 86). al-Rāzī's reading gives empirical anchoring: Q 6's order is *al-ḥujja*-genealogical, not chronological.

## 3. al-Qurṭubī, *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān* (d. 671 AH)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/qurtubi-jami-li-ahkam.openiti.raw.txt` (Q 6 chapter).

### Key positions

- **Q 6:1 *al-ḥamd-lillāh-alladhī khalaqa al-samāwāti wa-l-arḍa wa-jaʿala al-ẓulumāti wa-l-nūr***: al-Qurṭubī focuses on the *al-ẓulumāt wa-l-nūr* (darknesses-and-light) — emphasizing the singular *nūr* against plural *ẓulumāt* — interpreting it as: divine-truth is one (singular *nūr*), human-falsehoods are many (plural *ẓulumāt*).
- **Q 6:103 *al-Laṭīf al-Khabīr* paired-attribute**: al-Qurṭubī cites the kalāmī tradition that *al-Laṭīf* (the Subtle) signifies divine action that human reason cannot fully grasp; *al-Khabīr* signifies divine knowledge (omniscience). The pairing in this verse is the *iʿjāz al-tawḥīd* of attribute-completeness.
- **Q 6:138-144 livestock polemic**: al-Qurṭubī gives the most detailed *aḥkām* (rulings) commentary on the surah — the cattle/sheep/goat/camel pair-classification and the *jāhilī* prohibitions are systematically cataloged. The 8-paired-creature scheme (4 species × 2 sexes = 8 pairs in v. 143-144) is a polemical refutation of pre-Islamic gender-based sacralization.

## 4. Ibn Kathīr, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm* (d. 774 AH)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/ibn-kathir.openiti.raw.txt` (Q 6 chapter; Darussalam English edition partial).

### Key positions

- **Q 6:1 *al-ḥamd***: Ibn Kathīr opens with the Ibn ʿAbbās tradition (block-revelation with 70,000 angels) — see [[04-hadith-corpus]] for chain audit; Ibn Kathīr cites the tradition without grading it.
- **Q 6:84-87 prophet-list**: Ibn Kathīr provides genealogical commentary linking each prophet to Ibrāhīm's line, noting the inclusion of Lūṭ (cousin/nephew of Ibrāhīm) and the four "righteous" in v. 85 with hadith-cross-references on each prophet's biography.
- **Q 6:103 ʿĀʾisha tradition**: Ibn Kathīr cites ʿĀʾisha's negation of the Prophet's beatific vision (al-Bukhārī #4855 / #7380, al-Tirmidhī #3068) as the strongest reading; allows the Ibn ʿAbbās affirmation (heart-vision) as a minority position.
- **Q 6:151-153 Decalogue**: Ibn Kathīr names this passage *al-mathāqil* ("the weighty matters") and notes Ibn Masʿūd's tradition (al-Tirmidhī #3070 in the Q006-citations.json file): "Whoever wishes to look at the document with Muhammad's seal, let him recite these verses."

## 5. al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān* + *al-Durr al-manthūr* (d. 911 AH)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/suyuti-itqan.openiti.raw.txt` and `suyuti-durr.openiti.raw.txt`.

### Key positions

- **Meccan-with-exceptions chronology**: al-Suyūṭī (*al-Itqān*, nawʿ 1 *Makkī wa-Madanī*) gives the Ibn ʿAbbās tradition (Q 6 = #55 in revelation order) and the dispute over vv. 91, 151-153 being Madanī. Notes Ibn al-Ḥaṣṣār's conclusion that no nine-exception report is properly attested.
- **70,000-angel-procession tradition**: al-Suyūṭī (*al-Itqān*, nawʿ 14) catalogs the 5 surahs/passages with angelic procession (Q 1: 80,000; Q 6: 70,000; Q 36: 30,000; Q 2:255: 30,000; Q 16:36: 20,000). Notes Ibn al-Ṣalāḥ's chain-weakness verdict and al-Dhahabī's *aẓunnuhu mawḍūʿan* ("I think it is fabricated"). See [[04-hadith-corpus]] §3 for full chain audit.
- **Q 6:103 verses-paired-by-meaning**: al-Suyūṭī (*al-Durr al-manthūr*) catalogs cross-references between Q 6:103 and Q 75:22-23 (*wujūh yawmaʾidhin nāḍira ilā rabbihā nāẓira* — "faces on that day shall be radiant, gazing toward their Lord"). The pairing forms the eschatological-vs-this-worldly tawḥīd-vision typology.
- **Q 6:151-153 *al-mathāqil***: al-Suyūṭī cites Ibn Masʿūd via al-Tirmidhī #3070 confirming this is the Decalogue passage.

## 6. al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-Durar fī Tanāsub al-Āyāt wa-l-Suwar* (d. 885 AH)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/biqai-nazm-al-durar.pdf` (vol. 7, Q 6 chapter).

### Key positions

- **Q 5 → Q 6 → Q 7 munāsabah triad**: al-Biqāʿī reads Q 5 (legal-Medinan, food-rulings) as transitioning into Q 6 (Meccan-creedal, anti-polytheism) by way of the **food-and-livestock theme** continuation. Q 5 has the *baḥīra-sāʾiba-waṣīla-ḥām* hapaxes; Q 6 develops the polemic with general livestock-vocabulary. Q 6 → Q 7 transitions from creedal-Meccan (*al-ḥujja*) into narrative-Meccan (the prophet-cycles of Q 7). [The empirical signature: Q 5 → Q 6 cost rank 72/113 (moderate); Q 6 → Q 7 cost rank 103/113 (FREE) — confirming al-Biqāʿī's *munāsabah* triad.]
- **The 11-block *naẓm* structure**: al-Biqāʿī's *naẓm* divides Q 6 into 11 sub-arguments around the central *al-ḥujja* of vv. 74-90 (Ibrāhīm-cycle + prophet-list). See [[02-content-analysis]] §1 for the block table.
- **Q 6:84 within the *al-ḥujja***: al-Biqāʿī reads the prophet-list as the *exemplification* of the *ḥujja* (proof) given to Ibrāhīm in v. 83 — not a chronological succession but a *demonstrative roll-call* of all the inheritors of Ibrāhīm's tawḥīd-discovery.

## 7. Convergence and divergence

### Convergence (all 6 mufassirūn agree)

1. Q 6 is **predominantly Meccan** with disputed vv. 91, 151-153 being possibly Madanī.
2. Q 6:84-87 prophet-list is **genealogical-creedal**, not chronological.
3. Q 6:103 *lā tudrikuhu al-abṣār* is the **iʿjāz al-tawḥīd** locus (al-Bāqillānī's reading) and the **this-world-vision-impossibility** verse (orthodox reading).
4. Q 6:151-153 is the **Decalogue passage** (al-mathāqil per al-Suyūṭī, Ibn Masʿūd via al-Tirmidhī #3070).

### Divergence

1. **Number of Madanī exceptions**: 0 (Ibn ʿAbbās via al-Ṭabarī), 3 (vv. 151-153, Ibn ʿAbbās via al-Suyūṭī), 9 (some chains, rejected by Ibn al-Ḥaṣṣār), or 2 (Q 6:91 + one other, al-Kalbī).
2. **Q 6:103 vision-paradox**: ʿĀʾisha's negation (al-Bukhārī #4855 / Muslim #344, Ibn Kathīr's preferred) vs. Ibn ʿAbbās's heart-vision affirmation (al-Tirmidhī #3279).
3. **Block-structure**: al-Biqāʿī's 11 blocks vs. al-Rāzī's 4-major-cycle reading vs. modern reconstructions.

## 9. Honest limits

- The 6-mufassir survey covers only Sunnī-Sufī mainstream commentaries; Ibādī, Shīʿī, Muʿtazilī Q 6 commentaries (e.g., al-Zamakhsharī's *Kashshāf*) are not separately surveyed (al-Zamakhsharī largely agrees with the Sunnī mainstream on Q 6's structure, with minor Muʿtazilī flourishes on Q 6:103's anthropomorphism-rejection).
- Tafsir-positions are classified by their formal claims; rhetorical and balagha analyses (e.g., al-Khaṭṭābī, al-Sakkākī) on Q 6 are treated separately in [[05-classical-claims-audit]].
- The "convergence" reading is a project-internal synthesis; classical commentators didn't necessarily share a single typology.

# Q 6 al-Anʿām — Content Analysis

## 1. Major thematic blocks (165 verses)

Q 6 is structurally one of the most discussed surahs in the *naẓm al-Qurʾān* tradition. al-Biqāʿī (*Naẓm al-Durar*, vol. 7) reads it as one extended argument (*ḥujja*) against polytheism, with 11 sub-cycles. The block-structure given here follows al-Biqāʿī's *naẓm* with empirical confirmation of theme-shift verse-boundaries.

## 2. The Q 6:83-87 *al-ḥujja* genealogical-creedal block (vv. 83-90)

This is **the corpus-rank-1 LIST-FORM prophet-density block** (CONFIRMED by Q006-F-01 Cell B densest-5-window: rank 1/49; Q 6:84 alone has 9 prophet-name lemmas — rank 2/49 on max-tokens-in-verse, narrowly behind Q 4:163 which has 10 in a similar parallel listing).

The structural logic:
- **v. 83**: closing of the Ibrāhīm-narrative (vv. 74-82), introducing "this is Our argument (*ḥujja*) which We gave to Ibrāhīm against his people."
- **v. 84**: "And We gave him Isḥāq and Yaʿqūb; all We guided. And Nūḥ We guided before; and from his progeny: Dāwūd, Sulaymān, Ayyūb, Yūsuf, Mūsā, Hārūn — thus do We reward the doers of good." [9 prophet-lemmas in one verse]
- **v. 85**: "And Zakariyyā, Yaḥyā, ʿĪsā, and Ilyās — all of the righteous." [4 prophets]
- **v. 86**: "And Ismāʿīl, al-Yasaʿ, Yūnus, and Lūṭ — and to all We gave preference over the worlds." [4 prophets]
- **v. 87**: "And from their fathers, descendants, and brothers — We chose them and guided them to a straight path."
- **v. 89**: "Those are the ones to whom We gave the Book, judgment, and prophecy." (the prophets just named)
- **v. 90**: "Those are the ones whom Allāh has guided, so by their guidance be guided." (Prophetic continuity)

**Architectural function**: this is the *al-ḥujja* (proof) block — the foundational tawḥīd-evidence list, structurally homologous to creedal documents like the Apostles' Creed or the early-Christian baptismal formula. The list's structural compactness (5 verses, 18 lemma-tokens) is the LIST-FORM peak per [[Q006-F-01-prophet-density-per-verse-prereg|Q006-F-01]].

## 3. Repetition patterns

Q 6 has notable formulaic repetition:

- ***Qul* anaphora** — over 30 occurrences of "Say..." (vv. 14, 19, 40, 46, 47, 50, 51, 56, 57, 63, 64, 65, 66, 71, 90, 91, 109, 114, 119, 145, 147, 148, 149, 150, 151, 158, 161, 162, 163, 164). Q 6 is one of the corpus's *qul*-densest surahs (along with Q 109, Q 112, Q 113, Q 114).
- ***Lā ilāha illā huwa*** — divine-singularity formulae (Q 6 Cell A in [[Q006-F-03-tawhid-density-prereg|Q006-F-03]] = 24 hits, **rank 1/114** raw count).
- *The "did you not see" / *a-lam tara* polemic* (corpus-distinctive opener for rebuke-clauses).
- The "make for Allāh a portion / make for Allāh equals" anti-polytheism formulae (vv. 22, 100, 136-138).

## 4. Vocabulary distinctness

- Q 6 mean_content_distance = 1.068 (corpus z-score = +1.43) — Q 6's vocabulary is **more dispersed** than the corpus average. Long-Meccan polemic vocabulary (apologetic + creedal + legal-ethical + livestock-polemic) is heterogeneous by design.
- The eponymous *anʿām* (livestock) cluster {anʿām, ḍaʾn, maʿz, ibl, baqar} is **corpus-MAX** in Q 6 (Q006-F-02 Cell A = 10 tokens, rank 1/114; Cell B density = 0.30/100w, rank 1/3 of eligible).
- Q 6's distinctive 5-cluster livestock-vocabulary (vv. 138-144) is one of the surah's most lexically-distinctive features. The four named *jāhilī* categories (*baḥīra, sāʾiba, waṣīla, ḥām*) are NOT in Q 6 — they appear ONLY in Q 5:103 (Q 5 hapaxes per [[00-overview]] §8). Q 6 develops the polemic via general vocabulary (*ḥajr, muḥarram, fartharā*).

## 5. Cross-surah content references

- Q 6 contains the **Decalogue parallel** (vv. 151-153) — classically read alongside Exodus 20 / Deuteronomy 5. al-Suyūṭī (*al-Itqān* nawʿ on Madanī-revealed Q 6 verses) cites Ibn ʿAbbās's exception that vv. 151-153 are Madanī (the rest of Q 6 is Meccan).
- Q 6 cross-references with Q 4:163 (parallel prophet-roll-call): "We have inspired you as We inspired Nūḥ and the prophets after him, and We inspired Ibrāhīm, Ismāʿīl, Isḥāq, Yaʿqūb, the descendants, ʿĪsā, Ayyūb, Yūnus, Hārūn, and Sulaymān; and to Dāwūd We gave the Psalms" — 10 prophets in one verse, the corpus rank-1 max-tokens-in-single-verse per Q006-F-01 Cell A.
- Q 6 cross-references with Q 21 al-Anbiyāʾ (narrative-form prophet-MAX) — see [[Q006-F-04-q6-q21-antipodal-prereg|Q006-F-04]] for FR-distance test.
- Q 6:91's *mā anzala llāhu ʿalā basharin min shayʾ* polemic responds to a Madanī Jewish interlocutor; cross-references with Q 5 (al-Māʾida) and Q 9 (al-Tawba) interfaith-polemic.

## 6. Content register (summary)

**Q 6 al-Anʿām is a sustained creedal-polemical *ḥujja*-discourse**:
- Sustained tawḥīd-anti-polytheism argumentation (Blocks 2-6, 9).
- Ibrāhīm-cycle as patriarchal-tawḥīd founding (Block 7).
- 18-prophet *al-ḥujja* roll-call (Block 8) as cumulative tawḥīd-evidence.
- Eponymous anti-livestock-superstition polemic (Block 10).
- Decalogue parallel as ethical-tawḥīd codex (Block 11).
- First-of-Muslims personal-confession + individual-responsibility coda (Block 12).

The register is **creedal-genealogical** in Block 8, **legal-ethical** in Blocks 10-11, and **theological-cosmological** in Blocks 1, 5, 9. The surah is sometimes called *Sūrat al-Ḥujja* (the Sūra of the Argument) by classical commentators (al-Rāzī, *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* on Q 6:1).

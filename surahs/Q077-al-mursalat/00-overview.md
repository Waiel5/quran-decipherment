## 2. Classical name

The surah is named **al-Mursalāt** after the opening word of v.1, *wa-l-mursalāti ʿurfā*. Arabic *mursalāt* is the passive-feminine-plural participle of root r-s-l ("to send"). Classical disagreement on the referent (al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, al-Bāqillānī) — see §3 below.

## 3. Opening formula — the 5-element interleaved oath-cluster

Q 77:1-5 (no-tashkeel):

| v | Text | Gloss | Letter-count |

| 1 | والمرسلات عرفا | *By those sent forth in succession* (or "in waves of mercy") | 13 |

The 5-element oath has **two architectural signatures** corpus-EXACT (per Q077-F-02 PASS-DIRECTED PARTIAL 3/4):

1. **Interleaved wa/fa-particle pattern**: wa-fa-wa-fa-fa (corpus-unique among 5-element oath clusters; Q 79 is wa-wa-wa-fa-fa, Q 100 is wa-fa-fa-fa-fa, Q 51 is wa-fa-fa-fa with only 4 elements).
2. **Uniform-letter rhythm**: each of vv 1-5 is **EXACTLY 13 letters (no-tashkeel, no-space)**. Corpus-wide, only TWO surahs achieve 5 consecutive opener verses of identical letter-count: **Q 77 and Q 79** (also a 5-element oath, also 13-letter uniform). This is a corpus-EXACT 2-set rhythmic-architectural signature at the metric × oath-cluster intersection.

### Classical disagreement on the referent (al-Ṭabarī ad loc.; al-Rāzī *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb*, vol. 30 p. 189-194; al-Suyūṭī *al-Itqān* nawʿ 67 on al-aqsām):

- **angels in their five missions** (al-Ṭabarī, Ibn ʿAbbās majority view): the angels sent forth as *mercy / wind / scripture / decisive-distinguisher / reminder-casters*.
- **winds (rīḥ) in five modes** (Ibn Masʿūd minority): wind sent in waves → wind storming → wind scattering rain → wind distinguishing wholesome from corrupt → wind casting down memory of the heedless.
- **prophetic emissaries**: the messengers themselves sent in succession (al-Rāzī's preferred multi-voice reading).
- **a deliberately ambiguous 5-fold reference** (al-Bāqillānī iʿjāz reading): the referent is left grammatically open so that all three classical interpretations operate simultaneously — a stylistic feature of late-Meccan / mid-Meccan oath-openers.

al-Rāzī notes the parallel with Q 79 al-Nāziʿāt (5 oaths, similar interleaved active-feminine-plural-participle-with-cognate-accusative); see Q077-F-02 for the empirical 13-letter rhythm signature shared between Q 77 and Q 79.

## 11. ⭐ The Q 77:50 closing — verbatim cross-link to Q 7:185

Q 77 closes with a 4-word rhetorical question:

> فبأي حديث بعده يؤمنون
> *fa-bi-ayyi ḥadīthin baʿdahu yuʾminūn*
> "Then in what statement after this will they believe?"

The ENTIRE Q 77:50 is **verbatim-identical to the last 4 tokens of Q 7:185** — the only such corpus-rare verbatim cross-link involving the *fa-bi-ayyi ḥadīthin baʿdahu yuʾminūn* construction. Q 7:185 (mid-Meccan, al-Aʿrāf) ends:

> أولم ينظروا في ملكوت السماوات والأرض وما خلق الله من شيء وأن عسى أن يكون قد اقترب أجلهم ۖ فبأي حديث بعده يؤمنون
> "Have they not looked into the dominion of the heavens and the earth … then in what statement after this will they believe?"

This forms a **mid-Meccan ↔ early-Meccan rhetorical-question macro-link**. It also embeds Q 77 in the broader *fa-bi-ayyi* corpus-cluster:
- Q 7:185, Q 45:6 — *fa-bi-ayyi ḥadīthin*
- Q 53:55 — *fa-bi-ayyi ālāʾi rabbika tatamārā*
- **Q 55 (× 31) — *fa-bi-ayyi ālāʾi rabbikumā tukadhdhibān*** (the corpus-MAX refrain)
- Q 77:50 — *fa-bi-ayyi ḥadīthin baʿdahu yuʾminūn*

The Q 77:50 closing thus simultaneously (a) bridges back to Q 7:185 verbatim, (b) syntactically echoes the Q 55 refrain-question structure, and (c) inverts the surah's own 10× refrain — where the refrain ACCUSES the deniers ("woe to them"), the closing INTERROGATES them ("what will they believe in?"). A doubled rhetorical-question architecture: *waylun yawmaʾidhin li-l-mukadhdhibīn* (vv 15, 19, 24, ..., 49) + *fa-bi-ayyi ḥadīthin baʿdahu yuʾminūn* (v 50).

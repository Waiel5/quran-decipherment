## 2. Classical name

The surah is named **al-Munāfiqūn** after the lexical-anchor of its content — the *munāfiqūn* (hypocrites) attested as a lemma 4 times in 11 verses (corpus-MAX density: 2.21 per 100 words; 46.5× the corpus mean; **rank 1/114**, see §6 + 01-empirical-profile.md). The surah is a topical-monograph on the rhetoric and political behavior of the Medinan hypocrites, anchored on a specific historical event: the speech of **ʿAbd Allāh ibn Ubayy ibn Salūl** during *ghazwat banī al-Muṣṭaliq* / al-Muraysīʿ (5 AH or 6 AH per al-Wāqidī / Ibn Isḥāq), reported by **Zayd ibn Arqam**. See 03-tafsir-survey.md §1 for the asbāb al-nuzūl chain.

The Arabic *munāfiq* is the active participle of bāb-III *nāfaqa* "to act hypocritically" — etymologically derived from *nafaq* "the burrow of the jerboa with two openings", whence the metaphor: the hypocrite has two openings (one of profession, one of escape). The naming-by-titular-subject (rather than by opener-particle, as in *wa-l-* oath surahs Q 37, 51, 91-103, or by muqaṭṭāʿ letters as in الم/طس/etc.) places Q 63 in al-Suyūṭī's *al-Itqān* category of *tasmiya bi-mawḍūʿ al-sūra* (naming by topical content), shared with Q 9 al-Tawba, Q 24 al-Nūr, Q 49 al-Ḥujurāt, etc. — predominantly Medinan legal/community surahs.

## 3. Opening formula — conditional *idhā* + asbāb-anchor

Q 63:1 (no-tashkeel):

> إذا جاءك المنافقون قالوا نشهد إنك لرسول الله والله يعلم إنك لرسوله والله يشهد إن المنافقين لكاذبون

Gloss: *When the hypocrites come to you they say: "We bear witness that you are indeed the Messenger of Allāh." And Allāh knows that you are indeed His Messenger, and Allāh bears witness that the hypocrites are indeed liars.*

The opener is **architecturally distinctive** in three ways:

1. **NON-musabbiḥa** — Q 63 sits sandwiched between Q 62 al-Jumuʿa (*yusabbiḥu li-llāhi mā fī al-samāwāt*, imperfect-musabbiḥa) and Q 64 al-Taghābun (*yusabbiḥu li-llāhi mā fī al-samāwāt*, imperfect-musabbiḥa). Q 63 is the **only musabbiḥāt-cluster member that does NOT open with a tasbīḥ form** — H-NEW-58c locked the imperfect-musabbiḥa pair (Q 62 + Q 64) at exactly 0 cross-tense character-prefix overlap with the perfect set (Q 57, 59, 61). Q 63's NON-musabbiḥa interruption is therefore a deliberate cluster-sequencing choice, not an accidental break (see 06-falsifiable-tests.md Q063-F-03).

2. **CONDITIONAL *idhā* + present-tense report** — only 5 of 114 surahs open with *idhā* + a temporal-conditional clause (Q 56, 63, 81, 82, 99, 110). Of these, Q 63 alone names a **human social-class subject** (the others all open with cosmic / eschatological conditions: *al-wāqiʿa*, *al-shams kuwwirat*, *al-samāʾu nfaṭarat*, *al-ardu zulzilat*, *naṣr Allāhi wa-l-fatḥ*). Q 63's social-conditional opener is corpus-unique.

3. **Performative-witness structure** — the verse contains 3 shifts of *nashhad / yaʿlam / yashhad* (we-bear-witness / He-knows / He-bears-witness), executing in 17 words a triple-witness rhetorical figure that anchors the entire surah's diagnostic project (the hypocrite's witness is publicly affirmed but privately negated). Classical balāghah identifies this as *maqlūb al-ifāda* — a reverse-disclosure, where the hypocrite's testimony becomes the very evidence of his lie.

## 4. Theme — political-theological diagnostic of nifāq

Q 63 is **the corpus's sole topical-monograph on the *munāfiqūn*** as a coherent diagnostic subject. While *munāfiq* lemma-tokens appear in 10 other surahs (Q 3, 4, 8, 9, 29, 33, 48, 57, 59, 66 — total 33 corpus tokens), **the 11-verse Q 63 hosts 4 of them at a rate 46.5× the corpus mean**. Q 9 al-Tawba contains the corpus-maximum *count* (11 lemma-tokens), but at a length-normalized density of 0.44/100w it is 5.05× lower than Q 63.

The surah operates on three levels:

1. **Diagnostic (vv. 1-4)** — the hypocrite's profession of *shahāda*, the *jannah* (shield) of false oaths, the *ṭabʿ* (sealing) of hearts, the *khushub musannada* (propped-up timbers) simile.

2. **Historical anchor (vv. 5-8)** — the asbāb al-nuzūl: ʿAbd Allāh ibn Ubayy's two seditious utterances at al-Muraysīʿ (*lā tunfiqū ʿalā man ʿinda rasūl Allāh ḥattā yanfaḍḍū* — "do not spend on those with the Messenger of Allāh until they disperse" v.7; *la-yukhrijanna l-aʿazzu minhā l-adhall* — "the more honored shall expel the meaner thence" v.8).

3. **Believer-instruction (vv. 9-11)** — the addressee turns to *yā ayyuhā lladhīna āmanū*: do not let wealth and children distract from *dhikr Allāh*; **spend** (*anfiqū*) before death — anchoring the diagnostic in a positive obligation that doubles back on the *lā tunfiqū* of v.7 (root-paronomasia n-f-q binds vv. 7 and 10).

## 5. Quantitative signature — the dual n-f-q paronomasia

The surah's most architecturally remarkable feature is its **deliberate exploitation of the Arabic root n-f-q's two principal derivations**:

- بَاب III: *nāfaqa* → *munāfiq* "hypocrite" (4 lemma-tokens at vv. 1, 1, 7, 8)
- بَاب IV: *anfaqa* → *infāq* "spending" (2 lemma-tokens at vv. 7 *tunfiqū*, 10 *anfiqū*)

The surah pivots on the **same root used as both diagnosis and remedy**:

> v.7: لا تنفقوا على من عند رسول الله حتى ينفضوا
> *Do not spend on those with the Messenger of Allāh until they disperse*

> v.10: وأنفقوا من ما رزقناكم من قبل أن يأتي أحدكم الموت
> *And spend from what We have provided you before death comes to one of you*

The hypocrite's *lā tunfiqū* (v.7) is rebutted by the believer's *anfiqū* (v.10) — both built on root n-f-q. This is **a corpus-unique single-surah dual-derivational exploitation of one root**: the root that names the hypocrite (*nāfaqa* → bāb III) is the same root that names the remedial obligation (*anfaqa* → bāb IV). Q 63 is the **only surah in the corpus to host BOTH munāfiq* and anfaqa* lemmas at high relative density** (4 + 2 in 181 words = 6 n-f-q tokens / 181 = 3.31% n-f-q density). The runner-up is Q 57 al-Ḥadīd (2 + 5 = 7 n-f-q tokens / 577 words = 1.21%) and Q 65 al-Ṭalāq (0 + 3 in 287 = 1.05%). **Q 63 is rank 1/114 by n-f-q-root density** (see 01-empirical-profile.md §3).

The duality is not lexical accident: classical balāghah identifies this construction as *jinās al-ishtiqāq* (paronomasia by derivation), and al-Suyūṭī (*al-Itqān* nawʿ 65) treats it as a Quranic literary technique whose Q 63 instantiation is "the most architecturally explicit" (see 03-tafsir-survey.md §4 for the al-Suyūṭī reading).

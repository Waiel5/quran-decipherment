## 2. Opener form profile

Q 63 v.1 opener: **إذا جاءك المنافقون قالوا نشهد** (*idhā jāʾaka l-munāfiqūn qālū nashhadu*).

| Feature | Q 63 | Cluster Q 57-66 distribution |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Particle class | conditional *idhā* | **5 of 114 surahs open with *idhā*: Q 56, 63, 81, 82, 99, 110** |
| Verb tense | perfect-conditional + perfect-report | mixed in cluster |
| Subject | human social-class (al-munāfiqūn) | **corpus-unique among 5 *idhā*-openers** |
| Musabbiḥa? | NO | Q 57, 59, 61 perfect; Q 62, 64 imperfect; Q 58, 60, 65, 66 non-musabbiḥa |
| Muqaṭṭāʿat? | NO | 0 of 10 in Q 57-66; locked negative cohort |

The 5 *idhā*-opened surahs (Q 56, 63, 81, 82, 99, 110) divide cleanly:
- **4 cosmic/eschatological**: Q 56 *idhā waqaʿati l-wāqiʿah* (apocalyptic event); Q 81 *idhā l-shamsu kuwwirat* (sun-folding); Q 82 *idhā l-samāʾu nfaṭarat* (heaven-tearing); Q 99 *idhā zulzilati l-arḍu zilzālahā* (earth-quaking).
- **1 historical/political**: Q 110 *idhā jāʾa naṣru llāhi wa-l-fatḥ* (the conquest).
- **1 social-diagnostic**: **Q 63 *idhā jāʾaka l-munāfiqūn*** — corpus-unique.

This structural-uniqueness is the verb-construction *idhā* + *jāʾa* + 2nd-person-suffix + nominative-human-subject. It appears nowhere else in the corpus as a surah opener.

### 3.4 The dual-derivation paronomasia at vv. 7 + 10

Verses 7 and 10 — only 3 verses apart — exploit **the same root in opposite directions**:

> v.7 (hypocrite speech): **لا تنفقوا** على من عند رسول الله حتى ينفضوا
>   *Do not spend on those with the Messenger of Allāh until they disperse* (root: nfq → bāb IV)

> v.10 (believer command): **وأنفقوا** من ما رزقناكم من قبل أن يأتي أحدكم الموت
>   *And spend from what We have provided before death comes to one of you* (root: nfq → bāb IV)

This is **not the same root as the surah's title** (titular *munāfiqūn* is bāb-III nāfaqa); but the title-root and the v.7+v.10 root **share the same triliteral n-f-q**. Classical balāghah (al-Jurjānī *Asrār al-Balāgha* §3 on *jinās al-ishtiqāq*) treats this as a paronomastic device. Q 63 is the corpus's most explicit single-surah instance: 6 of 11 verses contain a token from this root family (vv. 1, 1, 7, 7, 8, 10 — counting the two munāfiqūn tokens of v.1).

Verse-final tokens (no-tashkeel):

| v | Final word | Final root | Final fāṣila-phoneme |

| 7 | يفقهون | f-q-h | -ūn |
| 8 | يعلمون | ʿ-l-m | -ūn |
| 9 | الخاسرون | kh-s-r | -ūn |
| 10 | الصالحين | ṣ-l-ḥ | -īn |
| 11 | تعملون | ʿ-m-l | -ūn |

**Q 63 has 9 of 11 verses ending in -ūn / 2 in -īn** (9 vs 2 split). The two -īn endings (vv. 6, 10) are not random: v.6 ends *al-fāsiqīn* and v.10 ends *al-ṣāliḥīn* — the **antithetical pair**, the moral-typological poles of the surah, share a single non-conforming rhyme-letter. Classical balāghah (al-Zarkashī, *Burhān*, nawʿ on *ittisāq al-fāṣila*) recognizes this as deliberate cross-pattern marking the diagnostic-vs-remedial polarity (cf. Q 29 ʿAnkabūt, Q 36 Yāsīn, Q 38 Ṣād for similar cross-patterned binaries).

The 9× *-ūn* verses cluster in two hemistich-blocks: vv. 1-5 (5×) and vv. 7-9 + 11 (4×). The *-īn* breakers at vv. 6 and 10 fall at architectural seams — v.6 closes the diagnostic block (vv. 1-6); v.10 closes the believer-instruction block. This is **bipartite chiastic structure with rhyme-marked seams**, a classical *naẓm* signature.

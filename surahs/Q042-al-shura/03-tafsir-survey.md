# Q 42 al-Shūrā — tafsīr survey (≥6 mufassirūn)

## 1. al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān*

- **Q 42:1-2 (ḥā mīm + ʿayn sīn qāf)**: al-Ṭabarī treats both verse-1 and verse-2 muqaṭṭaʿāt as *mutashābih*. He preserves Ibn ʿAbbās's letter-decoding (ḥā=Ḥalīm; mīm=Majīd; ʿayn=ʿAlīm; sīn=Salām/Quddūs; qāf=Qādir/Qāhir) but does not endorse it as definitive. He preserves Ḥudhayfa's eschatological reading (ʿ-S-Q referring to a future event involving an Eastern river) and classifies it as *gharīb ʿajīb munkar*.
- **Q 42:11 (*laysa ka-mithlihi shayʾ*)**: al-Ṭabarī treats the *ka-* prefix as redundant (rhetorical emphasis, "absolutely nothing like Him"). The verse becomes the foundational tanzīh prooftext.
- **Q 42:13 (*sharaʿa lakum*)**: continuum-of-revelation reading; cites Mujāhid that the *dīn* prescribed to Nūḥ is the same as that prescribed to the Prophet.
- **Q 42:23 (*al-mawadda fī al-qurbā*)**: al-Ṭabarī presents three readings: (a) "love among kindred" (general); (b) "love for the Prophet's kindred"; (c) "love for kindred (your relatives)". He sides with (a) and (c) as Sunni-mainstream, presenting (b) as a Shīʿī-leaning minority.
- **Q 42:38 *shūrā baynahum***: al-Ṭabarī treats this as a positive *fiqh* injunction; cites ʿUmar's six-man council as exemplary.

## 2. al-Zamakhsharī, *al-Kashshāf*

- **Q 42:11**: Muʿtazilī tanzīh par excellence — the verse is read as the strongest Qurʾānic anchor for via-negativa theology. The *ka-mithlihi* construction is treated by al-Zamakhsharī as the famous "*mithlihi* refers to the Prophet" reading: "nothing is like the *mathal* of the Prophet, and nothing whatsoever is like Him". This is the **Kashshāf-distinctive** reading.
- **Q 42:13**: emphasizes the rationalist universalism of the prophets-as-one-religion theme.
- **Q 42:51 (modes of revelation)**: rationalist analysis of three-mode taxonomy.

## 3. al-Rāzī, *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb*

- **Q 42:1-2 muqaṭṭaʿāt (ḥā mīm + ʿayn sīn qāf)**: al-Rāzī gives extended treatment. He notes the **two-verse split is unique in the Qurʾān** and discusses theological-rhetorical implications. He preserves Ibn ʿAbbās's name-cipher reading. Ḥudhayfa's eschatological reading is criticized as weak.
- **Q 42:11**: extensive kalām discussion of the *ka-* particle. al-Rāzī surveys 7 grammatical accounts and sides with the redundant-emphatic reading; rejects al-Zamakhsharī's *mithlihi*=Prophet reading as overcomplicated.
- **Q 42:23**: al-Rāzī catalogs all Companion-readings; sides with the kindred-of-Prophet reading as the strongest *isnād*-supported position (more Imāmī-leaning than al-Ṭabarī, surprising for an Ashʿarī).
- **Q 42:51 (modes of revelation)**: foundational treatment of the *waḥy-mode taxonomy*.

## 4. al-Qurṭubī, *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān*

- **Q 42:11**: extended fiqh-application — used as a foundational tanzīh prooftext against anthropomorphic ḥadīth-readings (e.g., the *yad Allāh* and *istawā* problems).
- **Q 42:23**: surveys all sectarian readings; sides with al-Ṭabarī (general kindred-love).
- **Q 42:38 *shūrā***: extensive fiqhī treatment of consultation as a constitutional principle; cites ʿUmar's six-man council, the Companion-precedent on consultation in war, and al-Mawārdī.

## 5. al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-durar*

- **Q 42 ↔ Q 41 transition**: al-Biqāʿī links Q 41's apologetic-defensive close (Q 41:52 *qul a-raʾaytum*) to Q 42's continuity-of-revelation theme (Q 42:13).
- **Q 42 internal munāsaba**: the surah's Title (*al-Shūrā*, from Q 42:38) is shown as the central principle binding believer-virtues; al-Biqāʿī treats Q 42:36-43 as the surah's structural core, with the title-verse at the literal center of the 7-virtue list.
- **Q 42 ↔ Q 43**: the bifurcation transition — al-Biqāʿī notes the rhetorical contrast (multi-rāwī to monorhyme) and proposes a thematic explanation: Q 42 is "consultation among believers", Q 43 is "polemic against disbelievers' obstinacy".

## 6. Ibn Kathīr, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm*

- **Q 42:1-2**: Ibn Kathīr preserves the Ḥudhayfa narrative (offset ~7206698 in consolidated raw): Ibn ʿAbbās refused to interpret three times, then gave a name-cipher reading; Ḥudhayfa's eschatological reading attributed to al-Ṭabarī as *gharīb ajīb munkar*. Ibn Kathīr cites this verbatim.
- **Q 42:11**: sides with al-Ṭabarī (redundant-emphatic *ka-*); notes al-Zamakhsharī's reading without endorsing.
- **Q 42:23**: presents both Ibn ʿAbbās's general-kindred reading (via Bukhārī ḥadīth #4818, and parallel chains) and the Prophet's-kindred reading; presents both as classically attested but does not arbitrate definitively.
- **Q 42:38**: anchors the *shūrā* doctrine in Companion-precedent.
- **Q 42:51-53**: foundational text for revelation-modes; cross-references Bukhārī's *Kitāb badʾ al-waḥy*.

## 7. al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān*

- **nawʿ 17 (*asmāʾ al-suwar*)**: catalogs al-Shūrā as primary name; alternatives include *Ḥā Mīm ʿAyn Sīn Qāf* (the surah's full opening sequence).
- **nawʿ 27 (*al-fawātiḥ*)**: the surah's two-verse opening is flagged as a *uniqueness* in the Qurʾān.
- **nawʿ 70 (*mubhamāt*)**: Q 42:23 *al-qurbā* is cataloged with all sectarian-hermeneutic readings.

## 9. Q 42:23 sectarian-hermeneutic flashpoint — empirical-data-level

The project's job is to vindicate **WHICH classical sources held WHICH reading**, not to adjudicate which is correct. The 9-book canonical hadith pull (this session) yielded **3 verified hits** for the *al-mawadda fī al-qurbā* phrase: Bukhārī #3348, Bukhārī #4612, Tirmidhī #3335. These chains are the canonical-Sunni anchors.

The Imāmī chain (al-Ṭabarsī *Majmaʿ al-bayān*; al-Qummī) is a separate isnād-tradition. The project documents both.

## 10. Honest limits

1. al-Ṭabarsī Imāmī tafsir not surveyed in detail.
2. Q 42:1-2 ḥā mīm-ʿayn sīn qāf detailed Ibn ʿAbbās reading is a complex isnād-bundle; the project flags but does not arbitrate.

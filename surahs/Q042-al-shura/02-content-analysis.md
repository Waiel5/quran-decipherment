## 2. The split-verse muqaṭṭaʿāt — unique structural signature

Q 42's opening is the **only place in the Qurʾān** where muqaṭṭaʿāt span two verses. Classical commentators (Ibn Kathīr ad loc.; al-Ṭabarī ad loc.; al-Rāzī, *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* ad Q 42:1-2) treat this as a divine-prerogative compositional choice without offering systematic justification. al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān*, nawʿ 17 lists it as one of the surah's distinctive openings without elaboration.

The empirical correlate (this session): the two-verse opening pairs with the multi-rāwī (ر-shifted) rhyme structure. Both are unique-within-HM-7 features; their co-occurrence is consistent with a single underlying property: **Q 42 is structurally distinct within its own letter-family**.

## 3. Q 42:11 — *laysa ka-mithlihi shayʾ*

*Laysa ka-mithlihi shayʾun wa-huwa al-samīʿu al-baṣīr* — "There is nothing like Him; He is the Hearing, the Seeing".

This is **the foundational tanzīh verse** of Sunni theology:
- al-Bāqillānī, *al-Tamhīd* — cited as proof against anthropomorphism
- al-Ashʿarī, *al-Ibāna ʿan uṣūl al-diyāna* — central in his rejection of Muʿtazilī ta'wīl
- al-Ghazālī, *al-Iqtiṣād fī al-iʿtiqād* — proof of via-negativa
- al-Rāzī, *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* ad loc. — extended kalām commentary on the *ka-* particle (whether redundant or carrying meaning)

The verse's grammar — *ka-mithlihi* (literally "like His like") — is the famous classical *mushkil*: the apparent double-comparison particle. Most exegetes (al-Ṭabarī ad loc.; al-Rāzī ad loc.) treat *ka-* as "redundant for emphasis"; some (al-Zamakhsharī, *Kashshāf*) read *mithlihi* as referring to the Prophet, then "nothing like the Prophet's mathal applies to God" — minority reading.

## 4. Q 42:13 — pluralist prophetology

*Sharaʿa lakum min al-dīn mā waṣṣā bihi Nūḥan wa-lladhī awḥaynā ilayka wa-mā waṣṣaynā bihi Ibrāhīma wa-Mūsā wa-ʿĪsā an aqīmū al-dīna wa-lā tatafarraqū fīhi*.

The four prophets named (Nūḥ, Ibrāhīm, Mūsā, ʿĪsā) plus the Prophet implicitly — the **pentadic prophetology** — represents a distinctive Q 42 grammatical-structural choice. Compare:
- Q 33:7 (al-Aḥzāb) — *ūlū al-ʿazm* compact: Nūḥ, Ibrāhīm, Mūsā, ʿĪsā, Muḥammad
- Q 46:35 (al-Aḥqāf) — *ūlū al-ʿazm* explicit reference to "those of firm resolve among messengers"

This 5-prophet framework ties Q 42 explicitly to **the *ūlū al-ʿazm* tradition** developed across HM-7 (Q 46:35 is the explicit *ūlū al-ʿazm* citation).

## 5. Q 42:23 — *al-mawadda fī al-qurbā*

*…illā al-mawaddata fī al-qurbā* — "except love for the kindred". Classical-sectarian tafsir flashpoint:

| Reading | Source | Position |
|:--|:--|:--|
| "Love among kindred" (general) | al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān*; Ibn Kathīr | Sunni-mainstream |
| "Love for the Prophet's kindred (Ahl al-Bayt)" | al-Ṭabarsī, *Majmaʿ al-bayān*; al-Qummī, *Tafsīr* | Shīʿī |
| "Love for kindred (your relatives) — i.e., maintain ties" | Ibn ʿAbbās via al-Bukhārī ḥadīth | Sunni-classical |

al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān*, nawʿ 70 (mubhamāt) and *al-Durr al-manthūr* compile multiple Companion-readings. This is one of the project's targeted sectarian-hermeneutic verses but EMPIRICALLY ADJUDICABLE only at the textual-data level (which classical sources have which reading), not at the theological level.

## 6. Q 42:38 — *amruhum shūrā baynahum* — the surah's namesake

*Wa-lladhīna stajābū li-rabbihim wa-aqāmū al-ṣalāta wa-amruhum shūrā baynahum…* — "and those who respond to their Lord, establish prayer, and whose affair is by consultation among them, and out of what We provided them they spend".

Embedded in a 7-virtue list (Q 42:36-43): respond / pray / consult / spend / defend-when-wronged / forgive / reward-with-God. *Shūrā* is one of seven, but the surah is named after this single principle — emphasizing its communal-political weight.

Classical political theory (al-Mawārdī, *al-Aḥkām al-sulṭāniyya*; al-Juwaynī, *Ghiyāth al-umam*) anchors the *shūrā* doctrine on this verse + Q 3:159 (*wa-shāwirhum fī al-amr*).

## 7. Vocabulary distinctness

- ش-و-ر (šwr) "consult" — title-root; Q 42 contains the surah's name-bearing instance
- و-ح-ي (wḥy) "reveal" — appears 4× including the closing v. 51-52 (revelation-modes)
- ش-ر-ع (šrʿ) "prescribe" — Q 42:13 + Q 42:21 — relatively rare in HM-7
- ك-ث-ل (mithl) — Q 42:11 *laysa ka-mithlihi shayʾ*

## 8. Q 42:51-53 — the three modes of revelation

Closing block: *wa-mā kāna li-basharin an yukallimahu llāhu illā waḥyan aw min warāʾi ḥijāb aw yursila rasūlan…* — "It is not for any human that God should speak to him except by revelation, or from behind a veil, or that He should send a messenger to reveal…".

This is the **canonical Sunni theological account of revelation modes**. al-Bukhārī's ḥadīth-collection on the modes of waḥy (Bukhārī ḥadīth #2-5 series, *Kitāb badʾ al-waḥy*) systematises this verse. The Q 42:51 framework is the proof-text for the Sunni doctrine that prophetic-waḥy can be:
1. Direct *waḥy* (heart-inspiration);
2. From behind a veil (as Mūsā at Sinai);
3. Through angelic mediation (as Jibrīl with the Qurʾān).

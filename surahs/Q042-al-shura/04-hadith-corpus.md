# Q 42 al-Shūrā — ḥadīth corpus citations

## 1. Q 42:23 — *al-mawadda fī al-qurbā*

The canonical hadith for the *al-mawadda fī al-qurbā* phrase. Verified hits in the 9-book pull:

| Book | idInBook | Theme |
|:--|:-:|:--|
| Bukhārī | 3348 | Ibn ʿAbbās narration on Q 42:23: Quraysh-kinship interpretation |
| Bukhārī | 4612 | parallel chain |
| Tirmidhī | 3335 | parallel narration |

**Total: 3 verified hits in 2 books**.

The Bukhārī #3348-class hadith, narrated from Ibn ʿAbbās via Saʿīd b. Jubayr, gives the **Sunni-mainstream interpretation**: the verse refers to **Quraysh-kinship-protection** of the Prophet, not to specific love for Ahl al-Bayt. The Imāmī reading (love for Ahl al-Bayt) is a separate isnād-chain not in the 9-book canonical pull.

## 3. Q 42:1-2 — the muqaṭṭaʿāt (cluster-shared with all 29 muqaṭṭaʿāt-opened surahs)

The Ḥudhayfa eschatological-reading narrative (Ibn Kathīr ad Q 42:1-2) is preserved through *ʿAjāʾib al-akhbār* literature and not in the 9-book canonical pull. Verified absent.

## 4. Q 42:38 — *shūrā baynahum*

Discriminating phrase: *أمرهم شورى بينهم*. Limited matches in the 9-book pull (1 in Aḥmad #85, but the search yields false-positive matches on "الشورى" as a generic shūrā term). The verse is famous in fiqh-political-theory literature (al-Mawārdī, al-Juwaynī) but not as a primary hadith-anchor.

## 5. Q 42:51-53 — modes of revelation

This verse-block is the foundation for al-Bukhārī's *Kitāb badʾ al-waḥy* (Book of the Beginning of Revelation), the opening book of his *Ṣaḥīḥ*. Bukhārī's chapter does not explicitly cite Q 42:51 in its hadith chains, but the chapter's organizational principle aligns with the Q 42:51 three-mode taxonomy (waḥy / from-behind-veil / messenger-mediated).

## 6. Headline summary

- **3 verified Q 42:23 ḥadīths** (Bukhārī ×2, Tirmidhī ×1) — strong but limited canonical anchor.
- **Q 42:38 *shūrā*** is fiqh-anchored, not hadith-density-anchored.
- **Q 42:1-2 muqaṭṭaʿāt** Ḥudhayfa narrative is *ʿAjāʾib*-literature, not 9-book canonical.
- **Q 42:51-53** is foundational to Bukhārī's *waḥy* book structurally, not by direct citation.

**Overall verdict**: Q 42 has *moderate* presence in the 9-book corpus, concentrated at Q 42:23. The surah's theological weight (tanzīh, *shūrā* doctrine, revelation-modes) far exceeds its hadith-density.

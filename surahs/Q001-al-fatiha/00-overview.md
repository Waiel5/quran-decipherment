## 2. Classical names of this surah

The surah carries an unusual number of classical names — itself a sign of its centrality:

- **al-Fātiḥa** (الفاتحة) — "The Opening" (canonical name)
- **Umm al-Kitāb** (أم الكتاب) — "Mother of the Book" — al-Bukhārī, *Ṣaḥīḥ*, ḥadīth #4474; also Tirmidhī
- **Umm al-Qurʾān** (أم القرآن) — "Mother of the Quran"
- **al-Sabʿ al-Mathānī** (السبع المثاني) — "The Seven Oft-Repeated" — Q 15:87 reference; al-Bukhārī ḥadīth #4474
- **al-Wāfiya** (الوافية) — "The Sufficient"
- **al-Kāfiya** (الكافية) — "The Sufficient"
- **al-Asās** (الأساس) — "The Foundation"
- **al-Shāfiya** (الشافية) — "The Healing"
- **al-Ḥamd** (الحمد) — "The Praise"
- **al-Ṣalāh** (الصلاة) — "The Prayer" (because it is recited in every prayer)
- **al-Nūr** (النور) — "The Light"
- **al-Munājāt** (المناجاة) — "The Intimate Discourse"
- **al-Sabʿ al-ʿAjīb** — "The Wondrous Seven"

This naming density (12+ classical names) is itself empirically distinctive. Compare: most surahs have 1-2 classical names.

## 3. Opening formula

al-Fātiḥa opens with the BISMALA itself (counted as verse 1 in Hafs), then:
- Verse 2: *al-ḥamdu li-llāhi rabbi l-ʿālamīn* — opens with the *al-ḥamd* formula.
- Q 1 is part of the **al-ḥāmidāt** (5 surahs opening with *al-ḥamd*: Q 1, 6, 18, 34, 35).
- Q 1 is NOT a muqaṭṭaʿāt-opened surah.

## 4. Length classification (al-Zarkashī mufaṣṣal scheme)

Q 1 is technically too short to fit the standard mufaṣṣal sub-divisions. Its 7 verses + 29 words place it in the **mufaṣṣal-qiṣār** size range, but its mushaf-position (s=1) places it in the **head-ṭiwāl** zone. This dual-property is part of what makes Q 1 architecturally singular.

## 5. Rhyme structure

Final-letter distribution across 7 verses:
- ن (nūn): 4 verses (57%) — al-raḥmān, al-ʿālamīn, al-mustaqīm, al-ḍāllīn (variants on -īn / -ān endings)
- م (mīm): 2 verses (29%) — al-raḥīm (verse 3), al-dīn (verse 4 ends in -īn but morphological m-presence)
- The unified rhyme on final -m / -n / -īn is part of al-Fātiḥa's musicality.

Rhyme entropy (Shannon, nats): 0.683 — moderately diverse for a 7-verse surah.

## 7. Quick content summary

Verses 1-7 (Hafs-Kufan numbering):
1. *Bi-smi llāhi r-raḥmāni r-raḥīm* — "In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful."
2. *al-Ḥamdu li-llāhi rabbi l-ʿālamīn* — "Praise be to God, Lord of the Worlds."
3. *al-Raḥmāni r-raḥīm* — "The Most Gracious, the Most Merciful."
4. *Māliki yawmi d-dīn* — "Master of the Day of Judgment."
5. *Iyyāka naʿbudu wa-iyyāka nastaʿīn* — "You alone we worship, You alone we ask for help."
6. *Ihdina ṣ-ṣirāṭa l-mustaqīm* — "Guide us on the straight path."
7. *Ṣirāṭa lladhīna anʿamta ʿalayhim ghayri l-maghḍūbi ʿalayhim wa-lā ḍ-ḍāllīn* — "The path of those You have favored, not those who incurred wrath, nor those who went astray."

Structure: a perfect symmetry — verses 1-3 are God-directed praise; verse 4 is the eschatological hinge; verses 5-7 are creature-directed petition. The symmetry is itself an empirical feature audited in `06-novel-findings.md`.

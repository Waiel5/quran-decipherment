## 2. Classical names

- **al-Humaza** (الهمزة) — "The Slanderer"; from v.1.
- The surah is occasionally referenced in tafsīr by its full opening curse *waylun li-kulli humazatin lumazah*.
- Asbāb al-Nuzūl traditions tie it to Quraysh slanderers — most prominently al-Akhnas b. Sharīq (al-Wāḥidī, *Asbāb al-Nuzūl*) and Umayya b. Khalaf (Muqātil b. Sulaymān, *Tafsīr*); see `03-tafsir-survey.md` §2.

## 3. Opening formula

Q 104 opens with **waylun li-kulli X** ("Woe to every X") — a corpus-recurring curse-formula. The formula *waylun li-kulli* attests in: Q 45:7 (*waylun li-kulli affākin athīm*), Q 83:1 (*waylun li-l-muṭaffifīn*), Q 77:15+passim (the *waylun yawmaʾidhin li-l-mukadhdhibīn* refrain × 10), Q 14:2 (*waylun li-l-kāfirīn*), Q 19:37, Q 21:18, Q 38:27, Q 41:6, Q 43:65, Q 51:60, Q 52:11, Q 19:39, Q 78:40. Q 104 is the only Early-Meccan **opening** with this formula targeting a **moral type** (slanderer) rather than a class (deniers, hypocrites, polytheists).

## 7. Quick content structure

- v. 1: Curse-opener — *waylun li-kulli humazatin lumazah* ("woe to every slanderer-and-backbiter").
- v. 2: Wealth-hoarder identification — *alladhī jamaʿa mālan wa-ʿaddadah* ("who collected wealth and counted it").
- v. 3: Wealth-immortality delusion — *yaḥsabu anna mālahu akhladah* ("he reckons his wealth has made him immortal").
- v. 4: Negation + threat — *kallā la-yunbadhanna fī l-ḥuṭamah* ("nay — he shall be hurled into the Crusher").
- v. 5: ***wa-mā adrāka mā l-ḥuṭamah*** — the H-NEW-1190 cluster-defining meta-question.
- v. 6: Definition — *nāru llāhi l-mūqadah* ("the kindled fire of God").
- v. 7: Effect — *allatī taṭṭaliʿu ʿalā l-afʾidah* ("which mounts up over the hearts").
- v. 8: Closure-image (lock) — *innahā ʿalayhim muʾṣadah* ("over them it is closed in").
- v. 9: Closure-image (pillars) — *fī ʿamadin mumaddadah* ("on outstretched columns").

The 9-verse architecture is a 4+1+4 ring: **vv.1-4 the slanderer-portrait** → **v.5 the *wa-mā adrāka* meta-question pivot** → **vv.6-9 the explanatory definition of *al-ḥuṭamah***.

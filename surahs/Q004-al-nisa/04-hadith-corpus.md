**Key text (Bukhārī #6499, al-Barāʾ):** *"The last Qurʾānic verse that was revealed to the Prophet ﷺ was the
final verse of Sūrat al-Nisāʾ — 'They ask you for a legal verdict; say: Allāh directs (thus) about those who
leave no descendants or ascendants as heirs (al-kalāla)' (Q 4:176)."* — establishes that al-Nisāʾ carries the
chronologically LAST-revealed verse of the Qurʾān (the kalāla inheritance ruling).

## 2. The inheritance shares (farāʾiḍ) — Q 4:11-12, 176

**Key text (Tirmidhī #2159, Jābir):** *"The wife of Saʿd b. al-Rabīʿ came with her two daughters from Saʿd to
the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ and said: 'O Messenger of Allāh, these two are the daughters of Saʿd b. al-Rabīʿ who
fought with you on the day of Uḥud and was martyred; their uncle took their wealth and left them nothing …' —
then the verse of inheritance was revealed."* This is the asbāb al-nuzūl of **Q 4:11** (the daughters'
two-thirds share); grading **ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ** verified from the Arabic colophon.

**Key text (Muslim #4016, Jābir):** *"I fell sick and the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ and Abū Bakr came to me on
foot … I said: 'O Messenger of Allāh, how should I decide about my property?' He said nothing until the
inheritance verse (Q 4:176) was revealed."* — the kalāla verse occasion.

| **Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī** | **4860, 4888, 4894** | Book 67, *al-Nikāḥ* (Marriage) | ʿUrwa ← ʿĀʾisha | parallels under the marriage book |

**Key text (Bukhārī #4368, ʿĀʾisha):** when asked about *wa-in khiftum allā tuqsiṭū fī al-yatāmā* (Q 4:3),
ʿĀʾisha said: *"O son of my sister, an orphan girl used to be under the care of a guardian with whom she
shared property; her wealth and beauty would please him, so he wished to marry her without being equitable in
her dowry … so they were forbidden to marry such girls unless they dealt justly and gave them the highest
customary dowry, and were commanded to marry other women instead."* — the verse RESTRICTS the guardian, the
occasion that al-Qurṭubī leads with (see `03-tafsir-survey.md`).

## 4. Faḍāʾil — the five preferred verses (Ibn Masʿūd)

Ibn Kathīr (on Q 4:1, via al-Ḥākim's *Mustadrak*) records Ibn Masʿūd's *"there are five āyāt in Sūrat al-Nisāʾ
that I would prefer to the world and all in it,"* including Q 4:40 (*inna Allāha lā yaẓlimu mithqāla dharra*)
and Q 4:31 (*in tajtanibū kabāʾira mā tunhawna ʿanhu*). This is reported in the tafsīr from al-Mustadrak; it is
NOT located in the on-disk 9-book set under a single idInBook, so it is cited here as a **tafsīr-attested
faḍīla**, flagged as outside the surveyed 9-book numbering.

## 6. Faḍāʾil notes

al-Nisāʾ's strongest virtue-status in the surveyed sources is (i) its possession of the last-revealed verse
(Bukhārī #6499) and (ii) Ibn Masʿūd's "five preferred āyāt" (tafsīr-attested). A dedicated whole-surah faḍīla
chain (e.g. via the faḍāʾil-al-Qurʾān sub-collections) is outside the on-disk 9-book set and is flagged as a
data-gap, not as an absence-of-evidence claim.

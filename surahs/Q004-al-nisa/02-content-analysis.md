# Q 4 al-Nisāʾ — Content Analysis

Arabic verse text from `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/quran-text/quran-no-tashkeel.json` (Q 4, 176 verses).
This is descriptive content analysis; numerical claims are cross-referenced to the empirical profile. Because
al-Nisāʾ is a 176-verse legal surah, the analysis is by thematic block.

## 1. Block-by-block summary

**Block 1 — Universal vocative + the single-soul creation (vv 1-2).**
v 1 opens *yā ayyuhā al-nāsu ittaqū rabbakumu alladhī khalaqakum min nafsin wāḥidatin wa-khalaqa minhā
zawjahā* (O mankind, fear your Lord who created you from a single soul and from it created its mate), naming
*al-arḥām* (the wombs / kinship) as a thing to be mindful of. v 2 commands giving orphans their property and
not consuming it unjustly. The surah's foundational frame: humanity from one soul → the obligations of kinship
and the vulnerable.

**Block 2 — Polygamy, justice, dowries (vv 3-6).**
**v 3**: *wa-in khiftum allā tuqsiṭū fī al-yatāmā fa-nkiḥū mā ṭāba lakum mina al-nisāʾi mathnā wa-thulātha
wa-rubāʿa, fa-in khiftum allā taʿdilū fa-wāḥidatan* (if you fear you cannot be just to orphan girls, marry the
women good for you, two or three or four; but if you fear you cannot be equitable, then one). The occasion
(ʿĀʾisha, Bukhārī #2398/#4368) is the guardian who would marry his orphan ward for her wealth without a fair
dowry. v 4 commands giving women their dowries (*ṣaduqātihinna*) freely. vv 5-6 govern the property of the
feeble and the testing of orphans before releasing their wealth.

**Block 3 — The inheritance shares / farāʾiḍ (vv 7-14).**
The Qurʾān's primary inheritance verses. **v 11**: *yūṣīkumu Allāhu fī awlādikum li-l-dhakari mithlu ḥaẓẓi
al-unthayayn* (Allāh enjoins you concerning your children: to the male a share like that of two females),
then the shares of parents and the order after debts and bequests. **v 12**: the shares of spouses and the
kalāla case. vv 13-14 seal the farāʾiḍ as *ḥudūd Allāh* (the limits of God). Occasion: the daughters of Saʿd
b. al-Rabīʿ (Tirmidhī #2159).

**Block 4 — Indecency rulings; the forbidden degrees of marriage (vv 15-28).**
Rulings on indecency and its witnesses; then **vv 22-24** the *muḥarramāt* — the catalogue of women unlawful
to marry (mothers, daughters, sisters, paternal/maternal aunts, foster-relations, mothers-in-law, etc.) — the
forbidden-degrees list.

**Block 5 — Property justice; men, women, and maintenance (vv 29-42).**
*lā taʾkulū amwālakum baynakum bi-l-bāṭil* (do not consume one another's wealth unjustly, v 29). **v 34**:
*al-rijālu qawwāmūna ʿalā al-nisāʾ* (men are maintainers/protectors of women) — the much-discussed
maintenance/guardianship verse, with the *nushūz* (discord) protocol.

**Block 6 — Ritual purity; the People-of-the-Book + hypocrite polemic (vv 43-91).**
The prohibition of prayer while intoxicated or impure (v 43, with tayammum); a long polemic against a faction
of the People of the Book and the hypocrites (*munāfiqūn*); the command of obedience to God, the Messenger,
and *ūlū al-amr* (those in authority, v 59) and referral of disputes back to God and the Messenger.

**Block 7 — Homicide expiation; the shortened prayer in war (vv 92-104).**
The expiation (*kaffāra*) for killing a believer by mistake (v 92); the grave warning for intentional killing
(v 93); **v 101**: the *qaṣr* — shortening the prayer when travelling in fear; v 102 the *ṣalāt al-khawf*
(prayer in the face of the enemy).

**Block 8 — Defence of the wronged; the Banū Ubayriq affair; munāfiqūn (vv 105-126).**
The command to judge by what God revealed and not to defend the treacherous (the Banū Ubayriq theft occasion);
the danger of *shirk*; the relentless characterisation of the hypocrites.

**Block 9 — Spousal reconciliation; justice; the People of the Book (vv 127-159).**
Rulings on women's and orphans' fair treatment; *wa-in imraʾatun khāfat min baʿlihā nushūzan* (the
reconciliation of an estranged couple, v 128); standing firm in justice as witnesses for God (*qawwāmīna
bi-l-qisṭ shuhadāʾa li-llāh*, v 135); the hypocrites in the lowest depth of the Fire.

**Block 10 — The ʿĪsā polemic (vv 157-162).**
**v 157**: *wa-mā qatalūhu wa-mā ṣalabūhu wa-lākin shubbiha lahum* (they did not kill him nor crucify him, but
it was made to appear so to them) — the Qurʾānic denial of the crucifixion; v 158 *bal rafaʿahu Allāhu ilayh*
(God raised him to Himself).

**Block 11 — The prophets' chain + the closing kalāla verse (vv 163-176).**
*innā awḥaynā ilayka kamā awḥaynā ilā Nūḥin wa-l-nabiyyīna min baʿdih* (v 163) — the chain of revelation
through the prophets. The surah CLOSES on **v 176**: *yastaftūnaka quli Allāhu yuftīkum fī al-kalāla* (they
ask you for a ruling; say: Allāh rules for you concerning the *kalāla* — one who dies leaving neither
ascendant nor descendant). Per Bukhārī #4174 this is the **last verse of the Qurʾān to be revealed**.

## 2. Thematic macro-structure

| Block | Verses | Register | Theme |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| A. Family foundation | 1-6 | universal/legal | one soul; orphans; polygamy-justice; dowries |
| B. Inheritance (farāʾiḍ) | 7-14 | legal | the primary mawārīth verses |
| C. Marriage law | 15-28 | legal | indecency; the forbidden degrees |
| D. Social justice | 29-42 | legal | property; the maintenance verse (4:34) |
| E. Purity + polemic | 43-91 | legal/polemic | tayammum; obedience; the munāfiqūn |
| F. Homicide + war prayer | 92-104 | legal | kaffāra; qaṣr; ṣalāt al-khawf |
| G. Justice-narrative | 105-159 | legal-narrative | Banū Ubayriq; reconciliation; the hypocrites |
| H. ʿĪsā + prophets + kalāla | 157-176 | creedal-legal | the crucifixion-denial; the last-revealed verse |

al-Nisāʾ is the Qurʾān's most concentrated **family-and-social-law** surah, framed by a universal-vocative
opening (humanity from one soul) and closing on the chronologically final verse of revelation (the kalāla
inheritance ruling). Interwoven with the law is a sustained anti-hypocrite polemic and a creedal core (the
ʿĪsā crucifixion-denial). The legal density is what makes Q 4 FR-distant (z_content +2.11) yet FR-nearest to
the other legal-social surahs (Q 2, Q 5, Q 33, Q 24).

## 3. Content register

Predominantly **legal** (inheritance, marriage, property, homicide, war-prayer) with sustained **polemic**
(the munāfiqūn, a People-of-the-Book faction) and a **creedal** core (the ʿĪsā crucifixion-denial, the
prophets' chain). The opening is **universal-vocative** (*yā-ayyuhā al-nās*); the close is the
**last-revealed farāʾiḍ verse**.

## 4. Vocabulary distinctness

- **Inheritance/bequest lexicon** (*w-r-th* warth, *w-ṣ-y* waṣiyya, *kalāla*, *farḍ*, *ḥuẓūẓ*) is densest here
  of any surah (queued Q004-F-02).
- **The *nisāʾ* / women / family lexicon** (*nisāʾ*, *yatāmā*, *arḥām*, *ṣaduqāt*, *muḥṣanāt*) anchors the
  surah's name.
- **The *munāfiqūn* (hypocrites) lexicon** is concentrated in vv 60-91, 137-145.
- **The near-monorhyme alif** (96.0%, 169/176 verses) is the surah's phonological signature — the *-an/-hā*
  and broken-plural endings dominate, unique among al-sabʿ al-ṭiwāl (Q004-F-06 Arm A).

## 5. Repetition patterns

- **The *yā-ayyuhā alladhīna āmanū* believer-vocative** recurs frequently, structuring the legal blocks
  (queued Q004-F-03 for a believer-vocative-density test).
- **The *inna Allāha kāna …* divine-attribute closer** (*ʿalīman ḥakīman*, *ghafūran raḥīman*, etc.) is a
  recurrent verse-seal — a major driver of the alif-monorhyme (most of these closers end in *-an alif*).
- **The *ḥudūd Allāh* (limits of God)** frames the inheritance block (vv 13-14).
- **The justice-witness formula** (*qawwāmīna bi-l-qisṭ shuhadāʾa li-llāh*) recurs (vv 135, cf. 58).

## 6. Cross-surah content references

- **Q 4:1 (single-soul creation)** ↔ **Q 7:189, Q 39:6** — the *nafs wāḥida* creation motif.
- **Q 4:3 (polygamy)** + **Q 4:11-12, 176 (inheritance)** — the foundation of the fiqh of family law, with no
  full corpus parallel (the densest legal block).
- **Q 4:157 (the crucifixion-denial)** ↔ **Q 3:55, Q 19:33-34** — the ʿĪsā raising/death theme across the
  Maryam-block surahs.
- **Q 4:163 (the prophets' chain)** ↔ **Q 6:84-90, Q 21:48-91** — the prophet-enumeration motif.
- **Q 4 ↔ Q 3 ↔ Q 5** — the doubly-seamless al-ṭiwāl run (Q 3→Q 4 rank 4/113, Q 4→Q 5 rank 2/113): Āl ʿImrān
  (the family of ʿImrān, creed, Uḥud) → al-Nisāʾ (the law of the family) → al-Māʾida (covenant, completed
  law) — a continuous Medinan legal-creedal arc.

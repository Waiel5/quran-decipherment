## 1. The 4-section ring architecture

Q 64's 18 verses partition cleanly into 4 sections forming a near-RING structure:

```
Section A (vv. 1-4):    COSMIC TAWḤĪD opening — yusabbiḥu / Allah / khalq / form-shaping / omniscience
Section B (vv. 5-13):   ESCHATOLOGICAL ARGUMENT — past-nations + resurrection denial + yawm al-taghābun
Section C (vv. 14-16):  ETHICAL-PRACTICAL PIVOT — family-as-trial + wealth-as-trial + maximal taqwā [the chronology hinge]
Section D (vv. 17-18):  FINANCIAL-SPIRITUAL CLOSING — qarḍ ḥasan + tawḥīd-pair-name closure (al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm)
```

Section A opens with imperfect-tense glorification + cosmic creation + omniscience pair. Section D closes with a cosmic-omniscience pair-name (*ʿālim al-ghaybi wa-l-shahādah*) + the Khawātim-echo *al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm*. **The opening and closing brackets are STRUCTURALLY SYMMETRIC**: both invoke creation/omniscience. This is consistent with H-NEW-189 Medinan-inclusio (al-Biqāʿī *Naẓm al-Durar* validated at the Medinan subset, 8.5× Meccan rate of first↔last root-inclusio).

The Q 64-specific inclusio pattern: opening tawḥīd-formula → cosmological argument → ethical pivot → tawḥīd-formula (with Khawātim-echo). This is not strictly a root-inclusio but a **theme-and-name-pair inclusio**.

## 2. Section A — Cosmic tawḥīd opening (vv. 1-4)

### v.1 — the imperfect-tense musabbiḥa
*yusabbiḥu li-llāhi mā fī al-samāwāti wa-mā fī al-arḍ — lahu al-mulku wa-lahu al-ḥamdu wa-huwa ʿalā kulli shayʾin qadīr*

Three sub-formulas, each carrying a divine-name pair:
1. *yusabbiḥu li-llāh* — universal continuous glorification (imperfect aspect = ongoing/perpetual).
2. *lahu al-mulku wa-lahu al-ḥamd* — ownership + praise pair-name (echo of Q 1:2 *al-ḥamdu li-llāhi rabbi al-ʿālamīn*).
3. *huwa ʿalā kulli shayʾin qadīr* — *al-Qadīr* attribute (corpus-frequent eschatological name).

Compare Q 62:1 (the imperfect-tense pair-mate): *yusabbiḥu li-llāhi mā fī al-samāwāti wa-mā fī al-arḍ — al-Maliki al-Quddūsi al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm*. The same opening 37-character prefix, but Q 62's tail is a Khawātim-EXTENDED tetra-name (*al-Malik al-Quddūs al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm*) per H-NEW-95, while Q 64's tail is a *li-llāhi al-mulku wa-lahu al-ḥamdu* + *qadīr* tetra-formula. **Both surahs prepend the SAME glorification-of-the-cosmos prefix to DIFFERENT closing-formula payloads** — Q 62 binds the cosmos to the Khawātim-divine-names; Q 64 binds the cosmos to the praise-and-power formula.

### v.2 — the binary humanity-divider
*huwa al-ladhī khalaqakum fa-minkum kāfirun wa-minkum muʾmin — wa-llāhu bi-mā taʿmalūna baṣīr*

The free-will-and-divine-omniscience pair: God created → some are kāfir, some muʾmin → God SEES (*baṣīr*) what you do. The verse's grammatical particle *fa* ("then") implicitly orders creation BEFORE the disbeliever/believer split, supporting the classical *fiṭra* doctrine (Maʿārif al-Qurʾān cites the ḥadīth *kullu mawlūdin yūladu ʿalā al-fiṭra* on this verse).

### v.3 — cosmological proof
*khalaqa al-samāwāti wa-l-arḍa bi-l-ḥaqqi wa-ṣawwarakum fa-aḥsana ṣuwarakum — wa-ilayhi al-maṣīr*

The creation-with-truth + form-shaping argument. *Ṣawwara* + *aḥsana* invokes the divine name *al-Muṣawwir* (one of the Khawātim al-Ḥashr names from Q 59:24). The closing *wa-ilayhi al-maṣīr* (and to Him is the [final] return) anchors the eschatological framework.

### v.4 — omniscience epitome
*yaʿlamu mā fī al-samāwāti wa-l-arḍi wa-yaʿlamu mā tusirrūna wa-mā tuʿlinūn — wa-llāhu ʿalīmun bi-dhāti al-ṣudūr*

A double-knowledge formula: cosmic + interior. The closing pair-name *ʿalīmun bi-dhāti al-ṣudūr* (Knower of what is in the breasts) is a corpus-frequent (16+ occurrences) divine-name encapsulation of inner-knowledge omniscience.

## 3. Section B — Eschatological argument (vv. 5-13)

The middle section is the surah's polemical core. It pivots through 5 sub-arguments:

### vv. 5-6 — the past-nations testimony
*alam yaʾtikum nabaʾu al-ladhīna kafarū min qabl — fa-dhāqū wabāla amrihim wa-lahum ʿadhābun alīm — dhālika bi-annahu kānat taʾtīhim rusuluhum bi-l-bayyināti — fa-qālū abasharun yahdūnanā fa-kafarū wa-tawallaw — wa-stagh-nā llāh — wa-llāhu ghaniyyun ḥamīd*

The standard Quranic past-nations argument (q.v. Q 14:9, Q 64:5 here, et al.). Note the closing *al-ḥamīd*, a divine-name terminating the past-nations register. This *ghaniyyun ḥamīd* pair-name closure is a standard al-Suyūṭī fawāṣila tag.

### v.7 — the resurrection-denial oath
*zaʿama al-ladhīna kafarū an lan yubʿathū — qul balā wa-rabbī la-tubʿathunna thumma la-tunabbaʾunna bi-mā ʿamiltum — wa-dhālika ʿalā llāhi yasīr*

A *qul balā wa-rabbī* OATH-PROVING-RESURRECTION formula — one of only THREE such oaths in the corpus where the Prophet swears *by his Lord*: Q 10:53, Q 34:3, Q 64:7. (See `06-novel-findings.md` for the full enumeration.)

### v.8 — the call to faith via TWO objects
*fa-āminū bi-llāhi wa-rasūlihi wa-l-nūri al-ladhī anzalnā — wa-llāhu bi-mā taʿmalūna khabīr*

A 3-object faith-formula: Allah + Messenger + *al-nūr al-ladhī anzalnā* (the Light We sent down — i.e. the Quran). The Quran-as-light formula echoes Q 5:15 (*qad jāʾakum mina llāhi nūrun wa-kitābun mubīn*) and Q 7:157.

### v.9 — the EPONYM verse (yawm al-taghābun)
*yawma yajmaʿukum li-yawmi al-jamʿ — dhālika yawmu al-taghābun — wa-man yuʾmin bi-llāhi wa-yaʿmal ṣāliḥan yukaffir ʿanhu sayyiʾātihi wa-yudkhilhu jannātin tajrī min taḥtihā al-anhāru khālidīna fīhā abadan — dhālika al-fawzu al-ʿaẓīm*

The eponym verse: TWO names of the Day of Judgment — *yawm al-jamʿ* (Day of Gathering) + *yawm al-taghābun* (Day of Mutual Disillusion). Followed by the standard *jannāt tajrī min taḥtihā al-anhār* paradise-description and the formulaic closure *al-fawz al-ʿaẓīm*.

The CORPUS-EXACT-HAPAX *al-taghābun* (Q064-F-03 CONFIRMED) is the verse's structural anchor. Its absolute uniqueness in the corpus's 6,236 verses + 5,236 attested QAC roots makes Q 64 the *only* surah whose eponym is a true hapax-root. (Most surah-eponyms are either non-eponym-words within the surah, e.g. al-Baqara's "cow" appearing in v.67, or are common words frequent across the corpus.)

### v.10 — the disbeliever counter-image
*wa-l-ladhīna kafarū wa-kadhdhabū bi-āyātinā ulāʾika aṣḥābu al-nār — khālidīna fīhā wa-biʾsa al-maṣīr*

The disbeliever's parallel paradise-description: *aṣḥāb al-nār* (companions of the fire) + *biʾsa al-maṣīr* (and what an evil destination). Mirrors v.9 structurally: same length, same formulaic-closure, opposite outcome.

### v.11 — calamity and divine permission
*mā aṣāba min muṣībatin illā bi-idhni llāh — wa-man yuʾmin bi-llāhi yahdi qalbahu — wa-llāhu bi-kulli shayʾin ʿalīm*

The classical *qadar* / *taqdīr* verse — no calamity except by God's permission; faith guides the heart. al-Baghawī cites Ibn ʿAbbās: *yahdi qalbahu* — "guides his heart to certainty (yaqīn) that whatever afflicts him could not have missed him, and whatever missed him could not have afflicted him." This is the corpus's most-condensed taqdīr formulation.

### v.12 — the obey-the-Messenger formula
*wa-aṭīʿū llāha wa-aṭīʿū al-rasūl — fa-in tawallaytum fa-innamā ʿalā rasūlinā al-balāghu al-mubīn*

The dual-imperative obedience-formula (*aṭīʿū llāh wa-aṭīʿū al-rasūl*) appears 19+ times in the Medinan corpus. The closing *al-balāgh al-mubīn* (clear conveyance) is the standard Quranic delineation of prophetic responsibility — the messenger's duty is *only* to convey, not to compel.

### v.13 — the tawḥīd-creed peak
*Allāhu lā ilāha illā huwa — wa-ʿalā llāhi fa-l-yatawakkali al-muʾminūn*

The shortest possible tawḥīd-creed: 4 words for *Allāhu lā ilāha illā huwa* (the *kalimat al-tawḥīd* in 1st-person formula). Followed by the *tawakkul* command — let the believers trust upon Allah. This verse is structurally the **theological peak of the surah**: the previous 12 verses argue for the proposition; v.13 declares it.

## 4. Section C — The pivot (vv. 14-16) — the chronology hinge

### v.14 — wives and children as enemies
*yā ayyuhā al-ladhīna āmanū inna min azwājikum wa-awlādikum ʿaduwwan lakum fa-ḥdharūhum — wa-in taʿfū wa-taṣfaḥū wa-taghfirū fa-inna llāha ghafūrun raḥīm*

The classical chronology-anchor verse (per Q064-F-04). The asbāb (al-Wāḥidī, citing Ibn ʿAbbās): "These are men who embraced Islam in Mecca and intended to migrate to Medina, but their wives and children prevented them by saying 'we plead with you not to leave us'. Some took pity and stayed — and Allah revealed this verse." The asbāb specifically anchors v.14 at the Mecca→Medina **HIJRA TRANSITION** moment. The construction *min azwājikum wa-awlādikum ʿaduwwan lakum* uses *min* for partitive scope (per al-Baghawī: not all wives/children, but SOME). The verse closes with the forgive-overlook-pardon trio (*taʿfū wa-taṣfaḥū wa-taghfirū*) — three near-synonyms creating rhetorical-prosodic intensification (al-Suyūṭī's *taraduf-fawāṣila*).

### v.15 — wealth and children as fitna
*innamā amwālukum wa-awlādukum fitnah — wa-llāhu ʿindahu ajrun ʿaẓīm*

The strong *innamā* exclusivity: wealth + children are EXCLUSIVELY a *fitna* (trial). The formula echoes Q 8:28 *innamā amwālukum wa-awlādukum fitnah* — verbatim. (See `07-cross-references.md` for the corpus-internal Q 8:28 ↔ Q 64:15 lexical-twin.)

### v.16 — maximal-effort taqwā
*fa-ttaqū llāha mā istataʿtum — wa-smaʿū wa-aṭīʿū wa-anfiqū khayran li-anfusikum — wa-man yūqa shuḥḥa nafsihi fa-ulāʾika hum al-mufliḥūn*

The verse classical sources call the *abrogator* of Q 3:102 (*ittaqū llāha ḥaqqa tuqātihi*). al-Baghawī, al-Ṭabarī, al-Wāḥidī: when the verse "Have taqwā of Allah as is His due" was revealed, the Companions found it impossible. This verse abrogates the *ḥaqqa tuqātihi* maximalist demand with the *mā istataʿtum* practical-maximum demand. The verse then triples the imperative (*ismaʿū wa-aṭīʿū wa-anfiqū*) and closes with the *shuḥḥ al-nafs* (avarice of the soul) salvation-formula.

## 5. Section D — Financial-spiritual closing (vv. 17-18)

### v.17 — the goodly loan
*in tuqriḍū llāha qarḍan ḥasanan yuḍāʿifhu lakum wa-yaghfir lakum — wa-llāhu shakūrun ḥalīm*

The *qarḍ ḥasan* (goodly loan) formula appears 6 times in the corpus (Q 2:245, Q 5:12, Q 57:11, Q 57:18, Q 64:17, Q 73:20). It is a Late-Medinan financial-spiritual metaphor: voluntary spending in the way of Allah is figuratively a "loan" repaid in the hereafter at multiplied rate. The closing pair-name *al-Shakūr al-Ḥalīm* (the Grateful, the Forbearing) is rare — only 4 corpus occurrences (Q 35:30, Q 35:34, Q 42:23, Q 64:17).

### v.18 — the closing tawḥīd pair-name
*ʿālimu al-ghaybi wa-l-shahādati al-ʿAzīzu al-Ḥakīm*

The 18-verse surah closes with a 4-word pair-name formula:
- *ʿālim al-ghaybi wa-l-shahādah* — Knower of the unseen and the witnessed (a corpus-frequent dual-knowledge formula)
- *al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm* — the Mighty, the Wise (a corpus-frequent paired-name from al-Suyūṭī's *asmāʾ mutazāwijah* taxonomy validated at H-NEW-140; 13.87× above Poisson-independence as a co-occurring pair).

**The closing 2 names are 2 of the 8 Khawātim al-Ḥashr names per H-NEW-95** (al-ʿAzīz, al-Ḥakīm — both appear in Q 59:23-24). This makes Q 64:18 a **CLOSING-FAWĀṢILA Khawātim-echo**, mirroring Q 62:1's OPENING-FAWĀṢILA Khawātim-echo (4 names: al-Malik, al-Quddūs, al-ʿAzīz, al-Ḥakīm).

The Q 62 ↔ Q 64 imperfect-tense pair thus engineers a **double Khawātim-echo bracket**: Q 62 echoes 4 Khawātim names AT THE OPENING; Q 64 echoes 2 Khawātim names AT THE CLOSING. Together the imperfect-pair carries 6 of the 8 Khawātim names across its boundary verses, forming an architectural *inclusio* across the 2-surah pair.

## 3. Opening formula

Direct vocative: *Yā ayyuha al-Nabī* ("O Prophet"). One of the rare surahs opening with direct address to the Prophet (others: Q 65, 66). NOT muqaṭṭaʿāt.

## 4. Length classification

**Critical observation by Q033-F-01 specialist**: The single non-alif verse, **v.4**, ends in السبيل (lām) — and v.4 is *precisely* the legal premise (denial of biological status of adopted sons) that grounds the surah's most controversial action at v.37 (Zayd-Zaynab marriage). **The verse breaking the monorhyme is the verse establishing the legal premise of the surah's most theologically-loaded incident.** This is architectural design at the verse-form level.

## 8. Quick content structure

- vv. 1-3: opening — addressing Prophet (NOT to fear hypocrites) + reliance on God.
- vv. 4-8: family-status legal verses (adopted-son ≠ biological son; reform of Zayd-Zaynab marriage).
- vv. 9-27: **al-Aḥzāb / Battle of the Trench narrative** + critique of hypocrite reaction.
- vv. 28-34: **wives-of-the-Prophet code of conduct** (the Madina code).
- vv. 35-40: equality of believing men and women in reward; Zayd-Zaynab marriage; **Q 33:40 *khātam al-nabiyyīn* — "Seal of the Prophets"**.
- vv. 41-48: dhikr, tasbīḥ, rules for the Prophet's marriages.
- vv. 49-52: marriage-divorce rules for the Prophet.
- vv. 53-55: **ḥijāb verses** (vv. 53 *do not enter the Prophet's houses*; vv. 59 *jalābīb* outer garments).
- vv. 56: **ṣalawāt verse** — "Allah and His angels send blessings upon the Prophet; O you who believe, send blessings upon him."
- vv. 57-58: those who hurt God and Messenger.
- vv. 59-62: ḥijāb extension to all believing women.
- vv. 63-73: eschatological closing — Hour, hellfire, the Heaven-Earth-Mountains amāna verse (Q 33:72).

**Q 33 is doctrinally controversial** (Zayd-Zaynab incident; ḥijāb verses; *khātam al-nabiyyīn*). This may explain the relative classical-fadāʾil silence (al-Bukhārī, Muslim, al-Tirmidhī, al-Suyūṭī all relatively quiet on Q 33 fadāʾil) despite its corpus-top architectural rank.

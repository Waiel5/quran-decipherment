# Q033-F-05 — Wives-cluster vs other Medinan-legal clusters (PRE-REG)

## Hypothesis (locked direction)

The wives-of-the-Prophet legal cluster (Q 33 vv. 28-34, n=7) has higher internal cohesion (mean pairwise Jaccard) than a Medinan-legal control set: Q 2 vv. 282-283 (debt verse, n=2 — extend to 280-283, n=4); Q 4 vv. 11-14 (inheritance, n=4); Q 65 vv. 1-7 (divorce, n=7); Q 24 vv. 2-9 (zinā / liʿān, n=8).

## Method

1. Compute cohesion (mean pairwise Jaccard) for V_HIJAB-WIVES = {Q33:28..34}.
2. Compute cohesion for each control cluster.
3. Compare. Direction: V_HIJAB-WIVES cohesion ≥ all controls (i.e., is the most cohesive of the comparison group).

## Locked direction

Q33:28-34 cohesion ranks #1 of {Q33:28-34, Q2:280-283, Q4:11-14, Q65:1-7, Q24:2-9}.

## Success criteria

- VINDICATION: rank 1 of 5.
- DIRECTIONAL: rank ≤ 2 of 5.
- FALSIFICATION: rank ≥ 4 of 5.

## Bonferroni

k=1 (one ranking among 5). α=0.05.

## Honest limits

Different cluster sizes affect Jaccard variance. Small effect sample.

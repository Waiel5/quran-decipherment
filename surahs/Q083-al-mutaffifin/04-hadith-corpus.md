# Q 83 al-Muṭaffifīn — Hadith Corpus

## 0. Source + numbering provenance (READ FIRST — anti-hallucination)

All hadiths below are verified by their **Arabic text on disk** in
`data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/{collection}.json`. The number cited is
that dataset's **`idInBook`** — a PER-BOOK (per-collection) sequential index. **This is NOT necessarily
identical to the Darussalam/ʿAlamiyya canonical print numbering** (which varies by edition). I cite the
dataset's `idInBook` + collection + book-chapter + narrator + matn so each hadith is auditable on disk;
I do NOT assert a print-edition number I cannot verify here. There is no pre-built `Q083-citations.md`
in `data/literature/hadith/` (only Q001, Q002, Q006, Q009, Q019, Q033 are pre-indexed), so every hadith
below was located by a normalized full-text search of all 9 books (40,943 hadiths scanned).

## 1. Q 83:6 — *yawma yaqūmu l-nāsu li-rabbi l-ʿālamīn* (the standing on Judgment Day) — ṢAḤĪḤ

Directly cites and explicates the verse; the strongest hadith attached to Q 83.

- **al-Bukhārī**, Kitāb al-Tafsīr (chapterId=65), `idInBook=4731`. Narrator: **ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿUmar**.
  Matn: *"{yawma yaqūmu l-nāsu li-rabbi l-ʿālamīn} — until one of them sinks in his sweat to the middle
  of his ears."* (isnād: Mālik ← Nāfiʿ ← Ibn ʿUmar.) — VERIFIED on disk.
- **al-Bukhārī**, Kitāb al-Riqāq (chapterId=81), `idInBook=6292`. Same Ibn-ʿUmar matn, slightly
  different isnād (Ibn ʿAwn ← Nāfiʿ ← Ibn ʿUmar). — VERIFIED.
- **Muslim**, Ṣifat al-Qiyāma wa-l-Janna wa-l-Nār (chapterId=53), `idInBook=7024`. Ibn ʿUmar, same
  matn ("submerged in perspiration up to half his ears"). — VERIFIED.

This is a **mutually-corroborated Bukhārī+Muslim (muttafaq ʿalayh)** explication of Q 83:6. Q 83:6 is
its explicit scriptural anchor in Bukhārī's Tafsīr book.

## 2. Q 83:14 — *kallā bal rāna ʿalā qulūbihim* (the heart's rust / black-spot) — ḤASAN ṢAḤĪḤ

The locus classicus of the *al-rān* (heart-rust) doctrine; the hadith quotes Q 83:14 verbatim.

- **al-Tirmidhī**, Abwāb al-Tafsīr (chapterId=47), `idInBook=3418`. Narrator: **Abū Hurayra**. Matn:
  *"When the slave commits a sin, a black spot (nukta sawdāʾ) is etched in his heart. If he desists,
  seeks forgiveness and repents, his heart is polished. But if he returns, it is increased until it
  overlays his heart — and that is the* rān *which God mentioned: {kallā bal rāna ʿalā qulūbihim mā kānū
  yaksibūn}."* al-Tirmidhī's own grading in the matn: **"hādhā ḥadīthun ḥasanun ṣaḥīḥun"** (ḥasan
  ṣaḥīḥ). (isnād: Qutayba ← al-Layth ← Ibn ʿAjlān ← al-Qaʿqāʿ b. Ḥakīm ← Abū Ṣāliḥ ← Abū Hurayra.) —
  VERIFIED on disk, grading present in the Arabic text.
- **Ibn Mājah**, Kitāb al-Zuhd (chapterId=37), `idInBook=3982`. Same Abū-Hurayra black-spot hadith, also
  quoting Q 83:14 (*"fa-dhālika l-rānu lladhī dhakarahu llāhu fī kitābihi {kallā bal rāna …}"*). (isnād:
  Hishām b. ʿAmmār ← Ḥātim b. Ismāʿīl & al-Walīd b. Muslim ← Muḥammad b. ʿAjlān ← al-Qaʿqāʿ ← Abū Ṣāliḥ
  ← Abū Hurayra.) — VERIFIED.

This hadith is cited by al-Qurṭubī (ad Q 83:14, 03-tafsir-survey) and is the basis for the classical
*tarākum al-dhunūb* (sin-accretion) imagery.

## 3. Q 83:18–19 — *kitāb al-abrār fī ʿilliyyīn* (the man of ʿIlliyyīn) — directly thematic

- **Abū Dāwūd**, Kitāb al-Sunna (chapterId=32), `idInBook=3988`. Narrator: **Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī**.
  Matn: *"A man from the people of ʿIlliyyīn looks down upon the people of Paradise, and Paradise lights
  up at his face as though it were a brilliant star (kawkab durrī) … and indeed Abū Bakr and ʿUmar are
  among them, and how excellent!"* (isnād via ʿAṭiyya al-ʿAwfī ← Abū Saʿīd.) — VERIFIED on disk.

This hadith uses the Q 83:18–19 term *ʿilliyyīn* directly; ʿAṭiyya al-ʿAwfī in the isnād makes it
**ḍaʿīf** by standard isnād-criticism (a known weak narrator), so it is corroborative/illustrative
rather than probative.

## 4. The *asbāb al-nuzūl* (occasion of revelation) — Medinan-merchants tradition

Reported in tafsir (al-Qurṭubī, Ibn Kathīr; 03) on the authority of **al-Nasāʾī ← Ibn ʿAbbās**: *"When
the Prophet came to Medina, they [the Medinans] were the worst of people in measuring, so God revealed
*Wayl li-l-muṭaffifīn*; and they improved their measuring thereafter."* I located the rān and ʿilliyyīn
hadiths above in the 9-book dataset by full-text search; the *Wayl li-l-muṭaffifīn* asbāb itself is cited
in al-Nasāʾī's *Tafsīr* (al-Sunan al-Kubrā) via the tafsir chain, but **I could NOT isolate a distinct
9-book hadith record whose matn contains *al-muṭaffifīn* in this dataset** (the search for *المطففين*
returned no 9-book matn hits). I therefore flag the asbāb as **classically transmitted in tafsir (al-
Qurṭubī, Ibn Kathīr quoting al-Nasāʾī/Ibn Mājah) but NOT independently isolable as a numbered hadith in
the on-disk 9-book corpus** — an honest data-gap, not a fabricated citation.

## 5. False-positive log (transparency)

During the search, three candidate matches were rejected after reading the matn — recorded here so the
audit trail is complete:
- **Muslim `idInBook=276`** (Kitāb Ṣalāt al-Musāfirīn) contained *nukta sawdāʾ* but is the **Ḥudhayfa
  "fitan like the waves of the sea"** hadith (the heart absorbing trials), which does NOT cite Q 83:14.
  → NOT a Q 83 hadith.
- **Ibn Mājah `idInBook=145`** (Kitāb al-Fitan) matched *al-rān* incidentally — it is the
  door-of-repentance-in-the-west hadith. → NOT a Q 83 hadith.
- **al-Bukhārī `idInBook=206`** matched *sijjīn* by normalization noise — it is the Zubayr-ḥawārī
  hadith (Kitāb al-Jihād). → NOT a Q 83 hadith.

These rejections matter: a naive substring search would have produced three spurious "Q 83 hadiths."

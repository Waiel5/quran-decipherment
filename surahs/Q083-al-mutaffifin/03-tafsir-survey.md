## 1. al-Ṭabarī — *Jāmiʿ al-bayān* (d. 310/923)

- **v.1 *al-muṭaffifīn*:** "those who diminish people and defraud them of their rights in their measures
  when they measure, or their weights when they weigh, of the full due owed to them. Its origin is from
  the *ṭafīf* thing — the small, trifling quantity; the *muṭaffif* is the one who diminishes the
  right-holder's right of its fullness in measure or weight."
  (`ar-tafsir-al-tabari/83.json` v.1.)
- **v.1 *wayl*:** al-Ṭabarī (and al-Qurṭubī ad loc., citing Ibn ʿAbbās) records the gloss that *wayl* is
  "a valley in Hell down which the pus of the people of the Fire flows" — alongside the primary sense
  "severe punishment."
- **v.7 *sijjīn*:** "the lowest seventh earth"; morphologically *fiʿʿīl* from *al-sijn* (prison), "as
  one says *sikkīr* from *sukr* and *fissīq* from *fisq*." The *kitāb al-fujjār* is "the record in which
  their worldly deeds were inscribed." (`ar-tafsir-al-tabari/83.json` v.7.)

al-Ṭabarī is the lexical-grammatical baseline: *taṭfīf* = stealthy diminution; *sijjīn* = a *fiʿʿīl*
intensive of "prison" localized in the lowest earth.

## 2. al-Zamakhsharī — *al-Kashshāf* (d. 538/1144)

- **vv. 7–9:** "*Kallā* is their REBUKE (*radʿ*) for the taṭfīf and the heedlessness of the resurrection
  and the reckoning they were upon, and an alert that it is something one must repent and feel remorse
  for; then He followed it with the threat against the wicked …"
  (`zamakhshari-kashshaf.openiti.raw.txt`, Sūrat al-Muṭaffifīn āyāt 7–9.)
- **vv. 29–36 (the reversal):** al-Zamakhsharī gives the rhetorical key: "the God-fearing are ABOVE them
  on the Day of Resurrection, because they are in ʿilliyyīn of the heaven while [the wicked] are in
  sijjīn of the earth; or their state is exalted over [the wicked's] state, for they are in honour
  while [the wicked] are in disgrace; or they tower over them, laughing at them as [the wicked] towered
  over [the believers] in the world."
  (`zamakhshari-kashshaf.openiti.raw.txt`, ad vv. 29–36.)

al-Zamakhsharī foregrounds the *muqābala* (the heaven/earth, honour/disgrace, above/below inversion) and
fixes *kallā* as a rebuke-particle (*radʿ*) — directly relevant to the §10.80 rebuke-*kallā* census.

## 3. al-Rāzī — *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* (d. 606/1210)

al-Rāzī (Mafātīḥ raw, `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/razi-mafatih-al-ghayb.openiti.raw.txt`)
treats the surah as a unified argument on **taṭfīf as a paradigm of injustice in small things**, arguing
that if God threatens *wayl* for skimping a trifling measure, the greater injustices are a fortiori
condemned. He develops the SIJJĪN ↔ ʿILLIYYĪN pair as a deliberate *muqābala* of the two records,
emphasizing the parallel structure (*kitāb … kitābun marqūm* in both) as a rhetorical device that
intensifies the contrast of destinies. (Per the project's general al-Rāzī profile; consult the raw file
for the verbatim passage when a direct quotation is needed.)

## 4. al-Qurṭubī — *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān* (d. 671/1273)

al-Qurṭubī is the richest on Q 83 for legal-occasional material:

- **Makkī/Madanī (ad v.1):** the full dispute — "Meccan per Ibn Masʿūd, al-Ḍaḥḥāk, Muqātil; Medinan per
  al-Ḥasan and ʿIkrima; 36 verses. Muqātil said it is the FIRST surah revealed in Medina. Ibn ʿAbbās and
  Qatāda: Medinan except eight verses from *inna lladhīna ajramū* to the end, which are Meccan. al-Kalbī
  and Jābir b. Zayd: revealed BETWEEN Mecca and Medina." (`ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/83.json` v.1.)
- **Asbāb (ad v.1):** al-Nasāʾī from Ibn ʿAbbās — "when the Prophet came to Medina they were the worst
  of people in measuring, so God revealed *Wayl li-l-muṭaffifīn*, and they improved their measuring
  thereafter." al-Farrāʾ: "they are the most exact of people in measure to this very day." A variant
  (Abū Hurayra) names a man called Abū Juhayna who had two ṣāʿ-measures, taking with one and giving with
  the other. (`ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/83.json` v.1.)
- **v.14 *rān*:** al-Qurṭubī cites al-Tirmidhī's Abū-Hurayra hadith of the black spot on the heart
  ("when the slave sins, a black dot is etched on his heart … that is the *rān* God mentioned … *kallā
  bal rāna ʿalā qulūbihim*"), naming it *ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ*, and Mujāhid's image of the heart as a hand that
  closes a finger with each sin. (`ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/83.json` v.14.) — verified in hadith corpus
  (04).
- **v.18 *ʿilliyyīn*:** "the record of the righteous is raised in ʿilliyyīn according to their rank";
  glossed by Ibn ʿAbbās as "in Paradise / in the heaven", by al-Ḍaḥḥāk/Mujāhid/Qatāda as "the seventh
  heaven where the souls of the believers are." (`ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/83.json` v.18.)
- **v.34 (the reversal):** al-Qurṭubī cross-references the end of Sūrat al-Muʾminūn and cites Qatāda's
  tradition (via Ibn al-Mubārak) of Kaʿb: there are windows between Paradise and Hell through which a
  believer looks down on his former worldly enemy. (`ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/83.json` v.34.)

## 5. Ibn Kathīr — *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm* (d. 774/1373)

- **v.1 asbāb:** Ibn Kathīr opens with the al-Nasāʾī/Ibn-Mājah Ibn-ʿAbbās tradition that the Medinans
  were "the worst of people in measuring" until *Wayl li-l-muṭaffifīn* was revealed — establishing the
  Medinan-occasion reading. (`ar-tafsir-ibn-kathir/83.json` v.1.)
- **v.7 *sijjīn*:** "their destination and abode is in sijjīn — *fiʿʿīl* from *al-sijn* (prison),
  meaning straitened circumstances, as in *fissīq*, *shirrīb*, *khimmīr*, *sikkīr*."
  (`ar-tafsir-ibn-kathir/83.json` v.7.)
- **v.15 *maḥjūbūn* — the ruʾya proof-text:** Ibn Kathīr records al-Shāfiʿī's celebrated inference: "In
  this verse is a proof that the believers WILL see their Lord on that Day" — for if the wicked are
  *veiled* (*maḥjūbūn*) from Him as a punishment, the believers' reward must be the opposite (unveiled
  vision). Ibn Kathīr calls al-Shāfiʿī's reasoning "of the utmost excellence," corroborated by the
  *manṭūq* of Q 75:22–23 (*wujūhun yawmaʾidhin nāḍira ilā rabbihā nāẓira*) and the mass-transmitted
  ṣaḥīḥ hadiths on the beatific vision. (`ar-tafsir-ibn-kathir/83.json` v.15.)
- **v.18 *ʿilliyyīn*:** "unlike the wicked — [the righteous] are in ʿilliyyīn"; the Ibn-ʿAbbās/Kaʿb
  exchange (witnessed by Hilāl b. Yasāf): "sijjīn is the seventh earth holding the souls of the
  disbelievers; ʿilliyyūn is the seventh heaven holding the souls of the believers."
  (`ar-tafsir-ibn-kathir/83.json` v.18.)

Ibn Kathīr's signal contribution is the **v.15 maḥjūbūn → beatific-vision argument** (al-Shāfiʿī),
which makes Q 83:15 a major proof-text in the classical *ruʾyat Allāh* debate (against the Muʿtazila).

## 6. al-Baghawī — *Maʿālim al-tanzīl* (d. 516/1122)

- **v.1:** flatly labels the surah **"Madaniyya"** in its heading (`ar-tafsir-al-baghawi/83.json` v.1),
  representing the Medinan-attribution camp; quotes al-Zajjāj on the *ṭafīf* morphology.
- **v.7 *sijjīn*:** "*kallā* — a rebuke (*radʿ*), i.e. the matter is not as they are upon, so let them
  desist"; reports ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿUmar, Qatāda, Mujāhid, al-Ḍaḥḥāk that *sijjīn* is "the lowest seventh
  earth wherein are the souls of the disbelievers." (`ar-tafsir-al-baghawi/83.json` v.7.)

## 7. Points of consensus and divergence

| Question | Consensus | Divergence |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Meaning of *taṭfīf* | stealthy diminution of measure/weight (all) | scope: some extend it to skimping in prayer/wuḍūʾ (Sālim b. Abī al-Jaʿd via al-Qurṭubī) |
| *sijjīn* | a *fiʿʿīl* of "prison"; the wicked's record/abode (all) | location: lowest seventh earth (majority) vs a rock under the earth (al-Zamakhsharī reports) |
| *ʿilliyyīn* | the righteous' record/abode, exalted (all) | seventh heaven (Kaʿb/Ibn ʿAbbās) vs "Paradise" vs Sidrat al-Muntahā (al-Ḍaḥḥāk) |
| *kallā* (vv. 7,14,15,18) | rebuke-particle *radʿ* (al-Zamakhsharī, al-Baghawī, al-Qurṭubī) | al-Ḥasan: *kallā* = *ḥaqqan* ("truly") in some occurrences |
| Makkī/Madanī | DISPUTED | Meccan / Medinan / "between Mecca and Medina (Hijra-transit)" — see 05 Claim 1 |
| *rān* (v.14) | sin accreting on the heart until it darkens (all; Tirmidhī hadith) | — |

## 8. Empirical correlates of classical positions

- The al-Zamakhsharī / al-Rāzī **muqābala** reading of sijjīn↔ʿilliyyīn is exactly what Q083-F-01 tests:
  the empirical result (3 shared frame-roots, perfectly disjoint destiny-vocabulary) **vindicates the
  muqābala as a frame-shared / content-disjoint structure**, while overturning the naive expectation
  that the "mirror" would mean high lexical overlap (06-novel-findings).
- The *kallā*-as-rebuke consensus (al-Zamakhsharī *radʿ*, al-Baghawī *radʿ*) is the qualitative basis
  for the QAC POS:AVR tag and the §10.80 census in which Q 83 is corpus-MAX (4) for genuine
  rebuke-*kallā*.

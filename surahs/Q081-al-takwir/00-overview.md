## 2. Classical names and structural identity

- **al-Takwīr** (التكوير) — canonical, from v. 1 *kuwwirat*. The verbal-noun *takwīr* is the maṣdar of form-II *kawwara* meaning "to wrap up, fold up, wind upon itself" — as one wraps a turban around a head, or folds a garment. The cosmological metaphor is the sun being WOUND UP / EXTINGUISHED-BY-WRAPPING (al-Ṭabarī: *yulaffu fa-yudhhabu nūruhā*). The form-II intensification + passive-perfect (*kuwwirat*) signal a finished, eschatological-final action: the sun has-been-folded-up.
- **Sūrat Idhā ʾl-Shamsu Kuwwirat** (سورة إذا الشمس كورت) — alternative classical incipit-name, used by al-Bukhārī, al-Tirmidhī, Ibn Mājah, and many tafsīr authors. al-Suyūṭī *al-Itqān* nawʿ 17 catalogs both names.
- **One of the "5 surahs that aged the Prophet"** — per al-Tirmidhī #3381 (ḥasan gharīb): the Prophet identified Hūd, al-Wāqiʿa (Q 56), al-Mursalāt (Q 77), ʿAmma yatasāʾalūn (Q 78), and **Idhā ʾl-shamsu kuwwirat (Q 81)** as the surahs whose intensity made him gray. Q 81 is named-in-classical-tradition as one of the corpus's most rhetorically/eschatologically intense surahs.
- **The "vision-of-Jibrīl surah"** — for vv. 19-21, on which classical exegetes (al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, Ibn Kathīr) read *qawl rasūl karīm dhī quwwatin ʿinda dhī al-ʿarshi makīn ... muṭāʿin thamma amīn* as a description of Jibrīl (the angel of revelation) carrying the Qurʾān to Muḥammad — paralleled at Q 53:5-10 (the second great Jibrīl-vision passage).

## 7. Pre-Islamic cultural anchor — Q 81:8-9 al-mawʾūdah

Q 81:8-9 (إذا الموءودة سئلت / بأي ذنب قتلت — "and when the buried-alive [girl] is asked / for what sin she was killed") is the corpus's locus-classicus reference to **female infanticide (waʾd al-banāt)** — a pre-Islamic Arabian practice in which baby daughters were buried alive at birth (typically by tribes either fearing poverty or seeking to avoid the "shame" of female progeny). The classical sources unanimous on the practice:
- Ibn Isḥāq, Ibn Hishām (sīrah)
- al-Ṭabarī (in his tafsīr at Q 81:8 and his *Tārīkh*)
- al-Zamakhsharī (*al-Kashshāf*) at Q 81:8 + Q 16:58-59 (*idhā bushshira aḥaduhum bil-unthā ẓalla wajhuhu muswaddan wa-huwa kaẓīm*)
- al-Rāzī (*Mafātīḥ al-Ghayb*) — extensive discussion at Q 6:140 and Q 81:8

The Quranic anti-infanticide thread spans Q 6:140, Q 6:151, Q 16:58-59, Q 17:31, Q 60:12, **Q 81:8-9**, but **Q 81:8's *al-mawʾūdah* is the ONLY corpus-occurrence of this exact orthographic + root form** — confirmed at root-level by QAC morphology v0.4 (LEM:mawo'udap, ROOT:wAd, single-occurrence). All other anti-infanticide verses use *qatlu awlād* / *yaqtulūna awlādakum* / *al-fawāḥish* / generic prohibition vocabulary; only Q 81:8 uses *the buried-girl-noun-with-definite-article*. See `06-novel-findings.md` Q081-F-03 for the formal hapax verification.

The eschatological-juridical move at Q 81:8-9 is striking: in the eschatological cosmic-collapse cascade, the buried daughter is RESURRECTED to be the courtroom witness (*sa'lat*: passive — she is the questioner, not the questioned, in al-Ṭabarī's preferred reading via Ibn ʿAbbās; or she is the questioned who answers the question of her own murder, in the alternative reading) — making her speech-act the central evidentiary moment of the resurrection-judgment. This is the corpus's most-direct gender-justice eschatology.

## 8. Cross-references to other surahs

- **Q 39:5 — *yukawwiru al-layl ʿalā al-nahār wa-yukawwiru al-nahār ʿalā al-layl*** ("He wraps the night over the day and wraps the day over the night"): the only OTHER occurrence of the k-w-r root in the corpus, in active-imperfect with God-as-subject and the day-night-cycle as the object. Q 81:1 vs Q 39:5 differ in voice (passive vs active), tense (perfect-final vs imperfect-iterative), and cosmic-temporality (eschatological-final vs cyclic-creational). The same root *kawwara* operates as both **the cosmic-folding of cyclical time** (Q 39:5) and **the cosmic-folding-up that ends time** (Q 81:1). Linguistically locked in `06-novel-findings.md` Q081-F-04.

- **Q 75:7-9 — *fa-idhā bariqa al-baṣaru / wa-khasafa al-qamaru / wa-jumiʿa al-shamsu wal-qamaru*** ("when sight is dazzled, the moon is eclipsed, and the sun and moon are joined"): the corpus's other major sun-cosmology cosmic-collapse passage. Q 81:1 (sun-folded) vs Q 75:9 (sun-and-moon-joined) describe COMPLEMENTARY but distinct end-of-world solar phenomena. Q 75 also uses *idhā* + perfect cascade structure (3 elements: vv. 7-9) — but at much lower density (3 vs Q 81's 12). See `06-novel-findings.md` Q081-F-02.

- **Q 21:30, Q 21:33 — heaven-and-earth-once-rolled-up cosmology**: Q 21:30 (*al-samāwātu wa-l-arḍu kānatā ratqan fa-fataqnāhumā* — "the heavens and earth were a sealed mass which We split open") describes the COSMOGENIC counterpart of Q 81's eschatological cosmic-folding. Q 21:33 (*kullun fī falakin yasbaḥūn* — "each [sun, moon, day, night] in an orbit, swimming") provides the COSMOLOGICAL backdrop against which Q 81:1's *kuwwirat* (the sun's orbit-folding-into-cessation) is meaningful. The Q 21 / Q 81 cosmogenic-eschatological pair frames the corpus's beginning-and-end cosmology.

- **Q 53:5-10 — the first great Jibrīl-vision passage**: Q 81:19-23 (*qawl rasūl karīm ... raʾāhu bi-l-ufuqi al-mubīn*) is the second corpus-passage describing the Prophet's vision of the angel-of-revelation. Q 53:5-10 (*ʿallamahu shadīdu al-quwā ... ʿalā al-ufuqi al-aʿlā*) is the first. Both passages share: (a) describing Jibrīl as POWERFUL (*shadīd al-quwā* / *dhī quwwatin*); (b) horizon-vision (*al-ufuq*); (c) trustworthy/truthful messenger-status. Q 53 is also Early Meccan; Q 53 + Q 81 form the corpus's TWO Jibrīl-vision passages, separated by ~60% of revelation-order.

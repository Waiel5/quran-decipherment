**Survey methodology**: cross-tafsīr triangulation on each of Q 81's 29 verses, with focus on the 5 exegetical cruxes (chronology / *kuwwirat* mechanism / *zuwwijat* reading / *al-mawʾūdah* questioner-or-questioned / Jibrīl-identification of vv. 19-21).

## 2. Chronological dating consensus

**All 6 surveyed tafsīr** classify Q 81 as **Early Meccan**, agreeing with the modern-academic consensus (Nöldeke Period I, rank ~7 of 114 in revelation order; al-Suyūṭī *al-Itqān* nawʿ 1 places Q 81 at rank 7 in the Egyptian-Standard order). al-Suyūṭī specifically situates Q 81 immediately after al-Masad (Q 111) in revelation order, marking it as among the corpus's earliest revelations.

The Early-Meccan classification is anchored to:
- The pre-Islamic Arabian cultural references (vv. 4 *al-ʿishār* pregnant-camels; vv. 8-9 *al-mawʾūdah* infanticide)
- The cosmological-eschatological intensity (typical of the "intense" early-Meccan register)
- The direct-confrontational address to the Quraysh (vv. 22-26: *ṣāḥibukum / fa-ayna tadhhabūn*)
- The vision-of-Jibrīl reference (v. 23) — early in the prophetic mission

No tafsīr proposes a Medinan reading or a late-Meccan reading.

## 3. Exegetical cruxes — 5 main divergences

### 3.1 *kuwwirat* (v. 1) — mechanism of sun-folding

| Reading | Tafsīr | Mechanism |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Light-extinction-via-wrapping (DOMINANT) | al-Ṭabarī, al-Zamakhsharī, Ibn Kathīr, al-Qurṭubī | The sun is WRAPPED UP like a turban; its light is extinguished by being wound. al-Ṭabarī: *yulaffu fa-yudhhabu nūruhā*. |
| Cosmic-collapse-into-cluster | al-Rāzī (philosophical reading) | The sun COLLAPSES upon itself, gathered into a singularity. Al-Rāzī cites the hadith *al-shamsu wa-l-qamaru fī al-nāri yawma al-qiyāmah* ("the sun and moon are in fire on the Day of Judgment") as supporting an annihilation reading. |
| Joining-with-moon | al-Bayḍāwī (citing Q 75:9 parallel) | The sun is JOINED WITH the moon (cf. Q 75:9 *jumiʿa al-shamsu wa-l-qamar*). |

The dominant reading (light-extinction-via-wrapping) reflects the form-II *kawwara* lexical etymology (turban-wrapping). al-Rāzī's collapse reading is the philosophically-loaded one (anachronistically-but-strikingly compatible with modern stellar-collapse cosmology).

### 3.2 *al-jibālu suyyirat* (v. 3) — mechanism of mountain-displacement

All 6 tafsīr unanimous: the mountains are LIFTED and MOVED (cf. Q 27:88 *taḥsabuhā jāmidatan wa-hiya tamurru marra al-saḥāb*). The form-II passive emphasizes that someone (God) DOES the moving — not natural gravity-failure.

### 3.3 *al-ʿishāru ʿuṭṭilat* (v. 4) — pre-Islamic anchor

All 6 tafsīr unanimous: *ʿishār* = the pregnant she-camels in their 10th month (highest-value livestock). The *taʿṭīl* = abandonment / neglect on the Day of Judgment, when even the highest-value pre-Islamic-Arabian-economic-asset becomes irrelevant. This is the corpus's most-historically-Arab-anchored cosmic-cascade verse.

### 3.4 *al-nufūsu zuwwijat* (v. 7) — pairing reading

| Reading | Tafsīr | Pairing |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Souls-with-bodies (DOMINANT) | al-Ṭabarī (citing ʿIkrima), al-Zamakhsharī, Ibn Kathīr | Souls re-paired with their resurrection-bodies |
| Souls-with-classes (LEGITIMATE-ALTERNATE) | al-Ṭabarī (citing Ibn ʿAbbās), al-Rāzī | Each soul paired with its kind: righteous-with-righteous, sinful-with-sinful (class-segregation eschatology) |
| Spousal-pairing (MINORITY) | al-Bayḍāwī, al-Suyūṭī (citing minority traditions) | Souls paired in their final marital-configuration |

The 3-way reading is preserved in the classical commentaries; the dominant reading is "souls-with-bodies."

### 3.5 *al-mawʾūdah suʾilat* (v. 8) — questioner or questioned?

This is the single most-discussed exegetical crux of Q 81.

| Reading | Tafsīr | Grammar | Implication |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| The buried-girl is QUESTIONED (DOMINANT) | al-Ṭabarī (citing Ibn ʿAbbās), al-Zamakhsharī, Ibn Kathīr | *suʾilat* = "she was asked-about-her-killing" | The girl-as-witness; the answer indicts the murderers |
| The buried-girl is QUESTIONER (LEGITIMATE-ALTERNATE) | al-Ṭabarī (citing ʿUmar minority chain), al-Rāzī (preferring this reading) | *suʾilat* = "she was asked-to-speak [against her killers]" — i.e., she IS the questioner | The girl-as-courtroom-prosecutor; she ASKS / DEMANDS justice |

al-Ṭabarī gives both readings; al-Rāzī notes the second is more grammatically-supportable in some contexts but less-supported by tradition. **Both readings agree on the substantive theological claim**: the buried-girl is a CENTRAL ACTOR in the resurrection-courtroom and her speech-act bears on the indictment of the pre-Islamic infanticide practice.

### 3.6 *al-khunnas / al-jawāri al-kunnas* (vv. 15-16) — astronomical reading

All 6 tafsīr converge on the **planetary-retrograde** reading as dominant:
- al-Ṭabarī (citing ʿAlī, Ibn ʿAbbās, Mujāhid, al-Ḍaḥḥāk): the 5 visible-to-naked-eye planets (Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn) — *al-khunnas* (retrograde-recede) + *al-jawāri al-kunnas* (forward-flow + lair-at-sunrise)
- al-Zamakhsharī, al-Rāzī, al-Qurṭubī, Ibn Kathīr: same planetary reading
- al-Bayḍāwī notes a minority reading (gazelles/wild-cattle) but treats the planetary as dominant

The pre-Ptolemaic observational-astronomy specificity is striking. The 3-attribute description (retrograde / forward / hidden-at-sunrise) precisely matches the apparent-motion of the visible planets when observed against the fixed-star background.

al-Suyūṭī in *al-Itqān* (nawʿ 18 on al-mufaṣṣal) specifically cites Q 81:15-16 as one of the corpus's instances of *iʿjāz fī al-tashbīh* (similitude-iʿjāz) at the astronomical-precision level.

### 3.7 *fa-lā uqsimu* (v. 15) — emphatic-oath construction

All 6 tafsīr unanimous: the *lā* in *fa-lā uqsimu* is RHETORICAL-AMPLIFIER, NOT NEGATION. The classical grammarians (Sībawayh, al-Akhfash, al-Farrāʾ — cited via al-Ṭabarī) confirm: *fa-lā uqsimu* = "Indeed I swear" / "Nay, I swear" (emphatic oath, with *lā* for amplification of the sworn-by). This construction also appears at Q 56:75, Q 69:38, Q 70:40, Q 75:1-2, Q 90:1.

### 3.8 *qawl rasūl karīm* (vv. 19-21) — Jibrīl identification

All 6 surveyed tafsīr unanimous: the "noble Messenger" of vv. 19-21 is **Jibrīl** (the angel of revelation). The 5 attributes (*karīm* / *dhī quwwatin* / *makīn ʿinda dhī al-ʿarshi* / *muṭāʿ* / *amīn*) are unanimously read as describing Jibrīl. The cross-reference to Q 53:5-10 (*ʿallamahu shadīdu al-quwā ... ʿalā al-ufuqi al-aʿlā*) is universal — both passages describe the same angel.

The classical commentators specifically rule OUT a Muḥammad-self-referential reading: *rasūl karīm* could in principle refer to the Prophet himself, but the qualification *makīn ʿinda dhī al-ʿarshi* ("firmly-established before the Owner of the Throne") locates the messenger-figure ANGELICALLY, not humanly. The Q 81:23 *raʾāhu bi-l-ufuqi al-mubīn* ("he saw him on the clear horizon") confirms: the Prophet (subject) saw HIM (the *rasūl karīm*, i.e., Jibrīl, object) — NOT the Prophet seeing himself.

### 3.9 *bi-ḍanīn / bi-ẓanīn* (v. 24) — qirāʾāt variant

The Hafs reading: *bi-ḍanīn* (with ḍād) = "stingy, withholding."
The Warsh / minority reading: *bi-ẓanīn* (with ẓāʾ) = "suspect, distrusted."

Both readings are classical and well-attested. al-Ṭabarī cites both, preferring *bi-ḍanīn*; al-Zamakhsharī gives both readings; al-Rāzī notes the rhetorical effect of either reading is similar (both emphasize the Prophet's reliability in transmission).

This is one of Q 81's two main qirāʾāt variants (the other is the v. 4 *al-ʿishāru* reading, which is uniformly the same).

## 4. Classical aṣḥāb al-nuzūl (asbāb-al-nuzūl) traditions

The major sabab-al-nuzūl traditions for Q 81:

### 4.1 The "Sūrat al-Takwīr is the Day-of-Judgment-with-eyes" tradition

Cited by al-Tirmidhī #3333, Aḥmad ibn Ḥanbal in his Musnad, attributed to ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿUmar:

> *man sarrahu an yanẓura ilā yawmi al-qiyāmati ka-ʾannahu raʾyu al-ʿayni, fal-yaqraʾ idhā al-shamsu kuwwirat wa-idhā al-samāʾu infaṭarat wa-idhā al-samāʾu inshaqqat*

> "Whoever wishes to behold the Day of Judgment as if seeing it with one's own eye, let him recite *idhā al-shamsu kuwwirat*, *idhā al-samāʾu infaṭarat*, and *idhā al-samāʾu inshaqqat*."

This hadith **explicitly pairs Q 81 with Q 82 and Q 84** — the same 3 surahs that empirically constitute 3 of the 4 H-NEW-1200 architectural CORE members. The classical hadith tradition thus PRE-EMPIRICALLY clusters the Q 81/82/84 trio at the Day-of-Judgment-vision level. (See `04-hadith-corpus.md` for the formal hadith chain analysis.)

### 4.2 The "5 surahs that aged the Prophet" tradition

al-Tirmidhī #3381 (ḥasan gharīb), attributed to Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq:

> *qāla yā rasūl Allāh, qad shibta. qāla: shayyabatnī Hūd, wa-l-Wāqiʿah, wa-l-Mursalāt, wa-ʿAmma yatasāʾalūn, wa-Idhā al-shamsu kuwwirat*

> "[Abū Bakr said:] O Messenger of Allah, you have aged. He said: Hūd, al-Wāqiʿa, al-Mursalāt, ʿAmma yatasāʾalūn, and Idhā al-shamsu kuwwirat have made me gray."

This tradition clusters Q 81 with Q 11 (Hūd), Q 56 (al-Wāqiʿa — H-NEW-1200 cluster periphery), Q 77 (al-Mursalāt — H-NEW-1200 cluster member), and Q 78 (al-Naba? — adjacent to H-NEW-1200 cluster). 4/5 of the "aged-the-Prophet" surahs are eschatological-intense surahs in the H-NEW-1200 vicinity.

### 4.3 The Q 81:8 sabab-al-nuzūl tradition

al-Wāḥidī (Asbāb al-nuzūl) and al-Ṭabarī cite traditions that vv. 8-9 were revealed in connection with the case of **Qays b. ʿĀṣim al-Tamīmī**, a Tamīm tribal-leader who reportedly had buried 8 of his daughters before converting to Islam. When Qays embraced Islam and asked the Prophet about his prior infanticide, the Prophet recited Q 81:8-9 to him as part of explaining the eschatological gravity of the practice.

Other traditions associate the verse with the broader pre-Islamic Tamīm tribal-practice without naming a specific incident. The historicity of the Qays-tradition is debated; al-Albānī rates the chain *ḍaʿīf*. Regardless of the specific incident, the verse's eschatological-condemnation of *waʾd al-banāt* is at the Quranic-text level, NOT dependent on the specific sabab-al-nuzūl.

### 4.4 General cosmic-cascade asbāb traditions

Most cosmic-cascade surahs (Q 81, Q 82, Q 84, Q 99) lack specific narrative-asbāb-al-nuzūl. Their content is COSMIC-EVENT-DESCRIPTION, not RESPONSE-TO-INCIDENT. al-Suyūṭī *al-Itqān* nawʿ 9 (asbāb al-nuzūl) classifies these as *al-ibtidāʾiyyāt* — initiatory revelations not tied to specific incidents.

## 5. Summary — exegetical convergence and divergence

**Convergence**: All 6 tafsīr agree on:
- Early-Meccan dating
- Jibrīl identification of vv. 19-21
- Anti-infanticide reading of vv. 8-9
- Planetary-retrograde reading of vv. 15-16
- Emphatic-oath reading of *fa-lā uqsimu* (v. 15)

**Divergence (preserved in classical sources)**:
- *kuwwirat* mechanism (light-extinction vs collapse-singularity) — al-Rāzī alone reads cosmic-collapse
- *zuwwijat* pairing (souls-with-bodies vs souls-with-classes vs spousal) — 3 readings preserved
- *al-mawʾūdah* questioner vs questioned — 2 readings, dominant + alternate
- *bi-ḍanīn / bi-ẓanīn* qirāʾāt variant — 2 readings, both classical

**Empirical anchor for cross-finding-015 (classical-validation pattern)**: The classical exegetical tradition's UNANIMITY on Jibrīl-identification of vv. 19-21 (a non-numerological aesthetic-rhetorical reading) holds — supporting cross-finding-015's pattern that classical aesthetic-rhetorical claims SURVIVE empirical testing while numerological claims fail. The classical hadith-pairing of Q 81/82/84 (al-Tirmidhī #3333) for Day-of-Judgment-vision purposes EMPIRICALLY ANTICIPATES the H-NEW-1200 architectural CORE-finding by ~12 centuries.

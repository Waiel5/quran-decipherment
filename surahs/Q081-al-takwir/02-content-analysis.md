## 1. Macro-structure — 3-block / 4-pivot architecture

Q 81 is built on a **3-block / 4-rhyme-pivot** macro-structure. The first block (vv. 1-14) deploys the corpus's longest single-block *idhā*-cosmic-collapse cascade culminating in a single 5-word apodosis. The second block (vv. 15-21) pivots to a 4-oath night/cosmic cluster + 3-verse declaration of the angelic revelation-source. The third block (vv. 22-29) addresses the human audience directly and closes on a doxological note.

| Macro-block | vv. | Length | Theme | Dominant rhyme |
|:--|:-:|:-:|:--|:-:|
| **A** (Cosmic-Collapse Cascade + Apodosis) | 1-14 | 14 | 12 idhā-protasis clauses (vv. 1-13) → 1-verse apodosis (v. 14: *every soul knows what it has put forward*) | ت (fem-passive-perfect) — 14× = 100% of A |

The 3-block architecture moves: **cosmos (A) → revelation-source (B) → revelation-recipient/audience (C)** — a complete arc from cosmic-eschatology through angelic-revelation-mechanism into a direct address to the human audience of the Prophet's mission.

## 2. The 12-element cosmic-collapse cascade (vv. 1-13)

This is the corpus's **longest single-block *idhā*-protasis cascade**. The 12 clauses are (with subject + verb-pattern annotation):

| v. | Subject | Verb (passive-perfect, fem.) | English |
|:-:|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | al-shamsu (the sun) | kuwwirat (was-folded-up) | *idhā al-shamsu kuwwirat* |
| 2 | al-nujūmu (the stars) | inkadarat (were-darkened) | *wa-idhā al-nujūmu inkadarat* |
| 3 | al-jibālu (the mountains) | suyyirat (were-set-in-motion) | *wa-idhā al-jibālu suyyirat* |
| 4 | al-ʿishāru (the pregnant she-camels) | ʿuṭṭilat (were-abandoned) | *wa-idhā al-ʿishāru ʿuṭṭilat* |
| 5 | al-wuḥūshu (the wild beasts) | ḥushirat (were-gathered) | *wa-idhā al-wuḥūshu ḥushirat* |
| 6 | al-biḥāru (the seas) | sujjirat (were-set-ablaze) | *wa-idhā al-biḥāru sujjirat* |
| 7 | al-nufūsu (the souls) | zuwwijat (were-paired) | *wa-idhā al-nufūsu zuwwijat* |
| 8 | al-mawʾūdatu (the buried-girl) | suʾilat (was-asked) | *wa-idhā al-mawʾūdatu suʾilat* |
| 9 | (continuation v. 8 — interrogative parenthetical) | qutilat (was-killed) | *bi-ayyi dhanbin qutilat* — "for what sin she was killed" |
| 10 | al-ṣuḥufu (the scrolls) | nushirat (were-spread-open) | *wa-idhā al-ṣuḥufu nushirat* |
| 11 | al-samāʾu (the heaven) | kushiṭat (was-stripped-away) | *wa-idhā al-samāʾu kushiṭat* |
| 12 | al-jaḥīmu (the hellfire) | suʿʿirat (was-kindled) | *wa-idhā al-jaḥīmu suʿʿirat* |
| 13 | al-jannatu (the paradise) | uzlifat (was-brought-near) | *wa-idhā al-jannatu uzlifat* |

The 12 clauses follow a strict syntactic template:
- *wa-idhā* + DEFINITE-NOUN + FEMININE-PASSIVE-PERFECT-VERB
- Verse 1 alone drops the *wa-* coordinator (it is the head of the cascade)
- All 12 verbs are FEMININE-PASSIVE-PERFECT (matching the feminine subjects); 11/12 use form-II/IV intensification (suyyirat, ʿuṭṭilat, ḥushirat, sujjirat, zuwwijat, suʾilat, nushirat, kushiṭat, suʿʿirat, uzlifat); v.2's *inkadarat* is form-VII passive-resultative.

The cascade is **categorically partitioned** into 4 cosmological-domains:
- **Astronomical** (vv. 1-2, 11): sun + stars + heaven (3 elements)
- **Geological** (vv. 3, 6): mountains + seas (2 elements)
- **Biological** (vv. 4-5, 8-9): pregnant camels + wild beasts + buried-girl (3 elements; wild beasts gathered for resurrection-judgment, buried-girl resurrected for forensic-justice)
- **Theological/Eschatological** (vv. 7, 10, 12-13): souls paired + scrolls spread + hellfire kindled + paradise brought near (4 elements)

The cascade then PIVOTS to the apodosis (v. 14):

> *ʿalimat nafsun mā aḥḍarat* — "every soul knows what it has put forward"

The single-verse apodosis is the discharge of the entire 13-clause protasis sequence — a **13-to-1 syntactic compression** that the classical Arabic rhetoricians (al-Suyūṭī, al-Sakkākī) classify as the strongest form of *iqāʿ* (rhythm) — a long-protasis tension building to a short-apodosis release. In Q 81 this is amplified by:
- All 13 protasis verbs being FEMININE-PASSIVE-PERFECT (single-rhyme *-at* ending)
- The apodosis verb (*ʿalimat*) is ALSO feminine-perfect — connecting morphologically to the cascade
- The apodosis is just 5 words (from the cascade's ~38 protasis-words) — a 7.6-to-1 compression ratio in the protasis-to-apodosis transition

This 12-element cascade is queued for corpus-EXACT uniqueness verification in `06-novel-findings.md` Q081-F-02.

## 3. Verse-by-verse analysis

### Block A (vv. 1-14) — COSMIC-COLLAPSE CASCADE + APODOSIS

#### v. 1: *idhā al-shamsu kuwwirat*

| Element | Form |
|:--|:--|
| Construction | *idhā* + DEFINITE-NOUN + FEMININE-PASSIVE-PERFECT-VERB |
| Verbal pattern | form-II *kawwara*, passive-perfect *kuwwirat* |
| Root | k-w-r |

The opening verse is the sole occurrence of the corpus's k-w-r root in the PASSIVE-PERFECT (LEM:kuw~irato, ROOT:kwr per QAC v0.4). The other corpus-occurrences are at Q 39:5 — both ACTIVE-IMPERFECT *yukawwiru* with God-as-subject and the day-night-cycle as the object. **Q 81:1 owns the only passive-perfect (eschatological-finished) form of the root corpus-wide.**

The classical exegetes interpret *kuwwirat* as:
- al-Ṭabarī: *yulaffu fa-yudhhabu nūruhā* — "wrapped-up so its light is taken away" (the dominant reading; light-extinction-by-wrapping)
- al-Zamakhsharī: rolled-into-a-ball, like a turban being wound around itself
- al-Rāzī: deposited / collapsed-into-singularity (anachronistically describable in modern cosmological-collapse terms)
- al-Bayḍāwī: *jumiʿa baʿḍuhā ilā baʿḍin fa-ramā bihā* — "gathered each part to the other and cast aside" (echoing the Q 75:9 *jumiʿa al-shams* parallel)

The metaphor is tightly cosmographic: the sun, normally OPEN/RADIANT, is **wound up like a garment**, which extinguishes its light — the eschatological analog of putting away a daily-used object. The 4-letter form (*ki-w-r-a-t*) is among the corpus's tightest cosmic-event verb-forms.

#### v. 2: *wa-idhā al-nujūmu inkadarat*

The stars *inkadarat* — form-VII passive-resultative of *kadara* "to be turbid, dimmed, sullied" — meaning they LOSE their LIGHT (al-Ṭabarī: *taghayyaru wa-tasāqaṭū*, "they change and fall down"). This continues the astronomical extinction-cascade from v. 1.

#### v. 3: *wa-idhā al-jibālu suyyirat*

The mountains *suyyirat* — form-II passive-perfect of *sāra* "to move, walk" — meaning they ARE-CAUSED-TO-MOVE / set-in-motion / dispersed. The classical doctrine reads this as the mountains being lifted up from their fixed bases and moved (cf. Q 27:88 *wa-tarā al-jibāla taḥsabuhā jāmidatan wa-hiya tamurru marra al-saḥāb* — "you see the mountains, thinking them solid, but they pass like clouds"). The form-II intensification connotes a forced, agentive motion (someone DOES the moving — implicitly, God).

#### v. 4: *wa-idhā al-ʿishāru ʿuṭṭilat*

The *ʿishār* (pregnant she-camels in their 10th month, on the verge of giving birth — the most-precious livestock-asset in pre-Islamic Arabian society) *ʿuṭṭilat* — form-II passive-perfect of *ʿaṭala* "to make idle, neglect, abandon." The classical commentaries unanimous: this is the ULTIMATE COSMIC-PRIORITY-INVERSION moment. In normal life, no Arab tribesman would ever neglect his pregnant camels. The verse signals that on the Day of Judgment, even the most-valued pre-Islamic-economic asset becomes meaningless, abandoned. al-Zamakhsharī notes that this verse alone among the cascade contains a culturally-specific economic-marker — making it the most-historically-anchored verse in the cascade.

#### v. 5: *wa-idhā al-wuḥūshu ḥushirat*

The wild beasts *ḥushirat* — form-I passive-perfect of *ḥashara* "to gather, assemble." Classical reading: the wild beasts are GATHERED for the resurrection-judgment (parallel to humans, see Q 6:38 *wa-mā min dābbatin fī al-arḍi wa-lā ṭāʾirin yaṭīru bi-janāḥayhi illā umamun amthālukum* — "and there is no creature on earth nor bird flying with its wings except they are communities like you" — implying animal accountability, even if at lower theological intensity). The biological-domain shift continues.

#### v. 6: *wa-idhā al-biḥāru sujjirat*

The seas *sujjirat* — form-II passive-perfect of *sajara* "to ignite, kindle, fill." The classical readings split:
- **Burning** (al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, al-Bayḍāwī): the seas BOIL / CATCH FIRE — eschatological reversal of the seas-as-water-source. al-Ṭabarī cites Ibn ʿAbbās: *idhā kāna yawm al-qiyāmati ūqida ʿalā miyāhi al-biḥāri al-shamsu wa-l-qamaru wa-l-nujūmu fa-tuṣbiḥu nāran tatasajjaru* — "on the Day of Judgment, the sun and moon and stars are kindled upon the waters of the seas, and they become a kindled fire."
- **Filling** (alternative minority reading): the seas are FILLED to overflowing — extending beyond their normal bounds. al-Zamakhsharī notes both readings, preferring the burning reading.

The burning reading is the dominant; *sujjirat* is the verb that classical exegesis pairs with *fa-naqada al-baḥru* ("the sea boiled over" Q 52:6, i.e., *al-baḥru al-masjūr*).

#### v. 7: *wa-idhā al-nufūsu zuwwijat*

The souls *zuwwijat* — form-II passive-perfect of *zawwaja* "to pair, couple." Classical readings split:
- **Pairing-with-bodies** (al-Ṭabarī majority): souls re-paired with their bodies for resurrection
- **Class-pairing** (Ibn ʿAbbās via al-Ṭabarī): righteous souls paired with righteous, sinful with sinful — class-segregation eschatology
- **Spousal pairing** (minority, al-Bayḍāwī): souls paired in their marital status (paradise or hell pairing)

The form-II intensification *zuwwijat* signals an active, agentive pairing — paralleling the form-II structure throughout the cascade.

#### v. 8: *wa-idhā al-mawʾūdatu suʾilat*

The buried-alive girl *suʾilat* — form-I passive-perfect of *saʾala* "to ask." This is the corpus's **HAPAX** of the *al-mawʾūdah* form (root w-ʾ-d, "to bury alive"; QAC LEM:mawo'udap, single-occurrence in entire Quran).

The pre-Islamic Arabian practice of *waʾd al-banāt* (burying baby daughters alive at birth) is well-documented in pre-Islamic poetry, sīrah literature (Ibn Hishām citing Ibn Isḥāq: the Tamīm tribe reportedly buried daughters out of fear of poverty or fear of war-time-shame; the famous case of Ṣaʿṣaʿa b. Nājiya, Ghaylān b. Salama's grandfather, who is reported to have ransomed 360 buried-alive girls from his fellow tribesmen before Islam), and confirmed in the Quranic anti-infanticide thread (Q 6:140, Q 6:151, Q 16:58-59, Q 17:31, Q 60:12).

**The grammatical question of who is being asked**: classical exegetes disagree —
- **Reading 1** (Ibn ʿAbbās via al-Ṭabarī, dominant): the buried-girl is the QUESTIONED-passive — God will resurrect her and ask her: "for what sin were you killed?" The girl-as-witness is the courtroom-evidence; the answer indicts the murderers.
- **Reading 2** (alternative; ʿUmar via al-Ṭabarī minority, also accepted by al-Rāzī): the buried-girl is the QUESTIONER — she will be asked TO ASK / accuse her killers. (Different parsing of *suʾilat* as "she was asked-to-speak" vs "she was asked-about-her-killing.")

Both readings agree the girl is the central evidentiary actor of the resurrection-courtroom. **This is the corpus's strongest gender-justice eschatology** — the murdered-because-female buried-girl is brought to RESURRECTION-COURT to indict her killers.

#### v. 9: *bi-ayyi dhanbin qutilat*

A continuation/extension of v. 8: "for what sin she was killed." This is grammatically and semantically a COMPLETION of v. 8's interrogation — but it is given its own verse-number in the canonical division. The 12-cascade enumeration treats vv. 8-9 as the **single 8th cascade element** with v. 9 as v. 8's parenthetical-continuation.

The verb *qutilat* — form-I passive-perfect of *qatala* "to kill" — is unambiguous. The killing-because-female premise is locked at the lexical level. The classical exegetes uniformly read this as the indictment of the pre-Islamic infanticide practice.

#### v. 10: *wa-idhā al-ṣuḥufu nushirat*

The scrolls *nushirat* — form-I passive-perfect of *nashara* "to spread out, unfold, publish." Classical reading: the deed-scrolls (each soul has a scroll of its lifetime-deeds, per Q 17:13-14, Q 18:49, Q 84:7-12) are UNROLLED for public examination. This is the eschatological courtroom's evidentiary phase — the scrolls of every soul's deeds are made readable.

#### v. 11: *wa-idhā al-samāʾu kushiṭat*

The heaven *kushiṭat* — form-I passive-perfect of *kashaṭa* "to skin, strip away, peel off." Classical reading: the sky is STRIPPED AWAY like the skin of an animal being flayed (al-Ṭabarī cites al-Suddī: *kushiṭat al-samāʾu kamā yukshaṭu al-jildu ʿan al-jazūr* — "the heaven is stripped as the skin is stripped off the camel").

This image (heaven-as-flayed-skin) is rare in the corpus; it parallels Q 21:104 (*yawma naṭwī al-samāʾa ka-ṭayyi al-sijilli li-l-kutub* — "the day We fold up the heaven like the folding of the scroll for the books"), which uses the *ṭ-y-y* root of folding. Q 81:11 uses *kashaṭa* instead — a different (and more visceral) folding-metaphor for the same eschatological event.

#### v. 12: *wa-idhā al-jaḥīmu suʿʿirat*

The hellfire *suʿʿirat* — form-II passive-perfect of *saʿara* "to kindle, set ablaze, intensify." The form-II intensification stresses the AGENTIVE causation: the hellfire is INTENSE-KINDLED-INTO-FULL-BLAZE for the eschatological-judgment moment. This is the eschatological apparatus's punishment-side becoming operational.

#### v. 13: *wa-idhā al-jannatu uzlifat*

The paradise *uzlifat* — form-IV passive-perfect of *zalafa* "to bring near, approach." The eschatological apparatus's reward-side: paradise is BROUGHT NEAR (meaning made VISIBLE to those who will inherit it; classical reading: it becomes near and obtainable for the righteous).

The pairing of vv. 12-13 (hellfire kindled / paradise brought near) is a classic Quranic punishment-reward antithesis. Both verbs are passive-perfect and feminine, completing the cascade's grammatical-rhyme uniformity.

#### v. 14: *ʿalimat nafsun mā aḥḍarat*

The apodosis. *ʿalimat nafsun mā aḥḍarat* — "every soul knows what it has put forward."

| Element | Form |
|:--|:--|
| Construction | finite-perfect verb + indefinite-noun-subject + relative-clause-object |
| Verbal pattern | form-I *ʿalimat* (knew, fem.) + form-IV *aḥḍarat* (brought-forward, fem.) |
| Root | ʿ-l-m + ḥ-ḍ-r |

The 5-word apodosis discharges the entire 13-clause protasis. The cosmological-fall-cascade RESOLVES INTO an EPISTEMIC-MOMENT: at the cosmic-collapse, every soul KNOWS what its deeds-record actually contained. The cosmic-spectacular collapses into the inner-individual-knowledge moment.

This is one of the corpus's strongest **macro-cosmos / micro-self** moments: the entire cosmos folds up to produce the single epistemic-truth-event of every individual soul knowing-its-own-deeds. The classical balāgha tradition (al-Suyūṭī, al-Bāqillānī) cites this as a paradigmatic example of *iqāʿ* (rhythm) at the macro-syntactic level.

### Block B (vv. 15-21) — COSMIC OATH + ANGELIC REVELATION

#### vv. 15-16: *fa-lā uqsimu bi-l-khunnasi / al-jawāri al-kunnasi*

| Form | Element |
|:--|:--|
| Oath formula | *fa-lā uqsimu* (form-IV imperfect) — "Nay! I do not swear" — a classical Quranic emphatic-oath construction (the *lā* is rhetorical-amplification, NOT negation) |
| First oath-object | *al-khunnas* (the retrograding [stars]) — root kh-n-s, "to retreat, withdraw" |
| Second oath-object | *al-jawāri al-kunnas* (the swimmers, the lairing [ones]) — root j-r-y "to flow" + k-n-s "to lair, hide" |

The oath-pair *al-khunnas / al-jawāri al-kunnas* is one of the corpus's most-cosmologically-mysterious oaths. Classical readings:
- **Stars/Planets** (al-Ṭabarī majority): the retrograding-then-swimming-then-lairing celestial bodies — the planets that move backward (retrograde), then forward (swim/flow), then disappear at sunrise (lair). Modern astronomy: the apparent retrograde motion of planets observed against the fixed-star background.
- **Wild Antelopes/Cattle** (alternative; al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī): the gazelles that hide in their lairs.
- **Stars of the Constellations** (al-Suyūṭī): specifically the 5 visible-to-naked-eye planets (Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn) — the *al-khunnas al-jawāri al-kunnas* triad.

The dominant classical reading is the planetary-retrograde reading. The oath's ASTRONOMICAL specificity is striking — it appears to encode pre-Ptolemaic observational astronomy at the level of naked-eye planetary motion.

#### v. 17: *wa-l-layli idhā ʿasʿasa*

The night *ʿasʿasa* — form-II reduplicated verb of *ʿasaʿa* (to walk-at-night, draw-on, depart). The classical readings split:
- **Approaching** (al-Ṭabarī majority): the night APPROACHES (begins to fall)
- **Departing** (al-Farrāʾ, al-Akhfash): the night DEPARTS (begins to recede)
- **Both** (al-Zamakhsharī, al-Rāzī): the verb can mean BOTH "approach" and "depart" — the inherent ambiguity is the rhetorical effect; the night is a thing-in-motion-toward-or-away. This dual-meaning is itself a classical balāgha device.

#### v. 18: *wa-l-ṣubḥi idhā tanaffasa*

The dawn *tanaffasa* — form-V reflexive of *naffasa* "to breathe." The dawn BREATHES — the wonderful personification of the sun's rising as a breathing-out of light. The classical commentaries unanimous: this is one of the corpus's most-poetic verbal images. The dawn (al-ṣubḥ) is a being that BREATHES (tanaffasa) — the breath being the spreading of light across the eastern horizon.

The vv. 15-18 pair the cosmic-night-oath (vv. 15-17) with the cosmic-dawn-counter-oath (v. 18). The structural symmetry of *al-layli ʿasʿasa / al-ṣubḥi tanaffasa* is one of the corpus's tightest cosmic-rhythm couplets.

#### vv. 19-21: *innahu la-qawlu rasūlin karīm / dhī quwwatin ʿinda dhī al-ʿarshi makīn / muṭāʿin thamma amīn*

The angelic-revelation declaration:

| v. | Form | Translation |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 19 | *innahu la-qawlu rasūlin karīm* | "Indeed it [the Qurʾān] is the speech of a noble Messenger" |
| 20 | *dhī quwwatin ʿinda dhī al-ʿarshi makīn* | "Possessor of strength, before the Owner of the Throne, firmly established" |
| 21 | *muṭāʿin thamma amīn* | "Obeyed, then trustworthy" |

These 3 verses describe the angel **Jibrīl** (the angel-of-revelation) — the classical reading unanimous (al-Ṭabarī, al-Zamakhsharī, al-Rāzī, Ibn Kathīr, al-Bayḍāwī, al-Qurṭubī). The 5 attributes:
1. *karīm* (noble)
2. *dhī quwwatin* (powerful)
3. *makīn ʿinda dhī al-ʿarshi* (firmly-established before the Throne-Owner = high-status before God)
4. *muṭāʿ* (obeyed, i.e., among the angels)
5. *amīn* (trustworthy, i.e., trusted to carry the message)

These attributes — strength, nearness-to-throne, leadership-among-angels, trustworthiness — are the exact attributes used to describe Jibrīl in Q 53:5-6 (*ʿallamahu shadīdu al-quwā / dhū mirratin fa-stawā*). **Q 53 + Q 81 are the corpus's TWO Jibrīl-vision passages** — both Early Meccan, separated by ~60% of revelation-order, sharing the exact attribute-set.

The revelation-source declaration LOCATES the Qurʾān ontologically: it is angelic speech, brought from God's Throne to the Prophet, by an angel (Jibrīl) who is powerful, near-to-God, leadership-positioned, and trustworthy. The declaration is the corpus's most-explicit Source-Of-Revelation-Identification passage in the Early Meccan period.

### Block C (vv. 22-29) — DIRECT ADDRESS + DOXOLOGY

#### v. 22: *wa-mā ṣāḥibukum bi-majnūn*

"Your companion [the Prophet] is not mad." Direct rebuttal of the Quraysh-accusation that Muḥammad was *majnūn* (possessed by jinn, "mad"). The pronoun *ṣāḥibukum* ("your companion") presupposes Quraysh-audience-familiarity with the Prophet (he is THEIR companion, NOT a stranger).

#### v. 23: *wa-laqad raʾāhu bi-l-ufuqi al-mubīn*

"And he saw him [Jibrīl] on the clear horizon."

This is the corpus's **second Jibrīl-vision-attestation** (the first is Q 53:7, *wa-huwa bi-l-ufuqi al-aʿlā* — "and he is on the highest horizon"). Both passages use *al-ufuq* (the horizon) as the locus of the Prophet's vision of Jibrīl. The classical commentaries treat Q 81:23 + Q 53:7 as describing the SAME vision-event (the vision of Jibrīl in his original angelic form, on the horizon, before the Prophet's first revelation in the Cave of Ḥirāʾ).

#### v. 24: *wa-mā huwa ʿalā al-ghaybi bi-ḍanīn*

"And he [the Prophet] is not stingy with the unseen [knowledge]."

Two readings (both classical):
- *bi-ḍanīn* (with ḍād): the Prophet does not WITHHOLD the revealed knowledge from the people
- *bi-ẓanīn* (with ẓāʾ): the Prophet is not SUSPICIOUS / not-distrusted regarding the unseen — i.e., his transmission is reliable

The first reading (with ḍ) is the Hafs reading; the second (with ẓ) appears in some other Qurʾānic readings. Both readings reinforce the trustworthiness of the Prophet's transmission.

#### v. 25: *wa-mā huwa bi-qawli shayṭānin rajīm*

"And it [the Qurʾān] is not the speech of a stoned-Satan."

This rejects the alternative-source theory: the Qurʾān is NOT diabolic-inspiration. Classical commentary: this rebuts the soothsayer-style (kāhin) accusation that Muḥammad was channeling a jinn-pact. The Qurʾān comes from a NOBLE messenger (Jibrīl, vv. 19-21), NOT from a stoned demon. The cosmological-source contrast is structurally tight.

#### v. 26: *fa-ayna tadhhabūn*

"So where are you [people] going?"

A direct rhetorical question to the audience: faced with all the above evidence, where else do you intend to turn? The verse marks the rhetorical CLIMAX of the address — a single 3-word interrogative that confronts the audience with the inadequacy of any alternative response.

#### v. 27: *in huwa illā dhikrun li-l-ʿālamīn*

"It is nothing but a Reminder for all the worlds."

The Qurʾān is identified as *dhikr* (reminder) li-l-ʿālamīn (for all worlds = universal). The universalist scope — *li-l-ʿālamīn* (for all worlds, not just for the Arabs) — is one of the corpus's most-explicit universalist declarations in the Early Meccan period.

#### v. 28: *li-man shāʾa minkum an yastaqīm*

"For whoever among you wishes to be upright."

The universal Reminder is conditioned on individual volition: it is for those who WISH to be upright. The verse emphasizes human-volitional response.

#### v. 29: *wa-mā tashāʾūna illā an yashāʾa Allāhu rabbu al-ʿālamīn*

"And you do not will except as Allah, the Lord of the Worlds, wills."

The closing verse is the classical theological compatibilism formulation: human will operates within divine-will. The closing *rabbu al-ʿālamīn* (Lord of the Worlds) parallels the *li-l-ʿālamīn* of v. 27, creating an **inclusio bracket** around the closing 2 verses (vv. 27-29 frame "the worlds / the Lord of the Worlds" as the surah's universal-scope closure).

## 4. Rhyme architecture and rhetorical analysis

### 4.1 Rhyme map (no-tashkeel final-letter)

| Block | vv. | Final-letter | Rhyme pattern | Verb-class |
|:--|:-:|:-:|:--|:--|
| A | 1-14 | **ت** (14×) | 100% ت — fem-passive-perfect-ending | passive-perfect-feminine |
| B (sub-1) | 15-18 | **س** (4×) | 100% س — sīn ending | nominal/verbal mixed |
| B (sub-2) + C | 19-29 | **ن** (8×) + **م** (3×) | mixed *-īn / -ūn / -ūm* | active-mood / nominal |

Q 81's rhyme architecture is **3-segment-monorhyme-block**: each block uses a single rhyme letter throughout. The transitions are CRISP — every rhyme shift is a hard transition (no graduated transitions), which is characteristic of Early Meccan rhetorical-style.

### 4.2 Verb-tense / mood pattern across the surah

| Block | vv. | Dominant verb-mood |
|:--|:-:|:--|
| A protasis (1-13) | 13 | passive-perfect feminine (12× kuwwirat / inkadarat / ... / uzlifat) |
| A apodosis (14) | 1 | active-perfect feminine (ʿalimat / aḥḍarat) |
| B oath (15-18) | 4 | active-imperfect (uqsimu) + cognate verbal (ʿasʿasa, tanaffasa) |
| B revelation (19-21) | 3 | nominal predication (innahu la-qawlu... — copular) |
| C address (22-29) | 8 | mixed: nominal predication + interrogative + imperative-mood-suggestive |

The verb-mood progression — **passive-perfect (cosmic events done-to-them) → active-perfect (souls knowing) → imperfect-oath (speaker swearing) → copular (revelation-source identification) → interrogative + nominal (audience confrontation)** — traces a complete grammatical-arc from the cosmic-passive-receivers to the human-active-deciders, mediated by the speaker (the Prophet via the angel) at the structural centre.

This is a **macro-rhetorical-grammatical-architecture** — Q 81's tense / voice / mood pattern is itself a structural device, not just decoration.

### 4.3 Cognate-accusative density

Q 81 uses several cognate accusatives:
- v. 1: *al-shamsu kuwwirat* (form-II maṣdar takwīr — surah-name root)
- v. 2: *al-nujūmu inkadarat*
- v. 3: *al-jibālu suyyirat*
- v. 4: *al-ʿishāru ʿuṭṭilat*

Plus the 4-oath cluster (vv. 15-18) using cognate-verbal forms:
- v. 17: *al-layli ʿasʿasa* (cognate verbal-noun *ʿasʿasa*)
- v. 18: *al-ṣubḥi tanaffasa* (form-V reflexive cognate)

The density of cognate verbal/maṣdar forms is high — supporting al-Bāqillānī's TOP-8/114 iʿjāz al-fawāṣil ranking.

## 5. Theological-rhetorical synthesis

Q 81 al-Takwīr accomplishes — in 29 verses, 104 words, 435 letters — a complete eschatological-revelational arc:
1. **The cosmos folds up** (vv. 1-13): 12 catastrophic events compress all of cosmology into a single judgment-moment.
2. **Each soul knows** (v. 14): the cosmic-collapse RESOLVES into individual epistemic-truth.
3. **The cosmic-witnesses swear** (vv. 15-18): the planets and the night and the dawn testify.
4. **The angel-source is identified** (vv. 19-21): Jibrīl carries the message.
5. **The Prophet confronts the audience** (vv. 22-29): rebutting madness, confirming the vision-of-Jibrīl, rejecting demon-source, asking "where are you going?", declaring universal-Reminder, conditioning on individual will, closing on divine-will-precedence.

The macroscopic theological-architecture is **cosmic eschatology grounding individual judgment grounding revelational source-claim grounding direct prophetic challenge**. This 4-layer compression in 29 verses is one of the corpus's most-architecturally-tight rhetorical structures, and is consistent with Q 81's TOP-8/114 iʿjāz al-fawāṣil ranking and its CORE-CENTRAL position in the H-NEW-1200 architectural cluster.

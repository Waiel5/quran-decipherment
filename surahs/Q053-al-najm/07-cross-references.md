### 1.4. Q 53 ↔ Q 55 al-Raḥmān (refrain-formula bilateral cross-reference)

**Strength**: MODERATE
**Axis**: Lexical-formula bilateral cross-reference
**Empirical**:
- Q 53:55 *fa-bi-ayyi ālāʾi rabbika tatamārā* ↔ Q 55 al-Raḥmān 31x *fa-bi-ayyi ālāʾi rabbikumā tukadhdhibān*
- Same root-construction (*fa-bi-ayyi ālāʾi rabbi-* + verb of doubt/denial)
- Q 53 has SINGULAR addressee (*rabbika*); Q 55 has DUAL addressee (*rabbikumā* — to jinn + mankind)
- Q 53 verb is *tatamārā* (you doubt); Q 55 verb is *tukadhdhibān* (you both deny)
**Classical anchor**: Ibn Kathīr + al-Suyūṭī read Q 53:55 as a "preview" of the Q 55 refrain. al-Biqāʿī reads Q 53 → Q 55 as a tight munāsaba pair (with Q 54 as the cosmological-interlude between).
**Significance**: One of the corpus's most-precise cross-surah formulaic-allusions. The Q 53:55 formula is the ONLY corpus occurrence of the *fa-bi-ayyi ālāʾi rabbika...* pattern OUTSIDE Q 55 al-Raḥmān.

# Q 72 al-Jinn — Content Analysis

## 1. Macro-structure: two clean blocks

Q 72 partitions into TWO blocks with a sharp boundary at v.19/v.20:

| Block | Verses | Length | Speaker | Register |
|:--|:--|:-:|:--|:--|
| **A — Jinn-confession** | 1-19 | 19 / 28 verses (67.9%) | the jinn (reported speech, marked by *wa-anna* / *wa-annahu* / *wa-annā* opening particles) | reported-creedal-monotheistic |
| **B — Prophet-cycle** | 20-28 | 9 / 28 verses (32.1%) | the prophet (first-person) | prophet's bounded role + eschatology of the unseen |

The boundary is reinforced morphologically: v.19 closes with the third-person observation *kādū yakūnūna ʿalayhi libadan* (jinn-perspective on the prophet's standing); v.20 OPENS the prophet-mode with a fresh *qul* — *qul innamā adʿū rabbī wa-lā ushriku bihi aḥadan*.

Note: the 9-verse prophet-cycle block also contains FOUR fresh *qul* incipits (v.20, 21, 22, 25), making the block a *qul*-saturated zone. This is the Q 72-internal *qul*-cluster: a micro-cluster of imperative-second-person-divine-direct-speech embedded within the surah after the long reported-speech opening.

## 2. Verse-by-verse content map

### Block A — Jinn-confession (vv. 1-19)

| v. | First words (no-tashkeel) | Content register |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 1 | قل أوحي إلي أنه استمع نفر من الجن... | introductory frame — prophet reports that *a nafar of jinn listened* to a "marvellous qurʾān" |
| 2 | يهدي إلى الرشد فآمنا به | jinn-speech: the qurʾān guides to *rushd*; "we believed and will not associate" |
| 3 | وأنه تعالى جد ربنا | "and the majesty of our Lord is exalted — He has taken no consort or son" |
| 4 | وأنه كان يقول سفيهنا | "and our foolish ones spoke falsehood about God" |
| 5 | وأنا ظننا أن لن تقول الإنس والجن | "and we thought neither humans nor jinn would speak falsely about God" |
| 6 | وأنه كان رجال من الإنس يعوذون | "men of humans used to seek protection from men of jinn, adding rahaqan" (oppression) |
| 7 | وأنهم ظنوا كما ظننتم | "and they thought, as you have thought, that God would resurrect none" |
| 8 | وأنا لمسنا السماء | "and we touched the heaven and found it filled with strong guards and shooting-stars" |
| 9 | وأنا كنا نقعد منها مقاعد للسمع | "we used to take seats there for listening; now whoever listens finds a shooting-star lying in wait" |
| 10 | وأنا لا ندري أشر أريد بمن في الأرض | "we do not know whether evil is intended for those on earth, or guidance" |
| 11 | وأنا منا الصالحون ومنا دون ذلك | "and among us are the righteous and others — we are factions [ṭarāʾiq qidadan]" |
| 12 | وأنا ظننا أن لن نعجز الله | "and we knew we cannot escape God in the earth nor by flight" |
| 13 | وأنا لما سمعنا الهدى آمنا به | "and when we heard the guidance we believed in it" |
| 14 | وأنا منا المسلمون ومنا القاسطون | "and among us are *muslimūn* [submitters] and *qāsiṭūn* [deviators]" |
| 15 | وأما القاسطون فكانوا لجهنم حطبا | "as for the *qāsiṭūn*, they are firewood for Gehenna" |
| 16 | وأن لو استقاموا على الطريقة | "and if they walk the straight path, We would water them with abundant water" |
| 17 | لنفتنهم فيه | "to test them in it; whoever turns from his Lord's remembrance, He guides him to a harsh torment" |
| 18 | وأن المساجد لله | "and the masājid belong to God — so do not call alongside God any other" |
| 19 | وأنه لما قام عبد الله يدعوه | "and when the servant of God stood to call upon Him, they nearly piled upon him in throngs [libadan]" |

The *wa-anna* opening of vv.3-19 is the **structural backbone** of the jinn-confession: 17 of 19 verses open with a particle of the form *wa-anna* / *wa-annā* / *wa-annahu* / *wa-an*. This is the surah's most distinctive syntactic-rhetorical feature: the jinn-confession is structured as 17 enumerated propositions ("and that...; and that...; and that...") in classical *taʿdād al-shawāhid* style.

### Block B — Prophet-cycle / eschatology of the unseen (vv. 20-28)

| v. | First words (no-tashkeel) | Content register |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 20 | قل إنما أدعو ربي | prophet's *qul*: "I call only upon my Lord and do not associate" |
| 21 | قل إني لا أملك لكم ضرا ولا رشدا | prophet's *qul*: "I do not control harm or guidance for you" |
| 22 | قل إني لن يجيرني من الله أحد | prophet's *qul*: "no one can protect me from God; I have no refuge but Him" |
| 23 | إلا بلاغا من الله ورسالاته | "(only) the delivery from God and His messages" — eschatological warning |
| 24 | حتى إذا رأوا ما يوعدون | "until they see what they were promised — they will know who is weaker and fewer" |
| 25 | قل إن أدري أقريب ما توعدون | prophet's *qul*: "I do not know if the promised event is near or if my Lord has set a term" |
| 26 | عالم الغيب فلا يظهر على غيبه أحدا | "Knower of the unseen; He does not disclose His unseen to anyone" |
| 27 | إلا من ارتضى من رسول | "except a Messenger He has approved — then He sends watchers before and behind him" |
| 28 | ليعلم أن قد أبلغوا رسالات ربهم | "so He knows that they have delivered their Lord's messages; He encompasses all and has counted all things" |

The block-B *qul* repetition (v.20, 21, 22, 25 — **four fresh *qul* incipits in 6 verses**, a Q 72-internal corpus-rare *qul*-saturation) marks the addressee-shift cleanly. v.26-28 close the surah with the famous theological declaration about the unseen: God reveals it only to His chosen messengers, with angelic surveillance fore and aft (*raṣadan*).

## 3. Diagnostic lexicon

| Term | Verses | Significance |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **al-jinn** | 1, 5, 6 | the named referents — 3 surface attestations of the LEM:jin~ lemma |
| **nafar** | 1 | "small group" (technically 3-10 persons per classical lexicography, al-Biqāʿī §Q72 op.); Quranic-rare; in jinn-pericope context, also appears at Q 46:29 |
| **qurʾān ʿajab** | 1, 2 | "marvellous qurʾān" — the jinn's evaluation; *ʿajab* is a Quran-formal modifier for divine speech-acts |
| **rushd / rashad** | 2, 10, 14, 21 | "right guidance" — appears 4× in Q 72, an intra-surah refrain anchoring the choice-between-paths motif |
| **qāsiṭ / muslim** | 14, 15 | "deviators / submitters" — paired typology of the jinn (vv.11, 14 introduce the bifurcation: *minnā al-ṣāliḥūn* / *minnā al-muslimūn* / *minnā al-qāsiṭūn*) |
| **ʿabd Allāh** | 19 | "the servant of God" — referring (per all classical mufassirūn) to the Prophet Muḥammad in his standing-to-pray |
| **al-masājid** | 18 | "the places of prostration" — a corpus-distinctive plural-form in this context, used to forbid syncretistic worship at the same locations |
| **ʿālim al-ghayb** | 26 | "Knower of the unseen" — divine epithet; the closing theological assertion |
| **raṣad / raṣadan** | 9, 27 | "watcher" — appears 2× in Q 72; v.9 = the heavenly watchers preventing jinn from eavesdropping; v.27 = the angelic watchers protecting prophetic disclosure |

The **raṣadan** echo (v.9 ↔ v.27) is a Q 72-internal **inclusio**: the same word that describes the heavenly guards against the jinn's eavesdropping at the START (v.9) describes the angelic guards around prophetic revelation at the END (v.27). al-Biqāʿī notes this thematic ring: the surah moves from "the jinn cannot eavesdrop" to "no one can eavesdrop on God's unseen except a Messenger He has approved" — the same protective architecture deployed twice (al-Biqāʿī §Q72 closing).

## 4. Vocabulary distinctness

Hapax / rare vocabulary in Q 72:

| Term | Attestations corpus-wide |
|:--|:--:|
| *libad-an* (v.19) | rare — appears only in Q 72:19 in this morphological form (with *kādū yakūnūna ʿalayhi libadan*) |
| *ghadaq-an* (v.16) | corpus-singleton ("abundant water") — only at Q 72:16 |
| *qidad-an* (v.11) | corpus-singleton ("varied factions") — only at Q 72:11 in this morphological form |
| *raṣad-an* (v.9, 27) | the form *raṣad-an* attested ×2 in Q 72; other forms of root *rṣd* appear ×3 elsewhere |
| *šaṭaṭ-an* (v.4) | corpus-singleton ("excess/falsehood about God") — Q 72:4 only |

These five rare or singleton tokens cluster in the jinn-confession block and contribute to the surah's distinctive lexicon. The hapax-density of Block A is empirically elevated.

## 5. Repetition patterns

- **The *wa-anna* enumeration** (vv. 3-19): 17 verses opening with *wa-anna* / *wa-annā* / *wa-annahu* / *wa-an* — the most concentrated *wa-anna* enumeration in the Quran. Classical exegesis identifies this as the "reported-jinn-creed enumeration" — the 17 propositions form a structured creedal-list.
- **The *qul* incipit at vv. 20, 21, 22, 25**: 4 *qul* incipits within 6 verses, marking the prophet-mode block.
- **The *rushd* refrain**: appears in vv. 2, 10, 14, 21 — a 4-fold thematic anchor.
- **The *aḥadan* / *waladan* terminal rhyme**: as established in `01-empirical-profile.md` §3, every verse ends in alif; the most common terminal word is *aḥadan* (vv. 2, 3, 7, 18, 20, 22, 26) — **7 occurrences of *aḥadan* as verse-final** in the surah. This is the corpus-rare *aḥadan*-saturation pattern, mirroring Q 112's *aḥad* refrain (Q 112:1, 112:4).

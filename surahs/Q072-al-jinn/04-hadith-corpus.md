# Q 72 al-Jinn — Hadith Corpus

This file catalogues ḥadīth citations and asbāb al-nuzūl for Q 72. All ḥadīth numbers below are VERIFIED from on-disk JSON corpora (`data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/`) — no ḥadīth numbers are stated from memory.

## 1. Bukhārī

Source: `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/bukhari.json`

### 1.1 Bukhārī ḥadīth #755 (Kitāb al-Aḏān, chapter 10)

Bukhārī's primary narration on the Q 72 event — narrated by Ibn ʿAbbās via Saʿīd b. Jubayr via Abū Bishr via Abū ʿAwāna via Musaddad:

> "The Prophet (ﷺ) set out with the intention of going to Suq ʿUkāẓ (market of ʿUkāẓ) along with some of his companions. At the same time, a barrier was put between the devils and the news of heaven. Fire commenced to be thrown at them. The Devils went to their people, who asked them, 'What is wrong with you?' They said, 'A barrier has been placed between us and the news of heaven. And fire has been thrown at us.' They said, 'The thing which has put a barrier between you and the news of heaven must be something which has happened recently. Go eastward and westward and see what has put a barrier between you and the news of heaven.' Those who went towards Tuhama came across the Prophet at..."
> (Bukhārī #755, English translation per ahmedbaset corpus; Arabic isnad and matn verified.)

This is the Suq ʿUkāẓ event tied to Q 72:1, 72:8-9.

### 1.2 Bukhārī ḥadīth #4713 (Kitāb al-Tafsīr — book 65, Tafsīr Sūrat al-Jinn)

A parallel narration through the same isnād (Musaddad → Abū ʿAwāna → Abū Bishr → Saʿīd b. Jubayr → Ibn ʿAbbās), also recorded by Mūsā b. Ismāʿīl. This is Bukhārī's TAFSIR-CHAPTER ḥadīth on Q 72, citing the same Suq ʿUkāẓ event but with the explicit framing as the asbāb al-nuzūl of Q 72:1:

> "Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) went out along with a group of his companions towards `Ukaz Market. At that time something intervened between the devils and the news of the Heaven, and flames were sent down upon them, so the devils returned. Their fellow-devils said, 'What is wrong with you?' They said, 'Something has intervened between us and the news of the Heaven, and fires (flames) have been shot at us.' Their fellow-devils said, 'Nothing has intervened between you and the news of the Heaven, but an important event has happened. Therefore, travel all over the world, east and west, and try to find out what has happened.' And so they set out and travelled all over the world, east and west, looking for that which had intervened between them and the news of the Heaven..."
> (Bukhārī #4713)

### Note on the prior brief's hadith-number reference

The dispatch brief cited "Bukhārī Aḏān 773" as a Q 72 recitation hadith. Verification on-disk yields the actual Q 72 hadiths at **Bukhārī #755 (Aḏān chapter 10)** and **Bukhārī #4713 (Tafsīr chapter 65)**. The brief's #773 reference does not match any Q 72-related hadith in the on-disk Bukhārī corpus. The correct numbers are recorded here.

## 2. Muslim

Source: `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/muslim.json`

### 2.1 Muslim ḥadīth idInBook #908 (Kitāb al-Ṣalāh, chapter 4)

Narrated by Ibn ʿAbbās:

> "The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) neither recited the Qurʾan to the Jinn nor did he see them. The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) went out with some of his Companions with the intention of going to the bazaar of ʿUkāẓ. And there had been (at that time) obstructions between satans and the news from the Heaven, and there were flung flames upon them. So satan went back to their people and they said: What has happened to you? They said: There have been created obstructions between us and the news from the Heaven. And there have been flung upon us flames. They said: It cannot happen but for some (important) event. So traverse the eastern parts of the earth and the western parts and find out why is it that there have been created obstructions between us and the news from the Heaven. So they went forth and traversed the easts of the earth and its wests. Some of them proceeded towards Tihama and that is a nakhl towards the bazaar of ʿUkāẓ and he (the Holy Prophet) was leading his Companions in the morning prayer..."
> (Muslim #idInBook=908 [JSON id=8185])

Muslim explicitly opens with **"The Messenger of Allah neither recited the Qurʾan to the jinn nor did he see them"** — Ibn ʿAbbās's position: the jinn-listening event was an OVERHEARD recitation during the dawn-prayer at the marketplace, not a direct delivery.

### 2.2 Muslim ḥadīth idInBook #909

Dāwūd reported from ʿĀmir, who asked ʿAlqama whether Ibn Masʿūd was present with the Prophet on the *laylat al-jinn* (the Night of the Jinn). This narration represents the ALTERNATIVE tradition that the Prophet did go to a separate meeting with the jinn at Nakhla, with Ibn Masʿūd accompanying. The two Muslim narrations preserve the classical exegetical division on whether the Prophet saw the jinn (Ibn ʿAbbās: NO; Ibn Masʿūd: YES, at the Nakhla event).

## 3. al-Tirmidhī

Source: `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/tirmidhi.json`

### 3.1 Tirmidhī idInBook #3342 (Kitāb Tafsīr al-Qurʾān, chapter 47)

ʿAlqama narrating from Ibn Masʿūd — the Ibn Masʿūd version of the jinn-event. ʿAlqama asked: "Did any of you accompany the Prophet on the Night of the Jinn?" Ibn Masʿūd replied: "None of us accompanied him. One night, while he was in Makka, we could not find him..." (the standard Ibn Masʿūd narration of the prophet's disappearance and subsequent revelation that he had been "called by a messenger of the jinn").

### 3.2 Tirmidhī idInBook #3375 (Kitāb Tafsīr al-Qurʾān)

Jābir narrates: "The Messenger of Allah came out to his Companions, and recited Sūrat al-Raḥmān from its beginning to its end for them, and they were silent. So he said: 'I recited it to the Jinn on the Night of the Jinn, and they had a better response than you...'"

This is a CROSS-SURAH Tirmidhī ḥadīth — the Prophet recited **Sūrat al-Raḥmān (Q 55) to the jinn**, not Sūrat al-Jinn itself. This is an important calibration: the *laylat al-jinn* event involved recitation of Q 55 (per Jābir's narration); Q 72 al-Jinn's revelation REPORTS the event in the prophet's voice but is not necessarily the surah recited DURING the event.

### 3.3 Tirmidhī idInBook #3407

A parallel Ibn ʿAbbās narration to Bukhārī #755 / Muslim #908 — same Suq ʿUkāẓ event with similar matn structure: "the Messenger of Allah did not recite for the jinn nor did he see them. The Messenger of Allah went out with a group of his Companions towards the ʿUkāẓ market. Something had been intervening between the *shayāṭīn* and the news from the heavens..."

## 4. Aḥmad b. Ḥanbal — *Musnad*

Source: `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/ahmed.json`

### 4.1 Aḥmad idInBook #1356 (chapter 7)

A narration from ʿIkrima (companion of Ibn ʿAbbās) reciting Q 46:29 *wa-idh ṣarafnā ilayka nafaran mina al-jinn yastamiʿūna al-qurʾān* and clarifying the jinn-count. Sufyān al-Thawrī comments (per the matn): "They were one [group]..."

This Aḥmad narration is the **explicit cross-pericope linkage** between Q 72 and Q 46:29 — ʿIkrima reciting Q 46:29 while discussing the Q 72 event. The ḥadīth supports the classical reading that the two pericopes refer to the SAME event (the "same-event" reading; cf. Q072-F-03 PASS).

## 5. al-Nasāʾī

Source: `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/nasai.json`

### 5.1 Nasāʾī idInBook #5503 (chapter 50)

ʿĀʾisha narrates: "The Messenger of Allah used to seek refuge from the evil eye of the Jinn and the evil eye of humans. When **al-Muʿawwidhatān** were revealed, he started to recite them and stopped reciting anything else."

This is a **muʿawwidhāt-cluster** ḥadīth — relevant to Q 72 only indirectly (the muʿawwidhāt are Q 113-114, which are 3 of the 4 short-creedal *qul*-tail surahs). It establishes the prophet's seek-refuge-from-jinn practice, which thematically links to Q 72:6 (humans seeking refuge from jinn — the practice the surah polemically rejects).

## 8. Asbāb al-nuzūl synthesis

Across the 9-book corpus, the asbāb al-nuzūl tradition for Q 72:1 converges on the **Suq ʿUkāẓ / Nakhla event**:

- The Prophet was leading dawn-prayer at the marketplace of ʿUkāẓ (or, per some narrations, en route through Nakhla on the way back from al-Ṭāʾif).
- A *nafar* of jinn (7-9 in number) heard the recitation.
- They went back to their kin and reported: "We have heard a marvellous Qurʾān" (Q 72:1-2).
- Q 72:1-19 is the revealed report of WHAT THE JINN SAID; Q 72:20-28 is the Prophet's commanded-response.

The CONTESTED point is whether the Prophet saw the jinn directly:
- Ibn ʿAbbās (Bukhārī 755, 4713; Muslim 908; Tirmidhī 3407): NO, the Prophet did not see them; the event was an overheard recitation during dawn-prayer, and the report came via revelation.
- Ibn Masʿūd (Tirmidhī 3342; Muslim 909): YES, the Prophet was called away by a "messenger of the jinn" on the *laylat al-jinn* and met them at Nakhla, with Ibn Masʿūd accompanying him part of the way.

Classical exegesis preserves BOTH narrations and harmonizes them via the **two-event** reading (al-Rāzī, al-Suyūṭī *Durr al-manthūr*): the Suq ʿUkāẓ event (overheard, no direct sight) and the Nakhla *laylat al-jinn* event (direct, witnessed by Ibn Masʿūd) are TWO separate occurrences, and Q 72 may refer to either or both. al-Biqāʿī cites Abū Ḥayyān: «المشهور أنه هو» — the famous view is that the Q 46:29 event is the SAME event as Q 72:1; the alternative view is that they are different.

## 9. Faḍāʾil (virtues of recitation)

The on-disk 9-book corpora yield **no Q 72-specific faḍāʾil ḥadīth** (no narrations on the special merit of reciting Sūrat al-Jinn, comparable to Q 36 Yāsīn or Q 67 al-Mulk).

What IS attested:
- **Q 55 al-Raḥmān recited TO the jinn** during the *laylat al-jinn* (Tirmidhī 3375 from Jābir) — supporting the inference that Q 55 (not Q 72) was the surah RECITED at the jinn-event.
- The Prophet's practice of reciting the muʿawwidhatān (Q 113, 114) as protection from jinn and human evil eye (Nasāʾī 5503; Ibn Mājah 3247) — thematically connects to Q 72:6.

The absence of dedicated Q 72 faḍāʾil is itself a notable empirical observation: Q 72 is liturgically **report-status** (the surah REPORTS an event involving jinn) rather than **performance-status** (a surah RECITED to deal with jinn). The latter role is occupied by Q 55 and the muʿawwidhāt.

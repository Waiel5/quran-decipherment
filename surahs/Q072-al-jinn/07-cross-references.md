## 3. Cross-surah verse-twin and pericope-pair couplings

### 3.1 Q 72 ↔ Q 46 al-Aḥqāf

**The major cross-surah pairing**. Q 72:1-19 (jinn-confession) and Q 46:29-32 (al-Aḥqāf jinn-pericope) are the only two Quranic narratives of the jinn-listening event.

Per **Q072-F-03 PASS** (p=0.0068, z=+2.81):
- Jaccard(Q 72:1-19, Q 46:29-32) = 0.0851 (vs length-matched null mean 0.0469)
- Shared diagnostic tokens: *al-jinn*, *samiʿnā*, *yahdī*
- Empirically supports the classical Abū Ḥayyān reading (the two pericopes refer to the SAME event from different framings)

This is the **first corpus-anchored test of this specific classical claim** and contributes a PASS-direction empirical signal to cross-finding-015 (classical-scholarship-validation-pattern).

### 3.2 Q 72 ↔ Q 55 al-Raḥmān

The al-jaAn~ (alternative jinn-being lemma) axis: **Q 55 is corpus-rank-1 by combined-lens (jin~ + jaA^n~) jinn-being density**; Q 72 is corpus-rank-2. The Tirmidhī 3375 narration (Jābir) explicitly identifies Q 55 as the surah RECITED to the jinn on the *laylat al-jinn*. The Q 55 ↔ Q 72 pair is a corpus-empirically-grounded RECITED-TO-the-jinn ↔ NAMED-FOR-the-jinn dyad.

This is a Q072 specialist contribution to the broader jinn-thematic network in the corpus.

### 3.3 Q 72 ↔ Q 112 al-Ikhlāṣ

Q 72's FR-rank-1 nearest neighbour is Q 112 (d=0.6945). Both surahs share:

This empirically validates al-Biqāʿī's reading that Q 72's *maqṣūd* is monotheistic-creedal even though its narrative-frame is the jinn-event.

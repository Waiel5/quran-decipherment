## 2. Major thematic blocks

| Block | Verses | Theme |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **A. Oath-opener and resurrection-denial** | 1-5 | ق + oath by *al-Qurʾān al-majīd*; Quraysh's astonishment + skepticism. |
| **B. Cosmic argument from sky/earth/water** | 6-11 | Sky-as-roof, earth-mountains, blessed-rain → resurrection-evidence. |
| **C. Catalogue of destroyed peoples** | 12-15 | Nūḥ, al-Rass, Thamūd, ʿĀd, Pharaoh, Lūṭ, al-Ayka, Tubbaʿ. |
| **D. The theatre of death and judgment (CENTRAL BLOCK)** | 16-30 | *ḥabl al-warīd*; the two recording angels; sakrat al-mawt; trumpet; driver-and-witness; cover removed; Hell-question dialogue. |
| **E. Paradise scene** | 31-35 | *uzlifat al-jannatu li-l-muttaqīn ghayra baʿīd*; entry in peace; "Day of Eternity." |
| **F. Closing: warning + Prophet-instruction** | 36-45 | Mightier nations destroyed; *qalb / samʿ / shahīd* triad; six-day creation; *fa-ṣbir* command; trumpet-call; *fa-dhakkir bi-l-Qurʾān*. |

The structural midpoint is the *ḥabl al-warīd* verse (v. 16) — Allah's *qurb* (closeness) to the human nafs is the geometrical and theological centre. The death-resurrection theatre (block D) occupies the central 1/3 of the surah (vv. 16-30, 15 of 45 verses, exactly 33%).

## 3. Content register — eschatological-creedal

Q 50 is *predominantly eschatological* with a *tafkīr-and-creedal* register:
- vv. 1-5: creedal (resurrection-denial as point-of-contention).
- vv. 6-15: cosmological-creedal (cosmic-argument; destroyed-peoples catalogue).
- vv. 16-30: eschatological theatre (death, resurrection, judgment, Hell).
- vv. 31-35: eschatological reward (Paradise).
- vv. 36-45: prophetic-rhetorical (admonition + Prophet-instruction).

There are NO legal verses, NO narrative-prophetic episodes (only the v.12-14 catalogue, which functions as enumeration not as story), and NO familial-or-social-conduct content. This is a tightly-focused eschatological surah — fitting its **Friday-minbar recitation** status (Sahih Muslim #1907): the Prophet recited it as the Friday *khuṭba* text because its content is *al-mawʿiẓa al-waʿīdiyya* (admonition + threat) of the kind that suits public sermon.

## 5. Repetition patterns

Two notable refrain-like patterns:

| Phrase | Occurrences | Verses |
|:--|:--|:--|
| `وعيد` (waʿīd, "Threat") | 4 | vv. 14, 20, 28, 45 |
| `ذلك يوم …` ("That is the Day of …") | 3 | v. 20 (al-waʿīd), v. 34 (al-khulūd), v. 42 (al-khurūj) |
| `بعيد` (baʿīd, "far") | 3 | vv. 3, 27, 31 |
| Verbs of resurrection / coming-forth (al-khurūj) | 2 | v. 11 *kadhālika al-khurūj*, v. 42 *yawm al-khurūj* |

The waʿīd-refrain (vv. 14, 20, 28, 45) is structurally important: it opens (v. 14, after destroyed-peoples catalogue), peaks at the trumpet (v. 20), reappears at the Hell-dispute (v. 28), and closes the surah (v. 45 *fa-dhakkir bi-l-Qurʾāni man yakhāfu waʿīd* — "Remind by the Qurʾān whoever fears the Threat"). The closing verse echoes the opener: oath BY the Qurʾān (v. 1) → command to REMIND BY the Qurʾān (v. 45). This is a head-tail bookend on *al-Qurʾān al-majīd*.

## 6. Cross-surah content references

- **Q 50:38** (six-day creation, no weariness) is doctrinally paralleled at Q 7:54, Q 10:3, Q 11:7, Q 25:59, Q 32:4, Q 41:9-12, Q 57:4. (Q 50:38 is the verse Ibn ʿAbbās and Qatāda alone classified as Medinan; the surah's Meccan classification is otherwise unanimous per al-Qurṭubī's opening on Q 50.)
- **Q 50:30** (the Hell-question dialogue *hal imtalaʾti / hal min mazīd*) is doctrinally paralleled at Q 7:18, Q 11:119, Q 32:13 (Hell will be filled with mankind and jinn). Tafsir traditions classify this as the *istifhām al-taqrīr* (rhetorical-confirmation question).
- **Q 50:1** *qāf wa-l-Qurʾān al-majīd* is paralleled by **Q 38:1** *ṣād wa-l-Qurʾān dhī al-dhikr* and **Q 68:1** *nūn wa-l-qalam wa-mā yasṭurūn*. These three are the only muqaṭṭaʿāt openers using the muqaṭṭaʿ-letter + oath-wāw construction (Q050-F-01: matching_surahs = [38, 50, 68]). The Qurʾān-as-oath-object is shared with Q 38; the *qalam* (Pen) at Q 68 is the writing-instrument, the writing of which is the Qurʾān — a thematic-trio.
- **Q 50:45** (closing: *fa-dhakkir bi-l-Qurʾāni man yakhāfu waʿīd*) is the prototype for the cluster of late-Meccan "remind-by-the-Qurʾān" closures — Q 51:55, Q 87:9, Q 88:21 (*fa-dhakkir innamā anta mudhakkir*), forming a *fa-dhakkir* family across the late-Meccan corpus.

- The 5-block thematic structure is an INTERPRETATION; classical scholars (al-Biqāʿī *Naẓm al-Durar* on Q 50) propose ring-structure analyses where the central block is vv. 16-30 with peripheral blocks B and E (cosmic-argument and Paradise-scene) framing it. The 5-block reading is consistent with al-Biqāʿī but not identical.
- The body-part vocabulary list is locked PRIOR to the test (Q050-F-02 prereg) but IS curated; sensitivity analysis is in `csv/Q050-F-02.json`.
- The "33% of surah is the death-and-resurrection theatre" figure is a direct verse-count (15 of 45 = 33.3%); no statistical inference is implied beyond descriptive.
- Q 50:38 (six-day-creation) Meccan-vs-Medinan classification is a known classical disagreement (Ibn ʿAbbās + Qatāda classifying it as Medinan; al-Ḥasan, ʿAṭāʾ, ʿIkrima, Jābir classifying the entire surah as Meccan). al-Qurṭubī catalogues both views.

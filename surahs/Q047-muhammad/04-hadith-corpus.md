## 1. al-Bukhārī, *al-Ṣaḥīḥ* — VERIFIED Q 47 references

### 1a. Tafsīr-of-Q 47:22 cluster (Bukhārī #4623-4625)

These three consecutive Bukhārī hadiths are the canonical Tafsīr Surah Muhammad bāb, attached to Q 47:22 (*fa-hal ʿasaytum in tawallaytum an tufsidū fī al-arḍ wa-tuqaṭṭiʿū arḥāmakum*).

- **Bukhārī #4623** (chapter 65 = kitāb al-Tafsīr): *ḥaddathanā Khālid b. Mukhlad...* Abū Hurayra: the Prophet said, "Allah created His creation. When He had finished it, the womb (*al-raḥim*) stood and held to the *ḥaqw* of al-Raḥmān. He said: 'What is your case?' She said: 'This is the place of one seeking refuge with You from severing [kinship-ties]'..."
- **Bukhārī #4624** (same chapter 65): variant chain with Abū Hurayra; the Prophet adds: *"iqraʾū in shiʾtum {fa-hal ʿasaytum...}"* — "Recite if you wish: 'Would you then...'" (explicit Q 47:22 citation).
- **Bukhārī #4625** (same chapter 65): another variant chain — *"wa-qraʾū in shiʾtum {fa-hal ʿasaytum} idhā wullītum"* — "Recite if you wish: 'Would you then if you were given the authority...'"

**This is the load-bearing Bukhārī ↔ Q 47 link**, and it is **VERIFIED on disk** (full Arabic + English text inspected). It is what the brief should have cited.

### 1b. al-Bukhārī — broader Q 47 thematic citations

The phrase *fa-iḏā laqītum* (Q 47:4 cognate, also occurring in Q 8:45) appears in:
- **Bukhārī #2844** (chapter 56 = kitāb al-Jihād): a letter from ʿAbdullāh b. Abī Awfā about the conduct in battle.
- **Bukhārī #2902, #2903** (kitāb al-Jihād): Abū Hurayra: the Prophet said, "Do not desire to meet the enemy, but if you meet them, be patient."

**Caveat**: these are not unambiguously Q 47:4-citation hadiths — the phrase *fa-iḏā laqītumūhum* occurs in both Q 8:45 and Q 47:4. The hadiths' battlefield-conduct topic is closer to Q 8:45 ("when you meet [the enemy] hold firm") and only thematically aligned with Q 47:4. Including them as "Q 47-related" is contextual, not citational.

The phrase *al-riqāb* (Q 47:4 + Q 2:177, Q 5:89, Q 9:60 — multi-attested) appears in many Bukhārī hadiths concerning *zakāt al-riqāb* (manumission); these are **NOT** Q 47-specific (the *al-riqāb* of Q 47:4 means battlefield-strike, not slave-manumission). The frequency-match in Bukhārī #2418, #2625, #2652 is to the slave-context, not the war-context.

## 2. Muslim, *al-Ṣaḥīḥ*

### 2a. Q 47:22 citation

- **Muslim #6358** (chapter 45 = kitāb al-Birr wa-al-Ṣila): *ḥaddathanā Qutayba b. Saʿīd...* Abū Hurayra: the Prophet says, "Allah created the universe. When He had finished it, ties of relationship came forward and said, 'This is the place for him who seeks refuge from severing'..." The hadith closes with: *"Are you not satisfied that I should keep relationship with one who joins your ties..."* — and concludes with *iqraʾū in shiʾtum {fa-hal ʿasaytum...}* (Q 47:22 explicit citation).

This is the Muslim parallel to Bukhārī #4623-4625. **VERIFIED on disk**.

### 2b. Q 47:15 (4-river parable) — partial citation

- **Muslim #7188** (chapter 54 = kitāb al-Fitan): Abū Hurayra: the Prophet said, "Shall I not inform you about the Dajjāl... He would bring along with him an *image* of Paradise and Hell-Fire; what he calls Paradise will be Hell-Fire..." The hadith uses *mithla al-jannati wa-al-nār* — paralleling Q 47:15's *mathalu al-jannati allatī wuʿida al-muttaqūna*.

The thematic match is structural (parable-of-paradise), not verbatim. Cite as related, not direct.

## 4. Abū Dāwūd, al-Nasāʾī, Ibn Mājah, Mālik

Searches across these collections produce matches mostly on the *al-riqāb* slave-manumission idiom (NOT Q 47-specific) and a small number of *kitāb al-Jihād* hadiths quoting the *fa-iḏā laqītum* phrasing. None of these are unambiguously Q 47-citations vs Q 8-citations.

Honest summary: **the Q 47-specific hadith corpus is THIN** outside the Bukhārī/Muslim Q 47:22 cluster. al-Qurṭubī's preferred name *sūrat al-Qitāl* is supported by classical tafsīr but NOT by a large hadith fadāʾil cluster — there is no equivalent of Q 36 (Yāsīn) fadāʾil, no equivalent of Q 1 (Fātiḥa) liturgical-recitation tradition. Q 47 is **legal-tafsīr-heavy**, not *fadāʾil*-heavy.

## 6. asbāb al-nuzūl (occasions-of-revelation) — Q 47 chronology

Classical asbāb al-nuzūl on Q 47 (per al-Suyūṭī *al-Itqān* nawʿ 9 and al-Wāḥidī *Asbāb al-Nuzūl*):

- Q 47:2: revealed in Medina, addressing the believers who held to Muḥammad's revelation.
- Q 47:4: revealed in the context of late-Medinan war-planning; al-Wāqidī (*Maghāzī*) suggests proximity to Hudaybiyya.
- Q 47:13: revealed on the hijra-emigration occasion, contrasting Mecca's expulsion with the divine reward of Medina-residents.
- Q 47:22: revealed in the context of Quraysh-Medinan kinship tensions.
- Q 47:31: the brief's hadith claim sits here, but unverified on disk (see §0 correction).

These asbāb are catalogued in al-Suyūṭī's *al-Itqān*, English translation available on disk at `data/literature/classical-tafsir/suyuti-al-itqan-fi-ulum-al-quran-english.pdf`.

## 7. Bukhārī #4567 — what is actually there

For completeness: Bukhārī #4567 (idInBook in the on-disk JSON) is in chapter 65 (kitāb al-Tafsīr) and reads:
> *ḥaddathanā Muḥammad b. Muqātil, akhbaranā Yaʿlā, ḥaddathanā Sufyān al-ʿUṣfurī, ʿan ʿIkrima, ʿan Ibn ʿAbbās: {la-rāddaka ilā maʿādin} qāla ilā Makka* — "{will bring you back to a place of return} means: to Mecca."

This is a tafsīr of **Q 28:85** (Sūrat al-Qaṣaṣ), not Q 47. The brief's hadith-number was incorrect; this dossier supersedes that part of the brief with the verified citations in §1a above.

## 8. Honest limits

There are non-canonical *zawāʾid* (Ibn Ḥajar al-Haythamī *Majmaʿ al-Zawāʾid*, al-Bayhaqī *al-Sunan al-Kubrā*) and *muṣannafāt* (ʿAbd al-Razzāq, Ibn Abī Shayba) NOT on disk. 3. The Q 47 fadāʾil-recitation negative-finding (§5) is a search-confirmed absence across the 9-book corpus, but absence in 9 books does not absolutely rule out a tradition; it does rule out *canonical* fadāʾil tradition.

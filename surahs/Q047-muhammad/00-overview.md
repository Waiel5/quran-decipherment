## 2. Position in the corpus

Q 47 occupies a load-bearing position in the back-Medinan war-and-community cluster:

- **Immediate neighbors**: Q 46 (al-Aḥqāf, Meccan ḥawāmīm tail) → Q 47 (Medinan) → Q 48 (al-Fatḥ, Medinan Hudaybiyya). Q 46→Q 47 is a Meccan→Medinan PERIOD boundary (H-NEW-130 boundary list).
- **Mushaf-cluster classical anchor**: al-Bukhārī's *Ṣaḥīḥ* (kitāb al-tafsīr) treats Q 47:22 (#4623-4626) and Q 48 Hudaybiyya (#4627-4633) as a single editorial cluster.
- **al-Biqāʿī Q 46→47→48 munāsabah** (*Naẓm al-Durar*): war-permission → war-instruction → conquest-promise — a 3-surah thematic sequence.
- **Q008-F-03 qitāl-cluster**: Q 47 is one of {Q 8, 9, 47, 48, 61} — Medinan surahs concentrated in *qitāl*-instruction and prophet-companion idiom.

## 3. Opening, closing, hinge verse

- **Q 47:1** (opening): الذين كفروا وصدوا عن سبيل الله أضل أعمالهم — "Those who disbelieved and obstructed from the path of Allah, He has made their deeds astray." A two-camp framing without preface.
- **Q 47:2** (Muhammad-naming): والذين آمنوا وعملوا الصالحات وآمنوا بما نزل على محمد وهو الحق من ربهم ۙ كفر عنهم سيئاتهم وأصلح بالهم — the surah's namesake clause.
- **Q 47:4** (war-conduct injunction): فإذا لقيتم الذين كفروا فضرب الرقاب حتى إذا أثخنتموهم فشدوا الوثاق فإما منا بعد وإما فداء حتى تضع الحرب أوزارها — *fa-ḍrabū al-riqāb* ("strike the necks") is the surah's load-bearing legal-tactical verse.
- **Q 47:20** (qitāl meta-verse): ويقول الذين آمنوا لولا نزلت سورة ۖ فإذا أنزلت سورة محكمة وذكر فيها القتال — the only Qurʾānic verse where *al-qitāl* is explicitly framed as *content of a* "muḥkama" *surah*. This is also the verse that classically names the surah's genre.
- **Q 47:24** (al-tadabbur anchor): أفلا يتدبرون القرآن أم على قلوب أقفالها — an ʿulūm-al-Qurʾān anchor verse cited by al-Rāzī for the *tadabbur* discipline.
- **Q 47:38** (closing): ها أنتم هؤلاء تدعون لتنفقوا في سبيل الله فمنكم من يبخل... — closing on the *infāq* (spending) exhortation with the *Allāh al-ghanīy* clause.

## 6. Naming history (classical)

The name *sūrat Muḥammad* derives from Q 47:2's *bi-mā nuzzila ʿalā Muḥammad*. al-Suyūṭī (*al-Itqān*, nawʿ 17) catalogues the surah under "asmāʾ al-suwar" with the alternate name *sūrat al-Qitāl* (after Q 47:20's *wa-dhukira fīhā al-qitāl*). al-Qurṭubī (*al-Jāmiʿ*, on Q 47) prefers *al-Qitāl* as the more characteristic title.

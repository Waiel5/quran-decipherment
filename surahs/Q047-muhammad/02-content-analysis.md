# Q 47 — Content Analysis

Verse-by-verse content summary derived from `quran-text/quran-no-tashkeel.json` (Q 47 verses 1-38). Themes verified against the comprehensive overview's prior content scan and cross-checked against tafsir (al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, Ibn Kathīr — see `03-tafsir-survey.md`).

## 1. Verse-by-verse summary

### Block A — Two-camp framing (vv. 1-3)

- **v. 1**: الذين كفروا وصدوا عن سبيل الله أضل أعمالهم — "Those who disbelieved and obstructed [people] from the path of Allah — He has made their deeds astray." The surah opens without preface; the *aḍalla* root (ḍ-l-l) immediately frames the two-camp structure.
- **v. 2** (*Muhammad-naming verse*): the parallel positive frame — "those who believed and worked righteous deeds and believed in what was sent down upon Muḥammad — which is the truth from their Lord — He removed their misdeeds and reformed their condition." This is one of 4 verse-internal Muḥammad-naming attestations (Q047-F-04 VINDICATED).
- **v. 3**: meta-reason — the disbelievers followed falsehood, the believers followed truth from their Lord; *kadhālika yaḍribu llāhu li-l-nāsi amthālahum* ("thus Allah strikes parables for the people").

### Block B — War-conduct injunction (vv. 4-6)

- **v. 4** (the load-bearing legal verse): فإذا لقيتم الذين كفروا فضرب الرقاب حتى إذا أثخنتموهم فشدوا الوثاق فإما منا بعد وإما فداء حتى تضع الحرب أوزارها — "When you meet those who disbelieve, [give] strike of the necks until you have subdued them; then bind them firmly; then either favor without payment or ransom — until war lays down its burdens." This verse contains *al-qitāl*-cognate language including *qutilū* (cf. Q047-F-05: this is 1 of Q 47's 2 *qtl*-root attestations).
- **vv. 5-6**: divine protection for the steadfast — "He will guide them and rectify their condition, and admit them to the Paradise He has identified to them."

### Block C — Comparison-rhetoric: garden vs scalding water (vv. 7-15)

- **vv. 7-9**: conditional promise — "If you support Allah, He will support you and plant your feet firmly... those who disbelieved — for them is the falling [of misery] and He has made their deeds astray." Mirror-couplet of v. 1.
- **v. 10**: travel-and-look — *afa-lam yasīrū fī al-arḍ* — the classical perambulation-imperative for the lessons of past peoples.
- **vv. 11-14**: protection/abandonment binary; cf. *anna llāha mawlā lladhīna āmanū wa-anna al-kāfirīna lā mawlā lahum*.
- **v. 15** (the most-famous parable of Q 47): مَثَل الجنة التي وعد المتقون فيها أنهار من ماء غير آسن وأنهار من لبن لم يتغير طعمه وأنهار من خمر لذة للشاربين وأنهار من عسل مصفى — "The likeness of the Garden which the God-fearing are promised: rivers of water unchanging, rivers of milk unaltered in taste, rivers of wine delightful to drinkers, rivers of purified honey..." 4-river parable; cited in liturgical and mystical traditions.

### Block D — Hypocrites in Medina (vv. 16-19)

- **v. 16**: those who listen but turn away — *mādhā qāla ānifan* (= "what did he just say?") — a hypocrite-marker idiom.
- **v. 17**: increase-of-guidance for the truly-receptive.
- **v. 18**: signs-of-the-Hour — *fa-hal yanẓurūna illā al-sāʿata an taʾtiyahum baghtatan* — eschatological warning.
- **v. 19** (*tawḥīd* anchor): *fa-ʿlam annahu lā ilāha illā Allāh* — "Know then that there is no god but Allah."

### Block E — qitāl meta-verse (vv. 20-23)

- **v. 20** (the surah's self-naming verse): ويقول الذين آمنوا لولا نزلت سورة ۖ فإذا أنزلت سورة محكمة وذكر فيها القتال — "And those who believe say: 'If only a sura had been sent down!' But when a muḥkama sura is sent down with mention in it of [al-qitāl]..." This verse is THE classical anchor for the surah's alternate name *sūrat al-Qitāl*. Q 47's 2nd *qtl*-root attestation (Q047-F-05).
- **v. 21**: obedience-and-fair-word better than [hypocrite excuse].
- **v. 22** (the *afa-ʿasaytum* verse cited in Bukhārī #4623-4625): فهل عسيتم إن توليتم أن تفسدوا في الأرض وتقطعوا أرحامكم — "Would you then, if you were given power, work corruption in the land and sever your kinship-ties?"
- **v. 23**: those whom Allah has cursed and made deaf and blind.

### Block F — Tadabbur, mark of hypocrites, divine knowledge (vv. 24-32)

- **v. 24** (ʿulūm-al-Qurʾān anchor): أفلا يتدبرون القرآن أم على قلوب أقفالها — "Do they not contemplate the Qurʾān, or are there locks upon hearts?" Cited by al-Rāzī as the *tadabbur* discipline foundation.
- **vv. 25-27**: hypocrites' fate after apostasy.
- **vv. 28-29**: divine displeasure with the hypocrites.
- **v. 30**: physical markers of hypocrites — *fa-la-ʿaraftahum bi-sīmāhum*.
- **v. 31** (*nablu* verse, sometimes cited classically): ولنبلونكم حتى نعلم المجاهدين منكم والصابرين ونبلو أخباركم — "We shall surely test you until We make evident the strivers among you and the patient, and try your reports." *N-b-l* root for *test/try*. This is the verse the brief's hadith claim referenced as Bukhārī #4567 — see `04-hadith-corpus.md` for the HONEST CORRECTION (the brief's specific hadith-number is unverified on disk).
- **v. 32**: those who disbelieved and obstructed and broke with the Messenger — their deeds frustrated.

### Block G — Closing exhortation (vv. 33-38)

- **v. 33**: *aṭīʿū llāha wa-aṭīʿū al-rasūl* — obey Allah and obey the Messenger.
- **v. 34**: punishment for those who blocked and died disbelieving.
- **v. 35** (*do-not-weaken*): فلا تهنوا وتدعوا إلى السلم وأنتم الأعلون — "Do not weaken and call for peace when you are the higher."
- **vv. 36-37**: world-life is play, hereafter is the reward.
- **v. 38** (closing): ها أنتم هؤلاء تدعون لتنفقوا في سبيل الله فمنكم من يبخل ۖ ومن يبخل فإنما يبخل عن نفسه ۚ والله الغني وأنتم الفقراء — "Here you are, called upon to spend in the way of Allah, but among you are the niggardly... and Allah is the Self-sufficient; you are the needy." The *infāq* exhortation framed against divine self-sufficiency.

## 3. Content register

- **Mode**: legal-juridical instruction + theological-warning + exhortation. NOT narrative (no Joseph/Moses-style ḳiṣṣa); NOT creedal-condensed (no Q 112-style tawḥīd compression).
- **Audience**: 2nd-person plural addressing the Medinan community of believers (Q 47:4, 7, 33, 38 — direct *fa-iḏā laqītum*, *in tanṣurū llāha*, *aṭīʿū*, *hā-antum hāʾulāʾi*).
- **Tone shift**: alternates between threat (vv. 1, 3, 13, 27-28, 32, 34) and reassurance (vv. 2, 5-6, 15, 35).

## 4. Vocabulary distinctness

- **Distinctive cluster** (war/qitāl/hypocrite): *qtl* (2×: vv. 4, 20), *al-riqāb* (1×: v. 4), *al-wathāq* (1×: v. 4), *fidāʾ* (1×: v. 4), *al-ḥarb* (1×: v. 4), *al-qitāl* (1×: v. 20), *al-marḍá-fī-qulūbihim* (1×: v. 20), *al-munāfiqīn* (implicit theme, vv. 16-32).
- **Distinctive cluster** (paradise-parable): *anhārun min* (4 occurrences in v. 15: water, milk, wine, honey).
- **Repetition patterns**: *aḍalla aʿmālahum* (vv. 1, 8; *aḥbaṭa aʿmālahum* v. 9; cognate at 28, 32) — the *thwarting-of-deeds* refrain is Q 47-specific corpus-distinctive at 4-5 attestations.

# Q 103 al-ʿAṣr — Content Analysis

All Arabic text from `quran-text/quran-no-tashkeel.json` (id 103). Morphology from QAC v0.4
(`data/morphology/quranic-corpus-morphology-0.4.txt`, surah-103 lines). The surah is three verses and
maps exactly onto the classical Arabic oath-form: **qasam → jawāb al-qasam → istithnāʾ** (oath →
answer-of-the-oath → exception). It is a single, self-contained argument compressed to its minimum.

## 1. Verse-by-verse

### v 1 — والعصر — *wa-l-ʿaṣr* — "By time / the age / the declining day"

- **QAC:** `wa` (P, oath-wāw) + `{lo` (DET) + `EaSori` (N, LEM EaSor, ROOT ESr, GEN). One word, six letters.
- **Function:** the **qasam** — a single wāw-oath. The wāw here is the *wāw al-qasam* (oath-particle),
  not the conjunction; the genitive *al-ʿaṣr-i* is the *muqsam bihi* (sworn object). H-NEW-2210 tags the
  sworn object's semantic class as **temporal**.
- **The four classical readings of *ʿaṣr*** (see `03-tafsir-survey.md`): (a) *al-dahr* — time/the age in
  general (Ibn ʿAbbās, the preferred reading of al-Ṭabarī); (b) *al-ʿashī* — the late afternoon, declining
  day (al-Ḥasan, Qatāda); (c) *ṣalāt al-ʿaṣr* — the mid/afternoon prayer (Muqātil, linking to *al-ṣalāt
  al-wusṭā*, Q 2:238); (d) "the age of the Prophet." The oath on time is read as a *tanbīh* — an alert to
  the vicissitudes of time as a proof of the Maker (al-Qurṭubī: "لما فيه من التنبيه بتصرف الأحوال").

### v 2 — إن الإنسان لفي خسر — *inna al-insāna la-fī khusr* — "Indeed humankind is in loss"

- **QAC:** `<in~a` (ACC, the emphatic particle) + `{lo` + `<insa`na` (N, LEM <insa`n, ROOT Ans, ACC) +
  `la` (EMPH lām) + `fiY` (P) + `xusorK` (N, LEM xusor, ROOT xsr, INDEF, GEN). Four words, fifteen letters.
- **Function:** the **jawāb al-qasam** — the answer the oath introduces. The double emphasis (*inna* +
  the *lām al-tawkīd* on *la-fī*) and the indefinite *khusr-in* ("a loss") make the claim maximally strong
  and maximally general: **all of humankind, in loss.** *al-insān* is read generically (al-jins, "the
  species"), confirmed by the plural exception in v 3 — al-Ṭabarī: "واستثنى الذين آمنوا من الإنسان، لأن
  الإنسان بمعنى الجمع، لا بمعنى الواحد" ("the exception of the believers from *al-insān* proves *al-insān*
  is meant collectively, not as a single individual").
- ***khusr*** — "loss" in the commercial sense: the squandering of one's capital. The classical glosses:
  *ghabn* (a bad bargain, al-Qurṭubī), *halaka* (ruin, al-Akhfash), *ʿuqūba* (penalty, al-Farrāʾ), *naqṣ*
  (deficiency). The metaphor is mercantile — life/time as a capital that humankind is steadily losing.

### v 3 — إلا الذين آمنوا وعملوا الصالحات وتواصوا بالحق وتواصوا بالصبر

*illā alladhīna āmanū wa-ʿamilū al-ṣāliḥāti wa-tawāṣaw bi-l-ḥaqqi wa-tawāṣaw bi-l-ṣabr*
— "except those who believe, do righteous deeds, enjoin one another to truth, and enjoin one another to patience"

- **QAC:** `<il~aA` (EXP, the exception-particle istithnāʾ) + `{l~a*iyna` (REL) + `'aAmanu` (V, PERF (IV),
  ROOT Amn) + `Eamilu` (V, PERF, ROOT Eml) + `{l S~a`liHa`ti` (N, ACT PCPL, ROOT SlH) + `tawaASa` (V, PERF
  (VI), ROOT wSy) `bi {lo Haq~i` (N, ROOT Hqq) + `tawaASa` (V, PERF (VI), ROOT wSy) `bi {l S~abori` (N, ROOT
  Sbr). Nine words, fifty-two letters — the verse carries 9 of the surah's 14 words.
- **Function:** the **istithnāʾ** — the single exception that rescues a remnant from the universal loss.
  Its structure is a **fourfold escalating list**:
  1. *āmanū* — **belief** (interior assent; QAC form IV, "had faith").
  2. *wa-ʿamilū al-ṣāliḥāt* — **righteous deeds** (exterior action). Together *āmanū wa-ʿamilū al-ṣāliḥāt*
     is the most frequent creed-action pairing in the Qurʾān — it sets the personal axis.
  3. *wa-tawāṣaw bi-l-ḥaqq* — **mutual enjoining to truth** (the social/communal axis begins). The form-VI
     *tawāṣaw* is reciprocal: "they counselled one another." *al-ḥaqq* glossed as the Book of God (al-Ḥasan,
     Qatāda: "الحق: كتاب الله") or faith/tawḥīd (Muqātil).
  4. *wa-tawāṣaw bi-l-ṣabr* — **mutual enjoining to patience** (perseverance in obedience and against
     adversity; al-Baghawī: "على أداء الفرائض وإقامة أمر الله").
- The progression personal → personal → social → social (believe → act → counsel-others-to-truth →
  counsel-others-to-endure) is a complete ethical programme in four terms. The doubled *tawāṣaw bi-… wa-
  tawāṣaw bi-…* (w-ṣ-y twice in one verse) is a tight reciprocal-imperative refrain — the only place in
  the surah a root repeats (candidate Q103-F-02).

## 2. Thematic block structure

| Block | Verse(s) | Register |
|---|---|---|
| Oath (qasam) | 1 | invocational — swearing by time |
| Universal verdict (jawāb) | 2 | sapiential/eschatological — humankind's default state is loss |
| The saving remnant (istithnāʾ) | 3 | ethical/communal — the fourfold condition for salvation |

The whole surah is a single sentence: *By time — humankind is in loss — except those who…* The oath
sets a witness (time itself), the jawāb states the verdict, the istithnāʾ names the acquitted. There is
no narrative, no named figure, no asbāb-tied episode — it is pure wisdom-aphorism (ḥikma) in oath-form.

## 3. Content register

**Sapiential-eschatological wisdom (ḥikma), oath-framed.** Q 103 is the densest "wisdom-aphorism" in the
mufaṣṣal-qiṣār: it makes a totalising claim about the human condition (universal loss) and supplies the
exact remedy (faith + works + mutual truth + mutual patience). al-Shāfiʿī's verdict that "if people
pondered this surah it would suffice them" (Ibn Kathīr) is precisely a reading of v 3 as a complete
soteriological programme.

## 4. Vocabulary distinctness

- **Only 9 distinct roots** for the whole surah — but they are thematically central, high-frequency
  Qurʾānic roots (ʾ-m-n faith, ʿ-m-l action, ṣ-l-ḥ righteousness, ṣ-b-r patience, ḥ-q-q truth), plus the
  two distinctive ones: **x-s-r (khusr, "loss")** and **w-ṣ-y (tawāṣaw, "mutual-enjoining")**.
- The surah is FR-dense (mean 0.787) precisely because its 9 roots are the *common core* of short-Meccan
  preaching vocabulary — it shares that core with Q 94-114 (its top-15 FR neighbours).
- The **ṣād concentration** is lexically grounded: three of the nine roots carry ṣād (ʿ-Ṣ-r, Ṣ-l-ḥ,
  Ṣ-b-r), which is what makes Q 103 the corpus's #2 emphatic-density surah (H-NEW-2340; see
  `01-empirical-profile.md` §7).

## 5. Repetition patterns

- **Doubled *tawāṣaw bi-…*** — the only intra-surah lexical refrain: *wa-tawāṣaw bi-l-ḥaqqi wa-tawāṣaw
  bi-l-ṣabr*. The repetition is rhetorically deliberate: it splits the communal duty into two parallel
  reciprocal imperatives (truth + patience), and the parallelism is what gives the 3-verse surah its
  top-decile local cohesion (H-NEW-750 local_cohesion rank 10/114).
- **Perfect rā'-monorhyme:** al-ʿaṢ**R** / khus**R** / al-ṣab**R** — all three verse-endings rhyme on
  rā' with a preceding ṣād/sīn cluster, giving a tight sonic seal (rhyme_entropy 0.0).

## 6. Cross-surah content references

- **No explicit citation of another surah.** Q 103 is self-contained.
- **Thematic twin (mercantile-loss):** the *khusr* / capital-loss image recurs across the Qurʾān (e.g.
  the "trade that will never perish" of Q 35:29; the *khasira al-dunyā wa-l-ākhira* of Q 22:11). al-Rāzī
  explicitly pairs Q 103's **loss-oath** with Q 93 al-Ḍuḥā's **profit-oath** (*wa-l-ḍuḥā* — reassurance,
  ascent) as the two halves of a mercantile diptych (`03-tafsir-survey.md`).
- **Formal twin (minimal-surah rā'-rhyme):** Q 108 al-Kawthar — the other 3-verse perfect rā'-monorhyme,
  and Q 103's rank-1 FR neighbour (Q103-F-01 Arm A; `06-novel-findings.md`).
- **The *āmanū wa-ʿamilū al-ṣāliḥāt* formula** links v 3 to the most frequent creed-action collocation in
  the corpus — Q 103 is its most compressed deployment (the formula carrying an entire surah's salvific load).

## 7. Honest limits

- The four readings of *ʿaṣr* (time / afternoon / prayer / age-of-the-Prophet) are not empirically
  decidable from the text alone; the H-NEW-2210 "temporal" tag captures only that the sworn object is a
  time-word, not which gloss is intended.
- "Universal loss" is a theological reading of *al-insān*; the empirical observation is only that the
  grammatical exception (v 3 plural) confirms the generic sense of *al-insān* (al-Ṭabarī's argument).
- The personal→social escalation of v 3's fourfold list is a rhetorical reading; the empirical correlate
  is the doubled reciprocal w-ṣ-y form-VI (queued as Q103-F-02 for a corpus-frequency test).

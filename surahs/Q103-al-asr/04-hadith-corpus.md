## 1. Verified on-disk recitation ḥadīth — al-ʿAṣr in the witr (Aḥmad, *Musnad* #639)

The single on-disk ḥadīth that names Sūrat al-ʿAṣr (by its incipit *wa-l-ʿaṣr*) and is about the surah's
**recitation**:

| Collection | idInBook | Narrator (isnād on disk) | Content |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **Aḥmad b. Ḥanbal, *Musnad*** | **639** | ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib ← al-Ḥārith ← Abū Isḥāq ← Isrāʾīl (Aswad b. ʿĀmir / Muḥammad b. ʿAbdillāh b. al-Zubayr) | the Prophet ﷺ used to pray **witr with nine surahs of al-mufaṣṣal** |

**Text (Aḥmad #639, verified on disk):** *"kāna rasūl Allāh ﷺ yūtir bi-tisʿ suwar min al-mufaṣṣal … yaqraʾu fī
al-rakʿa al-ūlā {alhākum al-takāthur} wa {innā anzalnāhu fī laylat al-qadr} wa {idhā zulzilat al-arḍ}, wa fī
al-rakʿa al-thāniya {wa-l-ʿaṣr} wa {idhā jāʾa naṣr Allāh wa-l-fatḥ} wa {innā aʿṭaynāka al-kawthar} …"*

**Why this matters for the project.** The ḥadīth groups al-ʿAṣr in the *second rakʿa* with **al-Naṣr (Q 110)
and al-Kawthar (Q 108)** — i.e. with the **other two 3-verse surahs** — and the *first rakʿa* opens with
**al-Takāthur (Q 102)**, Q 103's mushaf-prior neighbour. This is an independent (recitation-practice) echo of
the empirical groupings the surah profile foregrounds: the minimal-surah rā'-twin {Q 103, Q 108} (Q103-F-01
Arm A) and the cheap Q 102 → Q 103 seam (`01-empirical-profile.md` §5). The isnād (al-Ḥārith al-Aʿwar ← ʿAlī)
is classically considered weak (*ḍaʿīf*); reported here as an on-disk attestation of recitation-practice, not
as a strong-grade faḍāʾil claim.

## 2. The famed two-Companions tradition — al-Ṭabarānī (UNVERIFIABLE ON DISK)

The tradition al-Shāfiʿī's verdict is usually paired with — *"whenever two of the Companions met, they would
not part until one had recited Sūrat al-ʿAṣr to the other and given the greeting of peace"* — is cited by **Ibn
Kathīr** (*Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm*, on Q 103; `en-tafisr-ibn-kathir/103/1.json`) as recorded by
**al-Ṭabarānī**, via **ʿAbdullāh b. Ḥiṣn Abī Madīna**.

**STATUS: UNVERIFIABLE ON DISK — flagged, not asserted.** al-Ṭabarānī's *al-Muʿjam* (al-Kabīr / al-Awsaṭ /
al-Ṣaghīr) is **NOT** among the 9-book collection on disk (`the_9_books/` = al-Bukhārī, Muslim, Abū Dāwūd,
al-Tirmidhī, al-Nasāʾī, Ibn Mājah, Mālik *Muwaṭṭaʾ*, Aḥmad *Musnad*, al-Dārimī). A tashkeel-normalized scan of
all nine books for the distinctive Arabic (*lā yatafarraqā ḥattā yaqraʾa aḥaduhumā … wa-l-ʿaṣr*) returned **0
hits**. The tradition is therefore documented as a **tafsīr-transmitted ḥadīth from a collection not on disk**;
its grading cannot be checked against the on-disk 9-book text. (Classically it is reported by al-Ṭabarānī and
al-Bayhaqī in *Shuʿab al-Īmān*; chain considered acceptable / *ḥasan li-ghayrihi* by some, but that judgment
cannot be made from the on-disk materials.)

## 3. The Musaylima failed-imitation anecdote (Ibn Kathīr ← al-Kharāʾiṭī) — UNVERIFIABLE ON DISK

Ibn Kathīr (same passage) reports ʿAmr b. al-ʿĀṣ reciting al-ʿAṣr to Musaylima al-Kadhdhāb, who answers with
his hyrax-parody, and ʿAmr's *"you know that I know you are lying."* Ibn Kathīr sources part of the chain to
**Abū Bakr al-Kharāʾiṭī, *Masāwiʾ al-Akhlāq*, vol. 2** — **NOT** in the 9-book DB. **STATUS: UNVERIFIABLE ON
DISK.** It is an *iʿjāz*-anecdote (taḥaddī illustration), not a ḥadīth-collection narration; documented as such.

## 4. The Ubayy b. Kaʿb prophetic-tafsīr of v 3 — tafsīr-transmitted, graded *mawqūf*

al-Qurṭubī (*al-Jāmiʿ*, on Q 103:3; `ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/103/3.json`) preserves Ubayy b. Kaʿb's report that
the Prophet glossed *al-insān* = Abū Jahl, *āmanū* = Abū Bakr, *ʿamilū al-ṣāliḥāt* = ʿUmar, *tawāṣaw bi-l-ḥaqq*
= ʿUthmān, *tawāṣaw bi-l-ṣabr* = ʿAlī. al-Qurṭubī himself notes Ibn ʿAbbās preached it from the pulpit
**mawqūfan ʿalayhi** (halted at Ibn ʿAbbās, not raised to the Prophet). **STATUS:** a tafsīr-transmitted
identification, graded *mawqūf* by al-Qurṭubī; not located as an independent ḥadīth in the on-disk 9 books.

## 5. Faḍāʾil (virtues of recitation) — DATA-GAP, not absence-of-evidence

A tashkeel-normalized scan of all nine on-disk books found **no surah-named faḍāʾil ḥadīth** for Sūrat al-ʿAṣr
(no *"man qaraʾa sūrat al-ʿaṣr…"* construction). All 144 occurrences of the string *al-ʿaṣr* in the 9-book DB
refer to **ṣalāt al-ʿaṣr** (the afternoon prayer), not the surah. **Flagged as DATA-GAP, not as an
absence-of-evidence claim**: dedicated faḍāʾil sub-collections (e.g. al-Thaʿlabī's faḍāʾil chains, Ibn al-Ḍurays
*Faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān*) lie outside the on-disk 9-book set, and the most-cited al-ʿAṣr "virtue" material is in fact
the al-Ṭabarānī two-Companions report (§2) and al-Shāfiʿī's *aqwāl* (a juristic dictum, not a ḥadīth).

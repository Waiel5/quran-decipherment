# Q 54 al-Qamar — Content Analysis

Arabic verse text from `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/quran-text/quran-no-tashkeel.json` (Q 54, 55 verses).
Descriptive content analysis; structural/numerical claims are cross-referenced to `01-empirical-profile.md`
and `06-novel-findings.md`. The surah is built on **two interlocking refrains** that seal a 5-pericope
destruction-catalog — the architectural spine analysed below.

## 1. The two refrains (the surah's structural spine)

| Refrain | Form | Verses | Function |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **A — ʿadhābī wa-nudhur** | *fa-kayfa kāna ʿadhābī wa-nudhur* (×4: vv 16, 18, 21, 30) + *fa-dhūqū ʿadhābī wa-nudhur* (×2: vv 37, 39) = 6 instances | closes the **destruction-action** of each pericope | "How then was My punishment and My warnings!" / "So taste My punishment and My warnings!" |
| **B — yassarnā / muddakir** | *wa-laqad yassarnā al-Qurʾāna li-l-dhikr fa-hal min muddakir* (×4: vv **17, 22, 32, 40**) | closes the **whole pericope** as a remembrance-invocation | "And We have certainly made the Qurʾān easy for remembrance, so is there any who will remember?" |

Refrain B is the **H-NEW-2310 Q 54 strict refrain** (count 4, verified at runtime; positions 17/22/32/40).
Two further isolated *fa-hal min muddakir* terminals (v 15 *taraknāhā āyatan*; v 51 *ahlaknā ashyāʿakum*)
bracket the refrain-block, giving **6 corpus-monopoly *fa-hal min muddakir* terminals total** (vv 15, 17, 22,
32, 40, 51) — all 6 corpus instances are in Q 54 (Q054-F-01 Arm H1a, share 1.0). The interlock — refrain A
(action-seal) immediately followed by refrain B (remembrance-seal) — recurs across the Nūḥ, ʿĀd, Thamūd, and
Lūṭ pericopes; the āl-Firʿawn pericope drops both refrains and pivots into the closing block.

## 2. Verse-by-verse summary

**Block A — Cosmic event + judgment-day prelude (vv 1-8)**

- **v 1** *iqtarabati al-sāʿatu wa-anshaqqa al-qamar* — "The Hour has drawn near and the moon has split."
  A **declarative non-oath cosmic-event opener** (00-overview §3). The two perfect-tense verbs are read by
  the balāgha tradition as prophetic-perfects.
- **v 2** if they see a sign they turn away, saying *siḥrun mustamir* ("passing/continuous magic").
- **v 3** they rejected and followed their desires; *wa-kullu amrin mustaqir* ("every matter is settled").
- **v 4** *wa-laqad jāʾahum mina al-anbāʾi mā fīhi muzdajar* — "there has come to them of the news what is a
  deterrent." (First of 11 *wa-laqad*-opener verses — Q054-F-05, corpus rank-1 by density.)
- **v 5** far-reaching wisdom (*ḥikmatun bāligha*) — but the warnings (*al-nudhur*) avail not.
- **v 6** turn away from them; the Day the caller summons to *shayʾin nukur* (a horror).
- **v 7** eyes humbled, they emerge from the graves like *jarādun muntashir* (scattered locusts).
- **v 8** hurrying to the caller; the disbelievers say *hādhā yawmun ʿasir* ("this is a hard day").

**Block B — Nūḥ pericope (vv 9-17)** *(9 verses — the longest pericope)*

- **v 9** the people of Nūḥ rejected (*kadhdhabat*), called Our servant a madman, and he was driven off.
- **v 10** he prayed: *annī maghlūbun fa-ntaṣir* ("I am overcome, so help [me]").
- **v 11** We opened the gates of heaven with pouring water (*māʾin munhamir*).
- **v 12** We burst the earth with springs; the waters met for a matter already decreed (*qad qudir*).
- **v 13** We carried him on [the ark] of planks and nails (*dhāti alwāḥin wa-dusur*).
- **v 14** sailing under Our eyes — a reward for him who had been denied (*kufir*).
- **v 15** *wa-laqad taraknāhā āyatan fa-hal min muddakir* — isolated muddakir-terminal opening the seal.
- **v 16** *fa-kayfa kāna ʿadhābī wa-nudhur* — **refrain A**.
- **v 17** *wa-laqad yassarnā al-Qurʾāna li-l-dhikr fa-hal min muddakir* — **refrain B**.

**Block C — ʿĀd pericope (vv 18-22)** *(5 verses — the shortest before the pivot)*

- **v 18** ʿĀd rejected; *fa-kayfa kāna ʿadhābī wa-nudhur* — **refrain A** (here opening, not closing).
- **v 19** We sent against them a screaming wind (*rīḥan ṣarṣaran*) on a day of continuous misfortune.
- **v 20** tearing people away as if they were uprooted palm-trunks (*aʿjāzu nakhlin munqaʿir*).
- **v 21** *fa-kayfa kāna ʿadhābī wa-nudhur* — **refrain A** (repeated, closing).
- **v 22** *wa-laqad yassarnā…* — **refrain B**.

**Block D — Thamūd pericope (vv 23-32)** *(10 verses — the longest)*

- **v 23** Thamūd rejected the warnings (*bi-l-nudhur*).
- **v 24-25** they questioned following "a single human from among us," accusing him of being a brazen liar.
- **v 26** "Tomorrow they will know who is the brazen liar."
- **v 27** We are sending the she-camel as a trial (*fitnatan*) — so watch them and be patient.
- **v 28** inform them the water is shared (*qismatun baynahum*) — each share attended in turn.
- **v 29** they called their companion, who took [the sword] and hamstrung [her] (*fa-ʿaqar*).
- **v 30** *fa-kayfa kāna ʿadhābī wa-nudhur* — **refrain A**.
- **v 31** We sent one cry (*ṣayḥatan wāḥidatan*); they became like the dry stubble of a pen-builder.
- **v 32** *wa-laqad yassarnā…* — **refrain B**.

**Block E — Lūṭ pericope (vv 33-40)** *(8 verses — modified refrain)*

- **v 33** the people of Lūṭ rejected the warnings.
- **v 34** We sent a stone-storm (*ḥāṣiban*) except the family of Lūṭ, saved at dawn (*bi-saḥar*).
- **v 35** a favour from Us — thus We reward the grateful (*man shakar*).
- **v 36** he had warned them of Our assault, but they disputed the warnings.
- **v 37** they demanded his guests; We blotted out their eyes — *fa-dhūqū ʿadhābī wa-nudhur* (**refrain A
  variant**: *fa-dhūqū* "so taste," not *fa-kayfa kāna*).
- **v 38** at early morning a settled punishment (*ʿadhābun mustaqir*) overtook them.
- **v 39** *fa-dhūqū ʿadhābī wa-nudhur* — **refrain A variant** (repeated).
- **v 40** *wa-laqad yassarnā…* — **refrain B**.

**Block F — āl-Firʿawn pericope (vv 41-42)** *(2 verses — refrain-less pivot)*

- **v 41** *wa-laqad jāʾa āla firʿawna al-nudhur* — the warnings came to the people of Pharaoh.
- **v 42** they rejected all Our signs, *fa-akhadhnāhum akhdha **ʿAzīz Muqtadir*** — "We seized them with the
  seizure of a Mighty, Capable [One]." First member of the **doubled al-Muqtadir asmaic frame** (00-overview §12).
  **This pericope drops both refrains** — the asmaic name-of-power replaces the remembrance-refrain as the seal.

**Block G — Closing block (vv 43-55)**

- **v 43** are your disbelievers better than those, or have you an exemption in the scriptures (*al-zubur*)?
- **v 44** or do they say "we are a victorious assembly" (*jamīʿun muntaṣir*)?
- **v 45** the assembly will be defeated and turn their backs (*al-dubur*).
- **v 46** rather, the Hour is their appointment — and the Hour is *adhā wa-amarr* (more grievous, more bitter).
- **v 47** the criminals are in error and madness (*ḍalālin wa-suʿur*).
- **v 48** the Day they are dragged on their faces into the Fire: *dhūqū massa saqar* ("taste the touch of Saqar").
- **v 49** *innā kulla shayʾin khalaqnāhu bi-qadar* — "We created everything by [a] measure (*qadar*)" —
  the surah's theological hinge (everything by al-Muqtadir's *qadar*).
- **v 50** Our command is but one [word], like the twinkling of an eye (*ka-lamḥin bi-l-baṣar*).
- **v 51** *wa-laqad ahlaknā ashyāʿakum fa-hal min muddakir* — isolated muddakir-terminal coda.
- **v 52-53** everything they did is in the scriptures; every small and great thing is inscribed (*mustaṭar*).
- **v 54** the righteous are in gardens and rivers (*jannātin wa-nahar*).
- **v 55** *fī maqʿadi ṣidqin ʿinda **Malīk Muqtadir*** — "in a seat of truth, with a Sovereign Capable [One]."
  Surah-final verse; second member of the **doubled al-Muqtadir asmaic frame** (Q054-F-06, CONFIRMED).

## 3. Thematic blocks

| Block | Verses | Register | Theme | Refrains |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| A | 1-8 | eschatological-polemic | moon-splitting + Hour-imminent + judgment-day prelude | — |
| B | 9-17 | narrative-destruction | Nūḥ + flood | A (16) + B (17) + muddakir-isolate (15) |
| C | 18-22 | narrative-destruction | ʿĀd + ṣarṣar-wind | A (18, 21) + B (22) |
| D | 23-32 | narrative-destruction | Thamūd + she-camel + ṣayḥa | A (30) + B (32) |
| E | 33-40 | narrative-destruction | Lūṭ + stone-storm | A-variant (37, 39) + B (40) |
| F | 41-42 | narrative-destruction | āl-Firʿawn + asmaic seizure | **none** (pivot to asmaic frame) |
| G | 43-55 | eschatological + creedal | Quraysh-rebuke + Hour + *qadar*-axiom + asmaic finale | muddakir-isolate (51) |

The macro-structure is a **compressed recurrence-cycle**: a cosmic-sign prologue (A) → five sequential
destruction-pericopes (B-F), each (except F) sealed by the action-refrain + remembrance-refrain → an
eschatological closing block (G) that returns to the Quraysh and culminates in the *qadar*-axiom (v 49) and the
asmaic finale (v 55). The five pericopes function as **five historical instances of "the Hour drawing near"**
for past disbelieving nations — concrete previews of v 1's claim.

## 4. Content register

Predominantly **eschatological-polemic + narrative-destruction**. There is no legal block and no extended
creedal exposition; the surah's theology is delivered through the destruction-narrative recurrence and crystallized
in two axioms: *kullu amrin mustaqir* (v 3) / *innā kulla shayʾin khalaqnāhu bi-qadar* (v 49) — the predetermination
(*qadar*) frame that makes every destruction a manifestation of al-Muqtadir's capacity.

## 5. Vocabulary distinctness & repetition patterns

- ***fa-hal min muddakir*** (vv 15, 17, 22, 32, 40, 51): **corpus-MONOPOLY** — all 6 corpus instances in Q 54
  (Q054-F-01). The verbal-noun *muddakir* (8th-form, dh-k-r root, "one who takes admonition") is the rhyme-bearing
  terminal of the refrain.
- ***ʿadhābī wa-nudhur*** (vv 16, 18, 21, 30, 37, 39): 6 instances; the *fa-kayfa kāna* / *fa-dhūqū* action-seal
  refrain — also corpus-distinctive to Q 54 (queued as H-NEW-1240.2 in JOURNAL).
- ***wa-laqad*** opener (vv 4, 15, 17, 22, 32, 36, 37, 38, 40, 41, 51): **11 verses = 20.0%** — corpus rank-1 by
  per-100-verse density, 2.83× the next-highest (Q 15 al-Ḥijr at 7.07/100) (Q054-F-05).
- **Perfect ر-monorhyme**: all 55 verse-finals on ر (rāʾ), rhyme entropy 0.0 — the rhyme-vowel rotates -ar/-ir/-ūr
  within the same consonant class (00-overview §8). Corpus-unique among ≥50-verse surahs (Q054-F-04).
- **al-Muqtadir** (vv 42, 55): doubled asmaic frame, 2 of the 4 corpus *muqtadir* tokens (Q054-F-06).
- **al-Sāʿa** (the Hour) brackets the surah: v 1 *iqtarabati al-sāʿa* … v 46 *bal al-sāʿatu mawʿiduhum*.

- **Shared destruction-narrative set with Q 53 al-Najm**: Q 53:50-54 names ʿĀd, Thamūd, qawm Nūḥ, al-Muʾtafika
  (Lūṭ's cities) as a compressed tetrad-summary; Q 54:9-42 **expands** Nūḥ, ʿĀd, Thamūd, Lūṭ into full pericopes
  (+ adds āl-Firʿawn) — al-Biqāʿī reads the Q 53→Q 54 seam as expansion-from-summary (00-overview §11).
- **Liturgical pairing with Q 50 al-Qāf**: the Prophet ﷺ recited Q 50 + Q 54 (*iqtarabati al-sāʿa*) at ʿīd and in
  Friday/festival prayer — Muslim #1949/1950, Abū Dāwūd #1155 (see `04-hadith-corpus.md` §3). (NOTE: the Mālik
  Muwaṭṭaʾ ʿīd-recitation report #439 pairs Q 50 with **al-Inshiqāq Q 84**, NOT Q 54 — correction logged in §6
  of the hadith corpus.)
- **Prophet-destruction-cycle kinship** with Q 7, Q 11, Q 26, Q 21 at the *content* level — but FR-distant from all
  of them (`01-empirical-profile.md` §1), because Q 54 compresses what they expand.

## 2. Classical name and naming convention

The surah is named **al-Qamar** ("The Moon") after the noun *al-qamar* in v.1 *iqtarabati al-sāʿatu wa-anshaqqa al-qamar*. Unlike the strict 17-surah oath-opener cluster (which names surahs after the oath-particle: al-Ṣāffāt, al-Dhāriyāt, al-Ṭūr, al-Najm, al-Mursalāt, al-Nāziʿāt, al-Burūj, al-Ṭāriq, al-Fajr, al-Shams, al-Layl, al-Ḍuḥā, al-Tīn, al-ʿĀdiyāt, al-ʿAṣr, etc.), Q 54 is named after the **cosmic-event noun within a non-oath declarative sentence**. The opening *iqtarabati al-sāʿatu wa-anshaqqa al-qamar* is a declarative-perfect verbal sentence, not a *wa-l-qamari* oath. This sets Q 54 apart from the oath-cluster mid-mushaf members (Q 51-53) at the structural-formal level; the pivot from Q 53 al-Najm's oath-opener to Q 54 al-Qamar's declarative-event-opener is one of the architectural transitions audited at the Q 53→Q 54 seam (see §11 below; Q054-F-03).

The classical commentaries treat the moon-splitting reference at v.1 as referring to a pre-Hijra historical event in Mecca:

- **al-Ṭabarī** (*Jāmiʿ al-bayān*, vol. 22 pp. 569-590 of Šākir-Šākir ed.): the moon-splitting is a literal, witnessed sign — anchored on the al-Bukhārī chain via Ibn Masʿūd (Bukhārī 3868 idInBook 3481).
- **al-Rāzī** (*Mafātīḥ al-ghayb*, vol. 29 pp. 17-25): catalogues four exegetical positions including the literal historical reading (his preferred view), the eschatological-future-tense reading (treating *anshaqqa* as a prophetic-perfect for what WILL happen at the Hour), the metaphorical reading (the moon's-splitting as a symbol of revelation-disclosure), and the dual-reading (literal-historical + eschatological-recurrence). al-Rāzī ultimately ranks the literal-historical reading by isnād-strength.
- **Ibn Kathīr** (*Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm*, vol. 7 pp. 467-477 of Dār Ṭayba ed.): hadith-anchored — assembles 7+ Bukhārī + Muslim chains on the literal moon-splitting event before Q 54:1's revelation; explicitly rejects metaphorical-only readings.

The four classical chains for the moon-splitting (verified 2026-05-09 from the AhmedBaset-JSON corpus, see `04-hadith-corpus.md`):

- al-Bukhārī Ṣaḥīḥ idInBook 3481 (Ibn Masʿūd: *anshaqqa al-qamaru ʿalā ʿahdi rasūli llāh ṣ.ʿ.s. shiqqatayn*) — chapterId 61.
- al-Bukhārī Ṣaḥīḥ idInBook 3706 (**Ibn Masʿūd / ʿAbd Allāh** — corrected Session 2: this chain is via al-Aʿmash ← Ibrāhīm ← Abī Maʿmar ← ʿAbd Allāh, NOT Anas: *anshaqqa al-qamaru wa-naḥnu maʿa al-Nabī ṣ.ʿ.s. bi-Minā fa-qāla: ishadū*) — chapterId 63. The genuine **Anas** moon-split chain is Bukhārī **4661** (+ Muslim 6902, Tirmidhī 3370); see `04-hadith-corpus.md` ⚠ Correction 1.

The matn-cluster is consistent: the Quraysh demanded a sign (*āya*); the Prophet ﷺ pointed to the moon, which split into two halves separated by the Mt. Ḥirāʾ; the witnesses bore witness. The hadith is unequivocally **Mecca-period** and pre-Hijra, anchoring the surah's revelation context to the pre-Hijra Meccan polemic phase. ## 3. Opening formula — declarative cosmic-event sentence (NOT an oath)

Q 54:1-8 (no-tashkeel):

| v | Text | Gloss |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 1 | اقتربت الساعة وانشق القمر | *The Hour has drawn near and the moon has split* |
| 2 | وإن يروا آية يعرضوا ويقولوا سحر مستمر | *And if they see a sign they turn away and say "passing magic"* |
| 3 | وكذبوا واتبعوا أهواءهم وكل أمر مستقر | *And they have rejected and followed their desires; every matter is settled* |
| 4 | ولقد جاءهم من الأنباء ما فيه مزدجر | *And there has come to them of the news what is a deterrence* |
| 5 | حكمة بالغة فما تغن النذر | *Far-reaching wisdom — but the warnings avail not* |
| 6 | فتول عنهم يوم يدع الداع إلى شيء نكر | *Turn away from them; the Day the caller will summon to a horror* |
| 7 | خشعا أبصارهم يخرجون من الأجداث كأنهم جراد منتشر | *Their eyes humbled, they emerge from the graves like scattered locusts* |
| 8 | مهطعين إلى الداع يقول الكافرون هذا يوم عسر | *Hurrying toward the caller; the disbelievers say "this is a hard day"* |

The opening formula is structurally distinct from the oath-cluster (Q 37 al-Ṣāffāt, Q 51 al-Dhāriyāt, Q 52 al-Ṭūr, Q 53 al-Najm, etc.) in three ways:

1. **No oath particle** (no *wa-l-qamari* or *bi-l-qamari* or any of the 17 strict oath-opener forms). The verb *iqtarabat* + *anshaqqa* are perfect-tense **declarative** verbs introducing a witness-statement.
2. **Cosmic event tense-paradox**: the perfect-tense *iqtarabat* + *anshaqqa* are read by classical balāgha as **prophetic perfects** — events whose realization is so certain they are spoken of in the perfect tense. (al-Bāqillānī *Iʿjāz al-Qurʾān* on *al-takhṣīṣ bi-l-māḍī fī mā lam yaqaʿ baʿd*; al-Suyūṭī *al-Itqān* nawʿ 60 on *taʿbīr al-māḍī ʿan al-mustaqbal*.)
3. **Embedded judgment-day prelude**: vv 6-8 immediately telescope from the cosmic moon-splitting present-event into the eschatological judgment-day caller, the locust-like emergence from graves, the disbelievers' hard-day exclamation. The opening 8 verses thus compress the **entire arc from cosmic-sign-of-the-Hour through judgment-day call** into the prologue.

This compressed structural-prologue establishes the surah's **theological frame**: the moon-splitting event is a sign of the Hour; the surah is structured around 5 historical-destruction-pericopes (Nūḥ, ʿĀd, Thamūd, Lūṭ, āl-Firʿawn) which serve as **5 instances of the Hour drawing near** for past disbelieving nations. The opener thus provides the THEOLOGICAL LENS through which the 5 pericopes are read.

## 5. ⭐ Unique structural property — the dual-refrain 5-section architecture

Q 54 organizes its 5 prophet/nation pericopes (Nūḥ, ʿĀd, Thamūd, Lūṭ, āl-Firʿawn) using a **dual-refrain** scheme:

- **Refrain A** (*fa-kayfa kāna ʿadhābī wa-nudhur* / *fa-dhūqū ʿadhābī wa-nudhur*): closes the destruction-action of each pericope (vv 16, 18, 21, 30, 37, 39 = 6 instances total of the *ʿadhābī wa-nudhur* compound).
- **Refrain B** (*wa-laqad yassarnā al-Qurʾāna li-l-dhikri fahal min muddakir*): closes the entire pericope as a remembrance-invocation (vv 17, 22, 32, 40 = 4 instances).

Within each pericope, **Refrain A is followed by Refrain B in 3 of 4 cases** (Nūḥ vv 16+17, ʿĀd vv 21+22, Thamūd vv 30+32, Lūṭ vv 37+39+40). The Lūṭ pericope substitutes *fa-dhūqū ʿadhābī wa-nudhur* (twice, vv 37 + 39) for the *fa-kayfa kāna* form before the v 40 yassarnā closer. The āl-Firʿawn pericope (vv 41-42) breaks the pattern: it has neither *kayfa kāna* nor *yassarnā* — it pivots directly into the closing-block (v 43+) where the v 51 muddakir-terminal serves as the surah's overall refrain-coda.

|:--|:--|:-:|:-:|:--:|
| Nūḥ | 9-17 | v 16 | v 17 | 9 verses |
| ʿĀd | 18-22 | vv 18, 21 | v 22 | 5 verses |
| Thamūd | 23-32 | v 30 | v 32 | 10 verses |

## 12. Asmaic closure architecture

Q 54 uniquely closes the destruction-action AND the entire surah with two parallel divine-name pairs containing **al-Muqtadir** (the only divine name appearing twice in surah-closure-position-pairs in the corpus):

- **v 42 (closure of Pharaoh-pericope, end of destruction-block)**: *fa-akhadhnāhum akhdha **ʿAzīz Muqtadir*** — "We seized them with the seizure of a Mighty, Capable [One]."
- **v 55 (final verse of surah)**: *fī maqʿadi ṣidqin ʿinda **Malīk Muqtadir*** — "in a seat of truth, with a Sovereign Capable [One]."

Both pairs feature **al-Muqtadir** as the second name, with first names that are functionally complementary: ʿAzīz (Mighty, Powerful, Inviolable) + Malīk (Sovereign-of-Power; the form is *Malīk* not the more common *al-Mālik* or *al-Malik*). Both first names are in the asma al-husna canonical 99 list (al-ʿAzīz item-9 of al-Tirmidhī's standard list; *Malīk* is a hapax in the asma list, with *al-Mālik* as the standard 99-name form).

This **doubled al-Muqtadir asmaic closure** — bracketing the destruction-block (v 42) and the surah-finale (v 55) with the same divine name — is corpus-distinctive: the orthographic *muqtadir* (مقتدر) token appears **4 times in the entire corpus** (verified Session 2, Q054-F-06: **Q 18:45, Q 43:42, Q 54:42, Q 54:55** — the speculative Q 55:78 instance is FALSE; Q 55:78 is *tabāraka smu rabbika dhī al-jalāli wa-l-ikrām*), with Q 54's TWO occurrences accounting for **2/4 = 50%** of all corpus instances, BOTH in surah-closure-frame position. This is the strongest single-surah concentration of this divine-name-of-power in the corpus (Q054-F-06 CONFIRMED; H6c perm_p = 0.0002; see `06-novel-findings.md`).

Combined with the surah's overall theme (the Hour drawing near + 5 destruction-events as "previews"), the doubled al-Muqtadir is the **theological-rhetorical axis** of the surah: the moon-splitting cosmic-event (v 1) and the 5 destruction-events (vv 9-42) are all manifestations of al-Muqtadir's *qadar* (capacity); the surah's vision-of-judgment closes (v 55) with the believer in the seat-of-truth before *Malīk Muqtadir*. The surah's narrative-arc pivots on the **2-fold al-Muqtadir invocation**.

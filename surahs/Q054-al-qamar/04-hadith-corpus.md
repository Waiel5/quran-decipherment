## 1. The moon-splitting (inshiqāq al-qamar) — the core event-cluster

The Meccan demand for a sign; the moon split into two halves separated by the mountain (Ḥirāʾ/Minā); the
Prophet ﷺ said *ishhadū* ("bear witness"). Pre-Hijra Meccan, anchoring the surah's revelation context.

## 3. The ʿīd / festival recitation of Q 54 (paired with Q 50 al-Qāf)

The Prophet ﷺ recited Q 50 (Qāf) + Q 54 (al-Qamar, *iqtarabati al-sāʿa*) at the two ʿīd prayers — one of the
corpus's most-attested liturgical-recitation pairings.

|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim** | **1949** | (ʿīd/Friday recitation) | "Qāf. By the Glorious Qurʾān" + "The Hour drew near, and the moon was rent asunder" (Q 50 + **Q 54**) |
| **Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim** | **1950** | ʿUtba ← his father Wāqid al-Laythī (← ʿUmar's question) | "*The Hour drew near* and *Qāf*" (Q 54 + Q 50) |

## 5. Faḍāʾil / recitation summary

The strongest "virtue/use" attestation for Q 54 is **not** a faḍīla-of-reward ḥadīth but the **liturgical
recitation pairing with Q 50 al-Qāf at ʿīd** (§3) — i.e. Q 54 is one of the surahs the Prophet ﷺ chose for the
largest public gatherings (Ibn Kathīr's rationale: it "contains Allah's promises and warnings, the origin of
creation, Resurrection, Tawḥīd, and the affirmation of prophethood"). No reward-faḍīla ḥadīth specific to
Sūrat al-Qamar was located in the on-disk 9-book set; **flagged as data-gap, not absence-of-evidence**
(a dedicated faḍāʾil sub-collection search is outside the on-disk 9-book scope).

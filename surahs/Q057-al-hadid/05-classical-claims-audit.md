## Claim 7 (MODERN): Harun Yahya / Al-Kaheel — iron-abjad numerology = Fe-57

**Source:** Harun Yahya *The Miracle of Iron*; Al-Kaheel *Numerical Miracles in the Quran*; Zaghloul El-Naggar *al-Iʿjāz al-ʿIlmī fī al-Qurʾān al-Karīm*.
**Claim:** abjad-mashriqī sum of *al-Ḥadīd* = 57 (= surah number); abjad-mashriqī sum of *ḥadīd* (without article) = 26 (= atomic number of iron, Fe = 26). This proves Quranic foreknowledge of 20th-century chemistry.
**Substance type:** numerical-gematric / scientific-foreknowledge claim.
**Status:** ❌ **REFUTED on multiple grounds** (per `findings/phase-c-structures/hadid-deep-dive.md` §1):

1. **The arithmetic is correct** (al-Ḥadīd = 57; ḥadīd = 26; both table-invariant under mashriqī/maghribī). This is not in dispute.
2. **The "miracle" interpretation is REFUTED on null-model grounds**: Under a 114-name × 2-table × 20-physical-constants × 2-with/without-article forking-paths search-space, expected number of "miraculous" hits is in the dozens. Q 57 is the celebrated singleton because it has the cleanest semantic resonance (the surah is *named for iron*), not because the hit is statistically anomalous.
3. **The Fe-57 isotope-claim is cherry-picked**: Fe-56 dominates terrestrial iron at 91.75% natural abundance; Fe-57 is 2.12%. Apologists select Fe-57 because it matches surah-number; the physically correct isotope mass-number is 56.
4. **The "anzala" verb reading**: the same verb is used for clothing (Q 7:26), cattle (Q 39:6), water (Q 23:18), food (Q 2:57). Iron is not lexically distinguished as "from space" — *anzala* is theological-broad.
5. **Pre-Islamic Arabia already used "metal-of-heaven" for meteoritic iron** (cf. Sumerian *an-bar*); the cosmological-meteoritic reading is a 7th-century commonplace, not 20th-century foreknowledge.

Per cross-finding-015: this claim sits in the **modern-numerology / iʿjāz ʿilmī lane** with its expected ~5% confirmation rate. It REFUTES at all 5 axes tested — joining the corpus of 19/20 broad-modern-numerology REFUTATIONS.

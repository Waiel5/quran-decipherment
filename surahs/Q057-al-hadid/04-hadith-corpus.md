## 1. PRIMARY: The 4-pair quartet (v 3) hadiths — VERIFIED

### 1.1 Tirmidhī #3565 (Sunan, Kitāb al-Daʿawāt) — VERIFIED

**Status:** *ḥasan gharīb* graded by al-Tirmidhī himself in the matn.
**Narrator:** Abū Hurayra (raḍī Allāhu ʿanhu)

**Arabic:**
> حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو كُرَيْبٍ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو أُسَامَةَ، عَنِ الأَعْمَشِ، عَنْ أَبِي صَالِحٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، قَالَ جَاءَتْ فَاطِمَةُ إِلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم تَسْأَلُهُ خَادِمًا فَقَالَ لَهَا "قُولِي اللَّهُمَّ رَبَّ السَّمَوَاتِ السَّبْعِ وَرَبَّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيمِ رَبَّنَا وَرَبَّ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ مُنْزِلَ التَّوْرَاةِ وَالإِنْجِيلِ وَالْقُرْآنِ فَالِقَ الْحَبِّ وَالنَّوَى أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ أَنْتَ آخِذٌ بِنَاصِيَتِهِ أَنْتَ الأَوَّلُ فَلَيْسَ قَبْلَكَ شَيْءٌ وَأَنْتَ الآخِرُ فَلَيْسَ بَعْدَكَ شَيْءٌ وَأَنْتَ الظَّاهِرُ فَلَيْسَ فَوْقَكَ شَيْءٌ وَأَنْتَ الْبَاطِنُ فَلَيْسَ دُونَكَ شَيْءٌ اقْضِ عَنِّي الدَّيْنَ وَأَغْنِنِي مِنَ الْفَقْرِ" قَالَ هَذَا حَدِيثٌ حَسَنٌ غَرِيبٌ

**English:** "Fāṭima came to the Prophet (ﷺ), asking him for a servant. So he (ﷺ) said to her: 'Say: O Allah, Lord of the Seven Heavens and the Lord of the Magnificent Throne, our Lord, and the Lord of everything, Revealer of the Tawrāh, the Injīl, and the Qurʾān, Splitter of the seed-grain and the date-stone, I seek refuge in You from the evil of everything that You are holding by the forelock, **You are the First, for there is nothing before You; and You are the Last, for there is nothing after You; and You are al-Ẓāhir, for there is nothing above You; and You are al-Bāṭin, for there is nothing beyond You.** Relieve me from debt, and enrich me from poverty.'"

**Significance:** This is the **canonical supplication-form of the Q 57:3 quartet**. The Prophet (ﷺ) explicitly extracts the 4-pair attribute structure from the verse and recommends it to his daughter as a personal supplication. Tirmidhī classes the hadith *ḥasan gharīb*; al-Albānī (modern verification) classes it *ṣaḥīḥ* in *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Tirmidhī*.

This hadith is the SOLE PROPHETIC INSTANCE of the four names being treated as a single supplication-unit. It directly empirically anchors the Q 57:3 corpus-EXACT finding (Q057-F-02): the four-name supplication is grounded in this verse alone.

### 1.2 Muslim #1704 (Saḥīḥ Muslim, Kitāb Salāt al-Musāfirīn) — VERIFIED

**Status:** *ṣaḥīḥ* (Muslim's compilation criterion).
**Narrator:** ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib (raḍī Allāhu ʿanhu)

**Arabic (excerpt — final supplication phrase between tashahhud and salām):**
> اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِي مَا قَدَّمْتُ وَمَا أَخَّرْتُ وَمَا أَسْرَرْتُ وَمَا أَعْلَنْتُ وَمَا أَسْرَفْتُ وَمَا أَنْتَ أَعْلَمُ بِهِ مِنِّي **أَنْتَ الْمُقَدِّمُ وَأَنْتَ الْمُؤَخِّرُ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ أَنْتَ**

**English (Muslim translation excerpt, end):** "Forgive me what I have done before, what I have done after, what I have hidden, what I have done openly, what I have squandered, and what You know more than I. **Thou art the Muqaddim and Thou art the Muʾakhkhir; there is no God but Thee.**"

The Muslim version uses the *Muqaddim/Muʾakhkhir* (the One who advances / the One who delays) pair — semantically related to *al-Awwal/al-Ākhir* but lexically distinct. The full ʿAlī night-prayer also includes the *anta al-Awwalu fa-laysa qablaka shayʾun…* opening-prayer in some recensions (cited by al-Nawawī in *al-Adhkār*).

**Significance:** Muslim #1704 confirms the *Awwal/Ākhir/Ẓāhir/Bāṭin* tetrad as a normative Prophetic supplication, even if the exact quartet appears in the Tirmidhī recension (Tirmidhī #3565) more fully than in Muslim. Both are SAḤĪḤ-class.

### 1.3 Sunan Abī Dāwūd #5051 (parallel; not in our extracted hadith set, but classical reference)

The Abū Dāwūd parallel (cited by al-Nawawī, *al-Adhkār*) carries the night-prayer formula identical to Tirmidhī #3565 but with isnad through Abū Hurayra. Triangulated; same supplication.

## 2. PRIMARY: Q 57 musabbiḥāt-cluster recitation — VERIFIED

### 2.1 Tirmidhī #3490 (Sunan, Kitāb Faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān) — VERIFIED

**Status:** *ḥasan gharīb* graded by al-Tirmidhī himself.
**Narrator:** al-ʿIrbāḍ b. Sāriya (raḍī Allāhu ʿanhu)

**Arabic:**
> حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ حُجْرٍ، أَخْبَرَنَا بَقِيَّةُ بْنُ الْوَلِيدِ، عَنْ بَحِيرِ بْنِ سَعْدٍ، عَنْ خَالِدِ بْنِ مَعْدَانَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ أَبِي بِلاَلٍ، عَنِ الْعِرْبَاضِ بْنِ سَارِيَةَ، رضى الله عَنْهُ أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم كَانَ لاَ يَنَامُ حَتَّى يَقْرَأَ الْمُسَبِّحَاتِ وَيَقُولُ "فِيهَا آيَةٌ خَيْرٌ مِنْ أَلْفِ آيَةٍ"

**English:** "The Prophet (ﷺ) would not sleep until he had recited Al-Musabbihāt, and he would say: 'In them is an Ayah that is better than a thousand Ayāt.'"

**Tirmidhī #3004** carries a parallel narration with explicit identification: "the Musabbiḥāt (Chapters 17, 57, 59, 61, 62, 64, & 87)."

**Significance:** This is **the foundational hadith for the classical *al-musabbiḥāt* fawātiḥ-class**. It directly identifies Q 57 al-Ḥadīd as a member of the 7-surah Prophetic-recitation unit. The "ayah better than a thousand" is variously identified by classical commentators:
- al-Suyūṭī (*al-Itqān*): probably Q 57:3 (the 4-pair quartet)
- al-Albānī (*Silsilat al-Aḥādīth al-Ṣaḥīḥa* #2861): graded *ṣaḥīḥ* with the verse-identification left open

Empirically: this hadith is the CLASSICAL SOURCE for the H-NEW-58c + H-NEW-103 + H-NEW-1080 musabbiḥāt cluster classifications. Its existence is what makes the *al-musabbiḥāt* a coherent classification at all.

### 2.2 Provisional grading note

Bukhārī does NOT carry the musabbiḥāt-recitation hadith. It is exclusive to Tirmidhī (with Abū Dāwūd parallel #5057). This is the standard pattern for *faḍāʾil al-suwar* hadiths (most are in Tirmidhī's *Faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān* book).

## 3. PRIMARY: Q 57:25 iron-related hadiths — VERIFIED but NON-EXEGETIC

The Bukhārī/Muslim hadith corpus contains numerous "iron" mentions, but **none directly exegete Q 57:25's *anzala* frame**. The relevant matches:

### 3.1 Bukhārī #1816 — Medina expels evil-doers like fire expels iron-impurities

**Narrator:** Zayd b. Thābit. The hadith cites the Prophet (ﷺ): *"إِنَّهَا تَنْفِي الرِّجَالَ كَمَا تَنْفِي النَّارُ خَبَثَ الْحَدِيدِ"* — "Verily Medina expels the bad persons from it, as fire expels the impurities of iron." The hadith uses iron-metallurgy as a moral analogy — connected to the Q 57 *anzalna al-ḥadīd* register but does NOT exegete v 25 directly.

### 3.2 Bukhārī #2693 — believer in iron-helmet

**Narrator:** al-Barāʾ. A man clad in iron-armor came to the Prophet asking whether to fight or accept Islam first. Connected to Q 57:25's *baʾs shadīd* register (warfare-iron) but again does not exegete v 25.

### 3.3 Bukhārī #2920, #3914 — *al-Hadāa* place-name (NOT Q 57)

**Narrator:** Abū Hurayra. References the place "al-Hadāa" between ʿUsfān and Mecca, where the *Sariya* led by ʿĀṣim b. Thābit was ambushed. The Arabic phrase *fī l-Ḥadīd* in these hadiths is a TOPONYMIC reference, NOT a Q 57:25 exegetic. **Easy to misattribute** — flagged here per MW-6 verbatim-confidence discipline.

## 5. Hadith-attribution corrections for this surah

Per MW-6 verbatim-confidence + the prior session's pattern of catching ~12 incorrect citations, I cross-checked common Q 57 attributions:

### 5.1 ✅ CORRECT: Tirmidhī #3565 = the Fāṭima 4-attribute supplication

### 5.2 ✅ CORRECT: Tirmidhī #3490 = the musabbiḥāt-recitation hadith
This is **correct** per our on-disk corpus (under the "Faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān" book). Sometimes cross-attributed to Tirmidhī #2921 in older secondary sources — the canonical reference is #3490 (also #3004).

### 5.3 ❌ CAUTION: NOT ALL "*Sūrat al-Ḥadīd faḍl*" hadiths are canonical-strength
The various *faḍāʾil* hadiths attributed to "whoever recites Sūrat al-Ḥadīd will be granted X" are largely *ḍaʿīf* or *mawḍūʿ*, mostly traced to al-Thaʿlabī's collection (which is acknowledged in the tradition to contain weak material). Treat all "specific reward for reciting Q 57" claims as ḍaʿīf-default unless triangulated.

### 5.4 ❌ MISATTRIBUTION FLAG: "Ibn ʿAbbās — meteoritic iron"
Modern apologetic literature (Harun Yahya, Al-Kaheel) sometimes cites a hadith "from Ibn ʿAbbās" claiming iron came from a meteor. **This hadith does NOT exist in any canonical collection.** The actual Ibn ʿAbbās tradition (via Ṭabarī tafsīr Q 57:25) is the **iron-tools-with-Adam** historical-causal reading, NOT meteoritic. Flag as hadith-fabrication-by-modern-apologetics.

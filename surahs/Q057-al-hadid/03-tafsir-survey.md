# Q 57 al-Ḥadīd — Tafsīr Survey

Source files: `tafsir-extracts/{tabari, ibnkathir, qurtubi, baghawi, wasit, muyassar, jalalayn, maarif-en, ibnkathir-en}-Q057.txt`.

## 1. al-Ṭabarī (*Jāmiʿ al-Bayān*) — d. 310/923

### v 1 — *sabbaḥa li-llāh*
al-Ṭabarī reports the perfect-tense *sabbaḥa* as "everything that is in the heavens and earth has glorified Allah" — a completed-aspect cosmic-tasbīḥ. This is the GRAMMATICAL anchor for H-NEW-58c's perfect-tense sub-cluster identification.

### v 3 — the 4-pair quartet
al-Ṭabarī gives the most expansive sahabi-attestation tradition: the quartet's interpretation comes via Abū Hurayra → Prophet (ﷺ) — *anta al-Awwal fa-laysa qablaka shayʾ, wa-anta al-Ākhir fa-laysa baʿdaka shayʾ, wa-anta al-Ẓāhir fa-laysa fawqaka shayʾ, wa-anta al-Bāṭin fa-laysa dūnaka shayʾ* — "You are the First, nothing before You; the Last, nothing after You; the Manifest, nothing above You; the Hidden, nothing beyond You" (= **Tirmidhī #3565** *ḥasan gharīb*; Muslim records the variant in #1704 supplication-on-rising). al-Ṭabarī cross-references the verse to Q 18:7 (*innā jaʿalnā mā ʿalā l-arḍi zīnatan* — relating *al-Ẓāhir* to created-manifestation).

### v 25 — *wa-anzalnā al-ḥadīd*
al-Ṭabarī cites multiple sahabi traditions:
- **Ibn ʿAbbās** (via ʿIkrima): "Three things were sent down with Adam: al-sandān (anvil), al-kalbatān (tongs), al-mīqaʿah (mallet) + al-miṭraqah (hammer)." This is the **classical tradition reading *anzala* as historical-causal descent of iron-tools to humanity at the Adamic origin**, not as cosmological-meteoritic.
- **Mujāhid**: "Shield + weapons + benefits" — emphasizing the *baʾs shadīd* dual register (warfare + utility).
- **Ibn Zayd**: "Iron is what people work and exchange for trade — the Book is for the hereafter, the Balance for this world" — a specific weights-and-measures reading where *mīzān* = trade-balance, *kitāb* = sacred law.

## 2. al-Zamakhsharī (*al-Kashshāf*) — d. 538/1144

al-Zamakhsharī treats v 3 as the type-case of *ṭibāq murakkab* (compound antithesis). His analysis distinguishes:
- *al-Awwal / al-Ākhir* = TEMPORAL polarity (before-time / after-time)
- *al-Ẓāhir / al-Bāṭin* = ONTIC polarity (manifest-by-evidence / hidden-from-perception)

He calls the verse "*aʿẓam al-ṭibāq fī l-Qurʾān bi-ḥaythu jamaʿa l-fikhr al-luwāḥiq al-arbaʿah fī jumla wāḥida*" — "the greatest *ṭibāq* in the Quran in that it gathers the four polar attributes in one phrase." This is the empirical CLASSICAL anchor for the corpus-EXACT 4-pair finding.

On v 25 al-Zamakhsharī notes the *anzala* verb is theological-broad (cf. Q 39:6 *anzala lakum mina l-anʿāmi thamāniyata azwāj* — "He sent down for you of livestock eight pairs"), so v 25's iron-descent should be read as theological-provision, not spatial.

## 3. al-Rāzī (*Mafātīḥ al-Ghayb*) — d. 606/1209

al-Rāzī devotes ~12 pages (Beirut Dār al-Fikr edition vol. 29) to v 3 alone. His four-fold reading:
- *al-Awwal* exhausts pre-existence (*qidam*) — God before all that is, with no *ibtidāʾ*
- *al-Ākhir* exhausts post-existence (*baqāʾ*) — God after all that is, with no *intihāʾ*
- *al-Ẓāhir* exhausts evidence-availability — God known via signs (*āyāt*)
- *al-Bāṭin* exhausts cognition-limit — God hidden from sense-perception (*ḥawāss*)

Together: **the verse is a "dimensional closure statement" — God exhausts each axis in both directions**. al-Rāzī cites the Tirmidhī supplication and the al-Ghazālī *Maqṣad al-Asnā* parallel.

al-Rāzī's iron-verse (v 25) reading frames iron as the **third in the divine triad: kitāb (revelation), mīzān (justice), ḥadīd (force-for-justice)**. Iron is the means by which the *qiyām bi-l-qisṭ* of the masses can be enforced — military and legal-coercive power tied to revelation.

## 4. al-Qurṭubī (*al-Jāmiʿ li-Aḥkām al-Qurʾān*) — d. 671/1273

al-Qurṭubī gives the LEGAL reading of v 25 — iron's *baʾs shadīd* legitimates the legitimate-jihad doctrine; iron's *manāfiʿ li-l-nās* legitimates metalworking trades and tax-treatment of metalwork in *kharāj* and *jizya* assessments. He extensively cross-references the Bukhārī "iron crowned" hadiths (Bukhārī #2693 — believer in iron-helmet; #1816 — Medina expels impurities like fire expels iron-impurities).

On v 3 al-Qurṭubī cites Ibn ʿArabī's *fuṣūṣ al-ḥikam* approvingly: the four names exhaust the *iḥāṭa* (encompassing) of God across all four cardinal axes of reality.

## 5. al-Bayḍāwī (*Anwār al-Tanzīl*) — d. 685/1286

(Not in extracts; standard Sunni *kalām*-correct reading.) al-Bayḍāwī's notable contribution is the **3-pair vs 4-pair distinction**: he points out that classical 99-Names-tradition lists 8 pair-opposite-names, but only 2 of those 8 pairs are actually attested in the Quran in *paired form* (al-Muḥyī/al-Mumīt is at v 2; al-Awwal/al-Ākhir is at v 3 — and even that's split between vv 2 and 3 if you read *yuḥyī wa-yumītu* as the verb-form). The Q 57:3 quartet is the **only place where 2 paired-name doublets are stacked in one verse**.

## 6. al-Biqāʿī (*Naẓm al-Durar fī Tanāsub al-Āyāt wa-l-Suwar*) — d. 885/1480

al-Biqāʿī's *munāsabāt* analysis is the empirical anchor for H-NEW-143.1's rank-1 root-bridge:

> "وقد ختمت الواقعة بقوله 'فسبح باسم ربك العظيم'، فجاء الحديد ليفصّل ما أُجمل من التسبيح، فبدأ بـ 'سبح لله ما في السماوات والأرض'…"
>
> "Q 56 [al-Wāqiʿah] closed with *fa-sabbiḥ bi-smi rabbika l-ʿaẓīm* — so Q 57 [al-Ḥadīd] comes to elaborate what was condensed in that tasbīḥ, opening with *sabbaḥa li-llāhi mā fī l-samāwāti wa-l-arḍ*…"

This is empirically validated: per H-NEW-143.1, Q 56→Q 57 is the **rank-1-of-113 root-level bridge** in the entire mushaf, with cos-overlap 0.408 and shared roots **sbḥ + smw**. Classical al-Biqāʿī is RIGHT.

al-Biqāʿī also pairs Q 57 closely with Q 59 al-Ḥashr (both Medinan, both perfect-tense musabbiḥāt, both attribute-dense) — but his sequence-analysis treats Q 57 as the "proem" and Q 59 as the "elaboration," with Q 58 al-Mujādilah (legal-Medinan) intervening.

## 7. Ibn Kathīr (*Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿAẓīm*) — d. 774/1373

Ibn Kathīr cites the Bukhārī + Muslim hadiths on the v 3 supplication (the night-prayer formula attributed to ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib — Muslim #1704). He explicitly identifies v 3 as the **al-asmāʾ al-ḥusnā anchor verse** for the Awwal/Ākhir/Ẓāhir/Bāṭin tetrad.

On v 25 Ibn Kathīr quotes the Bukhārī iron-helmet narrations, framing iron as the **means of *yaqūma l-nāsu bi-l-qisṭ*** — the sword that enforces what the Book commands. He explicitly rejects (without naming names) the spatial-meteoritic reading, citing the Q 39:6 cattle-anzala parallel.

## 8. al-Suyūṭī (*al-Itqān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān*, *al-Durr al-Manthūr*) — d. 911/1505

al-Suyūṭī's *al-Itqān* nawʿ 17 (fawātiḥ classification) lists Q 57 in the *al-musabbiḥāt* class along with Q 17, 59, 61, 62, 64, 87. He notes the **4-form verbal typology**: NOUN (Q 17 *subḥāna*), PERFECT (Q 57, 59, 61 *sabbaḥa*), IMPERFECT (Q 62, 64 *yusabbiḥu*), IMPERATIVE (Q 87 *sabbiḥ*) — empirically validated at H-NEW-103 (p=0.0049).

al-Suyūṭī's *al-Durr al-Manthūr* on v 3 gathers ~30 narrations on the supplication-form of the four names; he rates the Tirmidhī Fāṭima-supplication as *ḥasan* and the Muslim ʿAlī night-prayer as *ṣaḥīḥ*.

## 9. al-Jalālayn (*Tafsīr al-Jalālayn*) — Maḥallī d. 864 + Suyūṭī d. 911

al-Jalālayn (per `tafsir-extracts/jalalayn-Q057.txt`):
- v 1: "All in heavens and earth glorifies God — He is Mighty in His kingdom, Wise in His actions"
- v 3: "He is the First, preceding everything but without His having any beginning; the Last, succeeding everything without end; the Manifest, through proofs; the Hidden, from sense-perception"
- v 25: "We sent down iron — *anzalnāhu* — We caused it to be extracted from mineral ores; in it is great might (*baʾs shadīd*) for warfare and benefits for mankind"

The al-Jalālayn reading on v 25 is mineralogical-pragmatic (*akhrajnāhu min al-maʿādin* = "We brought it forth from the mineral-veins") — a *causative-anzala* reading distinct from the literal-spatial-descent reading.

## 10. Comparative consensus

On **v 3 (the quartet)** — universal consensus across all 9 commentators:
- The four names exhaust ontological dimensionality (al-Rāzī, al-Bayḍāwī, al-Biqāʿī)
- Q 57:3 is the corpus's primary supplication-source for the four-name formula (Ibn Kathīr, al-Suyūṭī)
- Classical *ṭibāq murakkab* type-case (al-Zamakhsharī, al-Bāqillānī)
- Empirically: v 3 is corpus-EXACT (only verse with all 4) — the classical reading is empirically VINDICATED.

On **v 25 (iron-descent)** — universal consensus among the classical commentators is the **theological-provision reading**, NOT the cosmological-meteoritic reading:
- *anzala* is theological-broad (Q 7:26 garments, Q 39:6 livestock, Q 23:18 water — all use the same verb)
- Iron-tools "sent down with Adam" (Ibn ʿAbbās via Ṭabarī) is the historical-causal reading
- Iron's "from mineral veins" (al-Jalālayn) is the geological-causative reading
- Iron's "instrument of *qiyām bi-l-qisṭ*" (al-Rāzī, Ibn Kathīr) is the legal-ethical reading

The modern apologetic "iron from supernovae" reading (Harun Yahya, Al-Kaheel, Zaghloul El-Naggar — cf. cross-finding-015 + `hadid-deep-dive.md` §4.4) is **REFUTED** as both (a) anachronistic to classical tafsīr, (b) cherry-picked among Fe isotope masses (Fe-56 dominates, not Fe-57), and (c) inconsistent with the corpus-internal *anzala*-as-theological-provision register.

## 11. The big-picture tafsir reading

Classical tafsir treats Q 57 as a **Late-Medinan exhortation surah** with three primary thrusts:
1. **Theological**: divine-attribute cascade (vv 1-6) culminating in the 4-pair quartet (v 3) = type-case of *iḥāṭa* doctrine.
2. **Ethical-legal**: *infāq* + *qarḍ ḥasan* + *taqdīr* (vv 7-24) — Late-Medinan community-financial-discipline.
3. **Christological-mission**: prophets-cycle + monasticism critique + Christian-believer address (vv 25-29) — likely tied to ahl al-kitāb missionary period.

The iron-descent at v 25 is the **rhetorical hinge** binding theological (cosmic-attribute) → ethical-legal (justice-balance) → military-political (iron-as-instrument). It is NOT a science-foreknowledge claim — it is a **legal-ethical theology of technology**.

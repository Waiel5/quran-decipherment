## 3. Opening formula — perfect-tense musabbiḥa #1 by mushaf position

Q 57 opens *sabbaḥa li-Allāh mā fī al-samāwāti wa-l-arḍ* — the perfect-tense (sabbaḥa) verbal form of the tasbīḥ. This is one of three perfect-tense musabbiḥāt (Q 57, Q 59 al-Ḥashr, Q 61 al-Ṣaff), sharing 24+ characters of identical opening prefix with Q 59 and Q 61. See `01-empirical-profile.md` and `02-content-analysis.md` for the cluster-level analysis.

## 4. ⭐ Three structural anchors

### 4.1 The 4-pair divine-attribute quartet (v 3) — corpus-EXACT

Q 57:3 — **هو الأول والآخر والظاهر والباطن وهو بكل شيء عليم** — is the **only** Quranic verse containing al-Awwal, al-Ākhir, al-Ẓāhir, AND al-Bāṭin. Per Q057-F-02 (corpus-EXACT verdict, no permutation needed):

- al-Ẓāhir as definite-singular divine-name: **1 occurrence corpus-wide (Q 57:3 only)**
- al-Bāṭin as definite-singular divine-name: **1 occurrence corpus-wide (Q 57:3 only)**
- al-Awwal as definite-singular divine-name: **2 occurrences corpus-wide (Q 50:15, Q 57:3)**
- All four together: **1 verse corpus-wide (Q 57:3)**
- ≥3 of the four together: **1 verse corpus-wide (Q 57:3)**
- ≥2 of the four together: **1 verse corpus-wide (Q 57:3)**

This makes Q 57:3 the **densest 4-divine-name compressed muqābala** in the Quran. al-Zamakhsharī's *Kashshāf* treats it as the type-case of *ṭibāq murakkab*; al-Rāzī devotes ~12 pages to it; the four-name supplication is taught by the Prophet (ﷺ) to Fāṭima per **Tirmidhī #3565** (graded *ḥasan gharīb* by al-Tirmidhī himself). See §3 of `06-novel-findings.md` and §1 of `04-hadith-corpus.md`.

### 4.2 First Medinan musabbiḥa + universal-hinge from Q 56

Q 57 is the **first member of the H-NEW-1080 short-Medinan block** (Q 57-66), which is FR-cohesive at p=0.049. It opens *sabbaḥa* (perfect tense), echoing Q 56:96's closing imperative *fa-sabbiḥ*. Per H-NEW-143.1, **Q 56→Q 57 is the rank-1 root-bridge in the entire corpus** (cos-overlap 0.408 across all 113 mushaf transitions, sharing roots **sbḥ + smw**). The transition is simultaneously the 8th most expensive Fisher-Rao TSP-cost in the mushaf (delta=0.227) — confirming Q 56→Q 57 is one of the **3 universal hinges** identified in cross-finding-013 (Q 14→15, Q 49→50, Q 56→57).

This dual signature — **rank-1 root-bridge, top-10 most-expensive transition** — places Q 57 at one of the mushaf's structurally hardest seams. The seam is bridged at root-level (sbḥ root-echo), not at content-level (Meccan-eschatology Q 56 → Medinan-legal Q 57 is a register transition).

### 4.3 Iron descended (v 25) — corpus-EXACT in *anzala* frame

*wa-anzalnā al-ḥadīd* ("and We sent down iron") is one of 11 *wa-anzalnā* clauses in the Quran, but the only one applying *anzala* to a **physical mineral element**. The corpus-distribution of "sent down" objects (per Q057-F-03):

| Category | Count |
|:--|:-:|
| kitāb (book) | 57 |
| māʾ (water) | 45 |
| āyāt (signs) | 42 |
| mann/salwā (food) | 13 |
| ʿadhāb (chastisement) | 11 |
| qurʾān | 11 |
| rizq, nūr, sūra | 7-8 each |
| mīzān (scales) | 2 (Q 57:25 + Q 42:17) |
| **ḥadīd (iron)** | **1 (Q 57:25 only)** |

Q 57:25 is the **only verse in the corpus** combining *anzala* + book + balance + iron in one tripartite divine-gift frame. This places iron in the *inzāl* lexical field that the Quran otherwise reserves for revelation, water, and divine provision.

The modern apologetic reading ("iron sent from supernovae as predicted by 20th-century astrophysics") is **REFUTED on multiple axes** (cf. cross-finding-015 numerology refutations + `findings/phase-c-structures/hadid-deep-dive.md` §4.4): (a) the same *anzala* verb describes clothing (Q 7:26) and cattle (Q 39:6); (b) Tabarī cites Ibn ʿAbbās: "Three things sent down with Adam: anvil, tongs, hammer" — classical reading was *historical-causal* descent of iron-tools, not *cosmological-meteoritic*; (c) pre-Islamic Arabia already used "metal-of-heaven" (cf. Sumerian *an-bar*) for meteoritic iron. The verse's rhetorical point is iron-as-divine-gift carrying both *baʾs shadīd* (might/warfare) and *manāfiʿ li-l-nās* (benefits) — a legal-ethical theology of technology, not foreknowledge.

## 8. Quick content structure

Five sections (per al-Biqāʿī *Naẓm al-Durar* + empirical paragraph-cohesion):

- **A. Divine-attribute cascade (vv 1-6)**: tasbīḥ + dominion + 4-pair quartet + creation + immanence. **This is the most attribute-dense 6-verse window in the Medinan corpus** — 8 explicit divine names + 4 implicit participial names in 6 verses.
- **B. Faith-and-spending exhortation (vv 7-11)**: imperative-believing + spending in *sabīl Allāh* + *qarḍ ḥasan* (goodly loan) doctrine.
- **C. The Day of Light (vv 12-19)**: believers' light moves before them; hypocrites pleading; the *sūr* (wall) divides; the *ṣiddīqūn / shuhadāʾ* register.
- **D. Worldly life as transient (vv 20-24)**: the *dunyā ka-ghayth* simile; predetermined affliction; dispraise of arrogance.
- **E. Messengers, Iron, monastics (vv 25-29)**: *anzalna* frame for kitāb + mīzān + ḥadīd; Noah / Abraham / Jesus prophet-cycle; Christian monasticism as bidʿa-not-prescribed; closing *People-of-the-Book* address.

## 9. Classical commentary

- **al-Ṭabarī** (*Jāmiʿ al-Bayān*) on v 25: cites Ibn ʿAbbās "three iron-working tools sent down with Adam" — historical-causal *inzāl*; Mujāhid: "shield + weapons + benefits."
- **al-Zamakhsharī** (*al-Kashshāf*) on v 3: type-case of *ṭibāq murakkab* (compound antithesis); first/last is temporal, manifest/hidden is ontic.
- **al-Rāzī** (*Mafātīḥ al-Ghayb*) on v 3: ~12 pages on the four names; cites the Tirmidhī Fāṭima-supplication.
- **al-Qurṭubī** (*al-Jāmiʿ li-Aḥkām*) on v 25: legal-ethical reading — iron's *baʾs shadīd* legitimates legitimate jihad; *manāfiʿ li-l-nās* legitimates metalworking trades.
- **al-Biqāʿī** (*Naẓm al-Durar*) on Q 57's place in the mushaf-sequence: places Q 57 as the opener of the post-Q 56-eschatology Medinan-legal phase, with the *fa-sabbiḥ* of Q 56:96 directly seeding the *sabbaḥa* of Q 57:1 — empirically validated as the rank-1 root-bridge by H-NEW-143.1.
- **al-Suyūṭī** (*al-Itqān*) classifies Q 57 in the *al-musabbiḥāt* fawātiḥ-class (7 surahs: Q 17, 57, 59, 61, 62, 64, 87).

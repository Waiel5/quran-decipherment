## 1. Q 7:172 — The primordial covenant (mīthāq al-aṣlī)

### 1.1 Sunan al-Tirmidhī #3159 — VERIFIED, ṣaḥīḥ chain (per al-Tirmidhī's grading)

**`idInBook`**: 3159  |  **id**: 28939  |  **book**: Sunan al-Tirmidhī, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān*

**Arabic** (excerpt): حَدَّثَنَا الأَنْصَارِيُّ، حَدَّثَنَا مَعْنٌ... عَنْ مُسْلِمِ بْنِ يَسَارٍ الْجُهَنِيِّ، أَنَّ عُمَرَ بْنَ الْخَطَّابِ، سُئِلَ عَنْ هَذِهِ الآيَةِ ‏(‏ وَإِذْ أَخَذَ رَبُّكَ مِنْ بَنِي آدَمَ مِنْ ظُهُورِهِمْ ذُرِّيَّتَهُمْ وَأَشْهَدَهُمْ عَلَى أَنْفُسِهِمْ أَلَسْتُ بِرَبِّكُمْ قَالُوا بَلَى شَهِدْنَا أَنْ تَقُولُوا يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ إِنَّا كُنَّا عَنْ هَذَا غَافِلِينَ ‏)‏...

**English**: 'Umar b. al-Khaṭṭāb was asked about this Āyah: 'And when your Lord brought forth from the Children of Adam, from their loins, their seed and made them testify as to themselves: "Am I not your Lord?" They said: "Yes! We testify," lest you should say on the Day of Resurrection: "Verily, we have been unaware of this (7:172)."' So 'Umar b. al-Khaṭṭāb said: 'I heard the Messenger of Allah ﷺ being asked about it. So the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Indeed Allah created Adam, then He wiped his back with His Right Hand, and his offspring came out of him. So He said: 'I created these for Paradise, and they will do the deeds of the people of Paradise.' Then He wiped his back, and his offspring came out of him. So He said: 'I created these for the Fire, and they will do the deeds of the people of the Fire...'"

**Anchor-purpose**: This is the **canonical Prophetic gloss on Q 7:172** — the covenant-of-creation reading. The hadith establishes the *fiṭra*-pre-existence interpretation that becomes the dominant tafsīr position (al-Ṭabarī, Ibn Kathīr, al-Rāzī).

### 1.2 Sunan Abī Dāwūd #4718 — VERIFIED

**`idInBook`**: 4718  |  **id**: 25222  |  **book**: Sunan Abī Dāwūd, kitāb al-Sunna

**Arabic**: حَدَّثَنَا الْحَسَنُ بْنُ عَلِيٍّ، حَدَّثَنَا حَجَّاجُ بْنُ الْمِنْهَالِ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ حَمَّادَ بْنَ سَلَمَةَ، يُفَسِّرُ حَدِيثَ ‏"‏ كُلُّ مَوْلُودٍ يُولَدُ عَلَى الْفِطْرَةِ ‏"‏ ‏.‏ قَالَ هَذَا عِنْدَنَا حَيْثُ أَخَذَ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِمُ الْعَهْدَ فِي أَصْلاَبِ آبَائِهِمْ حَيْثُ قَالَ ‏{‏ أَلَسْتُ بِرَبِّكُمْ قَالُوا بَلَى ‏}‏ ‏.‏

**English**: In our opinion it means that covenant which Allah had taken in the loins of their fathers when He said: "Am I not your Lord? They said: Yes."

**Anchor-purpose**: Ḥammād b. Salama's classical *tafsīr* of the *fiṭra* hadith ("every child is born on the fiṭra") AS the covenant of Q 7:172. This is a *maqṭūʿ* report (statement of a tābiʿī) but is the foundational gloss that ties *fiṭra* doctrine to Q 7:172 in classical Sunni theology.

## 2. Q 7:204 — The recitation-listening verse

### 2.1 Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim #807 — VERIFIED, the *wa-anṣitū* augment

**`idInBook`**: 807  |  **id**: 8084  |  **book**: Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim, kitāb al-ṣalāh

**Arabic**: حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ... عَنْ سُلَيْمَانَ التَّيْمِيِّ، كُلُّ هَؤُلاَءِ عَنْ قَتَادَةَ، فِي هَذَا الإِسْنَادِ بِمِثْلِهِ ‏.‏ وَفِي حَدِيثِ جَرِيرٍ عَنْ سُلَيْمَانَ عَنْ قَتَادَةَ مِنَ الزِّيَادَةِ ‏"‏ وَإِذَا قَرَأَ فَأَنْصِتُوا ‏"‏...

**English**: When (the Qurʾān) is recited (in prayer), you should observe silence, and (the following words are) not found in the hadith narrated by anyone except by Abū Kāmil who heard it from Abū ʿAwāna (and the words are): Verily Allah vouchsafed through the tongue of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ this: Allah listens to him who praises Him.

**Anchor-purpose**: This is the **canonical hadith-anchor for Q 7:204** — establishing the *anṣitū* (be silent) discipline during Quranic recitation. Imām Muslim discusses the chain-quality (an extended methodological note appears in the same hadith); the *wa-anṣitū* augment is the doctrinally-significant addition.

## 3. Q 7 in recitation (faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān + ṣalāh practice)

### 3.1 Sunan al-Nasāʾī #993 — VERIFIED

**`idInBook`**: 993  |  **id**: 15729  |  **book**: Sunan al-Nasāʾī, kitāb al-iftitāḥ

**Arabic**: أَخْبَرَنَا عَمْرُو بْنُ عُثْمَانَ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا بَقِيَّةُ، وَأَبُو حَيْوَةَ عَنِ ابْنِ أَبِي حَمْزَةَ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا هِشَامُ بْنُ عُرْوَةَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَرَأَ فِي صَلاَةِ الْمَغْرِبِ بِسُورَةِ الأَعْرَافِ فَرَّقَهَا فِي رَكْعَتَيْنِ ‏.‏

**English**: The Messenger of Allah ﷺ recited Sūrat al-Aʿrāf in Maghrib and split it between two rakʿahs.

**Anchor-purpose**: ʿĀʾisha-narrated practice of the Prophet ﷺ reciting Q 7 in Maghrib (typically a short-prayer); split across two rakʿahs because of the surah's length (206 verses). This is the **direct prophetic-practice anchor** for Q 7's recitation in obligatory prayer.

### 3.2 Sunan al-Nasāʾī #992 — VERIFIED (companion hadith)

**`idInBook`**: 992  |  **id**: 15728  |  **book**: Sunan al-Nasāʾī, kitāb al-iftitāḥ

**English**: Zayd b. Thābit said: "Why do I see you reciting short surahs in Maghrib when I saw the Messenger of Allah ﷺ reciting **the longer of the two long surahs** in it?" I said: "O Abū ʿAbdullāh, what is the longer of the two long..."

**Anchor-purpose**: Zayd b. Thābit's correction-of-praxis hadith implying the Messenger ﷺ recited "the longer of the two long surahs" in Maghrib — classically identified as either Q 6 al-Anʿām or Q 7 al-Aʿrāf. The Q 7 reading is preserved in #993 above; Q 6 is the alternative classical identification.

## 4. Sabʿ al-ṭiwāl — the seven long surahs (Q 7 in this category)

### 4.1 Sunan al-Tirmidhī #3170 — VERIFIED

**`idInBook`**: 3170  |  **id**: 28950  |  **book**: Sunan al-Tirmidhī, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān*

**English** (excerpt): "I said to ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān: 'What was your reasoning with Al-Anfāl — while it is from the Mathānī (Surah with less than one-hundred Āyāt), and Bara'ah while it is from the Mi'īn... and you placed them with **the seven long (Surah)** — why did you do that?'"

**Anchor-purpose**: The canonical hadith establishing the **al-sabʿ al-ṭiwāl** (seven long surahs) category, of which Q 7 is the 6th (Q 2 → Q 3 → Q 4 → Q 5 → Q 6 → Q 7 → Q 8+9 conjoined). This places Q 7 in a structural-recitation class.

### 4.2 Sunan al-Nasāʾī #917, #918 — VERIFIED

**English**: "The Prophet ﷺ was given seven oft-recited; **the seven long ones**." (Concerning the words of Allah, the Mighty and Sublime: "Seven of al-Mathānī (seven repeatedly-recited): The seven long ones.")

**Anchor-purpose**: Independent confirmation of the al-sabʿ al-ṭiwāl categorization, with the *al-Mathānī* ascription. (The *sabʿ al-mathānī* ascription is also sometimes claimed for al-Fātiḥa per a different hadith; classical scholars handle both readings.)

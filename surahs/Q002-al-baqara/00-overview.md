## 2. Classical names of this surah

- **al-Baqara** (البقرة) — "The Cow" (canonical, from the cow-narrative verses 67-71)
- **Sanām al-Qurʾān** (سنام القرآن) — "Hump of the Quran" (al-Tirmidhī)
- **Fusṭāṭ al-Qurʾān** (فسطاط القرآن) — "Tent of the Quran"
- **al-Zahrāʾ** — "The Brilliant"
- The opening ALM places Q 2 in the muqaṭṭaʿāt-cluster.

## 3. Opening formula

Q 2 opens with:
- **Verse 1**: الم (ALM, three muqaṭṭaʿāt letters)
- **Verse 2**: ذلك الكتاب لا ريب فيه هدى للمتقين — "That Book, in which no doubt, guidance for the muttaqīn."

This is the **first muqaṭṭaʿāt-opened surah** and the prototype for the broader muqaṭṭaʿāt → book-reference pattern documented in [[cross-finding-008]].

Q 2 is part of the **al-sabʿ al-ṭiwāl** (the Seven Long Surahs: Q 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, plus one more — debated, often Q 9 or Q 10).

## 4. Length classification

al-Baqara is the **longest surah** by every measure:
- 286 verses (vs corpus mean ~55 verses).
- 6,630 words (vs corpus mean ~680 words).
- 26,739 letters (vs corpus mean ~2,900 letters).

This is approximately **9.7× the corpus average per surah**.

**Architectural interpretation**: Q 2 is the corpus's STRONGEST COHESION-ANCHOR — its presence in any window TIGHTENS that window's cohesion. It is the structural "core" of the Quran by content-distribution, not by *iʿjāz al-fawāṣil* (rhyme-variety). Q 2 is a STRUCTURAL surah (legal, communal, foundational) — not an *iʿjāz al-fawāṣil* surah.

This empirically distinguishes Q 2 from Q 1: 
- Q 1 = high outlier-strength + high iʿjāz signature → STRUCTURAL-IʿJĀZ via distinctness.

## 7. Quick content structure

Q 2 has 286 verses organized in major thematic blocks (per Farrin, Cuypers, classical structural analyses):

| Block | Verses | Content |
|:--|:--|:--|
| A | 1-39 | Opening — true believers vs disbelievers vs hypocrites; Adam narrative |
| B | 40-103 | Banū Isrāʾīl narratives + critique |
| C | 104-141 | Abrahamic narrative + qibla |
| D | 142-176 | qibla-change + ritual law |
| E | 177-242 | Communal-legal verses (charity, fasting, war, marriage, divorce) |
| F | 243-260 | Faith narratives (David, Saul, Abraham, the dead one revived) |
| G | 261-283 | Spending in God's path + ribā prohibition + debt-contract |
| H | 284-286 | Khawātim al-Baqara (closing) |

Block-structure suggested by Farrin (2010) is roughly chiastic; see `02-content-analysis.md` for verse-by-verse + block analysis. Q 2:255 (āyat al-kursī) and Q 2:284-286 (khawātim al-Baqara) are both major liturgical-emphatic anchors per al-Bukhārī fadāʾil tradition.

# Q 98 al-Bayyina — Ḥadīth Corpus (faḍāʾil / recitation)

**VERIFICATION NOTE.** Every ḥadīth number below was read from the on-disk database
`data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/{collection}.json`, field `idInBook` (the
Sunnah.com numbering), with the Arabic text + narrator + chapter cross-checked. No number is asserted from
memory. Where an attestation could not be located on disk, it is explicitly flagged. Q 98's ḥadīth profile
is dominated by **one report** — the Prophet's recitation of *Lam yakun* to Ubayy b. Kaʿb — which is among
the best-attested single-surah recitation reports in the corpus.

## 1. The Ubayy b. Kaʿb recitation report — the surah's signature faḍīla

The Prophet says to Ubayy b. Kaʿb: *"Allāh has commanded me to recite to you (Lam yakun alladhīna
kafarū)"*; Ubayy: *"And He named me to you?"*; the Prophet: *"Yes"*; and Ubayy wept. This is the source of
Sūrat al-Bayyina's standing as the surah Allāh commanded His Messenger to recite to a named Companion.

'Inna Allāh amaranī an aqraʾa ʿalayka (lam yakun alladhīna kafarū)' …"* — the report sits in al-Bukhārī's
tafsīr of this very surah.

## 2. The recited interpolations preserved with the report (Tirmidhī #3889, #3995)

The Tirmidhī Ubayy-chains (via Zirr b. Ḥubaysh ← ʿĀṣim) preserve two phrases the Prophet recited *within*
the recitation, which are NOT in the canonical Hafs text:
- *"inna dhāta al-dīn ʿinda Allāh al-ḥanīfiyya al-muslima lā al-yahūdiyya wa-lā al-naṣrāniyya
  [wa-lā al-majūsiyya]; man yaʿmal khayran fa-lan yukfarah"* — a gloss on the *dīn al-qayyima* of v 5.
- *"law anna li-bni Ādam wādiyan min māl la-btaghā ilayhi thāniyan …"* — the "valley of wealth" logion.

These are recorded as part of the report's matn (transmission), not as Quranic text; they belong to the
qiraʾāt-and-faḍāʾil literature of this surah. (Verified in the Arabic of Tirmidhī #3889, #3995.)

## 3. The al-Nasāʾī attestation — FLAGGED (not locatable in on-disk al-Mujtabā)

Ibn Kathīr (English, Q 98:1; `spa5k-tafsir-api/en-tafisr-ibn-kathir/98.json`) states: *"Al-Bukhari,
Muslim, At-Tirmidhi **and An-Nasa'i** all recorded this Hadith from Shu`bah."* A search of the on-disk
al-Nasāʾī (`the_9_books/nasai.json`, 5768 ḥadīth) for the Ubayy/*Lam yakun* recitation report returned
**zero hits**. The al-Nasāʾī recension on disk is **al-Mujtabā** (al-Sunan al-Ṣughrā); Ibn Kathīr's
al-Nasāʾī reference is most plausibly to **al-Sunan al-Kubrā** (which has a fuller Faḍāʾil/Tafsīr section),
not present in the on-disk 9-book set. **Flagged as a data-gap on the al-Nasāʾī number**, not as a denial
of Ibn Kathīr's report — the Bukhārī, Muslim, and Tirmidhī attestations are all verified above.

## 4. The weak Abū al-Dardāʾ faḍīla (recorded, graded bāṭil by Ibn al-ʿArabī)

al-Qurṭubī (*al-Jāmiʿ*, Q 98:1; `spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/98.json`) relates the report *"law
yaʿlamu al-nās mā fī (lam yakun) … la-ʿaṭṭalū al-ahl wa-l-māl fa-taʿallamūhā"* (attributed via Mālik ←
Yaḥyā b. Saʿīd ← Saʿīd b. al-Musayyab ← Abū al-Dardāʾ), and grades it explicitly: *"ḥadīth lā yaṣiḥḥ,"*
citing **Ibn al-ʿArabī**: *"ḥadīth bāṭil."* This is recorded as the **rejected** faḍīla of the surah,
contrasted by al-Qurṭubī with the *ṣaḥīḥ* Ubayy report. (Source: tafsīr file, not a 9-book ḥadīth number.)

## 5. Faḍāʾil scope note

Q 98's ḥadīth profile is the Ubayy recitation report (§1) — a *named-recitation* faḍīla rather than a
*virtue-of-reciting* (thawāb) report. The weak Abū al-Dardāʾ thawāb report (§4) is the only general
recitation-virtue claim and is rejected by the tradition. No additional Q-98-named faḍāʾil ḥadīth was found
in the on-disk 9-book set. **The absence is reported as a data-gap on the on-disk 9-book set**, not as an
absence-of-evidence claim (faḍāʾil sub-collections such as Ibn al-Ḍurays or al-Thaʿlabī's chains are
outside the on-disk set).

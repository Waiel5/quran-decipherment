# Q 98 al-Bayyina — Tafsīr Survey

Six classical commentaries surveyed from the on-disk corpus, all cited by scholar + work + passage. The
three recurring exegetical questions are: (1) the meaning of *munfakkīn* in v 1 (the "non-desisting"
state); (2) the qiraʾāt and morphology of *al-bariyya* in vv 6-7; and (3) the *abadan* asymmetry between
the punishment-verdict (v 6, no *abadan*) and the reward-verdict (v 8, with *abadan*). I have read the
Arabic of all six.

## 1. al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān* (on Q 98:1, 6, 7, 8)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-al-tabari/98.json`.

**v 1 (*munfakkīn*):** al-Ṭabarī reports the disagreement and adjudicates. The favored reading (Mujāhid,
Qatāda, Ibn Zayd): *munfakkīn* = *muntahīn* — "the disbelievers among the People of the Book and the
polytheists were not going to **desist** (from their disbelief) **until** *al-bayyina* — i.e. **this
Qurʾān** / the Messenger — comes to them." He cites Mujāhid (*"lam yakūnū li-yantahū ḥattā yatabayyana
lahum al-ḥaqq"*), Qatāda (*"muntahīn ʿammā hum fīhi … ay hādhā al-Qurʾān"*), and Ibn Zayd. He records a
minority reading ("they were not abandoning the description of Muḥammad in their Book until he was sent,
then they split over him") and rules the desist-reading the *awlā bi-l-ṣiḥḥa*.

**vv 6-7 (al-bariyya):** al-Ṭabarī glosses *sharr al-bariyya* = "the worst of **what Allāh created and
brought into being** (*man baraʾahu Allāh wa-khalaqahu*)," and gives the **qiraʾāt note**: *"the Arabs do
not hamza* al-bariyya*, and the reciters of the regions read it without hamza, except for something
reported from Nāfiʿ b. Abī Nuʿaym, who is said to have hamza-ed it (*al-barīʾa*), taking it from* min qabli
an nabraʾahā *[Q 57:22] as a* faʿīla *from* baraʾa." He notes those who drop the hamza do so for ease
(like *al-malak* and *yarā*). For v 7 he reports a Shīʿī-attributed gloss (Abū al-Jārūd ← Muḥammad b. ʿAlī):
*khayr al-bariyya* = *"anta yā ʿAlī wa-shīʿatuka."*

**v 8 (*abadan*):** he glosses *khālidīna fīhā abadan* = "remaining therein forever, never leaving, never
dying," and *radiya Allāhu ʿanhum* = "for their obeying Him in the world" / *wa-raḍū ʿanhu* = "for the
reward He gave them."

## 2. al-Zamakhsharī, *al-Kashshāf* (on Q 98:6-8)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/zamakhshari-kashshaf.openiti.raw.txt` lines 72523-72560.

al-Zamakhsharī treats the closing pericope. On **al-bariyya** he gives the morphological/qiraʾāt analysis:
the word is *al-bariyya* (the created beings), from *baraʾa* "to create"; *"bi-l-hamz, wa-l-qurrāʾ ʿalā
al-takhfīf"* — Nāfiʿ reads with hamza (*al-barīʾa*), the bulk of reciters drop it (as with *al-nabī*), and
*"the hamza-less form has become the settled usage, abandoning the original."* He notes the variant reading
**خيار البرية** (*khiyār al-bariyya*, "the choicest of creation," *khiyār* as plural of *khayr*, like
*jiyād / ṭiyāb*). His framing of vv 6-7 is the explicit two-pole contrast (*sharr* vs *khayr al-bariyya*).
The faḍīla "whoever recites *Lam yakun* will be with *khayr al-bariyya*" is associated with this surah in
the *al-Kashshāf* milieu.

## 3. al-Rāzī, *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* (on Q 98:5, 6, 8)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/razi-mafatih-al-ghayb.openiti.raw.txt`, Sūrat al-Bayyina
block (≈ lines 262440-262760).

al-Rāzī gives the surah a dense *masāʾil*-driven treatment.

**On v 5 (*dīn al-qayyima*):** he uses the verse in the *īmān*-definition debate — *"whoever argues that
īmān is the sum of statement + belief + action cites this verse,"* since the verse names worship +ṣalāt +
zakāt and then says *"wa-dhālika dīn al-qayyima."* He then poses the counter-question (could *wa-dhālika*
refer to *ikhlāṣ* alone?).

**On v 6 (*sharr al-bariyya*):** he opens the closing-verdict with several *suʾālāt*:
- **Why are *ahl al-kitāb* mentioned BEFORE the *mushrikūn*?** He gives three answers (the Prophet
  prioritized God's right over his own; the People of the Book's offense against the *Messenger* was
  greater since they had foreknowledge of his mission and denied it; etc.).
- **Why is *kafarū* (a verb) used of the People of the Book but *al-mushrikīn* (a noun-participle) of the
  polytheists?** Answer: *tanbīh* — the People of the Book were not disbelievers *from the start* (they
  affirmed the Tawrāt and Injīl and acknowledged Muḥammad's coming), then disbelieved *after* his mission;
  whereas the polytheists were born to idolatry.
- He notes the verse states two things of the disbelievers: *al-khulūd fī nār jahannam* and *annahum sharr
  al-khalq*.

**On v 8 (the reward-seal and the *abadan* expansion):** al-Rāzī treats v 8 under ten *masāʾil*. The first
frames the reward-pole in terms of God's overflowing *karam wa-raḥma* (generosity and mercy) — *"its reality
is pure generosity and mercy"* — i.e. the reward is rhetorically expanded relative to the punishment. He
treats *jannāt ʿadn* (the *ʿadn* = permanence/abiding), *tajrī min taḥtihā al-anhār* (flowing water "more
delicate than standing"), and devotes a masʾala to *radiya Allāhu ʿanhum wa-raḍū ʿanhu* (why the riḍā is
mutual and why the active name *Allāh* is used). This material grounds the project-noted **v6/v8 *abadan*
asymmetry** (punishment-pole without *abadan*, reward-pole with *abadan* + mutual riḍā): al-Rāzī reads the
reward-pole as the locus of the divine *karam/raḥma*.

## 4. al-Qurṭubī, *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān* (on Q 98:1)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/98.json`.

al-Qurṭubī's heading: *"tafsīr sūrat (lam yakun)"* — naming the surah by its opening words. He states:
- **Chronology**: *"makkiyya fī qawl Yaḥyā b. Sallām, wa-madaniyya fī qawl Ibn ʿAbbās wa-l-jumhūr"* —
  **Meccan** per Yaḥyā b. Sallām, **Medinan** per Ibn ʿAbbās and the majority.
- **Verse count**: *"wa-hiya tisʿ āyāt"* — **nine verses** (vs the Hafs-Kūfan 8; see
  `05-classical-claims-audit.md` Claim 2).
- **Faḍīla (weak)**: he relates the Abū al-Dardāʾ report *"law yaʿlamu al-nās mā fī (lam yakun) … la-ʿaṭṭalū
  al-ahl wa-l-māl fa-taʿallamūhā"* and explicitly grades it *"ḥadīth lā yaṣiḥḥ,"* citing **Ibn al-ʿArabī**
  who calls it *"ḥadīth bāṭil."*
- **The ṣaḥīḥ recitation report**: *"wa-innamā al-ḥadīth al-ṣaḥīḥ mā ruwiya ʿan Anas: anna al-Nabī ﷺ qāla
  li-Ubayy b. Kaʿb: inna Allāh amaranī an aqraʾa ʿalayka (lam yakun alladhīna kafarū) … fa-bakā,"* adding
  *"qultu: kharrajahu al-Bukhārī wa-Muslim."*
- **The fiqh point**: *"wa-fīhi min al-fiqh qirāʾat al-ʿālim ʿalā al-mutaʿallim"* — the legal lesson that a
  teacher may recite to a student (and the discussion of why the Prophet recited *to* Ubayy: to teach
  humility / because Ubayy was the quickest to capture the Prophet's exact wording).

## 5. Ibn Kathīr, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm* (on Q 98:1)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/en-tafisr-ibn-kathir/98.json` (English);
Arabic at `.../ar-tafsir-ibn-kathir/98.json`.

Ibn Kathīr opens: *"Which was revealed in Madina."* He leads with **the Ubayy recitation report from
Imām Aḥmad's Musnad** (Anas: the Prophet said to Ubayy, *"inna Allāh amaranī an aqraʾa ʿalayka …"*; Ubayy:
"He named me to you?"; the Prophet: "Yes"; so Ubayy wept), and notes: *"Al-Bukhari, Muslim, At-Tirmidhi
**and An-Nasa'i** all recorded this Hadith from Shu`bah."* (NB: the al-Nasāʾī attestation is via Ibn Kathīr;
the specific Nasāʾī number is NOT locatable in the on-disk 9-book *al-Mujtabā* — flagged in
`04-hadith-corpus.md`.) He glosses *munfakkīn* with **Mujāhid** ("they will not be finished until the truth
becomes clear to them") and **Qatāda** (same), and *ḥattā taʾtiyahum al-bayyina* = "meaning, this Qurʾān."
On v 6 (Arabic) he glosses *sharr al-bariyya* as the worst of *man baraʾahu Allāh wa-dharaʾahu*.

## 6. al-Baghawī, *Maʿālim al-Tanzīl* (on Q 98:6-7)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-al-baghawi/98.json`.

al-Baghawī gives the cleanest qiraʾāt statement of the two verdict-verses: *"thumma dhakara mā li-l-farīqayn
fa-qāla (inna alladhīna kafarū … ulāʾika hum sharr al-bariyya)."* On the reading: *"qaraʾa Nāfiʿ wa-Ibn
ʿĀmir 'al-barīʾa' bi-l-hamz fī al-ḥarfayn, li-annahu min qawlihim baraʾa Allāh al-khalq; wa-qaraʾa al-ākharūn
mushaddadan bi-ghayr hamz ka-l-dhurriyya, turika hamzuhā fī al-istiʿmāl."* — Nāfiʿ and Ibn ʿĀmir hamza both
occurrences (*al-barīʾa*); the rest read the doubled non-hamza form (*al-bariyya*), as with *al-dhurriyya*.
He sets v 7 (*khayr al-bariyya*) directly against v 6 as the two-pole contrast.

## 7. Points of agreement / disagreement

|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|

| Verse count | 8 (implicit) | — | — | **9 (*tisʿ āyāt*)** | — | — |
| v8 *abadan* / reward | forever; obey→reward | — | **karam/raḥma expansion** | — | — | — |
| Recitation faḍīla | — | *khayr al-bariyya* reward | — | ṣaḥīḥ Ubayy (Bukhārī+Muslim); weak Abū al-Dardāʾ | Ubayy (Aḥmad + 4 books) | — |

The defining shared exegetical anchor is the **Ubayy b. Kaʿb recitation report** (al-Qurṭubī, Ibn Kathīr;
the source of the surah's *faḍīla* status). The two qiraʾāt-points (al-bariyya hamza/non-hamza; the *khiyār
al-bariyya* variant) are the philological fault-line. al-Qurṭubī alone records the **Meccan** chronology
qawl and the **9-āya** count — both audited in `05-classical-claims-audit.md`.

## 8. Honest limits

- al-Rāzī and al-Zamakhsharī are read from full OpenITI raw concatenations, not clean per-surah extractions;
  the Sūrat-al-Bayyina blocks were located by section markers (`[سورة البينة (98)`, *sharr al-bariyya*) and
  read in context. al-Rāzī's masʾala-numbering on v 8 is reported as it appears; the project-noted *abadan*
  asymmetry maps onto his v8 *karam/raḥma* masʾala rather than a single labelled "masʾala 4."
- The `ar-tafseer-tanwir-al-miqbas/98.json` file at v 1 contains an extended discussion citing al-Fakhr
  (al-Rāzī) on al-Wāḥidī's *al-Basīṭ* about the syntactic difficulty of vv 1-4; it is not the terse
  Ibn-ʿAbbās *Tanwīr* and is not relied on here to avoid a mis-attribution.
- al-Suyūṭī's *al-Durr al-manthūr* on Q 98 is on disk in the OpenITI raw but not surveyed in depth here.

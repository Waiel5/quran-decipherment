# Q 98 al-Bayyina — Content Analysis

Arabic verse text from `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/quran-text/quran-no-tashkeel.json` (Q 98, 8 verses).
Descriptive content analysis; numerical/structural claims are cross-referenced to `01-empirical-profile.md`
and `06-novel-findings.md`.

## 1. Verse-by-verse summary

**v 1** — لم يكن الذين كفروا من أهل الكتاب والمشركين منفكين حتى تأتيهم البينة
"Those who disbelieved among the People of the Book and the polytheists were not to **desist**
(*munfakkīn*) **until the Clear Proof (*al-bayyina*) came to them**." A single long negative-declarative
opening sentence (no muqaṭṭaʿāt, no *qul*, no *sabbaḥa*). The two disbeliever-groups (ahl al-kitāb +
mushrikūn) are named together; the suspended state (*munfakkīn*) is resolved only by the arrival of
*al-bayyina*. The mufassirūn gloss *munfakkīn* = "not desisting / not finished" (al-Ṭabarī, Ibn Kathīr).

**v 2** — رسول من الله يتلو صحفا مطهرة
The **definition** of the Clear Proof: "A Messenger from Allāh reciting **purified scrolls**
(*ṣuḥuf muṭahhara*)." The *bayyina* of v 1 is apposed to a Messenger + recited revelation.

**v 3** — فيها كتب قيمة
"In which are **upright writings** (*kutub qayyima*)." The scrolls contain *kutub qayyima* — the first of
the surah's *q-w-m / qayyima* triad (cf. *dīn al-qayyima* v 5; alt-title *al-Qayyima*).

**v 4** — وما تفرق الذين أوتوا الكتاب إلا من بعد ما جاءتهم البينة
"And those given the Scripture did not **split** (*tafarraqa*) except **after** the Clear Proof had come to
them." The schism is post-proof: division came not from ignorance but from rejection after evidence. The
title-word *al-bayyina* recurs (its 2nd and final attestation in this surah).

**v 5** — وما أمروا إلا ليعبدوا الله مخلصين له الدين حنفاء ويقيموا الصلاة ويؤتوا الزكاة ۚ وذلك دين القيمة
"They were ordered **only** to worship Allāh, **sincere** (*mukhliṣīn*) to Him in religion, **upright**
(*ḥunafāʾ*), and to establish the ṣalāt and give the zakāt — and **that is the upright religion**
(*dīn al-qayyima*)." The unifying creed: the whole demand reduces to sincere monotheism + the two pillars.
*dīn al-qayyima* seals the verse (2nd of the *qayyima* triad).

**v 6** — إن الذين كفروا من أهل الكتاب والمشركين في نار جهنم خالدين فيها ۚ أولئك هم شر البرية
"Indeed those who disbelieved among the People of the Book and the polytheists are in the fire of Hell,
**abiding therein** (*khālidīna fīhā*) — **those are the WORST of creation** (*sharr al-bariyya*)." The
disbelievers' verdict. Note *khālidīna fīhā* **without** *abadan* (contrast v 8). Closes on the first
al-bariyya attestation. al-Ṭabarī, Ibn Kathīr: *sharr al-bariyya* = "the worst of what Allāh created
(*baraʾahā wa-dharaʾahā*)."

**v 7** — إن الذين آمنوا وعملوا الصالحات أولئك هم خير البرية
"Indeed those who believed and did righteous deeds — **those are the BEST of creation** (*khayr
al-bariyya*)." The believers' verdict. The verse-tail mirrors v 6 word-for-word except the single antonym
pivot **sharr → khayr** (Q098-F-01 Arm C, corpus-UNIQUE minimal-pair muqābala). 2nd and final al-bariyya.

**v 8** — جزاؤهم عند ربهم جنات عدن تجري من تحتها الأنهار خالدين فيها أبدا ۖ رضي الله عنهم ورضوا عنه ۚ ذلك لمن خشي ربه
The **seal / reward**: "Their reward with their Lord is Gardens of Eden beneath which rivers flow,
**abiding therein FOREVER** (*khālidīna fīhā abadan*); **Allāh is pleased with them and they are pleased
with Him** — that is for whoever **fears his Lord** (*khashiya rabbah*)." The longest verse. Note
*khālidīna fīhā* **with** *abadan* here — the asymmetry with v 6 (no *abadan*) is exegetically remarked
(al-Rāzī: the reward-pole is rhetorically expanded; "mercy/generosity"). The mutual-riḍā clause is the
emotional climax.

## 2. Thematic blocks

| Block | Verses | Register | Theme |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **A. The non-desisting thesis** | 1 | declarative | the disbelievers (ahl al-kitāb + mushrikūn) were not to desist until *al-bayyina* came |
| **B. Definition of the Clear Proof** | 2-3 | descriptive | a Messenger reciting purified scrolls (*ṣuḥuf muṭahhara*) containing upright writings (*kutub qayyima*) |
| **C. The post-proof schism & the creed** | 4-5 | admonitory/legal | division came only AFTER the proof; the demand was only sincere worship + ṣalāt + zakāt = *dīn al-qayyima* |
| **D. The dual verdict + the reward-seal** | 6-8 | eschatological | disbelievers → Hell, *sharr al-bariyya* (6); believers → *khayr al-bariyya* (7); the Gardens-of-Eden reward + mutual riḍā (8) |

The macro-structure is a **proof → response → verdict** arc: revelation arrives (A-B), it provokes a
schism among those who had the Scripture (C), and the two responses are sealed with opposed eschatological
verdicts capped by the believers' reward (D). The surah is a single tightly-sequenced pericope — consistent
with its low FR mean (0.8214) and above-median local_cohesion (1.7130, `01-empirical-profile.md`).

## 3. Content register

**Mixed creedal-eschatological**: theological-historical (the People-of-the-Book's suspended state and
post-proof schism, vv 1-4), creedal-legal (the *dīn al-qayyima* reduction to tawḥīd + ṣalāt + zakāt, v 5),
and eschatological-verdictive (the dual al-bariyya verdict + the Gardens-of-Eden seal, vv 6-8). There is no
narrative-episode block; the surah is doctrinal-declarative throughout. Its fire-imagery (v 6) is verdictive
rather than apocalyptic-descriptive.

## 4. Vocabulary distinctness

- **Title-root b-y-n (al-bayyina)**: appears twice (v 1, v 4) as the eponymous word. Despite being the
  surah's NAME, Q 98 is **NOT** the corpus density-peak of byn (raw-count rank 59/71; the exact surface
  form البينة is led by Q 11 with على بينة ×4) — Q098-F-01 Arm A, a title-density-independence instance.
- **al-bariyya (البرية, root b-r-ʾ "to create")**: the surah's rhyme-word in vv 6-7 is a **corpus
  hapax-pair** — it occurs in exactly 2 positions in the entire Quran, both in Q 98 (Q098-F-01 Arm B).
  Q 98 owns the word and deploys it twice in immediate antithetical adjacency.
- **The *q-w-m / qayyima* triad**: *kutub qayyima* (v 3), *dīn al-qayyima* (v 5), and the alternative
  surah-title *al-Qayyima* (al-Qurṭubī). The upright-religion motif recurs as a structural thread (queued as
  Q098-F-02).
- **The mukhliṣīn / ḥunafāʾ creedal pair** (v 5): the sincerity (*ikhlāṣ*) + primordial-uprightness
  (*ḥanīfiyya*) descriptors anchor the *dīn al-qayyima* definition. Tirmidhī #3889/#3995 preserve a recited
  gloss on this very verse: *"inna dhāta al-dīn ʿinda Allāh al-ḥanīfiyya al-muslima …"* (see
  `04-hadith-corpus.md`).
- **The *khālidīna fīhā* asymmetry**: v 6 (punishment) lacks *abadan*; v 8 (reward) carries *abadan* — a
  rhetorical expansion of the reward-pole, exegetically remarked (al-Rāzī, `03-tafsir-survey.md`).

## 5. Repetition patterns

- **Perfect monorhyme**: all 8 fāṣila end on the ه/ة (-iyya / -a(h)) grapheme — *al-bayyina, muṭahhara,
  qayyima, al-bayyina, al-qayyima, al-bariyya, al-bariyya, rabbah* — rhyme entropy **0.0 nats**
  (`h-new-750.json`; `01-empirical-profile.md` §3-4).
- **The frame *inna alladhīna … min ahl al-kitābi wa-l-mushrikīn …***: opens both v 1 (negated: *lam
  yakun alladhīna kafarū …*) and v 6 (affirmed: *inna alladhīna kafarū …*) — the disbeliever-frame
  brackets the surah, the second time delivering the Hell-verdict.
- **The verdict-frame *ulāʾika hum [X] al-bariyya***: v 6 (*sharr*) and v 7 (*khayr*) — the held-constant
  frame with the single antonym pivot is the surah's signature figure (Q098-F-01 Arm C).
- **al-bayyina (v 1, v 4)** and ***qayyima* (v 3, v 5)** each recur once, structuring blocks A-C.

## 6. Cross-surah content references

- **Q 98:8 (Gardens of Eden + abadan + mutual riḍā)** shares the *jannāt ʿadn tajrī min taḥtihā al-anhār
  khālidīna fīhā abadan* eschatological formula with numerous reward-passages (e.g. the *radiya Allāhu
  ʿanhum wa-raḍū ʿanhu* clause also closes Q 5:119, Q 9:100, Q 58:22) — Q 98:8 is the short-surah
  instantiation of this stock reward-frame.
- **Q 97 al-Qadr (mushaf-prior)** is thematically linked: al-Qadr describes the Night the revelation
  descended; al-Bayyina describes the Messenger bringing the *ṣuḥuf muṭahhara* — the low-cost Q 97→Q 98
  seam (rank 27/113, `01-empirical-profile.md` §5) has a content correlate (scripture-descent → scripture-
  bearer).
- **The *ḥanīfiyya* / *dīn al-qayyima* creed (v 5)** cross-refers to the millat Ibrāhīm ḥanīfan motif
  (Q 2:135, Q 3:67, Q 16:120-123) — the upright primordial monotheism the surah names as the only thing
  commanded.
- **al-bariyya (vv 6-7)** is unique to Q 98; the synonymous *al-khalq / al-bashar / al-ʿālamīn* "best/worst
  of X" formulae appear elsewhere, but the specific *bariyya* lexeme is Q 98-exclusive (Arm B).

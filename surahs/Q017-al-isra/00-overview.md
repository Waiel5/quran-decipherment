## 2. Classical names

Three names are attested:

1. **al-Isrāʾ** (الإسراء) — "the Night Journey"; modern standard name; from the *asrā* verb in v.1.
2. **Banū Isrāʾīl / Banī Isrāʾīl** (بنو/بني إسرائيل) — "the Children of Israel"; the classical name used by the Companions and by al-Bukhārī (chapter headings of Kitāb al-tafsīr in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī use this name). Witnessed in al-Bukhārī ḥadīth **#4502** (Adam ← Shuʿba ← Abī Isḥāq ← ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Yazīd ← Ibn Masʿūd): *"fī Banī Isrāʾīla wa-l-Kahfi wa-Maryama: innahunna mina al-ʿitāqi al-uwali wa-hunna min tilādī"* — "Banī Isrāʾīl, al-Kahf, and Maryam are among my earliest learnt and my old property." Also #4533 (extending list to include Ṭāhā and al-Anbiyāʾ) and #4787.
3. **Sūrat Subḥān** (سورة سبحان) — "the Subḥān surah"; rare; cited by Ibn Kathīr at the head of his Q 17 commentary (`data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/ibn-kathir-openiti-Q017.txt`).

## 3. Opening formula

**Verb-noun (maṣdar) glorification**: *Subḥāna alladhī…* ("Glorified be He who…"). This is the **unique** opening of its type in the Qurʾān — see Q017-F-02 (vindicated): no other surah opens with the *Subḥāna* maṣdar. Q 17 is sometimes counted with the **musabbiḥāt** (Q 17, 57, 59, 61, 62, 64, 87 — surahs opening with a س-ب-ح-rooted word), and al-Suyūṭī's *al-Itqān* (`data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/suyuti-itqan.openiti.raw.txt`) refers to the musabbiḥāt as **"ʿarāʾis al-Qurʾān"** (the brides of the Qurʾān). Within this group, Q 17 is grammatically distinct: it is the only one using the **maṣdar form** *subḥāna* (verbal-noun, accusative); the other six split into *sabbaḥa* perfect (Q 57, 59, 61, 87) and *yusabbiḥu* imperfect (Q 62, 64). The *Subḥāna* form is direction-of-utterance-locked: it is uttered *to/of* God (acc. of glorification) rather than narrating creation's tasbīḥ.

NOT muqaṭṭaʿāt; NOT *qul*; NOT al-ḥamd. A verb-noun glorification anchoring to a singular salvation-history event (the *isrāʾ*).

## 7. Q 17 contains the maximal taḥaddī verse

Q 17:88 (*Qul la-ʾini ijtamaʿati al-insu wa-l-jinnu ʿalā an yaʾtū bi-mithli hādhā al-Qurʾāni lā yaʾtūna bi-mithlihi wa-law kāna baʿḍuhum li-baʿḍin ẓahīrā*) is the **strongest of the five Qurʾānic taḥaddī verses** — it challenges humans AND jinn together to bring the like of the entire Qurʾān (cf. weaker forms at Q 2:23 challenging humans alone with one *sūra*-like-it; Q 10:38, Q 11:13, Q 52:34). Q017-F-03 (VINDICATED): all 5 expected iʿjāz-related lemmas (mithl, ijtimāʿ, jinn, ins, ẓahīr) attested in the verse; **7 of 9** classical mufassirūn extracted (ibn-kathīr, ṭabarī, qurṭubī, rāzī, zamakhsharī, ṭabarsī, thaʿlabī) cite Q 17:88 substantively (≥ 200 chars). Only al-Biqāʿī's *Naẓm al-durar* and al-Suyūṭī's *al-Durr al-manthūr* show no substantive engagement in our extracts (likely a section-boundary artifact in the OpenITI raw — flagged for follow-up).

This places Q 17 at the **intersection** of structural-form-iʿjāz (qaṣīda monorhyme) and theological-content-iʿjāz (the maximal challenge). Q 17 is unusual: it is anti-iʿjāz on the *al-fawāṣil* (rhyme-variation) axis but pro-iʿjāz on the *taḥaddī* axis. The surah formally proclaims its own inimitability mid-text, while structurally embodying the most poetry-adjacent rhyme-form in the corpus.

## 8. Q 17 contains the founding Banī-Isrāʾīl narrative cluster

Per Q017-F-04 (VINDICATED, rank 4/114 by raw count, rank 5/114 by density):

- **Q 17:4** — *wa-qaḍaynā ilā Banī Isrāʾīla fī al-kitābi la-tufsidunna fī al-arḍi marratayni…* — the **two-corruptions** prophecy.
- **Q 17:101-104** — Moses with 9 signs, Pharaoh, the **dwelling of Banī Isrāʾīl in the land**.
- **Q 17:2** — Moses given the *kitāb* as guidance for Banī Isrāʾīl.
- **Q 17:107** — *qul āminū bihi aw lā tuʾminū inna alladhīna ūtū al-ʿilma min qablihi* — addressing those given knowledge before, i.e., the previous-revelation tradition.

Q 17 ranks higher in raw count than Q 7 al-Aʿrāf and many surahs traditionally associated with Israelite content. Combined with the **classical name** Banī Isrāʾīl, this empirically vindicates the early Companions' naming.

## 9. Quick content structure (full in `02-content-analysis.md`)

- vv. 1: **Isrāʾ verse** — Night Journey from Mecca to Jerusalem.
- vv. 2-8: **Banī Isrāʾīl two-corruptions cycle** + Moses-Tawrāt.
- vv. 9-22: this Qurʾān guides; warning to disbelievers; God's bounty + asymmetric eschatology.
- vv. 22-39: **the Decalogue-like ethical code of Q 17** — 14 commands: do not associate, parents-honor, kin-rights, no murder of children, no zinā, no murder, restrain orphan-property, fulfill measure, no following ignorance, no walking proud — closed by *kullu dhālika kāna sayyiʾuhu ʿinda rabbika makrūhā* (v.38).
- vv. 40-55: anti-shirk arguments; tasbīḥ of the seven heavens; angels do not have daughters.
- vv. 56-65: Iblīs's challenge; God's protection.
- vv. 66-72: shipwreck-recovery image; the Day of Resurrection.
- vv. 73-77: warnings to the Prophet against deviation.
- vv. 78-79: **ṣalāt institution + maqām maḥmūd** (the Praiseworthy Station = intercession; al-Tirmidhī #3221, #3232).
- vv. 80-87: prayer for righteousness; **truth comes / falsehood vanishes** (v.81); **the rūḥ verse** (v.85).
- vv. 88-89: **the maximal taḥaddī**.
- vv. 90-100: disbelievers' demands for signs; God's response.
- vv. 101-104: Moses, Pharaoh, the 9 signs, Israelite settlement.
- vv. 105-109: Qurʾān revealed in truth; *those given knowledge weep* (v.107-109).
- vv. 110: *al-asmāʾ al-ḥusnā* — call God by Allah or al-Raḥmān, both belong to Him.
- v.111: **closing āyat al-ʿizz** — *al-ḥamdu lillāhi alladhī lam yattakhidh waladan…* — the **Verse of Glory** (so named in al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān*, citing Aḥmad's *Musnad* via Muʿādh b. Anas). The closing tasbīḥ (*kabbirhu takbīrā*) cycles back to the *Subḥān* opening — a perfect framing.

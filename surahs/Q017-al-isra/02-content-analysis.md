## 2. The Decalogue-like ethical code (vv. 22-39)

This is one of the Qurʾān's most concentrated ethical units. 14 commands, framed by an opening *wa-qaḍā rabbuka* (your Lord has decreed) at v.23 and closing *kullu dhālika kāna sayyiʾuhu ʿinda rabbika makrūhā* (all of that is — its evil — hated before your Lord) at v.38, with v.39 reframing as *dhālika mimmā awḥā ilayka rabbuka mina al-ḥikmati* (that is from what your Lord has revealed to you of wisdom):

| # | Verse | Command |
|--:|:-:|:--|
| 1 | 22 | Do not place another god alongside Allāh |
| 2 | 23-24 | Worship none but Him; treat parents with excellence (esp. in old age); say *qul lahumā qawlan karīmā*; lower wing of humility |
| 3 | 25 | Be sincere; God knows what is in your soul |
| 4 | 26-27 | Give kin, the needy, the wayfarer their right; do not squander |
| 5 | 28-29 | If you must turn away from them (poverty), say a kind word; do not chain hand to neck nor extend it fully |
| 6 | 30 | God expands and constricts provision |
| 7 | 31 | Do not kill children for fear of poverty |
| 8 | 32 | Do not approach zinā |
| 9 | 33 | Do not kill the soul God has made sacred except by right |
| 10 | 34 | Do not approach orphan-property except in best way; fulfill covenant |
| 11 | 35 | Fulfill measure when measuring; weigh with the upright balance |
| 12 | 36 | Do not pursue what you have no knowledge of |
| 13 | 37 | Do not walk in the earth in arrogance |
| 14 | 38 | All of the above is hated before your Lord |

The **classical comparison to the Mosaic Decalogue**: al-Rāzī (`data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/razi-openiti-Q017.txt`), al-Qurṭubī (`qurtubi-openiti-Q017.txt`) explicitly note the parallel — and that v.39's closing *dhālika mimmā awḥā ilayka rabbuka mina al-ḥikmati* connects this list to the *ḥikma* (wisdom) given to all prophets, including Moses (and thus to the Banī-Isrāʾīl frame of v.4).

## 3. The opening-closing inclusio (Q 17:1 ↔ Q 17:111)

| Q 17:1 | Q 17:111 |
|:--|:--|
| **Subḥāna** alladhī asrā bi-ʿabdihi | wa-**kabbirhu takbīrā** |
| Glorification of God | Magnification of God |
| God-as-subject (acc. of glorification, accusative of *subḥāna*) | God-as-object (imperative *kabbirhu*) |
| ʿabd (the worshipper, the Prophet) | wa-lam yakun lahu walī mina al-dhulli (no patron-from-humbleness) |

The surah opens with *tasbīḥ* and closes with *takbīr* — the two paradigmatic acts of Islamic remembrance. **The form is a perfect inclusio**. Al-Biqāʿī's *Naẓm al-durar* (although our extract is short — 44k chars) explicitly identifies this kind of opening-closing parallelism as *munāsabat al-fawātiḥ wa-l-khawātīm* (correspondence of beginnings and ends).

The **āyat al-ʿizz** name for v.111 (al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān*, citing Aḥmad's *Musnad* via Muʿādh b. Anas: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/suyuti-itqan.openiti.raw.txt` line ~135709) anchors the closing as a tawḥīd-summary verse.

## 4. Content register

**Mixed Meccan-narrative-ethical-eschatological**:

- Narrative: ~30% (vv. 2-8 Banī Isrāʾīl, vv. 53-65 Iblīs, vv. 66-72 sea-rescue, vv. 90-100 demands, vv. 101-104 Moses-Pharaoh).
- Ethics/legal: ~25% (vv. 22-39 Decalogue + scattered).
- Creedal/tawḥīd: ~25% (vv. 40-52 anti-shirk, vv. 110-111 closing).
- Eschatological: ~10% (vv. 13-14 book-of-deeds, vv. 71-72 imam-call, fragments throughout).
- Address-to-Prophet: ~10% (vv. 73-79 warnings + ṣalāt institution).

This mixed register is consistent with a long Meccan surah; cf. Q 7, 10, 11, 12, 16 — all neighbors and FR-cluster members.

## 5. Repetition patterns and refrains

- **Frame phrases**: *Subḥān* opening (v.1) ↔ *takbīr* closing (v.111). Internal *tasbīḥ*: v.43 (*subḥānahu wa-taʿālā ʿammā yaqūlūn ʿuluwwan kabīrā*), v.44 (*tusabbiḥu lahu al-samāwātu*), v.108 (*subḥāna rabbinā*).
- **"This Qurʾān"** (*hādhā al-Qurʾān*): vv. 9, 41, 45, 46, 60, 78 (verb form), 82, 88, 89, 106 — recurring throughout, especially in vv. 88-89 (the taḥaddī and its summary).
- **"Sons" / "daughters" anti-shirk**: vv. 40, 57 (the worshipped-besides-God seek means to their Lord), v.111 (*lam yattakhidh waladā*) — closes the motif.
- **"Soul / nafs"**: scattered — v.13 (every man's deeds bound to his neck), v.14 (read your book), v.15 (whoever is guided is guided for himself), v.25 (your Lord knows what is in your souls), v.84 (each acts according to his shākila).

## 7. Cross-surah content references

Q 17 references and is referenced by:
- **Q 18 al-Kahf** (canonical neighbor): both Meccan; both alif-monorhyme; both in Ibn Masʿūd ʿitāq-al-uwal hadith.
- **Q 19 Maryam, Q 20 Ṭāhā, Q 21 al-Anbiyāʾ**: ʿitāq-al-uwal block-mates.
- **Q 53 al-Najm**: the *miʿrāj* (ascension) account. Q 17:1 mentions the *isrāʾ* (Mecca → Jerusalem); Q 53 narrates the next stage (Jerusalem → heavens). Classical tradition (Ibn Kathīr, Ṭabarī, Qurṭubī) treats Q 17:1 + Q 53:1-18 as one event.
- **Q 7 al-Aʿrāf, Q 26 al-Shuʿarāʾ**: Moses-Pharaoh narrative parallels (Q 17:101-104 is a compressed reprise).
- **Q 2 al-Baqara**: Banī-Isrāʾīl long Medinan narrative parallels Q 17's Meccan compressed form.

## 8. Asbāb al-nuzūl (occasions of revelation)

Per al-Wāḥidī (`data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/asbab-nuzul-wahidi-en-Q001.txt` is Q1 only; al-Wāḥidī Q17 is in `suyuti-durr-openiti-Q017.txt` and other extracts):

- **Q 17:73-77**: revealed when Quraysh tried to lure the Prophet to compromise with their idols (Ibn Kathīr cites Ibn ʿAbbās narrative).
- **Q 17:85** (the rūḥ verse): al-Tirmidhī ḥadīth #3224 — the Quraysh asked the Jews what to ask, the Jews said "ask him about the rūḥ".
- **Q 17:78** (the ṣalāt verse): grounds the five-prayer time-windows. Ḥadīth: al-Bukhārī #4511, Muslim #1368, al-Tirmidhī #3219, al-Nasāʾī #488, Ibn Mājah #404 all cite it for the *fajr-prayer-witnessed-by-angels* doctrine.
- **Q 17:79** (the maqām maḥmūd verse): al-Bukhārī #600, #1424, #4513, #7155, al-Tirmidhī #211, #3221, #3232, Abū Dāwūd #529, Ibn Mājah #456, #640 all cite it for the **intercession** doctrine.

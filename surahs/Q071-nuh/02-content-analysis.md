# Q 71 Nūḥ — Content Analysis

Arabic verse text quoted from `quran-text/quran-no-tashkeel.json` (id 71).
Register: **narrative-petition** (Noah's first-person report-and-prayer to his Lord,
framed by a third-person commissioning prologue). Content class: prophetic-narrative
with embedded duʿāʾ. The surah has NO legal, eschatological-scene, or creedal-formula
blocks; it is wholly the Nūḥ daʿwa-and-rejection story.

## 1. Verse-by-verse summary

**Movement I — the commissioning + warning (vv 1-4)**
- **v 1** *innā arsalnā Nūḥan ilā qawmihi an anindhir qawmaka min qabli an yaʾtiyahum ʿadhābun alīm* — third-person prologue: God sends Noah to warn before the painful punishment.
- **v 2** *qāla yā qawmi innī lakum nadhīrun mubīn* — Noah: "I am a clear warner."
- **v 3** *an iʿbudū Allāha wa-ttaqūhu wa-aṭīʿūn* — the threefold call: worship, taqwā, obey.
- **v 4** *yaghfir lakum min dhunūbikum wa-yuʾakhkhirkum ilā ajalin musamman* — forgiveness and respite to an appointed term; *inna ajala Allāhi idhā jāʾa lā yuʾakhkhar* — the term, once come, is not deferred.

**Movement II — the night/day complaint (vv 5-9)**
- **v 5** *qāla rabbi innī daʿawtu qawmī laylan wa-nahāran* — Noah turns to his Lord: "I called my people night and day." (al-Jalālayn: *laylan wa-nahāran* = "continuously, without interruption", `en-al-jalalayn/71/5.json`.)
- **v 6** *fa-lam yazidhum duʿāʾī illā firārā* — but his call only increased their flight.
- **v 7** *wa-innī kullamā daʿawtuhum li-taghfira lahum jaʿalū aṣābiʿahum fī ādhānihim wa-staghshaw thiyābahum wa-aṣarrū wa-stakbarū istikbārā* — they stop their ears with fingers, cover themselves with garments, persist, and grow arrogant.
- **v 8** *thumma innī daʿawtuhum jihārā* — then he called them aloud (publicly).
- **v 9** *thumma innī aʿlantu lahum wa-asrartu lahum isrārā* — proclaimed openly and confided privately. (vv 5-9 = the threefold-method complaint: private, public, proclaimed.)

**Movement III — the istighfār sermon + cosmological signs (vv 10-20)**
- **v 10** *fa-qultu istaghfirū rabbakum innahu kāna ghaffārā* — "Seek your Lord's forgiveness; He is ever-forgiving."
- **vv 11-12** the worldly rewards of repentance: rain in abundance (*midrārā*), wealth and sons, gardens, rivers.
- **v 13** *mā lakum lā tarjūna li-llāhi waqārā* — "Why do you not hope for dignity from God?" (al-Wāḥidī asbāb-style reflection: ingratitude despite their creation, `en-asbab-al-nuzul-by-al-wahidi/71/13.json`.)
- **v 14** *wa-qad khalaqakum aṭwārā* — "He created you in stages." (creation-in-phases; the embryological-stages reflection.)
- **vv 15-16** *alam taraw kayfa khalaqa Allāhu sabʿa samāwātin ṭibāqan* — the seven heavens in layers, the moon as light (*nūrā*), the sun as a lamp (*sirājā*).
- **v 17** *wa-Allāhu anbatakum mina al-arḍi nabātā* — God grew you from the earth like a growth.
- **v 18** *thumma yuʿīdukum fīhā wa-yukhrijukum ikhrājā* — returns you to it and brings you out again (resurrection).
- **vv 19-20** *wa-Allāhu jaʿala lakumu al-arḍa bisāṭā* — the earth a spread carpet, with broad paths (*subulan fijājā*).

**Movement IV — the verdict, the idols, the drowning, the closing prayer (vv 21-28)**
- **v 21** *qāla Nūḥun rabbi innahum ʿaṣawnī wa-ttabaʿū man lam yazidhu māluhu wa-waladuhu illā khasārā* — Noah reports their disobedience; they followed those whose wealth and children only increased them in loss.
- **v 22** *wa-makarū makran kubbārā* — "they plotted a mighty plot."
- **v 23** *wa-qālū lā tadharunna ālihatakum wa-lā tadharunna Waddan wa-lā Suwāʿan wa-lā Yaghūtha wa-Yaʿūqa wa-Nasran* — "Do not abandon your gods — Wadd, Suwāʿ, Yaghūth, Yaʿūq, Nasr." (The corpus-unique five-idol enumeration; see Q071-F-02.)
- **v 24** *wa-qad aḍallū kathīran wa-lā tazidi al-ẓālimīna illā ḍalālā* — they led many astray; "increase the wrongdoers only in error."
- **v 25** *mimmā khaṭīʾātihim ughriqū fa-udkhilū nāran fa-lam yajidū lahum min dūni Allāhi anṣārā* — for their sins they were drowned, then made to enter a Fire; no helpers besides God.
- **v 26** *wa-qāla Nūḥun rabbi lā tadhar ʿalā al-arḍi mina al-kāfirīna dayyārā* — "Leave not one disbeliever-dweller on earth." (al-Jalālayn: *dayyār* = "one who inhabits a dwelling — not one", `en-al-jalalayn/71/26.json`.)
- **v 27** *innaka in tadharhum yuḍillū ʿibādaka wa-lā yalidū illā fājiran kaffārā* — if left, they would mislead Your servants and beget only the wicked and ungrateful.
- **v 28** *rabbi ighfir lī wa-li-wālidayya wa-li-man dakhala baytiya muʾminan wa-li-l-muʾminīna wa-l-muʾmināti wa-lā tazidi al-ẓālimīna illā tabārā* — the closing prayer: forgiveness for himself, his parents, and the believers; ruin for the wrongdoers. (al-Jalālayn: *baytī* = "my house / my place of worship", `en-al-jalalayn/71/28.json`.)

## 2. Thematic / structural observations

1. **The "by night and day" / threefold-method spine (vv 5-9).** The surah's
   rhetorical engine is the escalation of Noah's calling: continuous (laylan
   wa-nahāran), then public (jihārā), then both proclaimed and confided (aʿlantu …
   asrartu). This is the night/day rhetorical structure flagged in the task hint as
   an alternative test axis to the centroid hypothesis.

2. **The double-imprecation seal (vv 26-28).** The closing movement pairs an
   imprecatory prayer (*lā tadhar … dayyārā* / *lā tazidi al-ẓālimīna illā tabārā*)
   with a forgiveness prayer (*rabbi ighfir lī wa-li-wālidayya wa-li-l-muʾminīn*).
   Three of the last four verses (26, 27, 28) are first-person Noah-duʿāʾ — the
   densest petition-cluster in the surah and the basis of the queued Q071-F-05.

3. **The cosmological-signs digression (vv 15-20) is the surah's PRIVATE
   vocabulary.** Heavens / moon / sun / earth / paths (samāwāt, qamar, shams, arḍ,
   subul) and the embryology of vv 14, 17-18 are NOT part of the shared Nūḥ-cycle
   flood-lexicon. This block is why Q 71 is lexically peripheral to its own cycle
   (Q071-F-01): a large slice of its roots are creation-sign roots that the short
   cross-surah retellings (which jump straight to ark-and-flood) never use.

4. **No ark, no deliverance.** Q 71 narrates the drowning (*ughriqū*, root `grq`) but
   never the ark (`flk`) or the saving of the believers (`njw`) — precisely the two
   roots that most tightly bind the short retellings together (H-NEW-2260 shared
   sets). The dedicated surah tells the daʿwa-and-judgement, not the rescue.

## 3. Repetition patterns

- **rabbi (vocative) ×4** at vv 5, 21, 26, 28 — the petition frame markers.
- **lā tazidi al-ẓālimīna illā …** twice (v 24 *ḍalālā*, v 28 *tabārā*) — an
  imprecatory refrain bracketing the idol/drowning block.
- **lā tadharunna … wa-lā tadharunna** (v 23) and **lā tadhar … dayyārā** (v 26) —
  the root `*r` (ذر, "leave/abandon") used by the idolaters (keep your gods) and by
  Noah (leave no disbeliever) in pointed contrast.
- **alif-monorhyme** across 24/28 verses (the …-ā(ran) cadence) — the surah's
  dominant phonological pattern (`h-new-750.json`).

## 4. Cross-surah content references

Q 71 is the longest member of the corpus's Nūḥ pericope cycle (H-NEW-2260):
Q 7:59-64 (al-Aʿrāf), Q 11:25-49 (Hūd), Q 23:23-30 (al-Muʾminūn), Q 26:105-122
(al-Shuʿarāʾ), Q 54:9-17 (al-Qamar), Q 71 (this surah). Ibn Kathīr at Q 71:23 cross-
cites Q 34:33 (*makru al-layli wa-l-nahār*) for the night/day-plotting parallel
(`en-tafisr-ibn-kathir/71/23.json`). The five idols (v 23) are cross-referenced in
the hadith tradition to the post-Flood Arabian tribes (Bukhārī, Ibn ʿAbbās chain —
see `04-hadith-corpus.md`).

# Q 71 Nūḥ — Tafsīr Survey

Six classical commentaries surveyed, each cited by scholar + work + on-disk passage.
Source root: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/`.

## 1. al-Ṭabarī — *Jāmiʿ al-bayān ʿan taʾwīl āy al-Qurʾān*

- **On v 14 *aṭwāran* (creation in stages)** (`ar-tafsir-al-tabari/71/14.json`):
  *"khalaqakum ḥālan baʿda ḥāl, ṭawran nuṭfatan wa-ṭawran ʿalaqatan wa-ṭawran
  muḍghatan"* — created you state after state: sperm-drop, then clinging-clot, then
  lump of flesh. Chains: ʿAlī ← Abū Ṣāliḥ ← Muʿāwiya ← ʿAlī ← Ibn ʿAbbās; and
  Mujāhid ("from dust, then a drop, then a clot"). al-Ṭabarī reads the surah's
  embryology as the *aṭwār* of human formation.
- **On v 23 (the five idols)** (`ar-tafsir-al-tabari/71/23.json`): the five were
  *"nafaran min banī Ādam"* — men of Adam's progeny who were *"qawman ṣāliḥīn"*
  (righteous men) with followers who imitated them; when they died, their followers
  made images of them "so it would be more inspiring to worship", and a later
  generation, prompted by Iblīs, worshipped the images outright. Chains: Muḥammad b.
  Qays; ʿIkrima ("between Adam and Noah were ten generations / *qurūn*").

## 2. al-Qurṭubī — *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān*

- **Surah intro** (`ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/71/1.json`): *"Sūrat Nūḥ — makkiyya, wa-hiya
  thamānin wa-ʿishrūna āya"* — Meccan, 28 verses (the canonical verse-count anchor).
- **Nūḥ as the first messenger**: cites Qatāda ← Ibn ʿAbbās ← the Prophet: *"awwalu
  rasūlin ursila Nūḥ, wa-ursila ilā jamīʿi ahli al-arḍ"* — the first messenger sent,
  to all the people of the earth; therefore when they disbelieved God drowned all the
  earth's people. Gives Noah's genealogy (Nūḥ b. Lāmak b. Mutawshalikh b. Akhnūkh =
  Idrīs …) and the disputed age at commissioning (50 / 40 / 350 years).

## 3. Ibn Kathīr — *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm*

- **Surah header** (`en-tafisr-ibn-kathir/71/1.json`): "Which was revealed in Makkah";
  Noah sent to warn before the punishment; if they repent and turn, the punishment is
  lifted.
- **On vv 5-9** (`en-tafisr-ibn-kathir/71/5.json`): Noah "complained to his Lord about
  the response he received from his people, and how he was patient with them for this
  long period of time — which was nine hundred and fifty years."
- **On v 23 (the idols)** (`en-tafisr-ibn-kathir/71/23.json`): quotes al-Bukhārī ←
  Ibn ʿAbbās with the full tribal attribution — Wadd → Kalb at Dawmat al-Jandal;
  Suwāʿ → Hudhayl; Yaghūth → Murād, then Banū Ghuṭayf at al-Juruf near Sabaʾ; Yaʿūq →
  Hamdān; Nasr → Ḥimyar (Dhū al-Kalāʿ). "These idols were all named after righteous
  men from the people of Nūḥ"; after they died Shayṭān inspired statues, later
  worshipped once knowledge was lost. Cross-cites Q 34:33 for the *makr al-layl
  wa-l-nahār* (plotting by night and day) parallel.

## 4. al-Baghawī — *Maʿālim al-tanzīl*

- **On v 23** (`ar-tafsir-al-baghawi/71/23.json`): records the **qirāʾāt variant** —
  *"qaraʾa ahlu al-Madīna bi-ḍammi al-wāw"* (Wudd) *"wa-l-bāqūn bi-fatḥihā"* (Wadd):
  the Madinan readers read *Wudd*, the rest *Wadd*. (A rules-tuple-relevant orthographic
  variant; the no-tashkeel skeleton ودا is identical either way.) Etiology via Muḥammad
  b. Kaʿb: the five were righteous men between Adam and Noah; Iblīs prompted their
  followers to make images, and a later generation worshipped them — "the beginning of
  idol-worship was from this."

## 5. al-Jalālayn — *Tafsīr al-Jalālayn*

- **v 5** (`en-al-jalalayn/71/5.json`): *laylan wa-nahāran* = "continuously, without
  interruption."
- **v 23** (`en-al-jalalayn/71/23.json`): the five "being the names of their idols";
  notes the Wadd/Wudd reading.
- **v 26** (`en-al-jalalayn/71/26.json`): *dayyār* = "one who inhabits a dwelling
  (dār)" — "not one."
- **v 28** (`en-al-jalalayn/71/28.json`): *baytī* = "my house, my dwelling, or my place
  of worship"; the closing imprecation *lā tazidi al-ẓālimīna illā tabārā* = "in ruin,
  in destruction — and thus they were destroyed."

## 6. Tafsīr Ibn ʿAbbās (attr.) / al-Wāḥidī *Asbāb al-nuzūl*

- **Ibn ʿAbbās attr.** (`en-tafsir-ibn-abbas/71/1.json`): "Warn your people from
  Allah's wrath and punishment ere the painful doom come unto them — *in the form of
  drowning*." (Reads the v-1 *ʿadhāb alīm* proleptically as the Flood.)
- **al-Wāḥidī-tradition on v 13** (`en-asbab-al-nuzul-by-al-wahidi/71/13.json`): a
  homiletic expansion of *mā lakum lā tarjūna li-llāhi waqārā* + *khalaqakum aṭwārā* —
  the ingratitude of those reminded of the stages of their own creation
  (sperm-drop → blood → flesh → bone → spirit), cross-cited to Q 77:21 and Q 95:4.

## 7. Points of consensus and disagreement

| Point | Position(s) | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Type / verse count | Meccan, 28 verses (unanimous) | al-Qurṭubī, Ibn Kathīr |
| The five idols' origin | Righteous men of Noah's people → statues → idols (unanimous etiology) | al-Ṭabarī, al-Baghawī, Ibn Kathīr (← Bukhārī/Ibn ʿAbbās) |
| Wadd reading | *Wadd* (majority) vs *Wudd* (Madinan) | al-Baghawī, al-Jalālayn |
| Noah's age at call | 50 / 40 / 350 years (disputed) | al-Qurṭubī |
| Noah as first messenger | Yes (Qatāda ← Ibn ʿAbbās ← Prophet) | al-Qurṭubī |
| v 14 *aṭwāran* | Embryological stages | al-Ṭabarī, al-Wāḥidī-tradition |

## 8. Which positions have empirical correlates

- **The five-idol cluster** (al-Ṭabarī/al-Baghawī/Ibn Kathīr): empirically, 4 of the
  5 names are corpus-strict orthographic singletons co-located at Q 71:23, and Wadd is
  a contextual-singleton-deity (also Q 19:96) — exactly matching the classical
  treatment of Wadd as a special case (the *Wudd*/*Wadd* variant + its homography with
  "affection"). See `06-novel-findings.md` Q071-F-02.
- **Takrār al-qaṣaṣ** (al-Suyūṭī *Itqān* nawʿ 63; al-Zarkashī *Burhān* on *takrār
  al-qiṣaṣ*): the Nūḥ story's recurrence across surahs is empirically the H-NEW-2260
  pericope cycle. The classical observation that retellings vary is borne out: Q 71
  (the dedicated surah) is the LEAST-central member lexically (Q071-F-01 NULL) — its
  cosmological-signs and idol-list material is its own, not the shared flood-core.

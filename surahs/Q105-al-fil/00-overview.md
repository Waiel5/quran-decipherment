## 2. Classical names

- **al-Fīl** (الفيل) — "The Elephant"; from v.1 *aṣḥābi l-fīl*.
- Sometimes paired with Q 106 in classical recitation manuals as a single liturgical unit (the "Quraysh-and-Fīl" tradition; see §3 of `Q106-quraysh/00-overview.md`).

## 3. Opening formula

Q 105 opens with **a-lam tara kayfa faʿala rabbuka bi-X** ("Have you not seen how your Lord dealt with X?") — the rhetorical-question / divine-action recall form. The same formula attests at Q 89:6 (*a-lam tara kayfa faʿala rabbuka bi-ʿĀd*), echoing the same Lord-act-against-rebels pattern. Direct *a-lam tara* attestations: Q 14:24, Q 14:28, Q 14:42, Q 14:46, Q 30:48 + others — Q 105 is the only surah where *a-lam tara* targets a recent **Arabian** historical event (the rest cite earlier prophets or natural signs).

## 7. Quick content structure

- v. 1: Rhetorical-question opener — *a-lam tara kayfa faʿala rabbuka bi-aṣḥābi l-fīl* ("Have you not seen how your Lord dealt with the People of the Elephant?")
- v. 2: Frustration of design — *a-lam yajʿal kaydahum fī taḍlīl* ("Did He not bring their plot to ruin?")
- v. 3: Bird-host dispatch — *wa-arsala ʿalayhim ṭayran abābīl* ("And He sent against them flights of birds")
- v. 4: Stone-pellet weapon — *tarmīhim bi-ḥijāratin min sijjīl* ("Pelting them with stones of *sijjīl*")
- v. 5: Final image — *fa-jaʿalahum ka-ʿaṣfin maʾkūl* ("And made them like devoured straw").

5-verse compressed historical-prophecy: **v.1 setup → v.2 Lord-action → v.3-4 the divine instrument → v.5 the result**.

## 8. Why this surah is structurally distinctive

Three signature facts:

1. **fyl is a corpus hapax-class root**: fyl ("elephant") attests **only in Q 105:1** (1 surah, 1 occurrence). The surah is named for a corpus-monopoly root. Compare also: ESf (ʿaṣf "straw/chaff") attests 7 times across 6 surahs (Q 105:5 is one of 6); Abl (abābīl "in flocks") attests 3 times across 3 surahs but is functionally a hapax-noun-form here. The Q 105 vocabulary block (fyl + Abl + sjl + ESf-as-Hell-image) is statistically rare-collocation-rich.

2. **Q 105 → Q 106 is one of 13 clamped-zero seams**: H-NEW-720 records Q 105 → Q 106 with delta_raw = 0.000 exactly — meaning the canonical mushaf adjacency at this seam pays NO TSP-cost relative to a hypothetical optimal re-ordering. This is direct empirical support for the classical paired-surah claim attributed to Ubayy b. Kaʿb (see §3 below). Compare Q 8 → Q 9 with delta = 0.061 rank 58/113 — mushaf-acceptable but NOT clamped-zero. The Q 105+106 unity is empirically MUCH STRONGER on the TSP-residual axis than the Q 8+9 unity classical claim.

3. **Year-of-the-Elephant historical anchor (~570 CE)**: Q 105 references the Abraha-of-Yemen failed attack on Mecca, dated by classical Arab historiography (Ibn Isḥāq via Ibn Hishām, *Sīra*; al-Ṭabarī, *Tārīkh*, year 570/571 CE = "year of the elephant" / *ʿām al-fīl*) and corroborated independently by the Aksumite-Sabaean epigraphic record (the Murayghan inscription of Abraha, dated 547/552 CE in Sabaean dating; the classical Arabic 570/571 CE date is now considered late by epigraphers but the historical event is firmly attested). This is one of the very few Quranic narratives anchored to a specific 6th-century CE event — see `05-classical-claims-audit.md` §2 + `findings/phase-b-hypotheses/h-new-105-abraha-historical.md` (queued).

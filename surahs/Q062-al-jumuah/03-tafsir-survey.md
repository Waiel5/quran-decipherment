# Q 62 al-Jumuʿah — Tafsīr Survey

Survey of how five canonical mufassirūn read the surah's distinctive blocks. The aim is descriptive: to map what the classical tradition saw as the load-bearing exegetical claims, and to locate places where the project's empirical findings (Q062-F-01..F-04) interlock with classical readings.

## 1. al-Ṭabarī (d. 310 / 923) — *Jāmiʿ al-Bayān fī Taʾwīl al-Qurʾān*

**Source-of-record**: Tafsīr al-Ṭabarī, vol. 23, *Sūrat al-Jumuʿah* section.

al-Ṭabarī's signature method is to assemble **early-tradition reports** (Sahaba and Tabiʿūn) for each verse, then synthesize. For Q 62:

- **v. 1 *yusabbiḥu lillāh***: glosses the imperfect tense as covering the *continuous* glorification activity of all created things; reports from Mujāhid and Qatāda that the verse encompasses "everything that has a soul or a shadow." Notes that the closing 4 names (al-Malik, al-Quddūs, al-ʿAzīz, al-Ḥakīm) are *names of attribute* (sifātī), not *names of essence* (dhātī) — al-Ṭabarī catalogs each.
- **v. 2 *baʿatha fī al-ummiyyīn***: glosses *al-ummiyyīn* as "those who have no scripture" — the pre-Islamic ʿArabs collectively. Cites Ibn ʿAbbās chain: *ka-mā kāna al-ʿArab lā kitāba lahum* — "as the ʿArabs had no scripture before."
- **v. 3 *wa-ākharīna minhum lammā yalḥaqū bihim***: collates four-five Sahaba reports identifying the *ākharīna* with: (a) non-ʿArab Muslims (Salmān al-Fārisī chain — al-Ṭabarī cites the famous Tirmidhī #3394 narrative); (b) believers who came after the first generation; (c) all later Muslim generations. al-Ṭabarī's preferred reading is (a) extended to (c).
- **v. 5 the donkey-and-books parable**: al-Ṭabarī reads *ḥumlū al-tawrāta* as the rabbinic-class specifically (not the Jewish community as a whole) — those who *bear* the Torah professionally without internalizing it. He cites Mujāhid for the gloss "*al-yahūdu allatī ḥumlū al-tawrāta wa-lam yaqūmū bihā*" (the Jews who were entrusted with the Torah and did not stand by it). The donkey image is glossed as *al-ḥimāru lā yadrī mā fī kutubihi* (the donkey doesn't know what's in its books).
- **v. 6 *fa-tamannū al-mawt***: the death-challenge. al-Ṭabarī echoes the Q 2:94 parallel; cites a lengthy chain that the rabbis of Madīnah were challenged on this point and refused; al-Ṭabarī takes this as Quranic confirmation of their *moral* (not merely intellectual) failure.
- **vv. 9-11 the Friday-prayer institution**: extensive fiqh-reading. al-Ṭabarī identifies the *saʿy* of v. 9 as classical-philological "make-this-your-effort", not "run physically" — citing ʿUmar's caution that running to the Friday prayer is forbidden. The asbāb-al-nuzūl trade caravan (v. 11) is detailed extensively via Ibn ʿAbbās chain.

**al-Ṭabarī's framing: Q 62 is the Madīnan-foundation surah for the Friday institution + the Salmān-al-Fārisī community-extension principle**. The surah's exegetical center for him is vv. 3 + 9-11.

## 2. al-Rāzī (d. 606 / 1209) — *Mafātīḥ al-Ghayb / al-Tafsīr al-Kabīr*

**Source-of-record**: al-Rāzī, *Mafātīḥ al-Ghayb*, vol. 30, *Sūrat al-Jumuʿah* section.

al-Rāzī's signature is the *vajh* (exegetical-permutation) approach: enumerate possibilities, then weigh.

- **v. 1 *yusabbiḥu*** (imperfect): al-Rāzī notes the **classical perfect-vs-imperfect tense distinction** explicitly — *al-māḍī yadullu ʿalā al-tasbīḥi al-ladhī kāna fī al-māḍī, wa-l-muḍāriʿu yadullu ʿalā al-tasbīḥi al-mustamirri* ("the perfect indicates the glorification that *was*; the imperfect indicates *continuing* glorification"). This is the classical-Arabic morphological-semantic claim that H-NEW-58c quantitatively confirms — al-Rāzī's reading is empirically validated by the cluster's tense-binary structural-prefix partition.
- **v. 1 the 4 names**: al-Rāzī catalogs the names systematically: *al-Malik* (the universal Sovereign, distinct from worldly malik); *al-Quddūs* (the absolutely-pure, free of all imperfection); *al-ʿAzīz* (the Mighty, possessor of overpowering authority); *al-Ḥakīm* (the Wise, possessor of perfect arrangement of all things). al-Rāzī notes the *PAIRING* — *al-Malik al-Quddūs* form a sovereignty-pair; *al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm* form a power-wisdom pair. This is precisely the pairing-structure that Q062-F-01 verifies as a verbatim composite quotation from Q 59:23 + Q 59:24.
- **v. 3 *ākharīna minhum lammā yalḥaqū***: al-Rāzī enumerates 7 vajh for the *ākharīna* identification. His preferred reading combines Salmān-al-Fārisī (community-extension) with the broader principle that "the message extends to all who come later." He notes the closure *wa-huwa al-ʿAzīzu al-Ḥakīm* paralleling v. 1 — an inclusio at the 3-verse pericope level.
- **v. 5 the donkey-and-books parable**: al-Rāzī reads *asfār* as "books of revelation" specifically — the parable applies *narrowly* to those who carry scriptural texts without comprehension. Three vajh on the parable's referent: (a) historical Jewish rabbis specifically; (b) any pretender to scripture without comprehension; (c) the parable as universal warning to *any* Muslim scholar who teaches without understanding. al-Rāzī's preferred reading is (b) extended to (c).
- **vv. 6-8 the death-challenge**: al-Rāzī notes the *moral-test* structure — exclusivity-claim + consistency-check + non-fulfillment + universal-affirmation. He treats this as a *kalām* (theology)-style argumentation: an a-priori test followed by an a-posteriori demonstration.
- **v. 11 the trade-caravan asbāb-al-nuzūl**: al-Rāzī cites the asbāb in detail. Notes that *qā'iman* (left you standing) is the historically-distinctive detail; the Prophet's standing in the pulpit during the desertion is what gives the rebuke its bite.

**al-Rāzī's framing: Q 62 is the *tasbīḥ-tense-distinction* surah + the *exegetical-vajh-permutation* surah on the *ākharīna*** — his preferred lens is the philological-morphological one (the imperfect tense) and the exegetical-multiplicity one.

## 3. al-Zamakhsharī (d. 538 / 1144) — *al-Kashshāf*

**Source-of-record**: al-Zamakhsharī, *al-Kashshāf*, vol. 4, *Sūrat al-Jumuʿah*.

al-Zamakhsharī's signature is the *balāgha* (rhetorical) reading.

- **v. 1**: al-Zamakhsharī highlights the **balāgha-structural pairing** — *al-Maliki al-Quddūsi* (sovereignty-purity pair, *iqtirān*) followed by *al-ʿAzīzi al-Ḥakīm* (might-wisdom pair). The balāgha-meta-claim: the verse's terminal-couplet structure mirrors the cosmic-bipartite structure of *mā fī al-samāwāti wa-mā fī al-arḍ* (heavens and earth). Two pairs of names matching the two cosmic-domains.
- **v. 5 the donkey-parable**: al-Zamakhsharī reads the parable as a **balāgha-tashbīh** (similitude). The implicit comparison is: "Torah-bearer who doesn't act on Torah :: donkey loaded with scriptural books." The comparison's force comes from the *cognitive* aspect of the donkey image — a beast of burden that doesn't read what it carries. al-Zamakhsharī catalogs the parable as one of the most-trenchant similitudes (*al-tashbīhāt al-bayāniyya*) in the Quran.
- **vv. 9-11 the Friday-prayer institution**: al-Zamakhsharī gives less attention to the fiqh details and more to the *rhetorical structure* — the *yā ayyuhā alladhīna āmanū* opening (the believing-community vocative); the *fa-saʿaw / wa-dharū / fa-intashirū / wa-btaghū / wa-dhkurū* sequence of imperative verbs (5 imperatives in 2 verses); the *qul mā ʿinda Allāhi khayrun* conclusion. al-Zamakhsharī reads vv. 9-11 as a balāgha-rich call-and-response: God speaks to the community via 5 imperatives, then the Prophet (via *qul*) speaks the resolution.

**al-Zamakhsharī's framing: Q 62 is the rhetorical-iqtirān surah, with the v. 1 paired-names structure and the vv. 9-11 imperative-cascade as the balāgha-load-bearing axes**.

## 4. Ibn Kathīr (d. 774 / 1373) — *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿAẓīm*

**Source-of-record**: Ibn Kathīr, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿAẓīm*, vol. 4, *Sūrat al-Jumuʿah*.

Ibn Kathīr's signature is the *aṣālat al-asbāb* — primary-source asbāb-al-nuzūl + ḥadīth assembly.

- **v. 3**: Ibn Kathīr cites Tirmidhī #3394 verbatim — the famous narrative of the Prophet placing his hand on Salmān al-Fārisī and saying *law kāna al-īmānu ʿinda al-thurayyā la-nālahu rijālun min hāʾulāʾi* ("if faith were at the Pleiades, men from these would attain it"). Ibn Kathīr reads this as the canonical Q 62:3 anchor.
- **vv. 9-11**: Ibn Kathīr assembles the canonical Friday-prayer ḥadīth — Bukhārī #876, Muslim #855, etc. — including the trade-caravan asbāb-al-nuzūl (Bukhārī #936 chain via Jābir b. ʿAbd Allāh — *baynamā nuṣallī khalfa rasūli Allāhi yawma al-jumuʿati idh aqbalat ʿīrun min al-shām...*). Ibn Kathīr notes that v. 11 was revealed in the *first Friday prayer in Madīnah* — the very first Friday establishing the institution. This is the strongest historical-anchoring claim across all 5 mufassirūn surveyed here.
- **vv. 9-11 fiqh derivations**: Ibn Kathīr derives the obligation of Friday prayer for residents (vs. permission for travelers); the prohibition on commerce during the call; the post-prayer dispersal as commanded (not merely permitted); the standing of the imām during the khuṭba.

**Ibn Kathīr's framing: Q 62 is the Friday-prayer fiqh-foundation surah anchored in the al-jumuʿah-al-ūlā (first Friday) historical moment**.

## 5. al-Biqāʿī (d. 885 / 1480) — *Naẓm al-Durar fī Tanāsub al-Āyāt wa-l-Suwar*

**Source-of-record**: al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-Durar*, *Sūrat al-Jumuʿah* section (vol. 20 in modern printings).

al-Biqāʿī's signature is the *munāsabāt* (block-cohesion) approach: how does each verse fit with its neighbors and how does each surah fit with its mushaf-adjacent surahs.

- **Q 61 → Q 62 munāsabah**: al-Biqāʿī notes the perfect→imperfect tense transition (Q 61's *sabbaḥa* → Q 62's *yusabbiḥu*) as a *temporal-aspect handoff* — Q 61 closes the perfect-tense musabbiḥāt sub-cluster; Q 62 opens the imperfect-tense sub-cluster. This is the empirical pattern that H-NEW-58c verifies.
- **Q 62 → Q 63 munāsabah**: this is one of al-Biqāʿī's strongest munāsabah-claims in the corpus. The two surahs are read as a **paired-unit Friday-Ẓuhr-recitation set**: Q 62 (community-formation + Friday-institution) → Q 63 (denouncing the hypocrites who FAILED the Friday-institution). The empirical Q 62→Q 63 fraction-residual = 0.0005 (rank ~2/113 cheapest in the mushaf) **strongly validates al-Biqāʿī's Q 62↔Q 63 munāsabah claim** at the structural-content level.
- **Internal munāsabah**: al-Biqāʿī reads the surah's blocks as: (vv. 1-4) cosmic-then-Prophetic frame → (v. 5) parable-pivot → (vv. 6-8) polemical-test → (vv. 9-11) institutional-resolution. The structural inflection at v. 5 is al-Biqāʿī's own analysis; the project's Q062-F-04 hadith audit and Q062-F-01 Khawātim-echo verification jointly validate the dual-themed structural reading.
- **Q 62 in the mushaf-sequence**: al-Biqāʿī places Q 62 within the Medinan-back ṭiwāl-mufaṣṣal block; reads the musabbiḥāt-block (Q 57, 59, 61, 62, 64) as a deliberately-arranged liturgical unit with internal tense-binary substructure. The empirical 8.1%ile cohesion of {Q 57, 59, 61, 62, 64} per H-NEW-340 — the most-cohesive classical grouping tested in the H-NEW-321→340 series — quantitatively confirms al-Biqāʿī's munāsabah-cluster claim.

**al-Biqāʿī's framing: Q 62 is the structural-munāsabah PEAK between perfect-tense Q 61 and the Q 63 "hypocrite" sequel**.

## 6. Synthesis — what the classical tradition saw

| Mufassir | Distinctive emphasis | Q 62 verse-block focus |
|:--|:--|:--|
| al-Ṭabarī | Sahaba reports + Salmān al-Fārisī chain | vv. 3 + 9-11 |
| al-Rāzī | tense distinction + name pairing + exegetical permutations | v. 1 (tense + names); v. 3 (vajh) |
| al-Zamakhsharī | balāgha-iqtirān + tashbīh | v. 1 (paired names); v. 5 (parable) |
| Ibn Kathīr | aṣālat al-asbāb + ḥadīth assembly | vv. 9-11 + asbāb-al-nuzūl |
| al-Biqāʿī | munāsabāt + Q 61↔62↔63 sequence | mushaf-sequence + tense-binary |

**Convergent classical readings**:
1. The imperfect-tense *yusabbiḥu* opening is **morphologically distinct** from the perfect-tense musabbiḥāt — al-Rāzī and al-Biqāʿī both name this explicitly. **Q062-F-03 quantitatively confirms** this distinction at the prefix-character level (Q 62↔Q 64 = 37 verbatim chars, all cross-tense pairs = 0).
2. The **terminal 4 names** *al-Maliki al-Quddūsi al-ʿAzīzi al-Ḥakīm* are read by al-Rāzī and al-Zamakhsharī as a paired-pair structure. **Q062-F-01 quantitatively confirms** that this paired-pair is a **verbatim 2-word lift from Q 59:23 + Q 59:24** — the classical balāgha-structural reading is empirically validated as a corpus-internal composite quotation.
3. The **Friday-prayer institution** (vv. 9-11) is the surah's *fiqh-load-bearing block* — Ibn Kathīr's hadith-anchor is the canonical Quranic basis for the Friday prayer obligation. **Q062-F-04 hadith audit** documents the canonical anchor across 7 of the 9 books in the digital corpus, with TWO Friday-pair traditions co-existing (Q 62 + Q 63 vs Q 62 + Q 88).
4. The **Q 62↔Q 63 mushaf-adjacency** is read by al-Biqāʿī as the strongest munāsabah-claim in the surah's neighborhood. **Q 62→Q 63 fraction-residual = 0.0005** (`h-new-720.json`) — among the ~10 cheapest seams in the corpus — **quantitatively validates** this claim at the canonical-adjacency-cost layer.

**Where classical tradition and the project's empirical findings interlock**: at the imperfect-tense reading (al-Rāzī ↔ H-NEW-58c / Q062-F-03); at the paired-name balāgha-structure (al-Zamakhsharī ↔ Q062-F-01); at the munāsabah Q 62↔Q 63 claim (al-Biqāʿī ↔ H-NEW-720 fraction-residual 0.0005); at the cluster-membership 8.1%ile cohesion (al-Biqāʿī's musabbiḥāt-block ↔ H-NEW-340).

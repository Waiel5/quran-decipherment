## 7. Quick content structure

- **vv. 1**: ⭐ THE OPENING — *yusabbiḥu li-Llāhi mā fī al-samāwāti wa-mā fī al-arḍ al-Maliki al-Quddūsi al-ʿAzīzi al-Ḥakīm* — imperfect-tense cosmic glorification + 4-name Khawātim-echo composite quotation.
- **vv. 2-4**: the Prophet's mission — *bumiyyīn rasūlan minhum yatlū ʿalayhim āyātihi wa-yuzakkīhim wa-yuʿallimuhumu al-kitāba wa-l-ḥikma* (recital, purification, teaching of the Book and Wisdom). v. 3 *wa-ākhirīna minhum lammā yalḥaqū bihim* — "and others among them who have not yet joined them" — the verse Tirmidhī #3394 connects to the inclusion of non-ʿArab believers (asbāb al-nuzūl).
- **vv. 5**: ⭐ the **donkey-and-books parable** — *mathalu alladhīna ḥumlū al-tawrāta thumma lam yaḥmilūhā ka-mathali al-ḥimāri yaḥmilu asfārā* — "those who were entrusted with the Torah and then did not bear it are like the donkey carrying books" — a uniquely-incisive Medinan polemic against Jewish-community failure to honor scriptural responsibility.
- **vv. 6-8**: dialogue with the *yahūdu* (Jewish community) — challenge to "wish for death" if their claim of exclusive divine-favor is true; affirmation that the *mawt* (death) they flee will reach them.
- **vv. 9-11**: ⭐ THE FRIDAY-PRAYER INSTITUTION — *yā ayyuhā alladhīna āmanū idhā nūdiya li-l-ṣalāti min yawmi al-jumuʿati fa-saʿaw ilā dhikri Allāhi wa-dharū al-bayʿ* — when the call to prayer on Friday is made, leave commerce and proceed to remembrance of God. v. 10 then permits resumption of livelihood post-prayer; v. 11 critiques the Madīnan community's distraction by passing trade-caravans during the Prophet's Friday sermon (asbāb al-nuzūl: a caravan + drum entered Madīnah during Friday khuṭba).

The surah is structurally **dual-themed**: a cosmic-glorification + Prophetic-mission anchor (vv. 1-4) followed by a Madīnan community-formation block (the donkey-parable polemic at v. 5; the death-challenge polemic at vv. 6-8; the Friday-prayer institutional law at vv. 9-11). This dual-theme structure is the empirical correlate of the surah's 4-cluster meta-hub status: Khawātim-echo + musabbiḥāt-imperfect (cosmic-theological cohort) co-located with Friday-institution (liturgical-functional cohort).

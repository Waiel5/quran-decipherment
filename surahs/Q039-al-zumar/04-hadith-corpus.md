# Q 39 al-Zumar — Hadith Corpus

This document catalogs all hadith citations for Q 39 verses, recitation virtues, and asbāb al-nuzūl traditions, verified against the on-disk Bukhārī/Muslim/Tirmidhī/Abū Dāwūd/Nasāʾī/Ibn Mājah corpora. Each citation gives the collection's *idInBook* (the canonical numbering used by the standard editions and matched against `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/`).

**Verification protocol**: every citation in this document has been queried against the on-disk JSON corpus. Where a discrepancy was found between a classical commentary attribution and the on-disk numbering, the on-disk numbering is preserved with a note.

## 1. Recitation virtues

### Hadith #1 — ʿĀʾisha: the Prophet recited Q 17 + Q 39 nightly before sleep

**Tirmidhī #3003** (verified, *kitāb faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān*) and **Tirmidhī #3489** (verified, *kitāb al-manāqib*).

> *حَدَّثَنَا صَالِحُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا حَمَّادُ بْنُ زَيْدٍ، عَنْ أَبِي لُبَابَةَ، قَالَ قَالَتْ عَائِشَةُ كَانَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم لاَ يَنَامُ حَتَّى يَقْرَأَ بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ وَالزُّمَرَ. قَالَ أَبُو عِيسَى هَذَا حَدِيثٌ حَسَنٌ غَرِيبٌ. وَأَبُو لُبَابَةَ شَيْخٌ بَصْرِيٌّ.*

> "Ṣāliḥ b. ʿAbd Allāh narrated to us: Ḥammād b. Zayd narrated to us, from Abū Lubāba, who said: ʿĀʾisha said: 'The Prophet (peace be upon him) would not sleep until he recited Banī Isrāʾīl (Q 17) and al-Zumar (Q 39).' Abū ʿĪsā [al-Tirmidhī] said: This hadith is *ḥasan gharīb*. And Abū Lubāba is a Basran shaykh; Ḥammād b. Zayd narrated more than one hadith from him; his name is said to be Marwān." (Tirmidhī #3003)

**Classification**: *ḥasan gharīb* (acceptable, single-chain) per al-Tirmidhī's own classification. The "gharīb" qualifier means the hadith is transmitted only via Abū Lubāba (Marwān, freedman of ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Ziyād). The hadith is cited by al-Qurṭubī in his Q 39 muqaddima — al-Qurṭubī's attribution to al-Tirmidhī is correct (verified against on-disk Tirmidhī corpus, both #3003 and #3489 are in al-Tirmidhī's *Sunan*).

**Implication**: Q 39 is one of the two surahs the Prophet would not retire without reciting. The other is Q 17 al-Isrāʾ. This pairing places Q 39 in the corpus-recitation high-tier alongside Q 17, Q 18 (al-Kahf, Friday-recitation), Q 36 (Yā-Sīn — though see H-NEW-82 NULL on the "heart of Quran" claim), and Q 67 (al-Mulk, pre-sleep / grave-protection traditions).

## 2. Asbāb al-nuzūl traditions

### Hadith #2 — Q 39:53 *yā ʿibādiya alladhīna asrafū* — polytheist murderers/adulterers chain

**Bukhari #4604** (verified, *kitāb al-tafsīr*, chapter 65, on the tafsir of al-Furqān where Q 25:68 is cited; the tradition links Q 25:68-70 + Q 39:53 in the asbāb chain).

> *حَدَّثَنِي إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ مُوسَى، أَخْبَرَنَا هِشَامُ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، أَنَّ ابْنَ جُرَيْجٍ، أَخْبَرَهُمْ قَالَ يَعْلَى إِنَّ سَعِيدَ بْنَ جُبَيْرٍ أَخْبَرَهُ عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ أَنَّ نَاسًا، مِنْ أَهْلِ الشِّرْكِ كَانُوا قَدْ قَتَلُوا وَأَكْثَرُوا وَزَنَوْا وَأَكْثَرُوا، فَأَتَوْا مُحَمَّدًا صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالُوا إِنَّ الَّذِي تَقُولُ وَتَدْعُو إِلَيْهِ لَحَسَنٌ لَوْ تُخْبِرُنَا أَنَّ لِمَا عَمِلْنَا كَفَّارَةً. فَنَزَلَ {وَالَّذِينَ لاَ يَدْعُونَ مَعَ اللَّهِ إِلَهًا آخَرَ ... وَلاَ يَزْنُونَ} وَنَزَلَ {قُلْ يَا عِبَادِيَ الَّذِينَ أَسْرَفُوا عَلَى أَنْفُسِهِمْ لاَ تَقْنَطُوا مِنْ رَحْمَةِ اللَّهِ}.*

**Translation (verified Bukhari English)**: "Some persons from among the polytheists who had killed many [people] and committed adultery many times came to the Prophet and said: 'What you say and call us to is good, if only you could tell us that there is expiation for what we have done.' Then the verse [Q 25:68-70] was revealed... and the verse [Q 39:53] was revealed."

**Classification**: ṣaḥīḥ (Bukhari's standard).
**Chain**: Ibrāhīm b. Mūsā ← Hishām b. Yūsuf ← Ibn Jurayj ← Yaʿlā b. Muslim ← Saʿīd b. Jubayr ← Ibn ʿAbbās.

The hadith adds the *wa-lā yubālī* expansion — the Prophet recited Q 39:53 and added "and He does not mind". This is the universalizing-mercy framing of the verse.

### Hadith #3 — Q 39:67 *wa-mā qadarū Allāha ḥaqqa qadrihi* — the rabbi-on-fingers tradition

**Bukhari #4605** (verified, *kitāb al-tafsīr*, chapter 65, on Q 39 specifically), **Bukhari #7131** and **#7132** (verified, *kitāb al-tawḥīd*, chapter 97).

> *حَدَّثَنَا آدَمُ، حَدَّثَنَا شَيْبَانُ، عَنْ مَنْصُورٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنْ عَبِيدَةَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ جَاءَ حَبْرٌ مِنَ الأَحْبَارِ إِلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ يَا مُحَمَّدُ، إِنَّا نَجِدُ أَنَّ اللَّهَ يَجْعَلُ السَّمَوَاتِ عَلَى إِصْبَعٍ وَالأَرَضِينَ عَلَى إِصْبَعٍ، وَالشَّجَرَ عَلَى إِصْبَعٍ، وَالْمَاءَ وَالثَّرَى عَلَى إِصْبَعٍ، وَسَائِرَ الْخَلاَئِقِ عَلَى إِصْبَعٍ، فَيَقُولُ أَنَا الْمَلِكُ. فَضَحِكَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم حَتَّى بَدَتْ نَوَاجِذُهُ تَصْدِيقًا لِقَوْلِ الْحَبْرِ ثُمَّ قَرَأَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم {وَمَا قَدَرُوا اللَّهَ حَقَّ قَدْرِهِ وَالأَرْضُ جَمِيعًا قَبْضَتُهُ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ وَالسَّمَوَاتُ مَطْوِيَّاتٌ بِيَمِينِهِ سُبْحَانَهُ وَتَعَالَى عَمَّا يُشْرِكُونَ}.*

**Translation (verified Bukhari English)**: "A (Jewish) Rabbi came to Allah's Messenger and said: 'O Muḥammad! We learn that Allah will put all the heavens on one finger, and the earths on one finger, and the trees on one finger, and the water and the dust on one finger, and all the other created beings on one finger. Then He will say: I am the King.' Thereupon the Prophet smiled until his pre-molar teeth became visible, that being the confirmation of the Rabbi. Then Allah's Messenger recited: 'They have not appraised Allah with true appraisal: the whole earth will be in His grip on the Day of Resurrection, and the heavens will be folded in His right hand. Glorified and exalted is He above what they associate' (Q 39:67)."

**Classification**: ṣaḥīḥ (Bukhari, Muslim).
**Chain (Bukhari #4605)**: Ādam ← Shaybān ← Manṣūr ← Ibrāhīm al-Nakhaʿī ← ʿAbīda al-Salmānī ← ʿAbd Allāh b. Masʿūd.
**Chain (Bukhari #7131)**: Musaddad ← Yaḥyā b. Saʿīd ← Sufyān ← Manṣūr + Sulaymān ← Ibrāhīm ← ʿAbīda ← ʿAbd Allāh.
**Chain (Bukhari #7132)**: ʿUmar b. Ḥafṣ ← Ḥafṣ b. Ghiyāth ← al-Aʿmash ← Ibrāhīm ← ʿAlqama ← ʿAbd Allāh.

**The hadith's structural significance**: this is one of the most-cited hadiths in classical kalām discourse on the divine attributes. The Prophet's smiling reaction (*fa-ḍaḥika al-nabī ḥattā badat nawājidhuh*) is treated by classical theologians as a paradigm for the proper response to divine-attribute discourse: surprise + confirmation + recitation of the Quranic verse, neither denying nor anthropomorphizing.

al-Qurṭubī in his Q 39 commentary cites the hadith in extenso. Ibn Kathīr cross-references it. al-Bāqillānī in *al-Ibāna ʿan uṣūl al-diyāna* uses it as a paradigm for the Ashʿarī *ṣifāt*-without-*kayf* doctrine.

### Hadith #4 — Q 39:30 *innaka mayyitun* — Abū Bakr's eulogy at the death of the Prophet

**Bukhari #3511** (verified, *kitāb al-manāqib*, chapter 62), **Bukhari #3512** (verified, parallel chain), **Bukhari #4256** (verified, *kitāb al-maghāzī*, chapter 64).

> *حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، حَدَّثَنَا سُلَيْمَانُ بْنُ بِلاَلٍ، عَنْ هِشَامِ بْنِ عُرْوَةَ، عَنْ عُرْوَةَ بْنِ الزُّبَيْرِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ زَوْجِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم مَاتَ وَأَبُو بَكْرٍ بِالسُّنْحِ ... فَجَاءَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ فَكَشَفَ عَنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَبَّلَهُ ... فَحَمِدَ اللَّهَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ وَأَثْنَى عَلَيْهِ وَقَالَ أَلاَ مَنْ كَانَ يَعْبُدُ مُحَمَّدًا صلى الله عليه وسلم فَإِنَّ مُحَمَّدًا قَدْ مَاتَ، وَمَنْ كَانَ يَعْبُدُ اللَّهَ فَإِنَّ اللَّهَ حَىٌّ لاَ يَمُوتُ. وَقَالَ {إِنَّكَ مَيِّتٌ وَإِنَّهُمْ مَيِّتُونَ} وَقَالَ {وَمَا مُحَمَّدٌ إِلاَّ رَسُولٌ قَدْ خَلَتْ مِنْ قَبْلِهِ الرُّسُلُ}.*

**Translation (verified Bukhari English)**: "ʿĀʾisha (the Prophet's wife) narrated: 'Allah's Messenger died while Abū Bakr was at a place called as-Sunḥ. ʿUmar stood up and said: "By Allah! Allah's Messenger is not dead!" ... Then Abū Bakr came, uncovered the face of Allah's Messenger, kissed him ... Then Abū Bakr praised Allah and extolled Him and said: 'Whoever was worshipping Muḥammad — Muḥammad has died; whoever was worshipping Allah — Allah is alive, He does not die.' And he recited: '*innaka mayyitun wa-innahum mayyitūn*' (Q 39:30) and '*wa-mā Muḥammadun illā rasūlun qad khalat min qablihi al-rusul*' (Q 3:144)."

**Classification**: ṣaḥīḥ.
**Chain**: Ismāʿīl b. ʿAbd Allāh ← Sulaymān b. Bilāl ← Hishām b. ʿUrwa ← ʿUrwa b. al-Zubayr ← ʿĀʾisha.

**Significance**: this is perhaps the most-historically-load-bearing hadith citing a Q 39 verse in the entire corpus. Abū Bakr's recitation of Q 39:30 (with Q 3:144) at the moment of the Prophet's death is treated by classical historians (al-Ṭabarī's *Tārīkh*, Ibn Hishām's *Sīra*, Ibn Saʿd's *Ṭabaqāt*) as the founding moment of post-prophetic Islamic continuity — the assertion of the Prophet's mortality grounding the community's transition to caliphal authority.

**Parallel chain in Ibn Mājah #1361**: also verified — Ibn Mājah preserves a shorter version of the same tradition.

### Hadith #5 — Q 39:67 / Q 39:23 cosmic descriptions — Aḥmadī tradition

al-Qurṭubī cites a hadith via Ibn Masʿūd in his Q 39:67 commentary that traces the rabbi-fingers tradition with additional detail (the "*thumma yahuzzuhunna fa-yaqūlu anā al-malik anā al-malik*" — "then He shakes them and says: I am the King, I am the King" addition).

This addition appears in **Muslim #6872** but NOT in Bukhari #4605. The variant chain through Manṣūr → Fuḍayl b. ʿIyāḍ → Aḥmad b. ʿAbd Allāh in Muslim differs from the Bukhari chain through Shaybān, with the *thumma yahuzzuhunna* phrase as a transmission-difference. al-Qurṭubī's attribution is to "*al-Tirmidhī ʿan ʿAbd Allāh*" — verified Tirmidhī #3322 has the chain via al-Aʿmash + Manṣūr (without the *yahuzzuhunna* phrase, which al-Tirmidhī's chain does NOT carry).

**Note**: this is a transmission-difference, not a reliability question — both chains (with and without *yahuzzuhunna*) are within ṣaḥīḥ Sunnī corpora.

## 3. Faḍāʾil traditions (recitation virtues)

### Tradition A — al-Qurṭubī's citation of the ʿĀʾisha "lā yanāmu" hadith

Per al-Qurṭubī's Q 39 muqaddima: *"rawā al-Tirmidhī ʿan ʿĀʾisha qālat: kāna rasūl Allāh ṣallā Allāh ʿalayhi wa-sallam lā yanāmu ḥattā yaqraʾa al-Zumara wa-Banī Isrāʾīla"*.

**Verification**: confirmed against on-disk Tirmidhī corpus as Tirmidhī #3003 (and parallel #3489). al-Qurṭubī's attribution is correct.

### Tradition B — Wahb b. Munabbih's *Sūrat al-Ghuraf* recommendation

Per al-Thaʿlabī and al-Qurṭubī: *qāla Wahb b. Munabbih: man aḥabba an yaʿrifa qaḍāʾ Allāhi taʿālā fī khalqihi fa-l-yaqraʾ Sūrat al-Ghuraf*.

**Note**: this is a *Companion-Successor saying* (athar), not a marfūʿ hadith. It is preserved in al-Thaʿlabī's *al-Kashf wa-l-bayān* and in al-Qurṭubī's *Jāmiʿ*. It is not in the canonical hadith corpora. **Classification: athar, ḥasan in transmission, not raised to the Prophet**.

### Tradition C — Q 39:53 with "and He does not mind"

Tirmidhī #3321 (chain via Asmāʾ bint Yazīd via Shahr b. Ḥawshab): the Prophet recited Q 39:53 and added *wa-lā yubālī*. **Classification (al-Tirmidhī)**: *ḥasan gharīb*. al-Tirmidhī notes the chain is unique to Thābit via Shahr b. Ḥawshab.

This is a foundational text for the broad-mercy reading of Q 39:53 — the universal *yaghfiru al-dhunūba jamīʿan* expanded with the explicit *wa-lā yubālī* (and He does not concern Himself with it / does not mind).

## 4. Asbāb al-nuzūl traditions — secondary/dissenting

### Q 39:23 — alternative Medinan revelation tradition

al-Suyūṭī's *al-Durr al-manthūr* preserves an Ibn ʿAbbās tradition that v. 23 (*Allāh nazzala aḥsana al-ḥadīthi kitāban mutashābihan mathāniya*) is Medinan-revealed. The dominant tradition (al-Ṭabarī, Ibn Kathīr, al-Qurṭubī) classifies the verse as Meccan with the rest of the surah. The dissenting Medinan attribution is recorded but not endorsed by classical consensus.

### Q 39:53 — Wahshī b. Ḥarb tradition

al-Suyūṭī's *al-Durr al-manthūr* preserves the Ibn Isḥāq narrative that Wahshī b. Ḥarb (the killer of Ḥamza b. ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib at Uḥud) requested a Quranic verse to apply to him after his conversion to Islam. The Prophet recited Q 39:53.

This tradition is preserved in:
- al-Bayhaqī's *al-Sunan al-Kubrā* (verified to exist in classical sources but not in the on-disk 9-books corpus, which is limited to the canonical 9).
- Ibn Saʿd's *Ṭabaqāt* (al-Suyūṭī cites).
- Ibn Hishām's *Sīra* (the conversion narrative).

**Classification**: this tradition is sīra-historical, not hadith. It is preserved in the *maghāzī* literature but does not appear in the canonical 9-books corpus on its own. al-Suyūṭī's chain via Ibn Isḥāq is well-known but does not have direct ṣaḥīḥ-sanad in the canonical Sunnī collections. **Treat as historically attested but not raised to ṣaḥīḥ ḥadīth status.**

## 7. Summary

Q 39 al-Zumar carries a substantial hadith footprint, with at least 4 distinct verse-clusters generating canonical hadith citations:

- **The recitation-virtue cluster** (Tirmidhī #3003, #3489): the Prophet's nightly recitation of Q 17 + Q 39 establishes the surah as part of the canonical pre-sleep liturgical practice.
- **The Q 39:30 mortality cluster** (Bukhari #3511, #3512, #4256, Ibn Mājah #1361): Abū Bakr's eulogy at the death of the Prophet — among the most-historically-load-bearing hadiths in the entire corpus.
- **The Q 39:53 mercy cluster** (Bukhari #4604, Muslim #229, Tirmidhī #3321, Abū Dāwūd #4275, Nasāʾī #4013-#4014): the asbāb al-nuzūl for the universal-mercy verse, with multiple parallel chains.

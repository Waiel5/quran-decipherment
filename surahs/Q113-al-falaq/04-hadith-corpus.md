## 1. The muʿawwidhatān classical naming

### al-Bukhārī

| ḥadīth # | Kitāb / Bāb | Matn summary |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| **#4439** | k. al-maghāzī, b. marad al-nabī wa-wafātihi | ʿĀʾisha — In his final illness, the Prophet would recite **al-muʿawwidhatān** (Q 113 + Q 114) and blow over himself. |
| **#4985** | k. faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān (or k. al-tafsīr) | parallel chain: ʿĀʾisha — the muʿawwidhatān-recitation by the Prophet during illness. |
| **#5016** | k. faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān, b. faḍl al-muʿawwidhāt | ʿUqba b. ʿĀmir — *anzala ʿalayya āyāt lam yur mithluhunna qaṭṭ* — "verses unlike anything ever revealed to me" — referring to Q 113 + Q 114. |
| **#5017** | same | ʿĀʾisha — the hand-blowing tradition: when the Prophet went to bed, he cupped his hands, recited Q 112, Q 113, Q 114 into them, and passed them over his body, three times. |
| **#5018** | same | parallel chain |

The canonical name **al-muʿawwidhatān** appears explicitly in #4439 and is the classical pair-naming.

### Muslim

- **Muslim k. al-salām, b. ruqyat al-marīḍ** — multiple parallel chains for hand-blowing.
- **Muslim k. ṣalāt al-musāfirīn, b. faḍl qirāʾat al-muʿawwidhatayn** — recitation-merit chains.

### al-Tirmidhī

- **al-Tirmidhī k. faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān, b. mā jāʾa fī l-muʿawwidhatayn** — chain via ʿUqba b. ʿĀmir; al-Tirmidhī grades *ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ*.
- **al-Tirmidhī #2058** — *taʿawwadhū bi-llāhi min sharri sammin wa-ʿaynin wa-min sharri kulli dhī* (refuge-from-evil tradition closely tied to muʿawwidhatān).
- **al-Tirmidhī #3366** — Abū Hurayra: the Prophet pointed at the moon and said *taʿawwadhī bi-llāhi min hādhā fa-innahu l-ghāsiq idhā waqab* (linking to Q 113:3); graded *ḥasan*.

### al-Nasāʾī

### Aḥmad

- **al-Musnad** — multiple chains for muʿawwidhatān-recitation (via ʿUqba b. ʿĀmir, ʿĀʾisha, Ibn ʿAbbās).

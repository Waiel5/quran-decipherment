## 2. ⭐ Distinctive structural property — TRIPLE corpus-EXACT signatures

Q 61 carries **three independent corpus-EXACT structural signatures** within only 14 verses:

### (a) Q 61:1 — perfect-tense musabbiḥāt opener (3-surah class)

Q 61:1 is one of **only 3 verses in the corpus** opening with the perfect-tense *sabbaḥa li-Llāh* construction (Q 57:1, Q 59:1, Q 61:1). Q 59:1 and Q 61:1 are **character-identical for the first 56 characters** (the longest shared opener-prefix in the entire H-NEW-58c musabbiḥāt analysis).

### (b) Q 61:6 — the Aḥmad-prophecy verse (corpus-EXACT)

The token **أحمد** (Aḥmad) appears **EXACTLY ONCE in the entire 6,236-verse Qurʾān**, at Q 61:6: *wa-mubashshiran bi-rasūlin yaʾtī min baʿdī ismuhu Aḥmad* — "and bringing good news of a messenger to come after me whose name is Aḥmad." (See Q061-F-01 CONFIRMED corpus-uniqueness test.)

This is one of **3 verses corpus-wide** in which a prophet/messenger is annunciated by a specific Arabic name in the construction *ismuhu X*: Q 3:45 (Jesus → al-Masīḥ ʿĪsā ibn Maryam), Q 19:7 (Yaḥyā), Q 61:6 (Aḥmad). The Q 3:45 + Q 19:7 + Q 61:6 trio forms a corpus-EXACT prophetic-naming-annunciation triad.

### (c) Q 61:9 — corpus-EXACT-character-identical with Q 9:33

Q 61:9 *huwa alladhī arsala rasūlahu bi-l-hudā wa-dīni al-ḥaqqi li-yuẓhirahu ʿalā al-dīni kullihi wa-law kariha al-mushrikūn* is **character-by-character IDENTICAL with Q 9:33** (73 cleaned characters, all matching). The two verses form one of the corpus's perfect verse-twin pairs. The closely-related Q 48:28 modifies the tail (*wa-kafā bi-Llāhi shahīdan* in place of *wa-law kariha al-mushrikūn*), making this a 2-strict + 1-near triple. Classical *ʿulūm al-Qurʾān* commentators (al-Zarkashī, al-Suyūṭī) recognize this as a *mutashābih* (intra-Quranic verse-similarity).

These three signatures are independently striking; their **co-location in a 14-verse Medinan surah** is the architectural fact that makes Q 61 a structurally-disproportionate surah for its size.

## 3. ⭐ The Aḥmad-prophecy classical-tafsir context

Q 61:6 places in Jesus's mouth the explicit prophecy: *wa-mubashshiran bi-rasūlin yaʾtī min baʿdī ismuhu Aḥmad* — "and giving glad tidings of a messenger to come after me whose name is Aḥmad." This is the **corpus-unique explicit Christ-Muhammad annunciation**.

al-Ṭabarī, Ibn Kathīr, al-Rāzī, al-Qurṭubī, and al-Suyūṭī all interpret *Aḥmad* as the name-form of the Prophet Muḥammad (variant superlative-elative pattern from the same root ḥ-m-d). The classical tradition supports this with an extensive 5-names *aʿlām* hadith corpus (verified at Bukhārī #3382, #4688; Muslim #5949, #5950; Tirmidhī #2919; Mālik *Muwaṭṭaʾ* #1985; Darimi #2041). The hadith is multiply attested: *innī muḥammadun wa-anā aḥmadu wa-anā al-māḥī alladhī yamḥū Allāhu bī al-kufra wa-anā al-ḥāshiru alladhī yuḥsharu al-nāsu ʿalā qadamī wa-anā al-ʿāqib* — "I am Muḥammad, and I am Aḥmad, and I am the Erasure by whom Allah erases unbelief, and the Gatherer at whose feet humanity is gathered, and the Last [prophet]." See `04-hadith-corpus.md` §1 for verification.

The wider classical interpretive tradition (al-Ṭabarī ad loc.; al-Rāzī, *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* vol. 29 p. 308-313; al-Suyūṭī, *al-Durr al-manthūr* ad loc.) cross-references Q 61:6's *Aḥmad* with the New Testament Greek *paráklētos* (Παράκλητος, "Comforter / Advocate") at John 14:16-17, 14:26, 15:26, 16:7, identifying the homophone-resemblance with the Greek *periklytos* (περικλυτός, "much-praised, illustrious") — a classical Muslim apologetic position cross-referencing Christian and Islamic scriptures. See `03-tafsir-survey.md` §1-5 for the full classical-tafsīr survey on the Aḥmad-Paraclete reading.

## 4. ⭐ The al-ḥawāriyyūn closure (Q 61:14)

The surah closes with Q 61:14 *yā ayyuhā alladhīna āmanū kūnū anṣāra Allāh kamā qāla ʿīsā ibn maryam li-l-ḥawāriyyīna man anṣārī ilā Allāh qāla al-ḥawāriyyūna naḥnu anṣāru Allāh fa-āmanat ṭāʾifatun min banī isrāʾīla wa-kafarat ṭāʾifatun fa-ayyadnā alladhīna āmanū ʿalā ʿaduwwihim fa-aṣbaḥū ẓāhirīn* — "O you who believe, be helpers of Allah, as Jesus son of Mary said to the disciples (al-ḥawāriyyūn): 'Who are my helpers toward Allah?' The disciples said: 'We are Allah's helpers.' Then a faction of the children of Israel believed and a faction disbelieved; We supported those who believed against their enemy, so they became uppermost."

The token *al-ḥawāriyyūn* / *al-ḥawāriyyīn* / *li-l-ḥawāriyyīn* appears in only **4 verses corpus-wide** — Q 3:52, Q 5:111, Q 5:112, Q 61:14. Q 61:14 is the only non-Christ-narrative-surah occurrence: Q 3 and Q 5 are the established Christ-narrative anchors, and Q 61 is the **fourth member** of the corpus's *ḥawāriyyūn* citation set. The token *للحواريين* (with prefixed *li-* "to the disciples", Q 61:14) is itself a corpus-hapax (the prefix-form is unique to Q 61:14).

## 5. The tijārah passage (Q 61:10-13) — spiritual-commerce framework

Q 61:10-13 introduces the *tijārah tunjīkum min ʿadhāb alīm* construction — "a commerce that will save you from a painful punishment." The structure is:
- v. 10: *hal adullukum ʿalā tijāratin tunjīkum min ʿadhābin alīm* — "Shall I direct you to a commerce that will save you from a painful punishment?"
- v. 11: *tuʾminūna bi-Llāhi wa-rasūlihi wa-tujāhidūna fī sabīli Llāhi bi-amwālikum wa-anfusikum* — "Believe in Allah and His Messenger and strive in His path with your wealth and your selves."
- v. 12: *yaghfir lakum dhunūbakum wa-yudkhilkum jannātin tajrī min taḥtihā al-anhāru wa-masākina ṭayyibatan fī jannāti ʿAdnin* — forgiveness + paradise.
- v. 13: *naṣrun min Allāhi wa-fatḥun qarīb wa-bashshiri al-muʾminīn* — divine help + a *fatḥ qarīb* (a near victory).

Q 61's *tijārah* (literal: commerce, trade) is the corpus's clearest anchoring of the **commerce-with-God** metaphor. The corpus contains 7 *tijārah* verses (Q 2:282, Q 4:29, Q 9:24, Q 24:37, Q 35:29, Q 61:10, Q 62:11); only Q 35:29 (*yarjūna tijāratan lan tabūr* — "they hope for a commerce that shall not perish") and Q 61:10 use the spiritual-tijārah register. Q 35:29 + Q 61:10 form a corpus-EXACT 2-verse spiritual-tijārah pair.

## 9. Quick content structure

Q 61 has 4 macro thematic blocks within its 14 verses:

- **v. 1**: ⭐ MUSABBIḤA OPENER — perfect-tense *sabbaḥa li-Llāh*, character-identical with Q 59:1 for 56 chars.
- **vv. 2-4**: ⭐ THE *ṢAFFAN KA-ANNAHUM BUNYĀNUN MARṢŪṢ* declaration — believers must not say what they do not do; God loves those who fight in His path *as a tightly-cemented building*. Surah-title anchor at v. 4 (*ṣaffan*).
- **vv. 5-9**: TWO PROPHETIC NARRATIVES + THE AḤMAD PROPHECY:
  - **v. 5**: Mūsā's dialogue with Banū Isrāʾīl (*lima tu'dhūnanī wa-qad taʿlamūna annī rasūlu Llāhi ilaykum*).
  - **v. 6**: ⭐ ʿĪsā's prophecy of Aḥmad (corpus-EXACT *Aḥmad* token).
  - **vv. 7-9**: Polemic against those who fabricate falsehood + the *yurīdūna li-yuṭfiʾū nūra Llāhi bi-afwāhihim* image (the corpus-hapax verb *yuṭfiʾū* "to extinguish") + Q 61:9's corpus-EXACT-twin-of-Q 9:33 universal-religion-supremacy declaration.
- **vv. 10-13**: ⭐ THE TIJĀRAH PASSAGE — *tijāratin tunjīkum min ʿadhāb alīm*; faith + striving in path of God = forgiveness + paradise + a *fatḥ qarīb* (near victory).
- **v. 14**: ⭐ THE AL-ḤAWĀRIYYŪN CLOSING — believers as *anṣār Allāh* in the same way the disciples (al-ḥawāriyyūn) were anṣār to Jesus; one faction of Banū Isrāʾīl believed, one disbelieved; God supported the believers *fa-aṣbaḥū ẓāhirīn* (so they became uppermost).

The structure is **dual-prophet-frame + central tijārah-exchange + ḥawāriyyūn closure** — a tightly-architected Medinan exhortation of compact length but extreme structural-density.

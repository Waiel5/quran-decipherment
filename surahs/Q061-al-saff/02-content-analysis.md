## 2. Verse-level content highlights (Arabic-anchored)

### v. 1: musabbiḥa opener
- **Q 61:1**: *سبح لله ما في السماوات وما في الأرض ۖ وهو العزيز الحكيم* — "Whatever is in the heavens and on earth glorifies Allah; He is the Mighty, the Wise." Perfect-tense *sabbaḥa* form (the historical/cosmic-fact register per H-NEW-58c). **Character-identical with Q 59:1 for the first 56 characters** (the longest shared opener-prefix in the corpus per H-NEW-58c). Q 57:1 has identical opening but diverges slightly at *وما في الأرض* ↔ *والأرض*.

### vv. 2-4: the surah-title anchor
- **Q 61:2**: *يا أيها الذين آمنوا لم تقولون ما لا تفعلون* — "O you who believe! Why do you say what you do not do?" The corpus's clearest **say-vs-do consistency exhortation**. Direct address (*yā ayyuhā alladhīna āmanū*) characteristic of Medinan paraenetic.
- **Q 61:3**: *كبر مقتا عند الله أن تقولوا ما لا تفعلون* — "It is most hateful to Allah that you say what you do not do." Restatement, intensification.
- **Q 61:4**: ⭐ *إن الله يحب الذين يقاتلون في سبيله صفا كأنهم بنيان مرصوص* — "Indeed, Allah loves those who fight in His path **in ranks (ṣaffan), as if they were a tightly-cemented building.**" The **surah-title-anchor verse** (the surah is named al-Ṣaff after this). Two corpus-hapax tokens (*بنيان*, *مرصوص*) in a single 11-word verse — extreme lexical-uniqueness density.

### v. 5: Mūsā's complaint
- **Q 61:5**: *وإذ قال موسى لقومه يا قوم لم تؤذونني وقد تعلمون أني رسول الله إليكم ۖ فلما زاغوا أزاغ الله قلوبهم ۚ والله لا يهدي القوم الفاسقين* — "And when Mūsā said to his people: 'O my people! Why do you hurt/cause-pain to me, when you know I am Allah's messenger to you?' So when they deviated, Allah deviated their hearts. Allah does not guide the rebellious people." The verse contains **3 corpus-hapax tokens** (*أزاغ*, *زاغوا*, *تؤذونني*) — the Mūsā→Banū-Isrāʾīl tension is encoded with corpus-unique vocabulary.

### v. 6: ⭐⭐ THE AḤMAD-PROPHECY VERSE
- **Q 61:6**: *وإذ قال عيسى ابن مريم يا بني إسرائيل إني رسول الله إليكم مصدقا لما بين يدي من التوراة ومبشرا برسول يأتي من بعدي اسمه أحمد ۖ فلما جاءهم بالبينات قالوا هذا سحر مبين* — "And when Jesus son of Mary said: 'O children of Israel! I am Allah's messenger to you, confirming the Torah that came before me, and bringing good tidings of a messenger to come after me, **whose name is Aḥmad**.' But when he came to them with clear signs, they said: 'This is plain magic.'"

This verse is the **corpus's UNIQUE explicit Christ-Muḥammad annunciation**. Per Q061-F-01:
- **أحمد**: corpus-EXACT-singleton (1 occurrence in 6,236 verses; only at Q 61:6).
- **اسمه + name** annunciation pattern: 3 verses corpus-wide (Q 3:45 ʿĪsā / Q 19:7 Yaḥyā / Q 61:6 Aḥmad). Q 61:6 closes this prophetic-naming-annunciation triad.
- **مبشرا برسول**: corpus-unique formula joining the verb *bashshara* (to herald) with object *bi-rasūl* (a messenger) in the active-participle-with-bi-min-construction. The token *برسول* itself is corpus-hapax (the *bi-rasūl* prefix-form is unique to this verse; cf. *bi-rasūlika* in Q 3:53 and *li-l-rasūli* generic at Q 8:1, etc.).

The classical interpretive tradition (al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, al-Suyūṭī, al-Qurṭubī) interprets *Aḥmad* as the prophesied superlative-elative form of the Prophet Muḥammad's name (both from root ḥ-m-d). The classical anchoring includes the multiply-attested 5-names hadith (Bukhārī #3382, #4688; Muslim #5949, #5950; Tirmidhī #2919; Mālik *Muwaṭṭaʾ* #1985) — *innī muḥammadun wa-anā aḥmadu...* The Q 61:6 → 5-names-hadith linkage is the foundational tafsīr structure for the Aḥmad-prophecy reading. See `03-tafsir-survey.md` and `04-hadith-corpus.md` for full details.

The verse also carries a Christological-historical layer: the *al-tawrāt* (Torah) confirmation is the corpus's clearest Christ-as-Torah-confirmer statement, paralleling Q 5:46 (*āthārin min al-injīl muṣaddiqan li-mā bayna yadayhi min al-tawrāt*). Q 61:6 thus joins Q 3:50 / Q 5:46 / Q 19:30 in the corpus's **Christ-as-Torah-confirmer + Aḥmad-prophet-prophecy** Christology.

### vv. 7-8: polemic against falsehood
- **Q 61:7**: *ومن أظلم ممن افترى على الله الكذب وهو يدعى إلى الإسلام ۚ والله لا يهدي القوم الظالمين* — "And who is more unjust than one who fabricates falsehood against Allah while he is being called to Islam? Allah does not guide the unjust people." The token *يدعى* (yudʿā, to be called/invited) is corpus-hapax in this passive-imperfect form.
- **Q 61:8**: *يريدون ليطفئوا نور الله بأفواههم والله متم نوره ولو كره الكافرون* — "They want to **extinguish** Allah's light with their mouths, but Allah will perfect His light, however much the disbelievers may dislike [it]." Corpus-hapax: *ليطفئوا* (li-yuṭfiʾū). The image (*nūr Allāh + afwāh*) is paralleled at Q 9:32 (*yurīdūna an yuṭfiʾū nūra Llāhi bi-afwāhihim*) — same image, different verbal form, both Medinan polemic against unbelievers' verbal-attack-on-revelation.

### v. 9: ⭐ THE Q 9:33-TWIN VERSE
- **Q 61:9**: *هو الذي أرسل رسوله بالهدى ودين الحق ليظهره على الدين كله ولو كره المشركون* — **CHARACTER-IDENTICAL with Q 9:33** at 73 cleaned chars; word-for-word, letter-for-letter the same. Closely related Q 48:28 differs only in the closing: *wa-kafā bi-Llāhi shahīdan* in place of *wa-law kariha al-mushrikūn*. The three-verse triplet Q 9:33 / Q 48:28 / Q 61:9 is a corpus-EXACT *mutashābih* triple — 2 strict + 1 near.

The classical interpretive tradition (al-Zarkashī, *al-Burhān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān*; al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān*) treats Q 9:33 / Q 61:9 / Q 48:28 as a recurring divine-promise formula — the **universal-religion-prevalence** declaration. Each occurrence carries contextual specifics: Q 9:33 in the post-Tabūk Tawba context; Q 48:28 in the Ḥudaybiyya-treaty / fatḥ-makka context; Q 61:9 here in the Aḥmad-prophecy + ḥawāriyyūn-typology context.

The corpus-EXACT verse-twin between Q 9:33 and Q 61:9 is **structurally significant**: it is one of the very few cases of **whole-verse character-identity** in the corpus (excluding the *bismillāh*-counted-only-in-Q1 anomaly). This places Q 61 in a top-tier verse-twin relationship within H-NEW-66's network architecture.

### vv. 10-13: the tijārah passage
- **Q 61:10**: ⭐ *يا أيها الذين آمنوا هل أدلكم على تجارة تنجيكم من عذاب أليم* — "O you who believe! Shall I show you a **commerce** that will **save you** from a painful punishment?" Corpus-hapax: *تنجيكم* (tunjīkum). The *tijārah tunjīkum min ʿadhāb alīm* phrase is the corpus's clearest spiritual-commerce-with-God metaphor — paired with Q 35:29 (*yarjūna tijāratan lan tabūr* — "they hope for a commerce that shall not perish").
- **Q 61:11**: *تؤمنون بالله ورسوله وتجاهدون في سبيل الله بأموالكم وأنفسكم ۚ ذلكم خير لكم إن كنتم تعلمون* — "You believe in Allah and His Messenger and **strive** in His path with your wealth and your selves. That is better for you, if you know." Corpus-hapax: *وتجاهدون* (wa-tujāhidūna).
- **Q 61:12**: *يغفر لكم ذنوبكم ويدخلكم جنات تجري من تحتها الأنهار ومساكن طيبة في جنات عدن ۚ ذلك الفوز العظيم* — "He will forgive your sins and admit you into gardens beneath which rivers flow and pleasant dwellings in gardens of perpetual residence. That is the great triumph." Standard paradise-reward formulation.
- **Q 61:13**: *وأخرى تحبونها ۖ نصر من الله وفتح قريب ۗ وبشر المؤمنين* — "And another [thing] you love: **help from Allah and a near victory**. And give good tidings to the believers." Corpus-hapax: *تحبونها*, *وفتح*. The *naṣr min Allāh wa-fatḥ qarīb* couplet anchors the very-late-Medinan revelation context (the surah is canonically dated near Q 110 al-Naṣr / Q 48 al-Fatḥ in the Medinan post-Ḥudaybiyya / Mecca-conquest era — though the date is contested).

### v. 14: ⭐ THE ḤAWĀRIYYŪN CLOSING
- **Q 61:14**: *يا أيها الذين آمنوا كونوا أنصار الله كما قال عيسى ابن مريم للحواريين من أنصاري إلى الله ۖ قال الحواريون نحن أنصار الله ۖ فآمنت طائفة من بني إسرائيل وكفرت طائفة ۖ فأيدنا الذين آمنوا على عدوهم فأصبحوا ظاهرين* — "O you who believe! Be **anṣār Allāh** (helpers of Allah), as Jesus son of Mary said to the **al-ḥawāriyyūn** (the disciples): 'Who are my helpers toward Allah?' The disciples said: 'We are Allah's helpers.' Then a faction of the children of Israel believed and a faction disbelieved; We supported those who believed against their enemy, so they became uppermost."

This is a **corpus-density verse**: 5 corpus-hapax tokens (*للحواريين*, *فأيدنا*, *وكفرت*, *فآمنت*, *عدوهم*) in a single 32-word verse — the highest hapax-density verse in Q 61.

The verse establishes **the al-anṣār typology**: the Muslim community is to its Prophet what the al-ḥawāriyyūn (disciples) were to Jesus. This is one of two corpus passages where the parallel is made explicit — the other is Q 3:52, where the disciples themselves declare *naḥnu anṣāru Llāh* in the Christ-narrative.

The token *al-ḥawāriyyūn / al-ḥawāriyyīn / li-l-ḥawāriyyīn* appears in **only 4 verses corpus-wide**: Q 3:52, Q 5:111, Q 5:112, Q 61:14. Q 61:14 is the only non-Christ-narrative-surah occurrence; Q 3 and Q 5 are the established Christ-narrative anchors. The form *للحواريين* (with prefix *li-* "to the disciples") is a corpus-hapax — exclusive to Q 61:14.

The closing token *fa-aṣbaḥū ẓāhirīn* ("they became uppermost") echoes the *fa-aṣbaḥū* + *ẓāhirīn* combination at Q 98:8's closing (*riḍwānun min Llāhi*). The closing-verse phonological resemblance reinforces Q 61's FR-nearest neighbour structure (Q 98 al-Bayyina is FR-rank 6 in Q 61's row).

## 6. Honest limits of this content analysis

- **Hapax-counting uses substring-match on no-tashkeel orthographic tokens.** Strict philological hapax (unique verbal-form attestation) would yield a smaller and more accurate count. Many Q 61 hapax tokens are inflected variants of corpus-common roots (e.g., *وتجاهدون* is a hapax in this exact form, but root j-h-d is corpus-frequent). The substring-method overcounts.
- **The Q 61:9 ↔ Q 9:33 character-identity is at the cleaned-text level** (no-tashkeel, ۖ/ۚ/ۗ punctuation removed). At the full-tashkeel level, minor diacritic differences may exist; at the no-tashkeel level, the character equality is exact.
- **The "corpus-EXACT" Aḥmad token** depends on no-tashkeel-orthographic-token equality. The classical-Arabic phonology of أحمد and محمد (variants of the same root ḥ-m-d) is a separate question — the empirical claim here is purely orthographic-token-level uniqueness.
- **Chronology**: Q 61 is dated revelation-order #109 (very late Medinan) in `data/revelation-order.csv`. Some classical sources (al-Suyūṭī minority view) place Q 61 earlier, in the post-Uḥud Medinan phase (consistent with the *ṣaffan ka-annahum bunyānun marṣūṣ* military-ranks register). The architectural numbers above are independent of revelation-dating; the *fatḥ qarīb* (v. 13) reference is most-naturally read in the post-Ḥudaybiyya / pre-Mecca-conquest window (628-629 CE, the consensus dating).

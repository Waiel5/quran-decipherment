# Q 84 al-Inshiqāq — Ḥadīth Corpus

**VERIFICATION NOTE.** Every ḥadīth number below was read directly from the on-disk database
`data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/{collection}.json`, field `idInBook`
(the canonical Sunnah.com numbering). No number is asserted from memory. The Arabic of each record was
matched on the diacritic-stripped text. Two ḥadīth clusters dominate Q 84's reception: (1) the **sajdat
al-tilāwa** at v 21 (the Abū Hurayra prostration tradition — the single most heavily attested fact about
this surah), and (2) the **ḥisāb yasīr** of v 8 (the ʿĀʾisha "whoever is interrogated in the reckoning is
doomed" tradition). Both are surveyed exhaustively below.

## 1. The sajdat al-tilāwa at Q 84:21 (Abū Hurayra prostration tradition)

Q 84:21 (*wa-idhā quriʾa ʿalayhim al-Qurʾānu lā yasjudūn* ۩) is one of the recitation-prostration
verses. The core narration: **Abū Hurayra** recited *idhā al-samāʾu inshaqqat* and prostrated, reporting
that he did so behind the Prophet ﷺ. The paired report adds that the Prophet prostrated in BOTH
*idhā al-samāʾu inshaqqat* (84) AND *iqraʾ bi-smi rabbik* (al-ʿAlaq 96).

**Key text (Bukhārī #748):** *"ṣallaytu maʿa Abī Hurayra al-ʿatama fa-qaraʾa {idhā al-samāʾu
inshaqqat} fa-sajada, fa-qultu lahu? qāla: sajadtu khalfa Abī al-Qāsim ﷺ fa-lā azālu asjudu bihā ḥattā
alqāh."* — Abū Hurayra prostrated at 84:21 in the night prayer and traced the practice to the Prophet,
vowing to continue it "until I meet him." The al-Inshiqāq + al-ʿAlaq pairing (Muslim #1201/1202,
Abū Dāwūd #1408, Nasāʾī #965/968/969, Tirmidhī #573, Ibn Mājah #792) is the basis for classing the two
as the *sujūd al-mufaṣṣal* (with al-Najm 53 the third).

## 2. The ḥisāb yasīr of Q 84:8 (ʿĀʾisha "man nūqisha al-ḥisāba ʿudhdhib" tradition)

Q 84:7-8 (*fa-ammā man ūtiya kitābahu bi-yamīnih * fa-sawfa yuḥāsabu ḥisāban yasīrā*) prompts ʿĀʾisha's
question to the Prophet: if "whoever is reckoned [interrogated] is punished," how can there be an *easy*
reckoning? The Prophet's reply: *"innamā dhālika al-ʿarḍ; wa-lākin man nūqisha al-ḥisāba [yawma
al-qiyāma] yahlik / ʿudhdhib"* — the "easy reckoning" is a mere **display** (*ʿarḍ*), not an
**interrogation** (*munāqasha*).

**Key text (Bukhārī #103, ʿĀʾisha):** *"… anna al-nabī ﷺ qāla: 'man ḥūsiba ʿudhdhib.' qālat ʿĀʾisha:
fa-qultu: a-wa-laysa yaqūlu Allāhu taʿālā {fa-sawfa yuḥāsabu ḥisāban yasīrā}? qālat: fa-qāla: 'innamā
dhālika al-ʿarḍ, wa-lākin man nūqisha al-ḥisāba yahlik.'"* This is the most-cited Q 84-verse tradition
after the sajda; it fixes the exegesis of *ḥisāban yasīrā* (v 8) as *ʿarḍ* (display) — and the
Tirmidhī gradings (#2496, #3421) are both *ṣaḥīḥ ḥasan / ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ* per the Abū ʿĪsā colophon on disk.

## 3. The Jibrīl / *fa-innaka mulāqīh* counsel (Q 84:6) — NOT located on disk

Ibn Kathīr (tafsīr, v 6) cites the counsel *"iʿmal mā shiʾta fa-innaka mulāqīh"* ("do as you will, for
you will meet Him") as the prophetic echo of *fa-mulāqīh*. **A scan of the on-disk 9-book database
(Bukhārī, Muslim, Tirmidhī, Abū Dāwūd, Nasāʾī, Ibn Mājah, Aḥmad) for the phrase *ملاقيه* returned no
numbered ḥadīth.** It is therefore documented as an **Ibn-Kathīr tafsīr-citation, flagged as
not-located-as-a-numbered-ḥadīth** in the on-disk collections (it appears in Mustadrak/tafsīr chains
outside the 9-book set). Not asserted as a Ṣaḥīḥayn ḥadīth.

## 4. Faḍāʾil / recitation

No surah-named *faḍāʾil* (virtue-of-reciting) ḥadīth for Sūrat al-Inshiqāq was located in the on-disk
9-book database. This is consistent with the short-mufaṣṣal block generally (faḍāʾil concentrate on
Q 1, 2, 18, 36, 55, 56, 67, 112, 113, 114). **Flagged as data-gap, not an absence-of-evidence claim.**
The surah's heaviest ḥadīth footprint is the *sajda* tradition (§1), which functions as a recitation
practice rather than a faḍīla.

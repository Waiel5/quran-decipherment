# Q 84 al-Inshiqāq — Content Analysis

Text reference: `quran-text/quran-no-tashkeel.json` (surah id 84, 25 verses). Roots from QAC v0.4
(`data/morphology/root-index.json`). Register: **eschatological** — a tightly-built "great-event"
surah moving from cosmic dissolution to the two judgment-fates to an oath-sworn declaration of
graduated transformation, closing on the sajda-rebuke and the believer-exception.

## 2. Major thematic blocks

1. **Cosmic rending (vv 1-5)** — the *idhā…wa-idhā…* eschatological cascade. Two cosmic protases
   (heaven splits / earth flattens-and-disgorges), each followed by a *wa-adhinat li-rabbihā
   wa-ḥuqqat* comment, with v 5 repeating v 2 verbatim as an internal refrain. The jawāb al-sharṭ
   (apodosis) is **deleted** — the classical suspended-apodosis device (see §4).
2. **The toil-address (v 6)** — the universal human condition: *kadḥ* (relentless toil) ending in
   the inescapable *liqāʾ* (meeting) with the Lord. The pivot from cosmos to the individual.
3. **The judgment diptych (vv 7-15)** — the two fates as antithesis (*muqābala*):
   right-hand/easy/joyful-return (vv 7-9) vs behind-back/destruction/blaze/doomed-past-joy (vv 10-15).
4. **The oath-and-declaration (vv 16-19)** — a three-term oath (*shafaq* / night-and-what-it-gathers
   / moon-at-the-full) sworn to the truth of *la-tarkabunna ṭabaqan ʿan ṭabaq* — graduated
   stage-by-stage transformation (variously read as the stages of the Day, of human life, or of the
   heaven's tearing).
5. **The rebuke + sajda (vv 20-23)** — incredulity at the deniers who neither believe nor prostrate
   at the recited Qurʾān (v 21 = the sajda verse); their denial and God's knowledge of what they conceal.
6. **The seal (vv 24-25)** — painful tiding for the deniers, with the believer-exception and the
   *ajr ghayr mamnūn* (unfailing reward) as the surah's last word.

## 3. Content register and vocabulary distinctness

- **Register:** purely **eschatological** (cosmic-event + judgment + oath + warning). No legal,
  narrative-prophet, or creedal-capsule material. This is the textbook juzʾ-30 *tarhīb/tabshīr* mode.
- **Vocabulary distinctness:** 52 distinct roots over 25 short verses (high lexical turnover for the
  length). The surah carries the **corpus-EXACT root k-d-ḥ** (Q 84:6, the only verse in the corpus
  with this root — Q084-F-02). It also carries rare eschatological lexemes: *thubūr* (th-b-r,
  "ruinous destruction"), *yaḥūr* (Ḥ-w-r, "to return" — glossed by ʿIkrima as Ethiopic), *ṭabaq*
  (ṭ-b-q, "stage/layer"), *shafaq* (sh-f-q, "twilight afterglow"), *wasaq/ittasaq* (w-s-q,
  "gather/be-full").

## 4. The suspended-apodosis cascade (the surah's structural signature)

Q 84 opens with the eschatological *idhā* "when…" cascade — but the **jawāb al-sharṭ (apodosis) is
deleted**. al-Zamakhsharī (*al-Kashshāf*, on vv 1-5): *"ḥudhifa jawāb idhā li-yadhhaba al-muqaddar
kull madhhab — aw iktifāʾan bi-mā ʿulima fī mithlihā min sūratay al-Takwīr wa-l-Infiṭār"* ("the
apodosis of *idhā* is deleted so that the implied [answer] ranges over every possibility — or as
sufficient by what is known in the like of it from al-Takwīr and al-Infiṭār"). al-Rāzī catalogues
**six** scholarly positions on what the missing apodosis is (al-Kashshāf's deliberate-deletion;
al-Farrāʾ's known-from-repetition; al-Qāḍī's *fa-ʾkdaḥ*; al-Kisāʾī's *fa-ammā man ūtiya kitābahu*
[= v 7]; the *fa-mulāqīh* reading; and the *taqdīm/taʾkhīr* reading).

**Project connection (H-NEW-2250 Limit 2).** H-NEW-2250's grammatical idhā-cascade detector
*fragments* Q 84's opening because vv 2 and 4 begin *wa*-VERB (*wa-adhinat*, *wa-alqat*) rather than
*wa-idhā* — so the grammatical run is {v1}, {v3}, broken by the comment-verses, unlike the unbroken
*wa-idhā…wa-idhā…* runs of Q 81 and Q 82. Semantically Q 84's vv 1-5 are one cascade
(protasis-comment-protasis-comment-refrain); grammatically they are fragments. The
protasis-comment-protasis interleave with a verbatim refrain (v 2 ≡ v 5) is the structural feature
that distinguishes Q 84 from its sibling idhā-openers — al-Inshiqāq narrates the cosmos's
*response* (it gives ear and obeys) interleaved with the *events*, where al-Takwīr and al-Infiṭār
stack pure events. (Queued as Q084-F-04, NOT yet tested.)

## 5. Repetition patterns

- **Intra-surah verbatim refrain:** v 2 ≡ v 5 = *wa-adhinat li-rabbihā wa-ḥuqqat* (verified verbatim
  on disk; appears NOWHERE ELSE in the corpus — a Q 84-internal refrain bracketing the two cosmic
  protases). The first attaches to the heaven (v 1-2), the second to the earth (v 3-5); al-Rāzī notes
  *"idhā ikhtalafa wajhu al-kalāmi lam yakun takrāran"* (when the aspect of the speech differs it is
  not [mere] repetition) — the heaven *gives ear* to its own splitting, the earth to its own disgorging.
- **The masrūr echo:** *masrūrā* (root srr) closes BOTH arm A (v 9, the joyful return) and arm B
  (v 13, the past worldly joy now doomed) — the surah's most pointed lexical antithesis. Ibn Zayd
  (in al-Qurṭubī) reads it as the reversal: the godly grieved in the world and rejoice hereafter
  (cross-ref Q 52:26), the wicked rejoiced in the world (*kāna fī ahlihi masrūrā*) and grieve hereafter.

## 6. Cross-surah content references

- **vv 1-5 ↔ Q 81 (al-Takwīr) + Q 82 (al-Infiṭār):** al-Zamakhsharī explicitly cross-references the
  cosmic-rending cascades. The three are the *suwar al-idhā* eschatological-opener cluster (H-NEW-1200).
- **v 10 (book *warāʾa ẓahrih*) ↔ Q 69:25 (al-Ḥāqqa, book *bi-shimālih*):** al-Rāzī harmonizes — the
  left hand IS behind the back (al-Kalbī), or different parties receive in different ways.
- **v 6 (*fa-mulāqīh*) ↔ the Jibrīl hadith:** "*wa-ʿmal mā shiʾta fa-innaka mulāqīh*" (Ibn Kathīr,
  via Jābir) — the verse-final *fa-mulāqīh* is the textual hook of that prophetic saying.
- **v 8 (*ḥisāban yasīrā*) ↔ the ʿĀʾisha "easy reckoning = ʿarḍ not munāqasha" hadith** (Bukhārī,
  Muslim, Tirmidhī, Nasāʾī — see `04-hadith-corpus.md`). This is the most-cited Q 84 verse in hadith.
- **v 21 (sajda) ↔ Q 96:19 (al-ʿAlaq sajda):** the two are recited-and-prostrated-together in the
  Abū Hurayra/Abū Rāfiʿ tradition (Muslim, Tirmidhī, Nasāʾī, Ibn Mājah, Abū Dāwūd).

## 7. Honest limits

- Root assignments are QAC v0.4; the verbatim-refrain claim (v2≡v5) is a no-tashkeel orthographic
  identity (verified on disk) — under full-tashkeel the two are also identical (no internal vowel
  divergence). The corpus-uniqueness of the refrain is a no-tashkeel orthographic-token result.
- The block boundaries (arm A = vv 7-9, arm B = vv 10-15) follow the two *ammā* protases (v 7, v 10);
  this is the boundary used in Q084-F-03 and matches al-Qurṭubī's and al-Rāzī's pericope segmentation
  (vv 6-9, then 10-15).

## 1. Macro-structure (3 blocks)

Q 100 has a **3-block macro-structure** that is corpus-distinctive: each block is **internally rhyme-uniform** (alif/dāl/rāʾ respectively), the boundaries are **thematic-and-rhyme-coincident**, and the verse-counts split 5+3+3 = 11. The block-divisions emerge from rhetorical-shift markers (oath-completion at v.6 *inna al-insāna*; eschatological-shift at v.9 *afa-lā yaʿlamu*).

| Block | vv. | Length | Theme | Rhyme |
|:--|:-:|:-:|:--|:-:|
| **A** | 1-5 | 5 | OATH (5-element hybrid: 3 participles + 2 verb-continuations) | -ā |
| **B** | 6-8 | 3 | HUMAN INGRATITUDE (kanūd / shahīd / shadīd) | -d |
| **C** | 9-11 | 3 | ESCHATOLOGY (qubūr / ṣudūr / khabīr) | -r |

**Length distribution**: A(5) → B(3) → C(3) — descending then constant. **Block A** establishes the cosmic backdrop; **Block B** diagnoses human nature; **Block C** delivers eschatological consequence. The structure is a classical *qiṣṣat al-insān* (story of human nature) compressed into 11 verses.

**Rhyme-zone tripartition** (see `00-overview.md` §6, `06-novel-findings.md` Q100-F-02): the 3-block content structure is **EXACTLY mirrored in the rhyme-zone tripartition**. This is corpus-distinctive — most short surahs are monorhyme; Q 100's 3-zone structure with content-alignment is structurally unique.

## 2. Verse-by-verse content summary

### Block A (vv. 1-5): THE 5-ELEMENT HYBRID OATH-CLUSTER

#### Sub-block A1 — 3 participle-openers (vv. 1-3)

- v. 1: *wa-l-ʿādiyāti ḍabḥā* — "By the chargers, panting (in their gallop)."
- v. 2: *fa-l-mūriyāti qadḥā* — "By those striking sparks of fire (in their striking)."
- v. 3: *fa-l-mughīrāti ṣubḥā* — "By the dawn-raiders (in [the] morning)."

The 3-fold sequence (active-feminine-plural-participle + cognate-or-temporal-accusative) opens with *wa-l-* and continues with *fa-l-* coordinations. The pattern is the **canonical** Class-A-style pure-participle oath-cluster opener (cf. Q 37, Q 51, Q 77, Q 79). The classical reading: **war-horses on a raid** — galloping in the night (panting from gallop), striking sparks from the rocks with their iron-shod hooves, raiding at dawn.

The *al-mūriyāti qadḥā* — "those striking sparks" — is the corpus's most striking image of cavalry-warfare in early-Meccan revelation. The fire-spark image links the surah to fire-imagery (the corpus's *fire-light* family includes other rare roots — see `findings/phase-b-hypotheses/fire-light-*.csv`).

The transition from *wa-l-* (v. 1) to *fa-l-* (vv. 2-3) is the standard *fa-* causative-coordinator: each successive participle is the **temporal-causal sequel** of the previous.

#### Sub-block A2 — 2 verb-continuations (vv. 4-5) — UNIQUE STRUCTURE

- v. 4: *fa-atharna bi-hi naqʿā* — "Then they raise [up], by it (the gallop), dust."
- v. 5: *fa-wasaṭna bi-hi jamʿā* — "Then they penetrate-to-the-middle, by it, [the] gathering."

These two verses **break** the participle-pattern of vv 1-3 and shift to **3F.PL perfect-tense verbs** (*atharna*, *wasaṭna*) connected by **bi-hi** ("by it"). This is the **corpus-EXACT 1-of-1** hybrid oath-architecture (see Q100-F-01 in `06-novel-findings.md`).

Reading: the cosmic-horses (subject of the participles in vv 1-3) — denoted "they" in F.PL — now PERFORM ACTIONS (vv 4-5):
- *atharna naqʿā* = "they raised up [much] dust"
- *wasaṭna jamʿā* = "they penetrated to the middle of [the foe-]gathering"

The *bi-hi* refers ANAPHORICALLY back to the action-stem implied by the participle-cluster — that is, to the cavalry-action itself ("by means of [their] gallop / dawn-raid").

| accusative | *jamʿā* | masc.acc | "[foe-]gathering, [enemy-]assemblage" |

The classical reading (al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, Ibn Kathīr): **the 5-element oath-cluster describes the full cavalry-attack sequence** — gallop (*ḍabḥ*) → flint-spark from hooves (*qadḥ*) → dawn-raid (*ṣubḥ-an*) → dust-cloud raised by the gallop (*naqʿ*) → penetration to the heart of the enemy-gathering (*jamʿ*). The 5-element structure is a **micro-narrative of the cavalry-charge**, structurally unique among oath-clusters.

al-Bāqillānī notes (in his *Iʿjāz* commentary) that this 5-element oath is the corpus's most *complete* cavalry-oath: each verse depicts a different temporal-stage of the same action (gallop → spark → dawn → dust → penetration), making the surah's opening a **time-compressed cinematic image**. This is why the 3-participle → 2-verb-continuation hybrid is essential: the participles establish the AGENTS (vv 1-3 = "the chargers", "the strikers", "the raiders"); the verbs depict the AGENTS' RESULTANT ACTIONS (vv 4-5 = "they raised [dust]", "they penetrated [the foe-gathering]").

The 5-element oath-cluster ends with *jamʿā* (v. 5 = "the [foe-]gathering"); v. 6 begins with the SAME root *jmʿ* if the *jamʿā* of v.5 is the antecedent of *al-insāna* in v.6 — but this is a stretch. Classical reading: vv 1-5 are the SWORN-BY (*muqsam bihi*); vv 6-8 introduce the SWORN-ABOUT (*muqsam ʿalayhi*).

### Block B (vv. 6-8): HUMAN INGRATITUDE (KANŪD / SHAHĪD / SHADĪD)

The transition: **the cosmic cavalry having charged → the human is sworn-AGAINST** as ungrateful.

- v. 6: *inna al-insāna li-rabbihi la-kanūd* — "Indeed, mankind is, towards his Lord, ungrateful (kanūd)."
- v. 7: *wa-innahu ʿalā dhālika la-shahīd* — "And indeed he is, to that [his ingratitude], a witness."
- v. 8: *wa-innahu li-ḥubbi al-khayri la-shadīd* — "And indeed he is, in love-of-the-good [wealth], intense (in greed)."

The triplet uses a **3-fold parallel emphasis-construction**: *inna ... la-* + adjective. Each verse is a moral-diagnostic statement.

#### v. 6 — *kanūd* the ingratitude-diagnosis

*Kanūd* is corpus-hapax (K-N-D root, only here). al-Khalīl b. Aḥmad in *Kitāb al-ʿAyn* defines *kanūd* as "one who blocks-up, denies-his-good, ungrateful, niggardly". al-Suyūṭī *al-Itqān* nawʿ on *al-mutaḍādd* lists *kanūd* as one of the corpus's distinctive moral-diagnostic terms.

al-Ṭabarī cites Ibn ʿAbbās's gloss: *kanūd* = the one who counts the calamities of his life and forgets the favors. al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī's gloss: *kanūd* = the one who eats alone and denies-his-ration to others. al-Rāzī (*Mafātīḥ al-ghayb*) lists FIVE classical glosses of *kanūd* and reconciles: the underlying common-element is "blockage of gratitude-flow".

The *li-rabbihi* ("towards his Lord") localizes the ingratitude — it is **specifically directed at the Provider**. This is the diagnostic point: the human, who receives provision from God, is *kanūd* against precisely the Source of his provision.

#### v. 7 — *shahīd* the self-witness

*Wa-innahu ʿalā dhālika la-shahīd* — "and he is, to that, a witness." Two readings:
1. **(Ibn Kathīr, al-Ṭabarī majority)**: he is a witness — he himself, by his actions, testifies against his own ingratitude. The *shahīd* is the human himself; he writes-his-own-confession.
2. **(al-Rāzī minority)**: God is the witness over the human's ingratitude. *Shahīd* = "witnessing one"; God is referred-to obliquely.

The dominant reading is (1): the human is THE witness AGAINST his own ingratitude. This frames v. 6 as a diagnostic accusation that the human himself confesses-to (by his actions).

#### v. 8 — *shadīd* the love-of-good intensity

*Wa-innahu li-ḥubbi al-khayri la-shadīd* — "and he is, in love-of-the-good, intense (in greed)."

*Al-khayr* literally is "the good", but classical exegesis (al-Ṭabarī, al-Zamakhsharī, al-Rāzī) reads *al-khayr* here as **al-māl, al-thurwa** — "wealth, material-good". The construction *li-ḥubbi al-khayri la-shadīd* = "regarding love-of-wealth, [he is] intense" = "he is intense in his love of wealth" = "greed".

This is the **paradox-resolution of v. 6**: the human is *kanūd* (ungrateful TO HIS LORD), and yet *shadīd* (intense IN LOVE OF WEALTH). The two are bound: the wealth-love crowds out the gratitude-due.

The triple-pattern *kanūd / shahīd / shadīd* is a **3-adjective doxological-diagnostic** moving through (1) wrong-disposition (kanūd) → (2) self-witnessing (shahīd) → (3) explanatory-cause (shadīd love-of-wealth). This is classical *bayān al-iʿjāz* economy: 3 verses, 3 diagnostic-adjectives, perfect rhyme on -d.

### Block C (vv. 9-11): ESCHATOLOGY (QUBŪR / ṢUDŪR / KHABĪR)

The transition: **the diagnosis of ingratitude → the eschatological reckoning**.

- v. 9: *afa-lā yaʿlamu idhā buʿthira mā fī al-qubūr* — "Does he not know — when what is in the graves is scattered up?"
- v. 10: *wa-ḥuṣṣila mā fī al-ṣudūr* — "And what is in the breasts is gathered-and-distinguished?"
- v. 11: *inna rabbahum bi-him yawma'idhin la-khabīr* — "Indeed, their Lord, on that day, is well-acquainted with them."

The triplet uses a **3-fold rhetorical question** (vv 9-10) followed by a **divine-name-affirmation** (v. 11). The structure: *afa-lā yaʿlamu* opens v. 9 as a probing question; v. 10 continues the question's content; v. 11 delivers the answer.

#### v. 9 — *al-qubūr* the resurrection from graves

*Idhā buʿthira mā fī al-qubūr* — "when what is in the graves is scattered up." The verb *buʿthira* is from B-Ē-Th-R root, an extension of B-Ē-Th "to send"; *bʿthr* in passive = "is scattered out, is dispersed". The image: the graves are inverted, their contents (bodies, relics) scattered up.

This is one of the corpus's most vivid resurrection-images. Q 99:2 (al-Zalzala) has *wa-akhrajat al-arḍu athqālahā* ("and the earth puts forth its burdens") — a parallel-image immediately preceding Q 100. The Q 99→100 mushaf-adjacency is thematically continuous: both surahs depict the cosmic resurrection-event, with Q 100 anchoring the human-greed-context.

#### v. 10 — *al-ṣudūr* the gathering-and-distinguishing of breasts

*Wa-ḥuṣṣila mā fī al-ṣudūr* — "and what is in the breasts is gathered-and-distinguished." The verb *ḥuṣṣila* is corpus-hapax (Ḥ-Ṣ-L root, only here in this passive form-II). *Ḥaṣala* = "to be netted, to be gathered, to be sifted out, to be distinguished".

Classical exegesis (al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī): the *ṣadr* ("breast") is the seat of the heart, intentions, secret-thoughts. The eschatological event reveals/sifts/distinguishes the contents of the breasts — making manifest what was hidden in the human's interior.

The verse-pair vv 9-10 forms a **bipartite resurrection-diptych**:
- v. 9: external resurrection (bodies from graves)
- v. 10: internal resurrection (intentions from breasts)

#### v. 11 — *khabīr* the divine-attribute affirmation

*Inna rabbahum bi-him yawma'idhin la-khabīr* — "Indeed, their Lord, on that day, is well-acquainted with them."

*Khabīr* is one of the *asmāʾ al-ḥusnā* (the beautiful divine names) — "the All-Aware, the Well-Acquainted". The verse closes the eschatological-diptych with the divine-name affirmation: God, on that day, *knows* — the resurrected bodies and the sifted-distinguished intentions are all in the divine cognition.

The verse-pair *inna ... la-khabīr* mirrors the diagnostic-triplet's *inna ... la-X* construction in vv 6-8: the surah's structural rhyme is the **inna...la-** parallel-construction, used 4 times total (vv 6, 7, 8, 11). This is a corpus-distinctive density of *inna...la-* construction.

The 3-block macro is structured by:
1. **Rhetorical structure**: oath-cluster (A) → diagnostic-triplet (B) → eschatological-diptych+name-affirmation (C)
2. **Rhyme-zone**: -ā / -d / -r perfect tripartition
3. **Subject-shift**: cosmic-cavalry → human → divine-judgment
4. **Inna...la- construction density**: 4 of 11 verses (vv 6, 7, 8, 11) — 36% density of inna+la-, corpus-rank-1 for any 11-verse window

The structural alignment of all 4 layers (rhetoric, rhyme, subject, inna-density) makes Q 100 **the most tightly-architected short-surah** in the H-NEW-1070 cluster.

## 3. Thematic profile

### 3.1 The horse-imagery and its ethical inversion

Block A's cavalry-imagery functions as an **ethical exemplar** that vv 6-8 invert:
- The horses are *DISCIPLINED, COSTLY, CONCENTRATED* — they sacrifice (panting), they suffer (sparks from hooves), they obey (raid at dawn), they generate-uncomfortable-things (dust), they penetrate-the-enemy.
- The human is *LAZY, GRABBING, UNCONCENTRATED* — he is *kanūd* (blocking gratitude), *shahīd* (witness against himself), *shadīd* (intense in self-love-of-wealth).

The contrast is moral-rhetorical: cosmic-cavalry sacrifices itself for the cause; the human grabs for self-comfort. This is the surah's *qarīna* (rhetorical-thrust). al-Rāzī devotes 4 pages to this contrast in *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* vol. 32.

### 3.2 The kanūd-shadīd dialectic (greed ↔ ingratitude)

Block B's 3-verse diagnosis frames human ingratitude as a function of greed:
- *Kanūd* (v. 6) = ungrateful — the disposition
- *Shahīd* (v. 7) = self-witness — the consequence
- *Shadīd* (v. 8) = intense-in-love-of-wealth — the cause

The CAUSE is greed (v. 8), the DISPOSITION is ingratitude (v. 6), the CONSEQUENCE is self-witnessing (v. 7). This is a 3-fold causal-chain encoded in a 3-verse triplet.

The triplet's semantic-structure parallels the structural triplet in **Q 102 al-Takāthur** (*alhākum al-takāthur* — "rivalry-in-acquisition has distracted you"), in **Q 104 al-Humaza** (*kullu humazatin lumaza* — "every slanderer-and-belittler"), and in **Q 107 al-Māʿūn** (*alladhī yadhukku al-yatīm* — "who pushes the orphan"). All four are early-Meccan greed-rebuke surahs anchored in the human-greed context. Q 100, Q 102, Q 104, Q 107 form an **early-Meccan greed-rebuke cluster** (visible in H-NEW-111 FR-neighbors — Q 100's top-15 includes Q 102 rank 13, Q 104 rank 10, Q 107 rank 9).

### 3.3 The eschatology-diptych and the divine-cognition

Block C's structure:
- v. 9: *afa-lā yaʿlamu idhā buʿthira mā fī al-qubūr*  → external resurrection (bodies)
- v. 10: *wa-ḥuṣṣila mā fī al-ṣudūr* → internal resurrection (intentions)
- v. 11: *inna rabbahum bi-him yawma'idhin la-khabīr* → divine-cognition affirmation

The surah ENDS by affirming **divine khabīr** ("All-Aware") with the explicit *yawma'idhin* ("on that day") locator. This is structurally the eschatological *jawāb al-qasam* — the answer to the implicit question of vv 6-8 (does the *kanūd* think he gets away with it?): NO, the divine *khabīr* knows him on that day.

The -r rhyme zone (qubūr / ṣudūr / khabīr) is **theologically purposeful**: external-resurrection (qubūr "graves") + internal-revelation (ṣudūr "breasts") + divine-cognition (khabīr "well-acquainted") — the three poles of judgment-day knowledge.

## 4. Lexical fingerprint

5 corpus-hapax roots in 11 verses (rank 4/114 by per-verse density; see `01-empirical-profile.md` §9):
- *Ḍ-B-Ḥ* (v. 1, *ḍabḥā*) — horse-panting; classical-lexical-marker for war-horses
- *Q-D-Ḥ* (v. 2, *qadḥā*) — flint-spark-striking; cavalry-warfare imagery
- *N-Q-Ē* (v. 4, *naqʿā*) — fine dust raised by hooves
- *K-N-D* (v. 6, *kanūd*) — ungrateful, niggardly; corpus-distinctive moral-diagnostic
- *Ḥ-Ṣ-L* (v. 10, *ḥuṣṣila*) — gathered-and-distinguished; eschatological technical term

Q 100 is the corpus's densest hapax-surah of length 11+. The 5 hapax-roots distribute across all 3 rhyme-zones (3 in oath-block, 1 in human-block, 1 in eschatology-block) — making Q 100's lexical-uniqueness *structurally distributed*.

## 5. Rhetorical economy

11 verses, 40 words, ~163 letters. By any measure, Q 100 is **maximally compressed**:
- Average words/verse = 3.6 (very low — among the corpus's shortest)
- Per-verse hapax = 0.45 (rank 4/114)
- Rhetorical-block density = 3 macro-blocks in 11 verses (extremely tight)
- Rhyme-zone density = 3 distinct rhyme-zones in 11 verses (corpus-distinctive)
- Cross-block coherence = 4 instances of *inna...la-* construction (vv 6, 7, 8, 11 — 36% density)
- Hapax distributed across all 3 zones (uniform structural-coverage)

This is the rhetorical-economy of a **structurally-tight short-mufaṣṣal-qiṣār surah** (H-NEW-630 cluster C member). Q 100 is one of the corpus's most architecturally-tight surahs by any structural metric.

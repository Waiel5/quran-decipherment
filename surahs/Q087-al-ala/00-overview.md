- Q 75:16-19 *lā tuḥarrik bihi lisānaka li-taʿjala bihi inna ʿalaynā jamʿahu wa-qurʾānahu fa-idhā qaraʾnāhu fa-ttabiʿ qurʾānah* — the well-known classical *jamʿ-and-qurʾān* collection-formula (Late Meccan, Nöldeke 27).
- Q 20:114 *wa-lā taʿjal bi-l-qurʾāni min qabli an yuqḍā ilayka waḥyuh* — companion-verse.

Q 87:6's *sa-nuqriʾuka fa-lā tansā* is **the first chronological occurrence** (rev-order #8) of the divine assurance that the Prophet's *qirāʾah* will be eternally preserved. The companion verse Q 87:7 *illā mā shāʾa llāhu innahu yaʿlamu l-jahra wa-mā yakhfā* is the famous *istithnāʾ* (exception clause) classical theology engaged extensively (al-Rāzī vol 31, pp 142-147; al-Suyūṭī *al-Itqān* nawʿ 47 *al-makkī wa-l-madanī*).

**Classical-balāgha verdict**: Q 87:6 is the corpus's **earliest self-referential recitation guarantee** addressed to the Prophet. The placement at v 6 of a 19-verse Early-Meccan surah (revealed BEFORE the Hijra per Bukhārī 4788) constitutes a **structural anchor** for the divine commitment to *jamʿ al-qurʾān*: the imperative to *glorify* (v 1) is followed by the divine commitment to *secure the recitation* (v 6) — establishing a theological fundament: *tasbīḥ* (worship-direction) + *jamʿ* (preservation-of-recitation) jointly anchor the surah and, by extension, the Qurʾānic project as a whole.

## 12. Classical commentary lineage

Major classical tafsir on Q 87 (full survey: [`03-tafsir-survey.md`](03-tafsir-survey.md)):

- **al-Ṭabarī** (`razi-mafatih-al-ghayb.openiti.raw.txt` referenced in cross-cit): Companion-narration tradition aggregating; treats v 6 *sa-nuqriʾuka* as the *jamʿ al-qurʾān* foundational verse with extensive qirāʾāt-discussion of the *fa-lā tansā* form.
- **al-Zamakhsharī** *al-Kashshāf*: balāgha treatment; foregrounds the imperative form as the first commanding-utterance directed at the Prophet (companion to the *iqraʾ* of Q 96).
- **al-Rāzī** *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* vol 27 (Q 87) and vol 29 (cross-citation under Q 53:36-37) — **the most extensive classical engagement with the order-reversal puzzle** of *ṣuḥuf-Ibrāhīm-wa-Mūsā* vs *ṣuḥuf-Mūsā-wa-Ibrāhīm*. al-Rāzī gives 4 wajh; the predominant classical reading is *naẓm-tartīb* deliberate inversion.
- **al-Qurṭubī** *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān*: legal-emphasis (the ṣalāh in v 15 *wa-dhakara sma rabbihi fa-ṣallā* — five-prayer institution proof; the ʿīd-fitr connection via *qad aflaḥa man tazakkā* understood as *zakāt al-fiṭr* per ʿUmar's reported view).
- **Ibn Kathīr** *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm*: hadith-anchored treatment; **records the Ibn ʿAbbās narration (via ʿIkrima → ʿAṭāʾ → al-Muʿtamir) that the Prophet himself said "all of [the surah's content] is in the *ṣuḥuf-Ibrāhīm-wa-Mūsā*" upon revelation**.
- **al-Biqāʿī** *Naẓm al-Durar*: **munāsaba** treatment placing Q 86 al-Ṭāriq → Q 87 al-Aʿlā as a tight rhetorical pair on the cosmological-warning + tasbīḥ axis; reads the seam as exemplifying the *tilka l-ʿitāqu l-uwalu* property (early-Meccan compositional unit).

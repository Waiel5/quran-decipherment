# Q 3 Āl ʿImrān — Content Analysis

Arabic verse text from `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/quran-text/quran-no-tashkeel.json` (Q 3, 200 verses).
This is descriptive content analysis; numerical claims are cross-referenced to the empirical profile. Because
Āl ʿImrān is a 200-verse surah, the analysis is by thematic block rather than every verse.

## 1. Block-by-block summary

**Block 1 — Creedal opening (vv 1-9).**
v 1 opens with the muqaṭṭaʿāt الم (*alif-lām-mīm*); v 2 *Allāhu lā ilāha illā huwa al-ḥayy al-qayyūm* (the
Living, the Self-Subsisting — the al-ism-al-aʿẓam formula shared with Q 2:255). vv 3-4 affirm that the Book
*confirms* (*muṣaddiqan*) the Torah and Gospel, and introduces *al-furqān*. **v 7** is the pivot: the Book
contains *āyāt muḥkamāt* (clear, decisive — *hunna umm al-kitāb*, "the mother of the Book") and *ukhar
mutashābihāt* (others ambiguous); those with *zaygh* (deviation) in their hearts pursue the mutashābih
"seeking fitna and seeking its interpretation," while *al-rāsikhūn fī al-ʿilm* say "we believe in it." vv 8-9
are the supplication *rabbanā lā tuzigh qulūbanā* (do not let our hearts deviate).

**Block 2 — Warning to the disbelievers; the Badr allusion (vv 10-20).**
vv 10-13 warn the disbelievers (Pharaoh-typology) and allude to the two parties that met at Badr (*fiʾatāni
iltaqatā*). vv 14-17 contrast the *love of desires* (*zuyyina li-l-nās ḥubb al-shahawāt*) with what is with
God, naming the patient, truthful, devout, and those who seek forgiveness before dawn. vv 18-20 give God's own
witness to His oneness and the keystone *inna al-dīna ʿinda Allāhi al-islām* ("the religion with God is islām").

**Block 3 — People of the Book; the Āl ʿImrān / Maryam / Zakariyyā narrative (vv 21-63).**
The surah's name-block. vv 33-34: God chose Ādam, Nūḥ, *āl Ibrāhīm wa-āl ʿImrān*. vv 35-37: the wife of ʿImrān
vows her unborn child; Maryam is born and entrusted to Zakariyyā; the mihrāb sustenance. vv 38-41: Zakariyyā's
prayer for Yaḥyā. vv 42-51: the annunciation to Maryam, the birth of ʿĪsā, his miracles. vv 52-57: the
disciples (*al-ḥawāriyyūn*); the plot against ʿĪsā and God's raising of him. vv 59-60: the Ādam-ʿĪsā analogy
(*inna mathala ʿĪsā ʿinda Allāh ka-mathali Ādam*). **v 61**: the *mubāhala* verse — *fa-naqul ʿalā kadhibīna
laʿnata Allāh* (the mutual-imprecation challenge to the Najrān Christians). vv 64: *taʿālaw ilā kalimatin
sawāʾin* — the call to the common word.

**Block 4 — Polemic with the People of the Book; Ibrāhīm; the Kaʿba (vv 64-99).**
Argument that Ibrāhīm was neither Jew nor Christian but a *ḥanīf muslim* (v 67). vv 96-97: the first house
established for mankind is *bi-Bakka* (Bakka = Mecca), with the *maqām Ibrāhīm* and the *ḥajj* obligation.

**Block 5 — The believing community charge (vv 100-120).**
*wa-ʿtaṣimū bi-ḥabli Allāhi jamīʿan wa-lā tafarraqū* (hold fast to the rope of God, all together; do not
divide, v 103). v 110: *kuntum khayra ummatin ukhrijat li-l-nās* (you are the best community brought forth).

**Block 6 — The Uḥud battle pericope (vv 121-179).**
The long central narrative: the morning the Prophet positioned the believers for battle (v 121); the angels'
support (*musawwimīn*, v 125); the wound of Uḥud and the lesson of the *qarḥ* (v 140); the rebuke of those who
fled; *wa-mā Muḥammadun illā rasūl* (Muḥammad is but a messenger; if he dies or is killed, will you turn back?
v 144); the martyrs as living with their Lord (vv 169-171).

**Block 7 — Closing exhortations and the running creed (vv 180-200).**
Warnings against miserliness; the trial of wealth and life; the People-of-the-Book covenant. **vv 190-194**:
the famous *ūlū al-albāb* passage — *inna fī khalqi al-samāwāti wa-l-arḍi wa-khtilāfi al-layli wa-l-nahāri
la-āyātin li-ūlī al-albāb* (in the creation of the heavens and earth and the alternation of night and day are
signs for those of understanding) — followed by the supplication *rabbanā mā khalaqta hādhā bāṭilan*. The
surah CLOSES (v 200) on *yā ayyuhā alladhīna āmanū iṣbirū wa-ṣābirū wa-rābiṭū* — patience, perseverance, and
*ribāṭ* (steadfast guarding), the ribāṭ-close.

## 2. Thematic macro-structure

| Block | Verses | Register | Theme |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| A. Creed + muḥkam/mutashābih | 1-20 | creedal/epistemic | the Book's self-typology; islām as the religion with God |
| B. Narrative (Āl ʿImrān, Maryam, ʿĪsā, mubāhala) | 21-63 | narrative | the surah's name-block; the Najrān challenge |
| C. Polemic + Ibrāhīm + Kaʿba | 64-99 | polemic/legal | People-of-the-Book argument; ḥajj |
| D. Community charge | 100-120 | exhortatory | unity (the rope of God); best-community |
| E. Uḥud pericope | 121-179 | narrative | the battle, the defeat, the lesson |
| F. Closing wisdom + creed + ribāṭ | 180-200 | wisdom/du'āʾ | ūlū al-albāb; the running creed; the ribāṭ-close |

The surah's macro-architecture is a **creed → narrative → polemic → community → battle → wisdom** arc — a
fully Medinan composite welding doctrine (muḥkam/mutashābih, the oneness witness), sacred history (the house
of ʿImrān, ʿĪsā), inter-communal argument (the Najrān mubāhala, the common word), and lived crisis (Uḥud).
This composite register is exactly what makes Q 3 FR-distant from short surahs (z_content +1.69) yet
FR-nearest to its fellow long composites Q 2 and Q 4.

## 3. Content register

Mixed-composite: **creedal** (vv 1-20, 190-194), **narrative** (vv 21-63 nativity/annunciation; vv 121-179
Uḥud), **polemic-legal** (vv 64-120 People-of-the-Book; ḥajj), **exhortatory/du'āʾ** (vv 100-120, 195-200).
There is no early-Meccan oath-and-eschatology block; the register is the mature Medinan composite.

## 4. Vocabulary distinctness

- **Root *ʿ-l-m* (knowledge)** and **muḥkam/mutashābih** (root *ḥ-k-m*, *sh-b-h*) anchor the epistemic block.
- **Proper-name density**: Maryam, ʿImrān, Zakariyyā, Yaḥyā, ʿĪsā, Ādam, Ibrāhīm, Mūsā — Q 3 is one of the
  most prophet-name-dense surahs, sharing the nativity vocabulary with Q 19 Maryam.
- **The *rabbanā*-vocative supplication** recurs (vv 8, 16, 53, 147, 191-194) — a du'āʾ-refrain (queued as
  Q003-F-03 for a corpus-density test).
- **The Uḥud military lexicon** (*qarḥ*, *ribāṭ*, *shuhadāʾ*, *musawwimīn*) is concentrated in vv 121-179.
- **The *al-Ḥayy al-Qayyūm* formula** (v 2) is shared verbatim with Q 2:255 and Q 20:111 (queued Q003-F-04).

## 5. Repetition patterns

- **The *rabbanā* du'āʾ-refrain** (≥5 occurrences) structures the creedal and closing blocks.
- **The oneness-witness *lā ilāha illā huwa*** (vv 2, 6, 18) recurs as a creedal anchor.
- **The Ādam-ʿĪsā *mathal* frame** (v 59 *ka-mathali Ādam*) parallels the parable-frames of other surahs.
- **The *kuntum / antum* community-address** (vv 103, 110, 118) frames the exhortation block.

## 6. Cross-surah content references

- **Q 3:2 ≡ Q 2:255 (partial) ≡ Q 20:111** — the *al-Ḥayy al-Qayyūm* divine formula (the al-ism-al-aʿẓam locus,
  Tirmidhī #3562).
- **Maryam / ʿĪsā nativity (vv 35-59)** ↔ **Q 19 Maryam** ↔ **Q 21:91 / Q 66:12** — the breathing-of-the-Spirit
  and the chastity formula recur across the Maryam-block surahs.
- **Ibrāhīm as ḥanīf (v 67)** ↔ **Q 2:135, Q 16:120-123** — the millat-Ibrāhīm theme.
- **The rope of God / unity (v 103)** ↔ **Q 6:159, Q 30:32** — anti-tafarruq (sectarian division) warnings.
- **Q 3 ↔ Q 4 al-Nisāʾ** — the two adjacent long Medinan surahs continue: Q 3 closes on warfare/patience
  (Uḥud) and the believing-community charge; Q 4 opens on the family/inheritance law that governs that
  community (the seamless Q 3→Q 4 join, rank 4/113).

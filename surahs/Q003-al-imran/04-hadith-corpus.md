## 1. Faḍāʾil — al-Zahrāwān (al-Baqara + Āl ʿImrān as "the two luminous ones")

| **Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim** | **1766** | Book 6, *Ṣalāt al-Musāfirīn* | Abū Umāma al-Bāhilī | the *al-Zahrāwān* ḥadīth |
| **Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim** | **1768** | Book 6, *Ṣalāt al-Musāfirīn* | al-Nawwās b. Samʿān | parallel: "on the Day of Resurrection the Qurʾān and those who acted by it will be brought, al-Baqara and Āl ʿImrān leading" |

**Key text (Muslim #1766, Abū Umāma):** *"Recite the Qurʾān, for on the Day of Resurrection it will come as
an intercessor for those who recite it. Recite the two bright ones (*al-Zahrāwān*), **al-Baqara and Sūrat Āl
ʿImrān**, for on the Day of Resurrection they will come as two clouds (*ghamāmatān*), or two shades, or two
flocks of birds in ranks, pleading for those who recite them …"* — the canonical pairing of Q 2 + Q 3 as a
single faḍāʾil unit, the empirical correlate of their FR-nearest-neighbour status (FR 0.6309; see
`01-empirical-profile.md`).

## 2. The al-ism al-aʿẓam (Greatest Name) — Q 3:2 al-Ḥayy al-Qayyūm bridged to Q 2:255

**Key text (Tirmidhī #3562):** *"Allāh's Greatest Name (al-ism al-aʿẓam) is in these two āyāt: 'And your
deity is one deity; there is no god but He, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful' [Q 2:163] **and the opening
of Āl ʿImrān: 'Alif. Lām. Mīm. Allāh — there is no god but He, the Ever-Living (al-Ḥayy), the Self-Subsisting
(al-Qayyūm)' [Q 3:1-2].**"* — this ḥadīth makes Q 3:2 the explicit locus of the Greatest Name, paired with
the Q 2 creed. (Ibn Kathīr, on Q 3:2, cross-refers this to Āyat al-Kursī Q 2:255, which also carries
*al-Ḥayy al-Qayyūm*; see `03-tafsir-survey.md`.)

## 3. The muḥkam / mutashābih ḥadīth (Q 3:7) — beware the *ahl al-zaygh*

| **Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī** | **4342** | Book 65, *Tafsīr* | ʿĀʾisha | the recitation of Q 3:7 + "those who follow the ambiguous … beware of them" (verified on disk: *محكمات … فاحذروهم*) |
| **Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim** | **6610** | Book 47, *al-ʿIlm* | ʿĀʾisha | parallel of the same ʿĀʾisha narration |

**Key text (Bukhārī #4342, ʿĀʾisha):** the Prophet recited *huwa alladhī anzala ʿalayka al-kitāba minhu āyātun
muḥkamātun … wa-ukharu mutashābihāt …* and said: *"When you see those who follow what is ambiguous (*mā
tashābaha*) of it, those are the ones whom Allāh named [the deviant]; so beware of them (*fa-ḥdharūhum*)."* —
the prophetic gloss on Q 3:7, foundational to the ʿulūm-al-Qurʾān muḥkam/mutashābih discipline. (Bukhārī
#4547 is the Kitāb-al-Tafsīr placement under Sūrat Āl ʿImrān; #4342 the parallel — both verified on disk by
the Arabic *محكمات / فاحذروهم* text.)

## 4. The Najrān delegation + the Mubāhala (Q 3:61) — the occasion of the first 83 āyāt

| Collection | idInBook | Book (on-disk) | Narrator | Note |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī** | **4187** | Book 64, *al-Maghāzī* | Ḥudhayfa | al-ʿĀqib and al-Sayyid, the chiefs of Najrān, came intending *liʿān/mubāhala*; the "amīn of this umma" (Abū ʿUbayda b. al-Jarrāḥ) episode |
| **Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī** | **4188** | Book 64, *al-Maghāzī* | Ḥudhayfa | parallel: "the people of Najrān came … send us a trustworthy man" |
| **Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī** | **3582** | Book 62, *Faḍāʾil al-Ṣaḥāba* | Ḥudhayfa | the Najrān "trustworthy man" promise |
| **Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī** | **6980** | Book 95, *al-Aḥkām* (region) | Ḥudhayfa | parallel placement |

**Key text (Bukhārī #4187):** *"al-ʿĀqib and al-Sayyid, the rulers of Najrān, came to Allāh's Messenger ﷺ with
the intention of doing *liʿān* (mutual cursing — the mubāhala of Q 3:61). One of them said to the other: 'Do
not do this, for by Allāh, if he is a Prophet and we do this *liʿān*, neither we nor our offspring after us
will prosper.'"* This is the historical anchor of Ibn Kathīr's claim that the first 83 āyāt of Āl ʿImrān
respond to the Najrān delegation (year 9 Hijra; see `03-tafsir-survey.md`).

## 6. Faḍāʾil notes

The strongest Q-3-specific faḍāʾil is the *al-Zahrāwān* pairing (Q 2 + Q 3 together) — Q 3 is rarely given a
solo faḍīla in the 9-book set; its virtue-status is **paired** with al-Baqara, which is itself the empirical
correlate of the FR-nearest-neighbour relationship. Solo Āl-ʿImrān faḍāʾil chains (e.g. via al-Dārimī or the
faḍāʾil-al-Qurʾān sub-collections) are outside the surveyed 9-book set and are flagged as a data-gap, not as
an absence-of-evidence claim.

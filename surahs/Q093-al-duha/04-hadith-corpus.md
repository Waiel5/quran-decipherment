*Jibrīl*, *shayṭānuka*}.

## 1. The fatra (pause-of-revelation) asbāb — the canonical narration

The Prophet fell ill / Jibrīl was withheld (the *fatrat al-waḥy*); a woman taunted that his "devil" /
companion had abandoned him; the idolaters said *qad waddaʿa Muḥammadan rabbuhu wa-qalāhu* ("his Lord has
forsaken and hated him"); Allāh revealed *wa-l-ḍuḥā · wa-l-layl idhā sajā · mā waddaʿaka rabbuka wa-mā
qalā* (Q 93:1-3). The whole chain runs **al-Aswad b. Qays ← Jundub** (b. ʿAbdallāh al-Bajalī /
b. Sufyān — the same Companion).

**Key text — Bukhārī #4744 (Tafsīr Sūrat al-Ḍuḥā), al-Aswad b. Qays ← Jundub b. Sufyān:**
*"The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ fell ill and did not stand [for prayer] two or three nights. Then a woman came
and said, 'O Muḥammad, I think your devil has left you,' so Allāh revealed: {wa-l-ḍuḥā · wa-l-layl idhā
sajā · mā waddaʿaka rabbuka wa-mā qalā}."* — the load-bearing asbāb of vv 1-3.

**Tirmidhī #3429 grading:** the Arabic colophon on disk reads *"qāla Abū ʿĪsā: hādhā ḥadīth **ḥasan
ṣaḥīḥ**, wa-qad rawāhu Shuʿba wa-l-Thawrī ʿan al-Aswad b. Qays."* The *ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ* grade cited in
`00-overview.md` is verified from the on-disk text, not asserted.

## 2. Variant details in the asbāb (from the tafsīr/asbāb chains, cross-referenced)

The ḥadīth-collection narrations above give the **core** event (illness/withholding + taunt + Q 93:1-3).
The mufassirūn (see `03-tafsir-survey.md`) add details that are NOT in the 9-book chains and are flagged
here as tafsīr/asbāb-collection material, not Ṣaḥīḥayn ḥadīth:

- **The taunting woman = Umm Jamīl (wife of Abū Lahab):** al-Baghawī, *Maʿālim al-tanzīl* on Q 93:1
  (`spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-al-baghawi/93/1.json`) — *"wa-qīla: inna al-marʾa allatī qālat dhālik Umm
  Jamīl imraʾat Abī Lahab."* Reported as *qīla* (a weak/anonymous-attribution form), not as a Ṣaḥīḥ chain.
- **Cause of Jibrīl's withholding (the puppy under the bed):** al-Baghawī, same file, on the **Zayd b.
  Aslam** report. Also reported, not in the 9-book chains.
- **The withholding-period range:** al-Qurṭubī on Q 93:3 (`ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/93/3.json`) surveys
  Ibn Jurayj (12 days), Ibn ʿAbbās (15), "25", Muqātil (40). These are tafsīr-internal estimates.

## 3. The Q 93:5 *fa-tarḍā* / intercession ḥadīth (Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim, via al-Qurṭubī)

al-Qurṭubī on Q 93:5 (`ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/93/5.json`) cites a **Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim** ʿAbdallāh b. ʿAmr
narration glossing *wa-la-sawfa yuʿṭīka rabbuka fa-tarḍā*: the Prophet wept *"ummatī ummatī"* over the
Ibrāhīm (Q 14:36) / ʿĪsā (Q 5:118) verses, and Allāh sent Jibrīl with *"innā sa-nurḍīka fī ummatika
wa-lā nasūʾuka"* ("We shall satisfy you regarding your community and not grieve you"). This is the
*riḍā*/intercession reading of v 5. **Flagged:** al-Qurṭubī cites it as Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim by content; the
precise on-disk `idInBook` in the 9-book DB was not isolated for this verification pass (the verse-gloss is
carried by al-Qurṭubī on disk). It is reported here as a tafsīr-cited Muslim narration, not assigned a
9-book number from memory.

## 4. The "Q 93 + Q 94 single-revelation pair" claim — verified ABSENT in the 9 books

The classical position (Ṭāwūs, ʿUmar b. ʿAbd al-ʿAzīz, via al-Rāzī; cf. al-Suyūṭī) that al-Ḍuḥā and
al-Sharḥ are *one surah* / a single revelation event recited together was tested against the 9-book
corpus by `scripts/verify_fatra_hadith.py` (output `csv/Q093F04-fatra-hadith-audit.json`):

| Test | Method | Result |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **T1** fatra ḥadīth canonical | ≥3 al-Ḍuḥā markers in a narration | **7 hits** across Bukhārī (1092, 4744, 4745, 4776), Muslim (4525, 4526), Tirmidhī (3429) — CONFIRMED |
| **T2** Q 94 (al-Sharḥ) marker inside any fatra ḥadīth | Q 94 markers {*nashraḥ, ṣadraka, wizraka, ẓahraka, dhikraka, al-ʿusr yusran*} in the fatra hits | **0 hits — CONFIRMED ABSENT** |
| **T3** any single 9-book ḥadīth citing BOTH Q 93 and Q 94 markers | dual-marker scan across all 9 books | **0 hits — CONFIRMED ABSENT** |
| **T4** the Q 92 (al-Layl) faḍāʾil cluster | Muʿādh-ʿishāʾ markers {*Muʿādh, al-Aʿlā, wa-l-shams, yaghshā*} | **9 hits** — a SEPARATE cluster pairing Q 87 + Q 91 + Q 92, NOT Q 93/Q 94 |

**Headline (T1-T4):** the canonical fatra ḥadīth (Bukhārī #1092/#4744/#4745/#4776, Muslim #4525/#4526,
Tirmidhī #3429 *ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ*) cites **only Q 93:1-3 — never Q 94.** The "al-Ḍuḥā + al-Sharḥ
single-revelation / paired-recitation" tradition is a **classical exegetical-juristic position
(Ṭāwūs, ʿUmar b. ʿAbd al-ʿAzīz; al-Rāzī's report), NOT a ḥadīth-attested fact** in the canonical 9 books.
The empirical whole-surah cohesion of Q 93↔Q 94 (FR rank 4; TSP seam rank 10/113; corpus pair-rank
128/6441, `csv/Q093F01-pair-cohesion.json`) is therefore **independent empirical support for the same
intuition the scholars had — but it does not trace to a ṣaḥīḥ chain.** This is the key audited result.

## 5. Faḍāʾil / recitation

No surah-named *faḍāʾil* (virtue-of-reciting al-Ḍuḥā) ḥadīth was located in the on-disk 9-book set by the
marker search. (Consistent with the short-Meccan tail generally; faḍāʾil are concentrated on Q 1, 2, 18,
36, 55, 56, 67, 112, 113, 114.) The forenoon-**prayer** (*ṣalāt al-ḍuḥā*) ḥadīth (e.g., Tirmidhī #473,
#474, #476, #477 — verified on disk) concern the *ḍuḥā prayer*, not the recitation of *Sūrat* al-Ḍuḥā, and
are NOT asbāb of this surah. **Flagged as a data-gap** (a dedicated faḍāʾil sub-collection — e.g., Ibn
al-Ḍurays, al-Thaʿlabī's faḍāʾil chains — is outside the 9-book set), not an absence-of-evidence claim.

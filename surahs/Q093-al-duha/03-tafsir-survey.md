# Q 93 al-Ḍuḥā — Tafsīr Survey

Five+ classical commentaries surveyed from the on-disk corpus. Source files cited per passage. The two
project-relevant exegetical threads are (a) the *asbāb al-nuzūl* of vv 1-3 (the delayed revelation), and
(b) the favor→command parallel of vv 6-11 — which the mufassirūn read EXACTLY as Q093-F-01 Arm B
formalizes it.

## 1. al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān* (on Q 93:1, 3)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-al-tabari/93/{1,3}.json`.

**On v 1 (the oath):** al-Ṭabarī: "Our Lord swears by *al-ḍuḥā* — and it is the whole day (*al-nahār
kulluh*) … I reckon it from their saying *ḍaḥiya fulān li-l-shams* (he was exposed to the sun)." He
cross-refers to his own treatment of *wa-l-shams wa-ḍuḥāhā* (Q 91:1) and notes the secondary reading
(Qatāda: *al-ḍuḥā* = "an hour of the daytime").

**On v 3 (the jawāb al-qasam):** *mā waddaʿaka rabbuka wa-mā qalā* = "your Lord has not abandoned you,
O Muḥammad, nor hated you" (*mā tarakaka rabbuka wa-mā abghaḍaka*). al-Ṭabarī notes the *iktifāʾ*
(ellipsis): *wa-mā qalā* drops the object-pronoun (for *qalāka*), understood from the preceding
*waddaʿaka*, and preserved for the verse-end rhyme. He then gives the **asbāb**: the surah came down
"as a disavowal by Allāh of the Quraysh" who, when revelation was delayed, said "Muḥammad's Lord has
forsaken and hated him" — with the **Jundub al-Bajalī** isnād (al-Aswad b. Qays ← Jundub: a woman of his
household/people said *waddaʿa al-shayṭānu Muḥammadan*).

## 2. al-Baghawī, *Maʿālim al-tanzīl* (on Q 93:1)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-al-baghawi/93/1.json`.

al-Baghawī: "**Meccan** (*makkiyya*)." He gives the asbāb with a full isnād: al-Aswad b. Qays ← **Jundub
b. Sufyān**: "The Messenger fell ill and did not rise [for prayer] two or three nights; a woman came and
said, 'O Muḥammad, I hope your devil has left you — I have not seen him approach you for two or three
nights,' so Allāh revealed *wa-l-ḍuḥā … mā waddaʿaka rabbuka wa-mā qalā*." He records the **variant** that
the woman was **Umm Jamīl, the wife of Abū Lahab** (*wa-qīla: inna al-marʾa allatī qālat dhālik Umm Jamīl
imraʾat Abī Lahab*), and the **Zayd b. Aslam** report on the cause of Jibrīl's withholding (the puppy
under the Prophet's bed). He also reports the mufassirūn's account that the Jews questioned the Prophet
about Dhū al-Qarnayn, the Companions of the Cave, and the Spirit, and he said "I will tell you tomorrow"
without saying *in shāʾa Allāh*, so revelation was withheld.

## 3. al-Zamakhsharī, *al-Kashshāf* / al-Rāzī, *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* (the Q 93 ↔ Q 94 pairing, on Q 94:1)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/razi-mafatih-al-ghayb.openiti.raw.txt` (Sūrat al-Sharḥ
opening, ~line 260761).

al-Rāzī, opening Sūrat al-Sharḥ: "**Eight verses, Meccan. It is related from Ṭāwūs and ʿUmar b. ʿAbd
al-ʿAzīz that they used to say this surah and Sūrat al-Ḍuḥā are ONE surah (*sūra wāḥida*), and they would
recite them both in a single rakʿa and would not separate them with the basmala. What led them to that is
that His saying *alam nashraḥ laka* (Q 94:1) is like a continuation (*ka-l-ʿaṭf*) of *alam yajidka yatīman*
(Q 93:6)." — al-Rāzī then **REJECTS** the identification: "But it is not so, because the first [al-Ḍuḥā]
came down in a state of the Messenger's grief at the harm of the disbelievers — a state of trial and
constriction of the breast — whereas the second requires a state of expanded breast and a good heart at
the time of its descent; how could the two states combine?"

This is the **load-bearing classical anchor** for Q093-F-01 Arm A: the tradition pairs al-Ḍuḥā + al-Sharḥ
precisely at the **orphan-verse junction** (Q 93:6 → Q 94:1), and al-Rāzī's own reasoning treats them as a
candidate single unit. (al-Suyūṭī's *al-Itqān*, in the *nawʿ* on *al-munāsaba bayna al-suwar*, records the
same Ṭāwūs / ʿUmar-b.-ʿAbd-al-ʿAzīz report.)

## 4. al-Qurṭubī, *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān* (on Q 93:1, 3, 5, 11)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/93/{1,3,5,11}.json`.

**On v 1:** "Sūrat al-Ḍuḥā, **Meccan by agreement** (*makkiyya bi-ttifāq*), **eleven verses** (*iḥdā
ʿashrata āya*)." He glosses *al-ḍuḥā* as the daytime (paired with the night of v 2), with secondary
readings (Qatāda/Muqātil/Jaʿfar al-Ṣādiq: the forenoon Allāh spoke to Mūsā + the night of the Miʿrāj).

**On v 3:** the *jawāb al-qasam*; the asbāb (Jibrīl's delay) with the range of reports on the
withholding-period (Ibn Jurayj 12 days, Ibn ʿAbbās 15, "25", Muqātil 40). He cites **al-Bukhārī** (Jundub
b. Sufyān: the woman's taunt) and **al-Tirmidhī** (Jundub al-Bajalī).

**On v 5 (*fa-tarḍā*):** a rich survey of the future-gift: Ibn Isḥāq ("victory in this world, reward in
the next"); the *ḥawḍ* and the *shafāʿa*; Ibn ʿAbbās's "a thousand palaces of pearl, their soil musk";
ʿAlī's report that the Prophet will intercede until Allāh says "Have you been satisfied, O Muḥammad?";
and the **Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim** Ibn ʿAmr report (the Prophet wept *ummatī ummatī* over the Ibrāhīm/ʿĪsā verses,
and Allāh sent Jibrīl: "We shall satisfy you regarding your community and not grieve you").

**On v 11 (*fa-ḥaddith*):** "proclaim what Allāh has favored you with, by thanks and praise — and
recounting Allāh's blessings, and acknowledging them, is gratitude." Via **Mujāhid**: *bi-niʿmati rabbika*
= "the Qurʾān" / "the prophethood — i.e. convey what you were sent with." He adds the practical reports
(al-Ḥasan b. ʿAlī, ʿAmr b. Maymūn, Abū Firās) that telling of God's favors is commanded.

## 5. Ibn Kathīr, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm* (on Q 93:1-2, 6-8, 9-11)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/en-tafisr-ibn-kathir/93/{1,9}.json`
(Arabic at `.../ar-tafsir-ibn-kathir/93/`).

**On vv 1-3 (asbāb):** Ibn Kathīr's section-title is "The Reason for the Revelation of Surat Ad-Duha."
He leads with **Imām Aḥmad ← Jundub** ("the Prophet became ill, so he did not stand for prayer a night or
two; then a woman came and said, 'O Muḥammad, I think your devil has finally left you'") and notes
"**al-Bukhārī, Muslim, al-Tirmidhī, al-Nasāʾī, Ibn Abī Ḥātim and Ibn Jarīr all recorded this**." He gives
al-Aswad b. Qays's and al-ʿAwfī ← Ibn ʿAbbās's parallels (Jibrīl delayed; the idolaters said "his Lord
has abandoned him"). On v 2 he **cross-references Q 92:1-2** (*wa-l-layl idhā yaghshā · wa-l-nahār idhā
tajallā*) and Q 6:96, tying the al-Layl opening to the al-Ḍuḥā oath.

**On vv 6-8 (the past favors):** the orphan biography (father died before birth; Āmina at age six; ʿAbd
al-Muṭṭalib; Abū Ṭālib's protection; the Hijra and the Anṣār's shelter) — "all of this was from Allāh's
protection, guarding and caring for him."

**On vv 9-11 (the commands) — the favor→command parallel, stated explicitly:** Ibn Kathīr reads each
command as the moral counterpart of the corresponding favor —
- v 9 *fa-lā taqhar*: "**just as you were an orphan and Allāh sheltered you, do not oppress the orphan**"
  (Qatāda: "be like a merciful father to the orphan");
- v 10 *fa-lā tanhar*: "**just as you were astray and Allāh guided you, do not scorn the one who asks**
  for knowledge seeking guidance" (Ibn Isḥāq: do not be harsh to the weak);
- v 11 *fa-ḥaddith*: "**just as you were poor and Allāh enriched you, tell of Allāh's favor upon you**"
  (Abū Dāwūd ← Abū Hurayra: "whoever is not thankful to people is not thankful to Allāh").

This is the classical articulation of the **C→D favor→command mapping** that Q093-F-01 Arm B tests
empirically — and Ibn Kathīr's paraphrase makes explicit that the surah pairs v 6↔9 (orphan), v 7↔10
(guidance/asker), v 8↔11 (wealth/blessing). The empirical finding refines this: only the FIRST pair
(orphan, v 6↔9) shares a lexical root (`ytm`); the other two pairings are thematic, with no shared root.

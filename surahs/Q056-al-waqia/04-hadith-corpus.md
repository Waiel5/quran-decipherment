# Q 56 al-Wāqiʿa — Hadith Corpus

All citations verified on disk under `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/`. Q 56 (الواقعة) appears as an explicit Arabic-text mention in only **4 hadiths across the canonical 9 books**.

## 1. Canonical 9-book Q 56 hadiths

### 1.1 al-Tirmidhī, *Jāmiʿ* — #3381 (chapter 47, *Tafsīr*)

**Status**: graded *ḥasan gharīb* by al-Tirmidhī himself (in-text); not cited by al-Albānī as ḍaʿīf in his *Silsila* (counted *ḥasan*).

**Arabic** (verbatim from disk):
> *حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو كُرَيْبٍ، حَدَّثَنَا مُعَاوِيَةُ بْنُ هِشَامٍ، عَنْ شَيْبَانَ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ، قَالَ قَالَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ رضى الله عنه يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ قَدْ شِبْتَ. قَالَ "شَيَّبَتْنِي هُودٌ وَالْوَاقِعَةُ وَالْمُرْسَلاَتُ وَ عمَّ يَتَسَاءَلُونَ وَإذَا الشَّمْسُ كُوِّرَتْ"*
>
> *قَالَ أَبُو عِيسَى هَذَا حَدِيثٌ حَسَنٌ غَرِيبٌ لاَ نَعْرِفُهُ مِنْ حَدِيثِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ إِلاَّ مِنْ هَذَا الْوَجْهِ.*

**English** (in-source, Darussalam):
> "Abū Bakr said: 'O Messenger of Allah! You have become gray.' He said: 'I have gone gray from (Sūrat) Hūd, al-Wāqiʿa, al-Mursalāt, ʿAmma yatasāʾalūn, and Idhā ʾl-Shamsu kuwwirat.'"

**Significance**: This is the **canonical-9-book strongest** Q 56 fadāʾil hadith. It identifies Q 56 as one of FIVE surahs whose intensity made the Prophet gray. The five are: Q 11 Hūd, Q 56 al-Wāqiʿa, Q 77 al-Mursalāt, Q 78 al-Naba', Q 81 al-Takwīr. **All five are eschatological surahs** (Q 11 has the major prophetic destruction-narratives; the others are Day-of-Judgment cosmic catastrophes). This grouping is empirically meaningful: Q 56's nearest-10 FR-neighbors (`01-empirical-profile.md` §4) include Q 77, Q 78, Q 81, Q 105, Q 102 — overlapping the gray-hair set.

### 1.2 al-Dārimī, *Sunan* — #627 (chapter 2, *Ṣalāh*)

**Asbāb al-nuzūl for Q 56:74**:
> *أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ يَزِيدَ الْمُقْرِئُ، حَدَّثَنَا مُوسَى بْنُ أَيُّوبَ، حَدَّثَنِي عَمِّي إِيَاسُ بْنُ عَامِرٍ، قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ عُقْبَةَ بْنَ عَامِرٍ يَقُولُ: لَمَّا نَزَلَتْ {فَسَبِّحْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ الْعَظِيمِ} [سورة الواقعة آية 74]، قَالَ لَنَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: "اجْعَلُوهَا فِي رُكُوعِكُمْ". فَلَمَّا نَزَلَتْ {سَبِّحِ اسْمَ رَبِّكَ الأَعْلَى} [سورة الأعلى آية 1]، قَالَ: "اجْعَلُوهَا فِي سُجُودِكُمْ".*

**Translation**: 'Uqba b. ʿĀmir said: "When *fa-sabbiḥ bi-smi rabbika ʾl-ʿaẓīm* (Q 56:74) was revealed, the Messenger of Allah said to us: 'Make it (the recitation) in your *rukūʿ*.' And when *sabbiḥi sma rabbika ʾl-aʿlā* (Q 87:1) was revealed, he said: 'Make it in your *sujūd*.'"

**Significance**: This is the canonical **legal-sabab al-nuzūl** for the *subḥāna rabbiya ʾl-ʿaẓīm* / *subḥāna rabbiya ʾl-aʿlā* formulas in Muslim ritual prayer. al-Dārimī's chain is acceptable; this report is also in Abū Dāwūd #869 (different number, similar text) — though Abū Dāwūd #1397 in this corpus is a separate Ibn Masʿūd hadith on mufaṣṣal-recitation that mentions Q 56 as one of the *naẓāʾir* (paired surahs) in the Prophet's tahajjud. **The Q 56:74 → Q 87:1 link is the textual basis for the entire Muslim prayer-tasbīḥ practice.** Note that Q 56:96 repeats v 74 verbatim, creating the surah's internal-refrain ring.

### 1.3 al-Dārimī, *Sunan* — #2101 (chapter 20, *al-Jannah*)

**Q 56:30 prooftext** (*wa-ẓillin mamdūd* — "extended shade"):
> *حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو هُرَيْرَةَ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: "إِنَّ فِي الْجَنَّةِ شَجَرَةً يَسِيرُ الرَّاكِبُ فِي ظِلِّهَا مِائَةَ عَامٍ لَا يَقْطَعُهَا، وَاقْرَءُوا إِنْ شِئْتُمْ: {وَظِلٍّ مَمْدُودٍ} [سورة الواقعة آية 30]"*

**Translation**: Abū Hurayra: "The Messenger of Allah said: 'In Paradise there is a tree under whose shade a rider can travel for a hundred years and not cross it. Recite if you wish: *wa-ẓillin mamdūd* (Q 56:30).'"

This hadith is also in al-Bukhārī (#3251, #4881) and Muslim (#2826) — but in those collections the verse-citation Q 56:30 is implicit/paraphrased without the explicit *sūrat al-wāqiʿa āya 30* annotation that appears in al-Dārimī. Hence al-Dārimī's record is the only one matched by the project's Arabic-keyword search.

### 1.4 Mālik, *al-Muwaṭṭaʾ* — #478 (chapter 15, *Qurʾān*)

**Q 56:79 prooftext** for muṣḥaf-handling-purity:
> *قَالَ مَالِكٌ: "أَحْسَنُ مَا سَمِعْتُ فِي هَذِهِ الآيَةِ {لَا يَمَسُّهُ إِلَّا الْمُطَهَّرُونَ} [الواقعة: 79] إِنَّمَا هِيَ بِمَنْزِلَةِ هَذِهِ الآيَةِ، الَّتِي فِي {عَبَسَ وَتَوَلَّى}، قَوْلُ اللَّهِ تَبَارَكَ وَتَعَالَى: {كَلَّا إِنَّهَا تَذْكِرَةٌ. فَمَنْ شَاءَ ذَكَرَهُ. فِي صُحُفٍ مُكَرَّمَةٍ مَرْفُوعَةٍ مُطَهَّرَةٍ}"*

**Translation**: Mālik: "The best (interpretation) I have heard concerning this verse — *lā yamassuhu illā ʾl-muṭahharūn* (Q 56:79) — is that it is parallel to the verse in (sūrat) *ʿAbasa wa-tawallā* — God's speech: 'Nay! Indeed it is a reminder. Whoever wills shall remember. In honored, exalted, purified scrolls' (Q 80:11-14)."

**Significance**: Mālik's commentary establishes the cross-reference Q 56:79 ↔ Q 80:13-14 (*fī ṣuḥufin mukarrama / marfūʿa muṭahhara*). Both verses use *muṭahhar*-derived participles to describe the heavenly-tablets / scrolls. This is a key prooftext in classical Mālikī fiqh on whether Q 56:79 prohibits ritually-impure persons from touching the muṣḥaf.

## 2. The contested CHAIN — Ibn Masʿūd "every-night" hadith

This hadith is **NOT in the canonical 9 books** as an explicit الواقعة citation. It appears in:

### 2.1 In Ibn Kathīr's *Tafsīr* (Q 56:1 commentary)

**Disk verification**: `spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-ibn-kathir/56/1.json`. Ibn Kathīr cites:
> *قال الحافظ ابن عساكر في ترجمة عبدالله بن مسعود بسنده إلى عمرو بن الربيع بن طارق المصري حدثنا السري بن يحيى الشيباني عن شجاع عن أبي ظبية قال مرض عبدالله مرضه الذي توفي فيه فعاده عثمان بن عفان...*

The chain through Ibn ʿAsākir → al-Sarī b. Yaḥyā al-Shaybānī → Shujāʿ → Abū Ẓabya → Ibn Masʿūd. The hadith text:
> *إني أمرت بناتي يقرأن كل ليلة سورة الواقعة إني سمعت رسول الله ﷺ يقول: "من قرأ سورة الواقعة كل ليلة لم تصبه فاقة أبدا"*

> "I have instructed my daughters to recite Sūrat al-Wāqiʿa every night, for I heard the Messenger of Allah say: 'Whoever recites Sūrat al-Wāqiʿa every night, poverty will never afflict him.'"

### 2.2 Chain authenticity audit

**The chain has identified weakness on two narrators**:

1. **Shujāʿ** — almost certainly *Shujāʿ b. al-Walīd* (or possibly Shujāʿ b. al-Akhḍar). The classical *rijāl* literature (e.g., Ibn Ḥajar, *Tahdhīb al-Tahdhīb*) records multiple Shujāʿs from this period. The Shujāʿ in this chain is **majhūl** (unidentified) according to al-Bayhaqī's *Shuʿab al-Īmān* commentary on this hadith.

2. **Abū Ẓabya** — recorded in Ibn Kathīr as Abū Ẓabya (or a variant Abū Fāṭima per one alternate chain). al-Albānī classifies him as *majhūl*.

**al-Bayhaqī**, *Shuʿab al-Īmān* (the most thorough secondary citation): graded **ḍaʿīf** (weak). al-Bayhaqī records the chain but explicitly notes the *jahāla* (unidentifiability) of two of its narrators.

**al-Albānī**, *Silsilat al-Aḥādīth al-Ḍaʿīfa wa-l-Mawḍūʿa* #290 (PROJECT NOTE: not directly on disk; classification cited from secondary scholarly literature widely available): classified as **MUNKAR** (rejected) on multiple grounds:
- Two majhūl narrators (Shujāʿ, Abū Ẓabya)
- The text contradicts the Prophet's own teaching that *al-faqr* is sometimes a blessing
- The text is unsupported by parallel canonical chains

**Project verdict** (PER MW-6 verification protocol, hadith #-tag = SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED):
- The TRADITION (Ibn Masʿūd's deathbed instruction to his daughters; the practice of nightly Q 56 recitation in classical Islamic devotional life) is HISTORICALLY ATTESTED.
- The CHAIN AUTHENTICATING the Prophetic-attribution ("I heard the Messenger of Allah say") is FALSIFIED at canonical-strength. The hadith is *ḍaʿīf* / *munkar*; classical muḥaddithūn after al-Bayhaqī generally concur.
- The ECONOMIC PROMISE ("poverty will never afflict him") is a devotional-tradition claim NOT supported by canonical hadith; it should NOT be cited as a prophetic teaching at canonical-strength.

This is consistent with the user-prompt's seed framing: "the 'every night' hadith is contested; classify per al-Albānī or other muḥaddithīn rulings if available on disk."

## 3. The "deathbed reading" tradition — Tirmidhī #2987 audit

The user-prompt seed identified Tirmidhī #2987 as the canonical source for "the Prophet had Q 56 read at his deathbed." **This identification is INCORRECT**. Tirmidhī #2987 is on disk (verified at `ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/tirmidhi.json`, `idInBook=2987`). Its full Arabic text:

> *قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: "الَّذِي يَقْرَأُ الْقُرْآنَ وَهُوَ مَاهِرٌ بِهِ مَعَ السَّفَرَةِ الْكِرَامِ الْبَرَرَةِ وَالَّذِي يَقْرَؤُهُ... وَهُوَ شَدِيدٌ عَلَيْهِ... فَلَهُ أَجْرَانِ"*

Tirmidhī's grading: *ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ*. **Topic**: the reciter of the Qur'an in general (the proficient is with the noble angels; the struggling reciter has two rewards). **Not Q 56-specific. Not deathbed-specific.**

The actual Tirmidhī Q 56 hadith is **#3381** (the gray-hair hadith, see §1.1). The "Prophet's deathbed Q 56 reading" is **NOT recorded as a canonical-9-book Prophetic-deathbed hadith**. The closest classical attestation is the IBN MASʿŪD-deathbed story (recited Q 56 on his OWN deathbed), which is different from "the Prophet's deathbed."

**Project verdict on user-seed framing**: the claim "a Sahih hadith (al-Tirmidhī #2987) reports the Prophet had Q 56 read at his deathbed" is **TWO-PART INCORRECT**:
1. Tirmidhī #2987 is not Q 56-related.
2. There is no canonical 9-book hadith for the Prophet's-own-deathbed-Q56 tradition.

The actual canonical material is: (a) Tirmidhī #3381 — *gray-hair* hadith including Q 56; (b) al-Dārimī #627 — *fa-sabbiḥ bi-smi rabbika ʾl-ʿaẓīm* (Q 56:74) → *rukūʿ*-tasbīḥ; (c) Ibn Kathīr's-citation-of-Ibn-ʿAsākir — Ibn Masʿūd's-OWN-deathbed-instruction-to-daughters (chain ḍaʿīf).

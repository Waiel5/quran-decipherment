# Q 56 al-Wāqiʿa — Content Analysis

## 1. Macro-structure

The surah's 96 verses partition into **8 thematic blocks**:

| Block | Verses | Theme | Closing-marker |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | 1-9 | Cosmic prologue → 3-class declaration | *azwāj thalātha* (v 7) |
| **2 (A.1)** | **10-26** | **al-Sābiqūn al-muqarrabūn** — paradise of nearness | *jazāʾan bimā kānū yaʿmalūn* (v 24) |
| **3 (A.2)** | **27-40** | **Aṣḥāb al-Yamīn** — paradise of the right hand | *thullatun mina ʾl-awwalīn wa-thullatun mina ʾl-ākhirīn* (vv 39-40) |
| **4 (A.3)** | **41-56** | **Aṣḥāb al-Shimāl** — hellfire / al-zaqqūm | *hādhā nuzuluhum yawma ʾl-dīn* (v 56) |
| 5 | 57-74 | *afa-raʾaytum* — rational arguments for Resurrection | *fa-sabbiḥ bi-smi rabbika ʾl-ʿaẓīm* (v 74) |
| 6 | 75-82 | **META-OATH + Qurʾān-as-protected-text** | *wa-tajʿalūna rizqakum annakum tukadhdhibūn* (v 82) |
| 7 | 83-87 | The death-moment — *fa-lawlā idhā balaghat al-ḥulqūm* | *tarjiʿūnahā in kuntum ṣādiqīn* (v 87) |
| **8 (B)** | **88-94** | **3-class death-moment recapitulation** | *wa-taṣliyatu jaḥīm* (v 94) |
| 9 | 95-96 | Concluding affirmation + *fa-sabbiḥ* refrain | *fa-sabbiḥ bi-smi rabbika ʾl-ʿaẓīm* (v 96 — same as v 74) |

## 2. The 3-class ring (A → B)

### Block A.1 — al-Sābiqūn al-muqarrabūn (vv 10-26)

> *wa-l-sābiqūna ʾl-sābiqūn / ulāʾika ʾl-muqarrabūn / fī jannāti ʾl-naʿīm*
> "And the foremost are the foremost — those are the brought-near, in Gardens of Bliss" (vv 10-12)

Distinctive vocabulary (own count, no-tashkeel):
- *surur mawḍūnah* (v 15) — "thrones of woven gold/jewels" — **mawḍūnah is corpus-hapax**
- *bi-akwāb wa-abārīq wa-kaʾsin min maʿīn* (v 18) — "with goblets, ewers, and a cup from a flowing spring" — **bi-akwāb, abārīq, wa-kaʾs are all corpus-hapax** (akwāb appears elsewhere in plurals; the prefixed form here is unique)
- *wa-fākihatin mimmā yatakhayyarūn / wa-laḥmin ṭayrin mimmā yashtahūn* (vv 20-21) — *yatakhayyarūn is corpus-hapax*
- *wa-ḥūr ʿīn / ka-amthāli ʾl-luʾluʾi ʾl-maknūn* (vv 22-23) — **wa-ḥūr, ka-amthāli, al-maknūn are all corpus-hapax in this prefixed form**
- *lā yusmaʿūna fīhā laghwan wa-lā taʾthīmā* (v 25) — *taʾthīmā is corpus-hapax*

The *al-Sābiqūn* paradise vocabulary is **the densest hapax-cluster in Q 56**. See `06-novel-findings.md` Q056-F-02 — empirical test confirmed **10 corpus-hapax tokens** in vv 10-26, with **26 tokens of corpus-frequency ≤ 5**.

Thullatun mina ʾl-awwalīn wa-qalīlun mina ʾl-ākhirīn (vv 13-14) — "a multitude from the first generations and few from the latter" — is read by classical exegetes (al-Ṭabarī, al-Qurṭubī) as drawing a contrast: the Sābiqūn class is dominated by the early ummahs (pre-Islamic prophets' followers).

### Block A.2 — Aṣḥāb al-Yamīn (vv 27-40)

> *wa-aṣḥābu ʾl-yamīn mā aṣḥābu ʾl-yamīn / fī sidrin makhḍūd*
> "And the companions of the Right — what of the companions of the Right? — among lote-trees pruned of thorns" (vv 27-28)

Distinctive vocabulary:
- *sidrin makhḍūd* (v 28) — "thornless lote-trees" — *makhḍūd is corpus-hapax*
- *ṭalḥin manḍūd* (v 29) — "stacked banana-trees" (or "acacias" — classical disagreement) — *ṭalḥ is corpus-hapax*
- *fursh marfūʿah* (v 34) — "raised couches" — *fursh is corpus-hapax*
- *innā ansha'nāhunna inshāʾā / fa-jaʿalnāhunna abkārā / ʿuruban atrābā* (vv 35-37) — *abkārā, ʿuruban, atrābā are corpus-rare*
- closing: *thullatun mina ʾl-awwalīn wa-thullatun mina ʾl-ākhirīn* (vv 39-40) — note the contrast: BOTH classes are "multitudes" (vs the Sābiqūn's "few from the latter")

Theological reading (al-Rāzī): the Sābiqūn (foremost) are an elect minority concentrated in the early generations; the Yamīn (right-hand) are a vastly larger class drawn from all generations.

### Block A.3 — Aṣḥāb al-Shimāl (vv 41-56)

> *wa-aṣḥābu ʾl-shimāl mā aṣḥābu ʾl-shimāl / fī samūmin wa-ḥamīm / wa-ẓillin min yaḥmūm*
> "And the companions of the Left — what of the companions of the Left? — in scorching wind and scalding water, and a shadow of black smoke" (vv 41-43)

Distinctive vocabulary:
- *samūm* (v 42) — "scorching wind" — corpus-rare (also Q 52:27)
- *yaḥmūm* (v 43) — corpus-hapax
- *al-ḥinth al-ʿaẓīm* (v 46) — "the great oath/sin"
- *yaqūlūna a-idhā mitnā wa-kunnā turāban wa-ʿiẓāman a-innā la-mabʿūthūn* (v 47) — corpus-recurrent denial-of-resurrection formula
- *al-ḍāllūna ʾl-mukadhdhibūn* (v 51) — the named designation
- *shajarin min zaqqūm* (v 52) — al-zaqqūm tree (also Q 37:62, Q 44:43)
- *fa-shāribūna shurba ʾl-hīm* (v 55) — "drinking like the thirsting (camels)" — *al-hīm is corpus-hapax*

Closing: *hādhā nuzuluhum yawma ʾl-dīn* (v 56) — "this is their welcome on Judgment Day" — note the IRONIC use of *nuzul* (welcome / hospitality), which is then re-used in Block B (v 93: *fa-nuzulun min ḥamīm* — "his welcome is from boiling water").

### Block B — Death-moment 3-class (vv 88-94)

The 3 classes are recapitulated in compressed *fa-ammā... wa-ammā... wa-ammā* form:

| Sub-block | Verses | Class label | Description |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| B.1 | 88-89 | *al-muqarrabīn* | *fa-rawḥun wa-rayḥānun wa-jannatu naʿīm* |
| B.2 | 90-91 | *aṣḥāb al-yamīn* | *fa-salāmun laka min aṣḥābi ʾl-yamīn* |
| B.3 | 92-94 | *al-mukadhdhibīn al-ḍāllīn* | *fa-nuzulun min ḥamīm wa-taṣliyatu jaḥīm* |

The class labels in B exactly mirror Block A's labels (with one nuance: A.1 says *al-sābiqūn = al-muqarrabūn*; B.1 directly says *al-muqarrabīn*; the "primacy" attribute is dropped at the death-moment, leaving "nearness" as the operative class-property). A.3 says *aṣḥāb al-shimāl* and B.3 substitutes *al-mukadhdhibīn al-ḍāllīn* (which in A.3 also appeared at v 51 as the explanatory descriptor of the left-hand class).

**The structural ring is empirically clear at the LABEL level**, but at the FULL-VOCABULARY level, the lexical-overlap is dominated by function words (*min*, *fī*, *kāna*) and is NOT statistically significant under permutation null. See `06-novel-findings.md` Q056-F-01 (NULL).

## 3. The META-OATH section (vv 75-82)

> *fa-lā uqsimu bi-mawāqiʿ al-nujūm* (75)
> *wa-innahu la-qasamun law taʿlamūna ʿaẓīm* (76)
> *innahu la-qurʾānun karīm* (77)
> *fī kitābin maknūn* (78)
> *lā yamassuhu illā ʾl-muṭahharūn* (79)
> *tanzīlun min rabbi ʾl-ʿālamīn* (80)

The structural rarity of Q 56:75-76 — an oath followed by an immediate META-OATH ("and indeed it is a mighty oath, if you knew") — is empirically tested in `06-novel-findings.md` Q056-F-03 and confirmed to occur in **only 3 surahs** corpus-wide: Q 56, Q 75 (al-Qiyāma 1-2 — *lā uqsimu / wa-lā uqsimu*), Q 89 (al-Fajr 5 — *hal fī dhālika qasamun li-dhī ḥijr*). Q 56:75-76 is the canonical META-OATH form.

The block then makes the strongest single Quranic self-referential claim: *innahu la-qurʾānun karīm fī kitābin maknūn lā yamassuhu illā ʾl-muṭahharūn* — "indeed it is a noble Qurʾān in a hidden Book; none touches it except the purified." This is a key prooftext in classical Mālikī jurisprudence (Mālik, *al-Muwaṭṭaʾ* #478, see `04-hadith-corpus.md`) on the fiqh of Qurʾān-handling-purity.

## 4. The death-moment passage (vv 83-87)

> *fa-lawlā idhā balaghat al-ḥulqūm / wa-antum ḥīnaʾidhin tanẓurūn / wa-naḥnu aqrabu ilayhi minkum wa-lākin lā tubṣirūn / fa-lawlā in kuntum ghayra madīnīn / tarjiʿūnahā in kuntum ṣādiqīn*

This is one of the corpus's most viscerally physical depictions of dying: the soul reaches the *ḥulqūm* (throat), the bystanders watch, God is "nearer to him than you," but humans cannot perceive. The implicit challenge: if you are not subject to recompense (*ghayra madīnīn*), bring the soul back. *Madīnīn* here is the passive participle of d-y-n meaning "subject to recompense / accountable" — a corpus-rare usage.

This passage is the textual locus of the classical "Q 56 deathbed" tradition. Ibn Kathīr's chain (Ibn ʿAsākir → al-Sarī b. Yaḥyā → Shujāʿ → Abū Ẓabya → Ibn Masʿūd) recounts that Ibn Masʿūd on his deathbed tells ʿUthmān he has instructed his daughters to recite Q 56 every night, citing a hadith from the Prophet. The chain is graded ḍaʿīf (al-Bayhaqī, *Shuʿab al-Īmān*) and MUNKAR (al-Albānī, *Silsilat al-Ḍaʿīfa* #290). The textual association of the death-passage with the deathbed practice is robust; the canonical-strength claim is FALSIFIED.

## 5. The double-closing *fa-sabbiḥ* refrain

V 74: *fa-sabbiḥ bi-smi rabbika ʾl-ʿaẓīm* (closes the *afa-raʾaytum* rational-arguments block).
V 96: *fa-sabbiḥ bi-smi rabbika ʾl-ʿaẓīm* (closes the surah).

This double-closing creates an internal **structural ring** within Q 56 — vv 75-95 are bracketed by identical *tasbīḥ* commands. This pattern is also linked to the asbāb al-nuzūl tradition reported in al-Dārimī #627: when v 74 was revealed, the Prophet directed it to be made part of *rukūʿ* (the *subḥāna rabbiya ʾl-ʿaẓīm* recitation). When *sabbiḥi smarabbika ʾl-aʿlā* (Q 87:1) was revealed, he said make it part of *sujūd*. This is the ASBĀB AL-NUZŪL prooftext for the canonical *tasbīḥ*-formulae used in Muslim prayer.

| A.2 (vv 27-40 Yamīn) | not separately tested |  |  |
| A.3 (vv 41-56 Shimāl) | not separately tested |  |  |

The 10 Sābiqūn-block hapax: *al-maknūn, wa-kaʾs, bi-akwāb, yatakhayyarūn, mawḍūnah, ka-amthāli, al-sābiqūn, taʾthīmā, wa-ḥūr, wa-abārīq*. (Q056-F-02 result.)

This is consistent with the classical observation (al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, al-Qurṭubī) that the Sābiqūn-paradise contains the corpus's most distinctive paradise-imagery vocabulary, sharply differentiated from the Yamīn-paradise which uses Quranically-recurrent imagery (*sidr*, *ṭalḥ*, *fursh*).

## 7. Repetition patterns

- *fa-sabbiḥ bi-smi rabbika ʾl-ʿaẓīm* — vv 74, 96 (within Q 56)
- *aṣḥāb al-yamīn* — vv 8, 27 (×2), 38, 90, 91 (×2) — 7 occurrences
- *aṣḥāb al-shimāl / al-mashʾama* — vv 9, 41 (×2)
- *al-muqarrabūn / al-muqarrabīn* — vv 11, 88
- *al-mukadhdhibīn al-ḍāllīn* — vv 51, 92
- *thullatun mina ʾl-awwalīn / al-ākhirīn* — vv 13-14, 39-40

The class-label repetitions (*aṣḥāb al-yamīn* 7×, *al-shimāl* 3×, *al-muqarrabūn* 2×) sustain the 3-class structure across the surah's full extent, creating the 3-class ring at the label level.

## 8. Cross-surah content references

- *al-zaqqūm* (v 52) — also Q 37:62-66 (*shajaratu ʾl-zaqqūm*), Q 44:43-46 (*shajarata ʾl-zaqqūm*).
- *al-ḥulqūm* (v 83) → contrast Q 75:26 (*idhā balaghati ʾl-tarāqī*) — both "soul-reaching-the-throat" passages but with different anatomical loci.
- *fa-sabbiḥ bi-smi rabbika ʾl-ʿaẓīm* (vv 74, 96) → cf. Q 69:52 (*fa-sabbiḥ bi-smi rabbika ʾl-ʿaẓīm* — the ONLY other surah ending with this exact phrase).
- *kitābin maknūn* (v 78) → cf. Q 85:21-22 (*qurʾānun majīd / fī lawḥin maḥfūẓ*) — the celestial-tablet motif.

The Q 56:78 → Q 85:22 link (*kitāb maknūn* ↔ *lawḥ maḥfūẓ*) is the corpus's pair of celestial-Quran verses.

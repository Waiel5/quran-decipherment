### 2.4 The *fa-sabbiḥ bi-smi rabbika ʾl-ʿaẓīm* closing cluster

The Q 56-Q 69 pair both feature: temporal-conditional opening, eschatological 3-class structure (Q 69:19-21 *aṣḥāb al-yamīn* / vv 25-37 *aṣḥāb al-shimāl*), strong Resurrection rhetoric. ### 2.6 The *muʿawwidhāt*-style 3-class declarations (with Q 35:32)

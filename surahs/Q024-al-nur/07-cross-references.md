# Q 24 al-Nūr — Cross-References

## 1. Canonical neighbours (Q 23 ↔ Q 24 ↔ Q 25)

### 1.1 Q 23 al-Muʾminūn → Q 24 al-Nūr

Q 23 is a Meccan surah (118 verses, predominantly narrative-eschatological with the iconic *qad aflaḥa al-muʾminūn* opening). The Q 23 → Q 24 transition is at canonical-adjacency cost **rank 11 / 113** (delta = 0.2116, fraction-of-residual 2.55%; `findings/phase-b-hypotheses/csv/h-new-720.json`). One of the top-15 most-expensive single-surah transitions in the corpus.

**Lexical hinge**: Q 23 closes with vv. 117-118 — *fa-mā lahu min ʿawnin ʿalā Allāh; rabbi-ighfir wa-rḥam wa-anta khayru al-rāḥimīn* (Allāh's exclusive aid; the closing prayer for forgiveness and mercy). Q 24 opens with the meta-textual *sūratun anzalnāhā wa-faraḍnāhā* — "a surah which We sent down and made obligatory."

**Register pivot**: Q 23 is Meccan-narrative-doxological → Q 24 is Medinan-meta-textual-legal. al-Biqāʿī (`biqai-nazm-al-durar.openiti.raw.txt`, Q24:1) reads the transition as "from the description of the *muʾminūn* to the legislation that *constitutes* the *muʾminūn* community" — Q 23 names the believers; Q 24 legislates the believer-community.

**Empirical FR distance**: Q 23 ↔ Q 24 = **1.0497** (rank ~80 of 113, well above the corpus median 0.9235). Even at the FR-distance level, the two are far apart.

### 1.2 Q 24 al-Nūr → Q 25 al-Furqān

The cost-side transition: Q 24 → Q 25 is **rank 5 / 113 most expensive** (delta = 0.2896, fraction-of-residual 3.49%). One of the top-5 single-surah-transition costs in the corpus.

**Register pivot**: Q 24 is Medinan-legal-centerpiece → Q 25 is Meccan-narrative-prophetological. al-Biqāʿī reads the closing of Q 24 (the warning of *fitna or painful punishment* in v. 63 + the *bi-kulli shayʾin ʿalīm* refrain) as transitioning to Q 25's opening *tabāraka al-ladhī nazzala al-furqāna ʿalā ʿabdihi li-yakūna li-l-ʿālamīna nadhīran* (blessed is He who sent down the Furqān to His servant to be a warner for all worlds). The transition is from *muṣīb-al-fitna* (the punishment that strikes) to *al-nadhīr-al-ʿālamiyy* (the universal warner).

**Empirical FR distance**: Q 24 ↔ Q 25 = **1.1291** (rank ~95 of 113, far above corpus median).

### 1.3 The bracketing-cost claim

Q 24 is the **only surah other than Q 33 al-Aḥzāb to have BOTH canonical adjacencies in the top-15 expensive**. The empirical content: the mushaf "pays" significant length-cost (combined 6.04% of the entire 8.29-unit TSP residual) on Q 24's two adjacencies. This is the empirical correlate of the qualitative observation that Q 24 is *out of register* with both its Meccan-narrative neighbours (Q 23 and Q 25). The mushaf accepts this cost to keep Q 24 at index 24, between two Meccan surahs but as the Medinan-legal "centerpiece" of the early-mushaf zone.

- Q 47 (Muḥammad) — names the Prophet but not "this surah."
- Q 17 (al-Isrāʾ) — names a recent event.

Q 24's *farḍnāhā* ("we made it obligatory") is unique in the corpus. This is the project's strongest single case of a surah that *announces* its legal-binding status in its first verse.

## 5. The Q 24 / Q 33 *parallel surah* relationship

Q 33 al-Aḥzāb is the project's other top-UAS Medinan-legal surah and shares structural features with Q 24:

- Both have BOTH canonical adjacencies in the top-15 expensive (Q 32-33 rank 2, Q 33-34 rank 3; Q 23-24 rank 11, Q 24-25 rank 5). They are the only two surahs with this property.
- Both are Medinan-legal surahs inserted into Meccan-narrative neighbours (Q 32 al-Sajda is Meccan; Q 34 Sabaʾ is Meccan; Q 23 al-Muʾminūn and Q 25 al-Furqān are Meccan).

Q 24 and Q 33 are however *NOT lexically or content-similar*:
- Their light-vocabulary signatures are inverse: Q 24 has 27 light-tokens (3.07× expected); Q 33 has 4 light-tokens (0.44× expected, depleted).

Q 24 and Q 33 are therefore *structurally similar by mushaf-position and adjacency-cost*, but *content- and style-orthogonal*. They are the corpus's two "Medinan-legal-anchor surahs in Meccan-narrative zones," paired by structural role but disjoint in content register.

## 7. The istikhlāf-promise verse Q 24:55 — corpus cross-references

Q 24:55 is one of 5 Quranic *istikhlāf* (root *khlf*-Form-X) verses:
- Q 24:55 — to *al-ladhīna āmanū wa-ʿamilū al-ṣāliḥāt*: the believing community.
- Q 7:129 — to Mūsā's people: communal-historical promise.
- Q 38:26 — to Dāwūd: individual-prophetic *khalīfa*-ship.
- Q 27:62 — generic existential rhetorical question.

Q 24:55 is unique in the corpus as the *future-tense istikhlāf-promise to the believing community*. The Sunnī-Shīʿī interpretive dispute (rāshidūn vs. ahl al-bayt) hinges on the indefinite scope of the relative clause; the empirical observation (project-level) is that this verse is the only Quranic *community-istikhlāf-promise*.

## 8. The al-ifk asbāb-narrative — corpus cross-references

The al-ifk passage (Q 24:11-20) has no internal-Quranic verbal echo, but it is structurally paralleled by:
- **Q 4:113** (Allāh's protection of the Prophet from those who would mislead him).
- **Q 33:69** (the verse rejecting the slander against Mūsā by his people: "do not be like those who hurt Mūsā, but Allāh acquitted him of what they said").
- **Q 12:18-19, 12:25-29** (Yūsuf's slander-and-acquittal narrative: ʿazīz's wife's accusation, the chemise-from-behind verdict).

The classical parallel-narrative structure is: a chaste figure is slandered; God reveals the truth. Q 24:11-20 (ʿĀʾisha), Q 33:69 (Mūsā), Q 12:25-29 (Yūsuf). The Quran does not name ʿĀʾisha in Q 24:11-20 (consistent with its general avoidance of asbāb proper-nouns), and the structural parallel to Yūsuf's case is invoked by ʿĀʾisha herself in the asbāb (al-Qurṭubī Q24:11 cites ʿĀʾisha: *"my parable and yours is like Yaʿqūb and his sons; Allāh is the resort against what you describe"* — a direct invocation of Q 12:18 *fa-ṣabrun jamīl, wa-llāhu al-mustaʿānu ʿalā mā taṣifūn*).

This is one of the most explicit Quranic intra-narrative resonances: Q 24's al-ifk story is consciously cross-referenced to Q 12's Yūsuf story by the asbāb-protagonist herself. The empirical implication: ʿĀʾisha's defense at her father's house *quoted Q 12:18* — the Quran cites itself in the asbāb of Q 24.

## 2. Classical names

- **al-Nūr** (النور) — "The Light" (from the *āyat al-nūr* at verse 35)
- The *āyat al-nūr* (Q 24:35) is one of the most-celebrated verses in Quranic literature, alongside *āyat al-kursī* (Q 2:255).

## 3. Opening formula

**Meta-textual opening** — Q 24 announces ITSELF as a revealed surah making rulings obligatory. One of the rare surahs with such a self-referential opening (others include Q 47 "muḥammadun rasūlu llāh" pattern).

## 7. Quick content structure

- vv. 1-10: zinā-punishment + qadhf-punishment + liʿān (mutual-cursing for accusation between spouses).
- vv. 11-20: **al-ifk** — the false-accusation incident (against ʿĀʾisha). Major moral-legal narrative.
- vv. 21-26: warnings against hypocrites + *fawāḥish*.
- vv. 27-29: home-entry permissions.
- vv. 30-31: **gaze-modesty + women's outer-garment ḥijāb**.
- vv. 32-33: marriage of singles + slave-marriage rules.
- vv. 34-40: *āyat al-nūr* + light-darkness imagery.
- vv. 41-46: cosmic signs (clouds, lightning, water, animals).
- vv. 47-57: hypocrite-believer contrast in legal-judgment-acceptance.
- vv. 58-61: home-entry-permissions for children/relatives.
- vv. 62-64: closing — ummah-discipline; warning to dissenters.

## 1. al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān* (d. 310/923)

Source: cited via Ibn Kathīr (`/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/ibn-kathir-tafsir-quran.openiti.raw.txt:108401-108953`), al-Qurṭubī (`qurtubi-jami-ahkam.openiti.raw.txt:144641-145100`) and al-Rāzī (`razi-mafatih-al-ghayb.openiti.raw.txt:218240-218400`) — all carry al-Ṭabarī's positions verbatim or paraphrased.

Key positions documented in al-Ṭabarī ad Q 44:
- **Q 44:1 (ḥā mīm)**: classified among *al-mutashābih* (the ambiguous letters); refuses lexical decoding. Preserves the Ibn ʿAbbās tradition (ḥā = al-Ḥalīm/al-Ḥamīd; mīm = al-Mājid/al-Malik).
- **Q 44:3 (laylatin mubāraka)**: locks the **majority laylat al-qadr reading** via cross-reference to Q 97:1 (innā anzalnāhu fī laylati al-qadr) and Q 2:185 (shahru ramaḍāna alladhī unzila fīhi al-Qurʾān). Preserves the minority Shaʿbān reading via ʿIkrima but rejects it on Q 2:185 grounds.
- **Q 44:4 (yufraqu kullu amrin ḥakīm)**: cites Ibn ʿAbbās, ʿUmar (Ibn al-Khaṭṭāb), Mujāhid, Qatāda, al-Ḍaḥḥāk — the Night of Decree is when *ājāl, arzāq, aʿmāl* (lifespans, provisions, deeds) are decreed for the year. Some (Ibn ʿUmar) exempt *al-shaqāwa wa-l-saʿāda* as fixed.
- **Q 44:10-16 (smoke-sign)**: reports BOTH the past-event Ibn Masʿūd tradition (Quraysh famine — *narrated in al-Ṣaḥīḥayn*) and the future-eschatological Ibn ʿAbbās tradition (one of the *ʿalāmāt al-sāʿa al-kubrā*). Sides with the Ibn Masʿūd reading as preferred via *isnād*-strength but acknowledges both.
- **Q 44:43-46 (zaqqūm)**: links the *muhl* simile to the molten-metal eschatological imagery; cross-references the Q 18:29 *muhl-kalmuhl* attestation.

## 2. al-Zamakhsharī, *al-Kashshāf* (d. 538/1144)

Source: cited via al-Rāzī's extensive engagement with al-Kashshāf at `razi-mafatih-al-ghayb.openiti.raw.txt:218390-218430` and via al-Qurṭubī's *Zamakhshariyyāt*-citations at `qurtubi-jami-ahkam.openiti.raw.txt:144700-144750` (per al-Rāzī, "هذا الفصل نقلته من «الكشاف»").

al-Zamakhsharī's distinctive Muʿtazilī framing:
- **Q 44:3 (laylatin mubāraka)**: rationalist treatment — explains why God privileges a specific night for revelation as a *taklīf*-encouragement (the believer is moved to greater devotion by the unknown timing).
- **Q 44:4 (yufraqu)**: catalogs the four classical readings (yufraqu / nafriq / nafarriq / yufarriqu) and the resulting fragmentation-of-decree imagery.
- **Q 44:5 (amran min ʿindinā)**: treats *amran* as *ikhtiṣāṣ-naṣb* (specifying-accusative) — al-Rāzī adopts this analysis (`:218394`).
- **Q 44 angelic distribution-of-decrees**: expansive section (preserved verbatim in al-Rāzī `:218400-218450`) — Mīkāʾīl gets the *risq* copy; Jibrīl gets *ḥurūb wa-zalāzil wa-ṣawāʿiq*; Ismāʿīl gets the *aʿmāl* copy; Malak al-Mawt gets the *maṣāʾib* copy.

## 3. al-Rāzī, *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* (d. 606/1210)

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/razi-mafatih-al-ghayb.openiti.raw.txt:218240-219100` (Q 44 chapter, surah-marker verified at line 218243).

al-Rāzī's distinctive Q 44 treatment:
- **Q 44:1-9 (line 218240-218450)**: structures the opening as an 8-*masʾala* (issue-by-issue) analysis. *Masʾala* 5 (`:218321-218360`) is the most comprehensive classical defence of the laylat al-qadr reading: 5 arguments — (1) Q 97:1 cross-reference; (2) Q 2:185 Ramaḍān-locus; (3) Q 97:4 *tanazzalu al-malāʾika* parallels Q 44:4 *fīhā yufraqu*; (4) Qatāda's chronology of Books-revealed-in-Ramaḍān (Q 44 reads as the Quranic seal of this Ramaḍān-line); (5) the *qadr*-naming logic (the Night gets its qadr from the Quran, not from time-of-year alone).
- **Q 44:3 — Shaʿbān minority**: al-Rāzī catalogs the Shaʿbān-night's "five virtues" (decree-distinction, hundred-fold worship reward, descent-of-mercy, forgiveness, intercession-completion) preserving al-Zamakhsharī's full account, but explicitly deems "ما رأيت لهم فيه دليلا يعول عليه" — "I have not seen for them a proof that can be relied on" (`:218373-218380`). Sides with laylat al-qadr.
- **Q 44:6 (raḥmatan min rabbik)**: develops a Naẓm-analysis of the seven-step exaltation of the Quran in vv. 1-7 — quranic-magnification through three axes (intrinsic-revelation-night-recipient).
- **Q 44:10-16 (smoke-sign)**: surveys both the past-event and future-eschatological readings; al-Rāzī's lengthy adjudication parallels al-Ṭabarī's; the Ibn Ṣayyād hadith is cited as evidence for the eschatological reading's classical currency.
- **Q 44:25-28 (Pharaoh elegy)**: explicitly framed as *naʿy al-mahlikīn* (lamentation for the destroyed); al-Rāzī develops a paragraph on the *bukāʾ al-samāʾ wa-l-arḍ* (heaven-earth weeping) Q 44:29 idiom as a poetic-cosmic-mourning trope (verified `:219000-219040`, this session).
- **Q 44:43 (zaqqūm)**: short philological note that *zaqqūm* is from the Yemenite-Arabic *al-tazaqqum* (the rejection by gulping). Cited at `:5805` and `:6967`.

## 4. al-Qurṭubī, *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān* (d. 671/1273)

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/qurtubi-jami-ahkam.openiti.raw.txt:144641-145600` (Q 44 chapter, surah-marker verified at line 144641).

al-Qurṭubī's fiqhī-comprehensive treatment:
- **Q 44 opening (`:144641-144650`)**: classifies Q 44 as Meccan with the single exception of Q 44:15 (*innā kāshifū al-ʿadhābi qalīlan innakum ʿāʾidūn*) which some (al-Qurṭubī notes) read as Madinan-context referring to Badr; reports both views, sides with Meccan throughout.
- **Q 44 verse-count (`:144643`)**: states 57 (or 59) verses — al-Qurṭubī's count is split-tradition; the Hafs-Kufan count is 59.
- **Q 44:3 (laylatin mubāraka)** (`:144655-144680`): preserves the four-name tradition for the Shaʿbān night (al-mubāraka, al-barāʾa, al-ṣakk, al-qadr-confused-name) but quotes al-Qāḍī Abū Bakr Ibn al-ʿArabī's hard-line judgment: *"وهو باطل لأن الله تعالى قال في كتابه الصادق القاطع: شهر رمضان الذي أنزل فيه القرآن"* — "(the Shaʿbān reading) is false because God says in His decisive Book: the month of Ramaḍān is that in which the Qurʾān was sent down" (`:144675`). Sides decisively with laylat al-qadr.
- **Q 44:4 (yufraqu)**: catalogs the *qirāʾāt*-variants (yufraqu / nufarriq / yufarriqu); explains the *fasl*-decree framework.
- **Q 44:10 (dukhān mubīn)**: presents three readings — (a) yet-to-come eschatological sign; (b) past-event Quraysh famine; (c) day-of-Mecca's-conquest. Notes the (c) reading (Ibn al-Aʿraj) as *gharīb jiddan bal munkar* — extraordinarily rare and rejected.

## 5. al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-durar fī tanāsub al-āyāt wa-l-suwar* (d. 885/1480)

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/biqai-nazm-al-durar.openiti.raw.txt:121370-121400; 154340-154345`.

al-Biqāʿī's *munāsabāt* (inter-passage coherence) framework:
- **Q 43 ↔ Q 44 transition** (`:121372-121400`): al-Biqāʿī establishes the Q 43-Q 44 *kitāb mubīn* qasam as a deliberate paired-pair; the Q 43 closing apologetic ("they will know") prepares the Q 44 *fa-rtaqib* (watch-and-wait) imperative.
- **Q 97 ↔ Q 44 cross-link** (`:154340-154345`): under the *laylat al-qadr* discussion in Sūrat al-Qadr, al-Biqāʿī explicitly identifies the *layla* of Q 97:1 with the *layla mubāraka* of Q 44:3, citing *fīhā yufraqu kullu amrin ḥakīm* as the cross-reference. This is a **structurally-important inter-surah anchor**: al-Biqāʿī treats Q 97 and Q 44 as *naẓm*-paired for the laylat-al-qadr doctrine.
- **Q 44 internal munāsaba**: the surah's name *al-Dukhān* (from v. 10) is the *mufassal*-marker; the surah's central polemic is the eschatological smoke-sign + Pharaoh-precedent + zaqqūm-paradise paired-eschatology.

## 6. Ibn Kathīr, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm* (d. 774/1373)

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/ibn-kathir-tafsir-quran.openiti.raw.txt:108401-108953` (Q 44 chapter, surah-marker verified at line 108401, end-marker at 108953).

Ibn Kathīr's salient contributions:
- **Surah-preface (`:108401-108430`)**: opens with three classical *faḍāʾil* hadiths for Sūrat al-Dukhān recitation:
  - Tirmidhī Abū Hurayra: "Whoever recites *Ḥā Mīm al-Dukhān* at night, seventy thousand angels seek forgiveness for him" — Ibn Kathīr notes al-Tirmidhī's grading: "*gharīb lā naʿrifuhu illā min hādhā al-wajh*; *ʿUmar b. Abī Khathʿam yuḍaʿʿaf*; al-Bukhārī ruled *munkar al-ḥadīth*" (i.e., **weak-chain**).
  - Tirmidhī Abū Hurayra: "Whoever recites *Ḥā Mīm al-Dukhān* on the night of Friday is forgiven" — also flagged by Ibn Kathīr as *gharīb*; Hishām Abū al-Miqdām *yuḍaʿʿaf*; al-Ḥasan did not hear from Abū Hurayra (per Ayyūb, Yūnus b. ʿUbayd, ʿAlī b. Zayd).
  - Bazzār Musnad / Abū al-Ṭufayl ʿĀmir b. Wāthila: the **Ibn Ṣayyād hadith** — the Prophet hides Q 44:10 (*fa-rtaqib yawma taʾtī al-samāʾu bi-dukhānin mubīn*) for Ibn Ṣayyād to test his prophetic-claim; Ibn Ṣayyād answers *huwa al-dukh* (clipped utterance from *al-dukhān*); the Prophet says *ikhsaʾ mā shāʾa Allāhu kāna* (Be off — what God wills will be).
- **Q 44:3-6** (`:108428-108455`): locks the laylat al-qadr reading via the Q 2:185 + Q 97:1 cross-anchor; rejects the Shaʿbān reading as *abʿada al-najʿa* (extreme far-fetching).
- **Q 44:10-16 (smoke-sign — extensive `:108460-108600`)**: preserves the Ibn Masʿūd past-event Quraysh-famine narration as the *ṣaḥīḥayn*-attested reading; preserves the Ibn ʿAbbās/ʿAlī/al-Ḥasan eschatological reading via the *ten signs* hadith (Muslim #2901-class); preserves the Ibn Ṣayyād hadith as confirming the eschatological reading was active in the Prophet's environment. Final adjudication: both readings are classically defensible.
- **Q 44:43-46 (zaqqūm)**: extensive — anchors the Quranic *zaqqūm* in the three locations (Q 37:62, Q 44:43, Q 56:52); al-shajara al-malʿūna (Q 17:60) is also linked.

## 7. al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān* + *al-Durr al-manthūr* (d. 911/1505)

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/suyuti-al-itqan-fi-ulum-al-quran-english.pdf` (English; not extracted into raw text per file inventory); `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/suyuti-durr-manthur.openiti.raw.txt:7+` (al-Durr al-manthūr Q 44 section, surah-marker verified at line 7).

al-Suyūṭī's relevant entries:
- **al-Itqān nawʿ 16 (*fī kayfiyyat inzālihi*, "the manner of the Qurʾān's revelation")**: al-Suyūṭī catalogs the laylat al-qadr / laylat al-mubāraka identification, with extensive Companion-attestations. Locks the laylat al-qadr reading per Ibn ʿAbbās (the celestial-Qurʾān descended in one piece to *bayt al-ʿizza* in the lowest heaven on laylat al-qadr; then was revealed piecemeal over 23 years).
- **al-Itqān nawʿ 17 (*asmāʾ al-suwar*)**: al-Dukhān as primary surah-name; Ḥā Mīm al-Dukhān as alternate.
- **al-Itqān nawʿ 19 (*al-makkī wa-l-madanī*)**: classifies Q 44 as Meccan with the contested Q 44:15 exception (split tradition).
- **al-Durr al-manthūr** (`:7+`): catalogs all transmission-chains for the smoke-sign exegesis — Ibn Masʿūd, Ibn ʿAbbās, ʿAlī, Abū Hurayra, Hudhayfa, the *ten signs* hadith chains.

## 9. Rules-tuple sensitivity

- The laylat al-qadr identification depends on the Q 97-Q 44 cross-anchor; this holds under all tashkeel-rules-tuples (no-tashkeel, min-tashkeel, full-tashkeel) and verse-numberings (Hafs-Kufan, Basran).
- The *yufraqu* sense-disambiguation (decree-distribution vs separation-between-persons) is rules-tuple-stable; the Q 44:4 form is corpus-unique in the decree-distribution-sense (verified this session by full-corpus regex).
- The smoke-sign-past-vs-future ambiguity is **NOT** rules-tuple-fragile — it persists across all tashkeel variants because it is exegetical-temporal, not orthographic.

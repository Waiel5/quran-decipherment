## Interpretation

The "smoke" root (`dxn`) has only 2 corpus attestations, both in HM-cluster surahs. Q 44 wins on density (smallest HM surah with the highest concentration of the eponym word). The count-tie at 1 means the pre-committed "rank-1 by count" is only partially satisfied (tied with Q 41).

The substantive observation is that *dukhān* lexically threads ONLY through HM surahs in the entire corpus. The Q 44 eponym is *contextually* rich (Q 44:10 *yawma taʾtī al-samāʾu bi-dukhānin mubīn* — "the day the sky brings forth a clear smoke") but the *lexical* density is single-attestation. The "smoke" theme is therefore concentrated in Q 44 by *occurrence-position* (the famous verse), not by lexical-frequency.

- n=2 attestations; statistical power is weak.
- The English "smoke" gloss is interpretive; classical tafsir varies on whether Q 44:10's *dukhān* is literal (atmospheric) or eschatological (sign of the Hour). This test is purely lexical.
- The QAC v0.4 may not annotate all dukhān-cognates (e.g., metaphorical uses); the strict-root inventory is conservative.

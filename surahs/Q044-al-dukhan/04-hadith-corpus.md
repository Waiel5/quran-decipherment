## 3. The *ten signs of the Hour* hadith (eschatological-dukhān reading)

Discriminating phrase: *عشر آيات* (ten signs). The hadith catalogs the major signs of the Hour: rising of the sun from the west, the Beast, Yaʾjūj-Maʾjūj, ʿĪsā's descent, the Dajjāl, three eclipses (east, west, Arabia), fire from Aden, **and the smoke (al-dukhān)**.

| Dārimī | 1046 | Abū Hurayra: when mid-Shaʿbān comes, refrain from fasting (chain via ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Ibrāhīm — al-ʿAlāʾ — his father — Abū Hurayra) |

**Total: 6 verified hits across 3 books** — but ALL are weak-chain by classical hadith-criticism standards (Ibn Kathīr `:108433-108445` explicitly cites the Shaʿbān-night mawqūf as *ḥadīth mursal lā yuʿāriḍu bi-mithlihi al-nuṣūṣ* — "an irregular ḥadīth that does not contradict the [Quranic] texts").

This is the **hadith corpus's anchor for the minority Shaʿbān-night exegesis** of Q 44:3 — but the chains are weak, which is why Ibn Kathīr, al-Qurṭubī, al-Rāzī, and Ibn al-ʿArabī all reject the Shaʿbān-night reading in favor of laylat al-qadr.

## 9. Asbāb al-nuzūl

Per `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/` — the al-Wāḥidī Q 44 extraction file is **NOT confirmed on disk** (flagged as **DATA-GAP**). The al-Wāḥidī *Asbāb al-Nuzūl* is the canonical pre-modern *asbāb*-collection; its absence on disk for Q 44 is a project gap.

The dominant classical asbāb traditions for Q 44 (per Ibn Kathīr, al-Qurṭubī, al-Rāzī ad loc.) concentrate on Q 44:10-16:
- The Ibn Masʿūd narration (`Bukhārī Tafsīr Sūrat al-Dukhān`, ID-block 4774-4823): the smoke-sign refers to the Quraysh famine after the Prophet's supplication, *innā kāshifū al-ʿadhābi qalīlan* (v. 15) refers to the rain-relief, and *yawm nabṭishu al-baṭshata al-kubrā* (v. 16) refers to Badr.
- The Ibn ʿAbbās/ʿAlī/Hudhayfa eschatological reading (Muslim Kitāb al-Fitan ID-block 6892-7174 + the *ten signs* hadith ID-block 1775/7106/7107).

## 10. Headline summary

- **≥10 verified Q 44:10-16 (smoke + great seizure) hits** in 4 books — strongest direct-verse Quranic anchor.
- **25 verified *ten signs* hadith hits** in 8 books — densest eschatological-Hour cross-anchor.
- **≥18 verified Ibn Ṣayyād hits** in 4 books — single most distinctive Q 44-anchored eschatological hadith.
- **3 zaqqūm hits** in 3 books.
- **2 Tirmidhī faḍāʾil-Dukhān recitation hadiths — both classically WEAK-graded** (al-Tirmidhī himself).
- **6 mid-Shaʿbān-night hadiths — all classically weak-chain** (the minority Q 44:3 reading is hadith-supportable but classically rejected as not strong enough).

**Overall verdict**: Q 44 has **strong-to-extreme presence in the 9-book corpus**, concentrated at:
1. The smoke-sign + Greatest-Seizure cluster (Q 44:10-16) — Bukhārī's dedicated *Tafsīr al-Dukhān* chapter is the canonical anchor.
2. The eschatological *ten signs* hadith (Muslim + 8 books).
3. The Ibn Ṣayyād hadith — Q 44:10 hidden as Prophetic test of false-claimant.

The faḍāʾil-recitation tradition (70,000 forgiveness angels) is **CLASSICALLY WEAK** — flagged for cross-reference to the broader *faḍāʾil-fabrication* literature (al-Suyūṭī's *al-Laʾāliʾ al-Maṣnūʿa* preserves these as known-weak).

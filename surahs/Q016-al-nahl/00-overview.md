## 2. Classical names

- **Sūrat al-Naḥl** (سورة النحل) — "The Bee" — after the unique honeybee revelation Q 16:68–69.
- **Sūrat al-Niʿam** (سورة النِّعَم) — "The Surah of Blessings" — a classical alternative name reported by al-Qurṭubī (*al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām*, intro to Q 16) and al-Suyūṭī (*al-Itqān*, nawʿ 17) attributed to Qatāda — because the surah enumerates God's natural blessings (rain, livestock, crops, sea, mountains, sun, moon, stars, honey, etc.).

## 3. Opening formula — declaration of imminent judgment

Q 16 opens **without muqaṭṭaʿāt** with a declarative perfective: *atā amru llāhi* — "the command of Allah has come" (perfective, despite future-imminent meaning). al-Zamakhsharī (*al-Kashshāf*) and al-Rāzī (*Mafātīḥ al-ghayb*) both note the perfective-for-future as a rhetorical *taʾkīd*: the inevitability is so certain it is treated as accomplished.

This places Q 16 **OUTSIDE the muqaṭṭaʿāt-clusters** (no ALR, ḤM, ALMR, etc.) — relevant to its true-isolate status (see §7).

## 4. ⭐ Three structurally distinctive features

1. **NIʿMAH-CATALOG SATURATION** (Q 16:5–18, 65–69, 78–83): a sustained roll-call of God's natural-world blessings — livestock (vv. 5–8), rain → vegetation → fruits (v. 11), sea (v. 14), mountains as anchors (v. 15), stars (v. 16), milk (v. 66), grapes & dates (v. 67), the BEE & honey (vv. 68–69), homes from animal hides + wool/fur (v. 80), shade from trees + mountain-shelters + clothing (v. 81). Pre-registered as Q016-F-01.

2. **THE BEE PASSAGE** (Q 16:68–69): the unique honeybee-revelation *waḥy ilā l-naḥl*. The lemma `n~aHol` (the bee) appears **ONLY HERE** in the entire corpus (the other `nḥl`-root attestation, Q 4:4 *niḥla* "free gift", is a homonymous separate lemma). Pre-registered as Q016-F-02.

3. **ABRAHAM-AS-IMAM-OF-NATIONS CODA** (Q 16:120–123): the unique self-contained 4-verse Abraham portrait *inna ibrāhīma kāna ummatan qānitan li-llāhi ḥanīfan*. Pre-registered as Q016-F-04.

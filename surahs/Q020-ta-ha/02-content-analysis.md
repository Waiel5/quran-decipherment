The surah's spine is the **longest sustained Mūsā narrative in the Qurʾān** (blocks B-D, vv 9-98 — 90 of
135 verses), framed by a consolation prologue (A) and a consolation epilogue (G), with an eschatological
hinge (E) and the Ādam-covenant coda (F). The macro-pattern is a **double-consolation envelope around a
prophet-narrative core**: the opening tells Muḥammad the Qurʾān is not a burden (vv 2-3), the closing tells
him to be patient and not to covet (vv 130-131) — the Mūsā story in between is the exemplar of
prophetic perseverance under a tyrant.

## 2. Block-by-block close reading

### Block A — prologue (vv 1-8)
**v 1** طه — the two-letter muqaṭṭaʿ opener (see `03-tafsir-survey.md` for the gloss-spectrum).
**v 2** *mā anzalnā ʿalayka al-Qurʾāna li-tashqā* — "We did not send the Qurʾān down on you that you should
suffer." The consolation thesis. **v 5** *al-raḥmān ʿalā al-ʿarsh istawā* — the istiwāʾ statement (a major
*ṣifāt* locus for the mutakallimūn). **v 8** *Allāhu lā ilāha illā huwa lahu al-asmāʾu al-ḥusnā* — "Allāh,
there is no god but He; to Him belong the most beautiful names." This is **one of exactly four corpus
attestations of *lahu al-asmāʾ al-ḥusnā*** {Q 7:180, Q 17:110, Q 20:8, Q 59:24} (MASTER-FINDINGS-LEDGER
§asmāʾ-ḥusnā).

### Block B — the burning-bush prototype (vv 9-36) — the F-06 pericope
**v 9** *wa-hal atāka ḥadīthu Mūsā* — "Has the report of Mūsā reached you?" The narrative-opening formula.
**v 11-12** *fa-lammā atāhā nūdiya yā Mūsā · innī anā rabbuka fa-khlaʿ naʿlayka innaka bi-l-wādi al-muqaddasi
ṭuwā* — the call at the holy valley **Ṭuwā** (root `Twy`, an F-06 signature root). **v 14** *innanī anā
Allāhu lā ilāha illā anā fa-ʿbudnī wa-aqim al-ṣalāta li-dhikrī* — the divine self-affirmation; **the
rank-1/135 divine-self-reference verse** (Q020-F-05 CONFIRMED). **v 17-18** *wa-mā tilka bi-yamīnika yā
Mūsā · qāla hiya ʿaṣāya …* — the dialogue about the **staff** (`ESw`, F-06 signature root) — Mūsā's
unusually expansive answer ("I lean on it, beat down fodder for my sheep, and have other uses") is a
celebrated literary touch. The white-hand sign (*al-bayḍāʾ*, `byD`) follows. **v 25-35** the
*rabbi-shraḥ-lī-ṣadrī wa-yassir lī amrī* prayer and the request for Hārūn as helper — the dual-commission.
This pericope carries **5 of the 6 H-NEW-2260 Mūsā signature roots** (Q020-F-06 Arm A).

### Block C — before Firʿawn (vv 37-79)
**v 38-40** the infancy flashback (the casket on the river, the sister, the return to the mother); **v 40**
is the surah's **longest verse (35 words)**. **v 65-73** the magicians' contest: the ropes that "seemed to
move", Mūsā's staff swallowing them, the magicians' immediate prostration and confession (*āmannā bi-rabbi
Hārūna wa-Mūsā*) under Firʿawn's threat of crucifixion. **v 77-79** *wa-laqad awḥaynā ilā Mūsā an asri
bi-ʿibādī fa-ḍrib lahum ṭarīqan fī al-baḥri yabasan* — the night-march and the dry path through the sea.

### Block D — the Sāmirī (vv 80-98) — tested by Q020-F-04
**v 85** *qāla fa-innā qad fatannā qawmaka min baʿdika wa-aḍallahumu al-sāmirī* — "We have tested your
people in your absence, and the Sāmirī has led them astray." **v 88** *fa-akhraja lahum ʿijlan jasadan lahu
khuwārun fa-qālū hādhā ilāhukum wa-ilāhu Mūsā fa-nasiya* — the calf-body with a lowing sound. **v 96** the
Sāmirī's defence (*qabaḍtu qabḍatan min athari al-rasūl* — "I grasped a handful from the messenger's track").
**v 98** *innamā ilāhukumu Allāhu alladhī lā ilāha illā huwa wasiʿa kulla shayʾin ʿilmā* — the creedal seal
of the episode. Q 20:98 is one of the **three corpus *alladhī lā ilāha illā huwa* attestations**
{Q 20:98, Q 59:22, Q 59:23} (MASTER-FINDINGS-LEDGER). **Empirical note:** Q020-F-04 found this block is
NOT lexically isolated within Q 20 (rank 110/122 — among the LEAST distant windows); the surah's most
lexically-distant windows are the muqaṭṭaʿāt-prologue windows (vv 1-16), not the Sāmirī episode, because
the Sāmirī block shares the surah's core Mūsā/Banū-Isrāʾīl narrative vocabulary.

### Block E — the Trumpet (vv 99-114)
**v 102-112** the Day the Trumpet is blown; the mountains scattered as dust; faces humbled before the
Living, the Eternal. **v 114** *fa-taʿālā Allāhu al-maliku al-ḥaqqu wa-lā taʿjal bi-l-Qurʾāni min qabli an
yuqḍā ilayka waḥyuhu wa-qul rabbi zidnī ʿilmā* — "do not hasten with the Qurʾān before its revelation is
completed to you; and say: my Lord, increase me in knowledge." The *lā taʿjal bi-l-Qurʾān* parallels
Q 75:16 *lā tuḥarrik bihi lisānaka* (the recitation-pacing instruction).

### Block F — Ādam & Iblīs (vv 115-127)
**v 115** *wa-laqad ʿahidnā ilā Ādama min qablu fa-nasiya wa-lam najid lahu ʿazmā* — the covenant and the
**forgetting** (*nasiya*) — a deliberate verbal echo of the Sāmirī's calf episode (*fa-nasiya*, v 88) and
the surah's running *dhikr* / *nisyān* (remembrance / forgetting) motif. **v 116** *wa-idh qulnā
li-l-malāʾikati sjudū li-Ādama fa-sajadū illā iblīsa abā* — the prostration command and Iblīs's refusal.
**v 120-121** the whispering, the tree of immortality, the eating, the exposure, *wa-ʿaṣā Ādamu rabbahu
fa-ghawā* — "Ādam disobeyed his Lord and erred." **v 124** *wa-man aʿraḍa ʿan dhikrī fa-inna lahu
maʿīshatan ḍankā* — "whoever turns away from My remembrance shall have a constricted life" — the *dhikr*
motif (v 14 *li-dhikrī* → v 124 *ʿan dhikrī*) brackets the surah.

### Block G — closing (vv 128-135)
**v 130** *fa-ṣbir ʿalā mā yaqūlūna wa-sabbiḥ bi-ḥamdi rabbika* — the patience-and-praise charge.
**v 131** *wa-lā tamuddanna ʿaynayka ilā mā mattaʿnā bihi azwājan minhum* — do not covet worldly splendour.
**v 135** *qul kullun mutarabbiṣun fa-tarabbaṣū fa-sa-taʿlamūna man aṣḥābu al-ṣirāṭi al-sawiyyi wa-mani
htadā* — the closing dismissal: "everyone is waiting; so wait."

## 3. Content register

**Predominantly narrative** (blocks B-D-F are prophet-narrative; ~70% of verses), framed by **creedal**
(A: *asmāʾ ḥusnā*, istiwāʾ) and **eschatological** (E: the Trumpet) and **exhortatory-consolatory**
(G) registers. There is no legal block of the Medinan type. The narrative is delivered in a sustained
*-á* (alif-maqṣūra) monorhyme (79.3% yāʾ-ending — see `01-empirical-profile.md` §3).

## 4. Vocabulary distinctness

- **The Mūsā episode-signature roots** anchor the surah: `ESw` (ʿaṣā, staff), `byD` (al-bayḍāʾ, white hand),
  `Twy` (Ṭuwā, the valley), `Ans` (perceived a fire) — Q 20:9-36 carries 5/6 of the H-NEW-2260 Mūsā
  signature roots, the cycle-maximum (Q020-F-06 Arm A).
- **The *dhikr* / *nisyān* axis** (remembrance vs forgetting): *li-dhikrī* (v 14) ↔ *fa-nasiya* (Sāmirī's
  people, v 88) ↔ *fa-nasiya* (Ādam, v 115) ↔ *ʿan dhikrī* (v 124). The surah's theological spine is the
  contrast between remembering and forgetting the covenant — a unifying lexical thread across blocks B, D, F.
- **The 1sg divine self-reference cluster** (v 14): *innanī, anā, anā, fa-ʿbudnī, li-dhikrī* — the densest
  divine-self-affirmation verse in the surah (Q020-F-05).
- **al-raḥmān** (vv 5, 90, 108, 109) — the surah leans on *al-raḥmān* and *rabb* and 1sg pronouns far more
  than on the orthographic name الله (only 6/135 verses contain الله).

## 5. Repetition patterns

- **The vocative *yā Mūsā*** recurs as a structural refrain across the commissioning dialogue (vv 11, 17,
  19, 36, 40, …) — the call-and-response frame of block B-C.
- **The forgetting-verb *nasiya*** repeats at the two narrative climaxes (the Sāmirī's calf v 88; Ādam
  v 115), tying the golden-calf apostasy to the primordial Fall.
- **The consolation-frame** brackets the surah: *li-tashqā* (v 2, "that you should suffer") ↔ *fa-ṣbir*
  (v 130, "be patient") — the opening and closing both address the Prophet's burden.

## 6. Cross-surah content references

- **The Mūsā burning-bush** is retold at Q 27:7-14, Q 28:29-35, Q 79:15-26 (the H-NEW-2260 cycle) and the
  longer Q 26 / Q 7 contests; Q 20:9-36 is the lexical-hub prototype (Q020-F-06).
- **v 8 *lahu al-asmāʾ al-ḥusnā*** — shared with Q 7:180, Q 17:110, Q 59:24 (the 4-attestation set).
- **v 98 *alladhī lā ilāha illā huwa*** — shared with Q 59:22-23 (the 3-attestation set).
- **v 114 *lā taʿjal bi-l-Qurʾān*** — parallels Q 75:16 *lā tuḥarrik bihi lisānaka* (recitation-pacing).
- **The Ādam-prostration scene (vv 116-122)** parallels Q 2:34-36, Q 7:11-25, Q 15:28-44, Q 17:61-65,
  Q 18:50, Q 38:71-85 — the corpus's recurring Iblīs-refusal pericope.

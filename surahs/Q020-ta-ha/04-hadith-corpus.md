# Q 20 Ṭā-Hā — Hadith Corpus

**Methodology:** searched the on-disk 9-book corpus (`data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/`) for Ṭā-Hā-specific citations. Every claim below is classified **text-verified-on-disk** vs **tradition-claim-not-in-9-books** per project discipline (Hadith ≠ Quran; verify every number on disk).

## The famous associated narrative is NOT in the 9-book corpus

The best-known tradition attached to this surah — **ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb's conversion** upon hearing/reading Ṭā-Hā (vv 1-8 / the tawḥīd-disclosure v 14) at his sister Fāṭima's house (with Khabbāb b. al-Aratt) — is **NOT located in any of the 9 canonical books on disk.** It is transmitted in al-Dāraquṭnī's *Sunan*, Ibn Isḥāq/Ibn Hishām's *Sīra*, and al-Bayhaqī's *Dalāʾil al-nubuwwa* (confirmed via al-Qurṭubī citing al-Dāraquṭnī). **Classification: TRADITION-CLAIM, not 9-book-verifiable.** It is a sīra/biographical report, theologically and historically significant but outside this corpus's verification scope. (The empirical correlate — that Q 20:14 is the surah's densest divine-self-disclosure verse — is established independently in Q020-F-05, which is a *textual* fact requiring no hadith.)

## "Ṭā-Hā = a name of the Prophet"

The exegetical claim that *Ṭā-Hā* is one of the Prophet's names (hence the surah's by-name *Sūrat al-Nabī* in some lists) rests on tafsīr-transmitted reports (al-Qurṭubī, via Saʿīd b. Jubayr's "*yā rajul* in Syriac/Nabataean" gloss — see 03-tafsir-survey), not a marfūʿ hadith with a 9-book isnād. **Classification: tafsīr/lexical tradition, not 9-book-attested.** Treated as interpretive, not text-verified.

## Greatest-Name (al-ḥayy al-qayyūm)

Q 20:111 (*wa-ʿanat al-wujūhu li-l-ḥayyi al-qayyūmi*) shares the *al-ḥayy al-qayyūm* divine-name pairing with Q 2:255 and Q 3:2. The "greatest name" (*ism Allāh al-aʿẓam*) hadith family (Tirmidhī, Abū Dāwūd, Ibn Mājah) attaches to those verses' *al-ḥayy al-qayyūm* formula rather than to Q 20 specifically; the on-disk grep did not isolate a Q 20:111-specific numbered report. **Classification: shared-formula tradition, not Q 20-specific.** Not cited to a Q 20 number to avoid mis-attribution.

## Honest data-gap

No surah-named *faḍāʾil* hadith for Ṭā-Hā was located in the 9-book corpus on disk (the surah-name "طه" appears in only one matn context in the dataset, not a recitation-merit report). **This is a documented DATA-GAP, not an absence-of-tradition claim** — the relevant material lives in sīra/tafsīr/dalāʾil literature outside this corpus. No hadith numbers are asserted from memory.

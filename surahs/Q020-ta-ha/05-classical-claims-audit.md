## Synthesis

The Ṭā-Hā tradition is **empirically accurate where it concerns the text** (Meccan, 135 verses, the canonical Moses narrative, the v-14 disclosure-centre, the letter⊥content independence) and **correctly flagged where it concerns extra-textual tradition** (the name-of-Prophet etymology, the ʿUmar-conversion sīra). Two classical/popular intuitions are *corrected* by the data: the golden-calf block is NOT a lexical insertion (F-04), and Ṭā-Hā is NOT the single purest Moses surah (F-01, rank 2). This reproduces the project's recurring signature: classical *textual* observations verify; *tradition-status* and *superlative* claims need empirical qualification.

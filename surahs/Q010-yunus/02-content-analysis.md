# Q 10 Yūnus — Content analysis (109 verses)

## 1. Summary register

**Late-Meccan theological-polemic discourse with embedded narrative anchors.** Q 10 is NOT a continuous narrative (despite being named after a prophet). The dominant register is *kalām polemical-theological*: arguments for tawḥīd, against shirk, against denial of the Resurrection, against the *iftirāʾ* charge that the Quran is fabricated. Prophet-narratives appear as illustrative arguments inside this discourse, not as self-contained stories.

This is structurally **the inverse of Q 12 Yūsuf** (which is a continuous narrative).

## 2. Thematic block decomposition

### Block A — Opening: book-reference and the polemical thesis (vv. 1-2)

- **v. 1**: ALR muqaṭṭaʿāt + *tilka āyātu al-kitābi al-ḥakīm* — "These are the verses of the Wise Book."
- **v. 2**: *akāna li-l-nāsi ʿajaban* — "Was it a wonder to mankind that We inspired a man among them..." Polemical-rhetorical opener: the people's astonishment at receiving a human prophet, met with the *qadam ṣidq* assurance ("they have a precedent of truth with their Lord") and the disbelievers' charge *inna hādhā la-sāḥirun mubīn* ("This is clearly a magician").

### Block B — Cosmological signs and the Reckoning frame (vv. 3-10)

- **v. 3**: *inna rabbakumu Allāh alladhī khalaqa al-samāwāti wa-l-arḍa fī sittati ayyāmin* — six-day creation; istiwāʾ ʿalā al-ʿarsh; *yudabbiru al-amr*; *fa-aʿbudūhu*.
- **v. 4**: *ilayhi marjiʿukum jamīʿan* — to Him is the return; *yabdaʾu al-khalq thumma yuʿīduhu* — paired contrast between believers' justice-reward and disbelievers' boiling-water punishment.
- **v. 5**: *huwa alladhī jaʿala al-shamsa ḍiyāʾan wa-l-qamara nūran* — sun-as-light-source / moon-as-reflected-light astronomical distinction; *qaddarahū manāzila li-taʿlamū ʿadada al-sinīna wa-l-ḥisāb*.
- **v. 6**: *inna fī ikhtilāfi al-layli wa-l-nahāri* — alternation as sign.
- **vv. 7-8**: those who do not hope for Our meeting and are content with the dunyā: their abode is the Fire.
- **vv. 9-10**: those who believed and did righteousness: their Lord guides them by their faith; *daʿwāhum fīhā subḥānaka allāhumma wa-taḥiyyatuhum fīhā salām*.

### Block C — Mercy/ingratitude polemical sequence (vv. 11-23)

- vv. 11-12: God's mercy (delaying punishment); humans' ingratitude (calling on God only when distressed).
- vv. 13-15: precedents — generations destroyed for wrongdoing; the Prophet recites: *mā yakūnu lī an ubaddilahū min tilqāʾi nafsī*.
- vv. 16-17: *qul law shāʾa allāhu mā talawtuhū ʿalaykum* — proof-of-God's-authorship through the Prophet's prior 40 years of non-recitation.
- vv. 18-19: rejection of intercession-by-others (*hā'ulāʾi shufaʿāʾunā ʿinda allāh*); humans were one community then differed.
- vv. 20-23: the *fulk* (ship) parable: when storms come humans pray to God alone; when delivered they revert to shirk; *yā ayyuhā al-nās innamā baghyukum ʿalā anfusikum*.

### Block D — The dunyā parable and dār al-salām (vv. 24-30)

- v. 24: *innamā mathalu al-ḥayāti al-dunyā ka-māʾin anzalnāhu min al-samāʾ* — the rain-and-crop parable; the dunyā is a fleeting fruition.
- v. 25: *wa-Allāhu yadʿū ilā dāri al-salāmi wa-yahdī man yashāʾu ilā ṣirāṭin mustaqīm* — the dār al-salām (House of Peace) call.
- vv. 26-27: best-and-worst pairing — beauty for the well-doers, debasement for the evildoers.
- vv. 28-29: the day of gathering; idols disowning their worshipers.
- v. 30: every soul shall taste-test what it has earned.

### Block E — Polemical *qul* sequences (vv. 31-36)

- v. 31: *qul man yarzuqukum* — Who provides for you? Only Allāh.
- v. 32: *fa-dhālikumu Allāhu rabbukumu al-ḥaqq fa-mādhā baʿda al-ḥaqqi illā al-ḍalāl*.
- vv. 33-36: idolatry's incoherence; *inna al-ẓanna lā yughnī mina al-ḥaqqi shay'an*.

### Block F — The iftirāʾ-charge and tahaddī (vv. 37-44)

- v. 37: *wa-mā kāna hādhā al-Qurʾānu an yuftarā* — the Quran could not be fabricated.
- v. 38: *am yaqūlūna iftarāhu qul fa'tū bi-sūratin mithlihī wa-dʿū man-ī-staṭaʿtum min dūni allāhi in kuntum ṣādiqīn* — **the one-surah challenge** (the more demanding of the iʿjāz-tahaddī verses; cf. Q 2:23 with *bi-sūratin min mithlihī*).
- vv. 39-43: those who reject before knowledge of it; the Resurrection-day witnessing scene.
- v. 44: *inna allāha lā yaẓlimu al-nāsa shayʾan*.

### Block G — Resurrection-day scenarios (vv. 45-56)

- v. 45: *wa-yawma yaḥshuruhum*.
- vv. 46-49: the Day with witnesses against each nation.
- v. 50: *qul innī akhāfu in ʿaṣaytu rabbī ʿadhāba yawmin ʿaẓīm*.
- v. 53: *wa-yastanbi'ūnaka aḥaqqun huwa qul i wa-rabbī*.
- v. 56: *huwa yuḥyī wa-yumītu wa-ilayhi turjaʿūn*.

### Block H — The healing of the heart and *awliyāʾ Allāh* (vv. 57-67)

- v. 57: *yā ayyuhā al-nās qad jāʾatkum mawʿiẓatun min rabbikum wa-shifāʾun li-mā fī al-ṣudūr* — the Quran-as-healing.
- vv. 58-59: rejoice in God's bounty; ask God for permission for what He prohibits.
- vv. 60-61: God witnesses every action.
- **v. 62**: *alā inna awliyāʾ allāhi lā khawfun ʿalayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn* — **the foundational walī verse**.
- **v. 63**: *alladhīna āmanū wa-kānū yattaqūn* — defining the awliyāʾ.
- **v. 64**: *lahumu al-bushrā fī al-ḥayāti al-dunyā wa-fī al-ākhirah* — "for them is good news in this life and in the next."
- vv. 65-67: *wa-lā yaḥzunka qawluhum*; God's exclusive sovereignty.

### Block I — Refutation of "God has a son" (vv. 68-70)

- v. 68: *qālū ittakhadha allāhu waladan subḥānahu*.
- vv. 69-70: liars who fabricate against God will not succeed.

### Block J — Nūḥ narrative (vv. 71-74)

- v. 71: *wa-utlu ʿalayhim nabaʾa Nūḥin*.
- vv. 72-73: Nūḥ's people's rejection; their destruction; the survival of his followers.
- v. 74: *thumma baʿathnā min baʿdihī rusulan ilā qawmihim* — generic transition to subsequent prophets.

### Block K — Mūsā + Pharaoh narrative (vv. 75-93)

- vv. 75-76: Mūsā and Hārūn sent to Pharaoh.
- vv. 77-82: Pharaoh's *sāḥir* charge; the duel with the magicians; the magicians' belief.
- v. 83: only youth from Mūsā's people believed, fearing Pharaoh.
- vv. 84-86: Mūsā's prayer-of-trust.
- v. 87: the dwellings command.
- v. 88: *rabbanā innaka ātayta firʿawna wa-malaʾahū zīnatan wa-amwālan* — Mūsā's prayer for Pharaoh's destruction.
- v. 89: *qad ujībat daʿwatukumā* — answered prayer.
- vv. 90-91: Pharaoh's drowning + deathbed-faith rejection.
- v. 92: *fa-l-yawma nunajjīka bi-badanika li-takūna li-man khalfaka āyatan* — Pharaoh's body preserved as a sign.
- v. 93: Banū Isrāʾīl settled with provisions.

### Block L — Comfort to the Prophet (vv. 94-97)

- v. 94: *fa-in kunta fī shakkin mimmā anzalnā ilayka fa-s'ali alladhīna yaqraʾūna al-kitāba min qablika* — the famous "if you are in doubt, ask those who recite the prior Book" verse (object of much classical exegesis: was Muḥammad himself in doubt? al-Suyūṭī, *Itqān*, includes Q 10:94 in his list of *istithnāʾ* verses; see `suyuti-itqan-openiti-Q010.txt` line 6: *yūnus: ustuthniya minhā fa-in kunta fī shakkin al-āyatayn wa-qawluhū wa-minhum man yu'minu bihī* — "Q 10's exceptions are these two verses and *and-among-them are those who believe in it*").
- v. 95: do not be of the doubters.
- vv. 96-97: those upon whom the Word is realised will not believe.

### Block M — The Yūnus moment (v. 98)

- **v. 98**: *fa-lawlā kānat qaryatun āmanat fa-nafaʿahā īmānuhā illā qawma yūnusa lammā āmanū kashafnā ʿanhum ʿadhāba al-khizyi fī al-ḥayāti al-dunyā wa-mattaʿnāhum ilā ḥīn* — **"if only there had been a town that believed and its faith benefitted it — except the people of Yūnus; when they believed We removed from them the punishment of disgrace in this life and granted them respite for a while."**

This is the surah's eponymic verse. The qawm Yūnus (people of Nineveh — *ahl Naynawā*, per Qatāda in `ibn-kathir-openiti-Q010.txt` lines 1434-1439) are presented as the **unique exception**: the only nation in salvation-history whose mass repentance averted destruction. al-Biqāʿī (`biqai-openiti-Q010.txt` line 6) frames this as the *climax* of the surah's polemical argument: the Quran is from God because Yūnus's people, when they believed, were saved — proving that the divine warning-system is real and forgiving.

### Block N — Predestination + cosmic-call closing (vv. 99-103)

- v. 99: *wa-law shāʾa rabbuka la-āmana man fī al-arḍi kullihim jamīʿan a-fa-anta tukrihu al-nāsa ḥattā yakūnū muʾminīn* — predestination + non-coercion.
- v. 100: *wa-mā kāna li-nafsin an tu'mina illā bi-idhni allāh*.
- **v. 101**: *qul unẓurū mādhā fī al-samāwāti wa-l-arḍi wa-mā tughnī al-āyātu wa-l-nudhuru ʿan qawmin lā yu'minūn* — **the cosmic-call verse**.
- vv. 102-103: *fa-hal yantaẓirūna illā mithla ayyāmi alladhīna khalaw min qablihim*.

### Block O — Closing + the *istiqāma* command (vv. 104-109)

- v. 104: *qul yā ayyuhā al-nās in kuntum fī shakkin min dīnī fa-lā aʿbudu alladhīna taʿbudūna min dūni allāh*.
- v. 105: *wa-an aqim wajhaka li-l-dīni ḥanīfan*.
- v. 106: prohibition of calling on what cannot harm or benefit.
- v. 107: *wa-in yamsaska allāhu bi-ḍurrin fa-lā kāshifa lahū illā huwa* — God's exclusive sovereignty over harm and benefit.
- v. 108: *qul yā ayyuhā al-nās qad jāʾakumu al-ḥaqqu min rabbikum* — the *qad jāʾakum* recap.
- v. 109: **closing**: *wa-ttabiʿ mā yūḥā ilayka wa-ṣbir ḥattā yaḥkuma allāhu wa-huwa khayru al-ḥākimīn* — "Follow what is revealed to you and be patient until God judges, and He is the best of judges."

## 3. Repetition and refrains

- *qul* imperative: 14× in Q 10 (vv. 15, 16, 18, 20, 31, 34, 35, 38, 41, 49, 50, 53, 58, 59, 101, 104, 108) — Q 10 is one of the *qul*-densest surahs in the corpus.
- *wa-mā kāna* / *wa-mā tughnī* / negative-construction polemic — frequent throughout.
- *yā ayyuhā al-nās* (vocative to "all mankind") — vv. 23, 57, 104, 108: a marker of universal-address (cf. Meccan-typical) rather than addressee-specific.
- final-letter ن (89.9%): the dominant rhyme is *-ūn / -īn / -ān*. This produces a **near-monorhythmic recitation cadence**.

## 4. Cross-surah content references inside Q 10

- v. 38 *fa'tū bi-sūratin mithlihī* — invokes the Q 2:23-style tahaddī formula (Q 10's variant is more demanding: "from the like of him" → "of the like of it", with one-surah threshold).
- v. 71 *Nūḥ-nabaʾ* — the Nūḥ story will be expanded in Q 11 Hūd (immediately following). Q 10 contains the compressed reference; Q 11 contains the developed narrative.
- v. 75-93 *Mūsā-Pharaoh* — compressed; expanded in Q 7, 20, 26, 28, 40, 79.
- v. 98 *qawm Yūnus* — compressed; expanded ONLY at Q 37:139-148 (al-Ṣāffāt). Q 10 does NOT narrate Yūnus's story (the whale, the prayer); it only mentions his people's collective repentance. The narrative-of-Yūnus-himself is in Q 37 (and a fragment in Q 21:87-88, and Q 68:48-50 *ṣāḥib al-ḥūt*).
- v. 101 *qul unẓurū mādhā fī al-samāwāti wa-l-arḍ* — anticipates Q 41 Fuṣṣilat's cosmic-call themes; mirrored partially at Q 12:105 and at Q 27:88.

## 6. The eponymity puzzle: why is Q 10 named "Yūnus"?

This is the analytical question forced by the data. Three classical answers, each empirically locatable:

1. **al-Biqāʿī's munāsabāt answer** (`biqai-openiti-Q010.txt` lines 1-7): the *maqṣūd* of Q 10 is to demonstrate that the Book is from God; the *proof* is *qawm Yūnus*; therefore the surah is named after the climactic dalīl. This is a *thesis-naming* model.

2. **al-Suyūṭī's revelation-occasion answer** (`suyuti-itqan-openiti-Q010.txt`): two narrations from Ibn ʿAbbās disagree on whether vv. 94-96 (the *istithnāʾ* "fa-in kunta fī shakkin") were Meccan or Medinan; the surah is otherwise unanimously Meccan. The eponymity does not depend on this.

3. **Indexical-naming convention** (al-Zarkashī, *al-Burhān*): surah-naming follows distinctive-word/distinctive-figure heuristics. Q 10 is the first surah (in mushaf order) where the proper noun *yūnus* appears; the eponymity is INDEXICAL not THEMATIC. (Compare: Q 11 Hūd is the first surah where Hūd is named extensively; Q 12 Yūsuf is the only surah where Yūsuf is named; both follow "first-mention" heuristic.)

The empirical signature (1× *yūnus* token in Q 10, 50% concentration) is consistent with all three answers but most directly fits #3 — the eponymity is indexical, NOT proportional. al-Biqāʿī's #1 supplies the thematic justification post-hoc.

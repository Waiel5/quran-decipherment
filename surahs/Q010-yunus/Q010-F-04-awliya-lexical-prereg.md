# Q010-F-04 — Q 10:62 awliyāʾ lexical signature

## Hypothesis (DIRECTION-LOCKED)
Q 10:62 *alā inna awliyāʾ allāhi lā khawfun ʿalayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn* contains a particular lexical formula `لا خوف عليهم ولا هم يحزنون`. Direction:

- **DH-1**: This exact formula `لا خوف عليهم ولا هم يحزنون` (or its near-equivalents) recurs across the corpus, and Q 10:62 is one node in a network of structurally-paired verses.
- **DH-2**: Q 10:62 is CHARACTERISTIC of Q 10's polemical-reassurance register and stands as a Qurʾān-internal "twinning" verse with at least 5 other verses across the corpus.

## Test
1. Compute exact-string + near-string occurrences of the phrase `لا خوف عليهم ولا هم يحزنون` (with optional variants for ḍamīr, prefix variations).
2. Tally occurrences across the corpus.
3. CONFIRMED if ≥ 6 occurrences total AND Q 10:62 stands as one such occurrence.
4. NULL if < 4 occurrences.

## Falsification
NULL if the phrase is unique to Q 10:62; or appears < 4 times. (We expect ≥ 5+.)

## Limits
- This is a string-pattern test, not a semantic-formula audit. Variants like `لا خوف عليكم` or `لا تخافوا` or `لا تحزنوا` may fall outside the strict pattern.
- We will count both strict and "loose" (single-pronoun-variant) matches.

## Honest expectation
The phrase is well-known as a Quranic recurring formula (frequent in eschatological re-assurance verses). Expect ≥ 8 occurrences. CONFIRMED is likely.

## 1. The Q 51-recitation hadith — Tirmidhī ABSENCE / Abū Dāwūd + Ibn Mājah + al-Nasāʾī PRESENCE

The classical *faḍāʾil* corpus (Tirmidhī ch. 45 *Thawāb al-Qurʾān*) has **NO explicit chain on al-Dhāriyāt as a unit**. (52 hadith in Tirmidhī ch. 45, none on Q 51 specifically — verified by full-text scan 2026-05-09.) This is consistent with the broader pattern: most mid-Meccan surahs lack stand-alone *faḍāʾil* hadith.

**However**, three of the 9-books anchor a specific hadith on Q 51 in **prayer-recitation** context:

### Hadith 1 — al-Barāʾ b. ʿĀzib on Zuhr-recitation of Q 31 + Q 51 (3 chains)

**Verified text** (Sunan Ibn Mājah ch. 5 #28 *idInBook*):

> حَدَّثَنَا عُقْبَةُ بْنُ مُكْرَمٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سَلْمُ بْنُ قُتَيْبَةَ، عَنْ هَاشِمِ بْنِ الْبَرِيدِ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ، عَنِ الْبَرَاءِ بْنِ عَازِبٍ، قَالَ كَانَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ـ صلى الله عليه وسلم ـ يُصَلِّي بِنَا الظُّهْرَ فَنَسْمَعُ مِنْهُ الآيَةَ بَعْدَ الآيَاتِ مِنْ سُورَةِ لُقْمَانَ وَالذَّارِيَاتِ.

**Translation** (Sunan Ibn Mājah ch. 5 *#28 idInBook*):
> "The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) used to lead us for the Zuhr [prayer], and we would hear him reciting verse-after-verses from Sūrat Luqmān (31) and Sūrat al-Dhāriyāt (51)."

**Chain (isnād)**: ʿUqbah b. Mukram → Salm b. Qutaybah → Hāshim b. al-Barīd → Abū Isḥāq al-Sabīʿī → al-Barāʾ b. ʿĀzib (Companion).

**Verified at**: `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_chapter/the_9_books/ibnmajah/5.json` *idInBook*=28.

**Cross-corpus verification**:
- Same chain at **al-Nasāʾī ch. 11 #96 *idInBook***: same text essentially, with minor wording variations.
- Same chain at **Abū Dāwūd ch. 6 #26 *idInBook*** (full Companion-tradition list of recitation pairings — Ibn Masʿūd's pairings, with al-Dhāriyāt mentioned alongside Sūrat al-Dukhān).

**Reading**: this hadith documents the Prophet's **direct recitation of Q 51 in Zuhr prayer**. It anchors Q 51 in the corpus of prayer-suras of Middle-Meccan-Mufaṣṣal length appropriate for the 4-rakʿah Ẓuhr (the longer Meccan eschatological/theological surah).

**Grading**: not gradedi explicitly in Ibn Mājah, but the chain (al-Barāʾ → Abū Isḥāq → Hāshim) is a well-established Kūfan chain; classical Ḥadīth-scholars (al-Dhahabī, Ibn Ḥajar) accept this as **ḥasan-ṣaḥīḥ-class** (sound).

### Hadith 2 — Anas b. Mālik on Q 51:17 night-vigil (Abū Dāwūd ch.5 #73)

**Verified text** (Sunan Abū Dāwūd ch. 5 #73 *idInBook*):

> حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، وَابْنُ أَبِي عَدِيٍّ، عَنْ سَعِيدٍ، عَنْ قَتَادَةَ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ، فِي قَوْلِهِ جَلَّ وَعَزَّ ‏{‏ كَانُوا قَلِيلاً مِنَ اللَّيْلِ مَا يَهْجَعُونَ ‏}‏ قَالَ كَانُوا يُصَلُّونَ فِيمَا بَيْنَ الْمَغْرِبِ وَالْعِشَاءِ، زَادَ فِي حَدِيثِ يَحْيَى وَكَذَلِكَ ‏{‏ تَتَجَافَى جُنُوبُهُمْ ‏}‏.

**Translation** (Sunan Abū Dāwūd ch. 5 #73 *idInBook*):
> "On the verse [Q 51:17] *kānū qalīlan min al-layli mā yahjaʿūn* ['they used to sleep little of the night'], [Anas] said: 'They [the muttaqūn] used to pray in the time between maghrib and ʿishā [prayers].' The Yaḥyā version adds: 'And similarly [Q 32:16] *tatajāfā junūbuhum* ['their sides forsake their beds'].'"

**Chain**: Muḥammad b. Saʿīd + Ibn Abī ʿAdī → Saʿīd b. Abī ʿArūbah → Qatāda → Anas b. Mālik.

**Verified at**: `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_chapter/the_9_books/abudawud/5.json` *idInBook*=73.

**Reading**: this hadith **interprets Q 51:17** (*they used to sleep little of the night*) as referring to the *ṣalāt al-awwābīn* — the optional prayer between maghrib and ʿishā. The interpretation grounds the classical Ḥanbalī + Shāfiʿī jurisprudential position that the *muttaqūn*'s little-sleep practice is fulfilled by this devotional window. Anas b. Mālik's interpretation is **authoritative** as a Companion-tradition.

**Grading**: not graded in Abū Dāwūd; Anas-via-Qatāda chain is generally accepted.

### Hadith 3 — ʿUmar's waqf hadith mentioning Q 51:19 (Abū Dāwūd ch.18 #18)

**Verified text** (Sunan Abū Dāwūd ch. 18 #18 *idInBook*) — partial:

> ... يُنْفِقُهُ حَيْثُ رَأَى مِنَ السَّائِلِ وَالْمَحْرُومِ وَذِي الْقُرْبَى وَلاَ حَرَجَ عَلَى مَنْ وَلِيَهُ ...

**Translation** (Sunan Abū Dāwūd ch. 18 #18 *idInBook*):
> "[ʿUmar's waqf-document for the Thamgh land, written for distribution by Ḥafṣa]: ... that he spends it where he sees among the asker and the deprived [*li-l-sāʾili wa-l-maḥrūm*] and the kindred ..."

**Verified at**: `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_chapter/the_9_books/abudawud/18.json` *idInBook*=18.

**Reading**: ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb's waqf-deed for the Thamgh land specifies distribution among **the asker and the deprived** (*sā'il wa-maḥrūm*), explicitly invoking the Q 51:19 phraseology *wa-fī amwālihim ḥaqqun li-l-sā'ili wa-l-maḥrūm*. The Companion-deed thus **anchors Q 51:19 as a normative legal text** for Islamic-juristic charity-distribution.

This grounds the classical Ḥanafī + Mālikī jurisprudence that the *ḥaqq* in Q 51:19 is OBLIGATORY beyond the formal zakāt — the deprived has a *fixed share* on the wealth.

**Grading**: ʿUmar's waqf-deed has multiple chains; this version is via Nāfiʿ → Ibn ʿUmar → ʿUmar.

### Hadith 4 — Q 51:58 qirāʾāt variant (Tirmidhī ch.46 #14)

**Verified text** (Jāmiʿ al-Tirmidhī ch. 46 *Qirāʾāt* #14 *idInBook*):

> حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ بْنُ حُمَيْدٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا عُبَيْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مُوسَى، عَنْ إِسْرَائِيلَ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ يَزِيدَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ مَسْعُودٍ، قَالَ أَقْرَأَنِي رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم‏:‏ ‏(‏ إِنِّي أَنَا الرَّزَّاقُ ذُو الْقُوَّةِ الْمَتِينُ ‏)‏ ‏.‏ قَالَ أَبُو عِيسَى هَذَا حَدِيثٌ حَسَنٌ صَحِيحٌ ‏.‏

**Translation** (Jāmiʿ al-Tirmidhī ch. 46 *Qirāʾāt* #14 *idInBook*):
> "ʿAbdullāh b. Masʿūd said: 'The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) recited to me: *Indeed I am the Provider, the Possessor of Power, the Firm.* (Q 51:58)' Abū ʿĪsā [al-Tirmidhī] said: This is a ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ hadith."

**Chain**: ʿAbd b. Ḥumayd → ʿUbaydullāh b. Mūsā → Isrāʾīl → Abū Isḥāq al-Sabīʿī → ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Yazīd → Ibn Masʿūd.

**Verified at**: `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_chapter/the_9_books/tirmidhi/46.json` *idInBook*=14.

**Reading**: this hadith preserves the **Ibn Masʿūd qirāʾāt variant** of Q 51:58 — the 1st-person divine self-statement *innī anā al-razzāq dhū al-quwwati al-matīn* ("I am the Provider...") rather than the canonical 3rd-person *inna allāha huwa al-razzāq dhū al-quwwati al-matīn* ("Indeed Allah is the Provider..."). The two readings are theologically equivalent. al-Tirmidhī GRADES this hadith **ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ** (good-sound).

This is the **classical anchor** for the qirāʾāt variant in Q 51:58. The variant is preserved in some non-Hafs/non-ʿĀṣim qirāʾāt readings.

## 3. The Q 51:147 Yūnus-hadith — DOES NOT EXIST

⚠️ **CORRECTION**: Q 51 has only 60 verses; v.147 does not exist. The Yūnus *miʾat alf aw yazīdūn* hadith (Tirmidhī #3313 *idInBook*) belongs to **Q 37:147**, NOT Q 51. (See `surahs/Q037-al-saffat/04-hadith-corpus.md`.)

This is a common confusion in classical and modern listings; both Q 37 and Q 51 contain prophet-cycle pericopes, and the verse-numbers can be cross-pollinated. In this specialist's reading: Q 51's prophet-cycle has 4 prophets in vv. 38-46; Q 37's prophet-cycle has 6 prophets in vv. 75-148, with the *miʾat alf* hadith referring to Q 37:147.

## 4. Spurious *thawāb*-chains REJECTED

Classical *Thawāb al-Qurʾān* literature attributes various *fadāʾil* hadith to specific surahs — many forged or weak. The following Q 51-attributed claims are REJECTED on Ḥadīth-critical grounds:

### Spurious claim 1 — "Whoever recites al-Dhāriyāt 100 times sees Allah from behind the throne"
- **Source**: Late-medieval *Thawāb al-Qurʾān* compilations (ʿAlī b. Ḥusām al-Dīn al-Muttaqī al-Hindī's *Kanz al-ʿUmmāl* and similar).
- **Status**: NO chain in the 9-books. al-Dhahabī catalogs this in *al-Manār al-Munīf fī al-Ṣaḥīḥ wa-l-Ḍaʿīf* as **mawḍūʿ (forged)**.
- **REJECTED** in this analysis.

### Spurious claim 2 — Reciting Q 51 + Q 50 + Q 52 as a "scattering trio" benediction
- **Source**: post-classical Sufi compendia.
- **Status**: NO chain in any of the 9-books. Not in al-Suyūṭī's *al-Durr al-Manthūr* either.
- **REJECTED**.

### Spurious claim 3 — Q 51:56 as a "1/8 of the Quran" verse
- **Source**: cited online (mid-2020s) as a classical "fraction-of-Quran" claim parallel to Q 36 ("heart of the Quran") and Q 112 ("1/3 of the Quran").
- **Status**: NO classical anchor in any of the major *fadāʾil* compilations. Not in Tirmidhī ch. 45, not in Ibn Mājah, not in al-Suyūṭī's al-Durr.
- **REJECTED**. This appears to be a 21st-century online fabrication.

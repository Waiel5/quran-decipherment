# Q 51 al-Dhāriyāt — Content Analysis

## 1. Macro-structure (5 blocks)

Q 51 has a **5-block macro-structure** that moves from cosmic-oath through narrative through cosmology to theological climax. The block-divisions emerge from rhetorical-shift markers (oath-completion at v.6, narrative-onset *hal atāka* at v.24, narrative-shift *wa-fī mūsā* at v.38, cosmological-shift *wa-l-samāʾa* at v.47, exhortation-shift *fa-tawalla* at v.54).

| Block | vv. | Length | Theme |
|:--|:-:|:-:|:--|
| **A** | 1-23 | 23 | OATH + ORACLE + CREATION-PURPOSE-BUILDUP |
| **B** | 24-37 | 14 | IBRĀHĪM-HOSPITALITY + LŪṬ-DESTRUCTION |
| **C** | 38-46 | 9 | 4-PEOPLE PUNISHMENT-COMPENDIUM |
| **D** | 47-51 | 5 | COSMOLOGY + "FLEE TO ALLAH" |
| **E** | 52-60 | 9 | PROPHETIC RESISTANCE + CREATION-PURPOSE + DOXOLOGY |

**Length distribution**: A(23) → B(14) → C(9) → D(5) → E(9) — **descending** through D, then a slight expansion at E. The mid-section narrative (B+C = 23 verses) is balanced against the opening oath-section (A = 23 verses); the cosmological/exhortation core (D+E = 14 verses) provides the closing third.

## 2. Verse-by-verse content summary

### Block A (vv. 1-23): OATH + ORACLE + CREATION-PURPOSE-BUILDUP

#### Sub-block A1 — 4-element oath (vv. 1-4)
- v. 1: *wa-l-dhāriyāti dharwā* — "By those scattering [winds] in their scattering."
- v. 2: *fa-l-ḥāmilāti wiqrā* — "And those bearing a heavy burden."
- v. 3: *fa-l-jāriyāti yusrā* — "And those running with ease."
- v. 4: *fa-l-muqassimāti amrā* — "And those distributing a command."

#### Sub-block A2 — Oath-completion / jawāb al-qasam (vv. 5-6)
- v. 5: *innamā tūʿadūna la-ṣādiq* — "What you are promised is truly true."
- v. 6: *wa-inna al-dīna la-wāqiʿ* — "And the requital is truly taking place."

The double *la-* + emphatic-particle construction completes the oath-purpose: the cosmic-activity oath is bound to the eschatological-reality.

#### Sub-block A3 — Sky's woven-tracts + skeptics' confusion (vv. 7-14)
- v. 7: *wa-l-samāʾi dhāti al-ḥubuk* — "By the heaven of woven tracts." **(Ḥ-B-K corpus-hapax root.)**
- v. 8: *innakum la-fī qawlin mukhtalif* — "You are in inconsistent speech."
- v. 9: *yu'fak ʿanhu man ufika* — "Whoever is turned-aside is turned-aside from him."
- v. 10: *qutila al-kharrāṣūn* — "May the speculators be killed (cursed)."
- v. 11: *alladhīna hum fī ghamratin sāhūn* — "Those who are in confusion, neglectful."
- v. 12: *yas'alūna ayyāna yawmu al-dīn* — "They ask: when is the day of requital?"
- v. 13: *yawma hum ʿalā al-nāri yuftanūn* — "The day they will be tortured by the fire."
- v. 14: *dhūqū fitnatakum hādhā alladhī kuntum bihi tastaʿjilūn* — "Taste your torture: this is what you used to ask to be hastened."

The 8-verse stretch presents the skeptics' confusion + the eschatological-reality answer. The pattern is **dialogic** (skeptics ask → the divine voice answers).

#### Sub-block A4 — The taqwā-vignette (vv. 15-19)
- v. 15: *inna al-muttaqīna fī jannātin wa-ʿuyūn* — "The God-fearing are in gardens and springs."
- v. 16: *ākhidhīna mā ātāhum rabbuhum innahum kānū qabla dhālika muḥsinīn* — "Taking what their Lord has given them; they were before this doers-of-good."
- v. 17: *kānū qalīlan min al-layli mā yahjaʿūn* — "They used to sleep little of the night." **(H-J-Ḍ corpus-hapax root.)**
- v. 18: *wa-bi-l-asḥāri hum yastaghfirūn* — "And in the pre-dawn they would seek forgiveness."
- v. 19: *wa-fī amwālihim ḥaqqun li-l-sā'ili wa-l-maḥrūm* — "And in their wealth was a right for the asker and the deprived."

This 5-verse description of the *muttaqūn* — those who fear God — establishes the pious-vigil + charity doctrine. v. 17-18 ground the night-prayer practice; v. 19 grounds the right-of-the-deprived (a Meccan precursor of Medinan-zakāt). See `04-hadith-corpus.md` for Abū Dāwūd ch5 #73 grounding the v. 17 night-prayer practice.

#### Sub-block A5 — Signs in the earth and in yourselves (vv. 20-23)
- v. 20: *wa-fī al-arḍi āyātun li-l-mūqinīn* — "And in the earth are signs for the certain."
- v. 21: *wa-fī anfusikum afalā tubṣirūn* — "And in yourselves — will you not see?"
- v. 22: *wa-fī al-samāʾi rizqukum wa-mā tūʿadūn* — "And in the heaven is your provision and what you are promised."
- v. 23: *fa-wa-rabbi al-samāʾi wa-l-arḍi innahu la-ḥaqqun mithlu mā annakum tanṭiqūn* — "By the Lord of heaven and earth, it is as true as that you speak."

The "signs in the earth + signs in yourselves + provision in the heaven" triad is a classical proof-from-creation argument; v. 21 *wa-fī anfusikum afalā tubṣirūn* ("and in yourselves — will you not see?") is one of the corpus's most-cited verses on the inner-anfus argument. al-Rāzī devotes 4 pages to v. 21 alone (*Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* vol. 28, pp. 213-217).

### Block B (vv. 24-37): IBRĀHĪM-HOSPITALITY + LŪṬ-DESTRUCTION

#### Sub-block B1 — The angelic guests (vv. 24-30)
- v. 24: *hal atāka ḥadīthu ḍayfi ibrāhīma al-mukramīn* — "Has the report of the honored guests of Abraham reached you?"
- v. 25: *idh dakhalū ʿalayhi fa-qālū salāman qāla salāmun qawmun munkarūn* — "When they entered upon him and said 'salām,' he said 'salām' (and to himself): a strange people."
- v. 26: *fa-rāgha ilā ahlihi fa-jāʾa bi-ʿijlin samīn* — "He went secretly to his family and brought a fattened calf."
- v. 27: *fa-qarrabahu ilayhim qāla alā taʾkulūn* — "He brought it near them: 'will you not eat?'"
- v. 28: *fa-awjasa minhum khīfatan qālū lā takhaf wa-bashsharūhu bi-ghulāmin ʿalīm* — "He sensed fear from them; they said: 'do not fear,' and announced to him a knowing son."
- v. 29: *fa-aqbalat imraʾatuhu fī ṣarratin fa-ṣakkat wajhahā wa-qālat ʿajūzun ʿaqīm* — "His wife came in a clamor, struck her face, and said: a barren old woman!" **(Ṣ-K-K corpus-hapax root.)**
- v. 30: *qālū kadhāliki qāla rabbuki innahu huwa al-ḥakīmu al-ʿalīm* — "They said: 'thus your Lord said; He is the wise, the knowing.'"

The 7-verse hospitality-narrative is a **compressed dramatic vignette**: opening mystery ("a strange people"), generosity (the calf), recognition-failure ("will you not eat?"), divine-disclosure ("do not fear"), the Sarah-strikes-her-face cameo (v. 29 — corpus-unique vivid action), the divine-name reaffirmation. Q 51:24-30 is the classical *bayān al-iʿjāz* exemplar of compressed-narrative drama.

#### Sub-block B2 — The mission to Lūṭ-people (vv. 31-37)
- v. 31: *qāla fa-mā khaṭbukum ayyuhā al-mursalūn* — "He (Ibrāhīm) said: 'what is your mission, O messengers?'"
- v. 32: *qālū innā ursilnā ilā qawmin mujrimīn* — "They said: 'we have been sent to a criminal people.'"
- v. 33: *li-nursila ʿalayhim ḥijāratan min ṭīn* — "To send upon them stones of clay."
- v. 34: *musawwamatan ʿinda rabbika li-l-musrifīn* — "Marked, with your Lord, for the transgressors."
- v. 35: *fa-akhrajnā man kāna fīhā min al-muʾminīn* — "We extracted those who were believers."
- v. 36: *fa-mā wajadnā fīhā ghayra baytin min al-muslimīn* — "We did not find there but a single house of Muslims."
- v. 37: *wa-taraknā fīhā āyatan li-lladhīna yakhāfūn al-ʿadhāba al-alīm* — "And We left there a sign for those who fear the painful punishment."

The 7-verse Lūṭ-destruction sub-block matches the 7-verse hospitality sub-block — a **balanced 7+7 = 14-verse structural pair** (B1 + B2). v. 36 *baytin min al-muslimīn* ("a single house of Muslims") is the corpus's only such phrase about Lūṭ-people; classical exegesis (al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī) reads this as the proto-Islamic/proto-monotheist single-household identification.

### Block C (vv. 38-46): 4-PEOPLE PUNISHMENT-COMPENDIUM

The "compressed-prophet-cycle" — 4 prophet/people in 9 verses, each opened by *wa-fī [SUBJECT]* ("and in the affair of") with the fourth opened by *wa-qawmu nūḥin min qabl* ("and the people of Noah from before"):

#### Mūsā-Pharaoh (vv. 38-40, 3 verses)
- v. 38: *wa-fī mūsā idh arsalnāhu ilā firʿawna bi-sulṭānin mubīn* — "And in (the affair of) Mūsā, when We sent him to Pharaoh with clear authority."
- v. 39: *fa-tawallā bi-ruknihi wa-qāla sāḥirun aw majnūn* — "He turned away with his support and said: 'a magician or madman.'"
- v. 40: *fa-akhadhnāhu wa-junūdahu fa-nabadhnāhum fī al-yammi wa-huwa mulīm* — "We seized him and his troops and cast them into the sea, and he was blameworthy."

#### ʿĀd (vv. 41-42, 2 verses)
- v. 41: *wa-fī ʿādin idh arsalnā ʿalayhim al-rīḥa al-ʿaqīm* — "And in (the affair of) ʿĀd, when We sent upon them the barren wind."
- v. 42: *mā tadharu min shayʾin atat ʿalayhi illā jaʿalathu ka-l-ramīm* — "Sparing nothing it came upon, it made it like ashes."

#### Thamūd (vv. 43-45, 3 verses)
- v. 43: *wa-fī thamūda idh qīla lahum tamattaʿū ḥattā ḥīn* — "And in (the affair of) Thamūd, when it was said to them: 'enjoy until a time.'"
- v. 44: *fa-ʿataw ʿan amri rabbihim fa-akhadhathum al-ṣāʿiqatu wa-hum yanẓurūn* — "They defied their Lord's command, and the thunderbolt seized them while they were watching."
- v. 45: *fa-mā istaṭāʿū min qiyāmin wa-mā kānū muntaṣirīn* — "They could not stand and were not victorious."

#### Nūḥ (v. 46, 1 verse)
- v. 46: *wa-qawmu nūḥin min qablu innahum kānū qawman fāsiqīn* — "And the people of Noah from before — they were a depraved people."

The **chronologically-retrograde** sequence (Mūsā most-recent → ʿĀd → Thamūd → Nūḥ earliest) is rhetorically pointed: history is presented as a perennial pattern of arrogance + destruction, traced backward from the most-familiar (Pharaoh) to the most-archaic (Noah). al-Rāzī (*Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* vol. 28, pp. 220-221) catalogs this *taʿqīb* and notes it is one of three corpus-instances of retrograde-prophet-history (alongside Q 14:9 and Q 50:12-14).

The compendium's **unbalanced length distribution** (3+2+3+1) is striking. It contrasts with Q 7's 5-prophet-equal-length structure (~25 verses each, p=10⁻⁵ on equal-length under H-NEW-90 partial pass). Q 51's compendium is **explicitly compressed**, not parallelism-aspiring. See `06-novel-findings.md` Q051-F-03 for direct empirical test.

### Block D (vv. 47-51): COSMOLOGY + "FLEE TO ALLAH"

- v. 47: *wa-l-samāʾa banaynāhā bi-aydin wa-innā la-mūsiʿūn* — "And the heaven — We built it with strength, and We are the expanders." (One of the corpus's two *al-mūsiʿūn* verses; classical iʿjāz-ʿilmī claim about cosmic expansion REFUTED on philological grounds — *mūsiʿ* = "vast/expanding" in classical Arabic, not the modern cosmological sense.)
- v. 48: *wa-l-arḍa farashnāhā fa-niʿma al-māhidūn* — "And the earth — We spread it; what excellent levelers!"
- v. 49: *wa-min kulli shayʾin khalaqnā zawjayni laʿallakum tadhakkarūn* — "And from everything We created pairs, that you may remember." (The corpus's most direct statement of the *zawjayn* (paired/dual) creation-doctrine.)
- v. 50: *fa-firrū ilā allāhi innī lakum minhu nadhīrun mubīn* — "So flee to Allah; I am to you a clear warner from Him."
- v. 51: *wa-lā tajʿalū maʿa allāhi ilāhan ākhar innī lakum minhu nadhīrun mubīn* — "And do not make with Allah another god; I am to you a clear warner from Him."

The 5-verse cosmology + flee-to-Allah block presents a **cosmic-creation argument leading to a tawḥīd-imperative**. Verses 50 + 51 contain a **near-exact refrain**: both end with *innī lakum minhu nadhīrun mubīn* ("I am to you a clear warner from Him"). This is the surah's only refrain-pair.

Note v. 47 *innā la-mūsiʿūn* — corpus tradition has historically read this as the divine "we expand the heavens" (echoing classical cosmology). MODERN claims that this verse predicts cosmic expansion are PHILOLOGICALLY UNTENABLE — see `05-classical-claims-audit.md`.

### Block E (vv. 52-60): PROPHETIC RESISTANCE + CREATION-PURPOSE + DOXOLOGY

- v. 52: *kadhālika mā atā alladhīna min qablihim min rasūlin illā qālū sāḥirun aw majnūn* — "Thus, no messenger came to those before them but they said: 'a magician or madman.'"
- v. 53: *atawāṣaw bihi bal hum qawmun ṭāghūn* — "Did they (people) recommend it (the rejection) to one another? Rather, they are a transgressor people."
- v. 54: *fa-tawalla ʿanhum fa-mā anta bi-malūm* — "So turn away from them; you will not be blamed."
- v. 55: *wa-dhakkir fa-inna al-dhikrā tanfaʿu al-muʾminīn* — "And remind, for the reminder benefits the believers."
- v. 56: ⭐ *wa-mā khalaqtu al-jinn wa-l-ins illā li-yaʿbudūn* — "And I did not create the Jinn and humankind except to worship Me."
- v. 57: *mā urīdu minhum min rizqin wa-mā urīdu an yuṭʿimūn* — "I do not seek from them any provision, nor do I seek that they feed Me."
- v. 58: ⭐ *inna allāha huwa al-razzāqu dhū al-quwwati al-matīn* — "Indeed Allah is the Provider, the Possessor of Power, the Firm."
- v. 59: *fa-inna li-lladhīna ẓalamū dhanūban mithla dhanūbi aṣḥābihim fa-lā yastaʿjilūn* — "Those who do wrong have a portion like the portion of their companions; let them not seek to hasten."
- v. 60: *fa-waylu li-lladhīna kafarū min yawmihim alladhī yūʿadūn* — "Woe to those who disbelieve from their day they are promised."

The 9-verse closing block has the surah's two **theological centerpieces**:
- **v. 56**: the corpus-EXACT creation-purpose verse (the 1-of-1 *(ma + khlq + illā + ʿbd)* exclusivity construct; see `06-novel-findings.md` Q051-F-02).
- **v. 58**: three divine-name-attributes (*al-razzāq, dhū al-quwwa, al-matīn*) in this exact configuration only at Q 51:58.

The closing v. 60 is one of the corpus's most-direct eschatological-warning closes; it loops back to v. 6 (*wa-inna al-dīna la-wāqiʿ*), framing the surah as a **bracketed eschatological apparatus**:
- v. 5-6: oath-completion: "the requital is taking place"
- v. 60: closing wail: "woe to those who disbelieve from their day they are promised"

The bracketing is rhetorical-thematic; the surah's middle 53 verses unfold the substantive content within the eschatological frame.

## 4. Content register

- **Creedal**: vv. 4-6, v. 51 (*wa-lā tajʿalū maʿa allāhi ilāhan ākhar*), v. 56 (creation-purpose).
- **Eschatological**: vv. 12-14, vv. 59-60.
- **Narrative**: vv. 24-46 (the Ibrāhīm-Lūṭ + 4-people block).
- **Wisdom/exhortation**: vv. 50, 54-55.
- **Cosmological**: vv. 7, 20-23, 47-49.
- **Theological-attribute**: v. 58 (*al-razzāq, dhū al-quwwa, al-matīn*).
- **Doxological**: implicit in v. 23 (*fa-wa-rabbi al-samāʾi*), v. 30 (*al-ḥakīm al-ʿalīm*).

The mixed register matches Q 51's mid-Meccan classification: theological-narrative-rhetorical fluidity, with NO sharp Medinan legal-vs-spiritual separation. The unique feature is the **balance** between cosmological-creedal (D + parts of A) and narrative (B + C) — Q 51 is more *balanced* across registers than its oath-cluster siblings (Q 37 is heavily narrative; Q 53 is heavily prophetic-vision; Q 52 is heavily eschatological).

### Distinctive phrase fingerprints

- *fa-firrū ilā allāhi* (v. 50) — corpus-unique imperative: "flee to Allah" (no other verse contains *firrū* with *ilā allāh* as escape-direction).
- *innī lakum minhu nadhīrun mubīn* (vv. 50, 51) — repeated 2× in Q 51; appears once in Q 71:2 (Sūrat Nūḥ); 3 corpus instances total, 2 in Q 51.
- *al-ʿaqīm* (vv. 29 *ʿajūz ʿaqīm*, 41 *al-rīḥ al-ʿaqīm*) — 2 instances in Q 51, the same root meaning "barren"; corpus has 4 attestations total. Q 51 has the densest *ʿaqīm* concentration.
- *al-mūsiʿūn* (v. 47) — corpus-rare verbal-noun on "to expand/be vast"; 3 corpus instances of root *w-s-ʿ* in this morphological class.
- *al-razzāq* (v. 58) — **corpus-unique** as a divine-name attribute (the indefinite *razzāq* occurs at Q 22:58 with *al-rāziqīn*). Q 51:58 has the only *al-razzāq* with *al-* definite article.
- *dhū al-quwwati al-matīn* (v. 58) — corpus-unique configuration of these two divine-attributes together.

### Phrase-level cross-surah connections

- v. 39 *sāḥirun aw majnūn* ("magician or madman") — repeated at v. 52 (*illā qālū sāḥirun aw majnūn*); both occurrences in Q 51 itself, framing the Pharaonic + the universal-prophetic-rejection. Q 26 has the *sāḥirun ʿalīm* construction (about Mūsā), and Q 52:29 has *bi-kāhinin wa-lā majnūn* (refuting Muḥammad-as-soothsayer-or-madman).
- v. 37 *taraknā fīhā āyatan li-lladhīna yakhāfūna al-ʿadhāba al-alīm* — paralleled at Q 25:37, Q 29:35.
- v. 49 *zawjayn* ("pairs in creation") — paralleled at Q 36:36, Q 53:45, Q 75:39, Q 78:8 (corpus's principal pair-creation-doctrine verses).

## 6. Repetition patterns (refrains)

| Refrain | Occurrences | Vv. | Purpose |
|:--|:-:|:--|:--|
| *innī lakum minhu nadhīrun mubīn* | 2 | 50, 51 | the cosmic→tawḥīd transition; corpus-rare |
| *wa-fī [SUBJECT] idh* | 3 | 38 (Mūsā), 41 (ʿĀd), 43 (Thamūd) | the compressed prophet-cycle marker |
| *sāḥirun aw majnūn* | 2 | 39, 52 | universal prophetic-rejection refrain |
| *yūʿadūn / mā tūʿadūn / tūʿadūna* | 3 | 5, 22, 60 | the eschatological-promise frame |

The **3-fold *wa-fī [SUBJECT] idh* opener** in the compressed prophet-cycle (vv. 38, 41, 43) is the surah's most-distinctive structural-anaphora — it organizes the 4-people compendium as a **catalogic** rather than narrative-extended unit. (The 4th, Nūḥ at v. 46, breaks the pattern with *wa-qawmu nūḥin min qabl* — "and the people of Noah from BEFORE [these]" — explicitly chronologically-retrograde.)

- Q 51:1-4 4-element fa-coordinated oath — paralleled at Q 37:1-3 (3-element), Q 77:1-5 (5-element), Q 100:1-5 (5-element). All four belong to the strict-15 oath-opener cluster (H-NEW-1070).

- Q 51:19 right-of-the-deprived — paralleled at Q 70:24-25 (the *li-l-sāʾili wa-l-maḥrūm* fixed-share); Medinan zakāt-foreshadowing.
- Q 51:21 *wa-fī anfusikum afalā tubṣirūn* — one of the corpus's most-cited inner-anfus verses; al-Rāzī devotes 4 pages to v. 21.

- Q 51:38-40 Mūsā vs Pharaoh — paralleled across Q 7, 20, 26, 28; Q 51's 3-verse compression is the most condensed.
- Q 51:41-42 ʿĀd + barren wind — paralleled at Q 41:16, Q 46:24-25, Q 54:18-21, Q 69:6-7.

- Q 51:49 *zawjayn* (created in pairs) — paralleled at Q 36:36, Q 53:45, Q 75:39, Q 78:8.
- Q 51:50 *fa-firrū ilā allāhi* — corpus-unique imperative.
- Q 51:56 creation-purpose — corpus-EXACT under strict (ma + khlq + illā + ʿbd) construct (see `06-novel-findings.md` Q051-F-02).
- Q 51:58 *al-razzāq dhū al-quwwa al-matīn* — corpus-unique divine-attribute-triad. Tirmidhī ch46 #14 (*idInBook*) preserves the qirāʾāt variant *innī anā al-razzāq*.

## 8. The chronological-retrograde sequencing of vv. 38-46

A distinctive feature: the 4 prophet/people are sequenced in **REVERSE chronological order**:
- v. 38-40: Mūsā (most recent in classical chronology of the catalog — Mūsā post-dates ʿĀd/Thamūd/Nūḥ in classical Israelite-history reckoning)
- v. 41-42: ʿĀd (pre-Mūsā, post-Nūḥ)
- v. 43-45: Thamūd (also pre-Mūsā)
- v. 46: Nūḥ (the earliest)

This is **chronologically REVERSE** of the more-common forward-historical sequence (Nūḥ → ʿĀd/Hūd → Thamūd/Ṣāliḥ → Mūsā) found in Q 7, Q 11, Q 26. al-Rāzī (*Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* vol. 28 pp. 220-221) catalogs this *taʿqīb* and reads it as a **rhetorical move from familiar-recent to archaic-deepest**: the surah escalates its destruction-rhetoric by deepening into the past, as if to say: "this pattern is older than you imagined." al-Biqāʿī (*Naẓm al-Durar*) reads it as a *manner-of-recall* (the recipient's mental order), not a chronological-fact ordering.

The **statistical comparison to Q 7**'s prophet-cycle (Q 7 N=5, equal-length pericopes, **forward**-chronological under H-NEW-90 partial pass at z=+5.25) is empirically tested in `06-novel-findings.md` Q051-F-03. The unbalanced + retrograde structure makes Q 51 the **inverse** of Q 7's parallelism architecture.

## 9. The bracketing eschatological frame

Q 51 has a notable rhetorical frame:

**OPENING**:
- v. 5-6: *innamā tūʿadūna la-ṣādiq / wa-inna al-dīna la-wāqiʿ* — "What you are promised is true; the requital is taking place."

**CLOSING**:
- v. 60: *fa-waylun li-lladhīna kafarū min yawmihim alladhī yūʿadūn* — "Woe to those who disbelieve from their day they are promised."

The **opening promise** + **closing wail** form a *qasr* (bracket) around the surah's substantive content (vv. 7-59). al-Bāqillānī (*Iʿjāz al-Qurʾān*) cites this kind of opening-closing frame as one of the corpus's classical *iḥkām* (stylistic-structuring) devices.

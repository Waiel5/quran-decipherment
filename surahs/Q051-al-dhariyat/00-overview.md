## 2. Classical name

The surah is named **al-Dhāriyāt** after the opening word of v.1 *wa-l-dhāriyāti dharwā* ("by the scattering [winds] in their scattering"). The name fixes the surah by its first oath-element only; the surah is in fact a **4-element oath cluster** of which *al-dhāriyāt* is only the first. Classical disagreement on the referent (al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, al-Bāqillānī, al-Zamakhsharī) — see §3 below.

## 3. Opening formula — the 4-element fa-coordinated oath cluster

Q 51:1-4 (no-tashkeel):

| v | Text | Gloss |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 1 | والذاريات ذروا | *By those scattering [winds] in their scattering* |
| 2 | فالحاملات وقرا | *And those bearing a heavy load* |
| 3 | فالجاريات يسرا | *And those running with ease* |
| 4 | فالمقسمات أمرا | *And those distributing a command* |

The 4-fold sequence (active-feminine-plural-participle + cognate-or-paronomastic-accusative) opens with *wa-l-* and continues with three *fa-l-* coordinations. The pattern is morphologically nearly-identical to Q 37:1-3 (*wa-l-ṣāffāti / fa-l-zājirāti / fa-l-tāliyāti*), Q 77:1-5 (*wa-l-mursalāti / fa-l-ʿāṣifāti / wa-l-nāshirāti / fa-l-fāriqāti / fa-l-mulqiyāti*), and Q 100:1-5 (*wa-l-ʿādiyāti ḍabḥā / fa-l-mūriyāti qadḥā / fa-l-mughīrāti ṣubḥā / fa-athrna bihi naqʿā / fa-wasaṭna bihi jamʿā*). These four surahs constitute the **fa-coordinated 4+-element oath sub-genre** within the broader 15-surah *wa-l-* oath-opener cluster (per H-NEW-1070 CONFIRMED at p=0.0004). Q 51 is one of two **early-mid mushaf** members (with Q 37); Q 77 and Q 100 are short-Meccan-tail members.

### Classical disagreement on the referent (al-Ṭabarī ad loc.; al-Rāzī *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* vol. 28 pp. 195-202; al-Suyūṭī *al-Itqān* nawʿ 67 on al-aqsām; al-Zamakhsharī *Kashshāf*; al-Qurṭubī *al-Jāmiʿ*):
- **WINDS** (al-Ṭabarī, ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib (per Tafsīr al-Ṭabarī chain), Ibn ʿAbbās majority): the four elements as 4 phenomena of the wind: scattering dust, lifting clouds (heavy load), running gently across the air, distributing rain. The most widely accepted reading.
- **WINDS / CLOUDS / SHIPS / ANGELS** (al-Ṭabarī secondary; al-Rāzī "Vajh I"): four distinct subjects — winds scattering, clouds bearing rain, ships running, angels distributing decrees. Anchored on a chain from ʿAlī.
- **STARS / WINDS / SHIPS / ANGELS** (Ibn Kathīr; al-Rāzī "Vajh II"): four distinct subjects — stars (al-dhāriyāt = the heavenly bodies scattering light), winds, ships, angels.
- **The four are progressive STAGES of one cosmic process** (al-Bāqillānī's *Iʿjāz* reading): wind scatters the dust; clouds bear the moisture; running brings the rain; the angels distribute the consequence-decree. Iʿjāz-balagha integrative reading.
- **The verses' own self-reflexive reading**: the Quran's own verses (al-dhāriyāt = the verses scattering reminders, etc.) — al-Bāqillānī's tertiary reading; minority.

al-Rāzī catalogs **6 distinct vajh** in *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* on Q 51:1-4 and then reconciles them with the v.5-6 oath-completion (*innamā tūʿadūna la-ṣādiq / wa-inna al-dīna la-wāqiʿ* — "what you are promised is true; the requital is taking place"): the 4-element oath establishes cosmic activity that prefigures the inevitability of the divine decree.

Empirical test of trio-and-quartet cohesion at the lexical-token level: Q 51:1-4 share **zero orthographic-token overlap** pairwise — like Q 37:1-3, the four verses are held together by the *active-feminine-plural-participle + cognate-accusative* template parallel, NOT by lexical overlap. (Cf. Q037-F-03 PRE-COMMIT VIOLATION.) The 4-element oath is morphologically tight and lexically-orthogonal.

## 4. Oath-completion at vv. 5-6 — the la-/inna-particle-pair

## 5. ⭐ Unique structural property — the *creation-purpose* hapax-construction at v.56

Q 51:56 is corpus-EXACT under the **strict exclusivity construction** *(ma/wa-mā/fa-mā + khlq + illā + ʿbd)*:

> *وما خلقت الجن والإنس إلا ليعبدون*

Of 7 verses in the corpus that combine *(ma/wa-mā/fa-mā + khlq + illā)* — i.e. the "I/We did not create X except for Y" exclusivity construction — **only Q 51:56** has *ʿbd* (worship-root) in the purpose-clause. The other 6 verses (Q 10:5, Q 15:85, Q 30:8, Q 31:28, Q 44:39, Q 46:3) all use *bi-l-ḥaqq* ("with truth") or *ka-nafs wāḥida* ("as one soul") in the purpose-clause. ## 6. ⭐ Unique narrative — Ibrāhīm's hospitality-of-the-angelic-guests + Lot's destruction (vv. 24-37)

Q 51:24-37 contains the **hospitality narrative**: angels visit Abraham as guests, deliver the annunciation of Isaac, then proceed to destroy Lot's people. The narrative is paralleled at Q 11:69-83 and Q 15:51-77 (different details), Q 29:31-35 (compressed), but Q 51's account is **distinctive** for its compact dramatic structure: (a) hospitality (vv. 24-27: roasted-calf hospitality), (b) the Sarah-laughs-and-strikes-her-face annunciation (vv. 28-30), (c) "what is your business?" angelic-mission disclosure (vv. 31-32), (d) the marked-stones rain on the criminals (vv. 33-34), (e) the believers extracted (v. 35), (f) only one Muslim household found (v. 36), (g) the abandoned sign (v. 37).

The narrative contains **3 corpus-hapax roots** in 60 verses (see §10):
- **Ḥ-B-K** at v.7 (*al-ḥubuk* — "the woven [paths]/tracts" of the heaven). Corpus-unique.
- **H-J-Ḍ** (or *hjE* in QAC) at v.17 (*yahjaʿūn* — "they sleep"). Corpus-unique.
- **Ṣ-K-K** at v.29 (*fa-ṣakkat wajhahā* — "she struck her face"). Corpus-unique to Sarah's annunciation reaction.

This makes Q 51 a **3-hapax-root** mid-Meccan surah of 60 verses. By contrast Q 37 al-Ṣāffāt has 2 corpus-hapax roots in 182 verses. Per-verse hapax density: Q 51 = 0.050; Q 37 = 0.011 — Q 51 is **4.5× MORE hapax-dense than Q 37**.

## 7. ⭐ The 7-prophet/people compressed pericope-cycle (vv. 24-46)

Q 51 contains a compressed **7-prophet-or-people cycle** within a single 23-verse stretch:

| Block | vv. | Length | Subject | Length-share |
|:--|:-:|:-:|:--|:-:|
| 1 | 24-37 | 14 | Ibrāhīm + the angelic guests + Lūṭ-people destruction | 60.9% |

| 3 | 41-42 | 2 | ʿĀd (devastating barren wind) | 8.7% |

| 5 | 46 | 1 | Nūḥ (and earlier-still: the "people of Noah from before") | 4.3% |

The cycle is **highly unbalanced** — Ibrāhīm/Lūṭ takes 14/23 = 61% of the prophet-narrative real-estate; the remaining 4 prophet/people get 9 verses combined. This is the **inverse** structure of Q 37, where the 6 prophets receive 8/31/9/10/6/10 verses respectively (more balanced). This unbalance is itself architecturally meaningful: see `02-content-analysis.md` and `06-novel-findings.md` Q051-F-04.

The pericope-cycle is **chronologically reverse-ordered** (Mūsā → ʿĀd → Thamūd → Nūḥ), tracking destruction-history backwards from Mūsā/Pharaoh (most recent of the catalog) toward Nūḥ (earliest). al-Rāzī notes this *taʿqīb* ("retrograde sequencing") in *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* and reads it as a rhetorical device: the surah moves from mostproximate destruction (Pharaoh's drowning) backward to most-archaic (the Flood), framing destruction as a perennial divine-norm.

## 8. ⭐ The creation-purpose verse v.56 + creator-as-provider verse v.58

Q 51 contains **two of the corpus's most-cited theological-anchor verses**:

- **v.56**: *wa-mā khalaqtu al-jinn wa-l-ins illā li-yaʿbudūn* — "And I did not create the Jinn and humankind except to worship me." The corpus's most direct statement of theological purpose; cited by every classical theologian on the question of human teleology.
- **v.58**: *inna allāha huwa al-razzāq dhū al-quwwati al-matīn* — "Indeed Allah is the Provider, the Possessor of Power, the Firm." The verse's three divine-name-attributes (*al-razzāq*, *dhū al-quwwa*, *al-matīn*) appear in this exact configuration only at Q 51:58. (See `04-hadith-corpus.md` for Tirmidhī ch46 #14 *idInBook*-verified hadith on the qirāʾāt of this verse.)

The juxtaposition v.56 → v.57 → v.58 is theologically pointed: created beings exist to worship → I do not seek their provision → I AM the Provider. The closing chord makes the divine-self-sufficiency the foundation of the worship-purpose declaration.

## 2. Verse-by-verse summary

### Block A (vv. 1-13) — Imtihān prologue + duty-to-parents

- **v1**: الم.
- **v2-3**: THE ANCHOR — *a-ḥasiba al-nāsu an yutrakū an yaqūlū āmannā wa-hum lā yuftanūn? wa-la-qad fatannā al-ladhīna min qablihim* — testing as universal divine method.
- **v4**: rebuke of those who think they can outpace Allah.
- **v5**: those who hope to meet Allah; the appointed term.
- **v6**: *wa-man jāhada fa-innamā yujāhidu li-nafsihi* — STRIVING IS FOR ONESELF (anchor for the inner-jihād reading).
- **v7**: those who believe and work righteousness.
- **v8**: WAṢIYYA TO PARENTS — *wa-waṣṣaynā al-insāna bi-wālidayhi ḥusnā* — but: *wa-in jāhadāka li-tushrika bī mā laysa laka bihi ʿilmun fa-lā tuṭiʿhumā* (do not obey them in shirk).
- **v9-13**: the believers + the wavering hypocrites + the cause-of-others-burdens deception.

### Block B (vv. 14-40) — Prophet narratives

- **vv. 14-15**: NŪḤ — 1000 minus 50 years (= 950 years); the flood; ark made a sign.
- **vv. 16-27**: IBRĀHĪM — to his people; refuses idolatry; people try to burn him; rescued from fire; Lūṭ believed; Isḥāq + Yaʿqūb + nubuwwa-progeny gift.
- **vv. 28-35**: LŪṬ — to his people; men-with-men + cutting roads + assemblies of evil; angels announce destruction; Ibrāhīm's intercession; Lūṭ's wife perishes.
- **vv. 36-37**: SHUʿAYB to MADYAN — earthquake destruction.
- **vv. 38-39**: ʿĀD + THAMŪD + QĀRŪN + FIRʿAWN + HĀMĀN — Mūsā's adversaries; arrogance.
- **v40**: COMPREHENSIVE PUNISHMENT-MODES — wind, scream, earth-swallowing, drowning. *wa-mā kāna Allāhu li-yaẓlimahum* — they wronged themselves.

### Block C (vv. 41-44) — Spider parable

- **v41**: THE EPONYM — *mathalu al-ladhīna ittakhadhū min dūni Allāhi awliyāʾa ka-mathali al-ʿankabūti ittakhadhat baytan; wa-inna awhana al-buyūti la-baytu al-ʿankabūt; law kānū yaʿlamūn*.
- **v42**: Allah knows what they call upon besides Him.
- **v43**: *wa-tilka al-amthālu naḍribuhā li-l-nāsi wa-mā yaʿqiluhā illā al-ʿālimūn* — and only the knowing reason out parables.
- **v44**: Allah created the heavens and earth in truth — sign for believers.

### Block D (vv. 45-49) — Recitation imperative

- **v45**: *utlu mā ūḥiya ilayka mina al-kitābi wa-aqimi al-ṣalāta; inna al-ṣalāta tanhā ʿani al-faḥshāʾi wa-l-munkari* — RECITATION + SALĀH; salāh prevents indecency.
- **v46**: do not debate People of the Book except in the better way; "our God and your God is one" — INTERFAITH ENGAGEMENT VERSE.
- **v47**: the Book sent to the prophet; some People of the Book believe.
- **v48**: *wa-mā kunta tatlū min qablihi min kitābin wa-lā takhuṭṭuhu bi-yamīnika* — THE PROPHET'S ILLITERACY ANCHOR (he did not recite a book or write with his right hand before).
- **v49**: rather it (the Quran) is clear signs in the breasts of those given knowledge.

### Block E (vv. 50-58) — Eschatology + Meccan critique

- **v50**: they say: why no signs sent down? Reply: signs are with Allah; I am only a clear warner.
- **v51**: is it not enough that We sent down on you the Book...
- **v52-53**: testimony before Allah; they ask for hastened punishment.
- **v54-55**: the punishment will encompass disbelievers.
- **v56**: **MIGRATION VERSE** — *yā ʿibādiya al-ladhīna āmanū inna arḍī wāsiʿah fa-iyyāya fa-ʿbudūn* (My earth is wide, so worship Me alone). Anchor for the early-Hijra theology.
- **v57**: every soul tastes death.
- **v58**: those who believe and work righteousness — gardens with rivers.

### Block F (vv. 59-69) — Tawḥīd + concluding striving

- **v59**: those who are patient and trust in their Lord.
- **v60**: many a creature carries not its sustenance — Allah feeds it.
- **vv. 61-65**: the famous "they would say Allah" sequence — when asked who created heavens, sent rain, controls sun-moon, they affirm Allah. Yet inconsistent.
- **v64**: *wa-mā hādhihi al-ḥayātu al-dunyā illā lahwun wa-laʿib; wa-inna al-dāra al-ākhirata la-hiya al-ḥayawān* — this worldly life is play; the Hereafter is the [true] life.
- **v65**: ships in the sea, calling on Allah sincerely; on shore, returning to shirk.
- **v66**: ingratitude; soon they will know.
- **v67**: the sanctuary made safe; people are snatched up around them.
- **v68**: who is more unjust than one who fabricates lies on Allah.
- **v69**: THE CLOSING — *wa-l-ladhīna jāhadū fīnā la-nahdiyannahum subulanā; wa-inna Allāha la-maʿa al-muḥsinīn* — STRIVING + GUIDANCE + ALLAH WITH THE GOOD-DOERS.

## 4. Frame-bracket structure

The surah opens (vv. 2-3) on the testing/imtihān theme and closes (v 69) on the striving/jihād theme — both anchored at root `jhd` (within v 6, 8, 69) and root `ftn` (within vv. 2, 3, 10). 

This is a classical ring-structure observation: **test → strive → guidance**, the canonical believer's trajectory in the Late Meccan rhetorical register.

## 5. Content register

Late Meccan **mixed register**:
- Theological-doctrinal (vv. 1-13): testing.
- Narrative-prophetic (vv. 14-40): 7-prophet succession.
- Parabolic (vv. 41-44): spider; only the knowing reason.
- Practice-imperative (vv. 45-49): salāh; recitation; interfaith etiquette.
- Eschatological (vv. 50-58): the Hour; migration verse.
- Tawḥīd polemic (vv. 59-68): inconsistency-of-shirk; Allah's earth-wide.
- Concluding imperative (v 69): striving + guidance.

## 6. Cross-surah content references

- The 7-prophet narrative cluster (vv. 14-40) is paralleled with extensions in Q 7, Q 11, Q 26, Q 37, Q 54 — Late Meccan prophet-cycle is a corpus-recurring template.
- *Eankabuwt* (spider) lemma: corpus-hapax, only Q 29:41 (both tokens).
- *>awohan*: strict corpus-hapax, only Q 29:41:13.
- The migration verse Q 29:56 (*inna arḍī wāsiʿah*) is the asbāb al-nuzūl anchor for the Hijra to Abyssinia + Medina (per al-Wāḥidī tradition on Q 29:1-3).
- Q 29:48 (the prophet's illiteracy) is paralleled in Q 7:157 (*al-rasūla al-nabiyya al-ummiyy*), Q 7:158, Q 62:2.
- Q 29:69 (*jāhadū fīnā*) is paralleled in Q 5:35, Q 8:74, Q 9:20.

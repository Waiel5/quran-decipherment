This complements Tirmidhī #2468 by providing a specific historical-occasion anchor for the imtihān verses.

## 2. Q 29:1-3 imtihān ḥadīth-cluster (Tirmidhī #2468 and parallels)

The "*ashaddu al-nāsi balāʾan al-anbiyāʾ*" tradition (the people most severely tested are the prophets, then the next-best, etc.) is one of the most widely-cited Quran-anchored ḥadīth in the corpus. Parallel narrations exist in:
- Aḥmad b. Ḥanbal, *Musnad* (multiple chains, including via Saʿd b. Mālik / Saʿd b. Abī Waqqāṣ).
- Ibn Mājah, Sunan #4023 (per standard editions).
- Tirmidhī #2468 (cited above) — the canonical anchor in our corpus.

**Grading**: standard Tirmidhī assessment is *ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ* (`qāla Abū ʿĪsā: hādhā ḥadīth ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ`); the precise grading text was not in the spa5k extract for this entry. **MW-6 PENDING physical-edition verification**.

## 3. Q 29:8 — Saʿd b. Abī Waqqāṣ tradition (Tirmidhī #3273)

The "four āyāt revealed about me" tradition by Saʿd is the canonical asbāb for **Q 29:8 + Q 5:90 + Q 8:1 + Q 4:11** (different traditions specify the exact 4 verses). The Q 29:8 component is on Saʿd's mother's hunger-strike to force apostasy. Standard Tirmidhī grading: *ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ*.

This tradition is also in:
- Muslim, *Ṣaḥīḥ* Kitāb Faḍāʾil al-Ṣaḥābah (multiple chains via Muṣʿab b. Saʿd).
- al-Bukhārī, *Ṣaḥīḥ* Kitāb al-Adab al-Mufrad (parallel narration).

The corpus-anchor for filial-obedience-vs-tawḥīd doctrine is unanimous.

## 4. Q 29:41 spider parable

No DIRECT ḥadīth on Q 29:41 spider-parable in the keyword-search hits of Bukhārī / Muslim / Tirmidhī within our corpus. The parable is treated extensively in tafsīr literature (see `03-tafsir-survey.md` §2) but does not appear to have a single anchor-ḥadīth in the Sunni nine-books in our index. 

**Indirect linkage**: the Cave of Thawr tradition (the Hijra narrative, Bukhārī Kitāb Faḍāʾil al-Ṣaḥābah) — where a spider's web is said to have miraculously protected the Prophet ﷺ + Abū Bakr in the cave from Quraysh pursuers — has been linked to Q 29:41 in popular preaching. However, the Cave of Thawr web-tradition is NOT in the canonical Bukhārī ḥadīth catalogues we searched in this corpus; it is in later faḍāʾil and sīra works (Ibn Hishām, etc.). **MW-6 tag**: NOT-VERIFIED-as-canonical-ḥadīth in our corpus.

## 5. Q 29:48 (prophet's illiteracy) — paralleled across multiple ḥadīth

The Prophet's *ummī* status is referenced in numerous ḥadīth (e.g., the moon-fasting hadith Bukhārī #1913: "*innā ummatun ummiyyatun lā naktubu wa-lā naḥsubu*"). These ḥadīth do not cite Q 29:48 by chapter-and-verse but are doctrinally aligned. The direct verse-anchor is Q 7:157-158 (*al-nabī al-ummī*); Q 29:48 is the "before-the-Quran" form (he didn't recite or write before).

## 6. Q 29:69 (jāhadū fīnā) — striving doctrine

The classical "jihād al-nafs" tradition (the inner-jihad as "jihād al-akbar") is reported in some chains as a direct gloss on Q 29:69, but the chains for "*rajaʿnā mina al-jihādi al-aṣghar ilā al-jihādi al-akbar*" are widely considered weak (al-Bayhaqī flagged it; Ibn Taymiyya rejected the chain). 

**MW-6 tag**: the inner-vs-outer jihād framing is doctrinally CLASSICAL but the specific ḥadīth-anchor is contested. We document the doctrine without ḥadīth-attribution.

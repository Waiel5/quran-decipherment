# Q 78 al-Nabaʾ — Classical Tafsīr Survey

This file canvasses 8 classical authorities on Q 78. Citations follow MW-6 verification status: VERIFIED entries indicate physical-edition cross-check; PENDING entries are documented but await hard-copy lookup; SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED entries are confirmed by ≥2 independent secondary sources.

## 1. al-Ṭabarī — *Jāmiʿ al-Bayān* on Q 78 [SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED]

al-Ṭabarī's *Jāmiʿ al-bayān ʿan taʾwīl āy al-Qurʾān* — the foundational compilatory tafsir (d. 310 / 923).

### Verse 1-2 — *al-nabaʾ al-ʿaẓīm*

al-Ṭabarī catalogues opinions on the referent:
- **The Resurrection** (most chains): from Ibn ʿAbbās via ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭalḥa; from Mujāhid; from Qatāda; from al-Ḍaḥḥāk. The dominant view.
- **The Quran**: from Ibn ʿAbbās via a separate chain (less-frequently cited).
- **Muḥammad's prophethood**: anonymous chain, treated as marginal.

al-Ṭabarī's adjudication: he favors the Resurrection-reading because of (a) chain-density (most numerous chains), (b) thematic-coherence with the surah's vv.17-40 elaboration, and (c) Quranic parallel at Q 38:67 (*qul huwa nabaʾun ʿaẓīm*) where the *nabaʾ ʿaẓīm* refers to the eschatological-warning-message. al-Ṭabarī's pattern is to allow for multi-readings as legitimately within the *zāhir* (apparent meaning) but to flag the dominant chain.

### Verse 7 — *wa-l-jibāla awtādan*

al-Ṭabarī cites Mujāhid + Saʿīd b. Jubayr + Qatāda: the mountains as *anchors* preventing earth from shaking. al-Ṭabarī also reports the Hashshim b. Bishr → ʿIkrima chain on the cosmic-engineering of mountain-roots.

### Verse 9 — *subātan*

al-Ṭabarī cites Mujāhid: *al-subāt* = *al-rāḥa* (rest). al-Suddī: cessation. The connection to the Resurrection: sleep is small-death; resurrection is great-waking.

### Verse 13 — *sirājan wahhājan*

al-Ṭabarī ad loc.: Ibn ʿAbbās via two chains identifies *sirāj* with *al-shams* (the sun); *wahhāj* = *muḍīʾ* (radiating). al-Ṭabarī notes the rarity of the wahhāj-form and treats it as an iʿjāzī lexical-choice.

### Verse 33 — *kawāʿiba atrāban*

al-Ṭabarī catalogues:
- **Young women/maidens** (Ibn ʿAbbās dominant view, via ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭalḥa; Mujāhid; al-Ḍaḥḥāk; al-Suddī): the paradise-companions (ḥūr ʿīn) at maturity-of-age.
- **Adolescent (sense of "young grown")**: Ibn ʿAbbās via a different chain.
- **Grape-clusters** (al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī minority): the kawāʿib are the swellings of fruit, continuing v.32's grapes. al-Ṭabarī flags this as MINORITY but notes it is structurally-coherent.

al-Ṭabarī's adjudication: the young-women reading dominates because of chain-density and because *kawāʿib* in pre-Islamic poetry refers to maidens. The grape-reading is documented but not preferred.

### Verse 38 — *yawma yaqūmu al-rūḥu wa-l-malāʾikatu ṣaffan*

al-Ṭabarī cites:
- **al-Rūḥ = Jibrīl**: dominant chain (Ibn ʿAbbās; al-Ḍaḥḥāk; Qatāda).
- **al-Rūḥ = a class of angels**: Ibn ʿAbbās variant chain — a class higher than the standard angels.
- **al-Rūḥ = a single angelic-being distinct from the standard angels**: anonymous chain.
- **al-Rūḥ = the host of beings created by God**: marginal (Ibn Wahb chain via 'Aṭāʾ b. Yasār).

### Verse 40 — *yā laytanī kuntu turāban*

al-Ṭabarī cites a tradition (from Abū Hurayra, with reservations on the chain) that the animals will be turned to dust at the Judgment to be spared the standard-human-judgment, and the disbelievers will wish to share that animal-fate. This tradition has uncertain isnād-status; al-Ṭabarī cites it as a possibility, not as authoritative.

al-Ṭabarī's preferred reading: the disbeliever wishes for non-existence (or pre-existential-non-being).

## 2. al-Zamakhsharī — *al-Kashshāf* on Q 78 [SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED]

al-Zamakhsharī (d. 538 / 1144) — the rationalist Muʿtazilite tafsir; most cited for balāgha and grammar.

### Verse 1-3 — interrogative-rebuke

al-Zamakhsharī highlights the *ʿamma* contraction as a rhetorical-compressor: the question "about WHAT?" is answered immediately by "the Great Announcement," which is then characterized as that-over-which-they-disagree. The 3-step compressed-question-answer-frame is one of the corpus's tightest rhetorical-structures (al-Zamakhsharī classifies it under *al-istifhām al-taqrīrī* with a *fawātih taʿjīb* aspect — wonder-prefacing rhetorical questions).

al-Zamakhsharī's reading of *al-nabaʾ al-ʿaẓīm*: he favors the **Resurrection** reading on the same grounds as al-Ṭabarī, but adds a balāgha-argument: the *al-* in *al-nabaʾ al-ʿaẓīm* is the definite-article-of-the-known (the disputers know what the announcement IS, but they dispute whether it WILL happen).

### Verses 6-16 — the cosmology block

al-Zamakhsharī treats Block 2 as a *dalīl* (proof) for Block 3's eschatological claim. The cosmic-evidence is structured as a 7-fold witness (some count it as 6 or 8 depending on verse-segmentation):
1. Earth-as-cradle (v.6)
2. Mountains-as-pegs (v.7)
3. Pairing (v.8)
4. Sleep-as-rest (v.9)
5. Night-as-cover, Day-as-livelihood (v.10-11)
6. Seven sturdy heavens (v.12)
7. Sun-as-blazing-lamp (v.13)
8. Rain-clouds and produce (vv.14-16)

al-Zamakhsharī's argument: the *creation-power* witnessed in these signs is the SAME power capable of resurrection. This is the corpus's classic *qiyās al-aṣl ʿalā al-far' al-aqwā* (analogy from the original to the more-difficult-thing) — but here the original (creation) and the far' (re-creation/resurrection) are EQUIVALENT in difficulty for divine power.

### Verse 33 — *kawāʿiba atrāban*

al-Zamakhsharī confirms the young-maiden reading; cites the morphological pattern *fawāʿil* as a feminine-plural for women. He does not entertain the grape-reading (or considers it marginal).

## 3. al-Rāzī — *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* on Q 78 [PENDING — SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED]

Fakhr al-Dīn al-Rāzī (d. 606 / 1209) — the encyclopedic theological-philosophical tafsir; *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb*, vol. 30 (the juzʾ-30 commentary), pp. 1-25 in the Dār al-Fikr edition.

### v.2 — al-nabaʾ al-ʿaẓīm

al-Rāzī's hallmark: enumerating possibilities. He catalogues:
1. **The Day of Resurrection** (majority Resurrection-reading).
2. **The Quran** (sub-reading).
3. **The prophethood / mission of Muḥammad** (sub-reading).
4. **The Tawḥīd / monotheistic content** (sub-reading; al-nabaʾ = the announcement that there is one God).
5. **The Hereafter as a totality** (a slight variant of #1).

al-Rāzī's adjudication: he allows that all five are "permissible" but the Resurrection is the *zāhir / dominant* reading because the surah's body (vv.17-40) is overwhelmingly Resurrection-content. He observes that the multi-referential reading is NOT exclusive: the *nabaʾ al-ʿaẓīm* may simultaneously refer to the Quran-as-revealing-the-Resurrection-claim, with the Resurrection as the announced content.

### v.7 — wa-l-jibāla awtādan

al-Rāzī's cosmological-engineering reading: the mountains are *anchors* in the sense that their *roots* extend deeper than their visible parts. He cites a tradition (with anchoring reservations on chain): "the roots of mountains are like the limbs of trees." This is a classical-cosmological-philosophical claim about earth-structure.

### v.13 — sirājan wahhājan

al-Rāzī classifies the wahhāj-pattern as *faʿʿāl* with intensive-meaning. He notes the corpus-rarity and reads it as evidence of the Quran's lexical-precision: where standard *muḍīʾ* (radiating) would suffice, the lexical-choice *wahhāj* foregrounds the heat-and-light-fusion that defines a sun-as-lamp.

### v.20 — wa-suyyirati al-jibālu fa-kānat sarāban

al-Rāzī notes the lexical reversal between v.7 (mountains-as-pegs) and v.20 (mountains-as-mirage). He treats this as a deliberate iʿjāzī-balaghi feature: the same vocabulary anchored in opposite states. al-Rāzī is one of the few classical commentators who explicitly identifies this intra-surah ring-feature.

### v.33 — kawāʿiba atrāban

al-Rāzī catalogues:
- young-women reading (majority)
- grape-cluster reading (al-Ḥasan + minority chain)
- swelling-of-breast as a generic-young-stage-of-development (a more abstract reading)

He prefers the young-maiden reading on chain-density grounds.

### v.38 — al-rūḥ wa-al-malāʾika ṣaffan

al-Rāzī's adjudication on al-rūḥ:
- **Jibrīl**: majority (cites al-Ṭabarī).
- **A higher class of angels**: a sub-reading.
- **A specific cosmic-being distinct from angels**: a more philosophical reading.

al-Rāzī favors the Jibrīl-reading but notes the "higher-class-of-angels" variant is consistent with the cosmic-singularity-and-angels-in-ranks juxtaposition (the Spirit IS NOT in the angelic-rank; it stands SEPARATELY at the head).

## 4. Ibn Kathīr — *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿAẓīm* on Q 78 [VERIFIED — Dār Ṭayyiba 2nd edition vol. 8]

Ismāʿīl b. Kathīr (d. 774 / 1373) — the Salafi-Athari narrative-tafsir; the *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm*, vol. 8 (juzʾ-30 commentary), Dār Ṭayyiba 2nd edition, pp. 304-322.

### General method

Ibn Kathīr's pattern is hadith-driven exegesis: he opens with chain-attested traditions on the verse, then provides the dominant-meaning, then notes alternative readings only when chain-supported.

### v.1-2 — al-nabaʾ al-ʿaẓīm

Ibn Kathīr cites:
- Ibn ʿAbbās chain (via ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭalḥa): Resurrection.
- Mujāhid chain: Resurrection (with explicit identification *al-nabaʾ = al-baʿth*).
- Qatāda chain: Resurrection.

He prefers the Resurrection-reading exclusively, treating the Quran-reading as "permissible-but-marginal."

### vv.6-16 — cosmology

Ibn Kathīr's cosmology-section is short and direct: the verses describe the empirical creation-evidence; God who made these signs is the One who will resurrect.

### v.33 — kawāʿiba atrāban

Ibn Kathīr cites the dominant-young-maiden reading. He notes the al-Ḥasan grape-reading as a marginal opinion not supported by his preferred chain-density.

### v.38 — al-rūḥ

Ibn Kathīr cites multiple chains:
- al-Rūḥ = Jibrīl (dominant)
- al-Rūḥ = a class of angels (Ibn ʿAbbās chain)
- al-Rūḥ = a singular angelic-being distinct from the standard angels

He prefers the Jibrīl-reading.

### v.40 — yā laytanī kuntu turāban

Ibn Kathīr cites the al-Ṭabarī-recorded tradition (animals → dust at judgment) but with chain-reservation. He prefers the simpler reading: the disbeliever wishes for non-existence (had he never been individuated).

## 5. al-Qurṭubī — *al-Jāmiʿ li-Aḥkām al-Qurʾān* on Q 78 [PENDING — SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED]

Abū ʿAbdallāh al-Qurṭubī (d. 671 / 1273) — the Andalusian legal-tafsir; *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān*, vol. 19, pp. 162-184 in the Dār al-Kutub al-ʿIlmiyya edition.

al-Qurṭubī's pattern is to gather both narrative-chains and legal-implications. For Q 78 (an eschatological surah), the legal-implications are minor; al-Qurṭubī focuses on the chain-density.

### Notable adjudications

- v.7 mountains: cites cosmic-engineering tradition consistent with al-Ṭabarī.
- v.33 *kawāʿib*: prefers young-maiden reading; cites grape-reading as marginal.
- v.38 al-Rūḥ: cites the variant-readings; prefers Jibrīl.
- v.40 *kuntu turāban*: cites both the animal-tradition and the non-existence-wish; prefers the latter as the more-direct reading.

al-Qurṭubī's distinctive contribution: he discusses the legal-implications of *aḥqāban* (v.23) — does the multi-aged-stay imply a CONCLUSIVE-eternal Hellfire or a LIMITED-but-prolonged stay? He cites varying scholarly opinions; the dominant Sunni view is conclusive-eternal for the disbelievers, with *aḥqāban* meaning "ages" as a marker-of-the-eternal rather than a specifying-finite-stay.

## 6. al-Bāqillānī — *Iʿjāz al-Qurʾān* references to Q 78 [PENDING — SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED]

al-Bāqillānī (d. 403 / 1013) in his *Iʿjāz al-Qurʾān* discusses Q 78 in the context of the iʿjāz al-balāgha tradition. The relevant passages (Dār al-Maʿārif edition pp. 235-238):

- He cites Q 78:13 *sirājan wahhājan* and Q 78:14 *māʾan thajjājan* as evidence of the Quran's lexical-precision (the rare-word + intensive-pattern combinations).
- He cites Q 78:7 (*wa-l-jibāla awtādan*) as an example of the cosmic-engineering metaphor: the mountains are described as *awtād* (tent-pegs), drawing on the Bedouin nomadic-life lexicon to convey a cosmic-anchoring image.
- He treats Q 78:1-3 as a paradigm of *al-istifhām al-taqrīrī* — the rhetorical-question-as-rebuke. The 3-step compression ("about what?" / "the Great Announcement" / "the disputed thing") is cited as one of the corpus's tightest interrogative-structures.

## 7. al-Suyūṭī — *al-Itqān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān* on Q 78 [VERIFIED — Dār al-Kutub al-ʿIlmiyya 1st print, 4-volume edition]

Jalāl al-Dīn al-Suyūṭī (d. 911 / 1505) — *al-Itqān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān*. Multiple references to Q 78:

### nawʿ 1 (asbāb al-nuzūl) and chronology

al-Suyūṭī's chronology of revelation places Q 78 at rank 80 of 114 (Late Meccan, after Q 70 al-Maʿārij and before Q 79 al-Nāziʿāt). This is consistent with the Nöldeke chronology and the surah's stylistic-marker as Late Meccan.

### nawʿ 17 (asmāʾ al-suwar) — names of surahs

al-Suyūṭī catalogues 3 names for Q 78:
1. **al-Nabaʾ** — the dominant name, from v.2 *al-nabaʾ al-ʿaẓīm*.
2. **ʿAmma yatasāʾalūn** — from the opening phrase.
3. **al-Tasāʾul** — from the verb *yatasāʾalūn* in v.1.

Some chains add **al-Muʿṣirāt** (from v.14) as an alternative name; al-Suyūṭī notes this but treats it as a less-attested variant.

### nawʿ 18 (al-mufaṣṣal) — the mufaṣṣal classification

al-Suyūṭī classifies Q 78 as **mufaṣṣal-awsaṭ** (middle-mufaṣṣal): specifically, the mufaṣṣal-awsaṭ runs from Q 49 al-Ḥujurāt to Q 85 al-Burūj, with the mufaṣṣal-qiṣār beginning at Q 86 al-Ṭāriq (some scholars place the boundary at Q 100 al-ʿĀdiyāt). Q 78 falls in the middle of this awsaṭ-band.

al-Suyūṭī's tripartite mufaṣṣal classification:
- **mufaṣṣal-ṭiwāl**: Q 49 → Q 85 or so (cf. competing readings)
- **mufaṣṣal-awsaṭ**: middle-zone, ~Q 78-91
- **mufaṣṣal-qiṣār**: Q 100 onward

The competing-classification (al-Bāqillānī and others): the mufaṣṣal begins at Q 50 (Qāf) and Q 78 is in the LONG-MUFAṢṢAL.

### nawʿ 56 (al-mathal) and miscellaneous

al-Suyūṭī cites Q 78:33 *kawāʿiba atrāban* in his catalogue of contested-reading verses. He preserves both the young-maiden reading and the grape-reading; preferences match al-Ṭabarī.

### nawʿ 67 (al-aqsām) — oaths and oath-like openings

Q 78 is classified as **non-oath-opening** in al-Suyūṭī's catalogue of opening-formulas. The interrogative-opener is documented as a separate sub-class (*al-fātiḥa al-istifhāmīyya*).

## 8. al-Zarkashī — *al-Burhān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān* on Q 78 [PENDING — SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED]

Badr al-Dīn al-Zarkashī (d. 794 / 1392) — *al-Burhān*, vol. 1 chapters on surah-opening typology and chronology.

al-Zarkashī classifies Q 78's opening as:
- **al-fātiḥa al-istifhāmīyya** (interrogative-opening) — one of the corpus's 7 interrogative-openers.
- **al-tasāʾul al-taqrīrī** (rhetorical-question-as-rebuke).

al-Zarkashī also discusses the structural-position of Q 78 as the **juzʾ-30 frontispiece**. He notes that the juzʾ-30 frontispiece's content-matter (eschatology, the *yawm al-faṣl*, the *nabaʾ al-ʿaẓīm*) is structurally appropriate for the FINAL juzʾ of the recitation cycle: as the recitation closes the Quran, the content closes with eschatological-finalization.

## 9. al-Biqāʿī — *Naẓm al-Durar fī Tanāsub al-Āyāt wa-l-Suwar* on Q 78 [PENDING — SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED]

Ibrāhīm al-Biqāʿī (d. 885 / 1480) — *Naẓm al-durar*, vol. 21-22 (juzʾ-30 commentary).

al-Biqāʿī's distinctive contribution is the *munāsabāt* (inter-verse and inter-surah connections):

### Q 77 → Q 78 transition

al-Biqāʿī treats Q 77 → Q 78 as a thematically-coherent transition: Q 77 al-Mursalāt closes with v.50 *fa-bi-ayyi ḥadīthin baʿdahu yuʾminūn* — "in what discourse after this will they believe?" Q 78 al-Nabaʾ opens with v.1 *ʿamma yatasāʾalūn* — "about WHAT are they asking?" The closing-question of Q 77 is answered by the opening-question of Q 78. This is a CLASSICAL-MUNĀSABA reading.

**Empirical correlation**: Q 77 → Q 78 has TSP-cost rank 40/113 (per H-NEW-720) and FR-distance rank 38/113. al-Biqāʿī's munāsaba is QUALITATIVELY apt but the empirical-cost rank is mid-spectrum (not extremely seamless). Compare al-Biqāʿī's claim about Q 6 → Q 7 (TSP-cost rank 113/113 = empirically MOST-seamless) — Q 77→78 is NOT one of the empirically-validated seamless seams (H-NEW-1240).

### Q 78 → Q 79 transition

al-Biqāʿī links Q 78's *yawma yaqūmu al-rūḥu wa-l-malāʾikatu ṣaffan* (v.38) with Q 79's *wa-l-nāziʿāti gharqan / wa-l-nāshiṭāti nashṭan* (vv.1-2) — the angels-extracting-souls oath. The angelic-action of Q 78:38 (standing in ranks) → the angelic-action of Q 79:1-3 (extracting souls in ranks) is a thematic-bridge.

**Empirical correlation**: Q 78 → Q 79 TSP-cost rank 28/113 — slightly higher than Q 77→78. Mid-spectrum.

### Internal munāsabāt

al-Biqāʿī notes the Q 78 v.7 → v.20 mountain-reversal and treats it as a deliberate-naẓm signal: the surah's structural-design has the same vocabulary anchoring opposite poles. This is the empirical intra-surah ring-feature documented in `02-content-analysis.md`.

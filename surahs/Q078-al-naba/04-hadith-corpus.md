## 1. Direct Q 78 surah-name in hadith literature

Unlike Q 1, Q 2, Q 36, Q 67, Q 78 does NOT have a strong, widely-attested *Faḍl Sūrat al-Nabaʾ* (merits of recitation) tradition in the major hadith collections. The surah's hadith-presence is ANCHORED ON SPECIFIC VERSES rather than on whole-surah recitation-merit:
1. **Q 78:18** (the trumpet / *al-ṣūr*) — links to the broader trumpet-eschatology hadith-cluster.
2. **Q 78:38** (al-rūḥ wa-al-malāʾika ṣaffan) — links to the al-Rūḥ identity-question hadith-tradition.
3. **Q 78:40** (yā laytanī kuntu turāban) — links to the animal-judgment-tradition hadith.

This pattern is consistent with Q 78 being an EXEGETICALLY-RICH surah without being one of the high-merit-recitation surahs. The classical *faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān* compendia (al-Nasāʾī's *Faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān*; Ibn Mājah's *Sunan*; al-Tirmidhī's *Sunan*) do not preserve a Q 78-specific *faḍl* chain comparable to Q 36 or Q 67.

## 3. Q 78:18 — the trumpet (al-ṣūr) hadith-cluster

Q 78:18 *yawma yunfaḫu fī al-ṣūri fa-taʾtūna afwājan* — "The Day when the trumpet is blown, and you come forth in throngs" — is one of the corpus's central trumpet-verses. The hadith-cluster on the trumpet spans multiple collections.

### al-Bukhārī, kitāb al-tafsīr, bāb wa-yawma yunfaḫu fī al-ṣūr

al-Bukhārī preserves a chain on the trumpet-blast under the tafsir of Q 78:18 (and parallels Q 6:73, Q 18:99, Q 27:87). The classical-Bukhārī numbering for the Q 78:18-anchored chain is in the range #4716-4717 (Fatḥ al-Bārī classical numbering); the AhmedBaset-JSON `idInBook` is approximately in the same range but not directly verified for this exact chapter.

The matn (gloss): the Prophet ﷺ described the trumpet as a HORN (*qarn*) ready to be blown; the angel awaiting the command stands prepared. The blast itself is doubled: a first blast (the *al-faḫ al-awwal*) terrifies all of creation, and a second blast (the *al-faḫ al-thānī*) raises the dead. al-Bukhārī's Q 78:18 chain anchors the doubled-blast doctrine.

### al-Tirmidhī, kitāb ṣifat al-qiyāma

al-Tirmidhī also preserves the doubled-blast tradition with Companion-anchored chains (Abū Hurayra; ʿAbdallāh b. ʿAmr). The traditions emphasize that the period BETWEEN the two blasts is divinely-determined (sometimes specified as 40 years; al-Tirmidhī notes scholars dispute the period-length).

### Cross to Q 78 tafsir tradition

Ibn Kathīr's commentary on Q 78:18 cites the Bukhārī + Tirmidhī chains in extenso. The trumpet-as-doubled-blast tradition is treated as the dominant exegetical reading.

**MW-6 verification status: SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED**. The trumpet-cluster is widely cited; specific Q 78:18 anchor-chains are PENDING physical-edition verification.

## 4. Q 78:38 — al-Rūḥ wa-al-malāʾika ṣaffan

Q 78:38 *yawma yaqūmu al-rūḥu wa-al-malāʾikatu ṣaffan* — "The Day when the Spirit and the angels stand in ranks" — anchors the al-Rūḥ-identity hadith-cluster.

### al-Bukhārī, kitāb al-tafsīr (Q 17:85 wa-yasʾalūnaka ʿan al-rūḥ)

The PRIMARY al-Rūḥ hadith-anchor is at Q 17:85, where the Prophet ﷺ was asked about the Spirit's nature and replied with Q 17:85 *qul al-rūḥu min amri rabbī* — "the Spirit is from the affair of my Lord." The al-Bukhārī chain (idInBook approximately #4721; classical-Bukhārī numbering #4721 in Fatḥ al-Bārī) identifies the Spirit as al-Rūḥ in a non-specific sense (a divine-affair that humans cannot fully grasp).

### Connection to Q 78:38

Q 78:38 specifies that al-Rūḥ STANDS IN RANK alongside the angels but is grammatically SEPARATE (the conjunction *wa-al-malāʾika* implies al-Rūḥ is not himself one of the malāʾika). This is the classical basis for the **al-Rūḥ = Jibrīl** reading: al-Rūḥ is the chief-angel, the head-of-the-rank, set apart from the rest.

al-Tirmidhī, al-Nasāʾī, and Ahmad all preserve variants of the al-Rūḥ-identity tradition. The dominant view (al-Ṭabarī, Ibn Kathīr, al-Qurṭubī majority) is al-Rūḥ = Jibrīl.

A **minority chain** preserved in Ahmad and some smaller collections identifies al-Rūḥ as a CLASS of angels distinct from the standard malāʾika (cited in al-Suyūṭī *al-Durr al-manthūr*); this is the "higher-class-of-angels" reading documented in §3 of `03-tafsir-survey.md`.

**MW-6 verification status: SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED**.

## 5. Q 78:40 — yā laytanī kuntu turāban (the animal-tradition)

Q 78:40 *wa-yaqūlu al-kāfiru yā laytanī kuntu turāban* — "And the disbeliever will say: 'Would that I were dust!'" — anchors a contested hadith-tradition.

### The animal-tradition (anchor on Abū Hurayra)

A tradition recorded in **Aḥmad's *al-Musnad*** (with chain-reservations) and cited by al-Ṭabarī ad loc. + al-Suyūṭī *al-Durr al-manthūr*: the animals will be brought forth at Judgment to be retributed for grievances against each other (the horned-against-the-hornless), and after grievances are resolved, the animals will be turned into dust to be spared the human-judgment. The disbelievers will witness this and wish to share the animal-fate (whence Q 78:40 *yā laytanī kuntu turāban*).

Chain-status: the chain is uncertain in the AhmedBaset-JSON dataset (the chain via Abū Hurayra has multiple variants and includes some weak transmitters). Classical scholars including Ibn Kathīr cite the tradition as a possibility BUT NOT AS AUTHORITATIVE.

### al-Suyūṭī *al-Durr al-manthūr* on Q 78:40

al-Suyūṭī catalogues the animal-tradition variant chains. He preserves them under PROVISIONAL status, noting the chain-issues. He also catalogues the alternative reading (the disbeliever wishes for non-individuated existence) without privileging either.

**MW-6 verification status: PENDING / SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED**. The animal-tradition is SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED via classical-tafsīr citations but has chain-reservations that classical scholars themselves note.

## 6. Q 78 in faḍāʾil + recitation literature

Beyond the above verse-anchored hadith-clusters, the corpus's *faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān* literature does NOT preserve a strong, widely-attested *Faḍl Sūrat al-Nabaʾ* tradition. The standard *faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān* compendia (al-Bukhārī's *kitāb faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān*; Ibn Kathīr's introduction to Q 78; al-Suyūṭī's *al-Itqān* nawʿ on faḍāʾil) do not preserve a high-merit Q 78-specific recitation tradition.

This contrasts with:
- **Q 1**: dozens of faḍāʾil chains (the *Umm al-Kitāb*; the *Sabʿ al-mathānī*).
- **Q 2**: famous chain on the closing-verses' protective virtue (al-Bukhārī #5009).
- **Q 36 (Ya-Sin)**: the classical "Heart of the Quran" claim (al-Tirmidhī #2887) — empirically REFUTED at H-NEW-82 NULL; the tradition is ḍaʿīf jiddan.
- **Q 67 (al-Mulk)**: the *Mubajjila* / *Munjiya* hadith-tradition (Tirmidhī, Abū Dāwūd; multiple chains).
- **Q 113-114 (al-Muʿawwidhatān)**: numerous protective-recitation chains.

Q 78's hadith-presence is **VERSE-LEVEL**, not WHOLE-SURAH-LEVEL. This is consistent with Q 78's content-character: the surah is a SELF-CONTAINED ESCHATOLOGICAL ARGUMENT, and its hadith-anchoring is at the eschatological-content level (trumpet, al-Rūḥ, animal-judgment).

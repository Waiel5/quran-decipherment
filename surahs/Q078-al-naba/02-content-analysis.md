# Q 78 al-Nabaʾ — Verse-by-Verse Content Analysis

## 1. The 4-block thematic architecture

Q 78's 40 verses partition cleanly into 4 thematic blocks, each marked by a discourse shift:

| Block | Verses | Length | Theme | Discourse marker | Key vocabulary |
|:-:|:--:|:-:|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | 1-5 | 5 | Framing (the disputed announcement) | INTERROGATIVE-REBUKE *ʿamma* | nabaʾ, mukhtalifūn, kallā, sa-yaʿlamūn |
| 2 | 6-16 | 11 | Cosmic evidence / signs in creation | RHETORICAL-CONFIRMATION *alam najʿal* + 4 wa-jaʿalnā | jaʿala, arḍ, jibāl, samāʾ, sirāj, mā |
| 3 | 17-30 | 14 | Day of Decision (yawm al-faṣl) — Hell | DECLARATION *inna yawma al-faṣli* | yawm, ḥisāb, ʿadhāb, jahannam, ṭāghīn |
| 4 | 31-40 | 10 | Paradise + closure | DECLARATION *inna li-l-muttaqīna* | jannāt, kawāʿib, kaʾs, dihāq, rabb, al-Raḥmān |

The block transitions are **content-marked** by lexical-formula shifts, not just by topic: Block 1's interrogative-rebuke gives way to Block 2's *alam najʿal*; Block 2's *wa-jaʿalnā* runs hand off to Block 3's *yawma yunfaḫu*; Block 3 closes with the closing *fa-dhūqū fa-lan nazīdakum illā ʿadhāban*; Block 4 opens with the antithetical *inna li-l-muttaqīna mafāzan*.

## 2. Block 1 — Framing (vv.1-5)

```
v.1: عم يتساءلون                       — ʿamma yatasāʾalūn
v.2: عن النبإ العظيم                    — ʿani l-nabaʾi l-ʿaẓīm
v.3: الذي هم فيه مختلفون                — alladhī hum fīhi mukhtalifūn
v.4: كلا سيعلمون                        — kallā sa-yaʿlamūn
v.5: ثم كلا سيعلمون                      — thumma kallā sa-yaʿlamūn
```

### v.1 — *ʿamma yatasāʾalūn*

**Translation**: "About what are they asking one another?"

**Discourse**: rhetorical-question contraction (ʿan + mā). The verb *yatasāʾalūn* is form-VI (mutual-action) of saʾala — "they are asking one another." Subjects implicit (the disputing Quraysh interlocutors / the disbelievers).

**Classical-grammar note**: al-Zamakhsharī (*al-Kashshāf*) distinguishes the *ʿamma* contraction from the unmediated *ʿan mā* — the contraction increases the rhetorical compression and signals genuine question-form rebuke (*istifhām taqrīrī*).

### v.2 — *ʿani l-nabaʾi l-ʿaẓīm*

**Translation**: "About the Great Announcement."

**Tafsir disagreement on *al-nabaʾ al-ʿaẓīm***:
- al-Ṭabarī, Ibn ʿAbbās majority: the **Resurrection / Day of Judgment** (the surah's content vv.17-40 is the explication).
- al-Suyūṭī, al-Rāzī sub-reading: **the Quran itself** (the dispute = whether the revealed message is true).
- minority view (Ibn Kayyisān): **the prophetic claim of Muḥammad** — the announcement = his claim of being a prophet.
- al-Rāzī *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* multi-referential synthesis: ALL THREE — Resurrection, Quran, Prophethood — as a triple-referent under the unifying *al-nabaʾ al-ʿaẓīm*.

The triple-reading is internally coherent because the Quran's truth-claim, Muḥammad's prophetic-claim, and the Resurrection are CO-DEPENDENT in the Late-Meccan polemical context (per cross-finding-012 Pattern-B7 phase).

### v.3 — *alladhī hum fīhi mukhtalifūn*

**Translation**: "Concerning which they are in dispute."

**Note**: *mukhtalifūn* is form-VIII (reciprocal-of-different) participle — "those-disagreeing-among-themselves." The disputers are not unified; they vary. Some Meccan parties accepted post-mortem judgment in some attenuated form (the Ḥanīfī monotheists; some pagan-eschatological strands per al-Bāqillānī); others rejected it absolutely.

### vv.4-5 — *kallā sa-yaʿlamūn / thumma kallā sa-yaʿlamūn*

**Translation**: "Nay! They will come to know. Then nay! They will come to know."

**Discourse**: the parallel-double-rebuke. The *kallā* particle is a strong-negative (sometimes *ḥarf radʿ wa-zajr* — "particle of repulsion and rebuke"; al-Suyūṭī *Itqān* nawʿ on ḥurūf al-maʿānī catalogues 33 corpus instances of *kallā*).

**Corpus parallel — IDENTICAL formula**: Q 102:3-4 (*kallā sa-yaʿlamūn / thumma kallā sa-yaʿlamūn*). The Q 78:4-5 / Q 102:3-4 exact-formula match is one of the corpus's 5 known multi-verse exact-formula matches; queued for Q078-F-03 follow-up.

**Hermeneutic**: the *thumma* + *kallā* compounding suggests an INTENSIFIED reiteration ("nay! and again, nay!"). al-Rāzī catalogues two readings:
1. Repetition for emphasis (*tarāyud al-tawkīd*).
2. Two distinct levels of "knowing": eschatological-knowledge (vv.17-30) followed by paradisical-knowledge or hellfire-knowledge (vv.31-40).

The structural-reading favors al-Rāzī's second view, since vv.17-30 (Block 3) and vv.31-40 (Block 4) are precisely the two "knowings" that the disbelievers will acquire upon resurrection.

## 3. Block 2 — Cosmic evidence (vv.6-16)

```
v.6:  ألم نجعل الأرض مهادا                — alam najʿal al-arḍa mihādan
v.7:  والجبال أوتادا                        — wa-l-jibāla awtādan
v.8:  وخلقناكم أزواجا                       — wa-khalaqnākum azwājan
v.9:  وجعلنا نومكم سباتا                     — wa-jaʿalnā nawmakum subātan
v.10: وجعلنا الليل لباسا                     — wa-jaʿalnā l-layla libāsan
v.11: وجعلنا النهار معاشا                    — wa-jaʿalnā l-nahāra maʿāshan
v.12: وبنينا فوقكم سبعا شدادا                — wa-banaynā fawqakum sabʿan shidādan
v.13: وجعلنا سراجا وهاجا                     — wa-jaʿalnā sirājan wahhājan
v.14: وأنزلنا من المعصرات ماء ثجاجا            — wa-anzalnā mina l-muʿṣirāti māʾan thajjājan
v.15: لنخرج به حبا ونباتا                    — li-nukhrija bihi ḥabban wa-nabātan
v.16: وجنات ألفافا                          — wa-jannātin alfāfan
```

### Structural fingerprint of Block 2

- **5 jaʿalnā tokens** (vv.6, 9, 10, 11, 13) — 4 of which are *wa-jaʿalnā* parallels (vv.9, 10, 11, 13). Q 78 ranks **2/88 in jaʿala-rate corpus-wide**.
- **3 corpus-hapax roots** in this block: whj v.13 (*wahhāj* "blazing"); vjj v.14 (*thajjāj* "torrential"). The third hapax (dhq) appears at v.34 in Block 4.
- **Triple-faʿʿāl-an pattern**: vv.13 *wahhāj-an*, v.14 *thajjāj-an*, v.34 *dihāq-an* — three intensive-pattern adjectives, two corpus-hapax. The pattern is: noun-X + intensive-adj-Y in fall-pause -an position. This concentrates the surah's morphological-stylistic uniqueness on the cosmic-evidence + paradise blocks (NOT on the framing or judgment blocks).

### v.6 — *alam najʿal al-arḍa mihādan*

**Translation**: "Have We not made the earth a [resting] cradle?"

The *alam najʿal* construction is a rhetorical-confirmation: "Did We not make…?" — semantically asserts that We DID make. *Mihād* (root m-h-d) is "cradle / smooth-bed / restful-spread." 4 corpus instances (Q 2:206, Q 7:41, Q 13:18, Q 78:6) and 1 cognate variant *mihādan* (Q 38:56, Q 51:48) — a moderate-frequency root with consistent earth-as-flat-spread metaphoric reading.

### v.7 — *wa-l-jibāla awtādan*

**Translation**: "And the mountains as pegs?"

The mountains-as-pegs (*awtād*) image. *Watad* (root w-t-d) appears 4× in the corpus with this metaphoric reading: Q 16:15, Q 21:31, Q 38:38, Q 78:7 — concentrated in cosmic-evidence passages.

**Classical reading (Ibn Kathīr)**: the mountains are the **anchors** of the earth, preventing it from shaking. The image is a cosmic-engineering metaphor: tent-pegs anchor a nomadic structure; mountains anchor the earth.

**Modern interpretation note**: the *awtādan* image has been deployed in iʿjāz ʿilmī claims (mountains as deeply-rooted, isostasy, etc.). Per HONEST-LIMITS-LEDGER + the project's iʿjāz ʿilmī REFUTED finding (0/12 axes), this verse should be read as **classical metaphoric-cosmology**, not as a scientific-foreknowledge claim.

### v.8 — *wa-khalaqnākum azwājan*

**Translation**: "And We created you in pairs?"

The *azwāj* (pairs) image. The Quranic theme of created-in-pairs spans multiple cosmic-domains (vegetation Q 13:3; plants/people Q 35:11; "pairs of everything" Q 51:49). At v.8 the pair = male/female human couples — the procreative-pairing that grounds Block 4's eschatological-conjugal-paradise (v.33 *kawāʿiba atrāban*).

### v.9 — *wa-jaʿalnā nawmakum subātan*

**Translation**: "And We made your sleep a [deep-]rest."

*Subāt* (root s-b-t) — corpus-formal "rest" / "Sabbath-cognate." 4 corpus instances of root s-b-t in various forms (Q 2:65, Q 4:154, Q 7:163, Q 78:9), all rest/Sabbath-related. The Hebrew *shabbāt* cognate is well-attested.

**Tafsir tradition**: al-Ṭabarī cites Mujāhid: *as-subāt* = "the cessation of motion" — sleep as cessation. Ibn Kathīr: sleep as cessation-of-faculties yet preservation-of-life — a "small death" anticipating the great resurrection. The thematic link to resurrection is direct: just as sleep is reversed by waking, so death will be reversed by resurrection.

### v.10 — *wa-jaʿalnā l-layla libāsan*

**Translation**: "And We made the night a covering [garment]?"

*Libās* (root l-b-s) — "garment / cover." The night-as-garment image; a personification trope. The corpus has 23 instances of l-b-s root, all garment-related (clothing, wifely garment Q 2:187 *hunna libāsun lakum wa-antum libāsun lahunna*). The cosmic-personification (night = garment) is shared with the wifely-personification. This is one of the corpus's CLEAREST consistent-figurative-readings.

### v.11 — *wa-jaʿalnā l-nahāra maʿāshan*

**Translation**: "And We made the day a [time of] livelihood?"

*Maʿāsh* (root ʿ-y-sh) — "means-of-living / livelihood." The day as the working-time vs. night as the resting-time. A practical-cosmology: cosmic structure is for human function.

### v.12 — *wa-banaynā fawqakum sabʿan shidādan*

**Translation**: "And We constructed above you seven sturdy [heavens]?"

The seven-firmaments image — *sabʿan shidādan* ("seven sturdy [ones]"). Note: this verse uses **sabʿan with elided noun** (the *samāwāt* is implicit). Per H-NEW-119 retraction (5/8 strict count of "seven heavens"), Q 78:12 is one of the 8 EXTENDED-count attestations (with implicit *samāwāt*) but NOT one of the 5 strict-count attestations (with explicit *samāwāt*).

The image of seven sturdy heavens is shared with Q 2:29, Q 17:44, Q 23:17, Q 23:86, Q 41:12, Q 65:12, Q 67:3, Q 71:15. Classical Islamic cosmology accepted the seven-firmaments at the level of cosmic image; the "seven" was variously interpreted as a literal count, a perfection-symbol, or a generic-multiplicity-marker (al-Suyūṭī surveys these in *al-Itqān* nawʿ 56 on al-mathal).

### v.13 — *wa-jaʿalnā sirājan wahhājan*

**Translation**: "And We made a blazing lamp [the sun]?"

*Sirāj* = "lamp." *Wahhāj* (root w-h-j) — **CORPUS-HAPAX**. This is one of Q 78's 3 corpus-hapax roots. *Wahhāj* is in the *faʿʿāl-an* intensive pattern: "blazing / radiating-heat-and-light." The hapax-on-the-sun choice is deliberately distinctive: the sun is named not with the standard *shams* but as a *sirāj wahhāj* (lamp + intensive adj).

### v.14 — *wa-anzalnā mina l-muʿṣirāti māʾan thajjājan*

**Translation**: "And We sent down from the rain-clouds water torrentially?"

*Muʿṣirāt* (form-IV active-participle of ʿ-ṣ-r) — "rain-pressers / clouds-being-pressed-out." This is a corpus-hapax usage (the only place where this form-IV plural-feminine appears with a meteorological referent). *Thajjāj* (root v-j-j) — **CORPUS-HAPAX**. *Faʿʿāl-an* intensive pattern: "gushing, torrential, flowing-abundantly."

The double-hapax verse (v.14: muʿṣirāt + thajjāj) is one of the corpus's most lexically-distinctive. al-Bāqillānī's iʿjāz al-balāgha tradition cites this verse for its lexical-uniqueness as evidence of revelatory composition.

### v.15 — *li-nukhrija bihi ḥabban wa-nabātan*

**Translation**: "That We may bring forth thereby grain and vegetation."

The teleological-clause: the rain's purpose is the agricultural produce. *Ḥabb* = grain; *nabāt* = vegetation. This connects the cosmic-evidence (Block 2) to the eschatological-paradise (Block 4 vv.31-32 *ḥadāʾiqa wa-aʿnāban*) — the same agricultural vocabulary spans both poles.

### v.16 — *wa-jannātin alfāfan*

**Translation**: "And gardens densely-planted."

*Alfāf* (root l-f-f) — "intertwined / densely-planted." This is a rare corpus root (corpus-attestation < 5 with this morphology). The image: gardens with intertwined trees, a thick-canopy paradise-figure. Block 2 closes with this image; Block 3 opens with *inna yawma al-faṣli* — a sharp image-shift from agriculture to judgment.

## 4. Block 3 — Day of Decision (vv.17-30)

```
v.17: إن يوم الفصل كان ميقاتا              — inna yawma l-faṣli kāna mīqātan
v.18: يوم ينفخ في الصور فتأتون أفواجا       — yawma yunfaḫu fī l-ṣūri fa-taʾtūna afwājan
v.19: وفتحت السماء فكانت أبوابا            — wa-futiḥati l-samāʾu fa-kānat abwāban
v.20: وسيرت الجبال فكانت سرابا             — wa-suyyirati l-jibālu fa-kānat sarāban
v.21: إن جهنم كانت مرصادا                  — inna jahannama kānat mirṣādan
v.22: للطاغين مآبا                         — li-l-ṭāghīna maʾāban
v.23: لابثين فيها أحقابا                    — lābithīna fīhā aḥqāban
v.24: لا يذوقون فيها بردا ولا شرابا          — lā yadhūqūna fīhā bardan wa-lā sharāban
v.25: إلا حميما وغساقا                     — illā ḥamīman wa-ghassāqan
v.26: جزاء وفاقا                           — jazāʾan wifāqan
v.27: إنهم كانوا لا يرجون حسابا             — innahum kānū lā yarjūna ḥisāban
v.28: وكذبوا بآياتنا كذابا                  — wa-kadhdhabū bi-āyātinā kidhdhāban
v.29: وكل شيء أحصيناه كتابا                 — wa-kulla shayʾin aḥṣaynāhu kitāban
v.30: فذوقوا فلن نزيدكم إلا عذابا            — fa-dhūqū fa-lan nazīdakum illā ʿadhāban
```

### Structural fingerprint of Block 3

- **All 14 verses end in alif** (-an or -ā fall-pause). Block 3 is the densest alif-fāṣila block of the surah.
- **3 *yawm* references** (vv.17, 18 implicitly, then later v.38 in Block 4) — eschatological day-naming.
- **Mountains-as-mirage (v.20)** reverses the mountains-as-pegs of v.7 — INTRA-SURAH IMAGE-RING.
- **Hellfire vocabulary cluster**: jahannam (v.21), mirṣād (v.21), ṭāghīn (v.22), maʾāb (v.22), aḥqāb (v.23), ḥamīm (v.25), ghassāq (v.25), ʿadhāb (v.30). 8 distinct hellfire-vocabulary tokens in 14 verses.
- **The closing *fa-dhūqū fa-lan nazīdakum illā ʿadhāban*** (v.30) is the only IMPERATIVE in the surah — *fa-dhūqū* ("so taste!") — a direct-address-of-the-condemned that is dramatically distinctive.

### v.17 — *inna yawma l-faṣli kāna mīqātan*

**Translation**: "Verily the Day of Decision is an appointed time."

*Yawm al-faṣl* — the "Day of Decision / Distinction / Separation." The eschatological-judgment day; corpus parallel at Q 37:21, Q 44:40, Q 77:14, Q 77:38. *Mīqāt* (root w-q-t) — "appointed-time-marker." The combination *yawma al-faṣli kāna mīqātan* asserts that the Day of Decision is FIXED, not vague.

The verse opens the eschatological-affirmation block with a direct DECLARATION (no question, no rebuke — just statement). This is the discourse-shift that ends Block 1's interrogative posture and Block 2's rhetorical-confirmation posture.

### v.18 — *yawma yunfaḫu fī l-ṣūri fa-taʾtūna afwājan*

**Translation**: "The Day when the trumpet is blown, and you come forth in throngs."

*Ṣūr* — the eschatological trumpet. Multiple corpus references (Q 6:73, Q 18:99, Q 20:102, Q 23:101, Q 27:87, Q 36:51, Q 39:68, Q 50:20, Q 69:13, Q 74:8, Q 78:18, Q 79:13). The trumpet-image is one of the most consistent inter-surah eschatological motifs. *Afwājan* — "in waves / throngs / groups."

**Hadith link**: the al-Bukhārī chain on the *ṣūr* (idInBook 4435 in AhmedBaset) preserves a chain on the trumpet-blast as terror-inducer. Q 78:18 is one of the trumpet-verses that Ibn Kathīr cites in this chain.

### v.19 — *wa-futiḥati l-samāʾu fa-kānat abwāban*

**Translation**: "And the sky is opened, becoming gates."

The sky-as-opened-gates image. Heaven becomes accessible at the apocalyptic dissolution. *Abwāb* — "gates" — both literal (entryways) and metaphorical (the heavens opening to release angelic-judgment).

### v.20 — *wa-suyyirati l-jibālu fa-kānat sarāban*

**Translation**: "And the mountains are moved [scattered / made to disappear], becoming a mirage."

**INTRA-SURAH IMAGE-RING**: *jibāl* (mountains) reappears here from v.7, but in REVERSED valence. v.7: mountains-as-PEGS (anchoring); v.20: mountains-as-MIRAGE (illusion-dissolving). The same vocabulary, opposite poles. **This is an empirical-structural feature of Q 78 and is queued for follow-up testing on intra-surah image-rings (Q078-F-03 candidate).**

The *sarāb* (mirage) image suggests that what looked SOLID (mountains) was always MERELY APPARITION. This is an eschatological-revelation theme: the cosmic order's apparent solidity is contingent on God's sustaining-will.

### v.21-22 — *inna jahannama kānat mirṣādan / li-l-ṭāghīna maʾāban*

**Translation**: "Verily Hell is in ambush; for the transgressors a place of return."

*Jahannam* — Gehenna / Hell (Hebrew Hinnom-cognate). *Mirṣād* (root r-ṣ-d) — "ambush / lookout / surveillance-post" (the form-of *mifʿāl*). The hellfire as a HUNTING-POST waiting for the transgressors. *Maʾāb* (root ʾ-w-b) — "place-of-return," the antonym to a homecoming.

The 2-verse pair vv.21-22 has a **prepositional structure**: "Hell IS X, FOR the transgressors A Y." This is rhetorically-tight; the ṭāghīn (transgressors) is the implicit-from-Block-1 Quraysh polemical-target.

### vv.23-30 — the hellfire-tableau

These 8 verses describe the experience of Hell:

- v.23: *lābithīna fīhā aḥqāban* — "remaining therein for ages." *Aḥqāb* (root ḥ-q-b) — "long-stretches-of-time / ages." The plural-form suggests indefinite duration.
- v.24-25: drinks restricted to *ḥamīm* (boiling) and *ghassāq* (pus / vile-fluid) — no cool drink (*lā bardan wa-lā sharāban*). The double-negation (no cold + no drink) is paralleled at v.35 (paradise: *lā laghwan wa-lā kidhdhāban*).
- v.26: *jazāʾan wifāqan* — "recompense matching-precisely." The jazāʾ-doctrine at its most literal: punishment EXACTLY fits crime. *Wifāq* (root w-f-q) is corpus-rare in this morphology.
- v.27: *kānū lā yarjūna ḥisāban* — "they were not anticipating a reckoning." The unbeliever's sin-genealogy: not-fearing-judgment = the root cause.
- v.28: *kadhdhabū bi-āyātinā kidhdhāban* — "they belied Our signs with [wholesale] denial." The cognate-accusative *kidhdhāban* is itself an INTENSIVE-pattern usage; an emphatic-denial-of-emphatic-denial.
- v.29: *kulla shayʾin aḥṣaynāhu kitāban* — "and EVERYTHING We have registered in a written-record." The book-imagery; cosmic-registry of deeds.
- v.30: *fa-dhūqū fa-lan nazīdakum illā ʿadhāban* — "so taste! For We will not increase you in anything except torment." The IMPERATIVE *fa-dhūqū* is the only imperative in the surah. The semantic intensification: punishment WILL ESCALATE.

## 5. Block 4 — Paradise + closure (vv.31-40)

```
v.31: إن للمتقين مفازا                       — inna li-l-muttaqīna mafāzan
v.32: حدائق وأعنابا                          — ḥadāʾiqa wa-aʿnāban
v.33: وكواعب أترابا                         — wa-kawāʿiba atrāban
v.34: وكأسا دهاقا                            — wa-kaʾsan dihāqan
v.35: لا يسمعون فيها لغوا ولا كذابا           — lā yasmaʿūna fīhā laghwan wa-lā kidhdhāban
v.36: جزاء من ربك عطاء حسابا                  — jazāʾan min rabbika ʿaṭāʾan ḥisāban
v.37: رب السماوات والأرض وما بينهما الرحمن…    — rabbi l-samāwāti wa-l-arḍi wa-mā baynahumā l-Raḥmān…
v.38: يوم يقوم الروح والملائكة صفا…            — yawma yaqūmu l-rūḥu wa-l-malāʾikatu ṣaffan…
v.39: ذلك اليوم الحق…                         — dhālika l-yawmu l-ḥaqq…
v.40: إنا أنذرناكم عذابا قريبا…                — innā andharnākum ʿadhāban qarīban…
```

### v.31 — *inna li-l-muttaqīna mafāzan*

**Translation**: "Verily for the God-conscious is a place of triumph / safe-haven."

*Muttaqīn* — the God-conscious. *Mafāz* (root f-w-z) — "place of triumph / saving-success / refuge." Q 78:31 is one of 7 corpus instances of *mafāz / mafāza* with a triumph-meaning (Q 3:188; Q 24:62; Q 38:50, 60; Q 78:31).

The verse's antithesis to v.21 is structurally tight: v.21 *inna jahannama kānat mirṣādan* / v.31 *inna li-l-muttaqīna mafāzan*. The *inna li-X-Y* construction marks the antithetical-pair-opening at the Block 3 / Block 4 hinge.

### v.32 — *ḥadāʾiqa wa-aʿnāban*

**Translation**: "Enclosed gardens and grapevines."

*Ḥadāʾiq* (sing. ḥadīqa) — "enclosed / walled-garden." *Aʿnāb* (sing. ʿinab) — "grapes." The garden + vines paradise; one of the standard corpus paradise-images.

### v.33 — *wa-kawāʿiba atrāban*

**Translation**: "And [companions] young + matched-in-age."

*Kawāʿib* — corpus-rare. The classical commentators differ:
- **young women / nubile companions** (al-Ṭabarī, Ibn ʿAbbās, al-Suddī, al-Ḍaḥḥāk): the *ḥūr ʿīn* paradise-companions.
- **swelling-of-breast youth** (Ibn ʿAbbās variant): the prime-of-youth physical state, applicable to either sex.
- **grapes / fruit-clusters** (minority — al-Ṭabarī notes this view cites al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī ad loc., though it is a marginal reading): grape-cluster image continuing v.32's *aʿnāban*.

*Atrāb* (root t-r-b) — "matched-in-age / contemporaries." The classical reading reads this with the female-companion-reading: matched-in-age maidens.

The **modern-philological reading** by Christoph Luxenberg (controversial) revives the marginal-grape reading via Syriac cognates — but this is outside the project's mainstream classical-tafsir audit.

For the project's purposes, the verse is documented with both readings; no empirical-test claim depends on adjudication.

### v.34 — *wa-kaʾsan dihāqan*

**Translation**: "And a cup filled to the brim."

*Kaʾs* — "cup / chalice." *Dihāq* (root d-h-q) — **CORPUS-HAPAX**. This is the third Q 78 hapax. *Dihāq* is in the *fiʿāl-an* pattern: "fully-filled, to-brim, overflowing."

### v.35 — *lā yasmaʿūna fīhā laghwan wa-lā kidhdhāban*

**Translation**: "They do not hear therein idle-talk nor falsehood."

The paradise-soundscape: no *laghw* (idle / vain talk) and no *kidhdhāb* (lying). The double-*lā…wa-lā* construction echoes v.24's hellfire double-negation (no cold, no drink) — antithetical-parallelism at the verse-form level.

**Note**: *kidhdhāb* here at v.35 is the SAME word as at v.28 (*kadhdhabū bi-āyātinā kidhdhāban*) — but at v.28 it's the cognate-accusative of the disbelievers' wholesale-denial; at v.35 it's the absence in paradise. Same lexeme, opposite poles. Another intra-surah ring-feature.

### v.36 — *jazāʾan min rabbika ʿaṭāʾan ḥisāban*

**Translation**: "A reward from your Lord, a gift, [reckoning-]matched."

The compound construction *jazāʾan… ʿaṭāʾan… ḥisāban* layers the divine-gift-economy: it is RECOMPENSE (jazāʾ), it is GIFT (ʿaṭāʾ), and it is MATCHED-PRECISELY (ḥisāb-an). The ḥisāb-an antithetically echoes v.27 *kānū lā yarjūna ḥisāban* — the disbelievers expected no reckoning; the muttaqīn receive PRECISELY-MATCHED reckoning.

### v.37 — *rabbi l-samāwāti wa-l-arḍi wa-mā baynahumā l-Raḥmān…*

**Translation**: "[the] Lord of the heavens and the earth and what is between them, the Most Merciful…"

This is the **first occurrence of *al-Raḥmān* in juzʾ 30**. The juzʾ-30 frontispiece's first divine-name is *al-Raḥmān* — a structurally significant choice given that *al-Raḥmān* is the only corpus name with a dedicated surah (Q 55) and is one of the 99-name Tirmidhi-list canonical names (rank 2 of 99).

The verse's structure: *rabbi X + wa-mā baynahumā* (Lord of heaven, earth, and what's between). The *mā baynahumā* expression is corpus-formula, occurring 16 times.

### v.38 — *yawma yaqūmu l-rūḥu wa-l-malāʾikatu ṣaffan*

**Translation**: "On the Day when the Spirit and the angels stand in ranks…"

*Al-rūḥ* — capitalized "the Spirit," classically identified with **Jibrīl (Gabriel)** (al-Ṭabarī majority view ad loc.; al-Bāqillānī cites "Mujāhid", "al-Suddī"). Other minority readings: a class of angelic beings; the angel of revelation; or a metaphysical concept distinct from the angels. The al-Rūḥ + angels in ranks formation appears in Q 70:4, Q 78:38, and partially in Q 16:2 (rūḥ alone).

*Ṣaffan* — "in a row / rank." The angels-in-ranks image. **Cross-link to Q 37 al-Ṣāffāt** (whose name is from the same root ṣ-f-f). Q 37:1 *wa-l-ṣāffāti ṣaffan* and Q 78:38 *wa-l-malāʾikatu ṣaffan* are the two corpus attestations of the cosmic-rank vocabulary; angels-in-rank is a 2-surah corpus motif.

The verse continues: *lā yatakallamūna illā man adhina lahu l-Raḥmān wa-qāla ṣawāban* — "they do not speak except for whomever the Most Merciful permits, and he speaks rightly." The angelic-speech is conditional on divine-permission.

### v.39 — *dhālika l-yawmu l-ḥaqq…*

**Translation**: "That is the True Day; whoever wills may take a path-back to his Lord."

The verse pivots: *fa-man shāʾa ittakhadha ilā rabbihi maʾāban* — "whoever wills may take a path of return to his Lord." This is one of the corpus's most-cited free-will / human-volition verses. The same *maʾāb* lexeme as v.22 (where it was the transgressors' return-place to hell) — but here *maʾāb* = path-back-to-the-Lord.

Another intra-surah lexeme-reversal: v.22 *maʾāban* (return-to-hell) ↔ v.39 *maʾāban* (return-to-Lord). Same word, opposite directions of return.

### v.40 — *innā andharnākum ʿadhāban qarīban yawma yanẓuru l-marʾu mā qaddamat yadāhu wa-yaqūlu l-kāfiru yā laytanī kuntu turāban*

**Translation**: "We have warned you of a near torment, on the Day when the person sees what his hands have sent forward, and the disbeliever will say: 'Would that I were dust!'"

The closing-verse is the surah's CRESCENDO. The torment is *qarīb* (near). The Day is when each person SEES (*yanẓur*) what they have prepared. The disbeliever's lament — *yā laytanī kuntu turāban* — is one of the corpus's rare PERSONAL-LAMENT closures.

**Discourse interpretation of *yā laytanī kuntu turāban***: classical commentators offer 4 readings:
1. **Wish for non-existence** (al-Ṭabarī majority): the disbeliever wishes he had remained (or become) the inanimate dust he came from, to avoid the judgment.
2. **Wish to be like the animal-world** (some commentators cite a tradition that the animals will be turned to dust at judgment to be spared the human-judgment): the disbeliever wishes for the animal-fate.
3. **Wish to have been the cosmic-dust** that pre-dates creation (a more philosophical-eschatological reading).
4. **Wish for non-individuated existence** — wish to be dust-among-dust without distinguishing identity.

The classical readings agree on the despair-content; they differ on the ontological-content of the wish.

The closing word *turāban* (dust) brings the surah back to the EARTH from the cosmic-tableau of vv.6-16 (where the EARTH was *mihādan* — a cradle). The surah opens with cosmic-evidence (earth as cradle) and closes with cosmic-lament (wish for earth-as-dust). **A subtle book-end: earth-CRADLE (v.6) ↔ earth-DUST (v.40).**

## 6. Summary of intra-Q 78 ring-structures

The surah is laced with 5 distinct intra-surah ring-features:

| Ring | Block 1/2 anchor | Block 3/4 mirror | Lexeme |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Mountains-Pegs ↔ Mountains-Mirage | v.7 awtādan | v.20 sarāban | jibāl |
| Day-Reckoning ↔ Day-Reward | v.27 lā yarjūna ḥisāban | v.36 ʿaṭāʾan ḥisāban | ḥisāb |

**6 ring-features in 40 verses** is structurally dense. This is a candidate for an INTRA-SURAH RING-DENSITY metric (queued under H-NEW-1260+ candidate set).

## 7. Empirical-claim summary

The 4-block architecture is **stylistically demarcated by lexical-formula shifts at each block-boundary**:

- vv.1-5 → vv.6-16 boundary: interrogative *ʿamma* + double-rebuke *kallā* → confirmatory *alam najʿal* + serial *wa-jaʿalnā*
- vv.6-16 → vv.17-30 boundary: cosmic-evidence + agricultural close (*jannātin alfāfan*) → eschatological declaration *inna yawma al-faṣl*
- vv.17-30 → vv.31-40 boundary: hell-imperative (*fa-dhūqū*) → paradise-declaration *inna li-l-muttaqīna mafāzan*

Each block-boundary uses the formula *inna* + nominal-clause to mark the discourse-shift. **The *inna* particle appears 4 times in Q 78** (vv.17, 21, 27, 31) — exactly at the structural-pivot points of Block 3 and Block 4 transitions. This is one of the corpus's tighter inna-as-block-marker patterns.

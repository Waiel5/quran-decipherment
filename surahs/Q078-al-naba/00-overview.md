## 2. Classical name

The surah is named **al-Nabaʾ** ("the Announcement") after the noun *al-nabaʾ* in v.2 *ʿan al-nabaʾ al-ʿaẓīm* — "about the Great Announcement." The Arabic *nabaʾ* is from the root n-b-ʾ ("to inform, announce, give tidings"), the same root underlying *nabī* ("prophet") and *nubuwwa* ("prophethood"). The classical commentators differ on the referent of *al-nabaʾ al-ʿaẓīm*:

- **the Resurrection / Day of Judgment** (al-Ṭabarī, Ibn ʿAbbās majority view; al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī; al-Suddī; Mujāhid; Qatāda): the surah's content vv.17-40 (the *yawm al-faṣl*, the trumpet, paradise vs. hell tableau) is taken as the explanatory body of the *nabaʾ*.
- **the Quran itself** (al-Suyūṭī cites; al-Rāzī sub-reading): the *nabaʾ al-ʿaẓīm* = the revealed message; the disbelievers' dispute (v.3 *fīhi mukhtalifūn*) is over its truth-status.
- **Muhammad's prophethood / the prophetic mission** (Ibn Kayyisān; minority): the announcement = the prophetic claim the Qurayshī interlocutors disputed.
- **multi-referential** (al-Rāzī *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb*): the *nabaʾ al-ʿaẓīm* spans Resurrection, Quran, and Prophethood as a triple-referent — al-Rāzī catalogues all three views without final adjudication.

The **majority view** (Resurrection / Judgment) is empirically supported by the surah's own thematic distribution: vv.17-40 (24 of 40 verses, 60%) develop the *yawm al-faṣl* and its consequences; vv.6-16 (11 verses, 27.5%) lay out the COSMIC EVIDENCE for resurrection (the *signs in creation* arc); vv.1-5 (5 verses, 12.5%) frame the dispute. The surah is structurally an **eschatological argument-from-creation** unfolding in 4 thematic blocks.

## 4. Opening formula — interrogative-rebuke

Q 78:1-5 (no-tashkeel):

| v | Text | Gloss |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 1 | عم يتساءلون | *About what are they asking one another?* |
| 2 | عن النبإ العظيم | *About the Great Announcement.* |
| 3 | الذي هم فيه مختلفون | *Concerning which they are in dispute.* |
| 4 | كلا سيعلمون | *Nay! They will come to know.* |
| 5 | ثم كلا سيعلمون | *Then nay! They will come to know.* |

The opening *ʿamma yatasāʾalūn* is one of **7 corpus surahs that open with an interrogative**: Q 16 (*atā*), Q 76 (*hal*), Q 78 (*ʿamma*), Q 88 (*hal*), Q 94 (*alam*), Q 105 (*alam*), Q 107 (*araʾayta*). The 7-interrogative-opener set is itself a structural sub-pattern that may merit follow-up (queued under H-NEW-1260+ candidate set; see Q078-F-04 and §6 below).

The formula *ʿamma* is a contraction of *ʿan + mā* ("about what?"), one of only 7 such *ʿan-mā* phrasings in the corpus and the only one in surah-opening position. The opening structurally **demands a response**: v.1 poses the question, v.2 provides the referent (*al-nabaʾ al-ʿaẓīm*), v.3 frames the disagreement (*fīhi mukhtalifūn*), and vv.4-5 deliver the parallel double-rebuke (*kallā sa-yaʿlamūn / thumma kallā sa-yaʿlamūn*). The double-*kallā* + double-*sa-yaʿlamūn* in vv.4-5 is one of the corpus's **densest 2-verse parallelisms** (Q078-F-03 will quantify this).

The opening is also a **discourse-act class** that classical balāgha calls *istifhām taqrīrī mufīd al-tawbīkh* — "rhetorical-question conveying rebuke / blame" (al-Sakkākī *Miftāḥ al-ʿulūm*, *ʿilm al-maʿānī* on rhetorical questions; al-Zarkashī *al-Burhān* nawʿ on aqsām al-istifhām). The *ʿamma* opener sets up not a real query but a rebuke posed in the form of one.

## 5. Thematic structure — the 4-block architecture

Q 78 unfolds in **four thematic blocks**, each marked by a discourse shift:

### Block 1 — Framing (vv.1-5, 5 verses)

The opening interrogative-rebuke. The disbelievers' dispute is named (the *nabaʾ al-ʿaẓīm*) and answered with the parallel double-rebuke (*kallā sa-yaʿlamūn / thumma kallā sa-yaʿlamūn*). **Discourse shift at v.6**: from interrogative to *alam najʿal* construction (rhetorical confirmation: "Have We not made…?").

### Block 2 — Cosmic evidence / signs in creation (vv.6-16, 11 verses)

The cosmological argument: the same God who shaped the cosmos (vv.6-16) is the one bringing the *yawm al-faṣl*. Distinctive features:

- **Mountains-as-pegs (v.7)**: *wa-l-jibāla awtādan* — the mountains are pegs/stakes (the *awtād* metaphor; corpus-distinctive vocabulary).
- **Sleep-as-rest (v.9)**: *wa-jaʿalnā nawmakum subātan* — sleep as a *subāt* (deep-rest, root s-b-t cognate with Hebrew Sabbath; the only corpus instance with this morphology).
- **Night-as-cover (v.10)**: *wa-jaʿalnā al-layla libāsan* — night as a *libās* (garment / cover; corpus-formal trope of personification).
- **Day-as-livelihood (v.11)**: *wa-jaʿalnā al-nahāra maʿāshan* — day as a *maʿāsh* (means-of-living).
- **Seven-firmaments (v.12)**: *wa-banaynā fawqakum sabʿan shidādan* — Seven sturdy [heavens] built above. Note: this verse is one of the H-NEW-119 (sabʿ-samāwāt-fold) cluster targets, but Q 78:12 uses *sabʿan shidādan* (NOT *sabʿa samāwāt*); see §H-NEW-119 retraction (5/8 strict).
- **Sun-as-blazing-lamp (v.13)**: *sirājan wahhājan* — root w-h-j ("to blaze") is corpus-HAPAX in this attestation, occurring only here.
- **Rain-clouds (v.14)**: *al-muʿṣirāti māʾan thajjājan* — root v-j-j ("torrential, gushing") is corpus-HAPAX.
- **The 4 wa-jaʿalnā-X-Y verses (vv.8-11)**: 4 consecutive jaʿalnā verses (we-made-X-Y), all generative-cosmology. Q 78 contains 5 j-ʿ-l (jaʿala) tokens in 131 root-tokens (rate 0.0382), **rank 2/88 in jaʿala density** corpus-wide (corpus rate 0.0044; Q 78 enrichment = 8.7×). The 3-consecutive-verse run vv.9-11 of *wa-jaʿalnā* is matched only by Q 21:30-32 in the corpus.

The block ends with v.16 *wa-jannātin alfāfan* ("and gardens densely-planted") — providing the AGRICULTURAL / GENERATIVE link to the eschatological gardens of Block 4 (vv.31-36). The transition to Block 3 is at v.17 with the explicit eschatological declaration *inna yawma al-faṣli kāna mīqātan*.

### Block 3 — The Day of Decision (vv.17-30, 14 verses)

The eschatological tableau opens with **v.17** (*inna yawma al-faṣli kāna mīqātan* — "Verily the Day of Decision is an appointed time"), then unfolds:

- **v.18**: trumpet blast (*yunfaḫu fī al-ṣūr*) → ranks coming forth (*fa-taʾtūna afwājan*).
- **v.19**: heavens opened as gates (*futiḥati al-samāʾu fa-kānat abwāban*).
- **v.20**: mountains moved as mirage (*suyyirati al-jibālu fa-kānat sarāban*).
- **vv.21-30**: the disbelievers' fate — Hell as ambush (*mirṣād*) for the transgressors (*li-l-ṭāghīna maʾāban*); long stay (*aḥqāban*); no cool drink (*lā yadhūqūna fīhā bardan wa-lā sharāban*); only scalding water + pus (*illā ḥamīman wa-ghassāqan*); recompense matched-precisely (*jazāʾan wifāqan*); they did not anticipate reckoning (*lā yarjūna ḥisāban*); they belied Our signs (*kadhdhabū bi-āyātinā kidhdhāban*); but everything was registered (*kulla shayʾin aḥṣaynāhu kitāban*); so taste (the punishment), We will only increase you in torment (*fa-dhūqū fa-lan nazīdakum illā ʿadhāban*).

The semantic pivot at v.20 (*wa-suyyirati al-jibālu fa-kānat sarāban*) is striking: the same MOUNTAINS (*jibāl*) that v.7 anchors as PEGS (*awtādan*) are at v.20 dispersed as MIRAGE (*sarāban*). The cosmic-anchoring imagery is reversed at the eschatological transition — a structural ring within the surah itself (queued for Q078-F-03).

### Block 4 — Paradise + closure (vv.31-40, 10 verses)

The paradise tableau opens with **v.31** (*inna li-l-muttaqīna mafāzan* — "Verily for the God-conscious is a place of triumph"):

- **v.32**: enclosed gardens + grapevines (*ḥadāʾiqa wa-aʿnāban*).
- **v.33**: *kawāʿiba atrāban* — well-rounded female-companions matched-in-age (a famous and contested verse; classical commentators differ on the referent — see §6).
- **v.34**: a cup full-to-the-brim (*kaʾsan dihāqan*) — root d-h-q ("filled-to-overflowing") is corpus-HAPAX.
- **v.35**: no idle talk, no falsehood (*lā yasmaʿūna fīhā laghwan wa-lā kidhdhāban*).
- **v.36**: divine reward (*jazāʾan min rabbika ʿaṭāʾan ḥisāban*).
- **v.37**: cosmic Lordship statement — *rabbi al-samāwāti wa-l-arḍi wa-mā baynahumā al-Raḥmān* — featuring **al-Raḥmān** (the surah-opening juzʾ-30 first occurrence of this name).
- **v.38**: *yawma yaqūmu al-rūḥu wa-l-malāʾikatu ṣaffan* — the Day when the SPIRIT and the angels stand in ranks. The ANGELS-IN-RANKS image links Q 78 thematically to Q 37 al-Ṣāffāt (the *ṣ-f-f* root opener; see Q037-cross-reference).
- **v.39**: *dhālika al-yawmu al-ḥaqq* — that is the True Day; he who wills may take a path-back to his Lord (*fa-man shāʾa ittakhadha ilā rabbihi maʾāban*).
- **v.40**: the closing verse — *innā andharnākum ʿadhāban qarīban yawma yanẓuru al-marʾu mā qaddamat yadāhu wa-yaqūlu al-kāfiru yā laytanī kuntu turāban* — "We have warned you of a near torment, on the Day when a person will see what his hands have sent forth, and the disbeliever will say: 'Would that I were dust!'"

The closing-cry *yā laytanī kuntu turāban* is the final ringing image of the surah and is a **rare PERSONAL-LAMENT closure** — among only a handful of corpus surahs ending in a personalized direct-discourse-of-regret (cross: Q 25:27 *yā laytanī ittakhadhtu maʿa al-rasūli sabīlan*; Q 89:24 *yā laytanī qaddamtu li-ḥayātī*). Q 78:40's personalization is the eschatological crescendo of the surah's argument.

## 6. ⭐ Distinctive surah-level fingerprints (Q078-F findings)

### F-02 — Q 78 within H-NEW-1200 short-Meccan-tail-eschatology cluster: PERIPHERAL, not central
The H-NEW-1200 cluster is the 14-surah {56, 69, 74, 77, 81, 82, 83, 84, 86, 90, 97, 99, 101, 104} short-Meccan-tail-eschatology meta-cluster (mean pairwise FR 0.6462, p=0.00030). **Q 78's mean FR-distance to this cluster is 0.4732** — closer than the corpus mean (0.6665) but **not closer than 11 of the 15 surahs evaluated for centrality** (Q 78 ranks **11/15** in intra-cluster centrality, between Q 83 and Q 77; the centroid of the cluster is **Q 97 al-Qadr** at 0.3682). ### F-03 — Q 78 does NOT use the *wa-mā adrāka mā* formula
The H-NEW-1190 cluster of *wa-mā adrāka mā* surahs is corpus-EXACT 10 surahs: {69, 74, 77, 82, 83, 86, 90, 97, 101, 104}. **Q 78 is NOT in this set** despite being sandwiched between Q 77 and Q 82 (both of which use it). The neighbors-without-the-formula gap: a 1-surah hole in an otherwise contiguous-tail-cluster. Q 78 uses other rhetorical-question formulas (the *ʿamma* opener; the *alam najʿal* of v.6) but not *wa-mā adrāka*. This makes Q 78 a STYLISTIC OUTLIER among its eschatological-tail neighbors.

## 3. Verse-twin and pericope-link relationships

### Q 30:30 (fiṭra) ↔ Q 39:6, Q 50:38

The lemma `fiTorap` appears uniquely at Q 30:30 (per Q030-F-02 lemma-membership analysis). However, the conceptually-paired *root* `fTr` ("to originate, split open the heavens") appears at Q 39:6, Q 50:38, etc. The DOCTRINAL anchor of fiṭra is Q 30:30; the COSMOLOGICAL-creation anchor is the broader root.

Per Bukhārī #4561, #4614, #4619 (Ibn Masʿūd), the Roman defeat is one of "five (eschatological-historical) signs that have passed." The companion verses are:
- Q 25:77 — the constant punishment.

This is a cross-surah eschatological-temporal cluster.

### Q 30:6 ↔ Q 30:60 (frame-bracket on *waʿda Allāh*)

The surah opens (v6) and closes (v60) on the lemma *waʿda Allāh* (Allah's promise). This is a classical ring-structure observation (al-Biqāʿī's munāsabah-internal claim).

### Q 30:21 (azwāj sign) ↔ Q 4:1 (single-soul creation)

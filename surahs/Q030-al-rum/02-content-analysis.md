## 2. Verse-by-verse summary

### Block A (vv. 1-7) — iʿjāz al-ghayb prologue

- **v1**: الم (the muqaṭṭāʿat opener).
- **v2**: *ghulibati al-Rūm* — passive verb opening declaring Roman defeat.
- **v3**: *fī adnā al-arḍi wa-hum min baʿdi ghalabihim sa-yaghlibūn* — "in the nearest land; and they, after their defeat, will be victorious."
- **v4**: *fī biḍʿi sinīn ... wa-yawmaʾidhin yafraḥu al-muʾminūn* — "in a few [3-9] years ... and that day the believers will rejoice."
- **v5**: *bi-naṣri Allāh; yanṣuru man yashāʾ; wa-huwa al-ʿAzīzu al-Raḥīm* — "by Allah's help; He helps whom He wills; He is the Mighty, the Merciful."
- **v6**: *waʿda Allāh; lā yukhlifu Allāhu waʿdahu; wa-lākinna akthara al-nāsi lā yaʿlamūn* — Allah's promise is unchanging; most do not know.
- **v7**: *yaʿlamūna ẓāhiran mina al-ḥayāti al-dunyā wa-hum ʿani al-ākhirati hum ghāfilūn* — "they know an outward of the worldly life; about the hereafter, they are heedless."

The prologue is structured: 5 verses of historical-prophecy + 2 verses of meta-comment. The transition is from concrete-prophecy (vv. 2-5) to abstract worldly-hereafter contrast (vv. 6-7).

### Block B (vv. 8-27) — Cosmic signs and *afa-lā* exhortations

- **v8**: *a-wa-lam yatafakkarū fī anfusihim* — "have they not reflected within themselves?" — ANCHOR INTERROGATIVE.
- **v9**: travel-the-earth motif; *fa-yanẓurū kayfa kāna ʿāqibatu al-ladhīna min qablihim* — "and see how was the end of those before them" (HISTORICAL-CALAMITY argument).
- **v10**: bad-end of those who lie about the verses.
- **v11-16**: cosmological cycle (begin-restore-return); the Day of the Hour; believers in gardens vs unbelievers in punishment.
- **v17-18**: *fa-subḥāna Allāhi ḥīna tumsūna wa-ḥīna tuṣbiḥūn* — daily prayer-times invocation (cited in classical liturgy).
- **v19**: living-from-dead, dead-from-living.
- **v20-25**: SIX *wa-min āyātihi* (and among His signs) — the most concentrated *āyāt-of-creation* sequence in the corpus:
  - v20: dust → human spread.
  - v21: *azwāj* (mates) for tranquility.
  - v22: heavens-earth + linguistic-color diversity.
  - v23: sleep + sustenance-seeking.
  - v24: lightning fear-and-hope; rain reviving earth.
  - v25: heaven-earth standing by His command + final calling-forth.
- **v26-27**: divine ownership; *al-mathalu al-aʿlā fī al-samāwāti wa-l-arḍ*.

The 6 *wa-min āyātihi* in vv. 20-25 are paired with cognitive imperatives:
- v21 → *li-qawmin yatafakkarūn* (people who reflect)
- v22 → *li-l-ʿālimīn* (the knowing)
- v23 → *li-qawmin yasmaʿūn* (people who hear)
- v24 → *li-qawmin yaʿqilūn* (people who reason)

### Block C (vv. 28-32) — Tawḥīd reductio + fiṭra

- **v28**: the SLAVE-PARTNER PARABLE — *ḍaraba lakum mathalan min anfusikum; hal lakum mim mā malakat aymānukum min shurakāʾa fī mā razaqnākum?* "Has He struck for you a parable from yourselves: do your slaves share equally with you in what We provide?" Reductio against polytheism.
- **v29**: those who follow desires without knowledge.
- **v30**: *fiṭra* — the primordial nature: *fa-aqim wajhaka li-l-dīni ḥanīfan; fiṭrata Allāhi al-latī faṭara al-nāsa ʿalayhā; lā tabdīla li-khalqi Allāh*.
- **v31-32**: turning-to-Him; not from those who fragment their religion into sects.

### Block D (vv. 33-46) — Mercy after distress + sustenance signs

- **v33-37**: ingratitude cycle; harm-then-mercy-then-shirk.
- **v38**: *fa-āti dhā al-qurbā ḥaqqahu wa-l-miskīna wa-bna al-sabīl* — give kin / poor / wayfarer his right.
- **v39**: ribā does NOT increase with Allah; zakāh does.
- **v40**: cosmic chain create→provide→cause-to-die→bring-to-life.
- **v41**: *ẓahara al-fasādu fī al-barri wa-l-baḥr* — corruption appeared on land and sea by what people's hands earned.
- **v42-46**: prophet-affirmation; final-day; the wind-and-rain mercy cycle.

### Block E (vv. 47-53) — Eschatological hour

- **v47**: *kāna ḥaqqan ʿalaynā naṣru al-muʾminīn* — "We are bound to help the believers" — VERSE OF DIVINE OBLIGATION (frequently cited).
- **v48**: wind-cloud-rain-mercy.
- **v49**: prior despair → reception of mercy.
- **v50**: *fa-nẓur ilā āthāri raḥmati Allāh kayfa yuḥyī al-arḍa baʿda mawtihā* — look at the traces of mercy; how He gives life to the earth after its death.
- **v51-52-53**: HEARING-METAPHOR — *fa-innaka lā tusmiʿu al-mawtā* — "you cannot make the dead hear" — the prophet's limit.

### Block F (vv. 54-60) — Concluding patience

- **v54**: 3-stage human life: weakness → strength → weakness-and-old-age.
- **v55-57**: the Hour; oaths in the day of resurrection; the unbelievers' excuses fail.
- **v58**: *wa-la-qad ḍarabnā li-l-nāsi fī hādhā al-qurʾāni min kulli mathal* — "We have struck for people in this Quran every parable."
- **v59**: divine-sealing of the hearts.
- **v60**: *fa-ṣbir inna waʿda Allāhi ḥaqq* — concluding imperative: be patient; Allah's promise is true.

The Block-A → Block-F frame is sealed by *waʿda Allāh* (vv. 6 + 60) — the surah opens and closes on Allah's unchanging promise. This is a classical "ring-structure" anchored at the explicit lemma.

## 4. Content register

Late Meccan **mixed register**:
- Historical-narrative (vv. 2-5, 9): the iʿjāz al-ghayb pericope + travel-the-earth motif.
- Cosmological-pedagogical (vv. 8, 17-27, 33-46, 48-50): "look at the signs."
- Eschatological (vv. 11-16, 25, 55-57): Day of the Hour.
- Ethical-imperative (vv. 38-39, 60): zakāh, ribā, patience.
- Tawḥīd polemic (vv. 28-32, 40, 51-53): polytheism falsified.

The register is THEMATICALLY MULTI-FOCAL — H-NEW-93 is consistent with this: Q 30's content density does not concentrate on a single theme.

## 5. Cross-surah content references

- *al-Rūm* and *biḍʿi sinīn* are unique to Q 30:2-4 (lemma `r~uwm` corpus-hapax; lemma `biDoE` near-hapax with Q 12:42 only).
- *fiṭrata Allāh* (Q 30:30) is the corpus-anchor for the *fiṭra* doctrine (the QAC root `fTr` "to split/originate" carries the *fiṭra* nominal in Q 30:30 specifically; root *fTr* appears 20 times across the corpus, but the *fiṭra* lexical-form is corpus-rare).
- *yatafakkarūn / yaʿqilūn / yasmaʿūn* refrain in vv. 21-24 is shared with Q 16 al-Naḥl (vv. 11, 12, 67, 69) and Q 13 al-Raʿd — the late-Meccan cognitive-imperative cluster.
- Q 30:60 *fa-ṣbir inna waʿda Allāhi ḥaqq* is paralleled by Q 40:55, 40:77 (*fa-ṣbir* concluding imperative).

## 6. Honest limits

- The Block A through Block F partition is a classical-influenced reading (al-Biqāʿī's munāsabah-block decomposition); alternative segmentations (Cuypers's Semitic rhetorical) would yield ~7-8 blocks.
- The "ring-structure" claim on *waʿda Allāh* vv. 6 + 60 is qualitative; quantitative ring-structure tests on Q 30 are not pre-registered in this folder. Any future ring-structure test should be a NEW pre-reg.

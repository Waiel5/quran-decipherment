## Opening verses (Q 30:1-5)

```
الم
غلبت الروم
في أدنى الأرض وهم من بعد غلبهم سيغلبون
في بضع سنين ۗ لله الأمر من قبل ومن بعد ۚ ويومئذ يفرح المؤمنون
بنصر الله ۚ ينصر من يشاء ۖ وهو العزيز الرحيم
```

- v1: الم (3 disconnected letters, the muqaṭṭāʿat opener).
- v2: *ghulibati al-Rūm* — "The Romans have been defeated."
- v3: *fī adnā al-arḍ wa-hum min baʿdi ghalabihim sa-yaghlibūn* — "in the nearest land; and they, after their defeat, will be victorious."
- v4: *fī biḍʿi sinīn* — "in a few [3-9] years."
- v5: *bi-naṣri Allāh; yanṣuru man yashāʾ; wa-huwa al-ʿAzīzu al-Raḥīm* — "by the help of Allah; He helps whom He wills; and He is the Mighty, the Merciful."

This is the **iʿjāz al-ghayb** anchor passage — al-Bāqillānī, *Iʿjāz al-Qurʾān* cites this as a paradigmatic "knowledge of the unseen" prophecy. The Battle of Issus (622 CE, Persian victory) and the subsequent Battle of Nineveh (627 CE, Roman victory under Heraclius) bracket the *biḍʿi sinīn* (3-9 years) prediction.

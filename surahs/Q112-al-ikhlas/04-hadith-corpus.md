## 1. The *thuluth al-Qurʾān* core

The single most-prominent fadāʾil-tradition for Q 112 is the *thuluth al-Qurʾān* ("a third of the Quran") tradition. Multi-chain evidence:

### Bukhārī

| ḥadīth # | Kitāb / Bāb | Sanad summary | Matn summary |
|:-:|:--|:--|:--|
| **#5013** | k. faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān, b. faḍl Qul huwa Allāh aḥad | Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī → Mansūr → ... | A Companion is heard reciting Q 112 repeatedly through the night; reported to the Prophet who says *innahā taʿdilu thuluthal-Qurʾān* ("It equals a third of the Quran") |
| **#5014** | same | Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī, second chain | similar matn; another Companion who used to lead prayer always concluded with Q 112 |
| **#5015** | same | Abū Hurayra → ... → "ijtamiʿū fa-innī sa-aqraʾu ʿalaykum thuluthal-Qurʾān" | The Prophet says he will recite a third of the Quran; recites Q 112 |

### Muslim

- **Muslim k. ṣalāt al-musāfirīn, b. faḍl qirāʾat Qul huwa Allāh aḥad** — multiple chains reproducing the *thuluth al-Qurʾān* matn (canonical numbering varies by edition; the chains parallel Bukhārī #5013-5015).

### al-Tirmidhī

- **al-Tirmidhī k. faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān, b. mā jāʾa fī sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ** — chains paralleling Bukhārī, with al-Tirmidhī's grading typically *ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ* for the principal chain via Abū Saʿīd.

### Aḥmad

- **al-Musnad** — multiple chains with *thuluth al-Qurʾān* matn, including via Abū al-Dardāʾ.

**Chain-quality audit summary**: The *thuluth al-Qurʾān* matn is **mutawātir or near-mutawātir** in transmission — the multiple independent chains (Abū Saʿīd, Abū Hurayra, Abū al-Dardāʾ, ʿĀʾisha) crossing both Bukhārī and Muslim with *ṣaḥīḥ* grading make the core matn one of the strongest *fadāʾil* claims in canonical hadith literature. Chain-quality verdict: **highly authentic**. See [[Q112-al-ikhlas/05-classical-claims-audit|05-classical-claims-audit]] Claim 1.

## 2. The hand-blowing tradition (recitation as healing/protection)

### Bukhārī

| ḥadīth # | Kitāb / Bāb | Matn summary |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| **#5017** | k. faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān, b. al-taʿawwudh wa-l-qirāʾa ʿinda l-nawm | ʿĀʾisha — When the Prophet went to bed, he would cup his hands, recite Q 112, Q 113, Q 114 into them, and pass them over his body, starting from his head and face. He repeated this three times. |
| **#5018** | same | parallel chain; same matn |

### Muslim

- **Muslim k. al-salām, b. ruqyat al-marīḍ** — parallel matn with the Q 112-113-114 hand-blowing.

### Aḥmad

- **al-Musnad** — multiple chains paralleling.

This tradition is the basis of the folk-practice of **nightly recitation of Q 112-Q 113-Q 114** (the muʿawwidhāt-3 in the broad sense). It links Q 112 to Q 113-Q 114 as a recitation-cluster, even though Q 112 is doctrinally distinct (creedal-tawḥīd) from Q 113-Q 114 (refuge-formula).

## 3. The post-ṣalāh recitation tradition

### al-Tirmidhī

- **al-Tirmidhī k. al-daʿawāt, b. mā jāʾa fī al-qirāʾa baʿda al-maktūba** — *al-muʿawwidhatayn* + Q 112 recited after each obligatory prayer; al-Tirmidhī grades *ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ*.

### Abū Dāwūd

- **Abū Dāwūd k. al-witr, b. mā yuqraʾ baʿda l-witr** — Q 112 + Q 113 + Q 114 after witr prayer.

### al-Nasāʾī

- **al-Nasāʾī k. al-istiʿādha, b. al-amr bi-qirāʾat al-muʿawwidhāt** — parallel.

## 4. Q 112 as a love-of-God indicator

### Bukhārī

| ḥadīth # | Matn summary |
|:-:|:--|
| **#7375** | k. al-tawḥīd. ʿĀʾisha reports a Companion led prayer and concluded every rakʿa with Q 112. When asked, he said *li-annahā ṣifatu l-Raḥmān fa-anā uḥibbu an aqraʾa bihā* ("because it is the description of the Most Merciful and I love to recite it"). The Prophet says *akhbirūhu anna llāha yuḥibbuhu* ("Tell him that Allāh loves him"). |

This is the empirical-load-bearing tradition that grounds Q 112 as the **canonical scriptural locus of God's self-description** (*ṣifāt Allāh*).

## 5. Asbāb al-nuzūl chains

### al-Tirmidhī, Aḥmad

- **al-Tirmidhī (graded *ḥasan*)** — Ubayy b. Kaʿb: the polytheists asked the Prophet *unsub lanā rabbaka* ("describe your Lord to us in lineage-terms"); Q 112 was revealed as the response.
- **Aḥmad al-Musnad** — Abū al-ʿĀliya via Ubayy b. Kaʿb — parallel matn.
- al-Wāḥidī *Asbāb al-nuzūl* — collects these chains; the main asbāb has at least 3 cross-chain attestations.

The asbāb is anchored in the Meccan polemic-context (against pagan-Arabian deity-genealogies). Chain quality: *ḥasan*. The classical Jewish-vs-Christian-vs-Pagan asbāb-disagreement (cross-referenced in `03-tafsir-survey.md` §1, §5) is rooted in alternative chains; al-Wāḥidī catalogues them all.

## 6. Q 112 in nightly-recitation traditions (extended)

### al-Tirmidhī, Abū Dāwūd, Aḥmad
- Multiple chains for nightly recitation of Q 112 after the *muʿawwidhatayn* (Q 113 + Q 114), or before sleep.
- al-Tirmidhī grades the principal chain *ḥasan*.

### Bukhārī
- **#5051** — Anas b. Mālik: a Companion who could only recite Q 112 in his prayer was told the Prophet had said his recitation alone was sufficient (*ḥubbuka lahā adkhalaka l-janna* — "your love for it has admitted you to Paradise" — chain via al-Bukhārī, fadāʾil).

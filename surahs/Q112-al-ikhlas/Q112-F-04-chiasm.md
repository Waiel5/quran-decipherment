## Interpretation

Q 112 has an exact *aḥad*-bookend chiasm structure:
- v.1: *aḥad* (A — declaration of unity)
- v.2: *al-ṣamad* (B — positive attribute)
- v.3: *yūlad* (C — negation of procreation)
- v.4: *aḥad* (A' — return to unity, now via peer-negation)

All 4 verses end in د (-ad rhyme cluster). The v.1 == v.4 lexical bookend is rules-tuple stable across no-tashkeel, min-tashkeel, and full-tashkeel.

This is a **micro-chiasm** structurally analogous to Q 1 al-Fātiḥa's 7-verse chiasm ([[Q001-al-fatiha/Q001-F-01-chiastic-symmetry|Q001-F-01]]). The Q 1 / Q 112 chiasm-pairing — corpus-head 7-verse chiasm + corpus-tail 4-verse chiasm — is itself a candidate architectural feature.

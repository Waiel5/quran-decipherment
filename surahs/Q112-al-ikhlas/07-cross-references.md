## 6. Corpus-head ↔ corpus-tail FR-centrality pairing

A symmetric architectural feature noted in `01-empirical-profile.md` §4 and Q112-F-01:

| Position | Surah | FR-centroid rank |
|:--|:--|:-:|
| Corpus HEAD (s=1) | Q 1 al-Fātiḥa | **4 / 114** |
| Corpus TAIL (s=112) | Q 112 al-Ikhlāṣ | **1 / 114** |

**The corpus's two structural endpoints both rank in the top-4 FR-centroids.** This is an empirical signature of the mushaf's architecturally-symmetric framing: both endpoints are theologically-general / FR-central. Q 1 is *umm al-Kitāb* (al-Bukhārī #4474); Q 112 is *thuluth al-Qurʾān* (al-Bukhārī #5013-15). Both are canonical *fadāʾil*-anchored.

This is a candidate for a future cross-finding (corpus-endpoint-FR-centrality). See [[muawwidhat-cluster-synthesis|cluster synthesis]] §4 for the formal hypothesis.

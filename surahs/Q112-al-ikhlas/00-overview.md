## 2. Classical names of this surah

- **al-Ikhlāṣ** (الإخلاص) — "Pure devotion / sincerity" (canonical name; from the surah's content of pure tawḥīd)
- **al-Tawḥīd** (التوحيد) — "Oneness" — the surah is the canonical scriptural locus of *tawḥīd* (al-Bukhārī, *Ṣaḥīḥ*, kitāb al-tawḥīd usage; al-Ṭabarī)
- **al-Asās** (الأساس) — "The Foundation" (al-Suyūṭī *al-Itqān*, listing of surah-names)
- **Qul Huwa Allāh Aḥad** — by its opening verse (classical citation form, e.g. al-Bukhārī ḥadīth #5013)
- **Sūrat al-Ṣamadiyya** — referencing v.2 *al-Ṣamad* (al-Suyūṭī)
- **al-Marrifa** / **al-Tafrīd** — "the gnosis / declaration of unity" (later mystic usage; al-Qushayrī)

The naming-multiplicity (≥6 classical names) is moderate-high, comparable to Q 1 al-Fātiḥa (≥12) and Q 36 Yāsīn — itself a sign of canonical centrality.

## 3. Opening formula

Q 112 opens with the imperative **qul** (قل = "Say"). It is one of four consecutive *qul*-opening surahs that close the corpus:
- Q 109 al-Kāfirūn — *qul yā ayyuhā l-kāfirūn*
- Q 112 al-Ikhlāṣ — *qul huwa llāhu aḥad*
- Q 113 al-Falaq — *qul aʿūdhu bi-rabbi l-falaq*
- Q 114 al-Nās — *qul aʿūdhu bi-rabbi l-nās*

This *qul-cluster* of 4 (with Q 110-111 interrupting) is itself an architectural feature; al-Suyūṭī catalogues the *qul*-openings (*al-Itqān*, nawʿ 17 on opening-formulae). Q 112's *qul* is unique in the cluster as the only one that does NOT introduce a refuge-formula (*aʿūdhu*) or a confrontation-formula (*yā ayyuhā*); it introduces a positive theological declaration.

## 4. Length classification

Q 112 is in the **mufaṣṣal-qiṣār / muʿawwidhāt-zone** — the corpus's terminal short-surah cluster (Q 93-114). Per al-Zarkashī *al-Burhān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān* (kitāb al-mufaṣṣal sub-divisions), the mufaṣṣal-qiṣār covers Q 93-114; Q 112 is comfortably in the bottom decile by length (47 letters; bottom-15 of corpus by letter-count).

# Q 18 al-Kahf — Cross-References

## 1. Canonical neighbours (Q 17 ↔ Q 18 ↔ Q 19)

### 1.1 Q 17 al-Isrāʾ → Q 18 al-Kahf

Q 17 is a Meccan surah (111 verses, prophet-narrative + Banū Isrāʾīl theme). The Q 17 → Q 18 transition is at canonical-adjacency cost **rank 86 / 113** (delta = 0.0279, fraction-of-residual 0.34%; `findings/phase-b-hypotheses/csv/h-new-720.json`). One of the *cheapest* single-surah transitions in the corpus.

**Lexical hinge** (per al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-Durar* Q 17 closing, Q 18 opening): Q 17 closes (vv. 110-111) on *qul ud-ʿū Allāh aw ud-ʿū al-Raḥmān* + *al-ḥamdu lillāh alladhī lam yattakhidh waladā*; Q 18 opens *al-ḥamdu lillāh alladhī anzala ʿalā ʿabdihi al-kitāb*. The transition is from *al-ḥamd-as-monotheistic-affirmation* (Q 17:111) to *al-ḥamd-as-revelation-affirmation* (Q 18:1) — the same fāṣila-key (*al-ḥamdu lillāh*) bridging two surahs.

**Empirical FR distance**: Q 17 ↔ Q 18 = 0.9013 (lower than corpus median 0.9235). Both are Meccan-mid prophet-anchored surahs; their content-cluster is similar.

**Connection to alif-monorhyme cluster**: Q 17 al-Isrāʾ is in the H-NEW-750 alif-cluster as a partial member (ا frac in the high range). Q 17 → Q 18 is one of the corpus's clearest cases of *alif-monorhyme cluster siblings adjacent in the mushaf*.

### 1.2 Q 18 al-Kahf → Q 19 Maryam

The Q 18 → Q 19 transition is at canonical-adjacency cost **rank 92 / 113** (delta = 0.0193, fraction-of-residual 0.23%; `h-new-720.json`). Even cheaper than Q 17→Q 18 — among the cheapest 20% of canonical transitions.

**Register pivot**: Q 18 (ends with *qul innamā anā basharun mithlukum*) → Q 19 (opens with *kāf-hā-yāʾ-ʿayn-ṣād* muqaṭṭaʿāt + Zakariyyā-Yaḥyā narrative). The transition is from *Prophetic-exemplar* (Q 18:110) to *prophetic-narrative* (Q 19:1ff.). Both are prophet-anchored Meccan surahs; the muqaṭṭaʿāt opening of Q 19 is structurally distinct but content-near.

**Empirical FR distance**: Q 18 ↔ Q 19 = 0.9254 (at corpus median). Modest distance.

### 1.3 The cheap-bracketing claim

Q 18 is an empirical case of **cheaply-bracketed by canonical adjacency** — both adjacencies are in the bottom-third of cost. This is the *opposite* of Q 24 al-Nūr's bracketing-cost geometry. Q 18 fits its position naturally; Q 24 disrupts its position. The two surahs occupy opposite cells in the *register-fits-position* axis of cross-finding-026's typology.

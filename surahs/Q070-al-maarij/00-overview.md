## 2. Classical name

The surah is named **al-Maʿārij** (*the ascending stairways*) after v.3 *mina llāhi dhī al-maʿārij* — "from God, possessor of the ways-of-ascent." Alternative classical names:

- **Sa'ala** (سأل) — after the opening word *saʾala sāʾilun*; less common but recorded by al-Qurṭubī.
- **al-Wāqiʿ** (الواقع) — after v.1 *bi-ʿadhābin wāqiʿ*; rare.

The name *al-Maʿārij* is rooted in m-ʿ-r-j (to ascend, mount up by stages); the same root yields *miʿrāj* (the prophetic Night Journey ladder) and *yaʿruju* "ascends" in Q 32:5 and in Q 70:4 itself ("*taʿruju al-malāʾikatu wa-l-rūḥu ilayhi*"). The name is thematically anchored: angels and Spirit ascend to God on these *maʿārij* in a 50,000-year day.

## 3. Opening formula — INTERROGATIVE-WARNING

Q 70:1-3 (no-tashkeel):

| v | Text | Gloss |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 1 | سأل سائل بعذاب واقع | *An asker asked about a falling punishment* |
| 2 | للكافرين ليس له دافع | *for the disbelievers — there is no repeller for it* |
| 3 | من الله ذي المعارج | *from God, possessor of the ascending stairways* |

This opener is **corpus-rare**: a finite-verb past-tense narrative-interrogative reporting a third-person request. The closest parallels are Q 78:1 (*ʿamma yatasāʾalūn*, "what are they questioning each other about?" — present-tense, 3rd-pl) and Q 51:12 (*yas'alūna ayyāna yawm al-dīn* — embedded). Q 70:1 is the **only opening verse in the corpus that uses *saʾala* (perfect 3rd-singular) as a sentence-initial main verb to introduce the surah's theme**.

### Classical disagreement on *sāʾil* (al-Ṭabarī ad loc.; al-Rāzī *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* vol. 30 p. 113-117; al-Qurṭubī *al-Jāmiʿ* vol. 18; al-Bayḍāwī)

The identity of the *sāʾil* (asker) divides classical commentators into 4 main camps:

1. **al-Naḍr ibn al-Ḥārith** (Mujāhid, Ibn ʿAbbās in one report): the Qurayshī polemicist who challenged Muḥammad to "bring on the punishment if you are truthful" (cf. Q 8:32 *amṭir ʿalaynā ḥijāratan min al-samāʾi*). The *sāʾil* is mocking. The "falling-punishment" *fell on him* at Badr.
2. **Abū Jahl** (Ibn ʿAbbās in another report): same scenario, different identification.
3. **A generic disbeliever** (al-Ṭabarī's preferred reading): *sāʾil* is generic — "an [unspecified] questioner".
4. **A reading reciter / a true seeker** (minority view per al-Suddī; al-Rāzī gives this as a possibility): *sāʾil* asks about *the way* of the punishment ("by what path does it come?"). Under this reading, *bi-ʿadhāb* is *about* a punishment, with information-seeking not mocking intent.

The *bi-* preposition is grammatically over-determined (al-Farrāʾ, al-Zamakhsharī): *saʾala bi-* can mean "asked about" (information-bi) or "called for, demanded" (request-bi). The four camps map to disambiguation choices.

Empirical: see [[Q070-F-01-interrogative-opener-uniqueness-prereg|Q070-F-01]] (verse-1 *saʾala sāʾilun* opening is corpus-unique among 114 surah-openers).

## 5. ⭐ Q 70:9 ↔ Q 101:5 corpus-twin: cosmic-dissolution simile

The test result: Q 70:8-9 uses a **distinct register** from the *idhā*-cluster — it employs *yawma takūnu* (TEMPORAL "the day when X will be") + *tashbīh* (similes via *ka-* "like"), whereas Q 81/82/84/99 use **passive verbal ellipses** (*kuwwirat*, *infaṭarat*, *inshaqqat*, *zulzilat*). This places Q 70:8-9 in a **"qāriʿa-style cosmic-dissolution sub-cluster"** with Q 101 — distinct from the *idhā*-cluster — sharing the simile-rendered apocalyptic register.

## 6. ⭐ Q 70:4 ↔ Q 32:5 ↔ Q 22:47 — the day-ratio triplet

Q 70:4 *fī yawmin kāna miqdāruhū khamsīna alfa sanatin* — "in a day whose measure is fifty thousand years" — is part of a 3-verse corpus set on the divine-day-to-human-day ratio:

| Verse | Ratio | Phrasing |
|:--|:-:|:--|
| Q 22:47 | 1,000 yr / day | *wa-inna yawman ʿinda rabbika ka-alfi sanatin mimmā taʿuddūn* |
| Q 32:5 | 1,000 yr / day | *fī yawmin kāna miqdāruhū alfa sanatin mimmā taʿuddūn* |
| Q 70:4 | **50,000 yr / day** | *fī yawmin kāna miqdāruhū khamsīna alfa sanatin* |

Q 32:5 and Q 70:4 share the formula **"fī yawmin kāna miqdāruhū … sanatin"** (in a day whose measure is … years). The numerical figure differs: 1,000 vs 50,000.

Classical resolution (al-Ṭabarī ad Q 70:4; al-Rāzī *Mafātīḥ* vol. 23 p. 240-242 ad Q 32:5 + vol. 30 p. 119-121 ad Q 70:4; al-Qurṭubī ad loc.):

1. **al-Ṭabarī's preferred reading**: the 1,000-year and 50,000-year days describe **different divine-temporal axes**. Q 22:47/32:5 (1,000 yr) refer to the *yawm al-dunyā* — the "world-day" of God's command-and-execution cycle (matter rises, decision is given, matter descends in 1,000 of our years). Q 70:4 (50,000 yr) refers to the *yawm al-qiyāma* — the eschatological Day-of-Judgment for unbelievers (the duration during which the cosmic restructuring unfolds and the wicked stand to account).
2. **Ibn ʿAbbās report (al-Ṭabarī)**: the 50,000-year day is *what it feels like to the disbeliever*; for the believer it is *like a single ʿaṣr-prayer* (cf. Muslim, ḥadīth on the Day of Judgment subjective duration).
3. **al-Rāzī's harmonizing reading** (*Mafātīḥ* vol. 30 p. 120): the two figures describe sub-stations of one day — the *50,000* is the maximum length any unbeliever can experience; the *1,000* is the standard length of the angelic ascent path. al-Rāzī then offers an *iʿtibār* reading: the universe has 50 stations (cf. *fa-mar-ḥalan ʿalā nafsika*) and each station is 1,000 years, totaling 50,000.
4. **al-Bayḍāwī**: the difference is rhetorical-dramatic, not arithmetical-physical. Q 32:5 emphasizes God's transcendence; Q 70:4 emphasizes the disbeliever's terror. Both are *figures*, not measurements.

The formula's numerical resolution is **NOT** an arithmetic contradiction within classical theology — see [[Q070-F-04-day-ratio-classical-resolution-prereg|Q070-F-04]] for the formal audit. The resolution depends on whether one accepts **temporal-axis-distinction (al-Ṭabarī)** or **rhetorical-figure (al-Bayḍāwī)** as the operative interpretive principle.

## 7. ⭐ Q 70:40 — the "lā uqsimu" negative-oath cluster member

Q 70:40 *fa-lā uqsimu bi-rabbi al-mashāriq wa-l-maghārib* — "I do not swear by the Lord of the easts and the wests" — is the **interior negative-oath** of Q 70 and a member of the classical *lā-uqsimu* cluster:

| Surah | Verse | Text |
|:--|:-:|:--|
| Q 56 al-Wāqiʿa | 75 | *fa-lā uqsimu bi-mawāqiʿ al-nujūm* |
| Q 69 al-Ḥāqqa | 38 | *fa-lā uqsimu bi-mā tubṣirūn* |
| Q 70 al-Maʿārij | 40 | *fa-lā uqsimu bi-rabbi al-mashāriq wa-l-maghārib* |
| Q 75 al-Qiyāma | 1-2 | *lā uqsimu bi-yawm al-qiyāma / wa-lā uqsimu bi-l-nafs al-lawwāma* |
| Q 90 al-Balad | 1 | *lā uqsimu bi-hādhā al-balad* |

5 surahs share this construction. The *lā* here is classically read as **emphatic, not negation** (al-Zamakhsharī, *al-Kashshāf* on Q 75:1; al-Rāzī expands at *Mafātīḥ* vol. 30 p. 192-194). It functions as a *redditive lā* — strengthening rather than negating the oath. The construction is corpus-restricted: **5 surahs only**, all Late Meccan, all eschatological. Q 56 (Wāqiʿa), Q 69 (Ḥāqqa), Q 70 (Maʿārij) form a **mushaf-adjacent triple** (4-surah window, with Q 67-Q 68 as Mufaṣṣal-internal boundary). The next two members (Q 75, Q 90) are 5 and 20 surah-positions later.

Empirical: [[Q070-F-05-negative-oath-cluster-cohesion-prereg|Q070-F-05]] tests whether {Q 56, 69, 70, 75, 90} form an FR-cohesive cluster. Result: **z = -1.25, p = 0.109** (NULL at 4-of-114 level; modest direction). The negative-oath cluster does NOT meet Bonferroni-corrected significance under the FR-content metric. The construction is morphological-rhetorical, not lexical.

The *mashāriq wa-maghārib* PHRASE in Q 70:40 is **corpus-unique in this exact form** ("rabb al-mashāriq wa-l-maghārib"). Other mashriq/maghrib formulations in the corpus:
- SINGULAR (Q 2:115, 2:142, 26:28, 73:9): *al-mashriq wa-l-maghrib*
- DUAL (Q 55:17): *rabb al-mashriqayn wa-rabb al-maghribayn* — winter/summer solstice
- PLURAL-EAST-ONLY (Q 37:5): *rabb al-mashāriq*
- **PLURAL-BOTH (Q 70:40 ONLY)**: *rabb al-mashāriq wa-l-maghārib* — many easts, many wests

The classical reading (al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, al-Bayḍāwī ad loc.): the *plural* mashāriq/maghārib are the 360 daily sunrise/sunset points of the solar year — each day has its own east-west axis. Q 70:40 thus invokes the **maximum cosmographic scale** in the negative-oath cluster, consistent with Q 70's 50,000-year Day-of-Judgment scale.

## 12. Quick content structure

- **vv 5-7**: command to patience — *fa-ṣbir ṣabran jamīlan / innahum yarawnahu baʿīdan / wa-narāhu qarīban* (patience contrast: they see it far, We see it near).
- **vv 8-9**: **cosmic-dissolution similes** — *yawma takūnu al-samāʾu kal-muhli / wa-takūnu al-jibālu kal-ʿihn* (sky like molten metal, mountains like wool).

- **vv 19-21**: **the ḥalūʿ ("anxious-soul") triplet** — *inna al-insāna khuliqa halūʿan / idhā massahu al-sharru jazūʿan / wa-idhā massahu al-khayru manūʿan* (man created anxious — when evil touches, fretful; when good, withholding).
- **v 22**: **the EXCEPTION** — *illā al-muṣallīn* — "except those who pray" — pivot to believer-virtues block.
- **vv 23-34**: **the believer-virtues catalogue** — 9 virtues: prayer-perseverance, almsgiving, day-of-judgment-affirmation, fear-of-Lord's-punishment, modesty, trust-keeping, witness-standing, prayer-vigilance.

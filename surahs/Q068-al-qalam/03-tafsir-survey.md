# Q 68 al-Qalam — Tafsir Survey

## 1. al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān ʿan taʾwīl āyāt al-Qurʾān*

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-al-tabari/68/1.json`.

**On ن (v.1) — the great fish gloss with Ibn ʿAbbās chain**:

al-Ṭabarī catalogues multiple Companion-positions on the meaning of ن:

> *qāla al-Ṭabarī: "ikhtalafa ahlu al-taʾwīl fī taʾwīli qawlihi: (ن) fa-qāla baʿḍuhum: huwa al-ḥūtu alladhī ʿalayhi al-arḍūn"*  
> ("Interpreters disagreed on the meaning of ن. Some said: it is the great fish upon which the lands rest.")

The first chain cited:

> Muḥammad b. al-Muthannā ← Ibn Abī ʿAdī ← Shuʿba ← Sulaymān ← Abī Ẓabyān ← Ibn ʿAbbās: *"awwala mā khalaqa Allāhu min shayʾin al-qalam, fa-jarā bi-mā huwa kāʾin, thumma rafaʿa bukhāra al-māʾi fa-khuliqat minhu al-samāwāt, thumma khalaqa al-nūna fa-busiṭat al-arḍu ʿalā ẓahri al-nūn"*

("The first thing Allah created was the Pen. It wrote what will be. Then He raised water-vapor and from it the heavens were created. Then He created the *nūn* (the fish), and the earth was spread upon its back.")

al-Ṭabarī cites multiple additional chains: Mujāhid → Ibn ʿAbbās via the Shurayk → al-Aʿmash → Abī Ẓabyān route; Saʿīd b. Jubayr → Ibn ʿAbbās; al-Ḍaḥḥāk. All transmit substantially the same cosmological narrative — the *nūn* is identified with the bearing-the-earth great fish.

**Alternative glosses cited by al-Ṭabarī**:
- *Ḥasan al-Baṣrī* and *Qatāda*: ن is a tablet of light or an inkwell.
- Some: ن is a name of Allah, an oath formula.

al-Ṭabarī's own preference is the Ibn ʿAbbās tradition — the great fish + pen narrative — which he treats as the most well-attested. He links the gloss to Q 68:1's *wa-l-qalam* (and-by-the-pen) oath: the muqaṭṭaʿ ن functions as a SHORT-FORM REFERENCE to the cosmological pen-and-fish narrative that v.1's full clause unfolds.

**On Q 68:4 *wa-innaka la-ʿalā khuluqin ʿaẓīm***:

al-Ṭabarī cites ʿĀʾisha's famous response: *"khuluquhu al-Qurʾān"* ("his character was the Qurʾān itself") — the foundational hadith for the Prophet's character (referenced at Ibn Mājah #2067, see [[04-hadith-corpus]]).

## 2. al-Qurṭubī, *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān*

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/68/1.json`.

**On Q 68 type (Meccan/Medinan)**:

> *"makkīya fī qawli al-Ḥasan wa-ʿIkrima wa-ʿAṭāʾ wa-Jābir. Wa-qāla Ibn ʿAbbās wa-Qatāda: min awwalihā ilā qawlihi taʿālā: 'sa-nasimuhu ʿalā al-khurṭūm' makkī. Wa-min baʿda dhālika ilā qawlihi: 'akbaru law kānū yaʿlamūn' madanī. Wa-min baʿda dhālika ilā qawlihi: 'yaktubūn' makkī. Wa-min baʿda dhālika ilā qawlihi taʿālā: 'min al-ṣāliḥīn' madanī, wa-mā baqiya makkī."*

("Meccan per al-Ḥasan, ʿIkrima, ʿAṭāʾ, Jābir. Ibn ʿAbbās and Qatāda say: from the beginning to *sa-nasimuhu ʿalā al-khurṭūm* (v.16) is Meccan; from there to *akbaru law kānū yaʿlamūn* (v.33) is Medinan; from there to *yaktubūn* (v.47) is Meccan; from there to *min al-ṣāliḥīn* (v.50) is Medinan, and the rest is Meccan.")

**This is the MIXED-MECCAN-MEDINAN classification on Ibn ʿAbbās's authority** — a striking minority position for what is otherwise classified as Early Meccan revelation #2. The Ibn ʿAbbās subdivision implies that Q 68 underwent **later interpolations** during the Medinan period (vv. 17-33 garden parable + vv. 48-50 Yūnus close). al-Qurṭubī attributes the subdivision to al-Māwardī's chain.

**On the qirāʾāt of ن at v.1**:

al-Qurṭubī catalogues five qirāʾāt readings of the muqaṭṭaʿ ن:
- Abū Bakr, al-Mufaḍḍal, Hubayra, Warsh, Ibn Muḥayṣin, Ibn ʿĀmir, al-Kisāʾī, Yaʿqūb: idghām (assimilation) of the second nūn into the wāw of *wa-l-qalam* — pronounced as *ŋ-walqalam*.
- The remaining: iẓhār (clear pronunciation) of the second nūn — *nūn walqalam*.
- ʿĪsā b. ʿUmar: fataḥ-nūn (open-vowel) — *nūna* with implied verb.
- Ibn ʿAbbās, Naṣr, Ibn Abī Isḥāq: kasr-nūn (kasra-vowel) — *nūni*.

This qirāʾāt diversity confirms the ن's lexical-ambiguity in the early reception: it could be an oath-particle, a noun (fish, inkwell), or a pure abjad letter. **Rules-tuple sensitivity flag**: Q068-F-02 (ن-letter density test) operationalizes Q 68's ن at the *letter-grapheme* level, not at any particular qirāʾa — the test is qirāʾa-invariant by design.

## 3. Ibn Kathīr, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm*

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-ibn-kathir/68/1.json`.

**On ن (v.1)**:

Ibn Kathīr opens with the standard reference: *"qad taqaddama al-kalāmu ʿalā ḥurūfi al-hijāʾ fī awwali sūrati al-Baqara, wa-anna qawlahu taʿālā 'ن' ka-qawlihi 'ص — ق' wa-naḥwi dhālika min al-ḥurūfi al-muqaṭṭaʿa fī awāʾili al-suwar"* — i.e., ن is one of the muqaṭṭaʿāt letters whose discussion he defers to his Q 2 opening commentary.

He then cites Abū Jaʿfar b. Jarīr's (al-Ṭabarī's) chain from Ibn ʿAbbās with the same cosmological pen-and-fish narrative. **The first-thing-created sentence**:

> *"awwala mā khalaqa Allāhu al-qalam, qāla: uktub, qāla: wa-mā-dhā aktub? Qāla: uktub al-qadar, fa-jarā bi-mā yakūnu min dhālika al-yawmi ilā qiyāmi al-sāʿa. Thumma khalaqa al-nūn, wa-rafaʿa bukhāra al-māʾi, fa-futiqat minhu al-samāʾu wa-busiṭat al-arḍu ʿalā ẓahri al-nūn..."*

This is the canonical "the first thing Allah created was the Pen" hadith with the *qadar* (predestination) attestation — exactly the chain found at al-Tirmidhī #3403 and Abū Dāwūd #4702 (see [[04-hadith-corpus]] for the hadith citations).

**Ibn Kathīr's structural reading**: the surah opens with a cosmological-theological frame (Q 68:1) and closes with a prophetic-discipline frame (Q 68:48-52). The four-block structure (oath, parable, judgment-day, Yūnus close) is held together by the SHARED-ROOT theme: *patience-and-character in face of denial*.

## 4. al-Suyūṭī, *al-Durr al-manthūr* (compiled hadith-tafsir)

al-Suyūṭī's hadith-tafsir compilation `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/suyuti-al-itqan-fi-ulum-al-quran-english.pdf` (the English Itqān) plus the Durr al-Manthūr corpus integrate the al-Ṭabarī Ibn ʿAbbās chains plus additional ḥadīth-attestations on:

- ن = a tablet of light upon which Allah writes (al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī).
- ن = the great fish (Ibn ʿAbbās majority chain).
- ن = the inkwell of light (Yaʿqūb b. Ibrāhīm; minority).

**On chronological order**: al-Suyūṭī's *al-Itqān* (nawʿ on chronological order; al-Suyūṭī compiles from al-Bukhārī, *Ṣaḥīḥ*, the chronology-ḥadīth chains) places Q 68 as **revelation #2**, immediately after Q 96 al-ʿAlaq (#1). This is the foundational classical-chronology consensus, verified across al-Suyūṭī, Nöldeke, and the Egyptian standard.

## 5. al-Zamakhsharī, *al-Kashshāf*

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/zamakhshari-kashshaf/` (Arabic OpenITI raw).

al-Zamakhsharī, a Muʿtazilī rationalist, **rejects the cosmological fish-pen narrative as a tradition of unproven authenticity** (a typical Muʿtazilī posture on ḥadīth-attestation). He glosses ن as either:
- A reference to *al-nūn* = the inkwell-noun (linguistic gloss).
- An oath-mark abbreviation, syntactically standalone.
- A name of Allah requiring no creaturely referent.

His preference is for the abstract-letter reading: the muqaṭṭaʿ ن is a *sirr* (divine mystery) at the opening, and v.1's *wa-l-qalam* is the formal oath that follows.

al-Zamakhsharī's reading is theologically distinctive: it strips the cosmological-narrative layer that al-Ṭabarī, al-Qurṭubī, and Ibn Kathīr embrace. **Rules-tuple flag**: the Q068-F-01 writing-vocabulary-density test (which VINDICATED Ibn ʿAbbās's content-beacon gloss) operationalizes the *empirical-density* claim; al-Zamakhsharī's *theological* claim that ن is a *sirr* is not empirically testable by lexical-density methods.

## 6. al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-Durar fī Tanāsub al-Āyāt wa-l-Suwar*

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/biqai-nazm-al-durar.pdf` (referenced for the munāsabāt analysis of Q 68's four-block structure used in [[02-content-analysis]] §2).

al-Biqāʿī's *Naẓm al-Durar* is the project's primary classical source for **inter-block munāsaba** (thematic-connection) analysis. For Q 68, al-Biqāʿī's reading:
- Block 1 (oath/affirmation): the Prophet's character.
- Block 2 (garden parable): cautionary tale of the disinherited.
- Block 3 (judgment-day): eschatological consequences.
- Block 4 (Yūnus close): prophetic discipline.

al-Biqāʿī treats the entire arc as a single munāsaba: *the Prophet is firmly upon great character → the deniers are like the disinherited garden-owners → the Day of Judgment will reveal them → and the Prophet must not repeat Yūnus's haste*. The thematic ring closes at v.52's *wa-mā huwa illā dhikrun li-l-ʿālamīn*.

## 8. Cross-mufassirūn synthesis on Q 68:42 (yawma yukshafu ʿan sāq)

al-Ṭabarī and Ibn Kathīr both catalogue the major Companion-positions on the meaning of *al-sāq* (the leg, v.42):
- Ibn ʿAbbās (one chain): a divine theophany — Allah's leg uncovered.
- Ibn Masʿūd: a metaphor for *al-shidda* (severity, hardship) — the Day of the Severe Calamity.
- Mujāhid + al-Ḥasan: hardship of the matter.

al-Qurṭubī sides with the metaphorical-shidda interpretation; Ibn Kathīr presents both with no strong adjudication. The verse is a classical *cruxq* of theological exegesis. **No empirical test is run on this verse** in the project (it is theological-philosophical, not statistical).

## 2. Classical name and naming convention

The surah is named **al-Furqān** ("The Criterion") after the noun *al-furqān* in v.1, where it functions as the autonymic title of the very revelation being announced. The name-by-opening-content-word pattern is shared with Q 67 al-Mulk (where *al-mulk* is named from v.1 *bi-yadihi al-mulk*) and Q 76 al-Insān (named from v.1 *hal atā ʿalā l-insāni ḥīnun mina d-dahr*), among others.

### Classical disagreement on the referent of *al-furqān* (al-Ṭabarī *Jāmiʿ al-bayān* on Q 25:1; al-Rāzī *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* vol. 24 pp. 49–55; al-Qurṭubī *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān* vol. 13 pp. 1–4; Ibn Kathīr *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm* on Q 25:1):

- **The Quran itself, as criterion-of-discrimination between *ḥaqq* and *bāṭil*** (al-Ṭabarī, Ibn ʿAbbās majority): *al-furqānu huwa l-Qurʾān, sammāhu Allāhu bi-dhālika li-annahu yufarriqu bayna l-ḥaqqi wa-l-bāṭil*. This is the dominant classical view. (al-Rāzī wajh I; al-Qurṭubī primary gloss.)
- **The Mosaic scripture / al-Tawrāh** (al-Ḍaḥḥāk, Mujāhid minority on the *furqān*-attestations Q 2:53 and Q 21:48; not generally applied to Q 25:1): in Q 2:53 *wa-idh ātaynā Mūsā l-kitāba wa-l-furqān* and Q 21:48 *wa-laqad ātaynā Mūsā wa-Hārūna l-furqān* the *furqān* is glossed as Tawrāh or as Mūsā's specific scripture-set. Some classical traditions extend this to Q 25:1 by analogy, but the v.1 verb *nazzala* + the object *al-furqān* + the recipient *ʿabdihi* (sg. = the Prophet Muḥammad) make this analogy unsustainable for Q 25:1. (al-Rāzī wajh II — explicitly subordinated.)
- **The criterion-of-victory / yawm al-furqān of Badr** (Q 8:29, 8:41 specific): *al-furqān* as the moral/divine discrimination granted to believers, especially the Badr victory date. NOT applied to Q 25:1 by classical consensus. (al-Qurṭubī secondary at Q 8.)
- **Any divine scripture sent in stages / criterion of true revelation**: a general gloss in al-Bayḍāwī *Anwār al-tanzīl*.

al-Rāzī (vol. 24 p. 50): *al-aqwālu fī l-furqāni khamsatun: al-Qurʾānu, al-Tawrātu, al-Injīlu, naṣru Badrin, wa-mā yumayyizu bayna l-ḥaqqi wa-l-bāṭili. wa-l-arjaḥu fī hādhihi l-āyati huwa l-awwalu li-anna sūrata l-Furqāni naẓarat ilā nuzūli waḥyin ʿalā Muḥammadin, lā ilā nuzūli waḥyin ʿalā Mūsā* — "There are five views on *al-furqān*: the Quran, the Torah, the Gospel, the Badr-victory, and that which distinguishes truth from falsity. The strongest reading in this verse is the first, because Sūrat al-Furqān concerns a revelation descending upon Muḥammad, not upon Mūsā."

Empirical: the autonymic-titular use of *al-furqān* in Q 25:1 is **DESCRIPTIVELY UNIQUE** among the 7 corpus attestations of the lexeme *al-furqān/furqān*. See [`06-novel-findings.md`](06-novel-findings.md) Q025-F-02 (DESCRIPTIVE-CONFIRMED 3/3 cells).

## 8. ⭐ Q 25:63–77 — *ʿIbād al-Raḥmān* catalog (the 15-verse believer-portrait block)

Q 25:60 is the **sajdat al-tilāwah** verse for surah al-Furqān: *wa-idhā qīla lahumu sjudū li-l-Raḥmāni qālū wa-mā l-Raḥmānu a-nasjudu li-mā taʾmurunā wa-zādahum nufūrā ۩* — "And when it is said to them 'Prostrate to al-Raḥmān!' they say, 'And what is al-Raḥmān? Shall we prostrate to whatever you command us?' — and it increases them in aversion."

Q 25 is the **7th of the 14 sajda surahs in mushaf order** (Q 7, 13, 16, 17, 19, 22, **25**, 27, 32, 38, 41, 53, 84, 96). Per [[h-new-1330-sajda-cluster|H-NEW-1330]] (CONFIRMED-NULL via Q053-F-03 independent replication), the 14 sajda-surahs are NOT FR-cohesive on root-distribution (p_perm ≈ 0.57–0.59). Per [[h-new-1331-sajda-muqattaat|H-NEW-1331]] (PASS-DIRECTED, p_hyper = 0.032), 7/14 sajda-surahs ARE muqaṭṭaʿāt-opened (1.97× corpus baseline); Q 25 is NOT among the muqaṭṭaʿāt-opened sajda-surahs.

The sajdah is rhetorically positioned at the apex of the second polemic block: a verse that itself NAMES the polytheist refusal to prostrate to *al-Raḥmān* immediately becomes (for the believer) the call-to-prostration. al-Qurṭubī (*al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān* vol. 13 pp. 68–71) and al-Nawawī (*al-Majmūʿ*) catalog the four-madhhab positions on the obligatoriness of the Q 25:60 sajdat al-tilāwah.

## 10. Classical commentary lineage

Major classical tafsir on Q 25 (full survey at [`03-tafsir-survey.md`](03-tafsir-survey.md)):

- **al-Ṭabarī** (*Jāmiʿ al-bayān fī taʾwīl āy al-Qurʾān*, vol. 19 pp. 233-326 of Šākir-Šākir ed.): the foundational tradition-aggregating treatment. Cataloguing of all 5 wajh on *al-furqān*; full Ibn ʿAbbās dossier on the disbeliever-objection block vv 4–10; specific tradition-chains on the *yaʾkulu al-ṭaʿāma* objection. al-Ṭabarī notes that the *ʿibād al-Raḥmān* catalog at 63-77 was held by some early authorities (Mujāhid via Ibn Jurayj) to have a Medinan core (vv 68-70 the homicide-and-adultery prohibitions) embedded in Meccan-block 63-77.
- **al-Zamakhsharī** (*al-Kashshāf ʿan ḥaqāʾiq ghawāmiḍ al-tanzīl*, vol. 3 pp. 263–303 of al-Mahdī ed.): Muʿtazilite-balāgha treatment. Reads the 3 *tabāraka alladhī* refrains (vv 1, 10, 61) as the structural triple-buttress of the surah's rhetorical architecture, organizing the surah into a tripartite *naẓm* around the divine-blessedness-of-revelation, divine-blessedness-of-eschatological-recompense, and divine-blessedness-of-cosmological-signs.
- **al-Rāzī** (*Mafātīḥ al-ghayb*, vol. 24 pp. 49–125): the most exhaustive classical treatment. Major contributions: (i) the 5-wajh enumeration on *al-furqān* (cited above §2); (ii) detailed *naẓm* analysis of the 3 *tabāraka* refrains as theological-axis-organizers; (iii) full doctrinal treatment of the angel-objection (vv 7–9), connecting it to *ʿiṣma*-theology and the Q 21:8 parallel; (iv) the *ʿibād al-Raḥmān* block as a compressed *uṣūl al-fiqh* curriculum (12 attributes mapped to 12 *furūʿ* of practice); (v) the *atakhadha ilāhahu hawāhu* (v 43) gloss connecting it to nafsological-balāgha (*hawā* = passion-as-deity).
- **al-Qurṭubī** (*al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān*, vol. 13 pp. 1–82): legal-emphasis treatment. Detailed jurisprudence on (i) the sajdah at v 60 (four-madhhab positions); (ii) the *lā yaqtulūna* + *lā yaznūna* prohibitions at vv 68–70 as foundational *ḥudūd* texts; (iii) marriage-and-water and the *baḥrayn* verse (vv 53–54) as nikāḥ-related; (iv) the *qurrata aʿyun* prayer at v 74 as a model duʿāʾ.
- **Ibn Kathīr** (*Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm*, vol. 6 pp. 79–135 of Dār Ṭayba ed.): hadith-anchored treatment. Records the Bukhārī *sabʿat aḥruf* hadith (al-Bukhārī 2322, 4785, 4836, 6679, 7264 — Hishām b. Ḥakīm reciting Q 25 *ʿalā ḥurūfin lam yuqriʾnīhā rasūlu Allāhi*) as the central asbāb-and-reception-history for the surah; full Saʿīd b. Jubayr / Ibn ʿAbbās dossier on the homicide-prohibition Q 25:68-70 + Q 4:93 abrogation question.
- **al-Biqāʿī** (*Naẓm al-Durar fī Tanāsub al-Āyāt wa-l-Suwar*, vol. 14 pp. 1-86): *munāsaba* (sequence-coherence) treatment. The Q 24 → Q 25 → Q 26 munāsabah is treated extensively (see [`05-classical-claims-audit.md`](05-classical-claims-audit.md) §1) — al-Biqāʿī reads the three as a tight rhetorical triad sharing prophetic-defense + revelation-cosmology themes. Our empirical audit FALSIFIES the Q 24 → Q 25 part of this triad (top-15 expensive seam on h-new-720) and VINDICATES the Q 25 → Q 26 part (cheap seam, fraction-residual 0.67%).
- **al-Suyūṭī** (*al-Durr al-manthūr* + *al-Itqān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān* nawʿ 1 chronology + nawʿ 17 asbāb): aggregate-tradition treatment. Records the *ʿibād al-Raḥmān* faḍāʾil traditions; treats vv 68-70 with the Medinan-anomaly note; preserves multiple chains on the asbāb of v 70 (the *illā man tāba* exception clause as a Medinan-revealed retraction of the harsh Meccan exclusionism for previous-sinners).

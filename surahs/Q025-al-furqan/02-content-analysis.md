# Q 25 al-Furqān — Content analysis

## 1. Overview

77 verses (mid-Meccan), 932 words, mean verse-length 12.10 words. Eight thematic blocks organized around three internal *tabāraka alladhī* refrains (vv 1, 10, 61) that buttress the surah's architecture, and a closing *ʿibād al-Raḥmān* attribute-catalog (vv 63–77). The surah is dominated by two register-modes:

1. **Polemic-quotative register** (vv 4–9, 11–14, 21–24, 27–34, 41–44, 60) — disbeliever objections framed by *qālū* / *waqālū*, followed by divine refutations / replies framed by *qul* / *unẓur kayfa*.
2. **Cosmological-axiom + portrait register** (vv 1–3, 10, 45–55, 61, 63–77) — divine-blessedness affirmations, signs-in-nature (shadow, sun, night, water, marriage, two-seas), believer-portrait catalog.

The two registers are interleaved, with the three *tabāraka* refrains marking the rhetorical pivots.

## 3. Thematic blocks (summary)

The 8 blocks are summarized in [`00-overview.md`](00-overview.md) §7 and run as follows:

| Block | Verses | Theme summary |
|:-:|:-:|:--|
| A | 1–3 | Doxological opening + tawḥīd of dominion |
| B | 4–9 | First polemic block — disbeliever objections to revelation and Prophet |
| C | 10–20 | First *tabāraka* refrain + eschatological vindication + jinn-and-paradise + earlier-prophets defense |
| D | 21–34 | Second polemic block — angel-objection, *yawma yaʿaḍḍu ẓ-ẓālim*, scattered-revelation objection + prophet-typology summary |

| H | 61–77 | Third *tabāraka* refrain + ʿIBĀD AL-RAḤMĀN PORTRAIT + Prophet's final speech-act |

## 4. Content register

**Mixed creedal + polemic + eschatological + cosmological + portrait register**, organized around three *tabāraka alladhī* refrains (vv 1, 10, 61) that function as rhetorical-axis pivots:

- *tabāraka* @v.1 — opens the surah with revelation-axis (*nazzala l-furqāna*).
- *tabāraka* @v.10 — pivots from polemic-block-B into eschatological-vindication-block-C (*jaʿala laka khayran min dhālika*).
- *tabāraka* @v.61 — pivots from sajda-and-*qul* block-G into ʿibād-al-Raḥmān portrait block-H (*jaʿala fī s-samāʾi burūjan*).

al-Zamakhsharī (*Kashshāf* vol. 3 pp. 263–303) reads this **3-refrain architecture** as the surah's central *naẓm* device. Empirical confirmation: the 3 *tabāraka* attestations are not just lexical repetitions; they correspond to the three structural pivots of the surah's content-organization, which the *Naẓm al-Durar* tradition (al-Biqāʿī) also identifies.

## 6. Repetition patterns

- **3 *tabāraka alladhī* attestations** (vv 1, 10, 61) — the 3-refrain structural pivots.
- **8 *alladhīna / wa-lladhīna* relative clauses** in vv 63-77 (ʿibād al-Raḥmān portrait block).
- **5 *qul* imperatives** at vv 6, 15, 57, 60-implicit-in-quotation, 77.
- **5+ disbeliever-objection *qālū* / *waqālū* attestations** at vv 4, 5, 7, 8, 21, 32, 60 (parts of which are quoted refutations).
- **Repeated *yawma* / *yawmaʾidhin* eschatological-tableau markers** at vv 17, 22, 24, 26, 27.

## 7. Cross-surah content references

- **Mūsā + Hārūn pair** (v 35) — paralleled at Q 20:29 (Mūsā's prayer for Hārūn as wazīr) and Q 28:34 (Mūsā speaks Hārūn's appointment). The Q 25:35 *jaʿalnā maʿahu akhāhu Hārūna wazīran* is the most CONCISE statement of this in the corpus.
- **Aṣḥāb al-Rass** (v 38) — appears only in Q 25:38 and Q 50:12 (two corpus attestations total). al-Ṭabarī and Ibn Kathīr discuss the identity of these people inconclusively.
- **Two-seas-meeting** (v 53 *maraja l-baḥrayni hādhā ʿadhbun furātun wa-hādhā milḥun ujājun*) — paralleled at Q 35:12 and Q 55:19-22. Q 25's version specifies *barzakhan wa-ḥijran maḥjūrā* (a barrier AND a forbidden boundary), unique to Q 25 in this construction.
- **Shadow-and-sun cosmology** (vv 45-46) — unique in the corpus to Q 25; no parallel passage on the shadow's extension-and-retraction as cosmological sign.
- **Q 25:68-70 homicide-and-adultery prohibition** + abrogation exception — Saʿīd b. Jubayr / Ibn ʿAbbās discussion of this with Q 4:93 (Medinan *qatl-al-muʾmin* verse) is extensively narrated in Bukhārī 3855, 3858, 4590, 4762, 4763, 4764, 6863, 6885, 6896, 7308 etc. (see [`04-hadith-corpus.md`](04-hadith-corpus.md) §3). The *illā man tāba* exception at v 70 is the textual locus of one of classical *uṣūl al-fiqh*'s key abrogation/exception debates.
- **Q 25:74 *qurrata aʿyunin* prayer** — paralleled at Q 33:51 (where *taqarru aʿyunuhunna* is used) and the Q 19:26 *fa-kulī wa-shrabī wa-qarrī ʿaynan* (Maryam). The *qurra* lexeme has 3 attestations corpus-wide on the eye-coolness theme.

## 8. Distinctive Q 25-specific linguistic features

- **Autonymic title-use**: v 1 *al-furqān* names what the surah itself is. The construction *tabāraka alladhī nazzala l-X ʿalā ʿabdihi* with X = the surah's title-word is structurally unique to Q 25:1 (Q 67:1 uses a different syntactic frame — predicate-prepositional, not direct-object).
- **3 *tabāraka alladhī* refrains in one surah** — unique to Q 25; no other surah has 2+ *tabāraka alladhī* attestations.
- **8-fold *alladhīna* / *wa-lladhīna* portrait cascade** in vv 63-77 — second-longest *alladhīna*-cascade in the corpus (after Q 23:1-11 *muʾminūn*-portrait if you count its 7 markers + the *alladhīna yarithūna* @ v 11 yielding 8 markers; tied at 8 with Q 70:22-35 *muṣallīn*-catalog). - **Wa-jāhidhum bihi jihādan kabīrā** (v 52) — corpus-singleton imperative; the only place the Prophet is commanded to "strive against them with this [Quran] with great striving."
- **al-anʿām / aḍallu sabīlā** (v 44) — the *they are like cattle, indeed more astray* polemic; structurally paralleled at Q 7:179 (*ulāʾika ka-l-anʿāmi bal hum aḍall*) — only 2 corpus attestations of this exact construction.
- **The *hawā-as-deity* polemic** at v 43 (*man ttakhadha ilāhahu hawāhu*) — paralleled at Q 45:23; only 2 corpus attestations.

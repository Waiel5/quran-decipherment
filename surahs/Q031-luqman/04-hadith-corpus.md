## 1. The cardinal Q 31:13 / Q 6:82 chain (al-Aʿmash → Ibrāhīm al-Nakhaʿī → ʿAlqama → ʿAbd Allāh ibn Masʿūd)

The single most-attested ḥadīth-chain in the Q 31 corpus connects v.13 to Q 6:82 (the *ẓulm = shirk* clarification). The chain is recorded in **5 narrations in Bukhārī** + **1 in Muslim** + **1 in Tirmidhī** — a multi-collection Sound chain of the type al-Suyūṭī classes as *mutawātir-maʿnawī* (multiply-attested by meaning).

### 1.1 al-Bukhārī

Five narrations of the same incident with chain variations:

| Citation | idInBook | Asbāb al-Nuzūl pattern |
|:--|:-:|:--|
| **Bukhārī #3222** | 3222 | Companions ask: "Who has not done wrong?" Prophet replies: shirk is the *ẓulm* in Q 6:82, citing Q 31:13. |
| Bukhārī #3287 | 3287 | Same incident, parallel chain. |
| Bukhārī #4570 | 4570 | Same incident, parallel chain. |
| Bukhārī #6662 | 6662 | Same incident, parallel chain. |
| Bukhārī #6680 | 6680 | Same incident, parallel chain. |

**Verified Bukhārī #3222** (text snippet):
> "When the Verse: 'It is those who believe and do not confuse their belief with wrong (i.e. joining others in worship with Allah)' (Q 6:82) was revealed, we said, 'O Allah's Messenger! Who is there amongst us who has not done wrong to himself?' He replied, 'It is not as you say, for "wrong" in the Verse and "do not confuse their belief, with wrong" means SHIRK (i.e. joining others in worship with Allah). Have not you heard Luqman's saying to his son, "O my son! Join not others in worship with Allah, verily joining others in worship with Allah is a great wrong indeed."' (Q 31:13)"

The 5 Bukhārī narrations all preserve the **same chain backbone**: al-Aʿmash → Ibrāhīm al-Nakhaʿī → ʿAlqama → Ibn Masʿūd. The chain variations are at the level of al-Aʿmash's pupils (different students recorded the same Aʿmash-narration). This is one of the corpus's most-tightly-attested isnad-chains.

### 1.2 Muslim

| Citation | idInBook | Status |
|:--|:-:|:--|
| **Muslim #234** | 234 | Same Q 6:82 / Q 31:13 chain; same al-Aʿmash backbone; *ẓulm-shirk* clarification. |

**Verified Muslim #234** (text snippet):
> "It is narrated on the authority of 'Abdullah (b. Mas'ud) that when this verse was revealed: 'It is those who believe and confound not their belief with wrongdoing' (Q 6:82), the Companions of the Messenger of Allah were greatly perturbed. They said: Who amongst us (is so fortunate) that he does not wrong himself? Upon this the Messenger of Allah remarked: It does not mean that which you presume. It implies that which Luqman said to his son: O my son, do not associate anything with Allah, for indeed it is the gravest wrongdoing." (Q 31:13)

### 1.3 Tirmidhī

| Citation | idInBook | Status |
|:--|:-:|:--|
| **Tirmidhī #3151** | 3151 | Same Q 6:82 / Q 31:13 / al-Aʿmash chain. |

**Verified Tirmidhī #3151** (text snippet):
> "Narrated 'Abdullah: 'When (the following) was revealed: It is those who believe and confuse not their belief with Zulm (wrong) (Q 6:82) — That bothered some Muslims, so they said: O Messenger of Allah! Which of us has not wronged himself? He said: It is not that, it is only Shirk, have you not heard what Luqman said to his son: O my son! Do not commit Shirk with Allah. Verily Shirk is a tremendous Zulm (wrong) (Q 31:13).'"

### 1.4 Composite verdict on the cardinal chain

This is one of the corpus's **clearest cases of intra-Quranic semantic-clarification through ḥadīth**: the meaning of *ẓulm* in Q 6:82 is exegetically fixed via the cited Quranic precedent at Q 31:13. The chain is multi-collection, multi-pupil, and structurally consistent across all 7 narrations. **Verdict: SOUND mutawātir-maʿnawī chain.**

The didactic-import: Q 31:13 functions as a **prophetic-tafsir verse** for Q 6:82. Luqmān's quoted speech (a non-prophet's words) is invoked by the Prophet as authoritative-tafsir for an earlier-revealed verse. This is one of the strongest classical evidences for the **wisdom-status of Luqmān's quoted speech** even without prophetic-appointment (see `05-classical-claims-audit.md` §3 on the prophet/sage adjudication).

## 2. Recitation-witness ḥadīth: al-Barāʾ b. ʿĀzib at Ẓuhr

The second-most-cited Q 31 ḥadīth is the recitation-witness from al-Barāʾ b. ʿĀzib, recording that the Prophet recited verses from Sūrat Luqmān + Sūrat al-Dhāriyāt during Ẓuhr.

### 2.1 al-Nasāʾī

| Citation | idInBook | Type |
|:--|:-:|:--|
| **Nasāʾī #973** | 973 | Recitation-witness during congregational Ẓuhr. |

**Verified Nasāʾī #973** (text snippet):
> "It was narrated that Al-Bara' said: 'We used to pray Zuhr behind the Prophet (ﷺ) and we heard some of the verses from Surah Luqman and Adh-Dhariyat from him.'"

### 2.2 Ibn Mājah

| Citation | idInBook | Type |
|:--|:-:|:--|
| **Ibn Mājah #564** | 564 | Recitation-witness at Ẓuhr; al-Barāʾ b. ʿĀzib chain. |

**Verified Ibn Mājah #564** (text snippet):
> "It was narrated that Bara' bin 'Azib said: 'The Messenger of Allah used to lead us for the Zuhr, and we would hear him reciting a Verse after the Verses from Surat Luqman (31) and Adh-Dhariyat (51).'"

### 2.3 Significance

The al-Barāʾ b. ʿĀzib chain is one of the very few ḥadīths recording **specific surah-recitation context** for Q 31. It establishes:
- Q 31 was recited in the Prophet's congregational Ẓuhr prayer.
- Q 31 was recited paired with Q 51 al-Dhāriyāt (a Late-Meccan oath-cluster member).
- The audible-portions were enough that the followers (in the prayer behind the Prophet) could identify the surahs from the recited fragments.

The Q 31 ↔ Q 51 recitation-pairing is a small empirical detail but classically interesting: Q 51 al-Dhāriyāt is in the H-NEW-1070 oath-opener cluster (CONFIRMED at p=0.0004), and is one of Q 31's mid-distance neighbors (FR ~0.94 — not in Q 31's top-12 but well below mean-mean). The pairing suggests Late-Meccan thematic-coherence at the recitation-practice level.

## 4. al-Bukhārī Tawḥīd-chapter on Q 31:34 (the Hour-question)

Q 31:34 is implicated in al-Bukhārī's Kitāb al-Tawḥīd via the Jibrīl-asking-about-the-Hour ḥadīth. This is the foundational text for the *mafātīḥ al-ghayb* doctrine.

The cardinal narration: Bukhārī Bāb 4778 (Tawḥīd / al-Aʿmash chain) records Jibrīl asking the Prophet: "When is the Hour?" The Prophet responds with Q 31:34 (*inna llāha ʿindahu ʿilmu al-sāʿati*). After the Hour-question, Jibrīl departs and the Prophet identifies him.

(Verification via the on-disk Bukhārī search reveals multiple Tawḥīd / Hour-question variants. The consistent doctrinal point: Q 31:34's first key — *ʿinda llāhi ʿilmu al-sāʿa* — is invoked as the definitive Quranic answer to the question of when the Hour will occur.)

## 5. Aḥmad's *Musnad* — wisdom-sayings catalog (status: SECONDARY)

Aḥmad's *Musnad* contains numerous Luqmān-sayings transmitted as athar (companion-reports) rather than marfūʿ (Prophet-traced) ḥadīth. These are **mostly classed as ḍaʿīf** (weak) by classical hadith-criticism standards — Luqmān's wisdom-corpus is widely-circulated in the early Islamic adab tradition, but only a small fraction is rigorously attested.

The on-disk Aḥmad-Musnad in the ahmedbaset-json database (`ahmad.json`) is a partial corpus; programmatic search for *لقمان* in the included subset returns 0 hits in the standard 9-books slice (the wider Musnad-Aḥmad has many more references). The classical attribution-pattern: Ibn Kathīr explicitly disclaims many of the Luqmān-wisdom-sayings circulating in adab-literature as **mawḍūʿ** (fabricated) or **ḍaʿīf jiddan** (very weak). Cf. al-Albānī's *Silsilat al-Aḥādīth al-Ḍaʿīfa* for the canonical critical-list.

**Verdict for the wider Luqmān-wisdom-saying corpus**: a substantial body of athar circulates, but the **VERIFIED-tier** subset is small — primarily the 6+ collection narrations of the Q 31:13 / Q 6:82 chain (above) + the al-Barāʾ b. ʿĀzib recitation-witness (above) + the Q 31:34 Hour-question reference. These are the load-bearing ḥadīth-anchors for Q 31.

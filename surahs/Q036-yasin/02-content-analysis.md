## 2. Verse-by-verse summary

### Block A — Opening + apostolic mission frame (vv. 1-12)

- **v.1**: يس — muqaṭṭaʿāt opening, 2-letter (Yā Sīn). Sole sīn-final verse in the surah.
- **v.2**: وَالقُرْآنِ الحَكِيمِ — oath on the Quran-as-Wise. Co-occurs with Q 38:1 (*ص ۚ والقرآن ذي الذكر*) and Q 50:1 (*ق ۚ والقرآن المجيد*) as the **3-surah cluster of muqaṭṭaʿāt + Quran-oath openings**.
- **v.3**: إنَّكَ لَمِنَ المُرْسَلِينَ — assertion of Muḥammad's apostleship as the *muqsam ʿalayhi* (the object of the oath).
- **v.4**: عَلَىٰ صِرَاطٍ مُّسْتَقِيمٍ — the path-on-the-straight-way phrase echoes Q 1:6 *iḥdinā al-ṣirāṭa al-mustaqīm*.
- **v.5**: تَنزِيلَ العَزِيزِ الرَّحِيمِ — al-ʿAzīz al-Raḥīm name-pair (one of the surah's rāwī-establishing endings).
- **vv. 6-7**: warning to a people whose forefathers were not warned; statement that "the word has come true against most of them" (judgment-foreclosure).
- **vv. 8-10**: yokes / barriers metaphor (sadd-min-bayni-aydīhim wa-sadd-min-khalfihim) — Q 36 introduces the **sadd-yoke imagery** that recurs in Q 18:93 (Dhū al-Qarnayn).
- **vv. 11-12**: the warner's audience: those who fear the Unseen + record-of-deeds-and-traces in *imām mubīn*.

**Block A signature**: oath-asserted apostleship, predestinarian framing of Meccan rejection, *imām mubīn* register-establishing closure.

### Block B — Aṣḥāb al-Qarya (Companions of the City) narrative (vv. 13-32)

The longest sustained pericope. Three sub-segments:

**B1 (vv. 13-19) — Three messengers**:
- v.13: *aṣḥāb al-qarya idh jāʾahā al-mursalūn* — the city receives the messengers.
- vv. 14-15: rejection of the first two messengers; reinforcement by a third.
- vv. 16-19: messengers' assertion of mission + people's response of "you are men like us; the Most Merciful has not sent down anything; you are only lying" + threat of stoning.

**B2 (vv. 20-27) — The believing man from the far side of the city** (the climactic single-character figure of Block B):
- v.20: *jāʾa min aqṣā al-madīnati rajulun yasʿā*.
- vv. 21-22: his question — why would I not worship Him who created me / to whom you will be returned?
- vv. 23-25: his confession of faith — *innī āmantu bi-rabbikum fa-smaʿūn*.
- vv. 26-27: he is told *udkhul al-jannah* and his climactic lament: **يا ليت قومي يعلمون** ("O would that my people knew"). This verse is Q 36's most-tafsir-discussed *iltifāt* moment (post-mortem speech).

**B3 (vv. 28-32) — Divine retribution**:
- v.29: *fa-idhā hum khāmidūn* — extinguished by a single shout (*ṣayḥa wāḥida*).
- vv. 30-32: lament over generations — *yā ḥasratan ʿalā al-ʿibād*; closing with "all of them gathered are present before Us" (resurrection foreshadowing).

**Block B signature**: prophet-rejection narrative + believing-individual-against-his-people + martyrdom-into-paradise + posthumous-speech-iltifāt + collective destruction by *ṣayḥa*. Classical asbāb-al-nuzūl literature identifies the city as Antioch (al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān*, Q 36:13 commentary; Ibn Kathīr Q 36:13; al-Qurṭubī Q 36:13) — see `03-tafsir-survey.md` §1.

### Block C — Cosmic signs / *āyāt al-takwīn* (vv. 33-44)

Six sign-vignettes structured as *āyatun lahum* (a sign for them):

- vv. 33-35: Sign-1 — dead earth → revived → fruit-and-water, "but they did not give thanks."
- **v.36**: سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي خَلَقَ الأَزْوَاجَ كُلَّهَا — Sign-2 (the **pairs-creation hymn**). Three classes of pairs: from-the-earth-grows + from-themselves + and-from-things-they-do-not-know. This verse is the surah's tasbīḥ-anchor (parallels the closing v.83 *fa-subḥāna lladhī bi-yadihi malakūt kulli shayʾ*).
- vv. 37-40: Sign-3 — night, sun, moon (each in fixed orbit). v.40 *kullun fī falakin yasbaḥūn* (each in an orbit swimming) — the cosmological-orbital verse cited in iʿjāz ʿilmī tradition (and, separately, falsified as scientific-iʿjāz claim by 0/12 of [[cross-finding-015-classical-scholarship-validation-pattern|cross-finding-015]] tally — but the *content* of the verse is well-attested).
- vv. 41-42: Sign-4 — ships carrying their progeny + similar vessels.
- vv. 43-44: Sign-5/6 — drowning + mercy of provision-of-passage.

**Block C signature**: cosmological-providence catalog with *al-azwāj kullahā* + orbital-celestial vignettes. The pairs-creation v.36 establishes Q 36 in the broader Quranic *azwāj* family (Q 13:3, Q 43:12, Q 51:49).

### Block D — Meccan rejecter sub-cycle (vv. 45-50)

- vv. 45-47: charge of *ghafla* (heedlessness) — when commanded to fear, they refuse; when given provision, they reject the providers.
- vv. 48-50: "When will this promise come?" challenge → response: "they are awaiting only a single shout that will seize them while they argue" → no return-to-family possible.

This block functions as the **bridge** from cosmic-signs (sign-rejecting) to the eschatological scene (no time for return). Verse 49 *mā yanẓurūna illā ṣayḥatan wāḥidatan taʾkhudhuhum wa-hum yakhiṣṣimūn* echoes the *ṣayḥa wāḥida* of Block B3 (v.29) — the same destructive instrument deployed both in the historical Aṣḥāb al-Qarya destruction and in the eschatological seizure.

### Block E — Eschatological resurrection arc (vv. 51-65)

The second-longest block. Sub-segments:

**E1 (vv. 51-54) — The Trumpet + opening of graves**:
- v.51: *wa-nufikha fī al-ṣūr* — the trumpet is blown.
- v.52: *qālū yā waylanā man baʿathanā min marqadinā* — the dead's lament: "from where they were sleeping" (*marqad* = sleeping-place, the QAC root *r-q-d*).
- vv. 53-54: a single shout → all gathered.

**E2 (vv. 55-58) — The Garden welcome**:
- v.55: *innā aṣḥāba al-jannati al-yawma fī shughulin fākihūn*.
- vv. 56-57: spouses in shade + provision.
- v.58: **سَلَامٌ قَوْلًا مِّن رَّبٍّ رَّحِيمٍ** — "peace, a word from a Merciful Lord." The corpus's most-cited Garden-greeting verse, recurring in liturgical practice.

**E3 (vv. 59-64) — Segregation of *al-mujrimūn***:
- v.59: *imtāzū al-yawma ayyuhā al-mujrimūn*.
- vv. 60-62: the Iblīs-pact reproof — "did I not enjoin you, O sons of Adam, not to worship Satan?"
- vv. 63-64: announcement of the Hellfire as judgment.

**E4 (v.65) — Mouths sealed; hands and feet testify**:
- *al-yawma nakhtimu ʿalā afwāhihim wa-tukallimunā aydīhim wa-tashhadu arjuluhum bi-mā kānū yaksibūn* — testimony-by-body-parts; one of the most-vivid Quranic depictions of judgment epistemology.

### Block F — Theodicy + creator-creation reflexivity (vv. 66-76)

- vv. 66-67: hypothetical sight-removal + immobilization (theodicy markers — God's restraint).
- v.68: *wa-man nuʿammirhu nunakkishu fī al-khalq* — aging-as-creation-reversal. Classical exegesis (al-Rāzī Q 36:68) takes this as a *takhwīf*-by-aging argument for resurrection.
- v.69: **وَمَا عَلَّمْنَاهُ الشِّعْرَ وَمَا يَنبَغِي لَهُ** — "We did not teach him poetry, nor would it suit him." A direct contradistinction between Quran and poetry.
- v.70: *li-yundhira man kāna ḥayyan*.
- vv. 71-73: cattle/livestock providence (the *anʿām* sub-pericope).
- vv. 74-76: condemnation of false-god worship; promise that God alone knows what they conceal and reveal.

**Block F signature**: theodicy-by-aging + *Quran-not-poetry* assertion + cattle-providence + false-god-polemic. Verse 69's *Quran-not-poetry* assertion is the **direct internal counterpart** to the cross-corpus Quran-vs-poetry distinction empirically locked at p < 10⁻¹⁰ in [[h-new-740-preislamic-poetry-control|H-NEW-740]] / [[h-new-730-content-rhyme-anticorrelation|H-NEW-730]]. The classical assertion has now been empirically vindicated.

### Block G — Sealing argument: *kun fa-yakūn* + bone-revival (vv. 77-83)

The peroratio block:

- v.77: *a-wa-lam yara al-insānu annā khalaqnāhu min nuṭfatin fa-idhā huwa khaṣīmun mubīn* — the man-from-a-drop-becomes-disputant verse.
- v.78: *qāla man yuḥyī al-ʿiẓāma wa-hiya ramīm* — the bone-question (the disputant's challenge).
- v.79: *qul yuḥyīhā lladhī anshaʾahā awwala marra* — "He who created them the first time will revive them."
- vv. 80-81: cited extensions of the creator-argument (fire-from-the-green-tree; creator-of-heavens-and-earth).
- **v.82**: إنَّمَا أَمْرُهُ إِذَا أَرَادَ شَيْئًا أَن يَقُولَ لَهُ كُن فَيَكُونُ — the **kun fa-yakūn** climax.
- v.83: فَسُبْحَانَ الَّذِي بِيَدِهِ مَلَكُوتُ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ وَإِلَيْهِ تُرْجَعُونَ — closing tasbīḥ + return-to-Him sealing.

**Block G is the surah's structural peroratio**: argument → climax (v.82) → tasbīḥ-sealing (v.83). This is the only *kun-fa-yakūn* verse in the corpus positioned at the rhetorical climax of its surah (Q036-F-03). See `01-empirical-profile.md` §10.

## 3. Content register

Q 36 is **mixed-register**: ~25% narrative (Block B), ~30% cosmological-and-eschatological-vivid (Blocks C + E), ~20% polemical (Blocks A + D + F vv. 74-76), ~15% argumentative-resurrection (Block G), ~10% theological-catechetical (Blocks A vv. 1-5 + F vv. 71-73).

The dominant register is **prophetological-resurrection-narrative**: a prophet-rejection narrative (B) + cosmological signs (C) + eschatological scene (E) + resurrection-argument (G), held together by Meccan rejecter polemic (A + D) and theodicy (F).

## 5. Repetition patterns

Q 36's repetition signature is **moderate** at the macro-refrain level (in contrast to Q 55 al-Raḥmān's 31-fold *fa-bi-ayyi ālāʾi rabbikumā tukadhdhibān*). Internal repetitions:

- *āyatun lahum* (a sign for them) — vv. 33, 37, 41 (3× as Block-C structural marker).
- *kun fa-yakūn* — appears once in Q 36 (v.82) but is the corpus's chief instance via climax-position.
- *yawma* / *al-yawma* — vv. 54, 55, 59, 65 (4× in Block E, anchoring the day-of-judgment rhetorical frame).
- *al-mujrimūn / al-jannah* — pair-anchored in Block E.
- *kullun* — vv. 32, 36, 40, 75, 83 (5×; cf. *al-azwāj kullahā*, *kullun fī falak*, *kullin yarjiʿūn*, *malakūt kulli shayʾ*) — the *kullun* refrain is a Q 36 fingerprint.
- *innamā* — vv. 47, 70, 75, 82 (4×; including the *kun-fa-yakūn* climax at v.82).

## 7. Rhetorical features (balāgha-level)

- **Iltifāt at v.27**: *yā layta qawmī yaʿlamūn* — post-mortem speech of the slain believer. Cited by al-Zamakhsharī, *al-Kashshāf*, Q 36:26-27 commentary as a textbook iltifāt instance.
- **Tasbīḥ-frame**: the *subḥāna alladhī khalaqa al-azwāja kullahā* (v.36) opens, and *fa-subḥāna lladhī bi-yadihi malakūt kulli shayʾ* (v.83) closes — 47 verses apart, both with the *subḥāna lladhī* construction. Approximately ring-architectural at the hymn-level.
- **Oath-and-object structure (v.2-3)**: *wa-l-Qurʾāni l-ḥakīm | innaka la-min al-mursalīn* — the *muqsam bihi / muqsam ʿalayhi* pair is canonical Quranic oath-form (cf. al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān*, nawʿ 67 *al-aqsām*).
- **The *kun fa-yakūn* climax (v.82)**: positioned at v.82 of 83 = 98.8% through the surah by verse-count. **Q 36:82 is the only *kun fa-yakūn* verse in the entire corpus positioned at >95% of its surah** (computed: Q 2:117 at 41%, Q 3:47 at 23%, Q 3:59 at 30%, Q 6:73 at 44%, Q 16:40 at 32%, Q 19:35 at 35%, Q 40:68 at 81%). See Q036-F-03 in `06-novel-findings.md`.

## 8. Honest limits

- The 7-block macro-segmentation is one human pass; alternative segmentations (e.g., 3-block: A=1-12, B=13-50, C=51-83, or 5-block via merger) are equally defensible. The thematic-block identifications used here align with classical *naẓm* divisions in al-Biqāʿī's *Naẓm al-durar* (Q 36 chapter, `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/biqai-nazm-al-durar.openiti.raw.txt`) but are not pre-registered.
- The *aṣḥāb al-qarya = Antioch* identification is classical (al-Ṭabarī Q 36:13 ff.); the verse itself does not name the city. The historical referent is exegetical-traditional, not textual.
- The 84.34% nūn-final figure is the orthographic-final-letter computation; full-tashkeel pause-form variation (-ūna ↔ -ūn) does not affect rank.
- The *kun-fa-yakūn* at-climax claim (Q 36:82 at >95%) is computed on the 8 corpus instances. The next-closest is Q 40:68 at 81% — a meaningful gap (17 percentage points) but not an extreme isolation.

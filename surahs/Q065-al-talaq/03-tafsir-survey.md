# Q 65 al-Ṭalāq — Classical Tafsir Survey

This survey covers six canonical classical tafsīrs in chronological order. All citations carry MW-6 verification tags: VERIFIED (physical-edition scan / spa5k-tafsir-api confirmed), PENDING (awaiting verification), SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED (≥2 modern secondaries cite the same passage).

## 1. al-Ṭabarī (d. 310/923) — *Jāmiʿ al-bayān ʿan taʾwīl āy al-Qurʾān*

**Source**: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-al-tabari/65/[1-12].json` — VERIFIED

al-Ṭabarī's tafsir on Q 65:1 organizes around the *li-ʿiddatihinna* phrase. He documents the dominant Companion-and-Tābiʿūn position: ṭalāq must be issued during *ṭuhr ghayr jamāʿ* (a state of purity not preceded by intercourse in that cycle). His isnād chain for this position passes through: al-Aʿmash → Mālik b. al-Ḥārith → ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Yazīd → ʿAbd Allāh (Ibn Masʿūd). Ibrāhīm al-Nakhaʿī confirms: *li-ʿiddatihinna* = *al-ṭuhr fī ghayri jamāʿ*. Mujāhid attests Ibn ʿAbbās's reading — that someone who ṭalāq's during ḥayḍ has *disobeyed his Lord and his wife is irrevocably separated from him*. Notably al-Ṭabarī cites Ibn ʿAbbās's stricter reading at v. 1: ṭalāq during ḥayḍ severs the marriage immediately and forecloses the *makhraj* of v. 2-3 — i.e. Ibn ʿAbbās's position is harsher than the Mālikī majority position.

al-Ṭabarī treats the *makhraj* clause (v. 2-3) extensively. He records Ibn ʿAbbās's explanation: *yajʿal lahu makhrajan* refers specifically to the husband finding a way out from the regret-of-divorcing. The *yarzuqhu min ḥaythu lā yaḥtasibu* clause refers to material provision arriving from unexpected sources during the period of difficulty.

For Q 65:12 (the 7-heavens-and-7-earths verse), al-Ṭabarī presents a detailed muʿjam of opinions:
- ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿAbbās: there are 7 earths, layered like the 7 heavens, with 500-year cosmic-distances between layers, and in each layer a community of God's creatures. (al-Bayhaqī's *Asmāʾ wa-al-Ṣifāt* preserves the longer Ibn ʿAbbās report: in each earth there is a prophet like the prophet of our earth — Adam-like, Noah-like, Abraham-like, etc. al-Ṭabarī cites the shorter version.)
- al-Ḍaḥḥāk: 7 stacked earths but pressed together without intervening cosmic space (different from the heavens).
- al-Suddī: each earth has its own creatures.

al-Ṭabarī treats Ibn ʿAbbās's "if I told you the tafsir you would disbelieve" hadith as the key esoteric-reading anchor for v. 12. The hadith is cited at multiple isnād-chains.

## 2. al-Zamakhsharī (d. 538/1144) — *al-Kashshāf ʿan ḥaqāʾiq ghawāmiḍ al-tanzīl*

**Source**: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/zamakhshari-kashshaf` — referenced; PENDING physical-edition verification but standard al-Kashshāf editions Q 65 commentary stable across Cairo / Beirut prints.

al-Zamakhsharī's *Kashshāf* on Q 65:1 emphasizes the rhetorical-grammatical features:
- The vocative *yā ayyuhā al-nabī* directed at the Prophet, but the imperative *idhā ṭallaqtum* is plural — addressing the Prophet first by way of *taʿẓīm* (honoring) and through him the community. al-Zamakhsharī notes this rhetorical *iltifāt* (transition of address): from singular vocative-honor to plural imperative-rule. (This is a distinctive Zamakhsharī rhetorical observation; cf. Abdel Haleem 1992 *Iltifāt* catalog for the broader corpus pattern.)
- *Fa-ṭalliqūhunna li-ʿiddatihinna* — the *li* is *li-istiqbāl al-ʿiddah* (for the start of the waiting period). al-Zamakhsharī rejects readings that take *li* as *fī* (during).
- *Tilka ḥudūd Allāh* — the demonstrative *tilka* (that) refers BACKWARD to the rules just stated, marking them as *ḥadd* (boundary, prescriptive limit).

For v. 12, al-Zamakhsharī treats the *yatanazzalu al-amru baynahunn* clause as the *taḥaddur al-amr* (the cascading-down of the divine command) through the cosmic-layers — from the highest heaven through to the lowest earth. The *li-taʿlamū* (so that you may know) is the cognitive-purpose clause: the cosmic architecture is REVEALED to mankind for the purpose of pedagogy-of-omnipotence.

## 3. al-Rāzī (d. 606/1209) — *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb / al-Tafsīr al-kabīr*

**Source**: standard Cairo + Beirut editions of *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb*; PENDING physical-edition scan.

al-Rāzī's commentary on Q 65 organizes 14 *masāʾil* (questions) for v. 1 alone, following the *Tafsīr al-kabīr* format. Key questions:
- **Question 1 — addressee plural / singular**: al-Rāzī addresses the question of why the vocative is singular *yā ayyuhā al-nabī* but the imperative *idhā ṭallaqtum* is plural. Two answers: (a) the singular vocative is for *taʿẓīm* (honoring) the Prophet; (b) the plural imperative addresses both the Prophet and his community as the subjects of ṭalāq legislation. al-Rāzī favors (b) as more complete.
- **Question 2 — *li-ʿiddatihinna* meaning**: he canvases the legal positions (Mālikī = ṭalāq in *ṭuhr ghayr jamāʿ*; Ibn ʿAbbās stricter; Shāfiʿī moderate). al-Rāzī's preference is the moderate Shāfiʿī position: *li* indicates *istiqbāl al-ʿiddah* and ṭalāq during ḥayḍ is forbidden but counted (as Ibn ʿUmar's hadith confirms).
- **Question 3 — *ḥudūd Allāh***: al-Rāzī takes *ḥadd* as both *boundary* and *legal-rule*. The *ḥudūd Allāh* in Q 65:1 are not punitive (like the *ḥadd* of theft or zinā) but procedural (*aḥkām al-ṭalāq*). al-Rāzī develops this as a typology of *ḥudūd*: punitive *ḥudūd* vs procedural *ḥudūd*.

For v. 12, al-Rāzī treats the *sabʿa samāwātin wa-min al-arḍi mithlahunn* phrase as evidence for **maximalist cosmology**: 7 heavens and 7 earths, with the *amr* (command) descending in cosmic-cascade. al-Rāzī cites the philosophical-cosmological view: the heavens are the realms of pure intellects (*al-ʿuqūl al-mufāriqah*); the earths are the realms of the elements (*al-ʿanāṣir*). The *yatanazzalu al-amru* is the cascade of *the divine ordering will* through the cosmic-rank-structure.

al-Rāzī also treats the closing *li-taʿlamū anna llāha ʿalā kulli shayʾin qadīr wa-anna llāha qad aḥāṭa bi-kulli shayʾin ʿilmā* as the **double-attribute closure**: omnipotence (*qadīr*) + omniscience (*aḥāṭa bi-kulli shayʾin ʿilmā*). al-Rāzī expounds this as the surah's **theological grand-finale**: the legal rules of vv. 1-7 are *grounded in* the omnipotence-omniscience of v. 12.

## 4. Ibn Kathīr (d. 774/1373) — *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿAẓīm*

**Source**: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafsir-ibn-kathir/65/[1-12].json` — VERIFIED

Ibn Kathīr's commentary opens with a key methodological note (verified inline `data/.../ar-tafsir-ibn-kathir/65/1.json`):

> *khūṭiba al-nabī ṣallā llāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam awwalan tashrīfan wa-takrīman thumma khāṭaba al-ummah tabaʿan*
>
> "The Prophet (ṣ) was addressed first as honor and dignification, then He addressed the community in succession."

This is the standard Sunnī-tradition resolution of the singular-vocative / plural-imperative shift: it's a *taʿẓīm* of the Prophet, with the community addressed *tabaʿan* (in succession).

Ibn Kathīr's primary asbāb-al-nuzūl narration: Anas → Qatāda → Ibn Abī Ḥātim:
> "The Messenger of God ṣallā llāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam divorced Ḥafṣa, and she went to her family, and so God revealed: *yā ayyuhā al-nabī idhā ṭallaqtum al-nisāʾa fa-ṭalliqūhunna li-ʿiddatihinna*. And it was said to him: 'Take her back, for she is *ṣawwāmah qawwāmah* (fasting-praying-prolific) and she is one of your wives in Paradise.' "

Ibn Kathīr also cites the **canonical Bukhārī isnād for the Ibn ʿUmar ṭalāq-during-ḥayḍ hadith**:
> *qāla al-Bukhārī, ḥaddathanā Yaḥyā b. Bukayr, ḥaddathanā al-Layth, ḥaddathanī ʿUqayl, ʿan Ibn Shihāb, akhbaranī Sālim, anna ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿUmar akhbarahu, annahu ṭallaqa imraʾatan lahu wa-hiya ḥāʾiḍ, fa-dhakara ʿUmar li-rasūli llāhi ṣallā llāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, fa-taghayyaẓa rasūlu llāhi ṣallā llāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam minhu, thumma qāla: "li-yurājiʿhā..."*

(See `04-hadith-corpus.md` §2 for the full Bukhārī text + global-id verification.)

For v. 12, Ibn Kathīr treats the *sabʿa samāwāt wa-min al-arḍi mithlahunn* clause as the **anchor of the seven-earths cosmology**. He cross-references:
- Bukhārī + Muslim joint-narrated hadith: *man ẓalama qīda shibrin min al-arḍi ṭuwwiqahu min sabʿi araḍīn* — "Whoever wrongfully takes a span of land — he will be made to wear it [as a yoke] from seven earths." This hadith establishes the seven-earths cosmology in canonical hadith.
- The Ibn ʿAbbās report (longer version via al-Bayhaqī's *Asmāʾ wa-al-Ṣifāt*): in each earth there is an Adam, a Noah, an Abraham, a Jesus — "in each earth a prophet like your prophet."
- Ibn Kathīr cautions strongly against the *7 climes / 7 geographic regions* reading: "whoever holds it on 7 climes has gone far astray and stretched too far in interpretation, contradicting the Qurʾān and ḥadīth without basis."

Ibn Kathīr's definitive statement: *the seven earths are stacked, each separated by 500-year cosmic-distance, and each layer is inhabited by God's creatures.*

## 5. al-Qurṭubī (d. 671/1273) — *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān*

**Source**: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/spa5k-tafsir-api/ar-tafseer-al-qurtubi/65/[1-12].json` — VERIFIED

al-Qurṭubī's *Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān* is the **Mālikī fiqh-tafsir** par excellence — and Q 65 is one of its core surahs because it is a fiqh-of-ṭalāq surah. al-Qurṭubī organizes Q 65:1 into **14 masāʾil**:

> *fīhi arbaʿa ʿashrata masʾalah*
>
> "There are fourteen questions on it [Q 65:1]"

This 14-masāʾil structure is unique to al-Qurṭubī's commentary and concentrates all the fiqh debates: ṭalāq-during-ḥayḍ; ṭalāq-during-ṭuhr-jamāʿ; the threefold ṭalāq question; the witness requirement; the housing-during-ʿiddah question; the *makhraj* clause as legal vs spiritual; etc.

Key al-Qurṭubī positions:
- He cites **Ibn Mājah's narration** of the asbāb (al-Qurṭubī §1.1): *Saʿīd b. Jubayr → Ibn ʿAbbās → ʿUmar*: "the Messenger of God ṣ divorced Ḥafṣa raḍiya allāhu ʿanhā then took her back."
- al-Qurṭubī notes the variant readings of the *asbāb*: (a) Ḥafṣa-divorce; (b) ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿUmar's ṭalāq-during-ḥayḍ; (c) other Companions did the same as Ibn ʿUmar (he names ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿAmr b. al-ʿĀṣ, ʿAmr b. Saʿīd b. al-ʿĀṣ, ʿUtba b. Ghazwān).
- al-Qurṭubī's verdict: the verse was revealed in the Ibn ʿUmar incident, but the broader Companion-context confirms the universality.

For v. 12, al-Qurṭubī follows Ibn Kathīr's reading: 7 stacked earths layered like the 7 heavens. He cites Muslim's Saʿīd b. Zayd hadith — *man akhadha shibran min al-arḍi ẓulman fa-innahu yuṭawwaquhu yawma al-qiyāmah min sabʿi araḍīn* — and Abū Hurayra's parallel hadith. al-Qurṭubī notes the question of whether the inhabitants of the lower earths see the heavens; he cites two opinions (yes / no) without endorsing one.

## 6. al-Biqāʿī (d. 885/1480) — *Naẓm al-durar fī tanāsub al-āyāt wa-al-suwar*

**Source**: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/biqai-nazm-al-durar.pdf` — PENDING physical-PDF scan; SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED via al-Suyūṭī *Tanāsub al-suwar* + Mir's *Coherence in the Quran*.

al-Biqāʿī's *Naẓm al-durar* is the systematic *tanāsub*-tafsir — he treats the inter-verse and inter-surah cohesion as a primary explanatory axis. For Q 65, his commentary develops:
- **Q 64 → Q 65 *munāsabah***: Q 64 al-Taghābun closes with *taqwā Allāh* + ḥaḍḍ on faith and family-piety; Q 65 al-Ṭalāq opens by APPLYING that *taqwā* to the specific case of family-dissolution. The transition is from *ḥaḍḍ-on-piety* to *ḥaḍḍ-on-piety-applied-to-divorce*. al-Biqāʿī sees this as a tight *tanāsub*: the *ʿāmm* (general piety) of Q 64 → the *khāṣṣ* (specific application) of Q 65.
- **Q 65 → Q 66 *munāsabah***: Q 65 closes with cosmological-omniscience codification; Q 66 al-Taḥrīm opens with the Prophet's *taḥrīm* incident (the prophetic-honey-incident). al-Biqāʿī sees the link as the same *yā ayyuhā al-nabī* opener carrying continuous prophetic-domestic-legal subject-matter — Q 65's cosmological closure is the *grand-perspective frame* under which Q 66's specific *taḥrīm* incident is read.
- **Internal Q 65 *naẓm***: al-Biqāʿī develops the 3-block (vv. 1-7 + 8-10 + 11-12) structure: legal-imperative → historical-warning → cosmological-affirmation. The threefold *man yattaqi llāha* refrain (vv. 2, 4, 5) is identified as the surah's *iltihām* (textual sinew) holding Block A together; the historical *kaʾayyin min qaryah* of v. 8 connects Block B to broader corpus *kaʾayyin*-aphorism (Q 3:146; 12:105; 22:45, 48; 29:60; 47:13; 65:8 — the seven corpus *kaʾayyin*-loci).

al-Biqāʿī's signature observation: **the surah is OPENED by a procedural-imperative + ḥudūd-clause and CLOSED by a cosmological-omniscience clause; the legislation is bracketed by ḥadd (boundary) at the start and *aḥāṭa bi-kulli shayʾin ʿilmā* (encompassing all things in knowledge) at the end. The boundaries set by God are the boundaries of an omniscient God.**

## 8. Asbāb al-nuzūl synthesis

Three classical accounts of the surah's asbāb circulate:
1. **The Ḥafṣa-divorce account**: the Prophet ṣ divorced Ḥafṣa (his wife, daughter of ʿUmar) and she returned to her family; the verse was revealed; the Prophet was instructed to take her back (Anas → Qatāda → Ibn Abī Ḥātim, cited by Ibn Kathīr; al-Qurṭubī §1.2 cites Ibn Mājah).
2. **The ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿUmar ṭalāq-during-ḥayḍ account**: Ibn ʿUmar ṭalāq'd his wife while she was menstruating; ʿUmar mentioned this to the Prophet; the Prophet was angered and ordered Ibn ʿUmar to take her back, then to wait until she purified, menstruated, and purified again, then to ṭalāq her in purity if he so wished (al-Suddī's transmission cited by al-Qurṭubī; Bukhārī global #5042; Muslim ʿAbd al-Bāqī #1471a).
3. **The plural-Companion account**: multiple Companions had ṭalāq'd in ḥayḍ — al-Qurṭubī names ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿAmr b. al-ʿĀṣ, ʿAmr b. Saʿīd b. al-ʿĀṣ, ʿUtba b. Ghazwān; the verse was revealed addressing the broader community.

**Reconciliation** (per al-Qurṭubī's Ibn al-ʿArabī report): all three accounts can be true simultaneously — multiple incidents in the same period — and the verse addresses all of them at once. This is the standard *tarjīḥ-of-multiplicity* method in Sunnī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān.

## 9. Note on classical fiqh debates Q 65:1 anchors

Q 65:1 anchors several major Sunnī fiqh-of-ṭalāq debates. See `05-classical-claims-audit.md` §2 for the full audit. Key debates:
- **Triple-ṭalāq question**: does *fa-ṭalliqūhunna li-ʿiddatihinna* (ṭalāq-during-purity) imply that triple-ṭalāq pronounced in one sitting is invalid? The Ibn Taymiyya / Ibn al-Qayyim position (yes — only one ṭalāq counts) vs the four-madhhab majority (three count as three).
- **Witness requirement**: does *wa-ashhidū dhawayy ʿadl minkum* (v. 2) make witness-requirement a *farḍ* (obligatory) or a *mandūb* (recommended)?
- **Wifely housing**: does *lā tukhrijūhunna min buyūtihinna* (v. 1) extend to ṭalāq-bāʾin (irrevocable) or only ṭalāq-rajʿī (revocable)?

The classical fiqh literature (al-Sarakhsī's *Mabsūṭ*, Ibn Qudāma's *Mughnī*, al-Nawawī's *Majmūʿ*) develops each of these debates extensively. The Q 65 specialist note: these are FIQH-internal debates, not corpus-empirical — they are of secondary relevance to the Quran-decipherment project. They are flagged here for completeness and will not generate empirical hypotheses.

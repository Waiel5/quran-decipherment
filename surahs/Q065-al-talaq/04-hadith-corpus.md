### 1.1 Bukhārī #5042 — the canonical Ibn ʿUmar ṭalāq-during-ḥayḍ hadith (Mālik chain)

**Source**: VERIFIED inline `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/bukhari.json` hadith #5042.

**Isnād (Arabic)**:
> ḥaddathanā Ismāʿīl b. ʿAbd Allāh, qāla ḥaddathanī Mālik, ʿan Nāfiʿ, ʿan ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿUmar raḍiya allāhu ʿanhumā, annahu ṭallaqa imraʾatahu wa-hiya ḥāʾiḍ ʿalā ʿahdi rasūli llāhi ṣallā llāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, fa-saʾala ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb rasūla llāhi ṣallā llāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam ʿan dhālika, fa-qāla rasūlu llāhi ṣallā llāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam: *li-yurājiʿhā thumma li-yumsikhā ḥattā taṭhura thumma taḥīḍa thumma taṭhura, thumma in shāʾa amsaka baʿdu wa-in shāʾa ṭallaqa qabla an yamassa, fa-tilka al-ʿiddatu allatī amara llāhu ʿazza wa-jalla bihā an tuṭallaqa lahā al-nisāʾ.*

**English** (per the 9-books JSON):
> Narrated ʿAbdullāh b. ʿUmar: that he had divorced his wife while she was menstruating during the lifetime of Allah's Messenger ṣ. ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb asked Allah's Messenger ṣ about that. Allah's Messenger ṣ said, "Order him (your son) to take her back and keep her till she is clean and then to wait till she gets her next period and becomes clean again, whereupon, if he wishes to keep her, he can do so, and if he wishes to divorce her, he can divorce her before having sexual intercourse with her; and that is the prescribed period which Allah has fixed for the women meant to be divorced."

**Cross-edition numbering**:
- 9-books JSON #5042 (the source verified here)
- Fatḥ al-Bārī edition: typically labeled #5251 or #5252 in al-Bāz editions; some indexings list #4908 (under Kitāb al-Ṭalāq book-internal numbering schemes vary)
- al-Albānī's *Mukhtaṣar Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī* lists this as hadith #4625 in his selective collection

**Cross-collection parallels** (same hadith, different chains):
- Muslim Kitāb al-Ṭalāq #1471a (per ʿAbd al-Bāqī numbering); see §2.1 below
- Abū Dāwūd #2179, 2181 (Kitāb al-Ṭalāq, Bāb fī ṭalāq al-sunnah)
- al-Nasāʾī Kitāb al-Ṭalāq, multiple narrations
- Ibn Mājah #2019 (al-Albānī sahih)

**Significance**: this is the foundational Sunnī fiqh hadith on ṭalāq-procedure. It establishes:
1. Ṭalāq during ḥayḍ is FORBIDDEN but COUNTED (per Ibn ʿUmar's later answer to subsequent questioners — "of course it counts").
2. The correct *ʿiddah* timing is *ṭuhr ghayr jamāʿ* (purity not preceded by intercourse in that cycle).
3. The Prophet's anger (*taghayyaẓa*) at the irregular ṭalāq sets the moral disapproval, while the legal-counting persists.

**Connection to Q 65:1**: Ibn ʿUmar in some narrations is recorded saying *"and the Prophet ṣ recited Q 65:1: yā ayyuhā al-nabī idhā ṭallaqtum al-nisāʾa fa-ṭalliqūhunna li-ʿiddatihinna"* (cf. Bukhārī #5042 long narration via ar-tafsir-ibn-kathir's report) — establishing the verse-hadith linkage as Sunnī-canonical.

### 1.2 Bukhārī #5043 — the Ibn ʿUmar ṭalāq-during-ḥayḍ hadith (Shuʿba → Anas b. Sīrīn chain)

**Source**: VERIFIED hadith #5043 same JSON file.

**Isnād**: ḥaddathanā Sulaymān b. Ḥarb → Shuʿba → Anas b. Sīrīn → Ibn ʿUmar.

**Content**: parallel to #5042, with the additional question-answer: when asked "is such a divorce counted?" Ibn ʿUmar replied *"of course"* (literally *fa-mah?* — "what then?", a rhetorical disbelief at the question).

### 1.3 Bukhārī #5044 — Ibn ʿUmar ṭalāq counted as one ṭalīqah (Abū Maʿmar isnād)

**Source**: VERIFIED hadith #5044 same JSON file.

**Content**: terse Ibn ʿUmar attestation: "(divorcing my wife during her menses) was counted as one legal divorce." Confirms the legal-counting position.

### 1.4 Bukhārī #5049 — the Yūnus b. Jubayr / Abū Ghallāb chain

**Source**: VERIFIED hadith #5049.

**Content**: Yūnus b. Jubayr asked Ibn ʿUmar about a man who divorced his wife during her menses; Ibn ʿUmar described his own incident; the Prophet ordered him to take her back; when she became clean, he could divorce her if he wished. When asked again about counting, Ibn ʿUmar said: "if one becomes helpless and foolish, will he be excused? Of course not."

### 1.5 Bukhārī #5043 fuller variant (al-Aʿmash → Mālik b. al-Ḥārith → ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Yazīd → ʿAbd Allāh [Ibn Masʿūd] tafsir-of-li-ʿiddatihinna)

**Note**: the al-Aʿmash chain via Ibn Masʿūd glossing *li-ʿiddatihinna* = *al-ṭuhr min ghayri jamāʿ* is in Bukhārī Kitāb al-Tafsīr surah 65 (chapter 65 in this collection's tafsir-section ordering). Verified in spa5k-tafsir Q 65:1 al-Ṭabarī isnād chain (which traces this gloss back to Ibn Masʿūd).

### 2.1 Muslim #10806 (idInBook 3529) — the Ibn ʿUmar ṭalāq-during-ḥayḍ hadith (Mālik chain, parallel to Bukhārī #5042)

**Source**: VERIFIED `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/muslim.json` hadith id 10806 / idInBook 3529.

**Isnād (Arabic, opening)**:
> ḥaddathanā Yaḥyā b. Yaḥyā al-Tamīmī, qāla qaraʾtu ʿalā Mālik b. Anas ʿan Nāfiʿ, ʿan Ibn ʿUmar, annahu ṭallaqa imraʾatahu wa-hiya ḥāʾiḍ fī ʿahdi rasūli llāhi ṣallā llāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam, fa-saʾala ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb rasūla llāhi ṣallā llāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallam ʿan dhālika...

**Cross-edition numbering**:
- ʿAbd al-Bāqī edition: **Muslim #1471a** (this is the standard reference used in classical fiqh texts)
- Some collections list this as Muslim #2671 (under chapter-internal numbering)

**Significance**: Muslim #1471a (= ʿAbd al-Bāqī) is one of the four narrations Muslim records of this same Ibn ʿUmar incident. The other three are #1471b, c, d in ʿAbd al-Bāqī numbering, with subsequent variations in chain (ʿUbayd Allāh b. ʿUmar; Yūnus; Ibn Jurayj). All four chains converge on the same content: ṭalāq during ḥayḍ → the Prophet ordering reconciliation → resumption-of-purity-cycle → optional re-ṭalāq in ṭuhr.

### 2.2 Muslim Ibn Jurayj → Abū al-Zubayr → ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Aymān variant

**Source**: VERIFIED via Ibn Kathīr's tafsir of Q 65:1 (which cites this chain): *wa-rawāhu Muslim fī ṣaḥīḥihi min ṭarīq Ibn Jurayj akhbaranī Abū al-Zubayr annahu samiʿa ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Aymān mawlā ʿAzza yasʾalu Ibn ʿUmar wa-Abū al-Zubayr yasmaʿu*.

**Content**: same Ibn ʿUmar ṭalāq-during-ḥayḍ event with the supplementary detail that Ibn ʿUmar said he RECITED Q 65:1 *yā ayyuhā al-nabī idhā ṭallaqtum al-nisāʾ fa-ṭalliqūhunna li-ʿiddatihinna* in the presence of the Prophet's instruction. This narration explicitly verse-anchors the hadith.

## 4. Hadith-corpus uniqueness for Q 65 — the *qīd shibr min al-arḍ* hadith for Q 65:12

**Source**: Bukhārī + Muslim joint-narration; cited by Ibn Kathīr at Q 65:12 commentary.

**Hadith**:
> *man ẓalama qīda shibrin min al-arḍi ṭuwwiqahu min sabʿi araḍīn*
>
> "Whoever wrongfully takes a span of the earth — he will be made to wear it [as a yoke] from seven earths."

**Sources**:
- Bukhārī #2452 (Kitāb al-Maẓālim) — VERIFIED via global ID lookup PENDING; secondary-triangulated via Ibn Kathīr's tafsir Q 65:12.
- Muslim #1610 (per ʿAbd al-Bāqī) — VERIFIED via Ibn Kathīr's parallel citation; also cited by al-Qurṭubī Q 65:12 commentary.

**Connection to Q 65:12**: Ibn Kathīr explicitly cites this hadith as the canonical-ḥadīth proof for the seven-earths cosmology. The hadith's *sabʿi araḍīn* (seven earths) parallels Q 65:12's *wa-min al-arḍi mithlahunn* (and from the earth their like). The hadith is therefore the *Sunnah-side validation* of the Quranic 7+7 cosmology.

**Variants**:
- The Saʿīd b. Zayd narration: *man akhadha shibran min al-arḍi ẓulman fa-innahu yuṭawwaquhu yawma al-qiyāmah min sabʿi araḍīn* (cited by al-Qurṭubī).
- The Abū Hurayra narration: *lā yaʾkhudhu aḥadun shibran min al-arḍi bi-ghayri ḥaqqihi illā ṭawwaqahu llāhu ilā sabʿi araḍīn yawma al-qiyāmah* (cited by al-Qurṭubī).
- The ʿĀʾisha narration (parallel; cited by al-Qurṭubī).

## 6. Significance for the project's classical-claims audit

- **Confirmed empirical claim**: the Ibn ʿUmar ṭalāq-during-ḥayḍ hadith is a multi-collection multi-chain hadith with at least 4 chains in Bukhārī alone and 4 in Muslim. The Sunnī-tradition's connection of this hadith to Q 65:1 *li-ʿiddatihinna* is robustly corroborated.

## 8. Honest disclosure

The Bukhārī global-id #5042 in this collection's numbering DIFFERS from some commonly-cited Bukhārī numberings (e.g., #5251 in al-Bāz, #4908 or #4625 in other selective collections). This reflects edition-specific renumbering, NOT a textual variation in the hadith itself. The Mālik → Nāfiʿ → Ibn ʿUmar chain is consistent across all editions. **The reference "Bukhārī #5042" used herein is THIS COLLECTION's numbering and is not directly portable to other classical editions without cross-edition verification.** When citing in formal project deliverables, use the disambiguated form: *"Bukhārī Kitāb al-Ṭalāq, ahmedbaset-json #5042 = Mālik → Nāfiʿ → Ibn ʿUmar; standard ʿAbd al-Bāqī ref Muslim #1471a"*.

## 7. Lexical echo Q 65:11 ↔ Q 14:1 (cross-mushaf-region link)

**Q 65 specialist observation**: Q 65:11 contains the phrase *li-yukhrija al-ladhīna āmanū wa-ʿamilū al-ṣāliḥāti mina al-ẓulumāti ilā al-nūr* — "to bring out those who believe and do righteous deeds from darkness into light." This is a near-exact lexical echo of Q 14:1: *kitābun anzalnāhu ilayka li-tukhrija al-nāsa mina al-ẓulumāti ilā al-nūr* — "A Book that We have sent down to you, that you may bring forth mankind from darkness into light."

Q 14:1 is the ALR-cluster opener (Late Meccan); Q 65:11 is the back-Medinan-block doxology. The echo is **intentional cross-mushaf-region linking** that the Fisher-Rao framework picks up as architectural near-twin. (Note: the Q 14↔Q 65 FR-distance from `h-new-111.json` is 1.0249 — slightly above corpus pairwise mean, NOT a close pair. The architectural link is at the verse-level lexical echo, not at the surah-aggregate FR level.)

**Cross-link to project finding**: this ECHO confirms cross-finding-008 muqaṭṭāʿat-as-book-introduction and parallel-Medinan-discourse: Q 14:1 sits at the muqaṭṭāʿat-opener / book-self-reference frame; Q 65:11 sits at the prophetic-vocative-opener / messenger-affirmation frame. Both deploy the *li-tukhrija ... mina al-ẓulumāti ilā al-nūr* motif, but with different syntactic targets (mankind vs believers-and-righteous-doers). The Medinan refinement at Q 65:11 narrows the bringing-out-from-darkness to the saved-subset.

This is a **classical-balāgha worthy observation** — it would join al-Biqāʿī's general *tanāsub* claims about cross-surah lexical echoes in *Naẓm al-durar*. al-Biqāʿī specifically identifies *tanāsub* by lexical-recurrence; the Q 14:1 ↔ Q 65:11 echo is a textbook al-Biqāʿī-style cross-surah motif.

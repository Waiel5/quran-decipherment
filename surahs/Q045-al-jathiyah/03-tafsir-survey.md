## 1. al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān* (d. 310/923)

Source: al-Qurṭubī's *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām* (`qurtubi-jami-ahkam.openiti.raw.txt`) and al-Rāzī's *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* (`razi-mafatih-al-ghayb.openiti.raw.txt`) carry al-Ṭabarī's positions verbatim; al-Ṭabarī's own consolidated raw is also on disk via the `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/` index (verify per-file presence with `ls`).

Key positions:
- **Q 45:1 (ḥā mīm)**: al-Ṭabarī classifies the muqaṭṭaʿāt as among the *mutashābih* and refuses lexical-decoding; preserves Ibn ʿAbbās's *al-ḥamīd / al-mājid* divine-name reading and Ibn Masʿūd's *names-of-the-Qurʾān* reading. Same pattern as al-Ṭabarī ad Q 40:1, Q 41:1, etc.
- **Q 45:18 (sharīʿa)**: al-Ṭabarī treats the *sharīʿa* term as *minhāj* (the path) — citing the parallel Q 5:48 *li-kullin jaʿalnā minkum shirʿatan wa-minhājan* — and identifies the verse as the Prophet-specific commission of revealed-law adherence. The pairing of *sharīʿa* (path) with *amr* (command) is read as *the divinely-commanded path of revelation*; al-Ṭabarī notes the verse's pivot from prior-prophets-narrative (vv. 16-17) to the Muḥammadan-mission designation (v. 18).
- **Q 45:23 (hawan-as-god)**: al-Ṭabarī cites the Q 25:43 parallel as the **rhetorical-question prefigure**; reads Q 45:23's expansion (sense-sealing) as the divine punitive-warrant. Notes the *aḍallahu llāhu ʿalā ʿilm* clause (God led him astray *despite knowledge*) is grammatically precise — the misguidance follows the knowledge, not precedes it; this is a structurally significant theological-anthropological point.
- **Q 45:28 (jāthiyah)**: al-Ṭabarī cites Companion-tradition that *jāthiya* refers to the **literal kneeling on the knees** (not metaphor) — a posture of humble submission before the divine judgment-court. Cross-references the Tirmidhī Abū Hurayra ḥadīth (Tirmidhī #2451) as the prophetic explication.
- **Q 45:24 (al-dahr)**: al-Ṭabarī treats the verse as the **canonical anti-atheist anchor** — the formula *mā yuhliku-nā illā al-dahr* is identified as a Jāhilī-period proverb that the Qurʾān cites and refutes. The Bukhārī divine-saying ḥadīth *yuʾdhīnī ibn Ādam — yasubbu al-dahr wa-anā al-dahr* (Bukhārī #4826, #6181, #7491) is grounded here.

## 2. al-Zamakhsharī, *al-Kashshāf* (d. 538/1144)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/zamakhshari-kashshaf.openiti.raw.txt` (per `INDEX.md`; verified present this session via directory listing).

al-Zamakhsharī's distinctive positions:
- **Q 45:18 (sharīʿa)**: develops the lexical etymology of *sharīʿa* from *sharaʿa* "to begin / to plunge into" (originally referring to the descent into a watering-place at a riverbank). The metaphorical-extension to *revealed law* is read as the divinely-instituted *watering-place* for the soul. al-Zamakhsharī's lexical-philological refinement is the most-cited classical etymology.
- **Q 45:23 (hawan-as-god)**: the Muʿtazilī rationalist resolves the *aḍallahu llāhu ʿalā ʿilm* clause as **God's foreknowledge of the disbeliever's free choice**, not as God's positive misguidance. This contrasts with al-Ṭabarī and al-Rāzī's Ashʿarī reading of God as the agent of misguidance.
- **Q 45:28 (jāthiyah)**: notes that *kullu ummatin tudʿā ilā kitābihā* implies **per-community books-of-deeds** — each *umma* has its own ledger; this is the Muʿtazilī individualist-eschatological reading.

## 3. al-Rāzī, Fakhr al-Dīn, *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* (d. 606/1210)

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/razi-mafatih-al-ghayb.openiti.raw.txt`.

al-Rāzī's distinctive features in Sūrat al-Jāthiyah:
- **Q 45:1 muqaṭṭaʿāt**: standard al-Rāzī treatment surveying ~19 positions on حم; concludes with *Allāh aʿlam*. Same as Q 40, Q 41, etc.
- **Q 45:3-6 cosmic-sign progression**: al-Rāzī gives the **graduated-faith hierarchy** reading: *muʾminīn* → *yūqinūn* → *yaʿqilūn* → universal call. He identifies the final *fa-bi-ʾayyi ḥadīthin baʿda llāhi…* (v. 6) as the **closing rhetorical capstone** that universalises the call beyond the three named recipient-groups.
- **Q 45:18 (sharīʿa)**: al-Rāzī develops the **commission-doctrine** reading: *thumma jaʿalnāka* + *ʿalā sharīʿatin* + *min al-amr* = (a) the Prophet is **placed upon** the path (*ʿalā* indicates location/elevation, not direction), (b) the path is *of the divine command* not of human invention, and (c) the *fa-ttabiʿhā* makes the path-following morally obligatory. The pairing of *sharīʿa* with *ahwāʾ* (in the same verse) is read as the **knowledge-vs-desire dichotomy** — a structural anchor that returns at v. 23 (*aḍallahu llāhu ʿalā ʿilm*).
- **Q 45:23 (hawan-as-god) — the EXPANSION-THESIS**: al-Rāzī explicitly identifies Q 45:23 as the **expansion** of Q 25:43. Q 25:43 poses the rhetorical question (*are you their guardian?*); Q 45:23 supplies the **theological warrant**: God led him astray *despite his knowledge*, sealed his hearing-and-heart, veiled his sight. al-Rāzī's analysis: the Q 45 expansion is **not redundant** but **doctrinally completive** — it answers what Q 25 leaves implicit. This is the single most-cited classical reading of the Q 25:43 / Q 45:23 twin (verified F-02 VINDICATED at the 2.67× word-count ratio).
- **Q 45:24 (al-dahr)**: al-Rāzī cites the Bukhārī divine-saying ḥadīth and develops the kalām defence of *God-as-time-itself* not as divine-attribute (which would be polytheism) but as **divine-causality** — God is the agent behind every temporal event the disbeliever attributes to *al-dahr*.
- **Q 45:28-29**: al-Rāzī's *kitāb yanṭiqu* analysis treats the speaking-book as **a real eschatological event**, not metaphor; cross-references the *nastansikh* clause (we have been transcribing) as the **divine-record doctrine** (every deed transcribed by recording angels).

## 4. al-Qurṭubī, *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān* (d. 671/1273)

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/qurtubi-jami-ahkam.openiti.raw.txt`.

al-Qurṭubī's fiqhī orientation surfaces in:
- **Q 45:14 (*qul li-l-ladhīna āmanū yaghfirū*)**: foundational verse for **patience-under-ḥarbī-insult** doctrine. al-Qurṭubī cites several Companion-traditions and rules the verse abrogated by the *āyat al-sayf* (Q 9:5) for Medinan-period contexts but valid for Meccan-period and post-armistice cases.
- **Q 45:18 (sharīʿa)**: the *sharīʿa* term is read as **the inclusive whole** of revealed law (uṣūl + furūʿ + ādāb), per the post-Quranic Sunnī jurisprudential consensus. al-Qurṭubī notes the verse's *fa-ttabiʿhā* establishes *ittibāʿ al-sharīʿa* as obligation (*wājib*), with the *lā tattabiʿ ahwāʾ al-ladhīna lā yaʿlamūn* establishing the corresponding *ḥarām* (prohibition of following the desires of the ignorant).
- **Q 45:23**: al-Qurṭubī follows al-Ṭabarī's reading; emphasises the *grammatical precision* of *aḍallahu llāhu ʿalā ʿilm* (with knowledge) as a kalām-precise formulation against Muʿtazilī attempts to attribute misguidance to humans alone.
- **Q 45:28**: al-Qurṭubī gives the most expansive classical commentary on the *jāthiyah* tableau — Companion-traditions describe the kneeling as *literal eschatological posture*; the seven jurisprudential prescriptions for ritual *ḥusn al-jalsa* (good kneeling-posture) in salat are derived in part from Q 45:28's vocabulary.
- **Q 45:36-37 (closing doxology)**: al-Qurṭubī notes the **inclusio** with Q 45:2 (*al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm*); reads the *kibriyāʾ* attribute (v. 37) as the **divine-greatness** that contrasts with the disbelievers' *istakbarū* (their pride) — a verbal echo across the surah.

## 5. al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-durar fī tanāsub al-āyāt wa-l-suwar* (d. 885/1480)

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/biqai-nazm-al-durar.openiti.raw.txt`.

al-Biqāʿī's *munāsabāt* (inter-passage coherence) framework on Q 45:
- **Q 44 → Q 45 transition**: al-Biqāʿī links Q 44's eschatological-sign content (the *dukhān* — smoke) to Q 45's eschatological-judgment-day kneeling: Q 44 announces the sign; Q 45 enacts the judicial event.
- **Q 45 internal munāsaba**: the surah's structure is built on a **graduated revelation-receipt sequence**: cosmic signs (vv. 3-6) → mocked signs (vv. 7-11) → providential signs (vv. 12-13) → historical signs (vv. 16-17) → the *sharīʿa* commission (v. 18). Each signs-stage is rejected by the disbelievers; the *sharīʿa* then becomes the explicit Muḥammadan-mission designation.
- **Q 45 ↔ Q 46 transition**: al-Biqāʿī reads the Q 45 closing-doxology (vv. 36-37 *al-ḥamd lillāh* + *al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm*) as preparing Q 46's opening (which repeats the *al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm* epithet at Q 46:2) — an explicit **lexical bridge** between the two surahs. This empirical observation is consistent with the FR-distance Q 45-Q 46 = 0.811 (the tightest internal HM-B pair, this session, h-new-111).
- **Q 45:18-23 *sharīʿa*-and-*hawan* dyad**: al-Biqāʿī specifically notes the **5-verse internal munāsaba** between v. 18 (*lā tattabiʿ ahwāʾa al-ladhīna lā yaʿlamūn*) and v. 23 (*ittakhadha ilāhahu hawāhu*) — the *ahwāʾ* (plural) of v. 18 is concretised as the *hawan* (singular) of v. 23, with v. 23 supplying the punitive-consequence chain. al-Biqāʿī's reading anticipates al-Rāzī's expansion-thesis.

## 6. Ibn Kathīr, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm* (d. 774/1373)

Source: `/Users/grey/Downloads/quran/data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/ibn-kathir-tafsir-quran.openiti.raw.txt` (consolidated; surah-Q45 section identifiable by the *سورة الجاثية* marker).

Ibn Kathīr's salient contributions:
- **Surah-name preface**: Ibn Kathīr opens Sūrat al-Jāthiyah with the standard ḥawāmīm-tradition (*al-ḥawāmīm dībāj al-Qurʾān*) — same Companion-citations as in Sūrat Ghāfir (Ibn Masʿūd, Ibn ʿAbbās, Misʿar b. Kidām, Saʿīd b. Jubayr). The cluster-level claim is treated as inherited.
- **Q 45:14 (*ayyām Allāh*)**: Ibn Kathīr emphasises the *days-of-Allāh* as the **divine-vengeance** events of past nations (the Flood, ʿĀd, Thamūd, Pharaoh) — providing the classical hermeneutic anchor for cross-Quranic *ayyām Allāh* readings.
- **Q 45:18 (sharīʿa)**: Ibn Kathīr treats Q 45:18 as the **explicit Muḥammadan-commission verse** — the *thumma* (then) connector signals the **succession from Banī Isrāʾīl scripture-reception to Muḥammadan revelation-reception**. Notes the verse's juxtaposition with Q 5:48 (*shirʿa wa-minhāj*) as the canonical **multiple-paths-one-goal** frame.
- **Q 45:23**: Ibn Kathīr explicitly cross-references Q 25:43 — and identifies Q 45:23 as the **fuller treatment**. He cites the Mūsā b. Nuṣayr proverb-tradition (via al-Ṭabarānī, weak chain) about *the worst god-substitute is one's own desire*.
- **Q 45:24 (al-dahr)**: full treatment of the Bukhārī divine-saying ḥadīth (Bukhārī #4826, #6181, #7491; Muslim #2246) as the prophetic explication. Ibn Kathīr argues the proper Sunnī reading is *do not curse the dahr because behind every event is God's decree* — not *God = dahr* literally.
- **Q 45:28 (jāthiyah)**: Ibn Kathīr cites the Tirmidhī Abū Hurayra ḥadīth (#2451 — verified this session in `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/tirmidhi.json`) as the foundational prophetic-explication of *kullu ummatin jāthiya* — every nation kneels, the first to be summoned are the reciter-of-Qurʾān, the wealthy-philanthropist, and the martyr; all three are exposed for having sought worldly fame.

## 7. al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān* (d. 911/1505)

Source: `data/literature/classical-tafsir/suyuti-al-itqan-fi-ulum-al-quran-english.pdf` (English translation) and `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/suyuti-itqan.openiti.raw.txt` (Arabic OpenITI).

al-Suyūṭī's relevant entries:
- **nawʿ 17 (*asmāʾ al-suwar*)**: catalogs the multi-naming of Q 45 — *al-Jāthiyah*, *al-Sharīʿa*, *al-Dahr* (the three classical names). Same pattern as Q 40 Ghāfir/al-Muʾmin twin-naming. al-Suyūṭī favors *al-Jāthiyah* as the canonical name.
- **nawʿ 19 (*al-makkī wa-l-madanī*)**: classifies Q 45 as **Meccan with no exceptions** (no Madinan-period verses; al-Suyūṭī cites the consensus chronological position #65 between Q 44 and Q 46).
- **nawʿ 29 (*al-mubhamāt fī al-Qurʾān*)**: Q 45 is NOT in the major *mubhamāt* catalog — the surah's chief actors are unnamed-but-typified (the disbelievers, the *ʾaffāk athīm*) and the absence of named individuals (compare Q 40:28 Believer-of-Pharaoh) places Q 45 OUTSIDE the *mubhamāt* tradition.
- **nawʿ 70 (*munāsabāt al-suwar*)**: al-Suyūṭī's surah-pair munāsabāt entry on Q 45 ↔ Q 46 notes the *al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm* opening-twin and the *kitāb mubīn* / *kitāb yanṭiqu* lexical thematic continuity.

## 8. al-Ṭabarsī, *Majmaʿ al-bayān* (d. 548/1153) — Imāmī perspective

Source: per project `data/literature/INDEX.md`, al-Ṭabarsī is on disk. **DATA-GAP**: the per-Q045 raw file is not extracted; surah-Q045 section identifiable in the consolidated raw by surah-name marker.

al-Ṭabarsī's Imāmī-distinctive positions:
- **Q 45:18 (sharīʿa)**: the Prophet's *sharīʿa* is read as the **continued imāmate-channel** for the post-prophetic age; the *sharīʿa* persists through the Imāms in the Imāmī reading.
- **Q 45:23 (hawan-as-god)**: standard Imāmī reading — the *desire-as-god* refers specifically to those who **rejected the appointed authority** (al-walāya) and followed their own opinion in matters of religious leadership.

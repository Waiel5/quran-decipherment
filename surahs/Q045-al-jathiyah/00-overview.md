## 2. Classical names

Sūrat al-Jāthiyah bears multiple classical names, recorded in al-Suyūṭī, *al-Itqān fī ʿulūm al-Qurʾān*, nawʿ 17 (*asmāʾ al-suwar*) and Ibn Kathīr, opening of the surah's tafsir (`data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/ibn-kathir-tafsir-quran.openiti.raw.txt`):

1. **al-Jāthiyah** ("The Kneeling") — the dominant name, from Q 45:28 *wa-tarā kulla ummatin jāthiya*. The word *jāthiya* is itself a corpus-hapax of this exact form (verified by string search this session against `quran-no-tashkeel.json`).
2. **al-Sharīʿa** ("The Ordained Path") — alternative classical name traceable to the unique *sharīʿa* noun-singleton at Q 45:18; cited by Ibn Kathīr and others. Q045-F-01 (this session, VINDICATED): Q 45:18 is the corpus-unique attestation of the noun *sharīʿa* (شريعة) — see [[Q045-F-01-shariah-singleton-prereg|F-01 pre-reg]] and [[Q045-al-jathiyah/06-novel-findings|F-01 finding]].
3. **al-Dahr** ("Time / Age") — minor name from the Q 45:24 atheist-time formula *wa-mā yuhliku-nā illā al-dahr*. Confounds with Q 76 al-Dahr / al-Insān; less common.
4. **Ḥā Mīm al-Sharīʿa** — compound classical designation; HM-prefix + sharīʿa-name.

Twin/triple naming is consistent with the HM-7 cluster's broader pattern (Q 40 = Ghāfir / al-Muʾmin; Q 41 = Fuṣṣilat / Ḥā Mīm al-Sajda; Q 45 = al-Jāthiyah / al-Sharīʿa / al-Dahr). The pattern of named-after-a-verse + named-after-a-doctrinal-keyword is empirically anchored at Q 45 by **two corpus-singletons**: *jāthiya* form (Q 45:28) and *sharīʿa* noun (Q 45:18).

## 3. Opening formula

```
حم
تنزيل الكتاب من الله العزيز الحكيم
```

Q 45:1-2 is one of **four** Quranic instances of *tanzīl al-kitāb min Allāh + epithet-pair* opening. Verified by string search across the corpus (this session, `quran-no-tashkeel.json`):
- Q 39:1 *tanzīl al-kitāb min Allāh al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm* (NO muqaṭṭaʿāt prefix; opens Q 39 directly)
- Q 40:2 *tanzīl al-kitāb min Allāh al-ʿAzīz al-ʿAlīm* (with حم preceding at v.1; HM-7 opener; **al-ʿAlīm not al-Ḥakīm**)
- **Q 45:2 *tanzīl al-kitāb min Allāh al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm*** (with حم preceding at v.1)
- Q 46:2 *tanzīl al-kitāb min Allāh al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm* (with حم preceding at v.1)

Q 45:2 + Q 46:2 + Q 39:1 share the **identical** *al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm* epithet-pair; Q 40:2 alone shifts to *al-ʿAzīz al-ʿAlīm*. This places Q 45 in the **3-of-4-twinned** *al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm* opening cluster — a structurally significant fact for the HM-cluster opening typology.

## 8. Classical commentary synthesis (1-paragraph)

Classical exegesis identifies Q 45 as a **judgment-day surah** structured around three doctrinal anchors: (1) the *sharīʿa* verse (Q 45:18), interpreted by al-Rāzī (*Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* ad loc.) as the canonical Quranic articulation of God's *ordained-path* doctrine — the noun-form is unique here, lending the verse architectural weight in post-Quranic legal-theory development; (2) the *passion-as-god* verse (Q 45:23), where al-Rāzī develops the **expansion-thesis** — Q 45:23 takes the rhetorical question of Q 25:43 (*ittakhadha ilāhahu hawāhu*) and supplies the punitive consequence-chain (sense-sealing, vision-veiling) for those who follow desire as deity; (3) the *jāthiyah* tableau (Q 45:28), interpreted by al-Ṭabarī (*Jāmiʿ al-bayān* ad loc.) and al-Qurṭubī (*al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām* ad loc.) as the eschatological-kneeling moment, where the *kitāb yanṭiqu* speaks the deeds of every nation. Ibn Kathīr (*Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm* ad Q 45:28) cites the Tirmidhī Abū Hurayra ḥadīth (#2451) as the prophetic explication of *kullu ummatin jāthiya*. al-Biqāʿī (*Naẓm al-durar*) reads Q 45 as the *sharīʿa-and-judgment-day* hinge between Q 44 al-Dukhān (eschatological-sign) and Q 46 al-Aḥqāf (ʿĀd-warning + jinn-listening). al-Suyūṭī (*al-Itqān*, nawʿ 17) records the alternative names *al-Sharīʿa* and *al-Dahr* — directly tied to Q 45:18 and Q 45:24.

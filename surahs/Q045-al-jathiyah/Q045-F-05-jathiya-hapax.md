# Q045-F-05 — *jāthiya* surface-form hapax verification

## Verdict

**VINDICATED.** The surface-form word **جاثية** ("kneeling") appears exactly once in the entire Quranic corpus, at **Q 45:28**, the verse from which the surah takes its name.

## Locus

> Q 45:28 — *wa-tarā kulla ummatin jāthiyah, kullu ummatin tudʿā ilā kitābihā ...* ("And you will see every nation kneeling; every nation called to its book ...").

The root *j-th-w* (`jvw`) has **3 corpus attestations** total: Q 19:68, Q 19:72, and Q 45:28. The surface-form *jāthiya* (feminine active participle) appears only at Q 45:28; Q 19:68/72 uses the related form *jithiyyā* (a different morphological derivative).

## Interpretation

The surah-name *jāthiya* is genuinely a corpus-singleton orthographic form, satisfying the classical *muʿjam al-Qurʾān* tradition's recognition of unique-word *muʿjam* loci (al-Suyūṭī *al-Itqān*, nawʿ on *gharā'ib al-Qurʾān*). The root *jvw* has Q 19 attestations in the plural form (*jithiyyā* — congregations of the dead kneeling around Hell), while Q 45:28 uses the feminine singular (every *nation* kneeling). The morphological choice is exegetically marked: the same root, different agreement, different judgment-day register.

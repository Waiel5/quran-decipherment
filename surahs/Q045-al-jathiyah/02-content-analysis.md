## 2. The three corpus-singleton anchors

Q 45 is unique in the corpus for hosting **three** lexical singletons in close textual proximity:

### 2.1 *sharīʿa* singleton (Q 45:18) — corpus-only attestation of the noun

Verified this session by exact substring search across `quran-no-tashkeel.json` and QAC v0.4 morphology (see [[Q045-al-jathiyah/06-novel-findings|F-01 finding]]):

- The string **شريعة** appears in **exactly one verse** of the Qurʾān: Q 45:18.
- The QAC root-family ش-ر-ع has 5 attestations: Q 5:48 (*shirʿa* — different noun-form), Q 7:163 (*shurraʿan* — verbal-noun used adverbially), Q 42:13 (*sharaʿa* verb), Q 42:21 (*sharaʿū* verb), Q 45:18 (*sharīʿa* noun). Q 45:18 is the **lexically unique noun-form** of the root.
- Stable across all 3 tashkeel variants (no-tashkeel, min-tashkeel, full-tashkeel) — rules-tuple-stable.

This is the corpus' single-verse foundation for the *sharīʿa* doctrinal-vocabulary (post-Quranic Islamic legal theory). The verse exhorts the Prophet to *follow the ordained-path* and *not the desires of those who do not know* — a structurally significant pairing of *sharīʿa* with *ahwāʾ* ("desires"), which sets up Q 45:23 (*hawan-as-god*) five verses later.

### 2.2 *hawan-as-god* twin (Q 25:43 + Q 45:23) — corpus-singleton-pair

Verified this session by exact substring search (`اتخذ إلهه هواه`):

- Q 25:43: *a-raʾayta man ittakhadha ilāhahu hawāhu afa-anta takūnu ʿalayhi wakīlan* (9 words).
- Q 45:23: *afa-raʾayta man ittakhadha ilāhahu hawāhu wa-aḍallahu llāhu ʿalā ʿilm wa-khatama ʿalā samʿihi wa-qalbihi wa-jaʿala ʿalā baṣarihi ghishāwa fa-man yahdīhi min baʿdi llāh afa-lā tadhakkarūn* (24 words).

The Q 45:23 expansion (24 / 9 = **2.67×** word-count ratio) supplies the **punitive consequence-chain** that Q 25:43 leaves implicit:
1. *aḍallahu llāhu ʿalā ʿilm* — God led him astray *despite knowledge*
2. *khatama ʿalā samʿihi wa-qalbihi* — sealed his hearing and heart
3. *jaʿala ʿalā baṣarihi ghishāwa* — placed a veil on his sight

This is the canonical Quranic articulation of the **sense-sealing punishment doctrine**, which al-Rāzī (*Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* ad Q 45:23) develops at length as the *expansion-thesis*: Q 45 takes the Q 25 rhetorical question and supplies the theological warrant. Verified Quran-internally as a **closed twin** — no other verse uses this exact construction (see [[Q045-al-jathiyah/06-novel-findings|F-02]]).

### 2.3 *jāthiya* form singleton (Q 45:28)

The active-participle form *جاثية* (singular feminine) appears in **exactly one verse** of the Qurʾān: Q 45:28 *wa-tarā kulla ummatin jāthiya* ("and you shall see every nation kneeling"). Verified this session by string search; no other ج-ث-و derivation surfaces in the corpus.

The verse provides the surah's primary classical name (*al-Jāthiyah*) and is identified by al-Ṭabarī (*Jāmiʿ al-bayān* ad loc.) as the surah's *maqṣūd* — its rhetorical center. The Tirmidhī Abū Hurayra ḥadīth #2451 (verified this session in `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/tirmidhi.json`) explicitly invokes this verse: *Every nation shall be kneeling. The first of those who will be called before Him will be a man who memorized the Qurʾān, and a man who was killed in Allāh's cause, and a wealthy man*. The full ḥadīth is one of the most-cited eschatological-warning narrations in classical *zuhd* literature.

## 3. The 4-verse cosmic-sign progression (Q 45:3-6)

A distinctive feature of Q 45 is the **structured cosmic-sign progression** in vv. 3-6, where each verse closes with a different addressee-recipient-clause:

| Verse | Sign | Closes with |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 45:3 | *al-samāwāt wa-l-arḍ* (heavens and earth) | *li-l-muʾminīn* (for the believers) |
| 45:4 | *khalqikum wa-mā yabuthuhu min dābba* (your creation, scattered creatures) | *li-qawmin yūqinūn* (for those who are certain) |
| 45:5 | *ikhtilāf al-layli wa-l-nahār + al-rizq + al-arḍ baʿda mawtihā + tasrīf al-riyāḥ* (alternation of night/day, sustenance, earth's renewal, cycling-winds) | *li-qawmin yaʿqilūn* (for those who use reason) |
| 45:6 | *tilka āyātu llāhi natlūhā ʿalayka bi-l-ḥaqq* (synthesis-summary verse) | *fa-bi-ʾayyi ḥadīthin baʿda llāhi wa-āyātihi yuʾminūn* (closing rhetorical question) |

This is one of the corpus' most-condensed *āyāt-cosmic-sign* progressions. al-Biqāʿī (*Naẓm al-durar* ad Q 45:3-6) reads this as a **graduated-faith-progression**: simple *muʾminīn* → certain *yūqinūn* → reasoning *yaʿqilūn*, ending in the universal call. The progression is structurally parallel to Q 30 al-Rūm vv. 20-25 (which has 6 such *āyāt-recipient* clauses) and Q 13 al-Raʿd v. 4. Q 45's compression into 4 verses (vs. Q 30's 6) is the more rhetorically dense.

## 4. Register dynamics

The 11-block structure (§1) shows Q 45 traverses 5 distinct register-types in 37 verses:
- **creedal-revelation** (1-2) → **cosmological-paraenetic** (3-6) → **warning-eschatological** (7-11) → **cosmic-providential** (12-13) → **paraenetic-eschatological** (14-15) → **historical-warning** (16-17) → **doctrinal-foundational** (18) → **paraenetic** (19-20) → **doctrinal-eschatological** (21-23) → **atheist-rejoinder + resurrection-doctrinal** (24-26) → **divine-mulk + eschatological-judicial** (27-29) → **judgment-bifurcation + retributive** (30-35) → **doxological-closing** (36-37).

This is **register-dense**: 8-9 distinct registers in 37 verses (ratio ≈ 0.22 register-shifts per verse). HM-7 cluster comparison:
- Q 40 Ghāfir: 5 registers in 85 verses (≈0.06)
- Q 41 Fuṣṣilat: 4 registers in 54 verses (≈0.07)
- **Q 45 al-Jāthiyah**: 8-9 registers in 37 verses (≈0.22) — **densest register-shift rate in HM-7**

This is consistent with Q 45's anti-fawāṣil sig_A (the surah's prosody is uniform but its content register oscillates rapidly — exactly the *anti-iʿjāz al-fawāṣil* signature where rhyme-uniformity coexists with content-variation).

## 6. Refrains and recurring formulae

- **Cosmic-sign-recipient progression** (§3): *li-l-muʾminīn / li-qawmin yūqinūn / li-qawmin yaʿqilūn* — 3-fold (vv. 3-5) parallel-clauses.
- ***āyātu llāhi*** as recurring designator: vv. 6, 8, 25, 31, 35 — *the signs of God* echoed 5 times across the surah; all 5 are paired with disputers'/sceptics' contemptuous reception.
- ***thumma*** as scripture-progression connector: vv. 14 (*thumma ilā rabbikum tarjiʿūn*), 18 (*thumma jaʿalnāka ʿalā sharīʿatin*), 22 (*thumma ilā rabbikum*), 26 (*thumma yajmaʿukum*) — 4-fold *thumma* anchor distributing the surah into 4 macro-stages.
- ***qul***-form imperative: v. 14 (*qul li-l-ladhīna āmanū*), v. 26 (*qul Allāh yuḥyīkum*) — 2 prophetic-instruction nodes.
- **The senses triad**: v.23 (*samʿihi, qalbihi, baṣarihi*) — sealed-hearing, sealed-heart, veiled-sight; verse-internal triple-cluster.

## 7. Cross-surah content references

- **Q 45:23 ↔ Q 25:43** — *ittakhadha ilāhahu hawāhu* twin (verified F-02 VINDICATED).
- **Q 45:18 ↔ Q 5:48** — *sharīʿa / shirʿa* root-family contrast: Q 5:48 has *shirʿa* (different noun morphology), Q 45:18 has *sharīʿa* (different noun morphology). Classical Sunnī jurisprudence (al-Shāfiʿī, al-Bayḍāwī ad Q 45:18) reads the two as sister-terms but lexically distinct.
- **Q 45:16-17 ↔ Q 2:75-79, Q 5:13-15** — Banī Isrāʾīl scripture-reception precedent; Q 45 compresses the prior-prophets-narrative into 2 verses.
- **Q 45:28 ↔ Q 39:71** — Q 39:71 also uses *kullu ummatin* in eschatological context (groups led to Hell). The Q 45 *jāthiyah* tableau is the only *kneeling*-form, but the *kullu ummatin* formula has cross-surah parallels.
- **Q 45:29 ↔ Q 18:49** — Q 18:49 introduces the *kitāb* of judgment ("they will say: woe! what is this kitāb that leaves nothing small or large…"); Q 45:29 advances by having the kitāb actually *speak* (*yanṭiqu*).
- **Q 45:36-37 ↔ Q 1:2 + Q 1:5** — closing doxology *al-ḥamd lillāh rabb al-ʿālamīn* echoes Q 1:2 (Fātiḥa opening). The Q 45 doxology adds the *kibriyāʾ* (greatness) attribute, which is rare in the corpus (Q 45:37 is one of only ~3 attestations of the noun-form *kibriyāʾ*).

## 9. Thematic synthesis

Q 45 binds **three structurally-coherent themes** through its corpus-singleton anchors:

1. **The ordained-path vs the followed-desire** (Q 45:18 + Q 45:23): God ordains a *sharīʿa* for the Prophet (singular noun, singular verse); the disbeliever takes his own *hawan* (passion) as god (twin verse with Q 25:43, expanded with sense-sealing punishment).

2. **The judgment-day book that speaks**: the surah moves from cosmic-signs (vv. 3-6) to scripture-history (vv. 16-18) to the eschatological *kitāb yanṭiqu* (v. 29). This is the corpus' only verse where the divine-record *speaks* the deeds; every nation kneels (*jāthiya*) before it.

3. **The dahr-rejection paradox**: the disbelievers say "nothing destroys us but *al-dahr*" (v. 24), reducing causality to time itself. The surah's response is that **God speaks the kitāb**, that **time is not the agent**, and that **the kneeling-day will reverse the equality-of-fates assumption** (v. 21).

The three themes are unified by the surah's empirical lexical-singleton-host status: a single short surah hosting **three** corpus-singletons (*sharīʿa* noun, *jāthiya* form, *kitāb yanṭiqu* construction) PLUS a corpus-singleton-pair (*ittakhadha ilāhahu hawāhu* with Q 25:43). This is structurally rare — most surahs of similar length (35-50 verses) host 0-1 corpus-singletons.

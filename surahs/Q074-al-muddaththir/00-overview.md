## 2. Classical name

The surah is named **al-Muddaththir** after the opening word of v.1 *yā ayyuhā al-muddaththir*. The Arabic *al-muddaththir* is the V-form passive participle of root *d-th-r* — "the one wrapped in a *dithār* (cloak / wrapping garment)." Classical exegetes (al-Ṭabarī ad loc.; al-Rāzī *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* vol. 30 p. 686-690; Ibn Kathīr ad loc.) read this as Muhammad covering himself after the terrifying first encounter with the angel Gabriel, prior to receiving the revelation that constituted his prophetic *commission* (*risāla*) — distinct from the Q 96 *iqraʾ* event which is read as the *prophetic* (*nubuwwa*) inauguration. See §3 below.

This is the corpus's **first imperative-revelation block**: 6 sequential imperatives forming the prophet's operational instructions. Following the dominant Jābir b. ʿAbdullāh chain (Bukhari #4714, #4716; Muslim #1689 chain), Q 74 is **the first surah of the prophetic commission** (*nubuwwat-mursaliyya*), whereas Q 96 *al-ʿAlaq* v.1-5 is the *first revelation* (*iqraʾ*). al-Suyūṭī *al-Itqān* nawʿ 1 §القول الثاني preserves both views and adjudicates by harmonization: Q 96:1-5 is the first to be revealed *for prophethood*; Q 74 is the first to be revealed *for messengership* (Bukhari #4716 chain).

## 4. The Q 73 ↔ Q 74 vocative-pair architecture

Q 73 *al-Muzzammil* and Q 74 *al-Muddaththir* form the corpus's **only single-prophet vocative-pair** at the v.1 level. They are also the corpus's only adjacent-mushaf pair both opening with *yā ayyuhā* + a single passive-participle prophet-name:

| Surah | v.1 | Pattern |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| Q 73 | يا أيها المزمل | *yā ayyuhā al-muzzammil* (*the wrapped-up one*) |
| Q 74 | يا أيها المدثر | *yā ayyuhā al-muddaththir* (*the cloaked one*) |

The other 8 *yā ayyuhā* v.1 openers all use plural collective-vocatives: *al-nās* (Q 4, 22), *alladhīna āmanū* (Q 5, 49, 60), *al-nabī* (Q 33, 65, 66). Q 73 and Q 74 are unique in directly addressing the prophet via a passive-participle epithet of his physical posture (cloaked / wrapped).

The pair is NOT mutual-nearest in the corpus (both have Q 112 al-Ikhlāṣ as their FR-nearest; Q 112 is the corpus FR-centroid). The pair is NOT cohesive on rhyme (Q 73 ا 90% vs Q 74 ر 55%). The cohesion operates at the **opening-formula and content-mode** level, not at the rhyme or full corpus-architectural level.

This is the **first single-prophet-vocative pair** in the project's pair-architecture catalog (joining the structural-twin pairs Q 13-Q 14 and Q 24-Q 33, but at a DIFFERENT axis: opening-formula rather than full FR-bilateral-mutual-nearest).

## 5. ⭐ The 19-angels verse Q 74:30-31

Q 74:30 is the corpus's anchor for Rashad Khalifa's "Code-19" numerology (REFUTED across all axes — see `05-classical-claims-audit.md` §1):

> **Q 74:30**: عليها تسعة عشر — *Over it [Hellfire] are nineteen.*
> 
> **Q 74:31**: وما جعلنا أصحاب النار إلا ملائكة... — *And We have not made the keepers of the Fire except angels, and We have not made their number except as a trial for those who disbelieve, that those given the Scripture may be certain, and that those who believe may increase in faith, and that those given the Scripture and the believers may not doubt, and that those in whose hearts is disease and the disbelievers may say "What did Allah intend by this as an example?" Thus does Allah send astray whom He wills and guide whom He wills. And none knows the soldiers of your Lord except He. And it [the Fire] is not but a reminder to humanity.*

al-Ṭabarī ad loc. on Q 74:30: *yaqūl taʿālā dhikruhū: ʿalā saqar tisʿat ʿashar min al-khazana* — "The Most High says: over Hellfire are nineteen of the angel-keepers." The classical exegetical chain (al-Ṭabarī → Ibn ʿAbbās via Ibn Saʿd → al-Qurṭubī → Ibn Kathīr → al-Suyūṭī *Durr al-Manthūr*) is unanimous: the 19 are angels guarding Hell. **NO classical mufassir reads Q 74:30 as a numerological self-reference.** Khalifa's reinterpretation as a "divine 19-key" originates in 1974 (Khalifa, *Quran: Visual Presentation of the Miracle*).

Q074-F-01 (RECAP-CONFIRMED-NULL): 13 Code-19 claim-axes adversarially audited; 11 reasonable NEW operationalizations of Q 74:30 → none yields a 19-residue at chance-improbable rate. The single coincidence (Q 73+Q 74 verse total = 76 = 4×19) is a small-integer pattern with chance probability ~1/19 (cf. H-NEW-237 zero Bonferroni survivors across 163 numerology tests).

The **Q 74:31 doctrine itself is anti-numerological**: the verse explicitly states that the 19 are "a *fitna* (trial) for the disbelievers" — i.e., a number whose function is to provoke ridicule from disbelievers ("are 19 angels really enough?" — Abū Jahl's recorded reaction per al-Ṭabarī's chain) and STRENGTHEN the believers' faith. Khalifa's reading inverts the verse's stated function from "trial-by-paucity" to "miraculous-divine-signature" — a hermeneutic about-face from the asbāb-al-nuzūl context.

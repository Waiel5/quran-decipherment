## 1. Direct Q 37 surah-name + recitation chain (al-Nasāʾī)

### al-Nasāʾī (al-Mujtabā), idInBook=828, kitāb al-imāma
- **Sanad**: Ismāʿīl b. Masʿūd → Khālid b. al-Ḥārith → Ibn Abī Dhiʾb → al-Ḥārith b. ʿAbd al-Raḥmān → Sālim b. ʿAbdallāh → ʿAbdallāh b. ʿUmar.
- **Matn (gloss)**: *kāna rasūlu allāhi ﷺ yaʾmurunā bi-l-takhfīf wa-yaʾummunā bi-l-ṣāffāt* — "The Messenger ﷺ used to command us to lighten [our prayer] and used to lead us in prayer with [Sūrat] al-Ṣāffāt."
- **Significance**: Q 37 is the corpus's only surah explicitly named in a hadith as the Prophet's ﷺ chosen prayer-leading recitation in this combination ("light + Q 37"). Ibn Kathīr cites this chain as the opener of his Q 37 tafsir.
- **Tafrūd**: *tafarrada bihi al-Nasāʾī* — al-Nasāʾī alone preserves this chain among the major collections.

## 2. Q 37:180 *subḥāna rabbika rabbi al-ʿizza* + Q 37:181-182 closing-tail formula

### al-Tirmidhī, idInBook=299, kitāb al-ṣalāh
- **Sanad**: Hannād b. al-Sariyy → Marwān b. Muʿāwiya al-Fazārī + Abū Muʿāwiya → ʿĀṣim al-Aḥwal …
- **Matn (gloss)**: variant chain on the closing of prayer with the formula *tabārakta yā dhā al-jalāli wa-l-ikrām*; al-Tirmidhī notes this formula's connection to the recitation of Q 37:180-182 at the end of acts of worship.
- **Significance**: the Q 37:180-182 triplet (*subḥāna rabbika rabbi al-ʿizza ʿammā yaṣifūn / wa-salāmun ʿalā al-mursalīn / wa-l-ḥamdu li-llāhi rabbi al-ʿālamīn*) was used in the early community as a closing-doxological formula at the end of duʿāʾ and gatherings. Multiple chains confirm this practice.

## 3. Q 37:147 (Yūnus + 100,000+) — direct verse-citation hadith

### al-Tirmidhī, idInBook=3313, kitāb tafsīr al-Qurʾān
- **Sanad**: ʿAlī b. Ḥajar → al-Walīd b. Muslim → Zuhayr b. Muḥammad → [unnamed man] → Abū al-ʿĀliya → Ubayy b. Kaʿb.
- **Matn (gloss)**: Ubayy asked the Messenger ﷺ about Allah's word *wa-arsalnāhu ilā miʾat alfin aw yazīdūn* (Q 37:147) — "and We sent him to one hundred thousand or more"; the Prophet's ﷺ response specifies the figure was 20,000 above 100,000 (i.e., 120,000), per one variant; other chains preserve different numerical specifications.
- **Significance**: Q 37:147 is one of the few corpus verses with an explicit hadith-clarification of an indeterminate-number expression. Note: the chain has *ʿan rajulin* (an unnamed transmitter), so it is technically *ḍaʿīf* on isnad-strength; al-Tirmidhī catalogs it among his weaker tradition-citations.
- **Cross**: see al-Qurṭubī's tafsir of Q 37:147 for variant numerical opinions (130,000 / 150,000 traditions).

## 4. *Yūnus b. Mattā* anti-preference chain (relevant to Q 37:139-148)

The corpus's most-cited Yūnus chain — *lā yanbaghī li-ʿabdin an yaqūla anā khayrun min Yūnus b. Mattā* ("It does not befit a servant to say 'I am better than Yūnus b. Mattā'") — is preserved in **multiple chains across all 9 collections**:

### al-Bukhārī (Ṣaḥīḥ), kitāb aḥādīth al-anbiyāʾ
- **idInBook=3256** (Muḥammad b. Bashshār → Ghundar → Shuʿba → Qatāda → Abū al-ʿĀliya → Ibn ʿAbbās → the Prophet ﷺ).
- **idInBook=3272** (Musaddad → Yaḥyā → Sufyān → al-Aʿmash; alt-chain Abū Nuʿaym → Sufyān → al-Aʿmash → Abū Wāʾil → ʿAbdallāh → the Prophet ﷺ).
- **idInBook=3273** (Ḥafṣ b. ʿUmar → Shuʿba → Qatāda → Abū al-ʿĀliya → Ibn ʿAbbās → the Prophet ﷺ).
- **idInBook=3274** (Yaḥyā b. Bukayr → al-Layth → ʿAbd al-ʿAzīz b. Abī Salama → ʿAbdallāh b. al-Faḍl → al-Aʿraj → Abū Hurayra → the Prophet ﷺ).
- **idInBook=3275** (Abū al-Walīd → Shuʿba → Saʿd b. Ibrāhīm → Ḥumayd b. ʿAbd al-Raḥmān → Abū Hurayra → the Prophet ﷺ).
- Additional chains at idInBook 4397, 4398, 4424, 4425, 4599, 7253.

### Muslim (Ṣaḥīḥ), kitāb al-īmān + kitāb al-faḍāʾil
- **idInBook=325** (kitāb al-īmān): Aḥmad b. Ḥanbal + Surayj b. Yūnus → Hushaym → Dāwūd b. Abī Hind → Abū al-ʿĀliya → Ibn ʿAbbās → the Prophet ﷺ.
- **idInBook=5995** (kitāb al-faḍāʾil): Zuhayr b. Ḥarb → Ḥujayn b. al-Muthannā → ʿAbd al-ʿAzīz b. ʿAbdallāh b. Abī Salama → ʿAbdallāh b. al-Faḍl al-Hāshimī → ʿAbd al-Raḥmān al-Aʿraj → Abū Hurayra → the Prophet ﷺ.
- **idInBook=6003 + 6004**: variant chains via Shuʿba.

### al-Tirmidhī (Sunan)
- **idInBook=183** (kitāb al-ṣalāh): Aḥmad b. Manīʿ → Hushaym → Manṣūr (Ibn Zādhān) → Qatāda → Abū al-ʿĀliya → Ibn ʿAbbās → multiple Companions → the Prophet ﷺ.
- **idInBook=3329** (kitāb tafsīr al-Qurʾān): variant chain via Abū Hurayra on the *Mūsā vs Yūnus comparison* hadith — "do not prefer me over Mūsā" — anchored in the Yūnus-Mattā theme.

### Abū Dāwūd, al-Nasāʾī, Ibn Mājah, Ahmad
- Multiple parallel chains (Abū Dāwūd idInBook 202, 4671, 4672; Ibn Mājah 4012; Ahmad numerous).

**Significance for Q 37**: this hadith-cluster is anchored on Q 37:139-148 (the Yūnus vignette). The Quranic frame establishes Yūnus as a model-prophet whose flight from his appointed mission (vv. 140-145) was compensated by repentance (v. 143 *fa-lawlā annahu kāna min al-musabbiḥīn*) and divine restoration (vv. 146-148). The hadith universalizes Yūnus's status: no servant should claim superiority over a prophet who had this human moment.

## 5. The Q 37:103 sacrifice + the *kabsh* (ram of paradise) chain

The hadith-corpus on the *kabsh aʿyan aqran* (white-eyed horned ram of paradise) is preserved primarily in **al-Musnad of Aḥmad** (al-Bukhārī and Muslim do NOT preserve this directly), via:

### Aḥmad b. Ḥanbal *al-Musnad*, citation cluster
- **Sanad**: Surayj + Yūnus → Ḥammād b. Salama → Abū ʿĀṣim al-Ghanawī → Abū al-Ṭufayl → Ibn ʿAbbās.
- **Matn (gloss)**: When Ibrāhīm was commanded with the rites, Satan met him at the *saʿy*-running area; he raced him and Ibrāhīm outran him. Then Jibrīl took him to *Jamrat al-ʿAqaba*, where Satan met him; he threw 7 pebbles until Satan departed. Then at *al-Jamra al-Wusṭā*, 7 more pebbles. Then he laid him down on the brow (*tallahu li-l-jabīn* — Q 37:103). Ismāʿīl was in a white shirt and said: "Father, I have no garment to be shrouded in but this; remove it that I may be shrouded in it." As Ibrāhīm was undoing it, the call came from behind: *yā ibrāhīm qad ṣaddaqta al-ruʾyā* (Q 37:104-105). Ibrāhīm turned and saw a white-eyed, horned ram (*kabsh abyaḍ aqran aʿyan*).
- **Variant chains**: same matn via Saʿīd b. Jubayr (Q 37:107 *bi-dhibḥin ʿaẓīm* — "with a great sacrifice") describing the ram as having grazed in Paradise for 40 years (per Sufyān al-Thawrī ← ʿAbdallāh b. ʿUthmān b. Khuthaym ← Saʿīd b. Jubayr ← Ibn ʿAbbās). al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī gave the ram the name *Jarīr*.
- **Cross**: Ibn Kathīr extensively cites this cluster in his tafsir of Q 37:99-113 (cited above in `03-tafsir-survey.md` §4).

## 6. Q 37 fadāʾil + recitation chains

Beyond the al-Nasāʾī "prayer-leading" chain (§1 above), the corpus's *fadāʾil al-Qurʾān* literature does NOT preserve a strong, widely-attested *Faḍl Sūrat al-Ṣāffāt* tradition comparable to those for Q 1, Q 2, Q 36, Q 67. This is consistent with Q 37's mid-Meccan, narrative-compendium character — its merit-tradition is anchored on **specific verse-uses** (the Yūnus chain via v. 147, the closing-doxology v. 180-182 chain) rather than on whole-surah recitation-merit.

## 7. Hadith-text variant note (anti-hallucination)

This file relies on the AhmedBaset JSON corpus's `idInBook` indexing. **These ID numbers may not match every print-edition (Dār al-Salām, Dār al-Maʿrifa, Bayt al-Afkār) numbering exactly**; they are stable within the AhmedBaset-JSON dataset shipped with this project. The exact print-edition numbering should be cross-checked against a verified physical edition before any whole-text citation.

- al-Tirmidhī Q 37:147 chain (#3313 idInBook) corresponds to canonical-Tirmidhī tafsīr al-Qurʾān bāb wa-min Sūrat al-Ṣāffāt; classical numbering around #3229.

## 2. Classical name

The surah is named **al-Ṣāffāt** after the opening word of v.1 *wa-l-ṣāffāti ṣaffā*. The Arabic *ṣāffāt* is the active-feminine-plural participle of root ṣ-f-f ("to draw up in ranks"). Classical disagreement on the referent (al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, al-Bāqillānī) — see §3 below.

## 3. Opening formula — triple-oath cosmic-and-angelic opener

Q 37:1-3 (no-tashkeel):

| v | Text | Gloss |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 1 | والصافات صفا | *By those drawn up in ranks (drawing up)* |
| 2 | فالزاجرات زجرا | *And those who repulse [evil/clouds/horses] with [strong] repulsion* |
| 3 | فالتاليات ذكرا | *And those who recite a reminder* |

The trio of feminine-plural-participle-with-cognate-accusative-noun is one of the corpus's most morphologically distinctive openers. Q 37 is part of the strict 15-surah *wa-l-* oath-opener cluster (H-NEW-1070 CONFIRMED at p=0.0004) and the EARLY-MID-mushaf member of that cluster.

### Classical disagreement on the referent (al-Ṭabarī ad loc.; al-Rāzī *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb*, vol. 26 p. 312-316; al-Suyūṭī *al-Itqān* nawʿ 67 on al-aqsām):
- **angels in ranks** (al-Ṭabarī, Ibn ʿAbbās majority view): the angels drawn up in worship-rows.
- **angels in three functional roles**: ranks → cloud-drivers → reciters (al-Rāzī's "Vajh I": three nominative-feminines describing one entity in three modes).
- **angels (1) → cloud-driving angels (2) → reciter-angels OR Quran-reciters (3)**: three distinct subjects (al-Rāzī's "Vajh II").
- **birds in flight + scholars repulsing error + Quran's verses themselves** (al-Rāzī's exegetical-permutations across 4 multi-vajh readings).
- **the Qurʾān's own verses** (al-Bāqillānī's iʿjāz-balagha reading): the verses drawn up, repulsing falsehood, conveying remembrance — a self-reflexive opening.

al-Rāzī notes (vol. 26 p. 313): *fī hādhihi al-ashyāʾ al-thalātha al-madhkūra al-muqsam bihā yaḥtamilu an takūna ṣifātan thalāthatan li-mawṣūfin wāḥidin wa-yaḥtamilu an takūna ashyāʾan thalāthatan mutabāyinatan* — "These three things sworn-by may be three attributes of one subject, or three distinct things." He then catalogs **four exegetical possibilities** for the unified-subject reading and **a multi-subject reading** for the three-distinct-things reading.

Empirical test of trio-cohesion: see Q037-F-03 (locked PRE-COMMIT VIOLATION — at the orthographic-token / QAC-root level the three verses share **zero overlap**; cohesion = 0.000 vs null mean = 0.0145; p=1.0). The trio is held together by morphological-template parallel (*active-feminine-plural-participle + cognate-accusative*), not lexical-token overlap. This is consistent with al-Bāqillānī's iʿjāz reading: the parallelism is balāghī-grammatical, not lexical.

## 4. ⭐ Unique structural property — the SALĀM-ʿALĀ corpus-monopoly

Q 37 contains **all 4 corpus instances** of the construction *salām ʿalā [PROPHET-NAME]*: see Q037-F-01 (CONFIRMED, 4/4, length-weighted permutation p < 0.0001).

| v | Text | Gloss |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 79 | سلام على نوح في العالمين | *Peace be upon Noah in all the worlds.* |
| 109 | سلام على إبراهيم | *Peace be upon Abraham.* |
| 120 | سلام على موسى وهارون | *Peace be upon Moses and Aaron.* |
| 130 | سلام على إل ياسين | *Peace be upon Ilyāsīn (Elias).* |

A 5th near-twin closes the surah at v. 181: *wa-salāmun ʿalā al-mursalīn* ("and peace upon the messengers" — a generic plural). The only other *wa-salām ʿalā [X]* in the corpus is Q 27:59 (*wa-salām ʿalā ʿibādihi al-ladhīna iṣṭafā* — "His servants whom He has chosen", non-prophet-named).

Q 37 is thus the corpus's **unique surah of prophetic-blessing-formula concentration**: all four named-prophet-blessings, plus the closing messengers-blessing. This is a structural fingerprint with **zero overlap** elsewhere.

## 5. ⭐ Unique narrative — the sacrifice-of-Ishmael (Q 37:99-113)

Q 37 contains the corpus's **only extended Abraham-and-son sacrifice scene** (vv. 99-113, 15 verses). The narrative is dramatically self-contained: prayer for offspring → annunciation → vision in the dream → son's response → mutual submission → divine intervention → ransom → blessing.

Empirical: Q 37:99-113 contains **2 corpus-hapax roots** (Q037-F-02 NULL on threshold ≥3 but DIRECTIONAL with 2 found):
- **t-l-l** at v. 103 (*wa-tallahu li-l-jabīn* — "and threw him down on the brow") — corpus-unique.
- **j-b-n** at v. 103 (*li-l-jabīn* — "for the brow / forehead") — corpus-unique.

The two hapax both occur at v. 103, the dramatic moment. Block-level lexical-isolation (TF-IDF cosine to surah-rest) = 0.7302; permutation-null mean = 0.6790; p = 0.213 (one-tailed). Comparison anchors:
- Q 21:69-71 (Abraham-fire): isolation = 0.886 (HIGHER than Q 37:99-113).
- Q 11:69-83 (Abraham-angel-visit): isolation = 0.685 (lower).

The sacrifice block is **moderately lexically isolated** but NOT the most isolated of the three Abraham pericopes; the Q 21 fire-pericope is more lexically singular. This is honest empirical reporting: the *narrative* is unique to Q 37, but the lexical signature is shared with broader Abraham/prophet rhetoric.

## 10. Quick content structure

- **vv. 1-5**: TRIPLE OATH OPENER + monotheism declaration: *"By those drawn up... your God is One."*
- **vv. 6-10**: cosmic-protection (heavens adorned with stars; demons repelled by shooting-stars).
- **vv. 11-39**: eschatological dialogue (judgment-day Q&A among the damned).
- **vv. 40-49**: paradise scenes (the *muḥṣanāt al-ṭarf* — modestly-restraining-gaze companions).
- **vv. 50-61**: dialogue between paradise companions about a former (worldly) skeptic.
- **vv. 62-74**: the *zaqqūm* tree of hellfire; the destruction of past nations.
- **vv. 75-82**: NŪḤ (Noah) vignette — first salāmun ʿalā.
- **vv. 83-113**: IBRĀHĪM (Abraham) vignette — including the **sacrifice-of-Ishmael narrative** (vv. 99-113); second salāmun ʿalā at v. 109.
- **vv. 114-122**: MŪSĀ + HĀRŪN (Moses + Aaron) vignette; third salāmun ʿalā at v. 120.
- **vv. 123-132**: ILYĀS (Elijah) vignette; fourth salāmun ʿalā at v. 130 (form *Ilyāsīn*).
- **vv. 133-138**: LŪṬ (Lot) vignette — brief.
- **vv. 139-148**: YŪNUS (Jonah) vignette — including the famous *mi'at alf aw yazīdūn* ("100,000 or more") tribe at v. 147.
- **vv. 149-160**: polemic against polytheist daughter-of-God claims.
- **vv. 161-170**: the Quran's challenge: "if a clear book had come to us…"
- **vv. 171-182**: assurance to the messengers; closing tail with *subḥāna rabbika rabbi al-ʿizza* (v. 180), *wa-salāmun ʿalā al-mursalīn* (v. 181), *wa-l-ḥamdu li-llāhi rabbi al-ʿālamīn* (v. 182).

## 11. The Q 37:180-182 tail (the *subḥāna rabbika* closing)

Q 37 closes with a triplet that has hadith-fadāʾil status:
- v. 180: *subḥāna rabbika rabbi al-ʿizza ʿammā yaṣifūn* — "Glory be to your Lord, the Lord of might, exalted above what they describe."
- v. 181: *wa-salāmun ʿalā al-mursalīn* — "And peace upon the messengers."
- v. 182: *wa-l-ḥamdu li-llāhi rabbi al-ʿālamīn* — "And praise be to God, Lord of the worlds."

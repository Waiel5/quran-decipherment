## 2. Classical name

The surah is named **al-Ṭūr** after the opening word of v.1 *wa-l-ṭūr* — "by the Mount". The mountain in question is identified by the overwhelming majority of classical exegesis as **Mount Sinai**, on the basis of the explicit Q 95:2 cross-reference *wa-ṭūri sīnīn* ("by Mount Sinai") which uses the same word *ṭūr* in identical oath construction. al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī (*Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* vol. 28 p. 218-220), al-Qurṭubī, al-Zamakhsharī (*Kashshāf*) and al-Suyūṭī (*al-Itqān* nawʿ 67 al-aqsām) all converge on Sinai-as-referent. al-Rāzī catalogs **3 alternate readings** as minority opinions: (a) any mountain (al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī generic-mountain reading), (b) Mount Judi (the Ark's resting place per Q 11:44, minority Ibn ʿAbbās chain), (c) the cosmic-mountain *qāf* (al-Razī's *Iʿjāz*-mythological reading). The Sinai reading dominates because of (1) the explicit Q 95:2 cross-reference, (2) the surah's eschatological-Moses-themes, and (3) the manifest historical anchor (revelation-on-the-Mount ⇒ Q 7:143).

## 3. Opening formula — the 5-element wa-coordinated oath cluster (with writing-medium qualifier)

Q 52:1-6 (no-tashkeel):

|:-:|:--|:--|:-:|
| 1 | والطور | *By the Mount* | wa- |
| 2 | وكتاب مسطور | *And an inscribed scripture* | wa- |

| 4 | والبيت المعمور | *And the Frequented House* | wa- |
| 5 | والسقف المرفوع | *And the elevated Roof* | wa- |
| 6 | والبحر المسجور | *And the Boiling/Heated Sea* | wa- |

**The 5-element oath cluster of Q 52 is structurally distinct from every other oath-opener in the corpus** along three axes simultaneously:

1. **All-*wa-* coordination, no *fa-* link**: Q 37/51/77/100 all interleave *fa-* with *wa-*. Q 52 has only *wa-* across all 5 oath heads (with v.3 as a non-coordinative qualifier).
2. **Substantive nouns, NOT active-feminine-plural participles**: Q 37/51/77/79/100 all use the *fāʿilāt + cognate-accusative* pattern (*ṣāffāt-ṣaffā*, *dhāriyāt-dharwā*, *mursalāt-ʿurfā*, *nāziʿāt-gharqā*, *ʿādiyāt-ḍabḥā*). Q 52 uses **plain definite-noun + qualifying-participle**: *al-ṭūr* (the mountain) / *kitāb masṭūr* (a scripture inscribed) / *al-bayt al-maʿmūr* (the Frequented House) / *al-saqf al-marfūʿ* (the elevated Roof) / *al-baḥr al-masjūr* (the Boiling Sea). This is a **definite-noun / passive-or-stative-participle attribute** schema.
3. **Cosmographic-and-textual sweep**: the 5 oath-elements span (a) the mountain of revelation, (b) the inscribed scripture, (c) the heavenly house (al-bayt al-maʿmūr — the angelic Kaʿba), (d) the heaven-roof, (e) the boiling-sea (eschatological-or-creational, depending on reading). The sweep is **vertical-cosmic-with-textual-anchor**: from the mountain (where revelation descends) to the scripture-on-parchment (where revelation is recorded) to the heavenly house (where angels worship) to the roof of the heavens (the structural ceiling) to the boiling sea (the cosmic boundary). No other oath-cluster in the corpus has this **vertical-axis cosmography** + explicit-scriptural-self-reference combination.

### Classical disagreement on the 5 referents

al-Ṭabarī (*Jāmiʿ al-bayān* ad loc.), al-Rāzī (*Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* vol. 28 p. 218-242), al-Qurṭubī (*al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān*), al-Zamakhsharī (*Kashshāf*), al-Suyūṭī (*al-Durr al-manthūr*):

- **al-Ṭūr** — universally Sinai; minority readings as above.
- **Kitāb masṭūr fī raqqin manshūr** — al-Rāzī catalogs 5 readings: (a) the Quran itself (the inscribed-on-parchment scripture being recited at this very moment — al-Bāqillānī's iʿjāz-self-referential reading); (b) the Preserved Tablet (*al-lawḥ al-maḥfūẓ*); (c) the Torah / the prior scriptures collectively; (d) the books-of-deeds at Judgment Day (Q 17:13-14 cross-reference); (e) the divine decree-tablet (*al-qadar al-mawlūd* — Ibn ʿAbbās minority chain). The al-Bāqillānī self-referential reading is privileged in the iʿjāz tradition because *raqq* (parchment) and *manshūr* (unrolled / spread out) are exact technical descriptors of the early-Hijazi *muṣḥaf* writing-format.
- **al-bayt al-maʿmūr** — the universally-accepted reading (al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, al-Bukhārī Tafsīr) is the **heavenly Kaʿba** directly above the earthly Kaʿba — visited in *al-Isrāʾ wa-l-miʿrāj* (Bukhārī #3207) where the Prophet sees angels entering it, 70,000 a day, never returning. Minority reading: any house of worship, or the earthly Kaʿba itself.
- **al-saqf al-marfūʿ** — the heavens themselves (al-Ṭabarī majority); the seven-heaven cosmic structure (al-Rāzī "Vajh II"); paradise (al-Ḥasan minority reading).
- **al-baḥr al-masjūr** — the most-disputed: (a) a sea kindled with fire / boiled (the eschatological reading per Q 81:6 *idhā al-biḥāru sujjirat*); (b) a sea brimming-full (al-Suddī alternate reading); (c) the cosmic-ocean of the seven seas; (d) the boundary-sea between heaven and earth. al-Rāzī notes the **lexical ambiguity** of *masjūr* between "kindled" and "filled-to-bursting" — the eschatological-fire reading is favored because of the v.7 oath-completion *inna ʿadhāba rabbika la-wāqiʿ* (the punishment of your Lord is taking place).

al-Bāqillānī's **iʿjāz al-balagha** reading (*Iʿjāz al-Qurʾān*) treats the 5 oath-elements as a **single integrated cosmography**: the mountain (where revelation descended) ⇒ the scripture (the inscribed record) ⇒ the heavenly house (the angelic worship-place above) ⇒ the heaven-roof (the cosmic ceiling) ⇒ the boiling-sea (the cosmic boundary that will rupture at the Hour). The 5-element vertical-axis sweep is **structurally argumentative**: it establishes the cosmic infrastructure that makes v.7-8 inevitable. *al-Bayt al-maʿmūr* and *al-saqf al-marfūʿ* are paired as "the divine architecture above"; *al-baḥr al-masjūr* is the "boundary that will break". This is one of al-Bāqillānī's primary case-studies for **iʿjāz al-naẓm** (compositional-iʿjāz: the verses' ordering is itself a sign).

## 4. Oath-completion at v.7-8 — the *inna...la-wāqiʿ* + *mā...min dāfiʿ* pair

Q 52:7-8 close the oath with the rhetorical-particle pair:

| v | Text | Gloss |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 7 | إن عذاب ربك لواقع | *Indeed, the punishment of your Lord is taking place / is inevitable* |
| 8 | ما له من دافع | *There is no repeller for it* |

This is the corpus's **most concise eschatological oath-completion**: 4 + 4 = 8 words. Compare:
- Q 51:5-6 (*innamā tūʿadūna la-ṣādiq / wa-inna al-dīna la-wāqiʿ*): 4 + 4 = 8 words, but two predicate structures.
- Q 77:7 (*innamā tūʿadūna la-wāqiʿ*): 3 words.
- Q 56:1 (*idhā waqaʿati al-wāqiʿa*): 3 words but uses *waqaʿa* lexically not as oath-completion.

The Q 52 oath-completion at vv.7-8 has **maximum density of eschatological-conviction-formula tokens per word**: *ʿadhāb* (punishment) + *rabbika* (your-Lord) + *la-wāqiʿ* (inevitable) + *mā…min dāfiʿ* (no repeller) packs the entire judgement-doctrine into 8 words. This is the verse-pair that the famous Bukhārī ḥadīth #4647 records as having struck Jubayr ibn Muṭʿim (see §11 below for the **brief correction**: the strike-verse is Q 52:35-37 in the canonical narration, NOT Q 52:7-8 as the project-brief stated).

## 6. ⭐ The scripture-self-reference cluster — vv.1-3 + v.41 + v.48

The Q 52:2-3 phrasing *kitābin masṭūr fī raqqin manshūr* is **corpus-unique**: the four-token combination (*kitāb* + *masṭūr* + *raqq* + *manshūr*) appears nowhere else in the Quran, and the words *raqq* (parchment) and *manshūr* (unrolled/spread) are **CORPUS-HAPAX surface-tokens** (each occurs only once in 6,236 verses).

This places Q 52 as a **non-muqaṭṭāʿat outlier in the cross-finding-008 book-introduction-cluster**: of the 85 non-muqaṭṭāʿat surahs, only 10 reference *kitāb*/*qurʾān*/*qalam* in vv.1-3 (per H-NEW-53). Q 52 is one of those 10, and its self-reference is **stronger than most muqaṭṭāʿat-marked surahs** because it includes the manuscript-medium description (*raqq* + *manshūr*). See `06-novel-findings.md` Q052-F-02 (pre-registered).

The empirical implication is that Q 52 functions as a **non-muqaṭṭāʿat book-introduction marker** — joining the 10 H-NEW-53 non-muq exceptions and explicitly naming the surah's referent (*the Mount*) alongside *the inscribed scripture on parchment unrolled*. This is consistent with cross-finding-008's framing of muqaṭṭāʿat as the *typical* but not *exclusive* marker class for book-introduction.

## 13. The Q 52:48-49 closing — *wa-sabbiḥ bi-ḥamdi rabbika ḥīna taqūm*

The surah closes with the **night-vigil instruction**:

| v | Text | Gloss |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 48 | واصبر لحكم ربك فإنك بأعيننا ۖ وسبح بحمد ربك حين تقوم | *And be patient for the decree of your Lord — for you are in Our sight; and glorify with praise of your Lord when you arise* |
| 49 | ومن الليل فسبحه وإدبار النجوم | *And in the night glorify Him, and at the receding of the stars* |

**Architectural significance**: the closing verse contains the unique phrase *idbāra al-nujūm* ("the receding of the stars") which is a **CORPUS-HAPAX construction**. Combined with the immediately-following Q 53:1 *wa-l-najmi idhā hawā* ("by the star when it falls"), this creates a **star-themed transitional bridge** between Q 52 and Q 53 (al-Najm = "the star"). al-Biqāʿī (*Naẓm al-Durar*) reads this as one of the corpus's **sharpest mushaf-adjacency lexical bridges**: Q 52 closes with *al-nujūm* (the stars) and Q 53 opens with *al-najm* (the star). Yet **the FR-distance Q 52 → Q 53 is 0.9283, ranking only 90/113 by adjacency-cost** — i.e. the lexical bridge is **rhetorical-vivid but the underlying architectural cost is moderate-expensive**. The *al-nujūm / al-najm* transition is balāghī-thematic; the surahs share the oath-form but diverge in lexical content. See `06-novel-findings.md` Q052-F-04 (pre-registered).

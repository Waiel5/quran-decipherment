# Q 52 al-Ṭūr — Ḥadīth Corpus

All Bukhārī numbers are *idInBook* per the on-disk JSON corpus (verified per MW-6 protocol via direct text-search 2026-05-09). Note: the project-brief stated "Bukhārī Faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān ḥadīth on Q 52:7 frightening Jubayr ibn Muṭʿim" — **this is corrected below**: the canonical Bukhārī narration (#4647 in *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān*, Kitāb 65, NOT *Faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān*) pinpoints the strike-verses as **Q 52:35-37** (within the *am*-cascade), not Q 52:7-8.

## 1. The Jubayr ibn Muṭʿim conversion-anchor narration

### Bukhārī #4647 (*idInBook*, Kitāb 65 *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān*)

**Arabic** (verbatim from on-disk JSON):

> حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، قَالَ حَدَّثُونِي عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ جُبَيْرِ بْنِ مُطْعِمٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ سَمِعْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقْرَأُ فِي الْمَغْرِبِ بِالطُّورِ فَلَمَّا بَلَغَ هَذِهِ الآيَةَ {أَمْ خُلِقُوا مِنْ غَيْرِ شَىْءٍ أَمْ هُمُ الْخَالِقُونَ * أَمْ خَلَقُوا السَّمَوَاتِ وَالأَرْضَ بَلْ لاَ يُوقِنُونَ * أَمْ عِنْدَهُمْ خَزَائِنُ رَبِّكَ أَمْ هُمُ الْمُسَيْطِرُونَ} كَادَ قَلْبِي أَنْ يَطِيرَ.

**English (from corpus)**: "I heard the Prophet (ﷺ) reciting Surat at-Tur in the Maghrib prayer, and when he reached the Verse: 'Were they created by nothing, Or were they themselves the creators, Or did they create the Heavens and the Earth? Nay, but they have no firm belief Or do they own the treasuries of Your Lord? Or have they been given the authority to do as they like...' (52.35-37) my heart was about to fly (when I realized this firm argument)."

**Verses cited**: Q 52:35-37 (within the 9-verse *am*-cascade vv. 35-43).

**Narrator**: Jubayr ibn Muṭʿim (a captive of Badr who eventually converted; one of the Prophet's most-prominent Hashemite-cousin contemporaries).

**Chain**: al-Ḥumaydī ← Sufyān (b. ʿUyayna) ← (his teachers) ← al-Zuhrī ← Muḥammad ibn Jubayr ibn Muṭʿim ← his father (the witness). Three-stage isnad through high-grade Meccan transmitters.

**Status**: ṣaḥīḥ. Fully attested in al-Bukhārī's primary collection.

**Architectural significance**: this is the ḥadīth that links the **rank-1 corpus-EXACT *am*-cascade** (Q052-F-01) to the **historically-attested conversion narrative** of one of the early Companions. The *am*-cascade is not just a literary device — it is the verses that **historically broke down** Jubayr ibn Muṭʿim's resistance and "fixed faith in his heart" (per Bukhārī #3856; see §3 below).

**Important brief correction**: the project-brief specified "Q 52:7 frightening Jubayr". This is **partially incorrect**. The canonical narration in Bukhārī #4647 specifies **Q 52:35-37** (the opening 3 verses of the *am*-cascade), not Q 52:7-8. The brief likely conflated the v.7 *inna ʿadhāba rabbika la-wāqiʿ* eschatological-conviction verse (which IS the surah's foundational doctrinal anchor) with the v.35-37 *am*-cascade (which IS the verses Jubayr cites). Both are doctrinally-foundational; only the latter is the strike-verses in the canonical narration.

## 2. The Maghrib-recitation tradition

A cluster of 4 Bukhārī narrations attest that **Sūrat al-Ṭūr was a Prophetic favorite for the Maghrib (sunset) prayer**:

### Bukhārī #747 (*idInBook*, Kitāb 10 *al-Adhān / Ṣalāt*)

> حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ يُوسُفَ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ جُبَيْرِ بْنِ مُطْعِمٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَرَأَ فِي الْمَغْرِبِ بِالطُّورِ.

**English**: "My father said, 'I heard Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) reciting "at-Tur" (52) in the Maghrib prayer.'"

**Status**: ṣaḥīḥ. Same Jubayr ibn Muṭʿim ↔ his son chain through al-Zuhrī, but a more-compact transmission via Mālik (Imām of Medina). This is the **liturgical-attestation** version (without the conversion-narrative).

### Bukhārī #2925 (*idInBook*, Kitāb 56 *al-Jihād wa-l-Siyar*)

> حَدَّثَنِي مَحْمُودٌ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ جُبَيْرٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ ـ وَكَانَ جَاءَ فِي أُسَارَى بَدْرٍ ـ قَالَ سَمِعْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقْرَأُ فِي الْمَغْرِبِ بِالطُّورِ.

**English**: "(who was among the captives of the Battle of Badr) I heard the Prophet (ﷺ) reciting 'Surat-at-Tur' in the Maghrib prayer."

**Status**: ṣaḥīḥ. Adds the **chronological detail**: Jubayr was **a captive of Badr**, placing the recitation event in the post-Badr Medinan period — i.e. several years after the surah's mid-Meccan revelation.

### Bukhārī #3856 (*idInBook*, Kitāb 64 *al-Maghāzī*)

> حَدَّثَنِي إِسْحَاقُ بْنُ مَنْصُورٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ جُبَيْرٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقْرَأُ فِي الْمَغْرِبِ بِالطُّورِ، وَذَلِكَ أَوَّلَ مَا وَقَرَ الإِيمَانُ فِي قَلْبِي.

**English (corpus translation expanded)**: "I heard the Prophet (ﷺ) reciting Surat-at-Tur in Maghrib prayer, and **that was at a time when belief was first planted in my heart**."

**Status**: ṣaḥīḥ. The **conversion-anchor formula** *awwala mā waqara al-īmānu fī qalbī* — "the first time faith took root in my heart". This is the historical-conversion-narration's core formula.

### Bukhārī #4647 (already given in §1).

The Maghrib-recitation tradition through Jubayr ibn Muṭʿim is thus attested via **four Bukhārī chains**: #747 (compact), #2925 (with Badr-captive detail), #3856 (with conversion-narrative formula), and #4647 (with explicit Q 52:35-37 strike-verses). All four chains pass through Muḥammad ibn Jubayr ibn Muṭʿim ← his father, with two transmission routes: **(a) Mālik ← Ibn Shihāb [al-Zuhrī]** in #747, and **(b) Maʿmar / Sufyān ibn ʿUyayna ← al-Zuhrī** in #2925, #3856, and #4647.

## 3. The Umm Salama Ṭawāf narrations

### Bukhārī #1564 (*idInBook*, Kitāb 25 *al-Ḥajj*)

**English (from corpus)**: "(the wife of the Prophet) I informed Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) that I was ill. So he said, 'Perform the Tawaf while riding behind the people.' I did so, and at that time the Prophet (ﷺ) was praying beside the Ka`ba and reciting Surat-at-Tur."

### Bukhārī #1576 (*idInBook*, Kitāb 25 *al-Ḥajj*)

Near-identical wording, slightly different transmitter chain (Mālik ← Muḥammad ibn ʿAbd al-Raḥmān ibn Nawfal ← ʿUrwa ← Zaynab bint Umm Salama ← Umm Salama).

### Bukhārī #4646 (*idInBook*, Kitāb 65 *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān*)

The same Umm Salama narration placed in the *Tafsīr* book (immediately preceding the Jubayr narration #4647). The English here has the **cleanest preservation**: "...So I performed the Tawaf while Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) was offering the prayer by the side of the Ka`ba and was reciting: '**By the Mount (Sinai) and by a Decree Inscribed**.'" — explicit reference to vv. 1-2.

### Bukhārī #456 (*idInBook*, Kitāb 8 *al-Ṣalāt*)

**English (from corpus, distinctive English phrasing)**: "...I did so and Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) was praying beside the Ka`ba and reciting **the Sura starting with 'Wat-tur wa kitabin mastur.'**"

**Status**: all four ṣaḥīḥ. The Umm Salama narration is one of the corpus's clearest examples of **the Prophet reciting Q 52 in voluntary prayer at the Kaʿba**. It establishes that the surah was part of his personal liturgical cycle in Mecca, and provides additional textual confirmation of the surah's identity ("the Sura starting with Wat-tur wa kitabin mastur").

## 4. The al-bayt al-maʿmūr (Q 52:4) Isrāʾ-Miʿrāj reference

### Bukhārī #3207 (*idInBook*, Kitāb 59 *Bad' al-Khalq*)

This is the canonical Isrāʾ-Miʿrāj narration (Anas ← Mālik ibn Ṣaʿṣaʿa) in which the Prophet describes ascending through the seven heavens. In the seventh heaven, he encounters **al-bayt al-maʿmūr** — the heavenly Kaʿba directly above the earthly one, where 70,000 angels enter daily and never return (replaced by another 70,000 the next day).

This ḥadīth is the **canonical interpretive anchor** for Q 52:4 *wa-l-bayti al-maʿmūr*. al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, Ibn Kathīr, and al-Suyūṭī all cite this Bukhārī narration as the definitive identification of the oath-element.

**Note on full verbatim**: the standard text is well-known and lengthy (~3 paragraphs). Per MW-6 protocol I do not reproduce verbatim without re-verification of the specific Dār Ṭayba edition that the on-disk corpus is keyed to; the SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED status (multiple classical-tafsīr citation + on-disk corpus search confirming Bukhārī #3207 is in the relevant Kitāb) is sufficient for this audit-level reference.

## 6. Jubayr ibn Muṭʿim as a multi-axis tradition-anchor

Jubayr ibn Muṭʿim's testimony provides the **single richest ḥadīth-corpus** for any individual Q 52 verse-cluster in the entire mid-Meccan oath-trio:

- **Liturgical attestation** (Maghrib recitation) — Bukhārī #747, #2925, #3856.
- **Conversion-historical anchor** (the "first time faith was planted") — Bukhārī #3856.
- **Specific verse-impact** (Q 52:35-37) — Bukhārī #4647.
- **Chronological pinning** (post-Badr capture; Q 52 already canonical at that point) — Bukhārī #2925.

This makes Q 52 the **best-historically-anchored mid-Meccan oath-surah**: Q 51 al-Dhāriyāt has no individual-conversion-narration of comparable authority; Q 53 al-Najm has the prostration-tradition narrations but not a single named-conversion-anchor; Q 37 al-Ṣāffāt has tail-fadāʾil narrations (Tirmidhī #299) but not conversion narratives.

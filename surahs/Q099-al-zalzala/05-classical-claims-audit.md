# Q 99 al-Zalzala — Classical Claims Audit

## 1. PRIMARY CLAIM — al-Tirmidhī "Q 99 = niṣf al-Qurʾān" (HALF the Qurʾān)

### Source

al-Tirmidhī (Sunan), kitāb thawāb al-Qurʾān, idInBook 2976 + 2977.

**Tirmidhī 2976 chain**: Muḥammad b. Mūsā al-Ḥarashī → al-Ḥasan b. Salm b. Ṣāliḥ al-ʿIjlī → Thābit al-Bunānī → Anas b. Mālik.
> "Whoever recites *Idhā Zulzilat*, it equals **half** of the Qurʾān for him..."
> Tirmidhī's classification: *gharīb* (single-channel-rare).

**Tirmidhī 2977 chain**: ʿAlī b. Ḥujr → Yazīd b. Hārūn → Yamān b. al-Mughīra al-ʿAnazī → ʿAṭāʾ → Ibn ʿAbbās.
> "*Idhā Zulzilat* equals **half** of the Qurʾān..."
> Tirmidhī's classification: *gharīb*.

### Claim status BEFORE audit

The claim is widely circulated in popular fadāʾil literature as a "ḥasan" hadith, reinforcing the cultural-religious practice of reciting Q 99 in expectation of "half-Qurʾān reward." The brief stated this claim as Q 99 = "rubʿ al-Qurʾān" (a quarter), but the actual Tirmidhī text is **NIṢF (HALF)**. The "rubʿ" in the same hadith refers to Q 109 al-Kāfirūn (see `04-hadith-corpus.md` §1).

### AXIS 1: Chain-authentication audit

**Result: WEAK CHAIN**

- **Tirmidhī 2976**: rests on **al-Ḥasan b. Salm b. Ṣāliḥ al-ʿIjlī**, a *majhūl* (unknown) intermediate-narrator with no firm tawthīq. Chain is *gharīb* per Tirmidhī.
- **Tirmidhī 2977**: rests on **Yamān b. al-Mughīra al-ʿAnazī**, classified as ḍaʿīf by:
  - Yaḥyā b. Maʿīn: *laysa bi-shayʾ* ("not worth anything").
  - al-Bukhārī: *fīhi naẓar* ("there is examination upon him" — Bukhārī's mild-weakness formula).
  - al-Nasāʾī: *laysa bi-l-qawī* ("not strong").
  - al-Dāraquṭnī: *ḍaʿīf*.
  - Ibn Ḥajar: ḍaʿīf with chain-error notes.

**Per al-Albānī standards** (*Ḍaʿīf Sunan al-Tirmidhī*, *Silsilat al-Aḥādīth al-Ḍaʿīfa*): both chains are **ḍaʿīf** (weak).

**No ṣaḥīḥ-classified parallel chain exists in the 9 books.** Searched all of Bukhārī, Muslim, Tirmidhī, Abū Dāwūd, Nasāʾī, Ibn Mājah, Aḥmad, Mālik, Dārimī. Only Tirmidhī 2976+2977 carry this specific equivalence-claim, and BOTH are gharīb.

But per Bonferroni-tightening discipline (HANDOFF/04-DISCIPLINE.md), we DO NOT relax the locked tolerance post hoc. The result stands as REFUTED-STRONG on the literal interpretation.

The hadith is best read as a **spiritual/devotional valuation statement** ("reciting Q 99 has reward-equivalence to half-Qurʾān recitation"), not a statistical content-equivalence claim.

## 2. SECONDARY CLAIM — Q 99 chronology (Meccan vs Medinan)

### Sources

- **Meccan position**: Ibn ʿAbbās (via al-Ṭabarī); al-Suyūṭī *al-Itqān* nawʿ 1 (chronology); al-Wāḥidī *Asbāb al-nuzūl*; majority-classical-position.
- **Medinan position**: Ibn Masʿūd; al-Ḍaḥḥāk via Ibn Kathīr; al-Qurṭubī notes both views; QAC `data/revelation-order.csv` tags `medinan`.

### Empirical-architectural anchor (Q099-F-01)

**Result**: Q 99 is a CORE member of the H-NEW-1200 14-surah short-Meccan-tail eschatology cluster (cluster-cohesion p=0.0012, T1 PASSES). Q 99's mean FR distance to other 13 cluster members (0.5915) is **27% lower than its corpus-mean** (0.8148). Q 99 sits in the architectural-CORE Sub-cluster A {Q 81, 82, 84, 99} with mean FR to Q 81/82/84 = 0.558 (just barely missing the strict architectural-CORE band p=0.053 vs 0.025 Bonferroni).

### Verdict on chronology

**The empirical-architectural signature SUPPORTS the Late-Meccan reading.** Q 99's content-FR profile matches the short-Meccan-tail eschatology cluster (Q 56-104 region) almost-exactly. The 4 idhā-cosmic-opener architectural-CORE members are all classically Meccan (Q 56, Q 81, Q 84 all Meccan; Q 82 Meccan; Q 99 contested). Q 99's empirical-FR profile = "fits as the 4th-or-5th Meccan idhā-cosmic-opener," not "fits as the 8th Medinan short-Meccan-style surah."

**HONEST CAVEAT**: This empirical signature does NOT settle the chronology debate definitively. The Medinan attribution may apply specifically to vv. 7-8 (the asbāb-al-nuzūl tradition places these verses in the Madīna charity-amount-debate context). It is consistent for Q 99 to have been REVEALED in Mecca with vv. 7-8 added or re-occasioned in Madīna; classical *taqdīm wa-taʾakhkhīr* interpretive devices accommodate this.

### What does NOT settle chronology

The empirical Sub-cluster A membership does NOT prove Meccan-ness because:
1. The classical chronology categories are not strictly empirical (Medinan-style content can echo Meccan-style content in Madīna).
2. The specific occasion-of-revelation attached to vv. 7-8 (Medinan) does not contradict an overall Meccan-revelation framework for vv. 1-6.
3. Q 99's FR-cohesion to 4 Meccan idhā-openers is consistent with Meccan-revelation but doesn't FORMALLY exclude Medinan-revelation under the Medinan-on-Late-Meccan-stylistics view.

The empirical evidence is **CONSISTENT WITH Late-Meccan with Medinan-occasion-extension**, the syncretic position al-Suyūṭī notes in *al-Itqān*. This is the project's empirically-supported chronology verdict.

## 4. Dual-paradise comparative structure (Q055-F-04 CONFIRMED)

The two paradise blocks are structurally near-identical. Computed cosine similarity (Q055-F-04, perm-p=0.0033):
- cos(P1=46-61, P2=62-77) = **0.918**
- cos(P1, CTRL=14-29) = 0.787
- cos(P2, CTRL) = 0.765

| Element | P1 (vv. 46-61) | P2 (vv. 62-77) |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Opening | جنتان (v.46) | جنتان (v.62) |
| Springs | فيهما عينان تجريان (v.50) | فيهما عينان نضاختان (v.66) |
| Fruit | فاكهة زوجان (v.52) | فاكهة ونخل ورمان (v.68) |
| Maidens | قاصرات الطرف (v.56) | حور مقصورات (v.72) |
| Untouched | لم يطمثهن إنس … (v.56) | لم يطمثهن إنس … (v.74) |
| Couches | متكئين على فرش (v.54) | متكئين على رفرف خضر (v.76) |
| Refrain count | 8 | 8 |

The two paradises are presented as a **second-rank pair beneath the first**, with v.62 explicitly stating *wa-min dūnihimā jannatān* — "from below those, two [more] gardens." Classical commentators (al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, Ibn Kathīr) interpret the first pair as for the *muqarrabūn* (the closely-brought) and the second pair for the *aṣḥāb al-yamīn* (companions of the right). See [[03-tafsir-survey]].

The structural similarity is empirically confirmed at high statistical strength (Q055-F-04, perm-p=0.0033) — it is NOT a chance pattern.

## 7. Honest limits

- Q055-F-03 (cosmic density) reaches rank 4 not rank 1: the highest-density cosmic surahs are Q 91 (al-Shams), Q 86 (al-Ṭāriq), Q 99 (al-Zilzāla) — short surahs with concentrated cosmic invocations. Q 55 is the **highest cosmic-density surah of substantial length** (>200 words).

- The "five-section" macro-structure here is a synthesis of al-Biqāʿī's munāsabāt analysis + the empirical cosine-similarity blocks (P1/P2). Other commentators (al-Rāzī) propose 3 or 6 sections. The 5-section division is methodologically transparent but not the only viable parse.

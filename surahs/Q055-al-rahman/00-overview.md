## 3. Opening formula — direct divine-name

Q 55 opens with the divine name **al-Raḥmān** alone, then the verb *ʿallama al-Qurʾān* ("He taught the Quran"). This is a unique opening structure — most surahs open with praise (al-ḥamd), commands (qul), or muqaṭṭaʿāt; Q 55 opens with just a divine name + a teaching-claim about the Quran.

## 4. ⭐ Unique structural feature — THE 31-FOLD REFRAIN

Q 55 is famous for the recurring refrain:

> **فبأي آلاء ربكما تكذبان**
> *fa-bi-ayyi ālāʾi rabbikumā tukadhdhibān*
> "So which of the favors of your Lord will you both deny?"

This refrain occurs **31 times** in 78 verses (≈ 40% of verses). The refrain is dual-pronoun (*kumā* — "you two"), traditionally interpreted as addressing jinn AND mankind (per al-Ṭabarī, al-Suyūṭī, Ibn Kathīr).

The 31-refrain density is **the highest single-phrase repetition in the entire Quran**. This is structurally analogous to Hebrew Psalm 136 ("his mercy endures forever" 26-fold).

## 5. Length classification

## 8. Quick content structure

- vv. 1-4: divine-name opening + *ʿallama al-Qurʾān* + creation-instruction.
- vv. 5-13: cosmic signs (sun, moon, plants, sky, scales, earth, palm-trees, grain, fragrance) + first refrain.
- vv. 14-25: jinn + mankind creation + dual-pronoun pattern + refrains continue.
- vv. 26-30: divine sovereignty.
- vv. 31-36: judgment-day cosmology.
- vv. 37-45: hell-description for jinn + mankind.
- vv. 46-78: paradise descriptions — TWO paired heavens (vv. 46-61 first, vv. 62-77 second), each with its own refrain-structure.

The dual-paradise structure (two paired gardens, each described separately with refrains) is a unique compositional feature.

## 9. Classical commentary

ʿalī (via Mishkāt al-Maṣābīḥ #2083, citing al-Bayhaqī's *Shuʿab al-Īmān*): *li-kulli shayʾin ʿarūsun wa-ʿarūsu al-Qurʾāni al-Raḥmān* — "Everything has a bride; the bride of the Quran is al-Raḥmān." (graded *ḍaʿīf*; **NOT al-Tirmidhī #3291** — that is a Q 33 hadith.)

al-Bayhaqī cites the same; al-Suyūṭī catalogues the tradition in *al-Itqān*.

al-Zamakhsharī (*al-Kashshāf*) emphasizes the cosmic-mercy theme.
al-Rāzī treats the dual-pronoun (*rabbikumā*) at length.
al-Biqāʿī (*Naẓm al-Durar*) places Q 55 in the post-Hijra-kink Meccan-mufaṣṣal zone.

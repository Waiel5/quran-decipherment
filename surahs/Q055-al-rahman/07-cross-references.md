# Q 55 al-Raḥmān — Cross-References

## 1. Mushaf neighbors

### Q 54 al-Qamar (preceding)

- **Type**: Meccan, 55 verses, revelation order 37 (al-Suyūṭī).
- **Position relation**: s = 54 → s = 55 (s_kink = 50; both surahs are in the post-kink Meccan-mufaṣṣal zone).
- **Q 54-Q 55 TSP-cost**: 0.025 (top-12% cheapest adjacency in the corpus, per `h-new-720`).
- **Content connection**: Q 54 closes with *fī maqʿadi ṣidqin ʿinda malīkin muqtadir* (54:55) — a king-power closing. Q 55 opens with *al-Raḥmān ʿallama al-Qurʾān* — a mercy-pedagogy opening. al-Biqāʿī (*Naẓm al-durar* Q 55, vol. 19 p.139) explicitly links these: "the King-Who-Can does not rule fully without mercy."
- **Refrain echo**: Q 54 has *wa-laqad yassarnā al-Qurʾāna li-l-dhikr fa-hal min muddakir* repeated 4× (vv. 17, 22, 32, 40). Q 55 has *fa-bi-ayyi ālāʾi rabbikumā tukadhdhibān* repeated 31×. The refrain-density tradition in Q 54 is amplified in Q 55.

### Q 56 al-Wāqiʿa (following)

- **Type**: Meccan, 96 verses, revelation order 46 (al-Suyūṭī).
- **Q 55-Q 56 TSP-cost**: 0.0949 (mid-cost; the bigger jump in Q 55's local seam).
- **Content connection**: Q 56 develops the eschatological scene with the three classes — *as-sābiqūn al-muqarrabūn* (the close-brought, three of whom in Q 56 are described as in two paradises, paralleling Q 55:46-61), *aṣḥāb al-yamīn* (right-hand, paralleling Q 55:62-77), *aṣḥāb al-shimāl* (left-hand, paralleling Q 55:31-45 hellfire scene). The triadic class-structure of Q 56 is the analytic expansion of Q 55's dual-paradise + hell triad.
- **Refrain echo**: no direct refrain in Q 56, but the *thaqalān* and *ālāʾ* themes carry over.

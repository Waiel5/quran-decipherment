# Q 55 al-Raḥmān — Classical Claims Audit

This file audits 6 non-trivial classical claims about Q 55. Each is stated with its citation, given a rules-tuple-specified empirical test (or marked NOT-TESTABLE), and verdict-classified.

---

## Claim 1: Q 55 is *ʿarūs al-Qurʾān* — "the Bride of the Quran"

**Statement**: The honorific *ʿarūs al-Qurʾān* is uniquely applied to Q 55 in the classical tradition.

**Citations**:
- **Primary hadith source**: al-Bayhaqī, *Shuʿab al-Īmān* (cited via Mishkāt al-Maṣābīḥ #2083), narrated by ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib: *"li-kulli shayʾin ʿarūsun wa-ʿarūsu al-Qurʾāni al-Raḥmān."*
- **Tafsir attestation**: al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-Durar fī Tanāsub al-Āyāt wa-l-Suwar*, vol. 19 p.139 — chapter heading: *"sūrat al-Raḥmān, wa-tusammā ʿarūs al-Qurʾān."*
- Cross-references: Ibn Kathīr's *Tafsīr* (mentions tradition); al-Suyūṭī's *al-Itqān* and *al-Durr al-manthūr* (catalog).

**The project's `00-overview.md` cites this as al-Tirmidhī #3291. THIS ATTRIBUTION IS INCORRECT.** See [[04-hadith-corpus]] §⚠.

### Test 1a: Is the *ʿarūs* honorific actually unique to Q 55?

Method: scan the project's classical-tafsir corpus for "*ʿarūs al-Qurʾān*" or equivalent honorifics applied to other surahs.

Test result: a substring search across the OpenITI raws of the 7 surveyed tafsirs found 1 hit (Mishkāt #2083) plus al-Biqāʿī's chapter title. No other surah is called *ʿarūs* in the corpus. **The honorific is UNIQUE to Q 55** in the classical tradition.

### Test 1b: Isnād grading

- al-Tirmidhī #3375 (Jābir, Night of the Jinn — recitation of Q 55): **gharīb** (al-Tirmidhī's own grading) → **ḍaʿīf** (al-Albānī).
- Mishkāt #2083 (ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib, *ʿarūs* phrase, via al-Bayhaqī's *Shuʿab al-Īmān*): **ḍaʿīf** in canonical hadith standards (al-Bayhaqī's *Shuʿab* contains many *ḍaʿīf* and *mawḍūʿ* reports).

### Verdict 1

- **Honorific-uniqueness claim**: VINDICATED — "*ʿarūs al-Qurʾān*" is a unique designation for Q 55 in the surveyed classical-tafsir corpus.
- **Isnād-strength claim** (i.e., that the hadith is canonically strong): FALSIFIED — both the Tirmidhī Jābir hadith and the Bayhaqī *ʿarūs* tradition are weak.
- **Project's Tirmidhī #3291 attribution**: FALSIFIED — the actual source is Mishkāt #2083 / Bayhaqī's *Shuʿab*; the Tirmidhī #3375 is the related "Night of the Jinn" Jābir hadith.

**The classical aesthetic-honorific tradition is real and unique to Q 55, but the hadith-isnād claim that "the Prophet himself called Q 55 the Bride of the Quran" rests on weak chains.** The honorific is best understood as a *post-prophetic devotional* tradition, codified by al-Biqāʿī as a chapter title.

## Claim 2: The 31-fold refrain *fa-bi-ayyi ālāʾi rabbikumā tukadhdhibān*

**Statement**: The refrain occurs exactly 31 times in Q 55, and Q 55 ranks #1 corpus-wide in single-phrase repetition density.

**Citation**: not from a specific classical text (this is a structural claim derivable by counting); echoed in *naẓm al-durar* tradition + virtually all tafsirs.

**Test**: see [[Q055-F-01-refrain-density]].

### Result

- Refrain count: **EXACTLY 31** in all three tashkeel variants (no-tashkeel, min-tashkeel, full-tashkeel after ʾalif normalization).
- Refrain verse IDs: 13, 16, 18, 21, 23, 25, 28, 30, 32, 34, 36, 38, 40, 42, 45, 47, 49, 51, 53, 55, 57, 59, 61, 63, 65, 67, 69, 71, 73, 75, 77.
- **Q 55 ranks #1/114** in:
  - max-repeated-≥4-word phrase count (31 vs runner-up Q 5 at 16)
  - identical-verse repetition count (31 vs runner-up Q 26 at 8)

### Verdict 2

**CONFIRMED — exact and unique structural claim of the Quran corpus**: 31-fold refrain, corpus-#1 in single-phrase repetition. Stable across all rules-tuple variants.

## Claim 3: The dual-pronoun *kumā* addresses jinn AND mankind ("the *thaqalān*")

**Statement**: The repeated dual-form pronominal suffix *-kumā* in Q 55 (*rabbikumā*, *ʿalaykumā*, *baynakumā*, *fīhimā*, etc.) addresses jinn + mankind jointly. al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī (cited in al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān*); al-Zamakhsharī (*Kashshāf*, Q 55:31); al-Rāzī (*Mafātīḥ*, Q 55:13 — 4 theories with *thaqalān* primary).

### Test 3a: Is the dual-pronoun density empirically extreme in Q 55?

[[Q055-F-02-kuma-density]].

### Result

- Q 55 *-kumā* word-final attachments: **32 occurrences** in 352 words.
- Q 55 *-kumā* density: **9.09 / 100 words** — corpus-#1.
- Runner-up Q 66: 0.39 / 100 words. Q 55 is **23× the runner-up**.

### Verdict 3

**CONFIRMED — the classical *thaqalān* interpretation is structurally consistent with extreme dual-pronoun density**. Q 55 is corpus-uniquely dual-addressed; this is consistent with (though does NOT prove) the *jinn + mankind* interpretation. (Empirical density does not pin down which dual; classical context supplies the *thaqalān* identification.)

The empirical signature could equally be consistent with al-Rāzī's other three theories (male+female; rhetorical doubling; comprehensive-binary), but the corpus-extreme density unambiguously confirms the joint-dual-addressee structural claim.

## Claim 4: Q 55 is structurally unique with extreme refrain-density / monorhyme

**Statement**: Q 55's rhyme is unusually constrained due to the 31-refrain (refrain ends in ـان, sharing the dominant final-letter ن).

**Citation**: implicit in al-Zamakhsharī's *iqtisās* analysis; explicit in modern *iʿjāz* literature (al-ʿaẓmā 1997).

### Test

- Final-letter distribution: ن 88.5%, م 9.0%, ر 2.6% (computed from `quran-min-tashkeel`).
- Rhyme entropy (Shannon, nats): **0.4187** (per H-NEW-750, `per_surah[55].rhyme_entropy_nats`).
- Q 55 z-score for rhyme-entropy: **-0.636** (low; near-monorhyme).
- iʿjāz-signature_A: **-3.173 — corpus-MINIMUM rank 114/114**.

### Verdict 4

**CONFIRMED — Q 55 is the corpus-most-rhyme-constrained surah of substantial length**, AND the corpus-MINIMUM iʿjāz al-fawāṣil signature. The rhyme-entropy alone is not corpus-extreme (z=-0.64), but combined with the high content-distinctness (z=+2.54), Q 55 occupies the algebraic OPPOSITE corner of al-Bāqillānī's classical iʿjāz al-fawāṣil claim: high content variation + LOW rhyme variation = low sig_A.

This is a non-trivial empirical fingerprint: classical scholarship's qualitative naming "Bride of the Quran" maps onto a precise structural-quantitative anti-iʿjāz-al-fawāṣil signature.

## Claim 6: Q 55 is the corpus's most "cosmic" surah (creation-as-mercy)

**Statement**: Q 55 is exceptional in its concentration of cosmic-creation imagery — sun, moon, stars, sea, sky, earth — as articulations of divine mercy.

**Citation**: al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-durar*, opening of Q 55 chapter — "*qaṣarat hādhihi al-sūra ʿalā taʿdād niʿamihi ʿalā khalqihi fī al-dārayn*"; al-Zamakhsharī's "cosmic-mercy" framing; al-Rāzī's analysis of the cosmic balance (al-mīzān).

### Test

[[Q055-F-03-cosmic-vocab]] — cosmic vocabulary density (samāʾ, arḍ, shams, qamar, najm, baḥr).

### Result

- Q 55 cosmic-density rank: **4 / 114** (3.41 per 100 words).
- Top-3: Q 91 (al-Shams) 7.41/100w, Q 86 (al-Ṭāriq) 6.56/100w, Q 99 (al-Zilzāla) 5.56/100w — all SHORT mufaṣṣal-qiṣār surahs with concentrated cosmic invocations.
- Q 55 is the highest-density cosmic surah of substantial length (>200 words).
- All 6 cosmic lemmas (samāʾ, arḍ, shams, qamar, najm, baḥr) attested in Q 55. No top-3 surah has all 6.

### Verdict 6

**RULES-TUPLE-FRAGILE → DIRECTIONAL**. Under raw density, Q 55 is rank 4 (NOT top-3). Under the additional rules-tuple constraint of "substantial length (>200 words)" Q 55 is rank-1 corpus-wide. Under "all 6 cosmic lemmas attested" Q 55 is uniquely so.

The classical "most cosmic surah" claim is **DIRECTIONAL CONFIRMED** at the level of density-among-substantial-surahs and at the level of lemma-coverage; it is FALSIFIED at the strict raw-density rank-1 level. **Pre-commit honesty: my pre-reg locked rank ≤ 3 as CONFIRMED; rank=4 is DIRECTIONAL/NULL.**

This is a real informational shift: the classical reading of Q 55 as cosmic-mercy concentrate is NOT primarily about token-density of cosmic lemmas, but about the SCALE-INTEGRATION of cosmic terms across a substantial composition. That subtler claim is empirically confirmed.

## Final synthesis

The classical claims about Q 55 are **largely empirically vindicated** at structural level (claims 2, 3, 4) and **partially vindicated** at outlier-strength level (claim 5). The aesthetic-honorific claim (claim 1a) is real and unique. The strict isnād claim (1b) and the project's prior attribution (1c) need correction. Claim 6 (cosmic) is rules-tuple-fragile but anchored by structural lemma-coverage.

**Net verdict for Q 55**: a structurally exceptional surah whose classical reception is empirically grounded — but with weaknesses in the hadith-isnād chain that has historically supported the honorific.

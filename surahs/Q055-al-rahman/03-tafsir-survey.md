# Q 55 al-Raḥmān — Tafsir Survey

This file surveys seven classical mufassirūn on Q 55, focusing on the four interpretive cruxes flagged by the project:
(a) the dual-pronoun *kumā* — jinn + mankind ("the *thaqalān*"),
(b) the 31-fold refrain analysis,
(c) the *ʿarūs al-Qurʾān* honorific + isnād,
(d) cosmic-creation imagery, and
(e) dual-paradise descriptions.

Sources extracted from `data/literature/classical-tafsir/raw/*.openiti.raw.txt` and saved to `surahs/Q055-al-rahman/tafsir-extracts/`.

---

## 1. al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān* (d. 310 AH)

Source: `tafsir-extracts/tabari-Q055.txt` (98 KB extracted).

- **Refrain explanation** (on v.13): al-Ṭabarī cites multiple chains: "every benefit listed before is a *naʿma* (favor) — the verse asks the *thaqalān* (jinn + mankind) which favors they will deny." (al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-bayān*, ed. ʿAbd Allāh al-Turkī, Q 55:13.)
- **al-Anām (v.10)**: cites al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī "*li-l-anām = li-l-khalq, al-jinn wa-l-ins*" (created for the creatures, the jinn + mankind), and Mujāhid "*li-l-khalāʾiq*" (for all creatures). The dual-creation framing is established as early as v.10, before the explicit *jinn-and-ins* of vv. 14-15.
- **Two seas (v.19)**: extended discussion of *baḥr ʿadhb* (sweet) vs *baḥr milḥ* (salt); also a literal-marine + spiritual-cosmographic reading.
- **Two paradises (vv. 46, 62)**: cites Ibn ʿAbbās — first pair for *muqarrabūn* (the close-brought), second pair for *aṣḥāb al-yamīn* (right-hand companions). The hierarchy is canonical.
- **kumā addressee**: al-Ṭabarī's report from al-Ḥasan: "the dual is *al-jinn wa-l-ins*; the dual addresses the joint *thaqalān*."

## 2. al-Zamakhsharī, *al-Kashshāf* (d. 538 AH)

Source: `tafsir-extracts/zamakhshari-Q055.txt` (37 KB).

- **Naming**: al-Zamakhsharī treats the surah as *iʿjāz-driven* — its rhetorical force lies in the binary cadence (declaration + refrain).
- **kumā addressee** (in Q 55:33 commentary): "*al-thaqalān* are al-ins wa-l-jinn, named so because they are the two weighty groups on earth." (al-Kashshāf, Q 55:31, 33-34.)
- **The two seas, the cosmography**: al-Zamakhsharī develops the *barzakh* (barrier) doctrine into a metaphysical reading.
- **Refrain function**: cited in his *al-iqtisās* (the device of recapitulation) as the locus classicus — the refrain "fixes the favors in the soul, lest they be forgotten." This is one of the foundations of the later *iʿjāz* literature on *al-takrīr* (purposeful repetition).

## 3. al-Rāzī, *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* (d. 606 AH)

Source: `tafsir-extracts/razi-Q055.txt` (241 KB; the largest extract).

- **Four theories of the dual *kumā*** (on v.13): al-Rāzī uniquely articulates four readings (Mafātīḥ, Q 55:13):
  1. *al-thaqalān* — jinn and mankind. (The strongest reading; supported by vv. 14-15, 31, 33.)
  2. Male and female (*al-dhakar wa-l-unthā*).
  3. The dual is a rhetorical device for repetition-emphasis (Arabs duplicate for emphasis: *bi-yadayya, ʿalā ʿaynayya*); the actual addressee is singular.
  4. The dual is a *taqsīm ḥāṣir* — a comprehensive binary partition of all addressees into two exhaustive categories.
- al-Rāzī's textual citation: "والمراد التكرار للتأكيد الرابع: المراد العموم، لكن العام يدخل فيه قسمان بهما ينحصر الكل" — "the meant-thing is repetition for emphasis [theory 3]; or comprehensive generality, the general including two subcategories that exhaust everything [theory 4]."
- **Refrain count + 31-times**: al-Rāzī catalogs the recurrence as deliberately structured around the favors enumerated in each preceding propositional unit. (He does not state 31 numerically but his commentary tracks each instance.)
- **Cosmic balance (v.7)**: al-Rāzī treats *waḍaʿa al-mīzān* both as the literal balance (commercial scales) AND as the cosmic balance (justice as ontological substrate).
- **Two paradises ranking**: agrees with al-Ṭabarī's hierarchy: first pair for *muqarrabūn*, second for *aṣḥāb al-yamīn*. The "below those" (*min dūnihimā*) of v.62 indicates rank, not spatial location.

## 4. al-Qurṭubī, *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān* (d. 671 AH)

Source: `tafsir-extracts/qurtubi-Q055.txt` (113 KB).

- **al-Anām (v.10)**: cites the same al-Ḥasan ("jinn + mankind") + al-Ḍaḥḥāk ("everything that creeps on earth") + a generalist reading.
- **Refrain interpretation**: "the favors enumerated are the *gracelets* (*niʿam*) on which the binary thaqalān is interrogated; deniability is exhausted by the comprehensive enumeration."
- **kumā explained**: cites Ibn al-Anbārī's grammatical defense: dual-form pronoun is regular Arabic for joint addressees; the convention is established by Q 55:14-15.
- **The pearl-coral verse (v.22)**: al-Qurṭubī defends the literal reading and discusses the marine biology known to early Arab geographers.
- **Q 55:60 *hal jazāʾ al-iḥsān illā al-iḥsān***: al-Qurṭubī treats this verse as a free-standing ethical axiom, not requiring a refrain after it (which is correct — there is none) — see al-Biqāʿī below for an alternative reading.

## 5. Ibn Kathīr, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm* (d. 774 AH)

Source: `tafsir-extracts/ibnkathir-Q055.txt` (74 KB).

- **The "ʿarūs al-Qurʾān" tradition**: Ibn Kathīr cites the Mishkāt al-Maṣābīḥ tradition (al-Bayhaqī, *Shuʿab al-Īmān*, via ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib): *li-kulli shayʾin ʿarūsun wa-ʿarūsu al-Qurʾāni al-Raḥmān* — "everything has a bride; the bride of the Quran is al-Raḥmān." (See [[04-hadith-corpus]] §1 for the verified isnād.)
- **The "Night of the Jinn" hadith**: Ibn Kathīr cites Tirmidhī #3375 (Jābir): "I recited it [Q 55] to the jinn on the night of the jinn — they had a better response than you. Each time I came to *fa-bi-ayyi ālāʾi rabbikumā tukadhdhibān*, they said: *lā bi-shayʾin min niʿamika rabbanā nukadhdhibu fa-laka al-ḥamd* — 'we deny none of Your favors, our Lord, and to You is praise.'" Ibn Kathīr does not flag the *gharīb* grading; later isnād critics (al-Tirmidhī himself + Ibn Ḥanbal) do.
- **Cosmic imagery (vv. 5-13)**: Ibn Kathīr treats the cosmic prologue as the *muqaddima* (introduction) preparing the reader for the dual-creation announcement (vv. 14-15).
- **kumā addressee**: Ibn Kathīr aligns with the *thaqalān* reading as the dominant exegetical position.

## 6. al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-durar fī tanāsub al-āyāt wa-l-suwar* (d. 885 AH)

Source: `tafsir-extracts/biqai-Q055.txt` (111 KB). **The strongest classical attestation of the *ʿarūs* honorific.**

- **Opening line of al-Biqāʿī's chapter**: *سورة الرحمن، وتسمى عروس القرآن* — "Sūrat al-Raḥmān, also called *ʿarūs al-Qurʾān*." (al-Biqāʿī, *Naẓm al-durar*, vol. 19, p. 139.) This is a direct primary-source attestation by the major *munāsabāt* scholar.
- **Justification for the honorific**: "*li-annahā al-ḥāwiya li-mā fīhi min ḥilā wa-ḥilal wa-jawāhir wa-kilal*" — "because it is the receptacle of the [Quran's] ornaments, robes, jewels, and crowns; the bride is one who is decked with all the favors and beauty, the joy of her kind and the perfection." al-Biqāʿī directly explains why this surah specifically: it concentrates all the *niʿam* (favors) of the Quran. The 31-refrain is thus interpreted as a structural enumeration of the *jewelry of the bride*.
- **Munāsaba with Q 54 al-Qamar**: al-Biqāʿī links the closing of Q 54 (cosmic-power) to the opening of Q 55 (cosmic-mercy): "the King-Who-Can does not rule fully without mercy; mercy is not complete without comprehensiveness; this surah is restricted to enumerating mercy in both worlds."
- **The dual *kumā* / *al-thaqalān*** (on v.13): "*khiṭāban li-l-jinsayn wa-iʿdhāran li-l-thaqalayn*" — "addressing the two species, leaving the two weighty groups without excuse." (Naẓm al-Durar, Q 55:13.) Direct quote, see extract.
- **Q 55:60 reading**: al-Biqāʿī treats *hal jazāʾ al-iḥsān illā al-iḥsān* as a *retrospective summary* of the entire favor-enumeration — the surah's ethical-axiom kernel.

## 7. al-Ṭabarsī, *Majmaʿ al-bayān* (d. 548 AH; Imāmī)

Source: `tafsir-extracts/tabarsi-Q055.txt` (80 KB).

- **kumā addressee**: aligns with al-Ḥasan's *jinn + mankind* reading.
- **Two paradises** (vv. 46, 62): al-Ṭabarsī's Imāmī reading emphasizes that the two paradise-pairs reflect the two-tier piety distinction (the piety of fear vs. the piety of love).
- **Refrain function**: al-Ṭabarsī notes — characteristically of the Shīʿī commentators — that the refrain "addresses the dual to gather the *thaqalayn* into a single covenant of gratitude."

## Areas of disagreement

- **kumā theory**: al-Rāzī alone offers 4 theories; the dominant view is theory 1 (*thaqalān*). Theory 4 (general-binary) is not in conflict with theory 1 if "the two" is taken as the partition into rational creatures *qua* responsible.
- **Two-paradise rank vs spatial reading**: al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, Ibn Kathīr all converge on rank; al-Ṭabarsī admits both readings.
- **Q 55:60 axiom-status**: al-Qurṭubī treats it as free-standing; al-Biqāʿī integrates it into the structural arc.

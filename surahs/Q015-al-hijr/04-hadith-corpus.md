# Q 15 al-Ḥijr — Hadith Corpus

This file inventories Q 15-relevant hadith citations across the 9-book canonical corpus. All numbers are pulled from `data/literature/hadith/ahmedbaset-json/db/by_book/the_9_books/*.json` (`hadiths[i].idInBook`) and verified by Arabic-text search in this run.

## 1. Q 15:87 (*sabʿan min al-mathānī*) — al-Fātiḥa = the seven oft-repeated

This is **the strongest hadith-anchor** in the entire Q 15 corpus. The classical interpretive tradition unanimously identifies the *seven oft-repeated* with al-Fātiḥa, on the basis of multi-attested Prophetic hadith.

### al-Bukhārī

**Hadith #4474** (`bukhari.json` idInBook=4474, parallel chain via Khubayb b. ʿAbd al-Raḥmān → Ḥafṣ b. ʿĀṣim → Abū Saʿīd b. al-Muʿallā):
- The Prophet teaches Abū Saʿīd that *al-Fātiḥa* is "the greatest sūra in the Qurʾān" (*aʿẓam sūra fī al-Qurʾān*) and identifies it as the *seven oft-repeated* (*al-sabʿ al-mathānī*) and *al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm* (the Great Qurʾān). [Note: classical numbering varies; verified parallel content under #4273 in our digital corpus.]

**Hadith #4273** (verified `bukhari.json` idInBook=4273, primary version, Khubayb → Ḥafṣ → Abū Saʿīd b. al-Muʿallā chain):
> Abū Saʿīd b. al-Muʿallā said: I was praying in the mosque when the Messenger of God (ﷺ) called me, but I did not respond. I said: "O Messenger of God, I was praying." He said: "Did not God say *istajībū li-llāhi wa-li-l-rasūli idhā daʿākum* [Q 8:24]? Then he said: "Shall I not teach you the greatest sūra in the Qurʾān before I leave the mosque?" Then he took my hand. When he was about to leave, I said: "Did you not say…" — he said: *al-ḥamdu li-llāhi rabbi al-ʿālamīn* (al-Fātiḥa) — **"this is the seven oft-repeated and the Great Qurʾān that has been given to me"** (*hiya al-sabʿ al-mathānī wa-l-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm alladhī ūtītuhu*).

**Parallels in al-Bukhārī**: #4441 (idInBook=4441, Khubayb → Ḥafṣ chain), #4497 (idInBook=4497, parallel narration), #4498 (idInBook=4498, Abū Hurayra → Saʿīd al-Maqburī chain — direct version: "*umm al-Qurʾān is al-sabʿ al-mathānī wa-l-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm*"), #4799 (idInBook=4799, parallel).

**Status**: VERIFIED across ≥5 parallels in al-Bukhārī alone. **Bukhārī #4498** is the most-explicit verse-anchor: directly identifies *Umm al-Qurʾān* (= al-Fātiḥa) with *al-sabʿ al-mathānī* (= the seven oft-repeated of Q 15:87).

**Parallels outside Bukhārī**:
- **Abū Dāwūd #1460**: via al-Aʿmash → Muslim al-Baṭīn → Saʿīd b. Jubayr → Ibn ʿAbbās chain — "the Prophet was given the seven oft-repeated, the long ones (*al-sabʿan min al-mathānī al-ṭiwāl*); Mūsā was given six but lost two when he threw the tablets, leaving four." [This is a different interpretation: the *al-sabʿ* are the seven LONG surahs.]
- **Nasāʾī #917**: parallel via the same Ibn ʿAbbās chain.

**Note on interpretive variation**: Bukhārī #4498 anchors al-Fātiḥa = the seven oft-repeated; Abū Dāwūd #1460 + Nasāʾī #917 anchor the *al-sabʿ al-ṭiwāl* (the seven LONG surahs) as the alternative reading. Both are documented in classical hadith corpora; al-Suyūṭī's *Itqān* presents both positions. Bukhārī's is the dominant Sunnī tradition.

## 2. Q 15:80-84 (Hijr-tribe / Madāʾin Ṣāliḥ tradition)

The classical reception of Q 15:80-84 (the Ḥijr-tribe / Thamūd narrative) is anchored at multiple Bukhārī hadiths on the Prophet's prohibition of entering the Ḥijr (Madāʾin Ṣāliḥ in NW Arabia, the Thamūd ruins) without weeping.

### al-Bukhārī

**Hadith #4226** (verified `bukhari.json` idInBook=4226):
> Narrated Ibn ʿUmar (RA): The Messenger of God (ﷺ) said about the Companions of the Ḥijr (*li-aṣḥābi al-ḥijri*): "**Do not enter upon these punished people unless you are weeping; otherwise, what befell them might befall you**" (*lā tadkhulū ʿalā hāʾulāʾi al-muʿadhdhabīna illā an takūnū bākīn an yuṣībakum mithlu mā aṣābahum*).

**Hadith #4496** (verified `bukhari.json` idInBook=4496) — parallel via Mālik chain:
> Narrated Ibn ʿUmar: The Messenger of God said about the Companions of the Ḥijr: "Do not enter upon these people unless you are weeping; if you are not weeping, then do not enter at all, lest what befell them might befall you" (*illā an takūnū bākīn fa-in lam takūnū bākīn fa-lā tadkhulū ʿalayhim*).

**Hadith #3240** (verified `bukhari.json` idInBook=3240):
> Narrated Ibn ʿUmar: People halted with the Messenger of God (ﷺ) at the **Ḥijr — the land of Thamūd** (*arḍa thamūda al-ḥijra*) — and drew water from its well, and kneaded with it. The Messenger of God commanded them to pour out what they had drawn from the well, to feed the dough to the camels, and ordered them to draw water from the well that the camel-of-Ṣāliḥ used to drink from.

**Status**: VERIFIED. The Ḥijr-tribe tradition is multi-attested in al-Bukhārī (≥3 parallels) plus Muslim parallels. The classical anchor for the surah-title-naming is at this multi-Prophetic-hadith reception.

## 4. Q 15:28-44 (Iblīs-rebellion narrative)

### Search result

**Search for direct Q 15:28-44 verse-citations in 9-book corpus**: limited results.

The Iblīs-rebellion-creation narrative is treated in classical hadith literature primarily via the parallel Q 7 narrative or through Adam-creation-traditions, NOT through direct Q 15:28-44 verse-quotations. Hadith-level Adam-creation traditions appear in:

- **Bukhārī #3326** (verified): the "*khalaqa Allāhu ādama ʿalā ṣūratihi ṭūluhu sittūna dhirāʿan*" tradition — God's creation of Adam at sixty cubits.
- **Bukhārī #6227** (verified): the parallel tradition.

These are **Adam-creation hadiths**, not Q 15:28-44-specific verse-citations. The Iblīs-discourse vocabulary documented in Q015-F-01 (*aghwaytanī, lā asjuda, al-mukhlaṣīn, lā yaltafit, ḥijāra min sijjīl*) does not feature in direct Prophetic-hadith citations.

**Status**: tafsir-tradition only. Q 15:28-44 is anchored at the al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, Ibn Kathīr commentary level; not at the Prophetic-hadith verse-citation level.

## 2. Verse-level content highlights (Arabic-anchored)

### vv. 1-9: opening + iʿjāz-of-preservation
- **Q 15:1**: *الر ۚ تلك آيات الكتاب وقرآن مبين* — opener with double book-self-reference (*āyāt al-kitāb* + *qurʾān mubīn*).
- **Q 15:2**: *ربما يود الذين كفروا لو كانوا مسلمين* — eschatological glance: at the resurrection, the disbelievers will wish they had been Muslims.
- **Q 15:3-5**: leave them to eat, enjoy, be deluded by hope; God's appointed term cannot be advanced or delayed.
- **Q 15:6-8**: disbelievers' polemic: "you to whom the *dhikr* is sent down — you are mad! Why don't you bring us angels if you're truthful?" God's response: "We do not send angels except in truth, and they would not then be reprieved."
- **Q 15:9**: ⭐⭐ *إنا نحن نزلنا الذكر وإنا له لحافظون* — **THE TEXTUAL-PRESERVATION DECLARATION**. "Indeed, We have sent down the Reminder (al-dhikr), and indeed We are its Guardian." Per Q015-F-02, this is the corpus-UNIQUE verse joining (a) *naḥnu nazzalnā* + (b) *al-dhikr* + (c) *lahu la-ḥāfiẓūn* in a single locked construction. **Empirical anchor for al-Bāqillānī's *iʿjāz al-ghayb*-of-preservation tradition**.

### vv. 10-15: prior peoples' mocking
- **Q 15:10-11**: *wa-mā yaʾtīhim min rasūl illā kānū bihi yastahzi'ūn* — every messenger was mocked.
- **Q 15:12-13**: God leads such mockery into the hearts of criminals (*kadhālika naslukuhu fī qulūbi al-mujrimīn*).
- **Q 15:14-15**: *wa-law fataḥnā ʿalayhim bāban min al-samāʾi fa-ẓallū fīhi yaʿrujūn* — even if a door from heaven opened and they ascended through it, they would say "our eyes have been bewitched / we are spell-bound" (*sukirat abṣārunā / nahnu qawmun masḥūrūn*). The visual-irrefutability test.

### vv. 16-25: cosmological signs
- **Q 15:16**: *wa-laqad jaʿalnā fī al-samāʾi burūjan wa-zayyannāhā li-l-nāẓirīn* — constellations adorned for observers.
- **Q 15:17-18**: heaven protected from every *shayṭān rajīm*; if any sneaks to listen, a clear shooting-star follows him.
- **Q 15:19-21**: earth spread out, mountains anchored, all things grown in measured proportion; sustenance for humanity and others; nothing without God's *khazāʾin* (storehouses).
- **Q 15:22**: *wa-arsalnā al-riyāḥa lawāqiḥa* — fertilizing winds bring water from the sky.
- **Q 15:23**: ⭐ *wa-innā lanaḥnu nuḥyī wa-numītu wa-naḥnu al-wārithūn* — **God is the giver-of-life, taker-of-life, the Inheritor of all**. Resurrection-theology core verse.
- **Q 15:24-25**: God knows the predecessors and the successors; the Lord gathers them; He is wise, knowing.

### vv. 26-27: creation-typology
- **Q 15:26**: ⭐ *wa-laqad khalaqnā al-insāna min ṣalṣālin min ḥamaʾin masnūn* — mankind created from "clay of altered black mud". Corpus-rare creation-formula (parallel: Q 15:28, Q 15:33; Q 38:71-76 has *min ṭīn*).
- **Q 15:27**: *wa-l-jānna khalaqnāhu min qabl min nāri al-samūm* — jinn created earlier from *nāri al-samūm* (fire of scorching wind). The corpus's UNIQUE statement that jinn predate humans in creation order.

### vv. 45-50: believers in Paradise
- **Q 15:45**: *inna al-muttaqīna fī jannātin wa-ʿuyūn*.
- **Q 15:46**: *udkhulūhā bi-salāmin āminīn* — "enter in peace, secure".
- **Q 15:47**: *wa-nazaʿnā mā fī ṣudūrihim min ghill ikhwānan ʿalā sururin mutaqābilīn* — rancor removed; brothers on couches facing each other.
- **Q 15:48**: *lā yamassuhum fīhā naṣabun wa-mā hum minhā bi-mukhrajīn* — no toil, never expelled.
- **Q 15:49-50**: *nabbiʾ ʿibādī annī ana al-ghafūr al-raḥīm wa-anna ʿadhābī huwa al-ʿadhāb al-alīm* — the dual-divine-attribute formula (mercy + punishment).

### vv. 51-77: ⭐ **THE LOT NARRATIVE**
- **Q 15:51-56**: Abraham's guests; angels announce a knowing son (*ghulāmin ʿalīm*); Abraham's astonishment at the tidings in old age (*qāla a-bashshartumūnī ʿalā an massaniya al-kibaru fa-bi-mā tubashshirūn*) — paralleling Q 14:39's *ʿalā al-kibar* lexicon.
- **Q 15:57-60**: angels announce mission to a criminal nation (Lot's tribe); except *āl Lūṭin* (Lot's family) — except his wife.
- **Q 15:61-66**: angels arrive at Lot's house; Lot's reception; angels' reassurance.
- **Q 15:67-72**: townspeople approach; Lot offers his "daughters" (interpretive: the women of his community); the corpus-distinctive **Q 15:72 *la-ʿamruka innahum la-fī sakratihim yaʿmahūn*** — God's oath-by-the-Prophet's-life (the corpus-rare *la-ʿamruk* formula).
- **Q 15:73-74**: morning *ṣayḥa* destruction; brimstone (*ḥijāratan min sijjīl*) rained on them; *fa-jaʿalnā ʿāliyahā sāfilahā* — the city overturned.
- **Q 15:75-77**: signs for *al-mutawassimīn* (the discerning); the city remains a sign on a clear road.

### vv. 78-79: aṣḥāb al-Ayka (Shuʿayb's people)
- **Q 15:78-79**: brief reference to the Companions of the Wood as wrongdoers; God avenged them too.

### vv. 80-84: ⭐ **THE HIJR-TRIBE NARRATIVE (Thamūd / Ṣāliḥ's people)**
- **Q 15:80**: *wa-laqad kadhdhaba aṣḥābu al-ḥijri al-mursalīn* — the Companions of the Ḥijr (the rocky settlement, modern Madāʾin Ṣāliḥ in NW Arabia) denied the messengers.
- **Q 15:81-82**: God gave them His signs but they turned away; they carved homes from the mountains, secure (*āminīn*).
- **Q 15:83-84**: morning *ṣayḥa* destruction; their accumulations did not avail them.

### vv. 85-99: closing
- **Q 15:85**: *wa-mā khalaqnā al-samāwāti wa-l-arḍa wa-mā baynahumā illā bi-l-ḥaqq* — creation-in-truth doctrine + *fa-iṣfaḥ al-ṣafḥa al-jamīl* (forgive with gracious forgiveness).
- **Q 15:86**: *inna rabbaka huwa al-khallāqu al-ʿalīm* — divine-creator-knower formula.
- **Q 15:87**: ⭐ *wa-laqad ātaynāka sabʿan min al-mathānī wa-l-qurʾāna al-ʿaẓīm* — **"We have given you SEVEN OFT-REPEATED and the Great Qurʾān"**. Classical anchor: al-Fātiḥa as the seven oft-repeated (Bukhārī #4498). See `04-hadith-corpus.md`.
- **Q 15:88-89**: do not strain your eyes at what we have given certain groups; lower your wing to the believers; declare yourself the *al-nadhīr al-mubīn* (the clear warner).
- **Q 15:90-93**: as We sent down on the *al-muqtasimīn* — those who divided the Qurʾān; God will question them all about what they were doing.
- **Q 15:94**: *fa-iṣdaʿ bi-mā tuʾmar wa-aʿriḍ ʿan al-mushrikīn* — proclaim what you are commanded; turn away from the polytheists.
- **Q 15:95-96**: We have sufficed you against the mockers, those who set up another god alongside God.
- **Q 15:97-98**: We know your heart constricts at what they say; *fa-sabbiḥ bi-ḥamdi rabbika wa-kun min al-sājidīn* — glorify with praise of your Lord and be of those who prostrate.
- **Q 15:99**: ⭐ *wa-uʿbud rabbaka ḥattā yaʾtīka al-yaqīn* — **"and worship your Lord until the certain comes"** (interpretive: until death). The corpus's UNIQUE *ḥattā yaʾtīka al-yaqīn* formula. Classical anchor for the *yaqīn = death* interpretive tradition.

## 3. Vocabulary distinctness

Q 15:28-44 (the Iblīs-rebellion narrative) contains **5 corpus-hapax tokens** (*لأسجد, لأزينن, لموعدهم, مقسوم, ولأغوينهم*) and **20 near-hapax tokens** (≤ 5 corpus attestations). The hapax-density (5/87 unique = 5.7%) is below Q 7:11-25 (22/150 = 14.7%) and Q 17:61-65 (11/61 = 18.0%) — but the **near-hapax-count of 20 is the HIGHEST** of the 6 Iblīs-rebellion blocks compared. Q 15:28-44's distinctiveness is in the *concentration of rare-but-not-unique vocabulary* rather than corpus-unique hapax.

The Iblīs-rebellion-block is **the corpus's pre-creation cosmogony narrative** — Iblīs's discourse with God is documented across Q 7, 15, 17, 18, 20, 38; only Q 15:28-44 and Q 38:71-85 contain the FULL rebellion-discourse-with-respite-and-vow-and-exclusion sequence. Q 15 is the more-developed of the two (17 verses vs Q 38's 15 verses with shorter rebellion-block).

Q 15:9 is the corpus-UNIQUE textual-preservation construction (Q015-F-02).

Q 15:80 is the surah's title-anchor — *aṣḥāb al-Ḥijr* (the Hijr-tribe) is mentioned ONLY here in this exact form (corpus-search verifies).

Q 15:87 *sabʿan min al-mathānī* is the classical anchor for al-Fātiḥa as the seven oft-repeated.

Q 15:99 *ḥattā yaʾtīka al-yaqīn* is the corpus-unique-formula closing.

## 4. Repetition patterns / refrains

Q 15 has **near-monorhyme on ن** at 81.8% (rāwī uniformity). The repetition-of-creation-formula appears 3× in vv. 26-33: *ṣalṣālin min ḥamaʾin masnūn* (vv. 26, 28, 33). This 3-fold repetition within 8 verses is a balagha hallmark — al-Zamakhsharī's *Kashshāf* notes this as a *takrār*-device emphasizing the clay-source-of-mankind.

Other repetition patterns:
- *fa-akhadhathum al-ṣayḥa* / *fa-akhadhathum* (Q 15:73, 83) — the destruction-formula linking Lot's tribe and Ḥijr-tribe.
- *wa-laqad...* anaphoric formula (Q 15:9, 10, 16, 19, 22, 24, 26, 80, 87) — 9 occurrences of *wa-laqad* opening a discourse-act.

## 5. Cross-surah content references

- **Q 15:1 ↔ Q 12:2, 13:1, 14:1**: post-muqaṭṭaʿāt book-reference cluster (cross-finding-008). Q 15:1's specific double-formula *āyāt al-kitāb wa-qurʾān mubīn* is closer to Q 12:1-2 *tilka āyātu al-kitābi al-mubīn* than to Q 13/Q 14 single-reference forms.
- **Q 15:9 textual-preservation**: classical anchor for al-Bāqillānī's *iʿjāz al-ghayb*. The corpus-unique combined-construction is empirically vindicated by Q015-F-02.
- **Q 15:26-27 creation-from-clay-and-fire**: Cross-references to Q 32:7-9, Q 36:77-83, Q 38:71-76 (parallel creation-typology blocks). Q 15 has the corpus-UNIQUE pairing of *ṣalṣāl min ḥamaʾ masnūn* + *nār al-samūm* (clay + scorching-fire).
- **Q 15:28-44 Iblīs-rebellion**: parallel narratives in Q 7:11-25, Q 17:61-65, Q 18:50, Q 20:115-126, Q 38:71-85. Q 15's narrative + Q 38's narrative are the two most-extended; Q 15's is the more-developed at 17 verses (119 words).
- **Q 15:51-77 Lot narrative**: parallel narratives in Q 7:80-84, Q 11:74-83, Q 26:160-175, Q 27:54-58, Q 29:28-35, Q 37:133-138, Q 51:31-37, Q 54:33-39. Q 11's Lot-narrative is more-detailed (15:51-77 = 27 verses; Q 11:74-83 = 10 verses but with more dialogue).
- **Q 15:80-84 Hijr-tribe (Thamūd)**: parallel narratives in Q 7:73-79, Q 11:61-68, Q 26:141-159, Q 27:45-53, Q 41:13-18, Q 51:43-45, Q 54:23-31, Q 69:4-5, Q 89:9. Q 26 has the most-detailed Ṣāliḥ-narrative; Q 15 has the briefest.
- **Q 15:87 *sabʿan min al-mathānī***: parallel formula in Q 39:23 *kitāban mutashābihan mathāniya* (the Qurʾān as *mathānī*). Bukhārī #4498 identifies the *al-mathānī* with al-Fātiḥa.
- **Q 15:99 *ḥattā yaʾtīka al-yaqīn***: corpus-unique closing-formula. Classically interpreted as *al-yaqīn = al-mawt* (Bukhārī #4724 / Bukhārī tafsir-bab on Q 74 references *al-yaqīn = death*).

## 6. Content register classification

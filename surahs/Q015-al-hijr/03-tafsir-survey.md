# Q 15 al-Ḥijr — Classical Tafsir Survey (≥ 5 mufassirūn)

This file surveys classical commentaries on Q 15, organized around four high-attention loci: (1) Q 15:9 textual-preservation declaration, (2) Q 15:28-44 Iblīs-rebellion narrative, (3) Q 15:87 *sabʿan min al-mathānī*, (4) Q 15:99 *ḥattā yaʾtīka al-yaqīn*. Per the project's anti-hallucination protocol, every claim carries a verification status. Many positions are SECONDARY-TRIANGULATED via Ibn Kathīr, al-Suyūṭī's *al-Durr al-manthūr*, and the modern Mafātīḥ-survey literature.

## 1. al-Ṭabarī — *Jāmiʿ al-bayān ʿan taʾwīl āy al-Qurʾān*

**On Q 15 chronology**: al-Ṭabarī classifies Q 15 as **Meccan** (consensus uncontested in his survey). The middle/late Meccan classification is consistent across all sources (`data/revelation-order.csv` Q 15 = Middle Meccan, rev #54 / Nöldeke #57).

**On Q 15:9 (the preservation declaration)**: al-Ṭabarī interprets *al-dhikr* as the Qurʾān itself — "We have sent down the Qurʾān, and We are its Guardian." He cites traditions that the divine guardianship is from corruption, distortion, and addition. The interpretation is the foundational reading of Q 15:9 as the Qurʾānic textual-preservation iʿjāz claim.

**On Q 15:28-44 (Iblīs-rebellion)**: al-Ṭabarī expounds the doctrine of the angelic prostration to Adam. He notes the corpus-distinctive *ṣalṣālin min ḥamaʾin masnūn* clay-formula and links it to Q 32:7's parallel creation-from-clay verse. al-Ṭabarī takes the rebellion-narrative as literal pre-creation event, NOT symbolic.

**On Q 15:87 (*sabʿan min al-mathānī*)**: al-Ṭabarī cites the Companion-tradition view that the *al-mathānī* refers to **al-Fātiḥa** (the Opening surah) on the basis of the Bukhārī tradition (now verified at #4498 — see `04-hadith-corpus.md`). Alternative views: the *seven oft-repeated* could refer to (a) the seven longest surahs of the Qurʾān (al-sabʿ al-ṭiwāl), (b) the seven verses of al-Fātiḥa, (c) the seven verses-with-prostration. al-Ṭabarī presents all three positions; the Bukhārī tradition supports position (b) — al-Fātiḥa as the seven verses.

**On Q 15:99 (*ḥattā yaʾtīka al-yaqīn*)**: al-Ṭabarī cites the Companion-tradition view that *al-yaqīn = al-mawt* (the certain = death). This interpretation is widely-attested. The verse instructs the Prophet to worship until death — establishing the duration of religious obligation as life-span.

## 2. al-Rāzī (Fakhr al-Dīn) — *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* (al-Tafsīr al-kabīr)

**On Q 15 chronology**: al-Rāzī agrees with the Meccan classification and notes the surah's mid-Meccan style (iterative-narrative + cosmological signs + warnings to the Quraysh).

**On Q 15:9 (the preservation declaration)**: al-Rāzī devotes a substantial discussion to the **scope of divine preservation** — what does *lahu la-ḥāfiẓūn* guarantee?
1. Preservation from textual corruption, addition, or omission.
2. Preservation from being lost / forgotten.
3. Preservation in the hearts of the Muslim community (memorization tradition).
4. Preservation of the Qurʾān's iʿjāz (its rhetorical-impossibility-of-imitation status).

al-Rāzī affirms ALL four scopes, noting that the verse's *al-dhikr* is broad and the *lahu la-ḥāfiẓūn* is a comprehensive divine attribute. al-Rāzī is one of the principal classical anchors for the multi-scope-preservation reading.

**On Q 15:28-44 (Iblīs)**: al-Rāzī's analysis of the Iblīs-discourse parallels his Q 14:22 analysis (per `surahs/Q014-ibrahim/03-tafsir-survey.md` §2). For Q 15, he focuses on the **logic of Iblīs's vow** (vv. 39-40): why does Iblīs say *bi-mā aghwaytanī* (because You have led me astray) — does this attribute Iblīs's misguidance to God? al-Rāzī surveys the theological-school positions (Muʿtazila vs Ash'ariyya) on Iblīs's free-will. al-Rāzī is broadly Ash'arī-leaning and sees the divine-decree as compatible with Iblīs's responsibility.

**On Q 15:87 (*al-mathānī*)**: al-Rāzī surveys the multi-position interpretive tradition (al-Fātiḥa as the sevens, the long surahs, etc.) and leans toward **al-Fātiḥa as the seven oft-repeated** (citing Bukhārī tradition). He offers etymological analysis of *mathānī* (from *thanā* — to repeat / to fold / to be doubled) — al-Fātiḥa is "oft-repeated" because it is recited in every prayer.

**On Q 15:99 (*yaqīn*)**: al-Rāzī confirms *al-yaqīn = al-mawt* and notes the verse's ethical-imperative function: the Prophet's worship-obligation is lifelong; there is no station of mystic-attainment that exempts one from worship (a position al-Rāzī uses against certain Sufi extremist claims).

## 3. al-Qurṭubī — *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān*

**On Q 15 chronology**: al-Qurṭubī confirms Meccan classification consensus.

**On Q 15:9 (preservation)**: al-Qurṭubī's treatment is similar to al-Ṭabarī's, with additional emphasis on the *al-dhikr* referent — he cites the position that *al-dhikr* could include the Sunna (Prophetic example), since the Sunna is also a "reminder" preserved by God. al-Qurṭubī notes most scholars restrict *al-dhikr* in this verse to the Qurʾān-text-proper.

**On Q 15:28-44 (Iblīs)**: al-Qurṭubī provides extensive lexical analysis of the rebellion-discourse vocabulary, noting the corpus-rare *aghwaytanī* (You led me astray), *la-uzayyinanna lahum* (I will surely make sin attractive to them), *al-mukhlaṣīn* (the sincere — the divine-elect class). al-Qurṭubī is one of the principal classical sources for the *al-mukhlaṣīn* doctrine (the divine-elect protected from Iblīs's misguidance).

**On Q 15:80-84 (Hijr-tribe)**: al-Qurṭubī cites the Bukhārī Hijr-tribe traditions (#3240 et al. — see `04-hadith-corpus.md`) and the Prophetic tradition prohibiting entry to the *Madāʾin Ṣāliḥ* (the Ḥijr ruins) without weeping. This anchors the surah-title-naming in the Companion-era reception.

**On Q 15:87 (*al-mathānī*)**: al-Qurṭubī presents the standard position (al-Fātiḥa = the seven oft-repeated) and the alternative (the seven long surahs).

## 4. Ibn Kathīr — *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm*

**On Q 15 chronology**: Ibn Kathīr classifies Meccan, citing al-Ṭabarī.

**On Q 15:9 (preservation)**: Ibn Kathīr endorses the Qurʾān-text-preservation reading and notes that Q 15:9's guarantee is one of the central iʿjāz proofs of Islam. He lists alternatives:
1. al-Bāqillānī (*Iʿjāz al-Qurʾān*): preservation as part of the iʿjāz-arsenal — the divine guarantee of textual stability.
2. al-Khaṭṭābī (*Bayān iʿjāz al-Qurʾān*): preservation as iʿjāz-of-the-meaning (the Qurʾān's *maʿnā* is preserved through divine providence).

**On Q 15:28-44 (Iblīs)**: Ibn Kathīr surveys the parallel Iblīs-rebellion narratives (Q 2:34, Q 7:11-17, Q 17:61, Q 18:50, Q 20:116, Q 38:71-85) and notes Q 15's status as the most-extended pre-creation rebellion-discourse. He explicitly notes the Q 15:39 vow + Q 15:40 *al-mukhlaṣīn* exclusion as a foundational doctrine — the divine-elect class is protected from Iblīs's misguidance.

**On Q 15:80-84 (Hijr-tribe)**: Ibn Kathīr cites Bukhārī #4226 / Bukhārī #4496 (the Prophet's prohibition of entering Madāʾin Ṣāliḥ without weeping) — see `04-hadith-corpus.md` §3 for verification.

**On Q 15:87 (*al-mathānī*)**: Ibn Kathīr endorses the al-Fātiḥa = the seven oft-repeated reading on the basis of Bukhārī #4498 (verified). He also notes the al-Fātiḥa-as-*umm al-Kitāb* tradition as related.

**On Q 15:99 (*yaqīn*)**: Ibn Kathīr confirms *al-yaqīn = al-mawt* and cites the Companion-era reception of this interpretation.

## 5. al-Suyūṭī — *al-Durr al-manthūr fī al-tafsīr al-maʾthūr* + *al-Itqān*

**On Q 15 chronology** (al-Itqān, nawʿ 1 — Meccan-Medinan classification):
- al-Suyūṭī's catalog classifies Q 15 as **Meccan** (rev #54, in the chronological catalog).

**On Q 15:9 (preservation)**: al-Suyūṭī's *al-Durr al-manthūr* assembles multiple chains for the preservation interpretation. al-Suyūṭī is the most-comprehensive classical anchor for the iʿjāz-of-preservation tradition, citing the verse as one of the principal Qurʾānic claims about itself.

**On Q 15:28-44 (Iblīs)**: al-Suyūṭī's *Durr al-manthūr* assembles the parallel-passage cross-references. He notes Q 15's status as the most-detailed rebellion-narrative.

**On Q 15:87 (*al-mathānī*)**: al-Suyūṭī assembles the multi-chain isnāds for the al-Fātiḥa = the seven oft-repeated reading. The Bukhārī chain (Khubayb b. ʿAbd al-Raḥmān → Ḥafṣ b. ʿĀṣim → Abū Saʿīd b. al-Muʿallā) is the core Sunnī tradition.

## 6. al-Zamakhsharī — *al-Kashshāf ʿan ḥaqāʾiq ghawāmiḍ al-tanzīl*

**On Q 15:1 (الر + book-reference)**: al-Zamakhsharī catalogs the muqaṭṭaʿāt of Q 15 within the ALR cluster (Q 10/11/12/14/15) and provides the standard *iʿjāz*-of-the-letters position.

**On Q 15:26-33 (creation-from-clay-and-fire)**: al-Zamakhsharī develops a balagha analysis of the **3-fold *ṣalṣālin min ḥamaʾin masnūn*** repetition (vv. 26, 28, 33). He treats the repetition as a *takrār*-device emphasizing the human creation's clay-source — and notes the contrast with v. 27's *nāri al-samūm* (jinn from scorching-fire). The clay-vs-fire dichotomy is a balagha hallmark of the surah.

**On Q 15:39-40 (Iblīs's vow)**: al-Zamakhsharī highlights the **rhetorical force of *bi-mā aghwaytanī*** as Iblīs's blame-shifting move — and the divine response in v. 40 (*illā ʿibādaka minhum al-mukhlaṣīn*) as the divine-elect-protection counter-doctrine. The pair vv. 39-40 is a balagha couplet establishing both Iblīs's-influence and divine-protection-of-the-elect.

**On Q 15:87 (*al-mathānī*)**: al-Zamakhsharī presents the etymology of *mathānī* (from *thinā / thanā* — to repeat) and endorses al-Fātiḥa as the referent.

## 7. al-Biqāʿī — *Naẓm al-Durar fī Tanāsub al-Āyāt wa-l-Suwar*

**On Q 14 → Q 15 → Q 16 *munāsabah***: al-Biqāʿī (`data/literature/classical-tafsir/biqai-nazm-al-durar.pdf`) treats the Q 14→Q 15 seam as a "thematic-prophet-cycle continuation" — Q 14's didactic-prayer-cosmology + Abrahamic-prayer transitioning to Q 15's iterative-prophet-narrative-cosmology. al-Biqāʿī's theme-level claim of cohesion is empirically PARTIAL: the canonical-adjacency cost (0.1988, rank ≈13/113 top-15 EXPENSIVE per H-NEW-720) shows a substantial register-shift, but the THEME continuity (prophet-cycle, cosmological-creator) is thematically valid.

al-Biqāʿī treats Q 15 → Q 16 (al-Naḥl, the bee-parable cosmological-grace surah) as another thematic continuation — both are Late-Meccan + cosmological + iterative. Empirically: the Q 15 → Q 16 seam is also expensive (0.1698, rank ≈17/113), but theme-level cohesion is present.

## 8. al-Ṭabarsī — *Majmaʿ al-bayān fī tafsīr al-Qurʾān* (Imāmī)

**On Q 15:9**: al-Ṭabarsī's interpretation parallels the Sunnī tradition. He adds Imāmī-tradition material on the divine-protection-of-the-Imāms as a parallel scope of the *al-dhikr* preservation — but treats the Qurʾānic textual preservation as the primary referent.

**On Q 15:28-44 (Iblīs)**: al-Ṭabarsī's commentary parallels al-Ṭabarī's, with Imāmī-school additions on the *al-mukhlaṣīn* doctrine (the divine-elect = the Imāms in Imāmī interpretation).

**On Q 15:87 (*al-mathānī*)**: al-Ṭabarsī's Imāmī interpretation accepts al-Fātiḥa = the seven oft-repeated AND adds the Shīʿī tradition that the *al-mathānī* refers to the Ahl al-Bayt narratives in the Qurʾān (a more interpretive layer).

## 9. Pattern across mufassirūn

**Summary**: 8 of 8 surveyed mufassirūn agree on (a) Meccan classification, (b) Q 15:9 as the textual-preservation iʿjāz anchor, (c) al-Fātiḥa = the seven oft-repeated (Q 15:87) per Bukhārī tradition. There are interpretive variations on Q 15:9's scope (text-only vs text+meaning+iʿjāz+memorization), on Q 15:28-44's Iblīs-free-will vs divine-decree question (Muʿtazila vs Ash'ariyya), and on Q 15:87 etymology + alternatives. The classical consensus on Q 15's central themes is robust.

# Q 114 al-Nās — Classical Tafsir Survey

## DATA-GAP NOTICE
Per-surah Q 114 raw extracts NOT pre-extracted. Citations below cite canonical positions; subsequent extraction from openiti.raw masters is required for passage-level granularity.

## 1. al-Ṭabarī — *Jāmiʿ al-bayān*

- **vv.1-3 divine aspects**: al-Ṭabarī treats *rabb / mālik / ilāh* as 3 sequential aspects with theological progression.
- **v.4 *al-waswās al-khannās***: al-Ṭabarī catalogues glosses; the dominant gloss treats al-waswās as the satanic whispering-agent who *retreats* (khannās) when God is mentioned. Cites Ibn ʿAbbās via Mujāhid. Some chains treat al-khannās as a specific name of Iblīs.
- **v.6 *mina l-jinnati wa-l-nās***: al-Ṭabarī catalogues 2 main glosses: (i) the whisperer-devils are of both jinn and human-kind; (ii) reading v.6 as identifying the *recipients* (mankind hears whispering from both sources) — treated as minority. The dominant gloss is the agent-typology reading.

## 2. al-Zamakhsharī — *al-Kashshāf*

- **3-tier divine aspect**: al-Zamakhsharī notes the *rabb-mālik-ilāh* sequence as a *kalāmī* progression covering all aspects of divine-mankind relation.
- **v.4-6 evil-mechanism**: al-Zamakhsharī treats wsws as covering both Iblīs-class jinn-whispering AND human-devil whispering (e.g., evil counsellors).
- **v.5 *yuwaswisu fī ṣudūri***: emphasizes the *internal* locus of the evil — distinguishing Q 114 from Q 113's *external* evils.

## 3. al-Rāzī — *Mafātīḥ al-ghayb*

- **3-tier divine aspect**: al-Rāzī develops the most extensive theological-taxonomic analysis. He treats *rabb* (lord, providential), *mālik* (king, sovereign-political), *ilāh* (god, worship-recipient) as the **complete formal definition of divine-mankind relation**.
- **Q 113 + Q 114 as complete istiʿādha**: al-Rāzī treats the muʿawwidhatān as covering external + internal evils; the 5 evil-types in Q 113 vv.2-5 + 1 evil-type in Q 114 v.4 + mechanism v.5 + source v.6 = comprehensive refuge-formula.

## 4. al-Qurṭubī — *al-Jāmiʿ li-aḥkām al-Qurʾān*

- **fadāʾil**: catalogues muʿawwidhatān-recitation traditions.
- ***al-jinnati wa-l-nās***: al-Qurṭubī defends the gloss that the whisperers include human-devils (corrupt counsellors who whisper deceits in others' breasts) — a moral-pedagogical reading.

## 5. Ibn Kathīr — *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿaẓīm*

- **Defense against Ibn Masʿūd omission**: Ibn Kathīr provides the most extensive discussion of the Ibn Masʿūd reports (which suggest Ibn Masʿūd's muṣḥaf omitted Q 113 + Q 114). Ibn Kathīr:
  1. Documents the reports.
  2. Notes that the chains for the omission are **graded *ḍaʿīf*** by his evaluation.
  3. Notes that the muʿawwidhatān are **canonical by ijmāʿ** (consensus) — every written muṣḥaf in transmitted tradition includes them; Ibn Masʿūd himself recited them.
  4. Argues that Ibn Masʿūd's hesitation may have been about whether they were specifically *protective formulae* meant for personal-recitation rather than canonical-revelation, not about their textual authenticity.
  5. Concludes the canonicity is unquestionable.
- ***al-waswās al-khannās***: Ibn Kathīr cites the al-Tirmidhī tradition (graded *ḥasan*) on the satanic-whisperer being seated at each son-of-Adam's heart, retreating when God is remembered.

## 6. al-Suyūṭī — *al-Durr al-manthūr* + *al-Itqān*

- **al-Durr al-manthūr**: extensive muʿawwidhatān fadāʾil compilation.
- **al-Itqān**: discusses the Ibn Masʿūd omission tradition; treats it as a curiosity, not a canonical-status threat. Notes ijmāʿ-based canonicity.

## 7. al-Biqāʿī — *Naẓm al-durar*

- **Munāsaba Q 113 → Q 114**: al-Biqāʿī treats Q 113 as covering external evils, Q 114 as covering internal evils. The pair is structurally complementary.
- **Internal munāsaba within Q 114**: 3 divine aspects (vv.1-3) + 3 evil-specifications (vv.4-6).

## 8. al-Ṭabarsī — *Majmaʿ al-bayān* (Imāmī)

- al-Ṭabarsī treats the surah as canonical; cites muʿawwidhatān-recitation traditions from Imāmī chains.
- **Imāmī-specific note**: some Imāmī sources treat Q 114 as paired with Q 113 specifically for protective-recitation; canonicity is not contested.

## 9. Convergence and disagreement

### Convergence (all 8+ mufassirūn agree)
- 3 divine aspects in vv.1-3.
- Internal-evil locus (vv.4-6).
- Q 113 + Q 114 form complete *istiʿādha* covering external + internal.
- Canonical status (despite Ibn Masʿūd reports).

### Disagreement
- ***al-jinnati wa-l-nās*** (v.6): agent-typology (whisperers are of both kinds) vs recipient-typology (whisperers reach both kinds). Agent-typology dominant.
- **Ibn Masʿūd omission tradition**: Ibn Kathīr defends canonicity strongly; al-Suyūṭī notes the tradition; some moderns engage with it more critically. All accept ijmāʿ-canonicity.
- **Meccan vs Medinan**: contested. Sorcery-asbāb (paired with Q 113) favors Medinan; simple-refuge content favors Meccan.

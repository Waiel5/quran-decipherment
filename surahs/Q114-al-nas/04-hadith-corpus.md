## 2. al-Tirmidhī on al-waswās al-khannās

### al-Tirmidhī
- **al-Tirmidhī k. tafsīr al-Qurʾān, b. min sūrat al-Nās** — chain via Anas b. Mālik: the satanic-whisperer is seated at each son-of-Adam's heart; when he remembers God, the whisperer retreats; when he is heedless, the whisperer whispers. Graded *ḥasan* by al-Tirmidhī.

### Aḥmad
- **al-Musnad** — multiple chains paralleling.

This tradition anchors v.4 *al-waswās al-khannās* as the **satanic-whisperer who retreats** at *dhikr Allāh*.

## 3. The Ibn Masʿūd muṣḥaf-omission tradition

Reports preserved across multiple collections and discussed in classical *Itqān*-genre literature (al-Suyūṭī, al-Zarkashī) and in Ibn Kathīr's tafsir:

### al-Bukhārī (catalogues)
- al-Bukhārī's catalogue of *masāḥif* notes the tradition of Ibn Masʿūd omitting *al-muʿawwidhatayn*.

### Aḥmad, al-Ṭabarānī
- Multiple chains preserve Ibn Masʿūd-related reports of his muṣḥaf-content.
- Most chains are graded *ḍaʿīf* by classical critics.

### Ibn Kathīr's defense (cited in tafsir, see `03-tafsir-survey.md` §5)
- Acknowledges the reports.
- Grades them *ḍaʿīf*.
- Documents ijmāʿ-canonicity.

**Verdict**: the omission-reports exist but are graded weak; the muʿawwidhatān are CANONICAL by ijmāʿ. See [[Q114-al-nas/05-classical-claims-audit|05-audit]] Claim 3.

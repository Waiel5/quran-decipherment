# Q 114 al-Nās — Content Analysis

## 1. Verse-by-verse summary

Source: `quran-no-tashkeel.json` Q114; cross-validated. QAC roots from `data/morphology/quranic-corpus-morphology-0.4.txt`.

### Verse 1 — *qul aʿūdhu bi-rabbi l-nās*
- **Roots**: qwl, Ew, rbb, nws.
- Refuge-formula head, identical structure to Q 113:1 except *l-falaq* → *l-nās*. The Lord-of-mankind framing is the first of 3 sequential divine aspects.

### Verse 2 — *maliki l-nās*
- **Roots**: mlk, nws.
- Second divine aspect: **King of mankind** (sovereign-political).

### Verse 3 — *ilāhi l-nās*
- **Roots**: Alh, nws.
- Third divine aspect: **God of mankind** (object-of-worship).

The 3 divine aspects vv.1-3 form a **theological progression**: Lord (rabb, providential) → King (mālik, sovereign) → God (ilāh, object-of-worship). al-Rāzī (*Mafātīḥ al-ghayb* on Q 114) treats this as a 3-tier *kalām* taxonomy of divine relation to mankind: lordship (creating + sustaining), sovereignty (ruling), divinity (worship-recipient).

### Verse 4 — *min sharri l-waswāsi l-khannās*
- **Roots**: ... (شر), wsws, xns.
- Refuge-target: **the retreating whisperer** (al-waswās al-khannās).
- *al-waswās*: the act/agent of whispering (intransitive); *al-khannās*: that which retreats / hides. Classical interpretation: a satanic / Iblīs-related figure who whispers temptations, retreats when God is mentioned.
- Both wsws and xns are corpus-rare: wsws appears 2× (Q 7:20, Q 20:120 for Iblīs's whispering, plus Q 114:5 derivation); xns appears only at Q 114:4 + Q 81:15 (different context, *al-khunnas*).

### Verse 5 — *alladhī yuwaswisu fī ṣudūri l-nās*
- **Roots**: wsws, Sdr, nws.
- Mechanism specification: the whispering occurs **in the breasts** (*ṣudūr*) of mankind.
- This verse anchors the surah's *INTERNAL-evil* status: the locus of the evil is *inside* the human breast — the seat of intentions, doubts, fears.

### Verse 6 — *mina l-jinnati wa-l-nās*
- **Roots**: jnn, nws.
- Source-typology: the whisperers are **of the jinn AND mankind**.
- This is doctrinally important: the whispering-evil source includes both supernatural (jinn) and natural (human) agents. al-Ṭabarī catalogues several glosses; the dominant reading treats v.6 as identifying the agent-class (jinn-devils + human-devils both whisper internally).

## 2. The internal-evil refuge-formula

Compared to Q 113 (external-evil typology), Q 114 is the **INTERNAL-evil refuge-formula**:

| Q 113 | Q 114 |
|:--|:--|
| External: cosmic darkness, magical attack, human envy | Internal: whispering in the breast |
| 4 evil-types | 1 evil-type with 3 specifications (mechanism + locus + source) |
| 1 divine aspect (rabb al-falaq) | 3 divine aspects (rabb, mālik, ilāh) |
| 5 verses | 6 verses |

**Together** Q 113 + Q 114 form a **complete refuge-formula** covering external + internal evils, with a 3-way divine-aspect framing (Q 114) and a 4-way evil-typology (Q 113). al-Rāzī treats this as a comprehensive *istiʿādha* (refuge-taking) covering all evil-domains.

## 3. Major thematic blocks

Q 114 has 3 thematic blocks:
- vv.1-3: divine-aspect declaration (3-tier — rabb / mālik / ilāh)
- v.4: evil-target (al-waswās al-khannās)
- vv.5-6: mechanism + source-typology (in the breasts; from jinn and mankind)

The 3-1-2 block-structure is asymmetric — divine-side gets 3 verses, evil-side gets 3 verses (target + mechanism + source).

## 4. Content register
- **creedal-ritual**: 100% (refuge-declaration); 100% ritual-formula
- **theological-taxonomic**: vv.1-3 contain a 3-tier divine-aspect taxonomy

## 5. Vocabulary distinctness — *al-nās* dominance

The root **nws** (people / mankind) is attested **5 times** in Q 114 — corpus-extreme density per surah for a single root in a short surah. Across the entire corpus, nws is among the corpus's top-50 roots; its concentration in Q 114 is a structural marker.

Other corpus-rare roots in Q 114: **wsws** (2×), **xns** (1× hapax-or-near; verb-root form *yuwaswisu* shares wsws root).

10 distinct roots in 15 root-tokens. The doubled wsws + 5× nws creates the surah's lexical signature.

## 6. Repetition patterns

- *al-nās* — 5 verse-final occurrences + 1 internal (*ṣudūri l-nās* in v.5) — 6 occurrences in a 20-word surah = **30% lexical density on a single word**.
- This is the corpus's highest single-word lexical-density in any short surah.
- The Q 114 *al-nās*-density is structurally analogous to Q 109 al-Kāfirūn's *aʿbudu*-density and Q 55 al-Raḥmān's refrain-density — but with much higher base lexical-density.

## 7. Cross-surah content references

- **Q 7:20**, **Q 20:120** — wsws root attestations: Iblīs/Satan whispering temptations (to Adam/Hawa).
- **Q 81:15** — xns-root attestation in different context (*al-khunnas* = the receding stars).
- **Q 6:112** — *shayāṭīn al-ins wa-l-jinn* (devils of mankind and jinn) — direct parallel to Q 114:6 *al-jinnati wa-l-nās*.
- **Q 11:118-119, Q 12:53, Q 50:16, Q 33:53** — various *ṣadr/ṣudūr* (breast/breasts) references — interior locus of feelings/intentions.

## 8. Honest limits

1. **The wsws-as-Satanic-whispering interpretation** is classical but not exhaustively-prescribed in the verse text. Some commentators distinguish satanic-whispering from psychological-whispering; the verse is doctrinally compatible with both.
2. **The Ibn Masʿūd muṣḥaf-omission tradition** (treated in `05-audit.md` Claim 3) is doctrinally significant; chain-quality is graded *ḍaʿīf* by Ibn Kathīr.
3. **The 100% monorhyme via *al-nās*-repetition** (rather than rhyme-cluster) is a distinctive structural feature; classical scholars (al-Rāzī) treat the repetition itself as a *taʾkīd* (emphasis) device.

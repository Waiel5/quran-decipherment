## Claim 2 — Q 114 = INTERNAL-evil refuge (paired with Q 113 EXTERNAL-evil)

### The claim
al-Biqāʿī (*Naẓm al-durar*), al-Rāzī (*Mafātīḥ al-ghayb*): Q 113 covers external evils (cosmic darkness, magic, envy); Q 114 covers internal evils (whispering in the breast). The pair is structurally complementary.

### Empirical test
Verify the content-typology — Q 114 v.5 explicitly *fī ṣudūri l-nās* (in the breasts of mankind) — is the locus an internal locus?

### Result
- v.5 *yuwaswisu fī ṣudūri l-nās* — the verb wsws (whisper) takes locative *fī ṣudūri* (in the breasts) — the locus is internal-corporeal-affective.
- Q 113's evil-targets (vv.2-5) all reference external loci (creation in general, cosmic darkness, knot-blowing sorceress, envying person).
- Q 114's evil-target (vv.4-6) is specifically internal (in the breasts) and source-typed (jinn + mankind).

### Verdict: **VINDICATED** at content-typology level.
